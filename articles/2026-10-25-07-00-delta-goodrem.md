**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem), 41, returned to [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) on Saturday 24 October after missing week four with a fractured rib.
- In her first in-depth interview since the injury, she said it seemed to come from repeated lifts in rehearsal.
- She and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) danced a rumba to [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion)'s "It's All Coming Back To Me Now" with a rib-friendly routine.
- The Independent reports BBC rules let a contestant skip only one week before having to withdraw.

Delta Goodrem says she never thought about quitting Strictly, even as she waited on scan results and watched last week's show from her living room. The Australian singer was back on the BBC One ballroom floor on Saturday night, partnered again with Kai Widdrington, and her account of how the injury happened is the clearest yet.

## How the injury happened

According to a report on [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/strictly-come-dancings-delta-goodrem-193620000.html), which carried a Mirror story, Goodrem said the rib problem seemed to come from a build-up of lifts. "We've been trying a lot of different lifts," she said, adding that it was a combination of things leading into the night of the show.

The timing made it sharper. On 10 October she and Widdrington topped the leaderboard with a disco-themed salsa that scored 33 points, the same report says. She described the mood that night as thrilling, then said she started to feel worse and "the body said, 'Sorry'". Within 48 hours, the report says, her place in the competition looked in doubt.

## From hospital to the rumba

Goodrem's own Instagram clips, described by [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/injured-strictly-star-delta-goodrem-34812994), begin with a hospital visit last Sunday, where she said the pain was making her feel nauseous. An MRI scan the next day showed a fractured rib, she relayed. Later footage shows a video call with Widdrington and a first walk on day six.

She wrote that she filmed the moments "to remind myself that even on the frustrating days, there would be progress". She also said she was "gutted" when it happened because she had been "truly loving this experience".

## What recovery looks like

Goodrem described a daily routine rather than a dramatic fix. She said she checks in with the physio every day, listens to the doctors and gets strapped up before heading out. It is, she said, a new injury for her, and she is learning how to help her body get back up and moving.

AOL reports she wore bandages decorated with Strictly sparkles, and that she and Widdrington had only limited training time this week. Widdrington said the choreography was edited to be more "rib friendly", with no "crazy lifts" or deep back bends that could strain the injury. He said the aim was still to shine and find a moment in the routine.

## Why this return mattered

[The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/strictly-week-5-bbc-delta-goodrem-b3072724.html) notes that BBC rules allow a contestant to miss only one week before withdrawing. Goodrem had used hers. All 12 remaining contestants appear in week five, which airs at 6.30pm on BBC One.

Goodrem said that sitting out fired her up more. She told the AOL interviewer that she was in "waves of tears" on It Takes Two after messages of support arrived ahead of her return. In a separate Instagram post she wrote that she was heading into show day "grateful" to be able to take a few more steps, and signed off with #Teamdeltakai.

Fans responded warmly. Wales Online quotes comments calling her a "warrior" and an inspiration, though those are reader reactions, not a medical or competition update.

## What happens next

The rumba is done, but the rib is not mentioned as healed in any of the sources, and none gives a recovery timeline. Widdrington said they will keep monitoring it and adjusting while trying to pick up points from the judges. AOL's related headlines suggest [Craig Revel Horwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Revel_Horwood) had critical words about the return, but the full judges' scores were not in the sources we read.
