**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) thriller April X is heading to HBO Max in December, after a limited US cinema run that began on 2 October.
- Its IMDb score has fallen from 6.1 out of 10 in early October to 4.9 as of 20 November, according to figures cited by The Tab.
- Some [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) fans claim supporters of co-star [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) are review-bombing the film.
- No verified evidence supports that claim, and the screenshot at the centre of it could not be independently checked.

The streaming date for April X has landed alongside an online argument. Fans of Connor Storrie and Hudson Williams, the two leads of Heated Rivalry, are disputing why the actor's new film has lost ground on review sites, according to a report by [The Tab](https://thetab.com/filmshrine/2026/11/20/heated-rivalry-fans-war-connor-storrie-april-x-hudson-williams-fans-review-bombing-claims/) published on 20 November.

## What is confirmed about April X

April X is a psychological sci-fi thriller directed by Michel K. Parandi and starring Storrie. The Tab reports that its HBO Max debut was announced on 19 November, with the film opening in December. It had a limited theatrical release in the US on 2 October. We covered the release plans in our earlier post on [April X and its HBO Max date](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-april-x-hbo-max/).

The announcement put the film back in front of the Heated Rivalry audience, which is where the argument started.

## The ratings drop

The numbers are the firmest part of the story. The Tab, citing Film Draft, says April X sat at 6.1 out of 10 on IMDb at the start of October. By 20 November it had dropped to 4.9, a fall of 1.2 points.

The decline began at the start of November, the report says, which is a month after the film opened in cinemas. That timing matters, because it leaves open several explanations, including ordinary audience reaction as more people saw the film.

[Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) tells a similar story. The audience score is 53%, while the critics' score is 29%. Some fans point to the critics' figure because professional reviewers are not part of any fan campaign. One fan quoted by The Tab, who says they like both actors, argued that the film simply is not good.

## The disputed screenshot

The review-bombing claim rests largely on a screenshot circulating on X. It appears to show an Instagram Story from April X producer Lavinia Postolache. In it, a message from an unnamed Heated Rivalry fan says the rating drop is a coordinated effort by Williams' fans. Beneath it, Postolache appears to have written that she does not know why the ratings are so unfair, that she is proud of the film and that people should watch it and make up their own minds.

The Tab says the Story is no longer on her page, so it could not independently verify the screenshot. It also stresses there is no verified evidence that Williams' supporters are responsible. Treat the claim as unconfirmed.

Reaction online has been split. Some users blamed one side of the fandom, and others blamed the other. Comments are switched off on most of Postolache's recent Instagram posts, though one open post drew a user asking why she was dragging someone's name into it.

## A fandom already under strain

The row sits on top of older tension. Heated Rivalry made Storrie and Williams globally famous after its 2025 release, with the pair playing Ilya and Shane. The Tab says parts of the fandom have turned hostile, with some devoted supporters setting the two actors against each other despite their close friendship off screen.

In March, several cast members including Williams shared a statement rejecting abusive and bigoted behaviour from people who call themselves fans. It said none of them needed "hateful 'love'". The same statement said the cast respected and supported one another.

## What happens next

April X arrives on HBO Max in December, and a wider streaming audience could move the scores again, in either direction. Neither Storrie, Williams nor IMDb has been reported as commenting on the claims, and the producer's alleged post has disappeared. Until there is firmer evidence, the ratings fall remains a fact and its cause remains a guess. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has April X been review-bombed?

That is unproven. IMDb's score has fallen from 6.1 to 4.9 since early October, but The Tab says there is no verified evidence that any group of fans caused it.

### When can I watch April X?

It is due to stream on HBO Max in December. It opened in limited US cinemas on 2 October.

### What do critics think of April X?

Rotten Tomatoes lists a critics' score of 29% and an audience score of 53%, according to The Tab.
