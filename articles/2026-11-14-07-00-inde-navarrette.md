**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) says the blonde hair in her Universal Studios photos was "100% a wig".
- The bleached eyebrows are real, she told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) at the GQ Men of the Year event.
- She also said she does not believe Rogue will be blonde in Marvel's X-Men reboot.
- The film is due in cinemas on May 5, 2028, according to FandomWire and ComingSoon.net.

Inde Navarrette has cleared up the internet's latest theory about her future as Rogue. The *Obsession* star told Entertainment Tonight that the bleach-blonde look in recent photos from Universal Studios was a wig worn so she could enjoy a day out unnoticed, not a hint about Marvel's X-Men casting.

## Why the Inde Navarrette blonde wig made headlines

Photos of the actress posing beside Optimus Prime at Universal Studios spread online this week. They showed light hair and pale eyebrows, a sharp change from the brown hair and white streak fans associate with Rogue, [according to FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/x-men-inde-navarrette-debunks-blonde-rogue-rumor/).

Speaking to ET at the GQ Men of the Year event in Los Angeles, Navarrette said she wanted to visit the park with friends without being recognised, so they put a wig on. [ComingSoon.net reported](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2223056-inde-navarrette-blonde-makeover-x-men-wig-reveal) her saying the hair was "laid very beautifully", while the brows were a real and, in her words, fun change. The photo with Optimus Prime, she added, came about because her friends asked who would not want that picture.

The plan had an obvious flaw. A disguise meant to keep her out of the spotlight pulled her straight into it, as fans studied every detail of the images.

## What she said about a blonde Rogue

Asked directly whether the new colour pointed to a different look for Rogue, Navarrette was clear. "I can confirm that I don't believe that Rogue is going to be a blonde. Unfortunately. Sorry!" she said, as quoted by FandomWire.

That will please comic readers. FandomWire notes that the white streak is among the character's most recognisable features, and that rumours suggest the team will wear comic-accurate costumes. That is talk, not a studio announcement, so treat it with care.

Navarrette was announced as Rogue at Disney's D23 event in August, ComingSoon.net says. The film is directed by [Jake Schreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Schreier). The cast includes [Sadie Sink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadie_Sink) as Jean Grey, [Kit Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kit_Connor) as Cyclops, [Christopher Abbott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Abbott) as Professor X, [Maya Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Boyd) as Storm and [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) as Mister Sinister. ComingSoon.net also lists Samara Weaving as Emma Frost.

## Working on the accent and the role

FandomWire reports that at TIFF she tried Rogue's "Sugar" catchphrase for eTalk and admitted the Southern accent still needs work, saying she would talk to the Marvel team about the approach. On THR's Awards Chatter podcast she said she wants to pay respect to the writers while adding "my own sauce". She also praised Anna Paquin's earlier take on the character, which FandomWire says she called an honour to carry forward.

## The hardest scene in Obsession

Navarrette is 25 and has been in demand since Curry Barker's horror film *Obsession*, ComingSoon.net says. Bloody Disgusting, [citing an Elle interview](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3975688/obsession-inde-navarrette-hardest-scene-nikki-trivia/), reports that the toughest scene was not one of the possessed sequences. It was the moment in the trivia bar when her character Nikki returns to her real self.

"I had spent so much time playing 'Wish' Nikki that going into real Nikki was really, really difficult," she said. Bloody Disgusting adds that it is too early to say whether the performance will draw Golden Globe or Academy Award attention.

## A busy few weeks

The wig story lands in the middle of a run of appearances. She hosted *Saturday Night Live* on Halloween night, which a [State Times review](https://thestatetimes.com/2026/11/13/inde-navarrette-hosts-snl-for-the-first-time/) described as showing a more comedic side, with sketches including "Paw Patrol: Special Victims Unit". Reaction was not unanimous: the reviewer notes some viewers felt she fell flat in places. You can read more in our look at [her SNL Halloween debut](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-snl-halloween/) and her [GQ Men of the Year outfit](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-gq-men-of-the-year/).
