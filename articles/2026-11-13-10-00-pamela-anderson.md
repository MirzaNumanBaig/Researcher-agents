**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) attended the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards in New York, according to Hollywood PQ.
- The ceremony took place at the American Museum of Natural History and was hosted by [Keke Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keke_Palmer).
- [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon) attended with his 20-year-old daughter Isabella and helped present an award to Ralph Lauren.
- [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore) wore a denim dress by Zac Posen for GapStudio and presented the Innovation Award to Gap Inc.

Pamela Anderson was among the guests at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards in New York, a night that also brought out Matt Damon, Demi Moore and a long list of film, music and fashion names. Hollywood PQ reported on the evening on 11 November, describing a red carpet full of bold outfits at one of the American fashion calendar's key events.

## A star-heavy night at the museum

The annual awards honour American designers and were held at the American Museum of Natural History. Keke Palmer hosted, and the guest list also included [Hailey Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey_Bieber), Zoë Kravitz and [Megan Thee Stallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Thee_Stallion), according to Hollywood PQ.

For Anderson, the appearance fits a pattern the outlet describes as a high-profile return to major events. It points to her acclaimed performance in The Last Showgirl as the turning point in a career that began with Baywatch. Hollywood PQ also notes that she often attends big events without makeup, and says her New York appearance showed renewed interest from designers. That is the outlet's view rather than a statement from Anderson. Her official channels, including her [foundation website](https://www.pamelaandersonfoundation.org/), carry her own updates.

You can find more of our coverage on the [Pamela Anderson tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/pamela-anderson/).

## Matt Damon and his daughter

Matt Damon, known for [Good Will Hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting) and the Jason Bourne films, walked the red carpet with his daughter Isabella, who is 20. Hollywood PQ says she recently signed with IMG Models and wore a vintage-inspired silver dress, while Damon chose a black suit. During the ceremony he helped present the American Menswear Designer of the Year award to Ralph Lauren.

## Demi Moore's denim moment

Demi Moore wore a dress made entirely of denim, created by Zac Posen for GapStudio. The choice carried meaning: she also helped present the Innovation Award to Gap Inc. In her remarks she spoke of a personal connection to the brand and a shared memory with her daughter, [Rumer Willis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rumer_Willis), the outlet reports. Hollywood PQ links her appearance to the renewed attention around her film The Substance.

## Gold for Saint Laurent

Zoë Kravitz, Hailey Bieber and Rosé all wore gold and together presented the International Designer of the Year award to Anthony Vaccarello of Saint Laurent. [Giancarlo Esposito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giancarlo_Esposito), best known as Gustavo Fring in [Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad) and [Better Call Saul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul), was also among the guests.

## What happens next

Hollywood PQ's report does not mention any new project for Anderson, so the appearance is best read as a red carpet moment rather than a career announcement.
