**Quick summary**

- The Matrix films are charting in the top 10 in a handful of countries on streaming, according to Collider.
- The films are available free on Pluto TV, the outlet says.
- Warner Bros. has announced a new Matrix movie for after 2027, with Drew Goddard writing, per Collider.
- Collider reports that [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is not expected to return as Neo.
- Film writers are also revisiting his back catalogue, from Shakespeare to action.

Keanu Reeves Matrix streaming is climbing the charts again, and the star who made Neo famous may not be part of what comes next. [Collider reported on 14 November](https://collider.com/keanu-reeves-matrix-movies-streaming-success-pluto-tv-november-2026/) that the series is in the top 10 in several countries, while Reeves is not expected in the planned Matrix 5.

## Why Keanu Reeves Matrix streaming is back in the top 10

According to Collider, the films are "taking over streaming globally", charting in a handful of countries. The outlet points readers to the free catalogue on Pluto TV. It did not publish chart positions or viewing figures, so the size of the surge is unclear.

The timing is notable because the last new film, the 2021 sequel that Collider says opened in cinemas and on HBO Max on the same day, is now years behind us. The original 1999 film by the Wachowskis won four Academy Awards, including Visual Effects and Film Editing, the same report notes.

## What the new Matrix film means for Neo

Collider says Warner Bros. announced earlier this year that a new Matrix movie is in development, due after 2027. Drew Goddard is writing the script. Plot details are being kept under wraps.

The report adds that Reeves is not expected back, which Collider calls a blow to fans who have followed him as Neo for more than 25 years. That is the outlet's reading, not a statement from the actor or the studio, so treat it as unconfirmed until either side says more.

## Reeves beyond the Matrix

Collider also notes that Reeves earlier this year starred with Cameron Diaz and Matt Bomer in the Apple TV film Outcome, written and directed by Jonah Hill. The outlet says it is still in the top 10 in a few countries. It adds there is no word on whether he will return in the Cyberpunk 2077 sequel being developed at CD Projekt Red, where he voiced Johnny Silverhand.

His older work is getting fresh attention too. SlashFilm [argues that Kenneth Branagh's 1993 Much Ado About Nothing](https://www.slashfilm.com/2307161/keanu-reeves-denzel-washington-90s-shakespeare-movie-much-ado-about-nothing/) deserves a second look. The cast includes [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington), [Robert Sean Leonard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Sean_Leonard), [Emma Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thompson), [Kate Beckinsale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Beckinsale) and [Michael Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Keaton). Reeves plays the villainous Don John. The site is candid that he struggles with the verse, though it gives the film a 90% Rotten Tomatoes score from 48 reviews.

In a separate piece, SlashFilm says the new film How to Rob a Bank [name-checks Point Break](https://www.slashfilm.com/2315729/how-to-rob-a-bank-theme-90s-keanu-reeves-movie-point-break/), with an FBI agent saying that film made him want to join the Bureau. Meanwhile [Comic Book Resources ranked his ten best action films](https://www.cbr.com/keanu-reeves-greatest-action-movies-of-all-time-list/), noting that The Matrix Reloaded's freeway chase was filmed on a purpose-built 1.5-mile road on a disused naval base in California.

## What happens next

Nothing about Reeves' Matrix future is confirmed. Warner Bros. has not, based on the sources read, named a cast or release date for the new film. John Wick remains his main franchise, and Collider says there is still work for him there.
