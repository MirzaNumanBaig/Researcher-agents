**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) wore voluminous grey trousers with a tailored blazer at a night-time appearance in New York on Tuesday.
- Harper's Bazaar, as reported by CWIBC, listed a pie-crust-collar blouse, black loafers and a velvet Chanel handbag as the rest of the outfit.
- Grazia Magazine described the trousers as part of a fashion direction influenced by modern sculpture.
- The shape appeared in Balenciaga and Celine's Spring/Summer 2027 collections, according to the same reports.

[Nicole Kidman](https://nicolekidmanofficial.com/) brought one of the season's more experimental silhouettes to the street in New York on Tuesday, pairing wide, elongated grey trousers with a structured suit jacket. Fashion coverage of the outing, summarised by the news site CWIBC, says the look carries the sculptural tailoring seen on Spring/Summer 2027 runways into everyday evening dressing.

## What Nicole Kidman wore

According to Harper's Bazaar, as cited by CWIBC, the outfit was built around gray trousers with a generous volume. On top she wore a tailored grey blazer and a feminine blouse with a pie-crust collar, a softly frilled neckline. Polished black loafers finished the lower half, and a velvet Chanel handbag completed the ensemble.

The trousers were the focal point. Their length and width show how unconventional tailoring can still look elegant without the close fit that formal trousers usually rely on.

Kidman has worn the house before. Our earlier report covers [Nicole Kidman in Chanel trousers at a Sydney show](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-chanel-trousers/).

## Why the proportions work

The reports credit the contrast between the pieces. The structured blazer gives definition around the shoulders, while the delicate blouse adds a softer note against the volume below. That balance, the coverage argues, keeps oversized trousers looking polished rather than shapeless.

Grazia Magazine also picked out the outfit. It described the trousers as part of an emerging direction shaped by modern sculpture, and said Kidman's appearance moved the experimental proportions of the catwalk into ordinary celebrity wardrobes.

## The runway link: Balenciaga and Celine

The reports trace the trend to two Spring/Summer 2027 shows. At Balenciaga, designers worked with rounded, oversized trousers in earthy colours. Harper's Bazaar compared those shapes with the abstract, organic forms of British sculptor [Barbara Hepworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Hepworth).

Celine took a different route, with fluid fabrics and flowing proportions. The designs recalled the reflective surfaces and monumental curves of Anish Kapoor's Cloud Gate installation, according to Harper's Bazaar. Both houses leaned on wide legs and extended hems, but kept the cut wearable.

## How the magazines suggest styling the shape

Harper's Bazaar offers a few ways to wear the look. For evening, it points to silk camisoles, statement chandelier earrings and slingback shoes. Pointed pumps and a cinching belt can add definition at the waist.

For daytime, the publication suggests swapping the formal pieces for cashmere knitwear, trainers and a trench coat. Grazia also recommends fine cashmere sweaters to balance the volume. A carefully tucked-in sweater emphasises the waistline, and an oversized coat works when its length suits the trousers.

The source reports do not say which event Kidman attended, so the occasion behind the outfit is unconfirmed.
