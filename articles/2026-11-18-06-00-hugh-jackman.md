**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been announced as the next actor to play [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to LBC.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) is confirmed as director; no release date or other casting has been shared.
- LBC's roundup of actors who passed on 007 includes [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman).
- The report says Jackman declined a 2002 offer from his agent while he was working on X-Men 2.

The news that Robert Pattinson will play James Bond has sent attention back to the actors who could have had the part, and Hugh Jackman is on that list. According to [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/actors-turned-down-james-bond-5HjdmSj_2/), the Australian star turned down a 2002 approach because he was busy with the X-Men films and feared the role would close other doors.

## What was announced

LBC reports that Pattinson, 40, said he "couldn't be more excited" about a production that is yet to be named. It will be the first Bond film made with Amazon MGM Studios. Villeneuve is confirmed as director, but no further casting or release date has been announced.

Pattinson was described as a relatively late rumour. Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill) were among those tipped as frontrunners before the announcement. He follows [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) in the role.

## Where Jackman fits in

LBC says history is full of false starts around the franchise, with many well-known actors reportedly turning the part down. Jackman's entry is short. The outlet says he had the chance to audition but declined the offer his agent brought him in 2002, because he was involved with X-Men 2 at the time.

His reasoning, as LBC puts it, was a worry about the future. Jackman feared that committing to Bond would leave him unable to take roles beyond the big franchises. The report does not say how far any talks went, and it does not quote him directly, so the account is best read as a recollection of an old decision, not new comment from the actor.

## He was not alone

The same piece lists a string of other names. [Christian Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bale) was reportedly less keen when offered the chance to replace [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), and went on to play Bruce Wayne in [Batman Begins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Begins). Liam Neeson was in the frame in the early 1990s to succeed [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton). Clint Eastwood and Burt Reynolds each reportedly passed on Diamonds Are Forever, saying Bond should be British.

The roundup also notes that Ewan McGregor ruled himself out over typecasting fears, a concern close to Jackman's own. Several of these accounts are described as reported, not confirmed, and LBC itself notes conflicting versions in some cases.

## What happens next

Amazon MGM Studios has yet to confirm a title or release date, so the next step is more casting news around Pattinson. Jackman, meanwhile, kept to the path he chose.
