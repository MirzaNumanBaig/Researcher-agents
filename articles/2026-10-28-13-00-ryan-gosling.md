**Quick summary**

- Dead Man's Bones, the band of [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) and writer-producer Zach Shields, released "Lost in the Night" on Monday night.
- It is the group's first song in 17 years, according to Yahoo's report.
- [Eva Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Mendes) called it her new favourite song on Instagram and said Gosling sang it to her around 2012.
- The band says "more coming soon", though no album has been announced in the sources reviewed.

Ryan Gosling's band Dead Man's Bones has ended a long silence with a new single, "Lost in the Night", released on Monday night with a music video. Yahoo reports it is the duo's first new song in 17 years, and the person cheering loudest was Gosling's wife, Eva Mendes.

## What Dead Man's Bones released

The single arrived with a video featuring "The Summer I Turned Pretty" star [Rain Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_Spencer) and "Hamnet" actor [Noah Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Jupe). According to the same report, the song introduces a new sound from Gosling and Shields.

The label's description, quoted by Yahoo, says the pair tried several Los Angeles studios without finding the sound they wanted. They then took two guitars, two drum machines and two synthesizers to a farm outside the city and recorded the song in a day. Anti- Records called the result a "shadowy but undeniably hopeful homemade anthem" that hints at a new direction.

In an Instagram post, the band wrote: "Thanks for sticking with us all these years. New single, Lost in the Night. More coming soon."

## How Eva Mendes reacted

Mendes shared the song on Instagram on Monday, calling it "hauntingly romantic" and saying it was her new favourite. A day later she posted a fan video that set clips of her and Gosling from their 2012 drama "The Place Beyond the Pines" to the track.

Her caption explained the link. She wrote that it was "crazy" the fan had picked that period, because Gosling sang her the song during that time and she asked him to record it. "It's been haunting me ever since," she added.

## A band with a cult following

Dead Man's Bones released its self-titled debut album in 2009. Nearly two decades on, Yahoo says it has more than 430,000 monthly listeners on Spotify, a sizeable audience for a band that has been quiet for so long.

Gosling spoke about the project earlier this year while promoting [Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary), when British magazine NME asked about it. He said he and Shields formed the band after bonding over Disneyland's Haunted Mansion ride, and that he liked Disney's particular approach to spooky material.

He also described how the first record was made. He and Shields played all the instruments, he said, with the caveat that it was "true but not true", since others came in to help. The aim, in his words, was to play things they did not know how to play.
