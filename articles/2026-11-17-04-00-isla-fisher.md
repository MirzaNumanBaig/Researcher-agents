**Quick summary**

- [Isla Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_Fisher) is among the names set to headline Tribeca Festival Lisboa, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- The third Portugal edition of the festival runs from 9 to 13 December in Lisbon.
- Bob Odenkirk, Alec Baldwin, Zosia Mamet, Edward Burns, Zach Woods and W. Kamau Bell are also on the headline list.
- The festival moves to the Avenida da Liberdade this year and adds a live showcase called Tribeca Night Live.

Isla Fisher is one of the stars booked to headline Tribeca Festival Lisboa, the Portuguese spinoff of the New York festival, which opens in Lisbon next month. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/bob-odenkirk-alec-baldwin-tribeca-festival-portugal-edition-1236755048/) reported on 16 November that the third annual edition will run from 9 to 13 December, with film screenings and conversations built around its guests.

## Who is headlining Tribeca Festival Lisboa

Alongside Fisher, the trade outlet lists [Bob Odenkirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Odenkirk), [Alec Baldwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Baldwin), [Zosia Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zosia_Mamet), [Edward Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Burns), [Zach Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Woods) and [W. Kamau Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._Kamau_Bell). The report does not say which film or event Fisher will attend, so her exact role in the programme is not yet public.

The details it does give are for her fellow guests. Odenkirk will be in Lisbon on 11 December for a premiere of the documentary Bob and David Climb Machu Picchu, which he made with [David Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cross) and which played at Tribeca in New York in June 2026. Woods is bringing The Accompanist, his first feature as a director, and Burns will attend a screening of Finnegan's Foursome, which he directed and stars in.

## The films on the programme

The lineup mixes titles from Tribeca's New York edition with award winners from other festivals. According to The Hollywood Reporter, these include Sundance winner Josephine, starring [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum) and [Gemma Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Chan), and Berlinale Silver Bear winner Queen at Sea. The latter is directed by [Lance Hammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Hammer) and stars [Juliette Binoche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliette_Binoche) and [Tom Courtenay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Courtenay).

Other titles heading over from New York include Here I'm Alive, from director [Joshua Z. Weinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joshua_Z_Weinstein), and Rachel Rose's The Last Day, a modern take on Virginia Woolf's Mrs Dalloway. As in earlier years, the festival will also screen Portuguese features chosen by curator Rui Tendinha, among them O Dia Mais Feliz da Minha Vida and the documentary Mulherzinha.

## A new home and a new live strand

This year the festival leaves Lisbon's Beato Innovation District for the Avenida da Liberdade, the city's main tree-lined boulevard. Screenings will be held at venues including Cinema São Jorge, Teatro Tivoli and Capitólio.

The programme also introduces Tribeca Night Live, a showcase of comedy, conversation, music and live performance. Bell, actress Daniela Ruah and Portuguese comedian Luís Franco-Bastos will host the evenings. The festival has also planned Xmas with Mozart, marking 270 years since the composer's birth, and an audiovisual set by Portuguese electronic musician Moullinex.

Festival director Cara Cusumano said it was meaningful to see the festival's spirit taking root in Lisbon after a strong year in New York. Tribeca was founded in 2003 by Robert De Niro, Jane Rosenthal and Craig Hatkoff, and it recently extended its partnership with the Impresa Group to run the Portugal edition for three more years, to 2028.
