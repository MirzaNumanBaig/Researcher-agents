**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan)'s new album is called *She's the Best*, according to a Popzine write-up published on 8 November 2026.
- The album is described as mixing the high of nights out with loneliness and a longing for a relationship that has not happened yet.
- "FF" is the main single, the title track is a tribute to trans women and drag queens, and "Party" is the euphoric track.
- Popzine says its piece draws on supplied promotional material and is not an independent review of the recordings.

Troye Sivan's follow-up to *Something to Give Each Other* now has a shape. Popzine, the Swedish music site, published a portrait of *She's the Best* on 8 November 2026, describing an album that holds the thrill of a big night out and the loneliness that can sit behind it. The outlet is clear that it worked from supplied marketing material, so what follows is how the album is being presented, not a verdict on how it sounds.

## An album built on contrasts

Popzine describes the record as a single painting in which different moods sit side by side. Euphoria does not cancel out loneliness, the write-up says, and reflection does not rule out pleasure. That makes the project more than a collection of party songs: it is pitched as a portrait of the Australian artist's personal life as well as his nights out.

## The three songs that frame it

"FF" is named as the main single. Popzine says it is a love song to someone Sivan has not met yet, which turns the usual heartbreak premise around and points it at a future relationship. Gordon von Steiner is credited as director of its music video.

The title track, "She's the Best", is described as an affectionate thank-you to the women who have shaped Sivan's life and work, especially trans women and drag queens. The write-up calls it tender and playful, and says it gives the album an outward-looking side.

"Party" is the hypnotic, euphoric one, built around the feeling of a night that could last forever. According to the material, it takes inspiration from Madonna's "Music", which Popzine dates to 2000. Set against "FF", the two songs sit at opposite ends: one is about a shared moment, the other about a closeness that so far exists only in imagination.

## Who made it

Sivan has a producer role on the project and, per the supplied text, recorded much of his own vocal work in his home studio. Popzine lists [Jack Antonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Antonoff), [George Daniel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Daniel), Cirkut, Leland and Oscar Görres among the other contributors.

The write-up places the album after *Something to Give Each Other*, released in 2023, and recalls "Rush", "One of Your Girls" and "[Got Me Started](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Got_Me_Started)" from that record. It also mentions the North American SWEAT tour, where Sivan and Charli xcx shared the headline spot.
