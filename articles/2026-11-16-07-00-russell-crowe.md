**Quick summary**

- Edmonton Oilers goalie Devon Levi stopped all 19 shots in a 1-0 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs on Saturday, his first NHL shutout.
- Asked about touching the ice with his bare hand before games, Levi pointed to a scene in *Gladiator*.
- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) answered on X on Sunday, writing "Strength and Honour young man."
- Levi is 9-1 in his first 10 starts for the Oilers, according to NHL.com.

[Russell Crowe](https://x.com/russellcrowe) has given a hockey goalie his blessing. After Edmonton Oilers netminder Devon Levi explained that a pregame habit comes partly from *Gladiator*, Crowe shared the interview clip on X on Sunday, 15 November, with a line straight out of the film: "Strength and Honour young man. Devon Levi from the Edmonton Oilers."

## What Levi said about the ritual

Levi made 19 saves in a 1-0 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs on Saturday night, the first shutout of his NHL career. In the postgame interview, he was asked about a routine fans had noticed: he removes his blocker and presses his right hand to the ice before the game.

According to [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/topic/short-shifts/russell-crowe-gladiator-edmonton-oilers-devon-levi), Levi described it as "a little ritual just to connect with the ice" that "locks me in". He said he likes the feeling of the cold. He then pointed to the film. In *Gladiator*, he said, there is a scene where Crowe's character Maximus picks up sand and plays with it, and it has always stayed with him. He was careful not to overstate it: he told reporters he does not know if that is why he started, but it was a scene he thought of.

Bleacher Report says Levi gave the answer to Sportsnet. [Daily Hive](https://dailyhive.com/edmonton/oilers-levi-gladiator-themed-shoutout-russell-crowe) adds that the habit began at the start of this season, and that Levi has also been seen meditating on his knees during stoppages to reset his focus.

## Crowe's reply

Crowe, who won an Oscar for playing Maximus in the 2000 epic, posted the clip with his short message. According to NHL.com, *Gladiator* also won Best Picture. Crowe's reply quoted the film's tone rather than adding commentary, and both Daily Hive and [House of Hockey](https://www.houseofhockey.net/nhl-team/edmonton-oilers/famous-actor-russell-crowe-responds-to-devon-levi-s-latest-gesture) reported that the actor had noticed the habit after Levi linked it to the sand scene.

## The numbers behind the moment

The shout-out landed on a strong night. Levi is 24 and in his fourth NHL season, his first with the Oilers, per NHL.com. He is 9-1 in 10 starts for the Pacific Division leaders, with a 2.48 goals-against average. NHL.com puts his save percentage at .906, which Bleacher Report says is on pace for a career high, as is his goals-against average.

Daily Hive reports that the 9-1-0 mark is a franchise record for the best first 10 games of a career with Edmonton. That context explains why a goalie ritual became a story: the results gave it an audience.

## What happens next

Levi will keep playing as the Oilers' season continues, and the ritual is likely to be watched closely at his next starts. Crowe has not said anything further beyond his post.
