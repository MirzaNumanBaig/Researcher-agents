**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) says his wife, Sofia Wellesley, was disappointed when he lost the *Celebrity Traitors* final, because she assumed he had won.
- He told the Dish from Waitrose podcast he kept his result secret from his family while the show was filmed over three weeks.
- Blunt, 52, played as a Faithful and jokes his habit of wrong guesses will be studied "for years to come".
- He says the show has made him so recognisable that he struggled to leave Paddington Station.

[James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has revealed that his wife was convinced he had won [*Celebrity Traitors*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors), and was visibly let down when the final proved otherwise. The "[You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful)" singer shared the story on the Dish from Waitrose podcast, as reported by [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/tv/news/james-blunts-wife-thought-won-celebrity-traitors-1454212.html) on 25 November.

## Why James Blunt's wife expected a Celebrity Traitors win

Blunt said he told nobody how he had done. The show is filmed over three weeks, so contestants who return home after a few days have usually gone out early. His family, he explained, could guess he had lasted "a healthy period of time".

That made the final harder to watch. According to Female First, Blunt was banished in the End Game, where Romesh Ranganathan and Sebastian "Bash" Croft went on to win. He says his wife, Sofia Wellesley, looked at him afterwards and said she had thought he had won. "She literally looks down like, you lost," he joked, adding that at least he is good at keeping a secret.

## A strategy "studied for years to come"

Blunt played as a Faithful and, by his own account, was wrong almost daily. He said he would announce a theory about who the Traitors were, the group would banish that person, and he would apologise before choosing someone new the next morning.

"How can you get it wrong literally every day?" he asked, saying researchers would be looking at his approach for years. The self-mocking tone matches the rest of his podcast appearance.

## Fame after the series

The bigger surprise for Blunt has been the reaction from the public. He described stepping off a train at Paddington Station and finding he could not leave, because so many people wanted selfies and answers about the show. His laces were undone and he could not stop to tie them, so he texted his wife to say he was "so famous right now".

It has followed him to family life too. At children's school events, he said, he is surrounded by pupils asking why he threw in the red bag, a reference to his moment in the game.

## Where things stand with Romesh

The podcast also covered his relationship with the man who ended his run. ARY News reports that Blunt said Romesh had donated to his charity of choice, the [Blue Marine Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Marine_Foundation), after the final, and that he had texted the comedian "I know where you live". Romesh and Croft shared an £83,000 prize. We covered the donation in full in [James Blunt Touched by Romesh's Celebrity Traitors Donation](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-romesh-ranganathan/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Did James Blunt win Celebrity Traitors?

No. Blunt was banished in the End Game, according to Female First. [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan) and [Sebastian Croft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Croft) won the series.

### Was James Blunt a Traitor or a Faithful?

He was a Faithful, according to Female First, and he says he guessed the Traitors wrongly on a regular basis.

### How long is Celebrity Traitors filmed for?

Blunt said filming takes about three weeks, which is why a contestant who returns home after three days has not done well.
