**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) has signed with publicist Kelly Bush Novak of ID-PR, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- He leaves Vision PR, where Jami Kandel represented him. His wife, [Blake Lively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Lively), moved to ID-PR in September.
- The same profile has fans asking about Deadpool 4, but ComicBook.com says another Deadpool appearance does not mean another solo film.
- Reynolds has said he is writing something that is "a little ensemble".

Ryan Reynolds has changed publicists. The Deadpool star has left Vision PR and joined ID-PR, the agency run by Kelly Bush Novak, in a move first reported by The Hollywood Reporter and relayed on 26 November by [The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2642482/ryan-reynolds-leaves-longtime-publicist-as-he-joins-blake-livelys-new-pr-team). It is a small piece of business, but it landed in the same week as fresh questions about what comes next for his most famous character.

## What the Ryan Reynolds publicist move means

According to The Express Tribune, Reynolds was previously represented by Jami Kandel. Novak founded ID-PR and serves as its chief executive, and she will now handle Reynolds with her team.

She already represents his wife. The Tribune reports that Lively switched to the agency in September, after the end of her legal dispute with *It Ends With Us* director Justin Baldoni. That is the outlet's account of the timing; neither side's reasons for the change have been spelled out in the reports we read.

ID-PR's client list, as the Tribune lists it, is a heavyweight one: Christopher Nolan, Lady Gaga, Seth Rogen, Alejandro G. Iñárritu, Ben Stiller, Elliot Page, John Malkovich, Serena Williams, Lena Dunham, Diego Luna and Pamela Adlon. Reynolds now joins that roster.

We have covered the change before, including [Ryan Reynolds moving to publicist Kelly Bush Novak](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-publicist-id-pr/). The newer detail is how quickly it fed a Marvel debate.

## Is Deadpool 4 happening?

The Hollywood Reporter profile noted that Reynolds "has another Deadpool movie coming" after the success of 2024's *Deadpool & Wolverine*. Many fans read that as confirmation of a fourth solo film.

[ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/movies/news/is-deadpool-4-happening-ryan-reynolds-marvel-future-teased-in-new-report/) argues that is not quite what it says. Its reading is that Reynolds may well play the character in the next phase of the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe), but not necessarily in a stand-alone sequel.

There is some on-record support for that. Asked last year about Deadpool's future, Marvel Studios president [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) said there had "absolutely been discussions" and that the studio is in touch with Reynolds "a lot", according to ComicBook.com. Around the same time Reynolds said he was writing something right now and that it was "a little ensemble".

The site also notes that *Avengers: Doomsday* is due in cinemas on 18 December, and that *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which reunited Reynolds with Hugh Jackman, is streaming on Disney+.

So the honest position is this: a Deadpool return is signalled, the shape of it is not. Nothing in the sources puts a date or a title on it.

## A busy slate beyond the mask

Reynolds has plenty else running. The Tribune points to his recent film *Mayday*, which also stars Kenneth Branagh and Maria Bakalova. The film opened on 4 September, and Wikipedia records a Rotten Tomatoes score of 83% and a Metacritic score of 60 out of 100 from 24 critics. No box office or budget figures have been published for it yet.

On television, *Welcome to Wrexham* continues. The Tribune reports that FX has renewed the documentary series, which Reynolds co-produces with [Rob Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney), for three more seasons. It also says the pair are developing a Disney+ docuseries about their Australian sailing team, with Jackman involved. That detail is a reported plan, not a confirmed release.

The football side is going well on the pitch. The Daily Star notes that Wrexham defender Max Cleworth recently made his 200th appearance, in a 1-0 win at Lincoln City. He is the last starter from the club's National League title side still in the first team, as Wrexham push on in the Championship under Phil Parkinson. The paper credits the club's rise to Reynolds and Mac's 2021 takeover. Our earlier story covers [Reynolds thanking Steve Parkin as he leaves the club](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-steve-parkin-wrexham/).

## What happens next

Watch for two things. The first is whether Reynolds or Marvel say more about the ensemble project once *Avengers: Doomsday* opens. The second is whether ID-PR's involvement changes how and where he speaks about his projects, which would show up in the coming months of interviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Ryan Reynolds' new publicist?

Kelly Bush Novak, founder and CEO of ID-PR, according to The Hollywood Reporter via The Express Tribune. He previously worked with Jami Kandel at Vision PR.

### Is Deadpool 4 confirmed?

No. The Hollywood Reporter says another Deadpool movie is coming, but ComicBook.com reads that as a possible ensemble appearance in the MCU rather than a fourth solo film. Reynolds has described what he is writing as "a little ensemble".

### Does Blake Lively use the same publicist?

Yes. The Express Tribune reports that Lively joined ID-PR in September, and Novak now represents both.
