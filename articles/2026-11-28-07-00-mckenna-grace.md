**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) will play Daphne Blake in Netflix's live-action Scooby-Doo: Origins, due in 2027, according to ScreenRant.
- The 20-year-old told The Today Show that Netflix first said she was too old because producers wanted younger actors.
- She told Collider that a filmed argument between her character Maysilee and Haymitch was cut from The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.
- The film runs two hours and 38 minutes, and ScreenRant reports it has passed US$253 million at the box office.

Mckenna Grace has a busy few weeks of talking, and two new interviews show how hard she pushes for the parts she wants. She has explained why a fight scene she fought over never reached the final cut of [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, and she has described how she talked Netflix into letting her audition for its Scooby-Doo series after first being turned away.

## How Mckenna Grace won Scooby-Doo: Origins

Netflix's Scooby-Doo: Origins is set at a summer camp, where Daphne Blake, Velma Dinkley, Shaggy Rogers and Fred Jones investigate a supernatural murder, [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-netflix-scooby-doo-show-leading-role-new-era/). It is described as the franchise's first live-action scripted series based on the animated original. Grace plays Daphne, and the cast also includes Tanner Hagen, [Abby Ryder Fortson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abby_Ryder_Fortson), [Maxwell Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Jenkins) and [Paul Walter Hauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Walter_Hauser).

The route to the role was not smooth. Speaking to The Today Show, as quoted by ScreenRant, Grace said Netflix initially passed because "they wanted to go really young for it". She remembered being told she was too old and pleading to be part of it. She did not say what changed the producers' minds, but she credited her persistence and her love of the franchise for getting her into the audition room.

It is also not her first time as Daphne. ScreenRant notes she voiced a younger version of the character in the 2020 animated film Scoob!, while [Sarah Michelle Gellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Michelle_Gellar) played Daphne in the earlier live-action Scooby-Doo movies.

## Why it matters for her career

ScreenRant calls Scooby-Doo: Origins her first ongoing scripted series as a lead. Until now her television work has mostly been recurring or supporting parts, including The Young and the Restless, [Young Sheldon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Sheldon), Designated Survivor and The Handmaid's Tale. Her film credits include Gifted, I, Tonya and [Annabelle Comes Home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabelle_Comes_Home).

The timing is useful for Netflix. Grace plays twin sisters Maysilee and Merrilee Donner in Sunrise on the Reaping, and ScreenRant reports the film has grossed more than US$253 million in under a week. Wikipedia's film entry lists a budget of US$150 million and a release date of 28 October 2026, so the box office figure is a fair multiple of the cost, though counts differ between trackers.

## The scene that never made the cut

In the Collider interview, Grace said the scene that needed the most work with director Francis Lawrence came from Suzanne Collins' novel. In the book, Maysilee and others come across Gamemakers working in the arena, and Maysilee kills one. Grace confirmed the moment was filmed, including a chase through the woods that ends in a clearing where she and co-star Joseph Zada, who plays Haymitch, argue.

She said she and Zada kept questioning the dialogue because neither could make sense of why their characters would behave that way. After talking it through with Lawrence, the trio settled on an argument she called a "beautiful" one that backed up what each character believed. In her telling, Maysilee is angry at everyone who helped build the system, while Haymitch can see the good in Effie, who sincerely believes in it.

The cut also removed a callback to the 2012 film. Grace said Haymitch tells her she will never get to win now, and she answers that nobody ever wins these games. The scene went, she explained, because the film removed a day in the arena and there was no time left to repair a rift between the two characters. She said she still valued the experience of rewriting it with her castmates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Mckenna Grace play in Scooby-Doo: Origins?

She plays Daphne Blake, the member of Mystery Inc. known for her purple outfit and her intuition, in Netflix's live-action series due in 2027.

### Why did Netflix first say no to Mckenna Grace?

Grace told The Today Show that producers wanted to go very young for the lead roles and told her she was too old. She kept pushing and was allowed to audition.

A filmed argument between Maysilee and Haymitch after Maysilee kills a Gamemaker. Grace says it was cut because the film shortened the arena sequence and lacked time to resolve their rift.
