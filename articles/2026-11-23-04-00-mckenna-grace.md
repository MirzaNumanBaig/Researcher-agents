**Quick summary**

- Lionsgate projects a three-day global opening of $225 million for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, as of Sunday morning.
- That is $120 million from US and Canadian cinemas and $105 million from international markets, according to ScreenRant.
- The film's reported budget is $150 million, so the opening weekend alone has covered it.
- Critics give it 68% on Rotten Tomatoes, while audiences give it 94% and an A- CinemaScore.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace)'s latest film has had one of the strongest debuts of the year. *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, the newest chapter of the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series, is projected to take $225 million worldwide in its first three days, according to figures Lionsgate confirmed on Sunday and [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office-opening-weekend-global-result/) reported.

## How big is the opening?

The studio's projection splits into $120 million domestically and $105 million from overseas. ScreenRant notes that the domestic figure is the fifth-best opening of 2026 so far and the fifth-best weekend in Lionsgate's history.

The global number puts the film 23rd on the year's worldwide chart already. By ScreenRant's count, that is ahead of the full-run totals of several 2026 releases, including *Verity* ($181.1 million), *The Sheep Detectives* ($133.1 million) and *Supergirl* ($126.5 million).

It also passes *Scream 7*, which stars Grace too and finished on $208 million. ScreenRant describes that film as the biggest in its slasher franchise.

## Where it sits in the Hunger Games story

*Sunrise on the Reaping* is the sixth film adapted from [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)'s books and the second prequel, after *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes* in 2023. It follows a young Haymitch Abernathy, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), through the 50th annual Games. The character is played in the original films by [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson).

[Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) directs, as he has for most of the series. The supporting cast includes [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)), [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes), [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close), [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) and [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin).

Against the rest of the franchise, the $225 million is the best global start since *Mockingjay – Part 2* in 2015, which opened to $248.6 million. It is the fourth-best of the six and ahead of the original 2012 film's $214.3 million, ScreenRant says.

## Budget and break-even

ScreenRant puts the reported budget at $150 million. The three-day total therefore already exceeds it, though a budget is not the same as profit. Blockbusters often need to earn around two and a half times their cost to break even once marketing is counted, which would put this film near $375 million on that rule of thumb.

The outlet adds a caveat. Lionsgate is known for offsetting costs through international pre-sales, so its real break-even point is probably lower. That figure is an estimate, not a company statement.

Lionsgate Motion Picture Group chairman Adam Fogelson credited the filmmakers, including producers [Nina Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Jacobson) and [Brad Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Simpson_(producer)), and the fans who had waited years for this story, in the statement ScreenRant quoted.

## Reviews and the press tour

Reception is split in a familiar way. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) shows 68% from critics and 94% from audiences, with the A- CinemaScore a further sign that cinemagoers liked it.

Grace's press tour has drawn attention of its own. ## What happens next

The film has a clear run in cinemas for now. ScreenRant says it faces little direct competition until *Avengers: Doomsday* and *Dune: Part Three* arrive on 18 December, although *Focker-in-Law* and *Hexed* open over the US Thanksgiving weekend.

Final weekend figures usually follow the Sunday projections, so the actual numbers may shift slightly.
