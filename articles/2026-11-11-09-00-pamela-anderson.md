**Quick summary**

- Dylan Jagger Lee, the younger son of Pamela Anderson and Tommy Lee, is now 28, according to DoYouRemember?, published on 10 November 2026.
- He left the synth-pop duo Midnight Kids in 2021 and started a new project, Motel 7.
- His brother Brandon, 30, produced the Netflix documentary *Pamela, a Love Story*, which earned him an Emmy nomination.
- The family company, And-Her-Sons Productions, has announced a TV reboot of *Barb Wire* as its first project.

[Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson)'s sons are no longer the boys the 1990s tabloids chased. Dylan Jagger Lee turned 28 this year and Brandon Thomas Lee turned 30 in June, and both now work in the industries their parents made famous, from music and fashion to producing, according to a profile published by DoYouRemember? on 10 November 2026.

## Who are Pamela Anderson's sons?

Brandon was born in Malibu on 5 June 1996 and Dylan on 29 December 1997, according to TODAY and InStyle, as cited by DoYouRemember?. Their father is the Mötley Crüe drummer [Tommy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee). Anderson and Lee married on a beach in Cancún in February 1995 and the marriage ended in 1998. The parents later settled on joint custody.

Anderson was already well known by then. She played the original Lisa on [Tool Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Improvement_(TV_series)) from 1991 to 1993, then spent five seasons on *Baywatch* from 1992 to 1997. In her memoir, she called her sons "true miracles, considering their gene pool", and has said "they saved me".

Both boys went to boarding school in Canada, away from the photographers who followed their parents around Los Angeles.

## Dylan's path through fashion and music

Dylan began in fashion. He walked for Saint Laurent in 2016 at 18, then signed with EWG Management and worked with labels including Coach and Hugo Boss, per the same report.

Music took over. He spent five years in Midnight Kids before leaving in 2021, saying he wanted a direction that felt "more true to me", as Dancing Astronaut reported. He then formed Motel 7 with his friend Anton Khabbaz and released the single "Are We There Yet".

His father reportedly learned about the band late. Dylan told WWD in 2021 that Tommy Lee "didn't even know I was in a band until four days ago", though the drummer's reaction, he said, was delighted once he understood. Lee turned 64 on 3 October and spent this summer on Mötley Crüe's Return of the Carnival of Sins tour.

## Brandon's move behind the camera

Brandon made his film debut in 2017 in the big-screen *Baywatch*, then appeared in *Sierra Burgess Is a Loser* and the reality series *The Hills: New Beginnings*. In 2021 he launched a golf-wear line, Swingers Club.

His biggest credit is as a producer. He worked on the 2023 Netflix documentary *Pamela, a Love Story*, which brought him an Emmy nomination. He was also a producer on *The Last Showgirl*, the 2024 film that earned his mother a Golden Globe nomination.

## A family company and a *Barb Wire* reboot

The three now run And-Her-Sons Productions. According to People and Far Out Magazine, as cited in the report, the company's first announced project is a TV series reboot of Anderson's 1996 film *Barb Wire*, with both sons producing alongside her. The sons also appeared on her cooking and gardening shows.
