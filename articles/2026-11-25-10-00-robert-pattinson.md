**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) is now confirmed as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), and awards watchers are asking whether that helps his Oscar chances.
- IndieWire says his strongest shot is Best Actor for *Primetime*, in which he plays TV journalist Chris Hansen.
- The American Cinematheque will honour him on 9 February 2027.
- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) joked in a Focus Features interview that she is "deeply afraid" he will take her roles.

Robert Pattinson's Bond casting has landed in the middle of awards season, and the question now is whether 007 can do for his Oscar hopes what it has not done for other actors. According to [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/awards/consider-this/will-robert-pattinson-bond-casting-help-land-oscar-nom-1235230774/), the answer is a cautious "not directly, but it helps people watch the film".

## Robert Pattinson Oscar race: where he stands

IndieWire describes a busy year for the actor. He has three long-shot possibilities: Best Actor for A24's *The Drama*, and Supporting Actor for his villain roles in [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan)'s *The Odyssey* and *Dune: Part Three*. The outlet argues his best chance has always been *Primetime*, another A24 release, where he plays the TV journalist Chris Hansen.

The film opened on 5 September 2026 and carries a reported budget of US$14.5 million, according to [Wikipedia's entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film)), which also records an 86 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score. IndieWire puts its box office at about US$47 million in North America and US$12 million abroad. That split matters, the writer notes, because roughly a quarter of the Academy's more than 10,000 voters are international.

## Does playing Bond help with the Academy?

Not in any direct way, IndieWire argues. When Daniel Craig was cast as Bond during the awards campaign for *Munich*, a veteran publicist told the outlet that screenings felt different, but no nomination followed. The only Bond actor to win an Oscar after a long career, it adds, is Sean Connery, for *The Untouchables*.

What the role does is raise attention. One studio Oscar expert told IndieWire that Pattinson "will have heightened scrutiny" now that he has been chosen to anchor a franchise. An Oscar strategist said Bond "probably gets more people to watch the movie", though the film still has to do the work itself.

## The numbers so far

Early predictions are soft. IndieWire reports that Gold Derby users rank Pattinson fifth for Best Actor, while the site's experts have him seventh. Tom Cruise, for *Digger*, is slightly ahead of him in the Actor Awards race at this stage. Gold Derby's editor-in-chief Debra Birnbaum called the contest between the two "squishy".

The piece cautions that betting markets and user polls improve as the season goes on, and that critics' groups, guild awards and the BAFTAs have yet to weigh in. One firm date is already set: the American Cinematheque will present Pattinson with its annual award on 9 February 2027, with Amazon MGM Studios, the studio behind Bond, expected to attend in force.

## Inde Navarrette's joke about Pattinson's roles

Away from the awards chatter, Navarrette has turned the Bond news into a running gag. Speaking in a video shared by Focus Features, the *Obsession* actress was asked whether she worried Pattinson might come for her work. "I'm deeply afraid," she said, according to [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/tracewilliamcowen/inde-navarrette-robert-pattinson-jokes). "Every single day I wake up and I decide that I have to choose the roles that he's not gonna do."

She went further, joking that she was the second choice for Bond and that Pattinson only got the job because she was busy. [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/692461-inde-navarrette-deeply-afraid-of-robert-pattinson-heres-why) and the Daily Times both stress that this is a joke, not a casting revelation.

The gag refers to a 2025 Vanity Fair lie detector interview with Jennifer Lawrence, in which Pattinson said he likes "taking jobs off younger actors" and wants to play 17 again, according to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/inde-navarrette-jokes-avoiding-roles-160621000.html), which republished a Parade report. Navarrette is herself set to play Rogue in Marvel's upcoming X-Men film. The same report says major publications have floated her among possible Bond co-stars, and she told Extra, "Oh, give me a gun!"

## What happens next

The next signposts are the critics' awards, the BAFTAs and the guild nominations, which will show whether *Primetime* holds its place. Pattinson's next film, *The Take*, directed by Fernando Meirelles, is listed for release on 15 January 2027, per Wikipedia. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has Robert Pattinson been nominated for an Oscar?
No. IndieWire's headline refers to his "first Oscar nomination", and nominations have not been announced for this season.

### Which film gives him the best chance this year?
IndieWire points to *Primetime*, in which he plays Chris Hansen. He is also mentioned for *The Drama*, *The Odyssey* and *Dune: Part Three*.

### Was Inde Navarrette really considered for Bond?
No. Geo News and the Daily Times both describe her second-choice claim as a joke made during promotion for *Obsession*.
