**Quick summary**

- The Motion Picture Association has rated *The Exorcist: Martyrs* R for "bloody violent content, grisly images, language, and some sexual references", according to ScreenRant.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) leads the film, which [Mike Flanagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Flanagan_(filmmaker)) directs.
- The rating keeps the franchise's 53-year run of R ratings going.
- MovieWeb gives the release date as 12 March 2027.

*The Exorcist: Martyrs*, the new Mike Flanagan film starring Scarlett Johansson, has officially been rated R by the [Motion Picture Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association). The rating was reported on 25 November 2026, a little over two months before the film's planned March release, and its wording suggests a far bloodier film than many fans expected.

## What the R rating says

According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/scarlett-johansson-the-exorcist-martyrs-rating-streak/), the MPA cites "bloody violent content, grisly images, language, and some sexual references". MovieWeb, citing Bloody Disgusting, reports the same wording.

The R rating itself is no surprise. ScreenRant notes that every film in the franchise has landed it since the original, a streak now 53 years long. The detail that stands out is "grisly images". ScreenRant says the only earlier film in the series to list gruesome imagery in its rating reasons was the prequel *Exorcist: The Beginning*.

## What the film is about

Johansson plays Julie Miller, a New York City homicide detective. As ScreenRant describes the story, she is pulled into a murder investigation that seems tied to a former Satanic serial killer she helped stop. The synopsis puts that earlier case thirteen years in the past. A former prison chaplain and a rookie detective help her follow a connection between some of history's most notorious killers.

The first trailer leaned into the gore, ScreenRant says. It shows crime scenes with bodies mutilated in ritual ways, and one scene where something emerges from a barrel covered in blood.

## Flanagan on how bloody it gets

Flanagan told ScreenRant earlier that the film is "very, very bloody". He compared it with the acid-soaked scene in *The Fall of the House of Usher*, but said this one is vicious rather than funny. He called it a gear he rarely uses, since he usually favours hope and humanism, although he said a little of that is there "if you squint".

ScreenRant adds that Flanagan has drawn inspiration from the cult favourite *The Exorcist III*, and that he has described early test screenings as glowing. By ScreenRant's count, only two of his eight released films, *Before I Wake* and *Ouija: Origin of Evil*, earned a PG-13.

## A franchise that needs a win

MovieWeb describes Johansson as one of the highest-grossing actresses of all time, though not a horror regular. Her earlier genre credits include [*Eight Legged Freaks*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eight_Legged_Freaks) and *Under the Skin*, and it calls this her highest-profile horror project.

The franchise began in 1973 and, per MovieWeb, now spans five films and a television series. The most recent film, 2023's *The Exorcist: Believer*, scored 22% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). MovieWeb argues that Flanagan, known for *The Haunting of Hill House* and *Midnight Mass*, is well placed to turn that around.

## The cast

ScreenRant lists [Chiwetel Ejiofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiwetel_Ejiofor), [Diane Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Lane), [Jacobi Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_Jupe) and [Sasha Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sasha_Lane) alongside Johansson. MovieWeb's cast list also includes [Laurence Fishburne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Fishburne) and John Leguizamo.

The film is on track for 12 March 2027, according to MovieWeb. With the rating confirmed, the next signal will be further marketing. Australian release details have not been reported in the sources we read.
