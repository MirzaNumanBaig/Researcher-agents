**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) is recording her own versions of all 11 entries in the first [Eurovision Song Contest Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_Asia), according to Aussievision.
- Each performance is sung in the song's original language and released daily on Eurovision Asia's Instagram page.
- She began with Bangladesh's entry, Tumi Ele by Beatmosphere, singing in Bengali at the piano.
- The contest takes place at One Bangkok Forum in Bangkok, Thailand, on Saturday 14 November 2026.

Delta Goodrem has started a daily series of covers for the debut Eurovision Song Contest Asia, singing every one of the 11 competing songs in the language it was written in. [Aussievision reports](https://www.aussievision.net/post/delta-goodrem-is-tackling-all-11-eurovision-asia-songs-in-their-languages) that the clips are going up on the contest's official Instagram page, with the first already drawing an enthusiastic response from fans.

## How the series works

The Australian singer is performing each entry herself rather than simply promoting it. The field mixes English with local languages, and Goodrem is taking on both, according to Aussievision. The format is simple: one song, one clip, released day by day until all 11 are out.

There is a twist for the artists too. Each of the 11 participants has a surprise lined up for Goodrem in return. In the first clip, Beatmosphere perform her song Eclipse for her at the end of the video, Aussievision says.

## Starting with Bangladesh

Goodrem opened the series with Tumi Ele, Bangladesh's entry from Beatmosphere. She sang it in Bengali, accompanying herself on piano. Aussievision notes that the clip quickly gathered fan comments, including one Instagram user who wrote that they never would have expected to hear her sing in Bengali.

The remaining ten songs are due over the coming days. Aussievision names a few of them: Bhutan's Lhayi Semo, the Philippines' Sobra-Sobra, South Korea's DEEP DIVE and Vietnam's EM TIÊN. That leaves six entries whose names were not listed in the report.

## A busy few weeks

The project lands while Goodrem is also competing on the UK's [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing). Aussievision says she recently returned from a rib injury and scored the first 10 of the series. We have covered that run in detail, including [her first Strictly 10](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-scores-strictlys-first-10-after-rib-injury/) and [her return to the show](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-grateful-as-she-returns-to-strictly/).

Aussievision describes her as an Australian Eurovision star, and the new series puts her behind a contest that is only now getting started in Asia.

## What happens next

Expect a new clip each day until all 11 songs have been covered. The contest itself is set for Saturday 14 November 2026 at One Bangkok Forum in Bangkok, Thailand.
