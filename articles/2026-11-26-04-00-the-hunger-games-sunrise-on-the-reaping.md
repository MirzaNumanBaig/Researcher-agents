**Quick summary**

- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* took $12.9 million across its Australian opening weekend (19 to 22 November), according to Variety Australia.
- That is the biggest local debut in franchise history, passing the $12.5 million set by *Catching Fire* in 2013.
- It is the third-biggest Australian opening of 2026, behind *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* and *The Odyssey*.
- Sydney-born Joseph Zada leads the film as a young Haymitch Abernathy.
- Reviews are generally positive, though several critics say the pacing is rushed.

*The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* has become the biggest Australian opening in the history of the franchise, and a local actor is at the centre of it. According to [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/sunrise-on-the-reaping-third-biggest-australian-box-office-opening-2026-42868/), the prequel earned $12.9 million between Thursday 19 and Sunday 22 November, including Wednesday night advance screenings.

## A record that stood for 13 years

The figure edges past the $12.5 million opening of 2013's [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire), which had gone unbeaten since. It is also more than two and a half times the $5.09 million that *The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes* made when it opened in 2023. That film finished its local run with about $20 million, so the new release has a clear target to chase.

Val Morgan Cinema supplied the numbers. Its managing director, Guy Burbidge, called it a fantastic result for cinemas heading into summer. Nationwide admissions rose 48% on the previous weekend, and 71% of opening-weekend moviegoers were aged 14 to 39, Variety Australia reported. Two other titles, *You Can See Everything* and *Ramayana*, took second and third place. (We covered the former in our post on [Amanda Seyfried and the secret script](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-you-can-see-everything-3/).)

## A homegrown lead

[Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), a 21-year-old from Sydney, plays the young Haymitch, the role made famous by [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson). Zada told Rolling Stone AU/NZ that copying Harrelson was never the aim. He wanted to keep some of that energy while finding his own take. He also told Variety Australia that his Aboriginal and Afghan heritage keeps him grounded.

The film is directed by Francis Lawrence and set 24 years before the original story. It follows Haymitch into the 50th Games, also called the Second Quarter Quell. The cast includes [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) as fellow District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner, [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)) as Lenore Dove, [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) as Plutarch Heavensbee, [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) as Effie Trinket and [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) as President Snow. [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin) and Glenn Close also appear. The screenplay is by [Billy Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Ray_(screenwriter)) and Michael Lesslie, adapting the novel by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins).

## What critics are saying

Early reviews lean positive with reservations. Moviejawn called the film [vibrant, violent and worthwhile](https://www.moviejawn.com/home/2026/11/25/sunrise-on-the-reaping-review), praising Zada's softer take on Haymitch and his chemistry with Grace. The reviewer also said that at 2 hours 38 minutes, the longest in the series, the story moves so fast that deaths and minor tributes get little room to land. The 50th Games have 48 tributes, double the usual number.

The State News gave a warmer verdict, calling it [big and bright](https://statenews.com/article/2026/11/review-the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-is-a-big-and-bright-triumph). It singled out Plemons as the best performance but found the dialogue overly explanatory. It also noted that Zada's Appalachian accent occasionally slipped, which it put down to his Australian voice.

Book fans have their own complaints. A spoiler-heavy piece from [Cosmic Circus](https://thecosmiccircus.com/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-changes-haymitch-snow/) argues that the film fixes the novel's passive Haymitch and the way Snow treats him, but trims some of the book's most original moments. Wikipedia's summary of the reception lists a Rotten Tomatoes score of 68% and a Metacritic score of 55 out of 100 from 43 critics. Those scores may change as more reviews arrive.

## Off screen

People magazine has rounded up the cast's [real-life partners](https://people.com/sunrise-on-the-reaping-cast-real-life-loves-12274423). Grace is dating her *Regretting You* co-star Mason Thames. Zada has a girlfriend whom he has kept private. Plemons has been married to Kirsten Dunst since 2022.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Sunrise on the Reaping make in Australia?

It took $12.9 million over its opening weekend, 19 to 22 November, including advance screenings. That is the biggest Hunger Games opening in the country.

### Who plays Haymitch in Sunrise on the Reaping?

Sydney-born actor Joseph Zada, aged 21, plays the young Haymitch. Woody Harrelson played the older version in the earlier films.

### How long is the film?

It runs 2 hours 38 minutes, the longest in the franchise, according to Moviejawn.

### What is it about?

It follows Haymitch Abernathy as he is pulled into the 50th Hunger Games, set 24 years before the original series, and fights the Capitol from within.
