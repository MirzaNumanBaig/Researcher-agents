Hugh Jackman's 2006 thriller *The Prestige* is heading back to the big screen. According to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/the-prestige-hugh-jackman-theater-release/), Disney has confirmed a theatrical re-release for **29 January 2027**, marking 20 years since the film first arrived in cinemas.

## Premium formats for the first time

The anniversary run will be handled by Walt Disney Studios. MovieWeb reports that the film will screen in traditional 35mm and digital 2D, and, for the first time, in IMAX and 70mm.

Director Christopher Nolan welcomed the news in a statement quoted by the outlet. He said he was "very excited" to bring the film back to the big screen, and "particularly thrilled" to offer audiences the chance to see it on 70mm film and in IMAX for the first time.

MovieWeb's report does not detail screening plans for Australia, so local cinema-goers will need to watch for announcements.

## About the film

*The Prestige* follows two rival stage magicians whose obsession with outdoing one another leads to dangerous consequences. Jackman plays Robert Angier, a charismatic showman, opposite Christian Bale as the gifted illusionist Alfred Borden. Michael Caine and Scarlett Johansson also star.

The film is based on Christopher Priest's novel of the same name, and Nolan co-wrote the screenplay with his brother, Jonathan Nolan. MovieWeb notes that it received two Academy Award nominations, for Best Cinematography and Best Art Direction, and has built a reputation for its elaborate final reveal. The outlet also says it has often been overshadowed by Nolan's larger blockbusters, despite developing a devoted following.

## Timing with Nolan's latest hit

The re-release follows the success of Nolan's most recent film, *The Odyssey*. MovieWeb reports that it has earned more than US$1.7 billion worldwide, making it the director's highest-grossing movie to date.

## What happens next

For now, the confirmed details are the 29 January 2027 date and the formats on offer. Specific cinemas, session times and ticketing have not been outlined in the report we reviewed, so fans hoping to see Jackman's performance on 70mm or IMAX should check with their local cinemas as the date approaches.

A second report, from IMDb, described another Jackman title as a streaming hit. That page could not be read for this article, so we have not relied on it.
