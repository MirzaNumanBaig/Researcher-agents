**Quick summary**

- Reports published on 8 November 2026 say [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) is considering a book and a streaming docuseries about her marriage to [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand).
- The claim traces to one unnamed Hollywood source quoted by the Mail on Sunday, according to LBC and IBTimes UK.
- Neither Perry nor Netflix nor Hulu has confirmed anything, and Perry has not commented on Brand's conviction.
- Brand was convicted on 5 November 2026 of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

Katy Perry is reportedly weighing a book and a docuseries about her short marriage to Russell Brand, but none of it has been confirmed. The reports appeared on 8 November, three days after Brand was convicted at Southwark Crown Court, and they lean on a single anonymous source. Interest in the story has carried into this week.

## What the reports actually say

According to [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/news/entertainment/katy-perry-dark-marriage-secrets-to-ex-husband-russell-brand-to-be-exposed/), the original claim came from the Daily Mail, citing a "well-placed" Hollywood source who said Perry is in "active discussions" about telling her side. [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/katy-perry-memoir-docuseries-russell-brand-marriage-1830911) attributes the same account to an exclusive in the Mail on Sunday.

The source reportedly said Perry had received offers over the years but would not consider any of them until after Brand's trial. IBTimes UK adds that her representatives reportedly approached Netflix and Hulu, and that neither company has confirmed an agreement. The source gave no detail about what a project might contain, and the outlet points out that the "expose" framing in some headlines rests entirely on that one voice.

## Where Perry stands

Perry has not publicly commented on the verdict. Both outlets say her representatives were approached and no response was reported. She was not called as a witness. LBC reports that the Crown Prosecution Service said she was never approached for comment or asked for a witness statement.

## The conviction behind the headlines

Brand, 51, was convicted on 5 November 2026 after a four-week trial, according to IBTimes UK. The jury found him guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women, with offences between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted of two other rape charges and one attempted indecent assault charge. He denied the allegations and was remanded in custody, with sentencing listed for 9 November.

LBC's page carries conflicting information on the sentence, so this article does not state one. LBC also notes speculation about whether Perry was present at an incident in 2009, which came from a defence submission and is unconfirmed. We do not repeat it as fact.

## The marriage, briefly

The couple met in 2009 and married in India in October 2010, according to IBTimes UK. They separated in December 2011, about 14 months later, and the divorce was finalised in 2012.

IBTimes UK also recalls a 2013 Vogue interview in which Perry called parts of the relationship "very controlling" and mentioned a "real truth" she was keeping for "a rainy day". She never explained the remark publicly, and the outlet notes there is no established link between it and the convictions. Readers should treat any connection as speculation.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. No publisher, platform or release date has been named, and Perry has said nothing herself. If she or her team respond, or if Netflix or Hulu confirm talks, that would be the first solid news. Until then this is a report about possible plans, not an announcement.
