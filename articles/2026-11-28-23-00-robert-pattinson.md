**Quick summary**

- President [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump) was asked about [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)'s casting as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) in a November 24 Oval Office interview with the New York Post.
- He had aide [Natalie Harp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Harp) fetch printouts that included his 2012 tweets about Pattinson and [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart).
- Trump said "I wish him luck" and called the 40-year-old actor "nice" looking.
- Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting on November 16, 2026. No title or release date has been given.

Donald Trump has weighed in on the new James Bond, and he did it by dredging up an old tweet. During a November 24 Oval Office interview with the New York Post, the president asked whether Bond is "conservative", then had Harp bring him articles about Pattinson, according to a [Yahoo report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/donald-trump-revisits-2012-warning-210024542.html).

## What Trump said in the Oval Office

The printouts Harp returned with included Trump's 2012 advice that Pattinson should not reconcile with Stewart. "This was a long time ago," the president remarked, per Yahoo. He then softened. "He looks nice. You know, I wish him luck," he said of the British actor.

The exchange is a throwaway moment rather than a policy statement, but it shows how far the casting has travelled. The role has now reached the desk of a head of state, a little over a week after it was announced.

## Where the 2012 tweets came from

Stewart and director [Rupert Sanders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Sanders) issued separate public apologies on July 25, 2012, after photographs of them together were published, as ABC News reported and Yahoo repeats. Sanders had directed Stewart in Snow White and the Huntsman.

Trump also invited Pattinson to that year's Miss Universe pageant. The Twilight co-stars briefly reconciled and attended the premiere of the second [Breaking Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Dawn) film together, before separating in May 2013.

## How Stewart and Pattinson responded years later

Stewart addressed the tweets at Sundance on January 20, 2017, while promoting her directorial debut, [Come Swim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Come_Swim). Asked by Variety, she said she struggled to understand his interest and wondered whether discussing it would prompt another post.

On February 4 she displayed several of them in her [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) opening monologue and said Trump had tweeted about her 11 times. "I think he's in love with my boyfriend," she joked.

Pattinson spoke about it too. In an August 2017 Entertainment Weekly interview, quoted by Glamour, he reflected on how his public identity differed from his everyday life and wondered whether such odd experiences could be useful in his acting. That November he joked that the tweets had vanished, though The Daily Beast's interviewer found they were still online.

## The Bond job itself

Amazon MGM Studios announced Pattinson's casting on November 16, 2026. He becomes the seventh lead actor in the official film series, with [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) directing and Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight writing the screenplay. Pattinson called the role "the honour of a lifetime."

He follows Daniel Craig, whose last appearance as Bond was 2021's No Time to Die. Other headlines this week include a report that Villeneuve plans to embrace rather than rewrite Bond's DNA, and that Danny Boyle called Pattinson a true "movie star". We could only read the Yahoo report in full, so we have not repeated claims from those pieces.
