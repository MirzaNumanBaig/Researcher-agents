Elizabeth Debicki's route to Hollywood began in a ballet household in Melbourne, and a new Yahoo profile published on 27 November 2026 sets out how a student who also had a law school place ended up in films by Baz Luhrmann and Christopher Nolan. The piece is a career retrospective rather than breaking news, but it collects the turning points in one place.

## A childhood built around dance

According to the [Yahoo profile](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/slideshows/elizabeth-debickis-surprising-journey-hollywood-072100472.html), Debicki was born in Paris in 1990 to a Polish father and an Australian mother of Irish descent. The family moved to Melbourne when she was five. She is the eldest of three children.

Both her parents were professional ballet dancers, and she trained in dance herself as a child. Yahoo reports that she later gave up the idea of dancing professionally.

## History, law and a drama degree

The profile describes a student drawn to history and archaeology who did well academically and secured a place at law school. She chose drama at the Victorian College of the Arts, part of the University of Melbourne, instead. Yahoo says her parents were reportedly concerned by the decision.

The gamble paid off quickly. Shortly after she graduated, Luhrmann invited her to audition for *The Great Gatsby*.

## From Gatsby to Nolan

Debicki played Jordan Baker in *The Great Gatsby* (2013), opposite Leonardo DiCaprio and Carey Mulligan. Yahoo calls it her breakthrough.

Two years later she was the villain Victoria Vinciguerra in Guy Ritchie's *The Man from U.N.C.L.E.* (2015). The profile notes that one scene required her to drive a manual-transmission car, although she was not a confident driver at the time.

*Widows* (2018) is credited as the film that led producer Emma Thomas to recommend her to Nolan. He later cast her in *Tenet* (2020) as Kat, an art expert trapped in a controlling marriage to an arms dealer played by Kenneth Branagh. According to Yahoo, Nolan offered her the part without an audition, but she insisted on reading for it anyway.

Yahoo also lists her turn as Princess Diana in *The Crown*, using it to illustrate her wariness of the spotlight that surrounds public figures.

## Staying private

The profile says Debicki has kept her personal life largely away from social media and has said she does not much enjoy the internet or constant public scrutiny. The article presents this as a deliberate choice: she would rather have audiences drawn in by her performances than by her private life.
