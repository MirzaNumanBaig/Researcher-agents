**Quick summary**

- [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) has been seen with [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) again after falling ill before the week eight live show of *Strictly Come Dancing*.
- He was taken to hospital after becoming unwell in rehearsals, and [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in with about 24 hours' notice.
- Goodrem and Skorjanec danced the Argentine tango and scored 35, which left them second on the leaderboard.
- Widdrington says he is on the mend and is back for the rest of the series. The pair now head to the Blackpool special.

Delta Goodrem and Kai Widdrington are back together after a health scare that forced a last-minute partner swap on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing). According to a report from [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/strictlys-kai-widdrington-seen-delta-114157348.html), the Australian singer and her professional partner were filmed riding electric bikes through London during a break from training this week.

## What happened before the week eight show

The drama began ahead of the live show on Saturday, 14 November. Widdrington fell ill and was taken to hospital, which left Goodrem without her partner a day before the performance.

Skorjanec was brought in at 24 hours' notice. The pair danced an Argentine tango to *Missing* by Everything But The Girl and earned 35 points from the judges. Per the same Yahoo report, that put them second on the leaderboard.

Skorjanec was candid about the challenge. He said the Argentine tango is not his strongest style and that he surprised himself, and he told Goodrem the intimacy of the dance came from her, not him.

## What Goodrem and Widdrington have said

Goodrem addressed the situation on the night. She described an "extremely crazy week", said she was sad Widdrington had been so unwell, and said she was proud of him and expected him to come back strong.

Widdrington watched from home. He said he had been to hospital but was on the mend, and that he would be back for the rest of the season. He thanked the paramedics and staff at the hospital in Barnet, and his girlfriend, who he said had been making soup and pancakes.

On Instagram, Goodrem called it a "week of curveballs". She thanked Skorjanec for making her feel safe, praised Widdrington as a "trooper", and credited the choreographers and the hair, make-up and costume team. She finished by thanking viewers for voting.

## The bike ride that signalled his return

The latest footage shows the pair cycling through London. Filming as he rode, Widdrington told the camera they were "team healthy this week" and heading out for juice and fresh air. It is a light-hearted sign that rehearsals are back on track, though neither has given a detailed medical update beyond his own comments.

## What happens next

Goodrem made it through to the show's Blackpool special, and Widdrington has confirmed he will be dancing with her again. Blackpool is a milestone week in the series, so the pair will be keen to settle back into a normal routine after a disrupted week eight.
