**Quick summary**

- In 2003, [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey) and [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg) each said they would make *The Secret Life of Walter Mitty* only if the other came aboard, according to Far Out Magazine.
- The project never happened with them. [Ben Stiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stiller) directed and starred in the film, released in December 2013.
- Stiller had already replaced both men on *Meet the Parents*, as the lead after Spielberg and Carrey bowed out.
- Separately, Carrey's Maui estate, listed at US$35 million, is now pending sale, The Economic Times reports.

Two of Hollywood's biggest names were ready to make a film together in 2003, and each was waiting for the other to commit. Neither ever did. Far Out Magazine has revisited the story of how *The Secret Life of Walter Mitty* slipped away from Jim Carrey and Steven Spielberg and ended up with Ben Stiller a decade later.

## A meeting where each said yes to the other

The scene, as producer John Goldwyn recalled it, was almost comically polite. Carrey asked what had become of the *Walter Mitty* project. Spielberg said he would direct the remake of the old [Danny Kaye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Kaye) film if Carrey starred. Carrey replied that he would star if Spielberg directed.

Then-Paramount chief [Sherry Lansing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherry_Lansing) was in the room and offered to finance it on those terms. She told Goldwyn to go and get the rights. According to Far Out, he was already ahead of her: he had them.

On paper, everything lined up. A studio boss, a producer holding the rights, a director and a star who had each named the other as a condition. The film still did not get made with either of them.

## Not the first near miss

Far Out Magazine describes this as one of at least four occasions when the pair's paths almost crossed.

- **Jurassic Park:** before his 1994 breakthrough, the *[In Living Color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Living_Color)* alumnus wanted the role of Ian Malcolm, which went to [Jeff Goldblum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Goldblum).
- **The Truman Show:** after [Brian De Palma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_De_Palma) left, Spielberg was floated as a replacement director, but [Peter Weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Weir) took the job in early 1995.
- **Meet the Parents:** Carrey developed it and lined up Spielberg to direct. Far Out says Spielberg backed away after his wife reminded him he was not known for comedy, and Carrey dropped out too.

Each time, the near miss came down to timing or a change of mind rather than any falling out.

## The man who took both jobs

The twist is Stiller. He was cast as the lead in *Meet the Parents* once Spielberg and Carrey were out. When *Walter Mitty* finally reached cinemas in December 2013, he both directed it and played the title role, the two jobs the pair had offered to split between them.

Far Out also points out that Stiller has a foot in both camps. He directed Carrey in *The Cable Guy*, and Spielberg directed him in *Empire of the Sun*. That makes him the one filmmaker to have worked with each man on either side of the camera, while the two of them have never worked together.

Far Out's writer adds a dry note on where Carrey's career sits now: unless Spielberg fancies directing a *Sonic the Hedgehog* sequel, the writer suggests, their paths are unlikely to cross, since that franchise is where Carrey has been busiest lately.

## Meanwhile, Carrey's Maui home finds a buyer

In separate news, The Economic Times reports that Carrey's waterfront home on Keawakapu Beach in Maui is now listed as pending on Realtor.com, less than a month after it went on the market at US$35 million. A pending listing means a buyer has been found but the sale is not complete, and the final price has not been disclosed.

Carrey paid US$16.5 million for the property in 2016, so the asking price was more than double that. The house, built in 2002, has three bedrooms, 4.5 bathrooms and 4,717 square feet of living space on nearly an acre, with about 170 feet of ocean frontage.

The Economic Times also says Carrey married his partner, Min Ah, in Los Angeles on 30 September 2026, citing a TMZ report. It adds that he thanked her publicly, calling her his "sublime companion", when he received an honorary award at the César Awards in Paris in February 2026. In 2024 he sold his longtime Brentwood estate for US$17 million, after listing it at US$28.9 million in 2023.

## What happens next

Nothing is planned between Carrey and Spielberg, and no report suggests otherwise. The Maui sale remains open until it closes, and the price will only become clear then.
