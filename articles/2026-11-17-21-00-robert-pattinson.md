**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) will play [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), succeeding Daniel Craig.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) directs from a script by Steven Knight, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Pattinson beat fellow screen-testers Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden.
- The Mirror reports production is expected in 2027 and release in 2028, but no dates are confirmed.

Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Amazon MGM Studios revealed the casting on Monday through the character's official social accounts, showing a tuxedo-wearing Pattinson stepping out of the shadows with a gun in hand. The 40-year-old English actor takes over from Daniel Craig, who played 007 from 2005 to 2021, and becomes the seventh actor to hold the role on the big screen.

## How Robert Pattinson won the Bond role

The race lasted months. According to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/robert-pattinson-james-bond-cast-news-robert-eggers-werwulf-1236756972/), Pattinson beat Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden, all of whom screen-tested. The trade says Pattinson learned the news only on Sunday morning, when Villeneuve phoned him. Producer Amy Pascal was said to have told the other candidates.

The Mirror adds that the final group was chosen late last month and that each actor was seen on a different day in front of cameras and several directors. It reports that Turner and Lowden "didn't do enough" at the screen-test stage. The paper also notes that bookmakers once offered Pattinson at 50/1, while O'Connor was the favourite. These details are reported, not confirmed by the studio.

Entertainment journalist Matthew Belloni, quoted by the Mirror, argued that Pattinson was suited to the pressure of the job. "He's been a star since 2007," Belloni said, adding that Pattinson survived the backlash to his Twilight casting.

## What Pattinson and the producers said

In the Amazon MGM release, Pattinson called 007 "the honour of a lifetime" and said the role is a dream come true. He thanked Villeneuve, Pascal, producer David Heyman, Tanya Lapointe and the studio team.

Producers Pascal and Heyman said his "fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit, and his ability to continually reinvent himself" make him the right Bond "for our times". It is the first Bond film since long-time producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson stepped back and Amazon took creative control of the franchise.

## A prediction that has resurfaced

Pattinson's own words have drawn plenty of attention. The Hollywood Reporter notes that in 2012 he was asked whether he could be playing Bond in ten years. "Yeah, definitely," he said, "but more like 20 years", adding that after Craig the next Bond should have "some baggage".

His Twilight castmate Justin Chon joined in. [PerthNow](https://www.perthnow.com.au/entertainment/movies/robert-pattinsons-twilight-co-star-jokes-he-predicted-james-bond-role-as-2008-film-scene-resurfaces-c-23174214) reports that Chon posted a clip from the first Twilight film in which his character mentions "Bond. James Bond" just before Pattinson's Edward Cullen steps out of a car in a tuxedo.

Director Robert Eggers also weighed in. He told IGN that Pattinson was watching a screening of Eggers' upcoming film Werwulf when the announcement broke. Werwulf opens in the US on 25 December.

## Not everyone wanted a star

Reaction has been mixed but mostly warm. Writer Simon Dillon said on Substack that he would have preferred an unknown, though he expects Pattinson to do a good job and points to his work with David Cronenberg, Claire Denis and Christopher Nolan. Miss Flicks wrote that Pattinson was not its pick either, but praised the combination of Pattinson, Villeneuve and Knight. It also hopes the actor keeps making offbeat independent films between Bond jobs.

He has had a busy year. The Hollywood Reporter lists The Drama, The Odyssey and Primetime as 2026 releases, with Dune: Part Three still to come. He is also the star of The Batman, and Miss Flicks says the sequel is listed for February 2028.

## What happens next

The Mirror says the film, currently called Bond 26, is expected to start production in 2027 and reach cinemas in 2028, with no exact dates announced. Expect casting news for the supporting roles to follow. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who is the new James Bond?
Robert Pattinson, 40, was announced as the new 007 by Amazon MGM Studios on Monday 17 November 2026 (Australian time).

### Who is directing the next Bond film?
Denis Villeneuve is directing, working from a script by Steven Knight, according to The Hollywood Reporter. Villeneuve also directed Pattinson in Dune: Part Three.

### When will Bond 26 be released?
No official date has been given. The Mirror reports production is expected in 2027 and a cinema release in 2028.

### Who else was in the running?
Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden screen-tested alongside Pattinson, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
