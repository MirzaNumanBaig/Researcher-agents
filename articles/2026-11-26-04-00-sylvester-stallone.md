**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) told Cigar Aficionado that a Rocky prequel is more likely than a story in which he trains a protégé, according to MovieWeb.
- His idea is a story set with Mickey at 30 and Rocky at 16.
- Creed reached cinemas on 25 November 2015, so the franchise handover to Adonis Creed is 11 years old this week, ScreenRant notes.
- Creed IV is in development at Amazon MGM with no release date, and Stallone is not part of it.

A Sylvester Stallone Rocky prequel is now the most workable way back into the franchise he created, the actor says, and he has dropped the idea of mentoring a young fighter on screen. The comments, made to Cigar Aficionado and reported by [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/rocky-sylvester-stallone-pitches-prequel/) this week, land as the series marks 11 years since Creed took it in a new direction.

## What Stallone said about a Rocky return

Asked about bringing back [Rocky Balboa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Balboa), Stallone was frank. "It would be hard," he said, according to MovieWeb. He had planned to have a protégé and train him, he explained, but now thinks a prequel is the likelier route because it would be "so interesting". His pitch: Mickey at 30 years old, Rocky at 16.

That is a notable shift. The protégé idea grew out of a treatment Stallone teased on Instagram in 2023, MovieWeb reports, about a 27-year-old fighter called "Chucho the Mutt" and a Rocky defending his old neighbourhood from wealthy developers. The project was put on hold amid creative and ownership disputes and was never made.

## A prequel he has pitched before

The prequel is not new territory. MovieWeb says Stallone first shared handwritten treatment notes on Instagram in 2021 for a multi-season drama he described as "Lady and the Tramp, but with real people". It follows a teenage Rocky and Adrian in 1960s Philadelphia, alongside her brother Paulie and Rocky's future trainer, Mickey.

Like the scrapped sequel, that series remains in development limbo. MovieWeb also reports that Stallone has quietly written a script for a possible Rocky 7, in which an ageing Rocky helps the less fortunate in Philadelphia and never steps into the ring. It is also shelved.

## Why Creed still matters 11 years on

The timing is neat. [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/rocky-creed-switch-protagonist-save-franchise/) marked the anniversary of Creed, which opened on 25 November 2015 and moved Rocky into a supporting role. Ryan Coogler's film put [Adonis Creed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adonis_Creed), played by [Michael B. Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_B._Jordan), at the centre.

The numbers back up the gamble, according to ScreenRant. Creed grossed US$173.5 million worldwide, more than Stallone's last two Rocky sequels, and holds a 95% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), level with the 1976 original. Stallone earned an Oscar nomination for his supporting turn.

The franchise has kept moving without him. Stallone appeared in Creed II, then said he "bowed out" of Creed III, the first film in the wider series without Rocky. That film still made US$273.7 million, ScreenRant says, the second-highest total in the franchise.

## Where Stallone stands now

Per MovieWeb, Stallone is sitting out the upcoming Creed IV and has stepped back from the Creed spin-offs. He has, though, given his blessing to *I Play Rocky*, a biopic about his fight to get the 1976 film made. MovieWeb notes he went to extraordinary lengths to star in it, turning down lucrative offers for his script unless he could play the lead.

## What happens next

Nothing in the reports suggests the prequel is greenlit. Stallone described it as an idea, not a deal. Jordan is developing Creed IV for Amazon MGM with no release date, ScreenRant reports, while he finishes a Thomas Crown Affair remake and films Miami Vice '85. ScreenRant estimates 2028 as the earliest Adonis could return, and only if the film comes together quickly in 2027.

It also mentions a larger role for Adonis' daughter Amara, a possible Drago family spinoff and a Delphi TV series expected on Prime Video in 2027.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Rocky prequel officially happening?
No. Stallone has pitched it publicly, but MovieWeb describes it as still in development limbo, with no studio or release date announced.

### Will Stallone appear in Creed IV?
No. MovieWeb reports he is sitting out Creed IV, as he did Creed III. The film is in development at Amazon MGM without a release date.

### What is the Rocky prequel about?
Stallone's pitch centres on a teenage Rocky and Adrian in 1960s Philadelphia, with Paulie and the future trainer Mickey, according to MovieWeb.
