**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s *Ebenezer* opened at No. 1 in North America with an estimated $19 million, according to figures USA Today reported on 15 November.
- The film cost $38 million to make, per Variety as cited by USA Today.
- Reviews are mixed. Critics single out the ghosts and the supporting cast more than Depp's Scrooge.
- Audiences polled by CinemaScore gave it an A-minus.
- Depp's next release, the thriller *Day Drinker*, is due in March.

*Ebenezer*, Johnny Depp's first major studio film in eight years, has opened at the top of the North American box office, but the reviews tell a more divided story. Critics broadly agree that director Ti West's take on [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens)' ghost story looks striking and frightens well. They disagree on whether the star is the best thing in it.

## What the reviews say about Depp's Scrooge

USA Today's reviewer found Depp's performance enjoyable but out of step with the film around it. The paper described a Scrooge who is cruel to family, colleagues and beggars while fully aware of it, played partly for laughs under heavy old-age makeup. The reviewer said the horror elements around him are not funny at all, so he sometimes seems to belong to a different movie. The verdict: Depp is far from the best Scrooge on screen, but he does not embarrass himself or the source material.

The Niagara-on-the-Lake Local reached a similar view from a warmer angle. It called him occasionally too hammy and prone to showy choices, with real moments of vulnerability when the dialogue stays close to Dickens' own prose.

## The ghosts and the supporting cast

Both reviews give most of their praise to the supporting players. USA Today called Ian McKellen's Jacob Marley a truly haunting opening, and said Andrea Riseborough's pale, white-eyed Ghost of Christmas Past is the most frightening version of that spirit it has seen. It also highlighted Tramell Tillman as a hungry, pained Ghost of Christmas Present, with Robert Strange rounding out the phantoms as the Ghost of Christmas Future.

The Niagara-on-the-Lake Local picked out Tillman too, calling the performance masterful, and praised Rupert Grint as a grounded Bob Cratchit. USA Today added that Sam Claflin and Daisy Ridley play Scrooge's nephew Fred and his wife Clara, and that the extended Christmas Day redemption is where the film departs most from the original text.

The outlets differ on the film's bigger choices. USA Today felt it is too faithful to a story told many times, and wanted the ghosts' own hinted backstories explored. The Niagara-on-the-Lake Local liked the few new scenes about Marley's life and Scrooge's first love, Belle. It also noted the film may be too frightening for young children.

## How it performed at the box office

According to estimates from Rentrak released on Sunday, 15 November, *Ebenezer* earned $19 million over its opening weekend. USA Today called that decent but not spectacular, given the $38 million budget. It is the first Depp-led film to open at No. 1 since *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018.

For a fuller breakdown of the numbers, see our earlier report, [Johnny Depp's Ebenezer Opens at No. 1 With US$42M Worldwide](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-weekend/). Our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section has the rest of the coverage.

Audiences were kinder than critics. CinemaScore voters gave the film an A-minus, the same grade as Depp's *Alice in Wonderland* and *Charlie and the Chocolate Factory*, USA Today reported. The same piece noted that the other new wide releases fared worse: *How to Rob a Bank* took $5.175 million and finished seventh.

## A comeback in context

USA Today tied the film to Depp's recent years, noting that he was dropped from the *Fantastic Beasts* series in 2020 after losing a libel case against The Sun, and that a jury in Virginia largely sided with him in his 2022 defamation case against Amber Heard. The paper said the trailer leaned on the comeback angle, listing his best-known roles before Scrooge says it is "good to be back."

Depp's career began in 1984 with *A Nightmare on Elm Street*, followed by [*Private Resort*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Private_Resort) and *Platoon*, according to ScreenRant. The site also notes his Oscar nomination for *Finding Neverland*, a reminder of how wide his range has been. You can follow all of our [Johnny Depp coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/johnny-depp/) in one place.

## What happens next

The next test for Depp is *Day Drinker*, a thriller that also stars Madelyn Cline and Penélope Cruz, which opens in March. *Ebenezer*'s second weekend will show whether the A-minus audience score turns into steady holiday business, while the divided reviews will keep the debate about the film going into December.
