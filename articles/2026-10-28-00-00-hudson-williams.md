**Quick summary**

- [Lionsgate Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate%2B) has announced that the series Yaga will stream from 6 November 2026, according to Kerala TV.
- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays Henry Park, the missing heir at the centre of the mystery.
- The film April X, led by his [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) co-star [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie), follows on 27 November.
- Both titles are described as exclusive to Lionsgate Play.

Hudson Williams will be on Lionsgate Play in early November. The streamer has confirmed that Yaga, a fantasy horror mystery series in which he plays a missing fishing-business heir, premieres on 6 November 2026, [Kerala TV reports](https://www.keralatv.in/yaga-april-x-ott-release-date-lionsgate-play/). Three weeks later, Connor Storrie's sci-fi thriller April X joins it.

## What Yaga is about

The series was created and written by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler). According to Kerala TV, it blends crime investigation with fantasy and supernatural horror, and it opens with the disappearance of Henry Park, the heir to a powerful fishing business. Williams plays Henry.

[Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) plays private investigator Rapp, who travels to the coastal town of Whittock to look into the case. The report says he soon finds that several locals may be hiding secrets. [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo) appears as local detective Carson, and [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) plays university professor Katherine.

As the inquiry deepens, it becomes tied to the legend of Baba Yaga, the witch of Slavic folklore. Kerala TV says the show keeps viewers guessing about what is real and what might point to something beyond ordinary explanation.

## The cast and the directors

The ensemble is long. Besides the leads, the cast includes [Sheila McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheila_McCarthy), [Megan Follows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Follows), [Patrick Gilmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Gilmore_(actor)), [Ezra Franky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Franky) and [Emilija Baranac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilija_Baranac), among others listed in the report. The episodes are directed by [David Frazee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frazee) and [Rachel Talalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Talalay).

## April X: the second November release

April X is a separate project. Directed by [Michel K. Parandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_K._Parandi), it stars Storrie as Bax and [Lilly Krug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilly_Krug) as his twin sister April. Per Kerala TV, the story is set in a futuristic, post-Soviet city where the siblings can record their dreams onto discs. A deal involving a gangster named Sergei Tetris goes wrong, April vanishes, and Bax goes looking for her through a neon-lit criminal underworld.

Kerala TV frames the two releases as a chance to see both Heated Rivalry actors in darker, genre-heavy roles. Both stories, it notes, turn on a disappearance and hidden truths.

## What happens next

Yaga arrives on 6 November and April X on 27 November. The report does not say which countries Lionsgate Play is releasing them in, and it does not give episode counts or a weekly schedule for Yaga.
