**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) posted an Instagram carousel on Wednesday 25 November from a European trip with partner [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau).
- The caption, "The bob's euro debut", showed off her new short haircut.
- One photo shows the couple with French President [Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron), which drew most of the fan comments.
- Other pictures show card games, dinners and a stay on the Orient Express, according to HELLO!.
- The couple have been together since mid-2025.

[Katy Perry](https://moduscarry.com/tag/katy-perry/) has turned a holiday photo dump into one of the week's most talked-about celebrity posts. The 42-year-old singer shared a carousel on Instagram on Wednesday 25 November showing her new bob, a string of trip snaps with Justin Trudeau and, in one frame, a smiling selfie with the president of France.

## What the Instagram post showed

The first picture, according to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/11/26/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-europe-photos-hair-transformation/92969484007/), shows Perry in a golden bathtub as a tap runs, her blunt bob in full view. [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/celebrities/937383/katy-perry-major-change-daring-selfie-decadent-bath/) described the tub as brass and set beneath a stained-glass window. Perry captioned the set "The bob's euro debut".

The rest of the carousel was a mix of travel moments. USA Today reports pasta dinners, selfies and games. HELLO! says the images covered a stay on the Orient Express, a mini corset-dress dinner look and a visit to Venice, and that the trip took in Italy, France and Spain.

One card-game shot shows Perry holding three kings across the table from Trudeau, who wears a white blazer and black bow tie, USA Today reports.

## The Macron selfie

The picture that set off the comments was a group selfie of Perry, Trudeau and Macron, with Perry's large gold earrings hard to miss. [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/26/celebrity-news/katy-perry-and-justin-trudeau-third-wheeled-by-french-president-in-loved-up-selfie/) quoted fans joking that the couple had been "third-wheeled by the president of France" and that Perry was "collecting world leaders like side missions".

That second joke points back to a December 2025 photo of the pair with former Japanese prime minister [Fumio Kishida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumio_Kishida), which Page Six says made headlines at the time. HELLO! notes that Trudeau and Macron have been friends for years, first meeting at the May 2017 G7 Summit in Taormina, Sicily.

None of the outlets say why Macron was in the photo or where it was taken, so that detail remains unconfirmed.

## A new look, in its second outing

The Europe post was not the bob's first appearance. USA Today says Perry debuted the shorter cut in the United States at the Baby2Baby Gala in West Hollywood on Saturday 14 November, a clear change from her usual long, dark waves. It reports she had not worn a bob since spring 2024. She has said before that short hair suits her, telling W magazine in 2017, after a pixie cut, that "everything is more fun with short hair".

## How the relationship got here

Dating rumours first surfaced in July 2025, when the pair were seen walking in Montreal's Mount Royal Park, according to Page Six. That was a month after Perry ended her engagement to [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom), with whom she shares a six-year-old daughter, Daisy, USA Today reports. Page Six adds that Trudeau and his wife Sophie Grégoire Trudeau announced their legal separation in August 2023.

Perry told People that she "manifested" the relationship and that she met "the love of my life" on her [Lifetimes Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lifetimes_Tour), HELLO! reports. In June she told reporters at the Tribeca Festival premiere of her concert film, "Katy Perry: The Lifetimes Tour: Live from Paris", "I am very in love."

## What happens next

Perry has not said when the trip ends or what is next on her schedule. Her recent single [Watch It Burn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watch_It_Burn) and her catalogue, from [Teenage Dream](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teenage_Dream) onward, keep her in the headlines, and fans will be watching her Instagram for more from the trip.
