**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), director [Curry Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curry_Barker) and co-star [Michael Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnston_(actor)) discussed the future of *Obsession* in an interview with the LA Times, as reported by The Times of India.
- Barker said he has thought about a sequel but gave no details, and Johnston cut him off mid-tease.
- There is no official confirmation that a sequel is happening.
- The film cost under US$1 million and has earned US$525.25 million worldwide, according to The Times of India.
- Barker's next film, *Anything But Ghosts*, shares the same universe and is due in May next year. Navarrette says she is not in it.

The cast of *Obsession* has stopped short of announcing a sequel, but [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) and her colleagues made it clear the idea is not off the table. In a joint interview with the LA Times, covered by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/obsession-sequel-in-the-works-curry-barker-inde-navarrette-and-michael-johnston-tease-films-future/articleshow/136237094.cms), the actress, writer-director Curry Barker and co-star Michael Johnston talked about the film's runaway success and what could follow it.

## What the Obsession cast said about a sequel

The sequel talk began with a question about Nikki, the character Navarrette plays. According to The Times of India, the actress said she imagines Nikki coming through the ordeal and writing a book about it. She also agreed that her character would probably face a double homicide charge.

Navarrette added that she had seen videos from lawyers explaining how they would defend Nikki. Her own view, as quoted, was that Nikki would first have to understand what actually happened to her. From Nikki's side, she argued, the story is far more frightening than what the character Bear went through. She called women resilient and said Nikki would go on a journey.

That was Barker's cue. He began, "You know what would be absolutely insane," and Johnston interrupted with a shout of "Redact!" before any detail could escape. Barker also admitted to the interviewer that he has thought about a follow-up, though he said nothing more. Treat all of it as a tease, not a plan.

## Johnston earlier shut the door on Bear

Johnston has been less open to the idea in the past. The Times of India notes that he appeared on the *I've Never Said This Before* podcast earlier this year and said there was no plan for a sequel. He also questioned whether audiences would want Bear back, noting that the general view is that the character got what he deserved. At the same time he called it "a magical world" where anything can happen.

## Before the hit: dog walking, babysitting and coffee

The interview also looked at how different life was before *Obsession* landed. The cast described a nine-month gap around the film's premiere and theatrical release that left them in what they called limbo. Johnston said he had no auditions for a year and that it was "crickets".

Navarrette said she walked dogs, babysat and applied for a job at a coffee shop near her home, which she did not get. Barker worked as a barista while writing his scripts, describing the grind of thinking about the script all day at the cafe and then going home to write it.

Now the picture is reversed. Navarrette said studio executives, industry people and strangers all know the film, and that parents stop her to ask for a photo with her for their child. "It really is kind of an obsession," she said.

## The numbers behind the success

According to The Times of India, *Obsession* was made for less than US$1 million and has taken US$525.25 million at the box office worldwide, which the outlet describes as one of the most profitable films of all time. That works out to hundreds of times its budget, an unusual result for a horror release with a young cast.

## Who is Inde Navarrette?

A separate profile by [The Sunday Guardian](https://sundayguardianlive.com/sports/who-is-inde-navarrette-actress-spotted-with-ben-shelton-during-nba-game-after-rumoured-trinity-rodman-split-328010/) describes Navarrette as an American actress born Danielle Fabiola Navarrette in Tucson, Arizona. Her screen career began with the 2018 short *Cross Words Together*. She then appeared in the 2020 drama [*Wander Darkly*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wander_Darkly) alongside [Sienna Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sienna_Miller), and had television roles in Netflix's *13 Reasons Why* and The CW's *Superman & Lois*. The outlet calls the 2026 horror film *Obsession*, in which she plays Nikki Freeman, her breakthrough. It also cites Peacock's profile of her as saying she has finished filming the thriller *Invertigo*.

## What happens next

No studio or filmmaker has confirmed an *Obsession* sequel. The next confirmed project from Barker is [*Anything But Ghosts*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anything_but_Ghosts), which The Times of India says expands on the same universe and arrives in May next year. Navarrette confirmed during the interview that she does not appear in it. Fans hoping for answers about Nikki will have to wait for Barker to say more, and for Johnston to let him. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is there an Obsession sequel?

Not officially. Barker said he has thought about one, but no sequel has been confirmed by the cast or filmmakers.

### Is Inde Navarrette in Anything But Ghosts?

No. She said the film is set in the same universe as *Obsession* but that she does not appear in it. It is due in May next year.

### How much money did Obsession make?

The Times of India reports a worldwide total of US$525.25 million from a budget of under US$1 million.

### What did the Obsession cast do before the film was released?

Navarrette walked dogs and babysat, Barker worked as a barista, and Johnston said he had no auditions for a year.
