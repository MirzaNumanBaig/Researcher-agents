**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and [Priyanka Chopra Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra) are set to star in the sci-fi action thriller Bluefly, according to a notice published by FilmInk.
- Nimród Antal, who directed Predators and episodes of [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things), is directing.
- Principal photography is due to begin later this year on Australia's Gold Coast.
- Highland Film Group is handling worldwide sales and is launching them at TIFF.

Russell Crowe is heading back to an Australian set. The Oscar winner will co-lead Bluefly, a sci-fi action thriller about a black-ops team chasing a downed aircraft that shouldn't exist, with Priyanka Chopra Jonas, FilmInk reported in a release from Highland Film Group. Filming is planned for the Gold Coast later this year.

The announcement adds another title to a busy run for Crowe, who has several films lined up. First, the details of what Bluefly is actually about.

## What Bluefly is about

The film follows a grieving United Nations translator who is pulled into a black-ops mission deep in the Congo. A Special Operations team is sent upriver through militia-held territory to recover the aircraft. As they close in, the recovery turns into what the release calls a descent, and it becomes clear they were never meant to bring the craft home.

Director Nimród Antal described it as "an intense, character-driven thriller wrapped inside a bold science-fiction premise". He called it "grounded, propulsive, and full of dread". Antal's earlier credits include Predators and Stranger Things.

The script is credited to [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio), [William Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Eubank) and [Carlyle Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlyle_Eubank). Frigerio's credits include Beast and Land of Bad, and the Eubanks' include Underwater and The Signal.

## Who is attached

Crowe is a three-time Academy Award nominee who won the [Best Actor Oscar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Actor) for Gladiator. The release lists his recent work as Nuremberg and says he will next be seen in Netflix's thriller Unabomber, Vertical's The Weight, Walden Media's Billion Dollar Spy and Amazon MGM's Highlander reboot. We've covered the [streaming run of The Weight](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowes-the-weight-climbs-the-streaming-charts/) and [Gladiator's arrival on Netflix](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowes-gladiator-lands-on-netflix-this-weekend/) before.

Chopra Jonas brings her own streaming numbers. Her Prime Video film The Bluff debuted at number one worldwide, according to the release, and Heads of State, which she made alongside [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba) and John Cena, became Prime Video's fourth most-watched film. It has since earned an Emmy nomination for outstanding television movie. She is also in Citadel, and is due to appear in S.S. Rajamouli's Varanasi, set for release in April 2027.

The pairing is the selling point for buyers. Highland Film Group chief executive Arianne Fraser said the cast "will ignite the screen", while chief operating officer Delphine Perrier said the company can't wait to introduce the project to global buyers.

## The Australian connection

Bluefly is a local shoot as well as a local star. The producer Michael Jefferson founded Polite Society, an independent company with offices in Salt Lake City and Brisbane that works across the US and Australian markets. His earlier credits include Land of Bad, which starred Crowe and Liam Hemsworth.

Other producers named are David Frigerio for Broken Open Pictures, Deborah Glover for G2 Dispatch, Arianne Fraser and Delphine Perrier for Highland, and Vanessa Yao Guo. Executive producers include Lia Buman, Guy Davies, Brian Kavanaugh-Jones and James Norbury, among others. The release doesn't give a start date for filming or a release window.

## What happens next

Highland is launching worldwide sales at TIFF, so distribution deals are the first thing to watch. Shooting is expected on the Gold Coast later this year. No release date has been announced.
