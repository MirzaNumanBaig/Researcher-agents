**Quick summary**

- [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)'s sci-fi thriller Inception has spent more than 300 days in the VOD top 10 in 2026, according to Collider.
- The film has no streaming home in America, so viewers are renting or buying it on platforms such as Prime Video and Apple TV.
- Hardy is confirmed to lead MobLand Season 3 on Paramount+, after earlier reports that he had been fired were debunked.
- Critics are already comparing MobLand with Peaky Blinders, where Hardy played Alfie Solomons.

Fifteen years after it opened, Inception is still pulling in viewers, and its cast is back in the conversation. Collider reports that the Christopher Nolan film, which features Tom Hardy, has been in the video-on-demand top 10 for more than 300 days this year. That is a striking run for a film that first hit cinemas well over a decade ago.

## Tom Hardy Inception streaming run tops 300 days

[Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan)'s dream-heist thriller grossed more than US$830 million at the box office, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/tom-hardy-inception-streaming-success-pvod-100-days-november-2026/). The outlet notes that it has no subscription streaming home in America, which pushes fans towards paid rental and purchase. Prime Video and Apple TV are the two services it names.

The film follows a thief named Cobb, played by Leonardo DiCaprio, who steals secrets by entering people's dreams. His latest job is the reverse: planting an idea in a target's mind. Hardy sits in a cast that also includes Cillian Murphy, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Ken Watanabe, Elliot Page and Marion Cotillard.

Collider describes it as one of the most mind-bending science-fiction films of its era. A run of more than 300 days in the top 10 suggests the audience is not only nostalgic fans. Paid rentals tend to follow interest, so each new wave of attention on the cast may send more people back to it.

## Hardy's sci-fi record beyond Inception

Inception is not the only big genre title on Hardy's résumé. Collider points to Mad Max: Fury Road, the 2015 film directed by [George Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Miller_(filmmaker)) and co-starring [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron), as another high point of his 2010s run.

More recently, he has been tied to the Venom films as Eddie Brock, part of [Sony's Spider-Man Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony's_Spider-Man_Universe). Collider says that role has taken up so much of his time that he has had less room for other projects, and that his schedule looks more open now that his time as Brock appears to be behind him. That reading is the outlet's own and has not been confirmed by Hardy.

## MobLand puts Hardy back in a leading role

Hardy's 2026 has been quieter than most, Collider says, until he returned as Harry Da Souza in the second season of MobLand on Paramount+. The Guy Ritchie-directed crime series briefly raised alarm when news broke that Hardy had been fired and would miss Season 3.

According to Collider, those reports have since been debunked. MobLand has been renewed for a third season, and Hardy is confirmed to return in the leading role. We have followed the show closely, including [Hardy's return to set as Season 3 starts filming](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-filming/) and the [new rival Harry is set to face](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-rival/).

## Is MobLand the next Peaky Blinders?

Comic Book Resources critic Ben Morganti argues in an [opinion piece](https://www.cbr.com/tom-hardy-mobland-becoming-peaky-blinders/) that MobLand is quickly becoming the sprawling crime saga of the 2020s, though he says it needs a couple more seasons to overtake Peaky Blinders as the best London gangster drama.

His link is Hardy himself. Hardy played Alfie Solomons in Peaky Blinders, first an opponent of Thomas Shelby and later a steady ally, but always in a supporting role. MobLand gives him the lead. Morganti also wants [Cillian Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cillian_Murphy), who played Shelby, to take a small, purposeful part in the series as a nod to the shared setting.

That is a critic's wish, not a report. Nothing in the sources says Murphy has been approached or cast. For now it is a fan-friendly idea built on the fact that Hardy and Murphy already share a screen history, from Peaky Blinders to Inception.

## What happens next

MobLand Season 2 is still running, and Collider says the renewal for Season 3 is already in place with Hardy leading. No release date for the new episodes appears in the sources we read. Inception, meanwhile, remains available on rental and purchase platforms, and its VOD chart run shows no sign of ending on the evidence so far. For more on the actor, see [everything we publish about Tom Hardy](https://moduscarry.com/tag/tom-hardy/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Inception streaming in America?

Not on a subscription service, according to Collider. The film is available to rent or buy on platforms such as Prime Video and Apple TV.

### Is Tom Hardy returning for MobLand Season 3?

Yes. Collider reports that Hardy is confirmed in the leading role, and that earlier reports of him being fired have been debunked.

### Who does Tom Hardy play in MobLand?

He plays Harry Da Souza, a calculated criminal figure at the centre of the Paramount+ series directed by Guy Ritchie.

### Will Cillian Murphy join MobLand?

There is no such announcement. The idea comes from a Comic Book Resources opinion column, not from the show's makers.
