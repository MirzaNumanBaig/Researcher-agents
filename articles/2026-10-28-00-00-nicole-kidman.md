**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), 59, shared a series of photos on Instagram on 26 October.
- She wore a dark wash denim jacket and straight-leg jeans from the label Khaite, according to HELLO! Magazine.
- The jacket has a red rose appliqué, and she layered it over a black lace vest with a leather belt and snakeskin print pumps.
- Khaite is not new to her wardrobe, but Chanel remains her main red carpet house, HELLO! reports.

[Nicole Kidman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/nicole-kidman/) chose a head-to-toe denim look for her latest Instagram post. The Australian actress shared the photos on 26 October, and [HELLO! Magazine](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/931541/nicole-kidman-59-shows-off-longest-legs-in-daring-double-denim/) picked out the outfit the next day. Her height, listed by the magazine as 5'11, made the straight-leg cut a talking point.

## What Nicole Kidman wore

According to HELLO!, the outfit was a dark wash co-ord from Khaite. The jacket is buttoned and carries a red rose appliqué on the front. The matching jeans have a straight leg, which is the detail the magazine says made her legs look so long in the photographs.

Underneath, she wore a black lace vest, and a leather belt finished the middle of the outfit. The shoes were snakeskin print pumps, a sharper choice than the usual sneaker that tends to go with denim.

Her hair was swept back into a high ponytail with face-framing strands left loose. HELLO! describes the makeup as a bronze eye with a nude lip.

## A label she has worn before

The Khaite pairing is not a one-off. The magazine points to her appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in March, when she wore a cropped leather jacket from the same label. That jacket had a tailored shape, button detail along the bodice and strong shoulders. She styled it with a plain black maxi skirt, sheer stockings and pointed-toe heels.

Put the two looks side by side and a pattern shows. Both rely on structured tailoring, and both keep the colour palette tight, with the denim look adding a single red accent in the rose.

## Chanel is still her red carpet house

HELLO! is careful to say Khaite is not her main label. In its words, her go-to house for red carpets will always be Chanel. The magazine recalls that she announced in October that she would return to Chanel as an ambassador, after Matthieu Blazy made his debut as the house's new creative director. The article does not state the year of that announcement.

In the statement quoted by HELLO!, Kidman said she was thrilled to join Chanel as it begins a new chapter. She said she has a deep appreciation for Haute Couture and looks forward to wearing what she expects will be gorgeous creations. She also said Chanel has always been ahead of the curve, and compared Blazy to Karl Lagerfeld.

Blazy responded in kind, according to the same report. He pointed to the [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) film as part of the house's history with her, and called the reunion a dream come true. He also said he had worked with her before.
