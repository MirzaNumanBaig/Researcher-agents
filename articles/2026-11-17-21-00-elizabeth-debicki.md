**Quick summary**

- Elizabeth Debicki James Bond comments: she calls Tenet co-star [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) "perfect" for 007, per Female First, quoting Variety.
- Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Pattinson as the next 007 on Monday. The film is expected in cinemas in 2028.
- Debicki spoke at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, per ComingSoon.net.
- That Netflix film opens in IMAX on 25 November and streams from 23 December.

The Elizabeth Debicki James Bond reaction is in, and it is warm. [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki), the Australian actor, says Robert Pattinson, her co-star in Christopher Nolan's 2020 thriller Tenet, is the right person to take over 007 as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), days after Amazon MGM Studios made the casting official.

## Elizabeth Debicki James Bond reaction in full

Female First reports that Debicki, 36, is "so thrilled" for Pattinson, 40. She told Variety: "I'm so happy for Rob. He is an enigma himself." She went on to describe him as "beautiful", "funny" and "smart", and said: "I think he's perfect."

ComingSoon.net says she made the comments at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. Asked why he suits the role, she pointed to his character rather than his résumé. She also said he has "evolved so much as an actor" and has made "really interesting choices".

It is a personal endorsement from someone who has shared a set with him. ComingSoon.net notes that Tenet also featured John David Washington, Michael Caine and Kenneth Branagh.

## How the Bond casting unfolded

Amazon MGM Studios announced Pattinson on Monday, following months of speculation, according to ComingSoon.net. He succeeds [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), whose five-film run began with Casino Royale in 2006 and ended with No Time to Die in 2021.

[Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct, with Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight writing, Female First reports. Amy Pascal and David Heyman produce, and Tanya Lapointe is executive producer. Pattinson called the part the "honour of a lifetime". Villeneuve said the actor "has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new".

The Hollywood Reporter India says the screen tests reportedly also included [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor. That detail is reported, not confirmed by the studio.

## Other voices on Pattinson as 007

The Hollywood Reporter India says [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) backed the choice and said he would like to play the villain opposite Pattinson. [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes), who played M alongside Craig, said he will need time to let go of Craig's version of the spy. The outlet describes his tone as measured, not negative.

[George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby), the Australian who played Bond in 1969's On Her Majesty's Secret Service, also supported the casting, the same report says.

## A big week for Debicki

The Bond comments landed in the middle of a busy stretch. The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, directed by [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher), extends the world of Quentin Tarantino's [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood) and brings back Pitt and Timothy Olyphant. Headline Planet reports a limited IMAX run from 25 November, then a Netflix debut on 23 December. Debicki attended a Los Angeles screening and Q&A with Fincher, moderated by Jonathan Groff.

On premiere night she also collected an ELLE Women in Hollywood honour, wearing a white gown with a feathered clutch to the event in Beverly Hills, according to ELLE. In its cover story, she said it took a while to find work she wanted after playing Princess Diana in The Crown. She chose Cliff Booth and the fairy-tale comedy Wicker.

## What happens next

The new Bond film is expected in 2028, so there is a long wait. Closer to hand, Cliff Booth arrives in IMAX on 25 November and on Netflix on 23 December. Debicki's other project, Wicker, has no release date in the sources we read.
