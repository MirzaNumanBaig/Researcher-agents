**Quick summary**

- [Priyanka Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra) will play a United Nations interpreter named Myla Reese in the sci-fi film Bluefly, opposite [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe).
- Chopra told The Hollywood Reporter she is working with a dialect coach on Swahili, Zulu and Lingala.
- Production has already begun in Queensland, Australia, with Nimród Antal directing.
- [Michael Chiklis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chiklis) is also in the cast, as a US government operative.

Priyanka Chopra has described her role in Russell Crowe's new sci-fi film Bluefly, and it is a long way from the action parts she is known for. According to a [Times Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Now) report on her Hollywood Reporter interview, she plays a UN interpreter, and the film is already shooting in Queensland.

## What is Bluefly about?

The film follows two characters, Myla Reese (Chopra) and Benny (Crowe), who travel on a covert mission to the Congo. Benny is a former pilot. Their goal is to recover a missing military aircraft, and deep in the jungle they run into what the report calls a powerful force.

Nimród Antal directs. The screenplay is credited to [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio), [William Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Eubank) and [Carlyle Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlyle_Eubank). Chiklis, the American actor, rounds out the cast named so far as a US government operative.

We have covered the shoot before. Our earlier piece, [Russell Crowe's Bluefly: Sci-Fi Thriller Shooting in Queensland](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-bluefly-sci-fi-thriller/), set out the basics. This new interview adds the first real detail about Chopra's character.

## Priyanka Chopra's language challenge

The role asks a lot of her voice. Chopra said she is working with a dialect coach to get the words right in several African languages, "from Swahili to Zulu to Lingala." She called that "the challenge" of the part.

She has a personal link to the job. According to the report, Chopra has been a UNICEF ambassador for around 15 years and has met many UN translators in that time. That experience gave her a way into a character who works with words rather than weapons.

## A change from action roles

Chopra made her Hollywood film debut in Baywatch in 2017. Since then, the report notes, she has appeared in the spy series Citadel and the action films Heads of State (2025) and The Bluff (2026).

She said she was glad to try something different. "I've mostly played the opposite, someone very fond of conflict, especially physically," she told The Hollywood Reporter. She added that across her career she has played many kinds of characters, in drama and comedy, and that she enjoys the character work.

She is already in Australia for the shoot, and her daughter Malti Marie Jonas is with her, according to the report.

## What else is on Chopra's calendar

Bluefly is one of several projects. Times Now lists her as Mandakini in S. S. Rajamouli's big-budget Varanasi, due in 2027, alongside [Mahesh Babu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahesh_Babu) and [Prithviraj Sukumaran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prithviraj_Sukumaran). She has filmed a cameo in Mira Nair's biopic Amri, about the painter [Amrita Sher-Gil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amrita_Sher-Gil), and will appear in the Hollywood films Reset and Judgement Day in 2027.

She also had to leave Ritesh Batra's Long Years Ago because of a scheduling clash. [Vidya Balan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidya_Balan) took her place opposite [Saif Ali Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saif_Ali_Khan) in the period drama.

## What happens next

Filming is under way in Queensland, and none of the sources we read give a release date or a studio. Until a distributor confirms one, treat any date you see online as unofficial. Chopra's comments suggest the cast is still early in the shoot, with language preparation continuing.
