**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) returns as mercenary Tyler Rake in *Extraction 3*, according to MovieWeb.
- Filming began in New South Wales in July 2026 and moved to Thailand for its final stretch.
- Hemsworth posted from Thailand on 28 October suggesting the shoot had wrapped, though Netflix had not announced it.
- Netflix is also developing two spin-offs: a series called *Mercenary* and a South Korean film, *Tygo*.

*Extraction 3* is on its way to Netflix with Chris Hemsworth back in the lead, and the streamer is building a wider franchise around it. MovieWeb reported on 17 November that the third film has a returning core cast, several new faces and a production that started in Australia before finishing in Thailand.

## Why Netflix keeps coming back to Tyler Rake

The first *Extraction* arrived in 2020 and introduced Hemsworth as Tyler Rake, a black-ops mercenary on a rescue mission. *Extraction 2* followed in 2023 with bigger set pieces and an extended action sequence that MovieWeb says audiences still talk about.

The numbers explain the appetite. According to MovieWeb, citing Netflix, the original film drew 135.7 million views in its first 91 days. The sequel reached 129.3 million views in just 66 days, and it became the first film in the series to make Netflix's Most Popular list. Fewer days for a similar total is the detail that matters here: the second film moved faster than the first.

Director Sam Hargrave, who has built the series around practical stunt work, is back for the third film.

## Who is in the cast

Hemsworth leads again. [Golshifteh Farahani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golshifteh_Farahani) returns as Nik Khan, and [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba) reprises Alcott. That character was introduced at the end of *Extraction 2* as a mysterious figure offering Rake and Nik a new opportunity, so the reunion should pick up that thread.

The newcomers include [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), [Joe Taslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Taslim), [Chi Lewis-Parry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_Lewis-Parry) and [Siena Agudong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siena_Agudong). [Don Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Dong-seok) has also signed on. He will headline the Korean spin-off *Tygo* too, and MovieWeb points to his physical action roles in *The Roundup* and *Train to Busan*.

Netflix has not revealed the plot, so the details of the new assignment remain under wraps.

## A new writer and an Australian start

David Weil wrote the screenplay. According to MovieWeb, he takes over from [Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers), who wrote the first two films.

Production started in New South Wales in July 2026 before the crew moved to Thailand. On 28 October, Hemsworth shared a social media post from Thailand that indicated filming had finished. MovieWeb notes that Netflix had not made a formal announcement at that point, so treat the wrap as unconfirmed by the studio.

## The spin-offs

*Extraction 3* is one piece of a larger plan. Netflix is developing two spin-offs, and MovieWeb compares the strategy to how *John Wick* grew beyond its title character.

The first is *Mercenary: An Extraction Series*, an eight-episode action thriller. [Omar Sy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Sy) plays Kalidou Diallo, a mercenary caught between rival factions while trying to rescue hostages in Libya. It was created by [Glen Mazzara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glen_Mazzara), a veteran of *The Walking Dead*, and also stars [Boyd Holbrook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyd_Holbrook) and Natalie Dormer. Production wrapped in March 2026.

The second is *Tygo*, a South Korean action film led by Don Lee, Lee Jin-uk and BLACKPINK's Lisa. Lee plays a former child soldier turned mercenary who goes after revenge when a job goes wrong. His character is the link to *Extraction 3*.

## What happens next

Netflix has not set a release date or shown a trailer for *Extraction 3*, and the plot is still secret. The next confirmations to watch for are an official wrap announcement and a release window. Until then, the cast list and the two spin-offs are the clearest signals of where the franchise is heading.
