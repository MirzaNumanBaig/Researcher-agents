**Quick summary**

- Netflix has released the first poster for its heist thriller The Take, according to Metro Mode.
- The film stars Denzel Washington, Robert Pattinson and Daisy Edgar-Jones and arrives on 15 January.
- It lands on Netflix worldwide on the same day as a limited cinema release.
- No official trailer exists yet; clips online under that name are fan-made.

The first poster for Netflix's heist thriller The Take is out, and it puts [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) at opposite sides of a table, eyes locked. According to [Metro Mode](https://metromode.se/en/film-och-tv/denzel-washington-och-robert-pattinson-ranar-bank-i-netflix-the-take/), Netflix shared the artwork in a Facebook post and confirmed the film premieres on 15 January 2027, the same day it gets a limited cinema release.

## What the Netflix poster shows

The image is built on tension rather than action. Pattinson and Washington sit across from each other in a dark room with their hands clasped, according to Metro Mode's description. The tagline reads "Time to get what you deserve".

That restraint matches how Netflix has handled the film so far. The official description is short: a bank guard, a cashier and a master thief are pulled into a deadly game of deception and double-dealing. Netflix has also held back a trailer. Only the poster and a handful of stills from filming are public.

## Who plays who in The Take

Some plot detail has surfaced beyond the official line. Metro Mode reports, citing What's on Netflix, which in turn cites [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), that Pattinson plays Noah, a former army officer struggling to settle back into civilian life. Washington plays master thief Christian Carter, who recruits Noah to rob Manhattan's richest bank.

The complication is personal. [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones) plays Rose, Noah's girlfriend, who works as a cashier at that same bank. The whole film is reportedly told from Noah's point of view.

The supporting cast includes [Danai Gurira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danai_Gurira) as Oaklynn, [Sean Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Harris) as Logan, Moisés Arias as Gabriel and [Justin Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kirk) as Anderson.

## The team behind the camera

Brazilian director [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles), who was Oscar-nominated for City of God and made The Two Popes for Netflix, is directing. The screenplay is by [Simon Kinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Kinberg), whose credits include the X-Men films and Deadpool. He also produces through [Genre Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genre_Films) with Audrey Chon.

The project has been a long time coming. Netflix bought Kinberg's script in 2020 under the title Here Comes the Flood, and that was the working title during filming between November 2025 and January 2026. Locations included New York, Jersey City, Hoboken, Newark and Atlanta.

## A first for several of its stars

For Washington and Edgar-Jones, The Take is the first film made directly for Netflix, per Metro Mode. Pattinson has been there before, with The King and The Devil All the Time. Washington's two Oscars came for Glory and [Training Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Training_Day), while Edgar-Jones rose to fame in the series [Normal People](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_People) before moving into films such as Twisters.

Pattinson, who has also been named the next James Bond according to headlines on the same site, did not hesitate over this one. "Getting to make a heist film in New York with Denzel Washington and Daisy Edgar-Jones, what more is there to say?" he said, as quoted via Kinocheck. For more on his recent run, see our piece on [how Pattinson's quirky edge won him the Bond role](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-quirky/).

## What happens next

The next milestone is a trailer. Metro Mode says What's on Netflix expects a first teaser or trailer could arrive in December, though that is a forecast, not an announcement. Any video labelled "The Take Trailer" on YouTube at the moment is fan-made, not from Netflix.

Until then, 15 January is the date to note. Our earlier report covers [the cast and release details](https://moduscarry.com/the-take-film-pattinson-washington/), and you can follow more coverage in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is The Take released?
Netflix says The Take premieres on 15 January 2027. It streams worldwide on Netflix that day and also opens in a limited number of cinemas.

### Who stars in The Take?
Denzel Washington, Robert Pattinson and Daisy Edgar-Jones lead the cast. Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk appear in supporting roles.

### Is there an official trailer for The Take?
Not yet. Netflix has released a poster and a few stills. Trailers circulating online under the film's name are fan-made.

### Who directed The Take?
Fernando Meirelles directs from a script by Simon Kinberg.
