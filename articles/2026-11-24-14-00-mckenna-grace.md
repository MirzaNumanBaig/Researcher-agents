[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) has a streaming date for her next film. Nimrods, a coming-of-age road comedy rated R, will be available on Peacock from December 18, [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-nimrods-peacock-streaming-release-date/), after a quiet run in cinemas. The news lands in the same week her Hunger Games prequel is dominating the box office.

## What Nimrods is about

Lee Kirk wrote and directed the film. It follows three teenage friends who drive from Kansas City to Los Angeles, convinced their punk band has landed its big break: a slot opening for [Green Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Day) on New Year's Eve.

Mason Thames plays Tommy, a band member who meets Grace's character, Olivia, on the trip. According to ScreenRant, the two characters form a romantic connection along the way. The cast also includes Jenna Fischer and Angela Kinsey from The Office, Fred Armisen, and Green Day's Billie Joe Armstrong, Mike Dirnt and Tré Cool.

Nimrods first screened at TIFF. ScreenRant says reviews were largely positive and put the film at 72% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). Critics reportedly felt that a scrappy indie charm and lively performances, backed by a soundtrack of Green Day favourites and lesser-known tracks, outweighed a familiar road-comedy formula.

## A reunion with Mason Thames

This is the second film pairing Grace with Thames. The two appeared together in the Colleen Hoover adaptation Regretting You (2025), where he played Miller Adams and she played Clara Grant. ScreenRant notes that their characters again drift into romance in Nimrods, this time on the road rather than in a family drama.

## The Hunger Games backdrop

The streaming date follows The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which reached cinemas on November 20. ScreenRant reports an opening of $120 million in the United States and $225 million worldwide. Grace plays a dual role: Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute, and Maysilee's identical twin Merrilee, who stays behind. [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) stars as Haymitch Abernathy.

For a performer who earned an Emmy nomination for The Handmaid's Tale and played Phoebe Spengler in Ghostbusters: Afterlife and [Frozen Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghostbusters%3A_Frozen_Empire), the past few months have been unusually busy. ## The secret behind the Mockingjay wings

The press tour had its own storyline. Grace ended it on November 16 at the New York premiere, wearing a custom cobalt Pamella Roland gown with oversized Claudia Marin wings, a nod to the Mockingjay dress. Her stylist, Molly Dickson, told [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/style/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-mockingjay-dress-stylist) that keeping it hidden felt like a "top-secret mission". The team slipped the wings into a box in her car minutes before she left a busy Manhattan hotel.

Dickson said the look took roughly six months, starting with calls to designers in early May. The wings alone needed three days of non-stop work. The tour opened in Paris on October 6 with a recreation of Katniss's wedding gown, and Dickson said the plan was always to close with the wings. Nimrods streams on Peacock on December 18. The sources do not give an Australian release date, so local viewers should check their own streaming services closer to the time. Sunrise on the Reaping stays in cinemas, and its second weekend figures will show whether the opening holds.
