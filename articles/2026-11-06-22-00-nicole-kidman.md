**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on 5 November, wearing a black suit and a freshly cut fringe.
- Her hairstylist, Adir Abergel, told Vogue the soft, wispy bangs were designed to echo Sydney's harbour and sandstone.
- The show at The Cutaway in Barangaroo was Chanel's first runway presentation in Australia, according to Vogue.
- Margot Robbie, Kylie Minogue, Baz Luhrmann, Joel Edgerton and Cathy Freeman also attended, Hindustan Times reports.

Nicole Kidman arrived home in Sydney on 5 November for Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show with a new look: a long, feathery fringe and a sharply tailored black suit. The Oscar winner was one of the best-known guests at the label's first-ever Australian runway, held at The Cutaway in Barangaroo, according to [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/lifestyle/fashion/margot-robbies-sheer-white-dress-to-nicole-kidmans-chic-pantsuit-celebrities-bring-glamour-to-chanel-cruise-2026-27-show-in-sydney-101793959271630.html).

## What Nicole Kidman wore to Chanel in Sydney

Hindustan Times describes the outfit as classic power dressing with a contemporary twist. Kidman wore a black suit with a crisp white waistcoat layered over a matching top, which gave the look a monochrome finish. Her beauty look kept things relaxed: tousled, wavy hair, softly blushed cheeks and nude lips.

That loose styling is the point of the outfit. The tailoring is strict, while the hair and make-up soften it. Fashion outlets have been noting that balance across the week's coverage of the event.

## The fringe: what her hairstylist said

The detail that drew the most attention was the hair. In an exclusive comment to [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/nicole-kidman-fresh-fringe-is-cool-without-trying), hairstylist Adir Abergel said having Kidman back in Sydney for the show "felt really special". Kidman was born in Honolulu, Hawaii, and moved to Sydney shortly afterwards, as Vogue notes.

Abergel said the collection is about the seaside, and that Sydney shares that mood through its harbour, sandstone and salt air. He wanted her hair to feel part of that world: "easy, sun-kissed, a little undone". He also described an overlap between Australian ease and French "nonchalance", which suits a French house shown in an Australian city.

The cut itself is long, soft and wispy. Abergel said the bangs graze the brow and are broken up so they move, then blended into her long layers. They can be worn straight across, swept to the side or parted in the middle for a curtain effect.

Vogue also places the fringe in a sequence. It follows the shorter, rounder bangs she debuted at a Chanel show in October last year, and the fluffier curtain style before that. In other words, this is the third version of a look she has been refining at Chanel events.

## Who else was at the show

Kidman was far from the only familiar face. Hindustan Times lists fellow Australians [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), filmmaker [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), actor [Joel Edgerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Edgerton) and Olympic champion [Cathy Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathy_Freeman) among the guests.

- **Margot Robbie:** a sheer white slip dress with beadwork, black heels and a handbag, with short hair and nude make-up.
- **Kylie Minogue:** an all-white outfit of cardigan and pencil skirt, two-tone pumps and layered chain belts.
- **Cathy Freeman:** a white shirt made with French shirtmaker Charvet, worn with jeans.

## The collection behind the night

The Sydney presentation followed the collection's debut in Biarritz, France, in April, Hindustan Times reports. Creative director Matthieu Blazy designed it, drawing on the seaside town where Gabrielle Chanel opened her first couture house. The runway mixed the house's tweed suits with fishnet dresses, striped sailor-style tops and Mediterranean-inspired prints, along with woven bags and statement footwear.
