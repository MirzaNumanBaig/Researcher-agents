**Quick summary**

- A new off-Broadway play, *A Kidman Carol*, reimagines Dickens with [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) as Scrooge.
- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) is the visitor who teaches her the "true meaning of awards season", according to Vogue.
- [Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle) plays Kidman and [Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp) plays Blanchett.
- Playwright Kevin Zak wrote it, and the show has a six-week run at New World Stages, Vogue reports.

Cate Blanchett has been turned into a Christmas curmudgeon. In *A Kidman Carol*, a new off-Broadway comedy, the Australian actress takes the Ebenezer Scrooge role and is visited by her fellow Australian Nicole Kidman, who is there to show her what awards season is really about. Vogue [spoke to the three people behind the show](https://www.vogue.com/article/marla-mindelle-josh-sharp-and-kevin-zak-a-kidman-carol) just ahead of the holidays.

Neither star is reported to be involved in the production. It is a parody in which performers play the two actresses.

## How a meme became a play

Writer Kevin Zak began with a joke. According to Vogue, he is better known for his pop culture memes online, and last year wrote *Ginger Twinsies*, a Millennial satire of *The Parent Trap*. *A Kidman Carol* grew from a single idea: Blanchett gets the Dickensian treatment, with Kidman as the ghost.

A friend who runs the Alleyway Theatre in Buffalo, New York, offered him a few nights to workshop anything in 2024, and he chose this show. A workshop presentation followed last winter. Mindelle, known for the hit *Titanique*, and Sharp, whose film *Dicks: The Musical* is backed by A24, both started in small comedy clubs. Sharp joked that five performances turned into a full six-week run at New World Stages.

## Why Blanchett makes a good Scrooge

Sharp told Vogue that Blanchett is seen as a "pure actress" who might ask why she has to make TikToks, while Kidman has used her fame to make a wider range of projects. Because there is no well-known Blanchett impression, he said, the writers could bend her into a curmudgeon. Kidman, in the play, shares her frustration but is more hopeful.

Zak pointed to the intensity of Blanchett's screen work. In his reading, characters in *Tár*, *Carol* and *Notes on a Scandal* share a sense that the world is against them. That, he said, gives the Scrooge role its engine.

## Two stars, one difference

The trio spent time on what separates the pair. Mindelle, who rewatched [To Die For](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Die_For) while preparing, described Kidman as having about "10% more camp" and a knowing wink. Blanchett, she said, is all about the work. Zak put it another way: Kidman's characters get hurt, while Blanchett's get mad.

They also noted something odd. Zak said Kidman and Blanchett have never made a film together. For Sharp and Zak, Kidman's star moment was her turn in [Batman Forever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Forever).

The play is, in Vogue's words, an earnestly pro-awards show piece. Asked for a recent shocking Oscar win, Zak chose *Minari*, when [Youn Yuh-jung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youn_Yuh-jung) won best supporting actress and teased [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt), a producer of the film, for never visiting the set.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is A Kidman Carol?
It is an off-Broadway comedy by Kevin Zak that borrows the structure of Dickens's *A Christmas Carol*. Cate Blanchett is the Scrooge figure and Nicole Kidman is her visitor.

### Who stars in A Kidman Carol?
Marla Mindelle plays Nicole Kidman and Josh Sharp plays Cate Blanchett, according to Vogue. Neither Blanchett nor Kidman is reported to appear.

### Where is A Kidman Carol playing?
Vogue says it has a six-week run at New World Stages, an off-Broadway venue in New York.
