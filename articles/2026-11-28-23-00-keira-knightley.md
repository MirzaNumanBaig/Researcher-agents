**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley), 41, told Metro her 11-year-old daughter refuses to watch the 2005 film of Pride and Prejudice.
- The actress plans to introduce her daughter to the story through Netflix's new six-part adaptation, due in December.
- The interview was to promote Black Doves season 2, where Knightley plays the spy Helen Webb.
- Separately, her Atonement gown is estimated at £100,000 to £120,000 for Christie's London sale on 9 December.

Keira Knightley has a plan for the one audience member who won't watch her best-loved film. Speaking to Metro on 28 November about Black Doves, the 41-year-old said her 11-year-old daughter has flatly refused to see her 2005 turn as Elizabeth Bennet, so Netflix's forthcoming adaptation will become the girl's first Pride and Prejudice.

## Why the new adaptation matters to Knightley

Netflix's version arrives in December as a six-part series written by [Dolly Alderton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Alderton). According to Metro, Emma Corrin plays Elizabeth and Slow Horses actor Jack Lowden plays Mr Darcy, following the 2005 film in which Knightley starred opposite [Matthew Macfadyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Macfadyen).

Knightley said she is looking forward to it, and her reasons are personal. "My kids, they completely refuse to watch anything that I'm in," she told the interviewer. She added that her daughter "totally refused to watch mine", so "this will be her entry point".

Her own route into Jane Austen came the same way. She was 11 when the BBC series with [Jennifer Ehle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Ehle) and [Colin Firth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Firth) aired, and she said Ehle "was always my Elizabeth Bennet". That series, she said, introduced her to the novelist [Jane Austen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen).

## "Mum, I don't want to watch you do that"

The Metro writer admitted to wishing the daughter would watch the 2005 film as well. Knightley took it in good humour. "I wish she would watch mine as well," she said, relaying her daughter's reply: "Mum, I don't want to watch you do that." Her answer was a shrug: "I get it. It's fine."

She also made a point about age. People tend to claim the version they first saw at around 11, she suggested, which is why each generation has its own. [Ambika Mod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambika_Mod), her Black Doves co-star, said she was 10 when Knightley's film came out and called it "mine". Both The News International and Metro reported the exchange.

## Helen Webb and the "Godfather moment"

The interview itself centred on [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves), the spy series returning for a second season. Knightley plays Helen Webb, a covert operative married to a high-profile politician. She told Metro the role felt like her "Godfather moment", given how obsessed she was with the film as a teenager.

"I think she's as close as I'm ever going to get to a mafia boss," she said. Mod plays newcomer Laila, introduced as the muscle on a mission to retrieve an item from a safe, and Metro describes the pair's dynamic as both funny and gripping. Mod said little preparation went into the part because the character felt clear on the page.

## A gown with a price tag

Knightley's screen history is also in the news for another reason. Christie's will sell the emerald-green gown she wore in Atonement at a live sale in London on 9 December, according to Wisevoter, which credits ARTnews. The estimate is £100,000 to £120,000, roughly three times the £35,000 it made at a 2008 charity auction. Our earlier report covers the details in [Keira Knightley's Atonement gown heads to Christie's](https://moduscarry.com/keira-knightley-atonement-dress-2/). The gown was designed by Jacqueline Durran and is part of the Love & Lust: A Literary Guide sale.
