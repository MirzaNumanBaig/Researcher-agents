**Quick summary**

- Queen Camilla greeted singer James Blunt as "Ryan, formerly known as James" after the Royal Variety Performance on 20 November, according to TODAY.com.
- The joke refers to Blunt's promise to change his name to Ryan Air if his album *Airplane Mode* reached No. 1 in Britain.
- The album did reach the top, and Blunt then posted a name-change application showing the new name Ryan Bel Air.
- Blunt told the PA news agency his wife is "not pleased" with him.

[James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has spent years making himself the target of his own jokes, and this week the Queen joined in. After the Royal Variety Performance at the Royal Albert Hall on 20 November, Queen Camilla met the singer and greeted him with "Hello, Ryan, formerly known as James", according to [TODAY.com](https://www.today.com/popculture/music/james-blunt-name-change-ryan-bel-air-queen-camilla-rcna610921), which cited British news reports and video shared on social media.

## How the James Blunt Ryan Bel Air joke began

The line only makes sense with the promise behind it. Before his new album, *Airplane Mode*, came out, Blunt, 52, said that if it reached No. 1 on the British charts he would legally change his name to Ryan Air, after the Irish budget airline. His pitch to fans was to stream it and buy it, "otherwise, I'll have to carry on being James Blunt... and nobody wants that."

Fans delivered. "It's number one!" Blunt told PA after the show, adding that his mother had bought a lot of copies. According to TODAY.com, it is his first No. 1 album in Britain since *All the Lost Souls* in 2007. His 2005 debut, *Back to Bedlam*, spent 10 weeks at the top.

## From Ryan Air to Ryan Bel Air

Blunt first hinted in a video that he might back out. He then posted on Instagram an application to change his name from James Hillier Blount, his birth name, to Ryan Bel Air. The caption said the paperwork cost him £53.05, about US$70 by TODAY.com's conversion. The airline replied in his comments that its lawyers would be in touch.

The change has not gone down well at home. Asked by PA whether he had really become Ryan Bel Air, Blunt winced. "My wife is not pleased with me," he said, adding that she might change her name back to her maiden name. TODAY.com names his wife as Sofia Wellesley.

## Not the first name bet

This is not Blunt's first wager with his own name. In 2024 he said fans could choose a new name for him if the 20th anniversary reissue of *Back to Bedlam* hit No. 1. Fans picked Blunty McBluntface, but the reissue fell short and he stayed James.

His stage name was already a change. Blunt has said he dropped the "o" from Blount because other children teased that it rhymed with an obscene word, according to TODAY.com.

## The Royal Variety night

At the Royal Variety show, Blunt sang his new song "Circles". The host, [Jason Manford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Manford), introduced him as both James Blunt and Ryan Air. TODAY.com reports that Blunt was among the performers who met King Charles and the Queen afterwards.

## What happens next

Blunt has not said whether he will keep the name. The sources do not report any further plans, and the airline has not said whether it will act on its reply. With *Airplane Mode* at No. 1, the open question is how long the name stays on his passport.
