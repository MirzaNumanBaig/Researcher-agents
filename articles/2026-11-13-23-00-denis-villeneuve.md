A free way to revisit "Blade Runner 2049" has arrived just before Amazon's follow-up series. [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s 2017 sequel is now streaming at no cost on Pluto TV, [BGR reports](https://www.bgr.com/2315458/ryan-gosling-harrison-ford-sci-fi-epic-blade-runner-2049-streaming-free/), and "Blade Runner 2099" premieres on Prime Video on 25 November. BGR's report does not say which regions can watch it, so Australian viewers should check the Pluto TV app before planning a weekend around it.

## Why the timing matters

"Blade Runner 2099" takes place 50 years after the events of "2049", according to BGR. [Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh) plays Olwen, a replicant nearing the end of her life. Anyone who wants the background before the series lands can rent or buy both films on Prime Video, but BGR notes there is little reason to pay for the sequel while Pluto TV is offering it for free.

The film stars [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) in the lead role, with [Harrison Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Ford) returning as [Rick Deckard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Deckard) from the original. BGR describes the "Blade Runner" films as among Ford's best-ranked science fiction work outside the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) universe created by [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas). It runs 163 minutes, so it is a film to settle in for.

## How a fan became the director

Villeneuve has a personal link to the series. BGR reports that he was 13 or 14 when he began dreaming of directing, and that [Ridley Scott's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott) original [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner) was a big part of that. He has called the 1982 film "inextricable from my passion for cinema", and says he and his classmates became fanatics of it.

When a sequel was first floated, he did not jump at it. BGR says he described the idea as "the most fantastic, exciting and at the same time perhaps the worst idea I ever heard." He also held back because he was about to begin shooting "Arrival" and wanted to be sure he could make the world his own. He has joked that he accepted because he "didn't want someone else to ruin it."

The job was not easy. Villeneuve later called "2049" "the most challenging artistic journey of my life" and compared making it to going to film school. BGR adds that its handling of technology and its effect on human life made the story feel relevant to him, which is part of why it still appears on lists of the best films about artificial intelligence.

## A box-office miss with an awards haul

The commercial result did not match the cast or the reviews. BGR says "Blade Runner 2049" made about US$259 million worldwide in its original run, while the film was estimated to need around US$400 million to break even. That gap puts it among the science fiction releases BGR considers should have been huge hits but were not.

Critics were kinder. The film holds an 88 per cent score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), where the consensus calls it "visually stunning and narratively satisfying". It won two Oscars at the 2018 Academy Awards, for visual effects and for Roger Deakins' cinematography. It was the first Oscar for Deakins, who had been nominated 14 times since 1995.

Villeneuve is also the director BGR credits with helping bring the "Dune" universe to the big screen, which gives his sequel work added weight with fans heading into the new series.
