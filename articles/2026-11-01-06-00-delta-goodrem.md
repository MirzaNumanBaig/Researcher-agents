**Quick summary**

- Former Strictly professional Vincent Simone says [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) has been "too good too soon" on the show.
- Goodrem and partner Kai Widdrington scored 34 out of 40 for their Rumba last weekend, placing second on the leaderboard.
- The pair returned after a rib injury kept them out of Icons' Week, according to OK! Magazine.
- This week they dance a Viennese Waltz to Unchained Melody for the Halloween special.

Delta Goodrem is among the favourites to lift the Glitterball Trophy on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing), but a former professional on the show thinks her early form could become a problem. [Vincent Simone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Simone) told Paddy Power Games that the Australian singer "has just been too good too soon", as reported by [OK! Magazine](https://www.ok.co.uk/tv/strictly-come-dancings-delta-goodrem-37822245), which cited the Express.

## A strong comeback after injury

Goodrem, 41, returned to the dance floor last weekend after a rib injury sidelined her during Icons' Week. With her partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington), she scored 34 out of 40 for a Rumba, according to OK! Magazine. That put the couple second on the leaderboard.

The publication says the pair have regularly sat in the top three this series, which has made Goodrem one of the frontrunners for the trophy. Our earlier coverage looked at [her Celine Dion Rumba and first perfect score](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrems-celine-dion-rumba-wins-strictlys-first-10/).

## What Simone said

Simone was complimentary about the routine itself. "In the rumba, there was lots of basic," he said, adding that it was technically strong and that "you can tell they worked hard".

His worry is about momentum. "I like to see people grow more into the competition," he said. "Because she's been so good, you just want to see her excel every week." In other words, the concern is not the dancing but the expectation it creates. This is one pundit's opinion, not a judges' verdict or a result.

## The cautionary tale from last year

OK! Magazine pointed to [Lewis Cope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Cope), the Emmerdale actor who danced with [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones) in the 2025 series. He impressed viewers week after week, yet landed in the dance-off before the final against [Amber Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Davies) and [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin).

Simone has said before that talented performers with theatrical backgrounds rarely win. "The public tends to go for underdogs," he said at the time. Jones later told Mail Online she had doubted whether she could face the show again after that result.

## What happens next

For the Halloween special, Goodrem and Widdrington will dance a Viennese Waltz to [Unchained Melody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unchained_Melody), as they try to stay in the top three. The show airs at 6.30pm on BBC One.
