**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) attended the 6th Annual Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on 17 October 2026.
- She wore a two-piece Carolina Herrera look from the Spring 2027 collection with nearly 24 carats of Pandora lab-grown diamonds, according to a Vocal profile.
- She said she now dresses to suit herself, and described the night as a celebration of memories rather than status.
- Separately, the Daily Mail reported that a couple dressed as Anderson and Tommy Lee for Halloween on Saturday.

The Pamela Anderson Academy Museum Gala appearance has become a talking point for what she said as much as what she wore. On 17 October 2026 in Los Angeles, the actor arrived in a crisp white shirt and a full green skirt, wearing a lot of sparkle and talking about contentment, according to a profile on the writing platform Vocal. Two weeks later, her name was in the news again for a very different reason: a Halloween costume.

## Pamela Anderson at the Academy Museum Gala: the look

Vocal says the outfit came from Carolina Herrera's Spring 2027 collection. It paired a white button-down shirt with a voluminous green skirt, a mix the piece describes as polished but playful. Her platinum blonde hair was set in what she calls "Sweet Charity curls", a reference to the musical [Sweet Charity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweet_Charity) and its director [Bob Fosse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Fosse). Her makeup was minimal.

Anderson explained the thinking in simple terms. "There's something so romantic about getting dressed up," she is quoted as saying, adding that she wanted a classic Hollywood feeling with a little playfulness to soften it.

For more of her recent style choices, see our [Pamela Anderson archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/pamela-anderson/) and our post on her [soft French fade nails and bare-faced style](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-andersons-soft-french-fade-nails-and-bare-faced-style/).

## Nearly 24 carats of lab-grown diamonds

The jewellery came from Pandora, where Anderson is a brand ambassador. Vocal lists a custom tennis necklace of 19.75 carats and stud earrings totalling 4 carats, which brings the total to 23.75 carats, or nearly 24.

The detail that stands out is how she treats them. According to the piece, the earrings were the same pair she wore to the premiere of The Naked Gun. She also said the pink diamonds from her 2024 Met Gala remain among her most treasured pieces. "I'm sentimental that way," she said of re-wearing jewellery with history.

Her own definition of value had little to do with carats. Her sons made her a Pandora charm bracelet, and she made one for her mother. Those, she said, are the real treasures. The profile adds that she likes the brand's use of lab-grown diamonds and recycled metals.

## Style without boxes

Anderson said she no longer dresses to please other people. "I don't think we need to put ourselves in boxes," she is quoted as saying, explaining that most days she prefers something very simple and other days she wants a little glamour.

She framed her red carpet history as a record of her life. "I'm happy I've lived a lot of lives," she joked, "and every chapter has been reflected in the way I've dressed." On growing older, she said there is a real freedom in not having to be anything other than yourself, and that she has "no regrets".

One caveat: Vocal is an open publishing platform, and this account comes from a single contributor. The quotes were not found in a second outlet at the time of writing, so treat the exact wording with care.

## The Halloween echo

Anderson's image also turned up at a party. The Daily Mail reported on 2 November that [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears)'s 21-year-old son Sean Preston and his singer girlfriend Rebecca Guigui went as rocker [Tommy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee) and Anderson, both in black leather. The pair were seen leaving Dylan Sprouse and Barbara Palvin's annual Halloween party in North Hollywood on Saturday night, trying to stay out of the rain.

The costume is a nod to Anderson's long-running place in pop culture. She has not commented on it in the sources we read.
