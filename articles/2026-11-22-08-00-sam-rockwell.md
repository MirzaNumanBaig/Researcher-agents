**Quick summary**

- *Wild Horse Nine*, the new film starring [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) and John Malkovich, has been removed from 175 cinemas, according to Collider.
- The film has earned about US$30 million worldwide, roughly US$15 million of it in North America, against a reported US$15 million budget.
- Critics are strongly in its favour: Rotten Tomatoes lists a 93% critics' score and a 92% audience score.
- Collider says the film should keep earning, especially if it collects Oscar nominations.

The latest Sam Rockwell Wild Horse Nine news is a setback: the [spy comedy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) he leads with John Malkovich has been pulled from 175 cinemas, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/wild-horse-nine-john-malkovich-removed-175-theaters/) on 21 November. The cut lands on a film that critics love but audiences have so far turned out for in modest numbers.

## What the numbers say about Wild Horse Nine

Collider puts the film's budget at about US$15 million. So far it has grossed around US$30 million worldwide, with roughly US$15 million of that from domestic cinemas. That is about twice its cost, though ticket revenue is shared with cinemas, so it is not profit.

The site calls it the weakest box-office result of writer-director [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh)'s run with Searchlight Pictures. Their partnership began with [*Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing%2C_Missouri) in 2017, which Collider says took more than US$160 million worldwide on a budget of about US$15 million. *The Banshees of Inisherin* followed in 2022 with more than US$50 million against a reported US$20 million.

Collider adds that the film is far from finished. It expects further earnings in the coming weeks, particularly if awards nominations arrive.

## A film critics rate highly

The reviews are the strongest part of the story. According to Collider, *Wild Horse Nine* holds a "Certified Fresh" 93% critics' score and a "Verified Hot" 92% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The site's consensus line singles out Malkovich for a funny, cantankerous performance and praises the sharp dialogue and political bite of McDonagh's script.

The story follows two CIA officers in the 1970s who travel to Easter Island, where one of them is supposed to kill the other. [The Hype Magazine](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2026/11/21/wild-horse-nine-john-malkovich-sam-rockwell-removed-theaters/) describes them as disillusioned veterans named Chris and Lee, sent from Santiago to Rapa Nui just before the September 1973 Chilean coup. Their bureau chief is played by [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), and the plot turns when they meet rebellious local students played by Mariana di Girolamo and Ailín Salas.

The supporting cast also includes [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits), according to The Hype Magazine. It reports that Searchlight Pictures released the film after festival screenings at Venice, Telluride and Toronto.

## Why cinemas drop screens

Losing 175 screens is a sharp correction for an awards hopeful. The Hype Magazine frames it as a familiar squeeze on mid-budget, dialogue-driven films, which compete for screens with franchise releases and family titles. When attendance falls below what cinema owners expect, it says, screen counts are trimmed and attention shifts to later windows such as premium video on demand and streaming.

Neither outlet says the film is leaving cinemas altogether. The Hype Magazine says it still holds select screens and remains in the awards conversation.

## An Oscar link with McDonagh

Rockwell has history with this director. Collider notes that *Three Billboards* earned seven Oscar nominations and two wins, one for Rockwell and one for [Frances McDormand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_McDormand). *The Banshees of Inisherin* collected nine nominations, and both films were nominated for Best Picture. McDonagh also earned a Best Original Screenplay nomination for his debut feature, [*In Bruges*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Bruges). Collider says *Wild Horse Nine* shares that film's basic premise and is McDonagh's third collaboration with Rockwell.

Collider points to Oscar nominations as the likeliest way to bring audiences back.

## What happens next

The film's run now depends on how it fares with awards voters and with word of mouth. Neither source gives a date for a streaming or home release, so any timing there is unconfirmed.
