**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s 2016 mystery comedy The Nice Guys is No. 5 on Apple TV's US streaming chart, according to ScreenRant, which cites FlixPatrol.
- The climb follows the film's 10th anniversary and its return to Apple TV in the United States.
- The film took just under $63 million at the box office against a $50 million budget, which is the main obstacle to a sequel.
- Separately, Comic Book Resources has revisited a line from Crowe's Gladiator, released in 2000.

[Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s buddy-cop mystery The Nice Guys has climbed to No. 5 on Apple TV's streaming chart in the United States, a decade after it failed to find a big cinema audience. [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/russell-crowe-the-nice-guys-sequel-wait-apple-tv-streaming-success-november-2026/) reported the ranking on 2 November 2026, citing the tracker FlixPatrol.

## Where The Nice Guys sits on the chart

According to ScreenRant, the film moved into fifth place and pushed Brad Pitt's F1 out of that position. It also displaced the documentary The Last First: Winter K2. Elsewhere on the same chart, [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and Jake Gyllenhaal's Prisoners debuted at No. 9, while [A Charlie Brown Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Charlie_Brown_Christmas) slipped to No. 10.

The timing lines up with two things. The film has just passed its 10th anniversary, and it has only recently returned to streaming in the US, where Apple TV is the one place to watch it. It has been on the service before. In 2024 it reached No. 2, so there is a clear mark for it to beat this time.

## What the film is

[Shane Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Black) co-wrote and directed [The Nice Guys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice_Guys), set in Los Angeles in the late 1970s. Crowe plays Jackson Healy, an independent enforcer. His work for a young woman drags him into the orbit of Holland March, a struggling private detective played by [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling). The two reluctantly team up to find a missing client and end up deep in the city's seedy underside.

The cast also includes [Angourie Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angourie_Rice), [Matt Bomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bomer), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley) and [Keith David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_David). ScreenRant says critics praised Black's script and direction and the chemistry between Crowe and Gosling.

## Why there is still no sequel

The box office told a different story. ScreenRant notes the R-rated film opened against Neighbors 2 and The Angry Birds Movie and finished with just under $63 million worldwide, on a production budget of $50 million. Black and Gosling have both said that result left them wanting more, per the same report.

That gap has made a follow-up hard to justify to producers. A female-led TV remake was developed at Fox, written by Michael Diliberti, but it never got off the ground. Producer Ken Kao told ScreenRant earlier that producer Joel Silver had recently raised the idea of a sequel again, especially after Gosling's The Fall Guy renewed interest. Kao said the answer is to make it on a smaller budget, adding that streamers "aren't going to just cut blank checks" any more.

Gosling's schedule won't make it cheaper. ScreenRant points out he is now joining the Marvel Cinematic Universe in a Ghost Rider reboot, so reuniting the cast would still carry a price.

## A Gladiator line gets another look

Crowe's older work is also getting attention. Comic Book Resources published a piece on Maximus's pre-battle speech in the 2000 film Gladiator, in which he tells his soldiers, "Brothers, what we do in life... echoes in eternity." The outlet argues the line sets up Maximus's values early and that he holds to them after he loses his family, his rank and his freedom. It is an opinion piece rather than news, but it shows how much of Crowe's catalogue is still being discussed 26 years on.

## What happens next

No sequel has been announced. The next thing to watch is the chart itself: ScreenRant says the film has to pass its 2024 peak of No. 2 to beat its previous run. Any movement on The Nice Guys 2 is likely to depend on whether a lower-budget plan can be agreed, as Kao described.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I stream The Nice Guys?

In the United States it is currently on Apple TV, and according to ScreenRant it is not available on any other service there.

### Is there a Nice Guys 2?

Not yet. Talks have come up over the years, but the first film's box office has made the cost hard to justify. Producer Ken Kao said any sequel would need a smaller budget.

### How did The Nice Guys do at the box office?

It made just under $63 million against a $50 million production budget, according to ScreenRant.
