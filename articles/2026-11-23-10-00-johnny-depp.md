**Quick summary**

- Asked by Collider which day of filming he would relive, [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) chose a day on the set of Blow, directed by his late friend Ted Demme.
- Demme died at 38 after a heart attack on 13 January 2002, less than a year after Blow reached cinemas, according to 3DVF.
- Depp was promoting Ebenezer: A Christmas Carol, which took US$19 million to top the North American box office on its opening weekend, per Box Office Mojo figures cited by Lianhe Zaobao.
- Platoon, the 1986 war film with an early Depp role, is due on Tubi on 1 December, according to MovieWeb.

Johnny Depp had an easy answer available when a journalist asked which day of his career he would live again. He could have named Tim Burton or Pirates of the Caribbean. He named Ted Demme instead, the director of the 2001 crime drama Blow. "I worshiped and I adored him," Depp said, as reported by [3DVF](https://3dvf.com/en/asked-which-film-day-he-d-relive-depp-skipped-burton-and-pirates-for-a-director-who-died-at-38/).

## Why Depp chose a day with Ted Demme

The question came from Collider's Steve Weintraub during an interview that also included director Ti West. The pair were promoting Ebenezer: A Christmas Carol, in which Depp plays Scrooge. It opened in US cinemas on 13 November 2026.

Depp has made eight films with Burton, including Edward Scissorhands and Sleepy Hollow, so a day with him was a likely pick. Depp skipped all of it. He described Demme as "a great pal", and 3DVF reads the answer as friendship coming before his best-known work.

Demme was the nephew of Oscar-winning director Jonathan Demme. He died on 13 January 2002, at 38. That was less than a year after Blow came out, so the film was one of his last.

## What Blow was, and how it was received

Blow was released on 6 April 2001 and follows the life of cocaine trafficker George Jung, played by Depp. Penélope Cruz, Ray Liotta and Franka Potente are in the cast. The soundtrack uses songs from earlier decades, including Bob Dylan and the Rolling Stones, to mark the passing of time in Jung's story.

It arrived two years before Depp first played Jack Sparrow in 2003. Critics were split: 3DVF notes a 56 per cent score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). US viewers can stream it free with ads on Tubi, where it returned on 1 June 2026, the outlet reports.

## The friendship that carried on through their sons

Depp told the interviewer that Demme's son Dexter and his own son Jack met at school around age eight. They became friends "completely by accident", he said, and are still best friends. Jack was born in 2002, the year Demme died, so the two boys never knew each other's fathers together.

## Ebenezer opens at number one in North America

The film Depp was there to promote started well in the US. Lianhe Zaobao, citing Box Office Mojo, reports that [Ebenezer](https://www.zaobao.com.sg/en/entertainment/last-weeks-box-office-top-two-swap-places-johnny-depp-misses-top-three) took US$19 million over 13 to 15 November, ahead of The Cat in the Hat on US$11.3 million in its second week. Godzilla Minus Zero made US$8.5 million and Ramayana: Rise Of A Legend US$5.8 million.

Singapore was a different story. The same report says Ebenezer missed the top three there, where Ramayana took first place. The reviewer praised Depp's performance as a Christmas-hating miser and called the adaptation of Charles Dickens's 1843 novel safe and somewhat bland. For comparison, the piece recalls Michael Caine as Scrooge in The Muppet Christmas Carol in 1992 and Jim Carrey in the 2009 version.

## An early Depp role heads to free streaming

Separately, [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/platoon-johnny-depp-stream-free-tubi-december-2026/) reports that Platoon, the 1986 Vietnam War film directed by [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone), arrives on Tubi on 1 December. Depp plays Lerner alongside [Charlie Sheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Sheen), [Tom Berenger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Berenger) and [Willem Dafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willem_Dafoe).

The film grossed nearly US$139 million on a US$6 million budget and won Best Picture at the Oscars. [Roger Ebert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert) was among those who named it the best film of its year. It holds an 89 per cent critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Depp had only appeared in A Nightmare on Elm Street and Private Resort before it. Much of his footage was cut, and some of it later appeared on a DVD edition. Stone reportedly said he wished he had kept those scenes. MovieWeb does not say whether the Tubi listing covers Australia, so check the app before planning a viewing.
