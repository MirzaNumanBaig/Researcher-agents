**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) told the Los Angeles Times she is about to start shooting *Scapegoat*, the new film from director [Ari Aster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Aster).
- She says she does not yet know exactly what the film is, only that the script is unlike anything she has read.
- Her crime thriller *Paper Tiger*, directed by James Gray, opens in limited release in the US on Friday.
- She is also reported to be in *The Exorcist: Martyrs* and a still-secret role in *The Batman: Part II*.

Scarlett Johansson has agreed to star in one of the most closely guarded films in Hollywood without being able to say what it is about. In an interview published on 11 November, the actor told the Los Angeles Times she is about to begin filming *Scapegoat* with Ari Aster, just as her new thriller *Paper Tiger* reaches cinemas.

## What Johansson says about Scapegoat

Aster's plot is a secret. [The A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/scarlett-johannson-ari-aster-scapegoat-mysterious) notes that the rumours circulating online, including a surgeon and a social media influencer, or a science fiction romance, are unsubstantiated. Asked about it, Johansson said she does not know what it is either, and promised more information "after a few weeks".

She did say she has read the script. "It has this immediacy to it that is really different," she told the paper, adding that she has never read anything like it. According to the A.V. Club's report, her husband, [Colin Jost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Jost), kept asking what she was reading because she kept exclaiming as she went.

The A.V. Club lists a cast that also includes [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law), [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and [Ella Purnell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Purnell). It describes the film as being in pre-production, with Aster writing and directing. Aster's earlier work includes *Hereditary*, *Midsommar* and [*Beau Is Afraid*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beau_Is_Afraid).

## A very full calendar

Johansson told the Times she has worked almost without a break since February. Her projects include playing a New York homicide detective in Mike Flanagan's upcoming *The Exorcist: Martyrs*, and a role she was not able to describe opposite [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) in [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves)'s *The Batman: Part II*. We covered her earlier comments in [Scarlett Johansson on The Batman Part II](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-batman-part-ii/).

At home, she and Jost, who is busy with another season of *[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live)*, coordinate schedules around their children, Rose, 12, and Cosmo, 5. She would like to return to Broadway, where she won a Tony in 2010 for *A View From the Bridge*, but said theatre is hard to square with raising young children. "I consider myself the CEO of our household," she said.

## Paper Tiger: a mother under pressure

*Paper Tiger* is set in 1986. Johansson plays Hester, a mother in Queens whose husband Irwin ([Miles Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Teller)) and his former-cop brother Gary ([Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver)) get tied up in a dubious consulting job linked to the Russian mob and Brooklyn's polluted Gowanus Canal. When the deal turns violent, Hester and her two sons are in danger. She is also hiding a frightening medical diagnosis from her family, according to the Times.

Director [James Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gray_(director)) drew on his own family history. His mother was diagnosed with glioblastoma when he was 18, and he believes her effort to shield the family from the extent of her illness damaged their relationships. He first imagined the story with [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway), who played a character based on his mother in [*Armageddon Time*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_Time) in 2022, but she was committed to Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey*. Gray reworked the story and turned to Johansson.

The Times describes her performance as unglamorous and tightly wound, with a tight perm, oversized glasses and a thick outer-borough accent. Gray praised her face on screen: "I would just film one shot of her listening and it would tell something of the story." Watching her recreate parts of his experience was sometimes almost unbearable, he said.

Johansson sees Hester as a woman who set aside her own ambitions for her family, "as I think many women do". That, she said, is "the American dream, right?"

## What happens next

*Paper Tiger* is released by Neon in limited release on Friday. *Scapegoat* is about to start shooting, and Johansson has said she will know more in a few weeks.
