**Quick summary**

- Director [Jithu Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jithu_Madhavan) says Romancham 2 will not simply continue the first film's story.
- He says the sequel will not centre on Anamika, the ghost from the original.
- On Aavesham 2, he says talks are under way but nothing is confirmed, and the project is on hold.
- The comments came in an interview with Cue Studio, reported by Cinema Express on 12 November 2026.

Jithu Madhavan has addressed the sequel questions that have followed his Malayalam hits, and the answer for fans of Romancham is that Romancham 2 will not be the film they are picturing. The director shared the update in an interview with Cue Studio, according to [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/malayalam/news/2026/Nov/12/jithu-madhavan-opens-up-on-sequels-to-romancham-aavesham).

## What Romancham 2 will and will not be

The first Romancham, starring [Soubin Shahir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soubin_Shahir) and [Arjun Ashokan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arjun_Ashokan), arrived in 2023 and became a major theatrical hit, Cinema Express reports. A follow-up was an obvious expectation.

Madhavan says the sequel will neither carry on the story of the first film nor focus on Anamika, the ghost from that instalment. Instead, the same characters will face a different problem in a fresh storyline.

He also told the interviewer that when he first began writing, the idea was a direct continuation that still did not revolve around Anamika. He added that he needs more time before returning to the script, so no timeline has been given.

## Aavesham 2 is waiting on other films

Aavesham followed in 2024, headlined by [Fahadh Faasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahadh_Faasil) and also featuring [Sajin Gopu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sajin_Gopu). Cinema Express quotes the director as saying discussions about a sequel are happening, but nothing has been locked in.

The reason for the pause, according to that report, is uncertainty around a couple of other upcoming films that reportedly explore a similar subject. Once those are released, the team intends to move ahead with Aavesham 2.

Two other outlets ran headlines on the same interview. NDTV described the director as saying there are no plans for Aavesham 2 or a sequel to Scene, calling the buzz "assumptions", while The Indian Express framed it as him shutting down sequel talk. We could not open either full article, so those wordings come from their headlines only. The Cinema Express account, which we did read, describes a project on hold rather than a flat denial.

## Why the questions keep coming

The timing explains the interest. Madhavan's Tamil debut Scene, starring Suriya and Nasriya Nasim, is described by Cinema Express as a leading grosser in theatres right now, and the outlet calls it his hat-trick hit after Romancham and Aavesham.

The same report notes that the director had already denied rumours of a collaboration with [Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan) and Mamita Baiju. With a streak of three hits, audiences have been asking which film comes next. ## What happens next

No release dates or casting have been announced for either sequel. Romancham 2 depends on Madhavan finding the time to return to the script, and Aavesham 2 depends on the release of the similar films he mentioned. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Romancham 2 happening?

Madhavan says a sequel is being developed, but it will tell a different story with the same characters and will not centre on Anamika. He has said he needs more time before returning to it, and no date has been given.

### Is Aavesham 2 confirmed?

No. According to Cinema Express, discussions are under way but nothing is confirmed, and the project is on hold until other films with a similar subject are released.

### Is there a sequel to Scene?

Headlines from NDTV and The Indian Express say the director ruled out plans for a Scene sequel. We could not read those articles in full, so check them for his exact words.
