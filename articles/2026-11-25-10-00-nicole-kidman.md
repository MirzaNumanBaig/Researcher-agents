**Quick summary**

- The Off-Broadway comedy *A Kidman Carol* has released official merchandise, including a blonde wig inspired by [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), according to BroadwayWorld on 24 November 2026.
- The range also covers an acrylic ornament, crewneck sweatshirts, a canvas tote, a mug, a set of four pins and a magnet.
- The show is a Christmas parody in which [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) is visited by three spirits of Kidman on Oscar's Eve.
- Cast member [Ashby Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashby_Florence) told The Knockturnal how she landed her part.

You can now dress as Nicole Kidman for the price of a wig. *A Kidman Carol*, the Off-Broadway holiday comedy built around the Australian star, has put its merchandise on sale, and [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/Shop-A-KIDMAN-CAROL-Merch-in-the-BroadwayWorld-Store-20261124) reports that the blonde wig is a centrepiece of the range.

## What is in the A Kidman Carol merch range

BroadwayWorld lists a mix of holiday items and show in-jokes. There is an acrylic ornament carrying the show's logo, a mug with the same logo and a magnet "to commemorate your evening at the local movie theater", in the outlet's words. A set of four pins is inspired by the production's designs.

The apparel leans on the show's humour. One crewneck sweatshirt carries the line "Somehow a mental breakdown feels good in a place like this", and a canvas tote shows the three ghosts of awards season. A further hat and pullover use other quotes from the script. The wig, which BroadwayWorld says is made from heat-resistant fibre so it can be styled, is pitched at audiences who want to dress up for the night.

Prices were not given in the source text, so check the BroadwayWorld store before you plan a gift.

## The premise behind the show

According to BroadwayWorld, the play is set on Oscar's Eve. Academy Award winner Blanchett, cast as a Hollywood Scrooge, is visited by three spirits of Kidman to learn "the true meaning of Awards Season". The journey runs from Blanchett's own past in the Australian outback, through a present-day Tinsel Town on the brink of disaster, to a frightening future.

[Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp) plays Blanchett, and [Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle), whom the outlet credits with *Titanique*, plays Kidman. The Knockturnal describes Sharp as a Drama Desk Award nominee and Mindelle as a Tony Award nominee. The production plays at New World Stages in Midtown, according to that review.

## How a cast member won her place

The Knockturnal's Justin Rodriguez spoke with Florence, who plays a range of roles, among them a young Blanchett. She said she auditioned while in New York for an Apple TV film press junket, in a room of about eight people, and walked out sure she had not got the part. She even took a tearful selfie outside Ripley Grier Studios, she said.

She said she spent that night on a red carpet thinking about what the role would mean. It did come through, and the job meant staying in New York for the rest of the year. Florence credited Mindelle and Sharp with teaching her stage-door etiquette and comic timing. Her favourite scenes are the dramatic ones as young Blanchett, because they let her "suck the air out of a room for a minute".

The review also names Richard Perez and Phillip Taratula, who play several parts between them, including a Blanchett superfan and [Katharine Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharine_Hepburn).
