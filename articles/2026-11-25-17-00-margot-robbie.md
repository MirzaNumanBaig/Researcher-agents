**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) stars as Cathy in a new adaptation of *Wuthering Heights*, directed by Emerald Fennell.
- The 136-minute film is due to start streaming on OSN+ in the United Arab Emirates on 28 November 2026, according to the platform's listing.
- Jacob Elordi plays Heathcliff, with Hong Chau, Shazad Latif, Alison Oliver and Martin Clunes in support.
- The announcement has prompted fresh looks at Robbie's 2013 roles in *About Time* and *The Wolf of Wall Street*.

[Margot Robbie](https://moduscarry.com/tag/margot-robbie/) is back on screens in a new version of Emily Brontë's [*Wuthering Heights*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights), and viewers in the UAE can stream it from 28 November 2026. According to [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/margot-robbie-wuthering-heights-streaming), which cites OSN+'s title listing, the film is a standalone feature running 136 minutes, offered in HD with 5.1 audio.

## Who is in the Wuthering Heights cast?

The adaptation is directed by [Emerald Fennell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_Fennell), the filmmaker behind [*Promising Young Woman*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promising_Young_Woman), a film Robbie's company LuckyChap Productions backed. Robbie plays Cathy opposite Australian actor [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) as Heathcliff.

The supporting cast, as listed by the same report, includes [Hong Chau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Chau) as Nelly, [Shazad Latif](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shazad_Latif) as Edgar, [Alison Oliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Oliver) as Isabella and [Martin Clunes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Clunes) as Mr Earnshaw.

The story follows a destructive relationship shaped by class divisions in 18th-century England. Going by the synopsis published by TMDB, Heathcliff's feelings for Catherine Earnshaw are complicated by the gap between his circumstances and those of her wealthy family.

## What will it cost to watch in the UAE?

The report cites subscription details published by Etisalat. The Standard package is listed at AED 48.99 a month and Premium at AED 64.99, both including VAT. A promotional offer on the partner page cuts the first Standard month to AED 24.49, after which the regular charge applies. Prices and offers can change, so check the provider before signing up.

## Why Robbie's 2013 roles are back in the conversation

The streaming news arrived alongside a wave of career retrospectives. [SSBCrack](https://news.ssbcrack.com/margot-robbie-starred-in-about-time-alongside-the-wolf-of-wall-street-in-2013/) noted that in 2013 Robbie appeared in two very different films.

In Richard Curtis's romantic comedy *About Time*, she plays Charlotte, a friend of Tim Lake's sister Katherine who stays at the family home in Cornwall. Tim, played by [Domhnall Gleeson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domhnall_Gleeson), uses his family's ability to travel back through their own lives to try to win her over. [Bill Nighy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Nighy) plays his father. The plan fails, and when Tim meets Charlotte again in London he is with Mary, played by [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams). He goes home to Mary and proposes. SSBCrack describes Robbie's part as brief, but central to the film's idea that changing events does not guarantee the outcome you want. The film is available on Netflix, per the same outlet.

That year she also played Naomi Lapaglia, the wife of trader Jordan Belfort (Leonardo DiCaprio), in Martin Scorsese's *The Wolf of Wall Street*. Both outlets note she had to hold a Brooklyn accent as an Australian, and that the role helped establish her in Hollywood. She was not nominated for an Oscar for it.

## How her career has grown since

Both reports trace the same path. Robbie appeared opposite [Will Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Smith) in *Focus* in 2015 and first played Harley Quinn in *Suicide Squad* in 2016, returning to the role in *Birds of Prey* and *The Suicide Squad*.

Her first Academy Award nomination came for playing figure skater [Tonya Harding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonya_Harding) in *I, Tonya*, and a second followed for *Bombshell*. She has also played [Sharon Tate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Tate) in *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood* and Elizabeth I in *Mary Queen of Scots*.

In 2023 she led *Barbie*, which grossed more than US$1 billion and earned a Best Picture nomination, with Robbie nominated as a producer. Through LuckyChap she has also backed the television production *Sterling Point*.
