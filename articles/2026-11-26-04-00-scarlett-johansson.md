**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) plays Hester Pearl in James Gray's crime drama *Paper Tiger*, with [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) and Miles Teller.
- The film premiered in competition at Cannes in May 2026. Neon opened it in select US theatres on 13 November, according to The Playlist and TheWrap.
- A wider US release followed on 20 November, those outlets report. No Australian date appears in the sources.
- Critics have singled out Driver. They are less united on Johansson's part, with Time Out calling her strong and Sight and Sound calling the role underwritten.

A new round of trailers and reviews has put the spotlight back on Scarlett Johansson's role in *Paper Tiger*, James Gray's late-1980s mob story set in Queens, New York. The film is now in US cinemas, and the coverage this week centres on its final trailer and a fresh wave of reviews.

## What is Paper Tiger about?

According to Time Out's review, the story follows Irwin Pearl (Miles Teller), an engineer from a middle-class Jewish family. He is anxious about college fees, while his wife wants a bigger home on Long Island. His older brother Gary, an ex-cop played by Driver, offers him a business opportunity: clearing an oil-polluted waterway.

The job turns out to be a cover for Russian gangsters. Irwin's misstep leaves the family owing money to a crime boss. Sight and Sound adds that the gangsters assault Irwin and threaten his sons when he confronts them.

Gray wrote and directed the film. Time Out notes it returns to Queens, where he grew up and where his earlier films *Little Odessa*, *We Own the Night* and *The Yards* are also set.

## Scarlett Johansson's role as Hester

Johansson plays Hester Pearl, Irwin's wife. Time Out describes her as a warm, fiercely protective matriarch and calls her performance strong. The same review says Hester is stuck in a medical subplot that never pays off.

Sight and Sound agrees the thread is there, describing Hester as dealing with a private medical problem. It goes further, calling hers the least developed role in the film. The two reviews differ on how well the part serves her, though both are positive about Gray's direction.

The supporting cast, per Time Out, includes Gavin Goudey and Roman Engel as the Pearl sons and Victor Ptak as the crime boss.

## How critics have responded

Reviews from the Cannes premiere were broadly favourable. Time Out gave the film three stars out of five, praising a tense home-invasion scene and Driver's performance, which it calls the standout. It finds the storytelling less accomplished than the craft around it.

Sight and Sound calls the film handsome and serious, with a grounded crime story and real empathy for its characters. It does not rank it above Gray's best work, which it places in *We Own the Night*.

I could not open France 24's review of the film, so this article does not rely on it.

## Release details

The Playlist and TheWrap both report that Neon set a limited release for 13 November, followed by a nationwide expansion one week later on 20 November. The Playlist lists a running time of 114 minutes. Neon released a first teaser in August, and the final trailer is the latest promotion before and during the run.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Scarlett Johansson play in Paper Tiger?

She plays Hester Pearl, the wife of Irwin (Miles Teller). Time Out describes her as a protective matriarch.

### Who directed Paper Tiger?

James Gray wrote and directed it. It premiered in competition at the Cannes Film Festival in May 2026.

### When did Paper Tiger open?
