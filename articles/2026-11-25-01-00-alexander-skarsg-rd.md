**Quick summary**

- Wicker, the romantic fantasy comedy-drama starring [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) and [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman), reached digital and VOD on 24 November 2026.
- It earned US$40.3 million worldwide against a US$10 million budget, according to ComingSoon.net.
- Critics were warm: 94% positive on Rotten Tomatoes and 70 out of 100 on Metacritic.
- Australian cinemas got the film on 5 November through Roadshow Films.

Wicker has left cinemas and moved into living rooms. The film, in which [Alexander Skarsgård](https://moduscarry.com/tag/alexander-skarsgard/) plays a husband woven from wicker, became available on digital platforms and VOD on 24 November 2026, [ComingSoon.net reports](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2229180-wicker-alexander-skarsgard-digital-release-date-today). It follows a theatrical run that returned well over its modest budget.

## What Wicker is about

Directed by Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson, Wicker adapts Ursula Wills-Jones's short story "The Wicker Husband". According to ComingSoon.net, the story centres on an ostracised fisherwoman who commissions a husband made of wicker from the local basket weaver. Her unusual romance with her creation stirs up jealousy and chaos in her conservative seaside village.

Colman stars opposite Skarsgård. The supporting cast includes [Peter Dinklage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Dinklage), [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki), [Nabhaan Rizwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabhaan_Rizwan), [Marli Siu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marli_Siu) and [Richard E. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Grant). [Anna Meredith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Meredith) composed the score, which earned a Hollywood Music in Media Awards nomination for Best Original Score, per the same report.

## From Sundance to streaming

The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival on 24 January 2026. It opened in United States cinemas on 23 October and in the United Kingdom a week later, on 30 October, with Black Bear Pictures handling both territories. [Roadshow Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roadshow_Entertainment) released it in Australia on 5 November.

Principal photography wrapped in Budapest by September 2025, according to ComingSoon.net. Wētā Workshop in New Zealand built the prosthetics that turned Skarsgård into the Wicker Husband.

## How it performed

The numbers are solid for a small production. Wicker took US$24.4 million at the domestic box office and US$15.9 million internationally, for a worldwide total of US$40.3 million. That is roughly four times its reported US$10 million budget. The opening weekend was more modest, at US$5.7 million from 1,471 theatres, so the result came from steady word of mouth rather than a big first weekend.

## What critics and audiences said

Reviews were strong. On Rotten Tomatoes, 94% of 196 critics gave the film a positive review, with an average rating of 7.6 out of 10. The site's consensus describes it as a charming fable that balances the naughty and the nice. Metacritic scored it 70 out of 100 from 39 critics, and audiences polled by PostTrak gave it a 92% positive score.
