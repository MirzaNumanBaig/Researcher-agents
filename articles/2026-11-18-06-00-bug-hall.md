**Quick summary**

- [Bug Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_Hall) started a GoFundMe on 5 November with a $2,000 goal. It has raised about $29,300, according to Tyla.
- The 41-year-old told PEOPLE he had $10 in the bank after a rushed move to rural Arkansas.
- The fundraiser drew online criticism, partly because of his earlier "vow of poverty" and a sixth child on the way.
- Hall says the money has let him start building the family home, and he has a sawmill on site.

The former child actor from [Little Rascals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Gang) has explained why he asked strangers for help. The Bug Hall GoFundMe was meant to raise $2,000 and instead passed $29,000 within about two weeks, after a rushed move left his family short of cash.

## Why the Bug Hall GoFundMe started

Hall, who played [Alfalfa Switzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_%22Alfalfa%22_Switzer) in The Little Rascals, set up the page, titled "Helping the Hall-Barnetts Finish Before Baby Six", on 5 November, according to [Tyla](https://www.tyla.com/entertainment/celebrity/bug-hall-gofundme-sixth-child-backlash-response-376419-20261117). The appeal said he had avoided fundraisers for six years and had always called his lifestyle his own choice.

In the post he said he was a few materials short of having things working and had fallen behind on a handful of bills: the phone, propane for the camper and an unexpected electric hookup fee. With the baby due in under a month, he wrote that he needed help finishing the last jobs first.

He told PEOPLE the family was "in a bad spot financially because we had kind of a rushed move" to a new property in rural Arkansas. "By the time I was done, we had $10 in the bank," he said. Yahoo News UK, which also covered the interview, reported the family had only four days of propane left to heat their camper as temperatures dropped.

## From $2,000 to $29,000

Hall told PEOPLE he thought he was making a quiet appeal to people he knew. He planned to close the page once it reached its goal, but donations kept arriving well past it. Tyla put the total at $29,309 at the time of writing.

"I was overwhelmed. It was a really emotional few days for me," he said. He added that people kept giving "way over and above the stated amount", even though he had not raised the target.

Yahoo News UK reported that old friends from his Hollywood years also got in touch after not speaking for years. Hall recalled some of them telling him to keep his chin up.

There was a hitch with the page itself. Mass reports stopped him from closing it, Yahoo News UK said. He deleted it once the money was ready to transfer, and Tyla reports that GoFundMe later offered to restore it so he could update donors and clear up confusion. Tyla has asked GoFundMe for comment.

## The backlash and the vow of poverty

Plenty of people online were unimpressed. According to Tyla, critics pointed to Hall's earlier vow of poverty and to his decision to keep growing the family without a steady income.

The vow dates to 2021. Tyla reports it came about six months after Hall's June 2020 arrest in Texas over allegedly inhaling air duster cans. The charge was later dismissed. Tyla describes the episode as a relapse that broke a long stretch of sobriety. Hall then stepped back from Hollywood, embraced a Catholic vow of poverty and moved off the grid to build a homestead.

Under that vow, Tyla says, he gave his child-acting savings and earnings to charity and most of his possessions away. He takes cash-only odd jobs when a pressing need comes up.

Hall responded in a clip linked from a GoFundMe update on 11 November. He thanked supporters and said he loved people "whoever they are", whether or not they shared his beliefs, according to Tyla.

## What the money is paying for

Hall originally expected to spend two to three years saving for the equipment to build the house. He told PEOPLE the donations changed that. "We've got the sawmill here, and we're actually building our house," he said, as quoted by Yahoo News UK.

He married Jill DeGroff in 2017, and the couple have five children, four daughters and a son. Tyla reports the sixth is due in under a month. In his appeal he hoped to finish the main work within two weeks, then take paid work until the birth.

## What happens next

The next milestone is the birth of the baby, due within weeks, and the house build that Hall says is now under way. Whether the fundraiser page stays open for further updates has not been stated in the coverage we could read.
