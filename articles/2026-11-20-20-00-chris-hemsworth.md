**Quick summary**

- A new character poster for Avengers: Doomsday shows [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s Thor inside Doctor Doom's castle in Latveria, according to The Direct.
- Marvel Thailand released the poster on X. Thor lifts Stormbreaker, and the lightning is green rather than blue.
- Separately, Nine reports a 16-year-old has been charged over the 11 November break-in at Hemsworth's estate near Byron Bay.
- Hemsworth also posted a training session on Instagram on 19 November, which he linked to Extraction 3.

A fresh Avengers: Doomsday poster has put Chris Hemsworth's Thor in the last place his fans might expect: the throne room of [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom). The image arrived on 20 November, the same day Australian outlets reported a charge in the break-in at the actor's home.

## What the Thor poster shows

Marvel Thailand posted the artwork on X, and [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/chris-hemsworth-thor-doctor-doom-castle-avengers-doomsday-character-poster) was among the first to pick it apart. Thor stands in what appears to be Doom's throne room, holding Stormbreaker overhead while small bolts of electricity run from the weapon and from his chest.

The Direct points out that the stained-glass windows and walls match imagery Marvel has used for Doomstadt Tower, the castle in Latveria, in earlier marketing. In a separate Doomsday poster, a staircase sits in front of a near-identical window behind Doom himself. No stairs are visible behind Thor, though the outlet notes the low camera angle could simply hide them.

## Why the green lightning matters

The most talked-about detail is the colour. Thor's lightning is usually blue, but here it is green, close to the look of Doom's own powers, according to The Direct. The outlet adds that Doomsday marketing has already changed the colour of Thor's lightning once.

The film pairs [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) as Doom with an Avengers line-up that The Direct says will be led by Thor and [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor))'s Steve Rogers. It is the first team-up film since Avengers: Endgame in 2019, and [Earth's Mightiest Heroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers_(comics)) are expected to spend real time in the castle. The Direct notes the throne room has featured in marketing more than once.

All of this is reading between the lines of promotional art. The Direct is clear that the poster may carry no deeper meaning, and Marvel has not explained the green lightning. Thor's grimace suggests a fight, but who he is fighting is speculation.

## Teen charged over the Byron Bay break-in

Away from the screen, a different story has moved forward. According to [Nine](https://www.nine.com.au/australia-news/nsw/teen-arrested-over-break-in-at-hemsworth-estate-near-byron-bay-20261120-p61fyo.html), police were called to the couple's estate at Broken Head in northern NSW at about 7.30am on Wednesday 11 November, after four people allegedly broke in earlier that morning.

Security footage showed four men in balaclavas, two of them armed with knives, scaling the front fence and finding three vehicles in the garage, Nine reports. A BYD, a Ram and a Raptor ute were taken. All three were later recovered in Coraki, Tabulam and Lennox Head and are being forensically examined.

Officers searched a home in Coraki this week and found electronics, clothing and car keys, according to Nine. A 16-year-old boy has been charged with seven offences, including aggravated break and enter and destruction of property. He was refused bail and is due back in court on Friday 27 November. The charges are allegations, and nothing has been proved in court.

Nine says it understands Hemsworth and his wife [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky) were home at the time. Pataky woke to barking dogs around 2am but did not investigate. Our earlier report covers the first details: [Teen Charged Over Chris Hemsworth Byron Bay Break-In](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-byron-bay-break-in-charge/).

An unnamed source told [In Touch Weekly](https://intouchweekly.com/chris-hemsworth-and-elsa-pataky-were-home-during-armed-break-in-sense-of-security-is-shattered/) that the couple's security system failed to alert them and that they feel shaken. That account is not confirmed by the couple or police.
