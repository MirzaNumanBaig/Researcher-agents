**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended Chanel's Cruise show in her hometown of Sydney on Thursday 5 November 2026.
- She was joined by her 15-year-old daughter, and the two wore coordinated black and white outfits.
- The pair also went to Chanel's Spring/Summer 2027 show in Paris in October.
- Kidman has been a Chanel brand ambassador since October 2025, according to HELLO!.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) turned a Sydney fashion week moment into a family outing on Thursday 5 November, arriving at Chanel's Cruise show with her youngest daughter in matching-but-different black and white looks. The 59-year-old Oscar winner was back in her hometown for the label's first Australian presentation, and the Nicole Kidman Chanel Sydney appearance drew attention largely because of how closely the two coordinated.

## What Nicole Kidman wore to the Sydney show

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/933444/nicole-kidman-59-looks-incredible-alongside-lookalike-daughter-faith-15-in-rare-mother-daughter-appearance/), Kidman chose a black tuxedo-style suit with a cropped jacket over a white top, finished with pointed black heels and a small black handbag. Her blonde hair was worn down in a blowout.

[Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/nicole-kidman-her-look-alike-191627608.html), republishing a Parade story that cites People, described the same outfit with a few extra details: coattails at the back of the jacket, straight-leg trousers, layered white tops, sunglasses and black pumps. The outlets differ slightly on small items, which is common when several photo agencies cover one event, but they agree on the black-and-white palette.

## A coordinated look from her youngest daughter

Her 15-year-old daughter wore the reverse: a white Chanel set made up of a cropped, ribbed zip-up jacket and a matching miniskirt. Both outlets say she added cream heels and a brown handbag, and wore her brunette hair down in soft curls.

Harper's Bazaar Australia also placed Kidman at the top of its best-dressed list from the event, alongside fellow Australians [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), though the full page was not available to read. We covered the guest list in more detail in [Margot Robbie Joins Nicole Kidman at Chanel's Sydney Show](https://moduscarry.com/margot-robbie-chanel-sydney/).

## Not their first Chanel outing together

The Sydney show followed another mother-daughter appearance earlier in the autumn. Yahoo reports that the pair attended Chanel's Spring/Summer 2027 show during Paris Fashion Week, where Kidman wore a grey tweed blazer dress and her daughter paired a grey Chanel cardigan with jeans and heels. HELLO! adds that the older of the two daughters, Sunday Rose, was walking in Fashion Week at the time.

Kidman's link to the house is official. HELLO! notes she was named a Chanel brand ambassador in October 2025. At the time she told WWD she appreciated haute couture and looked forward to wearing the house's creations under creative director Matthieu Blazy.

## Why this trip was personal

The Sydney visit also ties into Kidman's film work. HELLO! quotes her telling the Herald Sun that she asked Warner Bros. whether she could take the new Practical Magic film to Sydney, and that the studio agreed. The film is a sequel to the 1998 comedy-drama [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic).

She said her older daughter was working and could not make the trip, but the 15-year-old insisted on flying with her so she would not be doing it alone. Kidman also described her parenting approach in the same interview: she wants to be a guide and a safe haven rather than a best friend.

Yahoo adds that the teenager has appeared with her mother more often lately, including at the 2026 US Open in September.

## What happens next

No further Chanel appearances were announced in the sources we read. Kidman's schedule around the Australian release of the Practical Magic sequel is the likeliest place to see her next. We will update this story if more details are confirmed.
