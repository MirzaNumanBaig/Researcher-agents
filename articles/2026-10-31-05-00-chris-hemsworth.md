**Quick summary**
- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth), 43, posted an Instagram photo carousel from Thailand on 30 October 2026.
- He says he is finishing his time in the country after "a crazy few weeks" filming Extraction 3.
- His children joined him, and the photos show fishing, swimming, sightseeing and a boxing session.
- The report comes from AOL.com, which republished an OK! story.

Chris Hemsworth has marked the end of his Thailand leg on **Extraction 3** with a family photo dump. The Australian actor shared the Instagram carousel on 30 October, according to [AOL.com](https://www.aol.com/articles/shirtless-chris-hemsworth-shows-off-120855000.html), showing how he spent his weekends away from the set.

## What Hemsworth said about the shoot

The caption was short and plain. "Finishing up my time in Thailand. Crazy few weeks of filming Extraction 3 but managed to fill the weekends with some fun non work adventures too!" he wrote, and credited photographer Evan Paterakis.

The post gives no release date, plot detail or word on how much filming remains. What it does confirm, in his own words, is that the Thailand block of the shoot is nearly done. For more on that stage of production, see our earlier report that [Chris Hemsworth wraps Thailand shoot for Extraction 3](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-wraps-thailand-shoot-for-extraction-3/).

## Family time on the coast

AOL describes a mix of outdoor scenes. In one picture Hemsworth stands waist-deep in water with limestone cliffs behind him. In another he dives off a boat into turquoise water. A black-and-white shot shows him on a boat holding a fishing rod while one of his sons watches.

His children feature throughout. One photo has him with two of them at a scenic lookout, and another is a silhouette of him and one child against a golden sunset. According to the report, Hemsworth shares daughter India Rose, 14, and twin sons Sasha and Tristan, 12, with his wife, [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky). The report does not say whether Pataky was in Thailand.

## Sightseeing and a boxing session

The carousel also covers the quieter parts of the trip. There is an ornate gold and white temple, a walk through a restaurant with one of his sons and a hike along a bamboo-lined trail.

Training did not stop either. One black-and-white image shows him throwing punches at pads held by a trainer near the ocean. The last image is a red sun setting behind dark mountains.

Fans responded in the comments. One joked about the serpent Jörmungandr from Norse mythology lurking in the water, a nod to his Thor role. Another praised him for getting involved as a father rather than just turning up.

## What happens next

Hemsworth says he is leaving Thailand, but the post does not say where filming goes next or when the movie will reach audiences.
