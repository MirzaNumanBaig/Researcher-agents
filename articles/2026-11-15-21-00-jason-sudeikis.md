**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) was ESPN's celebrity guest picker for College GameDay in South Bend, Indiana, on Saturday 7 November.
- He told The Pat McAfee Show that Notre Dame fandom runs deep in his family.
- He voices Red, now a father, in The Angry Birds Movie 3, which Paramount Pictures plans to release on 23 December.
- A new trailer for the film circulated in mid-November, according to IMDb's news coverage.

The [Jason Sudeikis Angry Birds Movie 3](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jason-sudeikis/) story is the second chapter of a busy few weeks for the actor. He picked winners on one of college football's biggest television stages, and he is back as the voice of Red in an animated sequel that puts the character through fatherhood.

## Why Sudeikis was on College GameDay

ESPN announced the pick on a Thursday, and KCTV5 reported that some viewers were confused, given that the Overland Park native is best known for loving the Kansas Jayhawks. He explained the choice himself on The Pat McAfee Show, calling the appearance "the pinnacle" of his Notre Dame fandom, according to [KCTV5](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/11/06/jason-sudeikis-make-appearance-college-gameday-guest-picker-heres-why/?outputType=amp).

The show came live from South Bend ahead of No. 1 Miami against No. 11 Notre Dame. For a guest picker, that is a tricky afternoon. KCTV5 listed a slate of ranked matchups that day, including No. 5 Alabama at No. 14 LSU and No. 2 Georgia at No. 10 Ole Miss, and described many of them as toss-ups.

## The family link to Notre Dame

According to KCTV5, Notre Dame was "big" in his family growing up, and his mother's family comes from Chicago's South Side. His maternal grandfather graduated from the university, as did his Uncle Jim, in 1975. His other uncle, actor George Wendt of Cheers, also attended but left after his junior year.

That background is why the choice made sense to ESPN, even if Kansas fans raised an eyebrow. It also sits comfortably alongside his well-known Kansas City roots, which we covered when [he hosted a benefit in Kansas City](https://moduscarry.com/jason-sudeikis-thundergong-2026/).

## Red becomes a dad in The Angry Birds Movie 3

On screen, Sudeikis returns as Red. [Cartoon Brew](https://www.cartoonbrew.com/animators/angry-birds-movie-3-trailer-263911.html) reported when Paramount released the first trailer in late June that the official synopsis casts fatherhood as Red's biggest challenge yet, with the world to save as well.

John Rice directs from a screenplay by Thurop Van Orman. The voice cast includes Josh Gad, Danny McBride, Emma Myers, Rachel Bloom, Tim Robinson, Nikki Glaser, Sam Richardson, Lily James and Keke Palmer, among others. Cartoon Brew noted that the film arrives almost seven years after The Angry Birds Movie 2.

IMDb's news desk headlined its mid-November coverage of a new trailer as Sudeikis "taking on fatherhood" with green pigs. We could not read that page in full, so we have not relied on its details.
