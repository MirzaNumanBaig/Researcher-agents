**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short), 76, was photographed on the set of a Wells Fargo commercial in New York City on 17 November.
- He wore a short brown wig resembling the hair of Saturday Night Live cast member Marcello Hernández, 29, plus a matching outfit.
- The ad is described as upcoming; no release date has been confirmed in the source reporting.
- Last month Short spoke publicly about the death of his daughter Katherine at a mental health charity benefit.

Martin Short has turned himself into a younger comedian for a new bank advertisement. The 76-year-old was photographed on 17 November on the set of a Wells Fargo commercial in New York City, wearing a short brown wig modelled on the hair of Marcello Hernández, according to [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/martin-short-nearly-unrecognizable-debuts-202607491.html).

## What was seen on set

Short and Hernández, 29, wore matching outfits made up of red jackets, khaki pants and hoodies, Yahoo reports. Hernández joined [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) in 2022. Short was a cast member of the show for a single season, from 1984 to 1985, which gives the pairing an easy generational joke across 40 years of the programme.

The report describes the clip as an upcoming advertisement. It does not say what the commercial is about, when it will air or whether Wells Fargo has commented, so those details remain unconfirmed.

## A quieter month away from the camera

The set photos arrive weeks after a far more sombre public appearance. In October, Short presented the first Katherine Short Legacy Award at the fourth annual benefit for the Rare Impact Fund, the mental health charity founded by [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez). The charity says its aim is to raise awareness and funds for youth mental health around the world.

Gomez introduced him by describing Katherine as a social worker who spent her career helping people at some of their hardest moments, as quoted by [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly). She said the award would carry Katherine's name forward by honouring people with the same commitment to their communities.

Katherine Short died on 23 February, according to Yahoo's account. Speaking on stage, Short called it a very difficult year and the heartbreak of his family. He said his family had come to understand over the previous eight months that mental illness and cancer, which took his wife, are both diseases and that sometimes diseases are terminal.
