## Perry and Trudeau cover three countries in a week

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau have spent the past week moving across Europe, with appearances in Barcelona, Milan, Paris and Venice. Perry has also been named as a recent guest at a West Hollywood gala before the pair flew out, according to a [report from cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/perry-trudeau-europe-travel-style) that draws on Elle and Vogue coverage.

That report says the couple were photographed outside the Ritz Paris and leaving the Four Seasons hotel in Milan. Their Venice airport arrival, where they were seen holding hands in separate photo coverage, rounded out the run.

The trip is only a few days old in the public record, and neither of them has confirmed an itinerary. Perry had not posted pictures from this leg on Instagram, the report notes.

## The High Court closes the Katie Perry trade mark case

Away from the travel pictures, a legal analysis published on Legal 500 sets out the end of a dispute that has run for more than a decade. The High Court of Australia ruled 3-2 in favour of Australian designer Katie Taylor, who trades under her birth name Katie Perry, and reinstated her KATIE PERRY trade mark for clothing, which has been registered since September 2008.

The court accepted that Perry, whose legal name is Katheryn Hudson, had built a substantial reputation in Australia before Taylor's priority date of 29 September 2008. But it stressed that this reputation was in music and entertainment, not in clothing and fashion. Celebrities often expand into fashion, the court held, yet that alone did not prove her name was known for clothing.

The result reverses a November 2024 Full Federal Court decision, which had found Taylor's mark likely to confuse the public and ordered it cancelled. It also restores the 2023 Federal Court finding that Perry's side had infringed the mark by selling merchandise clothing in Australia.
