**Quick summary**

- [Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro) is in talks to join the cast of the [Ocean's Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean's_Eleven) prequel, according to a Deadline report relayed by JoBlo.
- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper) star as the parents of George Clooney's Danny Ocean.
- The story is set around the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix, with a release date of June 25, 2027.
- Details of Barbaro's role have not been disclosed, and the deal is not yet confirmed.

[Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro) is reportedly in talks to join [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper) in the prequel to [Ocean's Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean's_Eleven). The report comes from Deadline, as summarised by [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/monica-barbaro-oceans-eleven-prequel/), which says her character is being kept under wraps.

## What is the Ocean's Eleven prequel about?

According to JoBlo, Robbie and Cooper play the parents of Danny Ocean, the character [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney) played in the Ocean's films. The action is set at the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix.

Robbie introduced the project at CinemaCon during the Warner Bros. presentation. "Before Danny Ocean ever stepped foot in Vegas, two masterminds taught him everything he knows: his parents," she said, as quoted by JoBlo. She added that audiences would see them "in all their prime" pulling off a heist at the Monaco race.

JoBlo also reports that [Wagner Moura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Moura) is set to play the main villain, a casting reported several weeks earlier.

## A change behind the camera

The film is made by Warner Bros. and Robbie's company LuckyChap. [Lee Isaac Chung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Isaac_Chung), the director of Twisters, was once slated to direct but left earlier this year. A Warner Bros. spokesperson described it as "an amicable split due to creative differences", according to JoBlo.

Cooper was named as his replacement soon after. He will direct and write the script, while also appearing opposite Robbie.

## Where Barbaro fits

JoBlo describes Barbaro as best known for Top Gun: Maverick and A Complete Unknown. Her casting would add another recognisable name to a project that already has two leads and a villain announced. Nothing about the part has been confirmed, and talks can still fall through.

## Another Ocean's film may be coming

JoBlo notes a separate sequel is also in development, which could bring back Clooney, [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) and other members of the original ensemble. Clooney teased it in 2023, saying there was "a really good script" and that the idea was "kind of Going in Style". He said then that Warner Bros. had approved the budget and that scheduling was the remaining hurdle.

## What happens next

Per JoBlo, production is expected to begin in late July in Paris before moving to the south of France. The prequel is scheduled for release on June 25, 2027. Barbaro's deal still needs to be confirmed.
