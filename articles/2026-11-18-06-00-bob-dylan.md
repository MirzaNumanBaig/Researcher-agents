**Quick summary**

- The [Bob Dylan Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan_Center) will stage "Not Dark Yet", a tribute to Bob Dylan's 1997 album Time Out of Mind, on Friday 29 January 2027.
- The one-night show is at Cain's Ballroom in Tulsa, Oklahoma, with Marc Maron as emcee.
- Confirmed performers include Elvis Costello, Jason Isbell, Steve Earle, Margo Price, Diana Krall and Angélique Kidjo.
- A members-only presale opens on Friday 20 November at 10:00 a.m. local time, with any leftover tickets on general sale on Wednesday 25 November.

The Bob Dylan Center has announced a star-studded concert to mark 30 years since [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan) released Time Out of Mind, the Grammy-winning album that many fans see as the start of his late-career renaissance. According to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Bob-Dylan-Center-to-Present-All-Star-Not-Dark-Yet-Concert-Marking-TIME-OUT-OF-MINDs-30th-Anniversary-20261117), the show takes place on 29 January 2027 at Cain's Ballroom, the 100-year-old Tulsa honky-tonk where Dylan has played himself.

## Who is playing the Bob Dylan Not Dark Yet concert

Comedian [Marc Maron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Maron), the former host of the WTF podcast, will emcee. The confirmed performers, listed alphabetically by the Center, are:

- [Ray Benson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Benson), frontman of Asleep at the Wheel
- [Elvis Costello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elvis_Costello), who curates the Center's Perspective Jukebox Experience
- [Steve Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Earle)
- Joy Harjo, the Center's inaugural artist-in-residence and a former three-term U.S. Poet Laureate
- [Joe Henry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Henry)
- Patterson Hood of Drive-By Truckers
- [Oscar Isaac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Isaac), who played a folk singer in the Coen brothers' 2013 film [Inside Llewyn Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inside_Llewyn_Davis)
- [Jason Isbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Isbell)
- Angélique Kidjo
- Diana Krall
- Margo Price

Tulsa-born drummer Jim Keltner will guest on select songs. Keltner first worked with Dylan in 1971 on the sessions for "Watching the River Flow" and "When I Paint My Masterpiece". He later played on albums including Saved, Shot of Love and Empire Burlesque, and his drumming drives many tracks on Time Out of Mind, per the announcement.

## How the night will run

Doug Keith, co-curator of the Center's earlier tribute shows, returns as music director. His house band includes Chatham County Line, brothers Brad and Phil Cook, and Darren Jessee, the Ben Folds Five drummer. [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2518423/bob-dylan-center-announces-not-dark-yet-a-30th-anniversary-time-out-of-mind-tribute-concert/news) reports the first set will cover the album, including "Love Sick", "Tryin' to Get to Heaven", "Not Dark Yet" and "Make You Feel My Love". The second set will range across the rest of Dylan's catalogue.

"These are imposing songs but they're also wild, bluesy and a blast to play," Keith said in the announcement, adding that there would be "some surprises" in the second half.

The night follows the Center's concert for the 60th anniversary of Blonde on Blonde in February 2026 and its show for the 50th anniversary of Blood on the Tracks in January 2025. Both sold out quickly, according to the Center.

## Why Time Out of Mind still matters

Stereogum notes that Dylan was 56 when the album came out, and that it dwells on ageing and death. Far Out Magazine [describes](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/1997-bob-dylan-made-an-album-he-called-the-blueprint-for-his-career/) it as one of his bleakest records, with songs such as "Tryin' to Get to Heaven" facing mortality head on.

Joe Henry, one of the featured artists, called it a milestone and a bridge between Dylan's deep past and whatever future he was imagining. He credited producer Daniel Lanois and engineer Mark Howard for forward-looking sounds under haunted lyrics.

Dylan has his own view. Far Out quotes him saying he never listens to his records once they are finished, though he has said the album taught him not to be caught short of up-tempo songs on later records.

A real-life footnote adds weight to the title song. [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/opinion/bob-dylan-hooded-vatican-visit-pope-leo-still-knockin-heavens-door) commentator Dr Marc Siegel writes that Dylan recorded "Not Dark Yet" months before a severe infection, pericardial histoplasmosis, nearly killed him in 1997. Dylan then performed for Pope John Paul II in Bologna. Our earlier report covered [Dylan's cut SNL sketch about meeting the pope](https://moduscarry.com/bob-dylan-snl-pope-sketch/).

## What happens next

Bob Dylan Center members in good standing get an exclusive presale from 10:00 a.m. on Friday 20 November. The Center warns that membership does not guarantee a ticket and that sales to non-members are unlikely. Anything left goes on sale at 10:00 a.m. on Wednesday 25 November.

The night before, on Thursday 28 January, Keltner will take part in an onstage conversation and listening session at the Church Studio. That event is invitation-only for performers and Center supporters, Stereogum reports.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Time Out of Mind tribute concert?

It is on Friday 29 January 2027 at Cain's Ballroom in Tulsa, Oklahoma, as a one-night-only event.

### Will Bob Dylan perform at "Not Dark Yet"?

The sources do not say so. The announcement lists other artists as performers and names no appearance by Dylan himself.

### How do I buy tickets?

Bob Dylan Center members get a presale on 20 November at 10:00 a.m. Any remaining tickets go on general sale on 25 November at 10:00 a.m.

### What will be played?

The first set covers every song on Time Out of Mind, and the second set draws from the wider Dylan songbook.
