**Quick summary**

- Inde Navarrette says she plans to play Grand Theft Auto VI with her brother once it is released.
- She told BBC Radio 1 she intends to follow the road rules in the game, including stopping at red lights.
- Before her breakout in the horror film Obsession, she ran a Twitch channel, and she has said it helped cover her rent for more than a year.
- Awards talk is building around her, and she is due to play Rogue in Marvel Studios' X-Men reboot.

[Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) has a plan for Grand Theft Auto VI, and it does not involve chaos. The Obsession star told BBC Radio 1 she will play the game with her brother when it arrives, and that she will be the one obeying traffic laws, according to a report from [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/inde-navarrette-gta-six-awards).

## A law-abiding player in a lawless game

The joke is built on the franchise's reputation. Rockstar Games' open-world series has long been associated with crime and reckless driving, and Navarrette leaned the other way. "You know, stopping at the red lights is really important," she said on the programme.

She kept the bit going, laughing as she described what she would be doing. "Like, traffic control safety in GTA 6, is what I'm gonna be doing," she said. The comments are light, but they show how comfortable she is talking about games, which is not new for her.

## The Twitch years before Hollywood

Navarrette had a Twitch livestreaming channel before Obsession lifted her profile, and she played video games through the COVID-19 lockdown, according to the same report. She has previously said that streaming helped her pay rent for more than a year at an important point in her life.

Her broadcasts have since been deleted. Clips from those sessions have resurfaced online as her visibility has grown, the report says. The report does not say who reposted the clips or where they are being shared.
