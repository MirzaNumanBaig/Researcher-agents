**Quick summary**

- Disney will re-release The Prestige in cinemas on 29 January 2027, according to Consequence, Hypebeast and MEAWW.
- It will be the first time the 2006 thriller is shown on IMAX or 70mm film.
- Hugh Jackman plays Robert Angier opposite Christian Bale's Alfred Borden.
- 35mm and 2D digital screenings are also planned.
- Separately, Jackman performed at Gateway for Cancer Research's Cures Gala on 17 October in Illinois.

[Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) is heading back to the big screen as a rival magician. Disney has set 29 January 2027 for the theatrical return of [The Prestige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prestige), the Christopher Nolan thriller that first opened on 20 October 2006, and this time audiences can see it on IMAX and 70mm for the first time.

The announcement came this week, along with a new teaser and poster, according to [MEAWW](https://meaww.com/when-does-the-prestige-return-to-theaters-christopher-nolan-2027-rerelease-date-imax-70mm-hugh-jackman-christian-bale-michael-caine-scarlett-johansson). The re-release marks the film's 20th anniversary.

## When is The Prestige back in cinemas?

Disney's date is 29 January 2027, a little over 20 years after the original release, [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/10/christopher-nolan-the-prestige-70mm-imax/) reports. The Australian release date has not been confirmed in the coverage we reviewed, so check local cinema listings closer to the time.

Nolan said in a statement published by Variety, and quoted by MEAWW, that he was "very excited to bring 'The Prestige' back to the big screen". He added that he was particularly pleased to offer 70mm film and IMAX for the first time.

Not every screening will be that large. Consequence says 35mm and 2D digital sessions will run too. So what is the film asking viewers to come back for?

## What is The Prestige about?

It follows two Victorian-era stage magicians whose friendship collapses after a trick goes wrong. Jackman's Robert Angier is described in Disney's official synopsis as a charismatic showman, while [Christian Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bale) plays Alfred Borden, a gifted illusionist. Their rivalry turns into an obsession built on deceit and sabotage.

The cast also includes Michael Caine, Scarlett Johansson, Rebecca Hall, Andy Serkis and David Bowie, according to [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2026/10/christopher-nolan-the-prestige-sets-re-release-first-ever-imax-70mm-screenings-christian-bale-hugh-jackman-news). Nolan co-wrote the script with his brother Jonathan, adapting Christopher Priest's novel, and produced with Emma Thomas and Aaron Ryder.

## How did the film perform the first time?

It opened a year after Nolan's Batman Begins and earned roughly $100 million (US) worldwide, per Hypebeast and MEAWW. It picked up two Academy Award nominations.

Hypebeast frames the re-release against Nolan's recent large-format successes, saying it follows record-setting box-office milestones for Oppenheimer and The Odyssey. For a film that began life well before that run, the new release is a first taste of the format for fans.

For viewers who cannot wait until January, Consequence notes the film is available on 4K Ultra HD through Amazon.

## What else is Hugh Jackman doing?

Jackman was part of the entertainment lineup at Gateway for Cancer Research's 35th Annual Cures Gala on Saturday 17 October, held at a private residence in Barrington Hills, Illinois, according to a [press release carried by Fortune](https://fortune.com/press-releases/gateway-for-cancer-research-35-years-2026-cures-gala-2026-10-20/).

The lineup also included [David Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Foster), who was emcee and musical director, as well as [Loren Allred](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loren_Allred), [Lee Greenwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Greenwood) and [Sydnie Christmas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydnie_Christmas). The charity said it set a $3.5 million goal for the anniversary and reached it. It also said it has raised more than $135 million since 1991. These figures come from the organisation's own release.

Jackman also voices a shepherd in the 2026 mystery comedy [The Sheep Detectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sheep_Detectives), which the Arizona Daily Star reviewed as pleasant family viewing earlier this month.
