**Quick summary**

- [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand), 51, was jailed for 10 years and six months on Monday 9 November at Southwark Crown Court.
- He was found guilty on 5 November of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women, between 2004 and 2009.
- [Sharon Osbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Osbourne) said on a podcast released Thursday that Brand must "pay" for his crimes.
- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry), his ex-wife, has not commented publicly. A tabloid report says she is weighing a book and series, which is unconfirmed.

Four days after Russell Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison, the reaction from the entertainment world is still coming in. On Thursday, Sharon Osbourne said the comedian has to "pay" for his crimes, while his former wife Katy Perry has stayed publicly silent.

## What Sharon Osbourne said

Speaking on The Osbournes podcast, released on Thursday, Osbourne, 74, said that no amount of change in a person's life erases the consequences. According to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/sharon-osbourne-condemns-russell-brand-rape-sexual-assault-conviction-20-year-industry-friendship-20261113-p61dwy.html), she acknowledged that Brand had turned his life around, pointing to his move into online wellness, addiction recovery and religion. Her view did not shift: "You play, you pay."

Her son [Jack Osbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Osbourne) spoke about the women at the centre of the case, saying they had lived with what happened to them for decades. The pair said their thoughts were also with Brand's wife, Laura Gallacher, and their three children.

The Osbournes said they have known Brand for more than 20 years through British and Hollywood television circles, and that they were never close friends. Sharon appeared as a guest on The Russell Brand Show in December 2006. The episode was recorded after the verdict but before sentencing.

## What the court decided

Nine reports that Brand was convicted of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others. [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/934755/katy-perry-42-steps-out-hand-in-hand-with-justin-trudeau-following-ex-russell-brands-rape-conviction/) puts the verdict at Southwark Crown Court in London on 5 November, with sentencing on 9 November. [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/katy-perry-reportedly-planning-tell-003019162.html) reports the offences took place between 2004 and 2009.

## Katy Perry's first public outing

Perry, 42, was photographed holding hands with her partner, former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau), 54, outside a Beverly Hills restaurant on Wednesday 11 November. HELLO! calls it her first reported appearance since the sentence. She has not commented on the conviction.

She and Brand married in 2010, and the divorce was finalised in 2012. In a 2013 Vogue interview that has resurfaced, Perry described his behaviour during the marriage as "very controlling" and said he told her by text message on New Year's Eve 2011 that he wanted a divorce.

## A reported book and docuseries

The Mail on Sunday, as relayed by Yahoo, quotes an unnamed insider saying Perry is considering an autobiography and a streaming-style series about the marriage, after waiting for the trial to end. The source said "active discussions" had taken place. No publisher or platform has been confirmed, and neither Perry nor her representatives have been quoted confirming any project. Treat it as a report, not a plan.

## Old comments return to the spotlight

The Nightly reports that earlier remarks from other public figures have been recirculated. Dannii Minogue told the Mirror in 2006 that Brand was "a bit of a vile predator" after an interview for his MTV show. Lily Allen has said that rape jokes made about her on a 2007 quiz show were "horrendous" in hindsight. Lorraine Kelly has said an exchange with Brand on a 2007 chat show is "uncomfortable to watch" now. These are past statements, and they are not part of the court's findings.

## What happens next

No appeal has been reported in the sources reviewed. The coverage so far points to continuing reaction rather than new legal steps.
