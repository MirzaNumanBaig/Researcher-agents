- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and her husband, guitarist Matthew Copley, have bought a Rose Bay mansion in Sydney for $10.9 million, according to The Sydney Morning Herald.
- Settlement documents in October revealed the price and the buyers. The private treaty sale was agreed on May 27 without a disclosed price.

Delta Goodrem and Matthew Copley have emerged as the buyers of a $10.9 million mansion in Sydney's Rose Bay, with settlement records showing the sale closed in October. The Australian singer-songwriter and her husband bought the four-bedroom home with a pool, [The Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/property/news/delta-goodrem-settles-on-sydney-mansion-with-pool-pays-10-9m-20261031-p618k5.html) reported, a story also carried by The Age.

## How the sale came to light

The purchase was done quietly. According to the Herald, the house sold by private treaty on May 27 and no price was made public at the time. That left the settlement paperwork, filed this month, to reveal both the figure and who was behind it.

The paper reports that the couple, who married in Malta in June 2025, bought from David Allen of Plato Investment Management, who is also president of the Eastern Suburbs Rugby Club. The agent who sold the property was D'Leanne Lewis of Laing+Simmons Double Bay. Simon Cohen of Cohen Handler is understood to have acted as the buyer's agent.

## Inside the Rose Bay house

The Herald describes a home with neutral interiors: French oak herringbone floors, an imported Italian marble fireplace and a white kitchen with a breakfast bar and butler's pantry. The bedrooms add colour through patterned wallpaper.

Outdoors, the property has several al fresco entertaining areas, including a bougainvillea-draped terrace, a pool and gardens designed by landscape designer Paul Bangay. It has three bathrooms alongside the four bedrooms.

## Where it sits in the market

Rose Bay is one of Sydney's priciest harbourside suburbs. The Herald cites Domain data putting its median house price at $6.35 million, up 22.1 per cent in the year to September. At $10.9 million, the Goodrem purchase is roughly 1.7 times that median.

Property records also show Goodrem owns a unit in Darlinghurst, bought for $4,825,000 in 2018, according to the report. The coverage does not say whether she plans to keep it or sell.

The same story notes other big deals in the suburb. A six-bedroom Rose Bay house sold for $26.5 million last December, and its buyer has lodged plans with council, estimated at $8.2 million, to renovate it. That gives some sense of the price range at the top end of the area.
