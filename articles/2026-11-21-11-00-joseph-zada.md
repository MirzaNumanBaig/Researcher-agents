**Quick summary**

- Audiences have rated *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* at 95% on the Popcornmeter, according to Rotten Tomatoes.
- Critics were more reserved, with the site describing their reviews as lukewarm.
- [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) plays a teenage Haymitch Abernathy in the 50th Hunger Games.
- Fans praise the visuals and the emotion, while some say the story feels rushed and too gentle compared with the book.
- The film is in cinemas now.

Audiences are embracing [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada)'s big-screen turn as young Haymitch Abernathy. According to a Rotten Tomatoes editorial published on 20 November 2026, *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* has reached "Verified Hot" status at 95% on the Popcornmeter, even though the critics' response was lukewarm.

## Why audiences are warming to Sunrise on the Reaping

The film is the second prequel in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) franchise. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) readers voted it the second most anticipated movie of November, and Zada leads the story as a teenage Haymitch competing in the 50th Hunger Games, known as the Second Quarter Quell.

The site's summary says fans find the film visually stunning and emotionally heavy. Its writer notes that one point of agreement across the reviews is that viewers should bring tissues. One viewer wrote that they cried and that the person beside them sobbed through the whole screening. Another called it a "gut-wrenching" watch and a heartbreaking prequel to the earlier films.

## Where fans push back

Not every viewer was won over. Several reviewers quoted by Rotten Tomatoes said the pacing felt fast and that scenes seemed cut, leaving the characters with less depth than in earlier films. Others compared the PG-style tone with the book, saying a harsher rating would have suited the story's violence better.

One reader said the film is more violent and less focused on relationships than the originals. Another said it is the best of the whole franchise. That spread of opinion explains why the audience score and the critics' verdict sit so far apart.

The cast also includes [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons), [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) and Ralph Fiennes, according to the Rotten Tomatoes listing. None of the quoted audience reviews single out a performance by name in the text we could read, so it is too early to say whose work will draw the most praise.
