**Quick summary**

- Australian musician [Georgia Maq](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Maq) won a Nathan Fielder lookalike contest in Los Angeles on 8 November, according to Stereogum.
- The event was a Fielder and Holmes lookalike contest at The Hunt, a vintage furniture store.
- Maq used it to promote her single "Rearranging Chairs On The Titanic", released on 18 November.
- The stunt nods to Fielder's film *You Can See Everything*, which has strong reviews.

The [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) lookalike contest in Los Angeles was won by an Australian singer-songwriter. Georgia Maq, the former lead singer of [Camp Cope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camp_Cope), took the prize on 8 November at The Hunt vintage furniture store, and followed it with a new single, as [Stereogum reported](https://stereogum.com/2519654/georgia-maq-shares-new-single-wins-nathan-fielder-lookalike-contest/news).

## How the Nathan Fielder lookalike contest played out

Stereogum describes the event as a Fielder and Holmes lookalike contest, a reference to the pairing at the centre of Fielder's recent film. Maq entered as Fielder. According to the report, she looked the part but took some liberties with the costume, adding hot pants and thigh-high platform stiletto boots. She won anyway.

The prize was modest. Stereogum says Maq and the winning Holmes lookalike shared US$50 between them. There is no suggestion in the report that Fielder himself attended or has commented on the contest.

## A four-step plan that ended in a song

Maq has framed the win as part of a plan with four steps: win the lookalike contest, discover The Hunt, then win people over with her song. The song is "Rearranging Chairs On The Titanic", and she shot its music video at the same store, which fits the furniture theme in the title.

Stereogum calls the track a reflective country song about working out who you are and feeling more settled as the pressure to have a plan fades. It came out on Wednesday 18 November.

In her own words, Maq wrote it alone on her living room floor while preparing to return to Australia for the first time in two and a half years. She said the move from Camp Cope to finding out who she is without the band has been an odyssey she is still living. The song was recorded live to tape with no click track, and she said she nearly cried a couple of times and wanted that kept in.

## Why Fielder is on people's minds

The joke lands because Fielder is currently in the spotlight. His film *You Can See Everything*, which he directed with Lance Oppenheim and stars in alongside Elizabeth Holmes and Christopher Abbott, opened on 6 September 2026. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything), it holds 97% on Rotten Tomatoes from 229 reviews and 97 out of 100 on Metacritic from 43 critics.

The same page lists a budget of US$7 million and a box office of US$93 million. That is roughly 13 times its cost, a strong return for a low-budget release.
