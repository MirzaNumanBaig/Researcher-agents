**Quick summary**

- [Nitesh Tiwari's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari) *Ramayana: Part 1* is reported to have crossed Rs 900 crore worldwide, according to a Telegraph India headline on 12 November 2026.
- The same report puts its India collection at Rs 500 crore.
- Veteran actor [Hema Malini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hema_Malini) posted a positive review on X on 10 November.
- Trade trackers publish different totals, so every figure here is a reported estimate.

Nitesh Tiwari's *Ramayana: Part 1*, released internationally as *Ramayana: Rise of Legend*, is reported to have passed Rs 900 crore at the worldwide box office, according to Telegraph India on 12 November. The film is also said to have reached Rs 500 crore in India. The numbers arrive alongside praise from one of Hindi cinema's best-known veterans.

## What the Ramayana box office numbers say

Telegraph India's headline gives the two milestones: more than Rs 900 crore globally and Rs 500 crore in India. The outlet's full article could not be opened for this report, so the figures rest on that headline alone.

Earlier tallies show how quickly the film has moved since its 5 November release. Koimoi reported a worldwide gross of about Rs 507 crore after four days, and Khaleej Times said the film had already overtaken the lifetime worldwide total of *Adipurush* in that time. The Week also ran a piece on 11 November about the ticket-sales records the film has broken, though only a short stub of it was available.

Trackers do not agree on day-by-day figures. Koimoi and Newsbytes, for example, differ by roughly ten crore for the third day. Treat the Rs 900 crore mark as a reported estimate until a producer statement or a consolidated tracker confirms it.

## Hema Malini's verdict

On 10 November, Hema Malini posted her thoughts on X after watching the film. She wrote that she went in with "least expectations", since the story is one "even young kids are aware of", and left "pleasantly surprised at the fabulous presentation". She singled out the special effects, which she said left her "breathless with anticipation".

She also praised the "apt casting" of the leads and described watching the audience being "transported" to another period. Calling it "a completely unique experience for the Indian audience", she credited the latest technical innovations for much of the effect, according to Moneycontrol, which reported her post. The 78-year-old actor's comments carry weight with older viewers, a group a mythological epic needs.

## The cast behind the film

[Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) plays Ram and [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) plays Sita. [Ravie Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey) is Lakshman, and Yash is also in the cast, Moneycontrol notes.

Not every reaction has been warm. Moneycontrol links to a separate report in which actor [Nitish Bharadwaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitish_Bharadwaj) is said to have withdrawn his praise and called the film "deeply disappointing" over its portrayal of Ravan. That report was not available to read in full, so the detail of his objection is not covered here.

Moneycontrol also points to a report that the film has been submitted for consideration at the 99th Academy Awards. That is an unconfirmed report and no official confirmation was seen.

## What happens next

Box office counts for the second weekend will show whether the film holds its pace. The title carries "Part 1", so a second instalment is expected, though no new details on it were found in the sources used here. Any awards submission would need to be confirmed by the film's makers or the Academy.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Ramayana crossed Rs 900 crore?
Telegraph India reported on 12 November that it has crossed Rs 900 crore worldwide. Other trackers had earlier shown lower running totals, so the figure is not yet independently confirmed.

### Who directed Ramayana: Part 1?
[Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari) directed the film, which stars Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi.

### What did Hema Malini say about the film?
She praised the special effects, the casting and the overall presentation, and said she was pleasantly surprised after expecting little.
