**Quick summary**

- [David Henrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Henrie) says the role of Gale in The Hunger Games came down to him and [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth).
- He told the Lila Rose Show that contracts for a three-picture deal had been negotiated before Hemsworth got the part.
- Henrie calls missing out "the best thing in the world", because the film he made next led him to his Catholic faith.
- The account was reported by Entertainment Weekly and republished by Yahoo News UK.

The Wizards of Waverly Place star David Henrie has revealed how close he came to playing Gale in The Hunger Games, a part that went to Liam Hemsworth. In a recent interview, Henrie said the loss changed his life for the better. The story was reported by Entertainment Weekly and carried by [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/disney-star-david-henrie-says-180000171.html).

## How close Henrie came to Gale

Speaking on the Lila Rose Show, Henrie said the casting was a two-horse race. "It was down to me and Liam Hemsworth for the role in The Hunger Games," he said. He described a strong audition and a contract negotiation for a three-picture deal worth millions of dollars. "I really wanted the role," he said. "But Liam got it."

That account comes from Henrie alone, and the report does not include any comment from Hemsworth or the film's producers. It should be read as one actor's recollection of events.

## The franchise he missed

Hemsworth went on to appear in all four films adapted from the novels by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins). He starred alongside [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson), [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson), [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks), [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci) and [Donald Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sutherland).

According to Entertainment Weekly, the series set box office records and is the 21st-highest-grossing film franchise of all time, with $3.3 billion earned worldwide. For a young actor, a three-film commitment to a series of that size would have shaped a career. Fans can find more on [Hemsworth's Hunger Games films](https://moduscarry.com/liam-hemsworth-hunger-games/) on our site.

## Why Henrie calls the loss a blessing

Henrie told host Lila Rose that missing the part was "the best thing in the world" for him. The reason, he said, was what came next. Because Gale went elsewhere, he took a role in the 2015 war drama Little Boy, where his co-stars included [Kevin James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_James), [Michael Rapaport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Rapaport) and [Emily Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Watson).

On set, he said, the cast held "philosophy nights" where they asked big questions about the universe. He recalled starting with something small, a leaf falling from a tree, and tracing the question back through the tree, the soil and the earth. "There had to be a start to create everything," he said.

Those conversations led him to think about how the universe began, and the answer that came to him was God. "It all clicked and I radically changed the way that I live," he said. He also described a decision to stop dating until he met the woman he would marry.

## What came after

Henrie married Maria Cahill in 2017, according to the report. The two were introduced by an archbishop. They have four children: Pia, James, Gemma Clare and Peter.

He has kept acting, though he says he is more selective now. He wrote, directed and starred in the 2020 film This Is the Year. In 2025 he played the young [Ronald Reagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Reagan) in a biopic, and he has returned as Justin Russo in the Wizards of Waverly Place revival on Disney Channel.
