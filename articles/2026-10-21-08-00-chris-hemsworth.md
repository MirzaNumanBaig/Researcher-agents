**Quick summary**

- Sydney distillery Archie Rose will sell three whiskies in the US from Friday 23 October, according to The Spirits Business.
- Chris Hemsworth became a co-owner in August and helped design the new flask-shaped bottle and packaging.
- The US prices are US$39.99, US$49.99 and US$59.99, as reported by AOL and The Spirits Business.
- A campaign film starring Hemsworth and his brother Luke Hemsworth was produced by Hemsworth and filmed at eight Australian locations.
- The range will be sold in eight US states, with Reserve Bar as the online partner.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) is taking Australian whisky to American shelves. Archie Rose Distilling Co., the Sydney distillery he co-owns, will put its three core whiskies on sale in the US on Friday 23 October, in a redesigned bottle he helped create and with a campaign film that stars him and his brother, [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth).

It is the first big public result of a deal announced in August, when Hemsworth joined founder Will Edwards as co-owner, according to Mediaweek and B&T.

## What is Archie Rose launching in the US?

The US range has three whiskies, all made at the Botany distillery in Sydney from Australian-grown grain, as AOL and The Spirits Business report. Straight Rye Malt Whisky (US$49.99) was the distillery's first release, in September 2019. Single Malt Australian Whisky (US$59.99) is made from six Australian barley malts. Australian Original Whisky (US$39.99) is the new one, a blend of the other two bottled at 40% ABV.

The Shout lists the same three in Australia, at RRPs of $99.99, $119.99 and $79.99 respectively. The Australian prices are in local dollars, so they can't be compared directly with the US figures.

The liquids and production methods for the two existing whiskies are unchanged. The Straight Rye Malt was previously called Rye Malt Whisky, and only the name and packaging have changed, according to Mediaweek.

The awards give the range its sales pitch. The Shout says the single malt has been named Australia's Best Single Malt Whisky five times, and the rye has twice been named World's Best Rye Whisky.

## How much of the design is Hemsworth's?

Quite a lot, by the account of the trade press. B&T reports that he worked with the Archie Rose team and Glasgow creative agency Thirst on the identity, the bottle, the packaging and the global creative direction.

The bottle is flask-shaped. Its textured glass is meant to evoke the soil where the barley and rye grow, and the idea behind it was being "forged from the earth", according to B&T. The glass is lighter than before, which cuts packaging weight and freight costs. The distillery, a certified B Corp, says that supports its export plans.

"I've been a fan of Archie Rose for years because, quite simply, they make brilliant whisky," Hemsworth said in the launch statement carried by Mediaweek.

## The campaign film with Luke Hemsworth

The campaign film was produced by Hemsworth and directed by Justin McMillan. It was shot across eight Australian locations and, per B&T, centres on the landscape, ingredients and ingenuity behind the whisky.

The outlets differ on when it premiered. AOL and Food & Beverage Magazine say 20 October, while The Shout says 21 October. Either way, it was due on Hemsworth's YouTube channel and the Archie Rose website.

## Where will it be sold?

Distribution details come from The Spirits Business. The whiskies will be available in eight states, with Reserve Bar handling e-commerce. Reyes Beverage Group distributes in California, Texas and Florida and shares Illinois with Chicago Beverage Systems. Manhattan Beer & Beverage Distributors covers New York, Fedway Associates covers New Jersey and Best Brands covers Tennessee.

Master distiller Dave Withers said the partnership lets the distillery "take that Australian craft to the global stage", according to The Shout.

## What happens next

The US launch begins on 23 October. The Shout reports that the new range began rolling out in Australia on 19 October, and The Spirits Business says Singapore and New Zealand are also getting the new identity. Archie Rose is shortlisted in two categories at The Spirits Business Awards 2026 next month, for Innovation in Production and for David Fisher as Blender of the Year.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Chris Hemsworth's Archie Rose whisky launch in the US?

It goes on sale in the US on Friday 23 October, according to The Spirits Business and AOL. It will be available in eight states, including California, Texas, Florida and New York.

### How much does Archie Rose whisky cost in the US?

Australian Original Whisky is US$39.99, Straight Rye Malt Whisky is US$49.99 and Single Malt Australian Whisky is US$59.99, per AOL and The Spirits Business.

### Does Chris Hemsworth own Archie Rose?

Yes, he is a co-owner. He joined founder Will Edwards in August, according to Mediaweek, B&T and The Shout.
