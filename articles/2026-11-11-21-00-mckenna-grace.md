**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) wore a custom Elisabetta Franchi gown to a fan screening of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* in Los Angeles on Monday.
- The black, embellished dress draws on Katniss Everdeen's Victory Ball look from *Catching Fire*, with burgundy-red feathers at the shoulders in place of wings.
- The event was held at AMC Century City 15 and featured a recreated Cornucopia, according to Yahoo.
- Grace plays Maysilee Donner in the prequel. A New York premiere follows next Monday.

Mckenna Grace has added another chapter to her *Hunger Games* press-tour wardrobe. At a fan screening of [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel *Sunrise on the Reaping* in Los Angeles on Monday, the actress turned up in a gown that borrowed from one of the franchise's best-known costumes.

## What she wore to the Los Angeles screening

According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/mckenna-grace-reimagines-katniss-everdeen-154246539.html), the cast joined fans at AMC Century City 15, where the centrepiece was a recreation of the Cornucopia dressed with floral displays and photo spots. Grace wore a custom Elisabetta Franchi gown inspired by the dress [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) wore as Katniss at the Victory Ball in *Catching Fire*.

Where Katniss wore wings, Grace's black embellished gown carried contrasting burgundy-red feathers at the shoulders. Aldo Thalie platforms finished the outfit, per the same report.

A second Yahoo piece, [first published by Reality Tea](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/mckenna-grace-channels-jennifer-lawrence-111546244.html), describes the detail more closely: a plunging V-neckline, sequins across the fitted bodice, shoulder caps in crimson and black feathers, and a skirt of semi-sheer black lace over a thigh-high slit. Her hair was in a sleek, centre-parted low updo. That outlet calls the event a premiere, while Yahoo's fashion report describes a fan screening, so this article uses the screening description.

## A look that nods rather than copies

The Yahoo fashion writer acknowledged that some readers are tired of the franchise references, but argued the gown works because it is not a direct recreation. In their view, the intricate embellishment is the strongest link between the two dresses, while the feathers and neckline give Grace's version its own identity. The same writer called it another successful chapter in her *Hunger Games* promotional run.

That run is part of a wider pattern. For this tour, Grace has leaned into the world of Panem on the red carpet. Readers who want the earlier outfits can find them in our [guide to her Hunger Games press tour looks](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-fashion/), and more in the [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

## Why her face feels familiar

The role has also prompted a round of "where do I know her from?" pieces. [PureWow](https://www.purewow.com/entertainment/hunger-games-mckenna-grace-movies-and-shows) notes that Grace is set to star as Maysilee Donner in *Sunrise on the Reaping*, and that she has often played younger versions of established characters.

Those roles include a young Sabrina Spellman in *Chilling Adventures of Sabrina*, opposite [Kiernan Shipka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiernan_Shipka); a young [Tonya Harding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tonya_Harding) in *I, Tonya*, with [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) as the adult skater; and a young [Carol Danvers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Danvers) in *Captain Marvel*. PureWow also lists young Theodora Crain in *The Haunting of Hill House*, where [Kate Siegel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Siegel) played the adult version.

Her own leading work includes *Gifted* alongside [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), Phoebe Spengler in *Ghostbusters: Afterlife* and [Frozen Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghostbusters%3A_Frozen_Empire), and an Emmy nomination for *The Handmaid's Tale*. More recent credits named by PureWow are *Regretting You*, *Five Nights at Freddy's 2* and *Scream 7*. Reality Tea gives her age as 20.
