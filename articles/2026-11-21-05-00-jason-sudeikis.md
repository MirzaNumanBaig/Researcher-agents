**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) said he had to follow Chris Rock at his first *Saturday Night Live* audition in 2003.
- He told the story at a live SmartLess recording at the Hollywood Bowl, according to Yardbarker.
- On the same night he said *Ted Lasso* began as a 2013 NBC Sports commercial.
- Season 4 of the Apple TV series ran from August 5 to October 7, 2026, and a fifth season has not been confirmed.

Jason Sudeikis has revealed that his first audition for [*Saturday Night Live*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) in 2003 came straight after a stand-up set by [Chris Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Rock). He shared the story during SmartLess LIVE at the Hollywood Bowl, [Yardbarker reports](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/i_had_to_follow_chris_rock_jason_sudeikis_recalls_high_stakes_snl_audition/s1_17785_44589842), in an evening that also covered the unlikely start of *Ted Lasso*.

## How the SNL audition came about

At the time Sudeikis was performing with Second City. According to the account, [Jeff Richmond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Richmond) saw him on stage and asked whether he would audition for *SNL*. Sudeikis said he thought the show was for "heroes", but he took the chance anyway.

The surprise came when he arrived. Chris Rock had dropped in to try out material for a monologue, working with [Jeff Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Ross), for the MTV Video Music Awards. "I had to follow Chris Rock who popped in to do like 10 minutes of like standup," Sudeikis said.

He said the situation had an upside. If the audition went badly, he could always point out that he had been following Chris Rock.

## What he performed, and what came next

Sudeikis did three characters and almost seven impressions, Yardbarker reports, including Dr. Phil, George Bush and [Jack Bauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Bauer) (the outlet spells it "Jack Bower"). He was not hired as a cast member straight away. He joined *SNL* as a writer, then became a featured performer and later a full cast member.

## Ted Lasso began as a three-day job

The second story from the night is about how *Ted Lasso* started. Sudeikis said the character first appeared in a 2013 NBC Sports commercial meant to get American viewers interested in soccer on NBC's channels, according to [a second Yardbarker report](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/hired_and_fired_jason_sudeikis_recalls_ted_lassos_unlikely_2013_nbc_sports_origins/s1_17785_44589748). He made it with [Brendan Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Hunt) and Joe Kelly, and NBC was the client.

"The whole idea of the first commercial is Ted Lasso gets hired and fired by Tottenham Hotspur in 3 days," Sudeikis said. He added that the coach "fell in love with London and he fell in love with football".

Sudeikis said the shoot also worked as a London trip for Hunt, an Arsenal fan who wanted to see a match. NBC would not fund travel for the 2014 follow-up, so that one was filmed in New York, where the coach became a tea-drinking optimist. A year later, [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde) encouraged him to return to the character, and a brainstorm in Brooklyn followed. Apple ended up the only buyer for the series.

## Home town and family

The report also says season 4 filmed in Kansas City, where Sudeikis grew up, with scenes shot at Country Club Plaza and Gates Bar-B-Q before production moved back to London. In a separate interview with E! News, he said one of his main parenting lessons is saying please and thank you and meaning it. He has two children, Otis and Daisy, with Wilde.

## What happens next

[*Ted Lasso*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso) returned for a fourth season on August 5, 2026, and it finished on October 7. As of November 2026 Apple TV has not renewed it for a fifth season.

Apple's head of programming, Matt Cherniss, told Deadline in July 2026 that Sudeikis was still working on season four and was looking to move on to the next season soon. Cherniss said that, to his knowledge, work on it had not started. For now, a fifth season is unconfirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### When did Jason Sudeikis audition for SNL?
He auditioned in 2003, after Jeff Richmond saw him perform at Second City and suggested it. He joined the show as a writer first.

### Who did Sudeikis have to follow at the audition?
He followed Chris Rock, who was doing about 10 minutes of stand-up while working on a monologue for the MTV Video Music Awards.

### How did Ted Lasso start?
It began as a 2013 NBC Sports commercial in which the character was hired and fired by Tottenham Hotspur within three days, Sudeikis said.

### Will there be a Ted Lasso season 5?
Apple TV has not confirmed one as of November 2026, though a programming executive told Deadline the show could continue past season 4.
