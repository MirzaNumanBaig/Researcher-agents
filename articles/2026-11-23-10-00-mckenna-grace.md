**Quick summary**

- A Change.org petition asking Lionsgate to release a four-hour cut of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is close to 70,000 signatures, according to ScreenRant.
- The film opened in cinemas on November 20.
- Director Francis Lawrence says his first cut ran four hours and 12 minutes before it was trimmed.
- One confirmed cut scene involves [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace)'s character, Maysilee Donner.

The Sunrise on the Reaping petition has grown quickly since the film reached cinemas on November 20. Fans are asking Lionsgate to release a four-hour version of the new [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, which stars Mckenna Grace as Maysilee Donner. According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-4-hour-cut-petition/), the petition is nearly at 70,000 signatures.

## What the petition is asking for

The petition was started by a fan named Kaitlin. It argues that the story of Haymitch Abernathy, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), deserves to be told in full. Its text says many scenes were filmed but left out of the finished movie, and that those scenes help explain his character, his relationships and what he endured in the 50th Hunger Games.

The request is specific about format. It asks Lionsgate to put the full cut out through an extended edition, a streaming release or physical media. ScreenRant reports more than 2,000 comments on the petition page, and fans have also been urging Lawrence on social media.

## What Francis Lawrence has said about the cuts

Lawrence told ScreenRant's Ash Crossan that the first cut he saw ran four hours and 12 minutes. He said trimming it meant losing material, and that this was never because the team disliked it. In his words, someone's favourite small moment would inevitably go.

He also said the finished film stays true to the spirit and themes of the story and to the emotional bonds at its centre. Lawrence added that the filmmakers are sometimes more protective of moments than author [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins). He described her as good at adapting her own work because of her screenwriting background, and said he has sometimes found himself telling her a scene cannot go.

## The scene involving Mckenna Grace

ScreenRant says one cut scene has been confirmed. It comes from the original novel and shows Haymitch and Maysilee, the District 12 tribute played by Grace, coming across three lower-level Gamemakers in the arena. The outlet does not say how long the scene was or whether it will ever be released.

Grace began acting as a child around 2012, with early roles in Crash & Bernstein, The Goodwin Games and The Young and the Restless. Her breakthrough came with Gifted in 2017, and she also played the younger version of [Carol Danvers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carol_Danvers) in Captain Marvel. The film's cast also includes [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close) as Drusilla Sickle and [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) as Plutarch Heavensbee.

Some readers of the novel have pointed to differences between the book and the film. ScreenRant notes the movie kept a PG-13 rating and softened some of the brutality found on the page.
