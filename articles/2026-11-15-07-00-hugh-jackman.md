**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster) were photographed together at Bondi Beach in Sydney, according to Fox News.
- The pictures follow Jackman's 58th birthday.
- The photos show the pair walking the shore, playing Frisbee, sitting on the sand and kissing.
- They were first photographed as a couple in January 2025, and worked together on Broadway's "The Music Man".

Hugh Jackman and Sutton Foster spent a day at Sydney's Bondi Beach this month, [Fox News reports](https://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/hugh-jackman-sutton-foster-enjoy-romantic-beach-day-australia), with photographs showing the pair relaxed and affectionate shortly after the actor celebrated his 58th birthday.

## What the Bondi photos show

According to Fox News, the images, supplied by the photo agency Backgrid, catch the couple walking along the shoreline and then settling on the sand. Foster wore a white T-shirt, black workout shorts, sunglasses and a black bucket hat, with her hair in braids. Jackman chose a blue long-sleeve shirt, sunglasses, pink-and-white patterned swim trunks and a green baseball cap.

The report says the two played Frisbee, sat side by side and shared a kiss. Another image shows Jackman taking a dip in the ocean. Fox News describes the pair as "all smiles" throughout the day.

## A familiar beach for Jackman

Bondi is well known territory for the Sydney-born star. Fox News notes that he has spent time there before with his children and his former wife. The outlet does not say how long the couple plan to stay in Australia, and it does not give an exact date for the photographs beyond calling the trip recent.

## How the pair got here

Jackman and Foster were first photographed together as a couple in January 2025, according to Fox News. Those pictures showed them holding hands in Santa Monica, California, and followed months of speculation. The report says it remains unclear when they actually began dating.

Their professional history is better documented. They starred together in the Broadway revival of "The Music Man", which ran from 2021 to 2023. In a June 2022 interview with Vogue quoted by Fox News, Foster called Jackman "one of my best friends" and praised his work ethic and kindness, saying he "disarms everyone".
