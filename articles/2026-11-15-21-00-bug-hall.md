**Quick summary**

- [Bug Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bug_Hall), 41, who played Alfalfa in the 1994 film *The Little Rascals*, started a GoFundMe in early November 2026.
- He says he is behind on a phone bill, propane for the camper and an unexpected electric hookup fee.
- The goal was US$2,000. By Sunday 8 November it had passed US$20,000, according to Gray News.
- The family's sixth child is due within weeks, and they live off the grid on an 80-acre Arkansas property.

The Bug Hall GoFundMe has raised more than ten times its target. The former child actor asked for help with a few overdue bills just before his sixth child is due, and strangers responded within days.

## What Hall asked for

Hall, best known as Alfalfa in the 1994 film adaptation of [The Little Rascals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Gang), set up the fundraiser in the first week of November. In his message he said the family had fallen behind on a handful of bills: the phone, propane for the camper and an unexpected electric hookup service fee, [Gray News reported](https://www.fox19.com/2026/11/08/little-rascals-star-asks-financial-help-gofundme-ahead-sixth-childs-birth/). The report did not give dollar amounts for those costs.

[TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/06/brandon-bug-hall-launches-gofundme-for-baby/) said Hall wrote that the family had reached "new land" but was still "a few materials short" of having things working properly. He told supporters he hoped to cover the costs within about two weeks, and that the baby was less than a month away.

He said he had "no other choice" but to ask. He wrote that he was no more deserving than anyone else and invited people to give, share the page or pray.

## How much has been raised

The campaign opened with a US$2,000 goal. TMZ counted more than US$8,800 on 6 November. By Sunday, Gray News reported, donations had passed US$20,000, roughly ten times the original target.

Hall wants everything ready on the property so he can go back to work once the baby arrives, according to the same report.

## Why the family lives off the grid

The appeal makes more sense against what Hall has said about the past two years. In January he told the Daily Mail he had taken a "vow of poverty" and moved his family to the Arkansas land to live off the grid and build their own home, as Gray News recounts. People reported that he gave away most of his possessions and all of his savings after that decision.

Hall has also said he left entertainment because acting no longer fitted his faith. In a 2025 YouTube video he described himself as a "radical Catholic extremist" and said the choice was hard because he loved the work, per the same report.

That work was extensive. His credits include *The Big Green*, *Honey, We Shrunk Ourselves*, *Hercules*, *Charmed*, *Cold Case* and *CSI: Miami*. ## The family

Hall has been married to his wife, Jill, since 2017, Gray News said. The couple already have five children, and TMZ reports the family is living in a camper on the land while they build. The reports do not name the children or give their ages, and we have not either.

## What happens next

The baby is due before the end of November. Hall has not said publicly whether he plans to return to acting or take on other paid work, only that he wants to be ready to work once the birth is behind him. The fundraiser remains open, and the total may keep rising.
