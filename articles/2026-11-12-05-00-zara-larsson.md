**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) is confirmed for Coachella 2027 and will play on Friday, 9 April and Friday, 16 April.
- She announced it herself on Instagram as the festival released its 2027 poster.
- [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa) headlines the same day, with Madonna booked for the Dance Tent.
- Festival passes go on sale on 13 November at 12pm PT through Coachella.com.

Swedish pop singer Zara Larsson will perform at Coachella 2027, telling fans on Instagram that she is "taking a break from my break" to bring the Midnight Sun to the desert. The festival published its full 2027 poster on 11 November, and Larsson sits among the biggest names on the Friday bill at Indio, California.

## Zara Larsson at Coachella 2027: what she announced

According to [Metro Mode](https://metromode.se/en/noje/zara-larsson-klar-for-coachella-2027-tar-midnight-sun-till-oknen/), Larsson posted the news at the same moment the poster went live. Her caption promised to bring "the Midnight Sun to the desert" on Friday, followed by a row of sun, cactus and fire emojis. She also shared her own version of the poster, showing her in a colourful bikini top and green hoops against a pink desert, palm trees and the festival's Ferris wheel.

Metro Mode reported that the post had passed 130,000 likes within hours. The "Midnight Sun" reference points to her latest album and the world tour that followed, which the outlet describes as one of her most acclaimed records.

The caption also confirms she is currently on a break after a busy year of touring and new releases. This announcement is a short interruption of it.

## Dates and the Friday lineup

Coachella runs at the Empire Polo Club in Indio across two weekends, 9 to 11 April and 16 to 18 April 2027. Metro Mode, citing the festival, says both weekends carry the same lineup, so Larsson plays on 9 and 16 April.

Dua Lipa is the Friday headliner. [That Grape Juice](https://thatgrapejuice.net/2026/11/coachella-2027-lineup-sza-dua-lipa-tame-impala-confirmed-to-headline-madonna-zara-larsson-victoria-monet-ayra-starr-join/) reports that Madonna has been given a special Dance Tent set, a return to a festival she last played in 2006. Also on Friday's bill, according to the two outlets, are:

- Kehlani, JISOO of Blackpink and Leon Thomas
- [Steve Lacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Lacy) and Kelela
- [Diljit Dosanjh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diljit_Dosanjh) and [Giorgio Moroder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Moroder)
- Social Distortion

That makes Friday the pop-heavy day of the festival. Saturday is led by [Tame Impala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_Impala), with Robyn, Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer and Tiësto, while SZA closes both Sundays.

## A strong year behind the booking

Metro Mode frames the booking as the latest step in a long climb. Larsson won the Swedish talent show Talang at ten and later had global hits including "Lush Life", "Never Forget You" and "Symphony".

The outlet lists her recent run of recognition: two awards at the AMA gala in May and a VMA in September for a video in which she plays a shop mannequin. It adds that she has become an increasingly visible voice in the industry. Those are Metro Mode's claims, and the awards are the verifiable part of the picture.

She is not the only Swede on the poster. Robyn plays Saturday and [Tove Lo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tove_Lo) plays Sunday, according to Metro Mode, so three Swedish pop acts share the 2027 lineup.

For more on her current era, see our earlier post on the [Blue Moon video from the Midnight Sun era](https://moduscarry.com/zara-larsson-blue-moon-video/), or browse the [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) section.

## What happens next

Passes go on sale on 13 November at 12pm PT via Coachella.com, per That Grape Juice. The same outlet says YouTube will livestream both weekends again, which matters for anyone in Australia who cannot make the trip to California. Set times have not been published in the sources.

Larsson has not said whether she will announce anything else before April. Her own post suggests the break continues until then.
