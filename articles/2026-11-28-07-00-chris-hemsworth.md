A film that struggled in cinemas has become the biggest movie on Prime Video this year. *Crime 101*, the Los Angeles heist thriller led by Chris Hemsworth, now sits at number one on the platform's 2026 worldwide movie chart, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/chris-hemsworth-crime-101-prime-video-top-movie-2026-streaming-chart/), which cites the tracking site FlixPatrol. It is a useful reminder that a quiet theatrical run does not always decide how many people end up watching a film.

## How Crime 101 reached the top of Prime Video

FlixPatrol credits the film with 28,536 ranking points. That puts it ahead of *Project Hail Mary* on 24,675 points and *The Wrecking Crew* on 23,057, two of the year's other big titles, as ScreenRant reports.

The streaming debut on 1 April moved fast. The film reached number one worldwide on Prime Video within 24 hours and topped the chart in 29 countries, according to Prime Video's figures quoted by ScreenRant. The platform also says it spent 10 weeks in its Top 10.

## A box office miss, a critical hit

The cinema numbers tell a different story. *Crime 101* arrived in theatres in February 2026 and took roughly US$72.9 million worldwide. ScreenRant puts the reported production budget at US$90 million, which means it did not recover its costs at the box office.

Reviews were stronger than the takings. The film carries an 88% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, and ScreenRant's own reviewer praised the performances and the deliberate pace, comparing it with classic crime films such as *Heat*. ScreenRant adds that the film joins a growing list of cinema disappointments that found a larger audience at home.

## What the film is about

Hemsworth plays Mike Davis, an elusive jewel thief behind a string of robberies along the 101 freeway in Los Angeles. He teams up with a disillusioned insurance broker to plan one last heist. The cast includes [Mark Ruffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo), [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry), [Barry Keoghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Keoghan), [Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro) and [Nick Nolte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Nolte).

[Bart Layton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_Layton), who made [American Animals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Animals) and *The Imposter*, directed. The script is based on Don Winslow's novella of the same name.

## Thor is next, and the training video has fans talking

The streaming result lands just weeks before Hemsworth's biggest release of the year. He returns as Thor in Marvel Studios' *Avengers: Doomsday*, due in cinemas on 18 December 2026. ScreenRant notes it will be his first big-screen outing as the character since *Thor: Love and Thunder* in 2022, and that it reunites him with directors Anthony and Joe Russo, who worked with him on *Infinity War* and *Endgame*. [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) and [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)) are in the ensemble.

Hemsworth also posted a workout clip on Instagram to promote a discount of up to 27% at his fitness company, Centrfit. Yahoo's entertainment desk reports that the clip drew comments from fans who said [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), the villain played by Downey, "is not ready" for Thor. The same report recalls that director [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh) once said Hemsworth ate whole chickens while preparing for the first *Thor* film. For more on that matchup, read our earlier piece on [Chris Hemsworth on Thor facing Doctor Doom](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-thor-doctor-doom/).
