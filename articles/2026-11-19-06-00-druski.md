**Quick summary**

- Netflix has released the first trailer for *Collect & Praise*, a six-episode, semi-scripted comedy starring Druski as a money-hungry megachurch pastor.
- The series premieres on Netflix on Tuesday, December 1, 2026, and is rated TV-MA.
- [Mike Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps) plays the pastor's unpredictable deacon.
- It is the first project under a multiyear deal between Netflix and Druski's company, 4LIFERS Entertainment.

[Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) is taking his most popular character from YouTube to Netflix. The streamer released the *Collect & Praise* trailer this week, confirming a December 1 premiere for the comedy, in which the comedian plays Pastor Dru, a megachurch leader who cares more about the offering plate than the congregation.

## What the Collect & Praise trailer shows

According to [Blex Media](https://blexmedia.com/netflix-drops-first-trailer-for-druskis-megachurch-comedy-collect-praise/), the series runs six episodes and follows Druski's pastor and Epps's deacon as they scheme their way through scandal, chaos and controversy. Guest stars include Navv Greene, Roy Wood Jr., Rob Moran and Michael Blackson. David Janoff directs, with 4LIFERS Entertainment, ASK Media and Janoff Films producing.

Epps is best known for [Friday After Next](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_After_Next), *The Upshaws* and decades of stand-up, [Baller Alert](https://balleralert.com/druski-netflix-series-collect-praise-trailer/) notes. Early teases lean into the kind of excess Druski's audience expects. [The Quintessential Gentleman](https://www.theqgentleman.com/post/2026-11-druski-is-a-money-hungry-megachurch-pastor-in-netflixs-collect-praise-trailer/) points to a funeral scene with Epps that ends in a shootout. Baller Alert, citing a GQ set visit, adds a sequence in a correctional facility and an elaborate car crash that was being prepared.

## From viral sketch to series

The show grows out of a January 2026 sketch, "Mega Church Pastors Love Money", set at the fictional Collect & Praise Ministries. Blex Media reports that Druski's pastor descended from the ceiling on wires, a nod to real-life "flying pastors", wearing a Christian Dior blazer and Christian Louboutin shoes before pitching a US$4 million mission trip.

The clip drew more than 43 million views in its first 24 hours and later passed 60 million, according to the same report. It also split opinion. Some argued it mocked sacred spaces, while others saw satire of real financial pressure on congregations. Baller Alert says some religious leaders criticised the parody and others acknowledged the issues it raised. "The mega-pastor is a thing," Druski has said, as quoted by The Quintessential Gentleman.

The path to Netflix was not smooth. Blex Media reports Druski said Netflix, FX and Amazon all passed on his original pitch for a full series. He then self-funded the sketch, reportedly spending upward of US$100,000, and the same platforms came back. "Now it's a bidding war," he said on *The Diary of a CEO* podcast.

## The Netflix deal behind it

*Collect & Praise* is the first announced project under a multiyear development and licensing agreement between Netflix, Druski and 4LIFERS Entertainment. Netflix will also license titles from his existing catalogue: *Coulda Been Love*, *Coulda Been Daddy*, *Coulda Been Records Auditions* and a selection of his sketches. Blex Media says that body of work has drawn more than 1 billion views on YouTube.

"I've built my platform by creating characters and comedy that resonate with people," Druski said in a statement. John Derderian, who oversees Next Gen Content at Netflix, called the series "just the beginning".

## A very busy year for Druski

The trailer lands in the middle of a run of firsts. Druski, whose real name is Drew Desbordes, hosted the 2026 BET Awards in June as its youngest-ever host and opened the show with a live version of the pastor bit. On November 7 he became the first digital creator to host [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live), and TODAY reports the episode gave the show its highest rating in two years.

Speaking on TODAY on November 18, the 32-year-old said the Netflix series was shot in under two weeks. He also pointed to a 2018 tweet in which he said he would be "1 of the Biggest Comedians in the World", and described himself as a big believer in manifesting. His next target is bigger. "I got to get that Oscar," he told the hosts.

## What happens next

*Collect & Praise* arrives on Netflix on December 1. Druski's first film role follows in 2027 in *The Catch*, a baseball romantic comedy starring Emma Stone and directed by Dave McCary, according to TODAY. Blex Media adds that Chris Pine is also attached, that Druski is developing *The Diggers* with Kenya Barris, and that he has another Netflix film in development.
