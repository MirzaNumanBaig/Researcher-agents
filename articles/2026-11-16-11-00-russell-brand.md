**Quick summary**

- Laura Brand read a message from her husband [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) on their YouTube channel on Sunday 15 November.
- In it he says he is "not guilty for the offences" he was jailed for and that he is held at HMP Littlehey.
- He was sentenced to 10 years and six months after a jury found him guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- One of his accusers has since waived her anonymity, Deadline reports.

Russell Brand has told supporters he is innocent of the crimes that put him in prison, in a message his wife read aloud on YouTube on Sunday. The comedian and actor is serving a sentence of 10 years and six months at HMP Littlehey, a jail for men convicted of sexual offences.

## What the message said

Laura Brand presented an episode of the couple's Sunday Service show and read from a notebook. According to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/russell-brand-claims-not-guilty-prison-message-wife-1236755764/), the message opened with: "As you know, I am not guilty for the offences I have been jailed for, so something strange is happening."

The rest was written in the language of his Christian faith. He described the staff as strong, kind and dedicated, and said he was "in no position to judge" the other prisoners. He added that he did not know which of the other men were not guilty, and asked viewers to pray for and forgive one another and to support his wife and family. [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/russell-brand-maintains-innocence-message-wife-behind-bars-1237224538/) described Laura as audibly emotional as she finished.

She said she was streaming to continue the community work started on Sunday Service, which she called close to both their hearts.

## The conviction behind it

The jury at Southwark Crown Court heard a four-week trial. The Hollywood Reporter says it found Brand guilty on one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. He was found not guilty on two other rape charges and not guilty of attempted indecent assault.

A claim of innocence is not a legal finding. The verdict stands unless it is overturned, and nothing in the sources says an appeal has been lodged. Our earlier coverage of the verdict looks at [what the judge said on consent](https://moduscarry.com/russell-brand-verdict-consent/).

## A pre-recorded section and fresh claims

The video also included a pre-recorded section from before the sentence. In it, Brand compared media coverage of his trial to commentary on a football match, and said it was not generally being covered in a way he felt was supportive.

Separate reporting has added to the pressure. The Hollywood Reporter notes a new report from The Times and the Netflix documentary on Brand in which six more women accuse him of alleged sexual misconduct. These are allegations, and he has not been convicted of anything relating to them.

Over the weekend, Deadline reports, one accuser waived her legal right to anonymity. Speaking to The Sunday Times, Mia Latter alleged that Brand orally raped her in 2006, when she was 16 and he was 30. She said he called her "the child" during their relationship. Read more in our post on how [Mia Latter named herself as a Russell Brand accuser](https://moduscarry.com/mia-latter-russell-brand/).

## What happens next

Brand's public voice is now limited to messages like this one, relayed by his family. Deadline notes that Laura Brand opened the broadcast as a continuation of their Sunday Service community, so more episodes appear likely. The sources do not say how the prison or authorities view messages from inside being posted online, and we have not treated that as established.
