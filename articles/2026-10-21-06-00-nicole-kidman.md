**Quick summary**

- Sunday Rose Kidman-Urban, 18, is on the cover of the November 2026 issue of Vogue Germany, wearing Louis Vuitton.
- In the cover interview, published on 20 October, she says she is "very aware" of the opportunities her family background gives her.
- She is in her final year of high school in Nashville and says school keeps her grounded.
- She wants to direct films, and names her mother's 1995 film To Die For as her favourite of Nicole Kidman's.
- The issue goes on sale on 24 October, according to Just Jared.

Sunday Rose Kidman-Urban, the eldest daughter of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban), has made her Vogue cover debut. The 18-year-old fronts the November 2026 issue of Vogue Germany, and used the accompanying interview to talk about privilege, schoolwork and a future behind the camera.

## What she said about privilege

The most quoted line came early. "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have, and I'm truly grateful to be able to take advantage of them," she told Vogue Germany, as reported by [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/10/20/nicole-kidman-keith-urban-daughter-sunday-rose-famous-parents-modeling-opportunities/) and [The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2635952/nicole-kidman-and-keith-urbans-daughter-sunday-rose-opens-up-about-modelling-with-famous-parents).

She said she shows that gratitude through her conduct. On a job, she makes sure to give her best, avoids a negative attitude however tired or jet-lagged she is, and treats everyone with respect. Just Jared reports that she credits her mother with teaching her and her younger sister, Faith Margaret, those values well before modelling came into the picture.

Asked whether she hides who her parents are, she was relaxed about it. She hasn't deliberately told anyone, she said, but few people live in her area, so most classmates already knew when she started school, according to [The News International](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1420673-nicole-kidmans-daughter-gets-honest-about-her-privileged-life).

## School as the counterweight

Sunday Rose is still in high school in Nashville, and she says that is the point. "Funnily enough, I think school is what keeps me grounded the most when I'm modelling," she told the magazine. She admitted the contrast between the two worlds was hard to manage at first.

Now she enjoys both, she said, including the comfort of routine after a trip. It has made her more organised, too: assignments get handed in on time, because "that's no excuse".

## A short career with a long list of names

[Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/10/20/style/nicole-kidmans-model-daughter-sunday-rose-18-covers-vogue-germany/) says she began modelling only two years ago because of a rule set by her mother. She had been asking about fashion since she was 14, she said, but Kidman made her wait until she was 16.

Her runway debut came at Miu Miu's spring/summer 2025 show in October 2024, which The News International dates to 1 October. Just Jared lists Dior, Calvin Klein and Omega among the brands she has since worked with. She also went to the 2026 Met Gala with her mother, in a pink Dior couture gown, per The Express Tribune.

Of that first show, she recalled thinking she knew a lot about modelling and finding out she knew nothing. "There's so much work behind a show, so many people, so much organization," she said.

One detail from Page Six is lighter: she says she constantly borrows from her mother's wardrobe, and that half of her own now consists of Kidman's clothes.

## Her real ambition is directing

Filmmaking, and directing in particular, has interested her since childhood, she told Vogue Germany. On sets she would skip the downtime and watch the preview monitor instead. "I was always staring at the preview monitor because I was so captivated by the work," she said.

She named [Osgood Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osgood_Perkins) and [Emerald Fennell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_Fennell) among her favourite directors. Her favourite film starring her mother is [To Die For](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Die_For), according to Just Jared.

## What happens next

The November issue of Vogue Germany reaches newsstands on 24 October, and the full interview and photographs are on the magazine's site. None of the outlets above reports a film project for Sunday Rose yet. For now she is finishing school while her modelling work continues.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Sunday Rose Kidman-Urban?

She is the 18-year-old daughter of actress Nicole Kidman and country musician Keith Urban. She models and is in her final year of high school in Nashville.

### When did Sunday Rose start modelling?

She made her runway debut at Miu Miu's spring/summer 2025 show in October 2024, at 16. Her mother had asked her to wait until that age, according to Page Six.

### Does she want to act like her mother?

Not according to this interview. She says she is drawn to directing and filmmaking, and she names Osgood Perkins and Emerald Fennell as favourite directors.
