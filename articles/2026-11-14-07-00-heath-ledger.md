**Quick summary**

- In *10 Things I Hate About You*, [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger) sings "Can't Take My Eyes Off You" in the stadium scene, according to RouteNote.
- RouteNote reports the vocal is Ledger's own, and that he pushed for the song over other options.
- The original was recorded by [Frankie Valli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankie_Valli) in 1967 and reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100.
- Separately, El Cronista reports Ledger's 2003 will left nothing to his daughter Matilda, but his family gave her the estate, valued at more than $16.3 million.

Few romantic-comedy moments are as easy to picture as Heath Ledger singing from the bleachers in *10 Things I Hate About You*. A report published on 13 November by RouteNote confirms the song behind it, and a second report revisits what happened to his estate after his death at 28.

## Which song does Heath Ledger sing?

The track is "Can't Take My Eyes Off You". In the [1999 film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You), Ledger's character, Patrick Verona, sings it to win over Kat Stratford, played by [Julia Stiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Stiles). A school marching band backs him while security staff try to stop him, [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/radar/what-song-does-heath-ledger-sing-in-10-things-i-hate-about-you/) says.

The outlet reports the vocal heard on screen is Ledger's own. It describes the delivery as informal and playful rather than polished, which suits a character who is not trying to launch a singing career.

RouteNote also notes the stadium is Stadium High School in Tacoma, Washington. Its large concrete seating turns a private gesture into a very public one.

## Who recorded it first?

Frankie Valli released the song as a solo single in 1967, when he was already known as the frontman of The Four Seasons. According to RouteNote, it reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100. The song was written by [Bob Crewe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Crewe) and [Bob Gaudio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Gaudio).

Many artists have covered it since. RouteNote lists [Andy Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Williams), the disco group Boys Town Gang and [Lauryn Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauryn_Hill), whose late-1990s version it calls particularly recognisable.

One practical point for listeners: RouteNote says the film scene and Valli's original are different recordings, so searching for the Valli track will not give you Ledger's version.

## Did Ledger choose the song?

RouteNote says he did, at least in part. Accounts from the film's writers indicate other songs were considered, including "I Think I Love You" by The Partridge Family, but Ledger strongly favoured the Valli classic. The outlet adds that he also paid attention to how his character looked in the scene. These details come from RouteNote's reading of those accounts, and we have not independently confirmed them.

## What happened to Ledger's estate?

A separate report from [El Cronista](https://www.cronista.com/en/today/heath-ledgers-will-left-nothing-to-his-only-daughter-what-his-family-did-with-his-163-million-fortune/) looks at the legal side of his death. Ledger signed his will in April 2003, aged 24, before he met [Michelle Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Williams_(actress)) on the set of [Brokeback Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brokeback_Mountain). Their daughter, Matilda, was born in New York on 28 October 2005.

The will was never updated. It left everything to his parents, Kim Ledger and Sally Bell, and his three sisters, Kate, Olivia and Ashleigh. When he signed it, it listed about $118,000 in assets and cash. By the time he died, El Cronista says, his estate was estimated at more than $16.3 million.

Many expected a court fight, but El Cronista says it never came. Kim Ledger said in 2008 that the family had gifted everything to Matilda, and that there was never any question about it. The executor, Adelaide accountant Mark Dyson, never disclosed the amount.

## What about his last film?

Ledger was making *The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus* when he died. According to El Cronista, Johnny Depp, Jude Law and Colin Farrell each played a version of his character to finish it, and they reportedly donated their fees to Matilda. The film was released in 2009.

Neither report points to a new project or announcement. Both are retrospectives, and the stadium scene remains the easiest way for new viewers to find Ledger's early work. ## Frequently asked questions

### What song does Heath Ledger sing in 10 Things I Hate About You?

He sings "Can't Take My Eyes Off You", first recorded by Frankie Valli in 1967. RouteNote reports the vocal in the film is Ledger's own.

### Where was the stadium scene filmed?

At Stadium High School in Tacoma, Washington, according to RouteNote.

### Who inherited Heath Ledger's estate?

His 2003 will named his parents and sisters, but El Cronista reports the family gave the estate to his daughter, Matilda. Her father's family confirmed this by September 2008.
