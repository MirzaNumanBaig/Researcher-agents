**Quick summary**

- The "hero" baseball bat [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) used as Harley Quinn in *Suicide Squad* is being sold by Heritage Auctions.
- Bidding opens at $12,000, according to ComicBook.com.
- A similar screen-used bat sold at Prop Store in March 2025 for $63,000, against an estimate of up to $24,000.
- The sale comes while Robbie's future as Harley Quinn is described as uncertain.

The Margot Robbie Harley Quinn bat is now a collector's lot. Heritage Auctions is offering the prop used for close-up shots in the 2016 film *Suicide Squad*, with an opening bid of $12,000, [ComicBook.com reports](https://comicbook.com/collectibles/news/you-can-get-your-hands-on-margot-robbies-harley-quinn-baseball-bat-for-a-price-that-is-no-laughing-matter/). It is part of the auction house's latest memorabilia sale.

## What is being sold

The item is a "hero" bat, the version made for close-ups and used by Robbie in several key moments of the film. According to ComicBook.com, it carries the words "GOOD NIGHT" in red, along with several handwritten lines, and has a taped handle.

The $12,000 figure is only a starting bid. ComicBook.com expects it to climb well beyond that, and the comparison it offers explains why.

## Why the price could rise

In March 2025, Prop Store sold a similar screen-used Harley Quinn bat. Its estimate was up to $24,000. It sold for $63,000, roughly two and a half times the top of that range, according to the same report.

That result is the main evidence behind the outlet's view that this sale could match or beat $63,000. It is a forecast, not a result. Final prices are set by bidders on the day, and the listing's own opening figure is far lower.

The bat is also not the only DC piece on offer. ComicBook.com notes that the sale includes Batman and Superman items, among them Val Kilmer's Batsuit and Christian Bale's Bat Pod, which is expected to bring in around $1 million. Against that, the outlet calls Harley's bat the cheaper option.

## Robbie's run as Harley Quinn

Robbie first played Harley Quinn in *Suicide Squad*, directed by David Ayer. The film was often criticised, but ComicBook.com says it still earned more than $749 million worldwide, a total it says even *Superman* struggled to match in 2025. Robbie's performance led to a solo film, *Birds of Prey*, in 2020, which took $205 million despite the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. She returned as the character in [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn)'s *The Suicide Squad* in 2021.

ComicBook.com says Gunn had previously suggested he fully expected Robbie to play Quinn again. It adds that his departure from the DCU CEO role has left that in doubt. The report does not cite a confirmation either way, so her return should be treated as unsettled. Gunn and [Peter Safran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Safran) led the revamped DCU, per the same article.

For readers who want Harley memorabilia without the auction price, the outlet points out that replicas are widely sold at reasonable prices. They lack the screen-used provenance.

## What happens next

The bat will go to whoever wins the bidding in Heritage Auctions' sale. The report does not give a closing date, so check the auction house's listing for timing. Until then, the opening bid and the Prop Store result are the only price markers.
