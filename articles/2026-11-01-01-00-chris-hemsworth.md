**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) has reportedly left Paramount's G.I. Joe reboot, according to The Direct, which cites a Variety report.
- Bradley Cooper is said to be the name being considered for the role of Duke.
- Hemsworth posted on Instagram that his time in Thailand filming Extraction 3 was ending.
- A Singapore restaurant manager says he dined there with his children on 25 October.
- Netflix has not announced a release date for Extraction 3; a 2027 debut is expected, per What's On Netflix.

Chris Hemsworth has stepped away from one big action franchise just as he finishes work on another. The Australian actor has reportedly dropped out of Paramount's G.I. Joe reboot, while his Instagram suggests the Thailand leg of the Extraction 3 shoot is done.

## The G.I. Joe exit

The Direct reports that Hemsworth has left the project, in which he had previously been attached in a lead capacity. The outlet says the news comes from a Variety report that backed up an earlier exclusive from Nexus Point News. Neither Hemsworth nor Paramount has commented in the material we reviewed, so treat the exit as reported rather than confirmed.

According to the same account, Bradley Cooper is being eyed to play Duke. The film is described as a full reboot, separate from earlier live-action G.I. Joe films and from the abandoned Transformers crossover teased at the end of 2023's Transformers: Rise of the Beasts.

The Direct links the timing to ongoing chatter about Thor. Hemsworth has said before that another outing might need to "close the book" on the character, though he added that this was not based on any plans he had been given. The outlet also notes he said in February 2026 that Thor will return after Avengers: Doomsday. That puts him in line for Avengers: Secret Wars. How much of this is speculation is worth keeping in mind: the death-of-Thor idea rests on fan theory and an April 2025 claim from Alex Perez of The Cosmic Circus, not on anything Marvel has said.

## Extraction 3 finishes in Thailand

The better-documented news is on Netflix's side. [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) reports that Hemsworth posted holiday photos with his two sons and wrote that he was "finishing up my time in Thailand", adding that the weeks of filming Extraction 3 had still left room for weekend adventures.

Digital Spy says the third film was announced after Extraction 2 arrived in June 2023, and that Hemsworth's return, with [Sam Hargrave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Hargrave) directing, was confirmed in April 2026. Filming began in July across Australia and Europe. We have covered the earlier stages, including the [Thailand wrap](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-wraps-thailand-stint-on-extraction-3/).

## Who is in the cast

Returning alongside Hemsworth as mercenary Tyler Rake are [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba) and [Golshifteh Farahani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golshifteh_Farahani). [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) joins as a newcomer. First-look images last month showed Hemsworth and Brosnan in a tense scene, with Brosnan grimacing and pressing his hand against glass.

Other additions include [Don Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Dong-seok) as a mercenary named Lee. [Joe Taslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Taslim), [Chi Lewis-Parry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_Lewis-Parry) and [Siena Agudong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siena_Agudong) are cast in roles that have not been disclosed. David Weil takes over screenwriting from [Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers), who stays on as a producer with Anthony Russo.

## A stop in Singapore

Hemsworth also turned up in Singapore. Mothership reports he was around the eastern side of the city on Sunday, 25 October, after his time in Thailand. Shoppers at Changi City Point recognised him and, it says, he posed for photos readily.

A manager at the Hunan restaurant Nong Geng Ji in Jewel Changi Airport told the site that he ate there with his three children before 6pm. Staff thought he looked like Thor, checked, and he confirmed it. The manager then asked for a photo, and two other staff members also took pictures.

## What happens next

Netflix has not given a release date for Extraction 3. What's On Netflix, quoted by Digital Spy, expects 2027. Any official word on G.I. Joe casting will likely come from Paramount. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has Chris Hemsworth left G.I. Joe?

The Direct reports that he has, citing Variety. No statement from Hemsworth or Paramount appears in the sources we reviewed, so it is not officially confirmed.

### Who could replace him as Duke?

The same reporting says Bradley Cooper is being eyed for the role. That is a report, not an announcement.

### Has Extraction 3 finished filming?

Not necessarily. Hemsworth's post suggests his Thailand work is over, but Digital Spy only says filming appears to have ended there.

### When is Extraction 3 coming out?

There is no official date. A 2027 release is expected, according to What's On Netflix.
