**Quick summary**

- Director Christopher Nolan joked that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) is "the most cowardly James Bond you've ever seen" at a West Hollywood screening of "The Odyssey" on Tuesday, according to Page Six.
- Amazon MGM Studios announced Pattinson as the new 007 on Monday. Denis Villeneuve will direct and Steven Knight is writing.
- Elizabeth Debicki, his "Tenet" co-star, told Variety she thinks he is a "perfect" choice.
- Filming could start as early as next summer, The Telegraph reported, as cited by Page Six.

Christopher Nolan has given the newest [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) a friendly ribbing. Speaking at a screening of "The Odyssey" in West Hollywood on Tuesday, the director said Pattinson "is the most cowardly James Bond you've ever seen", according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/18/entertainment/christopher-nolan-issues-withering-response-to-robert-pattinsons-james-bond-casting/). It was a joke, and it came two days after Amazon MGM Studios confirmed the casting.

## What Nolan said about Pattinson and Bond

Nolan, 56, was on stage in a Q&A with Steven Spielberg, 79. The punchline came from the set of "The Odyssey". Nolan said Pattinson was content to sit in a corner and watch co-stars Matt Damon and Tom Holland handle their fight scenes, "refusing to get involved" in the way only he could.

Spielberg praised the late fight between Pattinson's character, Antinous, and Damon's Odysseus. He then quipped that he wondered whether Nolan's film had influenced the Bond casting, Page Six reported, citing People.

Nolan and Pattinson have worked together twice, on 2020's "Tenet" and now "The Odyssey". Pattinson told "Today" in July that he first hesitated over the second film because he gets nervous when asked to do something outright. He added that he would say yes to Nolan again.

## Colleagues line up behind the casting

The Nolan joke was not the only reaction. Elizabeth Debicki, who starred in "Tenet" with Pattinson, told Variety at the Los Angeles premiere of "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth" that she was "so happy for Rob". She called him an enigma who is funny, smart and has made interesting choices, according to a [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/hollywood/elizabeth-debicki-says-tenet-co-star-robert-pattinson-perfect-choice-for-james-bond-101794982356712.html) report of an agency feed.

Pattinson said in his own statement that being trusted with 007 is "the honor of a lifetime". Villeneuve described him as having the "timeless soul of 007", the same report said. Pattinson is 40 and succeeds Daniel Craig, who last played Bond in 2021's "No Time to Die".

## How the choice was made

Jeff Bezos has said he was "heavily involved". He told a Semafor conference that five finalists did three screen tests each with full costume, real sets and other actors, according to the [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/movies/story/2026-11-17/jeff-bezos-heavily-involved-in-casting-robert-pattinson-as-james-bond). The others were Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Nicholas Hoult. Pattinson was the oldest at 40, while the rest were 36. Bezos said the team had first leaned towards someone less famous, but the tests made the answer "so obvious". Our earlier report covers [Bezos on the screen tests](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-casting-bezos-screen-tests/) in more detail.

## The film slate before Bond

Pattinson has a busy run before 007 arrives, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/robert-pattinson-upcoming-movies-release-dates/):

- "Dune: Part Three" opens on 18 December 2026, with Pattinson as the Face Dancer Scytale. It is his first film with Villeneuve.
- "The Take" arrives on Netflix and in limited cinemas on 15 January 2027. Pattinson plays a former soldier recruited by a thief played by Denzel Washington.
- "The Batman Part II" is currently dated for 18 February 2028, though ScreenRant notes that further delays are possible.

On Batman, Page Six reports that filming paused briefly while director Matt Reeves dealt with a family matter. It also cites The Sun saying Pattinson will leave the franchise before a third film to concentrate on Bond. That is a single-outlet report and has not been confirmed by the studio.

## Who might play opposite him

Speculation has already turned to the Bond girl. Variety listed 19 possible actresses, and [Vietnam.vn](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/5-bond-girl-co-the-sat-canh-cung-robert-pattinson) picked out Inde Navarrette, Margaret Qualley, Margot Robbie, Sydney Sweeney and Zendaya. Nothing is confirmed. For background, see our guide to the [Bond girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_girl) tradition, and all our coverage on the [Robert Pattinson tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).

## What happens next

Villeneuve's film is produced by Amy Pascal and David Heyman, with Tanya Lapointe as executive producer. The Telegraph says cameras could roll next summer, so a release is still some way off. For now, the next time Australian audiences see Pattinson on screen will be in "Dune: Part Three" in December.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is the new James Bond?
Robert Pattinson. Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting on Monday. He succeeds Daniel Craig.

### Who is directing the next Bond film?
Denis Villeneuve, who also directed Pattinson in "Dune: Part Three". Steven Knight is writing the screenplay.

### When does filming start?
No date is official. The Telegraph reported that production could begin as early as next summer.

### Is Pattinson leaving Batman?
The Sun reports that he will exit before a third film. Neither Warner Bros. nor Pattinson has confirmed this in the sources we read.
