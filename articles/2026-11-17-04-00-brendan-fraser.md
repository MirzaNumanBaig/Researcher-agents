**Quick summary**

- [Brendan Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Fraser) is confirmed to return as Rick O'Connell in a fourth *Mummy* film from Universal Pictures.
- Rachel Weisz is also back as Evelyn O'Connell, according to Variety.
- Universal has dated the film for a wide cinema release on 19 May 2028.
- Radio Silence, the directing duo behind *Ready or Not*, are named as directors by Variety. Plot details have not been shared.

## Brendan Fraser Is Back for The Mummy 4

Brendan Fraser is officially returning to the desert adventure that made him a household name. Universal Pictures has set *The Mummy 4* for a wide theatrical release on 19 May 2028, with Fraser reprising Rick O'Connell and Rachel Weisz returning as Evelyn, [Variety Australia reported](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/the-mummy-4-release-date-may-2028-brendan-fraser-rachel-weisz-32961/).

Their involvement was first reported in autumn 2025. The film-news site Film-News.co.uk says the pair have now closed deals to reprise their roles. Fraser is also an executive producer, so he has a hand in the film behind the camera as well as in front of it.

## Who Is Making the Film

According to Variety, the film will be directed by Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett of Radio Silence, the pair behind *Ready or Not* and its sequel *Ready or Not 2: Here I Come*. The screenplay is credited to David Coggeshall, whose earlier work includes *The Family Plan* and *Orphan: First Kill*.

Not every outlet names the directors. Film-News.co.uk does not mention them, so treat the Radio Silence detail as Variety's reporting.

The producing team mixes old and new. Variety says Sean Daniel, who has produced every instalment since the 1999 original, returns. He is joined by William Sherak, James Vanderbilt and Paul Neinstein of Project X Entertainment. Fraser's executive producing partners are Jason F. Brown and Denis Stewart of Hivemind.

## Why Weisz's Return Matters

The series has grossed US$1.8 billion, according to Variety, which gives the studio good reason to revive it. Weisz skipped the third film, *The Mummy: Tomb of the Dragon Emperor* (2008), where Maria Bello played Evelyn. Her return restores the original pairing at the heart of the first two films.

That absence is the most obvious gap fans noticed in the last chapter, and bringing Weisz back answers it directly. Neither outlet says how the story will handle the years since 2008.

## What We Do Not Know Yet

The studio has kept the story under wraps. Film-News.co.uk states plainly that plot details have not been shared, and Variety notes the same secrecy. Neither report confirms any other returning cast, so claims about further reunions remain unconfirmed.
