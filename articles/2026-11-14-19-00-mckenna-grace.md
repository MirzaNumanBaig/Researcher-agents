**Quick summary**

- Mckenna Grace plays Daphne Blake in Netflix's live-action series Scooby-Doo: Origins, due in 2027, according to The Mirror US.
- She told the outlet on 10 November that everyone was "perfectly cast" and that she kept Daphne as true to the character as she could.
- Grace has not yet seen the finished series.
- Her latest film, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, opens on 20 November.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) says she is proud of her live-action Daphne Blake, even though she hasn't watched the Netflix series yet. Speaking to The Mirror US at a Raising Cane's event in Hollywood on Tuesday 10 November, the 20-year-old said her co-stars were "perfectly cast", and that keeping Daphne recognisable was her priority.

## What did Mckenna Grace say about Daphne?

Grace was cautious about spoilers. She told [The Mirror US](https://www.themirror.com/entertainment/celebrity-news/mckenna-grace-scooby-doo-daphne-2107969) she "can't say nothing" about the coming season or this version of the character. What she would say was that, as a Scooby-Doo fan, she is "very proud of what we did".

She also described the balancing act. She tried to keep Daphne "as true to Daphne as I could", she said, but the show is an origin story, so viewers meet younger versions of the characters before they become the mystery-solving team. "Everybody was perfectly cast for their role, that's my favorite part," she added.

The role came with a visible change. The Mirror reports she dyed her natural blonde hair red for it.

## Who else is in the cast?

Scooby-Doo: Origins reimagines the teenagers and their dog as they take on the first case that makes them Mystery Incorporated. According to The Mirror, Tanner Hagen plays Shaggy Rogers, [Abby Ryder Fortson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abby_Ryder_Fortson) is Velma Dinkley and [Maxwell Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Jenkins) is Fred Jones.

Grace said Hagen and Jenkins came to one of the Hunger Games premieres the night before the interview, which she called "really cute". That is the closest she came to seeing any of the show: she said she hasn't watched it herself.

Behind the camera, the outlet lists [Greg Berlanti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Berlanti), [Sarah Schechter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Schechter_(producer)) and Leigh London Redman, through [Berlanti Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlanti_Productions), as executive producers. [Jeff Pinkner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Pinkner), Andre Nemec and Adrienne Erickson are executive producers through Midnight Radio. Toby Haynes, another executive producer, directed the first episode. The characters were created by Joe Ruby and Ken Spears, with designs by Iwao Takamoto, for Hanna-Barbera.

This isn't Grace's first time as Daphne. She voiced a young version in the 2020 animated film Scoob! and in the shelved sequel Scoob! Holiday Haunt, The Mirror notes.

## Dream roles and a Hunger Games press tour

Asked about dream parts, Grace said she has been lucky to work on many recently. She named two she'd still like: a dark gothic period piece, or the opposite, a character like Giselle in Enchanted, a film she said shaped who she is.

Her schedule is busy. She plays in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which The Mirror says comes out on 20 November, and the press tour has become a fashion story of its own. Reality Tea, via [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/mckenna-grace-blooms-la-fuori-072705000.html), reports she wore La Fuori's Floral Arbor Beaded Mini Dress, styled by Molly Dickson. It has a high halter neckline and tiny blue, white, yellow and pink beaded flowers, and she paired it with white heels and a low ponytail.

Reality Tea also points back to her black floral-embroidered look from Cultured Magazine's Artists' Dinner. For more of her looks, see our piece on [her Hunger Games tribute tour outfits](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-tribute-looks/).
