**Quick summary**

- The MobLand Season 2 finale premieres on Paramount+ on November 20, according to ComingSoon.net.
- [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) leads the Harrigan family cast, alongside Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren.
- The second season is described as the family's toughest test yet, torn apart from within and hunted from without.
- Yellowjackets Season 4 also arrives on November 20, and a new true-crime documentary lands on November 17.

The MobLand Season 2 finale arrives on Paramount+ on November 20, closing out a season that has kept [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) and the Harrigan crime family under pressure from every direction. The date comes from [ComingSoon.net's rundown](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/news/2221468-new-paramount-releases-this-week-november-16-22) of the service's releases for November 16 to 22.

## What the finale promises

ComingSoon.net describes the season as the Harrigans' toughest test yet. The family is being torn apart from within while being hunted from without, which sets up a finale built on loyalty and survival rather than a clean victory. The outlet does not reveal plot details beyond that, and neither will we.

If you want to catch up on the wider conversation around the show, we have covered it before, including [Tom Hardy's MobLand smile and the Season 3 question](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3/) and [Pierce Brosnan's view of the script row](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-2-scripts/).

## Who is in the cast

The lead and core Harrigan family cast features Hardy, [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren), [Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine) and [Anson Boon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anson_Boon). The ensemble is completed by [Lara Pulver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Pulver), [Mandeep Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeep_Dhillon), [Joanne Froggatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Froggatt) and [Teddie Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddie_Allen), per the same report.

That is a deep bench for a single family drama. It also explains why the finale is likely to be one of the busier viewing nights of the week for the streamer.

## A crowded week on Paramount+

MobLand is not alone on November 20. Yellowjackets Season 4 premieres the same day and is billed as the end of the dual-era psychological saga, following the teenage survivors in the 1990s as they fight for rescue and face a troubled return to society. The Antler Queen mystery grows over that return, according to ComingSoon.net.

A day earlier, on November 17, the service releases a three-episode true-crime documentary called He Said, She Said: Till Death Do Us Part. It covers the split between New York lawyers Allen and Catherine Kassenoff, which began as a bitter divorce and became a custody fight.

## What happens next

The finale streams on Paramount+ on November 20. ComingSoon.net's report does not say whether a third season has been confirmed, so any talk of Season 3 should be treated as unconfirmed until the studio announces it.
