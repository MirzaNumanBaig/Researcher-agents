**Quick summary**

- *Jurassic World Rebirth*, starring Scarlett Johansson, is due to be removed from Netflix on 1 December, according to MovieWeb.
- Sunday 30 November is the last day to stream it there; Netflix is currently the only streaming home for the film.
- The film grossed $869 million worldwide, despite a 50% Rotten Tomatoes score.
- No new streaming home has been announced, and a sequel is in development with no release date.

[Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson)'s dinosaur blockbuster has a short window left on Netflix. The Jurassic World Rebirth Netflix run ends on 30 November, with the title leaving the service on 1 December, [MovieWeb reports](https://movieweb.com/jurassic-world-rebirth-leaving-streaming-netflix-november-2026/). The report does not say which country's catalogue it refers to, so Australian viewers should check their own Netflix listing.

## Jurassic World Rebirth Netflix exit: the key dates

[Jurassic World Rebirth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World_Rebirth) arrived on Netflix in February 2026 after first appearing on Peacock, according to MovieWeb. It now sits on Netflix alone. The outlet says the streamer has not renewed its deal for the film.

That does not mean the film is gone for good. MovieWeb expects another platform to pick it up, but notes that streaming services usually publish their upcoming lists only at the very end of the month. Until those arrive, where it lands next is unknown. Anyone planning a rewatch has until the end of November.

## A big-budget role in a long-running franchise

Directed by [Gareth Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gareth_Edwards_(filmmaker)), the film is the seventh in the dinosaur series that began with Steven Spielberg's [Jurassic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_Park) and continued with the rebooted [Jurassic World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World) films. Johansson plays Zora Bennett, who leads a research team to the original island to collect dinosaur DNA that could lead to cures for serious diseases, as MovieWeb describes the plot.

It was her next big franchise role after finishing her run as [Natasha Romanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Widow_(Natasha_Romanova)) in the Marvel films, a move MovieWeb frames as typical of an actress who is comfortable with large properties. She also appeared in [Transformers One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers_One), the outlet notes.

## The numbers behind the film

Reviews were mixed. MovieWeb cites a 50% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), yet the film still earned $869 million worldwide. For context, MovieWeb points out that every film in the rebooted Jurassic World series has been a box office hit, which helps explain why Universal wants to continue.

## An older Johansson footnote from Scotland

A separate item shows how far her career stretches. The Cumnock Chronicle, in a 15-years-ago photo feature, recalls that she was seen in New Cumnock in Scotland in November 2011 shooting scenes for her science fiction horror film *Under the Skin*. It is a small piece of local history rather than new news, but a reminder of the smaller films she has mixed in with the blockbusters.

## What happens next

A sequel, unofficially called *Jurassic World Rebirth 2*, is in development, MovieWeb says. Johansson is expected to return, but no plot details, release date or further casting have been released. For now, the next confirmed date is 30 November, the last day to watch the first film on Netflix.
