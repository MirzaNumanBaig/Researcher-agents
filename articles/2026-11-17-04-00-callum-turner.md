**Quick summary**

- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) was widely reported as a finalist to play James Bond, but the role went to [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson).
- Amazon MGM announced Pattinson on Monday 16 November 2026, according to Entertainment Weekly.
- Turner, 36, reportedly screen tested in late October alongside four other actors.
- George Clooney and Pierce Brosnan had both publicly backed Turner in recent months.
- Turner never confirmed he was in the running. In June he said he knew "as much as you do".

Callum Turner's long run as a Bond favourite ended on Monday 16 November, when Amazon MGM named Robert Pattinson as the next 007. Turner was one of five actors reported to have screen tested for the part, and the 36-year-old had been tipped for years as a frontrunner to follow [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) in the [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) films.

## How the Callum Turner Bond chase played out

According to [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/everything-we-know-next-james-bond-search-12253697), the final group was Pattinson, Turner, [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), Josh O'Connor and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden). The outlet says all five reportedly recorded screen tests, citing The Hollywood Reporter. A spokesperson for Amazon MGM declined to comment on those reports.

Turner's name had circulated well before that. Entertainment Weekly says multiple outlets reported he screen tested in late October. Feminegra notes that Variety reported on 9 November that Pattinson and Hoult had also completed tests, which put two late challengers into a race Turner, Lowden and O'Connor had dominated for weeks.

Pattinson's late entry was a surprise to many. Tyla reports he is said to have auditioned during a break in filming The Batman: Part II.

## The famous names in Turner's corner

Turner never said he wanted the job. Entertainment Weekly recounts that when The Hollywood Reporter asked him in June, he answered that he knew as much as the interviewer did, and declined to comment on whether he was interested.

Others were more open. George Clooney, who directed Turner in 2024's The Boys in the Boat, told the same outlet he hoped Turner would be the next Bond, calling him tall, handsome, charming and British. Pierce Brosnan, a former 007, praised Turner in a Vanity Fair interview, saying he had worked with him and found him "so strong".

Turner also has a link to the producing side. Entertainment Weekly notes he previously worked with David Heyman, a producer on the new film, on the Fantastic Beasts sequels and the Peacock series The Capture.

## Why Pattinson won

The Hollywood Reporter called the casting the smart but safe choice. Its argument was that Pattinson arrives on a hot streak, with The Drama, The Odyssey and the A24 release Primetime behind him, and Dune: Part Three still to come. He has also already worked with [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who will direct the 26th Bond film from a script by [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight).

The trade also noted that at 40, Pattinson is older than the 36-year-old Turner, Lowden, O'Connor and Hoult, and three years older than Craig was when he was cast. It judged that Turner was probably the best fit on the traditional leading-man side of the job.

Not everyone is convinced. Tyla reports a debate among fans over whether casting an established star breaks the franchise's old preference for an unknown. Pattinson called the role "the honor of a lifetime", and Villeneuve thanked everyone who auditioned, saying there can be only one 007.

## What happens next

Pattinson becomes the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series, and the first under Amazon MGM's full creative control, according to Entertainment Weekly. No release date has been given in the sources we reviewed.

For Turner, nothing in the reports suggests a setback beyond the missed role. Feminegra points out that missing out does not close the door on the franchise, since actors who lose the lead have returned in other parts before. That is the outlet's view rather than a report from the studio.
