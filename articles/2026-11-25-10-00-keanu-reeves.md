**Quick summary**

- Mercedes posted a short comedy clip during the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend in which [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) plays a movie villain in team principal Toto Wolff's office.
- Reeves nearly tipped backwards in the chair, and Wolff was seen laughing in the doorway, according to Motorsport.com.
- Only two Reeves films have firm US release dates: Sonic the Hedgehog 4 on 19 March 2027 and Shiver on 13 August 2027, according to Startefacts.
- A longer list of projects is in development, including an animated John Wick prequel and John Wick 5.

[Keanu Reeves](https://moduscarry.com/tag/keanu-reeves/) spent the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend doing two things at once: acting the villain in a Mercedes social clip and shaping a film calendar that now runs well into 2027. The clip, posted by the team during the race weekend, shows him spinning around in Toto Wolff's hospitality office chair and nearly losing the scene to gravity, according to [Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/culture/news/keanu-reeves-nearly-tips-over-in-toto-wolffs-office-chair-during-viral-mercedes-las-vegas-gp-skit/10877389/).

## What happens in the Mercedes clip

The video carries the caption "POV: You're the villain, but the chair leaned way more than you expected." It opens with Reeves, in a black beanie, scarf and dark jacket, facing away from the desk. He turns slowly with his fingers steepled and delivers a drawled "Well, well, well."

Then the chair gives way more than he expected. Reeves leans back to sell the entrance, flails his arms for a moment to avoid going over, and pulls himself upright with a glance off-camera. The camera then swings to the doorway, where Wolff, wrapped in a full-length black Mercedes team coat, is peeking around the frame and laughing.

It is a small bit of fun, but it landed at a useful moment for the team. Motorsport.com reports that Saturday night's 50-lap race was chaotic for Mercedes: late mistakes dropped newly crowned 2026 world champion Kimi Antonelli to fourth, and George Russell finished ninth after a brush with the barrier on lap 48. Both 2026 championships were already decided ahead of the Qatar and Abu Dhabi rounds, so Wolff had reason to smile regardless.

## Why Reeves keeps turning up in the F1 paddock

Reeves is 62, and his links to Formula 1 go beyond the occasional guest appearance. After the race he drove a limousine carrying winner Max Verstappen and McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri to their post-race interviews and the podium celebrations at the Bellagio, as Motorsport.com describes it. We covered that [limousine ride down the Las Vegas Strip](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-limousine-las-vegas/) separately.

The Mercedes setting also carries some history. Reeves hosted the 2023 Disney+ docuseries Brawn: the Impossible Formula 1 Story, which tells how Brawn GP won the 2009 title before becoming Mercedes' works team in 2010. This season he is back in the paddock fronting a new documentary series on Cadillac's first Formula 1 campaign. He also made his professional racing debut in the Toyota GR Cup at Indianapolis Motor Speedway in 2024.

## The two Keanu Reeves films with release dates

Away from the track, Startefacts lists the Reeves projects as of 23 November 2026, and only two have firm US dates.

- **Sonic the Hedgehog 4**, 19 March 2027. Reeves returns as the voice of Shadow. Ben Schwartz voices Sonic, Idris Elba is Knuckles, and Kristen Bell joins as Amy Rose. Paramount attached the date to its title reveal.
- **Shiver**, 13 August 2027. Reeves plays a smuggler stuck in a time loop at sea, with pirates and sharks along the way. Tim Miller, who directed Deadpool, is behind the Warner Bros. sci-fi thriller.

A third film, The Entertainment System Is Down, has finished a 76-day shoot in Budapest. The British Council lists it for 2027. Reeves, Kirsten Dunst and Daniel Brühl play passengers on a flight where the seatback screens fail. Director Ruben Östlund told Nordisk Film & TV Fond he is "very curious about the audience reaction", adding that "the distributors are nervous."

## What else is in development

The rest of the list has no dates yet, according to Startefacts:

- An untitled animated John Wick prequel, which Lionsgate approved in November 2026, with Reeves voicing John and Shannon Tindle directing.
- John Wick 5, announced by Lionsgate in April 2025 with director Chad Stahelski. It remains in development. Weeks earlier, Reeves told Extra, "the character's dead."
- Hidari, a Japanese stop-motion feature in which he would voice a woodcarver who builds a mechanical arm.
- Maelstrom, a hurricane thriller from director Jaume Collet-Serra for Netflix.
- Kill Current, in which he is set to star and produce with Alexandra Grant. The May 2027 date attached to it is the publication date of Oliver Lowe's novel, not a film release.
- BRZRKR, Netflix's planned adaptation of the comic he co-created.
- An untitled thriller at Amazon MGM with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock), which would be their third film together after Speed and The Lake House.

Two further titles are less certain. Director Francis Lawrence said in November 2026 that he has "renewed hope" for Constantine 2, though it still needs a green light. Readers can follow that story in our report on [Constantine 2 after the DC shake-up](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-constantine-2-dc-shake-up/). Reeves is also in talks for a Universal Lego film with Josh Cooley, who directed him as Duke Caboom in Toy Story 4, and Variety reported in October that he was in talks for James Wan's remake of The Gangster, the Cop, the Devil.
