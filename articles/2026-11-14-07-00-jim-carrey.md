**Quick summary**

- [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey) starred in the 2009 motion-capture film A Christmas Carol, which Far Out Magazine says grossed $325 million.
- Its budget was reported to be as high as $200 million, and the film was estimated to have lost around $100 million.
- ImageMovers Digital, the studio behind it, closed and laid off all 450 staff, according to Far Out.
- Carrey called the Scrooge role "a dream come true" when production began in October 2008.

A decade and a half on, Jim Carrey A Christmas Carol is back in the conversation. In a retrospective published on 13 November, [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/jim-carrey-played-role-dream-come-true-lost-100-million-killed-studio/) revisits the 2009 film as a role the actor loved that ended in a costly flop and the end of the studio that made it.

## A role Jim Carrey called a dream

Production on the film began in October 2008. Carrey played Ebenezer Scrooge and also the ghosts of Christmas past, present and yet to come, four parts in one picture, all performed in a motion-capture suit.

"This role was a dream come true," Carrey said at the time, as quoted by Far Out. He described Scrooge as challenging "in every possible way" and said he could not have been more proud of the movie. It was his first experience of motion capture.

Far Out says the parts let him channel his idol, [Peter Sellers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sellers), and calls his work one of the film's highlights.

## The numbers behind the loss

On paper, $325 million at the global box office sounds like a success. Far Out points out why it was not. The budget was reported to be as high as $200 million, and that figure does not include marketing, distribution and theatrical costs. When the film left cinemas after opening in November 2009, the outlet says, it was estimated to have lost about $100 million.

That is a reported estimate rather than an audited figure.

## Zemeckis and the studio that did not last

The film was directed by [Robert Zemeckis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Zemeckis), who founded ImageMovers Digital. Far Out describes his run of performance-capture films as a short-lived obsession and says that, beyond The Polar Express being rewatched each year, little good came of it. That is the outlet's opinion, not a settled verdict.

What is factual is the studio's fate. A Christmas Carol was the first feature from ImageMovers Digital. Its second, [Mars Needs Moms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mars_Needs_Moms), arrived in March 2011 and lost another nine-figure sum, according to Far Out. By then the company had already stopped operating, shutting down completely and laying off all 450 employees a year before that film came out.

Far Out puts the timeline bluntly: less than 18 months after Carrey's first day on an empty stage as Scrooge, the studio no longer existed. Carrey did not cause that outcome. The outlet is clear that he was not to blame for the film's performance.

## Carrey's wider legacy

The flop sits oddly against the rest of his career. A separate [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/jim-carrey-movies-everyone-watch-130000051.html) list of his films worth watching points to The Truman Show, The Majestic, Me, Myself and Irene, [Liar Liar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liar_Liar) and Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind, which it calls his tour de force. It describes an actor who built his name on comedy and then took real risks in drama.

A fresh anecdote from the same week shows how far back his star power goes. Speaking to Scoop B Radio, [Tommy Davidson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Davidson) recalled that he and Carrey came up together on [In Living Color](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Living_Color). During the Ace Ventura: When Nature Calls shoot, Davidson said, Carrey asked for a bulldozer so a dirt-burial scene could be finished before Davidson's flight. Davidson says the two are still close friends.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Jim Carrey's A Christmas Carol lose?

Far Out Magazine says the 2009 film was estimated to have lost around $100 million, despite grossing about $325 million, because the budget was reported at up to $200 million before marketing and distribution costs.

### Which studio made the film, and what happened to it?

ImageMovers Digital, founded by Robert Zemeckis. According to Far Out, it shut down and laid off 450 staff within about 18 months of production starting.

### Who did Jim Carrey play in A Christmas Carol?

He played Ebenezer Scrooge and the three ghosts of Christmas past, present and yet to come, using motion capture.
