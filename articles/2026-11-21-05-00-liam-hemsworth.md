**Quick summary**

- [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) is not in the new Hunger Games prequel. The Liam Hemsworth Hunger Games question is answered in *Sunrise on the Reaping*, according to JustWatch, which has seen the film.
- The film is a prequel set 24 years before the first story, so his character, Gale Hawthorne, has not been born yet.
- On 19 November, [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) placed Hemsworth second in a ranking of her on-screen kisses, Us Weekly reports.
- AOL reports that his former wife, [Miley Cyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miley_Cyrus), will remarry this weekend. The report cites unnamed sources.

Fans hoping for a Liam Hemsworth Hunger Games cameo in the new prequel will be disappointed. Gale is not in *Sunrise on the Reaping*, and the reason is built into the story, according to [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/uk/liam-hemsworth-sunrise-on-the-reaping). His name has also come up this week for two other reasons, one about the franchise and one about his past.

## Why Liam Hemsworth is not in Sunrise on the Reaping

At the New York premiere, several generations of the franchise gathered on the red carpet. JustWatch lists new stars [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) and [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), *Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes* leads [Rachel Zegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zegler) and [Tom Blyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Blyth), and original cast members Lawrence, [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson), [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks) and [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson). Hemsworth was the notable absence.

The guide confirms that his absence is not a ploy. The new film is set 24 years before the first *Hunger Games* story, so Gale does not exist yet. By the end of the original series, JustWatch notes, Gale has moved to District 2 and works for Panem's new government, which puts him even further from the district where this story ends.

## Who does return

The film follows [Haymitch Abernathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) through his own Games, the 50th anniversary and Second Quarter Quell, which called for double the usual number of tributes. According to JustWatch, a closing sequence brings back [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) and Peeta in a short scene with Haymitch, played again by Lawrence, Hutcherson and Harrelson. That scene works as an extra ending to the original run, the guide says.

## Lawrence ranks the on-screen kisses

Hemsworth did get a mention elsewhere. Appearing on *Watch What Happens Live With Andy Cohen* on Thursday 19 November, Lawrence, 36, ranked her screen kisses, Us Weekly reports. Hutcherson, 34, who was also a guest, came first. Hemsworth, 36, took second place, ahead of Bradley Cooper and Timothée Chalamet. [Chris Pratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Pratt), her *Passengers* co-star, came last of the five.

Hemsworth has joked about the kissing scenes before. In a 2014 appearance on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon*, he said Lawrence would deliberately eat garlic or tuna before takes, per Us Weekly. He called her one of his best friends.

## Miley Cyrus wedding reports

Hemsworth's former wife is back in the headlines. AOL reports that Cyrus and her fiancé, Maxx Morando, will marry this weekend in Southern California. The claim comes from sources with direct knowledge of the plans, and neither of them has confirmed it publicly.

AOL notes that Cyrus and Hemsworth married in December 2018, announced their separation in August 2019 and finalised the divorce in January 2020. BollywoodShaadis, which revisited the split on Friday, quotes Cyrus telling Howard Stern that "there was too much conflict" and that she still loves Hemsworth. Hemsworth's own statement at the time wished her "nothing but health and happiness". Neither has added anything new this week.
