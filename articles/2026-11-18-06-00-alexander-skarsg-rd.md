**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) wore a layered [Tom Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Ford) look by [Haider Ackermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haider_Ackermann) to the Governors Awards in Los Angeles on Sunday.
- The outfit paired a shimmering low-cut shirt and a crystal-embroidered waistcoat with a black wool double-breasted jacket.
- CNN named it its "Look of the Week" and described it as a lesson in the going-out top.
- The stylist behind it is Harry Lambert, who has dressed Skarsgård through his recent run of playful appearances.

Alexander Skarsgård turned the Governors Awards in Los Angeles into a fashion moment on Sunday, arriving in a head-to-toe Tom Ford by Haider Ackermann ensemble that CNN has now picked as its Look of the Week.

## What Alexander Skarsgård wore from Tom Ford

According to [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/17/style/alexander-skarsgard-tom-ford-lotw), the outfit was built in layers. A shimmering, low-cut shirt sat under a waistcoat embroidered with crystals, and both were topped with a black wool double-breasted jacket decorated with a black orchid motif.

CNN's style reporter Rachel Tashjian framed the look as the latest step in what the network calls a winning streak of playful red carpet and press appearances for the *Wicker* star. She wrote that his recent outfits have been lessons in how to cheekily honour a co-star, be baby, or wear an unusual shoe.

## Why the going-out top is back

The piece uses the evening to explain a trend. CNN describes the going-out top as a staple of the 2000s: any sequined, zippered or embellished tank or halter that could be worn with trousers or a skirt for happy hour or a late night. It points to photos of [Paris Hilton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Hilton) and [Nicole Richie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Richie) from that era as the reference, and says the top worked best under a blazer for the move from office to club.

The style then went quiet for a long stretch as Gen Z dressed down to go out, or stayed home. CNN says it has been making a steady comeback, and that Skarsgård is carrying it forward. It calls his Governors Awards outfit dapper and elegant, and one of his more subtle options when set against the occasion he wore no trousers at all.

## The stylist behind the looks

CNN credits stylist Harry Lambert with the outfit. Lambert has also worked with [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles), [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and Josh O'Connor, whom CNN groups as male stars with a wider sense of what menswear can be.

The report says that since Lambert began working with Skarsgård, he has played with the actor's 6ft 4in frame, using thigh-high boots, halter tops and very short shorts. CNN ties the wardrobe to his recent film choices, from *Pillion* to *Wicker*. Readers who missed the film can catch up with our [Wicker review](https://moduscarry.com/wicker-review-alexander-skarsgard/), and our earlier piece on his [awards-season looks](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-wicker-tarzan-hbo-max/) covers the run so far.

CNN also notes that his career has leaned on unconventional roles since his breakout in [True Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Blood), and that his willingness to joke about masculinity is part of his appeal.
