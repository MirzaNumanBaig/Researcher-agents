**Quick summary**

- Eurovision Asia posted a teaser on its official social media account that has led to talk of an appearance by [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem).
- Organisers have not confirmed her involvement or what it would be, according to wheretowatch.tv.
- More details are expected on Tuesday, 27 October.
- The most likely role, the same outlet says, is as an interval act, since the contest songs are already set.

Fans of [Delta Goodrem](https://www.instagram.com/deltagoodrem/) have a new rumour to chew on. [Eurovision Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_Asia) released a short teaser video featuring the Australian singer, and organisers have promised more information on Tuesday, 27 October, according to [wheretowatch.tv](https://wheretowatch.tv/tv-news-2/delta-goodrem-eurovision-asia-teaser-appearance-rumours/26/10/).

## What the teaser shows

The video hints at the involvement of a world-famous artist, and the speculation has settled on Goodrem. Wheretowatch.tv reports that organisers have yet to say what her participation would be. The story was first reported by eurovisionfun.com, as cited by wheretowatch.tv, which we have not been able to read directly.

Because the competing songs have already been confirmed, wheretowatch.tv suggests the likeliest scenario is a performance as the interval act. That is the outlet's reading of the situation, not an announcement from Eurovision Asia or from Goodrem.

## Goodrem's history with Eurovision

The link to the contest is not new. In 2015 she co-wrote My Girls, performed by [Bella Paige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Paige) for Australia at the Junior Eurovision Song Contest. The entry finished eighth with 64 points.

Wheretowatch.tv then reports that Goodrem took the stage herself for Australia at the 2026 Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna with the song Eclipse. She placed fourth with 287 points. That result, if the report is accurate, gives her a profile with the contest's audience that few Australian acts have had.

## A busy season in Britain

The outlet also notes a packed 2026 for the singer, including an appearance at Radio 2 in the Park. It describes her as favourite to win this year's [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing). Her run on the show has been covered on this site, including [her first perfect 10 after a rib injury](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-scores-strictlys-first-10-after-rib-injury/) and [her return to the show](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-grateful-as-she-returns-to-strictly/).

Separately, wheretowatch.tv says it exclusively reported that BBC bosses are keen for Goodrem to appear in a special New Year's Eve programme on BBC One. Neither the broadcaster nor Goodrem has confirmed that. Treat it as an unconfirmed report.
