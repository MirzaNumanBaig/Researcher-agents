**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) plays Rogue in Marvel's untitled X-Men reboot, due in cinemas on 5 May 2028.
- She told Entertainment Tonight she will lean on the Southern women she knew growing up in Corpus Christi, Texas.
- Rogue is from Mississippi, and Navarrette says she wants to travel there to "do her justice".
- Jake Schreier directs the film, according to CinemaBlend and ComicBookMovie.

Inde Navarrette has explained how she intends to master Rogue's Southern accent before cameras roll on Marvel's X-Men reboot. Speaking to Entertainment Tonight, the *Obsession* actor said her answer starts at home in Texas and ends with a trip to Mississippi, according to [ComicBookMovie](https://comicbookmovie.com/x_men/x-men-star-inde-navarrette-reveals-how-shell-deliver-perfect-rogue-accent-in-mcu-reboot-a229848) and [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/inde-navarrette-dicusses-nailing-rogue-accent-x-men-reboot).

## Inde Navarrette's Rogue accent starts in Texas

Navarrette told the interviewer she spent most of her life in Texas, specifically Corpus Christi. She said the older Southern women around her there are "really my favorite", and that they will be her reference point for Rogue's voice.

That is a practical choice rather than an impersonation exercise. Rather than studying earlier screen versions, she is pointing to real people she has heard speak for years. CinemaBlend also notes that she is working from lived experience, which suggests a warmer, less theatrical take than a stock Southern accent.

## Why Mississippi matters

Rogue is from Mississippi, and Navarrette made a point of saying so. She told the interviewer she wants to go there, and that she really wants to "do her justice", as reported by both outlets.

Texas and Mississippi do not share one accent, which is likely why she singled out the second state. ComicBookMovie reports she plans to visit the character's home state as part of her preparation. No dates or details of that trip have been given.

## The history she is stepping into

Rogue's voice has been a talking point for decades. ComicBookMovie notes the character is known for a "Southern Belle" accent in the comics and in *X-Men: The Animated Series*. CinemaBlend recalls that Anna Paquin's Rogue in the first Fox *X-Men* film had a slight Southern accent that faded in later instalments.

Fans have been watching closely since the casting. CinemaBlend says viewers had guessed Navarrette might play Mystique, Kitty Pryde, Laura Kinney or Psylocke before Marvel announced her as Rogue. ComicBookMovie reports the announcement came at D23, and that she had earlier expressed interest in Mystique and met director Schreier.

## The film and the cast around her

The reboot is set for exclusive cinema release on 5 May 2028. According to ComicBookMovie, Jake Schreier, who made *Thunderbolts\**, is directing. The cast reported by the two outlets includes:

- Kit Connor as Scott Summers (Cyclops)
- Sadie Sink as Jean Grey
- Maya Boyd as Storm
- Christopher Abbott as Professor X
- Samara Weaving as Emma Frost
- Adam Driver as Nathaniel Milbury, an alias of Mister Sinister, per ComicBookMovie

ComicBookMovie also mentions unconfirmed rumours that Apocalypse could appear. Marvel has not confirmed that, so treat it as speculation.

Navarrette arrives with momentum. Both outlets credit her recent work in *Obsession*, and ComicBookMovie says that film has drawn Oscar buzz.
