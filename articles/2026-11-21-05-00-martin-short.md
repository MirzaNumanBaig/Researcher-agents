**Quick summary**

- [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) and [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) appeared on Jimmy Kimmel Live on Thursday 19 November (US time).
- Gomez described how she learned Short had cut into her wedding cake at her September 2025 wedding.
- Short first told the story on the same show in January, according to People.
- Season six of Only Murders in the Building, titled Only Murders in London, premieres on 8 December on Hulu and Disney+.

The Martin Short Selena Gomez cake story finally has the bride's side. Short has been asked about the wedding cake he cut too early for most of the year, and on Thursday Gomez got her turn. Selena Gomez told Jimmy Kimmel how she found out, and Short, 76, was sitting right next to her.

## The Martin Short Selena Gomez cake story, from the bride

Gomez married record producer [Benny Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Blanco) in Santa Barbara on 27 September 2025, according to People. Short, her [Only Murders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building) co-star, was a guest, along with [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) and [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd).

Gomez said Short "apparently insisted no one tell me". By the time she heard, the damage had been patched up, and she says she didn't mind. Then someone told her, "Marty cut your cake and I think he was eating it."

"So I run," she told Kimmel. "He looks like a little dog with his tail between his legs. And I go, 'I heard you ate my cake.'"

Gomez said she and Blanco wanted a small "Just Married" cake for themselves. She told Short, "We will always have that memory together," and thanked him for trying to salvage it, as quoted by [People](https://people.com/selena-gomez-recalls-moment-she-discovered-martin-short-cut-into-her-wedding-cake-before-it-was-served-12266915).

## How Short says it happened

Short's version, first told on Kimmel's show in January, hasn't changed. He assumed each seating section had its own small cake. Billboard reports that the sections held about 15 people each, and that Short called himself "blameless" this time.

Short offered Steve Martin a slice as Martin was getting ready to leave. He cut into it twice before people at the table shouted his name. "I tried to fix it with a fork," he said, according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/20/celebrity-news/selena-gomez-calls-out-martin-shorts-major-wedding-faux-pas-that-nearly-ruined-benny-blanco-nuptials/). That was roughly an hour before the cake was due to be presented.

He then asked Rudd whether the two of them should simply leave, and said Martin suggested telling the bride "in a month or so".

Kimmel put a photo of the cake on screen, and Gomez pointed to the fork marks, per [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/culture/tv-film/selena-gomez-martin-short-cut-her-wedding-cake-early-1236386422/). When Kimmel asked how many guests were at the wedding, Short guessed "more than 10, less than a thousand". Vogue, as cited by Page Six, put the figure at about 170 guests.

## A Friendsgiving left off the guest list

Short joked that the cake has cost him a seat at other events. Gomez recently hosted a Friendsgiving through [Rare Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rare_Beauty) for about 30 fans, with [Demi Lovato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Lovato) joining. According to People, Short said he wasn't invited because "she was afraid I'd start destroying the desserts in advance."

BroadwayWorld says the interview also covered Canadian Thanksgiving and the Netflix documentary about SCTV, Short's first major television job. Gomez admitted she hadn't known what SCTV was before the film.

## The Meryl Streep questions

Kimmel also steered the conversation to Meryl Streep, who returns as a guest star in the new season. The Daily Beast reports that Gomez teased Short about it and Kimmel kept returning to the subject.

Short has said before that he and Streep are not a couple. In January 2024 he told Bill Maher, "We're not a couple, we are just very close friends", according to The Daily Beast. Short brushed off the teasing on Thursday.

## What happens next

Only Murders in London, the sixth season, arrives on 8 December on Hulu and Disney+, per Billboard. It sends Charles, Oliver and Mabel to England. Billboard lists Olivia Colman, David Tennant, Nicola Coughlan, Martin Freeman, Jodie Whittaker, Simone Ashley, Richard E. Grant and Geri Halliwell-Horner among the new cast.

## Frequently asked questions

### What did Martin Short do at Selena Gomez's wedding?
He cut into what he thought was a table cake and was actually the wedding cake. He cut it twice before other guests stopped him, according to People and Billboard.

### When does Only Murders in the Building season six premiere?
It premieres on 8 December on Hulu and Disney+ as Only Murders in London, per People and Billboard.

### Did Selena Gomez mind about the cake?
She says no. Gomez told Kimmel she "didn't care" and was grateful Short tried to fix it, as reported by People.

Short has said they are just very close friends, according to The Daily Beast. No relationship has been confirmed.
