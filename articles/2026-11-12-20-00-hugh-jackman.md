**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) was photographed swimming and relaxing at Sydney's Bondi Beach with girlfriend Sutton Foster, according to Geo News.
- Jackman's longtime trainer and friend joined the pair for a swim and some beach games.
- Separately, Collider reports that 2013's *The Wolverine* is now among the top 10 most-streamed films on HBO Max.
- Geo News says Jackman is preparing to play Long John Silver in an upcoming *Treasure Island* film.

[Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hugh-jackman/) spent a sunny day at Bondi Beach this week, and the pictures have landed just as one of his older Wolverine films finds a new audience on streaming. The 58-year-old was seen taking a morning dip with [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster), according to [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/690735-hugh-jackman-shows-off-wolverine-worthy-physique-on-beach-date), and IMDb also ran a photo report of his swim.

## Hugh Jackman at Bondi Beach

Geo News describes the outing as a relaxed one. Jackman went into the water first, then spent time on the sand. Foster kept it casual in a T-shirt and biker shorts. The couple were seen tossing a frisbee, and Jackman's longtime trainer and friend Michael Ryan swam and spent time with them.

The report puts the visit within a Sydney getaway. One caution: Geo News gives the date as 20 November, although its story was published on 12 November, so the exact day of the swim is unclear. IMDb's own report was not readable for this article, so the details here rest on Geo News.

The pictures also fit a pattern. This site has already covered [Jackman showing Foster around his Bondi penthouse](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackman-bondi-penthouse/), and Sydney clearly remains a regular stop for them.

## How Jackman and Foster got here

According to Geo News, their relationship became public in late 2024 after they starred together in the Broadway revival of *The Music Man*. They made their red carpet debut as a couple in October 2025 and have since shared occasional glimpses of their life together. Neither has been quoted in the report about the beach day itself, so this is a photo story, not an announcement.

## The Wolverine finds a new audience on HBO Max

While the beach photos circulated, Collider reported that *The Wolverine* has climbed into the top 10 most-streamed films on HBO Max, 13 years after its release. The site argues the 2013 film does not get the credit it deserves.

Collider sets out the film's place in the franchise. Jackman was cast in 2000's *X-Men* as a replacement for first choice [Dougray Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dougray_Scott), and he has played the character across the main series and three solo films. *X-Men Origins: Wolverine* (2009) came first and *Logan* (2017) closed the story, with *The Wolverine* in the middle.

The film is set largely in Japan. It follows Logan in self-imposed exile, drawn to Tokyo by Yukio, played by [Tao Okamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tao_Okamoto), who leads him to the dying businessman Ichirō Yashida, played by [Haruhiko Yamanouchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haruhiko_Yamanouchi). Yashida, whom Logan once saved during the bombing of Hiroshima, offers him the chance to give up his healing power and live a mortal life.

Collider says the story draws on the 1982 *Wolverine* comic series by [Chris Claremont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Claremont) and [Frank Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Miller). It also notes the film grossed $416 million at the box office, which at the time made it the second most successful X-Men movie, just behind *X-Men: The Last Stand*. Collider's view is that the film is the first to sit with the weight of Logan's near-immortality, which makes the ending of *Logan*, with [Dafne Keen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dafne_Keen) as Laura, land harder.

## What happens next

The outlet adds that whether he will return as Wolverine remains uncertain, and nothing in the sources confirms a new appearance.
