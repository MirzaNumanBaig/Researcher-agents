**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, has lived on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, since 2019, according to Grazia.
- She has restored her grandparents' former seven-acre property in Ladysmith, which overlooks the coast.
- Her renovation was filmed for the HGTV Canada series *Pamela's Garden of Eden*, which aired in 2022 with a $750,000 budget.
- Grazia says she is also due back on the big screen in the new year.

Pamela Anderson has traded Malibu for a seven-acre garden on Vancouver Island, and her Instagram clips of rain-soaked roses and sunsets through the pines are the clearest view of it. Grazia USA [published a look at her home](https://graziamagazine.com/us/articles/pamela-anderson-59-left-hollywood-behind-to-restore-her-grandparents-7-acre-estate-inside-her-idyllic-new-life-on-vancouver-island/) on 14 November, describing a life far from Hollywood's pace. The actor, now 59, has been based there for seven years.

## Why Pamela Anderson Moved Home

Anderson settled on Vancouver Island in 2019, according to Grazia. Before that she spent two years in the south of France, and she left a Malibu home where she had lived for two decades. The island is her hometown, and the property sits in Ladysmith, British Columbia.

She explained the decision to Better Homes & Gardens, as quoted by Grazia. She said she "needed a change" and chose to move home and "disappear" into her garden. Building it, she said, felt like a metaphor for putting her life back together, and planting seeds made the smallest things feel profound.

That is a candid account from someone who spent decades in the public eye. It also sets up the project that followed.

## The Seven-Acre Property

The land belonged to her grandparents. Grazia reports that Anderson bought it from them more than two decades before her move, and that her family brought her there as a baby. She spent her childhood running barefoot there as what the magazine calls a "wild child".

The estate has three buildings:

- **The Roadhouse**, which her grandparents ran as a teahouse in the 1950s.
- **The Cabin**, one of the property's original nine holiday rental homes.
- **The Boathouse**, which became Anderson's own home.

Her renovation was the subject of *Pamela's Garden of Eden*, which HGTV Canada aired in 2022. Grazia says she set herself a $750,000 budget. The interiors it describes use white, sage green and soft pink walls, floral wallpaper, antiques and vintage wicker furniture.

## Baking, Gardening and Family

Grazia describes a quiet routine of gardening and baking. In 2024 Anderson released a plant-based cookbook, *I Love You: Recipes from the Heart*, inspired by her family's favourite recipes. The magazine adds that her interior designer, Francesca Albertazzi, has reportedly said she has never met anyone who enjoys laundry as much as Anderson does. That detail is a reported remark, not something Anderson has confirmed here.

Family is central to the move. She renovated one of the cabins for her parents, according to Grazia, bringing them back to the property where they lived when they first married so she could be close as they age. Her sons, Brandon, 28, and Dylan, 26, took their first steps there and, the magazine says, helped shape the gardens.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where does Pamela Anderson live now?

She lives in Ladysmith on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, her hometown. Grazia reports she has been there since 2019, after two years in the south of France.

### How big is Pamela Anderson's property?

It covers seven acres overlooking the coast and has three buildings: The Roadhouse, The Cabin and The Boathouse, which is her home.

It was an HGTV Canada series that aired in 2022. It followed the renovation of her family property, which Grazia says had a $750,000 budget.
