**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) hosted Muay Thai star Rodtang Jitmuangnon on the *Extraction 3* set in Ratchaburi, Thailand, according to MMA Sucka.
- Hemsworth thanked the fighter on Instagram for personalised gloves, saying they were headed "straight to the trophy room".
- British striker Ben Woolliss separately held pads for the actor in a gym session.
- Rodtang next fights Nong-O Hama at The Inner Circle 38 on Friday, 13 November.

Chris Hemsworth has spent part of his time in Thailand swapping the film set for the fight world. The Australian actor welcomed Muay Thai great Rodtang Jitmuangnon to the set of *Extraction 3* in Ratchaburi this past week, and trained with British striker Ben Woolliss, [MMA Sucka reported](https://mmasucka.com/news/one-championship-superstars-rodtang-and-ben-woolliss-meet-chris-hemsworth-in-thailand/).

## A set visit with a fighter Hemsworth admires

[Rodtang Jitmuangnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodtang_Jitmuangnon), a former ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Champion nicknamed "The Iron Man", visited Hemsworth while the third *Extraction* film is shooting. MMA Sucka describes Hemsworth, best known as Thor in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe), as currently in Thailand to make the third instalment of the action franchise.

Hemsworth shared the moment on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/chrishemsworth/). "Honoured to have Muay Thai legend Rodtang Jitmuangnon visit us on the Extraction 3 set today! All class, absolute champion. Thanks for the personalised gloves, straight to the trophy room!" he wrote, as quoted by MMA Sucka.

The two have a little history. According to the same report, Hemsworth sent Rodtang a video message of support last November, after a scheduled bout against Nong-O Hama was cancelled because the fighter was ill.

## Rodtang's reply

Rodtang answered with a video of his own, showing the pair hugging and laughing on set. "Thank you for the opportunity to meet you, my brother. I will always love and respect you," he said in the post, per MMA Sucka.

For context, the outlet lists Rodtang's career record at 274 wins and 44 losses overall, with a 17-3 record in ONE Championship. It calls him the most recognisable face in Muay Thai worldwide.

## Pad work with Ben Woolliss

The other half of the story happened in a gym. Woolliss, a 32-year-old British striker, stepped into the ring to hold pads for Hemsworth and run him through kicks, knees and combinations, MMA Sucka said.

"Pleasure to catch up and have a session with the bro @chrishemsworth. Throwing some power behind those shots!" Woolliss wrote, adding that the team around Hemsworth were "good people". Photos and videos from the session gave fans a look at the actor training under professional guidance. The report does not say whether the session was linked to preparation for the film.

## What happens next

Rodtang returns to competition soon. He is preparing for his quarterfinal against Nong-O Hama in the 2026-27 ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Grand Prix, set for The Inner Circle 38 on Friday, 13 November, according to MMA Sucka.
