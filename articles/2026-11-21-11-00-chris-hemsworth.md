**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) visited 17-year-old Livi Eddington in the Byron Bay area, bringing two of his children, according to the Ballina Times.
- Eddington was diagnosed with a rare form of brain cancer in January and now has radiation and chemotherapy in Brisbane.
- His GoFundMe page has passed $280,000 of a $300,000 target.
- The visit follows a break-in at Hemsworth's Broken Head home on 11 November, for which a 16-year-old has been charged.

Chris Hemsworth has made a personal visit to Byron Bay teenager Livi Eddington, who is fighting an aggressive form of brain cancer, according to a report from the [Ballina Times](https://timesnewsgroup.com.au/ballinatimes/news/chris-hemsworth-visits-byron-teen-battling-brain-cancer/) published on 20 November. The Thor actor, who lives nearby at Broken Head, brought two of his children along.

## What Hemsworth said to Livi

The Ballina Times reports that Hemsworth joked the 17-year-old had "agreed to join The Avengers" and that the pair would "take on the entire universe". He called him an "absolute legend".

He also said the two would go fishing together and catch "a massive carp". It is a light moment in what has been a long and hard year for the family.

## Livi's journey so far

Eddington was diagnosed with a rare form of brain cancer in January, the report says. He had experimental treatment in North Carolina in the United States and came home in October to rest and spend time with family.

Since then he has been receiving radiation and chemotherapy at Queensland Children's Hospital in Brisbane. The paper says he now uses a wheelchair and is struggling to talk and swallow. His family keeps posting daily updates.

Eddington has documented the experience on TikTok and Instagram under the name Livi's Journey. His videos have drawn millions of views worldwide, and that reach caught Hemsworth's attention, according to the report.

## Support from the wider community

Hemsworth is not the only visitor. The Ballina Times says comedy duo Tom and Kell Reese, known as The Reesebros, and NRL content creator Mitch Field have also stopped by. Field wrote online that he had met someone who leaves an impact without realising it and urged Eddington to keep fighting.

A GoFundMe campaign to cover the family's medical costs has raised more than $280,000 toward its $300,000 goal. People can find it by searching for Livi Eddington on GoFundMe.

## A tense fortnight at the Hemsworth home

The visit comes after a difficult few weeks at the family's property. Police were called to the Broken Head estate on the morning of 11 November after three vehicles were reported stolen, [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2228615-chris-hemsworth-home-burglary-update-arrest-made-details-here) reports, citing TMZ.

[Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky) was reportedly inside when the dogs began barking. All three vehicles were later found in nearby areas, including Coraki, Tabulam and Lennox Head, and no injuries were reported.

New South Wales Police say a 16-year-old was arrested after a search warrant was executed at a property in Coraki on 19 November. He faces seven charges and was refused bail in children's court, according to the same report.
