**Quick summary**

- [Hema Malini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hema_Malini) posted a positive review of Nitesh Tiwari's *Ramayana: Rise of Legend* on X on 10 November 2026, praising the casting and special effects.
- [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) plays Sita opposite [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) as Ram.
- NDTV reports that Lara Dutta has defended Pallavi's "Warrior Sita" amid backlash over the portrayal.
- The film is not without critics: actor [Nitish Bharadwaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitish_Bharadwaj) has withdrawn his earlier praise, according to Moneycontrol.

Sai Pallavi's take on Sita in Nitesh Tiwari's *Ramayana* is now at the centre of a public conversation in India, with a veteran star offering praise while another actor steps in to defend the performance against criticism. The latest round came this week, when Hema Malini shared her verdict on the film on 10 November and NDTV reported on Lara Dutta's defence of Pallavi's portrayal.

## What Hema Malini said about the film

According to [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/entertainment/hema-malini-reviews-nitesh-tiwari-s-ramayana-praises-ranbir-kapoor-sai-pallavi-s-casting-and-visual-effects-i-went-with-least-expectations-article-14076283.html), the 78-year-old actor and politician said she went in with "least expectations". Her reasoning was simple: the story is so familiar that even young children know it, so there is little room to surprise anyone.

She was won over anyway. In her post she described a "fabulous presentation" and special effects that left her "breathless with anticipation". She also singled out the "apt casting of the protagonists" and said she could see the audience being carried into another period. Her closing line called it "a completely unique experience for the Indian audience".

For Pallavi, an endorsement from one of Indian cinema's best-known names carries weight. It arrives while her version of Sita is being picked over online.

## The "Warrior Sita" debate

NDTV's headline says Lara Dutta told critics to "stop being so small-minded" as she defended what it calls Pallavi's "Warrior Sita" amid backlash around the film. NDTV's page could not be opened in full for this report, so the details of Dutta's remarks and of the objections she answered are not covered here.

The debate fits a pattern we have followed on the site, including how [Dhanush backed Sai Pallavi as Sita](https://moduscarry.com/sai-pallavi-sita-ramayana-dhanush/) and how [a sword-fighting Sita divided Ramayana fans](https://moduscarry.com/sai-pallavi-ramayana-sita/).

## A set that moved its lead to tears

A second NDTV piece, headlined "'Was A Bit Overwhelmed': When Sai Pallavi Cried On Ramayana Set", revisits the actor's emotional reaction during filming. Only the headline was available to us, so we are not describing the circumstances. It does suggest how much the role means to her.

## Not everyone is convinced

Moneycontrol notes that Nitish Bharadwaj, who is remembered for playing Krishna on television, withdrew his earlier praise and called the film "deeply disappointing" over the portrayal of Yash's Ravan. That is one reported view, and it is about the film's treatment of Ravan rather than Pallavi's Sita.

The same outlet also carries a report, which it describes only as a "report", that *Ramayana: Part 1* has been submitted for consideration at the 99th Academy Awards. We could not confirm that independently.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Sita in Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana?

Sai Pallavi plays Sita. Ranbir Kapoor plays Ram, [Ravie Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey) plays Lakshman and Yash plays Ravan, according to Moneycontrol.

### What did Hema Malini say about the film?

She wrote on X that she was "pleasantly surprised" by the presentation and special effects, and praised the casting of the lead roles.

### What is the film called internationally?

Moneycontrol says *Ramayana: Part 1* is internationally known as *Ramayana: Rise of Legend*.
