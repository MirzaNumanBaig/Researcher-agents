**Quick summary**

- In the Rebel Wilson The Deb dispute, AI Film and the co-producers have told a judge she should not be awarded costs, according to Lawyerly on 26 November 2026.
- They allege she gave false evidence and was in contempt. That is their argument, not a finding by the court.
- The NSW Supreme Court ruled on 23 October 2026 that Wilson did not sabotage the film and rejected almost all of AI Film's claim of about $11.9 million.
- The companies were ordered to pay Wilson's costs, and the fight is now over whether that order should stand.

[Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) is facing a new argument in the Rebel Wilson The Deb dispute, her long legal fight over the film. AI Film and its co-producers on the film have told a judge she should not receive her costs after winning the case, alleging she gave false evidence and was in contempt, [Lawyerly reported](https://www.lawyerly.com.au/ai-film-accuses-rebel-wilson-of-contempt-in-costs-spat/) on 26 November 2026.

The report is short and the full submissions are not public in the material available to us. Wilson's response was not included. The allegations are the producers' position only.

## What the court decided in the Rebel Wilson The Deb case

The costs argument follows a judgment in *AI Film Production Limited v Wilson*, handed down in the NSW Supreme Court on 23 October 2026, according to [The Lawyer Mag](https://www.thelawyermag.com/au/practice-areas/tmt-telecoms-media-technology/nsw-supreme-court-rejects-production-companys-119m-claim-against-rebel-wilson/595229). AI Film, which financed the film and co-owned it with Wilson's company Camp Sugar, had sought $11,861,507.04. It said Wilson's Instagram posts and anonymous websites made the film unprofitable.

Most of that failed. The court found only posts on the film's promotional account fell under consumer law, and most of those were not misleading or deceptive. The injurious falsehood claim failed, and so did the allegation that Wilson used her board seat to pressure the companies into selling her the film cheaply.

The court called the companies' "global loss" approach to damages "fundamentally misconceived". It also found their 2022 revenue forecast unreliable, because it assumed a $15 million budget when the final budget was $21.8 million.

## Where Wilson did not come out clean

The judgment was not a total win. The court found Wilson had the anonymous websites created and that most of their claims about one co-producer were false and malicious. It also found malice in an Instagram story that falsely suggested the co-producers stole money.

Wilson and Camp Sugar were also found to have committed technical breaches of confidentiality, publicity and professionalism clauses. The production subsidiary sought only nominal damages and was awarded $10, with the court saying the claim "ought not to have been brought".

## The money Wilson was awarded

On Wilson's cross-claim, the subsidiary must pay Camp Sugar the $359,000 balance of its producer fee, plus interest, [The Nightly reported](https://thenightly.com.au/australia/nsw/rebel-wilson-australian-actor-awarded-359000-after-winning-legal-battle-over-movie-the-deb-c-23060412). The court rejected the idea that payment could wait until AI Film recouped its outlay, finding that risk sat with AI Film.

The Nightly reported that *The Deb*, which had a reported budget of $20 million, took about $335,000 at the Australian box office after its April 2026 release. Both sides have traded accusations since 2024, including claims about the film's release and about conduct by the producers, which they dispute.

## The wider dispute

The Supreme Court case is one strand. In July 2026, Wilson was cleared of defaming actor Charlotte MacInnes in four social media posts, and MacInnes has said she will appeal, according to The Nightly. Separate defamation proceedings in the United States between the producers and Wilson were reported as ongoing.

## What happens next

A judge must now decide whether the costs order in Wilson's favour stands in light of the contempt and false evidence allegations. No hearing date or ruling was reported. Any appeal of the October judgment has not been reported either.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Rebel Wilson been found in contempt?

No. The contempt allegation comes from AI Film and its co-producers in their submissions on costs. No court finding has been reported.

### Who won the Supreme Court case over The Deb?

Wilson largely won. The court rejected almost all of AI Film's roughly $11.9 million claim and ordered the companies to pay her costs and her company's unpaid producer fee of $359,000.

### What is The Deb?

*The Deb* is a film Wilson co-produced and directed. It premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2024 and was released in Australia in April 2026, according to The Nightly.
