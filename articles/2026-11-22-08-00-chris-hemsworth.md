**Quick summary**

- A 16-year-old boy faces seven charges over the theft of three vehicles from the Broken Head home of [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and Elsa Pataky on 11 November.
- He was refused bail in children's court on 19 November, and his next court date is 27 November.
- Police are still looking for three other suspects. All three vehicles have been recovered.
- New Idea reports the family is shaken and that Hemsworth's Avengers: Doomsday promotional plans are affected. That account relies on an unnamed source.

A teenager has been charged over the Chris Hemsworth break-in near Byron Bay, while police continue to hunt for three other people. The 16-year-old is facing seven charges after three vehicles were taken from the actor's home at Broken Head on 11 November, according to [Thought Catalog](https://thoughtcatalog.com/jeremy-london/2026/11/police-charge-a-16-year-old-after-three-vehicles-were-stolen-from-chris-hemsworth-and-elsa-patakys-byron-bay-home/).

## What police say happened

Police allege four people entered the property before dawn. Officers were called at about 7:30am, and no injuries were reported. The three vehicles have since been found in Coraki, Tabulam and Lennox Head and seized for forensic examination.

The boy was arrested during a search of a home in Coraki. Police say they seized vehicle keys, electronics and clothing there. His charges include aggravated entry offences, being armed with intent to commit an indictable offence, taking a vehicle without consent and damaging property. The charges are allegations, and he has not been convicted of anything.

Thought Catalog reports he was refused bail when he appeared in children's court on 19 November. He is due back on 27 November. For earlier coverage, see our reports on the [teen charged over the Byron Bay break-in](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-byron-bay-break-in-charge/) and the [bail decision with three suspects still sought](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-home-burglary-arrest/).

## What is still unclear

Reports describing security footage say the group climbed a fence and reached a garage where keys had been left in the vehicles. They were reportedly wearing balaclavas, and two were said to be carrying knives. Police have not released the footage or confirmed those details, so they remain unverified.

It is also not settled who was home. Thought Catalog notes that accounts differ on whether Hemsworth was there, and that police have not said who was inside. It is unclear whether anyone entered the main house.

## A magazine's account of the family

New Idea, an Australian weekly, says Hemsworth and Pataky and their children were all at the property. It reports that Pataky was woken by the family's dogs at around 2am and went back to sleep, then learned of the intruders the next morning from CCTV.

The magazine quotes an unnamed source saying the incident "has thrown" Pataky and that she is asking her husband not to leave her alone at the house. That is one anonymous claim, and neither Hemsworth nor Pataky has confirmed it publicly. The two outlets also differ on who was home, which is a reason to treat the detail with care.

## The Avengers: Doomsday schedule

New Idea says Hemsworth, who returns as Thor in the Marvel film, was slated to attend fan events for Avengers: Doomsday on the Gold Coast on 4 December and in Seoul on 8 December. It describes his travel plans as in disarray, though it does not cite anyone who has said so on the record. Treat the dates as the magazine's report until the studio confirms them. Our earlier post on [Thor's poster for the film](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-thor-doomsday-poster/) covers the campaign so far.

## Meanwhile, a Furiosa fate resurfaces

Away from the police story, Hemsworth's villain is back in the conversation. ScreenRant looked at how his character Dementus ends in Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga, calling it one of the most brutal fates in the franchise. By its reading, Furiosa chains him up at the Citadel and plants a peach seed in him, so a tree grows from his body while he lives.

ScreenRant compares that with the deaths of other series villains, including [Immortan Joe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immortan_Joe) in [Fury Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Max%3A_Fury_Road). The film also offers other versions of his death, and the sources say the historical record does not settle which one is true.
