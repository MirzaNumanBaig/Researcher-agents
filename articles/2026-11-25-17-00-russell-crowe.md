**Quick summary**

- Russell Crowe's Western *The Weight* has reached the No. 1 spot on Prime Video, according to Collider.
- The film earned only a few million dollars at the box office before moving to rental and purchase platforms.
- Ethan Hawke co-stars, and Padraic McKinley directed.
- Collider describes the film as a sleeper awards contender for Crowe this season.

[Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) has a streaming hit on his hands. His Western *The Weight*, which made little noise in cinemas, has climbed to the top of Prime Video's chart for titles available to rent or buy, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/russell-crowe-the-weight-streaming-success-prime-video-november-2026/) on 25 November 2026.

## A small cinema run, a big second life

According to Collider, *The Weight* grossed only a few million dollars in theatres. That is a modest return for a film led by an Oscar winner. On video on demand it has found the audience it missed, and the outlet says it has quietly become one of the most-watched titles in the world on the service.

The chart in question covers transactional video on demand, where viewers pay to rent or buy a film. It is separate from the subscription streaming lists, which Collider says are currently led by the new season of *The Chosen*. The distinction matters because a No. 1 ranking on the rental chart reflects viewers paying extra, not simply pressing play on a service they already subscribe to.

## Who is behind the film

*The Weight* co-stars [Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke) and was directed by Padraic McKinley. Collider notes that McKinley has since been tapped to direct a new *Sicario* film, with a script by [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), the writer best known for *Peaky Blinders*. Collider praised the film as a breathtaking Western and singled out Crowe's performance as commanding.

## Where it sits in Crowe's career

Collider frames the result against Crowe's long record. He earned his first Oscar nomination in 2000 for *The Insider*, in which he starred opposite [Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino). He won the next year for *Gladiator* and was nominated again for *A Beautiful Mind*.

More recently, Crowe took his passion project *Nuremberg* to the festival circuit before its November 2025 release, according to Collider. The film arrived on Netflix at the start of 2026. Collider says Oscar buzz around that film did not turn into a nomination, and that *The Weight* now gives him another chance this year. That is the outlet's view of the awards race, not an announcement from the Academy.

Crowe's older work is also finding new viewers. ## What happens next

Nothing official has been announced about a wider release or any awards campaign for *The Weight*. The film is available to rent or buy on Prime Video and other VOD services now.
