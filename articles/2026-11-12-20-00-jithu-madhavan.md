**Quick summary**

- Director [Jithu Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jithu_Madhavan) says reports of a next film with Dulquer Salmaan and Mamitha Baiju are rumours.
- He spoke at the success press conference for his Tamil debut, Scene, in Kochi on 12 November 2026.
- He has not planned a next project and will not decide for about two more weeks, Cinema Express reports.
- The only sequel he has announced is Romancham 2; talk of Aavesham 2 is speculation.
- Scene has crossed the Rs 200 crore mark and is eyeing Rs 300 crore.

Jithu Madhavan has shut down speculation about his next film. Speaking in Kochi on 12 November 2026, the Scene director said that reports linking him with [Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan) and [Mamitha Baiju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamitha_Baiju) are only rumours, and that he has not planned his next project at all.

## What Jithu Madhavan said about his next film

The comments came at a press conference held as part of the success celebrations for Scene. According to [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/malayalam/news/2026/Nov/12/jithu-madhavan-clarifies-rumour-on-film-with-dulquer-salmaan-mamitha-baiju), he called the reports unfounded and said he is not in the mood to think about a new film right now. He added that he would consider his next move only after another two weeks.

The Times of India carried a similar account, saying he has not finalised anything and needs time before making a decision. He told the paper he has not yet reached the stage of seriously thinking about his next directorial venture and plans a short break first.

For a director who has just delivered three hits in a row, the pause is a notable choice. Cinema Express describes Scene as completing a hat-trick, following the Malayalam hits Romancham and Aavesham. The restraint also answers a fair question: which film does a filmmaker make after a run like that?

## Romancham 2 is the only confirmed sequel

The sharper part of his answer was about sequels. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/malayalam/movies/news/scene-director-jithu-madhavan-clarifies-aavesham-2-rumours-reveals-only-romancham-2-is-confirmed-else-is-speculation/articleshow/135761343.cms) quotes him saying the only sequel he has announced is Romancham 2, and that everything else is speculation.

He explained that the endings of Aavesham and Scene are not announcements of second parts. In his words, they are hints that the stories have not completely ended. The paper reports that he wanted audiences to leave theatres feeling the characters' lives could continue.

On Aavesham 2, he acknowledged that talks have happened. He said Fahadh had mentioned at an event that discussions were under way, but that nothing has been confirmed. He also raised a risk that many directors would recognise: if a sequel falls short, the same people may ask why it was made, so he would rather wait for the right time.

He sounded more hopeful about Romancham 2. He said he believes it will happen once the time and circumstances are right.

## How Scene is performing

The timing of the questions makes sense given the film's run. Scene, his first Tamil film, stars Suriya as DSP Inbaraj, [Nazriya Nazim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazriya_Nazim) as Maya and [Naslen Gafoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naslen) as Eeswaran. Cinema Express reports it has crossed Rs 200 crore at the box office, one of the biggest earners in the industry this year, and is now eyeing Rs 300 crore in the coming weeks. The Times of India says it reportedly passed Rs 200 crore worldwide within five days of release.

Praise has come from across the industry. Cinema Express notes that actors, directors and audiences have responded warmly, and that Telugu star [Mahesh Babu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahesh_Babu) called it "cool, smart and so much fun". Suriya and Jyotika produced it through 2D Entertainment and Zhagaram Studios, with [Sushin Shyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sushin_Shyam) making his Tamil debut as music director and Vineeth Augustian behind the camera.

## What happens next

Nothing is expected from the director on his next project for at least two weeks, going by his own timeline. Until he says otherwise, a Dulquer Salmaan and Mamitha Baiju film should be treated as unconfirmed. Romancham 2 remains the one sequel on his record, with no date given. Meanwhile, the next milestone to watch is whether Scene reaches the Rs 300 crore mark.
