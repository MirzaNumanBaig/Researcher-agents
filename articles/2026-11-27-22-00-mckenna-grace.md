**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) took part in rehearsals for the 2026 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York on November 24, according to Reality Tea via Yahoo.
- She wore a black knit top and mini skirt with brown faux-fur trim, sheer tights and black leather knee-high boots.
- Reality Tea reports she spoke with young fans and crew, then took a microphone and danced with her co-stars.
- She also stars in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opened on October 28.

## Mckenna Grace at the Macy's parade rehearsals

Mckenna Grace was out in the cold on November 24 for rehearsals ahead of the 2026 Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York, according to a [Reality Tea report published on Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/mckenna-grace-braves-cold-micro-055055772.html). The actress walked the outdoor route in front of the Macy's storefront, which was lined with holiday lights.

The report says she stopped to talk with young fans and crew members along a red carpet path. Later she picked up a microphone, danced and laughed with her co-stars during the street rehearsal. The report does not say what she will perform on parade day, so we are not guessing.

## The winter outfit

Most of the coverage is about the clothes. Reality Tea says Grace wore a black Show Me Your Mumu knit set: the Phoebe Top and the matching Phoebe Mini Skirt. Both pieces have rich brown faux-fur trim, along the plunging V-neckline on the top and around the hem and cuffs.

She added sheer black tights and black leather Larroude Carine boots with pointed toes and stiletto heels. The outlet lists the boots at US$288. Black leather gloves and Melinda Maria Ashley Lock Huggies earrings, listed at US$75, finished the look.

Her blonde hair was worn in loose waves, half up, with a small black ribbon bow on each side and wispy bangs. Reality Tea describes her make-up as soft and natural, with flushed cheeks, defined lashes and a pink-nude lip. Prices are as reported by the outlet and may change.

## Why she is in the spotlight

Grace is currently promoting a busy year on screen. She plays in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, directed by Francis Lawrence, which opened on October 28. According to the film's [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), the cast also includes Joseph Zada, Whitney Peak and Ben Wang.

The same entry puts its box office at about US$237.2 million against a reported budget of US$150 million, and lists a 68% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 55 from 43 critics. The same data lists *The Last Kiss*, directed by Yoko Okumura and co-starring Jaden Michael, as a September 15 release. No box office figures are available for it yet.
