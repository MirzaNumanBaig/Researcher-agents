**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman), 58, is reported to play the villainous pirate Blackbeard in a big-budget remake of Treasure Island.
- [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott) is directing the family adventure, according to Globe's report on Yahoo.
- The film is set to open in cinemas on 11 November 2027, per the same report, which cites Collider.
- The report says [Hugh Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Grant) and [Owen Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Cooper) have also signed on; this has not been checked against a studio announcement.

Hugh Jackman is lining up another blockbuster. According to a report from Globe, published on Yahoo on 24 October 2026, the Australian actor will play the pirate Blackbeard in a new version of Treasure Island, directed by Ridley Scott, the filmmaker behind Gladiator.

## What the report says about the film

Globe describes the project as a big-budget family adventure and says it is set to reach cinemas on 11 November 2027. It attributes the Blackbeard casting to Collider, which means the detail is second-hand here and worth confirming against the studio's own announcements as they arrive.

Blackbeard is a notable choice. The report calls him a villainous pirate, a long way from the song-and-dance roles many audiences associate with Jackman on stage and screen. It also notes that the actor, 58, is best known to film fans as the star of the X-Men series.

## Who else is in the cast

Globe says Hugh Grant and Owen Cooper have signed on too. That is a striking pairing: Grant is a veteran English screen actor, while Cooper, born in 2009, is one of the youngest names in the lineup. The report gives no character details for either of them, and it does not say who will play the book's main roles.

Readers should treat the cast list with some caution. Casting for large productions often moves between "in talks" and "confirmed" before a studio makes its own statement, and the Yahoo piece does not link to one.

## A shoot built on location work

The same article quotes an unnamed source who says the production involves "intense location work and a lot of globe-trotting", with Jackman expected to be away for long stretches. Because that comes from a single anonymous source, it should be read as colour rather than confirmed schedule detail. No filming dates are given in the material we reviewed.
