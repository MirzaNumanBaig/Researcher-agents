*Heated Rivalry* breakout Connor Storrie is heading into darker territory. Lionsgate Play has announced that his science-fiction action thriller *April X* will start streaming on 27 November 2026, according to a report from [Kerala TV](https://www.keralatv.in/yaga-april-x-ott-release-date-lionsgate-play/). The date comes three weeks after Williams, his *Heated Rivalry* co-star, returns in a separate series on the same service.

## What April X is about

Storrie plays Bax, one half of a pair of twins. According to Kerala TV, Bax and his sister April can record their dreams onto discs. Their lives turn dangerous after a deal involving a gangster named Sergei Tetris. When April disappears, Bax, already in debt and surrounded by criminals, goes looking for her through a neon-lit underworld.

The report describes the search as turning into a psychological journey, with Bax confronting questions about identity, love and survival. The film is set in a futuristic, post-Soviet city, and Kerala TV frames it as action-driven, with criminal conspiracies and science-fiction ideas at its centre.

## Who is in the cast

Lilly Krug plays April, and Tudor Chirilă and Șerban Pavlu are listed in important roles. [Michel K. Parandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_K._Parandi) is the director. Kerala TV lists the writer as not yet announced, so that detail remains open.

## A different path after Heated Rivalry

Both Storrie and Williams drew attention through the Canadian series [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), and their next projects move well away from sports romance. Kerala TV notes that both new titles revolve around disappearances and hidden truths, though in very different genres.

Williams stars in *YAGA*, premiering 6 November. Created and written by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler), it follows the disappearance of Henry Park, heir to a fishing business, in the coastal town of Whittock. [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) plays a private investigator, with [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo) and [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) also in the cast. The mystery links to the Slavic legend of Baba Yaga, and [David Frazee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frazee) and [Rachel Talalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Talalay) direct.
