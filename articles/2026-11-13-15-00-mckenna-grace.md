**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) worked a surprise shift at a Raising Cane's on Sunset Boulevard in Los Angeles, according to Decked Out Magazine.
- The appearance came days before *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens in U.S. theatres on 20 November 2026.
- Grace plays Maysilee Donner, a fellow District 12 tribute, in the prequel.
- She also wore a sheer black lace mini dress to a Cultured Magazine event in Los Angeles, as reported by theFashionSpot.

Mckenna Grace spent part of this week handing out chicken at a Los Angeles drive-thru and the rest of it turning heads at a magazine party, as the final press push for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* gathers pace. The film arrives in U.S. theatres on 20 November 2026, and the 20-year-old actress is one of its most visible faces.

## A surprise shift on Sunset Boulevard

According to [Decked Out Magazine](https://deckedoutmagazine.com/mckenna-grace-raising-canes/), Grace made a pop-up appearance at the Raising Cane's on Sunset Boulevard. She served Box Combos at the counter and the drive-thru, as part of her partnership with the chain.

The standard Box Combo is four chicken fingers and a 22 oz drink, the outlet notes. It also reports that this was not her first outing with the brand. She has previously appeared alongside founder Todd Graves at Super Bowl LX and the Formula 1 Miami Grand Prix.

The timing is deliberate. The shift landed just days before Lionsgate's latest prequel reaches cinemas, and it gave local fans a chance to meet one of the film's breakout names over lunch.

## Who she plays in Sunrise on the Reaping

Grace plays Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute who competes alongside a young Haymitch Abernathy, played by Joseph Zada, Decked Out Magazine reports. The ensemble cast also includes Ralph Fiennes as President Snow, Jesse Plemons as Plutarch Heavensbee, Elle Fanning as Effie Trinket and Kieran Culkin as Caesar Flickerman.

For readers following her run of promotion, we have also covered how [Mckenna Grace and Joseph Zada have talked about the film](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-sunrise-on-the-reaping/), and how [her premiere-season outfits turned into a tribute tour](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-tribute-looks/).

## The Cultured Magazine look

Away from the drive-thru, Grace attended a Cultured Magazine event in Los Angeles. TheFashionSpot says she wore an all-black sheer lace mini dress by Annie's Ibiza with Christian Louboutin pumps and a matching lace choker. The outlet credits stylist Molly Dickson, who shared photographs on Instagram, and says Grace swapped her recent sleek updo for tousled blonde waves with soft bangs.

[Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/11/12/mckenna-grace-see-through-mini-dress-hunger-games/) describes a similar black translucent dress with a square neckline and embroidered detail, and says other guests included Mamie Gummer, who played Joseph Zada's on-screen mother in *We Were Liars*, and Grace's *Scooby-Doo* co-star Maxwell Jenkins. The publication adds that fans praised the look in the comments. Reality Tea does not name the designer, so treat the brand details as theFashionSpot's account.
