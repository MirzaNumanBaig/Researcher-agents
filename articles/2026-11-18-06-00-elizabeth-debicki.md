**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) accepted an honour at ELLE's 2026 Women in Hollywood celebration on Monday night.
- She was introduced by her *Wicker* co-star Alexander Skarsgård, according to ELLE.
- Her speech credited casting director Ronna Kress and director Baz Luhrmann for her first big break.
- The night came a day after she attended the world premiere of *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth*.

The Australian actress used her **ELLE speech** to say that a career is never built alone. Debicki accepted her Women in Hollywood honour on Monday night, and instead of listing her own achievements she spent most of her time thanking the people who pushed for her, according to [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a74455466/elizabeth-debicki-elle-2026-women-in-hollywood-speech-details/).

## What Elizabeth Debicki said on stage

After being introduced by Alexander Skarsgård, Debicki thanked her fellow honourees and then spoke about work. "I really love to work. It is my great love," she said, adding that she felt "a really deep sense of gratitude" for what she called a small army of people who had fought for her to do it.

She named the two people she sees as the start of it all. Casting director Ronna Kress, she said, watched the "shoddy rookie tape" she made at home and managed to persuade Baz Luhrmann to watch it too. Debicki joked that anyone who knows Luhrmann will understand that as a minor miracle.

## The audition that changed everything

The story behind that tape is the heart of the speech. ELLE reports that Debicki was 20, had recently finished drama school in Australia and had never done a screen test. The role was in Luhrmann's *The Great Gatsby*, which became her breakout film part.

Debicki said Kress then pushed the studio to fly her to Los Angeles to read for the part. She had never been to the city before. "That was really, really my door," she said, and credited Kress and Luhrmann with holding it open. Luhrmann, she added, is her "fairy godfather" and fought hard for her after her audition.

## A tribute to the other honourees

Debicki also paid tribute to the women honoured alongside her: [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore), Penélope Cruz, [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), [Rosalind Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Eleazar) and [Carrie Coon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Coon). Their audiences, she said, are privileged to witness their talent and intelligence, and their generosity at work makes other actors feel "sane" and "seen".

## A busy week on the red carpet

The ELLE evening followed another big night. According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/fashion/celebrity-style/935542/elizabeth-debicki-channels-princess-diana-in-most-flattering-black-dress/), Debicki wore a black halter-neck gown with a deep V neckline to the world premiere of *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* on 16 November. The magazine noted that the look recalled the "revenge dress" Princess Diana made famous in 1994, which Debicki wore in a replica made for season five of *The Crown*. She won an Emmy and a Golden Globe for playing Diana, HELLO! notes.

Debicki has also been in the news for her reaction to Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond. Speaking to Variety, as reported by [Hungama Express](https://www.hungamaexpress.com/news/56195-elizabeth-debicki-is-thrilled-for-007-robert-pattinson), she called her *Tenet* co-star "perfect" for the role.
