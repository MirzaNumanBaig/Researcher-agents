**Quick summary**

- Norwegian actor [Thorbjørn Harr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorbj%C3%B8rn_Harr) plays Anders Platou in the Netflix series *Always on Call*.
- Netflix will release the medical drama worldwide on 26 November 2026.
- Sara Khorami leads as surgeon Sofia, sent to a chaotic Oslo emergency room.
- Netflix describes it as the first medical drama from Netflix Nordics.

Thorbjørn Harr, known to many viewers from *Vikings*, has a role in *Always on Call*, a Norwegian medical drama that Netflix will release on 26 November 2026, according to [Entertainment Vine](https://www.entertainmentvine.com/television/medical-drama-series-always-on-call-stars-sara-khorami-thorbjorn-harr-jan-gunnar-roise-trailer-and-release-date/). Harr plays Anders Platou in the series, which is set in a busy Oslo emergency room.

## What is Always on Call about?

The story follows Sofia, a promising surgeon who is sent to the Oslo ER against her wishes. Entertainment Vine describes the department as chaotic and exhausting, but full of heart. It is the last place Sofia wants to be, and it may turn out to be the place that challenges her most.

[What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/always-on-call-netflix-medical-drama-release-date/) adds that Sofia has more talent than patience and arrives to save her career. She meets disorder and resistance, and also an unexpected sense of community. Her choice is whether to keep chasing her ambitions alone or become part of the team.

## Who is in the cast?

Sara Khorami plays Sofia (listed as Sofia Karimi by Entertainment Vine). Harr appears as Anders Platou. The cast also includes Jan Gunnar Røise as Stig Solvang and [Silje Storstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silje_Storstein) as Renate.

What's on Netflix lists a longer supporting ensemble, among them Alfred Ekker Strande, Nader Khademi, Tora Dietrichson, Eili Harboe and Haakon Aunlie. Neither outlet says in what way Platou connects to Sofia's story, so that remains for viewers to find out.

Entertainment Vine notes that Harr is best known for *Vikings*, *22 July* and *Sex*. Khorami's credits include *Post Mortem: No One Dies in Skarnes* and *Troll 2*, and Røise is known for *The Thing* and *Beware of Children*.

## Who made it?

Harald Rosenløw-Eeg and Jan Trygve Røyneland created the series and wrote it. Stian Kristiansen and Liv Karin Dahlstrøm directed. Klynge is the production company, with Magnus Ramsdalen, Esben Selvig and Kaare Daniel Steen as producers.

According to What's on Netflix, the series runs to seven episodes. The same report quotes producer Esben Bundgaard-Jørgensen Selvig calling it Netflix Nordics' first ever medical drama, and saying it focuses on the people who show up for patients rather than only on operating-room drama.

## Why it matters for Netflix's Nordic slate

Hospital shows are a crowded field, and What's on Netflix says Norway is entering the genre during a recent surge of medical dramas on the platform. A Norwegian-language series with a recognisable lead and an ensemble of familiar local faces gives Netflix a way to add to its Nordic originals. For Harr, it is a return to a contemporary setting after the period drama that made him widely known.

## What happens next

Netflix will make all of *Always on Call* available on 26 November 2026, about three weeks after this report. Entertainment Vine points readers to the official website for further details.
