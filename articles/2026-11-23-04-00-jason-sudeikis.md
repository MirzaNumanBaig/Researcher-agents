**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) says he modelled his high school basketball game on NBA guard Jason Williams, according to a Basketball Network story republished by Yahoo Sports on 22 November 2026.
- He played left-handed point guard for Shawnee Mission West and says his coaches "hated" his no-look style while his teammates loved it.
- Separately, [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde) spoke about her past relationships on the "Call Her Daddy" podcast, SiriusXM reported.
- Wilde is promoting a new film, "The Invite", according to SiriusXM.

The man who plays a soccer coach on screen was once a flashy point guard with a habit of passing without looking. Sudeikis said in an interview, as reported by [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/teammates-loved-jason-sudeikis-claims-022800121.html), that his high school game was closest to that of NBA guard Jason Williams. It is a cheerful story, and it lands in the same week Wilde discussed their past on a popular podcast.

## Sudeikis on playing like "White Chocolate"

Before he became known for "We're the Millers" and [Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso), Sudeikis was serious about basketball. According to the Yahoo Sports piece, he played as a left-handed point guard at Shawnee Mission West.

Asked whose style he copied, he did not hesitate. "Jason Williams. One hundred percent," he said, adding that his father gave him Pete Maravich instructional basketball tapes and that he drilled the moves relentlessly. When Williams was playing for the Sacramento Kings, friends would phone Sudeikis to say they had seen someone playing just like him.

The report says Williams, nicknamed "White Chocolate", never became a true NBA superstar, but his passing and dribbling looked like something from a video game.

## Coaches against teammates

The flair did not please everyone. Sudeikis joked that his coaches hated it and his teammates loved it. When they asked him to make the easy pass, he said, he wondered why he should.

The coaches' complaint, as Yahoo Sports describes it, was that he cared more about the spectacle on offence than about defence. Sudeikis also acknowledged he was not the best player on his team, and says a dominant teammate still could not hide his amusement at the tricks. Sudeikis earned the nickname "White Magic", which he said he would happily take, since he was never looking while passing.

The article adds that he received a college offer for basketball but chose acting instead. His later path through comedy is the subject of [Jason Sudeikis on Following Chris Rock at His SNL Audition](https://moduscarry.com/jason-sudeikis-snl-audition/), a story we covered earlier.

## Olivia Wilde on "Call Her Daddy"

On the same weekend, SiriusXM published a recap of Wilde's appearance on "Call Her Daddy" with host Alex Cooper. Wilde reflected on her relationships with [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) and Sudeikis. These are her own recollections, as relayed by SiriusXM, and Sudeikis has not responded in the sources we reviewed.

Of Styles, whose relationship with her began in 2020, she said she was surprised by the intensity of the public reaction. She described the relationship as "so sweet and so beautiful" and "very domestic and kind and lovely".

On Sudeikis, SiriusXM says she recalled the moment their nearly decade-long relationship ended. After she asked whether he had bought her a birthday present, she said he replied, "What would I get you, Olivia? I don't know you." She said the comment made both of them realise it was over.

Wilde also told the story of meeting [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise) after the CinemaCon custody papers incident. She said he greeted her sympathetically with a remark about what had happened to her in Las Vegas. The episode also covered her new film "The Invite", double standards in her career and how her views on marriage have changed, according to SiriusXM.
