**Quick summary**

- [Owen Thiele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Thiele) praised co-star [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) in a new interview with Variety, reported on 26 October 2026.
- The pair share a few scenes in *Peaked*, an A24 comedy written, directed by and starring [Molly Gordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Gordon).
- Thiele called Storrie "genuinely a very well-trained actor" and said he learned from him on set.
- *Peaked* does not have a release date yet.

Owen Thiele has used a fresh round of press to talk up one of his co-stars again. Speaking to Variety, the 29-year-old said Connor Storrie, 26, brought care and craft to their shared scenes in the upcoming A24 comedy *Peaked*, according to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/10/26/owen-thiele-raves-over-working-with-connor-storrie-on-peaked-movie-says-he-cares-so-deeply/).

## What Thiele said about Storrie

Thiele, who stars in *Adults*, said the two actors have "a few scenes together" and described Storrie as "brilliant". He also joked about how often he steers interviews back to his co-star.

By his own account, he brings up Storrie in every interview, "accidentally, but also on purpose". He added that a tally has been circulating on Twitter counting how many times he has done it. His first reaction was that it had to be fake, but he said there is proof for every mention.

The praise was personal as well as professional. Thiele called Storrie "genuinely so humble and so down to earth" and said he cared deeply about making Gordon happy. He also described him as collaborative.

## A small moment on set

Thiele offered one example. In a scene where he had a single line, he said Storrie asked whether he was giving him what he needed. Thiele's answer, by his own telling, was that for one line, yes.

He went on to say Storrie "cares so deeply" and is a very well-trained actor. Thiele said he comes from an improv background, where performers constantly cut each other off, and that he picked up lessons from Storrie while filming.

## What *Peaked* is about

A24's comedy was announced last year, with Gordon writing, directing and starring. Just Jared describes the premise as following the two girls who traumatised you in high school as they try to relive their glory days at their 10-year reunion.

The ensemble is large. Alongside Gordon, Thiele and Storrie, the cast includes [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa), [Emma Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Mackey), [Laura Dern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Dern), [Simone Ashley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Ashley), [Levon Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levon_Hawke), [Mitra Jouhari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mitra_Jouhari), [Gabby Windey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabby_Windey), [Amy Sedaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Sedaris), Jaya Harper, [Emil Wakim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Wakim) and [Alex Consani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Consani).

Storrie is best known as the star of [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), the Canadian sports romance series. Just Jared notes that Gordon also praised working with him in another recent interview.
