**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been named the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) by Amazon MGM Studios, succeeding [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig).
- The Guardian says it received more than 700 reader responses and that opinion is split.
- Jeff Bezos says Amazon ran five finalists through screen tests and chose Pattinson three days before the announcement.
- Elizabeth Debicki, his Tenet co-star, says she texted him straight away.

The reaction to Robert Pattinson's casting as James Bond is mixed, and the people who chose him have started explaining why. Within days of Amazon MGM Studios confirming the news, readers, fellow actors and Amazon's founder have all had their say on a decision that [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/18/readers-casting-robert-pattinson-bond-007) says "came as a surprise".

## Why readers are divided

The Guardian reports that its callout drew more than 700 responses. Some were delighted. One reader in the US predicted something "deliciously dark" with [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) directing, while a Brighton reader compared Pattinson's potential to [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton), their favourite Bond.

Others were blunt. A reader from Australia called it the worst casting decision in cinema history and said Pattinson was "not sexy enough" for the part. Another argued that Bond works best when the actor is a blank slate, and that Pattinson is already too established.

Some readers had backed other names. One was rooting for [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), another for [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden). A London reader pointed to the link with the director: Pattinson plays the villain Scytale in Dune: Part Three, which Villeneuve made.

These are reader comments, not a poll, so they show how people are talking rather than how many agree.

## What Bezos says about the screen tests

Jeff Bezos has described a more orderly process. Speaking on stage at the Silicon Valley and the World conference, he said he was "heavily involved" and that the team "ran a really tight process", according to [Nine](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/jeff-bezos-amazon-james-bond-robert-pattinson-cast-timing-secret-20261118-p61f9b.html).

A large group was cut to five finalists, who each did "real, old-fashioned screen tests" in full costume on real sets with other actors. Each finalist performed three scenes. The group then met on a Friday to review the tapes, which Bezos said was three days before the announcement.

The five were reportedly Pattinson, [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Hoult. Bezos said all were "extraordinarily talented", but that there was "something special" about Pattinson that was "kind of immediate".

He also admitted the team started out with a bias. "We were slightly biased against him, just because he's already so famous," he said, adding that the screen tests made it "obvious". [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/entertainment/jeff-bezos-says-he-was-biased-against-robert-pattinson-playing-james-bond/3101723/) notes that the role has traditionally gone to less established actors, and that Villeneuve was the person the team "gravitated towards".

## Debicki and the cast of supporters

Elizabeth Debicki, who played Kat opposite Pattinson's Neil in Christopher Nolan's Tenet in 2020, was asked about the news at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. "I'm so pleased! I texted him straight away," she told Entertainment Tonight, as reported by [Indiablooms](https://www.indiablooms.com/showbiz/i-texted-him-straight-away-elizabeth-debickis-excited-reaction-to-robert-pattinson-becoming-james-bond/details). She called him "a total enigma" who is also "very funny, smart and completely unusual".

[Inshorts](https://inshorts.com/en/news/debicki-calls-pattinson--perfect--choice-for-james-bond-1794989732934) carried her view that he is "perfect" for the job. For more on her comments, see our earlier post on [Elizabeth Debicki calling Pattinson perfect for Bond](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-james-bond/).

Pattinson himself told Variety that being trusted with 007 is "the honour of a lifetime" and "a dream come true", and thanked Villeneuve and the Amazon MGM team.

## What happens next

Pattinson will headline the 26th Bond film, directed by Villeneuve and written by Steven Knight, according to Indiablooms. No release date appears in the sources we read. Amazon's takeover also means the Broccoli family, who oversaw the films for decades, no longer hold creative control, and several reports say they were not closely involved in this choice.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who announced Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond?

Amazon MGM Studios made the announcement. Amazon acquired MGM in 2022 and now controls the Bond franchise.

### How was Pattinson chosen?

Bezos says a large group was reduced to five finalists, each of whom performed three scenes in full-costume screen tests. Pattinson was picked after a review meeting on the Friday before the announcement.

### Who directs the next Bond film?

Denis Villeneuve directs, with Steven Knight writing. Pattinson has already worked with Villeneuve on Dune: Part Three.
