**Quick summary**

- Disney is developing a new Home Alone sequel with Macaulay Culkin, according to Variety reports repeated by Yahoo and bdnews24.
- Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg are writing the script with Culkin and will produce through their Point Grey banner.
- The film is in early development at 20th Century Studios, with no release date and no wider cast named.
- Original director Chris Columbus told Variety that Catherine O'Hara was "50%" of the first film. She died in January.

The [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) Home Alone sequel is moving ahead at Disney, and the film's original director is not convinced it can work. Rogen and [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg) are developing a new Christmas film with [Macaulay Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaulay_Culkin), reported on 21 November. In the same news cycle, [Chris Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Columbus_(filmmaker)) questioned whether the story can be recreated without its original mother.

## What Disney has confirmed so far

According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/disney-revives-home-alone-seth-154339178.html), which cites Variety, Rogen and Goldberg are writing the screenplay alongside Culkin. They will produce the project through Point Grey, their production company. The film sits in early development at 20th Century Studios, part of Disney.

There is very little else on record. [bdnews24](https://bdnews24.com/entertainment/t3a8cvu247) reports that plot details and wider casting remain under wraps and that Disney declined to comment. No release date has been announced.

The timing also depends on Rogen's diary. bdnews24 says the pair will give the sequel their full attention once they finish preparing an original horror-comedy they are directing for Universal. For readers who follow the actor's work, [everything we publish about Seth Rogen](https://moduscarry.com/tag/seth-rogen/) is collected in one place, including our earlier report that [Seth Rogen will write a new Home Alone film with Macaulay Culkin](https://moduscarry.com/seth-rogen-home-alone-sequel/).

## Culkin's return as Kevin McCallister

The 1990 film, [Home Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone), starred a nine-year-old Culkin as Kevin McCallister, a boy left behind when his family flies to Paris for Christmas. He has to defend the house against two burglars. Culkin played the role again in 1992's Home Alone 2: Lost in New York, and four later instalments went ahead without him, according to bdnews24.

If the project goes ahead, it would be his first feature appearance as Kevin since that second film. Yahoo, citing TheWrap, says Culkin has been in discussions about returning and has pitched a fresh approach. That has been reported, not confirmed by Disney.

## The idea Culkin has floated

Culkin has previously described a version in which Kevin is an adult and a father. In that outline, his work leaves him struggling to stay close to his son. The boy eventually locks him out of the house and sets elaborate traps to keep him from getting back in.

That would flip the original's setup, putting Kevin where the burglars once stood. The house would also stand in for the strained relationship between father and child. Yahoo stresses that Disney has not said whether the finished film will follow this premise, so it should be read as an idea, not a plot.

## Columbus: Catherine O'Hara was "50%" of the film

The director's remarks came in an interview with Variety. "Catherine, as I've always said, is 50% of Home Alone," Columbus said, according to Yahoo. He argued that O'Hara and Culkin supplied the emotional core that made the film more than slapstick. O'Hara, who played Kevin's mother, died in January at 71, so that pairing cannot be repeated.

Columbus was also critical of the later sequels, saying their makers did not grasp how much the original cast mattered. He singled out [Joe Pesci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Pesci) and [Daniel Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Stern_(actor)), whose Harry and Marv gave the first two films their comic identity.

Yahoo reports that he spoke shortly before he learned of Disney's plans. Asked for more afterwards, he declined to comment further.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. With no release date, no director named and no cast beyond Culkin, the next signs will be a script, a director and a start date. Rogen and Goldberg's horror-comedy for Universal comes first, according to bdnews24. More coverage of the project will appear in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a new Home Alone movie?

Yes, in early development. Disney's 20th Century Studios is developing it, with Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg writing and producing alongside Macaulay Culkin, as reported by Variety.

### Will Macaulay Culkin return as Kevin McCallister?

He is attached as a writer and is reportedly in talks to return. It would be his first feature as Kevin since Home Alone 2: Lost in New York in 1992.

### When will the new Home Alone sequel be released?

No release date has been announced. The pair must finish prep on a Universal horror-comedy before turning to it.

### What did Chris Columbus say about the sequel?

He told Variety that Catherine O'Hara was half of the original film's appeal, and doubted it could be recreated without her.
