**Quick summary**

- A video of [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) ripping off prosthetics during a prank on his Deadpool 2 makeup team has resurfaced online and drawn criticism.
- The clip is real. It was posted by the official Deadpool Movie account in 2019, according to BuzzFeed.
- Posts claiming the prosthetics took eight hours to apply appear to be wrong. The application took around two hours.
- Some viewers say the joke went too far, while others question whether the story is being overblown.

A years-old Ryan Reynolds prank is back in the spotlight. A clip from the making of the 2018 film Deadpool 2 resurfaced on social media this week, and the backlash has focused on how the actor treated his makeup artists. According to [BuzzFeed](https://www.buzzfeed.com/annasgood/ryan-reynolds-deadpool-prank-debunked), though, parts of the story spreading with the video are not accurate.

## What the video shows

In the footage, Reynolds appears to react badly to the prosthetics applied to his face and tears them off, telling the team they will need to start again. The makeup artists in the room visibly react as their work is undone.

BuzzFeed reports that the clip was first shared in 2019 by the official Deadpool Movie X account, captioned as a throwback in which makeup designer [Bill Corso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Corso) showed "Maximum Restraint" by not strangling Reynolds. The same outlet says Reynolds also uploaded an official version to YouTube more than seven years ago. A 2019 ComicBook.com article, quoted by BuzzFeed, said Reynolds thought the bit would be funny but that the crew's reactions suggested they did not share the enthusiasm.

## The eight-hour claim

The version now circulating comes with a caption saying Reynolds had just learned that filming was cancelled after eight hours in the makeup chair, and chose to pull the stunt on the team instead.

BuzzFeed says a Community Note on the recent post, along with coverage from the time, contradicts that. The full prosthetics took about two hours, not eight. The timestamp on the official video shows a start time of 6:02 a.m., which fits the shorter timeline.

Corso, who designed the look of Wade Wilson's scarred skin and appears in the clip, has also spoken about this. In a feature in The Credits, cited by BuzzFeed, he said the team cut the chair time from about four hours on the first Deadpool film to roughly two hours for the sequels.

## Why people are still upset

A shorter application time has not ended the criticism. BuzzFeed notes that some viewers on the original YouTube upload in 2019 called it unkind, with one commenter writing that a prank is only a prank if it is enjoyable for everyone. Others wondered whether it was staged after the day's shoot.

Some commenters have suggested the shoot really had been cancelled and that Reynolds delivered the news this way once the application was finished. BuzzFeed says that remains unclear, and the sources do not settle it. The BuzzFeed writer's own view is that the joke would still be hard on the artists even at two hours. That is an opinion, not an established fact.

## Reynolds and a possible Marvel project

BuzzFeed also mentions separate rumours that Reynolds is writing a Marvel script about the Canadian superhero team Alpha Flight. The outlet says it is not yet clear whether that would connect to the next Deadpool film or stand alone. Treat this as unconfirmed.

## What happens next

Neither Reynolds nor the Deadpool team had commented on the renewed criticism in the source reviewed for this article. For now, the video is a reminder that old clips can spread quickly with new, unchecked captions. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Ryan Reynolds Deadpool 2 prank video real?

Yes. BuzzFeed reports the clip was posted by the official Deadpool Movie account in 2019 and uploaded by Reynolds to YouTube.

### Did Reynolds really ruin eight hours of makeup work?

That claim appears to be false. A Community Note and earlier coverage say the prosthetics took about two hours to apply.

### Who is Bill Corso?

Corso is the award-winning makeup designer behind Wade Wilson's scarred look in the Deadpool films, and he appears in the clip.
