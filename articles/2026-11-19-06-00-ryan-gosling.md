**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios will put *Project Hail Mary* back in US and Canadian cinemas on December 11, 2026.
- The rerelease will play in IMAX and select 70mm theatres, and a new trailer has been released to promote it.
- The film has grossed more than US$685 million worldwide, according to the studio's release as reported by BroadwayWorld.
- It first opened in March 2026, then arrived on MGM+ on June 18 and Prime Video on July 3.
- The announcement covers the US and Canada only; no Australian date has been confirmed in the sources.

[Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s space adventure is heading back to the big screen. Amazon MGM Studios has announced that *Project Hail Mary* will return to theatres in the US and Canada on December 11, 2026, and it has released a new trailer to mark the occasion, according to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/NEW-Trailer-for-PROJECT-HAIL-MARY-Starring-Ryan-Gosling-Returning-to-Theaters-in-IMAX-and-70mm-20261118).

## Project Hail Mary rerelease: what is changing

The headline detail is the format. The December run will be in IMAX and select 70mm cinemas, which gives audiences a second chance to see the film on the largest screens available. [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/ryan-gosling-project-hail-mary-175701000.html) notes the timing puts it in front of viewers during awards season, and says the new trailer draws on positive reviews and the recognition the film has collected since March.

The studio's announcement only names the US and Canada. None of the three outlets we read mention Australian dates, so local readers should check with their cinema chains before planning a trip.

## A hit that keeps earning

The numbers explain why the studio wants another run. The film has grossed more than US$685 million globally, and the studio's own release, quoted by BroadwayWorld, describes it as one of the year's top-grossing films. AOL also calls it a major box-office success.

The home-viewing window has already come and gone. Per BroadwayWorld, the film reached MGM+ on June 18 and moved to Prime Video on July 3. A cinema return months after it landed on streaming is a notable move, and it suggests the studio sees the big-screen experience as the film's strongest selling point.

## What the film is about

Based on the 2021 novel [*Project Hail Mary*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary) by Andy Weir, the film follows science teacher Ryland Grace, played by Gosling. He wakes aboard a spaceship light years from home with no memory of who he is or how he got there. As his memory returns, he learns his mission is to solve the mystery of a substance that is dimming the sun, and to stop an extinction-level event on Earth.

He does not work alone. The studio's logline describes an unexpected friendship, and AOL identifies the companion as Rocky, an alien engineer trying to save his own star. [The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/ryan-gosling-project-hail-mary-returning-december-11-20261118/) points out that Weir is also the author behind *The Martian*, and describes Gosling's character as a reluctant hero and die-hard science fan rather than a traditional survival-film lead.

## Who is in it

Sandra Hüller plays Eva Stratt, per AOL. James Ortiz voices and performs Rocky. The cast also includes Lionel Boyce, Ken Leung, Milana Vayntrub and Priya Kansara.

Behind the camera, the film is directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller, whom BroadwayWorld describes as Academy Award-winning filmmakers, from a screenplay by Drew Goddard. The producers include Amy Pascal, Gosling, Lord, Miller, Aditya Sood, Rachel O'Connor and Weir. It is rated PG-13 in the US for some thematic material and suggestive references.

## What happens next

The rerelease opens on December 11. The Playlist also notes that a sequel may happen but that the studio says the decision rests with Weir, so nothing is confirmed there. For now, the trailer and the IMAX and 70mm dates are the firm news.
