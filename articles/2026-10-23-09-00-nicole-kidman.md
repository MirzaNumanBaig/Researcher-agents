Nicole Kidman's nails are getting as much attention as her seat at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show. Good Housekeeping reported on 22 October that her "cashmere coconut" manicure has become the beauty talking point of the season, while a separate Far Out Magazine piece looked back at a film role she once left behind.

## What are cashmere coconut nails?

Cashmere coconut nails are a soft, sheer, milky-white manicure with a glossy finish. Good Housekeeping spoke to Thea Green MBE, founder of Nails.INC, who described the look as understated luxury: creamy and healthy-looking rather than bright or glittery.

Green broke the name in two. The "coconut" comes from the sheer, milky shade, she said, and the "cashmere" from the smooth, almost velvety appearance. The magazine said the manicure has been discussed widely on social media since Kidman was seen at the show.

## How to get the look

Green's advice, as relayed by Good Housekeeping, is simple. File nails into a soft oval or gently rounded shape. Choose a sheer milky white or nude, because a more opaque or bright white won't give the same result.

Apply one thin coat, let it dry, then add a second so the colour builds gradually and a little of the natural nail shows through. Finish with cuticle oil. "The result should look creamy and luminous rather than glittery or overly pearlescent," Green said.

## The role Kidman walked away from

The second story is an older one. According to Far Out Magazine, screenwriter [Lucinda Coxon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucinda_Coxon) began adapting [David Ebershoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Ebershoff)'s novel The Danish Girl in 2004. The book is a fictionalised account of Lili Elbe, one of the first people to undergo sex-reassignment surgery.

By 2008, Far Out says, Kidman was attached to play Elbe, with [Tomas Alfredson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomas_Alfredson) reportedly set to direct. He was replaced by Lasse Hallström, and by 2014 [Tom Hooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hooper) had been announced as director.

Coxon told The Film Experience that Kidman and Charlize Theron were attached at the same time, with [Rachel Weisz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Weisz) also linked to the project. "We could never quite get it on its way," she said, adding that the subject was considered marginal.

## Redmayne's regret

Far Out reports that Kidman eventually left, presumably because the film kept stalling, and that Redmayne stepped in. His casting drew criticism over whether a man should play a transgender woman and whether the part should have gone to a trans actor. Redmayne was nominated for best actor that Oscar season.

He later told The Sunday Times: "I made that film with the best intentions, but I think it was a mistake." Far Out notes that on-screen transgender representation has progressed considerably since.
