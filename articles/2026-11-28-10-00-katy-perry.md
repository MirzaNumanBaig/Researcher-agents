**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) showed off a short black bob in Instagram photos from a European trip with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau), captioned "the bob's Euro debut".
- Two fashion posts on Yahoo, both from theFashionSpot, identify the outfits as a De La Vali corset minidress ($730) and a Zeynep Arcay snakeskin-print jacket and skirt ($4,150 together).
- The trip took in Spain, France and Italy, and the photo set also included a selfie that French President Emmanuel Macron appeared in.
- Perry and Trudeau have been linked since July 2025.

Katy Perry's new haircut is getting as much attention as her holiday. The singer shared a run of Instagram photos around 25 November from a trip through Spain, France and Italy with Justin Trudeau, and the short black bob in them has now been picked apart by fashion writers, who have also named the designer pieces she wore.

## The bob and the Instagram photo dump

Perry captioned the post "the bob's Euro debut", according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/katy-perrys-loved-photos-justin-035018706.html). The cut isn't entirely new. InStyle noted, as relayed by [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/katy-perry-european-bob-reveal), that she had already worn a similar short style at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala.

The set mixed hair reveal and holiday album. One photo showed her in a brass bathtub aboard the Orient Express, under a stained-glass window. Others showed the couple at festive markets, playing cards and backgammon, and standing on stone steps under lights. In a formal evening shot she wore a champagne satin-and-lace minidress, and Trudeau wore a white tuxedo jacket and black bow tie, according to the same report.

One selfie drew the most comments. Macron photobombed it with a smile, and fans joined in. "Third-wheeled by the president of France," one wrote, according to Yahoo.

## The De La Vali corset dress

theFashionSpot, in a piece carried by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/katy-perry-la-vali-corset-093506823.html), identified one outfit as De La Vali's Catalina Satin Mini Dress, priced at $730 and part of the label's Pre-Fall 2026 collection. It has a structured satin bodice and a sheer lace cape over the shoulders and neckline.

She posed in the wood-panelled corridor of a vintage train carriage, with warm sconces and mirrored panels behind her. The outlet described her hair as a blunt, jaw-grazing bob and listed white pointed-toe pumps, a small black clutch and stud earrings. Its verdict was that the lace softened a sharply cut piece, with party season in mind.

## The Zeynep Arcay snakeskin set

A second post, again from theFashionSpot via [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/katy-perry-goes-wild-zeynep-072319127.html), covered a head-to-toe snakeskin-print leather look from Zeynep Arcay's Fall 2026 collection. Citing the fashion account Katy Funky Fashion, it priced the Vintage Leather Jacket at $2,600 and the matching Leather Pencil Skirt at $1,550, a total of $4,150. The brand also shared the photographs on its own Instagram account, which the outlet took as confirmation of the label.

Perry added black pointed-toe pumps, gold earrings, a watch and narrow sunglasses. Her hair was a sleek, centre-parted bob. The playful touches were a red rose held to her face and a banana used as a telephone while she carried a copy of The New York Times.

## Where the relationship stands

Perry and Trudeau were first linked in July 2025, after meeting in Montreal during her [Lifetimes Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lifetimes_Tour), according to Yahoo. She told People she had "always wanted this epic legendary love" and called meeting him on tour "the greatest gift". Perry was previously engaged to [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom), with whom she has a daughter, Daisy Dove. Trudeau was married to Sophie Grégoire from 2005 to 2023 and they have three children.

## What happens next

Perry hasn't said whether the trip is over or whether more photos are coming. Neither fashion outlet reported that either designer has confirmed further looks. Prices in the fashion posts are in the figures the outlets printed, and the Zeynep Arcay prices come from a fan fashion account rather than the brand.

## Frequently asked questions

### What did Katy Perry wear on her Europe trip?

Fashion outlets identified a De La Vali Catalina Satin Mini Dress with lace sleeves and a Zeynep Arcay snakeskin-print leather jacket and skirt. She also wore a champagne satin-and-lace minidress to a formal evening, according to a report on her photo set.

### Is Katy Perry's bob new?

She debuted it publicly at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala, according to InStyle, but captioned the Europe photos "the bob's Euro debut".

### Where did Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau travel?

Spain, France and Italy, according to the reports. One photo was taken aboard the Orient Express, and another on the streets of Venice.
