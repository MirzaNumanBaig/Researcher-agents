**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) wore a neck-grazing blonde bob with flipped-out ends to Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on Thursday 5 November, according to The Zoe Report.
- She paired it with a sheer white dress, black heels and a black bag, as described by InStyle and Nine.
- The show was the first official Chanel runway on Australian soil, and the label's Sydney week drew about 150 international guests, Nine reports.
- Robbie reached the after-party later than most guests, where Kylie Minogue performed.

## The flippy bob that got everyone talking

Margot Robbie turned up to Chanel's first Australian runway with a fresh haircut, and fashion editors noticed it as quickly as the dress. The Oscar-nominated actor wore a [flippy blonde bob](https://www.thezoereport.com/culture/margot-robbie-flippy-bob) at the Cruise 2026/27 presentation in Sydney on 5 November, a cut that is currently popular with stylists.

The Zoe Report describes it as a neck-grazing, buttery blonde style, parted to the side, with long bangs tucked behind her ears and the ends turned out slightly. It called the look vintage-inspired, with a 1960s feel that stayed short of costume. [InStyle](https://www.instyle.com/margot-robbie-debuts-short-sharp-bob-chanel-show-12238062) saw it a little differently, calling it a sharp, blunt cut that sat just past her chin with a deep side part and a smooth, slightly flipped finish.

Both outlets agree on the main point. The cut is short, polished and new to her Sydney appearance. InStyle also reports that photographers first spotted her arriving in the city with the hair tucked under a navy headscarf.

## What Robbie wore

The dress was as talked about as the hair. Nine's coverage says Robbie chose a black and white look: a sheer white dress with a feathered flower at the centre of the bust, black heels and a handbag. InStyle adds that the slip was made of white mesh, with a checked bodice and a two-tiered fringed hem.

The Zoe Report says her makeup was kept fresh, with a glowing base, long lashes, a satiny nude lip and a pinky-nude manicure. For a cruise collection shown in the southern hemisphere's spring, it called her look vacation-ready.

We covered the outfit in more detail in our earlier report on [Robbie's sheer Chanel look in Sydney](https://moduscarry.com/margot-robbie-chanel-sydney-sheer-look/).

## A long Chanel history

Robbie is no stranger to the house. Nine notes that she was the last ambassador chosen by Karl Lagerfeld before his death in 2019. The Zoe Report adds that she first showed off her short hair at another Chanel event, the Fall/Winter 2026 presentation in March, which suggests the bob has been growing into a signature. InStyle's account of that earlier cut differs slightly, describing a wavy lob with messy bangs in March and a lighter blonde in July.

The same outlet quotes an older Glamour interview in which she said she relies on clip-in extensions and her stylists at events to make her hair look fuller. That is her own description, not a claim about this show.

## The night around her

The Sydney show was a replica of Chanel's Resort 2027 collection, first shown in Biarritz, France, and the first from creative director Matthieu Blazy, according to Nine. The two runway sessions ran at 2.30pm and 6pm at The Cutaway in Barangaroo.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), another Chanel ambassador, sat alongside Robbie. Just Jared lists [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), Joel Edgerton, Christine Centenera, [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), [Dom Dolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom_Dolla), K-pop singer Soobin and Olivia DeJonge among the other guests.

At the after-party at Pier 2/3 in Walsh Bay, Nine reports that Kidman and Luhrmann took the stage to introduce Minogue, who sang a medley including "On a Night Like This", "Can't Get You Out of My Head" and "Padam Padam". Nine says Robbie arrived a little later than most guests, in a sheer black Chanel dress.

More of her style coverage is on our [Margot Robbie page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/margot-robbie/) and in [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/).

## What happens next

Nine says the Sydney week leads into the reopening of Chanel's refurbished Melbourne boutique on Flinders Lane next week. Robbie's work schedule is busy as well: Just Jared reports she has spent recent months in Europe filming an Ocean's Eleven prequel with co-star and director Bradley Cooper.

## Frequently asked questions

### What haircut did Margot Robbie wear to the Chanel show in Sydney?
She wore a short blonde bob that sat around her chin, with a side part and flipped-out ends. The Zoe Report calls it a flippy bob, while InStyle calls it sharp and blunt.

### When was Chanel's Sydney show?
It took place on Thursday 5 November 2026 with two sessions, at 2.30pm and 6pm, at The Cutaway in Barangaroo.

### Who performed at the Chanel after-party?
Kylie Minogue performed a medley of her hits, introduced on stage by Nicole Kidman and Baz Luhrmann, according to Nine.
