The **Russell Brand deposition** has put the comedian's former marriage to Katy Perry back in the headlines. Transcripts filed in a New York civil case, and reported this week by the Daily Mail, show Brand being asked under oath about an alleged incident on the set of *Arthur* and about when he was married. Perry is not accused of anything in the case.

## What the transcripts say

According to [the Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/showbiz/russell-brand-forgot-married-katy-37901611), which cited the Daily Mail's reading of the unsealed documents, the plaintiff claims Brand exposed himself in July 2010 and later assaulted her in a bathroom. [Bsinews](https://bsinews.id/en/brand-deposition-perry-marriage) says she worked as an extra on the film and that the alleged exposure happened at Le Cirque restaurant in Manhattan.

Asked whether he had ever exposed himself to someone without consent, Brand said he felt he "must have done, even though I don't really remember it", adding that he could not "concretely and absolutely rule that out". Bsinews notes this is not an admission that the incident in the lawsuit took place. Brand maintains his sexual encounters were consensual and denies the plaintiff's allegations.

## The Katy Perry questions

Lawyers asked whether Brand was married at the time of the alleged incident. He answered that it "wouldn't be difficult" to find out, as it would be "on the internet somewhere", and said that even during the marriage he was "sort of drowning in love".

The timeline is simple. The two met in 2009 through *Get Him to the Greek*, where Perry had a cameo, and married in October 2010, roughly three months after the date in the lawsuit. They divorced in 2012, according to both outlets. Brand also described what he called a roughly ten-year pattern of seeking excitement outside relationships when he was not in love, a period he said ran to about 2015, before his 2017 marriage to Laura Gallacher.

## The civil case and the criminal case

The New York action is brought under the Adult Survivors Act. Bsinews reports that Warner Bros. and other production companies are named as defendants alongside Brand, and that a published court decision so far dealt with expert testimony, not whether the alleged assault happened.

It is separate from his criminal case in the United Kingdom. The Daily Star reports that he was recently sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault, and that he denied wrongdoing throughout his trial. For more background, see our earlier coverage of [the deposition being unsealed](https://moduscarry.com/russell-brand-deposition-unsealed/) and everything we have published under the [Russell Brand](https://moduscarry.com/tag/russell-brand/) tag.

## Perry's reported response

Perry has been dating Trudeau, Canada's former prime minister, since July 2025, according to [New Idea](https://www.newidea.com.au/celebrity/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-russell-brand/). The magazine quotes an unnamed source saying she will make no statement about Brand's imprisonment but has told friends it brought "some closure". The same source predicts a proposal at Christmas. Treat that as unconfirmed talk; neither Perry nor Trudeau has said so publicly in anything we have read.

New Idea adds that the pair were photographed on a family outing near her home in Montecito on 14 November, then attended the Baby2Baby gala in West Hollywood. She and ex-fiancé [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) share a daughter.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Katy Perry involved in the lawsuit?
No. Bsinews states that Perry has not been accused of wrongdoing. She was only referenced because Brand was asked whether he was married when the alleged incident occurred.

### Did Russell Brand admit to the allegation?
No. He denies the plaintiff's allegations. He said he could not rule out having exposed himself without consent, but the reports stress that this is not an admission about the specific incident.

### When did Russell Brand and Katy Perry marry?
They married in October 2010, after meeting in 2009, and divorced in 2012, according to the Daily Star and Bsinews.
