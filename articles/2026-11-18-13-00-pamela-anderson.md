**Quick summary**

- The [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) holiday season is now about quieter moments, she told ELLE this week.
- She says she spends her free time gardening at home on Vancouver Island and cooking vegetable dishes for family.
- Her sons are building traditions with their partners, and she says she is happy to watch rather than plan.

- The interview was given as part of her work as a face of Pandora.

The Pamela Anderson holiday season looks different this year. In an interview published by [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/fashion/jewelry/a74455413/pamela-anderson-pandora-interview-2026/) on 17 November, the actress described a calmer period built around her garden, her kitchen and her sons' growing families.

## Fall as "the season of the soul"

Asked what Thanksgiving week means to her, Anderson pointed to autumn itself. "I always say fall is the season of the soul," she told ELLE, adding that she thinks the line comes from [Virginia Woolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Woolf). In a garden, she said, the season pulls attention toward small details rather than an overload of abundance.

She compared the East Coast, where she was speaking from, with her home on Vancouver Island, which she said looks very similar in the fall, with the weather and changing leaves.

## Gardening and a working life

Anderson told ELLE she loves gardening and is gravitating toward it at this stage of her life. She described clearing leaves as a workout and said she does not mind the rain, adding that nothing would get done where she is from if she avoided it.

She framed it as a balance with her job. She said she works a lot, on film and onstage, and that she then goes home, puts her boots on and gets into the garden.

## How Pamela Anderson's holiday season has changed

The biggest shift, by her own account, is that her sons now spend the holidays with their wife and fiancée. Anderson said she tells them not to worry about her. She has done plenty of hosting, she said, and as a decorator she still loves it. This year, though, she wants to watch the traditions her sons have picked up and see them start their own lives.

"I'm not a pressure planner," she said, explaining that she does not want her sons to feel obliged to include her. She called it special, as a mother, to see them happy with their partners, and said the women in their lives have great taste and like to share it with the family.

On food, Anderson said she usually cooks the sides and vegetable dishes, and that her sons are good cooks too. It has been a while since everyone sat down to a big meal together, she noted. She said she prefers decorating to cooking because it sets the stage, and then guests can bring a dish and make it a potluck.

## Sentimental gifts and the Pandora link

The interview also covered gift-giving. Anderson described herself as a sentimentalist and said she has given much of the jewellery she has collected over her life to her sons and to their partners. One son has just married, she said, but the couple has no wedding pictures of themselves, so she is giving them wedding and engagement photographs along with other keepsakes.

She said she prefers passing pieces on while she is around to see them enjoyed, and that she does not hoard things. ELLE notes she is a face of Pandora. Anderson said her mother and her mother's sisters love the brand, and that she has worn Pandora pieces to red carpets and other events. She said she likes jewellery to be attainable, cruelty-free and sustainable.
