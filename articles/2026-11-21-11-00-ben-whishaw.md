**Quick summary**

- Sam, played by [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw), survives the season 2 finale of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves).
- He ends his contract with Patrick, says goodbye to Helen in Trafalgar Square and heads back to Italy.
- Whishaw told Town & Country he hopes Sam is free, but thinks his love for Helen would bring him back.
- Netflix has not renewed the show, though creator Joe Barton is reportedly already running a writers' room for season 3.

Sam does not die in the Black Doves season 2 finale. According to [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/does-sam-die-in-black-doves-season-2-ben-whishaws-final-scene-explained), Whishaw's hitman walks away from London and from the job in "Peace Summit", and his last scene is a goodbye rather than a death. It may not be a permanent one.

## How Sam got out of Patrick's debt

Sam spends the season paying off a deal he never really chose. He starts it in custody, until Patrick, played by [Sam Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Riley), offers to make his charges disappear in exchange for four contract killings. When Sam starts asking questions, Patrick shows him footage of Helen committing a murder years earlier. It is a scandal waiting to happen now that her husband is prime minister.

The jobs get worse. Sam kills Black Dove Erik Klein, then an elderly former agent known as Spitfire, then Alexi Chernov. The cruellest target is Jerry, the hotel concierge Sam has fallen for, played by [Samuel Barnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Barnett_(actor)). Kestrel makes Sam kill him to test his loyalty.

The final target is Wildflower, also known as Reed, played by [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire). Sam and Helen have just rescued her. In the finale Sam hands Reed over alive. Patrick then deletes the footage of Helen and tells Sam his contract is done. When Patrick offers more work, Sam refuses and says that if he ever sees Patrick again, he will kill him.

## The Trafalgar Square goodbye

After the violence at Black Doves headquarters, Sam meets Helen, played by [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley), in Trafalgar Square and tells her he is leaving. She is not ready. "If you ever need me, you just have to call," he says. "I need you now," she answers. "You'll be fine," he replies, and then he is gone.

He heads back to the Italian village where he once hid. Season 1 began with Sam pulled out of that same exile because Helen was in danger. Creator Joe Barton says the village is less a place than an idea, a symbol of a different life Sam glimpsed but could not keep. Barton adds that it is left open whether he will ever manage it this time.

## What Whishaw thinks happens next

Speaking to Town & Country alongside Knightley, Whishaw chose his words with care. "I hope that he's free, because I would wish that for him," he said. "But he does say to her, 'Just call me,' so..." He is more direct about what would bring Sam back: he believes Sam loves Helen so much that he would return.

Knightley is blunt about the pair. She says Helen and Sam have made some very bad life choices and cannot get out of them. Whishaw agrees that it is not working for either of them.

## Will Sam be in season 3?

Nothing is official. Netflix has not renewed Black Doves, according to Startefacts, but Barton is said to be plotting a third season already. Helen ends season 2 with a husband who now knows she is a spy and a Kestrel calling card in her pocket. Sam left her his number.

Watch for a Netflix decision on a third season. Until then, the finale leaves Sam alive, free for now, and one phone call away from returning.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Sam die in Black Doves season 2?

No. Sam is alive at the end of the finale and leaves London for Italy after ending his deal with Patrick.

### Has Black Doves been renewed for season 3?

Not officially. Netflix has not announced a renewal, although Joe Barton is reportedly already working with a writers' room on a third season.

### Does Ben Whishaw think Sam will return?

He hopes Sam is free, but says Sam loves Helen enough that he would come back if she called.
