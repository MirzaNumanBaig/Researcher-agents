Callum Turner's romantic comedy *One Night Only* arrives on Peacock on Friday 4 December 2026, giving the film a second life after a quiet cinema run. The date comes from [Business Upturn](https://businessupturn.com/entertainment/hollywood/hollywood-movies/one-night-only-release-date-cast-and-everything-to-know-about-monica-barbaro-and-callum-turners-rom-com/), which says the service will carry it exclusively. The news lands in the same week Turner's name resurfaced in the James Bond casting conversation.

## Callum Turner's One Night Only: what the film is

Will Gluck directed and produced the film. He also made *Easy A* and [*Anyone But You*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anyone_but_You). Travis Braun is credited as writer, and Jacqueline Monetta produces with Gluck for Universal Pictures and Olive Bridge Entertainment, per Business Upturn.

The premise is a New York where single people may have sex on only one night a year. Turner plays Owen, a man who has just been dumped. [Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro) plays Allie, a hopeful romantic. Their evening turns into a run of missteps and side quests as both look for something that lasts past sunrise.

[Julia Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Fox), [King Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Princess), Ziwe and Ben Marshall also appear, though Business Upturn does not describe their characters. Filming began in New York on 26 September 2025, and the finished film runs 102 minutes.

## How the film performed

The film opened in US theatres on 7 August 2026. Digital release followed on 8 September, and discs arrived on 20 October.

Critics were split. Business Upturn reports 43% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) and 42 on Metacritic, while audiences surveyed by CinemaScore gave it a B+. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Night_Only_(2026_film)), it cost about US$25 million to make and has grossed about US$25.2 million, roughly break-even before marketing costs. Wikipedia's summary of the reviews says some critics found the dystopian setting timely but thought the execution confusing.

Streaming is often where a film like this finds its audience. A reader who skipped the cinema now has a firm date to plan around.

## Villeneuve on what Bond needs

The other Turner thread this week is Bond. The Times of India, citing an interview with GQ, reports that Villeneuve described the qualities he sought in 007. Its headline says Pattinson beat Turner and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) to the part, though the quotes it prints do not name either of them.

"The first quality is that I need to believe that this man is a killer, because more than a spy, Bond is an assassin," Villeneuve said, as quoted by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/dune-director-denis-villeneuve-reveals-why-robert-pattinson-beat-callum-turner-jack-lowden-to-bag-james-bond-role/articleshow/136043801.cms). He also asked for charisma, dark humour and "deep intelligence in the eyes". He accepted the choice would divide audiences, saying some people would be excited and others disappointed.

The report adds that Pattinson succeeds [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) and is reportedly due to start filming in 2027. For earlier coverage of Turner's link to the role, see our report on [Callum Turner and the Bond tip from The Odyssey's Croasdell](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-james-bond-croasdell/).
