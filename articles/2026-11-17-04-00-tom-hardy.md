**Quick summary**

- The MobLand cast met for a dinner at London's Soho Mews House on Saturday, ahead of series 3 filming, according to the Daily Mail.
- Dame Helen Mirren and Pierce Brosnan attended; Tom Hardy, 49, was not among those pictured.
- The Daily Mail linked his absence to earlier rumours of tension on set. Neither Hardy nor the show has confirmed any problem.
- Separately, Yahoo News UK is pointing MobLand fans to Hardy's 2020 film Capone as a darker watch.

The MobLand cast gathered in London this weekend without its leading man. [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) was not among those pictured at a cast dinner on Saturday, the Daily Mail reported, while [Dame Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) were photographed mingling over wine and champagne. The evening comes ahead of filming on series 3, and it has put Tom Hardy and MobLand back in the headlines.

## What happened at the MobLand cast dinner

According to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16294463/Helen-Mirren-reunites-MobLand-Pierce-Brosnan.html), the dinner was held at Soho Mews House, an exclusive venue in central London. Mirren, 81, and Brosnan, 73, were joined by other cast members, and the group appeared to follow an unofficial dress code of black or dark grey.

Mirren wore a black outfit with sheer patterned sleeves and a white collar, with her usual blunt bob and fringe. Brosnan chose a grey suit and an open-necked white shirt. The pictures show a relaxed, social night, which is a notable detail for a show whose stars have been the subject of backstage talk.

## Why Hardy's absence is drawing attention

The Daily Mail described Hardy as "conspicuous by his absence" and tied that to speculation about on-set issues. It is worth being clear about what this is. The paper cites rumours, not a statement from Hardy, his representatives or the production. A missing name on a guest list can have many ordinary explanations, from filming schedules to travel to a simple diary clash, and none has been offered either way.

We have covered the earlier talk before. Brosnan has previously played down a dispute over scripts as a [“storm in a teacup”](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-2-scripts/), and our look at [the season 3 question](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3/) set out what had been reported on the show's future. Until someone involved says otherwise, the dinner is best read as a single night out that one cast member did not attend.

## Capone: the darker film fans are being pointed to

While the cast dinner drew the headlines, a quieter story is running alongside it. Yahoo News UK has called Hardy's 2020 film Capone a good next watch for anyone who enjoyed his turn as the Harrigan family's ruthless fixer Harry da Souza, describing it as "like MobLand but darker".

The film follows Al Capone after nearly eight years in prison, back at his Palm Island estate in Florida. His dementia is worsening, the family's money is shrinking and federal agents are watching. Capone tells an old associate he has hidden US$10 million somewhere on the property but cannot remember where.

Director Josh Trank told Esquire, as quoted by Yahoo, that he saw it as "more of an experiential film about dementia" than a standard biographical crime story. [Linda Cardellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linda_Cardellini) plays his wife, Mae. Yahoo's reviewer called Hardy's performance mesmerising, though it also warned that this is not a conventional gangster film.
