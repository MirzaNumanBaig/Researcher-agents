**Quick summary**

- Promotional imagery for *Star Wars: Starfighter* shows the Rebel Alliance symbol turned upside down with two crossed blasters beneath it, which looks like a skull and crossbones.
- The film opens in cinemas on 28 May 2027, with Shawn Levy directing and Ryan Gosling playing Kade Auberon.
- Lucasfilm has not announced a mature rating or said the emblem is the official title logo.
- Separately, Gabriel Luna told The Hollywood Reporter he is now open to returning as Ghost Rider alongside Gosling's Marvel reboot, due 28 July 2028.

A new Ryan Gosling Starfighter promo image has fans talking about [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s first [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) film looking a little different from the rest of the franchise. According to [Disney Dining](https://www.disneydining.com/disney-and-lucasfilm-break-star-wars-tradition-for-darker-2027-ryan-gosling-release-sb1/), the imagery for *[Star Wars: Starfighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars%3A_Starfighter)* flips the Rebel Alliance insignia and adds crossed blasters, creating something closer to a pirate flag than a symbol of heroic resistance.

## What the emblem looks like

The design takes a well-known rebel badge, inverts it and places two blasters underneath. The result reads as a skull and crossbones. [Disney Fanatic](https://www.disneyfanatic.com/lucasfilm-overhauls-classic-star-wars-formula-for-grittier-2027-ryan-gosling-film-sb1/) reports that it has appeared in imagery linked to Gosling's character, which has fuelled talk about his background.

Both outlets add a caution. The emblem is not necessarily the film's official title logo, and one image cannot settle the tone of a whole movie. It is a clue, not a confirmation.

## Who Ryan Gosling plays in Starfighter

Gosling plays Kade Auberon, described as a cynical outsider with a mysterious past. Comparisons to [Han Solo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Solo) have followed, mostly because Kade appears more guarded than the franchise's usual heroes.

The story is set about five years after *The Rise of Skywalker* (2019). It steps away from the Skywalker family and introduces new characters in a period the films have barely touched.

[Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) directs. He previously made *Deadpool & Wolverine* (2024) and [*Free Guy*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Guy) (2021), so he has plenty of experience with large action-comedy productions.

## A break from 50 years of branding

The original 1977 film gave Star Wars its yellow title lettering, and that look carried through the prequels and sequels. Even spin-offs such as [*Rogue One*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_One) (2016) and *Solo* (2018) kept recognisable franchise branding while using their own promotional styles.

Turning a rebel symbol into something threatening is a clear departure. The franchise has visited criminal territory before, in *The Mandalorian* (2019) and *Skeleton Crew* (2024), which featured space pirates. Gosling has also played troubled leads in *Drive* (2011) and *Blade Runner 2049* (2017), the second a sequel to the [1982 original](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner).

The film lands during the franchise's 50th-anniversary year. Disney Fanatic lists *Ahsoka* season 2 for 20 January 2027 and the documentary *The Hero Awakens* for 4 May on Disney+.

## Gabriel Luna is ready for Ghost Rider

[ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/ghost-rider-mcu-ryan-gosling-reboot-gabriel-luna-return-response/) reports that Marvel confirmed a Ghost Rider reboot at San Diego Comic-Con in 2026, with Gosling attached and Levy directing again. The outlet says *Heated Rivalry* actor Connor Storrie is in talks for a villain role and the film is on track for 28 July 2028. Which version of the character Gosling will play has not been revealed.

Luna played Robbie Reyes in the fourth season of *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* In an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, as relayed by ScreenRant, he said he used to give a "stock answer" about returning because he did not want to tarnish the original work. His position has shifted. He now says he is "absolutely open to it" if the timing and story work, and that a cameo could honour fans who have kept the character alive for ten years.

Luna is 43, and Reyes is usually much younger in the comics. He suggested ageing the character up as time passes in the story.
