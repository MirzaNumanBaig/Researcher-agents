**Quick summary**

- A pop song called "Nicole Kidman" by 22-year-old Adéla went viral on TikTok and reached No. 1 on Spotify, according to Yahoo.
- Yahoo reports Kidman was shown smiling and ducking her head as the song played at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show.
- Kidman's 18-year-old daughter, Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, spoke to Vogue Germany about growing up with famous parents.
- Sunday Rose made her runway debut in October 2024, and says her mother asked her to wait until she was 16 to model.

Two separate stories this week have put [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) at the centre of the conversation without a film release in sight. A pop song borrowing her name is climbing the charts, and her eldest daughter with [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban) has given a candid interview about life in a famous family.

## The song that borrows Kidman's name

According to a [Yahoo feature published on 20 October](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/article/adela-made-the-real-nicole-kidman-blush-how-the-grungy-pink-haired-ballerina-is-shaking-up-pop-music-200833758.html), Adéla's single "Nicole Kidman" went viral on TikTok and hit No. 1 on Spotify. The track name-checks the actor through loose references to her roles, and its video has the singer covered in cake and icing.

Yahoo says Kidman herself appeared to enjoy it. The piece describes her smiling and ducking her head as the song played at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show. The outlet frames the moment as a timely endorsement during a press tour, though it does not quote Kidman on the song.

Adéla, who is 22, released her first album only in September, per the same report. Her second charting track, "Ain't in LA", is also getting heavy play on TikTok and the charts.

## Who is Adéla?

Yahoo traces her path through The Debut: Dream Academy, a reality series that cut 20 hopefuls down to six members of the group Katseye. Adéla went out in the first round after receiving the fewest fan votes. She grew up in Slovakia and began intensive ballet lessons in Russia at age 3, the article says, while studying the work of [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears) and Madonna.

Spotify's pop editorial lead, Talia Kraines, told Yahoo that "pop loves an underdog". The feature also lists [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson), [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan), [Sabrina Carpenter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Carpenter) and Charli xcx as artists who broke through after years of grinding. Adéla has since opened for [Demi Lovato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Lovato) and played a sold-out New York show on 19 September.

## Sunday Rose on growing up with famous parents

The second story is more personal. Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, 18, told Vogue Germany she is "very aware" of the opportunities she has been given, as reported by [Yahoo, citing Parade](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/sunday-rose-kidman-urban-reflects-162140529.html) and by [Taste of Country](https://tasteofcountry.com/sunday-rose-kidman-urban-famous-parents-keith-urban-nicole-kidman/).

She said she tries to work hard, keep a good attitude and treat everyone with respect, however tired or jet-lagged she is. Asked whether she hides who her parents are, she said she had not intentionally told anyone, but that few people live in her area, so most knew when she started school.

School, she explained, is what keeps her grounded. She said she initially struggled with the contrast between modelling and ordinary life, and now enjoys both.

## A runway debut and one condition

Sunday Rose walked in Miu Miu's spring/summer 2025 show at Paris Fashion Week in October 2024. She told Vogue Germany she arrived believing she knew a lot about modelling and soon realised she knew nothing, calling the work behind a show "truly insane" and inspiring.

Taste of Country adds that she first raised modelling at 14, and Kidman told her to wait until she was 16. That is the only detail either outlet gives about her mother's role in the decision.

## What happens next

Neither report announces new projects. Adéla's rise will be tested by whether "Nicole Kidman" holds its chart position in the coming weeks, and Sunday Rose's modelling work continues alongside school.
