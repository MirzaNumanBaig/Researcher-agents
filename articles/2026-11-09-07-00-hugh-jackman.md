**Quick summary**
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) served as honorary captain for the University of Michigan Wolverines at their game against rivals Michigan State.
- He gave the team a pregame talk about Gerald Ford and Willis Ward, built around the No. 48 on his jersey.
- Michigan lost 15-6, according to HELLO!.
- His girlfriend, [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster), joined him in matching Michigan gear.

Hugh Jackman swapped the Broadway stage for a football sideline over the weekend, taking on the role of honorary captain for the University of Michigan Wolverines in their rivalry game against Michigan State. The Australian actor, 58, used the moment to tell the squad a story about loyalty rather than touchdowns, even though the Wolverines went down 15-6.

## The joke that started it

Before the game, Jackman posted on Instagram, as reported by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/hugh-jackman-girlfriend-sutton-foster-163614000.html), addressing the team "from one Wolverine to the others". He nodded to his long-running [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) character and joked that the playbook had not reached him yet, wondering aloud whether that was deliberate.

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/933827/sutton-foster-hugh-jackman-rare-date-photo/), neither Jackman nor Foster attended the university, but he arrived as a "Wolverine" in his own right, and he shared photos and videos from the visit throughout the day. Fans filled the comments with puns on the shared name. Our earlier report covers [Jackman's appointment as Michigan's honorary captain](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackman-michigan-honorary-captain/).

## Why the jersey said 48

Jackman wore a jersey with the number 48. It was not a random pick. As AOL reports, the number honours former President Gerald Ford, the 48th person to hold the office by the count Jackman used, and the story attached to it.

He told the players that in 1936 Ford was a Michigan teammate and close friend of [Willis Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willis_Ward), a Black athlete who was the star of the team. When Georgia Tech threatened not to play if Ward took the field, Jackman said, the university's sporting director agreed and benched him.

According to Jackman's retelling, Ford was furious, wrote to his father that he wanted to leave the university and told the sporting director he would not play. Ward then took Ford aside and told him to play and beat them. Jackman said that is what happened the next day, and that the two stayed friends for life. He added that the crooked "4" on the Ford jersey is a memento of that friendship.

The details are Jackman's account of the historical episode, as quoted by AOL from social media footage, and we have not checked them against other sources.

## The message to the team

Jackman said he wanted the players to take away something about "character and standing up for what matters". In his words, the story is about backing the teammate beside you whatever the stakes. "To me, that's what it is to be a Wolverine," he said.

The speech did not change the result. Michigan lost 15-6 to its in-state rivals, according to HELLO!. AOL printed the score the other way round, as 6-15, which reflects the same defeat.

## A rare couple appearance

Jackman and Foster, 51, wore matching Michigan hoodies, jeans and team hats, AOL reports. HELLO! notes the pair worked together on *The Music Man* in 2022 and began publicly dating in 2025, and that they have kept the relationship largely private. The photo from the game, with both smiling and Foster in blue and yellow sneakers, is a rare public glimpse of them together.
