**Quick summary**

- A line in Season 4, episode 9 of *Only Murders in the Building* nods to a real habit shared by Martin Short and Steve Martin, according to Mashable.
- The habit is described in Short's 2014 memoir, *I Must Say: My Life as a Humble Comedy Legend*.
- Short and Martin also discussed it on *Jimmy Kimmel Live* in 2018.
- Separately, Short says his comfort food is a club sandwich, according to Allrecipes via Artvoice.

[Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) have long traded insults on screen and off, and the latest run of [Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building) worked a real piece of their friendship into a scripted heart-to-heart. Mashable's entertainment editor Kristy Puchko flagged the moment in a piece published on 23 November 2026. Over the same weekend, a separate report had Short naming a simple favourite comfort meal.

## The scene in "Escape from Planet Klongo"

In Season 4, episode 9, titled "Escape from Planet Klongo", the two lead characters have a very public argument on the set of the latest film from [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard), according to [Mashable](https://mashable.com/article/only-murders-in-the-building-colonoscopy). Charles, played by Martin, snaps at Oliver, played by Short, over Oliver's demands for a top-notch bachelor party. Both are in motion-capture suits in front of background actors and crew when it happens.

Charles apologises soon after, and the pair head to a Chinese restaurant called Red Dragon Palace. Oliver tells Charles they will be friends to the end, and Charles, unable to cope with the sincerity, hides behind his menu. Oliver then reassures him that they can have colonoscopies together.

## Where the joke comes from

Mashable says the line is not a throwaway. In his 2014 memoir, Short described a tradition that he, Martin, [Tom Hanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks) and producer Walter Parks keep roughly every other year. They gather at Martin's home the night before their medical appointments, playing poker and sticking to Jell-O and broth. According to the book, as summarised by Mashable, they are not in the room together for the exam itself. They drive to their synchronised appointments together and go out for lunch and margaritas afterwards.

The two comedians also talked about the ritual on *Jimmy Kimmel Live* in 2018. Short joked then that the one drawback was that Martin's house has only one bathroom. Mashable quotes him comparing it by late evening to "day 14 of a Carnival Cruise".

Mashable also points to a possible echo in another series. It notes that the episode of [Hacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacks) titled "Join the Club" has the fictional comedian Deborah Vance invited to a similar gathering of older male comics. The outlet describes this as seemingly inspired by the same story, though that is its reading rather than anything the show has confirmed.

## A friendship that goes back to 1986

Mashable traces the pair's bond to the 1986 comedy *Three Amigos*. It argues that their on-screen sparring works because viewers can sense the decades of real affection under it. For fans of the series, the colonoscopy line is a small reward: it plays as a gag inside the story and as a nod to something the two men have said publicly they do.

## Short's club sandwich

In lighter news, Artvoice reports that Short has a plain answer when asked about comfort food: a club sandwich. The quote is attributed to Allrecipes, which says his preferred version uses turkey and cranberry, a combination it links to Thanksgiving leftovers. Artvoice adds that Short and Martin have joked before about a shared love of poached eggs and about judging hotels by how they cook them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which episode of Only Murders in the Building mentions colonoscopies?

It is Season 4, episode 9, "Escape from Planet Klongo", in a lunch scene at the Red Dragon Palace restaurant, according to Mashable.

### Is the colonoscopy joke based on something real?

Mashable says yes. Short wrote about a recurring pre-appointment get-together with Steve Martin, Tom Hanks and producer Walter Parks in his 2014 memoir, and discussed it on *Jimmy Kimmel Live* in 2018.

### What is Martin Short's comfort food?

A club sandwich, according to Allrecipes as reported by Artvoice. His favourite version has turkey and cranberry.
