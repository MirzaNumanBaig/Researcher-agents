**Quick summary**

- [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) says she burst into tears on the *Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* set after her dialect coach touched her feet following a scene.
- She shared the story in an interview with content creator Pallavi Raj, according to India Today and The Times of India.
- The film, directed by [Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari), opened in theatres on 5 November 2026.
- The Times of India, citing Sacnilk, reports a worldwide gross of Rs 1,033.55 crore within eight days.

The Sai Pallavi Sita role has meant to audiences than a film part, she says. The actor told Pallavi Raj on the YouTube channel Myshadowbeats that she wept on the set of *Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* when her dialect coach touched her feet after a scene, according to [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/movies/bollywood/story/sai-pallavi-broke-down-on-ramayana-sets-after-dialect-coach-touched-her-feet-3037886-2026-11-13). The interview was reported on 13 November, eight days after the film's release.

## What Sai Pallavi said happened on set

Pallavi told the interviewer this was one of the few times during filming that she felt overwhelmed. The coach had seen her making phone calls and getting ready between takes, so the gesture caught her off guard.

"She knows I am Sai Pallavi, but sometimes when you play a character that you worship every day... you do not care about whether that's an actor or any other human being," she said. "You just want that love."

She said she started crying before the coach did. "I said, 'What are you even doing?'" she recalled. The moment, she added, made her feel that the character "speaks so much more to so many people". [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/bollywood/news/sai-pallavi-recalls-breaking-down-on-ramayana-rise-of-a-legend-sets-reveals-her-dialect-coach-touched-her-feet-after-scene/articleshow/135786002.cms) read the episode as a sign of the emotional responsibility that comes with playing a figure many people worship.

## How she approached playing Sita

India Today points back to an earlier appearance on [Kaun Banega Crorepati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaun_Banega_Crorepati), where she promoted the film with co-stars [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) and [Ravi Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey). There she said she did not want to "use her head" this time.

Normally she asks what she can give a character. For Sita, she said, she asked what she could receive, and what she might learn. She chose to keep the process transparent, prayed to Sita and went ahead.

That approach sits oddly with the public argument around the casting. Our earlier coverage looked at how [Sai Pallavi's Sita has drawn praise and pushback](https://moduscarry.com/sai-pallavi-sita-ramayana-reactions/), and at [Lara Dutta's defence of a warrior Sita](https://moduscarry.com/sai-pallavi-warrior-sita-lara-dutta/). The on-set story gives a quieter counterpoint from someone who watched the work up close.

## The film behind the story

*Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* stars Ranbir Kapoor as Lord Rama, Sai Pallavi as Sita and Yash as Ravana. India Today lists Ravi Dubey as Lakshman, and says [Namit Malhotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namit_Malhotra) and Yash are co-producers.

On numbers, The Times of India reports that the film passed Rs 1,000 crore worldwide in eight days, reaching Rs 1,033.55 crore by Sacnilk's count. That makes it the 11th Indian film to join the club. These are tracker figures, not studio-confirmed ones.

A separate NDTV report mentions veteran actor Jaya Prada sharing a throwback picture from *Lav Kush* amid the Sita debate. We could not open that page, so we have not relied on it.
