**Quick summary**

- Singer Rosalía has joined the cast of *Wet*, a new film from director Alma Ha'rel, according to The Hollywood Reporter as relayed by Stereogum.
- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is also in the cast, alongside Odessa A'zion, Rebecca Ferguson, Javier Bardem and Lukas Haas.
- Lionsgate will distribute the film in North America and the UK.
- Production is set to begin in January in the Dominican Republic. No story details have been released.

The cast of *Wet* just got a lot more interesting. Rosalía, the Spanish singer who made her acting debut this year, has signed on to the new film from Alma Ha'rel, where she will appear opposite [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) and Odessa A'zion. The news was first reported by The Hollywood Reporter and picked up by [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2517751/rosalia-sets-next-acting-role-in-wet-alongside-connor-storrie-odessa-azion/news) on 12 November.

## Who is in the Connor Storrie Wet cast?

Stereogum's account of the Hollywood Reporter story lists a crowded line-up. Besides Storrie, Rosalía and A'zion, the film includes [Rebecca Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Ferguson), [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem) and [Lukas Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Haas). That is six names with very different screen histories in a single project, which is part of why the announcement has drawn attention.

Storrie was already tied to the project. Our earlier report, [Connor Storrie Leads Cast of Lionsgate's Wet](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-wet-lionsgate/), covered his place at the front of the film. Rosalía's arrival adds a high-profile musician to a cast that already mixes film veterans with rising names. You can follow everything we publish on the actor on our [Connor Storrie tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/connor-storrie/).

## Who is making the film?

Ha'rel directs. Stereogum describes her as the director of *Honey Boy* and the Apple TV+ miniseries *Lady in the Lake*. She co-wrote *Wet* with Christian Stangeby. Lionsgate has the North American and UK rights, so Australian release plans have not been confirmed.

Ha'rel gave a short statement about the film. She said she has lately had to find humour in chaos and humanity in unpredictable situations, and that she is grateful to Lionsgate for backing a film that speaks to "this wild moment in time". She added that she is excited to bring the cast together to make a film "that refuses to behave". That is the only hint of tone so far.

## Rosalía's next screen step

Stereogum notes that Rosalía has had a busy year since her 2025 album *LUX*. It lists a global arena tour, a performance on [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) and a duet with Fontaines D.C. She also made her acting debut in the final season of *Euphoria*, playing a character named Magick. Stereogum says her screen time was limited but she made an impression.

*Wet* would be her second known screen role. For readers following the crossover between music and film, our [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) and [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) sections track similar moves.

## What happens next

Production is due to start in January in the Dominican Republic, per the report. Plot, characters and a release date have not been announced, and none of the sources describe who plays whom. Until Lionsgate or the filmmakers say more, anything beyond the cast and crew list is guesswork.
