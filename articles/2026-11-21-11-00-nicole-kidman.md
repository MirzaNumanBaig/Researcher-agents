**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) wore a deconstructed black tuxedo with straight-leg trousers to Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney.
- The show was the French house's first Australian runway, staged at The Cutaway in Barangaroo, according to Grazia.
- Her 15-year-old daughter sat beside her, in a cream jacket and matching miniskirt.
- Fellow Australian guest [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) chose a sheer white slip dress.

Nicole Kidman turned up to Chanel's first Australian runway show in a pair of trousers, and it is the look people are talking about. The Oscar winner wore a cropped black evening jacket and fluid straight-leg trousers to the Cruise 2026/27 presentation in Sydney, [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/articles/nicole-kidmans-chanel-trousers-just-made-the-case-for-a-different-kind-of-eveningwear/) reports, while the guests around her leaned towards gowns and lace.

## The Nicole Kidman Chanel trousers look

The venue was The Cutaway in Barangaroo, an underground space beneath a sandstone cliff face. Grazia says the show drew a group of Australian ambassadors for the house.

Kidman's outfit was built around a tuxedo that had been taken apart. Working with stylist [Jason Bolden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bolden), she chose a short jacket with formal coattails at the back, a shape normally tied to white-tie dressing. Relaxed black trousers fell over satin pointed-toe pumps.

The layers underneath were the surprise. She left the jacket open over a thin white waistcoat, also unbuttoned, and a plain white scoop-neck tank top. Dark sunglasses finished it. Grazia calls the result polished but deliberately undone, and suggests it works as a template for tailoring in warm weather.

## Why trousers stood out

Grazia's argument is that trousers can carry as much presence as a dress. It sets Kidman's monochrome outfit against Robbie's sheer white slip dress, and says each made a different point.

The magazine also notes Kidman's long history with the label, from the era of designer [Karl Lagerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Lagerfeld) to the current chapter under Matthieu Blazy. Its reading is that she skipped tulle and floor-length hems on purpose, in favour of drape and posture. That is Grazia's view, not a statement from Kidman or Chanel, and neither has commented on the choice in the coverage we reviewed.

## A mother-daughter front row

Kidman was joined in Sydney by her 15-year-old daughter, who had already sat in the front row with her mother in Paris earlier this year, according to Grazia. For the Sydney show she wore a cream ribbed zip-up jacket with gold-studded pockets and a matching miniskirt, plus a camel suede bag and strappy silver mules.

The magazine describes the pairing as two generations of the house: Kidman in sharp black tailoring, her daughter in soft, textured cream.

## Kidman's busy pop-culture season

The fashion moment lands in a crowded few months for Kidman. The Film Experience wrote this week that she has been unusually visible in pop culture this autumn, even though [Practical Magic 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic) got a lukewarm audience response and her spring series *Scarpetta* was a hit with viewers but did not win over critics.

The same piece points to two recent music videos. Australian singer [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) praises her in the video for "She's the Best", and the song "Nicole Kidman" by Adela, which The Film Experience says reached the Billboard top 10, borrows imagery from *Eyes Wide Shut*, *Moulin Rouge!*, *Babygirl* and *Birth*.

On the film itself, Wikipedia lists *Practical Magic 2* as released on 26 August 2026 and directed by Susanne Bier, with a reported budget of US$75 million and a US$106 million gross. Wikipedia also records a 35% Rotten Tomatoes score.
