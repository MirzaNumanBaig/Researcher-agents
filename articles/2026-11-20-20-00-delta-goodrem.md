**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and professional partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) are through to Blackpool Week on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing).
- Kai was taken to hospital before week eight, so [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) danced with Delta at 24 hours' notice.
- That Argentine tango scored 35 from the judges and left the pair second on the leaderboard, according to the Blackpool Gazette.
- Kai says he is on the mend and will be back for the Tower Ballroom Quickstep.
- A body language expert argues Delta's polished image could cost her the Glitterball.

Delta Goodrem has made it to Blackpool Week on Strictly Come Dancing, and her regular partner Kai Widdrington is fit to dance with her again after a health scare that put him in hospital last weekend. The pair will perform a Quickstep to Eternal's "I Wanna Be the Only One" at the Tower Ballroom, according to the Daily Star.

## What happened to Kai Widdrington

The disruption hit just before week eight of the competition. The Blackpool Gazette reports that Kai fell ill ahead of the Saturday show and was taken to hospital. Skorjanec was called in at the last minute and rehearsed with Delta through the evening and again the next morning.

The result was an Argentine tango to "Missing" by Everything But The Girl. The judges gave it 35, which kept Delta second on the leaderboard, the Gazette says.

Kai later described the scramble on Instagram, as quoted by the Daily Star. He said Skorjanec got the call at about 5.30pm on Friday and came in to rehearse. Kai called dancing with someone new in 24 hours "absolutely unheard of" on the show, and said he watched from home "gleaming with pride".

On Monday he posted that he was recovering. He thanked the paramedics, hospital staff and his girlfriend, and wrote that he would be back and "can't wait" to go to Blackpool with Delta. The pair resumed training and posted a photo on Thursday to say they had arrived, the Gazette reports. You can read our earlier coverage in [Delta Goodrem and Kai Widdrington Reunite After Hospital Scare](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-kai-widdrington-strictly/).

## A bumpy run for the 42-year-old singer

This was not the first interruption. The Daily Star notes that Delta, 42, already missed a week earlier in the series because of injury. Even so, she has scored consistently high, and her stand-in tango left her second on the leaderboard.

Blackpool also brings Delta back to the Fylde coast. The Gazette says she last appeared in the area on the Lytham Festival bill in July 2024, a lineup headed by [Shania Twain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shania_Twain).

## The one thing that could cost her the Glitterball

Not everyone thinks high scores are enough. Body language expert Judi James, speaking to Paddy Power and quoted by the Daily Star, argues that Delta's "perfect" professional image may hold viewers at a distance. James says audiences have grown attached to other contestants who let their guard down, and describes Delta's manner as polished to the point of looking carved from concrete.

James singles out what happens after each routine. In her view, Delta tends to turn to presenter [Johannes Radebe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Radebe) on the balcony rather than staying with her dancing partner, and she did so again last week with Skorjanec beside her. James says this can leave partners looking sidelined and can make it seem like Delta's interview rather than theirs.

This is one commentator's reading of body language, not a judge's verdict or a fact about the contest, and it was commissioned by a betting brand. Delta's scores speak for themselves.

The Daily Star says she faces strong rivals in Blackpool, including [Lacey Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacey_Turner), Love Island winner [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) and Olympian Tabby Stoecker.

## What happens next

Delta and Kai take to the Tower Ballroom floor for the Blackpool show with their Quickstep. After a week of upheaval, it is the first time the pair will dance together on the live show since the scare. Whether the judges' scores and the public vote keep them in the hunt for the Glitterball will become clear as the series moves toward its final weeks.
