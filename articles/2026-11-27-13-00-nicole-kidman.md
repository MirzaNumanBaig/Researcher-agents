**Quick summary**

- *The Young People*, a horror film starring [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), has received an R rating from the MPA.
- The rating cites strong bloody violence, gore, language, some teen drug use and brief sexual material or nudity.
- [Osgood Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osgood_Perkins) writes and directs; NEON will release it in cinemas.
- There is no official release date yet, and plot details remain under wraps.

[Nicole Kidman](https://nicolekidmanofficial.com/) is heading into darker territory. Her new horror film *The Young People* has been given an R rating by the Motion Picture Association, according to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2231494-nicole-kidman-osgood-perkins-the-young-people-gruesome-mpa-rating) and [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/nicole-kidman-osgood-perkins-young-170737000.html), which carried the report on 26 November.

## What the R rating says

The MPA's bulletin, as quoted by both outlets, rates the film R for "strong bloody violence, gore, language, some teen drug use, and brief sexual material/nudity." That is a clear signal the film will not hold back on the horror front.

In the United States, an R rating means viewers under 17 need an accompanying parent or adult guardian. Australian audiences will get their own classification when the film is submitted locally, and nothing on that has been announced.

## Who is in the cast

Kidman joins an ensemble led by [Lola Tung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Tung) and [Nico Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nico_Parker). The cast also includes [Tatiana Maslany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatiana_Maslany), [Brendan Hines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Hines), [Cush Jumbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cush_Jumbo), [Heather Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Graham), [Johnny Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Knoxville), [Lexi Minetree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexi_Minetree) and [Lily Collias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_Collias).

Tung has recently described the shoot as strange but exciting, and said she felt scared on set at times, something she had not experienced before, per the same reports.

## The filmmaker and the studio

Perkins is writing and directing. ComingSoon.net reports that this is his fourth consecutive horror feature with NEON in a short span, after *Longlegs*, *The Monkey* and *Keeper*. It is also the first project under his first-look deal with NEON, held through his production company Phobos.

He is producing alongside Chris Ferguson and Brian Kavanaugh-Jones. NEON will distribute the film theatrically and is handling international sales. It is co-financing the project with [Lyrical Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyrical_Media).

## What we know about the story

Very little is confirmed. An unconfirmed synopsis suggests the film follows two school friends whose bond turns sinister after one begins to behave in a disturbing way. Treat that as a rumour until NEON says otherwise.

A script image shared by NEON also mentioned "The Old Ones", which has set off speculation about Lovecraft-style influences. That is fan theory at this stage, not a studio statement.

## What happens next

There is no official release date. The rating suggests the film is finished or close to it, though the sources do not say so directly. Expect NEON to announce a date and a first trailer in due course.
