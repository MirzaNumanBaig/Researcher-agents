**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is set to voice John Wick in Lionsgate's animated prequel, according to Variety as relayed by Inverse on 6 November 2026.
- The film is an origin story built around the "Impossible Task", the one-night job that frees Wick from his debt to the High Table.
- Shannon Tindle directs, and Ryan and Kaz Firpo wrote the script.
- No title, release date or further cast had been reported in the coverage we could read.

Keanu Reeves is returning as John Wick, but audiences will only hear him this time. According to a report from Variety, picked up by [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/entertainment/john-wick-animated-prequel-keanu-reeves) on 6 November 2026, Reeves will voice the assassin in an animated prequel from Lionsgate. IMDb also carried the news in its entertainment coverage this week.

## What the animated prequel is about

The film is an origin story. Inverse reports that it follows Wick through the Impossible Task, the single night of killing he must complete to cancel his obligation to the High Table. That job is mentioned in the live-action films as the feat that let him leave the criminal underworld, so the prequel goes back to a period fans have only heard described.

Because the film is animated, Reeves will provide the voice only. Inverse notes there will be no digital de-ageing of the actor, which is the usual route live-action franchises take when they revisit a character's younger years.

## Who is making it

Lionsgate is the studio. Shannon Tindle, described by Inverse as an animation veteran, is directing. The script comes from Ryan and Kaz Firpo, who previously co-wrote Marvel's *Eternals*.

The project is not new. Inverse says it was first announced the previous year without any casting details, so the latest report is the first to put Reeves' name on it. The coverage we could read does not name the animation studio, a title or a release date.

The prequel adds to a growing John Wick slate. One caution on what is confirmed. We found no official Lionsgate statement in the pages we could open, so the casting detail rests on trade reporting. Other outlets have also written about a fifth live-action film, but their accounts of whether Reeves has formally committed to it differ, and we have left that question out until there is a clear confirmation.

## What happens next

Expect Lionsgate to announce a title, the animation partner, a release window and the supporting voice cast in due course. None had been reported in the coverage we could read. Until then, the Impossible Task is the one story detail on the record, and it is the part of Wick's past fans have wanted to see for years.
