Sydney distillery Archie Rose, which counts Chris Hemsworth as a co-owner, has appointed Cosmo5 Group to lead its awareness media strategy as it prepares to expand into the United States, according to trade publication [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/archie-rose-pours-itself-into-the-us-market-with-cosmo5-group-chris-hemsworth/).

## What the appointment covers

B&T reports that Cosmo5 Group will handle awareness media strategy, planning and buying across Australia, as well as supporting the brand's move into the US market. The work will be managed by Ryvalmedia, a part of Cosmo5 Group, which will work alongside Archie Rose's in-house performance marketing team on what the publication describes as an integrated, full-funnel approach.

Ryvalmedia's Sydney general manager, Bianca Falloon, said the agency's job was to grow the Australian whisky category in the US and "make Archie Rose the first brand Americans reach for", B&T reported.

## A new campaign fronted by Hemsworth

The announcement coincided with a product and advertising launch. According to B&T, on Wednesday 21 October Archie Rose released an update to its three core whiskies, developed with Hemsworth, along with a global ad campaign. The campaign was written and co-directed by Hemsworth, who also appears in the film alongside his brother, [Luke Hemsworth](https://moduscarry.com/tag/luke-hemsworth/).

Victoria Tulloch, Archie Rose's head of marketing, said the company had "a massive job to do" in launching Australian whisky as a category in the US and around the world, with the new campaign created together with Hemsworth. She said the brand was pleased to work with Cosmo5 and Ryvalmedia on a media plan to build awareness with new global audiences.

## About Archie Rose

B&T notes that Archie Rose Distilling Co. was founded in Sydney in 2014 by Will Edwards, with Hemsworth announced as a co-owner in 2026. The distillery is known for using locally sourced Australian ingredients and a grain-to-glass approach, and makes whisky, gin and vodka. Its original distillery in Rosebery also operates as a bar, cellar door and spirits experience.

## What happens next

No launch date for the US rollout was given in the B&T report. The article does not detail the size of the media budget or which American markets will be targeted first. For now, the agency appointment and the updated whisky range suggest the brand is preparing a coordinated push, with Hemsworth's involvement as co-owner, writer and co-director of the campaign central to its pitch.
