**Quick summary**

- London studio Union VFX has released a breakdown reel of its effects work on Wild Horse Nine, the new thriller from director [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh).
- The work included computer-generated period aircraft, face replacements and enhanced on-screen violence, according to Animation World Network.
- It is Union's third film with McDonagh, after Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri and The Banshees of Inisherin.
- The film opened in cinemas on November 4 after a festival run that took in Venice and Toronto.

The effects you are not meant to notice are the subject of a new reel from Union VFX. The London studio has published a breakdown of its work on [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), Martin McDonagh's 1973-set thriller, showing how it built period aircraft, replaced faces and sharpened screen violence, [Animation World Network reported](https://www.awn.com/news/union-vfx-breaks-down-martin-mcdonaghs-wild-horse-nine).

## What is Wild Horse Nine about?

Animation World Network describes the film as a darkly funny thriller. It follows two CIA agents, Chris and Lee, played by John Malkovich and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell). Their mission takes them from Santiago, Chile, to Easter Island in 1973 and tests how far they trust each other.

The supporting ensemble includes [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits). Blueprint Pictures and Searchlight Pictures produced it.

Our [Martin McDonagh tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/martin-mcdonagh/) collects earlier coverage of the film.

## What did Union VFX actually do?

The brief was for effects that audiences would never clock. Union's creative director, Simon Hughes, led the work again. In London, the team handled cleanup, wire removal and plate fixes, then moved on to heavier jobs: CG period aircraft, face replacements and enhanced on-screen violence.

Hughes also did groundwork before a frame was shot. He spent two weeks on the production's location scout across Easter Island, Santiago and New Mexico, advising on what the effects shoot would need.

"The vfx brief demanded invisible effects and we feel we achieved this well and in turn added to the aesthetic of the film," Hughes said, as quoted by AWN. He added that the team kept a vintage look and feel and helped the filmmakers pull off technically difficult shots, from recreating period aircraft to making violence look realistic.

## A third film together

The partnership matters here. According to AWN, Union has now worked on three McDonagh films, following Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri and The Banshees of Inisherin. Hughes called it fantastic to work with McDonagh and Blueprint again, and to see "such a special place in the world".

Union was founded in 2008 by Tim Caplan and Adam Gascoyne. AWN describes it as an independently owned, BAFTA-winning and Emmy-nominated studio in London's Soho. Its credits include work for [Danny Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Boyle) and [Sam Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Mendes) as well as McDonagh.

## Where the film stands

After a festival run from Venice to TIFF, Wild Horse Nine opened in cinemas on November 4, per AWN. The breakdown reel is aimed at showing the craft behind a film that is meant to look entirely of its time. More on the release is in our [Movies section](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/).
