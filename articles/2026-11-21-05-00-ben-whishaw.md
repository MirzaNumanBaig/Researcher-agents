**Quick summary**

- Reviews of *Eurotrash* at London's Young Vic landed on 20 November 2026, and the lead pair, [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) and [Kathryn Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hunter), drew praise across The Guardian, The Telegraph, The Independent and WhatsOnStage.
- The play adapts Christian Kracht's novel, with a script by [Colin Teevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Teevan) and Walter Meierjohann directing.
- The Guardian and one independent blogger said the stage version softens the book's darker edges.
- The interval-free show runs until 9 January 2027.

Ben Whishaw and Kathryn Hunter have opened in *Eurotrash* at the Young Vic, and the first wave of London reviews, published on 20 November, is warm. Critics singled out the pair's chemistry as a mother and son on a chaotic Swiss road trip. A few reviewers also felt the adaptation leaves out some of the novel's sharper edges.

## What is Eurotrash about?

The play is based on Christian Kracht's novel, which [The Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/theatre/what-to-see/eurotrash-young-vic-review-ben-whishaw/) notes was longlisted for the International Booker Prize in 2021 and has now also been made into a film. Whishaw plays Christian, a writer closely modelled on Kracht himself, according to [WhatsOnStage](https://www.whatsonstage.com/news/eurotrash-review-ben-whishaw-and-kathryn-hunter-on-a-merry-messy-road-trip_1737755/). Hunter plays his 80-year-old mother, who has left a psychiatric clinic.

The pair set out across Switzerland to get rid of her fortune, which came from investment in the armaments industry. [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/theatre-dance/reviews/eurotrash-young-vic-ben-whishaw-review-b3088606.html) reports that they withdraw 600,000 Swiss francs in a bin bag. Their route takes in a vegetarian commune with a sinister streak and a cable car trip that goes badly wrong.

## What the critics said about the double act

The praise centres on the two leads. The Guardian called them a "dynamite duo" and wrote that their chemistry would hold an audience even if they simply watched paint dry. The Telegraph's Dominic Cavendish said Whishaw blends the awkward charm of Q with the tenderness of Paddington, while Hunter is "in her element as a rasping eccentric".

The Independent described the show as uproariously funny and noted that the two actors met on the Netflix spy series [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves). WhatsOnStage found real emotional weight under the slapstick, saying Whishaw "finds a truthful tenderness" in Christian that grounds the evening. Liam O'Dell, writing on his own site, called Hunter's performance something he had never seen from her, and said she was visibly enjoying it.

## The staging

The production is built around Lizzie Clachan's design. Reviewers describe airport lounge chairs that turn into a taxi, miniature cable cars crossing the stage and a conveyor belt of luxury goods heading for a bin. Glyn Pritchard plays a string of small roles, including the commune leader, and composer Deanna H Choi works as an on-stage sound engineer.

At 105 minutes with no interval, The Telegraph warned the show risks feeling like a shaggy-dog story, though it concluded the cast carry it. Several critics pointed to a late, quieter scene in which mother and son finally address a shared trauma.

## Where the reviews split

Not everyone was fully won over. The Guardian said the adaptation leaves out the book's unforgiving look at the mother and at Germany's past, and felt the key revelation arrives too late to be followed through. The Independent reached a different view, suggesting the story lands as more universal in London than it might in Germany, where the play has already been staged by several theatres.

O'Dell also noted that the critique of Europe's elite found in the novel is absent, along with any clear takeaway. He still called the evening hilarious. ## What happens next

*Eurotrash* plays at the Young Vic until 9 January 2027. According to O'Dell's review, access performances include audio description on 1 and 12 December, British Sign Language interpretation on 4 and 11 December, captioned shows on 5 and 10 December, and relaxed performances on 5 and 9 December. More on the actor is on our [Ben Whishaw page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ben-whishaw/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Eurotrash playing and until when?
It is on at the Young Vic in London until 9 January 2027, according to WhatsOnStage and Liam O'Dell's review.

### Who is in the cast of Eurotrash?
Ben Whishaw plays Christian and Kathryn Hunter plays his mother. Glyn Pritchard takes several supporting roles, and Deanna H Choi is the composer and on-stage sound engineer.

### How long is the show?
The Telegraph gives the running time as 105 minutes with no interval.

### Is the play based on a book?
Yes. It adapts Christian Kracht's 2021 novel of the same name, with a stage version by Colin Teevan.
