**Quick summary**

- Animated feature *Tangles*, with voice work from [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Bryan Cranston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Cranston) and Bowen Yang, has opened in a limited run in New York and Los Angeles.
- The run is designed to qualify the film for the 2027 Academy Awards, where it is aiming for Best Animated Feature.
- Leah Nelson directed, and the film adapts Sarah Leavitt's graphic memoir about her mother's Alzheimer's disease.
- Another limited release is set for January 15, 2027, with a wider rollout on January 22, according to Yahoo.

Seth Rogen, Bryan Cranston and Bowen Yang lend their voices to *Tangles*, an animated drama that began a limited theatrical run this week to put it in the Oscar race, Yahoo reported on November 27. The film is directed by Leah Nelson and is positioned for the Best Animated Feature category at the 2027 Academy Awards.

## What is Tangles about?

*Tangles* is adapted from Sarah Leavitt's graphic memoir, *Tangles: A Story About Alzheimer's, My Mother and Me*. According to [Yahoo's report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/seth-rogen-bryan-cranston-bowen-201230597.html), the story follows Sarah, who goes back to her family's small hometown to care for her mother, who has Alzheimer's disease. As the illness changes her mother's personality, Sarah starts to reassess her own life and her family relationships.

The official synopsis quoted by the outlet sets the story in 1990s San Francisco, where Sarah is finding love and independence. A visit home reunites her with her mother Midge and the "wonderfully imperfect" family around them. It promises a film about ties that endure "even as memories begin to fade".

## A large voice cast

Beyond the three headline names, the voice cast is long. [Abbi Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbi_Jacobson) voices Sarah and [Julia Louis-Dreyfus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Louis-Dreyfus) voices Midge, according to the synopsis. The list also includes [Beanie Feldstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beanie_Feldstein), [Pamela Adlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Adlon), [Sarah Silverman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Silverman), [Phil Rosenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Rosenthal), [Samira Wiley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samira_Wiley), [Wanda Sykes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanda_Sykes) and [Sugar Lyn Beard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sugar_Lyn_Beard), among others.

Yahoo's piece does not say which character Rogen, Cranston or Yang plays, so we have left that out rather than guess.

## The writers and the early reviews

Nelson also wrote the screenplay, working with Leavitt and Trev Renney. At the time of the report, the film held a 94 per cent Tomatometer score on Rotten Tomatoes from 36 reviews. Scores like that can move as more critics weigh in, but it is a strong start for a film that is still in a qualifying release.

A short run in two cities is a standard way for a film to meet awards rules before a wider launch. It does not by itself mean a nomination, and the Academy has not announced anything about this film.

## What happens next

The next date to watch is January 15, 2027, when the film is due to get a limited theatrical release, followed by a wider rollout on January 22. Yahoo's report does not give details of where the wider release will reach, and nothing in it covers Australian cinemas. We will update this story if a local date is confirmed.
