**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has promised to legally become "Ryan Air" if his album Airplane Mode reaches No. 1 on the UK Albums Chart.
- The album came out on 6 November and sat at No. 2 in the midweek chart, behind Royal Blood's Dead Company, according to Contactmusic.
- The final chart result is due on Friday, 13 November 2026.
- His [Airplane Mode Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airplane_Mode_Tour) runs through Europe in early 2027, with dates in Dublin, Belfast and Prague.

[James Blunt](https://moduscarry.com/tag/james-blunt/) says he will legally change his name to Ryan Air if his new album Airplane Mode tops the UK chart this week. The singer made the promise in a social media clip, and the result is expected on Friday, 13 November 2026.

## The James Blunt Ryan Air promise

In the clip, quoted by [Contactmusic](https://www.contactmusic.com/story/467/3643826/james-blunt-will-change-his-name-to-ryan-air-if-airplane-mode-lands-no-1), Blunt reminds fans that two years ago he vowed to take the name Blunty McBluntFace if an album of his reached No. 1. That did not happen, so he is offering a second chance. He then practises a [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond)-style introduction and tells fans to stream it, buy it and get it to the top.

The joke refers to the budget airline, and it ties neatly to an album titled Airplane Mode. Blunt, who is also known for a self-mocking online presence, has made the offer in the same spirit as his 2024 stunt.

## Where the chart race stands

Here the reports differ slightly. Contactmusic says Airplane Mode was No. 2 in the midweek Official Albums Chart, behind Dead Company by Royal Blood. The Brighton duo are chasing a fifth consecutive No. 1 album, having topped the chart with all four of their earlier studio albums.

[Hotpress](https://www.hotpress.com/music/james-blunt-on-course-to-change-name-to-ryan-air-as-album-expected-to-hit-no-1-23169085) reports that the album was at No. 2 when Blunt posted but has since reached the top spot, and says it is expected to finish at No. 1. Because the official result is not out until Friday, treat the No. 1 claim as unconfirmed for now. Our earlier report on the [midweek position](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-airplane-mode/) had the record heading for No. 2.

If it does win, it would be Blunt's third UK No. 1 album, after Back to Bedlam in 2005 and All the Lost Souls in 2007, per Hotpress. Contactmusic adds that every one of his studio albums has reached the UK Top 10. Airplane Mode is his eighth.

## What happened last time

The 2024 version of the pledge came with the 20th-anniversary reissue of Back to Bedlam. Contactmusic reports that Blunt told Radio X's Chris Moyles Show he would take whichever name the public chose. Fans picked Blunty McBluntFace, but the reissue stopped at No. 7, so the name stayed.

## Airplane Mode and the 2027 tour

Airplane Mode is a 12-track record released through Atlantic Records, according to Prague Morning. The singles so far are "[Tastes Like Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tastes_Like_Summer)" and "Weight of It All", which the site describes as a song about the pressures of modern life. Rayo describes the album as being about switching off from modern life.

The tour follows in early 2027. Hotpress lists Dublin's 3Arena on 30 March and Belfast's The O2 on 31 March. Prague Morning reports a stop at O2 universum in Prague on 5 March, tickets through Ticketportal and Ticketmaster, with the wider European run covering February, March and April. Blunt last played Prague three years ago at Forum Karlín. The report adds that the set will mix new songs with older hits such as "You're Beautiful" and "[Goodbye My Lover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goodbye_My_Lover)".

## A Prince story from the radio

Blunt has also been doing the promotional rounds. Speaking to [Ken Bruce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Bruce) on Greatest Hits Radio, he chose 1984 as his golden year and picked Prince's "When Doves Cry", according to [Rayo](https://www.hellorayo.co.uk/news/music/pop/james-blunt-prince-gig). He said his band used to play it before going on stage.

He then told a story from a show in Minneapolis, close to Prince's Paisley Park estate. The estate asked for two tickets, and Blunt assumed Prince himself would come. Two people slipped into the VIP seats, and he played his heart out. Afterwards he found the guests at the after party and learned they were the Paisley Park gardener and the man who staffed the gate. Prince had not been there, Blunt said, but the band had performed with real enthusiasm for them anyway.

Rayo also notes that Blunt was a finalist on The Celebrity Traitors, which has kept him in the public eye alongside the album launch.

## What happens next

The Official Albums Chart result arrives on Friday, 13 November 2026. If Airplane Mode is No. 1, Blunt will have to follow through on the name change, and we will report how he does it. If Royal Blood hold the top spot, he stays James Blunt, which by his own joke "nobody wants". Either way, the tour dates above remain on sale. For more, see our [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) coverage and the earlier piece on [the Ryan Air vow](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-ryan-air/).

### Will James Blunt really change his name to Ryan Air?

Only if Airplane Mode reaches No. 1 on the UK Albums Chart. He says he will change it legally. The result is due on Friday, 13 November 2026.

### Is Airplane Mode at No. 1?

Not confirmed. Contactmusic had it at No. 2 behind Royal Blood in the midweek chart, while Hotpress reports it has reached the top. The official final chart settles it.

It runs across Europe in early 2027. Confirmed dates include Prague on 5 March, Dublin on 30 March and Belfast on 31 March.
