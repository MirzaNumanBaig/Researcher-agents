**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios named Robert Pattinson the next James Bond on Monday 16 November 2026.
- Denis Villeneuve is directing, from a script by Steven Knight, with a 2028 release reported.
- According to The Wrap, as reported by The Guardian, delays to The Batman: Part II mean Pattinson is no longer contractually bound to a third Batman film.
- Prince Harry gave the casting his approval on ITV's This Morning.

The Robert Pattinson James Bond announcement has brought congratulations and a few awkward questions. The 40-year-old [actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was named the new 007 by Amazon MGM Studios on Monday, and the fallout now reaches [Batman and the Bond franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), his personal life and even the royal family.

## How Robert Pattinson won the James Bond role

Pattinson succeeds [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), who last played Bond in 2021's No Time to Die. He becomes the seventh actor to officially play the spy in the film series, after [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery), [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore), [George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby), [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton) Pierce Brosnan and Craig.

According to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/prince-harry-james-bond-robert-pattinson-1236759950/), he won a months-long race against fellow screen-testers [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), Josh O'Connor and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden). [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/celebrity/odysseys-adam-croasdell-shocked-screen-son-robert-pattinson-named-next-james-bond-1454018.html) adds [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) to the reported shortlist. Amazon bought the franchise's creative rights from Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli last year.

[Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who recently directed Pattinson in Dune: Part Three, said the actor has "the timeless soul of 007". Pattinson called the job an "honour of a lifetime" and "a dream come true". Steven Knight, the Peaky Blinders creator, wrote the script, and the timeline points to a 2028 release.

## A surprise to some who know him

[Adam Croasdell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Croasdell), who played Pattinson's on-screen father in Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, told BANG Showbiz he was "quite shocked" when the name emerged. He had expected Turner or [Aaron Taylor-Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Taylor-Johnson) to land the part. Even so, he called Pattinson the right choice, praising a "mechanical edge" on screen that can feel "disconcerting" and the "menace" he can bring alongside the action.

More reaction is in our coverage of [Brosnan and Lazenby's responses](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-reactions/) and [Brosnan's welcome](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-brosnan-welcome/).

## The Batman question

The bigger industry story concerns Gotham. In a column for [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/20/robert-pattinson-batman-james-bond-third-movie-delays-dc), the paper cites The Wrap as reporting that delays to The Batman: Part II have released Pattinson from his obligation to make a third film. The sequel is now due in February 2028, almost six years after the first film, which took more than US$770 million worldwide.

Other things have shifted too. Director Matt Reeves stepped away from the rest of the sequel's shoot in October for a family matter, and Steven Soderbergh has been brought in to finish filming, though Reeves says he remains committed. Skydance's takeover of Warner Bros has also seen James Gunn and Peter Safran leave DC Studios, with former Marvel executive Jeremy Latcham in charge. The Guardian notes that Gunn and Safran had planned a separate, non-Pattinson Batman for a project called The Brave and the Bold, and says it is unclear whether the new leadership will keep that plan.

Nothing says he cannot play both roles, but Warner Bros would need to win him over with a new deal.

## Spotlight on a low-key couple

The Guardian also looks at what the role means for Pattinson and his partner of eight years, Suki Waterhouse. It reports that the pair keep their careers separate rather than selling themselves as a joint brand, and share nothing about their daughter, born in 2024, online. Launchmetrics data cited by the paper put the media impact of their joint 2026 Venice film festival appearance, in Dior and Saint Laurent, at £4 million in a week.

## Prince Harry weighs in

On Friday, Prince Harry told ITV's This Morning the casting is "great" and that he "can't wait to see it", choosing Bond "100 per cent" over Mission: Impossible. The royal family's account had earlier welcomed Pattinson to "His Majesty's Secret Service". For more of the online response, read about the [UK government joining the Bond memes](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-memes/).

## What happens next

No filming dates are confirmed. Reports point to a 2028 release, the year The Batman: Part II is also due. Whether Pattinson signs on for a third Batman film is the next open question. More coverage is on our [Robert Pattinson tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is the new James Bond?
Robert Pattinson, announced by Amazon MGM Studios on Monday 16 November 2026. He replaces Daniel Craig.

### Who is directing the new Bond film?
Denis Villeneuve, working from a script by Steven Knight. A 2028 release is the current expectation.

### Will Robert Pattinson still play Batman?
He has not said. The Guardian, citing The Wrap, reports delays to The Batman: Part II have ended his contractual obligation to a third film, though he could sign a new deal.
