**Quick summary**

- The search for the next James Bond is reportedly in its final stage, with screen tests expected.
- Callum Turner and Jack Lowden are among the actors said to be taking part, according to Variety.
- Jacob Elordi, previously linked to the role, is reportedly not among those in the latest tests.
- Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed a shortlist or a preferred candidate.

**Jacob Elordi** is reportedly out of the final round of screen tests for the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to a report from [Mag the Weekly](https://magtheweekly.com/detail/27815-james-bond-casting-race-narrows-as-jacob-elordi-reportedly-misses-final-auditions) published at the end of October. The Australian actor, whose name has circulated in Bond speculation for some time, is not said to be among the candidates still being considered. None of this has been confirmed by the studio.

You can follow everything else we have published on [Jacob Elordi](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/) in our tag archive.

## Who is reportedly still in the running

The report says the hunt to succeed Daniel Craig is entering its last stage at Amazon MGM Studios. [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) are named as actors expected to undergo screen tests. The outlet attributes that to Variety, and adds that the exact list of participants has not been confirmed.

Other entertainment outlets have also reportedly named Josh O'Connor as a possible finalist, according to the same piece. It says the identity of one further candidate remains unclear. In short, the field is described as narrowing, but nobody outside the studio appears to hold a definitive list.

## The actors behind the names

Turner, 36, has drawn attention for his roles in [Fantastic Beasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Beasts) and Masters of the Air. Mag the Weekly says he has been tied to the part for months but has not publicly confirmed any involvement in the final process.

Lowden, also 36, plays River Cartwright in the spy drama [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses). Speculation around him grew after his co-star [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman) appeared to suggest the decision had already been made. No official announcement has followed, so that remains an interpretation of an offhand remark rather than a fact.

O'Connor is known for The Crown and Challengers. The report notes that Amazon MGM Studios has not officially confirmed the reported shortlist.

## What it means for Elordi

Elordi is described as reportedly absent from the latest tests, and Jack O'Connell, another actor previously discussed, is also reported to be out of the running. Both statements are second-hand and come without a named source inside the production.
