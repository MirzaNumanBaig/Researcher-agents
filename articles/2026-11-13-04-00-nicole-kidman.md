**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) will receive an honorary César from the French Film Academy.
- The tribute is set for the 52nd [César Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%A9sar_Awards) on 25 February 2027 at the Olympia in Paris, according to Variety.
- Canal+ will broadcast the ceremony live and unencrypted, so all viewers can watch.
- Recent honorary recipients include Jim Carrey, Julia Roberts and Christopher Nolan.

The Australian actor and producer will be honoured in Paris next February. Variety reported on 12 November that Nicole Kidman will receive an honorary César at the 52nd ceremony, to be held at the Olympia on 25 February 2027. It adds a French prize to a career that has already collected an Oscar, a Venice acting award and a lifetime honour from the American Film Institute.

## Why the French Film Academy is honouring Kidman

Variety describes Kidman as both a Hollywood star and a major name in auteur cinema. Her list of directors reads like a syllabus of admired filmmakers: [Stanley Kubrick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Kubrick), [Jane Campion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Campion), [Lars von Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_von_Trier), [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) and [Yorgos Lanthimos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yorgos_Lanthimos).

The Mezha.net report on the announcement makes the same point, noting that these are directors especially admired in France.

## The career behind the tribute

According to Variety, Kidman first broke through internationally with Dead Calm, then starred in Moulin Rouge!, [Eyes Wide Shut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eyes_Wide_Shut), The Others and Dogville. She won the best actress Oscar in 2003 for playing [Virginia Woolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Woolf) in The Hours. In 2024 she took the Volpi Cup for best actress at the [Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Film_Festival) for Babygirl.

She also works behind the camera. Kidman co-founded [Blossom Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blossom_Films), the company behind Big Little Lies, The Undoing and [Nine Perfect Strangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Perfect_Strangers), and she headlined all three. On stage, Variety notes, she won praise in London's West End for The Blue Room and Photograph 51.

## Honours, advocacy and company

The César is the latest in a long run of tributes. Variety lists her appointment as a Companion of the Order of Australia in 2006 and the Cannes Film Festival's 70th anniversary prize in 2017. In 2024 the American Film Institute gave her its Life Achievement Award, making her the first Australian to receive it.

Away from the screen, she serves as a UN Women goodwill ambassador and has helped raise several million dollars for women's cancer programs, including at Stanford and the Vanderbilt-Ingram Cancer Center, per the same report.

The honorary César has a short but star-heavy recent history. Mezha.net lists Jim Carrey (2026), Julia Roberts (2025), Christopher Nolan (2024), David Fincher (2023), Cate Blanchett (2022), Robert Redford (2019) and George Clooney (2017) among the honourees. Kidman joins that line next year. Note that Mezha's headline refers to February 2026, but Variety, which gives the full details, puts the ceremony on 25 February 2027.

## What happens next

The Academy has set the date and venue, and Canal+ will carry the tribute live on 25 February 2027. The sources do not say whether Kidman has confirmed she will attend in person, who will present the prize, or whether a clip package will accompany it. Those details are likely to emerge closer to the ceremony.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is Nicole Kidman receiving her honorary César?

At the 52nd César Awards ceremony on 25 February 2027, held at the Olympia in Paris, according to Variety.

### Where can I watch the ceremony?

Canal+ will show it live and exclusively, unencrypted, so all viewers can watch.

### Has Kidman won other major honours?

Yes. She won the best actress Oscar in 2003 for The Hours, the Volpi Cup in Venice in 2024 for Babygirl, and the American Film Institute Life Achievement Award in 2024.
