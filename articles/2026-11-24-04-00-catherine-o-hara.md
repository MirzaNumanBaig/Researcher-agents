**Quick summary**

- Chris Columbus, who directed the first two Home Alone films, has said a full sequel would be hard to justify without Catherine O'Hara.
- O'Hara played Kevin McCallister's mother and died in January 2026 at the age of 71.
- His comments came shortly before news that Disney had put a new Home Alone sequel with Macaulay Culkin into early development.
- Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg are reported to be writing the screenplay.

Chris Columbus does not think a new Home Alone film can work without the woman who played the mother in the original. In an interview with Variety, as relayed by a [news portal summary](https://gursesintour.com/en/aktualnye-novosti/rejisser-odin-doma-ysomnilsia-v-yspehe-sikvela-bez-ketrin-ohary/1272877/), the director said a full-fledged sequel would be close to impossible without [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara), who died in January 2026 aged 71.

## What Columbus said about Catherine O'Hara

[Chris Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Columbus_(filmmaker)) directed the first two instalments of the [Home Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone) series. According to the report, he described O'Hara as half the success of the 1990 original. He also called her pairing with [Macaulay Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaulay_Culkin) the "yin and yang" of the project.

In his view, that mother-and-son bond was the emotional centre of the story. Without it, he suggested, a sequel would lose the reason the first film worked. The report adds that Columbus criticised earlier attempts to continue the series without the original cast.

## The Disney sequel in development

The interview came shortly before it emerged that Disney had put a new sequel starring Culkin into early development. [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) and [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg) are reported to be writing the screenplay. The source does not say who would direct, who else would star or when filming might begin, so none of that is confirmed.

Columbus also said that if a new film goes ahead, it would be crucial to bring back the bumbling burglar pair played by [Joe Pesci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Pesci) and [Daniel Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Stern_(actor)). He argued that their on-screen chemistry has never been matched.

## Praise for Culkin's Christmas return

Columbus was warmer about a recent McDonald's Christmas commercial in which Culkin, now 46, appeared again as an older [Kevin McCallister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone_(franchise)). He called Culkin a wonderful actor and said he hopes to work with him again. He added that such a project would not necessarily belong to the Home Alone universe.

## What happens next

Nothing about the Disney project is final. It is described as being in early development, and Columbus's remarks read as a caution about how any follow-up should treat O'Hara's absence.
