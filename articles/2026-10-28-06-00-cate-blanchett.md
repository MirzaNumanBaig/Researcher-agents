**Quick summary**
- Parker Posey told CBS Sunday Morning she was in the running to play Katharine Hepburn in The Aviator, but gave up hope when she heard Cate Blanchett was also being considered.
- Blanchett won Best Supporting Actress for the role at the 2005 Academy Awards, according to Entertainment Weekly.
- Far Out Magazine reports that Blanchett has recalled director Alejandro G Iñárritu criticising her first Babel scene on day one of shooting.
- Babel went on to earn seven Oscar nominations and a Golden Globe for best motion picture, per Far Out.

Two new interviews and retellings have put [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett)'s mid-2000s run back in the spotlight. [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) says she was in contention for the part that won Blanchett her first Oscar, and Blanchett herself has described how a famously demanding director greeted her on the set of Babel.

## Did Parker Posey nearly play Katharine Hepburn in The Aviator?

Yes, by her own account. Posey told CBS Sunday Morning correspondent [Mo Rocca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Rocca) that [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese) was interested in her for the film, according to Entertainment Weekly, whose report was republished by AOL. The role was [Katharine Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharine_Hepburn).

Rocca had just told her he wanted to see her play Hepburn. Posey recalled asking who else was being considered. "They said, 'Cate Blanchett,'" she said. "I said, 'She's gonna get cast. Decision's already made.'"

She was right. But the story of how the Oscar race itself played out is worth a closer look.

## How Blanchett's Aviator win stacked up

Blanchett took Best Supporting Actress at the 2005 Academy Awards for the performance, Entertainment Weekly reports. Her rivals were [Laura Linney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Linney) for Kinsey, [Virginia Madsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Madsen) for Sideways, [Sophie Okonedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Okonedo) for [Hotel Rwanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Rwanda) and [Natalie Portman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Portman) for Closer.

It was one of five Oscars for The Aviator, which also won for art direction, cinematography, costume design and film editing. The film had 11 nominations in total, including best picture and best director, and a best supporting actor nod for [Alan Alda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Alda).

Blanchett has since added more. She had been nominated for Best Actress for 1998's Elizabeth and won that category for [Blue Jasmine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Jasmine) in 2013. She has also been nominated for Elizabeth: The Golden Age, Carol and Tár, plus supporting nominations for Notes on a Scandal and I'm Not There.

Posey, for her part, made Blade: Trinity and Laws of Attraction in 2004 and worked with Scorsese as an executive producer on the TV movie Frankenstein. She now has a supporting role in [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), the new Martin McDonagh film that Entertainment Weekly says is widely expected to feature in the coming awards season.

## What happened on Blanchett's first day on Babel?

Two years after The Aviator, Blanchett joined Brad Pitt in Babel, directed by Alejandro G Iñárritu. She played half of a married couple whose trip to Morocco turns tragic, Far Out Magazine reports.

Blanchett has spoken about the shoot this year. After a couple of takes on the first day, she said, Iñárritu told her and Pitt the scene wasn't working and that the movie wouldn't work if it didn't, then walked off. She didn't hold it against him. "Sometimes a director can lead with love, but incredibly tough love," she said, as quoted by Far Out.

She described it as a form of direction, while acknowledging that some people would find that approach upsetting. Far Out says she used the sting as fuel and feared a repeat, and that the work paid off.

## The numbers behind Babel and a busy 2006

Far Out puts Babel's profit at more than $100 million at the box office. It earned seven Oscar nominations and won the Golden Globe for best motion picture, according to the same outlet.

Babel wasn't Blanchett's only awards-season film that year. She also made Notes on a Scandal with Judi Dench, which brought her a third Oscar nomination, and appeared in Steven Soderbergh's The Good German, all within 12 months, per Far Out.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who else was considered for Katharine Hepburn in The Aviator?
Parker Posey says Martin Scorsese was interested in her for the part. She told CBS Sunday Morning she assumed Cate Blanchett would win it once she heard Blanchett's name.

### When did Cate Blanchett win her first Oscar?
Blanchett won Best Supporting Actress for The Aviator at the 2005 Academy Awards, according to Entertainment Weekly. She won Best Actress for Blue Jasmine in 2013.

### What did Alejandro G Iñárritu say to Cate Blanchett on Babel?
Blanchett says he told her and Brad Pitt after their first scene that it wasn't working, and left. She called it a form of tough love, according to Far Out Magazine.
