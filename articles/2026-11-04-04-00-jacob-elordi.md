**Quick summary**

- Kendall Jenner turned 31 on Tuesday, 3 November, and The Mirror US reports she has been dating Jacob Elordi for about nine months.
- Two PR experts told the outlet that his birthday gesture could signal how serious the pair are. These are opinions, not inside knowledge.
- The Mirror US reports the couple were first linked in February and went public at Coachella in April.
- Their red carpet debut came at the Academy Museum Gala in October.
- Neither Elordi nor Jenner has commented on birthday plans in the sources reviewed.

[Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) has landed in the middle of a guessing game about [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner)'s 31st birthday. According to The Mirror US, which spoke to two public relations specialists on 3 November, the present he chooses could say a lot about where the relationship stands. Nobody involved has confirmed any plans.

## How the Jacob Elordi and Kendall Jenner birthday story began

The talking point comes from two exclusive interviews published by [The Mirror US](https://www.themirror.com/entertainment/kendall-jenner-birthday-jacob-elordi-2095149). The outlet reports that the pair have dated for roughly nine months, after reportedly starting to see each other in February. Insiders quoted by the outlet say they got to know each other quietly in Los Angeles first.

That is a short history by celebrity standards. It is long enough, though, for observers to read meaning into small gestures, and a first birthday together is an obvious one.

## What the PR experts say

Jenna Guarneri, founder of JMG Public Relations, told The Mirror US that Elordi could make a "grand gesture" if the couple consider the relationship official and want the world to know. She described Jenner as the most private of her siblings, so she said any public display would be significant.

Guarneri also offered a second reading. If the pair prefer to stay low-key, she said, there will be no grand gesture, because they come across as a more subtle couple than others in Jenner's family. She pointed to Elordi sitting front row at Jenner's Bottega Veneta show during Milan Fashion Week in September as a quiet way of showing support. A third scenario she raised was that the relationship is a publicity exercise, in which case she expected something big. That last point is her speculation, and the outlet offers no evidence for it.

A different view came from Christina Kroll, founder of Kroll PR, in a [second Mirror US piece](https://www.themirror.com/entertainment/celebrity-news/jacob-elordi-kendall-jenner-birthday-2094947). She argued that a birthday is about one person, so the gift should not be about the couple. In her view Elordi should impress without overreaching, because expectations set too high this early are hard to meet. A necklace rather than a ring, she suggested, or a weekend away rather than a world tour.

Kroll also said Elordi is "still on trial" with Jenner's family, and that a dinner for her sisters and mother could go down well. The Mirror US notes that the Kardashian family has shown birthday dinners on its television show.

## The timeline so far

According to The Mirror US, the couple's public milestones have come in steady steps:

- **April:** they were seen together at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in California.
- **Summer:** they were pictured on trips to Hawaii, Japan and Elordi's home country, Australia.
- **September:** the Frankenstein actor sat front row as Jenner closed the Bottega Veneta show in Milan.
- **October:** they made their red carpet debut at the Academy Museum Gala.

The outlet adds that this is Jenner's first serious relationship in the public eye since her romance with [Bad Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Bunny), which lasted about a year and ended in 2024.
