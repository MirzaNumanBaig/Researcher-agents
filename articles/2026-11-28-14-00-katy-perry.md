**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) posted an Instagram carousel on 25 November from a European trip with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau), the former Canadian prime minister.
- The photos show a gold bathtub and a luxury train, which AOL identifies as the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express, plus a selfie with French President [Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron).
- The Daily Mail, as relayed by The Times of India, reports the couple travelled from Paris to Venice in a private carriage said to cost £80,000 a night. That price is a media report, not a confirmed figure.
- The post also debuts her new bob haircut, which first appeared at the Baby2Baby Gala on 14 November.

Katy Perry has shared the first full look at her European holiday with Justin Trudeau, and the headline detail is the train. The singer posted an Instagram carousel on 25 November showing a gold clawfoot bath, card games, formal dinners and a selfie with Emmanuel Macron, captioned "the bob's Euro debut", according to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/katy-perry-loved-euro-trip-105841000.html).

## The Orient Express carriage

The trip took the pair from Paris to Venice by rail. AOL reports that the pictures appear to show a hallway aboard the Venice Simplon-Orient-Express, and that the couple may have reached Venice on 20 November.

The Daily Mail, cited by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/inside-katy-perry-and-justin-trudeaus-80000-a-night-orient-express-escape-featuring-a-golden-bathtub-1000-books-and-a-private-butler-as-they-travelled-from-paris-to-venice/articleshow/136228276.cms), says they stayed in L'Observatoire, a private carriage added to the train last year. It reportedly has a double bedroom, a dining area and a library of 1,000 books. Skylights are said to have wipers for wet weather, and a personal butler looked after the couple.

The report puts the cost at £80,000 a night. Neither Perry nor Trudeau has commented on the price, so treat it as an estimate.

One detail in the same coverage is the bath. The train is said not to slow down while a guest bathes, so the water stays put. The Daily Mail account links the feature to the royal train. The carriage also sat at the end of the train, which would have kept the couple away from other passengers.

## What the photos show

The carousel was set to Perry's holiday track "Cozy Little Christmas". It opens with a solo shot of her in the gold bath, then moves to the pair in the train corridor.

For dinner on board, The Times of India reports, Perry wore a champagne satin minidress by De La Vali, reportedly priced at £660, with white heels. Trudeau wore a white tuxedo jacket, black trousers and a bow tie. Reality Tea, via [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-full-092143000.html), describes other frames: a selfie on crowded stone steps under festive lights, a candlelit restaurant dinner and a game of backgammon. In one she wears a plaid headscarf and sunglasses, and he wears a black cap with a red maple leaf.

The same outlet says the trip also took in Spain, France and Italy. The Times of India names Barcelona and Milan among the stops.

## The Macron selfie

The picture that drew the most attention shows both of them with Macron, who is in the frame with Perry's dark bob and gold earrings visible beside him. According to The Times of India, some fans joked that the French president looked like he had wandered into someone else's date. The outlet did not say where or when the photo was taken, and no source in this batch gives the reason for the meeting.

## Mixed reaction online

Not every comment was warm. AOL's report, which first ran on Bored Panda, notes that some users called the couple attention seekers, while others wished them well. One commenter hoped for a sibling for Perry's daughter Daisy Dove, whom she has with her former partner Orlando Bloom.

The couple's relationship has been public for some time. AOL reports that Perry credited her Lifetimes Tour with helping her meet Trudeau, who attended at least 10 shows. Trudeau separated from Sophie Grégoire Trudeau in 2023, and they share three children.

## What happens next

Neither representative has said whether the trip is over or where the couple go next. Perry's Instagram is the main place any further updates will appear.
