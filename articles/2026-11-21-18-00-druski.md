**Quick summary**

- [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski)'s first Netflix series, *Collect & Praise*, premieres on December 1.
- [Mike Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps) co-stars as the deacon to Druski's money-hungry megachurch pastor.
- Netflix announced a multiyear development deal with Druski's company, 4LIFERS Entertainment, on November 2.
- Netflix will also license three existing Druski titles: *Coulda Been Love*, *Coulda Been Daddy* and *Coulda Been Records Auditions*.

Druski is about to make the jump from viral sketches to a streaming platform. His semi-scripted comedy *Collect & Praise* arrives on Netflix on December 1, with Epps alongside him, according to [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/tv/netflix-druski-deal-shows/). The show is the first project under a multiyear deal that was announced at the start of the month.

## What Collect & Praise is about

The series grows out of "Pastor Dru", the megachurch preacher character Druski made famous in a sketch that went viral in January. [TheGrio](https://thegrio.com/2026/11/02/druski-netflix-collect-and-praise/) reports that the project was first billed as a YouTube series before it expanded into a Netflix show.

Druski plays a pastor driven by money. Epps plays his unpredictable deacon. According to TheWrap, the pair scheme their way through scandals and controversies. TheGrio adds that early glimpses include a funeral scene with Epps that ends in a shootout.

Neither outlet gives an episode count or a season length, so those details remain unconfirmed.

## The multiyear Netflix deal

The wider agreement is bigger than one show. Druski's company, 4LIFERS Entertainment, will develop and produce several original series for the streamer, TheWrap reports. The deal was brokered by WME, which represents him.

Netflix will also bring part of his back catalogue to the service. The three titles named are *Coulda Been Love*, *Coulda Been Daddy* and *Coulda Been Records Auditions*, along with some of his comedic skits.

John Derderian, a Netflix vice president of content, said Druski "has a gift for building characters that people can't stop talking about", and described *Collect & Praise* as "just the beginning", per TheWrap. Druski said the partnership gives him the resources to build on his comedy foundation and reach a new global audience.

## Why the church setting is drawing conversation

The character has already stirred debate. A recent episode of The Breakfast Club's podcast, listed on [iHeart](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/24992238/episode/350378066/?keyid=AutomatedPodcasts&ihrnetwork=true&embed=true), says the upcoming series is sparking conversations about the Black church, creative freedom and representation. That fits the wider reaction to the original sketch, which pokes at the idea of pastors living lavishly.

The same podcast episode also covers Tyler Perry's response to critics as his latest Netflix film finds an audience, a reminder of how closely Netflix's Black-led comedy and drama slate is being watched.
