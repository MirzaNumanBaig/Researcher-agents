**Quick summary**
- A former girlfriend of [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) says in the Netflix documentary *Russell Brand: Open Secret* that he always wanted to marry someone famous.
- The film was released on 13 November, days after Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault.
- Brand married [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) in India in October 2010 and filed for divorce 14 months later.
- The remarks are one woman's claims. Us Weekly said it had asked Brand and Perry's representatives for comment.

Jordan Martin, who dated Russell Brand in 2007, has claimed in a new Netflix documentary that the comedian always intended to marry someone famous, and that Katy Perry fitted that plan. The film, *Russell Brand: Open Secret*, arrived on 13 November, according to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/russell-brand-ex-jordan-martin-makes-bombshell-claims-katy-perry-marriage-documentary-russell-brand-open-secret-20261115-p61e7d.html). It landed just after Brand's sentencing, and the Perry link has drawn most of the headlines.

## What Jordan Martin says in the documentary

In the film, Martin says Brand was "doing exactly what he always wanted to do and be married to someone famous, which he always said he would be", as quoted by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/russell-brands-former-girlfriend-speaks-222553765.html). She also recalls him telling her: "I should be dating somebody more famous than you."

Martin dated Brand for several months while he was filming *Forgetting Sarah Marshall* in Hawaii, according to [Us Weekly](https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/russell-brands-ex-gf-claims-star-married-katy-perry-for-fame/), citing Page Six. She says he asked her to join him so that someone would stop him "trying it on" with people on set. She also claims he criticised her clothes and her sunbathing during the trip.

Yahoo reports that Martin makes separate and more serious allegations in the film, including an alleged sexual assault at a UK hotel. Those claims are not part of the charges Brand was convicted of. Brand was invited to respond through his lawyers and did not reply, according to the same report.

## The Katy Perry Russell Brand marriage: what is on record

Brand and Perry met in 2008, per Nine, and married in Rajasthan in October 2010. Brand filed for divorce in late 2011, reportedly by text message, and the divorce was finalised in July 2012.

Perry has spoken about the split before. In a 2013 Vogue interview, quoted by Nine, she said Brand "didn't like the atmosphere of me being the boss on tour" and called the marriage "very controlling". She said that when they met he wanted an equal, but then "can't handle the equalness". She also said she first felt responsible for the end of the marriage, then learned "the real truth", which she kept "locked in my safe for a rainy day".

Brand has spoken too. Us Weekly notes that on his podcast in 2025 he said Perry was "an extraordinary, massive star" who was not involved in anything "nefarious", and that he had "nothing but respect for her". Later he criticised her relationship with former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) at a December 2025 event.

## Where the Brand case stands

A London court found Brand guilty on 5 November of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009, Yahoo reports. He was acquitted on two further rape charges and one count of attempted indecent assault. He was sentenced on 9 November and is held at HMP Wandsworth.

Us Weekly says the Metropolitan Police are investigating further rape allegations from two women, with files sent to the [Crown Prosecution Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crown_Prosecution_Service). Brand has always maintained his innocence. Nine adds that The Times and The Sunday Times published allegations from six more women on 12 November.

Perry is not accused of anything in any of these reports. Her name appears because of her former marriage. ## What happens next

No response from Perry or Brand to Martin's comments had been published in the sources we read. Us Weekly said it had reached out to both sets of representatives. Perry has not commented on the documentary in those reports, so any reaction remains unconfirmed.
