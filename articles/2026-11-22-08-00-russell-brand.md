**Quick summary**

- [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was jailed for 10 years and six months on 9 November, after being convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
- The Christian Science Monitor says the case marks the culmination of a long shift away from Britain's so-called "lad culture".
- Brand's wife, Laura Gallacher, spoke publicly for the first time since the verdict on 15 November.
- Separately, deposition transcripts from a New York civil case, which Brand denies, have put his marriage to [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) back in the news.

Russell Brand's conviction is being read in Britain as more than one man's downfall. In an analysis published on 21 November, The Christian Science Monitor argues that his 10-and-a-half-year sentence, handed down on 9 November, sits at the end of a decades-long turn against the "lad culture" that once made his brand of provocation a career.

## From Radio 2 to Southwark Crown Court

According to [The Christian Science Monitor](https://www.csmonitor.com/World/Europe/2026/1121/russell-brand-david-walliams-uk-sexual-abuse-rape-lad-culture), Brand was at his peak in 2007. He hosted his own Saturday night show on BBC Radio 2 and was filming in Hawaii for *Forgetting Sarah Marshall*, the Hollywood comedy that opened the door to a US career.

The outlet reports that he was jailed after being found guilty of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others between 2004 and 2009. Brand pleaded not guilty and denied the accusations throughout, according to Us Weekly's account of the trial.

## What "lad culture" meant

The Monitor describes lad culture as a bawdy, hypermasculine reaction to political correctness. In the 2000s it was fuelled by lad magazines and by male celebrities who made lewd remarks about much younger women. Emily Coleman, who worked with Brand at MTV Europe and now lectures at the Open University, told the paper that part of his appeal was always rule-breaking. She said he was effectively paid to bend the rules.

Academics quoted in the piece say "banter" was often a cover. Lindsey Blumell, of City St George's, University of London, calls it framed as liberation while in practice being men telling women how to use their bodies. Alison Phipps, of York St John University, found in early campus research that women were groped or harassed under that banner.

## A shift driven from below

Vanita Sundaram, of the University of York, says the change in attitudes came from grassroots movements, often led by marginalised people, rather than from the entertainment industry. The Monitor points to the Me Too movement, founded by Tarana Burke in 2006, and to UK projects such as Everyday Sexism, which gave women a shared language to describe harassment. The paper adds that Brand's conviction is opening the way for more women to come forward, and that the UK has seen a spate of pushback against male celebrities this month.

## His wife speaks, and Perry returns to the headlines

On the 15 November episode of the couple's *Sunday Service* YouTube channel, Laura Gallacher broke her silence. Us Weekly quotes her saying the family trusts God's plan "even though it's painful", and that it would take time to get used to not having her partner beside her. She said she would continue the channel without him. The pair have been married since 2017 and have three children.

Brand's earlier marriage has also resurfaced. Transcripts of a 2025 deposition, obtained by the Daily Mail and filed with the New York Supreme Court, show Brand struggling to recall when he was married to Perry. The questioning related to a civil lawsuit by a woman who worked as an extra on the 2011 film *Arthur*. She alleges misconduct on set. According to cwibc.co.id, Brand denied wrongdoing and says his encounters were consensual, and the case is ongoing with no judicial finding on her claims.

Perry and Brand met on *Get Him to the Greek* and married in October 2010, according to [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/brand-deposition-perry-marriage). Page Six reports that Perry has been dating former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) for over a year, after her split from [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom). It said the pair were photographed holding hands in Italy on Friday. Perry has not been accused of any involvement in the allegations.

## What happens next

The New York civil case continues, and the deposition is evidence under examination rather than a ruling. The Times and The Sunday Times, as summarised by cwibc.co.id, report that six more women have since come forward with allegations. Those claims are separate from the convictions and have not been tested in court.

## Frequently asked questions

### What was Russell Brand convicted of?
He was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009. He was found not guilty on the remaining counts.

### What sentence did he receive?
He was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison on 9 November.

### What is "lad culture"?
It is the term for a boisterous, sexualised style of male humour and behaviour that was celebrated in 1990s and 2000s Britain, often defended as "banter".

### Is Katy Perry involved in the case?
No. Her marriage came up only because Brand was asked about its timeline in a civil deposition. She has not been accused of any involvement.
