**Quick summary**

- Rhino High Fidelity has announced The Last Waltz as a limited three-LP audiophile vinyl set, timed to the 50th anniversary of The Band's farewell concert.
- The concert took place on Thanksgiving Day, 25 November 1976, at the Winterland Ballroom in San Francisco.
- The edition is mastered directly from the original analog tapes and pressed on heavyweight vinyl.
- A companion reissue of Bobby Charles' 1972 album is also coming through the same series.

The Last Waltz vinyl is back, and it arrives on the exact anniversary. Rhino High Fidelity has announced a limited three-LP edition of The Band's 1976 farewell concert, according to [That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/11/25/the-bands-legendary-the-last-waltz-returns-on-audiophile-vinyl-for-its-50th-anniversary-with-bob-dylan-joni-mitchell-neil-young-and-more/). [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan) was one of the guests who joined the group on stage that night.

## What the new edition offers

The set is mastered straight from the original analog tapes and pressed on heavyweight vinyl. Engineer Kevin Gray cut it entirely through an analog mastering process, the report says. That is the approach Rhino's High Fidelity line is built around, and the same engineer handled the companion release.

The report does not give a price, a release date or the pressing run, so those details are still to come. Fans who want a copy should watch Rhino's own announcements. For more on the artist, see everything we have published on [Bob Dylan](https://moduscarry.com/tag/bob-dylan/).

## The night itself

The Band's five members were [Robbie Robertson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbie_Robertson), [Levon Helm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levon_Helm), [Rick Danko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Danko), [Richard Manuel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Manuel) and Garth Hudson. By 1976, years on the road had worn them down, so they chose to end their touring career with a show that honoured the people who shaped their music.

The guests included [Joni Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joni_Mitchell), [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young), [Eric Clapton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Clapton), [Van Morrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Morrison) and [Muddy Waters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muddy_Waters). According to the report, these were some of the highlights:

- [Ronnie Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronnie_Hawkins) performed "Who Do You Love", reuniting the group with the frontman they once backed as the Hawks.
- Neil Young sang "Helpless" and Joni Mitchell sang "Coyote".
- Muddy Waters delivered "Mannish Boy", and Eric Clapton joined for "Further On Up the Road".
- Van Morrison's "Caravan" is described as a defining moment of the evening.

## Where Dylan fits in

Dylan's link to The Band goes back to the 1960s, when the musicians played with him during his electric performances. The report calls that period one of the most consequential shifts in popular music.

At Winterland he returned for several songs, among them "Baby Let Me Follow You Down", "I Don't Believe You" and "Forever Young". Our earlier piece looks at how close he came to [pulling out of the film](https://moduscarry.com/bob-dylan-the-last-waltz/), which explains why so little of his set was shot for the finished movie.

## A companion Bobby Charles reissue

Rhino is also returning Bobby Charles' self-titled 1972 album to vinyl through the same series. Charles played "Down South in New Orleans" at the concert. His album was recorded in Woodstock and co-produced by Danko, with Danko, Helm, Hudson and Manuel all playing on it.

That record was mastered from its original analog tapes by Kevin Gray and pressed on 180-gram vinyl at Optimal in Germany. It includes "Small Town Talk" and "Street People".
