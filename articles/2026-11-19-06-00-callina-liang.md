**Quick summary**

- *Street Fighter*, which stars [Callina Liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callina_Liang) as Chun-Li, has reached about US$100 million worldwide, according to Collider.
- Its reported budget is US$82 million, and Collider says a film of this size usually needs roughly double that to break even in cinemas.
- The film opened in nearly 3,600 US theatres and now plays in a little over 800.
- Premium video on demand starts on December 1.

*Street Fighter* has crossed US$100 million at the worldwide box office, but the milestone comes as cinemas drop it quickly. Collider reported on November 18 that the film has lost about 2,700 theatres since opening, and that it now sits in a little more than 800 locations. For [Callina Liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callina_Liang), who plays Chun-Li, it is a modest finish for her biggest studio role so far.

## What the box office numbers say

According to [Collider](https://collider.com/street-fighter-box-office-100-million-removed-from-2700-theaters/), the film has earned exactly US$100 million worldwide. Wikipedia's figure is slightly higher, at about US$100.8 million, which is the kind of gap that usually comes from different tracking dates.

The reported budget is US$82 million. That puts the gross at roughly 1.2 times the cost. Collider notes that a film of this scale typically needs about twice its budget to turn a theatrical profit, so the studio will have to lean on home viewing and other revenue to make up the difference.

## From 3,600 theatres to about 800

The drop in screens is the sharper story. Collider says the film opened in nearly 3,600 domestic theatres. It is now in a little over 800, with the report putting the number removed at about 2,700.

That leaves a short window. Collider says the film arrives on premium video on demand on December 1, which gives it about two more weeks on the big screen.

## Who is in the film

*Street Fighter* is directed by Kitao Sakurai and runs 119 minutes with a PG-13 rating, according to Collider. Andrew Koji plays Ryu and Noah Centineo plays Ken Masters. Liang plays Chun-Li, one of the best-known fighters in the video game series. Wikipedia's cast list also includes Cody Rhodes and Orville Peck. Collider's own cast information is inconsistent on the actor playing Guile, so treat that detail with caution.

The film is an adaptation of the long-running fighting game. If you follow screen adaptations, our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section has more on how other game films have performed.

## How critics and audiences reacted

Reviews were mixed. Collider reports a 42% critics' score and a 78% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The critics' consensus on that site says the film shows real affection for the games and plenty of style, but that its storytelling is incoherent and its humour juvenile. Metacritic's score, per Wikipedia, is 43 out of 100 from 28 critics.

The gap between critics and audiences is common for game adaptations. Here it has not been enough to keep the film in cinemas.

## What happens next

The film heads to premium video on demand on December 1. Whether its home performance can make up for a theatrical run that fell short of break-even is not yet known, and no studio figures on that have been published.
