**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) appeared on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon in New York on Tuesday 24 November.
- She wore a Carolina Herrera Spring 2027 midi dress with black floral appliqués, according to Red Carpet Fashion Awards and WWD.
- Her shoes were Christian Louboutin Miss Z embellished mesh pumps, which WWD says have a 100 mm heel.
- She was there to talk about You Can See Everything, which Far Out Magazine reports has passed US$100 million worldwide.

Amanda Seyfried chose black florals over a classic little black dress for her Tonight Show visit on Tuesday, pairing a fresh-from-the-runway Carolina Herrera design with crystal-trimmed Louboutin pumps. She was in New York to talk about her scene-stealing role in the documentary You Can See Everything, and the outfit drew as much attention as the stories she told.

## The Carolina Herrera dress

The dress is a sleeveless midi from the label's Spring 2027 collection. Red Carpet Fashion Awards describes oversized black floral appliqués with crystal-embellished centres, and says the placement of the flowers lets glimpses of skin show without the sheer fabric taking over the look.

WWD, which also covered the outfit, describes the same design as black 3D florals set against black sheer material. The collection marked Carolina Herrera's 45th anniversary. Creative director Wes Gordon told WWD at a preview that he leaned into the idea of "Ladies Who Lunch", looking back to 1980s Park Avenue through the house's accessories, fabrics, prints and ruffles.

That makes a dark, textured dress a quiet choice for a late-night couch, and one that keeps the focus on the detail rather than on bold colour.

## The Louboutin pumps

WWD identifies the shoes as Christian Louboutin's Miss Z Degrastrass Perla, made from black mesh with a tonal leather lining. Hand-placed strass and pearls cover the upper, with the densest cluster at the pointed toe. The pump has a padded insole, a 100 mm stiletto heel and the brand's red sole.

The Miss Z launched at the start of 2025 as a more comfortable pump. WWD says it is unofficially named for Zendaya, as stylist Law Roach confirmed, and has since been worn by Dua Lipa, Ciara and Elle Fanning. Red Carpet Fashion Awards noted that the crystals on the shoes echoed those on the dress.

## What she talked about

The interview covered her role as Elizabeth Holmes in The Dropout, her part in You Can See Everything and her work with [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder). People.com listed the appearance in its daily photo roundup.

Seyfried told Fallon that she joined the documentary late. "I just jumped in kind of at the last minute, and it turned into something else," she said, as quoted by Far Out Magazine. She added that the script was so tightly controlled she might only read it the night before, through an authentication app. We covered that account in more detail in our report on [Amanda Seyfried and the secret You Can See Everything script](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-you-can-see-everything-3/).

HuffPost UK, meanwhile, reports that she also spoke to Rolling Stone about the film's prison phone call with Holmes. Seyfried said some viewers asked whether it was real, and her reply was: "It's a documentary. Who else would it be?" HuffPost adds that the film reached UK cinemas last week and that her work in the reenactments has made her an early favourite for a Best Supporting Actress nomination.
