**Quick summary**

- Renate Reinsve Bond talk: IndulgExpress listed [Renate Reinsve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Reinsve) as a "dark-horse prestige pick" for the next Bond film on 17 November 2026.
- The same piece says Amazon MGM has confirmed [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as 007 with [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) directing.
- No female co-star has been announced and the studio has said nothing on casting, so the list is speculation.
- Reinsve's drama *Fjord*, with [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan), reached UK and Irish cinemas on 13 November, according to Time Out.

The Renate Reinsve Bond talk is only speculation: she has been named as a long-shot contender for the next Bond film, though nothing about the role has been confirmed. The suggestion comes from a 17 November web story by [IndulgExpress](https://www.indulgexpress.com/ampstories/web-stories/2026/Nov/17/who-can-be-the-next-bond-girl-opposite-robert-pattinson), which ran a casting wish list for the female lead opposite Pattinson. It lands in the same week her latest film, *Fjord*, opened in Britain.

## What the Renate Reinsve Bond report actually says

IndulgExpress states that Amazon MGM has confirmed Pattinson as the next 007, with Villeneuve directing. It also says plainly that no co-star has been announced and that the studio has said nothing on female casting. Everything after that is the outlet's own opinion.

The list is long. It runs through [Emma Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Watson), [Elizabeth Olsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Olsen), [Simone Ashley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Ashley), [Marisa Abela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marisa_Abela), [Jayme Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jayme_Lawson), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy) and [Anna Sawai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Sawai), among others. Reinsve is the one labelled the dark horse.

The outlet's reasoning is that Villeneuve favours a European arthouse sensibility, and that Reinsve has the emotional depth to make a Bond film feel like a character study. That is a pitch, not a report. The piece does not cite any source close to the production.

## Why her name comes up

Reinsve is a Norwegian actress whose profile has grown through festival and arthouse work. Her latest release is the clearest recent example of the kind of film the list leans on.

## Fjord: the film behind the buzz

*Fjord* is written and directed by Cristian Mungiu, who won the 2007 Palme d'Or for *4 Months, 3 Weeks and 2 Days*. It premiered at Cannes in May 2026. Reinsve plays Lisbet, a Norwegian woman who returns to her home town with her Romanian husband Mihai, played by Stan.

According to [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/fjord-review-cannes-sebastian-stan-renate-reinsve/), the couple and their five children face growing tension with neighbours after their eldest daughter arrives at school with bruises. Child protection services step in, the children are placed in foster care and the parents face a legal fight. Time Out says corporal punishment is a crime in Norway and that the family risks prison and losing their children.

## How critics responded

Reviews are measured rather than rapturous. [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/movies/fjord-review-2026) gave the film three stars out of five. It called it a slow-boiling moral drama, found it chilly but gripping, and compared it unfavourably with Thomas Vinterberg's *The Hunt*.

TheWrap's Ben Croll praised the film's precision and its long takes, and said Stan and Reinsve suit the material well. He felt it stumbles when it turns to courtroom drama, where its themes become too explicit.

## What happens next

On Bond, there is nothing to wait for from Reinsve's side yet. Until Amazon MGM or Villeneuve names a female lead, every list, including this one, is guesswork. Readers should treat any casting claim without a named studio source accordingly.

On screen, *Fjord* is already in UK and Irish cinemas, and Time Out lists a US release of 9 October. The sources read for this story give no Australian release date.
