**Quick summary**

- [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw), who played Q in three Bond films, says the franchise "needs to be reinvented completely".
- He made the comments in an interview with The Sunday Times, reported by the Irish Examiner on 1 November 2026.
- He also called Q's storyline "unsatisfying" because the character's sexuality was only mentioned in passing.
- Daniel Craig's successor as 007 has not been announced, and the next film is still in development.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) has a clear view of where the [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) series should go next: somewhere new. The actor, who played the gadget-master Q opposite Daniel Craig, told The Sunday Times that the franchise "needs to be reinvented completely", according to the [Irish Examiner](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/arid-41931741.html). Entertainment sites picked the remarks up again this week, including a report from IMDb on 26 November.

## What Ben Whishaw said about the future of Bond

Whishaw appeared as Q in Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015) and No Time To Die (2021), Craig's final film as 007. He took over the role from John Cleese, according to the Irish Examiner.

His reasoning was practical. The world has changed a great deal since the last Bond film, he said, so the series has to work out what is still relevant today. He was also careful not to overstate his position, adding that it is not his place to decide, as reported by [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/11/02/ben-whishaw-bond-2026/).

That caution matters. Whishaw is describing his own expectation and wish, not announcing anything about the studio's plans. Neither report quotes the producers responding.

## Why he called Q's storyline unsatisfying

Whishaw also returned to a point he first raised in 2022. He described Q's arc as "unsatisfying" because the character's sexuality is mentioned only in passing and never followed up, PinkNews reported.

The scene he means is in No Time To Die, where Q is shown cooking dinner and appears to be expecting a date with a man. The story does not return to it. Whishaw came out as gay in 2013, according to the Irish Examiner, which gives his comment extra weight as a view on how the series handled the character.

## The hunt for the next 007

Craig's successor has not been named. Whishaw said he rarely thinks about the role. He described the secrecy around casting as deliberate, a way of building hype and suspense, and said actors on such projects see only their own script pages, according to the Irish Examiner.

Names have circulated in the press. Both outlets list Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor as reported contenders. These are press reports, not confirmations, and no casting has been announced.

## What is known about the next film

The next Bond film is in development. The Irish Examiner says Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight was announced last year as its writer. PinkNews adds that Denis Villeneuve is set to direct, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.

Whishaw's own place in that future looks unlikely if his comments are any guide. He is calling for a clean break, which suggests a fresh approach to the whole cast, though he has not said whether he has been asked back or not.
