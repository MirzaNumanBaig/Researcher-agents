**Quick summary**

- A new 60-second teaser for *Dune: Part Three* gives the first proper listen to [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the shape-shifting Scytale, according to Screen Realm.
- Warner Bros. releases the film in Australian cinemas and IMAX on December 16, and in the US on December 18, 2026.
- Pattinson has also been named the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), with Denis Villeneuve directing Bond 26.
- His heavy workload has become a social media joke, and film experts told CBC Kids News there are solid industry reasons for it.

Robert Pattinson's latest voice is a soft, playful and faintly menacing one. A new teaser for *Dune: Part Three*, which debuted through IGN, lets audiences hear him as Scytale for the first time, just weeks before the film reaches Australian screens on December 16, according to [Screen Realm](https://screenrealm.com/dune-part-three-teaser-robert-pattinson-voice/).

## What the teaser shows

The clip has Scytale, pale and white-haired, probing Chani (Zendaya) for news of Paul Atreides' children. "I've heard rumors of offspring," he says. Chani waves it off as "desert legend" and "children's stories". Pattinson's reply, "I love children's stories", is delivered with the kind of gentle unease that has made the line a talking point online, Screen Realm reports.

Scytale is a Face Dancer of the Bene Tleilax, a being able to take on other people's identities. He is among those plotting against Emperor Paul (Timothée Chalamet), whose fear of assassination runs through the teaser. Paul is married to Princess Irulan (Florence Pugh), so a stranger's interest in his children with Chani carries real weight.

Based on Frank Herbert's 1969 novel *Dune Messiah*, the film is set 17 years after *Dune: Part Two*, as Paul deals with the fallout of the holy war fought in his name. Jason Momoa, Rebecca Ferguson, Javier Bardem, Anya Taylor-Joy and Charlotte Rampling are also in the cast.

## A career built on unexpected voices

Screen Realm notes that Pattinson's voice work has become a running feature of his career. There is the gravelly bird in *The Boy and the Heron*, the contrasting voices of *Mickey 17* and the much-memed line about getting "those beggars" out of the room in *The Odyssey*.

He also spent months shaping a voice for *Primetime*, in which he plays *To Catch a Predator* host Chris Hansen. At the Venice Film Festival, as reported by Reuters, he joked that he can hear a little of Hansen in his *Odyssey* performance.

## Is he in too many films?

The teaser lands in the same week as Pattinson's Bond announcement, and social media has filled with memes saying he is in everything, from Batman to *Dune* to *The Odyssey*. CBC Kids News put the question to three film experts.

Hoai-Tran Bui, a film critic at Inverse, called him one of the most talented actors of his generation. She said he built "prestige arthouse cred" through independent films with directors such as David Cronenberg after the *Harry Potter* and *Twilight* years. Charlie Keil, a professor at the University of Toronto, said that balance of mainstream and quirky work established him as both a versatile actor and a movie star, and called him "a chameleon" well suited to Bond.

Gabriel Menotti Miglio Pinto Gonring of Queen's University offered the commercial view. Studios need a marketing plan from day one, he said, and a proven name is one of a film's biggest selling points. The cost, he argued, is fewer chances for other actors and a more uniform screen culture. Keil added that actors know their box office pull has a shelf life of roughly five to 10 years, so few can be blamed for making the most of it. The same CBC piece notes that [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway) and Zendaya are in a similar position.

## The Bond and Batman schedule

Euronews reports that Bond 26 is due to start production next year, with Villeneuve directing and Steven Knight writing. Before that, Pattinson must finish *The Batman Part II*. Production was paused when director [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves) faced a personal issue, and Steven Soderbergh has stepped in to help shoot the final weeks, according to [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/11/26/is-james-bond-conservative-trump-gives-verdict-on-robert-pattinson-but-asked-about-007s-po).

That puts the earliest Bond release in the second half of 2028, Euronews adds, seven years after Daniel Craig's last outing. Pattinson, 40, called the role the "honour of a lifetime". Cinemablend's writer points out he will be the first actor to have played both Bond and Batman.
