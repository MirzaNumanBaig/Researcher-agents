**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) are set to star in the meta action comedy *Stuntnuts*, according to Dark Horizons.
- Both actors will play heightened versions of themselves.
- The film is expected to be one of the biggest titles on sale at AFM, the film market.
- Matt Damon is reportedly making a cameo, and several real stunt performers will appear.

Chris Hemsworth and Sam Rockwell have signed on to *Stuntnuts*, a self-mocking action comedy set behind the scenes of a Hollywood blockbuster, [Dark Horizons](https://www.darkhorizons.com/hemsworth-rockwell-join-vaughns-stuntnuts/) reported on 8 November. The Chris Hemsworth Stuntnuts project is being lined up as a headline sales title at AFM.

## What Stuntnuts is about

The story follows two gifted stunt performers who take work on a huge Hollywood blockbuster, hoping the pay will save their struggling community gym. Dark Horizons says the production then spins out of control, driven by increasingly dangerous stunts and the clashing egos of its leading men. Saving the gym, it adds, may turn out to be the least of their worries.

The "meta" label matters here. Hemsworth and Rockwell will both play exaggerated versions of themselves, so the film pokes fun at the very business that made them famous. That gives a star who spent years as Thor in the Marvel films plenty of room to joke about the machinery of big-budget action.

## Who is attached

Dark Horizons names [Matthew Vaughn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Vaughn), the filmmaker behind *Kingsman* and *Layer Cake*, as the person helming the project. The same report later says [Damien Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Walters) will direct, describing it as his second film after the upcoming UK action feature [School Fight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuntnuts_Does_School_Fight). The report does not explain how the two roles divide, so the exact credits remain unclear until the producers confirm them.

Walters, a British stunt performer, won an Actor Award for his stunt work on *Skyfall*, according to the same outlet.

[Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon) is reportedly making a cameo. That detail is described as a report rather than a confirmed casting, and the site has [covered Damon's link to the film](https://moduscarry.com/stuntnuts-chris-hemsworth/) separately.

## Real stunt performers in the cast

The film leans on people who do this work for a living. Dark Horizons lists [Bobby Holland Hanton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Holland_Hanton), Hemsworth's real-life stunt double, alongside Greg Townley, who doubles for [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) as Spider-Man. Paul Lowe, a *Game of Thrones* alumnus, and Hakura Oshima, a stuntman from *The Matrix*, complete the named group.

Casting a star's actual stunt double in a film about a star and his stunt crew is an obvious in-joke, and it should help the action scenes feel grounded.

## Where the project came from

Legendary-backed banner 193, run by Patrick Wachsberger, is behind the film, per Dark Horizons. The project was first mentioned in 2024 under the title *The Stuntman*. The report calls it one of the biggest projects on sale at AFM, which means buyers will be shown it as a package before distribution deals are settled.

## What happens next

AFM is where distribution rights are sold, so the next news will most likely be territory deals and a confirmed production schedule. No release date has been reported.
