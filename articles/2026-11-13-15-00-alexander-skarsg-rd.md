**Quick summary**

- Wicker has now earned $30 million worldwide, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/alexander-skarsgard-wicker-box-office-30-million/).
- That is three times the film's reported $10 million budget.
- It is Black Bear's highest-grossing self-distributed release in North America.
- Alexander Skarsgård has also been linked to a new project, The Confessions.

The fairy-tale romance Wicker, led by [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsgård), has pushed the Wicker box office total past $30 million worldwide, three times its reported $10 million budget. Collider reported the milestone this week, after the film took $581,000 on its latest daily count and placed eighth in the US rankings.

## Wicker box office: how it reached $30 million

The film opened with $5.7 million over its first weekend. It has stayed in the domestic top ten since, and its most recent daily figure came from just over 2,000 locations. Last weekend it added $3.4 million and again finished inside the top ten.

Those numbers are modest next to the weekend's biggest release. According to Collider, Takashi Yamazaki's [Godzilla Minus Zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla_Minus_Zero) opened with $30 million, which is the same total Wicker has collected over its whole run so far. Wicker, by contrast, has kept a place in the top ten over several days.

## A win for Black Bear's own distribution

The milestone matters for the company behind the release too. Collider says Wicker is now Black Bear's highest-grossing self-distributed film in North America. Black Bear bought the distribution rights for $2.5 million, so the film's worldwide total is already far beyond what the company paid for the right to release it.

Collider's report does not say how much of the $30 million came from North America and how much from other markets, so the split remains unclear.

## What the film is about

Wicker is based on the short story The Wicker Husband by Ursula Wills and is written and directed by Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson. The official synopsis describes a smelly, single fisherwoman on the edge of a seaside village who is tired of ridicule from her narrow-minded neighbours. She commissions a husband made of wicker, and the unusual romance stirs up outrage, jealousy and chaos in a conservative town.

Collider describes Skarsgård's turn as a typically quirky performance in another elaborate costume. How the look was built is covered in our report on [the Wicker makeup that took 18 months to build](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-wicker-makeup/). The cast around him includes Academy Award winner [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman) as the Fisherwoman, plus [Peter Dinklage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Dinklage) and [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki).

Critics have been kind. Collider's Therese Lacson called it "an immensely entertaining watch" and "a fun and quirky story about love and overcoming loneliness" in her review.

## What else is next for Skarsgård

Collider also reports that Skarsgård has joined The Confessions, described as a "cautionary psycho-dramedy". His Pillion co-star [Harry Melling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Melling) is attached, along with Maya Hawke and [Hunter Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Schafer). The report gives no start date or release window.
