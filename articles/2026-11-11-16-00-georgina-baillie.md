**Quick summary**

- [Georgina Baillie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Baillie) gave a character statement for [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) at his sentencing on Monday 9 November 2026.
- She wrote: "I still choose to support him as a person I know him to be."
- Brand was jailed for 10 years and six months at Southwark Crown Court after being convicted of rape and sexual assault.
- Days earlier, Baillie told the Daily Mail "justice has been served" and that she wanted Brand to "right the wrongs".

Georgina Baillie, the granddaughter of the late actor [Andrew Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Sachs), put her name to a written statement supporting Russell Brand at his sentencing hearing in London on 9 November. Brand's barrister read from it as he asked the judge for leniency. The comedian was then sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison.

## What Georgina Baillie told the court

According to [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/crime/andrew-sachs-granddaughter-georgina-baillie-russell-brand-b3081721.html), Brand's barrister Ian Winter KC told the court that Baillie had provided a character statement as part of the defence case. In it she wrote: "I still choose to support him as a person I know him to be."

Winter said Baillie had "as good a reason as anyone" not to back Brand. He described her as the person "right at the eye of the Sachsgate storm", and said Brand was "rightly pilloried" for what he called a disgraceful prank on live radio.

The barrister also said Baillie had been sober for six years. In his account, Brand had persuaded her to go through rehabilitation and paid for it. [The Oxford Mail](https://www.oxfordmail.co.uk/news/26712910.andrew-sachs-granddaughter-declares-support-rapist-russell-brand/) reports that Winter used her statement as one example of people Brand had helped, and that he asked the judge not to "sentence him as the monster portrayed outside this courtroom".

Winter added that many others had offered statements but, in the main, did not want their names entered into the process.

## Her comments days earlier

The court statement came shortly after Baillie spoke to the Daily Mail following the guilty verdicts. As relayed by The Independent, she said she was disappointed, and that "justice has been served".

She also drew a line around her own experience. She said Brand never raped or assaulted her, but that she did not know what happened in the private rooms where the women were, and that their evidence was "compelling". "I now want Russell to right the wrongs," she said, adding that owning up would be the first part of making amends.

Baillie described Brand as a "sex addict" and "seriously unwell", while saying that did not excuse his actions. The [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/showbiz/breaking-georgina-baillie-gives-support-37858420) noted the contrast between those remarks and her court statement. Baillie, 41, has said she had a consensual relationship with Brand.

## The Sachsgate link

Baillie's name is tied to the 2008 Sachsgate affair. Brand and broadcaster [Jonathan Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Ross) left lewd voicemails on the answerphone of Sachs, best known for [Fawlty Towers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fawlty_Towers), during a BBC Radio 2 show, including remarks about Brand sleeping with his granddaughter. She was 21 at the time.

The Independent reports the row shook the BBC, led Brand and the Radio 2 controller to quit, and prompted a tighter approach to how the corporation vetted its output.

Baillie has said the calls strained family relationships. She told the Daily Mail she later saw her grandfather in a care home while he had dementia, and apologised. "And then he died," she said. Brand apologised to her years after the incident, according to the Oxford Mail.

## The sentence

Brand, 51, was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009, [The National](https://thenational.co.za/culture/2026-11-09-inside-russell-brand-s-sentencing-prayer-beads-celebrity-privilege-and-a-10-5-year-prison-term/) reports. He was acquitted of two further rape charges and one count of attempted indecent assault.

Judge Joel Bennathan gave nine years for the rape, plus further terms for the assaults, making 10 years and six months in total. Brand must serve two-thirds, seven years, before he can be considered for release on licence. The judge criticised his "staggering arrogance" and his belief that fame meant he could act as he liked.

The National says Baillie was one of six people who provided character references. The women who gave evidence described lasting anxiety and shame, and one told the court she had carried an "undeserved feeling of shame" for more than 20 years.

## What happens next

The Metropolitan Police have confirmed that Brand remains under investigation over two further rape allegations, according to The National, and have indicated they expect other potential victims to come forward. Brand has been jailed on the verdicts reached at trial, and any further charges would be a separate matter.
