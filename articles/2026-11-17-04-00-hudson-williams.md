**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) and [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) share a shirtless kiss in episode 5 of *Yaga*, according to Out Magazine, and the clip has gone viral.
- *Yaga* creator [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) says filming the male leads this way was a deliberate choice that serves the story.
- Bulgari has released a first teaser for its Christmas 2026 campaign with Williams, [Joey King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_King) and [Caleb McLaughlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caleb_McLaughlin).
- The campaign theme is "Joy Is in Our Hands", and a fuller reveal is still to come.

Hudson Williams is having a busy week on two fronts. On 16 November, Out Magazine reported that his kiss with Noah Reid in the mystery series *Yaga* was spreading fast online, while Bulgari, the Italian jewellery house where he is an ambassador, unveiled the first look at its Christmas 2026 campaign with him alongside Joey King and Caleb McLaughlin.

## The Yaga kiss everyone is talking about

According to [Out Magazine](https://www.out.com/gay-tv-shows/hudson-williams-and-noah-reid-yaga-kiss), episode 5 of *Yaga* features a scene in which Williams and Reid, best known for [*Schitt's Creek*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek), kiss while both are shirtless. Out says Williams takes the lead, placing a hand around Reid's neck.

The outlet describes the moment as one *Heated Rivalry* fans had been waiting for. Williams rose to fame in [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), and Out reports that the clip drew a strong reaction on social media, with viewers praising how the two actors looked together.

Out also says *Yaga* has gone viral most weeks when a new episode arrives, because of its twisty plot and its more explicit scenes. For many viewers, the series is their first chance to see Williams in a role far from his best-known one.

## Why the creator filmed it this way

The series was created by Kat Sandler, who also wrote the stage play it is based on. On the show's official podcast, Out reports, she explained why the camera lingers on the male leads.

Sandler compared the approach to how women's bodies have long been shown in thrillers, horror and mystery, citing [*Basic Instinct*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_Instinct) as a point of reference while noting it is not the same thing. In her view, the series turns that familiar gaze around.

She acknowledged that it was a bold decision. "It was definitely a choice, but I stand by that it's important to the story," she said, according to Out. The outlet adds that Williams has also appeared shirtless in earlier episodes, and in one episode plays two of the three people in a scene.

## Bulgari's Christmas campaign teaser

Away from the screen, Williams is the first name in Bulgari's new festive campaign. As [Xmag UK](https://en.xmag.live/bulgari-brings-together-hudson-williams-joey-king-and-caleb-mclaughlin-for-its-new-christmas-2026-campaign/) reports, the brand shared a short video on social media showing him with Joey King and Caleb McLaughlin around a decorated Christmas tree in a bright studio.

The mood is deliberately casual. Both Xmag and [DSCENE](https://www.designscene.net/2026/11/bulgari-holiday-hudson-williams.html) describe knitwear, relaxed styling and moments of laughter and conversation, rather than the grand sets often used for high jewellery. Bulgari asked followers what brings the three together over the holidays and told them to stay tuned.

The campaign is tied to "Joy Is in Our Hands", the holiday theme Bulgari introduced earlier in November. Xmag says the idea connects to the brand's Italian "Joy of Living" philosophy and focuses on small gestures and the meaning of giving a gift. Williams already has a relationship with the house as an ambassador, while King and McLaughlin are new to the line-up, and DSCENE notes that early online reaction has centred on how unexpected the trio is.

## What happens next

Bulgari has not yet said when the full campaign will arrive. DSCENE reports that further details will follow the teaser, so more images and video are likely before Christmas.

For *Yaga*, new episodes continue to drop, and Out suggests the show will keep drawing attention. Neither source has confirmed anything beyond what is already released.
