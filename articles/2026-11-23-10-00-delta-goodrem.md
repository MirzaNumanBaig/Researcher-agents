**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) landed in the Strictly dance-off for the first time on Sunday, November 22.
- They beat [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) and [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu), who left the competition.
- The judges were unanimous in saving Goodrem, according to the Manchester Evening News.
- Her Blackpool quickstep had earned 36 points, the highest score of her series.
- Strictly returns on Saturday, November 28, at 6.30pm on BBC One.

Delta Goodrem has survived her first Strictly Come Dancing dance-off. The Australian singer and Widdrington were saved by the judges on Sunday, November 22, while Winstone and Gu became the latest couple to leave the show.

## How the dance-off played out

The result followed the Blackpool special, when the series moves to the Tower Ballroom for one weekend. Winstone and Gu finished joint bottom of the leaderboard on 32 points, level with Graeme Hall and [Lauren Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Oakley), according to [Entertainment Daily](https://www.entertainmentdaily.com/tv/who-left-strictly-tonight-jaime-winstone-eliminated-delta-goodrem/). Their samba to George Michael's "Freedom! 90" had not been enough to lift them clear.

Goodrem and Widdrington were in the bottom two despite a strong night. Both couples danced again before the judges voted. Head judge [Shirley Ballas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Ballas) chose to save Goodrem and Widdrington first. [Anton Du Beke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Du_Beke) agreed, and [Craig Revel Horwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Revel_Horwood) followed, which settled the vote. Motsi Mabuse did not need to vote, but said she would have saved the Australian pair too.

For Winstone and Gu it was a familiar spot. Entertainment Daily reports it was their fifth dance-off of the series.

## A surprise given Goodrem's score

The dance-off was unexpected because Goodrem had just posted her best mark of the series. Their quickstep to "I Wanna Be the Only One" by Eternal and [BeBe Winans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeBe_Winans) scored 36, which left them second on the leaderboard, according to the [Manchester Evening News](https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/tv/strictly-come-dancing-fans-spot-34950570).

Three couples sat above them with a near-perfect 39 out of 40: [Cach Mercer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cach_Mercer) with [Maddie Ingoldsby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddie_Ingoldsby), John Nellis with [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones), and Tabby Stoecker with [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita). A 36 normally keeps a couple well clear of danger, which is why the public vote told a different story from the judges' scores.

## What viewers made of it

Reaction online was split. The Manchester Evening News gathered a few theories from viewers who tried to explain why Goodrem was at risk. One suggested the backing dancers overshadowed the routine. Another thought that dancing first may have meant the performance was forgotten by the time voting opened. Others commented on the song choice and on a costume piece that seemed to get in the way.

These are fan opinions, not anything the judges or the BBC have said. On Winstone's exit, Entertainment Daily quoted viewers who said they had seen it coming, with one writing that she was unlikely to survive against Goodrem.

## A difficult run before Blackpool

Goodrem had already missed a week earlier in the series because of a painful rib injury, the Manchester Evening News reports. Widdrington then fell ill before their previous performance, so fellow professional [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in at the last minute. Blackpool was the first week the two were back together on the floor.
