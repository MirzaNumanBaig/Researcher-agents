**Quick summary**

- Kylie Minogue has joined the voice cast of the animated musical comedy Bad Fairies, playing Clowder, the Cat Witch.
- Cynthia Erivo announced the casting from the Hollywood Bowl stage, according to Animation World Network.
- Warner Bros. Pictures releases the film in theatres on 21 May 2027.
- Separately, the International Confederation of Music Publishers says AI models have learned from songs by Minogue and Nick Cave, per a Guardian report cited by Yahoo.
- Australia is reportedly the only country weighing tech companies' request to loosen copyright rules for AI.

[Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) will voice a cat who is also a witch. The pop star has joined the cast of Bad Fairies, the animated musical comedy led by [Cynthia Erivo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cynthia_Erivo), and the news came in an unusual way: Erivo announced it from the stage of the Hollywood Bowl, then pointed Minogue out in the audience.

It has been a busy week for the singer, and not all of it is about music. A separate story puts her name at the centre of Australia's argument over AI and copyright.

## Who does Kylie Minogue play in Bad Fairies?

Minogue voices Clowder, the Cat Witch, according to [Animation World Network](https://www.awn.com/news/bad-fairies-fills-out-its-fairy-gang-minogue-groff-and-tate), which reported the casting from a Warner Bros. Pictures Animation and [Locksmith Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locksmith_Animation) release. Erivo announced it at her one-night concert, Let Me Sing to You, last Thursday night.

[Jonathan Groff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Groff) joins as Robin Goodfellow and [Catherine Tate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Tate) as the Fairy Gothmother. Erivo plays Jayne Staplegun, one of a group of rule-breaking fairies. [Ncuti Gatwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ncuti_Gatwa), [James Acaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Acaster) and [Dee Bradley Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dee_Bradley_Baker) were announced earlier.

Director Megan Nicole Dong said Clowder "needed to be impossibly glamorous and hilariously chaotic in the same breath", and that Minogue "was always the dream". Minogue's own take was shorter: playing a cat and a witch is "a little bit magical, a little bit mischievous and a whole lot of fun".

## What is Bad Fairies about?

[Bad Fairies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Fairies) is set in contemporary London and follows a gang of fairies who shake up their magical world. Dong directs, with [Olivier Staphylas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Staphylas) co-directing, from an original screenplay by [Deborah Frances-White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_Frances-White).

The music is a draw in its own right. [Toby Marlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Marlow) and [Lucy Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Moss), the pair behind Six: The Musical, are writing the songs. Isabella "Machine" Summers of Florence + The Machine is composing the score, and Ian Eisendrath, who worked on KPop Demon Hunters, is executive music producer. Erivo opened the second half of her concert with an orchestral piece built on "Hard to Believe", Jayne's song from the film.

The film is the first feature under the first-look deal between Warner Bros. Pictures Animation and Locksmith, whose earlier films are Ron's Gone Wrong (2021) and Netflix's That Christmas (2024). Production is under way in London with DNEG Animation as digital partner.

## Why is Kylie Minogue in the AI copyright debate?

The second story is less cheerful. According to a Guardian report, as relayed by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/world/articles/kylie-minogue-latest-artist-caught-132716067.html), the International Confederation of Music Publishers (ICMP) says AI models have already learned from songs by Minogue and [Nick Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cave), and neither artist was paid.

ICMP head John Phelan told Guardian Australia that AI firms are asking lawmakers to "legitimise larceny". Tech companies have reportedly put the same request to dozens of governments, and Australia appears to be the only one giving it a second look.

That sits awkwardly with what Prime Minister [Anthony Albanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Albanese) said in July, when he called it "theft" if creators could not decide whether AI learns from their work or how much they earn from it. Yahoo reports that lawmakers from both sides on a parliamentary AI committee described the issue as a deadlock this week. The companies building the most advanced models, the report says, will not train them in Australia unless local copyright law changes.

None of this is a finding against any company, and the sources do not say which AI systems are involved. It is the publishers' claim, reported second-hand.

## What happens next

Bad Fairies opens in theatres on 21 May 2027, with Warner Bros. Pictures releasing. The Australian release date has not been given in the sources. On the copyright question, no decision has been reported; the parliamentary committee's deadlock is the latest point on record.
