**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has landed his first UK number one album in 19 years with *Airplane Mode*, according to the Official Charts Company.
- He was voted out near the end of the BBC's [The Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) finale, weeks before the chart result.
- He has said he joined the show mainly to promote the 12-track album, not to win.
- *Airplane Mode* beat Royal Blood, Olivia Rodrigo, Skepta and Kings Of Leon.

James Blunt lost the prize on *The Celebrity Traitors*, but he has won the bigger contest he signed up for. The 52-year-old's eighth studio album, *Airplane Mode*, went straight to number one on the UK album chart, his first top spot since 2007, reports [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/showbiz/james-blunt-lands-first-uk-37876526), citing the Official Charts Company. For the full chart story, see our earlier report, [James Blunt Hits Number One With Airplane Mode After 19 Years](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-number-one-album/).

## How the Celebrity Traitors finale ended for Blunt

According to the Daily Star, the finale came down to a tied vote between eliminating King Kenny or Blunt. [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan) and Sebastian "Bash" Croft were given the chance to talk it through. Ranganathan switched his vote after trusting Croft's hunch that Kenny was a Traitor and Blunt a Faithful.

With Kenny gone, the pair chose to end the game. Ranganathan then caught Blunt off guard with an accusation, and the *You're Beautiful* singer opted to banish again. Ranganathan and Croft then voted him out and claimed victory, taking £83,000 for their chosen charities, Choose Love and the Refugee Council. The Daily Star credits the Manchester Evening News for those details.

The [Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/james-blunt-uk-album-charts-celebrity-traitors-b3084805.html) says Blunt reached the final three. Both outlets agree on the part that matters here: he never claimed to be playing for the trophy.

## A show used as a marketing platform

Blunt has openly said he took part to promote his new music rather than to win. The numbers suggest it worked. *Airplane Mode* is a 12-track record, and its chart-topping week came after the series ended.

His previous number one album was *All The Lost Souls* in 2007. His 2005 release [Back To Bedlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_Bedlam) spent 10 weeks at number one, so a 19-year wait since his last chart-topper is a long one.

The campaign also had a joke built in. Blunt promised to legally change his name to Ryan Air if the album reached number one, a nod to the budget airline. Whether he follows through is not addressed in either report.

## The rest of the chart

Blunt beat a strong field. Royal Blood sit at number two with *Dead Company*, and [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo) is third with *You Seem Pretty Sad For A Girl So In Love*. Skepta is fourth with *The Fork And Knife*, his first solo album in seven years, and Kings Of Leon complete the top five with *O My Beloved*.

On the singles chart, Welsh singer-songwriter Jamie Miller held number one for a fourth week with *Babydoll*. Ashe is second with *Stop The Wedding!*, [Tate McRae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_McRae) is third with *Housewife*, and [Sienna Spiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sienna_Spiro) is fourth. The Independent adds that festive songs have begun to enter the Top 40.

## What Blunt said

Blunt thanked listeners directly. "I can't tell you how grateful I am to every single person who has gone out there and downloaded, or bought, or streamed my album," he told the Official Charts Company. "From the bottom of my heart, thank you so much."
