**Quick summary**

- The Academy announced on 23 November that 38 animated films are eligible for Best Animated Feature at the 99th Academy Awards, a record.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson)'s film *Ray Gunn* is on the list.
- The previous high was 35 eligible films, for the 98th Academy Awards.
- Nominations will be announced on 21 January 2027, with winners revealed on 14 March 2027.

Scarlett Johansson's animated film *Ray Gunn* has been named among the 38 titles eligible for Best Animated Feature at the 99th Academy Awards, according to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2229808-coyote-vs-acme-scarlett-johansson-movie-36-others-oscars-record). The Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences released the list on 23 November, and it is the biggest the category has seen.

## A record-sized Scarlett Johansson Ray Gunn field

The previous record stood at 35 eligible films for the 98th Academy Awards, so this year's list adds three more. Only five of the 38 will be nominated, which makes the odds for any single title long at this stage.

*Ray Gunn* sits alongside *Coyote vs. Acme*, which ComingSoon.net names as the other headline entry. The report does not detail Johansson's role in the film, so this article does not describe it.

## The other films in contention

The list is crowded with family and franchise names. ComingSoon.net points to *Toy Story 5*, *Hexed*, *Hoppers*, *Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender*, *The Cat in the Hat*, *Forgotten Island*, *Goat*, *Minions & Monsters*, *Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom* and *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie*. A *Paw Patrol* film, *Paw Patrol: Dino Movie*, is also listed.

Those are only the titles the outlet singled out. The full list of 38 is longer than the highlights.

## Being on the list is not the same as qualifying

Eligibility on the list does not guarantee a place on the ballot. Some of the 38 films have not yet completed the required qualifications, the report says.

Under the Academy's special rules for the category, a film must meet the theatrical release requirements or qualify through the International Feature Film category. The theatrical route means screening for at least seven consecutive days in one of six qualifying US metropolitan areas. Screenings must run at least three times a day, with one starting between 6 pm and 10 pm each day.

An eligible film must also run longer than 40 minutes, and at least 75 per cent of it must be animated, not counting the opening and end credits.

## Who decides the nominees

Members of the Academy's Animation Branch are automatically eligible to vote on the five nominees. Other Academy members can also vote if they meet the minimum viewing requirements, according to the report.

## What happens next

The five Best Animated Feature nominees will be announced on 21 January 2027. Winners will be declared at the ceremony on 14 March 2027. Until then, films still short of the theatrical requirements have time to complete them.
