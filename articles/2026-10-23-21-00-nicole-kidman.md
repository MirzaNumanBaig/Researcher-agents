**Quick summary**

- Slovak pop singer Adéla's viral song "Nicole Kidman" rose from No. 9 to No. 6 on the Billboard Hot 100, a new high, according to Billboard.
- Adéla has two songs on the chart, the other being "Ain't In LA"; both come from her album Prima.
- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) discussed the track on The Tonight Show about a month ago.
- Adéla made her US television debut on the same show, performing a medley of both hits.

The Australian actress has become an unlikely chart booster. A song named after [Nicole Kidman](https://nicolekidmanofficial.com/) is now a top 10 hit in the United States, and its singer says the Oscar winner's praise on late-night television helped carry it there, Billboard reported on 23 October.

## From viral clip to the top 10

Adéla, a singer from Slovakia, now has two songs on the Billboard Hot 100. Her track "Nicole Kidman" moved from No. 9 to No. 6 on the latest chart, its highest position so far, Billboard said. The other entry is "Ain't In LA". Both appear on her album Prima.

Billboard describes the song as viral, and the attention has not come only from the charts. According to the magazine, Kidman, [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal) and other well-known names have all made TikTok videos set to Adéla's music.

## What Kidman said on The Tonight Show

About a month ago Kidman appeared on The Tonight Show, and the conversation turned to Adéla and her tribute. Billboard quotes the actress as saying she could not believe someone had written a song called "Nicole Kidman", and adding that the singer is "so talented".

Adéla later sat down with host Jimmy Fallon on the same programme. She called Kidman her muse and joked that the actress was "doing charity work" by mentioning her name around town. That line is her own, as reported by Billboard, and not a claim about anything Kidman has said or done beyond the interview.

## Disbelief and a TV debut

The singer told the audience she had assumed the celebrity TikToks were a prank, and that she first thought they were generated by AI. Her mother, she said, is also a Kidman fan and reacted with equal shock, wondering aloud whether people were going crazy or her daughter was a genius.

Adéla then moved from the couch to the stage for her television debut. Billboard says she led a group of dancers through a medley of "Nicole Kidman" and "Ain't In LA", in a performance it compared to a wild night at the Moulin Rouge.

## What happens next

Billboard does not report any planned collaboration between the two women, or any announcement about further performances.
