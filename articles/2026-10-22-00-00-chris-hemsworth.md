Chris Hemsworth is putting his name and face behind Australian whisky, with a new global campaign and a redesigned range for Archie Rose Distilling Co., the independent distillery he now co-owns. The trade press covered the launch on 21 October, while the actor was also reported to be training with a kickboxer in Thailand during filming.

## A parody ad with his brother

According to [Shots](https://shots.net/news/view/justin-mcmillan-directs-hemsworth-brothers-in-archie-rose-whisky-parody), the campaign is a 1:45 parody of a whisky commercial starring Hemsworth and his brother, Luke Hemsworth, and directed by Justin McMillan. The film, titled *True Story*, is set across eight locations in Australia. It plays the premise straight while the story becomes steadily more outrageous, with Chris telling a stranger, played by Luke, how he takes Australian whisky to the world.

McMillan told Shots the aim was a delicate balance of tongue-in-cheek comedy, letting viewers believe the story is serious before undercutting it with jokes. He said the brothers improvised together, which is not often seen in a commercial.

Time was tight. Shots reports that Hemsworth was available for only two days because of his film commitments, so the original script was too big to shoot. McMillan said that led the team to use AI for the "exposure" sequences showing Hemsworth taking whisky around the world. Those scenes were created by Lipstick in Berlin and cut in with the live-action footage.

Hamish Roxburgh, executive producer at Citadel, said the actor had just finished one film and went straight into a couple of weeks of night shoots on another project after the commercial. The ad was set in the Archie Rose bar, which Roxburgh described as having an old-school saloon feel with a cosmopolitan look.

## New look for three whiskies

Separately, [Transform magazine](https://www.transformmagazine.net/articles/2026/chris-hemsworth-and-thirst-co-create-identities-for-three-archie-rose-whiskies/) reports that creative studio Thirst has produced a new brand identity and bottle design for Archie Rose as the brand prepares for its next international chapter. The design draws on Australia's rugged landscapes, with a canteen-shaped bottle, weathered textures and earthy details meant to convey a mix of lightness and grit. It brings together three whiskies co-created with Hemsworth.

A headline from Bar Magazine also says the pair are bringing Archie Rose to the United States. We could not read that article, so we have not confirmed the details of any US launch.

## Padwork in Krabi

On the film side, [ONE Championship](https://www.onefc.com/news/a-great-session-ben-woolliss-feels-the-thunder-as-thor-star-chris-hemsworth-hits-the-pads-in-thailand/) reports that Hemsworth, who is filming *Extraction 3* in Thailand, trained at Huracan Fitness in Krabi with English kickboxer Ben Woolliss. Woolliss held the pads while Hemsworth worked through hooks and combinations, with kicks featuring heavily.

"It was a great session just hitting the pads and teaching him some techniques," Woolliss said, adding that Hemsworth has a great right hand and that his right kick was heavy. ONE says former Muay Thai world champion Rodtang Jitmuangnon also paid the actor a surprise visit earlier in the week.

## What comes next

ONE notes that Hemsworth returns for a third outing as Tyler Rake in *Extraction 3*, and that Thor is due back in *Avengers: Doomsday*, which it says opens in theatres this December. No date has been given in these reports for when the Archie Rose range will reach new markets.
