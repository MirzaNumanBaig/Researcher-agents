- The Take, a heist film from director Fernando Meirelles, is listed for release on 15 January 2027.
- Robert Pattinson plays Noah, a young man fresh out of the Army, with Denzel Washington as the thief who recruits him.

- Netflix first announced the film under the title Here Comes the Flood, according to Startefacts.

**The Take** is the next heist thriller to watch for in January, and its cast is stronger than most. [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) and [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) lead the film, which [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles) directs and which is listed for 15 January 2027, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Take_(2027_film)) and Wikidata.

## What is The Take about?

According to a [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/robert-pattinson-upcoming-movies-dune-the-take-batman-bond) roundup of Pattinson's upcoming work, the film follows Noah, a young man just out of the Army. Washington plays a thief who recruits him to rob what the outlet calls the richest bank in Manhattan.

The film adds a personal complication. The teller at the target bank is Noah's girlfriend, played by [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones). That sets up an obvious question about how much Noah will tell her, and how much the older thief will tell him.

Netflix originally announced the project as Here Comes the Flood, Startefacts reports, before it became The Take.

## How Meirelles tells the story

The director has spoken about how he stages the robbery. Startefacts says Meirelles told Netflix that the audience follows Noah through almost the whole film. In his words, "You only know what he knows."

That choice matters in a genre built on surprises. If the recruiter is selling Noah a false story, viewers are sold the same one, and they find out when he does. It is a different approach from heist films that cut away to show the planners at work.

## The cast

Besides the three leads, the cast listed on the film's Wikipedia page includes Danai Gurira, Sean Harris and Moisés Arias. The page lists no budget or box office figures yet, which is normal for a film still weeks from opening, and there are no critic scores to report.

## Where it sits in Pattinson's calendar

The Take is not Pattinson's next release. Startefacts says that is Dune: Part Three, which opens in US cinemas on 18 December. He plays Scytale, a Face Dancer, in [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s adaptation of Dune Messiah. The outlet counts The Take as arriving about four weeks after that.

After that, the outlet lists The Batman Part II, scheduled for 18 February 2028 in the US. Production paused when [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves) stepped away for personal reasons, and [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh) came in to help finish filming, according to the same report.

The outlet also notes that Amazon has named a lead, director and screenwriter, [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), for the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film, but had not given it a release date as of 19 November. Pattinson is reported as that film's lead. For more on the franchise, see our post on [Callum Turner and the Bond rumours](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-james-bond-croasdell/).
