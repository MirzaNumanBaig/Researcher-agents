**Quick summary**

- MobLand, the crime drama starring [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy), is currently the most-watched show on Paramount+ in the US, according to Collider.
- The ranking comes days before the Season 2 finale, the tenth episode, titled "The Queen Is Dead".
- Collider says the series has become one of the most-watched shows ever on the streaming platform.
- Season 2 finds the Harrigan family fractured from within while outside enemies close in.

MobLand has reached the top of the Paramount+ chart in the United States, and it has done so just before its second season ends. [Collider reports](https://collider.com/tom-hardy-mobland-paramount-plus-streaming-success-november-2026/) that the crime thriller is now the platform's most-watched show in the US as an "explosive" finale approaches. IMDb ran a similar report on the same milestone.

## Why MobLand is leading the chart

The series was created by [Ronan Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Bennett) and follows a family running a criminal empire in London. Collider describes it as fast, funny and tense, with the quick-fire dialogue and action associated with director Guy Ritchie's style. The leads are Hardy, [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren), and Collider credits sharp writing and strong performances for its appeal.

The outlet also says MobLand has become one of the most-watched shows Paramount+ has ever carried. Neither of the sources we could read gives viewing figures, so how large the audience is remains unconfirmed.

## Where to watch it

New episodes are streaming on several subscription and pay-per-view services around the world. According to Collider, that includes Paramount+ in the Americas and SkyShowtime in Europe. Earlier episodes can also be found on Amazon, iTunes and Netflix, depending on the country. Collider says the show has been dominating those platforms too. Availability in Australia is not covered in the sources, so check your local streaming services.

## What Season 2 is about

Collider says the second season arrived almost a year after the first ended. It finds the family divided, with several members competing to become the next patriarch or matriarch. The current heads, Conrad and Maeve, are not ready to give up their empire. At the same time, outside forces are trying to bring the Harrigans down, so the family faces enemies both inside and outside its own walls.

The episode 10 recap in the Collider piece lists how many threads are still open. The police finally have something to work with. Frankie, played by [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn), is trying to stay level-headed after his son's murder. Kevin, played by [Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine), wants his parents dead. Mad Dog is out of prison and wants revenge for his father. [Mandeep Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeep_Dhillon) appears as Seraphina.
