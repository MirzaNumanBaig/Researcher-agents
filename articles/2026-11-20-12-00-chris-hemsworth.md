**Quick summary**

- A 16-year-old boy has been arrested and charged over the break-in at [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s home at Broken Head, near Byron Bay, according to 7NEWS.
- He faces seven offences, including aggravated break and enter, and was refused bail.
- Three vehicles were stolen on 11 November. Police say all three have since been recovered and seized for forensic examination.
- Police say officers searched a house in Coraki and seized electronics, clothing and vehicle keys.

Police have charged a teenager over the break-in at Chris Hemsworth's Northern Rivers home, the first arrest in the case. A 16-year-old boy was arrested after a search warrant was executed at a house in Coraki, and he has been refused bail, [7NEWS reports](https://7news.com.au/entertainment/teenage-boy-arrested-and-charged-after-break-in-at-actor-chris-hemsworths-byron-bay-mansion-c-23189165).

## What police say happened

According to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16307237/chris-hemsworth-elsa-pataky.html), police were called to the property at Broken Head, about 9km south of Byron Bay, at 7.30am on Wednesday 11 November. Officers were told four young people had entered the property in the early hours and stolen three vehicles.

7NEWS says the vehicles were a BYD, a Ram and a Raptor ute. The alleged thieves reportedly climbed the front fence and reached a garage where the cars were parked with the keys inside.

Both outlets cite sources close to the actor. They say his wife, [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky), heard the dogs barking at about 2am but did not investigate. The couple checked their CCTV the next morning. The footage allegedly showed four people in balaclavas, two of them reportedly holding knives, and they called police.

The reports say the family was at home asleep during the incident. Neither outlet reports any injury.

## The arrest and the charges

7NEWS quotes a police statement saying that about 10am on Thursday, officers from the Tweed/Byron and Richmond Proactive Crime Teams executed a search warrant at a house in Coraki. Police say they found and seized several items of interest, including electronics, clothing and vehicle keys.

A 16-year-old boy was arrested and taken to Lismore Police Station. He has been charged with seven offences, including aggravated break and enter and being armed with intent to commit an indictable offence, according to 7NEWS. He was refused bail and was due to face a children's court on Friday 20 November.

Because of his age, he has not been named in either report, and he has not been found guilty of anything. The charges are allegations until a court decides.

## Where the cars ended up

The recovery trail covers a lot of ground. 7NEWS says one vehicle was found at Coraki, about an hour's drive from the property, and another at Tabulam, more than two hours away. Police have since recovered the third. All three were seized so they can be examined forensically.

The Daily Mail reported that the arrest came after the break-in had already drawn wide attention, with the couple described as the targets of an armed raid at their secluded rainforest estate. Police have not said whether they are looking for the other alleged intruders. Reports describe four people at the property, and one has been charged so far.

## What happens next

The teenager's case moves through the children's court, where bail and the seven charges will be dealt with. Police have not said whether further arrests are expected.
