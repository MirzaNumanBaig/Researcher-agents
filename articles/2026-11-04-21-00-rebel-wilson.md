**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson), 46, is being prosecuted by the DVLA for keeping an Audi Q5 in London without insurance.
- The agency alleges the car was uninsured on 26 May; Wilson has pleaded not guilty and wrote to the court to apologise.
- The case goes to an open hearing at Highbury Corner Magistrates' Court on 30 November.
- If convicted at court, the maximum penalty for this offence is a £1,000 fine.

Rebel Wilson will answer a criminal charge in London later this month after the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency (DVLA) said her Audi Q5 had no insurance in May. According to [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/home-news/rebel-wilson-car-court-insurance-b3078808.html), the Australian actor and comedian has pleaded not guilty to keeping a motor vehicle that does not meet insurance requirements, and the matter is listed for 30 November.

## What the DVLA alleges

Court papers seen by the Press Association, and reported by The Independent, say the DVLA claims the Audi was uninsured on 26 May. The agency wrote to Wilson at her central London apartment in Whitehall, which the paper puts at £3.5 million, and offered a fixed penalty. The DVLA says she did not respond, so it chose to prosecute.

The case was first handled under the Single Justice Procedure, which deals with minor offences on paper. Last week it was referred to an open court hearing at Highbury Corner Magistrates' Court. The Independent reports that Wilson could be asked to attend. The paper valued the car at £50,000.

## Wilson's explanation

In her letter to the court, Wilson said she and her wife, fashion designer Ramona Agruma, spent about five months in Los Angeles while Agruma was in the late stages of pregnancy and had complications. The couple could not fly home until after the baby arrived. They announced the birth of their second child, a daughter, on 4 May and returned to London with the baby on 28 May.

Wilson wrote that the car sat in a private underground garage the whole time and was never driven or left on the road. She said she noticed the lapse when she opened the post after returning, and bought a policy in her wife's name before anyone used the car. "I was unaware of the requirement to keep a car insured," she wrote, adding that she and Agruma are not UK residents.

## Why a parked car can still lead to prosecution

Trade title [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/uk/news/breaking-news/rebel-wilson-faces-uk-prosecution-over-uninsured-audi--and-her-defence-will-be-familiar-596117.aspx) explains why the garage detail may not settle the matter. Under the Continuous Insurance Enforcement rules, introduced in 2011, the registered keeper of a vehicle must have it insured or declare it off the road with a Statutory Off Road Notification (SORN). The DVLA does not ask whether a car was driven. It asks whether the car was insured or SORNed.

Insurance Business says that point is widely misunderstood, especially by people who live abroad for part of the year. Whether the court accepts Wilson's reasoning is for the magistrates to decide.

## Not the first well-known name caught out

The same outlet lists two recent cases. In August, [Ed Sheeran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran) was convicted and fined £666 at Bradford magistrates' court over a 1966 Aston Martin undergoing long-term restoration. He had assumed the workshop's motor trade policy covered him. In October, Tottenham forward [Omar Marmoush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Marmoush) was fined £408 over a £250,000 McLaren 720S that was neither insured nor SORNed.

Wilson, who is from Sydney, rose to Hollywood prominence in *Bridesmaids* in 2010 and went on to star in the [Pitch Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_Perfect) films and [Bride Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bride_Hard). She and Agruma announced their relationship in June 2022 and married in Italy two years later, according to The Independent.

Wilson's hearing is on 30 November at Highbury Corner. If she is convicted, Insurance Business reports the maximum penalty a court can set for this offence is a £1,000 fine. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has Rebel Wilson been convicted?

No. She has pleaded not guilty, and the case is due before magistrates at Highbury Corner on 30 November.

### Who is prosecuting Rebel Wilson?

The DVLA, the UK agency that licenses drivers and registers vehicles, brought the case after she did not respond to its offer of a fixed penalty.

### Does a car need insurance if it is not driven in the UK?

Under the Continuous Insurance Enforcement rules, a registered keeper must either insure the vehicle or make a SORN, even if it is parked in a private garage, according to Insurance Business.
