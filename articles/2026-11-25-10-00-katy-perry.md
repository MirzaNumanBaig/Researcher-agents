**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) was photographed in Venice with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) on Saturday, 21 November, according to Page Six.
- Two days later, a Vanity Fair piece on Taylor Swift referred to Perry as "California Girl" and called her "an adult pretending to be a child".
- Perry has not publicly reacted, and Swift has not responded directly, Page Six reports.
- A source told Page Six that Swift is "not losing sleep" over the article.

Katy Perry was on a romantic break in Venice with Justin Trudeau when a Vanity Fair profile of Taylor Swift pulled her back into a feud that most fans thought was long settled. The singer, 42, has stayed quiet since the piece went viral on Monday, 23 November, according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/24/entertainment/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-vacation-before-shes-roped-into-vanity-fairs-taylor-swift-drama/).

## What Vanity Fair wrote about Katy Perry

The magazine's article, written by journalist May Jeong, is mainly about Swift. Perry appears in it under a nickname, "California Girl". Jeong described her as "an adult pretending to be a child" and set her against Swift, who, in the writer's words, "might be a child pretending to be an adult".

The reference was brief. As Page Six reports, the piece mentioned the old tension only in passing before moving to Swift's disputes with [Kim Kardashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kardashian), [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo), Charli XCX and others. It did not go into detail about what happened between Swift and Perry.

## The Venice trip

Photographs taken on Saturday show Perry and Trudeau, 54, holding hands while sightseeing, walking arm in arm and taking selfies. They also shared a water taxi ride, according to [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/katy-perry-enjoys-loved-getaway-162912315.html), which republished the Page Six report.

Perry kept a low profile in a black trench coat, loafers, a plaid head scarf and sunglasses. Trudeau wore a baseball cap and sunglasses. [People](https://people.com/star-tracks-tuesday-november-24-2026-new-celebrity-photos-updated-daily-12275778) pictured the couple leaving Venice's Marco Polo Airport on 23 November, the day the Vanity Fair article appeared. Page Six says the two have been together since last summer. For more on their European travels, see our earlier report, [Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau Tour Europe as High Court Ruling Lands](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-europe/).

## A feud with a long history

The two singers fell out more than a decade ago over backup dancers, Page Six recalls. A group of dancers left Swift's "Red" tour to join Perry's "Prismatic" tour. In September 2014, Swift told Rolling Stone that a fellow artist had tried to "sabotage" her arena tour, which she cited as the motivation for her song "Bad Blood".

Perry reportedly tweeted the next day about a "[Regina George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_George) in sheep's clothing", a reference to the villain of [Mean Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_Girls) played by [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams). In 2017, Perry released "Swish Swish", which many listeners took as a response, although Page Six notes she has repeatedly denied it is about any one person.

The pair made peace in 2018, when Perry sent Swift an olive branch. In 2019 Swift baked Perry cookies and Perry appeared in the "You Need to Calm Down" video. Perry later attended an Eras Tour show.

## How Swift's camp has responded

Swift has not replied directly to the article. Page Six, citing a source, says she is not "losing sleep" over what she sees as a "collection of old grievances and unflattering anecdotes". The source added that, to Swift, "the conclusion was already written", and that she is proud of what she has achieved. These are anonymous claims and Swift's team has not put them on the record.

## Another recent red carpet moment

The Vanity Fair mention is not the only attention Perry has had this month. According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/fans-react-katy-perrys-sequin-004533695.html), she and Trudeau attended the Baby2Baby Gala in West Hollywood on 14 November. She wore a silver sequin mini dress over a champagne satin skirt from Antonio Grimaldi's Spring 2025 couture collection, with a matching cape and a new bob haircut. Social media reaction was split between praise and criticism, the outlet says. More style coverage sits in our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

Not publicly. Page Six reported on 24 November that she had yet to react.
