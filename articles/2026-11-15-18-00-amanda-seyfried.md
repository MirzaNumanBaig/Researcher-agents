**Quick summary**

- Amanda Seyfried will host Saturday Night Live for the first time on December 5, with Don Toliver as musical guest.
- NBC announced it during the November 14 episode, hosted by John Mulaney, the last before a Thanksgiving break.
- Her Netflix animated film Steps arrives November 20.
- She has been ruled out of acting nominations at the Golden Globes, BAFTAs and Actor Awards for the documentary You Can See Everything.
- The Academy is expected to decide her Oscar eligibility in early December.

The Amanda Seyfried SNL debut is booked for December 5, when the actress takes the host's spot on NBC's live sketch show with rapper Don Toliver as the musical guest. The news came at the end of the November 14 episode, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/amanda-seyfried-host-saturday-night-live-1237224388/).

[Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) is 40 and has never fronted the show before. That makes the timing interesting: she has two projects in the news at once, and one of them is a long way from sketch comedy.

## Amanda Seyfried SNL host debut: what was announced

[Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) revealed her booking during its November 14 broadcast, which was hosted by John Mulaney with [Phoebe Bridgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Bridgers) as musical guest. Deadline reports that was the final new episode before a Thanksgiving hiatus, so the show returns on December 5 with Seyfried in charge. [Don Toliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Toliver) joins her as the night's musical act.

The show is created and executive produced by [Lorne Michaels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorne_Michaels) and airs live on NBC and Peacock on Saturdays at 11:30 p.m. ET (8:30 p.m. PT). Seyfried confirmed the date in a short social post that read "see you on dec 5th!"

## Why she is in the spotlight now

Deadline notes she has a film to promote: Netflix's animated Steps, due on November 20. But it is her part in the documentary You Can See Everything, from [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and Lance Oppenheim, that Deadline says has people talking. She appears in it as herself and as [Elizabeth Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holmes_(writer)), the Theranos founder she played in Hulu's The Dropout.

That earlier role won her an Emmy for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Limited or Anthology Series or Movie, plus Golden Globe and Critics Choice wins in 2023, Yahoo's account of The Hollywood Reporter's coverage says. She also has an Oscar nomination on her record.

## How the documentary came together

According to the same [Yahoo report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/amanda-seyfried-elizabeth-holmes-role-030057257.html), the film opens with Holmes inviting a crew into her life 34 days before she reports to prison. A24 describes a project built over three years. The finished film runs two hours and 55 minutes and opened in American cinemas on October 16.

Fielder told Rolling Stone he reached out to Seyfried two years after the first filming, getting her number through Emma Stone. A 30-minute Zoom call led to her watching about 90 minutes of footage on camera. Fielder said she received no scripts and had no approval over the footage. Reenactments came later, letting the directors rebuild encounters they hadn't filmed.

Seyfried had spent time with 10 hours of Holmes's deposition tapes for The Dropout. She said the character's mannerisms returned through "muscle memory" when she saw the footage.

## The awards problem

Seyfried has been ruled ineligible for acting nominations at the Golden Globes, BAFTAs and Actor Awards for You Can See Everything. The reasons differ by body:

- The Golden Globes leave documentaries out of their motion-picture categories, and count docudramas with more than 25% documentary footage or audio as documentaries.
- BAFTA tells films entered in its documentary category to stay out of the acting categories.
- The Actor Awards, formerly the SAG Awards, require scripted features and expressly exclude documentaries.

The Critics Choice Awards have approved her for Best Supporting Actress consideration, per The Hollywood Reporter's November 12 report. SAG-AFTRA encouraged A24 to appeal, but sources told the publication the studio will focus on the Oscars instead.

Seyfried said she is ready to campaign now that she is allowed to appear. Her involvement had been kept secret during early publicity, which let her avoid the promotional circuit.

## What happens next

Steps reaches Netflix on November 20, and Seyfried hosts SNL on December 5. The Academy's Actors Branch Executive Committee handles acting eligibility questions, and Yahoo, citing The Hollywood Reporter, says it has given A24 informal assurances there is no apparent reason to exclude her. Formal determinations are expected in early December. Nominations for the 99th Oscars are due January 21, 2027, with the ceremony on March 14.
