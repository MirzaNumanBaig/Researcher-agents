**Quick summary**

- *Ebenezer* opened in first place in North America with about US$19 million, according to studio estimates reported on Sunday.
- Overseas it added US$23 million, for a US$42 million global start against a reported US$38 million budget.
- It is [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s first No. 1 opening since *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018.
- Critics are split at roughly 50 to 54 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes, while audiences gave it an A- CinemaScore.

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) has his first number one film in eight years. Paramount's *Ebenezer* took about US$19 million from 3,535 North American cinemas this weekend, according to [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/johnny-depp-film-ebenezer-first-no-1-opening-8-years-rcna609436), and the Johnny Depp Ebenezer box office total reached US$42 million worldwide once overseas sales were counted. It is a solid start rather than a blockbuster one.

## How the opening compares

The last time Depp topped the chart was with [*Fantastic Beasts*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Beasts) in 2018, which opened to US$62.2 million, according to [Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-weekend-godzilla-minus-zero/). The *Pirates of the Caribbean* films twice opened above US$100 million. Away from franchises, NBC News notes Depp's openings usually landed near US$20 million, as with *Black Mass* and *Public Enemies*.

*Ebenezer* is Depp's first studio film since that 2018 release. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-opening-how-to-rob-bank-1236755417/) says he stepped back from high-profile work after abuse allegations from ex-wife [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard), which he has denied, and the headlines around their 2022 defamation trial.

[IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/box-office/box-office-ebenezer-opens-20-million-johnny-depp-comeback-1235228068/) called the result solid but unremarkable. It said enough viewers can look past the scandals, but the debut also showed the limits of his appeal. The outlet reported that he took a pay cut, reportedly around US$5 million, to play Ebenezer Scrooge.

## The budget is what makes it work

The film cost a reported US$38 million to make, before global marketing, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/box-office-global-ebenezer-ramayana-godzilla-minus-zero-1237224479/). Against that figure, a US$42 million global opening is a respectable first weekend. IndieWire called it poised to be a modest financial success if crowds hold through the holidays and overseas business stays healthy.

Overseas, Deadline reported it was No. 1 in Italy, Mexico, Spain and Poland. It took US$5.4 million in the UK and finished second in Germany behind a local film. In Australia it came third with US$650,000 from 295 cinemas.

## Who turned up

Audience data points to an older crowd. NBC News, citing PostTrak exit polls, said 81 per cent of the audience was over 25 and about 52 per cent was female. About half of ticket buyers named Depp as their reason for going. IndieWire put the share of family groups at only 9 per cent, despite the PG-13 rating.

The film is [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West)'s horror-tinged take on Charles Dickens' *A Christmas Carol*. [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint), [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough), [Sam Claflin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Claflin) and [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) star alongside him, and Ian McKellen also appears, according to The Hollywood Reporter and Gold Derby.

## The competition and the reviews

Critics were cooler than audiences. Reported Rotten Tomatoes scores ranged from 50 to 54 per cent depending on the outlet and the hour, and Gold Derby cites a Metacritic score of 48. The Hollywood Reporter's own critic wrote that the film "fulfills its ostensible goal of restoring the actor to A-list status."

It was a quiet weekend overall. *The Cat in the Hat* was second with US$11.4 million, and *Godzilla Minus Zero* fell about 73 per cent to US$8.3 million in third. Amazon MGM's *How to Rob a Bank*, from [David Leitch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Leitch), and Sony's *Ghost Soldier* with [Jim Caviezel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Caviezel) each took about US$5.2 million.

## What happens next

*Ebenezer* faces a busier chart next weekend, when *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens, according to NBC News. Deadline expects the film to hold up abroad because of the Christmas setting and Depp's following.

Depp's next release is the revenge thriller [*Day Drinker*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_Drinker), set for March from Lionsgate. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Ebenezer make on opening weekend?
About US$19 million in North America and US$23 million overseas, for roughly US$42 million worldwide, according to studio estimates reported by NBC News and Deadline.

### Is Ebenezer Johnny Depp's first number one in eight years?
Yes. Gold Derby says it is his first No. 1 since *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018.

### What did critics and audiences think?
Critics were divided, with Rotten Tomatoes scores around the 50 to 54 per cent mark. Audiences gave it an A- CinemaScore, according to The Hollywood Reporter.

### What did Ebenezer cost to make?
Outlets including NBC News and Deadline report a net production budget of US$38 million, before marketing.
