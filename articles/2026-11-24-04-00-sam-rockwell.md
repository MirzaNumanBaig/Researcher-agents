**Quick summary**

- [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) stars as Eddie in an Off-Broadway revival of Arthur Miller's *A View From the Bridge*, according to Playbill.
- Previews start on 27 November 2026 at La MaMa's Ellen Stewart Theatre, with an official opening on 13 December.
- The run is a 13-week limited engagement directed by Neil Pepe.
- A weekly lottery offers $10 tickets, available only through the TodayTix app.

Sam Rockwell is heading back to the stage, and New York theatregoers can see him for the price of a coffee if they win a lottery. Playbill reports that his Off-Broadway revival of *A View From the Bridge* begins previews on 27 November 2026, with $10 tickets on offer through the TodayTix app.

## How the $10 ticket lottery works

According to [Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/heres-how-to-get-10-tickets-to-see-sam-rockwell-in-a-view-from-the-bridge-off-broadway), the lottery is open now and tickets are $10 each. Winners can buy up to two tickets for the coming week of performances.

Entries close at 12pm New York time every Monday, and winners are drawn at that point for shows from Tuesday through the following Monday. The first draw was set for 23 November, covering performances from 27 to 29 November.

Standard tickets are also sold through the production's website, ViewFromtheBridgePlay.com, and members of La MaMa get special rates. Anyone outside New York will need to watch the time difference, since the entry deadline is tied to Eastern time.

## The cast and creative team

Rockwell plays Eddie, the longshoreman at the centre of Miller's drama. He is joined by Alfred Molina as the lawyer Alfieri and Carla Gugino as Beatrice. Fred Hechinger plays Rodolpho, Tom Pecinka is Marco and Riley Prior is Catherine.

Neil Pepe directs. Scott Pask designs the sets, Sarah Laux the costumes, John Torres the lighting and Palmer Hefferan the sound. Rockwell also produces, alongside Mark Berger and Play Hooky Productions.

## A first for Off-Broadway in decades

Miller's play first reached Broadway in 1955. Playbill notes that this is the first major Off-Broadway staging since a 1965 production starring Robert Duvall and Jon Voight.

Rockwell and Berger have said they hope the production can demonstrate what Playbill describes as a new model of using commercial theatre to support nonprofit venues such as La MaMa. Cheap lottery seats sit comfortably with that aim, since they widen the audience for a limited run.

## What happens next

Previews begin on 27 November, and the official opening night is 13 December 2026. The engagement runs for 13 weeks. The lottery draws every Monday at noon New York time, so entrants have a fresh chance each week for as long as the show plays.
