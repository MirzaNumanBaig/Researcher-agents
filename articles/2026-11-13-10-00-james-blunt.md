**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt)'s eighth studio album, Airplane Mode, led the race for Number 1 on the UK Official Albums Chart in the latest update, according to Official Charts.
- The album had sat at Number 2 behind Royal Blood's Dead Company when Blunt made his pledge.
- He has promised to legally change his name to Ryan Air if the album reaches the top.
- The final chart is due on Friday 13 November 2026.

## Airplane Mode moves into the lead

Airplane Mode has overtaken Royal Blood's Dead Company in the Number 1 race on the UK albums chart, with less than 12 hours of counting left on Thursday 12 November, [Official Charts reported](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/james-blunt-airplane-mode-number-1-album-update/). If the position holds, it would be Blunt's third Number 1 album.

The jump came after Blunt announced on his social media accounts that he would change his name to Ryan Air if the record topped the chart. The name is a nod to the Irish airline. When he posted, the album was at Number 2.

## What the pledge involves

Official Charts says Blunt would make the change legally, and its report jokes that he may need to deal with the deed poll sooner than he expected. Neither the chart body nor the singer has said when any paperwork would be filed. Treat the name change as a stated promise, not a confirmed plan, until the result is in.

This is not his first chart pledge. Back in 2024 he said he would change his name if a reissue of his debut album, Back to Bedlam, reached Number 1. That reissue stopped at Number 7, according to Official Charts, so the promise never came due.

## The competition is still close

Royal Blood remain just behind at Number 2. If the Brighton duo overtake Blunt, Dead Company would give them a fifth Number 1 album in a row, per Official Charts.

Elsewhere in the update, Skepta's The Fork and Knife, his first project in seven years, rose three places from Monday's update to Number 3. Official Charts also listed possible new entries for Steven Wilson's Requiem For A Village at Number 4, Kings of Leon's O My Beloved at Number 5 and George Michael's The Faith Tour at Number 7.

Blunt, who is also appearing on Celebrity Traitors, has been busy promoting the record. ## What happens next

Sales, downloads and streams counted until 11.59pm on Thursday 12 November. The full Official Albums Chart is published on Friday 13 November, when it will be clear whether Blunt or Royal Blood takes the top spot. Our [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) section will carry the result.
