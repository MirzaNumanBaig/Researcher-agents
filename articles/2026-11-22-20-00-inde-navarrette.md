**Quick summary**

- Variety's Awards Circuit predictions, published on 21 November 2026, tip [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) for a lead actress nomination at the 2027 Actor Awards for *Obsession*.
- Nominations will be announced on 13 January 2027, and the Academy Awards ceremony follows on 14 March.
- *Obsession* reportedly took US$524 million worldwide on a budget of about US$1 million, according to Screen Rant.
- Navarrette is also preparing to play Rogue in Marvel Studios' X-Men reboot, due in cinemas on 5 May 2028.

Inde Navarrette has landed on another awards shortlist, at least on paper. Variety's latest predictions, published on 21 November, name the 25-year-old as a possible nominee in the female actor in a leading role category at the 2027 Actor Awards for her work in the horror hit *Obsession*, according to a report by [Bsinews](https://bsinews.id/en/actor-awards-inde-navarrette-predictions).

It is a forecast, not a nomination, and the field is crowded. But for a performer whose breakout came in a micro-budget horror film, the placement says a lot about how the season is shaping up.

## What Variety's Actor Awards prediction says

Bsinews reports that Variety describes Navarrette as an unexpected but deserving contender in the lead actress category. She sits alongside [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore), whose performance in *The Debut* is also drawing attention in the same predictions.

The wider forecast is dominated by other titles. Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* is projected to lead the film nominations with seven, which would match the record set by *One Battle After Another* in the previous season. The cast listed there includes [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon), [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway) and [Samantha Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Morton). In the lead actor race, [Andrew Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Garfield) is seen as an early favourite for playing [Sam Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman) in *Artificial*.

The Actor Awards will announce nominations on 13 January 2027.

## Why Obsession is the reason

*Obsession*, written and directed by Curry Barker, has become one of the year's biggest surprises. According to [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/inde-navarrette-obsession-oscar-momentum), citing Screen Rant, it earned US$524 million at the global box office from a production budget of roughly US$1 million, with a 93% critics' score and a 94% audience score. That is more than 500 times its cost.

Navarrette plays Nikki, a young woman whose relationship with Bear (Michael Johnston) turns disturbing after his wish for her unconditional affection comes true. [ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/movies/feature/an-obsession-sequel-could-go-anywhere-heres-what-curry-barker-inde-navarrette-have-said/) notes that a lead actress nomination for a horror performance is rare, which is part of why the talk has travelled so far.

She has said she is trying not to chase it. Speaking to The Playlist, as quoted by Demokratis, she said she does not want to set her expectations too high and that "if it doesn't happen, it doesn't happen". Navarrette is also expected to receive a Spotlight Tribute at the Gotham Film Awards, according to [Bsinews](https://bsinews.id/en/inde-navarrette-gotham-oscar-recognition), which cites The Hollywood Reporter. For more on her reaction to the buzz, see our earlier piece, [Inde Navarrette Responds to Oscar Buzz for Obsession](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-oscar-buzz/).

## Rogue and the Mississippi accent

Her next big job is already locked in. Marvel Studios announced Navarrette as the new Rogue on 14 August at Disney's D23 event, and the untitled film, directed by Jake Schreier, is set for 5 May 2028, according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/articles/inde-navarrette-plans-mississippi-trip-050916668.html). Anna Paquin played the character in Fox's earlier films.

Navarrette told E! News she is still working out the Southern accent and wants to visit Mississippi, where Rogue is from in the comics, to get it right. "Rogue means a lot to me," she said. The cast reported so far includes Sadie Sink, Kit Connor, Maya Boyd, Samara Weaving, Christopher Abbott and Adam Driver.

## A possible sequel, and a GTA joke

Navarrette has also said she would be open to an *Obsession* sequel, telling ComicBook.com's source interview she would like to explore Nikki's grief after the film's ending. Barker has floated a different route, an anthology series in which each episode follows a different wish, as he told Deadline. Neither idea is a confirmed project.

On the lighter side, Bsinews reports that she told BBC Radio 1 she plans to play *Grand Theft Auto VI* with her brother and joked that she would obey the red lights. She also streamed on Twitch during the COVID-19 lockdown, and says it helped cover her rent for more than a year.
