**Quick summary**

- France's film academy announced on 12 November that [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) will receive an honorary César for her life's work.
- The award will be presented at the 52nd César Awards at L'Olympia in Paris on 25 February 2027.
- The academy called her "a Hollywood star and a major figure in auteur cinema", according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Kidman won the best actress Oscar for The Hours and the best actress Volpi Cup at Venice in 2024 for Babygirl.

The Australian actress and producer will collect an honorary César, France's top film honour for a career achievement, at the 52nd César Awards in Paris on 25 February 2027. The Académie des Arts et Techniques du Cinéma announced the tribute on 12 November, according to [Digg's summary](https://digg.com/entertainment/fmmclz6x) of reports by The Hollywood Reporter and Deadline.

## What the academy said about Nicole Kidman

In its announcement, quoted by The Hollywood Reporter, the academy described Kidman as "a Hollywood star and a major figure in auteur cinema". It said she "has built a career that defies categorization".

The academy also said it wanted to pay tribute to an actress and producer who has never let her status as a star dictate her artistic choices. That is a pointed line for a performer whose filmography moves between the biggest studio productions and some of the most demanding directors working.

## A career that crosses genres

The range is the point of the award. Digg's report lists Aquaman at one end, and at the other director-driven films such as Stanley Kubrick's Eyes Wide Shut, Jane Campion's The Portrait of a Lady, [Lars von Trier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_von_Trier)'s Dogville and Baz Luhrmann's Moulin Rouge!.

Her best-known honour is the best actress Oscar in 2003 for playing [Virginia Woolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Woolf) in The Hours. More recently she won the best actress Volpi Cup at the 2024 [Venice Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice_Film_Festival) for Babygirl.

## Television and producing

The César recognises Kidman as a producer as well as an actress. Through her company Blossom Films, she has produced and starred in Big Little Lies, The Undoing and [Nine Perfect Strangers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Perfect_Strangers), according to the Digg report. That mix of screen work and production credits fits the academy's description of an artist who picks projects on her own terms.

Her latest big-screen release is also on the record. Practical Magic 2, with Sandra Bullock, opened on 26 August 2026. Wikipedia lists a US$75 million budget and about US$106 million at the box office, with a 35% score on Rotten Tomatoes. The critics' summary there credits the sisterly rapport between Bullock and Kidman while faulting the plotting.
