**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) told Vogue's podcast The Run-Through that fan screams outside a Saint Laurent show in Paris put him in "fight or flight mode".
- The noise was meant for K-pop group CORTIS, whose members arrived right after him in June, according to Complex.
- He put the crowd at "probably 500 people".
- In the same conversation he confirmed he is living with [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) while the two film season two of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).

Connor Storrie says the loudest moment of his Paris Fashion Week trip had nothing to do with him. The Heated Rivalry actor told Vogue's The Run-Through that screams from CORTIS fans outside Saint Laurent's June show left his body "on fire", according to a [Complex report](https://www.complex.com/music/a/jaelaniturnerwilliams/connor-storrie-cortis-saint-laurent-screams) on the episode, which aired on Monday 23 November.

## What Storrie said happened in Paris

Storrie was speaking to Vogue's editor [Chloe Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloe_Malle). He recalled arriving at the Bourse de Commerce for the Saint Laurent show, with "two K-pop boys" stepping out of a car straight after his. Complex identifies them as CORTIS members Martin Edwards and James Zhao.

The reaction from the crowd, he said, was unlike anything he had heard. "It literally put my body into this like fight or flight mode," Storrie said, as quoted by Complex. He guessed the crowd at roughly 500 people and described the sound as "blood-curdling screams".

The physical effect lingered. Storrie said he was standing beside a member of Saint Laurent's PR team and could not hear them, because his body "was like on fire". For the rest of that time, he added, he felt as if he had to "go and hunt something", and he said the noise sounded like "a cry for help".

The detail that makes the story land is the contrast. Storrie has become one of the most recognisable faces in television since Heated Rivalry, yet the fan frenzy that stopped him in his tracks was aimed at someone else.

## Fashion week from the inside

Storrie is no stranger to the front row. According to [AOL's summary of the Vogue interview](https://www.aol.com/articles/shirtless-unbothered-vogue-best-dressed-120000000.html), Saint Laurent has made him an ambassador, and his 2026 appearances have included the Met Gala in a polka-dot Saint Laurent halter top. The same piece says he attended the Vanity Fair Oscar Party in a sheer turtleneck and Tiffany & Co. diamonds.

The conversation came as Vogue named him to its Best Dressed 2026 list, with a digital cover shot by Collier Schorr. We covered that in [Connor Storrie Named Vogue's Best Dressed of 2026](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-vogue-best-dressed-2026/). On style, AOL quotes him as saying: "I love it. It's just self-expression."

## Living with Hudson Williams

The same episode returned to a topic fans have followed closely. Storrie confirmed that he and Williams are filming the second season of Heated Rivalry and are currently roommates, according to both Complex and AOL.

Quoted by AOL, he said: "Right now we're filming, so I literally live with him, which is amazing." He gave a practical reason for the move. The pair had been staying in a hotel, but he needed a washing machine and a kitchen. Our earlier report, [Connor Storrie Is Living With Hudson Williams During Filming](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-hudson-williams/), has more on that part of the interview.

Season two of Heated Rivalry is still filming, and no release date appears in the sources we read. AOL also reports that Storrie has already wrapped Halina Reijn's film Please, which co-stars Gracie Abrams, though no premiere date was given.
