**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) is developing a new Montecristo cigar that is due to debut in 2027, according to Cigar Aficionado.
- The blend is based on themes from his memoir, The Steps, and arrives in the 50th anniversary year of Rocky.
- The name, sizes, shapes and prices are still being finalised.
- Stallone is on the cover of the magazine's November/December issue and says he has smoked cigars since 1977.

Sylvester Stallone is moving into the cigar business. Cigar Aficionado reported on 17 November that the actor has teamed up with Tabacalera USA on a new Montecristo cigar, which is planned to go on sale in 2027. It is a fitting partner for a man whose career story has been told and retold this year.

## Sylvester Stallone Montecristo cigar: what we know

According to a release from Tabacalera USA, shared with Cigar Aficionado, the collaboration draws on the themes of Stallone's memoir, The Steps, which covers his hard road to success. The book lands as Rocky turns 50. Stallone wrote and starred in that film, which won three Academy Awards, including Best Picture.

Details are thin. The magazine says the full name of the cigar, its sizes, shapes and prices are all still being decided. Stallone was smoking a prototype for the cover shoot, so a version of the blend does exist. Rafael Nodal, vice president of product capability at Tabacalera USA, said the company has now reached the stage of finalising the blend, and that the aim is a cigar worthy of both Stallone and the Montecristo name.

Trade site halfwheel also covered the announcement, though it ran the story under the Altadis U.S.A. name and noted that a company representative had not yet confirmed the Cigar Aficionado account when the piece first appeared. A formal press release was promised. For now, the Cigar Aficionado report, which quotes the company's own statement, is the fullest account.

## What Stallone and the company said

Stallone told Cigar Aficionado he has been a cigar smoker since 1977. He said Montecristo's worldwide reputation was the reason he wanted to be associated with it. In the company statement he tied the project to the theme of his life: "Every step I've taken comes from refusing to quit," he said, describing the blend as the cigar he has always wanted.

Charles Gibb, chief executive of Tabacalera USA, drew a parallel with the brand's namesake, The Count of Monte Cristo, the story of a man who refused to be broken. He said few people have lived that story as Stallone has. Nodal described Montecristo as a name with more than 90 years of heritage.

## The Rocky story behind the theme

The "refusing to quit" line has real history behind it. The Spectator notes that in 1975 Stallone, then broke and sharing a one-room studio with his pregnant wife, wrote the Rocky script in three days. United Artists offered him US$300,000 for it, with a bigger name in mind for the lead. According to the Spectator, he had about US$80 to his name and still insisted that he play Rocky or the studio could not have the script. They gave in.

The film was shot for US$900,000 and released in November 1976. The Spectator argues it landed well with an America still shaken by Watergate, though that is the writer's reading rather than a measured fact.

The anniversary is also producing a screen version of the legend. Peter Farrelly's I Play Rocky dramatises how the film came to be made, and the CBC reports that Anthony Ippolito, who plays the young Stallone, filmed his own unsolicited audition tape to win the part. The Spectator reports that Stallone has distanced himself from that film.

## What happens next

The cigar is expected in 2027, but no release date has been given. Cigar Aficionado says the full interview with Stallone is in its November/December issue, which was on its way to subscribers when the story ran. A formal press release from the company was still to come at the time halfwheel published.

## Frequently asked questions

### When will the Sylvester Stallone Montecristo cigar be released?

Cigar Aficionado says it will debut in 2027. No exact date has been announced.

### Who is making the cigar with Stallone?

The cigar is a collaboration between Stallone and Tabacalera USA, the company behind the Montecristo brand in the United States, according to Cigar Aficionado.

### What is the cigar based on?

It is inspired by themes from Stallone's memoir, The Steps, and by his perseverance story, which began with writing Rocky 50 years ago.

### How much will it cost?

That is not known yet. Prices, sizes and the cigar's full name are still being finalised.
