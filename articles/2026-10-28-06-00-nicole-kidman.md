**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), 59, says she never keeps her phone in the bedroom at night and credits that habit with "pretty stable mental health".
- She made the comments in a Vogue Australia interview, as reported by Yahoo Style Canada on 27 October 2026.
- Harper's Bazaar UK says her Khaite "A-line" jeans, seen in a new Instagram post, are a smart pick for tall women.
- Grazia says her fringe at the 18 October Victoria's Secret Fashion Show is a prime example of "Gucci bangs".

Nicole Kidman has a simple answer for staying grounded: put the phone down. In a Vogue Australia interview reported by [Yahoo Style Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/nicole-kidman-shares-rule-she-180000378.html), the 59-year-old said she would rather meditate than doomscroll, and that she keeps her device out of the bedroom every night.

## The phone rule

The question came from [Shailene Woodley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shailene_Woodley), her Big Little Lies co-star, who teased that Kidman is "terrible" on her phone and asked what she gets in return. Kidman said she can be slow to answer texts and does not check email constantly. That, she said, lets her stay emotionally present.

She also said she knows when to step away. According to Yahoo, she described going into nature to realign when life gets overwhelming, and admitted she has doomscrolled before. Her view is that it takes discipline to tell yourself not to.

The cost, she conceded, is that she is rarely the first to answer a group chat, and friends can be miffed. Her workaround is to swipe a message back to unread once she has read it, so she remembers to reply.

## Rest, and a washing machine

The same interview drew questions from other friends and colleagues. [Alexander Skarsgard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd), another Big Little Lies castmate, asked whether to buy a combined washer-dryer or two machines. Kidman's reply was practical: it depends on space, and unless the apartment is small, buy separate ones.

[Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan), the Australian singer, asked how she rests when she works so much. Kidman said years on film sets taught her to wait, sometimes four or five hours at a stretch, and that she can lie down and zone out with minimal thoughts. She called that a form of rest in itself.

Sleep is harder, she said, because she travels a lot. Even so, she can sleep until five minutes before she has to leave, and says she has never pressed a snooze button.

This is not the first time Vogue Australia has drawn candid answers from her. ## The jeans tall women are copying

Fashion outlets also spent the day on what Kidman wore. [Harper's Bazaar UK](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/shopping/a74259047/nicole-kidman-tall-jeans/) says she wore an "A-line" jean from Khaite in a new Instagram post, paired with pointed pumps. The cut flares out from the hip to nip in the waist.

Bazaar notes that Kidman stands 5ft 11in, and recalls her telling the magazine she once grappled with her height but now stands tall and owns it. The piece is a shopping guide, and the publisher says it earns commission on some links, so treat the recommendations accordingly.

## Why her fringe is trending

Grazia reports that Kidman sat in the front row of the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show at Sony Pictures Studios in Los Angeles on 18 October. She wore a Jean Paul Gaultier look with a white shirt and striped tie, and laughed into her hands as models walked to Adéla's viral song "Nicole Kidman". We covered [Adéla's Fallon performance of that song](https://moduscarry.com/adela-performs-nicole-kidman-song-on-fallon-and-the-star-laughs/) earlier.

What Grazia noticed was her wispy, cheekbone-grazing fringe, which it calls "Gucci bangs". The magazine says the style began on Gucci's 2025 short film The Tiger, directed by [Spike Jonze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Jonze) and [Halina Reijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halina_Reijn), where hairstylist Dimitris Giannetos gave Demi Moore a full fringe. Kidman first showed her new bangs in April at the New York premiere of Margo's Got Money Troubles, per Grazia.
