**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) told Digital Spy she would happily play a Bond villain opposite [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson).
- She spoke at the UK premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth this week.
- Digital Spy reports the film holds an 82% Certified Fresh score from 117 reviews.
- WWD reports a limited cinema release from 23 November, with Netflix streaming from 23 December.

Elizabeth Debicki has said she would jump at the chance to play a villain in the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film, and to do it opposite her former co-star Robert Pattinson. The Australian actress made the comments to [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) at the London premiere of her new film this week.

## What Debicki said about Bond

Asked whether she would ever play a Bond villain with Pattinson on the other side, Debicki answered "Sure. Of course," according to [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/movies/a74532806/tenet-elizabeth-debicki-bond-role-robert-pattinson/). She added that she would love to work with him again and would be very happy if it happened in those films.

The two shared the screen in Tenet, Christopher Nolan's time-bending thriller. Digital Spy notes that Debicki had earlier told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) she texted Pattinson straight away when his casting was announced. The new Bond film is being directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), the outlet adds.

It is worth keeping this in proportion. Debicki was answering a hypothetical, and nothing in the reports suggests she has been cast or approached. This is an actor saying she would be keen, not a casting announcement.

## The film she was promoting

The premiere was for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, directed by [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher) and written by Quentin Tarantino. It follows his 2019 film [Once Upon a Time... in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood) and is set in 1977 Los Angeles.

[Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) returns as the stuntman of the title. Debicki plays talent agent Karen Crosby, whom WWD describes as the film's main love interest. [Carla Gugino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Gugino), [Scott Caan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Caan) and [Yahya Abdul-Mateen II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Abdul-Mateen_II) join the cast in new roles.

According to Digital Spy, the film is [Certified Fresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) on Rotten Tomatoes with 82% from 117 reviews. It quotes Little White Lies praising Fincher's "silky" direction, while TIME called it "more of a vibe" than a film, though it argued that a vibe can be just the ticket.

## Premiere night in London

The London Evening Standard's Londoner's Diary places the premiere at the BFI Imax on Monday night. It reports that Debicki and Caan attended, while Pitt missed the event owing to illness.

Our earlier coverage looked at [her Valentino couture look at the premiere](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-valentino-cliff-booth-premiere/) and [how the night went without Pitt](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-cliff-booth-premiere/). WWD describes a robin's egg blue sleeveless gown with a plunging neckline and criss-cross cutouts at the back.

## Fashion on the press trail

The promotion has also been a showcase for Debicki's wardrobe. After a Q&A on Tuesday, WWD says she was photographed leaving Claridge's in a tiger print cardigan and pencil skirt from Altuzarra's spring 2027 collection, finished with black pumps and a teardrop diamond necklace. The outlet notes she wore a leopard print combination in Midtown Manhattan earlier this month.

Style site Go Fug Yourself had a more mixed view, joking about the look and suggesting a black blouse would have let the skirt stand out. For more on this side of the story, see our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

## Industry comments

Debicki also spoke about the industry since the MeToo movement went viral in 2017. Digital Spy quotes her saying there have been many changes, but "we still have a long way to go", and that there is still a lot to fight for in terms of parity for women.

## What happens next

WWD reports that the film opened in select cinemas on 23 November and arrives on Netflix on 23 December.
