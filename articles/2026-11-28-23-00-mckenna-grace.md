**Quick summary**

- Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) says the ratings board made him tone down the death of Maysilee Donner, played by [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- He described a more graphic neck wound that was cut to secure the PG-13 rating, according to ScreenRant's report of his podcast interview.
- A fan petition for a four-hour cut had passed 266,000 signatures within five days, ScreenRant reports.
- The film opened on 28 October 2026 and has grossed about US$237.2 million against a US$150 million budget, according to Wikipedia.

The Mckenna Grace Maysilee death scene in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* was meant to be far more brutal than audiences saw, according to the film's director. Francis Lawrence told a podcast that the ratings board forced him to remove details of the injury that kills her character, and he called it the cut that "killed" him.

## What Lawrence said about the cut

Speaking on the *On Film With Kevin McCarthy* podcast, as reported by [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-maysilee-donner-death-truth-francis-lawrence/), Lawrence said the people who sit on the ratings board change over time, and so does their tolerance for mature content. On this film, he found the board's tastes "a little more conservative" than when he made *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes* three years earlier.

Maysilee dies after an attack by pink flamingo mutts in the arena. Lawrence said the original version showed a very specific neck wound. In that cut, Haymitch covers the wound with his hand, and when he lifts it, blood begins to drip and the hole is visible. That detail explained why she could not speak. The board asked him to remove it, and he said it was the one he most wanted to keep.

The result, ScreenRant notes, is a death that some viewers found hard to read, because it is unclear which injury is the fatal one.

## A second death was trimmed too

Maysilee was not the only casualty of the edit. Lawrence gave a second example: the death of Silka, the Career tribute who finishes runner-up in the 50th Games when her own axe rebounds off the arena's force field. That scene is in the final film, but he said it originally ran longer, with more shots of her gasping with the axe in her chest.

ScreenRant reports that other changes from [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)'s novel, including a porcupine scene and a gamesmaker scene, were filmed but dropped for creative reasons rather than ratings. It adds that most of the departures from the book involve Maysilee, which has frustrated some readers.

## The fan reaction

That frustration has turned into organised action. ScreenRant says a petition asking Lionsgate to release a four-hour cut had gathered more than 266,000 signatures in five days at the time of writing. It also reports that Grace has shared behind-the-scenes images on social media since the release, including cut Maysilee scenes and a bloodier makeup test of the death.

We have covered Grace's own take on the cut before, including [her comments on the deleted scene](https://moduscarry.com/sunrise-on-the-reaping-deleted-scene/) and [her push for Hunger Games details](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-easter-eggs/).

## How the film is performing

According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), the film opened on 28 October 2026 with [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) as Haymitch Abernathy, alongside Grace and [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang). It has taken about US$237.2 million worldwide on a US$150 million budget, roughly 1.6 times its cost. Rotten Tomatoes lists it at 68%, and Metacritic gives it 55 out of 100 from 43 critics. Our earlier report on the [opening-week box office](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office/) has the first-week numbers.

## What happens next

Nothing has been announced about a longer cut. ScreenRant's report does not say whether Lionsgate has responded to the petition, and Lawrence's comments describe what was removed rather than what might return. Grace's other recent film, *The Last Kiss*, opened in September, with no box office figures on record yet. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Mckenna Grace play in Sunrise on the Reaping?
She plays Maysilee Donner, a tribute in the 50th Hunger Games who forms an alliance with Haymitch Abernathy in the arena.

### Why was Maysilee's death toned down?
Francis Lawrence says the ratings board required him to remove a graphic neck wound detail so the film could keep its PG-13 rating.

### Is there a longer cut of the film?
Not that has been announced. A fan petition asks Lionsgate for a four-hour version, and it had passed 266,000 signatures within five days, according to ScreenRant.
