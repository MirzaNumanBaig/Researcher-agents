**Quick summary**

- Mckenna Grace wore a blue Mockingjay-style gown by designer Pamella Roland to the New York premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping on 16 November.
- Nine.com.au says the dress, with feathered wings that fan out when she raises her arms, is the latest in a run of looks inspired by Katniss Everdeen's costumes.
- On SiriusXM, Grace said playing Britney Spears in a biopic would be a dream, but only with the singer's approval.
- Grace and co-star Joseph Zada also said they got matching ladybug tattoos.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) arrived at the New York premiere of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* on 16 November in a blue organza gown that echoed one of the most recognisable costumes in the franchise. According to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/lifestyle/fashion-beauty/mckenna-grace-style-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-red-carpet-looks-20261117-p61eso.html), the dress is an homage to the Mockingjay gown worn by [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) in *Catching Fire*, and it comes with a surprise at the back.

It was one of several standout moments for the cast this week. Here is what happened on the carpet and in the radio studio.

## The Mockingjay gown, piece by piece

Nine.com.au reports that the gown is by designer Pamella Roland. It is an organza column dress with an embellished bodice and a dramatic strapless neckline. When Grace extends her arms, a pair of feathered wings opens out behind her, as they do in the film version of the dress.

The look was put together with stylist Molly Dickson, who Nine says has curated the whole press tour. Grace plays Maysilee Donner, described by the outlet as a fan-favourite tribute in the story. The New York event also drew Elle Fanning, Maya Hawke and Rachel Zegler, according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/16/celebrity-news/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-new-york-premiere-red-carpet-mckenna-grace-elle-fanning-maya-hawke-and-more/).

But the Mockingjay was not the first costume nod of the tour.

## A press tour built on Katniss references

Nine.com.au says Grace has already recreated Katniss's wedding dress at an event in Paris, with Australian co-star [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) beside her. She has also worn a version of the fiery gown that earned the character her "girl on fire" nickname.

Another look pointed to the franchise's lead actress instead. At the Academy Museum Gala, Grace wore a plunging red gown that Nine compares to the Calvin Klein dress [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) wore to the 2011 Oscars.

The other outfits were more original. Nine lists a hand-beaded gown with a nod to Van Gogh's *Starry Night* and the film's arena, a Barcelona look covered in floral and bird motifs, and a colourful London photocall outfit. For the Australian leg, she chose a pale pink gown with floral details and bows in her hair. That is a long way from the child roles that first made her name.

## From child star to leading lady

Nine.com.au notes that many film fans still remember Grace as the child actor who played younger versions of big names, including [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Brie Larson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brie_Larson). She is now 20, and the outlet calls her a style star to watch. It also credits her recent role in [Regretting You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regretting_You). Our earlier coverage looked at [her Katniss-inspired pop-up look](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-katniss-look/) and [what she said about femininity in the film](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-femininity/).

## Britney Spears, tattoos and a Woody Harrelson invitation

Away from the carpet, Grace joined [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes), Zada and [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) for a live SiriusXM Front Row session with host [Andy Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Cohen), as [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Mckenna-Grace-Says-Playing-Britney-Spears-in-Biopic-Would-Be-a-Dream-Cast-of-THE-HUNGER-GAMES-SUNRISE-ON-THE-REAPING-Talk-SiriusXM-20261116) reported.

Cohen raised a fan campaign for Grace to play Britney Spears in a biopic. Zada said he has been campaigning for it himself. Grace said she has been a fan for years: she began acting at five, lived about two hours outside Los Angeles, and played Spears's greatest-hits CD and music videos on the long drives to auditions. Playing the singer "would be a dream", she said, "but only if she was okay with it".

The pair also told Cohen they had matching ladybug tattoos done the day before, inspired by a pivotal scene in the film and meant to reflect the bond between their characters. Zada added that Woody Harrelson had asked him the previous night whether he smoked weed. He said he turned it down because it makes him paranoid.

## What happens next

Nine.com.au says the film reaches cinemas this week, so the press tour is nearly over. Grace's next public appearances are likely to be the cinema release itself. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who designed Mckenna Grace's Mockingjay gown?

According to Nine.com.au, the blue organza gown is by designer Pamella Roland, styled by Molly Dickson. It has an embellished bodice, a strapless neckline and feathered wings.

### Who does Mckenna Grace play in Sunrise on the Reaping?

She plays Maysilee Donner, a tribute in the story, according to Nine.com.au. The film is the latest adaptation of [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)' [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series.

### Will Mckenna Grace play Britney Spears?

No casting has been announced in the sources. Grace told SiriusXM it would be a dream role, but only if Spears was comfortable with it.
