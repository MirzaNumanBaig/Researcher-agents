**Quick summary**

- The writers of Beetlejuice Beetlejuice say a third film is "harder" after the death of star Catherine O'Hara earlier this year.
- Alfred Gough said the decision is "really up to Tim", meaning director Tim Burton.
- Beetlejuice Beetlejuice (2024) took more than $450 million worldwide, according to Bloody Disgusting.
- Warner Bros. teased talk of a third film in 2025, but the writers' remarks suggest Burton and the cast are not part of any plan.

The chances of a Beetlejuice 3 have dimmed, according to the people who wrote the last film. Alfred Gough and Miles Millar, who co-wrote 2024's Beetlejuice Beetlejuice, told ScreenRant that continuing the story is harder now that [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara) has died, as reported by [Bloody Disgusting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3974906/beetlejuice-3-plans-chances-catherine-o-hara-writers/) on 5 November.

## What the writers said about Beetlejuice 3

Asked about completing a trilogy, Gough kept the door ajar but only just. "You never say never," he said, adding that the call belongs to [Tim Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Burton) and that O'Hara's death makes it "certainly harder". [Miles Millar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Millar) agreed, saying the answer was "No" and that it is simply "harder".

Bloody Disgusting reports that there were no significant talks about another entry even before O'Hara died. It adds that Warner Bros. hinted in 2025 that discussions about a third film were under way, but the writers' comments imply neither Burton nor other cast members are involved.

## How the last film performed

[Beetlejuice Beetlejuice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beetlejuice_Beetlejuice) was not a flop, which is why the question keeps coming up. Bloody Disgusting says it took in more than $450 million worldwide and holds 75 per cent positive reviews on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). A sequel that clears that bar usually invites a follow-up, which makes the writers' hesitation notable.

O'Hara returned as Delia, alongside [Winona Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winona_Ryder) and [Michael Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Keaton), who also reprised their original roles. For readers who want a fuller look back, our own coverage includes [Catherine O'Hara Dies at 71: Her Career in Review](https://moduscarry.com/catherine-ohara-dies-at-71/), and everything we have published sits on the [Catherine O'Hara tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/catherine-ohara/).

## Who did not return in 2024

Not every piece of the original line-up made it back. [Glenn Shadix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Shadix), who played Otho, Delia's confidant, died in 2010 and the character was not recast. The story also moved on from the Maitlands, played in the first film by [Alec Baldwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Baldwin) and [Geena Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geena_Davis), who did not appear in the sequel.

Nothing has been announced. For now the franchise rests with Burton, and the writers have not suggested he has a plan. We will update this story if Warner Bros. or Burton comments.
