**Quick summary**

- [Julia Stiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Stiles) will dedicate her Dancing with the Stars routine to her late co-star [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger), according to ABC News.
- The contemporary dance is set to "Can't Take My Eyes Off You" by [Frankie Valli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankie_Valli), the song Ledger sang in the 1999 film *10 Things I Hate About You*.
- It will air on "Dedication Night", when the 10 remaining couples honour people and places that shaped their lives.
- Stiles dances with professional partner [Ezra Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Sosa).

Julia Stiles will pay tribute to Heath Ledger on the next "Dedication Night" episode of Dancing with the Stars, a press release from the show confirmed, as reported by [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/julia-stiles-pay-tribute-heath-ledger-dedication-night/story?id=137296564). The contemporary routine will use the Frankie Valli song that Ledger famously performed on screen more than 25 years ago.

## Why this song matters

The choice is a deliberate callback. In the 1999 teen romantic comedy [10 Things I Hate About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You), Ledger played Patrick Verona, who tries to win over Stiles' character, Kat Stratford, by serenading her with "Can't Take My Eyes Off You".

ABC News describes the scene as one of the film's most memorable. Ledger sings from the bleachers of the school stadium, backed by the marching band, before school security gives chase. For many fans, it remains the defining moment of a film that helped launch both actors.

## What Dedication Night involves

Dedication Night asks each couple to perform a dance in honour of a person or place important to them. According to ABC News, all 10 remaining couples will take part, and the dedications range from parents and spouses to a country and a late colleague.

Stiles is not the only contestant remembering someone who has died. Tatyana Ali will dedicate a Viennese Waltz to her "Fresh Prince of Bel-Air" co-star James Avery. Among the others, [Jackson Olson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Olson) will quickstep for his mother, [Harry Shum Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Shum_Jr.) will foxtrot for his wife, and [Maura Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maura_Higgins) will dance a paso doble dedicated to Ireland.

## A second tribute in two weeks

The Ledger routine follows another nod to Stiles' screen history. This week she performed a dance inspired by *Save the Last Dance*, which included a special appearance from her former co-star [Sean Patrick Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Patrick_Thomas), per ABC News.

The episode will also feature a guest judge, Australian dancer [Kym Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kym_Herjavec). Fans of the show can find more of our coverage in [TV & Dramas](https://moduscarry.com/category/tv-dramas/) and [Celebrities](https://moduscarry.com/category/celebrities/).

## What happens next

The performance is scheduled for the show's next Tuesday episode. ABC says Dancing with the Stars airs Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and streams on Disney+, with episodes available on Hulu the following day. The source does not say when or where the episode will be available in Australia, so local viewers should check their streaming services.
