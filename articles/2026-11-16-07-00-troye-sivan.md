**Quick summary**

- Australian singer [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) has designed Penfolds' Holiday gift collection, according to Broadsheet.
- It is the second project between Sivan and the winemaker, after Bin 389 Designed by Troye Sivan earlier in 2026.
- The wines come in limited-edition gift boxes he conceptualised, each with a handwritten message he curated.
- A Magill-inspired candle from his brand Tsu Lange Yor comes free with larger purchases.
- The collection is listed from 1 October to 31 December 2026, while stocks last.

Troye Sivan has put his name to this year's Penfolds Holiday collection, a limited run of gift-boxed wines with a candle from his own lifestyle brand attached to the biggest orders. Broadsheet reports, in a partner-content piece produced with Penfolds, that the range launched on 1 October 2026 and will be sold through to the end of the year.

## What is in the Penfolds Holiday collection

The set draws on Penfolds' best-known wines, including Bin 389 and St Henri. Other listings name further bottles, but Broadsheet confirms those two, so they are the ones to rely on. The wines themselves are familiar. The difference is the packaging, which Broadsheet says Sivan conceptualised.

The boxes borrow from the rituals of giving a present: folded wrapping paper, tactile textures and bright colour combinations. Each carries a handwritten message that Sivan curated. For a product that is usually bought as a gift anyway, the idea is that the box becomes part of the present rather than something thrown away.

## The Magill candle

The extra is a candle called Field Study: Magill with Penfolds. It was made by Tsu Lange Yor, Sivan's lifestyle brand, and is inspired by his visit to Magill Estate, which Penfolds describes as its spiritual home in South Australia. According to Broadsheet, the scent is meant to evoke the vineyards, the cellars and the surrounding landscape.

The candle is not sold on its own. It is a gift with purchase:

- a 210g glass candle with Holiday purchases over $300
- a 500g ceramic candle with Holiday purchases over $1000

That structure matters if you are shopping. The ceramic version only comes with a spend more than three times higher than the glass one.

## A second project with Penfolds

This is the second release from the partnership. The first, Bin 389 Designed by Troye Sivan, arrived earlier in 2026. Two collaborations in one year suggest the arrangement is more than a one-off endorsement, though Broadsheet does not describe how long it is meant to run.

The collection is sold through the Penfolds website and at select national and independent retailers, and Broadsheet's listing gives an availability window of 1 October to 31 December 2026. Neither Broadsheet nor Penfolds gives a quote from Sivan in that piece, so what he thinks of the finished boxes is not on the record there.
