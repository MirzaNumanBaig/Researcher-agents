- Project Hail Mary returns to cinemas on 11 December 2026, with IMAX and select 70mm screenings, as reported by Yahoo.

Ryan Gosling Starfighter news arrived alongside two other updates on his slate. Lucasfilm has revealed a new logo for Star Wars: Starfighter, Amazon MGM Studios has confirmed a rerelease date for Project Hail Mary, and Marvel's casting chief has explained why she believes Gosling suits Ghost Rider.

## The Ryan Gosling Starfighter logo

The new Starfighter design was shown at New York Comic-Con and then spread on social media, according to The Direct's report of 16 November, as relayed by [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/lucasfilm-starfighter-logo-gosling-projects). It turns the Rebel Alliance symbol upside down so it reads like a skull, with two blasters crossed beneath it.

Earlier looks included the familiar Star Wars lettering and a gold-and-yellow title. The same logo has been used to announce three books due after the film opens: an Art Of book by Phil Szostak, a Visual Dictionary by Pablo Hidalgo and an expanded novelisation by Delilah Dawson.

Gosling plays Kade Auberon, described by [SuaraGarut](https://suaragarut.id/en/ryan-gosling-projects-hail-mary-starfighter) as a cynical, Han Solo-like figure with a mysterious background. Aaron Pierre and newcomer Flynn Gray are also linked to the film, and Shawn Levy directs. The plot has not been disclosed. For more on the character, see our earlier piece on [Gosling's Star Wars, Ghost Rider and Hail Mary slate](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-star-wars-starfighter-2/).

## Project Hail Mary heads back to the big screen

According to Yahoo, as cited by both outlets above, Amazon MGM Studios has released a new trailer confirming that Project Hail Mary returns to cinemas on 11 December 2026. The rerelease includes IMAX sessions and select 70mm screenings. Our [rerelease report](https://moduscarry.com/project-hail-mary-rerelease/) has more.

The film first opened in March 2026 and made more than US$685 million worldwide. Gosling plays Ryland Grace, a middle-school science teacher who wakes aboard a spacecraft with no memory of who he is or why he is there. As his memory returns, he learns that a substance is dimming the Sun and threatening Earth. He works with Rocky, an alien engineer.

Sandra Hüller plays Eva Stratt, and James Ortiz provides Rocky's voice and performance. Phil Lord and Christopher Miller directed the adaptation of Andy Weir's novel, from a screenplay by Drew Goddard.

## Marvel's casting chief backs Gosling for Ghost Rider

Gosling was announced as Marvel's Ghost Rider at San Diego Comic-Con in July. Shawn Levy is directing, and [GeekTyrant](https://geektyrant.com/news/marvel-casting-director-talks-about-why-ryan-gosling-is-the-perfect-choice-for-ghost-rider) reports that Marvel president Kevin Feige has said the idea began with a pitch from Gosling himself, who had wanted the role for years.

Speaking to Collider at the Newport Beach Film Festival, Sarah Finn said his passion for the character matters because it comes through on screen. She also called him an unusually talented and versatile actor, and said the pairing with Levy makes her optimistic. GeekTyrant lists the release date as 28 July 2028.

Not everyone online has been so generous. [Comic Sands](https://www.comicsands.com/ryan-gosling-skull-shaming) reports that a post from Marvel Mania on 8 November compared the shape of Gosling's skull to what a flaming skeleton might need, and that fans pushed back on the idea. The outlet also notes that Nicolas Cage, who played Johnny Blaze in 2007 and returned for Spirit of Vengeance, has been welcoming toward his successor.

A report that [Rosamund Pike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosamund_Pike) and Betty Gilpin are in the mix for the film is only a rumour. We could not read the original, so we treat it as unconfirmed.
