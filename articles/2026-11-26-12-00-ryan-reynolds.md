**Quick summary**

- The Ryan Reynolds publicist change is confirmed: [Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) has signed with Kelly Bush Novak, founder and CEO of ID-PR, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- He switched from Jami Kandel at Vision PR. The move was first reported by Page Six Hollywood.
- His wife, [Blake Lively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Lively), joined ID-PR in September after her legal battle with [Justin Baldoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Baldoni) ended.
- Separately, fitness brand BowFlex has named Reynolds in a new campaign, according to Athletech News.

The Ryan Reynolds publicist question has a new answer. The Deadpool star is now represented by Kelly Bush Novak and her team at ID-PR, The Hollywood Reporter [confirmed on 25 November](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/ryan-reynolds-signs-kelly-bush-novak-idpr-blake-lively-1236763140/), after he left Jami Kandel at Vision PR.

## Why the move matters

Publicist changes rarely make headlines on their own. This one does because of the timing and the household behind it. The Hollywood Reporter says Reynolds is following Lively to ID-PR, which she joined in September, after the end of her legal dispute with the *It Ends With Us* director Baldoni.

The trade paper describes Lively as making a new start with the firm, and frames Reynolds' decision as him doing the same. Neither the report nor our other sources quote Reynolds, Lively or ID-PR on the reasons, so the motive is inference rather than fact. We have also seen no comment from Kandel or Vision PR.

We covered the first reports of the switch in [our earlier post on the new publicist](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-publicist-kelly-bush-novak/). Page Six Hollywood was the first outlet to break it, and The Hollywood Reporter has now confirmed it.

## Who else is on the ID-PR roster

Novak's client list is long. According to The Hollywood Reporter, it includes [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan), [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga), [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Ben Stiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stiller), [Elliot Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliot_Page), [Lena Dunham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lena_Dunham), [Diego Luna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Luna) and [Pamela Adlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Adlon). The same list names Alejandro G. Iñárritu, John Malkovich and Serena Williams.

For a client with as many business interests as Reynolds, a firm with that range of film, music and sport names is a logical fit. That is an observation, not a claim made in the reports.

## What Reynolds has on his plate

The Hollywood Reporter notes that Reynolds is a prolific producer and entrepreneur as well as an actor. Its summary of his current slate includes:

- Another *Deadpool* film, following the box office success of 2024's *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which he made with [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman).
- *Mayday*, an adventure with [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh) and [Maria Bakalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Bakalova) about an American pilot trapped in Soviet Russia and the ex-KGB agent who helps him.
- A three-season pickup from Disney-owned FX for *Welcome to Wrexham*, the docuseries he makes with [Rob Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney) about the Welsh football club.
- A new Disney+ docuseries from Reynolds and Mac following his and Jackman's Australian sailing team, which gives the slate a local angle for readers here.

*Mayday* opened on 4 September 2026, according to its [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayday_(2026_film)). That page lists a Rotten Tomatoes score of 83 per cent and a Metacritic score of 60 out of 100 from 24 critics. We have no reliable box office figures for it yet, so we are not drawing a conclusion about how it has performed.

## A fitness campaign on the side

Reynolds also turned up in business news this week. Athletech News reports that [BowFlex is betting on Reynolds](https://athletechnews.com/bowflex-ryan-reynolds-don-saladino-strength-training-campaign-comeback/) and the wider rise of strength training to drive a comeback for the at-home fitness equipment maker. The full article sits behind a members-only paywall, so we can only confirm the headline and the framing, not the terms of the deal or campaign details.

## What happens next

No announcement has been made about how the new arrangement will change Reynolds' public activity. The clearest things to watch are the promotion around the next *Deadpool* film and the Disney+ sailing series, both of which Novak's team may now handle.
