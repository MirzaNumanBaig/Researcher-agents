**Quick summary**

- Marvel Studios has announced a nine-city promotional tour for *Avengers: Doomsday*, with the Gold Coast among the stops.
- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) reshared the news on X with the caption "Doomsday is coming".
- The tour starts in Las Vegas and ends in Los Angeles. Dates and guest lists have not been announced.
- The film opens in North America on 18 December 2026, according to Koimoi and Mathrubhumi English.

Marvel Studios has named the nine cities on the *Avengers: Doomsday* world tour, and one of them is the Gold Coast. [Chris Hemsworth](https://moduscarry.com/tag/chris-hemsworth/), who returns as Thor, shared the announcement on X with a three-word message: "Doomsday is coming." The reveal came on 7 November, about six weeks before the film reaches cinemas.

## What the Avengers Doomsday world tour includes

According to [Mathrubhumi English](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/movies-music/news/avengers-doomsday-chris-hemsworth-teases-film-as-marvel-announces-nine-city-global-tour-hn55c1l7), the itinerary covers Las Vegas, São Paulo, Gold Coast, Rome, London, Seoul, Shanghai, Toronto and Los Angeles. [Koimoi](https://www.koimoi.com/hollywood-news/avengers-doomsday-marvel-reveals-9-cities-for-world-tour-chris-hemsworth-reacts-with-doomsday-is-coming/) lists the same cities.

Marvel revealed them through the film's "Doomsday Clock", the countdown it has been running towards release. Las Vegas opens the tour and Los Angeles closes it, per Mathrubhumi English.

The gaps matter as much as the list. Both outlets report that Marvel has not yet given dates, event details or the names of cast members who will attend each stop. For Australian fans, the Gold Coast is the only local city named, and nothing yet says who will walk the carpet there.

## Hemsworth's role in the build-up

Hemsworth did not add any new information about the film. He reshared Marvel's post, and the short caption was enough to carry the news across his feed. The tease fits a pattern for Australian audiences, who have followed his Marvel career since the first Thor film. Our own coverage includes his earlier plans to [visit Korea for Avengers: Doomsday](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-korea-visit/), and Seoul is now confirmed as a tour city.

That detail is worth a second look. Whether the Korea trip and the Seoul stop are the same event is not stated in either source, so it remains a question for Marvel's next update.

## A crowded cast for the Russo brothers

The film is directed by Anthony and [Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers) and produced by [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige), Louis D'Esposito and [Jonathan Schwartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Schwartz_(producer)). Mathrubhumi English says [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) plays [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom).

The ensemble named in the two reports includes [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd), [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Vanessa Kirby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanessa_Kirby), [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan), [Tom Hiddleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hiddleston), [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum), [Simu Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simu_Liu), [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) and [James Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Marsden). Koimoi's list is longer still, running past 30 names.

Both outlets say the film brings together characters from three universes. Mathrubhumi English names the Avengers, Fantastic Four and X-Men franchises, and Koimoi describes heroes from three universes on a collision course with an existential threat. A cast that size explains why the tour's guest list is the open question.

## Release dates and formats

Koimoi gives the worldwide release as 18 December 2026. Mathrubhumi English adds that India gets the film a day earlier, on 17 December, in English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. Both mention a premium presentation in Marvel Studios' Infinity Vision format. Neither source gives an Australian release date, so check local cinema listings.

## What happens next

Marvel still has to publish the tour dates, the events at each stop and the stars attending. With the film about six weeks away, those announcements should arrive soon, though neither outlet reports a timetable. That has not been confirmed. Marvel has not announced which cast members will attend each city.
