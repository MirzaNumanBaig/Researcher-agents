**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended the 2026 Victoria's Secret fashion show on October 18, according to HELLO! Magazine.
- She wore a tailored Jean Paul Gaultier look from the label's fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection instead of lingerie-style dressing.
- Models walked to "Nicole Kidman", a song by Slovak pop singer Adéla, and Kidman was seen smiling.
- Kylie Jenner modelled the returning Fantasy Bra, and Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Megan Moroney and Katseye performed.

While much of the front row leaned into sparkle and lingerie-inspired dressing, Nicole Kidman went the other way at the 2026 Victoria's Secret fashion show on October 18. The Australian actress arrived in a sharp, suited look by Jean Paul Gaultier, [HELLO! Magazine reported](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/930144/nicole-kidman-stands-out-in-unexpected-victorias-secret-look/), and was spotted smiling as a song with her name in the title played on the runway.

## The look: a crisp take on a lingerie night

According to HELLO!, the outfit came from Gaultier's fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection. It paired a white button-down shirt with a high-waisted black pencil skirt, finished with garter-inspired detailing. A black and pink striped tie and a burgundy Chanel bag completed it.

Kidman's hair was styled in soft waves with face-framing curtain bangs. The outlet described her makeup as a bronze eye, rosy cheek and pink lip. The pink in the tie was the one nod to the evening's theme, and that restraint is what set her apart from the other guests.

## The song with her name on it

The runway soundtrack included "Nicole Kidman", a hit by Adéla, a Slovak pop singer who rose to fame on the 2023 reality series Dream Academy. HELLO! said Kidman is a fan of the singer.

The connection goes back to the London premiere of the [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic) sequel. In a clip that went viral on social media, Kidman was asked whether she knew who Adéla was. "Oh, yes! She is! I do know!" she replied, as quoted by HELLO!.

Adéla told Pop Crave afterwards that she could not believe Kidman knew who she was. She called getting recognition from the subject of her song, "a legend", a big moment. Seeing Kidman smile as the song played on the runway was a neat sequel to that clip.

## What else happened at the show

Victoria's Secret brought back its Fantasy Bra for 2026, modelled by [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner). Runway looks were also worn by [Adriana Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriana_Lima), [Alessandra Ambrosio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandra_Ambrosio), [Candice Swanepoel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Swanepoel), [Bella Hadid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Hadid), Gigi Hadid, [Anok Yai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anok_Yai) and [Alex Consani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Consani). Live performances came from [Megan Thee Stallion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Thee_Stallion), [Tate McRae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate_McRae), [Megan Moroney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Moroney) and Katseye.

Adam Selman, the brand's chief creative officer, told PEOPLE he wanted the show to be "bigger and bolder than last year". He said no New York venue could hold everything the team planned, and he called this year's theme "Fandemonium", built around a different side of Americana and the American dream. He also teased "some unexpected people" in the mix. Kidman's appearance, in a collar and tie, fits that description.
