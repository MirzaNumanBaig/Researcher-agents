**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) is one of more than 150 Australian entertainers, musicians and writers who signed an open letter released in mid-November 2026.
- The letter asks Environment Minister [Murray Watt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Watt) to reject Woodside Energy's Browse gas development near Scott Reef.
- Scott Reef sits about 270 kilometres north-west of the Kimberley coast and supports more than 1,500 marine species.
- Woodside has changed its plans to protect turtle nesting sites, but the company still backs the project.

Rebel Wilson has added her name to a high-profile campaign to keep gas drilling away from Scott Reef, one of Australia's largest standalone oceanic coral systems. According to [Zoom TV](https://www.zoomtventertainment.com/hollywood/hugh-jackman-rebel-wilson-scott-reef-gas-drilling-protest-article-156543315), the open letter went to the federal government in mid-November 2026 and carries more than 150 signatures from the country's cultural sector.

## Who signed the Scott Reef letter

The Rebel Wilson Scott Reef connection is part of a long list of familiar names. Alongside Wilson, the signatories include actors [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Simon Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Baker). Music is heavily represented too: [Kevin Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Parker) of [Tame Impala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_Impala), [Missy Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missy_Higgins), Paul Kelly, [Tina Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Arena), [Jimmy Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Barnes), [Jessica Mauboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Mauboy), G Flip, [Tkay Maidza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tkay_Maidza), [Amy Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Shark), members of 5 Seconds of Summer, [Peter Garrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Garrett) and [Tim Minchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Minchin).

The Times of India, citing a report in The Guardian, says the push was led by environmental groups including Greenpeace Australia Pacific and Green Music Australia. The letter is addressed to Watt, who holds the federal environment portfolio and will have a say on the project.

## What the artists are saying

Several signatories added their own statements. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/in-2026-hugh-jackman-rebel-wilson-and-more-celebrities-sign-an-open-letter-to-save-australias-historic-scott-reef/articleshow/136024089.cms) reports that Higgins warned that drilling up to 57 wells under and around the coral system puts an extraordinary place at risk. "We need to preserve these beautiful places for our kids. If not now, then when?" she said.

Mauboy focused on the cultural side. "Caring for Country means caring for our oceans and all the life they hold," she said, adding that places like Scott Reef carry a responsibility to protect them for future generations. Neither outlet quotes a personal statement from Wilson, so her position is known only through her signature.

## Why Scott Reef matters

Scott Reef lies roughly 270 kilometres off the Kimberley coast in Western Australia and sits directly above the Browse gas basin. Both outlets describe it as an ancient ocean ecosystem that is home to more than 1,500 marine species. It is also a critical habitat for threatened animals, including endangered whales and sea turtles.

That habitat role is why the project has drawn so much scrutiny. Reports cited by the sources say the Browse development is forecast to produce 1.6 billion tonnes of climate pollution over its lifetime. A previously undisclosed letter from Western Australia's Environmental Protection Authority also warned that the wider development could cause "significant, serious, and irreversible" environmental impacts, according to The Times of India.

## Woodside's response

Woodside Energy has already revised its original proposal. The company has committed to drilling no wells within 20 kilometres of Sandy Islet, a step aimed at protecting turtle nesting sites. It has also pledged to restrict gas flaring, because the bright light can disorient hatchlings. Woodside maintains that the project is a necessary part of natural gas production.

The sources do not say that the company has responded directly to the letter. The debate between the developer and the coalition of artists and environmental groups remains open.

## What happens next

The next move belongs to the federal government. The letter asks Watt to reject the Browse development, and no decision has been reported in the sources. Campaign groups are likely to keep the issue in the public eye while the assessment continues, though that is an expectation rather than a confirmed plan.

For Wilson, the letter is a rare public step into an Australian environmental debate. ## Frequently asked questions

### Did Rebel Wilson sign the Scott Reef open letter?

Yes. Both Zoom TV and The Times of India list her among the more than 150 signatories, alongside Hugh Jackman and Simon Baker.

### What does the letter ask for?

It asks Federal Environment Minister Murray Watt to reject Woodside Energy's Browse gas development, which includes plans to drill up to 57 wells in and around Scott Reef.

### Where is Scott Reef?

It is about 270 kilometres north-west of Western Australia's Kimberley coast and is one of the country's largest standalone oceanic coral reef systems.

### Has Woodside changed its plans?

Yes. It has said it will not drill within 20 kilometres of Sandy Islet and will restrict gas flaring to protect turtle hatchlings.
