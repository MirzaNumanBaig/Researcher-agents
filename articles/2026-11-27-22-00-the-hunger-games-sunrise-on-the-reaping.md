**Quick summary**

- The Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack includes Stevie Nicks, Depeche Mode, Madison Cunningham, Noah Kahan, The Cure and Lizzy McAlpine, according to Radio Times.
- Jennifer Lawrence returns for a new version of "Deep in the Meadow", while Whitney Peak and Mckenna Grace also sing on the album.
- Arab News says the prequel "lacks bite" and has toned down the violence of the arena.
- Wikipedia lists a 68% Rotten Tomatoes score, a Metacritic score of 55/100 and US$237.2 million at the box office against a US$150 million budget.

The [Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games%3A_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping) has become one of the more talked-about parts of the new [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, which opened on 28 October 2026. Radio Times has published a full guide to the songs, and Arab News has run a review arguing that the film is a softer beast than the franchise that came before it.

## The songs in the film

Radio Times, in a guide by Molly Moss, describes the soundtrack as moody and atmospheric. Contributors include [Noah Kahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Kahan), The Cure and [Lizzy McAlpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzy_McAlpine). The tracklist it prints also names Stevie Nicks with "Doing The Best That I Can (Arena Mix)", Depeche Mode and KÁRYYN with "Survive (Arena Mix | KÁRYYN Rework)", and Madison Cunningham with "Show Me Where The Ending Starts".

The cast takes part too. [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)) and [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) both have new songs on the album. The biggest draw for longtime fans is [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), who recorded a new version of "Deep in the Meadow". Radio Times notes the song first appeared on the soundtrack of *Mockingjay: Part 2*.

## When the cast songs play

Radio Times also maps where the sung pieces land in the story. Lenore Dove sings on the morning of the reaping and again while the Second Quarter Quell is under way. Scot Greenan's Burdock Everdeen sings during a funeral for the fallen tributes. Lawrence's [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) sings in the epilogue. The film arrives 14 years after the first Hunger Games movie, and the music leans on that history.

## What one review says

The story itself follows a teenage Haymitch Abernathy, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), who is picked for the 50th Hunger Games on his 16th birthday. Arab News is not convinced by the result. Its reviewer calls Zada a likable lead but says he has little of the roguish charm [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) brought to the older Haymitch, and describes the dialogue about his growing political awareness as painfully on-the-nose.

The supporting cast fares better in that review. It singles out Mckenna Grace as the abrasive tribute Maysilee Donner, [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) as a younger Effie Trinket, and [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) as the young games designer Plutarch Heavensbee. The main complaint is that the arena violence has been softened, with threats reduced to stinging butterflies, rats and poisonous pollen, and that deaths barely register before the plot moves on. The reviewer also says a reassuring epilogue weakens the tragedy.

## How the film is performing

Critics are split rather than hostile. Wikipedia records a 68% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 55 out of 100 from 43 critics. Its summary of the reviews says the prequel retreads familiar ground but does so sturdily. The same page lists director Francis Lawrence's film at US$237.2 million worldwide on a reported US$150 million budget, roughly 1.6 times its cost. That is a profit on paper, though a modest one for a blockbuster of this size. These figures are as recorded on Wikipedia and may change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is on the Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack?
According to Radio Times, the songs come from artists including Stevie Nicks, Depeche Mode, Madison Cunningham, Noah Kahan, The Cure and Lizzy McAlpine. Jennifer Lawrence, Whitney Peak and Mckenna Grace also contribute.

### Does Jennifer Lawrence sing in the prequel?
Yes. Radio Times says her Katniss sings during the epilogue, and the soundtrack carries a new version of "Deep in the Meadow".

### Is Sunrise on the Reaping a hit?
It has earned US$237.2 million against a US$150 million budget, according to Wikipedia. Reviews are mixed, with a 68% Rotten Tomatoes score.
