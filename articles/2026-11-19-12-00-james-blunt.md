**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) posted what appears to be a deed poll application on Instagram on Monday, 16 November.
- It shows a change from James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air, a play on the budget airline Ryanair.
- The pledge was tied to his album Airplane Mode reaching No. 1 on the UK Albums Chart.
- He said the paperwork cost him £53.05.

James Blunt has made good on a joke promise. The 52-year-old singer shared what looks like a name-change application on Instagram on 16 November, listing his new name as Ryan Bel Air, days after his album Airplane Mode went to No. 1 in the UK. His caption read only: "This cost me £53.05."

## What the paperwork shows

According to [Yahoo Lifestyle Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/james-blunt-just-legally-changed-211816012.html), the document has Blunt changing his legal name from James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air. [Guitar Player](https://www.guitarplayer.com/guitarists/james-blunt-name-change-ryan-air-aiplane-mode) describes it as the first official step and says he "intends" to make the change, which is a fair reading of a photographed application. Neither outlet reports that the change has been formally completed, so treat "legally changed" headlines with some care.

The £53.05 is about US$70, according to Guitar Player. The new name is a nod to Ryanair, and the airline had already joined in on the gag.

## The promise behind it

Blunt first floated the idea in 2024. He said he would legally change his name to whatever fans chose if the 20th-anniversary edition of his debut album, Back to Bedlam, returned to No. 1. Yahoo reports that the most popular suggestion was "Blunty McBluntface", a nod to the 2016 public vote that produced Boaty McBoatface for a UK research vessel.

The reissue peaked at No. 7, so he was spared.

On 11 November he tried again in an Instagram video. He reminded fans of the earlier failure, with a joke at their expense, and said that if Airplane Mode reached the top he would change his name to Ryan Air. Ryanair replied on X with: "At least people would know you then."

## A first No. 1 in 19 years

The album delivered. Per the Official Charts Company, as relayed by Yahoo, it is Blunt's first UK chart-topping album in 19 years. His last was All The Lost Souls in 2007. Back to Bedlam, which included [You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful), reached No. 1 in 2005 and stayed there for 10 weeks.

Blunt thanked everyone who had streamed, downloaded or bought the record. Asked how he would celebrate, he said he would get "absolutely pis*ed", according to Yahoo.

## Humour as a defence

The stunt fits a public persona built on self-mockery. Guitar Player notes the backlash that followed You're Beautiful and Blunt's habit of joining in: when a critic said he had been irrelevant since 2009, he replied "2006, actually."

He also told The Guardian he was surprised that people were unhappy for him when he reached his dream. He had held a "real proper job" before music, he said, and friends who served in Afghanistan or Iraq put bad reviews in perspective, and "perspective is everything."

Fans loved the latest joke. Comments quoted by Yahoo included "You, Captain Blunt (Captain Air), are a national treasure" and "Well done, James. You have officially entered Airplane mode." One asked for a refund on a signed copy bearing his old name.

## What happens next

Blunt has not said whether the change is final or how he will be billed on future releases. His caption suggests he is treating it as a bit, but the cost and the paperwork are real. Watch his Instagram for any update.
