**Quick summary**

- A Kidman Carol, an off-Broadway spoof of Charles Dickens's holiday tale, runs at New World Stages from 16 November to 30 December.
- [Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp) plays [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) in the Ebenezer Scrooge role, set on Oscars eve.
- [Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle) plays three spirits of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman).
- The play is written by Kevin Zak and directed by Skylar Fox, according to New York Theatre Guide.

Cate Blanchett Scrooge is not a pairing many expected, but an off-Broadway Christmas comedy has made it real. In *A Kidman Carol*, which opens its run at New World Stages on 16 November, Josh Sharp plays the Australian Oscar winner as the grumpy centre of a Dickens parody, according to an interview published by [New York Theatre Guide](https://www.newyorktheatreguide.com/theatre-news/interviews/marla-mindelle-and-josh-sharp-bring-awards-season-to-the-holiday-season-in-a-kidman-carol).

## What A Kidman Carol is about

Playwright Kevin Zak's satire swaps Christmas Eve for Oscars eve. Blanchett, in the Scrooge role, is visited by three spirits of Kidman. Mindelle leads a five-person cast and plays all three spirits, each tied to a different era of Kidman's career.

Mindelle told the outlet those eras point to the musical *Moulin Rouge!*, the drama *The Hours*, which won Kidman her Oscar, and a viral AMC Theatres ad. Costumes and wigs help the audience tell the spirits apart. "You know Nicole loves a wig," Mindelle said, and Sharp added that the wigs do two-thirds of the work.

The rest of the cast is Ashby Florence, Richard Perez and Phillip Taratula, who plays Katharine Hepburn among other roles. Skylar Fox directs.

## How Josh Sharp plays the Cate Blanchett Scrooge

Sharp says he is not doing a straight impression. Because Scrooge is the straight role, his Blanchett has to stay grounded while the chaos builds around her. He compared the setup to *The Muppet Christmas Carol*, where [Michael Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Caine) played Scrooge among the Muppets. In his words, she is the one trying to sell the jokes the least.

He also said he sees Blanchett as a useful stand-in for a wider feeling in the industry. "I don't think the real Cate is that cynical," Sharp said, adding that she seems to be among the rare people in the culture who are still into the work more than the circus. The quote is his view of a stage character and not a claim about Blanchett herself.

## Why Christmas and the Oscars

Sharp argued the two events have more in common than they seem. Both, he said, are now heavily commercial, but at their roots they are about family and making art. That shared pull between the genuine and the corporate is what the show plays with.

Mindelle, a Tony nominee for co-writing and starring in *Titanique* as [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion), said Kidman and Dion share a camp sense of humour that suits her style. Both performers, the New York Theatre Guide reports, have taken their own theatrical approach and not a copy of the real women.

## What happens next

Tickets are on sale through 30 December 2026 at New World Stages, 340 West 50th Street, New York. Children under four will not be admitted. Neither Blanchett nor Kidman is quoted in the coverage we could read, and neither has commented on the show.
