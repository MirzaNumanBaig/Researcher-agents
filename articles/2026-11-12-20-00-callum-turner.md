## Brosnan weighs in on the Callum Turner James Bond talk

Pierce Brosnan has added his voice to the debate over who will be the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), singling out Callum Turner as a strong actor. The comments came in a recent interview, as reported by [Wake Up Singapore](https://wakeup.sg/pierce-brosnan-next-james-bond/).

Brosnan, an Irish actor who played the spy in four films between 1995 and 2002, said he now watches the franchise from the other side of the screen. "I'm just an audience member now," he said, adding that he is waiting for the news like everyone else.

Turner's name is one of several reportedly linked to the part. That wording matters: the report describes the candidates as being "reportedly considered", and nothing in it confirms a shortlist or an offer.

## A shared film from 2017

Brosnan and Turner have worked together before. They both appeared in the 2017 film The Only Living Boy in New York, which gives Brosnan's praise a first-hand basis rather than a fan's.

He did not make Turner his only pick. According to the report, Brosnan said each of the actors under discussion has a distinct voice, physical presence and the ability to hold an audience. He also pointed to their potential for long careers in film.

## Why Denis Villeneuve matters to Brosnan

Casting was not the only thing Brosnan was excited about. He said the involvement of Villeneuve, the Canadian filmmaker behind Dune and Blade Runner 2049, was another big reason to look forward to the film.

"His work is visionary, and his stealth and imagination of camera work and storytelling is so visceral and sensuous," Brosnan said, in the words quoted by Wake Up Singapore. He added that he expects the film to be a magnificent production and is confident the director can bring something fresh to a series that has run for decades.

Brosnan also wished whoever lands the role the best of luck, noting the weight that comes with following a long line of actors in the part.
