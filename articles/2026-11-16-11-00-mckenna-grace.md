**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) plays Maysilee Donner in "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping".
- She told Variety Australia that being feminine is often mistaken for weakness, and that Maysilee proves otherwise.
- The film follows a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy in the 50th Hunger Games, with 48 tributes in the arena.
- It opens in Australian cinemas on 19 November.

Mckenna Grace says her latest role has settled an argument she once had with herself: liking dresses and ribbons is not a weakness. In an interview with [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/mckenna-grace-interview-hunger-games-femininity-42581/), published on 15 November, the actress talked about playing Maysilee Donner in the new [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, which reaches Australian cinemas on 19 November.

## Why Maysilee's dresses matter

According to Variety Australia, Grace sees her character's love of dresses, ribbons and looking her best as far more than vanity. In a world where young people are forced to fight to the death for the Capitol's entertainment, she argued, holding on to small expressions of individuality becomes a form of rebellion.

"It's her way of holding on to her humanity," Grace said of Maysilee, adding that it is also her way of showing she still has "class" and "herself" in a system trying to strip her identity away.

The report notes that this matches the character in Suzanne Collins' novel, where Maysilee's attention to fashion and social etiquette hides a fiercely defiant streak. Grace described her as strong and hot-headed, yet someone who still cares deeply about how she is presented.

## A lesson Grace took home

The role also touched on something personal. Grace told the outlet she spent much of her childhood insisting she did not want to be "girly" or wear dresses. As she has grown older, she said, she has found she enjoys it. "I like being girly, I like being feminine," she said, "and I think that a lot of people can mistake that for weakness."

## What the film is about

"Sunrise on the Reaping" is set 24 years before the original films. It follows a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, played by Australia's Joseph Zada, who is selected for the 50th Hunger Games, known as the Second Quarter Quell. The Capitol doubles the field to 48 young people, per Variety Australia.

Grace's Maysilee is one of the District 12 tributes. She eventually forms an uneasy alliance with Haymitch as the Capitol's attempts to control the contestants grow more brutal.

[Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence), a veteran of the franchise, directs. The cast also includes [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) as [President Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow), [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons), [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close) and [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin).
