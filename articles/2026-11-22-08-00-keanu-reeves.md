**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) was seen in Las Vegas, walking the paddock at Friday's Grand Prix qualifying, according to Sporting News.
- [Colin Farrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Farrell) and [Kim Kardashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kardashian) were also among the famous faces in attendance.
- The race itself was scheduled to start at 8 p.m. local time on Saturday, with Max Verstappen on pole.
- Reeves has no new project tied to the weekend; the sighting is a night out, not an announcement.

Keanu Reeves Las Vegas Grand Prix news: the actor made the Grand Prix guest list on Friday night, walking through the paddock during qualifying while the Strip filled with racing fans and celebrities, Sporting News reported.

## Keanu Reeves in Las Vegas: who was in the paddock

The "John Wick" star was seen walking around casually at one point, according to [Sporting News](https://www.sportingnews.com/uk/culture/trending/news/f1-las-vegas-grand-prix-celebrities-kim-kardashian-keanu-reeves/75ed505d8aefa4ac2ce08cc3). The outlet also placed "Batman" actor Colin Farrell in the paddock, taking in the action.

Kim Kardashian drew the biggest crowd. The report says fans surrounded her in the paddock shortly after her boyfriend, Ferrari driver Lewis Hamilton, fought for a place near the front. Hamilton missed out on the final qualifying session and was set to start Saturday's race outside the top 10.

## A celebrity weekend on the Strip

Reeves was far from the only name. Sporting News says the cold weather did not keep guests away, and the city once again became a celebrity magnet, with stars moving between parties, red carpets and hospitality suites.

Other guests named in the report include NFL quarterback Russell Wilson, who shared photos on Instagram with former NBA player Russell Westbrook. Natalia Bryant, daughter of the late Kobe Bryant, and Netflix reality star [Alix Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alix_Earle) were also photographed during race week.

## Meanwhile, an old Reeves film gets a second look

Away from Nevada, tech site BGR published a piece this week arguing that Reeves' 2008 film "The Day the Earth Stood Still" deserves a fresh look. According to [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2322887/keanu-reeves-divisie-sci-fi-movie-remake-the-day-the-earth-stood-still/), Reeves played the alien messenger Klaatu in director Scott Derrickson's remake of the 1951 classic.

The film holds a 20% Tomatometer score on Rotten Tomatoes, BGR says, yet it earned $233.1 million on an $80 million budget, helped by an IMAX release. Derrickson has described making it as a "horrific experience", partly because of the writers' strike, and told /Film he struggled to find director work for almost two years afterwards. He went on to direct "Doctor Strange" in 2016.

This is an opinion piece, not new reporting, but it shows how Reeves' catalogue keeps drawing attention well beyond "The Matrix" and "John Wick".

## What happens next

The Las Vegas Grand Prix was due to start at 8 p.m. local time on Saturday, with Max Verstappen on pole after Kimi Antonelli's five-place grid penalty, Sporting News reports. The outlet expected the main event to draw just as many A-listers as qualifying did.
