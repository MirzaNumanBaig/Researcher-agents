**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is part of the cast of Saint Laurent's campaign for the 60th anniversary of Le Smoking, according to The Zoe Report.
- The black-and-white images were photographed by Mark Kean for the house's Winter 2026 collection.
- Kate Moss, Zoë Kravitz, Rosé and Charli xcx also appear.
- From 2027, Saint Laurent will make Le Smoking in France, in its own ateliers.
- Storrie was also named to BAFTA North America's 2026 Breakthrough US cohort, as reported by Latin Times.

Connor Storrie is one of the faces of Saint Laurent's new campaign marking 60 years of Le Smoking, the tuxedo the house introduced for women in 1966. The Zoe Report [published the cast list](https://www.thezoereport.com/fashion/saint-laurent-le-smoking-60th-anniversary-campaign) on 19 November, and Storrie sits in a line-up that mixes models, musicians and actors.

## Who is in the Le Smoking campaign

According to The Zoe Report, the images are shot by Mark Kean and belong to Saint Laurent's Winter 2026 collection. The cast includes [Kate Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Moss), Zoë Kravitz, Rosé and Charli xcx, along with [Charlotte Gainsbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Gainsbourg), [Anok Yai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anok_Yai), [Anja Rubik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anja_Rubik), [Frankie Rayder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankie_Rayder) and [Loli Bahia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loli_Bahia).

Storrie and Puerto Rican singer [Rauw Alejandro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rauw_Alejandro) are named among the men who join the cast. The outlet says their presence signals a new era for a suit that began as womenswear.

## Why Le Smoking still matters to the house

Yves Saint Laurent introduced the tuxedo for women in 1966. The Zoe Report describes it as a challenge to the rules of eveningwear, borrowing from menswear tailoring to offer a look that was both sensual and assertive.

Kean's portraits keep the focus on the silhouette: dark tailoring against a white backdrop. The outlet notes that this echoes the photographic history long tied to the suit, including Helmut Newton's original images of it.

## Production moves to France in 2027

The anniversary also came with a business announcement. The Zoe Report says that from 2027 Saint Laurent will bring Le Smoking production back to France, inside the house's own workshops. Both women's and men's versions will be made at the Atelier de Confection Saint Laurent, where the label's ready-to-wear has been produced for decades.

## A busy week for Storrie

The campaign lands in the same week as another piece of recognition. Latin Times reports that Storrie is one of 14 creatives in [BAFTA North America's 2026 Breakthrough US cohort](https://www.latintimes.com/bafta-north-america-2026-breakthrough-us-cohort-601513), alongside actress [Inde Navarrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette). Latin Times lists "Heated Rivalry" among the projects represented by the group.

The yearlong programme, sponsored by Apple TV, offers career guidance, one-on-one meetings with established professionals, access to BAFTA events and screenings, and full BAFTA voting membership. The group was chosen by separate film and television and games juries, and BAFTA says it is now in its seventh year in the United States.
