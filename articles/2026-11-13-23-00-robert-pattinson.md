**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) leads The Take, a Netflix heist thriller due on 15 January 2027.
- He stars opposite [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones).
- [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles), director of City of God, made the film.
- Boss Hunting says the first trailer is feeding the ongoing talk about who will play the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond). Nothing has been confirmed.

Robert Pattinson is the face of Netflix's new heist thriller The Take, and its first trailer has pushed his name back into the James Bond conversation. According to Australian outlet [Boss Hunting](https://www.bosshunting.com.au/entertainment/movies/robert-pattinson-the-take-netflix), the film arrives on 15 January 2027, and the clip reads to some as a showcase for his leading-man credentials.

## What The Take is about

Pattinson plays Noah, a former Army captain. Washington's character, a master thief named Christian Carter, recruits him to help rob Manhattan's richest bank. The complication, as Boss Hunting describes it, is that Noah's girlfriend Rose, played by Edgar-Jones, works at that bank as a teller.

The trailer points to a nonlinear, puzzle-like structure. Much of the story is told through Noah's point of view, so viewers only know what he knows. Boss Hunting expects that to make for a disorienting run of cons, double-crosses and shifting allegiances.

## What the director says

Meirelles has praised his lead. He told Netflix's Tudum that Pattinson is "never late, he's always available", adding that if he needs 20 takes, the actor is there for all of them. That is a notable endorsement from a filmmaker whose City of God earned him an international reputation.

## Why Bond keeps coming up

The Bond search has dragged on for more than five years since Daniel Craig's final outing in No Time to Die, according to Boss Hunting. The outlet notes that names such as Josh O'Connor, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) have been tied to the role in recent speculation, and that Pattinson has now been added to that list of talking points.

Its writer spots what they call a knowing nod to Bond's opening title sequence as Pattinson walks out of the vault, and says that, planned or not, it is the first visual cue linked to any of Amazon's rumoured candidates. They also point out that he will use an American accent in the film, while he has already shown he can play a posh Englishman on screen.

This is the writer's reading of a trailer, not an announcement. Amazon has not named its next Bond in anything Boss Hunting reports.
