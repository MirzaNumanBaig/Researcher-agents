**Quick summary**

- [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss), 59, told HELLO! that she and [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) are "dear friends".
- The interview was published on 31 October 2026 and centres on her new mystery thriller series, YAGA.
- Moss says she has no plans to chase a younger look and hopes to play women her own age.
- She turns 60 next August and calls her Matrix role as Trinity a highlight of her life.

Nearly three decades after The Matrix made them household names, Carrie-Anne Moss says her bond with co-star Keanu Reeves is still strong. Asked by HELLO! whether the pair remain close, the 59-year-old answered simply: "We're dear friends. He's such a lovely person."

## Why the friendship question came up

The comment came in an exclusive [HELLO! interview](https://www.hellomagazine.com/film/932060/carrie-anne-moss-exclusive-interview-yaga/) promoting YAGA, an eight-part mystery thriller. Moss, who plays Trinity opposite Reeves in the Matrix films, has been a steady screen presence for almost 30 years. HELLO! notes that her breakout came alongside Reeves, and that she then appeared in four major films in 2000 alone, including Memento and Chocolat.

Reeves is not part of YAGA. His mention is a short but warm moment in a longer conversation about Moss's career and how she sees herself at this stage of it.

## What Moss says about Trinity

Moss told the magazine the role "gave me my career" and shaped who she became, because of the fears she had to overcome. She listed riding a motorcycle with an actor on the back without a helmet, jumping off a 500-foot building 22 times, and shooting fight scenes over and over until she felt she had no energy left. "It still humbles me that they decided I was the woman to play her," she said.

## YAGA and ageing naturally

YAGA follows a private investigator looking into the disappearance of a wealthy college student in a coastal town. According to HELLO!, it was created by Canadian writer [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) and adapted from her 2019 play, giving the Baba Yaga folk tale a darker, more sensual spin. Moss plays Katherine, a manipulative and confident university professor with an appetite for younger men. She spent about three weeks filming in Victoria, British Columbia.

Moss was candid about the pressure placed on women in the industry. She said she feels freer now that she no longer has to be perfect, and added, "I'm not looking to look 30." She did not rule out changing her mind about cosmetic work one day, but said she loves real faces that move and expects audiences to want real people as AI and filters spread.

Her screen history, the magazine points out, is full of formidable women, from the defence attorney in [Jessica Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Jones) to a hardened survivalist in the zombie drama [Die Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Alone).

## What happens next
