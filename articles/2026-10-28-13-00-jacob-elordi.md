Jacob Elordi and Kendall Jenner turned heads at the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on October 17, and a short video from the night is still being discussed. [The Mirror US reports](https://www.themirror.com/entertainment/expert-says-jacob-elordi-possessive-2086811) that the pair arrived separately but were later seen sitting close inside the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures. The outlet describes it as the first time they have posed together at an event.

## What happened at the gala

According to The Mirror US, Elordi filmed Jenner as she walked the red carpet alone. In another moment he placed a hand on her lower waist as they walked. Jenner wore a custom polka dot Valentino gown. Elordi wore a black suit, a white shirt and sunglasses.

Inside, the outlet says one photo shows Jenner leaning against Elordi while he had an arm around her waist. She rested her hand over his, and he appeared to smirk for the camera.

## The body language reading

Celebrity body language expert Inbaal Honigman reviewed the footage for The Mirror US. She said Elordi looked nervous and "even a little possessive", and described the balance as favouring Jenner. She also said Jenner took his hand off her when he rested it on her lower back.

This is an interpretation of a few seconds of video by someone who has not spoken to either of them. It should be read as commentary. Neither Elordi nor Jenner has said anything about how the moment felt, and the same clip could be read many other ways.

## How the pair got here

The Mirror US says the two have kept their rumoured romance fairly private since speculation began in the spring of 2026. Jenner is 30 and Elordi is 29, per the report.

Earlier public moments, as the outlet describes them:

- On September 26, Elordi sat in the front row at the Bottega Veneta show during Milan Fashion Week, where Jenner walked the runway. Footage shared on X showed him photographing her with a digital camera and whistling in support.
- Days later in Paris, a video posted on September 28 shows Elordi asking photographers for space so they could walk back to their hotel.
