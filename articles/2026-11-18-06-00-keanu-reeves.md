**Quick summary**

- Director Francis Lawrence says the sequel to the 2005 film "Constantine" has an idea and a script, and he is more hopeful after leadership changes at DC Studios.
- [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) and [Peter Safran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Safran) have left their posts as heads of the studio, according to SlashFilm.
- Lawrence told Collider that the previous DC leadership was "an impediment" to making the film.
- "Constantine 2" has no release date yet.

The long-stalled Constantine 2 Keanu Reeves sequel may have a better chance of being made. Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) told Collider this week that the departure of [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) and [Peter Safran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Safran) from DC Studios gives the project "renewed hope", as reported by [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2319298/keanu-reeves-constantine-sequel-update-james-gunn-dc-universe-exit/). [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves), who played the title role in 2005, has been pushing for a follow-up for years.

## What Francis Lawrence said about Constantine 2 and Keanu Reeves

According to SlashFilm, Lawrence told Collider that he does not want anyone to lose their job. He still said the changes at DC Studios are ultimately good news for his film.

He said the team has an idea and a script, and described the people who were at DC as "an impediment" to making it. He added that the group has been trying to make the film for "20-something years", and that the obstacles were never Reeves, producer Akiva Goldsman or himself. J.J. Abrams is also involved, Lawrence said, and everyone wants to do it.

The next step, in his view, is persuading the people at Paramount, Warner Bros. and whoever now runs DC to agree to a sequel to "our version" of the story.

## A separate Constantine film at DC

The comments come soon after word that DC Studios was developing a new John Constantine movie. SlashFilm notes that report arrived before the news about Gunn and Safran leaving.

Lawrence does not see the two as rivals. He said he does not mind if someone has a plan for a shared universe and another version of the character. If they let his team make the sequel to the 2005 film, he said, the two can coexist.

## Why the sequel has taken so long

The sequel was reported to be officially happening in 2022, with Lawrence returning, but updates since then have been few, SlashFilm says. The site adds that Reeves kept asking Warner Bros. about "Constantine 2" until the studio agreed. That happened before Gunn and Safran were put in charge to launch their DC Universe, which complicated matters.

The 2005 film was not a clear hit on release, according to SlashFilm, but it has since become a fan favourite. The site's writer argues a legacy sequel would have mixed box office prospects, though it could work at the right price. Whether the new leadership at Skydance, the new parent company of Warner Bros., sees it as a sound investment is still open.

Lawrence has overseen the "[Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games)" films for years. SlashFilm also describes Reeves as a bigger star than he has ever been. Both points are the site's view, not a studio statement.

## What happens next

Nothing has been confirmed. No studio has announced approval, a start date or a release date for "Constantine 2". The film now depends on who takes charge at DC and how they see the character's future.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Constantine 2 officially happening?

Not yet. It was reported as official in 2022 with Francis Lawrence returning, but no studio has set a start or release date, according to SlashFilm.

### Is Keanu Reeves returning for Constantine 2?

Lawrence named Reeves as one of the people who want to make the film, saying the obstacles have never been him. Nothing further has been announced.

He told Collider that the people previously at DC were an impediment to the sequel. He said the change gives the team more hope of getting it made.
