**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) and [Hannah Waddingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Waddingham) sang "Islands in the Stream" at the Thundergong! charity concert in Kansas City.
- It was the 10th annual Thundergong!, held over the weekend before 16 November 2026.
- Whiskey Riff reports the 2026 event raised US$1.4 million.
- The money supports the Steps Of Faith Foundation, which Sudeikis founded with his friend Billy Brimblecom.

Jason Sudeikis turned the 10th Thundergong! into a tribute night, singing "Islands in the Stream" with his *Ted Lasso* co-star Hannah Waddingham in Kansas City. According to [Whiskey Riff](https://www.whiskeyriff.com/2026/11/16/ted-lasso-stars-jason-sudeikis-hannah-waddingham-perform-the-dolly-parton-kenny-rogers-classic-islands-in-the-stream/), the evening raised US$1.4 million for the Steps Of Faith Foundation. Here is what happened on stage and what the money is for.

## A duet for Dolly and Kenny

The performance was billed as a tribute to [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton) and [Kenny Rogers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenny_Rogers), the pair behind the original 1983 recording. A caption shared with the video says Sudeikis and Waddingham were backed by the band Summer Breeze.

Whiskey Riff describes how it unfolded. Sudeikis began alone at the microphone. When the song's familiar rhythm started, the Kansas City crowd realised a second singer was coming. Waddingham walked out when the song reached Parton's part, to loud applause.

This was not their first duet. The outlet notes the two sang "Shallow" together at an earlier Thundergong!, so the audience was hoping she would appear.

## A habit of singing with friends

Sudeikis has made the concert a showcase for his famous friends. Whiskey Riff says he has previously sung with [Will Forte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Forte) and with [Brendan Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Hunt), who co-created [Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso) and plays Coach Beard.

Waddingham also stars in that series alongside Sudeikis.

## Where the money goes

Thundergong! is a charity concert. Whiskey Riff reports that Sudeikis has gathered celebrity friends for a decade to raise money for the Steps Of Faith Foundation. He founded it with his longtime friend Billy Brimblecom. The foundation provides prosthetic care, hope and comfort to amputees who need financial help.

The 2026 total of US$1.4 million comes from Whiskey Riff's report. We could not find a figure for earlier years in the sources we read, so there is no year-on-year comparison here.

## The song's history

"Islands in the Stream" first came out in 1983 and became one of the best-known hits of both Rogers and Parton. Whiskey Riff says it was the first time the two country stars teamed up on a song. It reached number one on Billboard's Hot Country Songs chart, staying for two weeks. It also crossed onto the all-genre Hot 100, where Whiskey Riff says it spent two weeks at its peak position. Parton and Rogers last shared the stage for it in 2017, according to the same report.
