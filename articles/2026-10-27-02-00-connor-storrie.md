**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) returns as Ilya Rozanov in season two of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), alongside [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams).
- [Bell Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Media) says the new season is scheduled for spring 2027.
- Seven more cast members were announced on 13 and 25 August 2026, on top of two earlier additions.
- Season two draws mainly on The Long Game, the sixth novel in Rachel Reid's Game Changers series.

Connor Storrie will play Ilya Rozanov again when Heated Rivalry returns in spring 2027, according to a cast round-up from The Times of India that draws on Bell Media's official announcements. Storrie and Williams anchor a season that adds new couples and a long list of new faces to the hockey romance.

## Storrie and Williams stay at the centre

Storrie plays [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov) opposite Williams as [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander). The Times of India describes them as professional hockey players whose rivalry turns into a complicated relationship that has to coexist with their sporting careers.

Season one followed that connection from a secret romance between young players to something deeper, shaped by ambition and uncertainty. Season two, the outlet reports, takes most of its material from The Long Game, the sixth book in Reid's series. It looks at what happens when a relationship kept private becomes harder to separate from public, professional life.

Both leads are confirmed to return, the outlet says, citing the official announcements.

## A bigger ensemble

The new season widens the frame beyond Shane and Ilya. According to The Times of India, [Justice Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_Smith) joins as Harris Drover and [Charlie Gillespie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Gillespie) as Troy Barrett. Both characters come from Role Model, the fifth Game Changers novel. Harris is a social media manager for a hockey team, and Troy is a professional player. Crave announced that casting.

On 13 August 2026, Bell Media named five more actors:

- [Emily Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Hampshire) as Vanessa. She is known for [Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek).
- [Edvin Ryding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edvin_Ryding) as Luca Haas. He came to wider attention in [Young Royals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Royals).
- [Robert Naylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Naylor_(actor)) as Wyatt Hayes, whose credits include Cardinal.
- [Sabrina Jalees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabrina_Jalees) as Farah Jalali. Her credits include [Black Mirror](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Mirror).
- Priyanka as Tarek, known for drag performance and television.

Twelve days later, on 25 August, [Daniel Diemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Diemer) was announced as Ryan Price and [Shaheen Jafargholi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheen_Jafargholi) as Fabian Salah. Their characters are tied to Tough Guy, the third novel in the series. That means the season now draws on at least three of Reid's books, not one.

## What the producers have said

In the official announcement, series creator Jacob Tierney and executive producer Brendan Brady described the two August additions as bold and complicated characters, and said finding actors who could play their different sides mattered to the production, according to The Times of India.

Tierney writes, directs and executive produces. Brady and Jayme Alter Wilson are also executive producers, Reid stays on as consulting producer, and Mike Goldbach co-writes the new season. Crave commissioned the show, with participation from the Canada Media Fund and the Bell Fund.
