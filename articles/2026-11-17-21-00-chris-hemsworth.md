**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) will walk the green carpet and join a Q&A at an *Avengers: Doomsday* special event on December 4, 2026.
- The event is at EVENT Cinemas Pacific Fair on the Gold Coast, where invited guests get an early look at the film.
- *Avengers: Doomsday* opens in Australian and New Zealand cinemas on December 17.
- Separately, Netflix's *Extraction 3* has started filming on the NSW South Coast and in Sydney.

Chris Hemsworth is coming home to promote *Avengers: Doomsday*. Marvel Australia and New Zealand has announced that the Melbourne-born star will appear at a special event on the Gold Coast on December 4, 2026, [according to FilmInk](https://www.filmink.com.au/public-notice/chris-hemsworth-to-make-a-thunderous-arrival-at-avengers-doomsday-gold-coast-special-event-on-december-4/). He will walk the green carpet and take part in a Q&A before the screening.

## What the Gold Coast event involves

The night is being held at EVENT Cinemas Pacific Fair. FilmInk reports that invited guests will get an exclusive first look at the film after the carpet and the Q&A. Details on how guests were chosen have not been published.

Variety Australia, which [also reported the appearance](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/chris-hemsworth-avengers-doomsday-premiere-australia-42658/), says Disney confirmed the news on Tuesday. It describes the evening as the Gold Coast premiere. The film's wider promotional tour is due to stop in Las Vegas, Sao Paulo, Rome, London, Seoul, Shanghai, Toronto and Los Angeles, according to the same report.

## Why the film is already a big deal locally

Australian audiences have shown early appetite. Variety reports, citing Disney, that presales passed $1 million in their first 24 hours when tickets opened in September. Disney says that was the biggest opening day of presales for any film in Australia this year and the best 24-hour result in five years.

The timing helped. Ticket sales opened alongside a re-release of *Avengers: Endgame*, titled *Avengers Endgame: Encore*, which debuted at No. 1 at the Australian box office and carried a preview of *Doomsday*.

## The film and its cast

Anthony and Joe Russo, who directed *Avengers: Infinity War* and *Endgame*, are back in the director's chair. Kevin Feige, Louis D'Esposito and Jonathan Schwartz produce. The story puts heroes from three different universes on a collision course with what FilmInk calls an existential threat.

The ensemble is large. It includes [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd), [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) and [Vanessa Kirby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanessa_Kirby), alongside [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum), [Simu Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simu_Liu), [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) and [Hayley Atwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayley_Atwell). The film will screen in INFINITY VISION, a new certification for premium large format cinemas.

## Extraction 3 starts filming in New South Wales

Hemsworth's other big news this week is on the production side. Netflix's *Extraction 3* began shooting on the NSW South Coast, and it will also film in Sydney over the next few months, [FilmInk reports](https://www.filmink.com.au/public-notice/chris-hemsworth-idris-elba-and-golshifteh-farahani-return-for-another-mission-as-netflixs-extraction-3-begins-production-in-new-south-wales/). Hemsworth returns with [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba) and Golshifteh Farahani.

The production is expected to create 450 local jobs and add an estimated $127 million to the state's screen industry and wider economy. Sam Hargrave, who directed the first two films, is back, working from a script by David Weil. Hemsworth is also a producer through his company Wild State, with Benjamin Grayson.

Hargrave said the team wants to push the boundaries of action filmmaking and called the film "our biggest swing yet". NSW Arts Minister John Graham welcomed the cast and crew and said it was good to see Netflix continuing screen production in the state. The production is backed by the federal Location Offset and the Made in NSW Fund through Screen NSW.

## What happens next

The Gold Coast event on December 4 comes thirteen days before *Avengers: Doomsday* reaches Australian and New Zealand cinemas on December 17. *Extraction 3* is in the early weeks of production, and no release date has been given in the sources we read.
