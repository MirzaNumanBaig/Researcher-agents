**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) and [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) wore a matching Halloween costume pair on 31 October 2026.
- Storrie dressed as Michelle Fuller, the role [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone) plays in *Bugonia*; Williams went as Teddy Gatz, played by [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons).
- Both actors are currently filming season two of [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), according to Just Jared.
- Storrie has also been linked with a major role in an upcoming Marvel film.

## Two co-stars, one costume idea

Connor Storrie and Hudson Williams gave their Halloween a film-buff twist this year, turning up as the two leads of *Bugonia*. [Just Jared reported](https://www.justjared.com/2026/10/31/connor-storrie-hudson-williams-dress-up-as-emma-stone-jesse-plemons-in-bugonia-for-halloween/) that Storrie, 26, shared photos of himself as Stone's character Michelle Fuller, while Williams, 25, took on Plemons' character Teddy Gatz.

The pair are in the middle of shooting the second season of *Heated Rivalry*, the series that made both of them recognisable names. Just Jared said the show's set supervisor, Devan McHarg, also posted a photo of the two in costume on her Instagram story, which suggests the outfits were worn on set.

## Why Bugonia makes a good choice

*Bugonia* reached cinemas on 24 October 2025, so the costume lands almost exactly a year after its release. Just Jared lists four Oscar nominations for the film: Best Picture, Best Actress for Stone, Best Adapted Screenplay for [Will Tracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Tracy), and Best Original Score for [Jerskin Fendrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerskin_Fendrix).

The story follows two young men obsessed with conspiracy theories who kidnap the powerful chief executive of a major company, convinced she is an alien set on destroying Earth. That makes the pairing easy to dress up as: one character in a sharp corporate look, the other as her kidnapper.

## Other Heated Rivalry Halloween looks

Storrie and Williams were not the only cast members to join in. Just Jared noted that their co-star [Francois Arnaud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fran%C3%A7ois_Arnaud_(actor)) also dressed as another character from the show. The outlet did not describe that costume in detail in the piece we reviewed.

## What else is on their plates

Beyond the show, Just Jared says it was recently announced that Storrie is looking to join the Marvel Cinematic Universe in a major role in an upcoming film. We covered an earlier report on that, [Connor Storrie in talks for villain role in Ghost Rider](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-in-talks-for-villain-role-in-ghost-rider/). Treat it as an in-talks report until a studio confirms it.

Williams, meanwhile, can currently be seen in the Crave series *YAGA*, which also streams on AMC+ in the United States, according to the same report.
