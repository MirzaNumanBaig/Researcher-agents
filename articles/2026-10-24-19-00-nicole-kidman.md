**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) is on next month's cover of Vogue Australia, her twelfth, according to The Nightly.
- Almost two dozen friends and colleagues put questions to her, including Hugh Jackman, Kylie Minogue and Margot Robbie.
- She is 59 and reflected on a 45-year acting career.
- The role she says she always wanted: a McDonald's commercial.

Nicole Kidman has answered questions from almost two dozen A-listers for her next Vogue Australia cover story, covering motherhood, fashion and a 45-year career, [The Nightly reported](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/nicole-kidman-answers-questions-from-hugh-jackman-kylie-minogue-margot-robbie-in-vogue-australia-interview-c-23066037) on 24 October. It is the Oscar-nominated actress's twelfth cover for the magazine.

## Who asked the questions

The list is long. According to The Nightly, it includes [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), [Catherine Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Martin_(director)), [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Laura Dern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Dern), [Naomi Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Watts), [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman), [Michelle Pfeiffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Pfeiffer), [Joel Edgerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Edgerton), [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan), [Bruna Papandrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruna_Papandrea) and author [Liane Moriarty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liane_Moriarty).

Some questions were light. [Harris Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_Dickinson) and Alexander Skarsgård asked about hosting Christmas and buying a washing machine and dryer. Others were warmer and more personal.

## Early career and being too tall

Minogue asked about Kidman's breakout role in the 1983 Australian film BMX Bandits. Kidman called her "golden", said she has "enormous love" for her, and remembered thinking how "hardcore" it was to do one of her own stunts. She later told Pfeiffer it was one of the roles where she had the most fun.

Luhrmann asked about obstacles in her youth, and Kidman pointed to her height. "I was the tallest in the class, taller than all the boys," she said, adding that she couldn't become a ballet dancer because she was too tall. Even as an actress she was told she was taller than the leading man. That, she said, is what gave her fire.

## Phones, fashion and her daughters

A Big Little Lies co-star said Kidman is "terrible" on her phone, and she agreed. "I can be late with texting, I don't check my emails all the time; I'm just not that person," said the Practical Magic star. She said she prefers to be "emotionally present" and to spend time in nature, though she admitted to having "doomscrolled" and said discipline with devices matters.

On clothes, Kidman said she shares with her daughters Sunday Rose, 18, and Faith, 15, as all three are the same size. "I don't buy for me, I buy for us," she said. She wears her daughters' jeans, her eldest "has the best boots", and both girls raid her vintage wardrobe, which was set up with help from Dolly Parton. Her view of fashion is Cinderella-like: you dress up, feel good, then it all comes off and you go back to real life.

Asked by Zoe Saldaña about balancing work and family, she said there is "no such thing as perfection". She told her daughters that if push came to shove, nothing is more important than them. They can call the set at any time, she said, and "that's love".

## The McDonald's role

Robbie asked for her favourite Australian television ad, and Kidman said the role she always wanted was in a McDonald's commercial. "When I was growing up, McDonald's was it," she said, mostly so she could eat a Quarter Pounder on camera over and over again.

Moriarty's question was more poignant: where would Kidman go if she could time travel to a childhood moment? She chose a January Christmas holiday with her family at Avoca on the NSW coast and at Barrington Tops, "when there was not a care in the world". She remembered a fake tree, tinsel and the smell of summer and gardenias.
