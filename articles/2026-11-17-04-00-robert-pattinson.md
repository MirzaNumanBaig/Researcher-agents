**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios has confirmed [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), replacing [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig).
- The news broke on Monday 16 November 2026 through the official 007 Instagram account, not a London press event.
- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who directed Pattinson in Dune: Part Three, said the actor has "the timeless soul of 007".
- Pattinson called the role "the honor of a lifetime" in his statement.

Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Amazon MGM Studios confirmed the casting on Monday, ending months of speculation about who would take over the role from Daniel Craig in Denis Villeneuve's upcoming film. The reveal came through a short video on Instagram, with Pattinson in a black dinner jacket and bow tie, his face half in shadow.

## How the announcement happened

When Craig was unveiled as 007 in October 2005, he arrived in a Royal Marines speedboat on the River Thames, in front of the world's media. This time there was no boat and no London press call. According to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/bond-announcement-amazon-tradition-robert-pattinson-1236949030/), the official 007 Instagram account spent early Monday deleting thousands of posts that dated back almost a decade, most of them clips from earlier films.

Just before 3 pm London time, the account posted a ten-second clip of typed text on a black screen reading "Identity Confirmed." Four minutes later came the video of Pattinson.

Variety reports that even last week many of Amazon's own publicists did not know the news was coming. The trade paper suggests the announcement was put together quickly as speculation peaked. It also notes that Pattinson and Villeneuve are about to start a press tour for Dune: Part Three, and that Pattinson may be campaigning for Oscars for both The Odyssey and Primetime. Finding a joint gap in their diaries for a London press conference before the end of the year looked unlikely.

## What Villeneuve and Pattinson said

In a statement quoted by [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/denis-villeneuve-robert-pattinson-james-bond-b3085895.html), Villeneuve first thanked the other actors who had been considered, then said: "There can only be one 007." He added that Pattinson "has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new."

Pattinson's own statement, reported by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/royal-family-embraces-robert-pattinson-james-bond-1236949128/), called Bond "one of the most legendary characters in cinema." He thanked Villeneuve and several Amazon MGM colleagues for their "belief in me" and said the role was a dream come true. The Independent lists his credits as The Batman, the Twilight films and The Odyssey.

## A long list of names, narrowed late

The casting follows a month of intense guessing. Variety's account of the field runs like this:

- Jacob Elordi was an early favourite, though later reports said he never screen-tested.
- Jack Lowden entered the conversation after his Slow Horses co-star Gary Oldman suggested Bond had already been chosen and said his "fingers are crossed" for Lowden.
- Callum Turner was a leading name for a long stretch and was reportedly in the final stages.
- Late in the process, it emerged that both Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult had auditioned.

Pattinson's name arrived only at the eleventh hour, according to Variety.

## A different kind of Bond era

Variety frames the choice as a break with habit. Under the Broccoli family, with Barbara Broccoli and Michael Wilson overseeing the films since GoldenEye in 1995, the role usually went to a lesser-known actor. The thinking, Variety says, was that no performer should outshine the character. Pattinson has already led major franchises, including Twilight and Batman.

Amazon paid US$8.5 billion for MGM in 2022, mainly for the Bond rights, and reportedly paid a further US$1 billion last year to take creative control from the Broccolis. Variety says the social-media reveal hints at a younger, more global approach, and adds that "it remains to be seen what else they plan to change."

The royal family also noticed. Roughly 90 minutes after the news broke, an official royal account posted: "Welcome to His Majesty's Secret Service, Mr Bond." As Variety points out, Pattinson will be the first Bond to serve His Majesty rather than Her Majesty, since Queen Elizabeth II was monarch for all 25 earlier films.

## What happens next

The sources give no title, start date for filming or release date for the new Bond film. Villeneuve said only that he "cannot wait to begin." In the short term, Pattinson and Villeneuve face the Dune: Part Three press tour, and Pattinson may be busy with awards campaigns for The Odyssey and Primetime.
