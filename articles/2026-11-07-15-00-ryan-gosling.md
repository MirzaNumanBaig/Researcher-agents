**Quick summary**

- Ryan Gosling's production company Open Invite and Warner Bros. are in early development on a live-action Flintstones film, according to Deadline as reported by Polygon.
- The story is said to centre on a grown-up Bamm-Bamm.
- Gosling and Open Invite partner Jessie Henderson are still looking for a writer.
- No cast, story or release date has been announced, and it is unclear whether Gosling will act in it.

The Ryan Gosling Flintstones movie is now a real project, though a very early one. [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s company Open Invite and Warner Bros. are developing a live-action reboot built around an adult Bamm-Bamm, according to a Deadline report relayed by [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/ryan-gosling-flintstones-bamm-bamm-movie/) on 7 November 2026.

## What is known about the Ryan Gosling Flintstones movie

Polygon reports that the film is at the earliest stage. Gosling and his Open Invite partner [Jessie Henderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessie_Henderson) are searching for a writer, so there is no script, no story outline and no named cast.

The one firm creative detail is the focus on a grown-up Bamm-Bamm. That is a shift from how the character has usually been shown, and the report does not say what the adult version of him would be doing.

## Who is Bamm-Bamm?

Bamm-Bamm first appeared as a baby in the original 1960s Hanna-Barbera cartoon, as the young son of Barney and [Betty Rubble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Rubble), Polygon notes. He was a teenager in the 1970s series The Pebbles and Bamm-Bamm Show, where he formed a band with his sister Pebbles. He also turned up in the 1994 live-action Flintstones film.

That history gives the writers plenty to work with. The character has already been a baby, a teen and a screen presence in a live-action feature, but not an adult lead.

## Will Gosling star?

Polygon says that is the open question. His involvement is confirmed as a producer through Open Invite, and nothing has been said about acting. The outlet points to his performance as Ken in Greta Gerwig's Barbie as a reason he would suit the role, while noting his schedule may make that difficult.

That schedule is full. Polygon lists his lead role in the science-fiction hit [Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary), a part in [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars): Starfighter in 2027, and his Marvel debut as Ghost Rider in 2028, reuniting with director [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy). You can follow [everything we publish about Ryan Gosling](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-gosling/), including [his Ghost Rider plans](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-ghost-rider-nicolas-cage/).

Polygon also raises a possible Barbie sequel, suggesting Warner Bros. would likely put Gosling's return to that film ahead of The Flintstones, since Barbie is the studio's highest-grossing release. That is the outlet's own view, not an announced plan.

## Open Invite's other work

Open Invite has another film coming. Polygon says the romantic drama Love of Your Life is due to premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival, starring [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Aaron Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Pierre_(actor)) and [Patrick Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Schwarzenegger), among others.

## What happens next

The next visible step is a writer being hired. Until then there is nothing to say about the plot, the cast or when cameras might roll. Whether Gosling takes the role himself will likely depend on how his Star Wars and Marvel commitments line up.
