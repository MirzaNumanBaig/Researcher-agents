**Quick summary**

- [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage), who played Ghost Rider in 2007 and 2012, has publicly backed [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) as the character's next screen version.
- Cage made the comments to The Hollywood Reporter at the premiere of his new film, Madden.
- Marvel announced Gosling's casting at Comic-Con in July, with Shawn Levy directing.
- The new Ghost Rider film is currently dated for 28 July 2028.

The actor who first put Ghost Rider on screen has given his successor a warm send-off. Nicolas Cage told The Hollywood Reporter this week that he is looking forward to seeing what Ryan Gosling does with the Marvel role, and that the two men stay in touch.

## What Nicolas Cage said about Ryan Gosling's Ghost Rider

Speaking at the premiere of Madden, Cage kept his remarks short and generous. "Ryan's going to do his thing with it and I'm looking forward to seeing it," he told [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/nicolas-cage-ryan-gosling-ghost-rider-20-years-1236748268/). He added that Gosling "knows how I feel about him as an actor", that they have had "nice conversations", and that he wished him luck "on his adventures".

[ComicBook.com](https://comicbook.com/movies/news/nicolas-cage-breaks-silence-on-ryan-goslings-ghost-rider-reboot/) described the comments as the first concrete answer to a question fans had been asking since the casting news broke. The site noted that the reaction was almost entirely encouraging.

## Where the Ghost Rider film stands

Cage played stuntman Johnny Blaze in 2007's Ghost Rider, a man who sells his soul and becomes a bounty hunter of demons. He returned for the 2012 sequel, Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance. That is close to two decades of distance from the character's last big-screen outing, which The Hollywood Reporter points to when framing his endorsement.

Marvel announced at Comic-Con in July that Gosling will lead a new film, directed by Shawn Levy. According to The Hollywood Reporter, Gosling told the crowd: "This is a character I've wanted to play for a very long time."

The film is dated for 28 July 2028. ComicBook.com reports that it will arrive after Avengers: Secret Wars, when the Marvel Cinematic Universe is said to be getting a soft reboot. That timing is part of why this version is being treated as a fresh start rather than a continuation of Cage's films.

## A family link to the original film

There is a small personal thread running through the story. The Hollywood Reporter notes that Gosling's wife, Eva Mendes, appeared alongside Cage in the original 2007 film. According to the same report, she has also backed her husband taking over the fiery role.

On casting, The Hollywood Reporter says Connor Storrie, the breakout star of Heated Rivalry, is in talks to play the film's villain. That is a report of talks, not a confirmed casting, and Marvel has not announced it. For more on that thread, see our earlier piece on the [Connor Storrie role rumour](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-ghost-rider-connor-storrie/).

## What Cage is doing now

Cage is currently playing the football figure John Madden in David O. Russell's Madden, which follows Madden and his relationship with Raiders owner Al Davis. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the film opens in limited theatres and starts streaming on Prime Video on 18 November.

You can find everything else we have published on the actor on our [Ryan Gosling tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-gosling/), and more film coverage in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## What happens next

Nothing further has been announced on a start of production or a first look at Gosling in costume. With the release date more than two years away, official casting news is the next thing to watch, particularly on the villain role.
