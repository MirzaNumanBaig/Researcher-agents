**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) told Entertainment Tonight her role in The Batman Part II gave her "a lot of existential angst".
- Her character has still not been officially named; fans and trade sites point to Gilda, the wife or fiancée of Harvey Dent.
- The sequel is set for release on 18 February 2028, according to SYFY.
- Her crime thriller Paper Tiger, with Adam Driver and Miles Teller, opens in US cinemas this weekend, according to Awards Radar.

Scarlett Johansson has spoken for the first time about her part in the long-delayed sequel to Matt Reeves' Batman film, and it sounds like a hard shoot. Asked by [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) which of her upcoming roles pushed her furthest from her comfort zone, she picked The Batman Part II over The Exorcist: Martyrs. The interview surfaced this week, just as her next film heads into cinemas.

## What Johansson said about The Batman Part II

"It gave me a lot of existential angst for some reason," she said, as quoted by [SYFY](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/scarlett-johansson-says-the-batman-part-2-role-gave-her-existential-angst). She added that the role was "very hard to figure out" and that she could not say whether the difficulty came from the part itself or from where she is in her life. "It was a tough role in a lot of ways," she said.

That is all she offered. Neither she nor the studio has named the character. [Nerdist](https://nerdist.com/article/scarlett-johansson-the-batman-part-ii-role-challenging/) reports that director [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves) has confirmed Johansson and Sebastian Stan will appear opposite each other.

## Who she might be playing

Both outlets note the widespread theory that Johansson is Gilda, wife of Gotham district attorney Harvey Dent, with Stan rumoured as Dent. Treat that as speculation until the studio says otherwise.

The theory has some comic-book logic. Nerdist points out that the character first appears in Detective Comics #66 and is best known from the 1997 miniseries Batman: The Long Halloween, where she is revealed as the Holiday Killer, murdering Gotham mob figures on holidays to win her husband's attention. SYFY suggests that living beside Dent's split identity as Two-Face could well unsettle anyone's sense of self, which may hint at why the part troubled her.

## A film with a long road

SYFY reports that The Batman Part II, written by Reeves and Mattson Tomlin, will reach cinemas on 18 February 2028, nearly six years after the first film. The site adds that Steven Soderbergh recently stepped in to oversee the final weeks of filming while Reeves attends to a private family matter. Robert Pattinson returns as Batman, with Jeffrey Wright, Colin Farrell, Jayme Lawson and Charles Dance among the cast listed.

Before that, Johansson appears in Mike Flanagan's The Exorcist: Martyrs, due on 12 March 2027.

## Paper Tiger opens this weekend

Closer to hand is Paper Tiger, a crime thriller from director James Gray. It stars Johansson alongside [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) and [Miles Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Teller). Awards Radar, which reviewed it at the New York Film Festival, calls the trio's work some of their best, singles out Driver as "best in show" and says Johansson is strongest in the third act. The site used the release to rank her top ten performances, with Lost in Translation and Under the Skin among her most acclaimed. That is one critic's opinion, but it shows how the film is being framed.

## Her thoughts on relationships

In a separate conversation with David Harbour for Interview magazine, covered by [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/us/articles/scarlett-johansson-actress-ive-had-relationships-with-people-outside-of-the-film-industry-and-what-i-discovered-is-that-they-had-no-idea-what-i-needed-to-do-my-job/), the 41-year-old said partners outside the industry often "had no idea what I needed to do my job". She also noted that actors "form these very intimate relationships at work". Grazia stresses she did not tie those remarks to any specific breakup.
