**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) plays Frankie Cooper in MobLand, and Season 2 has now ended.
- He told Collider that his pub scene with [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) in Episode 9 was one of his favourites in the show.
- Flynn says Frankie's "superpower" is unnerving people, not gunplay.
- The finale reunites him with [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren), who he worked with on the Christmas film Goodbye June.

Johnny Flynn has named a tense pub scene with Tom Hardy as one of the best moments of his time on MobLand. Speaking to Collider after the Season 2 finale, he described the chess-like duel between their characters and explained why the show's quietest scenes suit Frankie Cooper best. This article contains spoilers for Season 2.

## A pub scene built like a chess match

In Episode 9, Frankie sits down with Harry, played by Hardy. Flynn told [Collider](https://collider.com/mobland-season-2-finale-johnny-flynn-tom-hardy-helen-mirren/) that the pair play "chess" on a level that is thrilling for an actor. Both men are master players with different styles, he said, and each seems to recognise something of their own power and darker nature in the other.

He called them "yin and yang" of the same ruthless attitude, and suggested that this is why each sits at the top of his own organisation.

Flynn also gave a hint about the plot. Frankie appears to give things away too easily in the scene, but Flynn said the mistakes are tactical and deliberate. He needs Gina out of the way because he is running a larger plan with Kat McAllister, played by [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer). Holding that back, then revealing it later, was part of the fun, he said.

His verdict on his co-star was warm and a little teasing. Hardy, in Flynn's words, is a wonderful person, "very strange", talented and surprising.

## Frankie is not an action hero

Flynn was also asked about the scene between Frankie and Gina, played by [Teddie Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddie_Allen), set in the panic room that doubles as a playroom. He said Frankie is not an action hero. Although the character gets some time in shootouts, Flynn sees his strength as his strangeness and his ability to unsettle people.

He described something "vampiric" in Frankie. The character wants to draw information and power out of people and improve his position before he ever pulls a trigger. That, Flynn said, is why the scene with Gina feels so dark: the audience cannot tell what Frankie wants from her or what he will do. He praised Allen's performance and said he enjoyed working with her.

## The finale and Helen Mirren

According to Collider, the Season 2 finale leaves the Harrigan family on the run and branded as terrorists by police. They shelter in Harry's remote safe house. Kat sends Frankie to kill them, but he walks into an ambush that reveals Harry's long game and puts the family back on top.

Frankie survives, which surprised the interviewer. His last scene is opposite Mirren, who plays Maeve. Flynn and Mirren previously worked together on the Christmas movie Goodbye June, and Collider notes that the new setting let them find a different dynamic. Flynn joked that the last time he had seen her, he had been crying over her in a hospital bed as her dying son in that film.

The published interview text available to us cuts off at that point, so what Flynn said next about the scene is not reported here.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Johnny Flynn play in MobLand?

He plays Frankie Cooper, a gangster who works with Kat McAllister and sparks off Hardy's Harry in several long scenes.

### Which scene with Tom Hardy does Flynn like most?

He pointed to the Episode 9 pub scene, which he described as a chess game between two ruthless leaders.
