**Quick summary**

- The original John Wick, released in 2014, debuted in third place at the China box office for Oct. 30 to Nov. 1, per Artisan Gateway data cited by MovieWeb.
- No John Wick film played in mainland China theatres during the franchise's rise, MovieWeb reports.
- John Wick: Chapter 4 reached Chinese cinemas in March 2025, about two years after its global release.
- Lionsgate is expanding the series, with a spin-off already out, an animated prequel in development and a fifth main chapter in the works.

[Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves)' first John Wick film has opened in third place in Chinese cinemas, 12 years after it first reached audiences elsewhere. According to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/john-wick-international-box-office/), which cites Artisan Gateway figures for the weekend of Oct. 30 to Nov. 1, the result is the latest sign that the action series is finally finding a theatrical audience in the world's biggest moviegoing market.

## A franchise that skipped China

The 2014 original was passed over by some distributors, MovieWeb says, because it was a modestly budgeted revenge film from two first-time directors with a star coming off a quiet run. It still grossed about $86 million worldwide. Each sequel then roughly doubled the one before, and Chapter 4 passed $440 million, according to the same report.

Through all of that growth, the series was absent from mainland Chinese theatres. MovieWeb points to the country's reluctance to import violent R-rated films and, later, to Reeves being effectively shut out after a 2022 Tibet House benefit concert, which it says led Chinese streaming platforms to quietly remove most of his filmography. That account comes from MovieWeb alone, and this article has not independently confirmed it.

## How the weekend chart looked

The first break came in March 2025, when John Wick: Chapter 4 received a surprise Chinese release two years after its worldwide debut. MovieWeb reports it was shown without cuts, which it describes as unusual given that regulators required Disney to trim Deadpool & Wolverine.

This weekend, the original film entered a chart still led by the Sony and Constantin Resident Evil reboot. That film took RMB28.5 million (about $4.3 million) in its fourth straight weekend on top and has passed $70 million in China since opening during Golden Week in early October, per MovieWeb. It is the third biggest Hollywood release in the market this year, behind Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Odyssey.

The outlet is careful not to oversell the result. It says it would overstate things to call this a full Wick phenomenon in China, though it calls the symbolism worth noting.

## Why Lionsgate will care

Lionsgate has spent the past couple of years widening the John Wick world. The spin-off Ballerina, starring [Ana de Armas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Armas), arrived in 2025. An animated prequel in which Reeves would voice the character is in development, and a fifth main chapter is in the works, MovieWeb says.

The outlet argues that opening China to a series with a reported billion-plus in global grosses is found money, and could make future instalments easier to approve at their rising budgets. That is MovieWeb's analysis rather than a studio statement.
