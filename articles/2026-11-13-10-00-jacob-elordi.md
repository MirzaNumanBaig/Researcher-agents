## Jacob Elordi takes on Gary Gilmore

Jacob Elordi as Gary Gilmore is the casting news this week: he has signed on to star in and produce a new film version of *The Executioner's Song*, with the news first reported by Deadline and picked up by outlets including [Dark Horizons](https://www.darkhorizons.com/?p=195629) and [Creepy Catalog](https://creepycatalog.com/jacob-elordi-will-play-gary-gilmore-the-murderer-who-insisted-on-his-own-execution/) this week. The Australian actor will play Gary Gilmore, the man at the centre of Norman Mailer's book. The announcement adds a heavy dramatic project to a run of roles covered in our [Jacob Elordi archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/).

Dark Horizons describes Elordi as an Oscar nominee. Creepy Catalog says the film is his first hands-on producing job. He is producing alongside his manager Matt Vioral and Sherry Marsh, while Stuber and Nesbitt produce for United Artists.

## Who was Gary Gilmore?

Gilmore was convicted of killing two men in Utah. His case became a national media sensation because he insisted on being executed while lawyers and the legal system worked to keep him alive, according to Creepy Catalog and Dark Horizons. He was executed in 1977.

Deadline's description, as quoted by [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/jacob-elordi-the-executioners-song/amp/), notes that Gilmore was the first person executed in the United States after a four-year de facto moratorium on capital punishment. That context is why the case drew so much attention at the time.

## The book and the 1982 version

The new film draws on Mailer's 1979 book, which won the Pulitzer Prize. It has been adapted before. The 1982 version was directed by [Lawrence Schiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Schiller), with [Tommy Lee Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee_Jones) as Gilmore. Dark Horizons reports that Jones won an Emmy for the role and that Mailer wrote that screenplay.

Schiller is returning as an executive producer on the new project, a link between the two productions. United Artists operates under Amazon MGM Studios, according to JoBlo.
