**Quick summary**

- A new behind-the-scenes featurette for the live-action *Street Fighter* film shows Capcom game director Takayuki Nakayama visiting the set.
- Nakayama says the filmmakers are fans of the game and calls the result "the Street Fighter I know", according to JoBlo.
- He challenges wrestler Cody Rhodes, who plays Guile, to a round of Street Fighter 6, and the clip then cuts to new footage.
- The film is directed by Kitao Sakurai and, per Wikipedia, has grossed about US$100.8 million against an US$82 million budget.

The creative head of the video game behind *Street Fighter* has given the film his approval. In a new featurette, game director Takayuki Nakayama walks the set of Kitao Sakurai's live-action adaptation, meets the cast and crew, and says the production has captured the spirit of the series, according to [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/street-fighter-featurette-nakayama/).

## What Nakayama said on set

Nakayama told the filmmakers that the director and everyone else involved love Street Fighter and are fans "just like us", which he said gave him great comfort, as quoted by JoBlo. He added that the team had managed something very difficult in a smart way and described what he saw as "the Street Fighter I know".

That matters to fans because game adaptations often live or die on whether the people behind the original feel the film respects it. Here the person who directed Street Fighter 6 is publicly vouching for the production, though a featurette is promotional material and not an independent review.

## The match against Cody Rhodes

The lighter moment of the clip has Nakayama challenging Cody Rhodes, who plays Guile, to a game of Street Fighter 6. JoBlo reports that Rhodes loses, then asks for "one more round". The featurette then switches to a "Montage Mode" sequence of fight footage from the film, with several characters visible, including Cammy.

Rhodes has been a regular name in coverage of the film. Our earlier report covers [what he said about The Rock texting him after a Street Fighter meeting](https://moduscarry.com/cody-rhodes-street-fighter-rock-text/).

## The story and the cast

The official logline, quoted by JoBlo, sets the film in 1993. Estranged fighters Ryu, played by [Andrew Koji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Koji), and Ken Masters, played by [Noah Centineo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Centineo), are pulled back into combat when Chun-Li, played by [Callina Liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callina_Liang), recruits them for the next World Warrior Tournament. A deadly conspiracy behind the contest forces the two to face each other and their pasts.

The screenplay is by [Dalan Musson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalan_Musson). The wider cast includes [David Dastmalchian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Dastmalchian) as M. Bison, [Orville Peck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orville_Peck) as Vega, [Jason Momoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Momoa) as Blanka, [Roman Reigns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Reigns) as Akuma, [Vidyut Jammwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidyut_Jammwal) as Dhalsim, [Hirooki Goto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirooki_Goto) as E. Honda and [Mel Jarnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Jarnson) as Cammy. Curtis "50 Cent" Jackson plays Balrog.

## How the film has performed

According to [Wikipedia's entry for the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Fighter_(2026_film)), *Street Fighter* has earned about US$100.8 million worldwide on an US$82 million budget, a gross of roughly 1.23 times its cost. Critics have been cooler than the fans in the featurette: the page lists a 42% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 43 from 28 critics. The review consensus it quotes credits the film's affection for the games and its style, but faults its storytelling and humour.

For the box office picture in more detail, see our report on how [the film passed US$100m but fell short of profit](https://moduscarry.com/street-fighter-movie-box-office/). More coverage sits in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## What happens next

The featurette is part of the ongoing promotion around the film. Nothing in the sources announces a sequel or a streaming date, so any next step beyond the current run is unconfirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Takayuki Nakayama?

He is the game director of Street Fighter 6, the Capcom fighting game, and visited the film's set for the featurette, according to JoBlo.

### Who plays Guile in the Street Fighter film?

Wrestler Cody Rhodes plays Guile. In the featurette he plays Street Fighter 6 against Nakayama and loses.

### Who directed the live-action Street Fighter film?

Kitao Sakurai directs, from a screenplay by Dalan Musson.
