**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) stars opposite [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) in *The Take*, described by Netflix as an unconventional heist movie.
- The film reaches Netflix on 15 January, with a limited theatrical run, according to Yahoo Entertainment.
- [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles) directs from a script by [Simon Kinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Kinberg).
- Pattinson plays Noah, a man recruited to help Washington's master thief pull off a bank job.
- The project was once known as *Here Comes the Flood*.

Robert Pattinson and Denzel Washington share the screen for the first time in *The Take*, a Netflix heist thriller due on the streaming service on 15 January, according to a [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/denzel-washington-robert-pattinson-know-193356556.html) rundown published this week. For Pattinson fans, it is the next release in a very busy few years.

## What *The Take* is about

Netflix bills the film as an unconventional heist movie, per the Yahoo report. Its official logline centres on a bank guard, a teller and a "master thief" who end up in a deadly game of cons and double crosses.

Washington plays the master thief, named Christian Carter. Pattinson plays Noah, the boyfriend of Rose, a teller at a prominent Manhattan bank. [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones) plays Rose. Carter recruits Noah to help him carry out a high-stakes heist.

The film was previously titled *Here Comes the Flood*. The name changed before release, and the Yahoo piece uses *The Take* throughout.

## Who is behind the camera and in the cast

Fernando Meirelles, the Brazilian filmmaker behind *City of God* and *The Constant Gardener*, directs. Simon Kinberg, who wrote *Mr. & Mrs. Smith*, is the screenwriter.

The supporting cast includes [Danai Gurira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danai_Gurira), [Sean Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Harris), Moisés Arias and [Justin Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kirk). Wikipedia lists the same principal cast and gives the same 15 January 2027 date.

## Why Washington took the job

Washington told [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), as quoted by Yahoo, that he is now focused on "doing the best work with the best filmmakers". He added that what he does from here, and who he does it with, matters to him "both spiritually and artistically".

He has not appeared on the big screen this year. His most recent film, according to Yahoo, is Spike Lee's *Highest 2 Lowest*, which also stars ASAP Rocky.

## A big year for Pattinson

Yahoo notes that many observers expected *The Take* to land before the end of this year. That would have given Pattinson five releases in 2026. He has already appeared in *The Drama*, *The Odyssey* and *Primetime*, and *Dune: Part Three* is still to come in December.

The outlet describes all three of his 2026 releases so far as drawing widespread acclaim, and calls him a likely 2027 awards nominee. That is the publication's view, not an official nomination. It also mentions another Batman film on the horizon, without giving further detail.

On *Primetime*, Wikipedia records a Rotten Tomatoes score of 86% and quotes the BBC's Nicholas Barber calling Pattinson's performance extraordinary. Wikipedia is a secondary source, so treat those figures as indicative.

## What happens next

The Take arrives on Netflix on 15 January, and Yahoo says it will also play in select theatres. Dune: Part Three follows in December, so Pattinson will be on screens twice in about a month. No trailer is mentioned in the sources, and no budget has been reported.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does The Take come out?
It streams on Netflix on 15 January, with a limited theatrical release, according to Yahoo Entertainment.

### Who stars in The Take?
Denzel Washington, Robert Pattinson and Daisy Edgar-Jones lead, with Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk in supporting roles.

### Who directed The Take?
Fernando Meirelles directed it. Simon Kinberg wrote the script.

Yes. It was first known as *Here Comes the Flood*.
