**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) has dressed in references to the earlier Hunger Games films at stops across the press tour for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, according to Indy100.
- The 20-year-old plays Maysilee and Merrilee Donner in the prequel, which is set 24 years before the original story.
- Her looks nod to [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire) costumes worn by Katniss Everdeen and Effie Trinket, and to a 2011 Oscars dress worn by Jennifer Lawrence.
- The film opens in cinemas on 20 November.

Mckenna Grace has spent the weeks before the release of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* wearing the franchise. Indy100 [rounded up her press tour outfits](https://www.indy100.com/celebrities/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-dress-2678366521) on 11 November, and almost every one points back to a costume from the earlier films. With the film out on 20 November, this is how the tour has played out so far.

## Why the dressing matters to her

The actress told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) that she is a huge fan of [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) and that fashion has not always been easy for her. She described growing up with insecurity about her body, and said that playing with clothes this way feels healing, because it now brings fun instead of anxiety.

She also framed the references as a thank-you. "Everything is a reference lately," she said, calling it homage to the people who were part of the franchise before her. Her message was one of feeling overwhelmed and thankful, rather than calculated.

## The looks, one by one

Indy100 lists five outfits, each tied to a specific moment from *Catching Fire*, the second film in the series, or to its star.

**Paris, 6 October.** At an event at the Musee Rodin, she wore a vintage [Monique Lhuillier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monique_Lhuillier) gown inspired by the wedding dress worn by [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen). Indy100 notes the original was designed by Tex Saverio and featured a sculptural metal bodice and ruffles.

**Los Angeles, 17 October.** At the Academy Museum Gala she chose a custom red Mônot gown. It was inspired by the Calvin Klein dress [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) wore to the 2011 Academy Awards, which makes it the one look that references the star rather than the character.

**London, 28 October.** For a UK fan screening at HMV Westfield, she wore an Almée Couture gown that Indy100 says resembles the arena of the Second Quarter Quell in the film.

**Berlin, 2 November.** Taking the "Girl on Fire" line literally, she wore a custom Cong Tri design at the photocall, inspired by the burning dress from the *Catching Fire* chariot ride.

**Los Angeles, 9 November.** At the Los Angeles fan event she wore a custom Elisabetta Franchi gown. It pays tribute to the dress Katniss wore at President Snow's mansion party after the Victory Tour.

There was also a smaller moment on BBC's *The One Show*, where she wore a butterfly dress and butterfly hair clips as a nod to Effie Trinket's butterfly dress.

## What the pattern says

Taken together, the tour reads as a planned countdown rather than a set of coincidences. Four cities, two continents and one franchise reference at nearly every stop is a lot of coordination, and fans have noticed. Indy100 describes viewers as loving her approach. For a lead in a prequel, borrowing the visual language of the original films is also a quiet way of connecting the new story to the old one.
