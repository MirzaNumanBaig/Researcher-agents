[Jeremy Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Edwards), the British actor known for Hollyoaks and [Holby City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holby_City), has described an on-stage moment with [David Walliams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Walliams) as "sexually humiliated". The claim, published in The Daily Mail and reported by Deadline on 16 November 2026, adds a new account to the fallout surrounding the Little Britain co-creator. Walliams has denied all allegations made against him.

## What Jeremy Edwards says happened

According to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/david-walliams-jeremy-edwards-pulled-pants-on-stage-1237224907/), Edwards told The Daily Mail that Walliams pulled his trousers and underpants down while the two were filming a Comic Relief Does Little Britain Live DVD around 20 years ago. The Mail published images that it said showed Walliams, dressed as one of the Little Britain characters, doing this and then rolling on top of Edwards.

"I was sexually humiliated. It got a massive laugh," Edwards told the paper. He said the issue is consent. "I didn't consent to it," he said, adding that he would not have taken part had he known the moment would be seen by a large audience and sold on DVD.

Edwards said he believes copies of the DVD are still available and that the incident has "haunted" him for years. He also said Walliams never apologised. Deadline reported that it had not received a response from Walliams on this specific claim. The footage relates to the [Little Britain Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Britain_Live) stage show era.

## The wider fallout

Deadline describes the Edwards interview as part of a week of reckoning for the British entertainment industry. [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was sentenced to prison, and Married at First Sight UK was axed after rape allegations, according to the trade outlet. Deadline also noted that Brand appeared in a similar Little Britain charity sketch around the same time as the Edwards footage.

The BBC has taken all Little Britain seasons off iPlayer. Deadline also lists a separate story about UK stars backing an open letter calling for change in the industry.

## What Walliams has said

Deadline reports that police are now investigating Walliams, who is 55, over "non-recent serious sexual offences". It says these allegations are distinct from those in the Guardian and ITV News investigations, which examined his alleged behaviour towards young women and teenagers.

In a statement to ITV News, Walliams denied all allegations. "I am not a sexual predator… I have not broken the law," he said. "I am devastated that some of the women I met ten to twenty years ago have now expressed hurt or anger towards me." Deadline also reported that his lawyers, while trying to prevent publication, disclosed that he had made two attempts to take his own life because of the allegations.

Deadline said it had contacted Walliams' legal representatives for comment on the Guardian and ITV News claims. A police investigation is not a finding of guilt, and none of the allegations has been tested in court.
