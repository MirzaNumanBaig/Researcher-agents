**Quick summary**

- Sephora temporarily renamed its store at The Grove in Los Angeles "Sethphora" to tease a holiday campaign with Seth Rogen.
- The retailer announced the "We All Sephora" campaign on 26 October for the United States and Canada.
- The full advertising rollout begins on 29 October across television, streaming and social channels.
- It is a gifting campaign, not a Seth Rogen beauty line, and the gift picks will be on Sephora's website and app.

[Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) has had a shop sign changed in his honour. For the launch of its holiday push, Sephora turned its storefront at The Grove in Los Angeles into "Sethphora", and on 26 October it announced the actor as the face of its "We All Sephora" campaign in the US and Canada. So what is he actually selling?

## What is Sethphora?

Sethphora is a one-off storefront joke, not a new chain. According to [Totally The Bomb](https://totallythebomb.com/sephora-sethphora-seth-rogen-holiday-campaign/), the name change applied to the store at The Grove and is not a nationwide rebrand.

[ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/sephora-seth-rogen-holiday/) reports that Sephora described the takeover as a teaser for its partnership with the comedian. The retailer did not say how many people visited or interacted with the installation.

The sign did its job before a single ad aired: anyone who recognised the pun understood the casting without seeing the commercial. The ad itself is where the joke gets a plot.

## What happens in the ad?

Rogen plays a shopper who needs help, not a beauty expert. In the commercial he walks into Sephora with a list of people to buy for, and a Beauty Advisor guides him through the choices, according to both outlets.

Two of his purchases are named in the coverage: a setting spray for his producer and a fragrance for his cousin. ContentGrip says the comedy rests on the gap between his familiar screen persona and the detail-heavy job of choosing someone else's beauty products.

Director Jason Woliner worked on the spot, which was created with the agency GUT New York, ContentGrip reports. It adds that the advisor is built into the idea rather than sitting in the background.

Rogen is quoted in the brand's announcement as saying: "Holiday gifting can be stressful, so anything that makes it easier is a win in my book."

That quote hints at the point of the whole campaign, which is a gifting service rather than a celebrity product.

## A gifting campaign, not a Seth Rogen range

Totally The Bomb is explicit that this is not an announcement of a Seth Rogen makeup line. Sephora says shoppers can find the campaign's selected gifts on its website and app.

ContentGrip notes that the campaign has so far offered no evidence of sales lift, ad reach or gift-guide conversions. What is visible is the mechanism: take a common doubt about buying for someone else and turn it into a friendly encounter with a store advisor.

Sephora's approach also differs from last year. ContentGrip points out that in 2025 the retailer used singer [Mariah Carey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariah_Carey) and her seasonal "It's time!" ritual, which signalled the start of Christmas shopping. Rogen, by contrast, plays the person who still has gifts to buy once the season is under way.

## What happens next

The official advertising rollout starts on 29 October across connected television, linear television, streaming and social channels, according to ContentGrip. Sephora has also shared a campaign video on its official Facebook account.

The announcement covers the United States and Canada, and neither outlet mentions an Australian launch. Readers here may see the clips online first. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Sethphora a real Sephora store?

No. It is a temporary storefront takeover of the Sephora at The Grove in Los Angeles, used to tease the holiday campaign, according to Totally The Bomb.

### Is Seth Rogen launching a beauty line with Sephora?

No. The campaign is about holiday gifting, and Sephora says the selected gifts will be on its website and app.

### When does the Seth Rogen Sephora campaign start?

Sephora announced it on 26 October, and the full advertising rollout begins on 29 October in the US and Canada.

### Who directed the Seth Rogen Sephora ad?

Jason Woliner directed the commercial, which was created with GUT New York, according to ContentGrip.
