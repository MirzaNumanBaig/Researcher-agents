**Quick summary**

- Director Francis Lawrence says he wants Keanu Reeves Constantine 2 to be an R-rated film by design, according to a report on AOL.
- Lawrence says he is still developing the sequel with Keanu Reeves, writer Akiva Goldsman and producer J.J. Abrams.
- Reeves and Sandra Bullock announced an action rom-com in March 2025, and LaineyGossip reports no public update since.
- Far Out Magazine has revisited Reeves' role in Little Buddha, which opened 11 days before Speed.

[Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has two long-gestating projects still alive. The Keanu Reeves Constantine 2 sequel is being steered towards harder violence, while a reunion with Sandra Bullock is waiting on a finished script.

## Keanu Reeves Constantine 2 is aiming for an R rating

[Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence), who directed the 2005 original, spoke about the sequel on Kevin McCarthy's On Film podcast, as reported by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/keanu-reeves-constantine-2-could-123000000.html) (the piece originally ran on ComingSoon.net). He said the first film fought the [Motion Picture Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association) for a PG-13 rating and lost, even though his team followed the rules on violence, language, nudity and sexuality.

That experience shaped his plan for the follow-up. "If I knew we were going to get an R, I would've made an R-rated movie," Lawrence said. He also named Reeves, Goldsman and Abrams as the people he is still developing it with.

The report notes the original kept most of its violence tied to demons and angels, sparing human characters the grisliest fates. An R-rated sequel would let the film sit closer to the darker comic-book character. Reeves played occult detective John Constantine, with Rachel Weisz co-starring. AOL says the film drew mixed reviews and modest box office before a cult following grew.

## Why the sequel has taken so long

The studio confirmed Constantine 2 in 2022, with Reeves, Lawrence and Goldsman all returning. AOL reports that development has since hit roadblocks, including leadership changes at DC Studios and pitches that stalled under James Gunn and Peter Safran. A separate adaptation from Francis Galluppi has also entered development.

The sources give no release date or production start. We covered the earlier comments in our [report on the R-rated sequel](https://moduscarry.com/constantine-2-sequel-keanu-reeves/).

## The Sandra Bullock action rom-com is waiting on a script

The second project is a reunion with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock). According to [LaineyGossip](https://www.laineygossip.com/how-long-will-we-have-to-wait-to-see-sandra-bullock-keanu-reeves-in-their-action-rom-com/), the pair will star in and produce an action rom-com alongside Mark Gordon, who produced Speed. The site calls it a triple reunion.

The announcement came in March 2025, when the script was still being written. LaineyGossip says nothing has been announced since, and hopes cameras might roll in the new year. That is the outlet's hope rather than a confirmed schedule.

Bullock has just finished promoting [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic) 2, and the same report says she has no other project planned. It describes the long wait as a sign of how slowly development can move, even for two major stars.

## A look back at Little Buddha

Far Out Magazine has also revisited Reeves' casting as Prince Siddhartha in [Little Buddha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Buddha). Director [Bernardo Bertolucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernardo_Bertolucci) admitted he knew the choice was outrageous. "How can Keanu, the idol of American teenagers, be Buddha?" he said, adding that he was enchanted by what Reeves came up with.

According to [Far Out](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/1993-keanu-reeves-played-most-outrageous-role-called-it-best-thing-ever-did/), the film opened at number nine at the American box office and faded quickly. Reeves was unbothered. He valued working with Bertolucci and cinematographer Vittorio Storaro, and said his research introduced him to "a philosophy; a religion; a way of being that I hadn't experienced before."

The piece also notes the timing. Little Buddha was released on 25 May 1994, 11 days before Speed made Reeves an action star. Far Out says he balanced crowd-pleasers like [Point Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Break) with riskier work.

## What happens next

Neither project has a release date. For Constantine 2, the next signal would be a formal go-ahead from DC Studios or a casting and production announcement. For the Bullock film, the first sign would be a finished screenplay and a director. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Constantine 2 officially happening?

The studio confirmed the sequel in 2022 with Reeves, Lawrence and Goldsman returning. It has not announced a release date, and AOL reports development has been delayed by studio changes.

### Will Constantine 2 be R-rated?

Lawrence says he wants it to be. He told the On Film podcast that he would have made the first film R-rated had he known it would receive that rating.

### What is the Keanu Reeves and Sandra Bullock movie?

It is an action rom-com that both stars will lead and produce, with Mark Gordon. It was announced in March 2025, and LaineyGossip says there has been no update since.
