**Quick summary**

- Apple TV has confirmed Ted Lasso Season 5, less than two months after season four ended.
- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) will return as Ted and as an executive producer, according to Variety.
- Which other cast members come back has not been settled, and a writers' room is already open, The Hollywood Reporter says.
- The fourth season premiere recorded 296.6 million viewing minutes in two days, per Nielsen figures reported by Variety.

Apple TV has confirmed that [*Ted Lasso*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso) will return for Ted Lasso Season 5, with Jason Sudeikis back in the title role. The announcement landed on 24 November 2026, only weeks after the fourth season wrapped.

## What Apple TV has confirmed

According to a Variety report summarised by [UA.NEWS](https://ua.news/en/dovkolo-svitu/apple-tv-prodovzhiv-serial-ted-lasso-na-piatii-sezon-iz-dzheisonom-sudeikisom-variety), Sudeikis will again play the lead and serve as an executive producer. The [A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/ted-lasso-season-5-renewal) adds that he is also a co-creator of the series,.

The cast list for the new season is not locked. The Times of India, citing The Hollywood Reporter, says decisions on which ensemble members return are still being worked out, although a writers' room for season five has already opened.

## Why the renewal was an easy call

The numbers made it simple. Variety reports that the season four premiere, which arrived in August after a three-year break, drew 296.6 million viewing minutes on 4 and 5 August, according to Nielsen. Variety describes it as the biggest launch in Apple TV history, ahead of every earlier season of the show and every other series on the service.

The run held up. The Times of India reports that season four spent 12 weeks in the streaming chart's Top 10 and gathered about 9.8 billion minutes of viewing, which put it among the most-watched original shows of 2026.

The show also has the awards history to back it up. It won the Emmy for Outstanding Comedy Series for each of its first two seasons. Sudeikis said earlier on the Talk Easy With Sam Fragoso podcast that he would like to continue with a fifth and sixth season, building another three-season arc, according to UA.NEWS.

## Where season four left the story

Season four brought Ted back to London to coach AFC Richmond's new women's team, alongside returning faces including [Hannah Waddingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hannah_Waddingham), [Juno Temple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juno_Temple), [Brett Goldstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brett_Goldstein), [Brendan Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Hunt) and [Jeremy Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Swift). Newcomers included [Tanya Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Reynolds), [Jude Mack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Mack), [Faye Marsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faye_Marsay), [Rex Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rex_Hayes), [Aisling Sharkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aisling_Sharkey) and [Abbie Hern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbie_Hern).

In the finale, Ted stepped back from head coach to become an assistant manager next to Coach Beard, handing the top job to Alice Chilton, played by Reynolds. Roy Kent agreed to take over at Chelsea, and he and Keeley are expecting a child. Rebecca, the club's owner, proposed to her boyfriend Matthijs, according to UA.NEWS.

Not every reviewer was won over: the A.V. Club called the return uneven and the finale subpar. It still expects Alice to get more to do in season five, and sees a chance for the writers to correct course.

## Sudeikis on how the idea began

The renewal arrives as Sudeikis has been telling the origin story again. On the Smartless podcast, as quoted by [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/streaming-news/jason-sudeikis-olivia-wilde-complicated-split-credit-ted-lasso), he said [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde) encouraged him over dinner to do something with the character he and his collaborators enjoyed playing. He said the idea then sat untouched for about three years, until Bill Lawrence read the pages he had and saw a show in them.


## What happens next

No premiere date or episode count has been announced. The confirmed pieces are the renewal itself, Sudeikis's return and the open writers' room. The A.V. Club expects the new episodes to follow Roy and Keeley as parents and Rebecca's wedding, though that is the outlet's guess rather than a studio plan.
