**Quick summary**

- A new teaser for *Dune: Part Three* arrived on 24 November 2026, and social media reaction has centred on Robert Pattinson's character, Scytale.
- Scytale is an assassin who leads a rebellion using a Face Dancer's ability to copy faces and voices, according to Korean outlet Dispatch.
- Pattinson reportedly spent five hours in makeup for a look proposed by director Denis Villeneuve.
- India.com lists the worldwide release as 18 December 2026; Dispatch says Korea gets the film on 16 December.

A fresh teaser for *Dune: Part Three* has turned attention to the film's newest villain. [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s closing chapter of his Dune trilogy showed [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the shape-shifting assassin Scytale, and fans online spent the following day debating his voice, his pale look and what he means for Paul Atreides.

## Who is Scytale in Dune: Part Three?

The film adapts [Dune Messiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_Messiah), Frank Herbert's sequel novel. According to [India.com](https://www.india.com/entertainment/dune-part-3-teaser-trailer-twitter-review-robert-pattinson-timothee-chalamet-zendaya-impress-fans-netizens-call-it-absolute-work-of-art-check-reactions-8547816/), the story is set roughly 17 years after the events of *Dune: Part Two*, with Paul Atreides now emperor and facing political plots and the consequences of his rule.

Pattinson plays Scytale, described by [Dispatch](https://www.dipe.co.kr/2351249) as an assassin who uses a Face Dancer's power to replicate faces and voices. As forces gather against Paul, played by [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet), Scytale takes part in what the outlet calls a dangerous game. He hides both his appearance and his intentions, which is a large part of why viewers are curious about him.

Pattinson told the outlet it was interesting that the character is not a typical villain. That framing matters, because the film's wider setup is less about one clear enemy than about a ruler whose own decisions are catching up with him.

## The five-hour makeup and Villeneuve's verdict

The most striking detail is the transformation. Dispatch reports that Pattinson sat through five hours of makeup to reach the look Villeneuve proposed, a pale face with a sharp gaze that gives the assassin a cold presence on screen.

Villeneuve, according to the same report, was pleased with the result and said Pattinson portrayed the complex sides of Scytale well. The quote is relayed through a single Korean outlet, so it is worth treating as that outlet's account rather than a wide-circulating statement.

## How fans reacted to the teaser

India.com gathered reactions from X, and Pattinson drew most of the attention. Several viewers praised his voice modulation and the unusual approach he has taken to the character. Others pointed to the darker tone and large-scale visuals, with one user calling the footage "an absolute work of art".

There was also a debate about where the story is heading. One fan wrote that the first trailer made Paul look like a certain villain, while the second suggested he could become a tragic anti-hero. India.com notes that audiences are waiting to see the clash between Paul and Scytale, along with developments for Zendaya's Chani.

These are social media posts from individual fans, not audience research, so they show enthusiasm rather than any measure of how the film will be received.

## Cast and a crowded release window

India.com lists Chalamet, Zendaya, Pattinson, [Jason Momoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Momoa) and [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem) among the stars. The same report says the film opens on 18 December 2026, the same day as *Avengers: Doomsday*, and several fans referred to the head-to-head clash. One commenter predicted the Marvel film would earn more while *Dune* would be the better film, which is opinion, not a forecast based on figures.

Dispatch adds that Korean audiences will see *Dune: Part Three* on 16 December, ahead of the rest of the world. India.com says Indian previews begin on 16 December. Release dates for individual countries can shift, so check local cinema listings, including for Australia.
