**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) plays District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opens on 20 November.
- Her press tour has taken in Paris, Sydney, New York, London and Berlin, with stylist Molly Dickson behind the outfits, according to Page Six.
- Many looks copy earlier costumes from the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) films, including Katniss, Effie Trinket and Rue.
- Grace told Entertainment Tonight the theme is homage to everyone who worked on the franchise before her.

[Mckenna Grace](https://moduscarry.com/tag/mckenna-grace/) has turned the *Sunrise on the Reaping* press tour into a game of spot the reference. The 20-year-old, who plays Maysilee Donner in the film arriving on 20 November, has worn a franchise nod at nearly every stop, and Page Six has now catalogued the whole run.

## The tour at a glance

According to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/style/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-red-carpet-looks/), Grace has worked with stylist Molly Dickson through Paris, Sydney, New York and London, plus a Berlin premiere and a Los Angeles fan screening. The tour started at the Musée Rodin in Paris, at the opening of a costume exhibition on the style of Panem.

"Everything is a reference lately," Grace told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight). "It's just homage to all the people that were a part of this franchise before I was."

Her Paris dress was a white strapless Monique Lhuillier gown with three-dimensional detail on the bodice. Page Six says it was modelled on the wedding dress [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) wears in *Catching Fire*.

## The big premiere dresses

New York got the most literal one. Grace arrived as a mockingjay in a custom Pamella Roland creation with oversized wings, and our earlier report on the [New York premiere wings](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-premiere-wings/) has more on that night.

In Berlin she wore a custom Cong Tri gown with a halter cutout and a pleated skirt covered in red, yellow and black crystals. A vintage 1920s headpiece completed a take on Katniss's flaming chariot look from *Catching Fire*.

London brought a beaded Almée Couture gown picturing a pastoral scene, with 3D flowers across the skirt. "I'm just missing a cornucopia in here somewhere," she joked to Entertainment Tonight.

In Sydney she chose an asymmetrical ballet-pink House of Gilles gown with a matching bow, a floral hairpiece and a side braid that carried a quiet Katniss reference.

## The deeper cuts

Some of the choices were aimed at dedicated fans. On *The One Show* on the BBC, Grace wore an archival Alexander McQueen butterfly dress with monarch clips, which Page Six says echoes Effie Trinket's winged gown in *Catching Fire*.

For interviews she wore an archival Gucci gown from autumn/winter 2016 with rainbow ruffles, a nod to Rachel Zegler's Lucy Gray Baird in the 2023 prequel. On the *Today* show, an aqua Betsey Johnson minidress copied the puff-sleeved look [Amandla Stenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amandla_Stenberg) wore as Rue in 2012, down to the twin butterfly clips.

A Céline wood-grain minidress by [Phoebe Philo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Philo) looks puzzling until Dickson's Instagram explanation: "District 7. Lumber. Trees." Moss in Grace's braid made the point. A black Chloé dress with magenta feathers at the shoulders played on Katniss's feathered gown from the second film.

Two events were not tied to the franchise. At the Academy Museum Gala, Grace paid tribute to Jennifer Lawrence's red Calvin Klein gown from the 2011 Oscars with a Mônot halter dress. At the Newport Beach Film Festival Honors and Variety's 10 Actors to Watch event, she wore a Nicole + Felicia dress with silk butterfly appliqués, a motif Page Six says nods to a scene in the film.
