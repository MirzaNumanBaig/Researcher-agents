- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) attended the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards in November 2026.
- She wore a matte black Carolina Herrera dress with feathers at the neck.

- The look is a change for an actress who has often skipped make-up on red carpets.

Pamela Anderson turned heads at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards this month with an Old Hollywood-style look, trading her usual bare face for a soft berry-red lip. [Women.com](https://www.women.com/2308273/pamela-anderson-muted-red-lip-is-the-perfect-shade-for-older-women/) reported the details of the outfit and the make-up on 8 November.

## What Pamela Anderson wore to the CFDA Awards

According to Women.com, Anderson chose a matte black Carolina Herrera dress trimmed with feathers at the neckline. She added slingback heels and a polka-dotted clutch, and pinned her hair back in a bouffant of soft curls.

The outlet describes the overall effect as classic and glamorous. It is a deliberate contrast with the stripped-back approach Anderson has taken to many public appearances in recent years. Women.com notes she has made a habit of going make-up-free on the red carpet, which is why this polished version drew attention.

## The make-up: a muted berry red

Women.com says Anderson wore a touch of blush, some mascara and a glossy lip in a muted, berry-toned red. Under camera flash the colour read as pink-toned, the outlet adds, but most photos from the night show a deeper red.

The piece argues that softer reds are often recommended for mature skin. Its reasoning is that a true cherry red can draw attention to fine lines and to lipstick feathering, although it stresses that anyone who feels best in a bold red should wear it at any age. Anderson's shade sits between the two: it keeps the drama of a red lip without the starkness.

Note that the exact products Anderson used have not been confirmed. Women.com says it does not know which lip colour or gloss she wore.

## Why the glossy finish matters

The second point in the report is about texture rather than colour. Women.com quotes make-up artist Caroline Hernandez, who told the outlet that women who have always worn very matte lipsticks may find them emphasising lines or leaving lips dry as they age. She suggests satin, cream or balmy formulas instead.

By that measure, Anderson's gloss did two jobs. It gave the lips a fuller, more hydrated look, and it softened the effect of the red. This is the outlet's interpretation of a styling choice, not a comment from Anderson or her team.
