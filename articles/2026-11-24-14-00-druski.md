**Quick summary**

- The City of Chicago announced on Monday that [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) will host its New Year's Eve celebration at Navy Pier.
- The event is part of ABC's "Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with Ryan Seacrest 2027", made with [Dick Clark Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Clark_Productions).
- A fireworks and drone show is planned for midnight, with fireworks launched from bridges along the Chicago Riverwalk.
- Free tickets, up to two per person, go on release at 10 a.m. Wednesday and again at 10 a.m. on Dec. 2.

Comedian and actor Druski will host Chicago's New Year's Eve celebration at Navy Pier, the city confirmed on Monday, putting the creator-turned-television-host at the centre of a live ABC broadcast as 2027 begins. According to [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/comedian-druski-host-chicago-years-022028790.html), the ticketed party in Crystal Gardens will also feed the city's segment of the network's annual countdown special.

## What Chicago has announced

The live televised celebration forms part of "Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with Ryan Seacrest 2027" and includes a countdown moment at midnight Central time. This year it moves to a new backdrop: Navy Pier.

Mayor Brandon Johnson said the night is a chance to share Chicago's music, culture and talent with audiences across the country while bringing communities together at home. Kenya K. Merritt, commissioner of the Chicago Department of Cultural Affairs and Special Events, said the city wants celebrations that are accessible to residents and visitors alike, from Navy Pier to the Chicago Riverwalk.

Local broadcaster [ABC7 Chicago](https://abc7chicago.com/videoClip/20072438/) also carried the news about Druski's role, though its page offered little beyond the headline.

## The night at Navy Pier

Doors open at 6 p.m., and live music from local Chicago artists runs across the Pier from the same hour. The ticketed celebration in Crystal Gardens starts at 7 p.m.

The announced performers are DJ Mike P, Opal Staples and Isaiah Sharkey, the 911 Mambo Orchestra, and Grammy-nominated producer and songwriter Steve "Silk" Hurley. Druski is billed as host of the ABC special.

At midnight the city plans a fireworks and drone show. The fireworks will be launched from bridges spanning the Riverwalk between Franklin Street and Columbus Drive, so viewers along the river should have a clear line of sight.

## How to get tickets

Up to two free tickets will be available per person. Two release windows are listed: 10 a.m. Wednesday and 10 a.m. on Dec. 2. Guests at the ticketed celebration must be 18 or older unless a parent or legal guardian comes with them. The city points people to chicago.gov for details.

## Why this is a notable booking

The announcement follows a busy autumn for Druski. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/snl-season-52-hosts-musical-guests-list-1236695508/) reported in its season 52 schedule that he was set to make his debut hosting [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) on Nov. 7, with The Strokes as musical guest. The outlet described him as the first person to move from the online creator world to the SNL host's chair, and noted earlier TV, film and commercial roles plus a turn hosting the BET Awards in June.

## What happens next

Tickets open on Wednesday, so the first release is the one to watch. The city has not yet said whether more musical guests will be added to the broadcast, and the sources do not list the ABC air time beyond the midnight Central countdown. Details are on chicago.gov.

### Who is hosting Chicago's New Year's Eve celebration?

### Where will the fireworks be launched from?

Fireworks will go up from bridges across the Chicago Riverwalk, from Franklin Street to Columbus Drive, alongside a drone show at midnight.
