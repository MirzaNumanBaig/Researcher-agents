**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) drove a limousine carrying race winner Max Verstappen and McLaren's Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri after the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, according to Sports Tiger.
- Formula 1 shared video of the ride, which took in a lap of the Strip circuit and a trip towards the post-race interviews.
- Verstappen won by 0.639 seconds from Norris, with Piastri a further 1.529 seconds back.
- Separately, a Startefacts feature revisits why Reeves passed on the *Speed* sequel in the 1990s.

Keanu Reeves has played a hitman, a hacker and an alien, but on race night in Las Vegas the 62-year-old took on a new part: limousine driver for the Formula 1 podium. Sports Tiger reports that the actor drove Max Verstappen, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri along the Strip once the chequered flag had fallen.

## What happened after the Las Vegas Grand Prix

According to [Sports Tiger](https://www.sportstiger.com/other-news/keanu-reeves-takes-the-wheel-for-max-verstappen-lando-norris-and-oscar-piastri-after-las-vegas-gp/), a video shared by Formula 1 shows Reeves behind the wheel of a limousine with the three podium finishers on board. The drivers wave to fans as the car moves through the lit-up streets. The outlet adds that Reeves took the trio on a post-race lap of the Las Vegas Strip Circuit and was also pictured driving them towards their interviews.

It is a very Las Vegas ending to a race that already had plenty of drama. We have also covered [the actor's earlier paddock appearance](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-las-vegas/) and [a separate account of the podium ride](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-las-vegas-grand-prix-chauffeur/).

## The race behind the ride

Verstappen started from pole and held off repeated challenges to take his third Las Vegas Grand Prix win, Sports Tiger reports. He finished 0.639 seconds clear of Norris, with Piastri another 1.529 seconds behind in third. The race featured 35 lead changes, many of them between Verstappen and the Mercedes drivers. Mercedes faded late, George Russell hit the barriers, and Kimi Antonelli dropped behind both McLarens, which handed Norris and Piastri their podium places.

## Why Reeves fits in the paddock

The cameo was not out of character. Sports Tiger notes that Reeves hosted and executive-produced the documentary series *Brawn: The Impossible Formula 1 Story*, which looks back at Brawn GP's 2009 championship-winning season. He has raced himself, too, making his professional debut in the Toyota GR Cup at Indianapolis in 2024. The outlet says he was one of several celebrity names in the Las Vegas paddock over the weekend.

## The sequel he turned down

A separate Startefacts feature this week looks back at a different choice. *Speed* made Reeves an action star in 1994 and took about US$350 million worldwide, according to [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/why-keanu-reeves-turned-down-a-sequel-and-fox-didnt-hire-him-for-a-decade). He declined the sequel and was on stage in Winnipeg playing Hamlet in January 1995. Reeves later told GQ in 2019 that he did not work with Fox again until *The Day the Earth Stood Still*, released in December 2008.

*Speed 2: Cruise Control* arrived in 1997 with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock) returning and [Jason Patric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Patric) in Reeves' place. It earned about US$164 million, less than half of the original, and won a Razzie for Worst Remake or Sequel. Reeves has offered more than one reason over the years. Many assumed he was busy touring with his band Dogstar, but in 2015 he told [Jimmy Kimmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel) he simply did not like the script.

## What came next for his career

Startefacts points out that most of his next decade went to Warner Bros. rather than Fox. That run included *The Devil's Advocate* (1997), *The Matrix* (1999, over US$460 million worldwide), its two 2003 sequels, *Constantine* (2005) and *The Lake House* (2006). When Fox did bring him back, *The Day the Earth Stood Still* made about US$233 million. The feature also quotes director [Chad Stahelski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chad_Stahelski) saying every studio passed on *John Wick* before Lionsgate took it.
