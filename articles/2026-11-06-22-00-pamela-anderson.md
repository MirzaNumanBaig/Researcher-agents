**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) will be one of 12 career achievement honorees at the 44th Torino Film Festival, which runs 24 November to 2 December.
- She joins John Travolta, [Isabelle Huppert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabelle_Huppert) and [Josh Safdie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Safdie) on the list, according to Variety.
- Artistic director [Giulio Base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giulio_Base) called her "another dream come true" and praised her ability to reinvent herself.
- Honorees receive their trophies on opening night at the Teatro Regio in Turin.

The Torino Film Festival has named Pamela Anderson among its 2026 honorees, and the man who picked her has now explained why. Speaking to Variety ahead of the lineup announcement, festival director Giulio Base said her career "looks like a cocktail" that somehow still tastes good. The 44th edition runs from 24 November to 2 December in Turin, Italy.

## Who else is on the honours list

Variety reports that 12 career achievement awards will be handed out, each shaped like the Stella della Mole. That is the 12-point sculpture on top of Turin's Museo Nazionale del Cinema, and it is an emblem of both the city and the festival.

Anderson sits alongside Travolta, Huppert and Safdie. The festival will also pay tribute to [Luc Besson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luc_Besson), [Fanny Ardant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fanny_Ardant), [Margarethe von Trotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margarethe_von_Trotta), [Stephen Frears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Frears), [Mike Newell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Newell_(director)), [Maria de Medeiros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_de_Medeiros), [Andy Garcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Garcia) and [Mira Sorvino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mira_Sorvino).

Travolta, one of the first names announced, will open the festival with director Roland Joffé and the international premiere of "November 1963", a drama about the assassination of John F. Kennedy. The trophies themselves will be presented on opening night at the Teatro Regio, the same venue that hosted the Turin premiere of "Grease" in 1978.

## What the festival chief said about Anderson

Variety describes Anderson as a "Canadian-born pop-culture phenomenon" whose moves from B-movies to studio comedies to contemporary auteur films capture the spirit of Zibaldone. That is Base's programme of restorations, tributes, special screenings and premieres, named after a miscellany by the poet Giacomo Leopardi.

Base was direct about why she fits. "She had the courage to reinvent herself, surprising those who thought they already knew her," he told the trade title. "She's an example of how to succeed and transform." He added that having her on the list is a personal highlight.

The comment lines up with how the festival has framed this edition. Base is three editions into a five-year mandate. According to Variety, he has built a populist streak, favouring guests with proven appeal to audiences and asking them to spend time with local viewers.

## A deliberately compact festival

Base says he dislikes festivals with an abundance of everything. "I think a festival is like an art gallery," he said, where every work has its own space for anyone who walks through. Between the opening and closing galas, honorees will meet audiences for Q&As at two city-centre cinemas only 500 metres apart. Base told Variety he had his first job at one and his first kiss at the other.

For Anderson, that format means close contact with Italian audiences rather than a distant red carpet. The festival has not yet said which of her films, if any, will screen.
