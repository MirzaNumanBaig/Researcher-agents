**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays two characters in the Canadian series Yaga, his first major project since [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).
- Mashable's review says the show wastes its cast and never delivers on its witchcraft premise.
- Williams told Entertainment Weekly he is writing a film that includes an ex-hockey player, a detail he says is tripping up the team.
- Season two of Heated Rivalry is filming in Toronto, Montreal and Ottawa through December 2, according to Just Jared as cited by Out.

Hudson Williams has two things in the news this week: a mixed review for his first big post-Heated Rivalry role, and a hint that he is writing a movie of his own. Yaga, the eight-episode series that arrived on AMC+ and Crave, puts him in a dual role. Meanwhile, an Entertainment Weekly interview suggests his own script has a hockey problem.

## What Yaga is about

Yaga is based on a play by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler), who is also the showrunner. It is set in the fictional fishing town of Whittock, where Henry Park, heir to a powerful fishery owner, has gone missing. [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) plays private investigator Charlie Rapp, who is paired with local detective Carson, played by [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo).

According to [Mashable](https://mashable.com/entertainment/yaga-review-hudson-williams), what starts as a missing persons case turns darker, with chicken carcasses on doors and bloody symbols at crime scenes. Rapp keeps hearing whispers of Baba Yaga, the Slavic witch said to live in a house on chicken legs. [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) plays Katherine Hargrove, a university professor.

## Williams in a double role

Williams plays Henry and also Logan Casey, Henry's roommate, podcast partner and lookalike. [Out](https://www.out.com/celebs/hudson-williams-hockey-player) reports that he wore a prosthetic nose for Logan. Sandler told Entertainment Weekly the doppelganger was added to widen the list of suspects, and that the school setting was meant to push a dark academia mood with a "rich mouse, poor mouse" contrast.

Logan was not in the original play. Mashable argues that shows: the reviewer finds Henry a confident, if sleazy, charmer and Logan an awkward foil, with neither given enough screen time for the double turn to land. The review also says Williams makes for an exciting prospect as a villain, but the writing tells us too little about either man to make them compelling.

## The critical verdict

Mashable's review is not kind to the series as a whole. It calls the show a dark feminist fable that feels hollow, says its dream sequences lean on familiar horror shorthand, and finds the witch voiceovers predictable. It says Moss is good fun but kept on the margins, and that the Backo and Reid pairing works until it is dragged down by Rapp's constant flirting.

The review also puts the stakes plainly. Yaga is Williams' first major release since Heated Rivalry made him and co-star [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) famous, and the reviewer sees it as a chance to show range beyond his Canadian Screen Award-winning turn as [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander). One critic's view is not the final word, and audience reaction is still to come.

## The script with a hockey problem

The more intriguing news came in the Entertainment Weekly interview with Williams and Moss. Out reports that Williams, who has written and directed several short films including Soakers, Buttons and Inside Augustine Mendoza's Quotidian Apartment, said he is writing a movie in which one character is an ex-hockey player.

"We're getting tripped up on it and we're considering other sports," Williams said, adding that the character simply made more sense as a former hockey player. Entertainment Weekly pressed for details, but Out says his publicists declined the question. Nothing has been confirmed about the film's title, cast or timeline.

The same interview touched on a curious link. Baba Yaga is also the name of the legend behind the John Wick franchise's hitman, played by [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves), and Moss's character mentions John Wick on screen. Moss said her first instinct was that it was her friend's movie, not hers.

## What happens next

Williams, who Out says is 25, has several projects lined up. Out lists the comedic thriller Apparatus with Dylan O'Brien, and a culinary thriller called Tyrant with Charlize Theron, Julia Garner, Demi Moore and Omar Apollo. Heated Rivalry season two is currently filming in Toronto, Montreal and Ottawa until December 2, per Just Jared as cited by Out.
