**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays two characters, Henry Park and lookalike roommate Logan Casey, in the supernatural series Yaga.
- Creator [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) told [Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times) the actor helped make the show "more unexpected and bolder and sexier".
- Yaga releases on Sky and NOW on 31 October, and The Guardian lists it for 9pm on Sky Atlantic that night.
- It is Williams's next major screen role after [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).

Hudson Williams arrives in Yaga on Halloween, 31 October, in a double role that its creator says he shaped himself. In an exclusive interview with [Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/tv/fantasy/yaga-hudson-williams-input-kat-sandler-exclusive-newsupdate/), Sandler credited the Heated Rivalry star with building both characters from the ground up and nudging the whole series toward a riskier tone.

## Who Hudson Williams plays in Yaga

Yaga retells the Baba Yaga legend from a feminist angle, according to Radio Times. The mystery driving the story is the disappearance of Henry Park, played by Williams. The cast also includes [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss), [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) and [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo).

Williams also plays Logan Casey, a second part added for the screen version. Logan is Henry's strange lookalike roommate. Radio Times reports that Sandler first wrote Yaga as a play, where one actor covers Henry and the private investigator Rapp, who is played in the series by Reid. Giving Williams two roles was a small nod to that stage setup.

## What Kat Sandler says about his input

Sandler told Radio Times that Henry is very different in the play, where he is the only version of the character. She said Williams was excited to play two distinct people and "brought his beautiful cinephile hardworking actor self" to the job.

She described his approach as obsessive in a good way. "From minute one, he was really nerdy about the character," she said, adding that he tried to build something that would lift the whole show, not only his own scenes. Her summary: "He really created these characters."

For a performer best known for one breakout series, that is a notable endorsement. It is also a single account from the show's creator, and viewers will judge the performances for themselves.

## A show that leans into its own provocations

Radio Times says fans were already talking about the racy scenes teased in early trailers, and that Moss told the outlet the show changed her mind about intimacy coordinators. Sandler was upfront about the tone. "The show is sexy and it's unapologetic and it's not for everybody," she said, though she added that it takes a few big swings.

One of the central relationships pairs Moss's character Katherine, a professor, with her student Henry. Sandler said she wanted the series to be self-aware about that. She pointed out that audiences rarely blink at a 30-year age gap when the genders are flipped, and said the show calls this out on purpose. She quoted a line from an early episode in which Rapp says it is not as creepy because Katherine is a woman, but Henry is far younger than she is. Sandler admitted this can make some lines feel on the nose, and said she is fine with that.

## The wider mystery, per The Guardian

In its Halloween viewing guide, [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/tv-and-radio/2026/oct/31/tv-tonight-halloween-weirdness-with-heated-rivalrys-hudson-williams) describes Yaga as a fun, creepy drama with witchy weirdness. Reviewer Phil Harrison writes that it uses familiar beats, including mismatched cops, a missing rich kid and a mysterious coastal community, but lands them in a fresh way.

According to The Guardian, Reid's Charlie Rapp is a private investigator hired to find the heir to a local fishery. He is paired with a sceptical cop, Carson, played by Backo. The longer they dig, the stranger things get.

## What happens next

Yaga is available on Sky and NOW from 31 October, with its Sky Atlantic broadcast at 9pm UK time that evening, per The Guardian. ## Frequently asked questions

### When does Yaga release?

Yaga releases on Sky and NOW on 31 October, according to Radio Times. The Guardian lists the Sky Atlantic showing at 9pm that night.

### Who does Hudson Williams play in Yaga?

He plays Henry Park, whose disappearance sits at the centre of the story, and Logan Casey, Henry's lookalike roommate. Logan was added for the series and does not appear in the original play.

### Who else stars in Yaga?

Carrie-Anne Moss, Noah Reid and Clark Backo star alongside Williams. Moss plays the professor Katherine, Reid plays private investigator Charlie Rapp, and Backo plays the cop Carson.
