**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), 40, was confirmed last week as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), and reaction is still arriving.
- Dame Judi Dench, who played M, told ITV's Lorraine she "can't wait" to see him, according to Euronews.
- Tenet co-star Elizabeth Debicki said she would happily play a Bond villain opposite him, Digital Spy reports.
- The new Bond film is not expected in cinemas before the second half of 2028, and Pattinson's Dune: Part Three opens next month.

## Robert Pattinson Bond reaction keeps building

A week after the announcement, the Robert Pattinson Bond reaction is coming from every direction: former Bond cast members, co-stars, a veteran agent and the president of the United States. The British actor was named the seventh 007 last week, ahead of reported contenders Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor, according to Euronews. He called the job "the honour of a lifetime" and "a dream come true".

Here is what has been said in the past two days, and what sits between him and the first day on set.

## Judi Dench: "he looks terrific to me"

Dame Judi Dench, 91, played MI6 chief M across seven Bond films, from GoldenEye in 1995 to Skyfall in 2012, and was the first woman to hold the role. Speaking on ITV's Lorraine, she told viewers she "can't wait" to see Pattinson in action, Euronews reports.

There was a catch. Dench has age-related macular degeneration, diagnosed in 2012, and said she can only vaguely see. "He looks terrific to me," she said. Earlier this year she told BBC Radio 4 that the condition means she can no longer perform on stage, act in films or read scripts.

## Elizabeth Debicki would play the villain

Australian actress [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) worked with Pattinson on Tenet, and [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) caught up with her at the UK premiere of [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher)'s The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. Asked whether she would play a Bond villain opposite him, she answered: "Sure. Of course."

She added that she would love to work with "Rob" again. Debicki had earlier told Entertainment Tonight she texted him "straight away" when his casting was announced. Nothing suggests a role has been discussed; it is a friendly answer to a hypothetical question.

## The agent who turned him away

The Telegraph reports that Michael Whitehall, father of comedian Jack Whitehall, once declined to represent a teenage Pattinson. Speaking on his podcast The Wittering Whitehalls, he said the future star turned up at the family home to be photographed by Whitehall's wife, Hilary, and asked for advice. Whitehall says he told him acting was a hard life and suggested a "proper job".

Hilary Whitehall noted that a different agent found Pattinson at the Old Sorting Office Theatre in Barnes when he was 18 or 19, and that he is still with her. It is a light story, told with good humour by the people involved.

## Trump wishes him "luck"

The New York Post reports that Donald Trump, asked about the casting in an Oval Office interview, said: "He looks nice. You know, I wish him luck." The paper says the president had an aide bring articles about the actor before answering, and that Trump said he likes "people that like me".

## Dune: Part Three arrives first

Before Bond, Pattinson is on screen in [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s Dune: Part Three, where he plays the assassin Scytale. A new teaser landed this week with [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet) returning as Paul Atreides. India.com says fans on X focused on Pattinson's unusual voice and the film's darker look, though those are social media reactions, not reviews.

DIPE, a Korean outlet, reports Pattinson sat through five hours of makeup for the role and quotes him calling Scytale "not a typical villain". The film is scheduled for cinemas on 18 December 2026, according to India.com, with Korea getting it on 16 December.

## What happens next

Pattinson must finish The Batman Part II first. Euronews says production was briefly paused after director Matt Reeves faced a personal issue, and that Steven Soderbergh has stepped in to help shoot the final weeks. Bond 26 is due to start filming next year with Villeneuve directing and Steven Knight writing, according to both Euronews and The Telegraph, and Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.

Euronews calculates that the earliest release would be the second half of 2028. That would be seven years after Daniel Craig's No Time To Die, the longest gap since the six years between Licence To Kill and GoldenEye.
