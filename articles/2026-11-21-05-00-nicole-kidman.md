## Nicole Kidman's ponytail in New York

Nicole Kidman has traded her signature loose waves for a tousled ponytail with a maxi fringe, and ELLE UK says the look is the easiest way to give mature hair more body. The magazine reported on 20 November that the actor was seen wearing the style while out in New York City, with soft pieces left around the face and a little lift at the crown.

The timing suits the season. ELLE's writer points to a wider return of big blowouts and a growing appetite for effortless texture, which puts volume back on the beauty agenda. Kidman's version is notable because it gets there without extensions or heavy styling, at least according to the stylists the magazine consulted.

Neither outlet reports a comment from Kidman about the look, so what follows is expert opinion on how it works, not her own account.

## Why stylists say the look works

Nico Noviello, a stylist at Hershesons, told ELLE that the softness around her face, the relaxed texture and the subtle volume at the crown give the ponytail a modern, sophisticated finish. He described it as effortless elegance.

His key point is restraint. The style does not need to be perfect, he said, and that slightly undone finish is what makes it look chic. A few loose face-framing pieces and a gentle lift at the roots create the impression of thicker hair, and the fringe adds to that effect by breaking up the forehead and softening the frame.

For a fuller ponytail, the ELLE piece suggests a few simple moves:

- Use a lightweight product, such as a foam made for fine hair, so the hair is not weighed down.
- Add soft waves before tying the hair back.
- Gently backcombing the crown for extra height.
- Leave a few strands loose around the face.
- Finish with a light mist of shine spray.

## How to recreate it for the festive season

Hair stylist Joe Pickering-Taylor suggested adding texture before the hair goes up. He recommends curling the lengths loosely with an auto-wrap styling tool, which he says shapes the hair with airflow and controlled heat rather than extreme temperatures. ELLE notes it earns a commission on some products linked in its article, so the product mentions are worth reading with that in mind.

The advice is aimed at party season. A ponytail with some structure at the crown holds up through a long evening better than loose waves, and it still looks polished. The experts' line is that the result should feel modern, easy and wearable rather than styled to the last strand.

## The black UGG boots outfit

A second styling story put Kidman's footwear in the spotlight. Woman & Home, in a piece published on 20 November, argues that black UGG boots are a cold-weather must-have and uses an older outfit as proof.

The magazine says that in 2024 she was seen on set in UGG classic mini short boots. Rather than pairing them with relaxed denim, she wore them with a sharp tailored coat, a black crossbody bag and black tights. Woman & Home says the combination shows the boots can sit inside a smart-casual outfit.

The writer's case for black is practical. The colour is less of a statement than lighter shades and hides scuffs and marks better. The article is also tied to the Black Friday sales, and it lists cheaper alternatives to the brand, so it is partly a shopping guide. Treat the retail suggestions accordingly.
