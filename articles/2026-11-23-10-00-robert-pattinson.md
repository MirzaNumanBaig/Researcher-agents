**Quick summary**

- Pinewood Studios says it cannot yet confirm that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)'s Bond film will shoot there, but is keeping its "fingers crossed", according to Variety.
- Of the 25 Bond films, only two were not filmed at Pinewood: "License to Kill" (1989) and "GoldenEye" (1995).
- Pattinson was announced as the new 007 about five days before Variety's report, succeeding Daniel Craig.
- Denis Villeneuve is reported to be directing, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay.

Pinewood Studios is hoping the next James Bond adventure comes home. Andrew M. Smith, the studio's corporate affairs director, told a panel reported by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/robert-pattinson-bond-movie-shoot-pinewood-studios-1236954829/) that he cannot yet confirm whether Pattinson's first outing as 007 will film on the lot, but that he and his team are keeping their fingers crossed.

## Pinewood's long Bond history

The connection runs back to the very first film. Variety notes that Bond productions have been made at Pinewood since 1962's "Dr. No". Of the 25 films in the series, just two missed the lot. "License to Kill" in 1989 shot mainly in Mexico and Florida. "GoldenEye" in 1995 was filmed at Leavesden because Pinewood was booked for "First Knight", though its sound mixing was still done at Pinewood.

That record explains why Smith's tone is hopeful rather than certain. Studio space is a practical question as much as a sentimental one, and nothing about the schedule has been announced.

## A new owner, a new deal

The studio's hopes sit against a changed backdrop. [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) played Bond in five films, starting with "Casino Royale" in 2006 and ending with "No Time to Die" in 2021. According to Variety, that was also the last Bond release before longtime producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli sold creative control of the franchise to Amazon MGM for US$20 million.

Amazon MGM is already a major client of the group. Variety reports that Amazon has a long-term deal at Pinewood's sister site, Shepperton Studios, where it has taken nine stages of a newly opened 17-stage expansion. But Bond may struggle to find room there. Shepperton currently houses "The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power", and season four of that series is expected to start production in early 2028.

## Why productions choose the UK

Smith used the panel to talk about what draws large American productions to Britain. He pointed to infrastructure, a skilled workforce and incentives, and said the financial incentive is probably the biggest pull. Asked whether Pinewood beats a Hollywood lot, he replied that it does not: "It's the money."

He said the studio has been "very fortunate", with 59 of its 61 stages under lease to major American companies including Disney and Netflix. Variety adds that 21 of the 38 Marvel films were shot at Pinewood. On expansion, Smith was cautious, saying there are many empty stages in mature markets and that building more is not a priority now.

## The man in the tuxedo

Pattinson's casting ended nearly a year of speculation. Instinct Magazine reports that [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Josh O'Connor and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) were among the names reportedly in the frame. The same outlet says Villeneuve will direct and Knight will write.

Pattinson comes to the role after a run of varied work, from [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen) in Twilight to [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan)'s "Tenet" and "The Odyssey", and the lead in "The Batman". Instinct points to his Nolan spy thriller as an early hint that the genre suits him.

## What happens next

Nothing about the shoot is confirmed. Pinewood has said only that it is waiting, and no production dates or locations have been released for the Bond film. Shepperton looks crowded, which may strengthen Pinewood's case, but that is an inference and not something either studio has said. Watch for an announcement from Amazon MGM on schedule and base.
