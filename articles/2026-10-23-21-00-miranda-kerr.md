**Quick summary**

- Australian model [Miranda Kerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Kerr) and her husband, Snap CEO Evan Spiegel, backed a campaign that relieved more than US$550 million in medical debt.
- The nonprofit Undue Medical Debt carried out the relief, which reached more than 250,000 Californians, according to AAP. Other outlets cite about 261,000.
- Recipients do not apply; they receive a letter saying the debt has been forgiven.
- The Times of India reports the couple are now turning attention to Los Angeles wildfire survivors.

Miranda Kerr and Evan Spiegel have helped wipe more than US$550 million (about A$798 million) in unpaid medical debt from the books of Californians, working with the nonprofit Undue Medical Debt. The couple announced the partnership in late June, and coverage this week, led by The Times of India, has revisited the gift and what the pair plan to do next.

## What the couple actually did

According to a report from Australian Associated Press, [published by The Canberra Times](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9300294/kerr-and-spiegel-help-wipe-medical-debt-in-california/), the donation was made earlier in 2026 and was not disclosed at the time. Undue Medical Debt, which was founded in 2014, buys portfolios of unpaid medical bills and cancels them.

The headline figure is more than US$550 million. The number of people affected varies by outlet. AAP says more than 250,000 Californians, as does [KGTV in San Diego](https://www.10news.com/homepage-showcase/snapchat-ceo-and-wife-erase-more-than-half-a-billion-dollars-of-medical-debt-in-california-san-diego-impacted-most), while The Times of India uses 261,000. The gap is small, but it is worth knowing when you see the number quoted elsewhere.

## How recipients find out

Nobody has to apply. People whose debts were bought receive a letter saying the balance has been forgiven. Hoodline, [citing the Los Angeles Times](https://hoodline.com/2026/06/snap-power-couple-wipes-out-550-million-in-medical-debt-across-california/), reported that San Diego County received the largest share, about US$99 million for roughly 40,000 people, and that Los Angeles County saw about US$26.7 million erased for around 17,466 residents.

Because unexpected letters about debt can look like scams, the couple posted an Instagram video to say the notices are genuine. "We want you to know it's real," Kerr said, according to AAP. Spiegel, 35, said they were announcing a partnership "to relieve over half a billion dollars of unpaid medical debt."

Kerr, 43, also spoke about why the cause matters to her. "When someone you love is sick, all you want to do is focus on helping them get better," she said, adding that they wanted to highlight the work Undue Medical Debt has done since 2014.

## The turn towards wildfire recovery

The Times of India's headline says the couple are now turning to survivors of the Los Angeles wildfires, and its page lists related items including a group called Department of Angels and funding for research into the long-term health effects of the fires. The version of that article available to this site showed only those headings, not the full text, so details such as amounts and timing are not confirmed here. Neither AAP nor the other reports we read mention a wildfire project.

## Kerr on health and family

Separately, Kerr has been talking about her own wellbeing. According to [Celebitchy](https://www.celebitchy.com/1002971/miranda_kerr_eliminated_eggs_from_her_diet_because_she_had_proctitis/), which quotes an interview in Stellar magazine, she said she has an inflammatory bowel condition that shapes her diet, and stressed that what works for her is not advice for everyone. She told the magazine her children do not eat the way she does. That interview is secondhand here, so treat the detail as her own account as relayed by that outlet.

Kerr is mother to four sons. AAP notes that her eldest, Flynn, is from her marriage to Orlando Bloom, and that Hart, Myles and Pierre are her children with Spiegel.

## What happens next

Letters from Undue Medical Debt were expected to reach recipients from mid-July, according to Hoodline. Any wildfire-related giving from the couple would need confirmation from them or from the organisations involved. We will update this story if either publishes details.
