**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) spoke about a "complicated relationship" with men in a confessional video, according to Queerty on 22 October 2026.
- He said a mandatory school swim day was one of his worst childhood memories, until a teacher let him sing instead.
- His fourth studio album, *She's the Best*, was released on 9 October, per Vanity Teen.
- Nicole Kidman appears in the title track's video, and Ian McKellen narrates the "Party" video.

The Australian pop star has been talking about where he came from just as his new record lands. In a video shared by the Instagram account "A View, from a bridge", Sivan described growing up in Western Australia feeling out of step with the boys around him, [Queerty reported](https://www.queerty.com/troye-sivan-gets-candid-about-his-complicated-relationship-with-men-20261022/). His fourth album, *She's the Best*, came out two weeks earlier.

## The swim day he dreaded

The account collects short confessionals from public figures, and Sivan speaks into a red phone for his. Queerty says he described a "complicated relationship" with men, which he traced largely to never being sporty.

He gave a small, specific example. Walking past a group of men playing soccer and being expected to kick the ball back is, in his words, "a terrifying experience". At school, the annual swim competition was worse. It was compulsory, and he said he could barely get through four strokes before stopping to catch his breath. Other boys teased him. "It was a really, really horrible experience," he said, adding that he wanted to cry in the middle of the race.

## The teacher's deal

One teacher changed that day. When Sivan told her he didn't want to swim, she offered a trade: he could skip the race if he sang instead. "I got to go home, and didn't have to swim," he said.

He credits her with showing him the "real version" of himself, and with the idea that what makes someone different is also what makes them special. He doesn't know whether she remembers him, but said the gesture "meant the world". Queerty notes that he began performing as a preteen and released his debut album, [*Blue Neighbourhood*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Neighbourhood), at 20. The top reply under the post, quoted by the outlet, thanks the teachers who made kids feel big despite everything.

## What the album sounds like

Vanity Teen's [review of *She's the Best*](https://www.vanityteen.com/troye-sivan-shes-the-best-album-review/) places the record after the "sweaty, uncomplicated euphoria" of his 2023 album *Something to Give Each Other*, which produced "Rush" and "One of Your Girls". The reviewer argues this one is less about keeping the party going and more about what comes after: loneliness, friendship and the hope of finding someone.

The sound moves toward trip-hop, hazier pop-rock and psychedelic production, the review says, with Sivan leaning further into speak-singing. It singles out "Embarrassingly" and "Sex Star" as songs where confidence cracks, and "FF" as a love song to an imagined partner, with a home-movie style video of beach houses and underwater shots.

## Guests and a Madonna sample

The collaborators are high profile. [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) appears in the video for the title track. Madonna personally approved a sample of her 2000 single "Music" for "Party", according to Vanity Teen. The "Party" video is a medieval fantasy narrated by [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen).

The same review says Sivan announced the songs with memes that spelled out each track's emotional theme, instead of cryptic teasers. He has also called this his favourite album cover yet, with oversized posters included in the vinyl edition.
