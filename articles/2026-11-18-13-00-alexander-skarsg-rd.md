**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) wore pink satin shorts, a mint "Baby" polo and dress shoes to Deadline Contenders Film: Los Angeles on Saturday 14 November, according to Fashion Times UK.
- At the Governors Awards, SSBCrack reports, he paired a black suit with a low-cut silver sequinned tank and a matching vest-style layer.
- Both outfits belong to a run of playful looks during the press tour for his film *Wicker*.
- Stylist Harry Lambert is credited with the earlier tour outfits, including a gold suit with see-through shoes.

Alexander Skarsgård spent the past few days showing how far a leading man can bend the rules of awards-season dressing. On Saturday 14 November he turned up to the Deadline Contenders Film: Los Angeles event in pink satin shorts, and he then wore a silver sequinned tank under a black suit to the Governors Awards, according to two fashion reports.

## The pink satin shorts at Deadline Contenders

Fashion Times UK says the 6ft 4in actor arrived at the DGA Theatre in a mint polo embroidered with the word "Baby", rumpled satin shorts in pink, black socks and formal dress shoes. The outlet identifies the polo as a JW Anderson piece and says the look drew the label "elevated Adam Sandler fit" online, a nod to the comedian [Adam Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Sandler) and his love of baggy shorts.

The outlet frames the outfit as part of a wider menswear shift, in which expensive pieces are styled to look as if they were grabbed off the bedroom floor. It argues that stars are moving away from stiff tailoring, and that Skarsgård has taken the idea further than most.

Reaction online was mixed, as Fashion Times UK tells it. Some users praised his "playfulness" and called his taste delightfully odd, and one predicted he would grow into a modern-day James Spader, picking bizarre indie films. Others were less taken, with one describing the look as playing dress-up with a toddler. The outlet reports all of this as social media commentary, not as a measured verdict.

## A sequinned tank at the Governors Awards

A different mood followed at the 17th Governors Awards in Los Angeles, which SSBCrack describes as the unofficial start of awards season. The ceremony is hosted by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences and honours lifetime achievements and contributions to the industry.

SSBCrack says Skarsgård took a less traditional approach to formalwear. He wore a black suit with a low-cut silver sequinned tank and a matching vest-like layer, a look it places in the same line as the graphic T-shirts, kilts and transparent shoes seen on the *Wicker* tour. We covered a separate account of the same evening in our report on the [Tom Ford look at the Governors Awards](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-tom-ford/).

The red carpet around him was busy. SSBCrack lists [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) in a pale pink gown with a ruffled skirt, [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron) in vivid red with a high leg split, and [Lily James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_James) and Australian actor [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) in silver. [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone) wore black with a striking back, and [Gemma Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Chan) chose a white column gown.

## How the Wicker tour wardrobe built up

Fashion Times UK traces the pattern back through the tour. It says Skarsgård and stylist Harry Lambert have leaned into bold looks for *Wicker*. At the Los Angeles premiere he wore a custom Chopova Lowena black beaded top featuring a portrait of his co-star Olivia Colman, paired with a green-and-pink tartan midi skirt. Earlier, at the London Film Festival, he wore a glittery all-gold suit with transparent shoes.

For readers who want the film itself, our [Wicker review](https://moduscarry.com/wicker-review-alexander-skarsgard/) and our piece on [Skarsgård playing sweet in Wicker](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-wicker-sweet-role/) cover the performance. You can follow every look in our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

Neither outlet says what the clothes cost beyond calling the polo high-end, and neither reports a comment from the actor about the choices. The pink shorts are therefore best read as the press's interpretation of a stylist-led strategy, not a statement from Skarsgård.
