**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) was honoured as an advocate at the Time100 Next Gala, held on Thursday at Chelsea Piers in New York.
- He attended just after releasing his fourth studio album, "She's the Best", according to WWD.
- Actress [Sarah Pidgeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Pidgeon) told the outlet she is a big fan and praised his music video with [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman).
- It was the sixth edition of the gala, which honours rising figures in business, entertainment, sports and politics.

Singer Troye Sivan was one of the honourees at the Time100 Next Gala on Thursday night, collecting recognition as an advocate while he is in the middle of a new album campaign. [WWD reports](https://wwd.com/eye/parties/troye-sivan-sarah-pidgeon-raye-time100-next-gala-1239393336/) that the event took place at Chelsea Piers, in partnership with Rolex, Toyota and Target.

## What the Time100 Next Gala is

The gala is now in its sixth year. According to WWD, it celebrates people in business, entertainment, sports, politics and other fields who are shaping the next generation. This year's guest list mixed honourees with friends and supporters from music, film and fashion.

Among those named by WWD were Raye, Druski, [Joey King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_King), [Alex Warren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Warren), designer [Gabriela Hearst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Hearst), basketball player Tyrese Maxey, [Ashley Padilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Padilla), [Himesh Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himesh_Patel), country singer [Megan Moroney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Moroney) and Australian actress [Yerin Ha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerin_Ha).

## Sivan on love and the dance floor

Sivan has just released his fourth studio album, "She's the Best", and WWD says he was honoured on the night as an advocate. Asked whether he believes in finding love on the dance floor, he answered with an emphatic yes, calling it one of the best places to meet someone.

His advice was simple. "Dance and be free. And be friendly," he told the outlet, adding that he thinks everyone on a dance floor is there to connect, either with themselves or with other people.
## A fan in the room

Pidgeon, who stars in "Love Story", told WWD she had only recently got to know Sivan. She said she is a big fan of his music and was excited to meet him in person, and she sang along to his latest songs during the evening.

She singled out the music video that features Kidman. "What a way to start an album cycle," Pidgeon said. WWD does not name the song, so this article does not either.

## Other moments from the evening

Joey King, who stars in "Practical Magic 2", spoke about a new project with LaKeith Stanfield called "Safe Space". She described it as not quite science fiction but also a comedy, and said a trade announcement had just come out. Dinner opened with a performance of "Cry Me a River" by Grammy-winning singer Raye, who began her set seated on the floor. Singer Ravyn Lenae said she is working on new music. The night ended with desserts and a Halloween candy bar, and a crowd of fans waited outside the pier for Alex Warren.

## What happens next

Sivan's new album is only just out, so the next few weeks are likely to show how it is received. WWD does not report tour dates or further appearances, and none are confirmed in the source.
