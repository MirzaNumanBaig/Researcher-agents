**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) was the honorary captain for Michigan's home game against Michigan State on Saturday 7 November (US time).
- Michigan Football posted a video on Friday 6 November in which he calls himself "one Wolverine" speaking to "the entire Wolverine Nation".
- He spoke to the team at the football practice facility earlier in the week, according to Michigan Football and 97.9 WGRD.
- Michigan has won the rivalry four years in a row and leads the all-time series 75-38-5, WGRD reports.

Marvel's Wolverine has picked a side in a different kind of Wolverine fight. Hugh Jackman, who played the clawed X-Men hero for about two decades, was named honorary captain for Michigan's rivalry game against Michigan State, and he took the job seriously enough to turn up in team colours and give the squad a pep talk.

## What Jackman said to Michigan

In a clip shared by Michigan Football on Facebook on Friday 6 November, Jackman wears a Block M hat and a maize and blue jacket. "From one Wolverine to the entire Wolverine Nation, I'm so pumped to be the honorary captain for the big rivalry, the big game in the Big House tomorrow," he says, according to [Yahoo Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Sports) (via the Lansing State Journal).

He finishes with a snarled "Go Blue" and a raised fist. A second post from the program showed photos of him addressing the players, captioned "Wolverine x Wolverines".

Yahoo notes that the 58-year-old Australian has played the comic-book character in Marvel films and video games more than half a dozen times. The pun was always going to write itself, since the University of Michigan's teams are the Wolverines.

## The game he was invited to

The honorary captain role includes appearing at the pre-game coin toss, WGRD reports. The station says the game, known as the Battle for the Paul Bunyan Trophy, kicked off at 7:30pm local time on Saturday.

The two teams are not at their best. Yahoo's figures put Michigan State at 0-5 in the Big Ten and 2-6 overall, coming off a bye week. Michigan was 5-3 overall after a 28-21 win over Rutgers. The outlets differ slightly on the Wolverines' position: WGRD has them 11th in the Big Ten, with both sides struggling for much of the year.

History favours the home side. Michigan beat the Spartans 31-20 last year, per Yahoo, and WGRD says it has won four meetings in a row and leads the all-time series 75-38-5. Over the last ten games it is 7-3. WGRD's tongue-in-cheek line is that with "the Wolverine" in attendance, Michigan would be aiming for 8-3.
