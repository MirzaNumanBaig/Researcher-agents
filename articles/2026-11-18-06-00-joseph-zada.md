**Quick summary**

- Australian actor [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) attended the New York premiere of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* on Monday, 16 November (US time).
- He told Extra that co-star [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) "carried our entire press tour".
- Zada, 21, plays a young Haymitch Abernathy, the role [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) played in the original films.
- The film opens in cinemas on 20 November.

Joseph Zada has spent months promoting his first major franchise lead, and the New York premiere of *Sunrise on the Reaping* gave him a chance to say thank you. Speaking to Extra on the red carpet, the 21-year-old Australian singled out Mckenna Grace as the person who kept the press tour going, days before the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel reaches cinemas.

## Zada and Grace trade compliments

According to [Extra](https://extratv.com/2026/11/17/joseph-zada-and-mckenna-grace-sing-each-other-s-praises-at-hunger-games-premiere/), the two were interviewed separately, yet the praise matched. Zada said he was proud of the whole cast, and "especially proud" of Grace. In his words, the film would not be the same without her.

Grace returned the favour. She said she would miss being on the road with him and described the experience as one she may never have again. She also spoke about the fan base around the series, saying it was surreal that fans already know who she and Zada are. Grace plays both Maysilee Donner and her twin, Merrilee.

Her red-carpet look carried a story too. She told Extra it paid homage to Katniss and the Mockingjay gown, a moment the Capitol built to turn Katniss into an attraction that was then turned into an act of rebellion.

Fans of the pair may also enjoy our earlier story on [Joseph Zada and Mckenna Grace's matching ladybug tattoos](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-ladybug-tattoo/).

## Stepping into Haymitch's shoes

Zada plays Haymitch at 16, on the day of the 50th Games. Extra reports he was nervous when filming began, given the pressure of following a character first played by Harrelson. He said the role let him set that pressure aside and act as a support for the younger actors around him, which he named as his favourite part.

In a conversation with [GQ](https://www.gq.com/story/joseph-zada-hunger-games-get-ready-with-me) on the morning of the premiere, Zada said this was the most preparation and support he has had for any part. He also linked Haymitch to his previous role as Cal in *East of Eden*, another troubled young man, saying he prides himself on showing vulnerability. For more on how he approached the part, read our piece on [Zada following Woody Harrelson as Haymitch](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-haymitch-woody-harrelson/).

## A premiere night in custom Louis Vuitton

GQ reports that Zada wore a custom Louis Vuitton suit, finished with gold-toed cowboy boots from Pharrell's first collection for the brand. He credited his stylist, Jason Bolden, for the look and said getting dressed has become more enjoyable and now feels more like armour than an obligation.

Asked about the tour, he named London as a highlight because his little sister was there, and Mexico as the biggest event, with several thousand fans. He said the premieres have been fun and that he is unsure he will see a reception like it again. See more in our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) coverage.

## The wider cast and the original stars

Extra also spoke to [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), who plays Wyatt Callow and called the experience a run of "pinch-myself" moments, and [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke), who plays past winner Wiress. [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) plays a young Plutarch Heavensbee, a part [Philip Seymour Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman) played in the earlier films. Plemons said he watched some of Hoffman's work but stressed that the younger version is a different man.

In an interview with [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/entertainment/hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-cast-reunion-details), Zada and co-star Whitney Peak described the day [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), Josh Hutcherson and Harrelson returned to set as "the trippiest thing ever". Director Francis Lawrence said it was not hard to bring them back, and that he wanted the ending to feel hopeful.

One early review, from [Movies We Texted About](https://movieswetextedabout.com/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-movie-review-joseph-zada-gives-haymitch-the-story-he-deserved/), called Zada "extremely compelling" in his first big lead, though the reviewer noted his accent slips at times.

## What happens next

*The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens in cinemas on 20 November. More on the film is in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section. Zada has told GQ the press run is nearly over, and he admits to a little sadness that it is ending.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Joseph Zada play in Sunrise on the Reaping?
He plays a young Haymitch Abernathy, whom Woody Harrelson played in the original Hunger Games films. The story follows Haymitch through his own Games, about 25 years before the first film.

### When is the film released?
Extra reports that *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* is in theatres from 20 November.

### How old is Joseph Zada?
GQ describes him as a 21-year-old Australian actor.

### Who else is in the cast?
The cast includes Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Ben Wang, Maya Hawke and Jesse Plemons, with Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson returning for the reunion scene, according to Bustle.
