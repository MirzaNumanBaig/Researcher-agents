**Quick summary**

- Ebenezer, Johnny Depp's take on A Christmas Carol, opened at No. 1 in the US with US$19 million domestic and US$42 million worldwide, according to TheWrap.
- Audiences gave it an A- on CinemaScore, while critics' reviews were mostly negative.
- Paramount's film cost US$38 million to make, plus an estimated US$50 million in marketing.
- Apart from a possible Pirates return and the finished Day Drinker, Depp has no other announced projects.

Johnny Depp's first major Hollywood lead since 2018 has given him a win at the box office, though not a runaway one. [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) plays Scrooge in Ebenezer, a horror-tinged retelling of A Christmas Carol that took the top spot over the weekend. The split between happy fans and unimpressed critics is the story now.

## How Ebenezer performed at the box office

TheWrap reports that Ebenezer opened to US$19 million in the US and Canada and US$42 million worldwide. That was enough to win the weekend. The outlet called the result "not bad, but not spectacular", and noted that the market has changed. In its telling, even big names struggle to deliver blockbuster numbers outside existing franchises.

The numbers look better against the cost. TheWrap puts the budget at US$38 million, with US$50 million spent on marketing. The worldwide opening alone therefore covers more than the production cost, though the marketing spend means the film still has work to do.

Our earlier report on the [US$42 million worldwide opening](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-weekend/) has the weekend in more detail.

## Fans came for Depp

TheWrap cites audience surveys showing that 50 per cent of opening-weekend viewers named Depp as their main reason for going. Those viewers rated the film A- on CinemaScore. Critics were far less generous: the outlet says reviews were mostly negative.

That gap is not new for this film. Paramount has pointed to [fan support behind the Ebenezer result](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-audience/), and earlier coverage found [critics saying the ghosts steal the show](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-reviews-2/).

## What one critic made of it

Highbrow Magazine's review is warmer about Depp than about the film. The reviewer says he disappears into the role, with an English accent that convinces and glaring eyes that do much of the work. Scenery-chewing, the review admits, is also what makes it fun. [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) is singled out as Jacob Marley, and [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint) appears as Bob Cratchit, though the reviewer calls the part paper-thin.

The main complaint is the question of why the story needed another version. The review lists plenty of earlier adaptations, including the 1951 Scrooge with [Alastair Sim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alastair_Sim), and says Ebenezer adds little beyond a slightly bigger role for Marley and more on Scrooge's past love, Belle. Director Ti West, known for horror, gets credit for controlling the gothic mood. The ghosts, in the reviewer's view, fail to frighten, and Scrooge's sudden change of heart still feels too quick.

The rest of the cast includes [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough), [Sam Claflin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Claflin), [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley), [Tramell Tillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramell_Tillman) and [Arthur Conti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Conti). The screenplay is by Nathaniel Halpern.

Depp's career long leaned on eccentric parts such as Captain Jack Sparrow, [Edward Scissorhands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scissorhands) and [Willy Wonka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willy_Wonka), the reviewer notes, and Scrooge fits neatly into that run. ## What happens next

TheWrap says Ebenezer was probably a test balloon. Beyond a hypothetical Pirates of the Caribbean return and Day Drinker, which has already been shot, Depp has no publicly attached projects. The outlet reads the result as a sign more work is likely, and wonders whether he will pick franchise films or lower-risk, mid-budget ones. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Johnny Depp's Ebenezer make on opening weekend?

According to TheWrap, it earned US$19 million in the US and US$42 million worldwide, enough to take No. 1.

### Did critics like Ebenezer?

Mostly not. TheWrap says reviews were largely negative, although audiences gave it an A- CinemaScore.

### Who directed Ebenezer?

Ti West, best known for horror films such as X and The Innkeepers, directed it from a script by Nathaniel Halpern.

### What is Johnny Depp doing next?

No new projects are announced, TheWrap says, apart from a possible Pirates return and the already-shot Day Drinker.
