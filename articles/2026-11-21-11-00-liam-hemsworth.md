**Quick summary**

- Author Andrzej Sapkowski is reportedly writing a new Witcher book with Geralt of Rivia at its centre.
- He hopes to finish it by the end of 2027, according to reports from a Polish fantasy festival.
- The book is separate from the Netflix series, where Liam Hemsworth plays Geralt.
- No title, plot or publication date has been announced.

Geralt of Rivia is heading back to the page. [Andrzej Sapkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrzej_Sapkowski), the Polish author who created the monster hunter, is reportedly working on another Witcher novel and aims to complete it by the end of 2027, [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/the-witcher-geralt-2027-return-wont-feature-liam-hemsworth/) reports. The Netflix series, which stars [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth), is not part of that project.

## What Sapkowski has said about the new Witcher book

FandomWire, citing O2 Poland, says the details came from Sapkowski's appearance at the Copernicon fantasy festival in Poland. Geralt will again be the central figure, but the author has not shared a title, a plot or a release date.

The same report says Sapkowski turned down the idea of writing a story built around Yennefer, keeping his focus on the Witcher himself. This is a secondhand account of a festival appearance, so treat the specifics as reported rather than officially confirmed.

## How it connects to Liam Hemsworth and Netflix

The books and the television series share source material but are separate projects. Hemsworth took over as Geralt from [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill) after Cavill's exit following Season 3, according to FandomWire. His first season in the role, Season 4, arrived in October 2025, and Netflix has confirmed that Season 5 will be the show's last.

That means the book is not an adaptation of the series, and the series is not tied to the book. Sapkowski's novel will reach readers directly from the man who invented the character. For fans of the show, it is a reminder that Geralt's story will outlast the Netflix run.

## Where the new story could go

FandomWire notes one possibility: Sapkowski's most recent novel, Crossroads of Ravens, followed a much younger Geralt in his early days after leaving Kaer Morhen. The new book could pick up from there or explore a different stretch of his life. Sapkowski has not said which, so this is speculation until he does.

## What happens next

Finishing a manuscript by the end of 2027 does not mean a shop release that year. Editing, publishing and translation could push the international release well beyond it, as FandomWire points out. Meanwhile, Hemsworth's run as Geralt continues on Netflix through its final season.
