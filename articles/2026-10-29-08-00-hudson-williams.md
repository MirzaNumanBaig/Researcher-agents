**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) says he is writing a movie in which one character is an ex-hockey player.
- He made the comment to [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) while promoting the series Yaga, according to FanBolt.
- No title, genre, studio or co-writer has been announced.
- Yaga premiered on 23 October, and Season 2 of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) is in production in Toronto.

Hudson Williams has revealed he is writing a feature film, and one of its characters used to play hockey. The actor shared the detail with Entertainment Weekly during interviews for Yaga, and [FanBolt](https://www.fanbolt.com/185953/from-heated-rivalry-to-the-writers-room-hudson-williams-teases-a-new-film-with-a-hockey-twist/) reported the exchange this week.

## What he actually said

"I'm writing a movie right now, but one character is an ex-hockey player," Williams told the magazine, as quoted by FanBolt. He said the people working on the script with him began talking themselves out of the idea, weighing other sports because of the overlap with his best-known role. He kept returning to hockey because the character only made sense that way.

He described himself as "tripped up" and said he had to bust through "the prison of your own mind". That is the full extent of what has been confirmed. There is no title, no logline, no named co-writer, no studio and no director. Williams has not said whether he plans to act in the film.

## Why hockey is a sensitive subject

Williams became widely known for playing a closeted professional hockey player in Heated Rivalry. FanBolt notes that the show lives in active careers, locker rooms and road trips, while a character who used to play is a different story about life after the sport.

The series has been a hit. FanBolt cites [Bell Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Media) as saying the Crave original became the service's biggest original series debut on record, with viewership climbing nearly 400 per cent after the premiere. Williams won a [Canadian Screen Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Screen_Awards) for the lead performance, and HBO Max licensed the show for the United States.

That success has a side effect. A hockey character in any project carrying his name is likely to be compared with [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander), his Heated Rivalry role, at least for a news cycle. FanBolt also suggests a simpler explanation: after two years of studying the game for work, hockey is simply the world he can now write about with authority. That is the outlet's reading, not something Williams said.

## Yaga and the rest of his calendar

The nearest project is Yaga. Kat Sandler's eight-episode adaptation of her own stage play premiered on 23 October on Crave in Canada and AMC+ in the United States, according to FanBolt. Williams plays two characters, Henry Park and Logan, alongside Carrie-Anne Moss, Noah Reid and Clark Backo.

Two features are also waiting on release dates: the dark comedy thriller Apparatus, opposite Dylan O'Brien, and Tyrant, an Amazon MGM ensemble from David Weil. FanBolt reports that Williams has been on a Toronto set since August.

That set belongs to Heated Rivalry. Creator Jacob Tierney has six episodes of Season 2 in production in Toronto through the end of the year, with a spring 2027 window and April the month those involved keep naming, FanBolt reports.

Williams also appears in the beauty aisle. [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437580/the-best-k-beauty-gifts-under-s25-including-hudson-williams-skincare-pick) lists a SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella travel kit in a Korean beauty gift guide and describes Williams as the brand's ambassador. It is a shopping feature, not news about his career.

## What happens next

The film is the least certain item. A script still being debated during a press round is a long way from a start date, and FanBolt's own estimate is that a feature he writes himself is a 2028 conversation at the earliest. Treat any version of this story that arrives with a plot attached as invention.

Closer to hand, Yaga is streaming now, and Heated Rivalry Season 2 is expected in spring 2027. For more coverage, see everything we publish on [Hudson Williams](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hudson-williams/) and our [TV & Dramas](https://moduscarry.com/category/tv-dramas/) page.
