**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) was among the guests at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on Thursday 5 November, the first runway the French house has staged in Australia.
- The show was held at the Cutaway in Barangaroo and re-staged Matthieu Blazy's debut cruise collection, first shown in Biarritz in April.
- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), [Dom Dolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom_Dolla) and [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) also attended, according to The Age.
- The collection reaches stores the day after the show, and a renovated Chanel boutique in Melbourne is due to open next week.

Margot Robbie flew home to Australia this week for a landmark night in local fashion. The Barbie star was among the celebrity guests at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 runway in Sydney on 5 November, the first show the luxury house has ever staged in the country, according to [The Age](https://www.theage.com.au/lifestyle/fashion/kylie-minogue-dom-dolla-and-more-attend-chanel-runway-in-sydney-20261105-p61bbx.html).

## Who was at the Chanel show in Sydney?

The Age reports that Robbie stood alongside Kidman and Luhrmann in a crowd that also took in Minogue, the producer Dom Dolla and the designer Jenny Kee. The artists Ramesh Mario Nithiyendran and [Atong Atem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atong_Atem) were there too, along with fashion editors and Chanel's top clients.

On the runway, Australian models who walked in the original Biarritz show returned, including [Julia Nobis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Nobis), Emma Balfour and Josephine Akuei. They were joined by [Gemma Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Ward), [Catherine McNeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_McNeil) and [Charlee Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlee_Fraser), plus newcomers Latahlia Hickling and Tatyana Perry, who made their Chanel debuts during Paris Fashion Week.

The Australian Women's Weekly had earlier tracked the arrivals. It reported that Robbie, a Chanel ambassador, came through Sydney Airport in black Capri pants, a crew-neck top and a blue paisley headscarf, in a look it likened to Audrey Hepburn in Roman Holiday. It also said Kidman arrived with her daughter, and that fellow ambassador Jacob Elordi was returning home for the event. At the time of that report, the full guest list had not been confirmed.

## Robbie's history with Chanel and Minogue

Robbie's ties to the brand are not new. The Women's Weekly notes that she and Minogue appeared together in Chanel's campaign for the Chanel 25 handbag. The magazine describes the two as friends of many years, who bonded over cocktails soon after Robbie moved to the United States.

The same piece quotes Elordi, her Wuthering Heights co-star, who told Fandango that anyone with the chance to share a set with Robbie would stay within a few metres of her. Chanel's ambassador roster is clearly a close-knit one: Kidman returned to the brand as an ambassador last October, more than two decades after her No5 campaign, which the magazine says cost $33 million.

## Why Chanel chose Sydney and this collection

Bruno Pavlovsky, president of fashion at Chanel, told The Age the choice was not about the beach or the surf. He said Biarritz stands for the roots of Gabrielle "Coco" Chanel, who set up her first atelier there in 1915 and began designing haute couture in the town.

Blazy's collection draws on that history, including the little black dress, which turns 100 this year and which he calls the "first revenge dress". Pavlovsky called it "a very meaningful collection" and said the house wants to grow in the Australian market. "It's the same collection, but through a new angle," he said, adding that the Sydney version would bring a different mood and energy.

The relationship is long. Chanel opened its first Australian boutique in Sydney in the 1980s, per The Age. The runway also comes just before the reopening of a heavily renovated store on Melbourne's Flinders Lane.

## What happens next

The collection goes on sale the day after the show, and the Melbourne boutique is due to open next week. The Women's Weekly reported two Sydney showings on 5 November, at 2.30pm and 6pm. Neither outlet has said whether Robbie has further Chanel appearances planned while she is in Australia.
