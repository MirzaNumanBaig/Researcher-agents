**Quick summary**

- MobLand, the crime drama starring [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy), is fiction. No real crime family is behind it, according to Startefacts.
- Hardy's fixer, Harry Da Souza, descends from the Hollywood fixer genre that began with Showtime's Ray Donovan.
- The show premiered on Paramount+ on 30 March 2025 and drew 2.2 million viewers on day one.
- Hardy's 2013 one-man thriller Locke has been added to Lionsgate+ in the UK, per Digital Spy.

Tom Hardy's MobLand is not based on a true story, but the man he plays has a surprisingly real family tree. The Paramount+ drama is the invention of writer [Ronan Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Bennett), and its fixer traces back to Hollywood rather than London, according to a Startefacts explainer published on 19 November. Meanwhile, Hardy's older film Locke has found a new home on UK streaming.

## Is Tom Hardy's MobLand a true story?

No. Startefacts reports that no real crime family or criminal case sits behind the Harrigans, the gang at the centre of the series, or their rivals, the Stevensons. Bennett did research, though. He told Creative Screenwriting he spoke to people from that world who gave him details of how things are done, and he blended what he learned into the fictional families.

That mix of invention and research is common in crime television. It also explains why the show feels grounded without pointing at any one gangland story.

## Where Harry Da Souza really comes from

The fixer is the part with a documented lineage. MobLand began as a planned spinoff of Showtime's [Ray Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Donovan), announced in February 2024 as The Donovans, with [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie) attached to direct. By October 2024 it had been renamed The Associate and every link to the Showtime series was cut. Hardy, [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) signed on. Production began in London in November 2024, with Hardy also an executive producer.

Ray Donovan ran from 2013 to 2020 and starred [Liev Schreiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liev_Schreiber) as a Hollywood fixer. Its creator, [Ann Biderman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Biderman), prepared by speaking to real fixers, and told the Jewish Journal in 2013 that these are the people you call when you cannot call the police. She never named the fixers she met. The names she did offer came from film history:

- [Howard Strickling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Strickling), MGM's publicity chief, who worked with general manager [Eddie Mannix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Mannix) from the 1930s to bury studio scandals.
- [Fred Otash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Otash), a former LAPD vice cop turned private investigator whose clients included [Peter Lawford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Lawford), [Errol Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errol_Flynn), [Bette Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bette_Davis) and [Judy Garland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judy_Garland). Lawford also hired him to look into [Marilyn Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Monroe).

Startefacts adds that Otash was part of the inspiration for Jack Nicholson's detective in [Chinatown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinatown_(1974_film)). Its conclusion is that Harry is the same type of man, moved to London and put on a crime family's payroll.

The show launched on Paramount+ on 30 March 2025 as MobLand. The platform's figures, as reported by Startefacts, put day-one viewing at 2.2 million, its biggest global series launch. Hardy's character is deliberately low-key. Esquire UK quotes costume designer Rebecca Hale saying Hardy told her everything about Harry is not meant to provoke, including his clothes. Beyond the occasional loud shirt, the fixer sticks to dark denim and unfussy layers. ## Locke lands on Lionsgate+

Fans wanting another Hardy performance have a new option in the UK. Digital Spy reports that Locke, released in 2013, has been added to Lionsgate+, which can be taken as a Prime Video channel. The outlet says it currently comes with an offer of two months at £1.25 a month, down from the usual £4.99. That pricing is for UK viewers, so check availability before you subscribe from Australia.

Locke follows Ivan Locke as he drives from Birmingham to London to be present at the birth of his child, the result of a one-night stand. Hardy is the only person on screen. Everyone else, including [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman) as his colleague Bethan, is heard only by phone. Writer and director Steven Knight also created Peaky Blinders.

The film holds a Certified Fresh 90 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes, with a 72 per cent audience score. Variety called it an exceptional one-man show for Hardy, according to Digital Spy. Digital Spy also notes Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy, another Hardy title, is on BBC iPlayer throughout November.
