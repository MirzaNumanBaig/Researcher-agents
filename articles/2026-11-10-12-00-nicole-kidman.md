**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday, 7 November.
- According to Sports Illustrated Lifestyle, she wore a cream tulle Chanel dress from the label's Spring 2027 collection, citing Red Carpet Fashion Awards.
- Glam and ELLE both singled out her long, milky almond-shaped nails as the beauty story of the night.
- This was not her first bare-nail appearance this season: she wore a similar clear finish at the Emmys and the London premiere of "Practical Magic 2".

Nicole Kidman turned heads at the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala over the weekend, pairing a layered Chanel tulle dress with a soft, glossy manicure that fashion and beauty editors have been writing about ever since. The event, held at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art on Saturday, 7 November, honoured visual artist [Vija Celmins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vija_Celmins) and film director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve).

## What Nicole Kidman wore to the LACMA gala

Writing for [Sports Illustrated Lifestyle](https://lifestyle.si.com/fashion-beauty/nicole-kidman-kylie-jenner-best-lacma-art-film-gala-2026-looks), staff writer Allie Hayes described the dress as a playful Chanel design built from lush layers of cream-coloured tulle. The outlet said it leaned into the "balletcore" trend and, citing Red Carpet Fashion Awards, placed it in Chanel's Spring 2027 collection.

Our own earlier coverage noted the same outfit, in a post on [Nicole Kidman wearing a Chanel tutu to the LACMA gala](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-chanel-tutu/). It also fits a pattern: Kidman has been a regular in Chanel recently, including at the label's Sydney event, which we covered in [Nicole Kidman's new fringe at the Chanel Sydney show](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-chanel-fringe/).

She was far from the only guest drawing attention. Sports Illustrated Lifestyle also highlighted [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner) in an Ashi Studio couture gown with a fawn-inspired metallic print, and said silver was a recurring colour of the night, with supermodel Swanepoel in a custom Gucci gown and creator [Alix Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alix_Earle) in a shimmering gown of her own. Of all the looks it picked, the outlet said it had "adored everything Kidman has worn lately".

## The manicure that got its own coverage

The dress was only half of it. Glam reported that Kidman wore the "no-mani mani", a manicure meant to look like naturally healthy, glossy nails, in the same way the no-makeup makeup look imitates bare skin. In her case, according to Glam, the natural nail was topped with a milky, mostly clear coat on a longer, almond-shaped length.

[ELLE UK](https://www.elle.com/uk/beauty/nails/a74373483/nicole-kidman-long-almond-winter-nails/) described the same nails as softly tapered and a glossy milky shade, and argued that winter manicures do not need to be dark or glittery to make an impact. Susan Carroll, founder of the beauty spa Young LDN, told the magazine the almond shape is flattering because the sides taper in before finishing in a soft, rounded point, which she said elongates the fingers. She added that keeping the colour subtle makes the whole look feel more elegant.

## A look she has worn before

Glam pointed out that Kidman has been loyal to this style for months. In September, the "Babygirl" star wore a clear finish on shorter nails at the Primetime Emmy Awards. A month earlier, in August, her nails were shorter again for the London premiere of "Practical Magic 2". The LACMA gala, then, was the longest version of a look she has already repeated at three major events.

Glam also compared her to [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), who wore the natural-nail trend at the Venice Film Festival. Both women chose longer almond shapes, according to the outlet, though Glam stressed the trend works on short nails too.

## How to get the look

ELLE's advice from Carroll is practical. Ask a nail technician for a soft almond silhouette with softly rounded tips rather than anything sharply pointed. Keep the cuticles tidy, because with such a simple manicure there is nowhere to hide imperfections. To finish, she suggested two coats of sheer milky pink polish under a glossy top coat. She also said the shape can be achieved without extensions, and that length should suit your lifestyle and comfort.
