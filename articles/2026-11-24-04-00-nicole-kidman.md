**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) wore ballet-inspired Chanel to the annual LACMA gala in Los Angeles.
- Makeup artist [Gucci Westman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gucci_Westman) created the look, using Clé de Peau Beauté products only.
- The base was the brand's Radiant Fluid Foundation in shade 010 Light Ochre, according to Woman & Home.
- Westman chose a rose-inspired palette to echo the tulle skirt of the dress.

The Nicole Kidman LACMA makeup look has drawn plenty of attention since the gala, and the artist behind it has now explained exactly how it was built. Westman told followers she wanted the skin to glow from within, and she took her cue from the dress itself, according to [Woman & Home](https://www.womanandhome.com/beauty/makeup/nicole-kidman-foundation/).

## The dress that set the tone

Kidman arrived at the Los Angeles event in Chanel. The outfit paired a long-sleeved bodice with a semi-sheer tulle skirt, a ballet-inspired shape that Woman & Home described as a standout of the awards-season calendar. Her hair was pulled into a high, deliberately messy ponytail by stylist Adir Abergel.

Westman said the delicate tulle reminded her of a rose. That image drove the colour choices on the cheeks and mouth, which she kept soft and natural rather than bronzed.

## Inside the Nicole Kidman LACMA makeup

According to Westman, every product came from Clé de Peau Beauté. The details she shared were specific:

- **Foundation:** Radiant Fluid Foundation in 010 Light Ochre.
- **Under the foundation:** The Luminizing Face Enhancer, applied only above the cheekbone for a controlled lift of light.
- **Blush:** Powder Blush Duo in 102 Cherry Blossom.
- **Lips:** Lipstick Matte in 122 Pink Petunia, a soft pink.

"I really wanted to focus on the skin and creating a gorgeous complexion," Westman said, as quoted by the publication.

## Why the foundation is getting attention

Woman & Home describes the formula as lightweight, buildable and long wearing, with a smooth, velvety finish that still keeps some radiance. It also contains the brand's Skin-Empowering Illuminator, a skincare complex the company says supports skin against everyday environmental stress.

The outlet notes the price is the catch. It lists the foundation at £88.20 in a Black Friday sale, down from £126, and suggests several cheaper alternatives for readers who want a similar feel. Those retail figures come from the publication and may change.

Clé de Peau Beauté is also popular with working artists. Woman & Home says [Lisa Eldridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_Eldridge) is among the professionals who reportedly keep it in their kits.

## A week of jewellery headlines

Kidman also appeared in the news this week in a smaller way. Following the death of Bulgari chairman Paolo Bulgari at 89, Fashion Times UK listed her among the stars who have worn the Roman jewellery house, alongside Angelina Jolie and Zendaya. The report says his funeral is scheduled for Monday, 23 November, in Rome. It does not say Kidman has commented.
