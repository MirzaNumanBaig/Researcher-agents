**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) plays Kade Auberon, the lead of *Star Wars: Starfighter*, which opens in cinemas on 28 May 2027.
- Disney has not announced any park appearance for the character, according to AllEars.Net.
- Disneyland in California widened its Galaxy's Edge timeline on 29 April 2026. The Florida park has not followed.
- New Star Wars park features have tended to reach California first.

A character does not need to be in cinemas yet to start a conversation about the theme parks. Fans of [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) are asking whether Kade Auberon, the hero of [*Star Wars: Starfighter*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars%3A_Starfighter), will one day roam Galaxy's Edge. Disney has said nothing either way, but a [report from AllEars.Net](https://allears.net/2026/11/19/will-ryan-goslings-new-star-wars-character-show-up-in-galaxys-edge/) lays out why it is a reasonable bet.

## What we know about Star Wars: Starfighter

The film is set for cinemas on 28 May 2027. It is directed by [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) and written by [George Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lucas) and [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper), according to AllEars.Net. It is a standalone story rather than a direct sequel to the [Skywalker Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_films).

The supporting cast includes [Flynn Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flynn_Gray), [Mia Goth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Goth), [Matt Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Smith), [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams), [Simon Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Bird), [Jamael Westman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamael_Westman) and [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings), alongside Gosling. The listing also names Aaron Pierce.

At the 2026 D23 Expo, Levy said the story takes place in a period of the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) timeline that has never been seen on screen. He described its themes as hope, family and adventure. The audience also saw an exclusive first look at the film.

## Who is Kade Auberon?

Gosling plays the central role. Levy described Kade at D23 as a man from the wrong side of the galaxy whose fate is tied to the fastest starfighter ever built. That is all the public has been told so far, and the plot summary on IMDb remains a single line about an unexplored period of time.

## What Galaxy's Edge offers right now

AllEars.Net says six characters currently meet guests at Galaxy's Edge in Disney's Hollywood Studios in Florida. They include roaming [First Order Stormtroopers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stormtrooper_(Star_Wars)) near the TIE Echelon, and The Mandalorian and Grogu around Black Spire Market. [Darth Vader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darth_Vader) meets guests too, but outside the land, near Backlot Express in Echo Lake close to [Star Tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Tours).

California is further ahead. On 29 April 2026, Disneyland Resort changed the Galaxy's Edge timeline to cover all of Star Wars, including the original trilogy, *The Mandalorian* and *Ahsoka*. That brought in Princess Leia, Han Solo, Luke Skywalker and Ahsoka Tano. Florida has not been confirmed to follow.

## Why California would likely go first

The outlet points to a pattern. The Batuu Adventure Buddies reached Disneyland on 13 August 2026 and arrived at Disney World on 17 November 2026, a gap of about three months. By that logic, Kade could appear in California before Florida, and possibly well after the film's release. AllEars.Net is clear that this is its own expectation and that Disney has confirmed nothing.

Disney has shown it is willing to change the land as the franchise grows, which makes a future Kade meet-and-greet plausible rather than certain.

Nothing is scheduled for the parks yet. The next markers are Disney's own announcements and the film's release on 28 May 2027. If a park appearance is planned, it would most likely be revealed closer to that date.
