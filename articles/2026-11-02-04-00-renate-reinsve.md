**Quick summary**

- A rumour published on 1 November 2026 says [Renate Reinsve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Reinsve) has met Marvel Studios about a role in its X-Men reboot.
- The part is reportedly Moira MacTaggert, and it would likely be a brief appearance.
- Knight Edge Media first reported the claim, according to ComicBookMovie.com.
- Marvel has not confirmed anything, and the report rests on a single source.
- The X-Men film is due in cinemas on 5 May 2028.

Norwegian actor Renate Reinsve is reportedly in talks with Marvel Studios about joining the X-Men reboot, according to a rumour covered by ComicBookMovie.com on 1 November 2026. The report says she has met the studio about playing Moira MacTaggert. It is unconfirmed, and neither Marvel nor Reinsve has commented.

## What the rumour says

ComicBookMovie.com says the claim began with Knight Edge Media, after some chatter on social media the month before. The site describes Reinsve as the star of *Backrooms* and says the character would probably appear only briefly.

Moira MacTaggert was played in the Marvel Cinematic Universe by Rose Byrne, who made her an American CIA agent. The report says the reboot's director, Jake Schreier, wants a European actress for the part. That would bring the character closer to her comic-book roots as a Scottish geneticist.

## Why the casting would be a surprise

ComicBookMovie.com notes that Reinsve has said in the past that superhero roles do not interest her. The site suggests a smaller part like Moira, and the chance to work with Schreier, could change her mind. That is the outlet's reading, not something Reinsve has said about this project.

Readers who follow her work can find more in our [Renate Reinsve coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/renate-reinsve/), including a recent piece on [her role in Cristian Mungiu's Fjord](https://moduscarry.com/renate-reinsve-shines-in-cristian-mungius-fjord-critic-says/).

## Who else is reportedly in the film

The same report lists several other names tied to the reboot. None of this is confirmed by Marvel in the material we read:

- Kit Connor as Scott Summers
- Sadie Sink as Jean Grey
- Inde Navarrette as Rogue
- Asa Germann as Angel
- Maya Boyd as Storm
- Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier
- Samara Weaving as Emma Frost
- Adam Driver as Nathaniel Milbury, said to be an alias of Mister Sinister

ComicBookMovie.com adds that signs point to a story set in the First Class era, with Mister Sinister and the Hellfire Club involved. It also says rumours suggest Apocalypse could be the main villain of the next Marvel saga. These are all unverified.

## Who Moira MacTaggert is

According to the report, Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum created Moira, who first appeared in *Uncanny X-Men* #96. Her comic history is tangled. She later became a mutant with reincarnation powers, helped found the mutant nation of Krakoa, and then sided with the anti-mutant group Orchis. A brief film appearance would only touch part of that.

## What happens next

Marvel has not announced a cast for the reboot, so the next sign would be an official statement from the studio or from Reinsve's representatives. Until then, treat the report as a rumour.
