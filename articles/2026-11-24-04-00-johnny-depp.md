**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) plays Ebenezer Scrooge in *Ebenezer*, a new film of Charles Dickens' *A Christmas Carol*, released in cinemas on 13 November.
- Patheos critic Kate O'Hare says director [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West) brings a Gothic look, dark humour and some jump scares.
- The same review reports almost US$47 million worldwide so far.
- An IMDb headline says the film slid down the US box office chart in its second weekend.

*Ebenezer* is giving a very old story a darker coat of paint. Johnny Depp stars as Scrooge in the new adaptation of [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens)' 1843 novella, which opened on 13 November. A review published on 22 November by Kate O'Hare at Patheos says it has earned almost US$47 million worldwide, and an IMDb news headline from 23 November says it dropped down the box office chart in its second weekend.

## What kind of Christmas Carol is this?

According to [Patheos](https://www.patheos.com/blogs/kateohare/2026/11/ebenezer-johnny-depp-dickens-christmas-carol/), the film is a US and UK co-production shot in the UK and Dublin, and released in the US by Paramount Pictures. Ti West, best known for horror films, directs from a script by Nathaniel Halpern, whose credits are mostly in television, including *Tales From the Loop* and *Legion*.

O'Hare describes West's approach as distinctly Gothic, with dark humour on top. She says it stops well short of splatter horror, but there are disturbing images and jump scares. Crows gather, zombie-like spirits wail in the streets and mist drifts through graveyards.

The review also points to an unusual take on the ghosts. The Ghost of Christmas Past, played by Andrea Riseborough, is far more wraithlike than usual. Tramell Tillman's Ghost of Christmas Present skips the jolly start and goes straight to the darker turn.

## Depp and the supporting cast

Depp's Scrooge arrives with muttonchops, a pointed nose and what O'Hare calls a snarling attitude. Ian McKellen (credited in the review as "McKellan") plays his business partner Jacob Marley and later Marley's ghost. [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint) is the clerk Bob Cratchit, while [Sam Claflin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Claflin) and [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) play Scrooge's nephew Fred and his wife.

The reviewer places Depp in a long line of Scrooges, naming [Alistair Sim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alastair_Sim), [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart) and [George C. Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_C._Scott) among them. She estimates there have been around 200 screen versions, which she puts down partly to the story being in the public domain.

## Why the faith angle stands out

The most distinctive point in the review is the religious content. O'Hare writes that the Christian side of Christmas is more present here than in any other version she has seen, with church scenes, a preached Gospel message and a reminder of mortality. Faith, mercy, grace, repentance and forgiveness are central themes, she says, and one sequence features haunting renditions of *Silent Night*.

She also notes that the script follows the broad outline of the novella with some variations and a slightly different ending, though she reassures readers that Tiny Tim is not given a changed fate. Some of Dickens' best-known lines are kept, while she missed others.

## The box office picture

The financial picture is less clear. The only figure available from the sources is O'Hare's near-US$47 million worldwide total. The IMDb story reporting the film's slide in its second weekend could not be read in full, so no weekend takings or chart position are reported here. A second-weekend drop is common for wide releases, but the size of this one is not confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Scrooge in Ebenezer?

Johnny Depp plays Ebenezer Scrooge, according to Patheos. Ian McKellen plays Jacob Marley, Rupert Grint plays Bob Cratchit, and Sam Claflin and Daisy Ridley play Fred and his wife.

### Who directed Ebenezer?

Ti West, known mainly for horror films, directed it from a script by Nathaniel Halpern.

### When did Ebenezer open?

It reached cinemas on 13 November, and Paramount Pictures is releasing it in the US.
