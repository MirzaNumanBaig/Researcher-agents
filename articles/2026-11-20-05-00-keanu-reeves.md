**Quick summary**

- The Matrix began streaming free on Pluto TV in November and now sits at No. 5 among the service's movies, according to FlixPatrol figures cited by Comic Book Resources.
- The 1999 film earned US$473.3 million worldwide on a US$63 million budget.
- A fifth Matrix film is in development with Drew Goddard, but a rights dispute with the Wachowskis could complicate it.
- Separately, [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is set to star in and produce the spy thriller Kill Current, with no director or release date announced yet.

**Keanu Reeves' sci-fi classic The Matrix has turned into a free streaming hit, reaching No. 5 on Pluto TV's movie chart within weeks of arriving on the platform.** [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) reports, citing FlixPatrol, that the film sits between Quentin Tarantino's Django Unchained at No. 4 and the Christmas comedy [Bad Santa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Santa) at No. 6. It is a timely reminder of how much pull Reeves still has, just as he lines up a new action project.

## Why The Matrix is back in the charts

The film joined Pluto TV's free, ad-supported library in November. According to Comic Book Resources, it climbed into the service's Top 10 quickly, a strong result for a movie that is more than a quarter of a century old.

The story follows a computer hacker named Thomas Anderson, who learns that the world he lives in is a shared illusion. As Neo, he joins a band of rebels fighting the machines that rule Earth. Reeves leads the cast alongside [Laurence Fishburne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Fishburne) as Morpheus, [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) as Trinity, [Hugo Weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugo_Weaving) as [Agent Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent_Smith) and [Joe Pantoliano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Pantoliano) as Cypher. The [Wachowskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wachowskis) directed.

## The numbers behind the phenomenon

When it opened in 1999, The Matrix made US$473.3 million at the global box office from a production budget of US$63 million, according to Comic Book Resources. That is roughly 7.5 times its cost.

Critics were just as enthusiastic. The film holds an 83 per cent score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), with an 85 per cent positive audience score. The site's critics' consensus praised its mix of spectacular action and groundbreaking effects, a nod to the "bullet time" technique that reviewers singled out at the time.

The franchise grew into two sequels, The Matrix Reloaded and The Matrix Revolutions, the anime anthology The Animatrix, and several video games. Warner Bros. revived it in 2021 with The Matrix Resurrections, which the outlet describes as a box office underperformer.

## A fifth Matrix film, and a rights fight

Warner Bros. has hired [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard) to write and direct a new Matrix film. He told Variety, as quoted by Comic Book Resources, that the story is still being written and that he feels the weight of doing right by the fans and the original creators.

Whether it moves ahead is less certain. Comic Book Resources reports that the Wachowskis are trying to reclaim the rights to the property, two years before Warner Bros.' copyright expires. That is a surprise, given that the filmmakers have reportedly supported Goddard's project. The outlet adds that it is unclear whether Warner Bros.' merger with Paramount Skydance played a part, and that both sides appear to be preparing for a long legal battle and possible renegotiation.

## Kill Current: Reeves' next action project

Reeves is also heading back to spy territory. TVovermind reports he will star in and produce Kill Current, a geopolitical thriller adapted from Oliver Lowe's forthcoming novel Kill Current: A Jon Hendricks Thriller. He would play Hendricks, a burned-out CIA operative pulled back into espionage after his former protégé is killed. The story is built around microchips, supply chains and technological warfare rather than Cold War intrigue.

The film is the first project for Two Dragonflies, a production company founded by Reeves and his partner, the artist [Alexandra Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandra_Grant). The Arena, the independent studio launched by former Lionsgate chief Erik Feig, has secured the rights to finance and produce it. TVovermind notes the novel is meant to start a series, which hints at franchise ambitions.

TVovermind also lists other Reeves titles in the pipeline: the comedy drama The Entertainment System Is Down, with Samantha Morton and Kirsten Dunst, and Tim Miller's time-loop adventure Shiver. For more on his recent streaming news, see our story on [Point Break leaving Netflix on 1 December](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-point-break-netflix/).

## What happens next

No director, supporting cast or release date has been announced for Kill Current, so the next step is a filmmaker who can handle both the action and the political tension.
