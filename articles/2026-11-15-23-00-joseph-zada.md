**Quick summary**

- Australian actor [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), 21, plays the young Haymitch Abernathy in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- He told Empire he deliberately avoided copying Woody Harrelson's version of the character.
- The Canberra Times reports that thousands of young men auditioned before Zada won the part at 19.
- The film opens in Australian cinemas on November 19, according to The Canberra Times, and in the United States on November 20.

Joseph Zada has spent the week moving from fan events to talk shows, and the reason is a single role. The 21-year-old Australian plays the teenage Haymitch Abernathy in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, the second prequel in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series, which reaches Australian cinemas on November 19, according to [The Canberra Times](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9390483/joseph-zada-lands-major-hunger-games-role-as-young-haymitch/).

## Why Zada did not copy Woody Harrelson

Haymitch is the cynical, hard-drinking mentor from the original films, played by [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson). Zada says he never tried to imitate him. "This is a completely different person at a very different part of their lives," he told Empire for its December 2026 issue, as reported by [SYFY](https://www.syfy.com/syfy-wire/hunger-games-why-young-haymitch-actor-did-not-copy-woody-harrelson). "I wanted to try and make a very big contrast between the two."

Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) backed that approach. He described the young Haymitch as "very apolitical", a kid from the poorest district who is "kind of fine with his life". The story is set 24 years before Katniss Everdeen enters the arena, during the Second Quarter Quell, which doubled the number of tributes from each district. Lawrence called it "one of the more tragic of the stories", balanced with spectacle and some humour.

Zada told The Canberra Times he started far from Harrelson's performance and then set himself the job of bridging the gap over the shoot.

## How an Australian teenager won the part

Casting was a huge search. The Canberra Times says thousands of young men put themselves on tape. One Spanish-language outlet, Foro3D, puts the figure at more than 2,700 candidates, though that number comes from a single report.

Zada was 19 and close to finishing Netflix's *East of Eden*, where he played Cal Trask, a role [James Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dean) once took, when the audition arrived. Producer [Nina Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Jacobson) said the character has an "inherent" mischief, "a quality that you kind of have or you don't". [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin), who plays Caesar Flickerman, said he realised only after seeing the finished film how good Zada was, and told him: "You're going to be around a while."

## A tough shoot, from Spain to Germany

The production ran for five months and moved the main cast from Spain to Germany, with many real locations. Zada and co-star [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), who plays Maysilee Donner, worked six-day weeks on physical arena sequences. Scenes were shot out of order. Zada and Whitney Peak, who plays Lenore Dove, filmed the first and last scenes of the film back to back and did not know which order they would go in until late. That, he says, "freaked me out so much". They shot the opening scene first.

He also found the role of leader hard. "Having young actors look to you to set the pace and the tone" was the toughest part, he said.

The supporting cast is large. [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) plays [President Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow), [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) is Plutarch Heavensbee, [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) is Effie Trinket and Glenn Close plays Drusilla Sickle.

## The press tour so far

Zada appeared on [The Jennifer Hudson Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Hudson), where he surprised a viral superfan who had missed meeting him by four minutes, [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Video-Joseph-Zada-Surprises-Superfan-Who-Missed-Him-by-4-Minutes-on-THE-Jennifer-Hudson-SHOW-20261114) reported on November 14. On Friday he and Grace attended a red-carpet fan event in Mexico City, according to the [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/lifestyle/fashion/mckenna-grace-brings-mockingjays-to-the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-fan-event-in-mexico-with-shimmering-sculptural-gown-101794624817798.html).

The franchise is a big one to join. The Canberra Times notes the novels have sold more than 100 million copies and the films have grossed US$3.3 billion. Zada was about seven when the first film came out, and like many of his castmates he grew up on it. ## What happens next

The film opens in Australia on November 19 and in the United States on November 20, and is rated PG-13 for strong violence, bloody images, disturbing material, some smoking and teen drinking, according to SYFY. Woody Harrelson, Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson are also set to appear, SYFY reports. Critics' first verdicts on Zada should follow within days.
