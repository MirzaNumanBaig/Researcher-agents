**Quick summary**

- A new trailer for *Ted: The Animated Series* confirms that [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) returns as John's wife, Sam.
- Eight episodes arrive on Peacock on December 17, 2026, with more to follow in 2027.
- [Mark Wahlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Wahlberg) and creator [Seth MacFarlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_MacFarlane) also return.
- Separately, Elizabeth Holmes' partner Billy Evans says he misread why Seyfried visited him in *You Can See Everything*.

Amanda Seyfried is heading back to the world of the foul-mouthed teddy bear. Peacock released a red-band trailer this week for *Ted: The Animated Series*, and Seyfried is confirmed to reprise her *Ted 2* role as Sam, according to [The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/ted-the-animated-series-trailer-seth-macfarlane-mark-wahlberg-peacock-20261117/). The same week, her scenes in the documentary *You Can See Everything* are back in the conversation. Here is what is known about both.

## What is Ted: The Animated Series?

The show is a sequel to the films, not a continuation of Peacock's live-action prequel. That earlier series followed John and Ted as teenagers in the 1990s. The new one moves forward and is set 12 years after the events of 2015's *Ted 2*, per The Playlist.

John and Ted now live next door to each other. Both are married, raising families and stuck in dead-end jobs, according to [Toy People](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=116680). Their favourite pastime is trading bad advice about marriage and parenting. The Playlist notes that neither has dropped the drug use and poor judgement that defined their friendship.

## Who is in the cast?

The core film cast comes back. Wahlberg plays the adult John, Seyfried plays Sam, and MacFarlane voices Ted and also created the series. Jessica Barth returns as Ted's wife, Tami-Lynn. Kyle Mooney and Liz Richman are new additions, and Richman also appeared in the live-action prequel as John's high school classmate Polly, as Toy People reports.

For Seyfried, the role is a return to comedy after a stretch of heavier projects. Neither source says how large Sam's part is, so it is too early to say how much she will feature in each episode.

## Why is Seyfried in the news for a documentary too?

Seyfried also appears in *You Can See Everything*, the documentary co-directed by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder). One of its most uncomfortable scenes is a dinner at the Texas home of Holmes' family. Seyfried and Fielder visit after Holmes has been jailed on fraud convictions, and Seyfried ends up in a tense exchange with Evans, Holmes' partner, over how he treats Holmes, according to [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/amanda-seyfried-elizabeth-holmes-partner-billy-evans-had-very-different-intentions-infamous-dinner-scene-you-can-see-everything).

Evans has now told The Hollywood Reporter that he did not understand her intentions at the time. He says he believed he was offering an olive branch: a message that the couple did not blame her for playing Holmes in *The Dropout*, since an actor does not write the script or research the story. Only after watching the film, and hearing Seyfried say she wanted to break the couple up, did the evening make sense to him, he said.

CinemaBlend's writer argues that Seyfried never intended to seek forgiveness. By that point in the film, the site says, she had developed sympathy for Holmes. That is the outlet's reading of the scene, not something Seyfried has said in the sources reviewed here. The footage she had watched with Fielder was filmed before Holmes reported to prison in 2023.

Evans has been speaking about the film more often, in interviews and in a Reddit AMA, per CinemaBlend, which calls it a box office hit.
