[Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) and [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) have joined forces to salute [Guillermo del Toro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guillermo_del_Toro) as his 2006 fantasy [Pan's Labyrinth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan's_Labyrinth) heads towards a 4K release for its 20th anniversary. According to [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-cate-blanchett-jacob-elordi-honor-guillermo-del-toro-ahead-of-4k-release-of-pans-labyrinth), the two actors appear in a clip from the anniversary edition that has since spread widely online.

## What Blanchett and Elordi said

Netflix Junkie reports that Collider first released the clip exclusively a couple of days earlier. In it, both actors describe del Toro as a standard-bearer for art house cinema.

Blanchett said del Toro "cares about his audience, and he cares about his collaborators, and the rest can be dealt with". As Netflix Junkie describes it, she links art house filmmaking to creative freedom and bravery, the idea of breaking barriers to make something new. She sees del Toro doing that each time he sets out to tell a story.

Elordi gave a more personal read. He says the director follows a voice in his head and that everything he does is meant to satisfy it. Near the end of the clip, Blanchett adds that del Toro does not listen to anything or anyone that pulls him away from his vision.

## Why Pan's Labyrinth still matters

Set in Spain in 1944, after the Civil War, the film follows a young girl named Ofelia. She escapes her oppressive stepfather by entering a dark underground world ruled by mythical creatures, among them the Pale Man and the Faun, according to Netflix Junkie.

The outlet notes that del Toro combined practical creature effects, cinematography and the brutality of real history. The film won three Academy Awards: for art direction, cinematography and makeup. Netflix Junkie calls it one of the defining films of the 21st century, which is the outlet's view rather than an official ranking.

## What happens next

The report does not give a release date or list the extras on the 4K edition, so those details are still unconfirmed. What it does confirm is that the anniversary edition is on the way and that the Blanchett and Elordi clip is part of its promotion.
