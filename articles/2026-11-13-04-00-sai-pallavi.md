**Quick summary**

- [Lara Dutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Dutta) has defended [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi)'s portrayal of Sita as a fighter in *Ramayana*, according to The Times of India.
- Dutta told NDTV viewers should "just enjoy the cinematic experience".
- The film, directed by [Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari), opened in theatres on 5 November 2026.
- Criticism has shifted from the casting to one sequence showing Sita in a warrior avatar.

Lara Dutta has stepped into the debate over Sai Pallavi's Sita, arguing that a princess trained in weapons is not a stretch. Speaking to NDTV about *Ramayana*, which reached cinemas on 5 November, Dutta said audiences should embrace the film as cinema, according to [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/hindi/bollywood/news/lara-dutta-defends-sai-pallavis-warrior-sita-in-nitesh-tiwaris-ramayana-rise-of-a-legend-youre-not-going-to-be-a-helpless-woman-in-that-situation/articleshow/135767194.cms). Her comments land a week after release, as a fresh round of criticism targets how the film shows Sita.

## What Lara Dutta said about the warrior Sita

Dutta pointed out that there are many versions of the *Ramayana*. She argued that, in the culture of those times, children born into royal families were generally given some training in the arts and in weaponry.

On that basis, she said, it is not far-fetched to imagine Sita was also trained in warfare. A woman placed in that situation, she added, would not be helpless. She also reasoned that a woman who chooses a 14-year forest exile would grow strong and resilient, and at some point "will pick up something to defend herself".

Her closing line was blunt: people should stop being so small-minded and embrace the cinematic experience.

## How the film shows Sita

According to The Times of India's description, this adaptation does not leave Sita waiting to be rescued. She takes part in farming work and teaches local children and young people sword fighting and combat. The outlet calls her a trained warrior princess.

The biggest moment comes during the exile. Sita stands alongside Ram and Lakshman in the forest, armed with twin swords and a bow, and fights demons and other dangers directly.

Ram is played by [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor). The cast list in the same report also includes Yash, [Ravi Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey), [Arun Govil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arun_Govil), [Rakul Preet Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakul_Preet_Singh), [Indira Krishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Krishnan), [Ajinkya Deo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ajinkya_Deo) and [Vivek Oberoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivek_Oberoi). Dutta herself plays Kaikeyi, the third wife of King Dasharatha and mother of Bharata, who claims the two boons that send Rama into exile.

## A casting debate that has moved on

Sai Pallavi's casting has been a talking point since the first glimpse of the film. The Times of India says early objections focused on her looks, with some online claiming she did not fit the image of a goddess, and her hair texture and accent were also picked apart. Now that the film is in theatres, the criticism has narrowed to the warrior sequence.
