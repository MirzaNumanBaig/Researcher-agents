**Quick summary**

- [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger)'s 2001 film A Knight's Tale is due to join Netflix on 1 December 2026.
- The date comes from What's on Netflix, as reported by ComingSoon.net.
- Ledger plays William Thatcher, a squire who competes in jousting tournaments under a false identity.
- Critics were split on release, but audiences rated it well above the reviewers.

[Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger)'s medieval adventure A Knight's Tale is heading to Netflix on 1 December 2026, according to What's on Netflix, as relayed by [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2228262-heath-ledger-a-knights-tale-new-streaming-home-netflix-release-date). The film arrived in cinemas on 11 May 2001, and the move gives a new audience an easy way to find one of the actor's most liked early roles.

## What is A Knight's Tale about?

Ledger plays William Thatcher, a young squire who sees competitive jousting as a way out of his humble beginnings. When the knight he serves dies during a tournament, William takes his place under that knight's name and wins.

Keen for more victories, he persuades his friends Roland and Wat to travel with him from tournament to tournament. His lack of noble blood is a problem, so he turns to a little-known writer named [Geoffrey Chaucer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geoffrey_Chaucer), who agrees to forge papers proving a royal family tree. Chaucer then acts as his herald as William faces tougher opponents and chases a romantic interest.

[Brian Helgeland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Helgeland) wrote and directed the film, which is set in medieval England.

## Who is in the cast?

Ledger leads the film. [Mark Addy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Addy) plays Roland and [Alan Tudyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Tudyk) plays Wat. [Paul Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bettany) appears as Chaucer, [Rufus Sewell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rufus_Sewell) plays Count Adhemar of Anjou, and [Shannyn Sossamon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shannyn_Sossamon) plays Jocelyn.

ComingSoon.net notes the film remains one of Ledger's best-loved titles beyond his acclaimed performance in The Dark Knight. For readers who follow the actor's career, our [Heath Ledger archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/heath-ledger/) collects everything we have published about him.

## How was it received?

ComingSoon.net describes the response as mixed from critics but stronger from audiences. On Rotten Tomatoes, the film holds a 59 percent critics' score from 152 reviews. The audience score is considerably higher at 79 percent, a gap of 20 points.

[Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times) was among the more enthusiastic voices, calling it "tremendous, infectious fun" with a rocking soundtrack and a cast that enters into the spirit of it, as quoted by ComingSoon.net.
