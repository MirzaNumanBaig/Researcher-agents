**Quick summary**

- Actor [Aaron Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Pierre_(actor)) says [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) recommended him to director [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) for *Star Wars: Starfighter*.
- Pierre told IndieWire's ScreenTalk podcast the introduction followed a read-through for *Love of Your Life*, which Gosling produced.
- A long FaceTime call with Levy ended with the role being offered, according to Pierre.
- *Starfighter* is in post-production and is scheduled for release on 28 May 2027.

Ryan Gosling's backing helped [Aaron Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Pierre_(actor)) win a place in *Star Wars: Starfighter*, according to Pierre himself. Speaking on IndieWire's ScreenTalk podcast, the *Rebel Ridge* actor said Gosling passed his name to the film's director, and a single video call did the rest. The account was reported on 23 November by [Star Wars News Net](https://www.starwarsnewsnet.com/2026/11/aaron-pierre-says-ryan-gosling-helped-secure-his-star-wars-starfighter-role.html).

## How the introduction happened

Pierre said Gosling was a producer on *Love of Your Life*, a project he was developing as it came together. After the cast's read-through, Pierre said, Gosling contacted Levy and put his name forward for a particular character in *Starfighter*. Pierre added that he only knows this because Gosling told him.

The film itself has a public track record. According to [Wikipedia's entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_of_Your_Life_(film)), *Love of Your Life* was released on 13 September 2026 and directed by Rachel Morrison. Pierre appears in the cast alongside Margaret Qualley and Patrick Schwarzenegger. The same entry puts its Rotten Tomatoes score at 81 per cent from 57 reviews. No box office figures are listed yet, so there is nothing to say about how it has performed commercially.

## The call that settled it

Within days of Gosling's message, Pierre said, he and Levy spoke on FaceTime. He described the call as long, and said most of it had nothing to do with work. Levy later told him that he had only meant to get to know him at that stage, with the idea of asking him to read for the part down the line.

The work talk that did happen covered the character, the script and how each of them approaches the job. Pierre said Levy ended the call by telling him the role was his if he wanted it. His reply, as he tells it, was that there was no version of him saying no.

He also said the rapport carried onto the set. He called the shoot a great time and singled out working with Gosling as a highlight. These are Pierre's own recollections, and neither Levy nor Gosling is quoted in the report.

## Where Starfighter stands

Per Star Wars News Net, *Starfighter* wrapped shooting in late 2025 and is now in post-production, running ahead of schedule for its 28 May 2027 release. Levy directs from a script by [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper), and the outlet describes the film as the start of a new era for the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) franchise.

The cast named in the report is Gosling, [Flynn Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flynn_Gray), [Matt Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Smith), [Mia Goth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Goth), Pierre, [Simon Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Bird), [Jamael Westman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamael_Westman), [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings) and [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams). Pierre has not said which character he plays, and the source does not say either.
