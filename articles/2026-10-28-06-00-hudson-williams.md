**Quick summary**

- *Yaga*, starring [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams), premiered quietly on AMC+ on 23 October.
- As of 25 October it held a 95% audience score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), according to MovieWeb.
- Critics are warmer but more measured, at 81%.
- The first two episodes are streaming; the other six arrive weekly on Fridays.

[Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) has a hit on his hands with his first major role since *Heated Rivalry*. His eight-part thriller *Yaga* launched on AMC+ on 23 October with little fanfare, and by 25 October it had a 95% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, [MovieWeb reported](https://movieweb.com/yaga-rotten-tomatoes-score/).

## How the audience and critics are responding

The audience number is the headline. Only two days after launch, viewers on the aggregator were already rating the series close to the top of the scale. MovieWeb says some say they can barely wait for episode three, due next Friday. One reviewer singled out the cinematography, the soundtrack and the cast.

Critics sit lower at 81%, which is still a strong score. According to MovieWeb, the critical consensus calls the show a darkly comic supernatural mystery, praising its mix of small-town gossip and witchy woods. Some critics were less convinced by the characters' questionable desires.

The gap between the two scores is typical for a new series: a small group of early reviewers, and viewers who sought it out on purpose.

## What Yaga is about

The series is adapted by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) from her own stage play and reimagines Baba Yaga, the witch of Slavic folklore who lives in a hut in the forest. Williams plays Henry Park, a billionaire heir who goes missing. His worried parents hire investigator Rapp, played by [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid), who works with fellow investigator Carson, played by [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo). Their search leads to rural British Columbia.

[Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) and [Patrick Gilmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Gilmore_(actor)) also star. When the first-look images were released in July, [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/07/23/first-look-at-hudson-williams-in-new-witchy-thriller-yaga/) said Sandler described the show as "dark, funny, and unapologetically bold" and noted it was her first greenlit series.

## Why this role matters for Williams

Williams became widely known through [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), the Canadian sports romance series. PinkNews called *Yaga* a notable next step after that breakout, and the early scores suggest audiences are following him to a very different genre.

The timing helps too. MovieWeb notes the series landed just ahead of Halloween, and Baba Yaga is a figure many viewers will know in some form, including a version in [*Spirited Away*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirited_Away).

## What happens next

The first two episodes are on AMC+, with the remaining episodes released weekly on Fridays. That puts the finale in mid-December. Audience and critic scores can shift as more reviews arrive, so the current numbers are an early reading only.
