**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) is reportedly not planning any public comment on her ex-husband's imprisonment, according to an unnamed source quoted by New Idea.
- The same source says she is concentrating on her relationship with former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau). None of this is confirmed on the record.
- [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) is back in the news because of his conviction and newly reported court transcripts from a separate civil case in New York.
- Perry is not accused of any wrongdoing in that case, according to bsinews.id.

## Katy Perry stays quiet as the headlines return

Katy Perry is reportedly choosing silence while her former husband's legal troubles dominate the news. According to a source quoted by Australian magazine [New Idea](https://www.newidea.com.au/celebrity/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-russell-brand/), the singer will not make a statement about Brand's imprisonment. The same insider claims she has told friends the situation brought her "some closure".

That is a single anonymous account, and Perry has not said anything publicly that matches it. Treat it as reported, not established.

New Idea says Brand was recently convicted on rape charges and is serving a 10-year sentence. The [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/showbiz/russell-brand-forgot-married-katy-37901611) puts the term at 10 years and six months and notes that Brand denied wrongdoing throughout his trial. The two outlets differ slightly on the length, so the exact figure is worth checking against court records.

## The relationship with Justin Trudeau

Perry and Trudeau have reportedly been together since July 2025. New Idea says they first connected while she was on her [Lifetimes Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lifetimes_Tour) in Canada, and were seen on a dinner date in Montreal. It adds that Perry, 42, has described the 54-year-old as the "love of my life", and has called the romance "super healing".

The magazine also reports a recent outing. On 14 November, Perry, Trudeau and her daughter Daisy were seen on a bike ride and running errands near her home in Montecito, California. That evening the couple attended the Baby2Baby gala in West Hollywood.

Daisy, who is six, is Perry's daughter with actor [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom). The pair ended their engagement in June 2025 after about six years, according to New Idea. Trudeau has three children with his former wife, Sophie.

## What the Brand court documents say about Perry

The renewed attention follows newly reported transcripts from a civil lawsuit in New York. bsinews.id, citing transcripts obtained by the Daily Mail, says the suit was brought by a woman identified as Jane Doe, who worked as an extra on the film Arthur. Brand denies her allegations and says his encounters were consensual.

Perry appears only as part of the timeline. Brand and Perry met in 2009 through his film Get Him to the Greek, in which she had a cameo, and married in October 2010. The alleged incident is dated to July 2010, about three months before the wedding. The Daily Star says the marriage lasted about 14 months, with the divorce finalised in 2012.

In his testimony Brand said he could not pin down exactly when he was married, and described being "sort of drowning in love" with a very high-profile woman. bsinews.id stresses that these comments are not an admission about the specific incident, and that Perry has not been accused of anything.

For more on that testimony, see our earlier reports, [Katy Perry Marriage Raised in Russell Brand Deposition](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-russell-brand-deposition/) and [Russell Brand Deposition: Katy Perry Marriage Questions](https://moduscarry.com/russell-brand-deposition-katy-perry/).

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled that would force a response from Perry. The civil case in New York continues separately from Brand's criminal conviction in the United Kingdom, according to bsinews.id.

On the personal side, New Idea's source claims Trudeau could propose at Christmas. That is speculation from an unnamed insider, and neither Perry nor Trudeau has said anything about engagement.
