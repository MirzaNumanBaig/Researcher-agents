**Quick summary**

- Sylvester Stallone I Play Rocky update: [Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) told USA Today that [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) was "fantastic" playing him in *I Play Rocky*.
- The film, directed by [Peter Farrelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Farrelly), is in select US theatres now and expands nationwide on November 20.
- Reviews praise Ippolito, though one critic found his Stallone "too sweet and saintly".
- A 4K UHD SteelBook of the original *Rocky* arrives on December 15 for its 50th anniversary.

The Sylvester Stallone I Play Rocky story has a new chapter: Stallone has blessed the actor who plays him in the comedy-drama about how the original Rocky got made. Speaking to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2026/11/18/anthony-ippolito-sylvester-stallone-i-play-rocky/92793009007/), Stallone called 27-year-old Ippolito "fantastic" as the film heads from limited release to a nationwide opening on November 20.

## Stallone on watching someone play him

Stallone told USA Today he never believed the project would work, asking: "Do you understand how surreal this is watching someone play you?" He still backed the casting, adding that Ippolito is "a nice kid" and that he hoped the young actor "didn't get punched too much".

He also explained why the film needed a newcomer rather than a name. According to Stallone, the part calls for a kind of naivete, and for the "fake it till you make it" bravado he says he had to put on in the 1970s. In his view, Ippolito pulls that off.

That fits what the film's casting director told the Motion Picture Association's [The Credits](https://www.motionpictures.org/2026/11/a-knockout-performance-i-play-rocky-casting-director-rick-montgomery-on-finding-his-young-stallone/). Rick Montgomery said Stallone had final approval on who played him, and that Stallone suggested to Farrelly that an unknown would be ideal. Ippolito sent in an unsolicited audition tape of four scenes, which Montgomery described as "never an impersonation".

## How Ippolito built the role

Ippolito, who previously played a young Al Pacino in the Paramount+ series *The Offer*, told USA Today he gained 40 pounds for the part through a strict protein and calorie plan. He trained at Gleason's Gym in Brooklyn alongside co-star Stephan James. The casting director said he spent a year training and learning to box.

He recorded his audition tape in his grandmother's basement, he said, after tracking down Peter Gamble's script.

## What reviews of Sylvester Stallone I Play Rocky say

Most early reviews lean warm. The Las Vegas Review-Journal called the film a "comic and crowd-pleasing" tale and said Ippolito nails Stallone's demeanour and voice without slipping into caricature. It also had a reservation: the performance is "too sweet and saintly", and the film runs low on steam once *Rocky* starts shooting.

The review site [Movies We Texted About](https://movieswetextedabout.com/i-play-rocky-movie-review-anthony-ippolitos-transformative-performance-as-a-young-sylvester-stallone/) went further, calling it a "genuinely transformative performance" and suggesting Ippolito could enter the awards conversation.

The film's centrepiece is the fight over Stallone's script. The Review-Journal reports that he turned down $360,000, four times the first offer, because he would not sell *Rocky* without also starring in it. The studio and producers eventually settled on a much lower price and a budget of about $1 million, and the production moved to Philadelphia.

The real film went on to win Best Picture, and [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/rocky-50th-anniversary-steelbook-bluray-release-date/) notes it holds a Certified Fresh 95% on Rotten Tomatoes.

## A new Rocky release for the 50th anniversary

The attention comes with a home-video tie-in. According to ScreenRant and [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/rocky-50th-anniversary-4k-steelbook/), Amazon MGM and Alliance Home Entertainment will release a two-disc *Rocky* 50th Anniversary 4K UHD SteelBook on December 15. It includes three commentaries with Stallone, cast and filmmakers, a three-part making-of documentary called "In the Ring", archival 8mm home movies, and a souvenir mini-poster. MovieWeb lists US retail prices between $46.99 and $56.99.

## What happens next

*I Play Rocky* opens nationwide in the US on November 20, and the *Rocky* SteelBook follows on December 15. The sources here are US-based, so check local listings for Australian dates. MovieWeb also reports that Stallone has recently spoken about an idea for a seventh *Rocky* film, which would be the first since 2006's *Rocky Balboa*. No project has been announced. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Sylvester Stallone in I Play Rocky?

Anthony Ippolito, 27, plays the young Stallone. It is his first leading film role, according to the Motion Picture Association interview.

### When does I Play Rocky open?

It has been in select US theatres since early November and goes nationwide on November 20, according to USA Today and MovieWeb.

### Did Stallone approve the casting?

Yes. The casting director says Stallone had final approval, and Stallone told USA Today he thought Ippolito was "fantastic".

### When is the Rocky 50th anniversary SteelBook released?

December 15, as a two-disc 4K UHD set, according to ScreenRant and MovieWeb.
