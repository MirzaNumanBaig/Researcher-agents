**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox), 65, attended his foundation's annual Parkinson's gala at the Waldorf Astoria in New York on Saturday, 14 November 2026.
- His wife, [Tracy Pollan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Pollan), and their three daughters posed with him on the red carpet, according to HELLO! and Parade.
- Denis Leary hosted, with music from John Mayer and Brad Paisley and comedy from Josh Johnson and Tom Papa, Parade reports.
- Pollan told People the family takes his health "day by day" and that she sees "so much progress" in research.

Michael J. Fox turned up at the Waldorf Astoria in New York on Saturday night with his wife and three daughters beside him, for the annual benefit gala run by [The Michael J. Fox Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Michael_J._Fox_Foundation). The evening, titled "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson's", raises money for research into the condition. HELLO! described the family's red carpet appearance as a rare one.

## The family on the red carpet

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/935066/michael-j-fox-joined-by-wife-tracy-and-three-daughters-for-rare-red-carpet-appearance/), Fox wore a dark blazer and T-shirt, while Pollan, 66, chose a black sleeveless gown. Their daughters Esmé, 25, and twins Aquinnah and Schuyler, 31, also dressed up: Esmé in cream satin, Schuyler in black and Aquinnah in burgundy velvet.

The couple met while filming [Family Ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties) and married in 1988. They also have a son, Sam, 37, who was not pictured at the event, HELLO! notes. Parade, in a piece republished by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/michael-j-fox-happily-poses-025155000.html), reported that Fox was beaming on the carpet and that the family looked proud to be together.

Other guests were photographed too. A caption from the Associated Press, carried by [The Killeen Daily Herald](https://kdhnews.com/living/health/2026-michael-j-fox-foundation-benefit-gala/image_d724da31-3a84-58e0-b34e-e550d3c9037a.html), shows actor [Ted McGinley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_McGinley) arriving, and [The Goshen News](https://www.goshennews.com/news/national_news/2026-michael-j-fox-foundation-benefit-gala/image_79c15686-1119-5792-8073-78766d85dc42.html) published a picture of Fox and Pollan posing with John Mayer.

## Who hosted and performed

Parade says the gala was marking its 26th year. [Denis Leary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Leary) hosted. John Mayer and [Brad Paisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Paisley) gave musical performances, and comedians Josh Johnson and [Tom Papa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Papa) performed sets. The mix of music and comedy suits the event's title, which plays on Fox's own habit of using humour when he talks about living with Parkinson's.

## What Pollan said about the foundation

Speaking to People at the event, Pollan said the night is about the foundation's work and the people who have backed it for more than two decades. "Just the huge and amazing amount of money that's been raised that goes directly into the scientists' pockets, and they can just do all the work that's necessary," she said, adding: "We really are seeing so much progress."

Asked how the family stays positive, she gave a plain answer. "I think you just have to do it day by day," she said. "You have to look at what's in front of you … and you have to stay in the moment." Both quotes appear in the Parade report.

## Background: diagnosis, foundation and a recent honour

Fox was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease in 1991, at the age of 29, and launched his foundation in 2000, HELLO! reports. Parade quotes a 2023 CBS Mornings interview in which he said people with the diagnosis "didn't have money, they didn't have a voice", and that he wanted to "step in for these people and raise some hell."

HELLO! adds that earlier this year Fox accepted the Bob Hope Humanitarian Award at the Emmy Awards, where he thanked his family for their "sacrifices," "patience" and "love." Saturday's gala offered a public follow-up to that thank-you, with the same family at his side.
