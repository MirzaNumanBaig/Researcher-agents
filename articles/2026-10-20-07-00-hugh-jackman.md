**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman)'s A24 thriller The Death of Robin Hood is the most-watched movie on HBO Max in the US, according to FlixPatrol data cited by ScreenRant.
- It reached HBO Max on 16 October, four months after its US cinema release on 19 June.
- In cinemas it earned only $9.9 million against a reported $20 million budget.
- Digital Spy reports it is also available to rent or buy on Prime Video and Apple TV.

Hugh Jackman's grim take on the English folk hero has found the audience it missed in cinemas. The Death of Robin Hood debuted at No. 1 on HBO Max's US chart this week, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/hugh-jackman-the-death-of-robin-hood-hbo-max-streaming-success-october-2026/), which cited FlixPatrol and said the film pushed Sydney Sweeney's The Housemaid out of the top spot.

## A streaming hit after a box office miss

The numbers explain why the chart position matters. ScreenRant reports the R-rated film took $9.9 million in cinemas against a budget of about $20 million. [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2290580/hugh-jackman-thriller-the-death-of-robin-hood-streaming-hbo-max-october-2026/) puts it more bluntly: the film "appears to have finally found an audience" after grossing under $10 million.

Reviews were mixed in the way that sinks a quiet film. ScreenRant cites a 56% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 69% critics' score. Digital Spy gives the critics' figure as 69% across 189 reviews.

On HBO Max it now sits above a varied list that includes the 2026 sci-fi horror Backrooms, Coraline and The Shining, ScreenRant says. Sweeney's thriller has a sequel due in 2027. Jackman's film is a standalone.

## What the film is about

Writer-director Michael Sarnoski, who made Pig, has no interest in the swashbuckler. In SlashFilm's description, Jackman's Robin is an old recluse hunted by the relatives of the people he has killed. After being badly hurt helping his friend Little John (Bill Skarsgård) win back his farm and family, he recovers on a remote island under the care of Sister Brigid, played by [Jodie Comer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Comer).

The film's poster carries the line "He was no hero". SlashFilm notes the story is openly indebted to Logan and Clint Eastwood's Unforgiven, and that it is a far cry from the Robin played by [Errol Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errol_Flynn). The reviewer adds that there is almost no violence after the first act.

Not everyone buys the premise. SlashFilm points out that New Yorker critic Richard Brody dismissed the film as "crude cynicism" for stripping the outlaw of his reputation as a man who robbed the rich to help the poor.

## What critics said

Digital Spy, which calls the film "magnificent", gathered a run of reviews. The Times wrote that Jackman is "astounding throughout", free of the "naff ironic kitsch" he brings to bigger commercial films. The Observer called Comer "remarkable" while warning the film is not an enjoyable watch. The Atlantic described the approach as daring and "never uninteresting".

The film was released in UK cinemas on 2 September, Digital Spy reports.

## Jackman and the Van Helsing question

Jackman is also doing press for the film. In an interview with JoBlo, he was asked about returning as the monster hunter in 2004's Van Helsing, as Brendan Fraser is doing for a new Mummy sequel. His answer was "Never say never", according to [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/hugh-jackman-van-helsing-reboot/). There is no sign of a sequel being planned, and the interviewer presents it as a fun idea rather than news.

## What happens next

The Death of Robin Hood is streaming on HBO Max in the US, and Digital Spy says it can be rented or bought on Prime Video, Apple TV and other platforms. None of the sources say when it will reach Australian streaming services, so check local listings. The next FlixPatrol readings will show whether the film holds the top spot.
