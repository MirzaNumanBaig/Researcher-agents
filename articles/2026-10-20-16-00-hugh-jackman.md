**Quick summary**

- Asked by Variety whether he will appear in Avengers: Secret Wars, Hugh Jackman answered "I don't know what you're talking about", according to ComicBookMovie.com.
- He joked that a "literal chip" shuts him down if he says the wrong thing. Neither a yes nor a no has been given.
- Avengers: Doomsday is due on 18 December 2026 and Secret Wars on 17 December 2027.
- Reports of a Jackman cameo in Doomsday and a bigger part in Secret Wars remain unconfirmed rumours.
- Separately, Jackman is helping Bilt members win a month of free rent while his Manhattan triplex stays on the market, per The Times of India.

[Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) has again sidestepped the question fans keep asking: is Wolverine coming back for the next Avengers films? In an interview reported this month, the Australian actor said he did not know what the interviewer was talking about, then joked that he is policed by a chip.

## What Jackman actually said

According to [ComicBookMovie.com](https://comicbookmovie.com/avengers/avengers-secret-wars/hugh-jackman-breaks-silence-on-possibly-returning-as-wolverine-in-avengers-secret-wars-a230322), which dates its report to 9 October 2026, Variety asked Jackman about Avengers: Secret Wars after his repeated denials that he is returning as Wolverine in Avengers: Doomsday. His reply was "I don't know what you're talking about". He added that there is "a literal chip, and if I say the wrong thing, I get shut down."

That is a joke, not a confirmation. Entertainment sites have read it as a hint, but no studio announcement backs it up, and the reported answer neither confirms nor rules out a role.

## How he got here

Jackman has been careful with this question for some time. After Logan in 2017 he treated Wolverine as finished. Then came Deadpool & Wolverine in 2024, which ComicBookMovie.com says grossed more than $1.3 billion.

In November 2025, [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2025/film/news/hugh-jackman-wolverine-return-30346) reported that he told The Graham Norton Show he was "never saying 'never' ever again". Variety also reported that Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige had at first advised him against returning, because Logan was too good an ending to undo.

## What is rumoured, and what is not

ComicBookMovie.com says Jackman may have a small role in Doomsday and a larger one in Secret Wars, but stresses that nothing is confirmed. Treat that as speculation.

The film's cast is better documented. The same outlet lists [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) as [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom) and [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) among those described as confirmed or semi-confirmed for Secret Wars. Joe and Anthony Russo are named as directors of both films, with Stephen McFeely writing the screenplay.

The outlet also says Wolverine looks unlikely to feature in the planned X-Men reboot, which would leave the Avengers films as the main route back.

## Rent-free offer and a Manhattan listing

Away from Marvel, The Times of India's [Jackman news page](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/topic/Hugh-Jackman/news) reports that he is helping Bilt members win a month of free rent through the company's "Rent Free" game show, ahead of the Global Citizen Festival. Bilt's founder is [Ankur Jain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankur_Jain).

The outlet says his 11,000-square-foot New York triplex is listed at $28.75 million, down from an earlier $38.9 million. It previously rented for $125,000 a month. The same page recalls that he founded Laughing Man Coffee in 2011, with earnings funding the Laughing Man Foundation for coffee-farming communities.

## What happens next

Avengers: Doomsday opens on 18 December 2026, which will be the first real test of the rumours. Secret Wars follows on 17 December 2027. Until Marvel publishes a cast list or Jackman gives a straight answer, his involvement stays unconfirmed. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hugh Jackman in Avengers: Secret Wars?
Not confirmed. When Variety asked, he said he didn't know what the question meant and joked about a chip stopping him from talking. Marvel has made no announcement.

### When are the next Avengers films released?
Avengers: Doomsday is set for 18 December 2026 and Avengers: Secret Wars for 17 December 2027, according to ComicBookMovie.com.

### Did Hugh Jackman retire from playing Wolverine?
He treated Logan (2017) as his farewell, but returned in Deadpool & Wolverine in 2024. In November 2025 he said he was "never saying 'never' ever again".
