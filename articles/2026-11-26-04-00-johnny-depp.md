**Quick summary**

- The US ratings board has rated [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s new film [Day Drinker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_Drinker) R for "strong bloody violence, language and brief drug use", according to Bloody Disgusting and Dread Central.
- Lionsgate releases the film in US theatres on March 26, 2027.
- Depp's Christmas film Ebenezer has reached US$79.6 million worldwide, according to Koimoi, citing Box Office Mojo.
- A new biography, The Many Faces of Johnny Depp, came out on November 24 and is generating fresh stories about his past.

Johnny Depp's next film has its rating. The Motion Picture Association has given Day Drinker an R for strong bloody violence, language and brief drug use this week, reports Bloody Disgusting. Lionsgate will release the Marc Webb thriller in US theatres on March 26, 2027.

## What Day Drinker is about

[Marc Webb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Webb), who made 500 Days of Summer and The Amazing Spider-Man, directs from a screenplay by [Zach Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Dean). The official synopsis, quoted by Bloody Disgusting, follows a private-yacht bartender played by [Madelyn Cline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madelyn_Cline) who meets a mysterious guest on board, played by Depp. The two are soon tangled up with a criminal figure played by Penélope Cruz, and linked in ways none of them expected.

Lionsgate is billing the film as horror. Dread Central describes it as a supernatural revenge thriller that mixes a criminal conspiracy with a teased ghost story. The outlet says the teaser leaves the supernatural element unexplained, and compared the trailer's stylised look and angry lead character to Cape Fear. That is the outlet's reading of a teaser, not a plot confirmation.

The R rating is the first hard signal of how far the film will push. Bloody Disgusting notes it follows Depp's turn in Ti West's Ebenezer, another film with genre roots.

## Ebenezer keeps climbing

Depp's Christmas release is the bigger commercial test right now. According to Koimoi, which cites Box Office Mojo, Ebenezer took US$1.1 million on its second Monday in North America, down 20.5% on the previous Monday. That took its North American total to US$33.7 million and its worldwide total to US$79.6 million after 11 days.

Context matters here. Koimoi reports that the figure has passed the lifetime worldwide gross of DreamWorks' Forgotten Island, US$79.3 million, a film that reportedly failed to recover its US$80 million production cost. Ebenezer's own budget is listed at US$38 million, so the worldwide gross is roughly double what it cost to make, before marketing and cinema shares are counted. That is why the film is not yet called a clear profit.

Critics have been more mixed than ticket buyers. Wikipedia's summary of the reception lists a 55% Rotten Tomatoes score and 46 out of 100 on Metacritic. The Guardian's Peter Bradshaw gave it three stars out of five and called Depp "pretty good" as Scrooge.

The Toronto Star notes that 51% of opening-weekend ticket buyers told the PostTrak polling service they came because of Depp, and that the film opened at number one. The paper frames the role as part of a hoped-for Hollywood comeback after a stalled decade. It also points to Tom Cruise's "Digger" as a counter-example of an older star's darker role being rejected by audiences.

## The new biography adds old stories

Mark Seal's The Many Faces of Johnny Depp, published by Gallery Books on November 24, is feeding a second round of coverage. Seal drew on accounts from nearly 100 people.

Entertainment Weekly's takeaways focus on [Kate Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Moss). Journalist George Wayne recalls introducing the pair at New York's Café Tabac in February 1994. Moss later helped arrange Depp's first meeting with Hunter S. Thompson in Aspen, according to Los Angeles club owner Alan Finkelstein. Depp went on to play a version of Thompson in Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas in 1998. Entertainment Weekly says it asked representatives for Depp and Moss for comment.

Yahoo, drawing on Entertainment Weekly's excerpts, reports another account from the book. Former Mark Hotel general manager Raymond Bickson recalls that Marlon Brando phoned to vouch for Depp after his September 1994 arrest. These are one person's recollections, and Yahoo notes the outlet contacted Depp's representative for comment. We covered the hotel story in more detail in [Johnny Depp's 1994 Mark Hotel Arrest Recalled in New Book](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-mark-hotel-arrest-book/).
