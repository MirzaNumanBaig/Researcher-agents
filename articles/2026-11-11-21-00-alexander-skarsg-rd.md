**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) says the online reaction to his fantasy romcom Wicker has been "quite overwhelming", according to Pedestrian TV.
- Wicker opened at number four at the Australian box office and has taken A$1.39 million, including advance screenings, per Variety Australia.
- He has also joined The Confessions, a film produced by [Ari Aster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Aster), according to Variety as reported by tbreak.
- The Confessions has no release date, shooting schedule or character names yet.

Alexander Skarsgård has two reasons to be talked about this week. His comedy Wicker has become an internet phenomenon as it reaches Australian cinemas, and he is now attached to a new film produced by Ari Aster, the filmmaker behind Hereditary and Midsommar.

## Alexander Skarsgård on the Wicker frenzy

Speaking to [Pedestrian TV](https://www.pedestrian.tv/entertainment/olivia-colman-alexander-skarsgard-wicker-horny-reactions) ahead of the Australian release, Skarsgård and co-star Olivia Colman said they had not expected the reaction the film has drawn. "It's been quite overwhelming," Skarsgård said.

Wicker is based on Ursula Willis-Jones' 2008 short story The Wicker Husband. It follows a fisherwoman, teased by her neighbours for being unpartnered, who asks a basketmaker to weave her a husband. The wicker husband comes to life and proves a better partner than the village men, which causes tension. Pedestrian TV notes the film also questions how society treats older single women.

The trailer, which featured a run of intimate scenes between the two leads, set off much of the online chatter. Colman said friends had sent her creations inspired by the film, including a photo of a woman who made her own wicker suit.

Skarsgård credited IKEA Australia with kicking off the craze, pointing to its "husband material" advertisement for the Tolkning bench. He also described a screening where cinemagoers could collect popcorn in buckets shaped like a wicker torso. Asked whether the design was modelled on him, he laughed and confirmed it was.

## How Wicker is performing in Australia

The buzz has translated into ticket sales. Pedestrian TV, citing Variety Australia, reports that Wicker debuted at number four at the local box office and has earned A$1.39 million so far, a figure that includes advance screenings. That is a solid start for a romantic fantasy comedy rather than a franchise title, though the sources do not give figures for other markets.

## Wicker furniture gets its moment

The film has even reached the design pages. AD Middle East reports that the film has put wicker furniture back in focus, quoting designers who say interest in handmade, tactile materials was already growing. Interior designer Chirag Swain told the magazine that the thickness of the frame and the scale of the weave determine whether a piece looks modern. Designers also advise buyers to run a hand over the weave and check for loose strands, and to choose UV-resistant synthetic wicker for outdoor use.

## Alexander Skarsgård joins Ari Aster-produced The Confessions

Skarsgård's next announced project is very different in tone. According to Variety, as relayed by [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/alexander-skarsgard-the-confessions-ari-aster/), he will star in The Confessions, a psycho-dramedy from Canadian filmmakers [Guy Maddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Maddin), Galen Johnson and Evan Johnson. The story follows condemned nuns who witness a globe-spanning love triangle involving a missing sea captain and his poet wife.

The cast also includes [Hunter Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Schafer), [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke) and [Harry Melling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Melling). Skarsgård and Melling previously appeared together in A24's Pillion, released in 2025.

Aster and [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Knudsen_(producer)) produce through Square Peg, which worked with the trio on their 2024 comedy Rumours, a Cannes premiere starring [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett), [Alicia Vikander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Vikander) and [Charles Dance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dance). Aster said the filmmakers rank among his favourites in the world. For earlier coverage, see our report on [Skarsgård joining The Confessions](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-the-confessions/).

## What happens next

The Confessions is being launched at the American Film Market. CAA Media Finance and WME Independent co-represent North American rights, and the French company Charades handles international sales. No release date or shooting schedule has been announced. Meanwhile Wicker continues its Australian run, and its box office total will show whether the online noise keeps turning into ticket sales.
