**Quick summary**

- Netflix released a new trailer for *Ray Gunn* on 12 November 2026, according to its Tudum site.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) voices pop megastar Venus Nova; [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) voices detective Ray Gunn.
- The film opens in select cinemas and limited 70mm screenings on 4 December 2026.
- It reaches Netflix on 18 December 2026.

[Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) lead the new trailer for *Ray Gunn*, Brad Bird's animated science-fiction noir. Netflix published the footage on 12 November, and it gives the clearest look yet at a film that arrives in cinemas on 4 December before streaming two weeks later.

## What the Ray Gunn trailer shows

According to [Netflix's Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/ray-gunn-trailer), Rockwell voices Raymond "Ray" Gunn, a hardboiled detective. Johansson plays Venus Nova, described as one of the city's biggest and most controversial megastars. Tom Waits voices Eye-Ra, Ray's cyclopean alien partner.

The story is set in Metropia, a retrofuturistic city where humans and aliens live side by side, with flying cars and buildings a mile high. Tudum says the polished Art Deco look hides murder, corruption and a struggling detective. Tiny "pry bots" do much of the legwork, yet Ray and Eye-Ra still investigate on foot.

[SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2266236/ray-gunn-trailer-netflix-brad-bird) describes the trailer as showing Ray and Venus Nova together in a car, and says the case pulls the private eye into a mix of aliens, murder and a multimedia star. The same outlet calls the look a retro-futurism, a future designed as if by people still stuck in the past. Its writer was not fully sold on the character designs but praised Bird's handling of movement.

## Where Johansson's character fits

Venus Nova is the pivot of the plot. Tudum says she enters Ray's orbit and scandal follows her, sending him through her circle of associates between the city's glamorous heights and its seedy underworld. That is as much as the published material reveals, so the exact nature of her role in the murder stays unclear for now.

## Brad Bird's long-held idea

Bird directs. Tudum points to his earlier Academy Award-winning films *The Incredibles* and *Ratatouille*, and SlashFilm adds *The Iron Giant*. SlashFilm also notes that he has tried to get this project made for a long time.

Bird has a simple way of describing it. "The elevator pitch I came up with is *The Maltese Falcon* meets *Buck Rogers*," he said, according to Tudum. He cites Bogart detective films, the noir *Out of the Past*, and the worlds of Philip Marlowe and Sam Spade as influences. Of the city, he says it is hopeful, but "there's a dark underbelly" with things happening "in the shadows".

He also defends the medium. "Animation is too fun a medium, and too interesting, to limit what kind of stories can be told," Bird said, per Tudum.

## The wider voice cast and runtime

Beyond the three leads, SlashFilm lists Alan Tudyk, Matt Berry, Bobby Cannavale, Michael Chernus, Kether Donohue, Michelle Rodriguez, Aasif Mandvi, Matt Jones, Kathryn Hunter, John Ratzenberger, Justin Peck and Patton Oswalt in the cast, though it does not name their roles. SlashFilm says the film is made by Netflix with Skydance Animation. Tudum gives a runtime of 1 hour 59 minutes and a PG rating.
