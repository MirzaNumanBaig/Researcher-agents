**Quick summary**

- Christopher Lloyd, 87, writes in his memoir *In My Own Time* that he and Michael J. Fox were not close while making *Back to the Future*.
- Lloyd says the friendship only grew after the trilogy ended, following "two more" films.
- Fox replaced Eric Stoltz as Marty McFly six weeks into production in 1985, so Lloyd had to reshoot scenes as Doc Brown.
- On Lloyd's 88th birthday on October 22, Fox posted a message on Instagram and Lloyd replied publicly, according to Geo News.

[Christopher Lloyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Lloyd) and [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) are now one of Hollywood's best-known on-screen duos, but Lloyd says it took years for the two to become real friends. In his memoir *In My Own Time*, reported by [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/689291-christopher-lloys-reveals-surprising-truth-about-michael-j-fox-friendship) on 1 November 2026, the actor describes a working relationship that only turned into friendship after the *Back to the Future* trilogy was finished.

## What Lloyd says in his memoir

Lloyd, who is 87 according to the report, writes that it "would take two more Back to the Future movies" before he and Fox "truly got to know each other and become friends". He adds that it did eventually happen.

That is a candid admission for a pair so closely linked in the public mind. Fans of the films often assume that the chemistry between Marty McFly and Doc Brown came from a long personal bond. Lloyd's account suggests the connection on screen came first and the one off screen followed.

## A late change in the lead role

The two first worked together on the original film in 1985. According to Geo News, Fox famously took over the part of Marty from [Eric Stoltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Stoltz) six weeks into production. That meant Lloyd had to go back and redo scenes he had already shot as Doc Brown.

Lloyd says he was so focused on getting those retakes right that he had little attention left for building a friendship. He is nonetheless grateful for who stepped in. In the memoir he says he was "extremely grateful" the new actor was Fox, because Fox "brought his own energy" and helped him get past his own anxieties.

## Why they stayed apart between takes

Geo News reports that the co-stars largely kept to themselves between takes. Fox was also working on the sitcom [Family Ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties) at the same time, which Lloyd acknowledges put considerable pressure on his younger colleague. Add the age gap between them, and the report says there was little room for a friendship to form during filming.

## The bond today

The pair are clearly close now. The report says they often celebrate one another in public. On Lloyd's 88th birthday on 22 October, Fox wrote on Instagram, "Great Scott you're 88!" and told his co-star he loved him. Lloyd answered: "We truly are timeless. Love you friend."

Geo News also links to earlier coverage of Fox speaking about his bond with Lloyd, and of Fox paying a heartfelt tribute on the birthday. We have not read those pieces, so this article relies on the single report above for the details.
