[Anders Danielsen Lie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Danielsen_Lie) appears in *Brave New Love*, a relationship drama from director Maria Bäck that is listed for release on 10 December 2026. The film, a Danish, Swedish and Greek production, has been working its way through the festival circuit and is described as a provocative look at monogamy and commitment.

## What Brave New Love is about

According to the film-trends site [InsightTrendsWorld](https://www.insighttrendsworld.com/post/brave-new-love-2026-by-maria-b%C3%A4ck), the story centres on Kate, played by Kathrine Thorborg Johansen. Her romantic life becomes complicated when her lover leaves for Greece, and she decides to follow him. The journey pushes her to rethink what she believes about love, family and commitment.

The site says the film treats an extramarital affair as a starting point rather than a simple moral failing. Instead of asking which partner Kate should choose, it asks whether traditional models of commitment can hold up against what individual people want and need. That framing is the publication's reading of the film, not a statement from the filmmakers.

## The cast and the director

Johansen leads the cast. The same source names Danielsen Lie among the cast, noting that he is known for *The Worst Person in the World* and *Oslo, August 31st*. [Simon Sears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Sears) is also listed. Wikipedia's entry for the film adds Angeliki Papoulia and Noemi Carbone Mannov to the cast list.

Bäck is described as the director of *Psychosis in Stockholm*, and *Brave New Love* is billed as another study of emotionally complex relationships. The film runs 105 minutes and is classed as a drama and romance.

## Festival recognition

InsightTrendsWorld reports three nominations for the film, at Locarno, Thessaloniki and Gijón. It reads those as a sign of the film's standing in contemporary European independent cinema. The listing says the film is currently available to industry professionals through Festival Scope Pro and on the festival circuit, so general audiences have had little chance to see it yet.

No budget, box office figures or review scores have been recorded, which is normal for a film that has not opened.
