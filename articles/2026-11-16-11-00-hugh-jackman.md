**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) is among more than 150 Australian artists who have signed an open letter calling for Scott Reef to be protected.
- The letter asks Federal Environment Minister [Murray Watt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Watt) to stop Woodside drilling up to 57 wells in and around the reef.
- Scott Reef sits 270 kilometres off the Kimberley coast of Western Australia.
- A release issued on Monday said the project is forecast to produce 1.6 billion tonnes of climate pollution.

Hugh Jackman has added his name to an open letter from more than 150 Australian artists urging the federal government to save Scott Reef from oil and gas drilling. According to [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/hugh-jackman-rebel-wilson-among-150-plus-australian-artists-calling-for-famous-reef-to-be-saved-42614/), the letter went public on Monday, with the Hugh Jackman Scott Reef appeal joined by actors and some of the country's best-known musicians.

## Who signed the Scott Reef letter

The list reaches well beyond film. Variety Australia and [Rolling Stone Australia](https://au.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kevin-parker-hugh-jackman-greenpeace-open-letter-scott-reef-103394/) both report that [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) and [Simon Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Baker) signed alongside Jackman. Musicians include 5 Seconds of Summer, [Missy Higgins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missy_Higgins), Ninajirachi, [Jessica Mauboy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Mauboy), [Amy Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Shark), [Peter Garrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Garrett), [Tim Minchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Minchin) and [Darren Hayes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darren_Hayes).

Rolling Stone Australia also names [Tina Arena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Arena), Jimmy Barnes, G Flip and the singer-songwriter Paul Kelly. Tame Impala's [Kevin Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Parker) is on the list too, and he has protested against Woodside before. Earlier this year, at a show in the United States, Parker wore a Fremantle Dockers jersey with the energy company's logo covered in black tape.

That detail matters because of the football link. Woodside has sponsored the Dockers since 2010, and in 2024 it renewed the deal to run until at least 2027, according to both outlets.

## What the artists want protected

The letter is addressed to Murray Watt. It asks him to stop plans that would see Woodside drill as many as 57 wells under and around Scott Reef, an ancient coral reef roughly 270 kilometres from the Kimberley coast in Western Australia.

Higgins described the reef as home to endangered whales, vulnerable turtles and hundreds of other marine species. "We need to preserve these beautiful places for our kids. If not now, then when?" she said.

Mauboy framed it around Caring for Country, saying places like Scott Reef are precious and that people have a responsibility to protect them for future generations.

The climate figure is the largest number in the campaign. A release issued on Monday said the project is forecast to emit 1.6 billion tonnes of climate pollution, a claim reported by both outlets as part of the campaign's own material rather than an independent assessment.

## Greenpeace and the government

Hannah Schuch, a senior campaigner at Greenpeace Australia Pacific, said she was heartened to see so many artists come together. She called Scott Reef an iconic, irreplaceable piece of Australian nature and said Woodside's plans risk threatened species and a unique ecosystem.

Schuch also put the decision squarely on the Albanese Government, saying it has a chance to "make history" by saving the reef. Neither report included a response from Woodside or from Watt's office.

## What happens next

The next move belongs to Watt, who as environment minister holds the federal decision on the project. The sources do not give a date for that decision, so none is known. Expect more artists to be added to the list as the campaign builds, and watch for any reply from Woodside.
