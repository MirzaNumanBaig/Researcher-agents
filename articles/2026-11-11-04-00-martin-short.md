**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) appears on "Merry Christmas", the final track of [Alanis Morissette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alanis_Morissette)'s first holiday album.
- The album, "Good Weather For Immaculate Conception", has 14 tracks and was reported as out now on 10 November 2026.
- [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) and [Brandi Carlile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandi_Carlile) feature on "Beautiful Star of Bethlehem".
- Short's series "Only Murders in the Building" returns for Season 6 on Hulu in December, according to TVLine.

Martin Short has joined Alanis Morissette for her first-ever Christmas record. The comedian is credited on "Merry Christmas", the closing song of "Good Weather For Immaculate Conception", which music publicist and writer Eric Alper reported on 10 November was out now.

## What is on the album

The collection runs to 14 tracks and is released through Epiphany Music and [Virgin Music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Music), according to [That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/2026/11/10/alanis-morissette-brings-brandi-carlile-steve-martin-and-martin-short-to-good-weather-for-immaculate-conception/). Most of it is familiar seasonal material, including "O Holy Night", "What Child Is This?", "Ave Maria", "Last Christmas" and "Little Drummer Boy". It also takes in John Lennon and Yoko Ono's "Happy Xmas (War Is Over)" and Joni Mitchell's "River".

Morissette wrote two originals for it, "Christmas on the Dance Floor" and "To An Empath at Christmas Time". That is two new songs among 14, so the record leans on well-worn carols and pop standards rather than new writing.

## Who else appears

Short is not the only guest. Brandi Carlile and Steve Martin sing on "Beautiful Star of Bethlehem", and Eric Alper notes that Martin is also an accomplished banjo player. [Joy Oladokun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy_Oladokun) is on "Silent Night" and [Amythyst Kiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amythyst_Kiah) is on "Momma Mary".

Short and Steve Martin are longtime comedy partners, so the album puts both of them on a Morissette record, on separate tracks. The track listing places "Merry Christmas featuring Martin Short" at number 14, the last song on the record.

## A busy stretch for Morissette

The album lands while Morissette is also staging "Butterfly With a Machete" in Los Angeles. Alper describes the limited-run show at YouTube Theater as a mix of music, acting, dance and personal storytelling that follows her from childhood in Ottawa to international fame. She performs songs such as "You Oughta Know" and "Ironic" in it. The remaining performances were listed for 10 and 11 November.

## Short's other work and a quote that is circulating

Short's best-known current project is "Only Murders in the Building", in which he stars with Steve Martin and Selena Gomez as three neighbours who run a true crime podcast. TVLine says Season 6 is due on Hulu in December.

TVLine also ran a "quote of the day" from Short this week, taken from a 2019 AARP interview. In it, he spoke about his wife, Nancy Dolman, who died in 2010 after almost 30 years of marriage. He said he still talks to her and wonders how she would react to decisions, especially those involving their three children. TVLine reports he also said losing her emboldened him to take more creative risks. The quote is years old and not new news, but it has resurfaced alongside his current projects.

## What happens next

Listeners can find the album now, based on Alper's report. Morissette's Los Angeles run ends with the 10 and 11 November shows. Fans of Short can look for Season 6 of "Only Murders in the Building" on Hulu in December, though TVLine did not give an exact date.
