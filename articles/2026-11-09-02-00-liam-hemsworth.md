**Quick summary**

- A Hunger Games marathon arrives on Peacock on November 14, according to Crave Online.
- [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) stars in the first four films of the series.
- The original four films earned $2.968 billion together, and the 2023 prequel lifted the five-film total to $3.316 billion.
- Peacock's same-week slate also includes Southern Charm Season 12 and the thriller The Good Daughter, both on November 13.

The Hunger Games films that made [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) a household name are heading to Peacock. According to [Crave Online](https://www.mandatory.com/news/1885541-new-peacock-releases-this-week-november-9-15), the streamer adds a marathon of the franchise on November 14, part of its release list for the week of November 9 to 15.

## What is arriving on November 14

The marathon covers all five films: The Hunger Games, Catching Fire, Mockingjay – Part 1, Mockingjay – Part 2 and the prequel The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes. The series is adapted from the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) books, and the second film takes its name from the novel [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire).

The outlet's list of the week's releases names The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes as a separate entry. The report does not say how long the films will stay on the service.

## Who is in the cast

[Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson), Hemsworth and [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) lead the first four films, per Crave Online. Supporting players include [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks), [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci) and [Donald Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sutherland). The later sequels add [Philip Seymour Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman), [Jeffrey Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Wright) and [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore).

The prequel, released in 2023, goes back 64 years before the first story and brings in a new lead cast. [Tom Blyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Blyth) and [Rachel Zegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zegler) headline, with [Peter Dinklage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Dinklage), [Jason Schwartzman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Schwartzman) and [Viola Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola_Davis) in key roles. Hemsworth is not part of that film's cast as listed by the outlet.

## The box office numbers

Crave Online puts the original four-film run at $2.968 billion worldwide. Adding the prequel brings the franchise to $3.316 billion across five films. That is the "$2.9 billion" in the outlet's headline: it refers to the four films Hemsworth appears in, not to his own earnings.

## Also on Peacock this week

Southern Charm Season 12 premieres on November 13, following a group of Charleston socialites. Returning cast members include Craig Conover, Austen Kroll and Madison LeCroy, with newcomer Allie Newman joining. The same day brings The Good Daughter, a six-part thriller starring [Rose Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose_Byrne), Meghann Fahy and Brendan Gleeson, about estranged sisters reunited after a school shooting in their hometown.

## What happens next

The marathon is due on November 14, and the other new titles land a day earlier on November 13. Availability differs by country, and the report is written for Peacock's US audience, so Australian readers should check their own streaming services.
