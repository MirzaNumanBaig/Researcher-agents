**Quick summary**

- Apple TV's *Mayday* is set to compete in the TV movie categories at the Primetime Emmy Awards next year, according to Gold Derby.
- The film premiered on the streaming service on 4 September.
- Two contenders are being put forward for Best TV Limited Series or Movie Actor: Ryan Reynolds and Kenneth Branagh.
- Apple TV will also submit Maria Bakalova in the supporting actress categories.

[Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) is heading into awards season with a streaming film instead of a cinema release. *Mayday*, the Cold War action comedy he made with [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh), will compete in the TV movie categories at next year's Emmys, Gold Derby reported exclusively on 3 November.

## What Apple TV is submitting

Gold Derby says *Mayday* will run as a streaming-exclusive film. Apple TV has described it as its biggest original film launch ever, though the outlet notes that the platform has not released viewing figures.

The plan is to put forward two names for Best TV Limited Series or Movie Actor. That means Reynolds and Branagh could end up in the same category. The most recent winner there was [Matthew Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Rhys), for *The Beast in Me*.

Apple TV will also submit Academy Award nominee [Maria Bakalova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Bakalova), known for *Borat*, in the supporting actress categories at the television honours.

## What the film is about

According to Gold Derby, the 1980s-style action comedy follows a downed Navy pilot, played by Reynolds, who has to get back to the West from Soviet territory. His helper is an America-obsessed former KGB operative, played by Branagh, and the story is set at the height of the Cold War.

It comes from the writer-director pair John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein, whose earlier credits include *Game Night* and *Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves*. For a sense of how the film has landed, the publication points to the critics, and Goldstein told it he was glad to see reviewers "embracing it".

## A cinema film that went straight to streaming

The most interesting detail in the report is how the film ended up on Apple TV. Gold Derby says *Mayday* began as a pitch bought by Skydance, which Apple TV later joined. Daley and Goldstein made it with Reynolds and Branagh on the assumption that it would play in theatres.

"When we sold it, we actually assumed that it would be theatrical," Daley told Gold Derby, adding that they only learned later that Apple's plan leaned towards streaming. He said they would not have done anything differently had they known.

Goldstein spoke about the gap between the two release models. In cinemas, he said, the opening weekend gives everyone clear numbers, while streaming is "a little more of a black box". Daley said they had "some indication" of how the film performed for the platform, but nothing definitive and no real figures.

Goldstein also admitted a streaming release can bring a bias with it. Some critics, he said, may assume a straight-to-streaming film was not good enough for cinemas, so the filmmakers have to get past that first impression.

## What happens next

The Emmys are next year, and the submission is the first step. Nominations have not been announced, so a Reynolds and Branagh face-off is a possibility only.

Daley and Goldstein have moved on to other work. Gold Derby says their main focus is a *Star Trek* feature reboot for Skydance, which they are set to write and produce, and possibly direct. The script is still in an early phase.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Mayday going to the Emmys?

Yes, as a submission. Gold Derby reports that Apple TV will enter it in the TV movie categories for next year's Primetime Emmy Awards. A submission is not a nomination.

### Who is competing from Mayday?

Ryan Reynolds and Kenneth Branagh are the two contenders for Best TV Limited Series or Movie Actor. Maria Bakalova is also being put forward for supporting actress.

### Where can I watch Mayday?

*Mayday* is a streaming exclusive on Apple TV, where it premiered on 4 September.
