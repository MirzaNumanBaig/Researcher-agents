**Quick summary**

- Netflix has not officially renewed *Black Doves* for a third season, according to both Marie Claire and Country & Town House.
- Creator Joe Barton says a writers' room is already working on a possible season 3.
- Season 2 arrived on Netflix on 5 November 2026 with six episodes, per Marie Claire.
- Any release date is a guess. Both outlets float late 2028 based on the gap between earlier seasons.

[Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley)'s spy thriller has no official third season yet, but its creator is not waiting for the green light. Joe Barton says a writers' room is already open for *Black Doves* season 3, even though Netflix has not confirmed a renewal.

## Where the renewal stands

Netflix has not ordered a third run, according to [Marie Claire](https://www.marieclaire.com/culture/tv-shows/black-doves-season-3/). Barton told the Pilot TV podcast the streamer has been supportive and that "we have a writers room for it", while admitting the decision rests on market forces. His words on the odds were a simple "we hope so".

Timing may matter. Marie Claire notes that Netflix ordered season 2 a few months before season 1 premiered in December 2024, and suggests it may now be waiting to see how season 2 performs. The same report says the executive who ordered both seasons, Bela Bejaria, was replaced by Peter Rice in October.

Season 1 reached number one on Netflix, according to [Country & Town House](https://www.countryandtownhouse.com/?p=417531). We have also covered how [Black Doves season 2 climbed the IMDb chart](https://moduscarry.com/black-doves-season-2-imdb-chart/).

## What Barton has hinted

Country & Town House quotes Barton on where the story could go. Of the group at the centre of the show, he said it is fun to imagine how that organisation might reinvent itself. Of Knightley's character, Helen Webb, he said she cannot be "the spy wife" any more but remains a highly capable agent.

The outlet lays out the reasoning behind a rebuild. The Black Doves are damaged and low on numbers, and the report speculates the group could shift from an ideological model to a more capitalist one. Patrick, played by Sam Riley, is still on the run. Helen has also not yet given her husband Wallace a full confession, so that thread stays open.

Marie Claire adds that Wallace becomes Prime Minister in season 2, which makes Helen's double life harder to manage. Both points are the outlets' reading of the story, not confirmed plans.

## Who could return

Both reports expect Knightley to remain at the centre as Helen. Marie Claire lists Ben Whishaw (Sam) and Sarah Lancashire (Reed) among the likely leads, with Andrew Buchan (Wallace), Ella Lily Hyland (Williams) and Gabrielle Creevy (Eleanor) also expected. None of this is confirmed.

One name will not come back. Marie Claire says Agnes O'Casey's character, Dani, was revealed as a traitor and killed in the season 2 finale. Whishaw's Sam, per Country & Town House, has returned to an Italian village, convinced his presence endangers the people he loves. For the season 2 ending in detail, see our piece on [Helen's secret in the finale](https://moduscarry.com/keira-knightley-black-doves-finale/).

## What happens next

Nothing is official. If Netflix renewed the show quickly, Marie Claire estimates a season 3 around autumn 2028, and Country & Town House also guesses late 2028. Both stress these are estimates. Season 2 took about two years from its early renewal to reach screens, with production starting in November 2025.
