**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) accepted Japan's Praemium Imperiale at a black-tie ceremony in Tokyo on Wednesday, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- In her speech she said her doubt, often read as a lack of confidence in women, is "my artistic starting point".
- Each laureate receives a medal, a testimonial letter and a 15 million yen honorarium, just under $100,000.
- Blanchett is next due at the Tokyo International Film Festival on Friday, including a session on her film [Alpha Gang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_Gang).

Cate Blanchett used her moment at one of the arts world's biggest prizes to defend uncertainty. The two-time Oscar winner accepted the Praemium Imperiale in Tokyo on Wednesday, and told a news conference beforehand that doubt is "vital to the creative process" in a world "riddled with unthinking certainty".

## What she said about doubt

[The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/cate-blanchett-japan-praemium-imperiale-award-tokyo-1236742351/) says Blanchett described doubt as the thing that "starts the challenge, the questioning, the exploration". In her words, it also "allows conversation and connection" and "allows change".

Her acceptance speech turned to language itself. Opening with a reference to the surrealist artist Leonora Carrington, she called acting a profession that "at its worst sinks into a quagmire of literalness but at its best can leave people speechless".

She said she looks for meaning in the spaces between what is said and what is not, including what people wish they had said. Then came the line that headlined most coverage. "As a woman that doubt is often interpreted as a lack of confidence. It is not. That doubt is my artistic starting point," she said, adding that it lets her hear, see and sense.

She also credited courage and rigour as the qualities needed to keep taking risks over a long career, and praised her fellow laureates for them.

## A prize with a heavyweight history

The Japan Art Association presents the award for lifetime achievement in painting, sculpture, architecture, music and theatre or film. Türkiye Today notes it is often described as the Nobel Prize of the arts.

Past theatre and film recipients include [Federico Fellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Fellini), [Akira Kurosawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Kurosawa), [Jean-Luc Godard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Luc_Godard), [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese), [Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench) and [Catherine Deneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Deneuve). [Ang Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ang_Lee) was the last Hollywood-associated winner, in 2024.

Princess Hitachi presented Blanchett's medal at the Meiji Kinenkan in Tokyo's Moto-Akasaka district. This year's other laureates are artist [Jenny Holzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Holzer), sculptor Andy Goldsworthy, architect [Daniel Libeskind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Libeskind) and conductor Herbert Blomstedt.

Blanchett closed by calling the honour "an Olympus of sorts that I never conceived of even touching".

## Hillary Clinton on the arts and AI

Former US Secretary of State Hillary Clinton, the prize's US international adviser, also spoke at the news conference. Clinton belongs to the advisory network whose committees recommend candidates, while the Japan Art Association makes the final selections.

She called the prize "a global declaration that artists and culture are essential pillars of our societies". Its role, she argued, has grown more important amid threats to democratic values and anxiety about artificial intelligence. She called for limiting the technology's risks while protecting human imagination and connection.

## What happens next

Blanchett's Japan trip continues at the 39th Tokyo International Film Festival, which runs through 4 November. On Friday she is scheduled to join filmmakers [Mohammad Rasoulof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Rasoulof) and [Shahrbanoo Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrbanoo_Sadat) for a Q&A after the Japanese premieres of five short films backed by the Displacement Film Fund. She co-founded and leads that initiative, which supports displaced filmmakers.

Later that day she is due to appear with directors David and Nathan Zellner to discuss their alien comedy Alpha Gang, in which she stars. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Praemium Imperiale?
It is an international arts prize presented by the Japan Art Association for lifetime achievement in painting, sculpture, architecture, music and theatre or film. It is often compared to a Nobel Prize for the arts.

### How much does the Praemium Imperiale pay?
Each laureate receives a medal, a testimonial letter and an honorarium of 15 million yen, which The Hollywood Reporter puts at just under $100,000.

### What did Cate Blanchett say about doubt?
She said doubt is vital to the creative process and is her "artistic starting point", adding that in women it is often wrongly read as a lack of confidence.

### What is Cate Blanchett doing at the Tokyo International Film Festival?
She is scheduled for a Q&A on the Displacement Film Fund's short films and a discussion of Alpha Gang, both on Friday.
