**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) 2010 film Robin Hood, directed by Ridley Scott, is rising up the charts on Prime Video in the United States, according to Collider.
- The film cost more than US$200 million to make and finished its cinema run with US$321 million worldwide.
- It holds a 43% score on Rotten Tomatoes, and it was the last film Crowe and Scott made together.
- Later Robin Hood films have also struggled at the box office, which makes the streaming interest notable.

A film that disappointed in cinemas 16 years ago is finding a second audience at home. Collider reported on 24 November that Russell Crowe's Robin Hood, directed by [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott), is climbing the charts on Prime Video in the United States, where it is currently streaming.

## A big-budget epic with a modest return

The 2010 film reunited Crowe and Scott after the success of Gladiator, which won the Oscars for Best Picture and Best Actor. According to [Collider](https://collider.com/robin-hood-ridley-scott-russell-epic-prime-video-success/), Robin Hood cost more than US$200 million to produce and ended its global run with US$321 million. That is a gross of roughly one and a half times the reported budget, before marketing costs are counted, and the outlet describes the result as falling short of expectations.

The cast included [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) as Maid Marian, with [Oscar Isaac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oscar_Isaac), [Mark Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Strong), [William Hurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hurt), Max von Sydow and [Danny Huston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Huston) in supporting roles. Collider says the film now sits at 43% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). The site's consensus line credits the fine acting and a few gripping action scenes, but says the film lacks the sense of adventure that made the legend endure.

## The end of a long partnership

Collider describes the film as the final project Crowe and Scott made together after a largely successful run of about a decade. A planned biopic of chef [Marco Pierre White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marco_Pierre_White) that the pair had been developing fell apart, according to the outlet. Gladiator II eventually reached cinemas more than 20 years after the original without Crowe, and Collider notes that the actor has publicly dismissed the sequel on more than one occasion.

For readers following the pair, the streaming surge is a small footnote to a partnership that began with one of the most decorated films of the early 2000s. ## Other Robin Hood films have struggled too

Collider points out that the character has had a rough time on the big screen. A 2018 film starring [Taron Egerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taron_Egerton) took about US$85 million against a reported US$100 million budget and sits at 15% on Rotten Tomatoes. Earlier this year, [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) starred in The Death of Robin Hood, which took under US$10 million against a reported US$20 million budget. [Paul Greengrass's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Greengrass) The Uprising delivered a nearly identical result a few weeks later.

The only recent success based on the legend, per the outlet, is an MGM+ series that premiered in 2025. Against that record, Crowe's version, flawed as the reviews were, now looks like the most watched Robin Hood film at home.

## Crowe's other recent work

Crowe is also in The Weight alongside [Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke), which Collider calls his best-reviewed film on Rotten Tomatoes in nearly three decades. His September release Unabomber has fared less well. According to Wikipedia, it holds 25% on Rotten Tomatoes and 46 out of 100 on Metacritic, and no box-office figures have been recorded yet. Collider adds that Scott is developing a Treasure Island adaptation with Jackman, after his new sci-fi film The Dog Stars underperformed with critics and at the box office.

## What happens next

Collider does not say how long Robin Hood will stay on the Prime Video chart, and the report covers only the United States. The film is also available through other streaming bundles and for premium rental. Whether the renewed interest reaches Australian viewers will depend on local availability, which the source does not address.
