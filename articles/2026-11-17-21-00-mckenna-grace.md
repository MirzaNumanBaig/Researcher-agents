**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) attended the New York premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping on Monday evening.
- She wore a custom Pamella Roland cobalt-blue organza gown with a crystal Mockingjay motif and a stole that opened into wings.
- The look recalls the Katniss Everdeen dress transformation in Catching Fire, according to Allure and Red Carpet Fashion Awards.
- Her makeup artist, Nadia Tayeh, added a smoky eye with rhinestones along the lower lashes.
- The film opens on November 20.

The Mckenna Grace premiere look in New York ended the Hunger Games press tour with a costume change on the carpet itself. At the New York premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping on Monday evening, the actress opened what looked like a feathered stole and revealed a pair of enormous wings, a nod to a famous scene from the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series.

## Mckenna Grace premiere gown and wings

According to [Red Carpet Fashion Awards](https://www.redcarpet-fashionawards.com/2026/11/17/mckenna-grace-spreads-her-mockingjay-wings-at-the-hunger-games-premiere/), Grace wore a custom Pamella Roland column gown in cobalt-blue liquid organza. The bodice was draped and decorated with a crystal-embellished Mockingjay motif, the bird emblem of the franchise.

The stole was colour-matched to the dress, which is why the reveal worked. Grace unfurled it into wings to recreate [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) as Katniss Everdeen in [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire), when a wedding dress turns into a Mockingjay gown. Allure ties the beauty look to the same moment.

The coverage was not all praise. The Red Carpet Fashion Awards writer enjoyed the reference and the look of the extended wings, but called it the weakest outfit of the press tour and said they wished Grace had saved her Mexico City premiere look for New York. That makes the New York event the second premiere of the run, after Mexico City.

## The makeup behind the look

Allure spoke to makeup artist Nadia Tayeh, who said the eye makeup took its cue from the same scene. "The look paid homage to Katniss Everdeen's iconic wedding dress transformation into the blue Mockingjay dress," Tayeh told the magazine, describing a dramatic smoky eye with delicate rhinestones.

In practice that meant icy, silver-blue shimmer on the lids, brighter at the inner corners, with deep slate shadow swept into an elongated wing at the outer edge. Gradient rhinestones ran along the lower lash line. Allure compared the sparkle with the way Lawrence wore it in the original films, when the makeup was done by [Ve Neill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ve_Neill).

The rest of the face stayed soft. Tayeh used a cool-toned cream blush and a light cream bronzer, then finished the lips with a lip liner stain and a sheer pink gloss, all from E.L.F., according to Allure.

## A press tour built on references

The premiere look fits a pattern. Allure notes that the cast has leaned into theme dressing at each stop, and that Grace has stayed committed to the idea from the start. We have also covered [her earlier Mockingjay gown](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-mockingjay-gown/) and [her Katniss look at the pop-up](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-katniss-look/).

Not every appearance has been so formal. [Reel 360 News](https://reel360.com/article/mckenna-grace-serves-up-a-pleated-miniskirt-look-at-raising-canes/) reported that Grace stepped behind the counter at a Raising Cane's in Hollywood on November 10, wearing a grey button-up top, a dark pleated miniskirt and white knee-high socks. The outlet placed the visit within her publicity run for the film.

## What happens next

Entertainment coverage of the New York event describes it as the final press stop, so the next time audiences see Grace as part of this story will be on screen. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opens on November 20, according to Reel 360 News. Grace's earlier film The Last Kiss, directed by Yoko Okumura and co-starring Jaden Michael, was released on September 15, according to Wikipedia, though no box office figures are listed yet.
