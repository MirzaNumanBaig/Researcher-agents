**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), 59, attended the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show and sat in the front row, according to New Idea.
- A video clip of her at the show drew 2.4 million views on X within hours of being posted on 19 October 2026.
- Adéla's song "Nicole Kidman" reached No. 9 on the Billboard Hot 100, and Madonna joined the TikTok trend on 15 October.
- Kidman's eldest daughter, Sunday Rose, 18, already walks at Paris Fashion Week. Whether she will join the Victoria's Secret show is speculation only.

Nicole Kidman was one of the most talked-about guests at this year's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, sitting in the front row on her own and prompting a wave of online chatter about why she was there. The Australian actress's appearance came days after a pop song bearing her name climbed the US charts.

## What happened at the show

According to [New Idea](https://www.newidea.com.au/celebrity/nicole-kidman-momager-sunday-rose-victorias-secret-model/), Kidman attended solo and sat beside Vanessa Bryant, whose daughter Natalia, 23, was making her Victoria's Secret debut. The magazine described it as a chance to pick up "momager" tips, though that framing is its own reading of the moment, not something Kidman has said.

A short video of Kidman in her seat was posted to X by the account Mario Nawfal on 19 October. [The post](https://x.com/MarioNawfal/status/2112006570242425257/video/1) showed 2.4 million views at the time the page was captured. The replies were mixed, ranging from jokes to criticism of the show itself, which says more about the internet than about Kidman.

## Is Sunday Rose next?

New Idea suggests fans are wondering whether Kidman was scoping out the event for her daughter. Sunday Rose is 18 and, per the magazine, has walked in Paris for Miu Miu and Christian Dior. It points to two precedents: Taylor Hill joined the brand at 19, and Australian model Alannah Walton walked the show at 18.

None of this is confirmed. Neither Kidman nor her daughter has said Sunday Rose will appear in a Victoria's Secret show, and the idea is the outlet's speculation.

What is on record, per New Idea, is that Kidman was wary when Sunday Rose started modelling at 16 and set a firm rule that school came first. Sunday Rose graduated earlier this year. Kidman and her former husband, [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban), largely kept their daughters, Sunday Rose and Faith, out of the public eye while they grew up.

## The song that keeps saying her name

Kidman's name is also having a pop-culture moment for another reason. According to [Super Hits 106](https://www.superhits106.com/2026/10/17/madonna-joins-adelas-viral-nicole-kidman-tiktok-trend/), singer Adéla's track "Nicole Kidman" reached No. 9 on the Billboard Hot 100, her first top-10 hit. Her debut album, PRIMA, entered the Billboard 200 at No. 4.

Madonna posted a TikTok on 15 October showing her in a gold dress, set to the song. The station also names Kesha, Dua Lipa and Pedro Pascal among those who have shared their own connection to it, and says Kidman has acknowledged Adéla and shown interest in her music.
