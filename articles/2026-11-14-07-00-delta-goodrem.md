**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) will dance the Argentine Tango with Aljaž Škorjanec on Saturday's live *Strictly Come Dancing*, because her partner Kai Widdrington is ill.
- The change was announced on Friday, 13 November, on the spin-off show *It Takes Two*.
- It is a one-week swap, according to Digital Spy.
- Goodrem has already missed one round this series after fracturing a rib.

The Australian singer's *Strictly* run has another twist. [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) will miss this weekend's live show with illness, so [Aljaž Škorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) will take his place for Goodrem's Argentine Tango on BBC One on Saturday. It is the second disruption to her campaign in a matter of weeks, and it leaves little time to build a new partnership.

## How the Delta Goodrem Strictly partner swap was announced

The news came on Friday's episode of *It Takes Two*. Presenter [Ellie Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellie_Taylor) told viewers, as quoted by [RTE](https://www.rte.ie/entertainment/2026/1113/1599176-delta-goodrem-forced-into-strictly-partner-swap/): "Due to illness, Kai is unable to perform on tomorrow night's show. Instead, Aljaž will step in to dance the Argentine Tango with Delta."

The show's Instagram page shared a matching statement. According to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16291161/Strictlys-Kai-Widdrington-forced-pull-Saturdays-illness-replaced-Alja-korjanec-weeks-Delta-Goodrems-rib-injury.html), Widdrington is 31 and Goodrem is 42. Wales Online reported that Widdrington had not yet commented on his own accounts.

Head judge [Shirley Ballas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Ballas) wished him well in the comments, according to [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/tv/reality-tv/a74425903/strictly-come-dancing-kai-widdrington-illness-delta-goodrem/). The report adds that Škorjanec is covering for one week only.

## A series of setbacks for Goodrem

This is not the first interruption. Earlier in the series Goodrem was given a bye through to the next round after fracturing a rib, and she missed Icons Week, Wales Online reported. Under the show's rules she was given a pass for a week to recover.

She came back on 24 October with Widdrington and scored the series' first 10 for a Rumba to Céline Dion's *It's All Coming Back to Me Now*, according to the [London Evening Standard](https://www.standard.co.uk/showbiz/celebrity-news/bbc-one-delta-goodrem-aljaz-skorjanec-jaime-winstone-charleston-b1304829.html). That score makes the timing of the swap harder, because the pair had been building momentum.

Some fans were sympathetic. One comment quoted by the Daily Mail read: "Poor Delta (and Kai) going through the trenches this series."

## Who is stepping in

Škorjanec is no newcomer to this year's series. Digital Spy notes he earlier danced with [Melanie Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melanie_Walters), who was the second contestant eliminated. That leaves him free to partner Goodrem now.

The Argentine Tango is a dance built on close contact, which makes a late switch tricky. On *It Takes Two*, former professional Luba Mushtuk said she thought Goodrem was "in such safe hands" and described Škorjanec as "wonderful at leading", according to Wales Online. Ashley Roberts called Goodrem a "trooper". Katie Piper was more cautious, saying a new partner "could derail you" if she does not like change.

## Another return on the floor

Saturday's show also brings back [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) and her partner [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu). Winstone missed last week with a torn abdominal muscle. In an Instagram video quoted by the Standard, she said they had been "given the all-clear under certain rules by the doctors" and planned to dance a Charleston.

Goodrem has a busy day off the dance floor too. Digital Spy reports she released a new single, "Telescope", on Friday, from her upcoming album *Pure*.

## What happens next

*Strictly Come Dancing* airs on BBC One on Saturday at 6:25pm UK time, with the results show on Sunday at 7:15pm. Goodrem and Škorjanec are due to dance the Argentine Tango in the live show.

Fans have raised hopes that Widdrington will be back soon. A number of comments cited by the Daily Mail mention the Blackpool special, which Digital Spy says Goodrem is competing to reach. No return date for Widdrington has been announced in the sources.
