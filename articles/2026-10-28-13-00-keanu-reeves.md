**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is reported to be taking the lead in Netflix's action thriller *Maelstrom*, according to Collider and a Hypebeast headline on 28 October 2026.
- [Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra) is directing the film.
- Collider reports Reeves is replacing Alan Ritchson in the role.
- Separately, *John Wick: Chapter 4* is back in the ten most-watched films on Prime Video in the US.

Keanu Reeves is reported to be heading to Netflix for *Maelstrom*, a one-night action thriller set in the middle of a hurricane. Collider says the actor steps into a part previously linked to *Reacher* star Alan Ritchson, with Collet-Serra in the director's chair.

## What we know about Maelstrom

According to [Collider](https://collider.com/keanu-reeves-john-wick-chapter-4-streaming-hit-prime-video-october-2026/), Reeves would play a Federal Marshal assigned to guard the fiancée of an arms dealer. She is seeking witness protection while she prepares to testify against him.

The story unfolds across a single night as a hurricane closes in. That setup suits a director who has already made action films for the streamer. Hypebeast ran the news under the headline "Keanu Reeves to Star in Netflix Action Thriller 'Maelstrom'", though its page could not be read in full, so this article relies on Collider for the detail.

We have not seen a Netflix announcement. Treat the casting as reported rather than officially confirmed until the studio says so.

## A franchise that keeps paying off

Collider links the news to the actor's best-known series. *John Wick: Chapter 4* ran 169 minutes including credits, the longest entry in the series. The length did not hurt it. The film took a reported $447 million worldwide against a reported $100 million budget, making it the highest-grossing film in the franchise and the 16th biggest release of 2023, according to Collider.

Three years on, it is again among the ten most-watched films on Prime Video in the US at the time Collider wrote. Collider's Ross Bonaime called it "a goofy, ridiculous, three hours of fun" in his review.

## Why this casting stands out

Playing a lawman guarding a witness through a storm is a change from the assassin of the Wick films, but it keeps Reeves in the physical, high-pressure territory audiences know him for. A single-night, single-location structure also echoes the pressure-cooker style of films such as [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard), a comparison that is ours rather than the sources'.
