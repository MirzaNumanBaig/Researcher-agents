**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) scored 35 out of 40 for her Argentine Tango on Saturday 14 November, her highest total on Strictly so far.
- Her usual partner, [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington), was ruled out by illness, and [Aljaž Škorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in.
- Goodrem told the Daily Mail that Widdrington became so unwell he had to go to hospital.
- Next week's show is the Blackpool special at the Tower Ballroom.

Delta Goodrem turned a last-minute partner swap into her best night on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing). The Australian singer and Aljaž Škorjanec earned 35 points from a possible 40 for their Argentine Tango on Saturday 14 November, after Kai Widdrington dropped out through illness.

## How the partner swap happened

The change was announced on Friday. According to [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy), presenter [Ellie Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellie_Taylor) confirmed on the spin-off show It Takes Two that Widdrington could not perform the next night and that Škorjanec would dance the Argentine Tango with Goodrem. The Daily Star reports the BBC also posted a statement on its official Instagram page, wishing Widdrington a quick recovery.

That left very little time to prepare. Goodrem told the Daily Mail that she had rehearsed with three different partners during the week, and called it "an extremely crazy week". The pair danced to "Missing" by Everything But The Girl.

Škorjanec was a familiar face to the show's viewers. According to JournalArta, he left the competition on 11 October, when he was eliminated alongside Melanie Walters, so he was available to step in.

## The score and the dedication

The judges gave the routine 35 out of 40. Digital Spy says the pair impressed despite the short rehearsal time, and that Goodrem dedicated the dance to Widdrington before the marks were read out. The Daily Mail also describes the total as her highest of the series.

The panel for the night was Shirley Ballas, Motsi Mabuse, Craig Revel Horwood and Anton Du Beke, according to the Daily Star. To put the result in context, 35 is only five marks short of the maximum, and it came in a week when the Argentine Tango, a dance that fans on social media called tricky, was performed with a partner she had barely trained with.

## Kai Widdrington's illness

Goodrem gave the most detail on Widdrington's condition. Speaking to the Daily Mail, she said the dancer had become so unwell on Friday that he needed hospital treatment, and that she and Škorjanec were "sending him so much love". She added that she was proud of her partner and expected him to come back stronger.

Neither the BBC nor Goodrem has said what the illness is, and no return date has been given. A tabloid headline has suggested Widdrington might leave the show altogether, but the outlets we could read do not support that, and we have not repeated it as fact.

## A bumpy run for Goodrem

This is not the first disruption in her campaign. Digital Spy notes that Goodrem sat out Icons Week last month with a rib injury. At the time she said she was "absolutely gutted" to miss it and wanted to rest and recover. She has since returned and, in the words of the same report, kept improving week by week.

The Daily Star reported that some viewers feared the swap would hurt her chances of reaching Blackpool. Others were more upbeat, backing Goodrem and Škorjanec to deliver. The score settled that debate for now.

## What happens next

The results show airs on Sunday from 7.15pm on BBC One and iPlayer, according to JournalArta. Nine couples danced on Saturday, and those who stay in move on to the Tower Ballroom in Blackpool, which Digital Spy describes as a major milestone for the celebrity contestants.

Whether Widdrington is well enough to dance with Goodrem in Blackpool has not been confirmed.
