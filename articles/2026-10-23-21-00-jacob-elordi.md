**Quick summary**

- Jacob Elordi is reportedly not among the actors being screen-tested for the next James Bond, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Deadline says at least four actors remain in the running, and names Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor.
- Final auditions are reported to start on Friday 23 October and continue into the following week.
- Producer Amy Pascal has said the new 007 could be announced before the end of 2026. Amazon has not confirmed any names.

Days after a report claimed he had won the part, Australian actor [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) has reportedly been left off the list of actors screen-testing for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond). The Hollywood Reporter and Deadline both published casting updates on 22 October, and neither names the Euphoria star among the finalists, according to [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/movies/a74219069/james-bond-final-contenders/).

## What the trade reports say

Deadline's sources say four actors are still in contention. Three are known: [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and Josh O'Connor. The fourth has not been identified. The Hollywood Reporter puts the number at up to five and backs up the Lowden and Turner names, Digital Spy reports.

The Hollywood Reporter also says Elordi is not being screen-tested. Jack O'Connell, another actor previously linked to the role, is likewise reported to be outside the process for now.

Australian morning television picked up the story quickly. 7NEWS ran a segment titled "Jacob Elordi out of James Bond race", and Nine's Today show carried its own report on the shortlist.

## Where the Elordi rumour came from

The reversal is sharp. According to Digital Spy, Elordi had been linked to the part the day before, when a report from The Insider claimed Amazon MGM had seriously considered him. Other outlets then ran headlines saying he had been cast. Those claims were never confirmed by the studio, and the trade reports now point the other way.

Elordi has been tied to the franchise for some time, and Digital Spy describes him as someone continuously linked to the role. A link in the rumour mill is not a casting decision, and nothing in the sources shows he has been offered or has turned down anything.

## How the search has run so far

Digital Spy reports that first-round auditions were held over the summer and only a handful of names remain. Five years have passed since Daniel Craig's last film as 007, No Time To Die.

The iXBT.games site, citing Deadline, says the final auditions begin on Friday 23 October and run into next week. They were first meant to happen earlier but were pushed back because of scheduling conflicts among the actors.

The film already has its creative team. Dune director Denis Villeneuve is attached to direct, and Peaky Blinders creator Steven Knight is writing the script. Amy Pascal and David Heyman are producing. Asked last month whether he had an actor in mind, Knight said it helps to know who the person is, but that a writer has to write the character and not the actor, Digital Spy reports.

Other names that have circulated in recent years include Aaron Pierre, Richard Madden, Harris Dickinson, James Norton, Theo James and Idris Elba. None of them is reported to be in the final group.

## What happens next

Pascal said over the summer that an announcement could come before the end of the year. Amazon's only public line so far is that the search for the next Bond is under way and that it will not comment on specific details during casting. The company says it will share news as soon as the time is right.

If the reported schedule holds, the screen tests finish within days, though a decision could still take longer.
