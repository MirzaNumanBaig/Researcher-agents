**Quick summary**

- Sci-fi thriller *April X* stars [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) as Bax, a man searching for his missing twin sister.
- It runs 1 hour 32 minutes and opened in the US on 2 October, according to Fangirlish.
- It reaches HBO Max on 18 December, with a first HBO broadcast on 19 December at 8 pm ET.
- The film was shot before Storrie's breakout in *Heated Rivalry*.

Fans who came to Connor Storrie through *[Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry)* now have an earlier project to catch up on. *April X*, a sci-fi thriller filmed before that series made him famous, is heading to streaming next month. Here is what is known about the film, based on a rundown by [Fangirlish](https://fangirlish.com/2026/11/21/everything-we-know-about-connor-storries-april-x/).

## What is April X about?

The story follows Bax, played by Storrie, whose twin sister April goes missing. He searches a futuristic, post-Soviet cityscape for her and slides into madness along the way. [Lilly Krug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilly_Krug) plays April.

Fangirlish notes the role shares a few traits with Storrie's breakout part as [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov), though it sets that kind of intensity in a science-fiction world. The outlet describes the film as proof of his range.

## Who made it?

[Michel K. Parandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_K._Parandi) directed the film and co-wrote it with Jack Coulton. Alongside Storrie and Krug, the cast includes Tudor Chirila, [Serban Pavlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%98erban_Pavlu) and Tourialay Akbari.

At 92 minutes, it is a short watch by modern standards.

## The soundtrack

[Milan Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Records) released "He Won't Get Far", the lead single from composer Matthew Atticus Berger's score, with vocals by Amanda Achen. Fangirlish reports the full soundtrack was timed to the film's theatrical release.

Berger said the track is the theme the whole score grew from and that he wrote it before seeing any footage. He described it as built entirely from voice and drums, with Achen singing throughout as April. The title comes from a line spoken by the villain, and this version of the theme accompanies a motorcycle chase.

## Where and when can you watch it?

According to Fangirlish, the film opened in the US on 2 October. It streams on HBO Max from 18 December, and it debuts on HBO's linear channel on Saturday, 19 December at 8 pm ET. The report does not give an Australian release date, so local viewers should check their own streaming services.

Storrie has other recent credits too. He appeared as a guest star in *[Criminal Minds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_Minds): Evolution* and hosted *Saturday Night Live*, Fangirlish says.
