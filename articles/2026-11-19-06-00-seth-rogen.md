**Quick summary**

- The documentary "Taking Care" screened at the Ashley Gibson Barnett Museum of Art in Lakeland, Florida, during Arts and Health Week, according to GreaterGood.
- It follows Adele Miller, mother of Lauren Miller Rogen and mother-in-law of Seth Rogen, who was diagnosed with early onset Alzheimer's at 55 and died in 2020.
- About 125 people attended, and roughly two-thirds said someone close to them had been affected by Alzheimer's or dementia.
- Separately, Rogen told the Happy Sad Confused podcast that the *12 Years a Slave* cast visited his *This Is the End* set in New Orleans, BroBible reports.

**Seth Rogen** and his wife, [Lauren Miller Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Miller_Rogen), have taken their Alzheimer's documentary "Taking Care" back to the Florida city where she grew up. [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) described the film as a story of resilience, according to a GreaterGood report published this week on the Lakeland screening.

## A screening in Lakeland

The film played at the Ashley Gibson Barnett Museum of Art during Lakeland's Arts and Health Week. Museum executive director Daryl Ward asked the audience of about 125 people to raise a hand if someone close to them had lived with Alzheimer's or dementia. Roughly two-thirds did, GreaterGood reports.

Miller Rogen, a screenwriter, introduced the film in a prerecorded message. She recalled walking through the museum's galleries every day as a student at Lakeland's Harrison School for the Arts, and called the city and the museum a significant part of her own story.

## Who Adele Miller was

"Taking Care" centres on Adele Miller. Her family moved to Lakeland in the late 1980s, and she taught at Sikes Elementary School, where she was named Teacher of the Year in 2003. Her son Dan told the outlet that the award came "three or four years before things really went downhill."

Adele was 55 when she was diagnosed with early onset Alzheimer's. A friend, Marlene Wlodawski, described her as someone devoted to her family, her students and her community, who liked to say she always had to do her part. Wlodawski also said Adele was a very private person who at first resisted sharing the diagnosis beyond a small circle.

## What the film shows

As the disease advanced, Adele and her husband, Scott, moved to Los Angeles, where their daughter and her husband were already working in the entertainment industry. GreaterGood says the couple's success helped the family arrange round-the-clock care at home. The documentary follows Adele's gradual loss of speech, mobility and the ability to feed herself. She died in 2020.

According to Dan, the film went through many versions. Some scenes made him uncomfortable, but the family chose to include them in the belief that the full picture would help others understand Alzheimer's and caregiving. GreaterGood reports few dry eyes in the room, and says the film also leaves space for humour and hope.

## The 12 Years a Slave story

In lighter news, Rogen has been talking about a very different set. On the Happy Sad Confused podcast, hosted by [Josh Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Horowitz), he said *This Is the End* was shot in New Orleans at the same time as *12 Years a Slave*, according to [BroBible](https://brobible.com/culture/article/12-years-a-slave-cast-this-is-the-end/).

Rogen said the *12 Years a Slave* cast and crew were often in a "bummed-out headspace" given the material. Director Steve McQueen, [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) and [Chiwetel Ejiofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiwetel_Ejiofor) would drop by his set, he said, and a few drinks later they would perk up. We have more in our earlier piece, [Seth Rogen on Cheering Up the 12 Years a Slave Cast](https://moduscarry.com/seth-rogen-12-years-a-slave/).

*This Is the End* was released in 2013. Rogen co-wrote and co-directed it with [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg), and the cast included [James Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Franco), [Jonah Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonah_Hill), [Jay Baruchel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Baruchel), [Craig Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Robinson_(actor)) and [Danny McBride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_McBride). BroBible notes that *12 Years a Slave* earned nine Academy Award nominations and won three, including Best Picture.

It is a documentary about Adele Miller and her family's experience of early onset Alzheimer's, including her decline and the home care she received. Lauren Miller Rogen is her daughter.
