**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) is set to star in The Confessions, according to an exclusive in Variety on 10 November 2026.
- Hunter Schafer, Maya Hawke and Harry Melling are also cast. Ari Aster's company Square Peg is producing.
- Canadian trio Guy Maddin, Galen Johnson and Evan Johnson are making the film, which is being sold at the American Film Market.
- No start date or release window has been announced.

Alexander Skarsgård will star in The Confessions, a new film from Canadian directors Guy Maddin, Galen Johnson and Evan Johnson, with [Ari Aster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Aster) and Lars Knudsen's Square Peg producing. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/skarsgard-hunter-schafer-maya-hawke-harry-melling-confessions-1236944297/) broke the news this week as the project was launched to buyers at the American Film Market (AFM).

## Who is in The Confessions cast?

Variety lists Skarsgård (Wicker), [Hunter Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Schafer) (Blade Runner 2099), [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke) (Stranger Things) and [Harry Melling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Melling) (Pillion) as the four leads. It's a reunion for Skarsgård and Melling, who appeared together in A24's Pillion, [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/alexander-skarsgard-confessions-movie-cast-hunter-schafer-harry-melling/) notes.

The outlet also points to Skarsgård's earlier run of television and film work: True Blood, an Emmy-winning turn in Big Little Lies, and two more Emmy nominations for playing tech billionaire Lukas Matsson in Succession.

## What is the film about?

The Confessions is billed as a "cautionary psycho-dramedy". It follows a group of condemned nuns who bear witness to the accursed, globe-spanning love triangle of a missing sea captain and his poet wife, according to Variety. Which of the four actors plays which part hasn't been revealed.

Aster, who is producing, gave an eccentric statement. He called himself a devoted fan of Maddin and the Johnsons, and described the film as "part religio-maritime rough-music psychomachy, part hallucinated saltwater shaming parade".

## The filmmakers and the money behind it

[Guy Maddin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Maddin) and the Johnson brothers made their first feature together with Rumours, an absurdist political comedy about world leaders lost in the woods during a G7 summit. It stars Cate Blanchett, Alicia Vikander and Charles Dance, and it premiered out of competition at Cannes in 2024.

Square Peg came on board during early development after working with them on that film. Variety lists the company's credits as Bugonia, Midsommar and The Drama. [Scythia Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scythia_Films), the Canadian company behind The Witch and The Apprentice, is also attached, along with the directors' own Thin Stuff and support from Telefilm Canada.

On the sales side, CAA Media Finance and WME Independent are co-representing North American rights, and French company Charades is handling international sales. Elevation Pictures will release the film in Canada, [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2221281-alexander-skarsgard-hunter-schafer-maya-hawke-the-confessions-announcement) reports.

## A busy week for Skarsgård

The casting news landed in the same week as a very different project. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2221026-alexander-skarsgard-wicker-nsfw-spin-off), as relayed by ComingSoon.net, says Skarsgård will narrate Felled, a story by Emma Parkinson. It won a fan-fiction contest run by Black Bear and the audio app Quinn, set in the world of his film Wicker. ComingSoon.net says there are no concrete plans for a Wicker sequel.

Skarsgård said he hadn't been part of a project that "has taken on a life of its own like this" before. [Her Campus](https://www.hercampus.com/wellness/alexander-skarsgard-quinn-original-audio/) reported that Quinn teased the release on 8 and 9 November and set it for 10 November, exclusively in the Quinn app. It's an adult-themed audio story, so it isn't for everyone. Wicker co-stars [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman).

## What happens next

The Confessions is at the sales stage. The filmmakers haven't announced a production start date or a theatrical window, ComingSoon.net reports, so the AFM deals are the next thing to watch. Further international distribution announcements would normally follow once buyers sign.
