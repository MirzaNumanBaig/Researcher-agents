**Quick summary**

- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) posted a tribute on Instagram after the death of Australian music figure [Molly Meldrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Meldrum).
- Meldrum died on Monday aged 83, according to Rolling Stone Australia.
- Minogue said she grew up watching him on Countdown and appeared on the show by her late teens.
- Details of a celebration of his life will be announced in due course.

Kylie Minogue has paid tribute to Molly Meldrum, the Countdown presenter who died on Monday at the age of 83, saying she will "turn the music up" in his honour. The singer shared her message on Instagram, as reported by [Rolling Stone Australia](https://au.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/kylie-minogue-tribute-molly-meldrum-103632/).

## What Kylie Minogue said about Molly Meldrum

Minogue began with a childhood memory. She wrote that she watched Meldrum on Countdown every weekend, "in awe of the show and the magic it brought to our little TV in the lounge room", and dreamed of how music might become part of her life.

She then described how close that world later came. She was thrilled to sit in the Countdown audience once, and by her late teens she was appearing on the show herself. Meldrum also visited the set of Neighbours, she noted, adding that "there was never a dull moment".

Her closing words credited him with a wider legacy. "He championed so many Aussie artists and welcomed international artists to Australia in his own wonderful and inimitable way," she wrote, before signing off with "Vale Molly".

## Why Meldrum mattered to her career

According to Rolling Stone Australia, Meldrum was an early supporter of Minogue, backing her rise from Neighbours actor to international pop star. The outlet describes their friendship as one of the most recognisable in Australian entertainment, spanning decades of milestones and public appearances.

Countdown ran from 1974 to 1987 and helped bring Australian music into living rooms across the country. Many musicians have credited Meldrum with launching their careers and introducing their songs to wider audiences.

## Other tributes

Minogue is one of many artists to respond. Rolling Stone Australia lists [Jimmy Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Barnes), [Kate Ceberano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Ceberano), Savage Garden's Darren Hayes, Human Nature, [Jason Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Donovan), [Marcia Hines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcia_Hines), Michael Bublé and Matchbox Twenty among those who have shared their respects.

Mushroom Group CEO Matt Gudinski also issued a statement. He said Meldrum helped shape the Australian music industry when it was still in its infancy, and described a close bond with his own family, including his late father Michael Gudinski. He recalled Christmas celebrations, family dinners and weekends at the footy as fellow St Kilda supporters.

Meldrum's work was recognised with inductions into the Logies Hall of Fame in 2012, the ARIA Hall of Fame in 2014 and the Music Victoria Hall of Fame in 2018.
