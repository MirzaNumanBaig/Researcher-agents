**Quick summary**

- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) wore a cream Chanel outfit to the Cruise 2027 presentation in Sydney on Thursday, according to WWD.
- Her shoes were Chanel's two-toned slingback pumps with a block heel and a black cap toe, in a mascarpone shade.
- It was the first time Sydney has hosted the brand's repeat cruise show.
- Margot Robbie and Chanel ambassador Nicole Kidman were also at the event.

[Kylie Minogue](https://kylie.com) arrived at Chanel's Cruise 2027 presentation in Sydney on Thursday dressed head to toe in the French house, and the detail fashion writers picked out was her footwear. According to [WWD](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/kylie-minogue-two-toned-chanel-heels-sydney-2027-cruise-collection-1239405638/), the singer chose the label's classic two-toned pumps to finish a soft, restrained look. It is one of the quieter outfits of the week, and the shoes are the reason it stands out.

## What Kylie Minogue wore

WWD describes the outfit as semi-formal and built around cream. Minogue wore an ivory button-down cardigan with a midi skirt in a slightly lighter white. A multi-strand belt of leather and gold chain sat at her waist, a Chanel signature that gave the otherwise plain pieces some weight.

The publication says she kept her makeup natural and carried a clutch under her right arm. The overall effect, in WWD's words, was elegant and professional rather than showy.

## The Chanel Kylie Minogue heels

The Chanel Kylie Minogue heels are the house's best-known shoe. WWD notes the pair she chose has slingback straps, a block heel and the black cap toe that gives the style its name. Her pair was in a mascarpone shade, a creamy off-white that matched the clothes rather than contrasting with them.

That choice is part of why the look reads as subtle. Instead of a bold colour or a high stiletto, she repeated the tones of the outfit and let the cap toe be the single dark accent. ## Why the Sydney show matters

The evening was a repeat of a collection that has already been shown once. WWD reports that creative director Matthieu Blazy first presented the Cruise 2027 line in the French seaside resort of Biarritz in April. Chanel usually stages a large cruise show in spring and a smaller repeat in early autumn, and last year's repeat took place in Singapore.

This year the repeat came to Australia for the first time. WWD points out that eight of the brand's Australian boutiques are in Sydney, which makes the city a logical host. Other Australian guests included [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and Chanel ambassador [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman).

The cruise line itself is a long tradition at the brand. WWD says it dates back more than 100 years to 1919, when Coco Chanel created it for wealthy women who wanted out-of-season clothes for holidays in warmer climates. The report adds that the 2027 products were due in stores shortly after Thursday's event.

WWD does not report any further Chanel events in Australia, so there is nothing confirmed beyond the Sydney show. The next thing to watch is the in-store arrival of the Cruise 2027 products, which the publication says is imminent.
