**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) headed the London premiere of season 6 of *Only Murders in the Building* on Monday evening.
- The event was held at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) did not attend.
- The new season, subtitled *Only Murders in London*, starts on Disney+ with two episodes on 8 December.

Martin Short and Steve Martin brought the cast of [*Only Murders in the Building*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building) to central London on Monday for the season 6 premiere, a night that mixed the show's American core with a long list of British and Irish guests. The Hollywood Reporter says fans turned out at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square despite freezing temperatures.

## A premiere built around the British cast

The new season moves the story to London, so the guest list leaned heavily on local talent. Alongside the two leads, series regular [Michael Cyril Creighton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Cyril_Creighton) walked the carpet. So did [Nicola Coughlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Coughlan), [Martin Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Freeman), [David Tennant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Tennant), [Jim Broadbent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Broadbent) and [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman).

The list went on: [Simone Ashley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simone_Ashley), [Geri Halliwell-Horner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geri_Halliwell), [Lesley Nicol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_Nicol_(actress)), [Anjana Vasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anjana_Vasan), [Amar Chadha-Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amar_Chadha-Patel), [Jane Horrocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Horrocks), [Sean Teale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Teale), [Sharon Horgan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharon_Horgan), [Jodie Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Whittaker), [Jamie Demetriou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamie_Demetriou), [Richard E. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Grant), [Tim Key](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Key), [Joe Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Anderson_(actor)) and [Simon Callow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Callow), among others.

Coughlan and Ashley, who both starred in *Bridgerton*, gave the carpet what the trade paper called a mini reunion. Co-creator and executive producer John Hoffman also made the trip, with fellow executive producers Jess Rosenthal and Kristin Bernstein.

## Short and Martin warm up the room

Before the screening of episodes 1 and 3, chosen because they feature more of the British faces, the two leads opened the night in their usual style. Martin joked that travelling with Short meant no paparazzi. He then said it was an honour to stand beside someone who looked like a former women's tennis player.

Short answered with a line of his own, telling the crowd that of all the people he admires, Steve is very close to being one of them. The exchange drew laughs, according to The Hollywood Reporter. It also matches the duo's long-running double act, which our earlier piece on [Martin Short and Steve Martin's real friendship ritual](https://moduscarry.com/martin-short-only-murders-easter-egg/) looked at in more detail.

## What season 6 is about

The series follows three true-crime podcasters: Charles-Haden Savage (Martin), Oliver Putnam (Short) and Mabel Mora (Gomez). This season they investigate the death of Cinda Canning, played by Tina Fey. The trail takes them to London, where they meet a large cast of suspicious and eccentric characters.

The official synopsis, quoted by the trade paper, asks whether the trio can follow the woman whose podcast started it all without ending up the way she did.
