**Quick summary**

- The six-episode second season of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) ends with the episode "Peace Summit", in which Helen Webb finally admits her spy life to her husband.
- The reveal is forced: her children's lives are threatened, according to The Daily Beast's recap.
- Rival agency Kestrel is behind the siege on London and plans to pin two assassinations on the Black Doves.
- Spoilers follow for the whole of season 2.

[Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) spends the season 2 finale of Black Doves doing something her character has avoided for years: telling the truth. In "Peace Summit", Helen Webb admits to her husband, the Prime Minister, that she is a spy, in the middle of an attack on London. The Daily Beast [described the episode](https://www.thedailybeast.com/obsessed/keira-knightley-blows-up-her-life-in-explosive-black-doves-finale) as the moment the Netflix thriller throws a hand grenade into its own premise.

## What happens in the Keira Knightley Black Doves finale

Prime Minister Wallace Webb, played by [Andrew Buchan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Buchan), confronts Helen after their motorcade is attacked on the day of a major summit. What gives her away, as the recap tells it, is that she shows little fear during a clearly frightening moment. He tells her he thinks she is a liar and does not know how far back the lie goes.

Helen's first slip comes while talking to her friend and fellow spy-assassin Sam, played by [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw). She realises she has just told Wallace she is a spy. The confession is forced, because her children's lives are in danger, but it is a confession all the same.

Wallace had already been given a name for her. According to the recap, MI5 agent Perryman, played by [Adam Best](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Best_(actor)), told him earlier that Helen is really called Lily Morris. Perryman had that information from Kestrel, the rival spies-for-hire organisation.

## Who is behind the siege

The attack was planned by Katia Chernov, played by [Sylvia Hoeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvia_Hoeks). She turns out to be Vita, the long-lost daughter of Black Doves boss Reed, played by [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire), and she runs Kestrel.

Her plan, per The Daily Beast, is to blame the Black Doves for the killings of U.S. defence envoy Cecile Mason, played by [Neve Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Campbell), and of the Prime Minister. Helen would be unmasked as the person behind his death. Katia tells Helen through an earpiece to kill Wallace and then confess under her real name, or her children will die.

The recap also credits the season with making that marriage work as drama. The series opens with Wallace telling their couples therapist that the relationship feels like an act. Helen also admits to him that the qualities he loves in her were chosen because the Black Doves had researched what would appeal to him, before she fell for him herself. [Kathryn Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hunter) also appears as Lenny at the Black Doves headquarters.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Keira Knightley play in Black Doves?

She plays Helen Webb, the wife of the British Prime Minister, who secretly works as a spy for the spies-for-hire organisation the Black Doves.

### How many episodes are in Black Doves season 2?

Season 2 has six episodes, according to The Daily Beast. The finale is titled "Peace Summit".

### Who is behind the attack in the Black Doves finale?

Katia Chernov, played by Sylvia Hoeks, who is really Vita, Reed's long-lost daughter and the head of the rival group Kestrel.

### Does Wallace find out Helen is a spy?

Yes. Helen's forced confession in the finale tells him she is a spy, though he already knew she had lied about who she was.
