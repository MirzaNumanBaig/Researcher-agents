**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) attended the New York Knicks' home game at Madison Square Garden on Sunday night.
- The Knicks beat the Indiana Pacers 113-106, and the team's social accounts highlighted her with a nod to her breakout film role.
- Yahoo Sports reports that she chugged a drink after a jumbotron shoutout, which made her a crowd favourite.
- She is campaigning for an Oscar nomination for the horror film Obsession and is due to play Rogue in Marvel's X-Men reboot in May 2028.

Inde Navarrette was the talk of Celebrity Row at Madison Square Garden on Sunday night, when the Obsession star turned up to watch the Knicks beat the Pacers 113-106 and ended up one of the most-discussed people in the building.

According to [Yahoo Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Sports), the Knicks' official social media accounts singled her out with a nod to her 2026 breakout role. Its headline says she chugged a beer on the jumbotron, and a Sportskeeda headline describes her chugging a drink after the shoutout, with Knicks fans wooed by the moment. Neither outlet's full detail of the moment could be confirmed beyond those reports.

## What happened on the court

The game itself was a strong night for the home side. Yahoo Sports says Karl-Anthony Towns led the way with 30 points and 13 rebounds. Josh Hart recorded a triple-double with 10 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists. Jalen Brunson, whom the outlet calls the NBA Finals MVP, added 22 points and 7 assists.

Even so, the report says the person who stood out after the final buzzer was not on the court. It was Navarrette, who sat among the famous faces on Celebrity Row.

## Why she is everywhere right now

Navarrette, 25, plays Nikki in Curry Barker's horror film Obsession, which Yahoo Sports describes as an overwhelming success. She is currently campaigning for an Oscar nomination for the role, and the outlet says she has already collected the Rising Star Award at the Palm Springs International Film Awards. She was also named IMDb's Breakthrough Star of 2026.

## The projects lined up behind Obsession

Yahoo Sports says Navarrette is set to play Rogue in Marvel Studios' X-Men reboot, which is due in May 2028. She is also attached to the sci-fi thriller The Waffle House Index, in which she is both starring and serving as an executive producer.
