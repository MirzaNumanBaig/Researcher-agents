**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) opened [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing)'s Blackpool special on Saturday 21 November.
- Their quickstep scored 36 out of 40, the highest mark of their series, and left them second on the leaderboard.
- Many viewers said online that the marks were too low, according to the Manchester Evening News.
- A TV Zone UK headline says [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) went out after a dance-off against Goodrem.

Delta Goodrem's Blackpool quickstep earned her best score of the series, yet it was the reaction from viewers that drew attention afterwards. Many fans argued the judges had underrated a routine danced in a difficult costume, and by Sunday the singer was reported to have come through a dance-off against Jaime Winstone.

## Fans say the scores were too low

Goodrem and Widdrington went first on the Blackpool night, after a festival-style opening number from Basement Jaxx. Their quickstep to "I Wanna Be the Only One", originally by Eternal with [Bebe Winans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BeBe_Winans), earned 36 points, according to the [Manchester Evening News](https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/tv/strictly-fans-left-delta-goodrem-34949362).

That put the pair second. Three couples tied at the top on 39: [Cach Mercer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cach_Mercer) with [Maddie Ingoldsby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddie_Ingoldsby), John Nellis with [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones), and Tabby Stoecker with [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita).

Viewers were not happy. The newspaper quoted several posts on social media, including one asking what Goodrem would have to do to earn a 10. Others argued she was marked down for going first, with one saying the dance would have drawn higher scores later in the running order. Several pointed out how hard the dress must have been to dance in, and one said a costume malfunction could easily have happened.

These are opinions from individual viewers, not a measure of the wider audience. The judges' marks are only half the picture, because the public vote also counts towards who stays.

## A hard road through the series

The Blackpool night was a return to normal for a couple whose run has been interrupted. The Manchester Evening News notes that Goodrem missed a week earlier in the competition with a painful rib injury. Last weekend Widdrington fell ill before their performance, and fellow professional [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in to dance with her.

The same report says Goodrem was the first contestant to score a 10 earlier in the series, which is why some fans felt a 36 was a step back from what she had shown.

For the full account of the performance and the judges' comments, see our earlier report on [Goodrem's series-high 36 in Blackpool](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-strictly-blackpool-quickstep/).

## The dance-off with Jaime Winstone

Sunday's results show brought a dance-off. A TV Zone UK headline reports that Winstone was eliminated after facing Goodrem in it. We could not open that article, so only the headline is confirmed, and we have no detail on the judges' votes or the dances performed. We will update coverage when a full account is available.

If the headline is right, Goodrem and Widdrington stay in the competition for another week after being sent to the bottom two despite their series-best score from the judges.

## What happens next

The couple now move on to the next live show, with the finals getting closer. Goodrem has so far recovered from an injury and a change of partner within a few weeks, so the next test is whether the judges' marks and the public vote line up for her.
