**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s action thriller *The Get Out* was the No. 9 movie on Paramount+ in the US on 5 November 2026, according to ScreenRant.
- *The Nice Guys*, his 2016 film with [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling), was the most-watched movie on Apple TV in the US, per FlixPatrol data cited by Comic Book Resources.
- *The Nice Guys* grossed $62.8 million against a $50 million budget, so it lost the box-office race but won a cult following.
- Both films sit on the charts at the same time, which shows how far Crowe's catalogue is travelling on streaming.

Russell Crowe streaming hits are suddenly everywhere: two very different films are doing well on US charts this week. On 5 November 2026, his recent action thriller *The Get Out* ranked No. 9 on Paramount+, while *The Nice Guys*, a film that struggled in cinemas a decade ago, held the top spot on Apple TV, according to reports from ScreenRant and Comic Book Resources.

## The Get Out finds a second life on Paramount+

*The Get Out* stars Crowe as Marco Kapak, a nightclub owner who is robbed by gunmen, falls into conflict with a cartel and tries to retire from a dangerous business. [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/russell-crowe-the-get-out-paramount-plus-streaming-success-november-2026/) that the film arrived on video on demand in June 2026 before moving to streaming.

On the chart data ScreenRant cites for 5 November, the film sits at No. 9 on Paramount+ in the US. That puts it ahead of the 2007 comedy *Norbit* and just behind the action comedy *Rush Hour*. Elsewhere it reached No. 4 worldwide on MGM+ and No. 14 worldwide on Prime Video, the same report says.

Critics were less kind. ScreenRant notes that [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) averages the reviews at 47%, which signals mixed opinion. Audiences have clearly not followed the critics. In the US the film can only be watched on Paramount+, so its chart position reflects one service rather than a spread across several.

## Russell Crowe streaming hits: The Nice Guys leads Apple TV

The bigger story may be the older film. According to [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/the-nice-guys-action-comedy-success-apple-tv-november-2026/), citing FlixPatrol, *The Nice Guys* is currently the most-watched movie on Apple TV in the US. Crowe plays enforcer Jackson Healy opposite Gosling's down-on-his-luck private investigator Holland March, and the pair team up in 1970s Los Angeles to find a young woman named Amelia before dangerous people do.

At release in 2016 the film grossed $62.8 million on a $50 million budget, which is a thin return once marketing is counted. Time has been kinder. Comic Book Resources says it holds a 91% Certified Fresh score from critics on Rotten Tomatoes, a 79% audience score, and a 7.4 rating from more than 420,000 reviews on IMDb.

We have followed this film's run on Apple TV before, including [Russell Crowe's The Nice Guys Hits No. 1 on Apple TV](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-the-nice-guys-number-one/) and [Russell Crowe's The Nice Guys Climbs Apple TV Charts](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-the-nice-guys-apple-tv/).

## A cast that still draws attention

The supporting cast helps explain the film's afterlife. [Angourie Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angourie_Rice) plays March's daughter Holly, and [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley) plays Amelia Kuttner. [Matt Bomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bomer) and [Keith David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_David) also appear, alongside Kim Basinger, Yaya DaCosta and others, according to Comic Book Resources.

The outlet also quotes reviewers who singled out the central pairing. Richard Roeper of the Chicago Sun-Times wrote that Crowe and Gosling "save the day", while Amy Nicholson of MTV described them as a perfect mismatch. That chemistry is the usual reason fans give for wanting a sequel, though Comic Book Resources describes the updates on one as disappointing.

## A busy year on screen

ScreenRant frames the two chart placings as part of a wider run for Crowe in 2026. It lists *Beast*, an MMA comeback drama that did well on Starz, and the mystery thriller *Sleeping Dogs*, a Paramount+ hit. It also mentions the Netflix crime drama *Unabomber* and his spy thriller *Body of Lies*, which has charted on Apple TV. Our earlier piece, [Russell Crowe's Body of Lies Reaches Apple TV Top 10](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-body-of-lies-apple-tv/), covered that one.

ScreenRant's conclusion is that Crowe has become a dependable name on streaming across genres. Chart positions shift daily, so these figures are a snapshot of 5 November rather than a lasting ranking.

## What happens next

Streaming charts move quickly, and neither film is guaranteed to hold its place. ScreenRant says more Crowe releases are coming, though it does not name them. For now, *The Get Out* remains exclusive to Paramount+ in the US, and *The Nice Guys* is available on Apple TV. ## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I watch The Get Out in the US?
Only on Paramount+, according to ScreenRant. It was first released on video on demand in June 2026.

### Where is The Nice Guys streaming?
Comic Book Resources says fans can stream *The Nice Guys* on Apple TV, where it is currently the most-watched movie in the US per FlixPatrol.

### Was The Nice Guys a box-office flop?
It was not a hit on release. It grossed $62.8 million against a $50 million budget, but it earned strong reviews and has built a cult following since 2016.

### Is there a Nice Guys sequel?
Not confirmed. Comic Book Resources refers to disappointing updates on a possible sequel and says fans would still like to see Crowe and Gosling return.
