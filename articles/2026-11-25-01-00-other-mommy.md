**Quick summary**

- A new review in the Massachusetts Daily Collegian argues that *Other Mommy* suffers a "tonal identity crisis".
- The film holds a 19% critic score and a 48% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, according to the review.
- Director Rob Savage reportedly touted around 50 jump scares, which the reviewer says crowd out the story.
- Wikipedia's records put the box office at about US$119.4 million against an US$18 million budget.

The horror film *Other Mommy* is still dividing viewers, with a fresh review in the Massachusetts Daily Collegian arguing that its jump scares and uneven tone undercut a promising idea. The [Other Mommy review](https://dailycollegian.com/2026/11/the-tonal-identity-crisis-of-other-mommy/), published on 24 November, says the film promised far more than it delivered.

## A trailer that outshone the film

According to the Daily Collegian, [*Other Mommy*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Other_Mommy) went viral before its release. Its trailer ran ahead of screenings of *The Odyssey* and, the paper says, had audiences jumping in their seats, thanks largely to one shot of [Jessica Chastain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Chastain) twisting into a demonic contortion.

[Rob Savage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Savage) directed the film, which is based on the book *Incidents Around the House* by [Josh Malerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Malerman). It reached cinemas in early October. The reviewer says expectations were high, then notes that the result landed with a 19% critic score and a 48% audience score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes).

## What the story is about

The film centres on Bela Martin, an eight-year-old girl played by Arabella Olivia Clark. She is caught between a work-obsessed mother, Ursula (Chastain), and an overwhelmed stay-at-home father, Russ ([Jay Duplass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Duplass)).

As the marriage fractures, a shapeshifting entity emerges from Bela's closet wearing her mother's face. Bela calls it "Other Mommy". It offers friendship and promises to take her pain away, but only if it can enter her body.

The Collegian reviewer finds the setup compelling. Even so, the review calls it a pale imitation of Henry Selick's 2009 film *Coraline*, which handled a child's longing for an idealised mother with more nuance.

## Where the review says it goes wrong

The main complaint is tone. The reviewer argues that the film leans on shock rather than unease, and says the real fear of a shapeshifter lies in not knowing who is real, not in constant stretching and distorting bodies.

Chastain's performance is the bright spot. The review, echoing other critics and viewers, says she is creepy in quiet moments, such as when she looms in the doorway of Bela's bedroom. The computer-generated effects then work against her, with limbs and faces morphing so far that the scene reads as cartoonish.

The reviewer also says Savage promoted the film as having about 50 jump scares. In their view, the writing spells out its themes. Ursula and Russ state their marital fears aloud instead of letting body language carry them, and a quiet family moment is followed straight away by a campy, brightly lit chase. The review quotes Loud and Clear Reviews writer Edgar Ortega, who called the tonal choices "strange".

## Panned, yet a hit

The critical picture matches the wider record. Wikipedia's film entry lists Metacritic at 39 out of 100 from 16 critics and quotes the Guardian's two-star review, which said the film begins as a tense thriller before turning into an increasingly absurd battle with a special effect.

Commercially, the numbers tell a different story. Wikipedia records a budget of US$18 million and a worldwide gross of about US$119.4 million, roughly 6.6 times the cost. Those figures come from Wikipedia and have not been confirmed by a studio statement in the sources reviewed here.

## What happens next

The Collegian's verdict is that viewers will leave laughing rather than sleeping with the lights on. There is no sign of a sequel in the sources reviewed for this article. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in Other Mommy?
Jessica Chastain plays both the mother, Ursula, and the demon that wears her face. Jay Duplass plays her husband, Russ, and Arabella Olivia Clark plays their daughter, Bela.

### What is Other Mommy based on?
It is based on the book *Incidents Around the House* by Josh Malerman, according to the Daily Collegian.

### How did Other Mommy do with critics?
Rotten Tomatoes shows a 19% critic score and a 48% audience score, according to the Daily Collegian. Wikipedia lists a Metacritic score of 39 out of 100.

### Did Other Mommy make money?
Yes, according to Wikipedia, which records a gross of about US$119.4 million against a US$18 million budget.
