**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) reacted on Instagram to a New South Wales Supreme Court ruling in her favour over her directorial debut, The Deb, according to OK Magazine.
- Her production company, Camp Sugar, was awarded $359,000 in unpaid producer fees plus interest.
- AI Film, the company behind the claim against her, received $10 in damages for technical breaches of a social media contract.
- Wilson called the case a David and Goliath battle and said it had cost her personally, emotionally and financially.
- A separate defamation case in California involving the film's producers is still going.

Rebel Wilson has broken her silence on her latest courtroom win, posting a graduation photo on Instagram with a caption that read: "This girl never knew how much she was going to need that law degree!! Two lawsuits. Two wins." The Australian comedian, 46, was responding to a New South Wales Supreme Court decision in a dispute with AI Film, a production company connected to The Deb, [OK Magazine reported](https://okmagazine.com/p/rebel-wilson-thankful-went-law-school-winning-trial-against-producers/).

## What Wilson said about the case

The Instagram post paired the joke with a more serious statement. Wilson said she had spoken up because she believed there were serious concerns that needed to be raised on The Deb, and that she did it to protect others.

She did not hide the toll. "Defending myself has come at an enormous personal, emotional and financial cost," she said, adding that she is proud she stood her ground "for myself and for others I was trying to protect."

Wilson also described the proceedings as "a true David and Goliath battle", pointing to what she called a huge imbalance of resources between the parties. She said there were times she wondered how much more she could take, but that she was proud she kept going.

Her message ended with a note for anyone in a similar position. She said she understands how hard it can be to speak up, especially when the people you are challenging have far greater resources.

## What the court decided

The figures show a split result. OK Magazine reports that Wilson's production company, [Camp Sugar](https://moduscarry.com/rebel-wilson-wins-359000-in-the-deb-fee-case/), was awarded $359,000 in unpaid producer fees, plus interest. AI Film was awarded just $10 for technical breaches of a social media contract.

That is a long way from what AI Film wanted. The company had sought $15 million, alleging that social media posts and a website carrying damaging claims about producer Amanda Ghost hurt its reputation and the film's commercial prospects.

The judge did find that Wilson had arranged for a website containing false allegations about Ghost to be created, according to the report. But the court concluded AI Film had not shown that the website caused it any financial loss, which is why the damages stayed at $10. Our earlier coverage of the ruling is in [Rebel Wilson wins $359,000 as The Deb producers get $10](https://moduscarry.com/rebel-wilson-wins-359000-as-the-deb-producers-get-10/).

## One of several legal fights over The Deb

This is not the only case to come out of the film. Earlier this year Wilson won a separate defamation case brought by Charlotte MacInnes, the film's lead actor. MacInnes had challenged social media posts suggesting she withdrew a complaint about an uncomfortable encounter involving Ghost to advance her career. The court found the posts were not defamatory, and MacInnes has appealed.

That earlier result is the "second win" behind Wilson's caption, and we covered it in [Rebel Wilson wins second court case over The Deb](https://moduscarry.com/rebel-wilson-wins-second-court-case-over-the-deb/). Separate defamation proceedings involving the film's producers are still under way in California.

## What happens next

The MacInnes appeal is the next open item in Australia, and the California defamation proceedings continue. OK Magazine does not say whether AI Film plans to challenge the latest ruling, so that remains unconfirmed. We will update if any party confirms a next step.
