**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) attended David Yurman's dinner at Chateau Marmont in Los Angeles on Tuesday night.
- The event celebrated the reopening of the brand's Beverly Hills flagship on Rodeo Drive.
- WWD describes Navarrette as enjoying a breakout year after her starring role in the horror hit "Obsession".
- Singer Role Model performed after dinner, according to WWD.

## A night out for the breakout star of "Obsession"

Inde Navarrette was one of the guests at a David Yurman dinner at Chateau Marmont in Los Angeles on Tuesday night, held to mark the reopening of the jewellery house's Rodeo Drive store in Beverly Hills. [WWD](https://wwd.com/eye/parties/inde-navarrette-eiza-gonzalez-david-yurman-chateau-marmont-1239411377/) says she is enjoying a breakout year following her starring role in the horror hit "Obsession".

Headlines from [Variety](https://variety.com/gallery/inde-navarrettes-role-model-david-yurman-party/) and IMDb's news service say she wore a 40-carat diamond necklace to the party. Those pages could not be read in full, so no further detail on the piece is given here.

## Who else was there

WWD lists a crowd drawn from film, fashion and wellness. Alongside Navarrette were Eiza González, Camila Morrone, Emily Bader, Yerin Ha, [Tyriq Withers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyriq_Withers), Liv Perez, Chloe Kim, Stephen Kalyn, Analuisa Corrigan, Brendan Fallis and Hannah Bronfman.

Founder David Yurman said he was in "a room of creators". Guests posed against a gold brand backdrop and mingled around cases of jewellery during cocktail hour, WWD reports.

## The reopened Rodeo Drive store

The Rodeo Drive boutique first opened in 2006. According to WWD, Italian architect Andrea Tognon has redesigned the 2,717-square-foot space, following his work on the brand's Miami Design District flagship in 2025.

The new look draws on Southern California's landscape, with natural stone, onyx and a glass facade meant to recall Pacific waves. The store also introduces jewellery exclusive to Beverly Hills, including gemstone-set Sculpted Cable Flex designs and diamond-set men's necklaces. In the hotel lobby, archival pieces included sculptural bronze necklaces from the 1970s and an early Renaissance Cable bracelet from around 1980.

## Dinner, dancing and a live performance

WWD says David and his wife Sybil, a painter and ceramicist who co-founded the brand, spoke during dinner about their relationship of nearly six decades. Sybil joked that the attraction between them 59 years ago was instant, and offered a tip for working as a couple: "You get through it as long as you can dance."

After dinner, guests moved to the hotel's living room, where Role Model played his hit "Sally, When the Wine Runs Out" while the Yurmans danced.

Hannah Bronfman, who wore an Ashlyn outfit with David Yurman jewellery, told WWD she wears the brand's pinky ring every day.
