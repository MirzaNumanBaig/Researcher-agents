**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie), 26, was photographed at Toronto airport on Wednesday 18 November 2026, according to Just Jared.
- He was named in the 2026 BAFTA Breakthrough US cohort earlier in the week, as reported by Variety.
- His next film is A24's *Please*, directed by Halina Reijn. Plot and role details have not been released.
- Heated Rivalry is reported to be wrapping seasons two and three at the end of November.

[Connor Storrie](https://moduscarry.com/tag/connor-storrie/) kept a low profile on Wednesday 18 November, walking through Toronto airport with an airline employee before a flight out of town. The 26-year-old star of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) was photographed days after being named in the 2026 BAFTA Breakthrough US cohort, according to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/18/connor-storrie-arrives-at-toronto-airport-after-being-named-bafta-breakthrough-us-cohort/).

## What he wore through the terminal

Just Jared describes an all-black outfit with white sneakers and the same camouflage LA Dodgers cap he wore on another recent outing in Toronto. The detail fans noticed was the hoodie. It had the word "please" in pink on the front and a baby deer printed on the back, which appears to nod to his upcoming film.

## The BAFTA Breakthrough US cohort

Earlier in the week Storrie was named among the rising talent chosen for the 2026 BAFTA Breakthrough US cohort. Quoting Variety, Just Jared says the programme is designed to support promising creative talent "at a key inflection point" in their careers. Members take part in a year-round schedule of networking and professional development. We covered the announcement in our earlier post, [Connor Storrie Named in BAFTA Breakthrough U.S. Cohort](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-bafta-breakthrough/).

## Why the airport sighting sparked talk

The sighting led to speculation that he has finished filming on Heated Rivalry. The series was recently revealed to be shooting seasons two and three back-to-back, and Just Jared notes that a standalone film about the characters Scott Hunter and Kip Grady is reportedly due to be filmed in December. It cites Deadline, which reported earlier this month that the two seasons wrap at the end of November.

That wrap date has not been confirmed for Storrie specifically, so his departure is a reading of the evidence rather than a fact. Other cast members have been busy elsewhere. [Edvin Ryding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edvin_Ryding) attended the New York premiere of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* on Monday 16 November. [Daniel Diemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Diemer) went to the *Percy Jackson and the Olympians* season three premiere on Tuesday 17 November. [Shaheen Jafargholi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheen_Jafargholi) was at the GQ Men of the Year Awards in London the same day.

## A busy film slate

Storrie's calendar is full beyond the series. Just Jared lists several projects:

- *Please*, an A24 film from writer-director [Halina Reijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halina_Reijn). He joins [Gracie Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gracie_Abrams) in her acting debut, alongside [Tom Burke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Burke_(actor)), [Lola Tung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Tung) and [David Jonsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Jonsson).
- *Turpentine*, a thriller with [Melissa McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_McCarthy), which he filmed before returning to Heated Rivalry.
- *The Bell Jar*, with [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish), which has also finished filming.
- *Wet*, from director Alma Har'el, where he is set to appear with Rosalia and [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem).
- The Marvel feature *Ghost Rider*, where Just Jared says he is in talks to join [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling). That is a report of talks, not a confirmed casting.

More of his film and TV work is collected on our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) page.

## What happens next

The next checkpoint is the end of November, when Heated Rivalry's two seasons are reported to finish shooting. A December shoot for the standalone film is also reported. No release date for *Please* has been given in the source, and nothing official has been confirmed on *Ghost Rider*. Just Jared also reports that fans voted Storrie its Sexiest Male Celebrity of 2026, with more than three million votes.
