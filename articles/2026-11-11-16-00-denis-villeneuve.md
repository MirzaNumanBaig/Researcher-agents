**Quick summary**

- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) declined to comment on a report that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) screen tested for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- He spoke to Variety at the LACMA Art and Film Gala and said only that he is "having a great time".
- Deadline has reported that five actors were screen tested. Those reports come from unnamed sources and are not confirmed.
- The decision on the next 007 is widely expected at the end of the year.

Denis Villeneuve is not giving anything away about the search for the next James Bond. Asked about reports that Robert Pattinson auditioned for the role, the director told Variety at the LACMA Art and Film Gala that anything he says right now "is going to be scrutinised", according to [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/movies/a74373310/james-bond-denis-villeneuve-robert-pattinson/).

## What Villeneuve actually said

Villeneuve's answer was short and careful. He said he should not comment, then added: "But I will just say that I am having a great time." He did not confirm or deny anything about Pattinson, or about any other actor.

That restraint is notable because Villeneuve is the director attached to the next Bond film. It is the first 007 film since Amazon MGM took full creative control of the franchise in February 2025, Digital Spy notes.

## The screen test reports

The question comes from a Deadline report, as relayed by Digital Spy, that Pattinson is one of five actors who were screen tested for the part. Deadline's sources also named [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Josh O'Connor and [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) as part of the casting process.

None of this has been confirmed by the producers or the studio. Treat the shortlist as reported, not official. Digital Spy also links to its own earlier report that [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi), another name often floated, is not among the final contenders.

Producer [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) told Deadline in August that the end of the year was "a good bet" for an announcement and that the team was being "really, really methodical". Since Daniel Craig finished his 15-year run with No Time to Die in 2021, fans have waited for a successor. [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba), long linked to the part, has since distanced himself from it, the outlet says.

## Pattinson and Villeneuve already share a project

Pattinson and Villeneuve have a connection beyond the Bond rumours. Digital Spy says the actor appears in Dune: Part Three, which is due in cinemas on 18 December. Pattinson is also known for The Batman, the Twilight saga and Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire, and the outlet lists The Drama with Zendaya, Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey and the thriller Primetime among his 2026 releases.

Some observers believe the announcement could come through Prime Video, which would be a first for a franchise that began in 1962. That is an expectation, not a confirmed plan.

For more on the director, see everything we publish about [Denis Villeneuve](https://moduscarry.com/tag/denis-villeneuve/) and our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) coverage.

## What happens next

Nothing official is due until the studio speaks. If the end-of-year timing holds, the new Bond could be named within weeks, and Dune: Part Three arrives on 18 December. Until then, Villeneuve's "great time" is the only comment on record.
