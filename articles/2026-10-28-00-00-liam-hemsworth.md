[Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) is the youngest of three brothers who between them have worked on some of the biggest film and television franchises of the past two decades. A new [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/hemsworth-brothers-explained-chris-liam-230000052.html) explainer lays out how Luke, Chris and Liam got there, and how they lean on each other along the way.

## Luke, the brother who went first

Luke, the oldest, was born in November 1980. [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth) was the first of the three to act professionally, joining the Australian soap Neighbours in 2001. Yahoo reports he later stepped away from acting to run a flooring business, where his younger brothers briefly helped out, before returning to screen work full-time.

His international breakthrough came as security officer Ashley Stubbs in HBO's [Westworld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westworld_(TV_series)), a role he kept through the show's four seasons. He has also appeared in Land of Bad, alongside Liam and [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe), and in Taika Waititi's Next Goal Wins. Yahoo says he has been married to Samantha since 2007 and that they have four children.

## Chris, the biggest name of the three

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth), born in August 1983, came through Home and Away and appeared as George Kirk in the 2009 Star Trek. In 2011 he was cast as Thor, and he has since played the character in four standalone films and four Avengers films, according to Yahoo. Rush, the Extraction films and Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga are among his other credits.

He lives in Byron Bay with his wife, [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky), and their three children. Chris told Men's Health that acting is a fragile business, so the brothers have only ever supported each other.

## Liam's path from Neighbours to The Witcher

Liam also began on Neighbours before moving to the United States in his late teens. His first big break was The Last Song in 2010, opposite Miley Cyrus. He then played Gale Hawthorne in the four original Hunger Games films, released between 2012 and 2015.

Later credits include Independence Day: Resurgence and Isn't It Romantic. Yahoo notes he also took over as Geralt of Rivia in Netflix's The Witcher after Henry Cavill left the series.

On his private life, Yahoo says his relationship and brief marriage to Cyrus ended in divorce in 2020. He has been with Gabriella Brooks since late 2019, and the couple announced their engagement in September 2025.

## The Thor audition

The best-known family story is that Chris and Liam both went for Thor. Chris told W Magazine he thought his first audition with director Kenneth Branagh went well, then heard nothing. Liam, in Australia at the time, sent in a tape and reached the last four or five candidates.

That pushed Chris's manager to ask for a second audition, which he got. Chris said a bit of sibling rivalry kicked in. Liam, for his part, told Men's Health he looks up to Chris and phones him often for advice.

## What happens next

The Yahoo piece is a profile rather than an announcement, and it does not name any new project or a wedding date for Liam and Brooks. Neither brother's reps are quoted on future plans.
