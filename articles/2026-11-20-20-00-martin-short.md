**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) retold the story of his wedding cake mishap on Jimmy Kimmel Live, according to Rolling Stone.
- Short says he cut into the cake with a fork, believing it was one of several for guests. It was the wedding cake.
- Gomez says she was not upset, and the new season of Only Murders in the Building premieres on 8 December on Hulu.
- Separately, Short was photographed on 17 November in a Marcello Hernandez-style wig while filming a Wells Fargo commercial in New York, per The Daily Beast as reported by cwibc.co.id.

Martin Short has now heard Selena Gomez's side of the wedding cake story he first told in January, and her verdict is gentle. Appearing together on Jimmy Kimmel Live to promote the new season of Only Murders in the Building, the pair revisited the night he dug into the dessert, [Rolling Stone reported](https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/selena-gomez-martin-short-wedding-cake-jimmy-kimmel-live-1235661552/) this week.

## How Martin Short ended up eating the wedding cake

[Jimmy Kimmel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Kimmel) asked Short to summarise the tale before turning to Gomez. Short began by declaring himself "blameless". He described a large tent where guests moved after the ceremony, with 18 to 20 musicians, a dance floor and a nightclub feel. The space was divided into seating areas with sofas and chairs.

In his telling, a cake sat in the tent and he assumed it was one of several, one for each group. It turned out to be the wedding cake. Short, who admitted he had "maybe" had a drink, cut into it with a fork as [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) was leaving. Kimmel then showed a photo of the damaged cake.

Short said he turned to [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd) afterwards and asked whether they should just leave. Rudd was among the Only Murders cast members at the celebration, according to Rolling Stone.

## What Selena Gomez said

Gomez took it well. She joked that she loved the fork marks where Short had tried to repair the cake, calling it nicely done. She told Kimmel that Short had "apparently insisted" nobody tell her what happened, but word reached her anyway.

She said she did not mind, since she was at the wedding but had not seen the incident. When someone told her "Marty cut your cake", she ran over and found him looking, in her words, like a little dog with its tail between its legs. Her greeting was that she had heard he ate her cake.

Gomez married producer [Benny Blanco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Blanco) last September, after the couple got engaged in December 2024 following more than a year of dating, Rolling Stone said. Guests included [Taylor Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Swift), who gave a speech, as well as several Only Murders cast members.

## A Wells Fargo shoot with Saturday Night Live's Marcello Hernandez

Short was also in the news for his hair. According to [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/martin-short-marcello-hernandez-hair), which cited The Daily Beast, the 76-year-old was photographed on 17 November in New York wearing a thick dark brown wig with a full fringe. The look closely resembles that of Marcello Hernandez, 29, a current Saturday Night Live cast member who was filming the same Wells Fargo commercial.

The two reportedly wore near-identical outfits: light hoodies, bright red jackets, loose beige trousers and black-and-white shoes. Short plays a comedian far younger than himself in the spot.

The pairing links two generations of the NBC sketch show. Short was a cast member from 1984 to 1985, while Hernandez joined in 2022, and the show is now in its 52nd season. Short returned to host in 2022 and 2024. In February 2025 the two shared a sketch called Domingo: Vow Renewal, in which Short played the father of the bride. The cwibc.co.id report said it drew wide attention.

Details of the Wells Fargo campaign, including when it will air, were not given in the report.
