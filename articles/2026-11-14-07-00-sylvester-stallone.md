**Quick summary**

- *I Play Rocky*, directed by [Peter Farrelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Farrelly), dramatises how Sylvester Stallone wrote *Rocky* and fought to play the lead.
- TODAY.com reports a nationwide US release on 20 November.
- Reviewers and explainers say the story is largely true, with some characters merged and events compressed.
- Separately, Stallone told Entertainment Weekly he would like Andrew Garfield as a villain in *Tulsa King*.

[Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) turned down a $360,000 offer for the *Rocky* script because the buyers would not let him star. That bet is the spine of *I Play Rocky*, the new biopic from Farrelly, and reviews published on 13 November say the film mostly sticks to what happened.

## What the film covers

The film is not about the fame that followed. It follows a broke, struggling actor in New York who decides to write his own lead role. Anthony Ippolito plays Stallone, with AnnaSophia Robb as his first wife, Sasha Czack, and Matt Dillon as his father, Frank. According to [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/uk/i-play-rocky-sylvester-stallone-true-story), Peter Gamble wrote the screenplay, and both he and Farrelly said they would not have made it without Stallone's blessing. Stallone read the script and gave it, but chose not to take part.

Stephan James plays Carl Weathers, and Jay Duplass plays *Rocky* director John G. Avildsen. Third Coast Review's Steve Prokopy [called the film](https://thirdcoastreview.com/film-tv/2026/11/13/review-i-play-rocky/) "exceptional" and said it will likely get awards attention.

## The gamble that built Rocky

JustWatch says Stallone was a 29-year-old actor who had slept in a bus terminal. A boxing match between Muhammad Ali and Chuck Wepner in 1975 is said to have inspired the script. Third Coast Review notes that Wepner lasted 15 rounds.

Agents urged him to take the money. United Artists wanted an established name, with Nick Nolte, James Caan and Burt Reynolds among those mentioned. Stallone held out, and [United Artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Artists) relented on strict terms. JustWatch lists a budget of $900,000 to $1 million, a 30-day schedule, and a clause that let the studio fire him in the first 14 days. It reports the shoot ran 28 days in Philadelphia in January 1976.

The film went on to win multiple Academy Awards, and Stallone received two nominations, for acting and for screenwriting, according to Third Coast Review.

## What the biopic changes

Not everything on screen is literal. JustWatch says the character played by Tracy Letts is a composite of several United Artists executives, and some events are compressed. ScreenRant adds that Stallone had sold scripts before *Rocky*, a fact it says the film leaves out. It also says he was reportedly fired from a cinema job for attempted ticket scalping, which does not appear either.

Ippolito told TODAY.com he had no voice coach and studied early Stallone interviews instead. He also spoke with Stallone about that period. Producer Toby Emmerich told NBC News that Stallone texted after seeing Ippolito's audition tape: "This is not an impersonation. This is a performance."

## Stallone's other news: Tulsa King

Stallone is also in the headlines for television. Collider, citing Entertainment Weekly, reports he said he had met Andrew Garfield and "never thought we would hit it off". He said he would love Garfield as a villain in [*Tulsa King*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King), where Stallone plays Dwight "The General" Manfredi. This is a wish, not a casting announcement.

## What happens next

The film opens nationwide in the US on 20 November, according to TODAY.com. The sources do not give an Australian release date, so check local cinema listings. Awards talk will likely build, since Third Coast Review expects attention soon. Nothing about a *Tulsa King* casting has been confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is I Play Rocky a true story?
Largely, yes. JustWatch and ScreenRant both say it follows real events but merges some characters and compresses others.

### Who directed I Play Rocky?
Peter Farrelly directed it, according to JustWatch and Third Coast Review.

### Did Stallone get involved in making the film?
He read the script and gave his blessing, but chose not to be personally involved, per JustWatch. Ippolito also said he got tips from Stallone.

### Is Andrew Garfield joining Tulsa King?
No. Stallone only said he would like him as a villain, as reported by Collider.
