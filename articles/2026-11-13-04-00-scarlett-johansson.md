**Quick summary**

- Netflix released a new trailer for *Ray Gunn* on Thursday 12 November 2026, according to UPI.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) voices pop star Venus Nova opposite [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) as the title detective.
- The animated noir opens in select cinemas on 4 December, then reaches Netflix on 18 December.
- It is Brad Bird's first film since *Incredibles 2* in 2018, per Punch Drunk Critics.

Netflix has released a second trailer for [*Ray Gunn*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn), the animated sci-fi noir that casts Scarlett Johansson as a pop star at the centre of a murder case. The clip arrived on Thursday, according to [UPI via Yahoo News Canada](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/watch-sam-rockwell-scarlett-johansson-160624713.html), about three weeks before the film reaches cinemas.

## What the new trailer shows

Sam Rockwell voices the title character, the last human private detective in a city shared with aliens. [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn-netflix-new-trailer/) describes the footage as follows: Gunn buys a teleportation weapon, visits a client and finds that person murdered. He is then hired to protect Venus Nova, voiced by Johansson, whose public popularity clashes with what the people around her say in private.

ScreenRant reports that Venus appears tied to the wider case Gunn is working, which sets him against some of the city's most corrupt figures. The outlet says the trailer holds back the biggest reveals while still packing in action.

## The world of Metropia

The story takes place in Metropia, a retro-futuristic city where humans and aliens live side by side, per [Punch Drunk Critics](https://punchdrunkcritics.com/2026/11/ray-gunn-trailer-sam-rockwell-and-scarlett-johansson-voice-brad-birds-long-awaited-sci-fi-noir/). UPI says the film is set in an alternate 1939 and that Gunn works with his alien partner Eye-Ra, voiced by Tom Waits. Johansson's character is described by UPI as an "icy blonde femme fatale".

Director Brad Bird told Netflix's Tudum that his elevator pitch was "The Maltese Falcon meets Buck Rogers", as quoted by UPI. The wider voice cast includes Alan Tudyk, Matt Berry, Bobby Cannavale, Michelle Rodriguez, Kathryn Hunter, John Ratzenberger and Patton Oswalt, according to Punch Drunk Critics.

## A project nearly three decades in the making

Bird first conceived *Ray Gunn* in the 1990s as an animated project that was shelved by Turner Feature Animation, ScreenRant says. Over the years it shifted from hand-drawn animation to 3D computer animation. Punch Drunk Critics calls it a career-long obsession for the director, spanning nearly three decades.

The film is the fourth produced by Skydance Animation, after *Luck*, *Spellbound* and *Swapped*, per ScreenRant. Bird's earlier credits include *The Incredibles*, *Ratatouille* and *The Iron Giant*.

Early reaction is positive. ScreenRant says reviews have been extremely favourable since the film's October premiere at the BFI London Film Festival, with praise for its world, characters and story. Punch Drunk Critics' reviewer saw it last month and called it one of the standout animated films of the year. These are individual critics' opinions, and the film has not yet reached a wide audience.
