**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) has been nominated for a 2027 Grammy for best audiobook, narration and storytelling recording.
- The nomination is for his memoir *Future Boy*, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- He already won this category once, in 2010, for *Always Looking Up: The Adventures of an Incurable Optimist*.
- The 2027 Grammys air live from Los Angeles on 7 February 2027.

Michael J. Fox has earned a Grammy nomination for narrating his own memoir, *Future Boy*. The Recording Academy announced the nominees for the 2027 Grammy Awards on Monday, and the actor's name sits in the best audiobook, narration and storytelling recording category, as reported by [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-news/michael-j-fox-tim-curry-celeb-grammy-nominations-2027-1236755091/).

## A second Grammy chance for Michael J. Fox

This is not Fox's first time in the spotlight at the Grammys. According to The Hollywood Reporter, he already won the best spoken word album award in 2010 for *Always Looking Up: The Adventures of an Incurable Optimist*. A win in 2027 would give him a second trophy in the spoken word field.

The category has since been renamed, but the idea is the same: it honours the performer and the recording of a book, not only the writing. That makes Fox's nomination a recognition of how he tells his story aloud as well as what he wrote.

## Who he is up against

The shortlist is a notable one. Fox's competition, as listed by The Hollywood Reporter, includes:

- Michelle Obama for *The Look*
- Paul McCartney and Morgan Neville for *The Man on the Run*
- Lil Jon for *Remix Meditation*
- the late Dolly Parton for *Star of the Show: My Life on Stage*
- the late [Tim Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Curry) for his memoir *Vagabond: A Memoir*

Curry, who died in August, has been nominated in this field before for narrating *A Series of Unfortunate Events – Book 1: The Bad Beginning*, but has never won. Of the six nominees, Fox is the only one the report describes as an existing winner in the category.

## The wider 2027 Grammys picture

The Recording Academy announced nominees for the 69th Grammy Awards across 101 categories, including five new awards. They are best Asian pop music performance, best R&B collaboration or duo/group performance, best traditional pop vocal performance, best traditional folk album and best Latin song.

The ceremony will air live from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on 7 February 2027 and will be simulcast on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu. No host has been announced yet. The eligibility window ran from 31 August 2025 to 28 August 2026. For more of our music coverage, see the [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) section.

## A quieter family moment earlier this month

The nomination arrives in the same fortnight as a more private bit of news. On 3 November, Fox, 65, marked the 25th birthday of his youngest daughter, Esmé, with a set of rarely seen photos on Instagram, according to [Grazia USA](https://graziamagazine.com/us/articles/michael-j-fox-65-shared-rare-photos-of-his-youngest-daughter-on-her-25th-birthday-youve-been-many-people-25497/). "You've been many people over the last quarter century…all of them fabulous!" he wrote.

His wife, [Tracy Pollan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Pollan), who played his girlfriend on [Family Ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties), added her own tribute. The couple married in 1988 and have four children. Grazia reports that Esmé graduated from Duke University in 2025 and now works as a production assistant on the Today show.
