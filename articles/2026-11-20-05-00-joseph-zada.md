**Quick summary**

- [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) and McKenna Grace revealed matching ladybug tattoos on 16 November, according to InkedMag.
- They said they got the ink one day earlier, and Zada's is on his ankle.
- The design ties back to a ladybug scene in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opens in cinemas on 20 November.
- LaineyGossip reports the film is projected to open with US$90-120 million in the United States.

## The tattoo reveal

Joseph Zada and McKenna Grace have marked the release of their new film with matching ladybug tattoos. According to [InkedMag](https://inkedmag.com/culture/mckenna-grace-and-joseph-zada-reveal-matching-tattoos), the pair showed off the ink during a 16 November appearance on SiriusXM's *Front Row with Andy Cohen*, and said they had been tattooed the day before.

Zada showed his ankle tattoo during the interview. InkedMag notes that Grace's tattoo is in a different spot, though it does not say where.

## Why a ladybug

The design is linked to the characters they play. InkedMag reports that the two characters form a family-like bond after a key ladybug sequence in the film. The tattoo works as a keepsake of that moment rather than a random choice.

The pair also joked about getting director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) and co-star Ralph Fiennes to join them, according to the same report. There is no sign either has taken them up on it.

You can read more of our coverage in the [Joseph Zada](https://moduscarry.com/tag/joseph-zada/) archive, including [Joseph Zada Praises Mckenna Grace at Hunger Games Premiere](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-hunger-games-premiere/).

## A big week for Zada

The tattoo news lands as *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* reaches cinemas. Zada plays a younger Haymitch Abernathy, the role [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) originated in the earlier films, based on the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) book series.

[LaineyGossip](https://www.laineygossip.com/will-we-were-liars-co-stars-joseph-zada-esther-mcgregor-become-robert-pattinson-kristen-stewart-of-their-generation/) says the film is projected to take US$90-120 million at the domestic box office on opening weekend and US$160-190 million worldwide, with totals expected to climb the following week because of the US Thanksgiving holiday. The same piece describes critics as divided and calls the film unapologetic fan service. These are projections and one writer's view, not final results.

Zada also appears in Netflix's *East of Eden*, which premiered a few weeks ago, and is one of the young leads in Prime Video's *We Were Liars*.

## Esther McGregor and the comparisons

LaineyGossip also asks whether Zada and [Esther McGregor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_McGregor), his *We Were Liars* co-star, could become the [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) and [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart) of their generation. That is the site's opinion piece, not a confirmed relationship or announcement.

What the article does report is that McGregor attended the *Sunrise on the Reaping* premiere in New York this week to support Zada. It adds that she has been cast as Violet Sorrengail in the *Fourth Wing* adaptation, produced by Michael B. Jordan, a role it calls one of the most sought after for actors her age.
