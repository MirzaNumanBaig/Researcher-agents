**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) told Vogue that [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) has a "street style or edgy" look that he envies.
- Storrie was named Vogue's best dressed of 2026, and the interview was published this week.
- Teen Vogue's Olive Young Black Friday guide describes a SKIN1004 ampoule as having Williams' stamp of approval.
- Season 2 of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) is reported to be slated for April 2027.

Hudson Williams style has become the talking point of a busy news week for the Heated Rivalry star. His co-star Connor Storrie praised his fashion sense in a Vogue interview published this week, and Teen Vogue named him as a fan of a Korean skincare serum that is now on sale. Neither story brings new casting or release news, but both show how much attention the show's leads still draw.

## What Connor Storrie told Vogue about Hudson Williams

According to [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/connor-storrie-named-best-dressed-000452797.html), which republished a Parade report, Storrie appears on the cover of Vogue's digital edition in Gucci. He spoke for about an hour on a podcast with Vogue's head of editorial content, [Chloe Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloe_Malle), recorded at the magazine's offices.

Malle asked whether the two actors coordinate their red carpet outfits, since they have often looked alike. Storrie said they do not discuss what they will wear. He described a "natural cohesion" between them and said the two are "cut from the same cloth."

He also drew a clear line between their tastes. "I think Hudson always looks good, but he's also different than me," Storrie said, adding that there is "something street style or edgy" about Williams' choices and that he envies it. Storrie credited his stylist, James Yardley, with teaching him how much thought goes into a look.

## Hudson Williams style and the Teen Vogue connection

Williams' name also turned up in a shopping feature. Teen Vogue's guide to the first Black Friday sale at the K-beauty retailer Olive Young says the SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule has the approval of "Heated Rivalry star Hudson Williams and countless more." The outlet calls it the hottest Korean skincare serum right now.

Teen Vogue reports that the discounts run from November 23 until the night of November 29 on the retailer's US website. More than 4,000 products are marked down by up to 50 per cent. The code BFNOLIMIT30 takes a further 30 per cent off most items, and Teen Vogue says the ampoule falls under US$11 with it. The piece is a retail guide, so the Williams mention is the outlet's own framing. The excerpt we could read does not quote him.

## Living together during filming

The Vogue conversation also repeated news that had already spread. Storrie said he is living with Williams while the new season is filmed. "I literally live with [Williams], which is amazing," he said. He explained that their earlier hotel was fine, but he needed a washing machine and a kitchen.

[Queerty](https://www.queerty.com/wait-connor-storrie-hudson-williams-are-living-together-20261123/) reports that the series is filming its second and third seasons back to back. That means the pair spend long days together on set and then share an apartment. We covered the first reaction in our earlier post, [Hudson Williams Lives With Connor Storrie as Yaga Is Renewed](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-connor-storrie-living-together/). For more on Storrie's side of the interview, see [Connor Storrie Is Living With Hudson Williams During Filming](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-hudson-williams/).

## Where Heated Rivalry season 2 stands

Season 1 followed hockey players Shane Hollander, played by Williams, and Ilya Rozanov, played by Storrie, across about ten years. Queerty describes it as a surprise hit that built a devoted fandom. The series is based on Rachel Reid's book The Long Game, according to Yahoo News UK.

Both outlets say season 2 is slated for April 2027. Yahoo News UK words it as a rumoured date, and Queerty says it is expected to explore how the two men manage a relationship while playing for rival teams. Most other details are still under wraps.

## What happens next

The Olive Young sale ends on November 29. Filming on the next seasons continues, with a release in April 2027 still unconfirmed in the reports we read.
