**Quick summary**

- Billy Evans, partner of Elizabeth Holmes, says he did not consent to his naked body appearing in the documentary *You Can See Everything*.
- He made the comments on CBS Sunday Morning on 16 November, according to BuzzFeed.
- Co-director Lance Oppenheim told GQ that Evans himself offered to be filmed in the shower.
- Evans says he would still take part again, knowing what he knows now.

The Billy Evans consent dispute is the latest twist: he has said he never agreed to the scene in which he appears fully naked in the shower in *You Can See Everything*, the documentary co-directed by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder). Speaking on CBS Sunday Morning, Evans described the fallout as awkward, according to a report that first appeared on HuffPost and was republished by [BuzzFeed](https://www.buzzfeed.com/paigeskinner/elizabeth-holmes-partner-consent-full-frontal-scene).

## Billy Evans on consent and the scene

Evans separated being filmed from being shown. "I did not give consent to do that," he told the programme. He accepted that he knew cameras were rolling while he showered, but said the crew filmed around 16 hours a day and that assurances were made about how the footage would be handled.

He said he was "just as surprised as the rest of the country" to see his whole body on screen. The consequences have been personal. Evans said it has been uncomfortable to know that his children's teachers have seen the scene.

He also said the filmmakers asked to film things "not appropriate for television". He declined to say what, preferring to "spare the audience".

## The directors' version

The directors remember it differently. [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim) told GQ that the shower came up in the car on the way back from the gym. He said he told Evans the crew would give him time to shower, and that Evans replied by asking whether they wanted to film him doing it.

Oppenheim said he offered to frame the shot to avoid showing Evans's genitals, and that Evans said he did not mind being seen naked. The two accounts do not conflict on one point: Evans knew the shower was being filmed. They differ on what he agreed would appear in the finished film.

## What the film is

*You Can See Everything* follows Fielder as he lives with Evans and Holmes for the 34 days before Holmes reported to prison, after her conviction for defrauding investors in her blood-testing company Theranos. The film records both the mundane and the extraordinary parts of their lives, per BuzzFeed.

It has been a notable success. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything), it was made on a reported budget of about US$7 million and has grossed around US$86.5 million, roughly 12 times its cost. The same entry lists a 96% score from 226 reviews on Rotten Tomatoes and 97 out of 100 on Metacritic. Critics have been warm about Fielder's commitment to the bit, though the consent question now sits alongside the praise.

Evans did not suggest any legal action in the interview as reported. He said he would take part again, because he would now better understand what he was walking into.
