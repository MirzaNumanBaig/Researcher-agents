**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) has backed [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), calling him "perfect".
- She spoke to Variety at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, according to The Shillong Times.
- Amazon MGM Studios has named Pattinson as Bond, with Denis Villeneuve directing.
- The film is not expected before 2028, and its title and supporting cast have not been revealed.

Elizabeth Debicki has added her voice to the praise for Robert Pattinson after Amazon MGM Studios named him the next James Bond. The Australian actor, who worked with Pattinson on Tenet, told Variety this week that she is "so happy" for her former co-star, according to a report in [The Shillong Times](https://theshillongtimes.com/2026/11/19/jeff-bezos-elizabeth-debicki-hail-pattinson-as-the-perfect-new-007/).

## What Elizabeth Debicki said about Pattinson

Debicki was speaking at the Los Angeles premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of [Cliff Booth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood) when Variety asked about the casting. "I'm so happy for Rob. He is an enigma himself," she said, adding that he is "beautiful, and he's funny, and he's smart". Her verdict was short: "I think he's perfect. I'm so thrilled."

According to the same report, she also praised the way Pattinson has developed as an actor and the interesting choices he has made along the way. The pair share screen history through Tenet, which is why her comments carry some weight with fans following the Bond announcement.

You can find more of our coverage of the actor on the [Elizabeth Debicki](https://moduscarry.com/tag/elizabeth-debicki/) page.

## How Pattinson won the role

Amazon founder and chairman Jeff Bezos explained the decision at Semafor's Silicon Valley & The World conference. The report says five finalists took part in screen tests: Pattinson, [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), Josh O'Connor, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult).

Bezos said all of them were "amazing", but that there was "something special" about Pattinson. He added that the makers had first leaned towards a relatively unknown actor, since Pattinson is already a household name. The screen tests changed that. "It was so obvious: He is James Bond," Bezos said.

Pattinson, whose credits include the [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) films, Twilight, The Batman and Dune, called the job the "honour of a lifetime" and a dream come true.

## The film and the team behind it

The new Bond film will be directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), the filmmaker behind Dune and Arrival. [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) and [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman) will produce, [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight) will write the screenplay, and [Tanya Lapointe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanya_Lapointe) is attached as executive producer.

Villeneuve welcomed his new lead by saying Pattinson has the "timeless soul of 007" while bringing a fresh spirit to the character. The project will be developed under a new partnership between Amazon MGM Studios and the long-time Bond rights holders, [Michael Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Wilson_(director)) and [Barbara Broccoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Broccoli).

Pattinson follows [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), who last played Bond in No Time to Die in 2021. The report puts the franchise's worldwide box office during Craig's era (2006 to 2021) above US$7.8 billion, a number the new film will be measured against. Earlier Bonds include [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery), [George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby), [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore), [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), going back to Dr. No in 1962.

## What happens next

The film is expected in 2028 at the earliest. The title and supporting cast have not been announced, so any talk about who might play the villain or the Bond girl is speculation for now. Debicki has not been linked to the film in the sources we read, and nothing suggests she will appear in it.
