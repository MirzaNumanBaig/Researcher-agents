**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Hailey Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey_Bieber) and BLACKPINK's Jennie have each been photographed in the same Chanel jacket.
- The piece is an iridescent metal mesh jacket from Chanel's Fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection, with a reported price of about US$15,000.
- Robbie wore hers with denim and a white top at the Met Gala after-parties in May 2026.
- Yahoo Creators ranked Robbie's casual styling first for everyday wear, while noting the choice comes down to personal taste.

One Chanel jacket, three very different outfits. According to a [Yahoo Creators](https://creators.yahoo.com/lifestyle/story/battle-of-the-chanel-jacket-hailey-bieber-margot-robbie-and-jennie-all-wore-the-same-15000-jacket-but-heres-who-wore-it-best-104314573.html) comparison published on 23 October 2026, Margot Robbie, Hailey Bieber and Jennie have all worn the same statement jacket from the French house's Fall 2026 ready-to-wear line, and the Australian actress comes out on top for wearability.

## The jacket at the centre of the comparison

Yahoo Creators describes the piece as an iridescent metal mesh jacket with a colourful, tweed-inspired design. It reports a price of about US$15,000, which is a figure from the outlet and not one confirmed by Chanel in the article. The metallic finish is what sets it apart from a classic tweed jacket, and it is the quality all three wearers chose to build their outfits around.

## How Margot Robbie wore it

Robbie wore the jacket to the Met Gala after-parties in May 2026, according to Yahoo Creators. The "Barbie" star kept the rest of the outfit simple: relaxed denim, a white tank top and a red clutch. The plain base lets the jacket's colour and texture do the work.

Yahoo Creators calls her approach a "classic" mix of everyday wardrobe staples with one statement piece. That is the reason the outlet gives for naming her its top pick for everyday styling.

## Bieber and Jennie take different routes

Bieber wore the jacket to the WYouth Magazine launch in West Hollywood. Like Robbie, she chose blue jeans, adding a minimal top and black heels. The outlet says the contrast between shiny fabric and everyday denim made the look feel less formal.

Jennie went the other way. She wore the jacket at a Chanel Métiers d'Art after-party in Seoul with a custom matching mini skirt and a red floral top. Yahoo Creators describes it as the most coordinated and playful of the three, and says the shorter skirt gave the structured jacket a different silhouette from the denim looks.

## Who wore it best

Yahoo Creators is clear that the verdict is subjective. It sums up Bieber's look as casual and minimal, Robbie's as classic basics with a statement piece, and Jennie's as a full designer set. For everyday wear it picks Robbie, with Bieber close behind, and says Jennie's matching skirt gives the most complete ensemble.

The piece also offers a budget tip: a colourful, structured, tweed-inspired jacket worn with straight-leg jeans and a plain white top gets close to Robbie's formula without the designer price. That is the outlet's styling advice, not a claim about any specific product.

## What happens next

The article does not report any further appearances in the jacket or any statement from the three women or from Chanel. Fall 2026 pieces are still new, so more red-carpet and event sightings are possible, but none are confirmed.
