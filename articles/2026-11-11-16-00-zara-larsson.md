**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) has released the music video for "Blue Moon", according to Popzine.
- The video is directed by Charlotte Rutherford and moves from an empty car park at dusk to a colourful, surreal night.
- Larsson has called the song a sister to "Midnight Sun", per the press material Popzine cites.
- "Blue Moon" also appears on the remix album Midnight Sun: Girls Trip, with Kehlani on its remix.

Zara Larsson's video for "Blue Moon" is out, and it starts with almost nothing: the singer alone on an empty stretch of asphalt as the daylight fades. By the end, that ordinary space has become a vivid night world. Swedish outlet [Popzine](https://popzine.se/uutiset/zara-larsson-builds-on-midnight-sun-with-charlotte-rutherfords-video-for-blue-moon) reported the release this week, working from the press material that came with it.

## What happens in the Zara Larsson Blue Moon video

Charlotte Rutherford directed the clip. Based on the video description quoted by Popzine, the night brings fantasy creatures, strong colours and choreography to the car park. The singer does not travel to a fantasy location. The location changes around her.

Popzine reads that contrast as the video's main idea. A car park is a place people pass through, not a typical fantasy backdrop, so the shift from the everyday to the imagined is easy to see. The outlet notes that its account rests on the submitted description, not on an interview with the director.

## A sister song to Midnight Sun

The video sits inside the wider campaign for Midnight Sun. According to the same press material, Larsson described "Blue Moon" as a sister to "Midnight Sun" while appearing on the podcast Las Culturistas, hosted by Matt Rogers and [Bowen Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowen_Yang). The framing suggests a companion piece rather than a replacement for the title track.

The press material adds that she previewed parts of the video during the second of her two shows at the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles. That means the clip reached fans through a live show and a podcast conversation before the full release.

Popzine's view is that the titles point to different phases of the same imagined sky, and that the move from twilight to night makes the link visible. That is the outlet's interpretation, not a statement from Larsson.

## The Girls Trip remix album

"Blue Moon" also features on Midnight Sun: Girls Trip, a remix album that extends the original project through collaborations. The material lists Kehlani as the guest on the "Blue Moon" remix. Shakira, Robyn, Tyla, PinkPantheress and JT are named among the album's other contributors.

A separate listing in this week's news, from Tooxclusive, points to a Tyla track featuring Larsson titled "She Did It Again". That page could not be opened, so details of the song are not confirmed here.

## What happens next

No further release dates were given in the material available. Fans can expect more content around Midnight Sun, as the remix album and the new video show the project is still being built out.
