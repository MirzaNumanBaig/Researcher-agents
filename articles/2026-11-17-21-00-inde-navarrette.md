**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) attended ELLE's Women in Hollywood event in Beverly Hills as a first-time honouree, according to ELLE.
- She wore a white two-piece Ralph Lauren suit with a train, a floral brooch and a blue tie.
- At the Governors Awards on 15 November she told E! News she wants to visit Mississippi to prepare for Rogue in the upcoming X-Men film.
- Her breakout horror film Obsession has grossed more than US$500 million worldwide, Substream Magazine reports.

The 25-year-old actress made a statement at the **Inde Navarrette Women in Hollywood** night, turning up in a white pinstripe-style suit with a train to collect her first ELLE honour. [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a74392490/inde-navarrette-photos-elle-women-in-hollywood-event-2026/) says the look was by Ralph Lauren and marks a milestone evening in a busy awards season that followed her breakout role in Obsession.

## The suit that turned heads in Beverly Hills

ELLE describes the outfit as a red carpet twist on tailoring. The two-piece was white, finished with a statement floral brooch and a blue tie, and the jacket carried a train that added a formal flourish to an otherwise menswear-led shape.

It is the sort of detail that suits an honouree's night. Women in Hollywood is ELLE's annual celebration, and Navarrette also features in the magazine's accompanying issue, where she gave an interview about her path in the industry. Readers who want more of her recent appearances can browse our [Inde Navarrette tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/inde-navarrette/) or the [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

## What she told ELLE about trusting herself

In that interview, Navarrette said her life changed once she stopped putting off the things she wanted to do. She described herself as a people-pleaser until 23, and said a shift came at 24 when she felt something needed to change.

That led to a deliberate approach on the Obsession set. She told herself she was not there to make friends, and she described it as the first job where she arrived and left purely as an actor. The friendships, she said, formed naturally after production wrapped and are now among her closest.

Asked what she would tell her younger self, she said very little. She would offer a hug and a high five, because every difficult step helped shape where she is now.

## Rogue, a southern accent and a trip to Mississippi

Two days earlier, at the Governors Awards in Hollywood, Navarrette spoke to E! News about the role that follows Obsession. [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1438168/inde-navarrette-reveals-how-shes-prepping-to-play-rogue-in-x-men) reports that she plans to build Rogue's southern accent into her performance and joked in a drawl that she was still working it out.

She also said she hopes to visit Mississippi, where the Marvel character grew up on a commune before her mutant powers emerged. Rogue can absorb the powers and memories of others through touch. Navarrette said the character means a lot to her and that she wants to give her everything she has.

There is no new casting or release information in that interview, so the timing of the film remains unconfirmed. For her earlier comments on preparation, see our report on [Rogue prep and the Elle cover](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-rogue-training/).

## Obsession, by the numbers

The film behind the awards run is the story here. [Substream Magazine](https://substreammagazine.com/2026/11/curry-barker-and-inde-navarrette-are-still-taking-in-the-runaway-success-of-obsession/) notes that writer and director Curry Barker premiered it at the Toronto International Film Festival's Midnight Madness section on 5 September 2025, and that Focus Features bought it about a month later for US$14 million.

According to the same outlet, it then grossed more than US$500 million worldwide, which is a striking multiple of that purchase price. Navarrette plays Nikki, a woman who becomes obsessed with her friend after he makes a wish that backfires, and she told E! News that the role let her express feelings women are often discouraged from showing.

In the Substream interview she and Barker described watching the film grow from one festival screening into a worldwide hit. The film did not reach cinemas until May, Barker said, so there was a long wait before anyone knew how the public would respond.

## Staying grounded through heritage

Away from the carpets, [HipLatina](https://hiplatina.com/inde-navarrette-follows-her-mexican-intuition-when-it-comes-to-superstition/) reports on a W Magazine interview in which the Mexican-American actress described the rituals she and her all-Latine team follow before events. She said they cleanse with sage and avoid walking on opposite sides of a pole, which she calls their "Mexican intuition".

She has also spoken about learning cleansing traditions, including egg cleansing, from her 13 Reasons Why co-star Yadira Guevara, according to the same report.
