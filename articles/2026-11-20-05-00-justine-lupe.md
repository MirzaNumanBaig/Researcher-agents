**Quick summary**

- [Justine Lupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Lupe), known for "Nobody Wants This", will appear at TheWrap's Power Women Summit 2026.
- She joins Emma D'Arcy, Zazie Beetz, Jessica Williams, Dale Dickey and Lola Petticrew on the Actress Roundtable.
- The summit takes place on 1 December at The Maybourne Beverly Hills and is presented by STARZ.
- The panel will cover career-changing roles and the obstacles of working in the industry today.

Justine Lupe, the actress from "Nobody Wants This", has been announced as one of six performers on the Actress Roundtable at TheWrap's Power Women Summit, which runs on 1 December at The Maybourne Beverly Hills, according to an announcement republished by [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/emma-d-arcy-zazie-beetz-173144674.html).

## Who is on the Actress Roundtable

The roundtable brings together a mix of screen credits. Emma D'Arcy is billed for "House of the Dragon", while [Zazie Beetz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zazie_Beetz) is listed for "Nocturne". [Jessica Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessica_Williams_(actress)) is named for "Shrinking" and [Lola Petticrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Petticrew) for "Furious". [Dale Dickey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dale_Dickey) is there for ["Widow's Bay"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Widow's_Bay), a 2026 comedy horror series.

Lupe is credited for "Nobody Wants This". The announcement describes all six as acclaimed actors, and says they will talk about the roles that changed their careers and the choices behind their most memorable performances.

## What the panel will discuss

According to the organisers, the conversation will also look at the opportunities and obstacles that come with navigating the industry now. The announcement does not give a moderator, a running order or a time for the roundtable, so those details remain to be confirmed.

For readers following Lupe's career, this is a speaking appearance rather than a project announcement. The source does not say she has a new role to promote at the event, and it does not mention any upcoming work.

## The rest of the summit

The Power Women Summit is described as a one-day gathering of influential women in entertainment and media, with keynotes, panels, workshops and networking. Other sessions announced alongside the actress panel include:

- "Main Character Energy: Women Dominating Unscripted TV", with [Bronwyn Newport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronwyn_Newport) of "The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City", Willam of "RuPaul's Drag Race" and BBC Studios executive Krystal Whitney.
- "Money Talks: Women, Wealth and the Power of Financial Control", featuring authors [Nicole Lapin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Lapin), Kathleen Griffith and Kristin Wong, with Kristina Royce of Blank Rome and Mia Samson of Gerber Kawasaki Wealth and Investment Management.

The wider speaker list includes [Louisa Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisa_Levy), creator and showrunner of ["Off Campus"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Off_Campus), and Katori Hall, creator of "P-Valley". Executives from TikTok, Disney Entertainment Studios, Lionsgate, Shondaland, BritBox and Google TV are also listed.

TheWrap's 2026 edition is presented by STARZ. Sponsors named in the release include BBC Studios, Blank Rome LLP, BritBox, Delta Air Lines, Gerber Kawasaki, Google TV, JustWatch Media, Lionsgate, NRG, South Coast Plaza, UTA and Whalar.

## What happens next

The summit is set for 1 December. Further details, including how to attend, are on TheWrap's Power Women Summit page at thewrap.com/pws.
