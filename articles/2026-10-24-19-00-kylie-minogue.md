**Quick summary**

- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) was one of almost two dozen high-profile people who put questions to [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) for Vogue Australia.
- Minogue asked about Kidman's memories of the 1983 Australian film BMX Bandits.
- Kidman, 59, appears on next month's cover, her twelfth for the magazine, according to The Nightly.
- Other questioners included Hugh Jackman, Margot Robbie and Baz Luhrmann.

Kylie Minogue has joined a long line of famous names quizzing Nicole Kidman for Vogue Australia. The interview accompanies Kidman's cover for next month's issue, and The Nightly reported on its highlights on 24 October 2026.

## A question about BMX Bandits

Minogue's contribution looked back to the start of Kidman's screen career. According to [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/nicole-kidman-answers-questions-from-hugh-jackman-kylie-minogue-margot-robbie-in-vogue-australia-interview-c-23066037), the pop star asked about Kidman's memories of BMX Bandits, the 1983 film that was her breakout role.

Kidman began by expressing her "enormous love" for "golden" Kylie. She then recalled thinking it was "hardcore" to perform one of her own stunts in the film. She later told [Michelle Pfeiffer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Pfeiffer), in answer to another question, that it was one of the roles in which she had the most fun.

## The wider line-up

The questions came from a long guest list. It included [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman), [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Laura Dern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Dern), [Naomi Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Watts), [Joel Edgerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Edgerton), [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) and author [Liane Moriarty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liane_Moriarty).

Some questions were light. [Harris Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_Dickinson) and Alexander Skarsgård asked for advice on hosting Christmas and buying a washing machine and dryer. Others were more personal.

## Height, phones and family

Asked by Luhrmann about obstacles in her youth, Kidman said her height made things hard. "I was the tallest in the class, taller than all the boys," she said, adding that she could not become a ballet dancer and was told as an actress she was taller than the leading man. She said it gave her fire.

On phones, she admitted she can be late with texts and does not check emails often. She prefers being "emotionally present" and spending time in nature, though she conceded she has "doomscrolled".

She also said she shares clothes with her daughters Sunday Rose, 18, and Faith, 15, who are the same size as her. "I don't buy for me, I buy for us," she said.

## Beach holidays and a McDonald's ad

Robbie asked about Kidman's favourite Australian television ad. Kidman said she had always wanted to appear in a McDonald's commercial, mainly to eat a Quarter Pounder on camera repeatedly.

Moriarty asked which moment of her childhood Kidman would revisit if she could time travel. Kidman chose a January Christmas holiday, recalling beach trips to Avoca and visits to the Barrington Tops in New South Wales, with a fake tree, tinsel and the smell of gardenias.
