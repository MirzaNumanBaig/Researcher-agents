**Quick summary**

- Alakina Mann played [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman)'s on-screen daughter, Anne, in the 2001 horror film [The Others](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Others_(2001_film)).
- HELLO! reports that Mann made only three screen projects before leaving acting.
- She now works as an artist and medical illustrator, and founded a paid midwifery service called Kove Care, according to HELLO!.
- The Others earned Kidman Golden Globe and BAFTA nominations for best actress.

Twenty-two years after The Others frightened cinema audiences, one of its child stars has built a life far from the screen. HELLO! this week caught up with Alakina Mann, who played Nicole Kidman's daughter in the film, and reports that she stepped away from acting soon after her debut.

## Alakina Mann's role opposite Nicole Kidman

The Others was Mann's first screen role, and [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/932084/what-happened-to-nicole-kidmans-daughter-in-the-others-22-years-on-after-quitting-hollywood-for-totally-normal-life/) says it earned her a Saturn Award nomination. She played Anne, the daughter of Kidman's character, Grace, alongside fellow child actor [James Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bentley_(actor)).

The 2001 film follows Grace and her two children, both extremely sensitive to light, after they move into a new house in Jersey. Grace is waiting for her husband to come home from the Second World War and becomes convinced the house is haunted. HELLO! notes the performance brought Kidman Golden Globe and BAFTA nominations for best actress.

It remains Mann's best-known part, according to the magazine.

## A very short screen career

Mann made just two more appearances after The Others. In 2003 she turned up in Girl with a Pearl Earring, the film that starred [Colin Firth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Firth) and [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson). HELLO! describes it as a three-time Oscar-nominated feature.

A year later, in 2004, she appeared in two episodes of the children's series Fungus the Bogeyman, playing Alice. According to HELLO!, that is still her last on-screen acting credit.

Three projects in four years is a small body of work for a child actor who debuted opposite one of the biggest names in film. It also sets up the turn her career took next.

## What Alakina Mann does today

HELLO! reports that Mann now works as an artist and medical illustrator. She is also the founder of Kove Care, which the magazine describes as a paid midwifery service. Her illustrations, shared on Instagram, focus on reproductive systems, women's bodies and space-age scenes.

The magazine frames the change as a deliberate move to a "totally normal life", and contrasts it with Kidman, who it says continues to reign as a queen of the red carpet.

## Kidman's career since The Others

Kidman's own path has gone the other way. CableTV.com describes the Australian-American actor and producer as one of Hollywood's highest-paid performers, with an Academy Award, two Primetime Emmy Awards, a BAFTA and six Golden Globes to her name.

In its ranking of her best roles, [CableTV.com](https://www.cabletv.com/entertainment/nicole-kidman-best-roles) lists The Others alongside Moulin Rouge!, Cold Mountain, Rabbit Hole, To Die For, Eyes Wide Shut and [Boy Erased](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boy_Erased), in which she plays Nancy Eamons, mother of a boy sent to a conversion camp. It also points to her work on television in Big Little Lies, which she performed in and produced. The site credits her Rabbit Hole performance, as a grieving mother, with an Oscar nomination.
