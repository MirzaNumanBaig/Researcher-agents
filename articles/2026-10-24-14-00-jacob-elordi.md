**Quick summary**

- [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) is reportedly no longer in the running to play James Bond, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Deadline says Amazon MGM Studios is screen testing four actors for the role over the coming week.
- Three of the four are named: Jack Lowden, Callum Turner and Josh O'Connor.
- The tests are reported to begin on Friday, so a decision could follow soon after.
- Denis Villeneuve is set to direct the reboot from a script by Steven Knight.

Jacob Elordi is no longer in the race to play 007, according to a report relayed by MovieWeb on 22 October, just as Amazon MGM Studios is said to be narrowing the field to a handful of actors. The Australian actor had been linked to the part for months. Now the shortlist is taking shape without him.

## Who is on the James Bond shortlist?

According to Deadline, Amazon MGM Studios is putting four actors through screen tests across the next week. Deadline reportedly names only three of them: [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) of [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses), [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and Josh O'Connor, who stars in [Disclosure Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disclosure_Day). The fourth name has not been reported.

MovieWeb says the tests are due to start on Friday. That timing suggests the studio wants to settle the casting soon, though none of the outlets has put a date on an announcement.

## What happened to Jacob Elordi's James Bond rumours?

Elordi and Jack O'Connell had both been rumoured to be in the mix earlier. MovieWeb reports that The Hollywood Reporter now says that is no longer the case. Neither actor, nor Amazon, has commented in the material we reviewed, so this remains an industry report rather than a studio statement.

We have followed this thread for a while. Our earlier posts covered the [first report tipping Elordi as the next Bond](https://moduscarry.com/jacob-elordi-tipped-as-next-james-bond-in-unconfirmed-report/) and the [claim that he was not in the final screen tests](https://moduscarry.com/jacob-elordi-reportedly-not-in-the-final-james-bond-screen-tests/). The latest reporting lines up with the second of those.

## Who is making the film?

The reboot, known as Bond 26, has a notable team behind it. MovieWeb lists [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), the director of Dune, as the film's director, working from a script by [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), the writer behind Peaky Blinders. The producers are [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) and [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman), and casting director [Nina Gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Gold) has been helping to cut down the long list.

The new actor follows [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), whose run MovieWeb calls record-breaking. MovieWeb reads the three named finalists as a sign that Amazon wants an up-and-coming star rather than an established name. That is the outlet's interpretation, not a confirmed studio strategy.

## Where do the finalists stand?

MovieWeb describes Lowden as one of the most popular choices online, thanks to his role as a dysfunctional MI5 agent in Slow Horses. Turner, it says, has been mentioned in rumours since the reboot was first announced and has a history with Heyman's [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) films. O'Connor, meanwhile, has led acclaimed work in Disclosure Day, Wake Up Dead Man and Challengers.

## What happens next

The screen tests are reported to begin on Friday and run over about a week. Nothing has been officially announced by Amazon MGM Studios, and the fourth finalist is still unnamed.
