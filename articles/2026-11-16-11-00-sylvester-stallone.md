**Quick summary**

- A new Variety report puts Sylvester Stallone among the best-paid actors on television, at roughly $2 million an episode.
- He ties with [Chris Pratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Pratt), according to ScreenRant's summary of the report.
- Stallone leads the Paramount+ drama [Tulsa King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King); Pratt stars in Prime Video's The Terminal List.
- The figures are reported estimates, not confirmed by the actors or the studios.

[Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) is now one of the best-paid people on television. A new salary report from Variety, summarised by [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/chris-pratt-sylvester-stallone-2026-tv-actors-highest-paid-salary-report/), says the Rocky star and Chris Pratt each earn roughly $2 million per episode, the top of the current chart. The report appeared on 15 November 2026.

## Stallone's TV salary and who shares the top spot

According to ScreenRant, the Variety piece ranks 20 stars who make more than $1 million per episode. Fourteen are men and six are women. Stallone and Pratt sit at the head of that list, and they are level with [Jennifer Aniston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Aniston) and [Reese Witherspoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reese_Witherspoon), the co-stars of The Morning Show.

Both men earn their money on streaming. Stallone fronts Tulsa King, the crime drama from [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan) on Paramount+. Pratt leads the action thriller The Terminal List on Prime Video. ScreenRant notes that Pratt began on television with Parks and Recreation before moving to blockbusters such as [Jurassic World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World) and Guardians of the Galaxy.

These are reported per-episode figures. Neither actor, nor the streamers involved, is quoted confirming them in the coverage we read, so treat them as industry estimates.

## A film career behind the number

Stallone's name carries weight because of his film record. ScreenRant points out that he broke out by writing and starring in Rocky, the 1976 Best Picture winner, and later led the Rambo and The Expendables franchises. He is also listed among the Oscar nominees on the chart.

That history helps explain the pay. The report suggests streamers are still paying most for people who made their names on the big screen. For readers who want more on the character that started it all, our earlier piece on [Stallone's favourite Rocky films](https://moduscarry.com/sylvester-stallone-favourite-rocky/) covers his own picks.

## Movie stars dominate the chart

ScreenRant's reading of the list is that big-screen fame still commands the highest rates, even as the gap between film and television shrinks. Several Oscar winners appear, including Witherspoon, [Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh), [Robert De Niro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_De_Niro) and [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage). Fellow nominees include [Harrison Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Ford), [Jake Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Gyllenhaal), [Andrew Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Garfield) and [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law).

Only a handful made their name on television. ScreenRant counts Aniston from Friends, [Jason Segel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Segel) from How I Met Your Mother and Pratt from Parks and Recreation among those with legacy-hit backgrounds. Only three are best known for current TV work: [Kelly Reilly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Reilly) and [Cole Hauser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cole_Hauser) from Yellowstone, whose spinoff is [Dutton Ranch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutton_Ranch), and [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson).

Streaming also dominates the pay. According to ScreenRant, only one show on the list, HBO's Euphoria, airs on a traditional network or cable channel, and it ended its third season in May.

## What the list leaves out

The chart covers only the top of the pay scale. ScreenRant notes it leaves out the casts of shows such as [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things), The Pitt, Tracker and [High Potential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Potential), as well as other Sheridan series, Landman and Lioness. The three leads of [Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building) are on the list, and that show returns for season 6 on 8 December.
