**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), 25, spoke about sudden fame on The Ankler's Prestige Junkie podcast, released on 27 November 2026.
- She says she has a "very deep fear of being misunderstood", and that this shaped how she played Nikki in *Obsession*.
- According to The Ankler, the low-budget horror film has made more than half a billion dollars worldwide.
- Navarrette is also playing Rogue in the new X-Men movie and is described as an Oscar contender.

Inde Navarrette has described the past year as one that changed everything, telling The Ankler's Prestige Junkie podcast that her breakout horror hit *Obsession* feels like a child she is responsible for. The interview, released on 27 November 2026, was recorded before the Governors Awards.

## From press-tour fan to breakout star

[The Ankler](https://theankler.com/inde-navarrette-obsession-video-interview/) reports that Navarrette grew up loving movie press tours. She cited [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) and [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)) joking around over *Cinderella*, and [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) drinking wine on *The Graham Norton Show*, as favourites.

Before the spring release of *Obsession*, she says, she assumed the press tour would be the high point of her career and decided to enjoy it as much as possible.

It turned out to be the start. According to The Ankler, Curry Barker's low-budget film became a word-of-mouth phenomenon with more than US$500 million at the box office. Navarrette has since hosted *Saturday Night Live* and landed a role in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) as Rogue. Her earlier screen work included The CW's *Superman & Lois*.

## "I had a baby; its name is Obsession"

Asked whether she had expected any of this, Navarrette said she had no idea what was coming. When the host compared the experience to having a baby, she agreed it was a very good analogy. "I had a baby; its name is Obsession, and I feel very responsible for it," she said.

## Protecting Nikki

In the film, Navarrette plays Nikki, a young woman trapped in her own body after her friend Bear, played by [Michael Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnston_(actor)), wishes that she would love him more than anyone in the world. The Ankler describes Nikki as both the film's villain and its clear victim. The brief moments when the real Nikki breaks through are among the film's most memorable.

Navarrette said she never wanted audiences to feel there was no inner world inside the character. She did not want Nikki to be misunderstood, she said, because she felt she understood her. When Focus released the film in May, she was nervously waiting to see how people would respond.

## A personal fear

The role touched something real. Navarrette was born in Arizona and moved often because her father served in the U.S. Marine Corps. She told the podcast she has a deep fear of being misunderstood, and that if the world misread Nikki, it would feel like proof of her own worry. When audiences understood the character, it was satisfying as an actor and on a personal level.

The host also noted seeing Navarrette and [Andrew Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Scott_(actor)), a star of *Elsinore*, laughing together at the Governors Awards. This is the interview's only mention of that conversation, and the full discussion is in the podcast.
