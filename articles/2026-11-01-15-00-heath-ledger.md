**Quick summary**

- [Julia Stiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Stiles) dedicated her *Dancing with the Stars* routine on 20 October to [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger).
- She danced to "Can't Take My Eyes Off You", the song Ledger's character sings in *10 Things I Hate About You* (1999).
- Several castmates joined her on the floor, and the judges scored the dance 36 out of 40, according to Gulf News.
- The tribute sparked a wave of clips and a fresh look at why the film has lasted 27 years.

Heath Ledger has been gone since January 2008, yet a single ballroom routine has pulled one of his earliest hits back into the spotlight. On 20 October, Julia Stiles used the *Dancing with the Stars* Dedication Night to honour her *10 Things I Hate About You* co-star, and a [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/heath-ledger-and-the-joyful-charm-of-10-things-i-hate-about-you-a-romcom-that-still-sings-27-years-on-1.500731347) retrospective now asks why the film still sings.

## The dance that started it

According to Gulf News, Stiles and her partner [Ezra Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Sosa) performed a contemporary routine to Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off You". It is the number Ledger's Patrick Verona performs for Stiles's Kat Stratford in the film. A photo of the pair appeared on screen, and Stiles laid a red rose on the stairs.

Then came the surprise. [Joseph Gordon-Levitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gordon-Levitt), [Larisa Oleynik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Larisa_Oleynik), [Gabrielle Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabrielle_Union), [Andrew Keegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Keegan) and [Susan May Pratt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susan_May_Pratt) walked out to join her. The judges gave the routine 36 out of 40. We covered the moment at the time in [Julia Stiles Honours Heath Ledger on Dancing With the Stars](https://moduscarry.com/julia-stiles-honours-heath-ledger-on-dancing-with-the-stars/), and looked at how it came together in [Julia Stiles Asked Michelle Williams Before Heath Ledger Tribute](https://moduscarry.com/julia-stiles-asked-michelle-williams-before-heath-ledger-tribute/).

Gulf News notes that clips of the bleachers, the paintball fight and the prom flooded timelines almost straight away. Ledger died aged 28, and the piece points out that he was never given another romantic comedy. His Joker in *The Dark Knight* won him a posthumous Oscar. His Patrick, the Australian-accented bad boy, set a different kind of bar.

## A Shakespeare reworking with a Seattle accent

The film is a modern retelling of *The Taming of the Shrew*. Screenwriters Karen McCullah and [Kirsten Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Smith_(writer)) kept the bones: the names, the Padua setting, the two sisters and the rule that the younger cannot be courted until the elder is. Gulf News says they dropped the play's misogyny. Padua becomes a Seattle-area high school, Petruchio of Verona becomes Patrick Verona, and the only taming that happens is mutual.

The plot runs on a neat rule. Kat and Bianca Stratford (Stiles and Oleynik) are told by their father that Bianca may only date once Kat does. Cameron (Gordon-Levitt) falls for Bianca and, with his friend Michael, persuades the preening Joey Donner (Keegan) to pay Patrick to take Kat out.

Patrick, of course, falls for her anyway. The writer's argument is that predictability was never the point. The film earns its ending, and its toxic boys get their comeuppance, including Bianca's prom-night punches at Joey.

## A soundtrack that looks backwards

One of the more interesting observations in the piece is that, for a film so firmly set in 1999, almost none of its defining songs date from that year. It opens with Kat's car pulling up to school playing Joan Jett's "Bad Reputation", a 1980 track that introduces her before she speaks.

The bleachers scene leans on a 1967 ballad that belongs to Kat's parents' generation. Gulf News calls it a deeply uncool choice and says that is exactly the point. Kat's persona is armour against looking foolish, and Patrick wins her by doing the one thing she never would: making himself ridiculous in public.

At the prom, [Save Ferris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Save_Ferris) back [Kay Hanley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kay_Hanley) of Letters to Cleo on Nick Lowe's 1979 hit "Cruel to Be Kind". The writer calls that scene gloriously 90s, and a personal favourite.

## What happens next

No new Ledger project is part of this story. The tribute itself was a single night on television, and the renewed interest comes from fans revisiting the film rather than from any announcement. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who performed the Heath Ledger tribute on Dancing with the Stars?

Julia Stiles, with her partner Ezra Sosa, on Dedication Night on 20 October. They danced a contemporary routine to "Can't Take My Eyes Off You".

### What score did the tribute receive?

The judges gave the routine 36 out of 40, according to Gulf News.

### Which song does Heath Ledger sing in 10 Things I Hate About You?

His character Patrick Verona sings Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off You" to Kat Stratford in front of the school, with the marching band in tow.

### When did Heath Ledger die?

He died in January 2008, aged 28, as reported by Gulf News. He posthumously won an Oscar for playing the Joker in *The Dark Knight*.
