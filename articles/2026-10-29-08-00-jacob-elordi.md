**Quick summary**

- [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) is reportedly looking to buy a home in Los Angeles, according to a source quoted by Page Six and relayed by the International Business Times UK.
- The same source says Kendall Jenner is helping with the search but the two have no plans to live together.
- [Brody Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brody_Jenner) called a separate report about who pays for the couple's trips "clickbait", per Us Weekly.
- Neither Elordi nor Jenner has publicly commented on the relationship or the reports.

Jacob Elordi is reportedly searching for a house to buy in Los Angeles, with Kendall Jenner helping him look, according to an unnamed source quoted by Page Six. The insider said the pair are not planning to move in together. The claim, relayed by the [International Business Times UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-los-angeles-home-search-1827984) this week, has not been confirmed by either of them.

## What the source said about the house hunt

"Jacob is looking for his own place," the insider told Page Six, adding that the relationship is going well and that the two are not rushing into anything serious. The search, the source stressed, is for Elordi alone and not a shared home.

Elordi, 29, grew up in Brisbane and moved to Los Angeles after filming Netflix's The Kissing Booth in 2017, the International Business Times UK reports. Jenner is 30 and turns 31 on 3 November. Both are, for now, silent on the subject. The only details about their plans come from unnamed insiders, so the reports are best read as unconfirmed.

## A relationship built on reports, not statements

Reports have linked the pair since spring 2026, and they have reportedly travelled to Japan and Hawaii together. According to the International Business Times UK, photographers caught them holding hands in London on 20 August after dinner at China Tang at The Dorchester. Elordi then reportedly watched Jenner walk at Milan Fashion Week from the front row in September.

Their most visible outing came on Saturday 17 October at the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles. They walked the red carpet separately, moments apart, then sat side by side at dinner, the outlet says. Jenner wore a Valentino gown and Elordi a Bottega Veneta tuxedo. Photographers later saw them leave a Chateau Marmont after-party in the same car.

That public run of appearances sits alongside a steady stream of source-led stories. Page Six has also reported that insiders believe Jenner could marry before her sister Kylie. Those claims come only from unnamed sources, and no outlet has reported an engagement.

## Brody Jenner pushes back on the "sugar mommy" story

A different report, published on 27 August, alleged that Jenner was paying for the couple's luxury holidays. Social media users joked that she had entered a "sugar mommy" era. According to [Fashion Times UK](https://www.fashiontimes.co.uk/brody-jenner-dismisses-kendall-elordi-trip-payment-claims-1766438), citing Us Weekly, Brody Jenner replied on Instagram on 31 August: "I can confirm that this is clickbait."

Brody did not explain how the couple handle their expenses. His comment rejects the story but does not settle the question. Fashion Times points out that neither Jenner nor Elordi has addressed the claim, and that nobody has publicly verified who paid for any trip. It also notes that The Sun separately reported, again via a source, that Elordi arranged an expensive London hotel suite for a visit with Jenner. That report has not been independently established either.

## The family behind the actor

Away from the headlines, a [Legit.ng profile](https://www.legit.ng/entertainment/celebrities/1738252-meet-jacob-elordis-parents-sisters-family-euphoria-star/) looks at where Elordi comes from. He is the youngest of four children and the only son of John and Melissa Elordi. John is a Basque-born house painter who emigrated to Australia as a child and, the profile says, spent 13 years building the family home himself. Elordi told El País that his father arrived with $8.

Melissa was a stay-at-home mother. Legit.ng reports that Elordi took her as his date to the 98th Academy Awards in January 2026, keeping a promise he made at 15. He has three older sisters, including Isabella, who trained at the Australian Ballet School before moving into photography. In a 2022 GQ interview, Elordi called his father a "totem of the kind of man" he would like to be.

## What happens next

Nothing in the reports suggests an imminent announcement. Elordi's own career keeps him busy: the Legit.ng profile describes him as the star of Euphoria and [Wuthering Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights). Any confirmation of a Los Angeles purchase, or of the relationship itself, would have to come from Elordi or Jenner directly.
