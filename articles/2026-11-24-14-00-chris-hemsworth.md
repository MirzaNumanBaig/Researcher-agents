The Chris Hemsworth Avengers Doomsday push has a new centrepiece: the actor posted a fresh promotional video on Monday 23 November, less than four weeks before the film reaches cinemas on 18 December 2026. The clip arrives at the same time as reports of a frightening night at his home near Byron Bay.

## What the Chris Hemsworth Avengers Doomsday video shows

According to [Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/forum/topic/chris-hemsworth-unites-marvel-heroes-in-new-avengers-doomsday-trailer-ahead-of-december-premiere-2/?part=1), Hemsworth posted the video with the line "Team up and stand together" and a prompt for fans to book tickets. The footage follows several familiar characters preparing for a major confrontation, and Thor is shown telling the group to put their differences aside as the threat closes in.

The trailer also gives a closer look at the return of Steve Rogers, played by [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), and Peggy Carter, played by [Hayley Atwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayley_Atwell). Tekedia says their scenes point to a personal storyline involving their son.

## Doctor Doom and a crowded cast

The film's central threat is Victor von Doom, better known as [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), played by [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), who is best known as Tony Stark. The Fantastic Four also join the story as the crisis spreads across different corners of the Marvel universe.

The X-Men are back too. [James Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Marsden) returns as Cyclops, and the report lists [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart), [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen), [Rebecca Romijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Romijn), [Alan Cumming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Cumming) and [Kelsey Grammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Grammer) among the returning cast. Cyclops gets the line "To me, my X-Men!" in the footage.

The wider ensemble includes [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) as Sam Wilson and [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan) as Bucky Barnes. Wakanda and Talokan also feature, with Letitia Wright as Shuri and Tenoch Huerta Mejía as Namor.

Anthony Russo and Joe Russo direct, the pair behind Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, from a script by Stephen McFeely. Per Tekedia, it is the Avengers franchise's first return to cinemas since Endgame finished its run in 2019, and it sits within Marvel's Multiverse Saga. More on the actor is in [everything we publish about Chris Hemsworth](https://moduscarry.com/tag/chris-hemsworth/) and our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) coverage.

## Reports of a break-in at the Byron Bay home

The promotion comes against a more troubling backdrop. [Now To Love](https://www.nowtolove.com.au/celebrity/celebrity-news/chris-hemsworth-break-in/) reports that four masked intruders, two of them armed with knives, entered the family's Broken Head property on 11 November while the household was asleep upstairs.

Its account says CCTV showed the group climbing a security fence at the front of the property and entering a garage, where three cars were parked with keys inside. The incident was reported at about 7.30am. All three vehicles were later found in separate locations, one of them more than two hours from the house.

Much of the rest of the article rests on an unnamed source speaking to Woman's Day, so treat it as unconfirmed. That source says the family is shaken and that they are weighing a move away from Byron Bay. Neither Hemsworth nor his representatives are quoted, and we have not seen a statement confirming any plans.
