**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) told IndieWire that shooting *Project Hail Mary* left him lonely, as reported by SlashFilm on 1 November 2026.
- A chance meeting with [Julia Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Roberts) on a neighbouring set pushed him to ask for a scene partner.
- The result was a mop in a dress and glasses, nicknamed Moppy Ringwald.
- Moppy has a brief cameo in the film and appears more in deleted scenes, according to SlashFilm.

Ryan Gosling has revealed the unlikely fix for the isolation of making *Project Hail Mary*: a mop with a face, a dress and sunglasses. In an interview with IndieWire, summarised by [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2294709/project-hail-mary-julia-roberts-ryan-gosling-acting-problem/), the actor said a conversation with Julia Roberts convinced him he needed someone to act against.

## A film that leaves one actor alone

Gosling plays Dr. Ryland Grace in the adaptation of [Andy Weir's novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary). According to SlashFilm, Grace is the only human aboard a spaceship sent to investigate a distant sun. He was meant to be part of a three-person crew, but his companions died on the long journey.

That leaves Grace waking up light-years from home with a long beard and little memory. He later gains company in Rocky, an intelligent alien rock spider played by [James Ortiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ortiz). Until then, the story asks Gosling to carry long stretches of the film by himself.

Gosling told IndieWire that the same solitude followed him off screen. For a long time he was the only actor on set, and he said he grew "tired of acting alone".

## The chat that changed the day

Gosling said he stepped outside for some air and ran into Roberts, who was filming *After the Hunt* for the same studio, Amazon MGM Studios. They had never met. He recalled that she sat down and talked to him about how much fun she was having on set with director [Luca Guadagnino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Guadagnino), and invited him over.

He took her up on it. By his account, the crew was shooting a party scene when he arrived, and everyone seemed to be having a great time. SlashFilm points out the irony: *After the Hunt* is a tense drama about allegations of sexual assault and the secrets of a Yale philosophy professor, and the site gave it 6.5 out of 10. Even so, for Gosling the appeal was simple human company.

## Enter Moppy Ringwald

Back on his own set, Gosling went to directors [Phil Lord and Christopher Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lord_and_Christopher_Miller) with a plan. He said he asked for a mop that could be turned into a person so he could talk to it. According to his telling, the filmmakers paused an expensive shooting day and searched the sets for tape, glasses and a dress to build it.

The creation was named Moppy Ringwald. Gosling described the set as a high-tech space full of working LED screens, yet it was the mop in sunglasses that rescued the day. Moppy often stood just out of camera as his stand-in scene partner.

His takeaway, delivered with a straight face: "If you're having a problem and you can't solve it, talk to Julia Roberts."

## Where to spot Moppy

SlashFilm says Moppy Ringwald makes a blink-and-you'll-miss-it cameo in the finished film and features more in the deleted scenes. Viewers who rewatch *Project Hail Mary* may now notice how much of Gosling's performance was shaped by an off-camera mop.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Moppy Ringwald?

Moppy Ringwald is the nickname given to a mop dressed with a face, glasses and a dress. The *Project Hail Mary* crew built it so Ryan Gosling had something to act against.

### Why did Ryan Gosling ask for a mop?

He told IndieWire he was tired of acting alone, since his character is the only human on the ship for much of the story. A visit to Julia Roberts' set made him realise he missed other actors.

### Is Moppy Ringwald in the finished film?

According to SlashFilm, Moppy has a very brief cameo in *Project Hail Mary* and appears more in the deleted scenes.
