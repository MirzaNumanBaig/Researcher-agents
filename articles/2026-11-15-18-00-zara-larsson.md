**Quick summary**

- A 34-song tracklist for *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* has leaked, and it reportedly includes [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson).
- The list surfaced when images of the vinyl release appeared online, and sources confirmed it to Rolling Stone.
- That Grape Juice reports that Larsson's track is called "Fast Emotion". Rockstar Games has not officially released the full running order.
- Rolling Stone says the album arrives on Friday, November 20, a day after the game launches digitally.

The Swedish pop star Zara Larsson is among the names on the leaked tracklist for *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, according to reports published on 14 and 15 November 2026. The list is said to have escaped when pictures of the game's soundtrack vinyl, which had reportedly gone on sale early, spread online. Rockstar Games has not yet confirmed the full lineup.

## How the Zara Larsson GTA VI track list leaked

[Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-dua-lipa-zara-larsson-gta-vi-album-track-list-1235658416/) reported that the 34-song list was revealed by accident this weekend, after images of the "prematurely sold" vinyl turned up online. Its sources confirmed the track list to the magazine. That is stronger than a fan-forum rumour, but it is still not an announcement from the publisher.

The outlet names Larsson alongside [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa) and [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey) as contributors of new music. Other acts it lists include Rauw Alejandro, Jennie, Young Thug, Arca, Playboi Carti and [Tove Lo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tove_Lo).

## Where Larsson sits in the lineup

[That Grape Juice](https://thatgrapejuice.net/2026/11/gta-vi-soundtrack-tracklist-sza-cardi-b-dua-lipa-zara-larsson-more/) goes further and prints part of the list. It places Larsson's song, "Fast Emotion", among the pop standouts next to Dua Lipa's "Go Easy" and Del Rey's "Crazy for You". Only that outlet gave the title in the material we reviewed, so treat it as reported rather than settled.

The same list reportedly features [Camila Cabello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camila_Cabello) with "Sweet Like Candy" and Jennie of BLACKPINK with "Power Trip". [Rina Sawayama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rina_Sawayama), Tove Lo, PinkPantheress and Kali Uchis add to the pop and alternative R&B side. Rap is just as crowded, with Cardi B, Playboi Carti, Future, Metro Boomin, [Burna Boy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burna_Boy), Gunna, Young Thug and Kodak Black named.

## Songs already out

Some of the album is already public. Rolling Stone points to earlier releases from [Travis Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Travis_Scott), Morgan Wallen, Keith Richards ("Bright Lights, Big City") and Justice featuring [Tame Impala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_Impala) and SZA. Justice, who also scored the game, appear again with Michael McDonald. According to That Grape Juice, Cardi B's "Don't Chart" came out in October as part of the rollout, and SZA's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Justice and Tame Impala can already be streamed.

Del Rey told Rolling Stone that contributing her voice was "super special and a privilege", adding that her family first learned the roads of Los Angeles by playing the games. Her new song shares a title with an unreleased track she has held for more than a decade.

## A busy week for Larsson

The leak lands days after Larsson, Dua Lipa and SZA were announced for Coachella 2027, according to That Grape Juice. Our earlier [music coverage](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) follows her recent run of announcements, and more of her news sits on our [celebrities page](https://moduscarry.com/category/celebrities/).

## What happens next

The two outlets disagree on the release date. Rolling Stone says the album arrives on Friday, November 20, a day after the game's digital launch. That Grape Juice gives November 19, alongside the game on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Until Rockstar publishes the details, the date, and Larsson's place on the list, remain reported rather than official.

Both outlets also report a one-off *Grand Theft Auto VI* music festival in Miami on November 21. Rolling Stone names Cardi B, Kodak Black and Don Toliver alongside Travis Scott and Justice; That Grape Juice lists Scott, Cardi B and Metro Boomin as headliners. Neither names Larsson on that bill.
