**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) will play Daphne in Netflix's new series Scooby-Doo: Origins, due in 2027.
- She says the producers first wanted someone much younger and told her she was "too old".
- Grace was born on 25 June 2006, so she is 20.
- She shared the story on the Today Show while promoting The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Mckenna Grace has revealed that she was almost left out of Netflix's Scooby-Doo: Origins, with the production first deciding she was too old to play Daphne. The 20-year-old actress told the story on the Today Show, according to [Capital UK](https://www.capitalfm.com/news/tv-film/mckenna-grace-age-how-old-daphne-scooby-doo/), and the series is now set to arrive in 2027.

## What Mckenna Grace said about the audition

Grace was on the programme to promote The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping when the conversation turned to her next big role. She described the Daphne part as a childhood dream. By her own account she has dressed as the character for three different Halloweens, and her very first lunchbox carried the Mystery Machine.

The casting team, she said, wanted to go "really young" for the series. She recalls being told plainly that she was too old, and she remembers pleading to be part of it anyway. Her reaction, as she put it, was that she had to play the character. She closed the exchange by saying she could not believe she had landed the job.

## How old is Daphne in Scooby-Doo: Origins?

Capital reports that it is not yet clear how old Daphne, Velma, Fred, Shaggy and Scooby will be in the new series. What is known is that the show is set when the characters are teenagers at summer camp. The other actors are around Grace's age, and in some cases slightly older, with the group ranging from 18 to 21, according to the outlet.

For comparison, Daphne is 16 in the original animated series, Scooby-Doo, Where Are You!. That makes it possible the new show will keep the gang close to that age, though Netflix has not confirmed it. Casting actors in their late teens and early twenties to play younger teenagers is common in television, so the age gap itself is not unusual for the format.

The Daphne role caps a long run of work. Capital describes Grace as one of the most prolific stars of her generation, pointing to Chilling Adventures of Sabrina, The Handmaid's Tale, Captain Marvel and Scream 7 on her résumé.
