**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been announced as the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) in the Denis Villeneuve Bond film, directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) and backed by Amazon MGM Studios.
- Brad Pitt told a red carpet reporter he would like to play the villain, according to MARCA.
- Florence Pugh, who worked with Pattinson on Dune: Part Three, also asked about a villain part at an Elle event.
- There is no confirmed release date. The Guardian suggests fall 2028, while MARCA says 2027 is possible.

The Denis Villeneuve Bond film has its 007, and within days the conversation has moved on to who should stand opposite him. Amazon MGM Studios announced Robert Pattinson as the new Bond in mid-November, and this week Brad Pitt and Florence Pugh both put their hands up for the villain, while critics began fantasy-casting the leading women.

## How the Bond announcement landed

The news arrived through a short clip on the official 007 account, according to [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/life/entertainment/in-seventh-heaven-robert-pattinsons-role-of-a-lifetime-article/3000970). It showed Pattinson in a black tuxedo with a gun pointed off-camera, and ended with the words "James Bond will return". Forbes India reports that the 40-year-old becomes the seventh actor to play the MI6 agent, after Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig).

It is also the first Bond film not produced by Eon Productions since Dr No in 1962, per the same report. Villeneuve said in the announcement that Pattinson "has the timeless soul of 007". Reports cited by Forbes India say Pattinson beat frontrunners including Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor.

Reaction was split. Some fans were excited, while others questioned a choice that runs against the franchise habit of picking lesser-known actors. The Guardian argues Pattinson is arguably already as famous as Brosnan and Craig were at their 007 peak.

## Brad Pitt and Florence Pugh want the villain role

MARCA reports that Pitt was told about the casting by an ExtraTV reporter at the red carpet for The New Adventures of Cliff Booth. "I love this idea, he is a real actor," Pitt said. He then pitched himself directly, pointing to [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem)'s villain in Skyfall as a high bar. "Pick up the phone and call Denis Villeneuve," he added.

Pugh made a similar play at Elle's Women in Hollywood event, according to MARCA. She praised the Pattinson choice and asked whether there was "any villain worthy of a five-foot-tall woman". Neither actor has been approached publicly, and nothing has been confirmed. These are red carpet remarks, not casting news.

## Who plays opposite Pattinson?

The Guardian's Nov 24 piece turns to the leading women, the so-called [Bond girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_girl). Its writer floats Lily James as a femme fatale, and Callina Liang and Amber Midthunder as an ally who could fight at Bond's side. Phoebe Bridgers, who acted alongside Pattinson in Primetime, gets a mention too. The piece also argues that Sydney Sweeney and Zendaya would be awkward fits.

All of this is opinion and wish-listing. No actress has been announced for the film.

## Why Villeneuve suits the job

Man of Many describes Villeneuve's career in three phases: French-language dramas in Quebec, English-language thrillers such as Prisoners, Enemy and Sicario, then large-scale science fiction with Arrival, Blade Runner 2049 and Dune. Its ranking of his 11 features places Dune: Part Two first, Incendies second and Arrival third. It notes that Dune: Part Three is due in 2026 before he turns to Bond, and says details of the Bond film are "painfully sparse".

Our earlier coverage of [Villeneuve and Pattinson's link to the role](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-villeneuve/) tracked how the pairing came together, and you can find more in our [Denis Villeneuve archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/denis-villeneuve/).

## What happens next

The story has no release date yet. The Guardian says fall 2028 looks likely, based on the gaps between earlier Brosnan and Craig films. MARCA says the first film could arrive in 2027. Until Amazon MGM confirms, treat both as estimates.

The supporting cast and script have not been announced. More will appear in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is directing the new James Bond film?
Denis Villeneuve, the Canadian director of Arrival, Blade Runner 2049 and the Dune films, is directing. Amazon MGM Studios is backing the project.

### Who is the new James Bond?
Robert Pattinson, star of The Twilight Saga, The Batman and The Odyssey, was announced as the seventh actor to play 007.

### Who will play the villain?
Nobody has been named. Brad Pitt and Florence Pugh have both said publicly they would like the role, but that is not a casting announcement.

### When is the new Bond film out?
There is no official date. Reports range from 2027 to fall 2028.
