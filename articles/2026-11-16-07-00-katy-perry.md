**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) and [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) attended the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday, November 14, at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood, according to ELLE.
- It was their third red carpet appearance as a couple, after the Tribeca Festival in June and a Los Angeles museum opening in September.
- Perry wore Antonio Grimaldi spring 2025 couture; Trudeau wore a cream or white tuxedo jacket with a black bow tie.
- Vogue says the pair have settled on a formula: classic tailoring for him, maximalist fashion for her.

## A third red carpet together

Katy Perry and former Canadian prime minister Justin Trudeau walked the red carpet at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday night, [ELLE reported](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a74432886/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-red-carpet-date-night-baby2baby-gala-2026/). The event took place at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood, California, and the couple posed together in coordinated light-toned outfits.

ELLE counts it as their third red carpet appearance together. [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-baby-2-baby-gala-2026-11-15) says the two "may have, at first, seemed like an unlikely pairing" but are becoming regular fixtures on the event circuit. For readers who follow Perry's public life, it adds to a run of recent outings covered in our [Katy Perry archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/katy-perry/).

## What they wore

Per Vogue, Perry chose a look from Antonio Grimaldi's spring 2025 couture collection: a strapless, silver sequined minidress layered over a floor-length champagne satin skirt, with a sweeping cape at the back. She added nude Paris Texas sandals, glittering earrings and a sleek, flicked-out black bob.

Trudeau went for a white tuxedo jacket with satin lapels, black trousers and a black bow tie. ELLE describes the jacket as cream and the pair's outfits as complementary. The outlets differ on the exact shade, so "cream or white" is the safest description. For more on the dress itself, see our earlier post on [Perry's metallic couture at the gala](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-baby2baby-gala/).

## The pattern behind the outfits

Vogue's argument is that the couple's wardrobes come from "very different worlds" yet work together. Its summary: classic suits for Trudeau, and room for Perry's more maximalist fashion to do the talking.

The magazine points back to the earlier outings to make its case:

- **Tribeca Festival, June:** the couple's official red carpet debut, at the premiere of Perry's concert film *Katy Perry: The Lifetimes Tour*. Perry wore a vintage spring 1987 Lanvin gown with sculptural, cone-like embellishments. Trudeau wore a black suit and white shirt.
- **Lucas Museum of Narrative Art, September:** Perry wore a white velvet Christian Siriano gown from his spring 2027 collection. Trudeau wore a black suit with an open-necked white shirt. Vogue notes they posed arm in arm in matching tones. The [Lucas Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Museum_of_Narrative_Art) is in Los Angeles.
- **October Dolly Parton tribute:** Vogue says Perry wore a Roberto Cavalli spring 2004 printed cutout gown with a leather bustier set, and carried an embellished suede Dior Saddle bag from the John Galliano era. The event celebrated [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton). Vogue does not say Trudeau attended that one.

## About the Baby2Baby Gala

Baby2Baby is a nonprofit that provides essential items to children living in poverty across the United States, according to ELLE. Its annual gala raises millions of dollars and draws celebrities, philanthropists and business leaders.

ELLE lists Cardi B, Barbara Palvin, Dylan Sprouse, Ciara, Jessica Alba and [Kerry Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerry_Washington) among the other attendees. Demi Moore received this year's Giving Tree Award for her commitment to children in need. Earlier honorees include Serena Williams and Charlize Theron.

## What happens next

Neither outlet reports further joint plans. If the pattern holds, the next public outing will test Vogue's formula again.
