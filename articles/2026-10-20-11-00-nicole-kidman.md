Nicole Kidman has taken notice of a pop song written in her honour. The ABC reported on 20 October that "Nicole Kidman", a track by Slovak artist Adéla, has reached number nine on the Billboard Hot 100, and that the Australian Oscar winner used it on an Instagram reel of her own.

## A song that travelled from TikTok to the charts

The track sits on Adéla's debut album, Prima. The [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-20/ten-songs-named-after-famous-people-nicole-kidman-elon-musk/107283250) says it went viral on TikTok, where everyone from Madonna to Lizzo has shared it. A number nine peak on the Billboard Hot 100 is a high placing for a debut album track by an artist most listeners had not heard of a few months ago.

Kidman's own use of the song came through her Instagram account. The ABC does not say when she posted the reel or what it showed, so those details remain unconfirmed.

## Who is Adéla?

The ABC describes Adéla as a solo artist and former ballerina. She was cut in the first round of a YouTube reality show that aimed to form the next big girl group. The result, on the broadcaster's account, is a long way from that early exit.

## Why she wrote it

Adéla told W Magazine that the idea came after she received an invitation to the Golden Globes. "I've never been in a movie," she said, according to the ABC. The song, then, is about the pull of the star rather than a story from Kidman's life, and the ABC does not suggest the two have worked together.

That is a pattern the broadcaster notes more widely. Its list of ten songs named after famous people points out that a title doesn't always mean the song is about its namesake. Weezer's Buddy Holly has nothing to do with the rock pioneer, and Duck Sauce's Barbra Streisand has little to do with the singer, though the name helped the 2010 track reach the top 10 in Australia.

## What happens next

Neither Kidman nor Adéla has announced anything beyond the reel and the chart position, based on the source reviewed.
