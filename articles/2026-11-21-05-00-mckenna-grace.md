**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), 20, has dressed in references to [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) on the press tour for *Sunrise on the Reaping*, which is now in cinemas.
- Her stylist, Molly Dickson, told Cosmopolitan the approach was Grace's idea from the start.
- Her latest look, a purple dress on Despierta America, resembles her character Maysilee's reaping dress, according to CinemaBlend.
- Grace told People she "wept" when she learned she had the role.

Mckenna Grace has turned the *Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* press run into a game of spot-the-reference, and this week both the actress and her stylist explained how it came together. Fans have been decoding her outfits since the tour began, and the latest purple dress has reopened the debate over what it is meant to say.

## The purple dress that split opinion

CinemaBlend's Corey says Grace wore a purple dress while appearing on the Spanish-language morning show [Despierta America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C2%A1Despierta_Am%C3%A9rica!). The colour and white collar echo the reaping dress worn by her character Maysilee Donner in the film. It has no white belt, though, and carries flower details running from the bodice down the skirt.

The writer also floats a second reading. Grace is due to play Daphne in the live-action *Scooby-Doo: Origins*, a series CinemaBlend says is expected on Netflix in 2027, and purple is Daphne's colour. Grace has history with the character. According to the same report, she voiced Daphne in the 2020 animated film *Scoob!*. Neither explanation is confirmed by Grace, so treat it as a fan theory.

## What her stylist says about the strategy

In an interview with Cosmopolitan, stylist Molly Dickson said Grace was "absolutely certain from the beginning" that she wanted to embrace method dressing, the trend where actors wear looks that reference details of the film they are promoting. The magazine places it alongside the pink-and-green run for *Wicked* and the Barbie-inspired outfits of recent years.

Dickson said the process was collaborative. Grace would bring an idea and Dickson would work out how to turn it into fashion. The ladybug look was Grace's suggestion, a nod to the "Ladybug, Ladybug" song that Haymitch Abernathy sings to Maysilee in the story. Small ladybugs appeared in her hair for a New York outing.

The stylist also said the team wanted fans to feel recognition without making costumes. "We wanted someone who knew nothing about The Hunger Games to look at Mckenna and simply think she looked incredible," Dickson told the magazine. Grace wanted to honour the franchise's strong women, specifically Katniss and Joanna Mason, and the team later added inspiration from Rue.

## Katniss, wedding dresses and fan reaction

The most talked-about outfit was a recreation of Katniss's wedding dress from *Catching Fire*, worn in Paris. Dickson called it her personal favourite and said the team went back and forth on whether it was too much, until Grace's team encouraged them to go for it. Cosmopolitan reports that recreating the sculptural silver elements was the hardest part.

CinemaBlend adds that she has also worn a look inspired by the flaming gown from the same film, and a deep-cut reference to Rue's dress from the first movie. Many of these nods point to [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), who played Katniss. Dickson said the team did not expect fans to dig so deeply into the Easter eggs, and that the online conversation about the characters and the film was the best part of the tour.

## Why the role means so much to her

Grace told People she "wept" when she got the call about the part, and that she still cannot quite believe she gets to promote the film. She plays Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute reaped alongside a young Haymitch, played by Joseph Zada, in the first Quarter Quell. Per CinemaBlend she also plays Maysilee's twin, Merrilee.

She was close in age to Lawrence when she signed on, and says the weight of that is not lost on her. She did not get to speak with Lawrence before filming, but she did call her *Five Nights at Freddy's 2* co-star Josh Hutcherson, who played Peeta, to ask about the physical demands of the series. He confirmed the training is hard and shared on-set stories, including what it was like filming in water on *Catching Fire*.

## What happens next

CinemaBlend notes it is unclear whether Grace will carry method dressing into publicity for *Scooby-Doo*, since no release date beyond 2027 has been given. For now, *Sunrise on the Reaping* remains in cinemas, and the writer says the tour's franchise-themed looks may be nearly over.
