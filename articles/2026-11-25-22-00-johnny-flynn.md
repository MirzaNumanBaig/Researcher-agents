**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn), who plays Frankie Cooper in MobLand, and [Jerome Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerome_Flynn), Bronn in Game of Thrones, are half-brothers.
- They share a father, the actor and singer [Eric Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Flynn), who died of cancer in 2002.
- The link went around again on 24 November 2026 after a meme about it spread online, according to reports citing The Sun and the Daily Mail.
- Jerome, 63, has said he helped pay for Johnny's drama school; Johnny, 43, is set to play Lucius Malfoy in HBO's Harry Potter series.

Many viewers who watch Johnny Flynn's villain in MobLand and Jerome Flynn's sellsword in Game of Thrones have only just learned that the two actors are half-brothers. A meme spelling out the connection circulated on 24 November 2026, and fans' surprise was picked up by several outlets, among them the three that supplied the details here.

## How the Flynns are related

Both men are sons of Eric Flynn, a British actor and singer. They have different mothers and about 20 years between them. According to [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/flynn-brothers-acting-legacy), Johnny was born during Eric's second marriage, to Caroline Forbes, while Jerome came from an earlier marriage.

The family is full of performers. [CWIBC](https://cwibc.co.id/en/johnny-flynn-jerome-flynn-brothers), citing The Sun and the Daily Mail, says an older brother, Daniel Flynn, 65, has appeared in The Bill, Unforgotten and The Crown. Jerome also has a sister, Kerry, and a half-sister, Lillie, who works in musical theatre. Lillie also made an album with Johnny; our earlier piece covers [Johnny Flynn and Sister Lillie's Beam Cottage album](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-flynn-beam-cottage/).

## Two careers, one famous surname

Jerome first became widely known in the 1990s as Corporal Paddy Garvey in [Soldier Soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldier_Soldier). He later played Bronn in Game of Thrones and Bennet Drake in Ripper Street. [SuaraGarut](https://suaragarut.id/en/jerome-johnny-flynn-brothers) adds that he had pop chart success in the 1990s as half of the duo Robson & Jerome.

Johnny's credits include Lovesick, Ripley and Emma, and he plays the criminal Frankie Cooper in MobLand. He also works as a folk singer-songwriter. His next big screen role is Lucius Malfoy in the upcoming HBO series based on the [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) books, according to the same reports. More of his work is collected on our [Johnny Flynn tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/johnny-flynn/).

## What the brothers have said

The reports lean on older interviews. Speaking to the Belfast Telegraph, Jerome said his father's early death added "an extra element" to his bond with Johnny and Lillie, because they lost their dad much younger than he did, and he gets to be there for them.

He has also described Johnny as a brother rather than a half-brother. In an interview reported as being with The Telegraph, he said being with Johnny "feels beyond brotherhood". The outlets say he paid for Johnny's drama school place.

Johnny returned the warmth in an interview with the Sunday Times. He recalled thinking of his big brother as Baloo the bear when he was small: loving, warm, a little raggedy and full of energy. He also said Jerome would spend whatever he had on the best toys for him and Lillie, long before he had money.

## How fans reacted

Social media was mostly amused. One fan wrote on X, "Bronn is his brother? That's cool." Another said there was "a lot of talent in that family". One viewer said the news explained why they liked Frankie Cooper even though he is a nasty character, and another suggested Johnny could make a great Joker. These are individual posts, not a measure of wider opinion.

## What happens next

Nothing official has been announced about the brothers working together. The news is old family history resurfacing, and none of the three outlets reports new comment from either actor this week. Johnny's Lucius Malfoy casting is the next major item to watch, though the sources give no date for the HBO series.
