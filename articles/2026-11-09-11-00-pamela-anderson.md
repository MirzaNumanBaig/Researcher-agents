**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, shared a video of her Vancouver Island home, showing a slow morning of gardening, reading and a walk to her private dock.
- The property in Ladysmith, British Columbia, sits on land her grandparents bought in the 1950s.
- Realestate.com.au reports that the overhaul was paid for by selling her Malibu estate in late 2021 for $US11.8m (about $A17.8m).
- She bought the Malibu block in 2000 for $US1.8m (about $A2.7m).

Pamela Anderson has given fans a fresh look at her Vancouver Island estate, a coastal property in Ladysmith, British Columbia, that was rebuilt with the proceeds of her Malibu home. The 59-year-old posted a video of an unhurried morning there, [reports Realestate.com.au](https://www.realestate.com.au/news/inside-pamela-andersons-secluded-estate-funded-by-15m-sale/), drawing on coverage first published by the New York Post.

## What the video shows

The clip follows a simple routine. According to the report, Anderson starts in the sunroom, pulls a sweater over her dress and heads into the garden with her dog to cut flowers. She also reads philosophy, and the morning ends with her watching the sunrise from a private dock.

One detail points to her business interests: a garden basket holding shears, her glasses and a moisturiser from her vegan Sonsie skincare line.

## From Malibu beachfront to a family property

The report traces how the Canadian estate was paid for. Anderson bought a beachfront block in Malibu for $US1.8m in 2000 and spent about a decade building a luxury residence on it. She sold the estate in late 2021 for $US11.8m. That is roughly six and a half times the original purchase price, before the cost of building the home is counted.

The surplus went into her childhood property, known as "The Roadhouse". The land was bought by her grandparents in the 1950s. Anderson purchased the parcel in the early 1990s but did not return to live there permanently until 2019, the New York Post reports.

## A renovation that became a TV series

The multi-year rebuild became the subject of her series, *Pamela's Garden of Eden*. It also shaped her personal life. Anderson fell for one of the builders on the crew, Dan Hayhurst, and the pair married on the property on Christmas Eve 2020. According to the report, the marriage ended in divorce a little over a year later.

Anderson has described the project as a deliberate step away from celebrity culture. "When I came home, I thought, 'I am going to rewrite my life and I'm going to start with this property.' And it became like an art project," she said, as quoted in the report.

## Life on the Pamela Anderson Vancouver Island estate

Anderson's fame came from playing C.J. Parker on *Baywatch* from 1992 to 1997, and the report notes that coastal living was always her goal. She previously told the Post she felt more settled on what she calls her sustainable ranch, with room to rescue more animals, even if she misses her sons, Brandon and Dylan, whom she shares with ex-husband [Tommy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee) and who still live in the United States.

Speaking to the Vancouver Sun, she said that growing up on the beach left her expecting its mornings, and that she always knew she would come back. Keeping the place, she said, would let her hold on to "a little bit of my sanity".
