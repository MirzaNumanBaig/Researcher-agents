**Quick summary**

- MUBI has announced that Her Private Hell will stream exclusively on its service from 27 November 2026.
- The film is directed by [Nicolas Winding Refn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Winding_Refn), the filmmaker behind Drive, Only God Forgives and The Neon Demon.
- The cast includes Sophie Thatcher, Charles Melton and Havana Rose Liu, with a score by Pino Donaggio.
- Critics have been divided since the film reached cinemas earlier this year.

The wait for Her Private Hell on the small screen is nearly over. According to [Future of the Force](https://thefutureoftheforce.com/2026/11/13/mubi-announces-the-streaming-date-for-nicolas-winding-refns-her-private-hell/amp/), MUBI announced on 13 November that the film will stream exclusively on its platform from 27 November 2026. The Danish director's latest feature is a neon-soaked thriller set in a surreal, futuristic city.

## What Her Private Hell is about

MUBI describes the film as a hypnotic moodscape wrapped in pink mist. The story follows Elle, a rising film star played by [Sophie Thatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Thatcher), who is preparing to shoot a space odyssey. Her co-star is her ex-lover turned stepmother, played by [Havana Rose Liu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havana_Rose_Liu).

Elsewhere in the city, an American soldier played by [Charles Melton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Melton) walks the empty streets looking for his missing daughter. A sinister figure known only as the Leather Man stalks the shadows. As their paths cross, the film blurs the line between past and present, and between memory and fate, according to the announcement.

Refn's earlier work set the expectations. The film keeps his trademark neon cinematography, and it carries an orchestral score by composer [Pino Donaggio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pino_Donaggio). Refn's previous features include [Only God Forgives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_God_Forgives) from 2013.

## How the film has been received

The streaming date follows a theatrical run in which reviews were far from unanimous. Time Out's reviewer was strongly negative, finding the film heavy on mood and style but light on story, with performances described as lifeless. The review also says it leans on older influences such as David Lynch, Italian giallo horror and 1960s exploitation cinema. [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/movies/her-private-hell-review-2026) lists a US cinema release on 24 July and a UK and Ireland release on 25 September.

The Skinny reached a similar but milder verdict after the film's Edinburgh International Film Festival screenings in August. Its reviewer called the film stylish, camp and violent, but ultimately tedious, while singling out the action scenes as its funniest and strongest moments. [The Skinny](https://www.theskinny.co.uk/film/new-releases/her-private-hell-nicolas-winding-refn) also lists Kristine Froseth, Dougray Scott and Diego Calva in the cast.

The MUBI announcement itself, by contrast, points to what it calls many positive critical notices. Both things can be true: Refn has long divided audiences, and this film appears to be no exception.

## Why the streaming debut matters

Refn's films tend to find a second life at home, where viewers can seek them out on their own terms. For anyone who missed the film in cinemas, 27 November gives a single date to mark. MUBI says the film will be available exclusively on its service, so it will not appear on other streaming platforms at launch.

The film arrives on MUBI on 27 November 2026. The announcement gives no word on regional availability beyond calling MUBI a global service, so check your local MUBI listing for the date in Australia.
