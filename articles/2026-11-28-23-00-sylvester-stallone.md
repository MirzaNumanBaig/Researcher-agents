**Quick summary**

- A new biopic, "I Play Rocky", dramatises how [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) wrote and fought to star in "Rocky".
- Anthony Ippolito plays Stallone, and The Dominion Post's reviewer calls the transformation uncanny.
- Reports cited by Yahoo and Men's Journal say Stallone did go through homelessness in the early 1970s.
- One detail, whether he really sold his dog Butkus, is described as disputed.

The story of how Sylvester Stallone got "Rocky" made is back in front of audiences. "I Play Rocky", directed by Peter Farrelly, opens with a broke young actor in New York and follows him to the film that would win Best Picture. Reviews published on 28 November 2026 are praising the lead performance, and a companion piece asks the obvious question: how much of it actually happened?

## A crowd-pleaser built on an underdog tale

[Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) plays Stallone, and reviewer Alise Chaffins, writing in The Dominion Post, says he disappears into the role. She singles out his voice and mannerisms, and says there are moments when it feels like watching a young Stallone tell his own story.

She is less convinced the film is deep. In her view it follows a familiar biopic template and smooths its subject into something palatable, and she contrasts it with "Tony", which she calls a more honest and complicated portrait. Still, she describes the theme of chasing a dream as well realised, and she rates the film as a genuine holiday-season crowd-pleaser.

The film's version of events goes like this. Stallone cannot get work beyond bit parts and a soft-core film. His wife, Sasha, played by AnnaSophia Robb, tells him to write a movie he can star in. He writes screenplay after screenplay until he produces "Rocky".

## The producers and the shoestring shoot

The script reaches producers [Irwin Winkler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Winkler), played by P.J. Byrne, and [Robert Chartoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Chartoff), played by [Toby Kebbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Kebbell). According to The Dominion Post, they see potential but want a bigger name attached. Stallone insists he will play Rocky himself. [Jay Duplass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Duplass) appears as director John Avildsen.

The cast also includes [Tracy Letts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Letts) as a studio executive who tries to block Stallone, and Stephen James as [Carl Weathers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Weathers), who the reviewer says brings much of the humour. The review also credits the film with showing how tough a low-budget shoot can be, and with showing the invention of the Steadicam, which is used for the famous arm-raise at the top of the Philadelphia Museum of Art steps.

## Was Stallone really homeless?

Yahoo, republishing a Men's Journal piece, says yes. It cites ABC 7 New York reporting that Stallone was often destitute, and at times homeless, in the early 1970s. It quotes him saying on a 2001 "Rocky" DVD that he was at the very end of his rope.

Rolling Stone, as quoted in the same piece, reported that Stallone slept in a bus station while a 24-year-old struggling actor. He told Playboy in 1978 that the choice was to take a role in a film he disliked or to rob someone, and that he worked two days for $200 to get out of the bus station.

He did not live on the street the whole time. Page Six, also cited, describes a cramped walk-up apartment in New York. The same outlet lists the deli work, ushering at a movie theatre and cleaning lion cages at the Central Park Zoo. Our earlier piece on [Stallone's lion cage years and the friend who backed him](https://moduscarry.com/sylvester-stallone-henry-winkler/) covers that period, including Henry Winkler's memory of how hard he worked.

The one detail the report flags as uncertain is whether Stallone really sold his dog Butkus, as the film shows. That is described as disputed, so treat the film's version with care.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Sylvester Stallone in I Play Rocky?

Anthony Ippolito plays Stallone. The Dominion Post's reviewer says he captures Stallone's voice and mannerisms convincingly.

### Who directed I Play Rocky?

The review credits Peter Farrelly as the director.

### Was Sylvester Stallone really homeless before Rocky?

Reports cited by Yahoo, including ABC 7 New York and Rolling Stone, say he was at times homeless in the early 1970s and once slept in a bus station. He also had periods in a small New York apartment.

### Did Stallone really sell his dog Butkus?

The film shows it, but the Yahoo piece says whether it happened is in dispute.
