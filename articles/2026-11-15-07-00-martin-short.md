**Quick summary**

- At the Martin Short MoMA gala, [he](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) performed a nearly six-minute comedy set at the Museum of Modern Art's 2026 Film Benefit in New York on 12 November, according to RadarOnline.
- The set included a joke about President Donald Trump's face while climbing stairs, as reported by RadarOnline, which cites USA Today.
- Two days earlier, on 10 November, Short read The Cat in the Hat to kindergarten students at a Toronto school, Toronto.com reports.

The Martin Short MoMA gala set on 12 November drew headlines: he and Steve Martin took the stage at the Film Benefit in New York to tribute [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg) with a comedy routine that included a barb at [President Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump). Two days before, he had quietly dropped into a Toronto school library to read Dr. Seuss to five-year-olds.

## The Martin Short MoMA gala routine

[RadarOnline](https://radaronline.com/p/steve-martin-martin-short-trump-mocking-prezs-ice-gulf-of-mexico-name-change/) reports that the duo performed a set of nearly six minutes at the benefit honouring Spielberg. Guests included [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise), [Emily Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Blunt) and [Bruce Springsteen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Springsteen).

Martin raised The Color Purple, which RadarOnline notes earned 11 Academy Award nominations, and called it a true Spielberg classic. Short replied that when he thinks of The Color Purple, he thinks of Trump's face when he is climbing a flight of stairs. RadarOnline attributes the quote to USA Today.

The pair also teased Spielberg's 2026 science fiction film [Disclosure Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disclosure_Day). When Martin asked which Spielberg movie had changed Short's life, Short named the newest one, turning an apparent compliment into a joke at the director's expense.

## Not the first time they have aimed at Trump

RadarOnline places the MoMA joke nearly 21 months after the pair's appearance on SNL50: The Anniversary Special on 16 February 2025. There, Martin opened the NBC show with a line about spending time on a friend's boat in the "Gulf of Steve Martin", a reference to Trump's January 2025 order to use "Gulf of America" in federal communications.

Short, who was born in Canada, was asked by Martin whether he had his passport. He said no, Martin shouted "ICE! Get him!", and performers dressed as immigration officers pretended to drag Short off. According to the report, Short called out "I'll name names. Mike Myers, Jim Carrey!"

In a 2018 Daily Beast interview quoted by RadarOnline, Short said they do not want only a liberal audience and want the room to feel safe whatever people's vote.

## A surprise story time in Toronto

The gentler news came from Ontario. [Toronto.com](https://www.toronto.com/news/martin-short-cat-in-the-hat-toronto/article_357ba12b-9aef-5474-8f25-95f7cc571d94.html) reports that Short, who was born in Hamilton, visited John Ross Robertson Junior Public School on Tuesday 10 November and read The Cat in the Hat to kindergarten students during story time in the library.

Short once voiced the title character in the animated series The Cat in the Hat Knows a Lot About That!, which makes the choice of book fitting. Dr. Seuss's official Instagram account joined in, commenting "Nothing short of amazing!" on a post about the visit, per the [Niagara Falls Review](https://www.niagarafallsreview.ca/news/martin-short-cat-in-the-hat-toronto/article_fb234570-6476-5dca-b8fa-9f6820ee33a8.html), which ran the same Metroland report.
