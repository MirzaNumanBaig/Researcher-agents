**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) told Entertainment Weekly that Thor feels a "strange, hypnotic familiarity" toward Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday.
- Doom is played by [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), Hemsworth's former Iron Man co-star.
- Avengers: Doomsday opens on December 18.
- Marvel has not announced a fifth Thor film.
- Hemsworth's horror film The Cabin in the Woods leaves HBO Max on December 1.

Chris Hemsworth has given a first taste of how Thor will react to [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom) in Avengers: Doomsday, and the answer is not a warm one. In Entertainment Weekly's cover story, published on November 24, the Australian actor said the God of Thunder senses something familiar in the villain, but does not trust it. The film arrives on December 18, according to [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-theres-an-uncertaintychris-hemsworth-teases-thors-encounter-with-doctor-doom/).

## Thor and Doctor Doom: familiar face, wary hero

The twist at the heart of the casting is that Doom wears the face of Robert Downey Jr., the actor who spent years as Tony Stark alongside Hemsworth. Netflix Junkie quotes Hemsworth describing a "strange, hypnotic familiarity" for Thor, followed by "an uncertainty and a hesitation and a mistrust".

The report adds that Hemsworth found his old colleague's turn as the Latverian ruler unsettling, though in a way he found rewarding as a performer. For a character who has lately been played for laughs, a wary, uncertain Thor could be a notable change of register. It follows the broad comedy of Thor: Love and Thunder, which the same outlet describes as having threatened to turn Asgard's mightiest warrior into a sitcom figure.

## A veteran among Marvel's newer faces

Hemsworth also told the magazine he first felt out of place among Marvel's newer recruits. According to Netflix Junkie, conversations with [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)) and the Russo brothers, who are directing, helped him accept his role as one of the franchise's veterans.

The story the film tells is a crowded one. The report says the coming multiversal collapse puts the Avengers, the Fantastic Four and the X-Men on one battlefield. Hemsworth's first appearance as Thor came well before that, which gives him one of the longest records in the cast.

## Will there be another Thor film?

Nothing is official. Marvel Studios has not announced a fifth Thor movie, and no release window exists. Netflix Junkie points to a February appearance on the SmartLess podcast, where Hemsworth said he expected to play Thor "a couple more times" after Doomsday and that talks with [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) had touched on fresh creative ideas for the character.

One unconfirmed rumour, reported by The Direct and passed on by Netflix Junkie, claims Hemsworth wants Extraction director [Sam Hargrave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Hargrave) for a possible sequel. Treat that as talk only. Marvel has not commented, as far as the sources show. Hemsworth has also told the BBC that Thor's future depends on how Doomsday performs.

Readers who like his action work can find more in our coverage of [how Extraction is growing into a Netflix universe](https://moduscarry.com/extraction-3-netflix-universe/) and [Hemsworth's comments on feeling like an elder on Doomsday](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-avengers-doomsday-elder/).

## A horror favourite leaves streaming

Away from Marvel, ComicBook.com reports that The Cabin in the Woods, in which Hemsworth played the jock Curt before he became Thor, leaves HBO Max on December 1. The film runs 95 minutes and follows college friends whose remote cabin turns out to be part of a monitored project, with technicians played by Richard Jenkins and Bradley Whitford.

The site notes it was shot for a 2010 release, but MGM's financial troubles shelved it. Lionsgate later bought the distribution rights and it premiered in 2012, just weeks before The Avengers reached cinemas. ComicBook.com puts its box office at about $70 million and calls that moderately successful.
