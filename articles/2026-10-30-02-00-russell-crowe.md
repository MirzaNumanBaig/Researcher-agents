**Quick summary**

- *American Gangster*, the 2007 crime epic starring [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington), has been added to the Starz library, according to Collider.
- [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott) directed the film, which is based on a true story and is close to its 20th anniversary.
- It grossed nearly $270 million worldwide on a reported $100 million budget.
- The film now holds a "Certified Fresh" 81% score on Rotten Tomatoes.

A film that put two of Hollywood's biggest names in the same frame has a new streaming home. *American Gangster*, the period crime drama led by Russell Crowe and Denzel Washington, was recently added to the Starz library, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/russell-crowe-ridley-scott-american-gangster-streaming-starz-november-2026/) this week. It gives a younger audience an easy way to catch a movie that is about to turn 20.

## A rare Scott, Crowe and Washington pairing

Collider notes that Ridley Scott has hinted over the years that working with Crowe and Washington is not always easy. Even so, the director made a two-hander with both of them, and that film is the one now heading to Starz. Crowe and Washington star opposite each other, and the story follows a gangster and the agents who set out to bring him down.

Scott has not worked with Crowe since *Robin Hood* in 2010, while he reunited with Washington more recently on *Gladiator II*. Washington also built a close friendship with Scott's late brother, [Tony Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Scott), with whom he made *Unstoppable*, *Man on Fire* and *Crimson Tide*, according to the same report.

## Where the film sits in Crowe's career

*American Gangster* arrived in cinemas in 2007, at the peak of the long creative partnership between Crowe and Scott. That decade also produced *Gladiator*, *Body of Lies* and *A Good Year*, Collider says.

The film is a gangster epic based on a true story. Its supporting cast is deep: [Josh Brolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Brolin), [Chiwetel Ejiofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiwetel_Ejiofor), Common and [Cuba Gooding Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuba_Gooding_Jr.) all appear. Collider describes Washington as playing Frank Lucas, and shows Brolin as one of the agents closing in.

## The numbers behind the film

The commercial result was strong. Collider puts the worldwide gross at nearly $270 million against a reported budget of $100 million, so it returned more than two and a half times its cost at the box office.

It was also an awards contender in a crowded year. It competed with *No Country for Old Men*, which also starred Brolin, and with the Western *There Will Be Blood*. Nearly two decades on, the film holds a "Certified Fresh" 81% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the review aggregator.
