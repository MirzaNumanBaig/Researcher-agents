**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) was photographed in New York on Tuesday 17 November in loose, floor-skimming tailored trousers.
- She paired them with black loafers and a Chanel bag, according to Harper's Bazaar UK and Red magazine.
- Both magazines call the shape a defining trouser trend for the coming months.
- Red points shoppers to H&M's wrap detail tailored trousers at £54.99 as a high street match.

Nicole Kidman's trousers were the talking point of her latest New York outing. The actress was photographed in the city on Tuesday evening, 17 November, in a tailored pair cut with so much volume that the hems almost skimmed the pavement. Two British fashion titles picked up the look within a day.

## What Nicole Kidman wore in New York

The two magazines describe the top half differently. [Harper's Bazaar UK](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/shopping/a74464481/nicole-kidman-oversized-trousers/) says she wore an elegant grey suit over a blouse with a pie-crust collar. [Red](https://www.redonline.co.uk/fashion/fashion-news/a74462853/nicole-kidman-voluminous-suit-trousers/) describes a cropped navy blazer over a white ruffled shirt. Both agree on the rest: polished black loafers and a Chanel handbag, which Harper's calls velvet and Red calls quilted.

The bag fits her wider history with the label. ABC News has described her as a Chanel brand ambassador, and it was a Chanel gown she chose for this year's Met Gala. For more of her recent style moments, see our earlier piece on [Nicole Kidman's jazz shoes as a fall trend](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-jazz-shoes/).

## Why the oversized trousers matter

Harper's Bazaar calls the shape "art" trousers and says slouchy silhouettes can look luxurious when the details are right. Its advice is to balance the volume with a pretty blouse and a structured blazer, which keeps a borrowed-from-the-boys feel without looking sloppy.

Red makes a similar point from the practical side. Balloon and cocoon-leg trousers with a fluid shape have been replacing the more structured wide-leg cut this autumn, it says, and the trick is a fitted top half. A tucked-in shirt with a cropped blazer, or a lightweight knit, does the job. Flat shoes such as loafers or slim trainers keep the outfit workable for daytime.

## Where the trend comes from

Harper's ties the look to the spring-summer runways, which it says took cues from sculpture. At Balenciaga, it describes trousers as almost abstract, with earthy tones and rounded curves, as if [Barbara Hepworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Hepworth) had carved them from clay. At Céline, it says, the fluidity echoed Anish Kapoor's [Cloud Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_Gate) sculpture in Chicago. The magazine expects to see the shape "everywhere" in the coming months.

For party season, it suggests dressing the trousers up with a silk camisole, chandelier earrings and slingbacks. For something more relaxed, it recommends a cashmere knit, trainers and a trench coat.

## How to get the look

Red's high street pick is H&M's wrap detail tailored trousers, which come in beige and black and cost £54.99. At a higher price, it points to a checked balloon design from Jaeger that tapers at the ankle. Red notes it earns a commission on some links, so treat these as editorial suggestions, not endorsements from us.

## Kidman's recent fashion run

The New York outing follows a busy year for her on the style front. ABC News reported that Kidman co-chaired this year's Met Gala and arrived with her daughter Sunday Rose, who was making her Met debut. Kidman wore a red sequined Chanel gown with feathers, styled by [Jason Bolden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bolden). "For me, fashion is art," she told Vogue, which fits neatly with the sculptural trousers now being praised. ## What happens next

Neither magazine reports a new project tied to this outing, so the story is about the clothes, not an announcement. Expect more voluminous trousers on the high street through the party season, if Harper's and Red are right. If Kidman wears the shape again at an event, we will update this page.
