**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen)'s 2015 Christmas comedy *The Night Before* is now available to stream free on Tubi, according to ScreenRant.
- The film was directed by [Jonathan Levine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Levine) and also stars [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) and [Joseph Gordon-Levitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gordon-Levitt).
- It earned about $52 million worldwide on its original release.
- ScreenRant's report does not say which countries can watch it, so check Tubi's listing where you live.

Eleven years after its cinema release, *The Night Before*, the R-rated Christmas comedy starring Seth Rogen, has landed on Tubi's free streaming service, [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/seth-rogen-night-before-christmas-comedy-streaming-free-tubi/). For anyone building a holiday watchlist, it is a no-cost addition from one of comedy's most recognisable names.

## What The Night Before is about

Levine's film follows three old friends who keep an annual Christmas Eve tradition in New York City. According to ScreenRant, the group has drifted apart over the years. Rogen plays Isaac, who has a child on the way. Mackie plays Chris, who has become a famous football player. Gordon-Levitt plays Ethan, whose life has not worked out as he expected and who clings to the friendship as it fades.

Their night out is built around a hunt for the elusive Nutcracker Ball. Along the way, ScreenRant says, the trio take far too many drugs and are beaten up by a group of Santas. The outlet describes the result as hilarious and filthy, while arguing that its themes of ageing and friendship give it a warmer core than the raunchy surface suggests.

## The cast around Seth Rogen

The leads are joined by several familiar faces. ScreenRant names [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder), [Michael Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Shannon) and [Mindy Kaling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindy_Kaling) among the supporting appearances. The outlet also notes that many in the cast have gone on to bigger things since 2015, singling out Mackie and Fielder.

## How the film performed

The Night Before was never a blockbuster. ScreenRant puts its worldwide gross at $52 million on release in 2015 and suggests that was probably enough to break even. That figure is the outlet's estimate rather than a studio-confirmed profit, but the point stands: plenty of viewers have likely never seen it, and a free stream removes the usual barrier.

The R rating, driven by drug use and crude sexual humour, will also put some viewers off. ScreenRant is upfront that the film is not for everyone.
