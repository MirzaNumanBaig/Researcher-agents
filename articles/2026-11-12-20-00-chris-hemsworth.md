**Quick summary**

- Thieves broke into the Broken Head home of [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky) in the early hours of Wednesday 11 November 2026.
- Three vehicles were taken: a BYD, a Ram and a Ford Raptor ute, according to 7NEWS.
- Police say two have since been found, at Coraki and Tabulam. The third is still missing.
- Investigations are continuing and police are asking anyone with information to come forward.

The Chris Hemsworth home break-in has become a police matter in northern New South Wales. Officers say they were called to a vehicle theft at a property in Broken Head, south of Byron Bay, at about 7.30am on Wednesday, and the Hollywood actor's family home is the address in question, according to [7NEWS](https://7news.com.au/entertainment/police-investigate-break-in-and-theft-at-hollywood-actor-chris-hemsworths-northern-rivers-home-c-23152107).

## What we know about the Chris Hemsworth home break-in

Police told 7NEWS that officers attended and were told four unknown people had entered the property and stolen three vehicles earlier that day. The cars are reported to be a BYD, a Ram and a [Ford Raptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Raptor) ute.

The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/news/article-16286359/Chris-Hemsworth-house-break-theft.html) reported that NSW Police confirmed the theft on Thursday and that the incident happened in the early hours of Wednesday. Both outlets describe the property as being at Broken Head, in the Northern Rivers region.

Neither the actor nor his representatives had commented in the reports we reviewed. Police have not named the family in their statement, as quoted by 7NEWS.

## What the CCTV reportedly showed

The account of the night comes from a source close to the couple, quoted by 7NEWS and the Daily Mail. According to that source, Pataky woke at about 2am to the sound of the dogs barking, then went back to sleep. Later that morning she checked the property's CCTV cameras.

The footage reportedly showed four men in balaclavas, two of them holding knives. Both outlets say the men scaled the secure front fence and got into a garage where the three cars were parked with their keys inside. Pataky and Hemsworth then called police.

The Daily Mail says the couple live at the home with their three children. It values the property at $75 million, a figure the outlet gives and that we have not been able to confirm elsewhere. Police have not said that anyone was confronted inside the house.

## Two cars recovered, one still missing

The more reassuring part of the story is the recovery. 7NEWS reports that one vehicle was later found at Coraki, about an hour's drive from the property, and another at Tabulam, more than two hours away.

Police are still searching for the third car. The reports do not say whether anyone has been arrested, and police have not released a description of the four people beyond what the family's cameras reportedly captured.

## What happens next

Police say investigations are ongoing. Anyone with information is urged to contact them, and the quickest route is the usual local police line.

For now, the open questions are straightforward: where the third vehicle is, who the four people were, and whether the CCTV footage will lead to charges. We will update this story when police or the family say more.
