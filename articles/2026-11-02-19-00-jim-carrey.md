**Quick summary**

- [Zayn Malik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zayn_Malik) shared a Halloween 2026 transformation into the title character of [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey)'s 1994 comedy *The Mask*.
- He posted a photo and video carousel and a second reel on Instagram, according to MEAWW News.
- His caption borrowed the film's best-known line and thanked two makeup teams.
- Separately, actor [Scott Speedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Speedman) compared [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s early comic work to Carrey's, in an interview with PEOPLE.

Zayn Malik turned up as Stanley Ipkiss's green-faced alter ego this Halloween, posting a Zayn Malik Jim Carrey Mask tribute to Instagram that reached news sites on 2 November. The 33-year-old former One Direction singer paid homage to the 1994 comedy, which still draws affection three decades on, and the nod landed alongside a fresh Carrey comparison from a fellow actor.

## What Zayn Malik posted

According to [MEAWW News](https://news.meaww.com/zayn-malik-turns-into-jim-carreys-the-mask-for-iconic-halloween-transformation), Malik's first Halloween post was a carousel of images and videos. It showed him in a bright orange suit with a polka-dot tie and hand tattoos, photographed from several angles. He also shared behind-the-scenes clips of the makeup being applied.

His caption read "Shombody shtop mehhh..", a play on the film's famous line. He thanked @gothamfx and @rbfxmakeupgallery for helping build the look. A second post, a reel, showed him in different outfits, pulling faces and playing up the character's manic energy, with the same caption.

[Hungama Express](https://www.hungamaexpress.com/news/55396-zayn-malik-channels-jim-carreys-iconic-mask-character-for-halloween) described the costume slightly differently. It reported an orange three-piece suit with a matching fedora, green face paint and slicked-back hair. The two outlets agree on the suit, the tie and the source of the idea.

## The film behind the costume

*The Mask* was released in 1994 and starred Carrey opposite [Cameron Diaz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Diaz). Carrey, now 64, played Stanley Ipkiss, a bank employee who becomes what IMDb calls a manic superhero when he puts on a mysterious mask, MEAWW reported. The character first appeared in [The Mask comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mask_(comics)).

Hungama Express called the film a cult classic that remains a fan favourite decades after release, and a fun throwback among Halloween picks. Neither outlet reported any comment from Carrey about the costume.

## Scott Speedman on Ryan Gosling and Carrey

Carrey's name also surfaced in a different story the same day. [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/scott_speedman_compares_ryan_gosling_to_jim_carrey_30_years_since_their_collaboration_on_goosebumps/s1_17785_44504117), republishing an article from Netflix Junkie, said Speedman spoke to PEOPLE about his ABC series *R.J. Decker* and recalled working with Gosling on *Goosebumps* when both were young.

Speedman said he had long watched Gosling on Canadian television as "sort of the Jim Carrey comic relief" in shows, and called him a very gifted comedian, pointing to his Saturday Night Live appearances. Their shared episode, "Say Cheese or Die", premiered on 9 February 1996, per Yardbarker.

Gosling went on to star in *The Notebook* and *Barbie*, and more recently *Project Hail Mary*, the report said. His band Dead Man's Bones released a new single, "Lost in the Night", in October, its first music in more than 17 years.
