Hugh Jackman has appeared as a surprise guest at a concert by Broadway star Sutton Foster, performing two duets with her and the Pacific Jazz Orchestra. Video of the pair singing "A Million Dreams" from *The Greatest Showman* was shared this week.

## A surprise on stage

According to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/Video-Sutton-Foster-Hugh-Jackman-Duet-on-Million-Dreams-From-THE-GREATEST-SHOWMAN-20261021), Jackman first walked on while Foster was performing Peggy Lee's "A Doodlin' Song". The audience greeted him with applause, and he stayed on stage to sing "Million Dreams", reprising his role from *The Greatest Showman*.

[Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/watch-hugh-jackman-and-sutton-foster-share-2-duets-with-pacific-jazz-orchestra) reports that Jackman was Foster's special guest at her September 19 show at The Soraya. It describes the pair as a Broadway couple and *The Music Man* co-stars, and says they began dating in 2025 after the Broadway run of that musical. Playbill has also published video of the "A Doodlin' Song" duet.

## The rest of the program

Foster also sang material from her stage career, BroadwayWorld says, including "Blow Gabriel Blow" and "I Get A Kick Out Of You" from *Anything Goes*, and "Till There Was You" from *The Music Man*.

The program used new arrangements by Chris Walden, a seven-time Grammy-nominated composer and arranger who is also lead arranger for the Oscars, according to BroadwayWorld. Playbill says Walden conducted the orchestra, with arrangements by him and Michael Rafter.

The concert was Foster's debut at The Soraya and opened the venue's 16th season and the Pacific Jazz Orchestra's fourth, BroadwayWorld reports.

## Two Tony winners

Both performers have won Tony Awards. Playbill notes that Foster won for *Thoroughly Modern Millie* and *Anything Goes*, while Jackman received his Tony for *The Boy From Oz*. BroadwayWorld adds that Foster took the title role in *Thoroughly Modern Millie* in 2002, earning her first Tony, and has also starred in the TV series *Younger* and *Bunheads*.

Neither outlet reported any plans for further joint performances. Fans can watch the duets through the videos published by BroadwayWorld and Playbill.
