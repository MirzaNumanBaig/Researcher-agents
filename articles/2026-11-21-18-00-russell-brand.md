**Quick summary**

- Newly unsealed testimony from a 2025 New York deposition shows [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was questioned about his marriage to [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry).
- The deposition is part of a civil lawsuit by a woman who worked as an extra on the 2011 film Arthur. Brand denies her allegations.
- The alleged incident dates to July 2010, about three months before the Brand and Perry wedding. Perry is not accused of anything.
- The civil case is separate from his UK criminal case, in which he was sentenced to 10 years and six months this month.

The Russell Brand deposition is back in the headlines after newly unsealed testimony showed lawyers pressing him on his marriage to Katy Perry. According to [JubileeCast](https://www.jubileecast.com/articles/41230/20261120/russell-brand-questioned-about-katy-perry-marriage-in-newly-unsealed-sexual-assault-deposition.htm), which cited Page Six and records obtained by the Daily Mail, Brand said he could not recall whether he was already married when he filmed Arthur.

## What Brand said about Katy Perry

Asked for a timeline, Brand said the dates would be easy to find online. He recalled being in a relationship with a "very high-profile woman" and said that even during the marriage he was "sort of drowning in love," as reported by [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/russell-brand-makes-wild-katy-183000671.html).

The two married in October 2010, and the marriage ended in 2012. The woman's allegations relate to July 2010, which is before the wedding. Both outlets stress that Perry is not accused of wrongdoing.

## The lawsuit behind the questions

The plaintiff is identified in court records only as Jane Doe. She says Brand exposed himself to her in front of cast and crew while Arthur was filming at Le Cirque restaurant in Manhattan, and that he sexually assaulted her in a bathroom later that day. Brand has said his sexual encounters were consensual.

Yahoo News UK reports that a published New York Supreme Court decision confirms she sued under the Adult Survivors Act and named Brand, Warner Bros. and other production companies. That decision dealt with a dispute over expert testimony. It did not rule on whether the alleged assault happened.

## Other parts of the testimony

Asked whether he had ever exposed himself without consent, Brand answered that he felt he "must have done" but did not remember it, and could not "concretely and absolutely rule that out." Yahoo News UK notes his answer was about past conduct in general. It does not confirm the plaintiff's account.

Lawyers also raised his old tabloid reputation. Brand said a title The Sun gave him three times had once been "a very proud accolade." He described a roughly ten-year pattern of behaviour that he said lasted until about 2015, before his 2017 marriage to Laura Gallacher.

On the production side, he said there was "nothing of that nature" to report to the studio. He also confirmed his Alcoholics Anonymous sponsor visited the set.

## How it fits with the criminal case

The testimony comes after a UK judge sentenced Brand on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison. A jury had convicted him of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009. He maintained his innocence at trial. For earlier coverage, see our report on the [unsealed deposition](https://moduscarry.com/russell-brand-deposition-unsealed/) and everything under the [Russell Brand](https://moduscarry.com/tag/russell-brand/) tag.

A separate story from News24 says Brand has dismissed his barrister and wants to appeal. That page could not be read for this report, so we have not confirmed the detail.

## What happens next

The New York case is ongoing and no verdict has been reported. The outlets give no trial date. Brand's comments in the deposition are not findings of fact, and he continues to deny the plaintiff's specific allegations.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Katy Perry accused of anything?

No. Both reports say she is not accused of wrongdoing. She was asked about only as part of the timeline of Brand's life in 2010.

### Did Brand admit the assault in the deposition?

No. According to Yahoo News UK, his remarks about possible past conduct are not an admission of the specific assault alleged, which he denies.

### Is this the same case as his UK conviction?

No. The New York lawsuit is a civil case about the Arthur set. The UK sentence relates to offences against four women between 2004 and 2009.
