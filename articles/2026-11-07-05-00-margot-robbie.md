**Quick summary**

- The Legend of Tarzan, the 2016 film starring [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), arrived on HBO Max in select regions on 6 November.
- According to FlixPatrol, cited by Comic Book Resources, it is the most-watched film on HBO Max worldwide and ranks No. 1 in 19 countries.
- It is not on HBO Max in the United States, where it streams free on Tubi.
- The film earned $356.7 million worldwide in 2016 against a $180 million budget and was widely seen as a disappointment.

Margot Robbie's 2016 adventure The Legend of Tarzan has become the most-watched film on HBO Max this week, ten years after it struggled in cinemas. [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) reports that the film landed on the service in select regions on 6 November and almost immediately took the top spot, with FlixPatrol's data showing it at No. 1 in 19 countries.

## How a decade-old film reached No. 1

The jump came fast. Per [CBR's report](https://www.cbr.com/the-legend-of-tarzan-hbo-max-streaming-success-november-2026/), the film went live on 6 November and was leading the platform's global ranking within a day. The streak comes with a caveat that matters for American readers: the film is not on HBO Max in the United States. There, it can be watched free on Tubi.

That means the chart position reflects viewing in the regions where it was added, not a worldwide rollout. The figures come from FlixPatrol, a chart tracker, rather than from HBO Max itself, and the service has not published its own viewing numbers.

## What the film is about

The 2016 story is not a retelling of Tarzan's childhood. According to CBR, it picks up after he has left the jungle for Victorian England. Alexander Skarsgård plays John Clayton III, the Earl of Greystoke, with Robbie as his wife, Jane Parker. The cast also includes [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson), [Djimon Hounsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djimon_Hounsou) and [Christoph Waltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Waltz), and it was directed by [David Yates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Yates).

The plot sends the couple back to the Congo. Waltz's character, Leon Rom, an envoy for King Leopold, plans to hand Tarzan to an old enemy in exchange for diamonds. When Jane is caught up in the scheme, Tarzan has to return to the jungle to rescue her. The character itself dates to the 1912 novel Tarzan of the Apes by [Edgar Rice Burroughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs).

## A costly run in 2016

The numbers explain why the new chart position is a surprise. CBR says the film took $126.6 million in the United States and Canada and $230.1 million elsewhere, for $356.7 million worldwide. The production budget was $180 million, and the outlet says it needed at least $400 million to break even.

Financial analysts estimated that Warner Bros. lost around $40 million on it, according to CBR, although the studio has maintained it broke even. Reviews were cool as well. The film holds a 35% critic score and a 58% audience score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), as quoted by CBR.

## What happens next

Whether the film holds its place will show in the next few days of FlixPatrol rankings. Nothing in the sources says HBO Max plans to bring it to the United States, so American viewers remain on Tubi for now. Neither Robbie nor Warner Bros. had commented on the chart run in the reports we read.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I stream The Legend of Tarzan?
It is on HBO Max in select regions as of 6 November. In the United States it is available free on Tubi, according to Comic Book Resources.

### Who stars in The Legend of Tarzan?
Alexander Skarsgård plays Tarzan and Margot Robbie plays Jane Parker. Samuel L. Jackson, Djimon Hounsou and Christoph Waltz co-star.

### Did The Legend of Tarzan make money?
It grossed $356.7 million worldwide on a $180 million budget, short of the roughly $400 million CBR says it needed to break even. Analysts estimated a loss of about $40 million, while the studio says it broke even.
