**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) says a scene in which her character Maysilee kills a Gamemaker was filmed but cut from the finished movie.
- She told Collider the argument with Haymitch that followed took the most workshopping of any scene.
- The cut came because the film dropped a day of the arena, leaving no time to repair the rift between the two characters.
- The film runs two hours and 38 minutes and opened on 28 October 2026.

Mckenna Grace has revealed which scene from [The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games%3A_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping) she most wishes had survived the edit. Speaking on Collider Ladies Night, the actress said a chase through the woods and a heated clash between Maysilee and Haymitch were shot but did not make the final cut, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-deleted-scene-mckenna-grace/).

## The scene that never reached the screen

In [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)' novel, Maysilee and other tributes come across Gamemakers working in the arena. Maysilee does not start the attack, but she kills one of them. Haymitch does nothing, and she resents it.

Grace told Collider the sequence was filmed in full. "I kill a Gamemaker, which we shot," she said. "We have this whole chase sequence. We run through the woods, and we end up in this clearing, and Joe and I are screaming at each other and fighting." Joe is Joseph Zada, who plays Haymitch.

According to Grace, the scene was dropped because the film no longer had room for it. Her explanation was that the two characters could not clash so sharply when the movie had lost a day of the arena and had no time to repair the rift afterwards.

## Why it took so much work

Grace singled this argument out as the scene that needed the most workshopping with director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence). She joked that she and Zada were "probably so annoying" to him because they kept questioning their lines.

The problem, as she described it, was motivation. Zada's concern was that Haymitch would not submit to the Capitol and was already rebelling against it, so it was unclear why he would seem weak in the argument. Grace's own worry ran the other way: Maysilee had good reason to be angry at him.

After talking it through, the three of them settled on what Grace called a "beautiful argument" that supported the points each character was making. Grace said she had arrived on set each day with a long list of questions and that Lawrence always listened.

## What the cut scene would have said

Grace explained that the argument was about a split in outlook. Maysilee is furious with everyone who played any part in building the system the tributes are trapped in. Haymitch, she said, sees the humanity in Effie, who sincerely believes the Games are for the good but stands on the wrong side morally without realising it.

The trim also lost a line that echoed the 2012 film. In the scene, Haymitch says she will never get to win now because she killed a Gamemaker, and Maysilee answers, "Nobody ever wins these games, Haymitch!" Grace said she was pleased to have delivered a line from one of the original movies.

She did not sound bitter. "It was such a cool experience to get to completely rewrite that and work through it for so long, all of us together," she said, adding that it "didn't make sense in the grand scheme of the time that we had for the movie."

## How the film has fared

The prequel is set in Panem 24 years before the original series and follows the reaping of the 50th Hunger Games. Its cast includes Zada, Whitney Peak, Grace and Ben Wang. According to [Wikipedia's film entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), it has taken about US$237.2 million at the box office against a reported budget of US$150 million, and holds 68% on Rotten Tomatoes and 55 out of 100 on Metacritic.

Critics have been split. Our earlier coverage looked at how [reviews divided Hunger Games fans](https://moduscarry.com/sunrise-on-the-reaping-review/) and at the [nostalgia-over-story verdict](https://moduscarry.com/sunrise-on-the-reaping-review-nostalgia/).

## What happens next

Nothing in Collider's report says the cut scene will be released. Studios often include deleted footage on home releases, but no such plan has been announced for this film. For now, Grace's account of the chase and the clearing is the only description of it. Readers can find more film coverage in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.
