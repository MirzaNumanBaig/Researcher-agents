**Quick summary**

- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) has explained his choice of Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, saying the actor has "the timeless soul of 007".
- Pattinson replaces Daniel Craig in Bond 26, which Villeneuve directs and Steven Knight writes.
- Production is expected to start in 2027, according to Time Out.
- Pattinson can next be seen in Dune: Part Three in December and the Netflix thriller The Take in January.

The Denis Villeneuve Bond decision is now explained by the director himself, who says [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was picked as the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) because he combines the character's timeless quality with something new. The statement followed Amazon MGM Studios' announcement on 16 November, as reported by The Independent.

## What Villeneuve said about Pattinson

Villeneuve directed Pattinson in the upcoming Dune: Part Three, so this is a reunion. In his statement he first thanked the actors who had been part of the search. "We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007," he said, according to [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/denis-villeneuve-robert-pattinson-james-bond-b3085895.html).

He then turned to his pick. "He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin," Villeneuve said.

Pattinson called the job "the honour of a lifetime" and "a dream come true", according to [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/news/its-official-robert-pattinson-is-the-new-james-bond-111626). He thanked Villeneuve, producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman, and the team at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in him.

## Who else was in the running

The shortlist was long. Time Out says Pattinson arrived as a serious candidate only in the last week, passing earlier favourites Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner. [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/analysis/robert-pattinson-james-bond-risky-casting-denis-villeneuve-1235228305/) adds Nicholas Hoult to the finalists and reports that Amazon was keen on Jacob Elordi before the search began last May.

That report also frames the choice as a gamble. Many past Bonds were relative unknowns when cast, whereas Pattinson has been famous for about two decades and already fronts The Batman films. IndieWire argues that Villeneuve's comfort working with him and his track record at the box office both counted in his favour. The outlet's industry sources put his likely fee well above that of a cheaper candidate, but the figures are speculation, not confirmed numbers.

## A new era for the franchise

Bond 26 is the first film made without the Broccoli family in control. [Nexus Point News](https://nexuspointnews.com/robert-pattinson-is-set-to-star-as-james-bond-in-denis-villeneuves-bond-26/) notes that Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli remain co-owners, while creative rights moved to Amazon MGM in a 2025 deal.

Villeneuve was announced as director that same year, calling himself a "die-hard James Bond fan" who wants to "honour the tradition". Steven Knight, creator of Peaky Blinders, is writing the script. Pattinson takes over from [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), who first played Bond in 2006's Casino Royale.

Time Out points out that Pattinson, born and raised in Barnes in London, is the third Englishman in the role after Craig and Roger Moore. For readers following the director's other work, our earlier piece covers [Villeneuve's reaction to the Bond talk](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-pattinson/) before the announcement.

## What happens next

Time Out reports that production is expected to begin in 2027. Before that, Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three reaches cinemas in December, and our report on [the finished cut of Dune Part Three](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-dune-part-three/) explains where that film stands. Pattinson is also reported to be filming The Batman: Part 2. You can find everything we publish about the director on our [Denis Villeneuve tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/denis-villeneuve/) and in the [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

No release date for Bond 26 has been given in the sources we reviewed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why did Denis Villeneuve choose Robert Pattinson as James Bond?

Villeneuve says Pattinson "has the timeless soul of 007" and brings "a spirit that is completely new". The pair have also worked together on Dune: Part Three.

### Who is writing Bond 26?

Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders, is writing the film. Villeneuve is directing.

### When will Bond 26 start filming?

Time Out reports that production is expected to start in 2027. No release date has been announced in the coverage we read.

### Who else was considered for the role?

Reported contenders included Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Nicholas Hoult, according to Time Out and IndieWire.
