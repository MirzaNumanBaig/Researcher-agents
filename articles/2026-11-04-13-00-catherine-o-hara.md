- [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara), the Emmy-winning comedy actress, has died aged 71, according to ABC News.
- Her manager confirmed her death. The cause has not been made public.
- The Los Angeles Fire Department told ABC News it took a medical aid call connected to an address linked to her and that she was taken to hospital in serious condition.
- She is best known for *Schitt's Creek*, *Beetlejuice* and *Home Alone*, and leaves behind more than 100 screen roles.

Catherine O'Hara, the Canadian-born comedy actress who played Moira Rose in *Schitt's Creek* and Kate McCallister in *Home Alone*, has died at 71. Her manager confirmed the news, [ABC News reported](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/actress-catherine-ohara-dies-71/story?id=129711879). Tributes are expected to follow from a career that covered sketch comedy, blockbuster films and prestige television.

## What is known about her death

ABC News reports that O'Hara's cause of death is still unknown. The Los Angeles Fire Department confirmed to the network that it received a call for medical aid at 4:48 a.m. on the day of her death, at an address connected to her. She was taken to hospital in serious condition, according to the LAFD.

Beyond that, no further details have been confirmed. Until her family or representatives say more, it is best to treat anything else circulating online as unverified.

## From SCTV to a career of more than 100 roles

Catherine Anne O'Hara was born in Toronto on 4 March 1954, the sixth of seven children. Her younger sister is the singer Mary Margaret O'Hara. She began her screen career on the Canadian sketch show [Second City Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_City_Television) in 1974, after first auditioning two years earlier. She replaced [Gilda Radner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilda_Radner) when Radner left the programme, according to ABC News.

On the show she impersonated [Meryl Streep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meryl_Streep), [Brooke Shields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooke_Shields) and [Lucille Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucille_Ball), and created her own characters, including Lola Heatherton and Dusty Towne. She also wrote for the series, which brought her a first Primetime Emmy, for writing, in 1982. It was also where she first worked with [Eugene Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Levy), a collaborator for decades afterwards, and with [Joe Flaherty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Flaherty).

## Beetlejuice, Home Alone and the voice work

Her film breakthrough came in 1988 as the overdramatic artist Delia in *Beetlejuice*, opposite [Michael Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Keaton) and [Winona Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winona_Ryder). Two years later she played the mother of [Macaulay Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaulay_Culkin)'s character in [Home Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone), a warmer role that showed a different side of her.

She was also in demand as a voice actress. ABC News lists Sally in Tim Burton's *The Nightmare Before Christmas* (1993), Tina in *Chicken Little* (2005), Mrs Walters in *Monster House* (2006) and Mrs Frankenstein in *Frankenweenie* (2012). Her other credits include *Best in Show* with Levy, the series *30 Rock* and [Curb Your Enthusiasm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curb_Your_Enthusiasm), and Christopher Guest's *A Mighty Wind* and *For Your Consideration*.

## Moira Rose and a late-career peak

Her best-known later role was Moira Rose, the grandiose, wonderfully detached actress in [Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek). The series also starred Levy, [Dan Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Levy_(Canadian_actor)) and [Annie Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Murphy). The part earned O'Hara a Primetime Emmy in 2020 and a Golden Globe in 2021.

Her recent work kept the awards coming. ABC News notes Emmy nominations for her guest turn as Gail Lynden in HBO's *The Last of Us* in 2025 and for her supporting role as Patty Leigh in *The Studio*. In 2024 she returned as Delia in *Beetlejuice, Beetlejuice*, again alongside Keaton and Ryder, with Jenna Ortega joining the cast. She had been due back for the second season of *The Studio*.

In a 2024 interview with Elle Canada, quoted by ABC News, she urged people turning 70 to imagine they had at least 20 more years ahead and to ask what they would do with them. "You look forward," she said.

She is survived by her husband, the Oscar-nominated production designer Bo Welch, whom she married in 1992, according to People as cited by ABC News. The pair met on the set of *Beetlejuice*.
