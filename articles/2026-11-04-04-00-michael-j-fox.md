**Quick summary**

- Christopher Lloyd, 88, has spoken about working with [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) on *Back to the Future*, in an interview published on 3 November 2026.
- Fox replaced Eric Stoltz about six weeks into filming, which Lloyd called a "stunning announcement".

Christopher Lloyd has described the moment he knew his *Back to the Future* partnership with Michael J. Fox would work. In an interview published on 3 November 2026 and carried by Yahoo News New Zealand, originally from PEOPLE, the 88-year-old said Doc and Marty "were born" once Fox joined the set.

## The late recasting that worried Lloyd

According to the report, Fox, now 65, took over the role of Marty McFly from Eric Stoltz about six weeks into production. Lloyd recalled that director Robert Zemeckis, co-writer Bob Gale and producer Steven Spielberg gathered the cast and crew late one night to announce the change.

Lloyd called it a "stunning announcement". He explained that the news concerned him because the scenes he had already shot with the earlier actor would have to be done again, six weeks of work in all. Fox was also still busy with *Family Ties* at the time, according to the report.

## What changed when Fox arrived

Lloyd told the outlet that his concerns faded once Fox started work. He pointed to Fox's energy, spontaneity and sense of humour as the qualities that made the partnership click, and described it as though Doc and Marty came into being on set.

That bond, he said, has lasted. The 1985 film went on to be the highest-grossing release of its year, and sequels followed in 1989 and 1990, so the pairing Lloyd describes became one of the best-known in popular film.

## Reunions and a new book

The two actors have shared a stage at fan conventions over the years. Lloyd said they bring the same energy to joint signings and added that he hopes they can do more together.

The interview comes while Lloyd's memoir, *In My Own Time*, is out. In it he jokes about turning 88, the same speed Doc's DeLorean needs to travel through time. Fox marked Lloyd's birthday with an Instagram post that referenced the films, the report said.
