**Quick summary**

- Disney has announced a theatrical re-release of Christopher Nolan's *The Prestige* for its 20th anniversary, according to [Hypebeast](https://hypebeast.com/2026/10/christopher-nolan-the-prestige-sets-re-release-first-ever-imax-70mm-screenings-christian-bale-hugh-jackman-news).
- It will be the first time the 2006 film is shown on 70mm and IMAX screens.
- The re-release date is 29 January 2027.
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Christian Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bale) play the two rival magicians at the centre of the story.

Disney will bring *The Prestige* back to cinemas on 29 January 2027, and for the first time audiences will be able to see it in 70mm and IMAX. The announcement marks 20 years since the film first opened on 20 October 2006, and gives fans of Hugh Jackman's turn as a Victorian-era stage magician a new way to see it on the big screen.

## What Disney has announced

According to Hypebeast, the anniversary run is the first time the film has been presented in either large format. The outlet describes it as the first time the thriller has played on 70mm and IMAX screens, which is a notable step for a film that originally reached cinemas in a standard release.

Hypebeast also places the re-release in the context of Nolan's recent large-format successes. It points to record-setting box-office milestones for *Oppenheimer* and *The Odyssey*, and the report links to a separate item on *The Odyssey* extending its IMAX 70mm run. The report does not give ticket prices, cinema lists or a release plan for Australia, so local dates are still to be confirmed.

## The story and the cast

*The Prestige* follows the obsessive, decades-long rivalry between two stage magicians in Victorian times, played by Jackman and Bale. The supporting cast includes [Michael Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Caine), [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson), [Rebecca Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Hall), [Andy Serkis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Serkis) and [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie).

Behind the camera, Christopher Nolan directed and co-wrote the screenplay with his brother, [Jonathan Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Nolan). Hypebeast lists [Emma Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thomas) and [Aaron Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Ryder) as producers alongside Nolan.

## How the film performed

Hypebeast reports that the film earned roughly US$100 million at the global box office and received two Academy Award nominations. That is a solid result for a period mystery, though the outlet frames the re-release less as a revival of a flop and more as a celebration of a film that has kept its audience across two decades.

## What happens next

The next confirmed date is 29 January 2027, when the re-release is due to open. Details on which cinemas will carry the 70mm and IMAX prints have not been published in the report we read, and nothing has been announced for Australian screens.
