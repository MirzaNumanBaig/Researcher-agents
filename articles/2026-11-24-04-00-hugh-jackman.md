**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and ex-wife [Deborra-Lee Furness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborra-Lee_Furness) have a buyer for their West Village triplex, according to Realtor.com.
- The five-bedroom home is listed at US$28.75 million and has entered contract.
- The price the couple accepted has not been made public.
- They bought the apartment for US$21 million in 2008.

The Hugh Jackman home sale looks close to done. The actor and Furness, his former wife, have reportedly found a buyer for the New York triplex they bought in 2008, with the property now in contract, [Realtor.com reported](https://www.realtor.com/news/celebrity-real-estate/hugh-jackman-ex-wife-deborra-lee-furness-sell-new-york-city-martial-home/) on 23 November. The Real Deal ran a matching report the same day, headlined that Jackman had inked a deal to sell the home with an asking price of about US$29 million.

## A long road to a sale

Realtor.com says the pair paid US$21 million for the apartment in 2008. They first listed it in June 2022 at US$38.9 million, more than a year before they confirmed their split, but it came off the market about two months later.

They then turned it into a rental, asking US$125,000 a month in September 2023, around the time they announced their separation. Realtor.com's earlier sources said a tenant had been due to stay until December 2026, yet the home returned to the market on 31 March at US$28.75 million.

The listing makes no mention of its famous owners. It does note "very limited showings", which Realtor.com reads as a sign the sellers want to keep things private. Property records still show the unit registered to the limited liability company the couple used to buy it, the same company behind most of the homes they owned during the marriage.

## Inside the Perry Street apartment

The home sits in a building on Perry Street designed by architect [Richard Meier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Meier). The listing calls it a "modernist masterpiece". It spans exactly 11,000 square feet over three floors joined by a spiral staircase, with a double-height living room and walls of windows facing the Hudson River.

The lower level has a recreation room, a terrace and a library that can double as a guest room. Four of the five bedrooms are there, each with an en-suite bathroom. Above that sit the great room, den, professional-grade kitchen and a separate dining room. The whole top floor is the primary suite, with a spa-style bathroom, sauna, gym, two dressing rooms and a private terrace.

Residents also get a fitness centre and access to chef Jean-Georges Vongerichten's Perry St restaurant, which is in the building and offers in-house dining.

## What happened to their other homes

As part of the divorce agreement, the couple divided their other properties, according to Realtor.com. A penthouse on Eleventh Avenue, bought for US$21.12 million in 2022, went to a company linked only to Furness for around US$23 million in value, at roughly half price. Realtor.com suggests that points to her buying out Jackman's half. It is not clear whether the two will share the proceeds of this sale.

The report notes that Jackman, 58, is in a relationship with his former Broadway co-star [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster), 51. Dating rumours began during their run in The Music Man, which started previews in December 2021, and the couple confirmed the relationship after the Jackman and Furness split in September 2023.

## What happens next

The deal is in contract, not closed. The final sale price should appear in public property records once the sale completes. Until then, the US$28.75 million asking price is the only figure on record.
