**Quick summary**

- A new animated short, *Sonic the Hedgehog: Memories and Beyond*, was released on 12 November 2026 for the franchise's 35th anniversary.
- The short teases Metal Sonic, who is also set to join the live-action films, according to ComingSoon.net.
- [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey) is expected to return as Dr. Robotnik, also called Dr. Eggman, in *Sonic the Hedgehog 4*.
- The film is scheduled for cinemas on 19 March 2027.

A new Sonic short has landed online, and it points straight at the next live-action film. *Sonic the Hedgehog: Memories and Beyond* arrived on 12 November 2026 and sets up Metal Sonic as a threat, while Jim Carrey is expected to return as Dr. Robotnik in *Sonic the Hedgehog 4*, according to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2222151-sonic-the-hedgehog-new-movie-watch-now-jim-carrey-dr-eggman-return).

## What the new Sonic short is about

The short is part of celebrations marking 35 years since the original *Sonic the Hedgehog* game appeared in 1991. It can be watched on the franchise's official X account and other official channels.

The official synopsis, as quoted by ComingSoon.net, says Dr. Eggman plans to upgrade Metal Sonic into the "ultimate fighting machine" by stealing Sonic's life-data and using the power of the Chaos Emeralds. Sonic and his friends have to stop the supercharged robot.

Behind the scenes, the story comes from Sonic Team head Takashi Iizuka. The script is by longtime franchise writer [Ian Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Flynn). It is another collaboration between SEGA and the animation studio GXS Productions.

## Where Jim Carrey fits in

Carrey plays the villain Dr. Robotnik, known as Dr. Eggman in the games, in the live-action series. ComingSoon.net reports he is expected to reprise the role in the fourth film. The wording matters: the outlet says "expected", so treat his return as likely rather than formally confirmed by the studio in the sources we reviewed.

The short and the film share a link. Metal Sonic is also set to make his live-action debut in *Sonic the Hedgehog 4*, which makes the short a neat preview of the villain's next move.

## The cast and crew for Sonic the Hedgehog 4

[Jeff Fowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Fowler), who directed the first three films, is back in the director's chair. According to ComingSoon.net, the returning cast includes:

- [Ben Schwartz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Schwartz) as Sonic
- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) as Shadow
- [Idris Elba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idris_Elba) as Knuckles
- Colleen O'Shaughnessey as Tails
- [James Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Marsden) as Tom
- [Tika Sumpter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tika_Sumpter) as Maddie

[Kristen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Bell) joins the franchise as Amy Rose.
