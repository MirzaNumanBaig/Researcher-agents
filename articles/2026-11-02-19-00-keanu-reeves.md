**Quick summary**

- The original Matrix trilogy became available on Pluto TV, a free streaming service, from 1 November, according to Comic Book Resources.
- The three films are The Matrix, The Matrix Reloaded and The Matrix Revolutions.
- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) plays Neo in all three.
- A fifth film, to be led by Drew Goddard, is still in development. No release date has been given.

The Keanu Reeves Matrix trilogy has a new free home. As of 1 November, The Matrix, The Matrix Reloaded and The Matrix Revolutions are all streaming on Pluto TV at no cost to viewers, [Comic Book Resources reported](https://www.cbr.com/keanu-reeves-the-matrix-trilogy-pluto-tv-november-2026/). The report does not say which countries the titles are available in, so Australian viewers should check the service's local lineup before planning a marathon.

## What is now streaming

The package covers the full original run. The first film arrived in 1999, and its two sequels, Reloaded and Revolutions, were both released in 2003. Comic Book Resources describes the first film as the most important sci-fi trilogy for Millennial audiences, and the free placement means anyone who has not seen it, or has not seen it in years, can now do so without a subscription.

The 2021 follow-up, The Matrix Resurrections, is not mentioned as part of the Pluto TV listing in the report.

## Why the first film still matters

The Matrix was written and directed by Lana and Lilly Wachowski, known together as [the Wachowskis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wachowskis). Reeves plays Thomas Anderson, a programmer and hacker better known as Neo, who is drawn into a fight over the future of humankind. [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) plays Trinity, the hacker who first approaches him, and Laurence Fishburne plays Morpheus.

According to Comic Book Resources, the film's use of sequential still shots to create "bullet time" effects helped popularise techniques that have since become standard in cyberpunk and sci-fi noir storytelling.

The numbers back up that reputation. The film grossed more than US$473 million at the box office against a budget of US$63 million, which the outlet says was enough to secure both sequels. For context, that is roughly 7.5 times its production cost.

## The sequels and the later film

Comic Book Resources notes that neither Reloaded nor Revolutions matched the success or affection earned by the original. The franchise carried on anyway, through tie-in media and then The Matrix Resurrections in 2021.

That film drew generally favourable responses from critics and audiences, according to the outlet, but it struggled commercially. It took US$160.2 million worldwide against a US$190 million production budget, which means it earned less than it cost to make.

## What happens next

A fifth Matrix film was announced in 2024, and Comic Book Resources says it was reiterated earlier this year that the project is "still in the works". [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard) is set to direct. While promoting [Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary) earlier this year, he said he was in his "writing cave" and would share news when he came out. The report does not confirm a cast, a start of production or a release date, and it does not say whether Reeves will return.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I watch The Matrix trilogy for free?
The three original films are streaming on Pluto TV as of 1 November, according to Comic Book Resources. Availability can differ by country, so check the service in your region.

### Is The Matrix Resurrections included?
The report only lists The Matrix, The Matrix Reloaded and The Matrix Revolutions as joining Pluto TV.

Yes, it is in development. It was announced in 2024 and Drew Goddard is attached as director, but no release date has been confirmed.
