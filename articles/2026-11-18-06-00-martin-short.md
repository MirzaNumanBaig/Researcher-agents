**Quick summary**

- A new trailer for Season 6 of *Only Murders in the Building* shows the trio's sleuthing moving to London.
- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short), [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) and [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) return to investigate the murder of Tina Fey's character, Cindy Canning.
- The season arrives on Dec. 8 with a two-episode premiere on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+, according to ABC7.
- Guest stars include Martin Freeman, David Tennant and Olivia Colman.

[Martin Short](https://moduscarry.com/tag/martin-short/) and his co-stars are crossing the Atlantic. The new trailer for Season 6 of [*Only Murders in the Building*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building) shows Short, Steve Martin and Selena Gomez chasing a killer in London, and the Emmy-nominated comedy returns on Dec. 8, [ABC7 Bay Area reported](https://abc7news.com/post/murders-building-season-6-trailer-selena-gomez-steve-martin-short-head-london/20046434/).

## Why the trio is in London

According to ABC7, the new season picks up from the Season 5 cliffhanger. That ending leaves the amateur detectives following the trail of Cindy Canning's killer overseas. Canning is the podcasting character played by Tina Fey, and the trio reunite to solve her murder as the case takes an international turn.

The setting is a clear change for a show that has been built around one New York apartment building. The trailer leans into that, promising what ABC7 calls a mystery full of suspicious suspects and delightfully eccentric British characters.

## The guest cast

The guest list is long. ABC7 names Martin Freeman, David Tennant, Olivia Colman, Richard E. Grant, Jodie Whittaker, Simone Ashley, Matthew Beard, Jim Broadbent, Peter Capaldi, Nicola Coughlan and Jamie Demetriou, "among others".

Several familiar faces are also back. Tina Fey, Meryl Streep, Michael Cyril Creighton and Da'Vine Joy Randolph will reprise their roles, per the same report. That mix of returning regulars and British newcomers suggests the production wants London to feel like a full reboot of the mystery, not a side trip.

## Where and when to watch

The two-episode premiere lands on Dec. 8 on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+. ABC7 notes that The Walt Disney Company is the parent of Hulu, Disney+ and the station itself. Indian entertainment site Pinkvilla also ran a viewing guide for the new season, but its page text could not be reliably read, so details such as release times in other regions are not confirmed here. Australian viewers should check their local Disney+ listing for the date in this country.

## What happens next

The trailer is out now, and the premiere follows on Dec. 8. Until then, the main open question is who killed Cindy Canning, and which of the British guest stars is hiding the answer.
