**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) is the face of Sephora US and Canada's 2026 holiday campaign, "We All Sephora", according to Cosmetics Business.
- The campaign was teased last week with a "Sethphora" shopfront stunt at Sephora's The Grove store in Los Angeles.
- Separately, his comedy The Invite has reached No. 1 on HBO Max's US top 10 movies chart, per FlixPatrol.
- The film reportedly cost $15 million and took $61 million worldwide, according to Box Office Mojo.

Seth Rogen has a busy week on two fronts. Sephora has named the 44-year-old its holiday campaign star for 2026, and The Invite, the A24 comedy he made with Olivia Wilde, has climbed to the top of the HBO Max movie chart in the United States.

## Sephora turns Rogen into a Christmas shopper

[Cosmetics Business](https://www.cosmeticsbusiness.com/sephora-unveils-seth-rogen-as-2026-holiday-campaign-star) reports that the LVMH-owned retailer has built its "We All Sephora" Christmas campaign around the Canadian actor, writer and producer. The idea is to make beauty gifting "more approachable, personal and fun", and to argue that it is "truly for everyone".

In the ads, Rogen plays an unlikely beauty shopper hunting for the right Christmas presents. With help from a Sephora Beauty Advisor, he works through the aisles in his usual comic style and picks out gifts for family and friends.

Sam Bennett, Sephora's SVP of Brand, Creative, Experience & Insights, told the trade title: "At Sephora, we believe the best gifts are the ones that make people feel seen." The publication describes Rogen as best known for Superbad and [Knocked Up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knocked_Up).

## The "Sethphora" stunt in his home city

The partnership was hinted at a week before the announcement. Sephora hung a "Sethphora" facade, a mash-up of the retailer's name and his first name, on the front of its The Grove store in Los Angeles, which is Rogen's home city, Cosmetics Business says. It was a guerrilla-style tease rather than a formal launch, and the full campaign followed days later.

Only an extract of the Cosmetics Business article is public, so details such as the campaign's run dates, channels and any in-store tie-ins were not available to us.

## The Invite tops the HBO Max chart

On the film side, AOL's report, which was first published by Mandatory, says The Invite is now No. 1 on HBO Max's US top 10 movies list, according to FlixPatrol. It sits ahead of The Death of Robin Hood, The Housemaid, Backrooms, Freddy vs. Jason, Coraline and Corpse Bride.

Wilde directs and plays Angela. Rogen is her husband, Joe, Penélope Cruz plays Piña and Edward Norton plays Hawk. The story starts with a shaky marriage: Joe and Angela invite their upstairs neighbours for dinner, and an ordinary evening turns awkward and boundary-pushing. Rashida Jones and Will McCormack wrote the screenplay, drawing on Spanish director Cesc Gay's film Sentimental.

## How the film performed

The numbers cited by AOL are solid for a mid-budget comedy. Rotten Tomatoes gives The Invite a 96% critics' score and an 89% audience score, and IMDb users rate it 7.4 out of 10. On the money side, a reported $15 million budget turned into $61 million worldwide at the box office, according to Box Office Mojo. That is roughly four times the reported cost.

A24 released the film in cinemas alongside Annapurna Pictures, FilmNation Entertainment and Permut Presentations. David Permut, Ben Browning and Megan Ellison are the producers.

A third item in this week's coverage, an IMDb piece on Rogen's views about the animated film Tangles, could not be read in full, so it is not covered here.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Seth Rogen's Sephora campaign called?
It is called "We All Sephora". Cosmetics Business says it is Sephora US and Canada's 2026 holiday campaign and casts Rogen as a shopper looking for Christmas gifts.

### What was the "Sethphora" stunt?
Sephora hung a "Sethphora" facade on its The Grove store in Los Angeles the week before the campaign was announced. It combined the retailer's name with Rogen's first name.

### Where can I watch The Invite?
According to FlixPatrol figures cited by AOL, The Invite is streaming on HBO Max in the United States, where it is currently No. 1 on the movies chart. Availability in Australia was not covered in the sources.

### Who stars in The Invite?
Seth Rogen and Olivia Wilde play the married couple, with Penélope Cruz and Edward Norton as their neighbours. Wilde also directed.
