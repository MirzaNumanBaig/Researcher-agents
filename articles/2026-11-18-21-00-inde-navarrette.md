**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), 25, says her *Obsession* character Nikki Freeman "comforted" her.
- She made the comments to E! News, as reported by Yahoo Style Canada on 18 November 2026.
- She also told Complex she struggled to find work after the film, walking dogs and streaming to pay rent.
- Yahoo reports she has been cast in the new X-Men movie.

[Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) says the character who made her name has given something back. The 25-year-old actress told E! News that playing Nikki Freeman in the supernatural horror film [*Obsession*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsession_(2025_film)) left her feeling "comforted", according to a report published by [Yahoo Style Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/inde-navarrette-feels-comforted-obsession-100000177.html) on 18 November.

## Why Nikki Freeman stayed with her

Navarrette opened with a line she attributed to a familiar phrase: "Art should comfort the disturbed and should disturb the comforted." Nikki, she said, did the first part for her.

In her telling, the character gave her room to show things women are often not allowed to show. She called it an honour to do that on a film. She believes Nikki will stay connected to her "forever".

She also spoke about trust. Playing Nikki, she said, meant trusting herself, and she argued that people are "capable of so much more" than they think and mostly need the opportunity.

## The hard stretch after the film

The same report revisits comments Navarrette made earlier to Complex about what came after *Obsession*. She said she auditioned constantly, but "nothing was sticking".

To cover rent she walked dogs and streamed. Streaming, she said, grew out of playing video games alone in her room and wondering why she did not share it. She still likes the idea of it, but says she no longer has the time.

She also remembered the Zoom audition for Nikki as difficult. She told the outlet she was in a place where she felt shut off from her own emotions, and that the role demands all of them.
