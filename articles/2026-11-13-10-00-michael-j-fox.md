**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) has described his surprise guitar cameo with Coldplay at Glastonbury in an interview with Esquire, reported by Guitar World.
- He says a kick he threw in rehearsal, and repeated on stage, was timed by the crew to fireworks and explosions.
- He told Esquire that 300,000 people "went nuts" at the moment.
- Separately, Fox has spoken about joining Harrison Ford on the Apple TV series Shrinking, his first acting role since 2020.

## What Fox said about the Glastonbury moment

Michael J. Fox has given his own account of the night he walked out to play guitar with Coldplay at Glastonbury, and it turns on a single kick. According to [Guitar World](https://www.guitarworld.com/artists/guitarists/michael-j-fox-on-his-surprise-glastonbury-guitar-cameo-with-coldplay), which quoted his interview with Esquire, the actor says a well-timed kick can seal a show.

Fox was diagnosed with young-onset Parkinson's at 29. He told Esquire he could not properly hold the guitar and found it hard to play. What carried the moment, in his telling, was movement rather than technique.

## The kick, the fireworks and the crowd

Fox said the song changes tempo at one point, and he had been kicking there in rehearsal without planning it. He described it as an expression of the music. He did it a few times in rehearsal, then again in front of the festival crowd.

"I kick, and fireworks went off and explosions," he said, as quoted by Guitar World. The production team had watched the rehearsal and timed the effects to his kick, but he had not realised. "It scared the hell out of me," he said. He put the crowd at 300,000, and said they "went nuts".

## How the cameo came together

Guitar World reports that the appearance came during Coldplay's record-breaking fifth headline set at Glastonbury in 2024. Fox, carrying a peach and pink [Fender Stratocaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fender_Stratocaster), joined the band on Humankind, a 2021 track. He then layered his playing over guitarist Jonny Buckland's lead line on Fix You.

The outlet also says Fox inspired the members of Coldplay to form a band, a link that makes the cameo something of a full-circle moment. It was not his first time on stage with them. In 2016 he appeared at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey with a [Les Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Paul) guitar and played [Johnny B. Goode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_B._Goode) and Earth Angel, a nod to his Back to the Future character Marty McFly. Martin called that night a "dream come true", the outlet notes.

Last year, Guitar World adds, he played alongside [Jackson Browne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackson_Browne) at a charity gala.

## A busy stretch for Fox

The Glastonbury recollections arrive as Fox is also discussing his return to acting. Parade, in a story republished by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/michael-j-fox-asks-why-115040000.html), reports that Fox was so moved by Harrison Ford's portrayal of Parkinson's disease on Shrinking that he called co-creator [Bill Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Lawrence_(TV_producer)), his former boss on [Spin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_City), to ask why he was not on the show.

He told the Los Angeles Times he put it to Lawrence bluntly. The result was a guest role in the Season 3 premiere, which aired on January 28, and his first acting job since 2020. The series stars [Jason Segel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Segel) as a grieving therapist. Ford plays his mentor, Dr. Paul Rhoades, who meets Fox's character, another patient with Parkinson's, at the doctor's office. The two become friends over the season.

Fox told Vanity Fair he recognised Parkinson's "in his eyes" in Ford's performance, and said it brought him to tears. Ford described Fox as "an extraordinarily powerful person". ## Frequently asked questions

### When did Michael J. Fox play with Coldplay at Glastonbury?

Guitar World places the appearance in 2024, during Coldplay's fifth headline performance at the festival. Fox played on Humankind and Fix You.

### What did Michael J. Fox say about the kick?

He told Esquire that he kicked in time with a tempo change, as he had in rehearsal, and fireworks and explosions went off. He said it scared him, and that 300,000 people went nuts.

### Has Michael J. Fox played with Coldplay before?

Yes. Guitar World says he joined the band at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey in 2016 for Johnny B. Goode and Earth Angel.

### Is Michael J. Fox on Shrinking?

Yes. He guest stars in the Season 3 premiere, which aired on January 28, as a patient who befriends Harrison Ford's character.
