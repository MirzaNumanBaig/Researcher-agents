**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) said on the "Talk Easy with Sam Fragoso" podcast in October that making [Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso) helped him through a period of struggling with thoughts of self-harm, according to The Blast.
- The Blast cites Variety as reporting his words: "Some form of self-harm. Flat out."
- Separately, Sudeikis told The Hollywood Reporter in July that he and former showrunner Bill Lawrence never yelled at each other.
- Jack Burditt, who runs the show's fourth season, has said Sudeikis has a reputation but is easy to work with.

Jason Sudeikis has spoken openly about how much Ted Lasso means to him. In an October episode of the "Talk Easy with Sam Fragoso" podcast, the actor said the Apple TV comedy helped him through a time when he was struggling with thoughts of self-harm, according to [The Blast](https://theblast.com/884551/jason-sudeikis-how-ted-lasso-saved-him/). Fresh coverage this week has also revisited older reports of tension behind the scenes.

## What Sudeikis said about Ted Lasso

Host Sam Fragoso asked Sudeikis to expand on an earlier remark that the series had saved him. Variety reported his answer, as relayed by The Blast: "Some form of self-harm. Flat out."

Sudeikis said the feelings were not new. He traced them to what he called his "confusing teenage years" and said they resurfaced after a long absence. The Blast, citing OK! Magazine, reports he described the admission as raw and felt shame about the thoughts. He also said he had once thought about becoming a drummer or a dancer as a way to express what he found hard to say.

He did not claim the show fixed everything. As The Blast describes it, he credited the work itself with carrying him emotionally, much as creative work had at other points in his life.

## Why it resonates with the show

Ted Lasso follows an American football coach hired to manage an English soccer club. The Blast notes that Ted is an optimistic, kind coach who is privately dealing with his own struggles, and that anxiety, vulnerability and therapy are part of the storytelling. Sudeikis created the series and stars in it, so the overlap with his own experience is hard to miss.

The same podcast touched on his split from [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde). The pair, who share two children, ended their relationship in 2020. Asked what the public had misunderstood, he answered, "a million little things," and suggested public accounts often leave out important detail.

## The Bill Lawrence tension claims

A second Blast report looks at the show's creative history. In a July interview with The Hollywood Reporter, Sudeikis addressed talk of friction with [Bill Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Lawrence_(TV_producer)), who helped develop the series and was its original showrunner. "Bill never yelled at me, and I never yelled at him," he said. He added that he would have preferred to "land the plane with the same people we took off with."

That does not rule out creative differences, and he did not say it did. Lawrence has given his own account. Speaking to The Hollywood Reporter in 2023, he said stepping back before season three was healthy because "Jason is Ted Lasso" and deserved to shape the ending. He also said he is not good at listening to others over his own opinion. Lawrence stayed linked to the show through his company, [Doozer Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doozer_Productions).

[Jack Burditt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Burditt), whose credits include 30 Rock and [Modern Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Family), took over as showrunner for season four. According to The Independent, as cited by The Blast, Lawrence called him for about 20 minutes before he started, with advice on how to handle the show and Sudeikis. Burditt said no follow-up calls happened. He also said that despite the reputation, "Jason's easy."

These are accounts from people involved, not confirmed disputes, and none of them describes a public falling-out.
