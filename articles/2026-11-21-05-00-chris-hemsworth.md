**Quick summary**

- A 16-year-old has been charged with seven offences over the break-in at [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s home in Broken Head, New South Wales.
- He was refused bail at his first appearance in children's court, according to TMZ, which cites NSW Police.
- Police say four suspects entered the property on 11 November and stole three vehicles, all since recovered.
- Officers are still looking for the three other suspects.

## The Chris Hemsworth home burglary: what police have confirmed

One of four suspects in the Chris Hemsworth home burglary is in custody. [TMZ reported on Friday](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/20/chris-hemsworth-home-burglary-suspect-arrested/) that New South Wales Police charged a 16-year-old on Thursday. His name has not been released because of his age.

The charges number seven. They include entering enclosed land without lawful excuse, being armed with intent to commit an indictable offence, and destroying or damaging property. The teenager was refused bail when he first appeared in children's court, TMZ said.

For readers following the case, this is the first arrest in an incident we have covered before, including the [earlier charge story](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-byron-bay-break-in-charge/).

## What happened on 11 November

According to the TMZ account of the police statement, officers were called on the morning of 11 November to an estate at Broken Head, near Byron Bay, that the actor shares with his wife, [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky). Hemsworth was reportedly not home. Pataky was, and she woke when the family's dogs began barking.

Police were told four people had entered the property and taken three vehicles. All three were later found by officers in separate localities: Coraki, Tabulam and Lennox Head.

## How the arrest came about

Police say they executed a search warrant at a home in Coraki on Wednesday. They reported finding "several items of interest", including electronics, clothing and vehicle keys. The 16-year-old was taken into custody the next day.

TMZ's report does not say what, if anything, ties the items to the Broken Head property, and no one has been convicted of anything. The charges are allegations that a court will now test.
