**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) stars in Sweetsick, a drama written and directed by [Alice Birch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Birch).
- [Spike Fearn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spike_Fearn) co-stars.
- Searchlight Pictures is backing the film, and no release date has been announced.
- Blanchett is also a producer on the project.

[Cate Blanchett](https://moduscarry.com/tag/cate-blanchett/) will play a woman with an unusual gift of perception in Sweetsick, a new drama from writer-director Alice Birch, with Spike Fearn alongside her. The film is backed by Searchlight Pictures and has no release date yet, according to a report from [Sortir à Paris](https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/what-to-do-in-paris/cinema-series/articles/358568-sweetsick-cate-blanchett-faces-a-troubling-gift-in-alice-birch-s-film).

## What Sweetsick is about

Blanchett's character can sense what other people need most deeply. According to Sortir à Paris, that ability often costs her personally. The story follows her on a journey home, and the film puts her relationship with other people's desires at the centre of that trip.

The outlet's description is brief, and no further plot detail was available in the coverage we read. Nothing about the setting, the period or the people she meets on the way has been confirmed, so it is too early to say how the premise plays out on screen.

## Who is making it

Alice Birch both wrote and directed the film. Spike Fearn is named as Blanchett's co-star. The report does not say which role he plays.

On the production side, the film comes from House Productions, [Dirty Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Films) and Lee Groombridge, with Film4 as executive producer. Searchlight Pictures is the studio behind it, and its press site is listed as the official source for the project.

## Blanchett behind the camera too

Blanchett is credited as a producer as well as the lead. She shares that role with [Tessa Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessa_Ross), [Juliette Howell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juliette_Howell), Theo Barrowclough and Lee Groombridge. That puts her in front of the camera and in the producing team at the same time, a pattern readers of our [Cate Blanchett coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/cate-blanchett/) will recognise. For another example of her work off screen, see our report on the [Displacement film fund and its five shorts](https://moduscarry.com/cate-blanchett-displacement-film-fund/).

## What happens next

No release date has been given, and Sortir à Paris does not say when filming starts or finishes. Searchlight Pictures' press site is the place where official announcements are expected.
