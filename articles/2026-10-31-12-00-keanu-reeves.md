**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has signed on to star in Netflix's action thriller Maelstrom, according to Comic Book Resources.
- He replaces Alan Ritchson, who was attached in July 2026 but had left by October.
- Jaume Collet-Serra, who directed Netflix's Carry On, is the director.
- The script was first sold in 1993 and sat unmade for more than 30 years.

Keanu Reeves, 62, is the new lead of Maelstrom, a Netflix action thriller built on a script that Hollywood bought in 1993 and then shelved for three decades. [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) (CBR) reported this week that Reeves took over the role after Alan Ritchson dropped out.

## A script pitched as Die Hard in a hurricane

Gregory Fitzpatrick, then a first-time screenwriter, sold Maelstrom in 1993. According to CBR, it was pitched as "[Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard) in a hurricane", a nod to the 1988 film that reshaped the genre by putting an ordinary man, rather than a bodybuilder, at the centre of the action.

The story follows a federal agent assigned to protect a woman who is due to testify against her husband, a powerful crime lord who has put a contract on her life. His men find her hideout just as a hurricane arrives and cuts off all communication, leaving the agent and the witness alone against a small army.

CBR sums it up as "John Wick meets Twister". That is the outlet's shorthand, not an official description from Netflix.

## From a 1990s bidding war to a long wait

Warner Bros. executive Jeff Robinov bought the script, with CBR putting the price between US$225,000 and US$625,000. The outlet says that is roughly US$500,000 to US$1.4 million in today's money. It was a sizeable sum for a debut script in an era when studios were hunting for the next Die Hard.

The report says stars such as [Clint Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Eastwood), [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) and [Steven Seagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Seagal) were considered at the time. The film was never greenlit, though, and the script stayed on a shelf for more than 30 years.

## How Netflix revived it

The turnaround came after Netflix had a large streaming hit with Carry On, an airport thriller starring [Taron Egerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taron_Egerton) that was based on a script about 11 years old. According to CBR, Netflix executive Ori Marmur then asked producers for the best high-concept action scripts that had been gathering dust.

Robinov had recently signed a deal with Netflix. When he brought Maelstrom back out, the project moved into production with Carry On director Jaume Collet-Serra. Alan Ritchson was linked to the lead in July 2026, but by October Reeves had replaced him. CBR does not say why Ritchson left.

## Why Reeves fits

CBR argues that Reeves has a strong claim to four of the great modern action films: [Point Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Break), Speed, The Matrix and John Wick. It also points out that Speed is, in effect, Die Hard on a bus, which makes a Die Hard-style thriller a natural next step for him. That is the outlet's opinion rather than a statement from the production.
