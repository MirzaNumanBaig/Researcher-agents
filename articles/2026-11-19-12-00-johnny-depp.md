**Quick summary**

- An unnamed industry insider has reportedly graded [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s return in *Ebenezer* a "C", according to CinemaBlend.
- The claim comes from writer [Rob Shuter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Shuter)'s Substack, and it has not been confirmed by any studio.
- CinemaBlend reports the film has made $43.4 million against a reported $38 million budget.
- The same source suggests a return as [Captain Jack Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Sparrow) is now harder to justify. CinemaBlend advises treating that with caution.

An unnamed insider says *Ebenezer* was a test for Johnny Depp and that he passed, but only just. The claim was published on 18 November 2026 by CinemaBlend, which was passing on remarks first printed on Shuter's Naughty But Nice Substack. It is a single anonymous opinion, not a studio verdict, and it should be read that way.

## What the insider said

According to CinemaBlend, the source called the film "Johnny's test" and said he "got a C". The reasoning, as reported, is that a studio planning to spend heavily on a franchise needs more than a middling result. A flop would have let everyone move on, the source argued, while these numbers leave Hollywood with a harder choice.

CinemaBlend itself flags the quotes as something to take "with a grain of salt". Neither the insider nor the studios behind the film are named, so none of it can be checked.

## The numbers behind the grade

*Ebenezer*, directed by Ti West, is a screen version of the Dickens story with Depp in the title role. CinemaBlend says it opened at the top of the box office last weekend. At the time of writing, it reports a gross of $43.4 million on a reported budget of $38 million.

That puts the film ahead of its cost on paper, but not by a wide margin, and well short of the returns of Depp's biggest hits. CinemaBlend notes the Pirates of the Caribbean films as the comparison. It also says Depp took a large pay cut to keep the budget low. Final results will depend on how the film holds up in the coming weeks.

## Depp's comeback in context

CinemaBlend describes *Ebenezer* as Depp's return to a major English-language production. He stepped back from Hollywood, and from the United States, to seek privacy after the verdict in his defamation case against [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard). His most recent screen role before this was in *Jeanne du Barry*, a French film made outside the Hollywood system.

He was also front and centre in the promotion, including a San Diego Comic-Con appearance dressed as Scrooge, the outlet says.

## What the source says about Pirates

The insider described a new Pirates film as "the ultimate prize" for Depp. They said Hollywood first needs proof that audiences still want to see him, and that nostalgia alone will not sell hundreds of millions of dollars in tickets. No studio has announced a new Pirates project with Depp in this reporting. For more on the film's opening, see our earlier look at [the mixed weekend verdict](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-weekend-verdict/).

## What happens next

The next signal is the film's second and third weekends, which will show whether the $43.4 million figure keeps climbing. Depp's next project has not been announced in the sources reviewed.
