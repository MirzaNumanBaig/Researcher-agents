## Johnny Depp's Ebenezer loses hundreds of screens

Two weeks after a chart-topping debut, [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s Scrooge film *Ebenezer* is being pulled from 365 US cinemas. According to a [Men's Journal report carried by Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/box-office-results/articles/johnny-depps-hit-movie-officially-050751320.html), the count drops from 3,545 to 3,180 heading into the film's third weekend.

Shrinking theatre counts are normal as a release ages. The report notes, though, that losing this many locations so soon after a wide release is a notable shift for a film that started at No. 1.

## The numbers so far

Directed by [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West), the film opened on 13 November. It took US$19 million in North America and US$42 million worldwide in its first weekend, which the report describes as Depp's first studio movie in eight years.

The second weekend was harder. Domestic takings fell 59% as the film faced *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, according to the same report. Even so, the worldwide total is approaching US$80 million by late November. Wikipedia's film entry lists a budget of US$38 million and a gross of about US$79.6 million, which is roughly twice what the film cost to make. That entry, however, does not yet give a verdict on its overall performance, and it is too early to call it a hit or a miss.

For context on the earlier reaction, see our post on [Ebenezer's budget and reviews](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-reviews/).

## Audiences warmer than critics

The gap between viewers and reviewers is wide. Men's Journal reports a 90% audience score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), based on more than 1,000 verified ratings, against a 55% critics' score. Metacritic lists 46 out of 100 from 38 critics, according to Wikipedia.

Depp plays Ebenezer Scrooge in a darker take on [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens)' *A Christmas Carol*. The cast includes [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint), [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough), [Sam Claflin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Claflin), [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) and [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen).

Critics were more divided than united. Peter Bradshaw of The Guardian gave three stars out of five and called it a serviceable, faithful adaptation in which Depp is "pretty good", according to Wikipedia's summary of reviews. The New York Times' Brandon Wu found it bland.

## A crowded holiday calendar

The pressure comes from the schedule rather than any single rival. *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opened the weekend after *Ebenezer*, and Disney's [Hexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexed_(2026_film)) and *Focker-in-Law* arrived on 25 November.

December is busier still. *Dune: Part Three* is due on 18 December, *The Angry Birds Movie 3* on 23 December and David Corenswet's *Mr. Irrelevant* on Christmas Day. Men's Journal says that competition could make it harder for *Ebenezer* to hold its cinema count, holiday theme and strong audience rating notwithstanding.

Depp has spent recent years mostly on independent and international projects, including touring with his rock band The Hollywood Vampires, so a No. 1 opening was a significant return. ## What happens next

The open question is whether *Ebenezer* can keep enough momentum to stay widely available through December, or whether its run keeps shrinking as new releases arrive. Its third-weekend takings, due in the coming days, will show how far the screen losses cut into its total.
