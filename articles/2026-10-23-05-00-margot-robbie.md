**Quick summary**

- Bustle says [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) was ahead of this fall's military jacket trend, wearing a gold-embroidered one in May.
- She wore it to the West End opening of *1536* in London, with black trousers, a tee, pointed-toe heels and a clutch.
- Separately, The Direct argues a new DC Studios Poison Ivy series could bring back her Harley Quinn.
- Robbie hasn't ruled out returning as Harley, according to The Direct. No casting has been announced.

Margot Robbie's spring red-carpet choice has turned into a fall fashion talking point. Bustle published its guide to military jackets on 22 October, and it credits Robbie with getting there first. On the same day, The Direct floated a very different Robbie story: a way back into the DC universe.

## The jacket Bustle says started it

Bustle describes the military jacket, also called a Napoleon or band jacket, as fall 2026's most dramatic outerwear trend. It's a fitted style with rows of metal or braided buttons, baroque embroidery and sometimes a high Mandarin collar. The outlet says it's a favourite of the late-2000s indie sleaze era, and that it returned on the Spring/Summer 2026 runways of McQueen, Burberry and Ann Demeulemeester.

Robbie wore her version in May, Bustle reports, to the West End opening of *1536* in London. The jacket had intricate gold embroidery. She kept the rest of the outfit plain, in black, so the jacket carried the look.

She isn't the only name in Bustle's roundup. It also points to [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones), who wore an ivory version on her *Sense and Sensibility* press tour, and [Jenna Ortega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Ortega), who wore a sleeveless military vest to Dior's Spring/Summer 2026 show. [Lola Tung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lola_Tung) is cited for a matching indigo denim set. Bustle ties the look back to the jackets of [Kate Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Moss)'s indie sleaze years.

## What Bustle suggests for wearing it

The advice is simple. Bustle's stylists say a statement jacket works best over basics: a tank and jeans, or black trousers like Robbie's. For a softer take they suggest ivory with a white tank and lighter denim, and for something relaxed, a denim version worn with jeans in a similar shade.

## The Harley Quinn question

The second story is opinion rather than an announcement, and it should be read that way. The Direct writes that DC Studios is developing another Batman-adjacent spinoff: a Poison Ivy series for HBO, set in the DCU rather than the Bat-Verse. Citing Deadline, it says *The Penguin* showrunner Lauren LeFranc is preparing to produce it. The Direct says no one has been cast in the title role.

From there, the outlet makes its case. Poison Ivy's closest ally in the comics, it notes, is Harley Quinn, and the two have been a couple in recent comic runs and in the animated *Harley Quinn* series. Robbie has been DC's main live-action Harley since 2016's *Suicide Squad*, according to The Direct. Lady Gaga played the role in *Joker: Folie à Deux*, which the outlet calls a one-off.

The Direct also notes that James Gunn, who directed Robbie in *The Suicide Squad*, hasn't yet found a place for her since taking over DC Studios. It says she hasn't ruled out a return. That's the extent of the sourced facts. Nobody at DC Studios or on Robbie's side is quoted in the piece as planning a Harley comeback.

## What happens next

Neither story has a firm date attached. The jacket trend is already on shelves for fall, and the Poison Ivy series is in early development, with its lead yet to be named. Any Harley Quinn appearance would need an official announcement from DC Studios.
