**Quick summary**

- Director Francis Lawrence told Collider that former DC leaders were "an impediment" to making Constantine 2, according to Startefacts.
- He says the delay was never down to Keanu Reeves or writer Akiva Goldsman.
- DC Studios bosses James Gunn and Peter Safran were ousted by parent company Skydance on 9 November.
- Lawrence says the sequel would need a lean budget, with Reeves and himself accepting lower fees.
- Separately, Francis Ford Coppola's old comments on Reeves' Dracula accent are back in the news.

The long-stalled sequel to Constantine may have a path forward. Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) said this week that the people who ran DC were the obstacle to making a follow-up starring [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves), and that their exit has given the team "renewed hope", according to reports of his interview with Collider.

## What Francis Lawrence said about Constantine 2

Lawrence was promoting Sunrise on the Reaping when Collider asked about the sequel. According to [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/constantine-s-director-finally-said-who-blocked-the-keanu-reeves-sequel), he put it plainly: "The people that were at DC were an impediment to us making it."

He said the problem was never Reeves or producer and screenwriter [Akiva Goldsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiva_Goldsman). J.J. Abrams is also involved, and the team has an idea and a script ready. What it lacks is a yes from Paramount, Warner Bros. or whoever now runs DC.

The [Toy People](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=116413) report adds that Lawrence did not want anyone to lose their job and was not celebrating. He said he would not object if DC introduced another version of the character inside its shared universe, provided his team could still make a sequel to its own version. He believes the two can coexist.

## Why the sequel has been stuck since 2022

Startefacts notes that Warner Bros. announced Constantine 2 in September 2022, with Reeves starring, Lawrence directing and Goldsman writing. Two months later, [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) and [Peter Safran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Safran) took over DC Studios, and the project never moved to a shoot.

Startefacts also points out that a separate Hellblazer film, with [Francis Galluppi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Galluppi) directing and no Reeves, was in development under Gunn's DC. In its reading, the studio wanted the character but not the version fans have asked about for years.

Skydance removed Gunn and Safran on 9 November, Toy People reports. Startefacts says Skydance then named Jeremy Latcham as DC Studios president and chief creative officer from 1 December. Latcham is a Marvel Studios veteran who also produced Guardians of the Galaxy alongside Gunn.

## The catch: money and the queue

Lawrence was realistic about the odds. According to Toy People, he acknowledged that the first film's middling box office means Constantine 2 will not get a blockbuster budget. He said both he and Reeves would have to accept less than their usual fees, and that the team has understood for about two decades that this could not be a $100 million film.

There is also a line of bigger titles ahead of it. Startefacts, citing TheWrap, says Latcham is expected to put Wonder Woman, Green Lantern and The Flash at the front of the queue. A sequel to an R-rated 2005 film about a chain-smoking exorcist is unlikely to be his first priority.

Still, as Startefacts frames it, the obstacle may now be the release calendar rather than the people in charge. That is a smaller problem than the one Lawrence described.

## Coppola's view of the Dracula accent

In other Reeves news, a Men's Journal piece republished by [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/francis-ford-coppola-says-keanu-183400000.html) revisits [Francis Ford Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Ford_Coppola)'s explanation of Reeves' English accent in the 1992 film Bram Stoker's Dracula.

In a 2015 interview with [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), Coppola said Reeves "tried so hard" to be perfect that the delivery came off as stilted. The article notes that Looper's updated ranking of vampire films still places the movie at No. 11, one spot below [Park Chan-wook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Park_Chan-wook)'s Thirst.

The film is also part of "The Horror Show" at the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures in Los Angeles. The exhibition opened on 26 September 2026 and runs until 25 July 2027, and it displays Gary Oldman's red armour alongside costume designer Eiko Ishioka's drawings.

## What happens next

Nothing is greenlit. Latcham starts on 1 December, and Lawrence says the next step is getting the studios involved to agree. Expect the sequel to depend on how the new DC chief orders his slate. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Constantine 2 officially happening?

No. Warner Bros. announced it in September 2022, but it has not entered production. Lawrence says it still needs approval from the studios involved.

### Who does Francis Lawrence say blocked Constantine 2?

He said the former DC leadership were "an impediment", according to Startefacts. He ruled out Keanu Reeves and Akiva Goldsman as the cause.

### Will Keanu Reeves return as John Constantine?

Reeves remains attached, according to the reports, and Lawrence says everyone on the creative team wants to make the film. Both would have to accept lower fees for a smaller budget.
