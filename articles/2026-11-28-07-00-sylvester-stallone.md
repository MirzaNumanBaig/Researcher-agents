**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) posted a video on Instagram on 21 November to thank fans for 50 years of Rocky.
- Rocky cost under US$1 million, grossed US$225 million worldwide and won three Oscars, including Best Picture, according to Yahoo.
- The anniversary coincides with I Play Rocky, a Peter Farrelly film about how Stallone got the original made.
- Separately, Stallone told Cigar Aficionado he would like Arnold Schwarzenegger to lead a Tulsa King "extension" series.

Sylvester Stallone has thanked fans for half a century of Rocky, posting an emotional video on Instagram on Saturday 21 November to mark 50 years since the film premiered in New York. The 80-year-old actor, who wrote the screenplay and played Rocky Balboa, said he never expected the day to come.

## What Stallone said about 50 years of Rocky

According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/sylvester-stallone-thanks-fans-50-234650861.html), Stallone said he only wanted to make "a little movie about the things that we're going through", and that the world had related to it far beyond his hopes. He thanked viewers on behalf of his family and signed off with a line in his character's spirit: always keep punching.

His caption went further. "Rocky may have started with me, but he belongs to all of YOU now," he wrote.

The numbers explain the affection. Yahoo reports, citing TIME, that Stallone finished the first draft in roughly three and a half days and turned down offers worth as much as US$360,000 because he insisted on playing the lead himself. The film was made for less than US$1 million and earned US$225 million worldwide, the highest total of any film in 1976. It collected 10 Oscar nominations and won three, and its legacy runs on through the Creed films.

## Why the film still lands

Writing for [HeadStuff](https://headstuff.org/entertainment/film/rocky-at-50-a-then-unknown-stallone-shines-out/), Charlie Kendellen argues that Rocky works best as a romance rather than a boxing picture. The quiet scenes, such as Rocky talking to his pet turtles or sharing time with Adrian, played by Talia Shire, are where the film shines, she writes.

HeadStuff also points out the company Rocky beat to the top of the 1976 box office, including Taxi Driver, Carrie, All the President's Men, Network and The Omen. It credits the film with paving the way for later underdog stories such as The Karate Kid, Flashdance and 8 Mile.

## I Play Rocky puts the backstory on screen

The anniversary lands alongside I Play Rocky, directed by Peter Farrelly of Green Book. The film follows the young Stallone as a writer and his fight to play the lead while producers tried to buy him out and hire an established star. Stallone had no hand in the production, according to Yahoo, but he approved of Anthony Ippolito's portrayal after watching the actor's audition tape. Farrelly quoted him as saying: "This kid didn't impersonate me. He became me."

Ippolito told blue News at the Zurich Film Festival that he studied Stallone's early films and television work closely, from the way he moves to his low, sometimes slurred delivery. blue News says the film is now in cinemas and streaming on blue Cinema.

## Philadelphia keeps celebrating

Philadelphia has made a season of it. Yahoo reports that the annual Rocky Run on 14 November drew about 40,000 participants, and the celebrations continue until 3 December, officially recognised as Rocky Day in the city.

## A Tulsa King idea for Schwarzenegger

Stallone is also looking ahead. The Playlist reports that, speaking to Cigar Aficionado (as relayed by JoBlo), he floated an "extension" of [Tulsa King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King) starring Arnold Schwarzenegger in his own show. "Since we're comrades in the fantasy world, our characters meet and discuss things, like we did in The Expendables," he said.

The idea is a pitch, not a commission. The Playlist notes that Stallone's own series is heading into a New York-set fifth season at Paramount+, and that a Samuel L. Jackson-led spinoff called Frisco King is planned for Texas.

Far Out Magazine, meanwhile, revisited a Stallone story from the Rambo years. He recalled being offered a US$34 million pay-or-play deal for a fourth Rambo film during the making of Rambo III, and replying that they should not jump the gun. The site says he later made Rambo in 2007 for a reported US$10 million.

## What happens next

Rocky Season in Philadelphia runs to Rocky Day on 3 December, and I Play Rocky is already showing. Nothing has been confirmed for a Schwarzenegger series, and no studio has responded to Stallone's comments in the sources we read. ## Frequently asked questions

### When did Rocky premiere?
Rocky premiered in New York City in 1976. Stallone's anniversary video was posted on 21 November 2026, marking 50 years.

### How much money did Rocky make?
It was made for less than US$1 million and grossed US$225 million worldwide, according to Yahoo and blue News.

### What is I Play Rocky?
It is a film directed by Peter Farrelly about Stallone writing Rocky and insisting on starring in it. Anthony Ippolito plays Stallone.

Not yet. Stallone said he would love it in a perfect world, but no project has been announced.
