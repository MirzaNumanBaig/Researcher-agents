**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) says [Michael Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Jackson) used to make prank calls to his hotel rooms.
- Crowe shared the memory on The Howard Stern Show and called the pranks "really dumb".
- He says he never worked out how Jackson found him, and suspects hotel staff tipped the singer off.
- Crowe admits he was a keen prank caller himself as a teenager.
- Lionsgate is reportedly developing a sequel to the Jackson biopic Michael.

Russell Crowe has revealed that Michael Jackson once made a habit of ringing him at hotels with prank calls. Speaking on The Howard Stern Show, the Oscar winner described the singer's jokes as "really dumb", according to a report syndicated by [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/really_dumb_russell_crowe_recalls_michael_jacksons_bizarre_prank_calls/s1_17785_44574056).

## How Michael Jackson found Crowe

The calls never came to a personal number. According to the report, Jackson rang whichever hotel Crowe happened to be staying in. Crowe said he never understood how the singer tracked him down.

His theory is that Jackson had friends among hotel staff who warned him whenever Crowe checked in. That is Crowe's own guess, and there is no confirmation of it in the coverage.

Stern asked whether these were simply silly, fake phone calls. Crowe said they were, and the report adds that his verdict on them was blunt.

## Crowe was a prankster too

Crowe was in no position to complain, by his own account. At 14 or 15, he has said, he used to impersonate radio presenters and phone strangers to tell them they had won a holiday in Fiji. Some of the people on the other end were so overcome that they burst into tears.

It is a lighter side of an actor best known for intense roles such as Maximus in Gladiator. The report says the story surfaced just after footage went viral of Crowe scolding autograph hunters outside his Paris hotel. He later defended how he handled that crowd.

## The Michael sequel

The report also ties the anecdote to renewed interest in Jackson on screen. Lionsgate is said to be developing a sequel to Michael, the biopic that starred Jackson's nephew Jaafar and ended during the Bad tour era.

According to the same report, the original film earned more than a billion dollars at the box office. Lionsgate chairman Adam Fogelson has said production could begin in late 2026 or early 2027, with a release aimed at late 2027 or the first half of 2028. He also said that musical sequences already filmed could be used in the sequel.

[Antoine Fuqua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_Fuqua) directed the first film, but his return has not been officially confirmed. Jaafar Jackson told Variety that he has no firm schedule yet but is "more than ready" to come back.

## What happens next

Crowe has not said whether the calls ever stopped or whether he took the bait. Nothing in the coverage suggests he will say more on the subject soon.
