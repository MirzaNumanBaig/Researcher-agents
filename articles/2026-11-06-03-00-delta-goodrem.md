**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) covered "DEEP DIVE", the song South Korea's JUNHEE is performing at Eurovision Song Contest Asia 2026.
- JUNHEE answered with his own take on Goodrem's "Eclipse", according to K-pop outlet allkpop.
- Goodrem finished 4th in the grand final of the 70th Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna with "Eclipse".
- The Asia final takes place at One Bangkok Forum in Bangkok on 14 November.

Australian singer-songwriter Delta Goodrem has swapped song covers with South Korea's JUNHEE, the A.C.E member who will represent his country at the Eurovision Song Contest Asia 2026 grand final in Bangkok on 14 November, [allkpop reports](https://www.allkpop.com/article/2026/11/aces-junhee-and-eurovision-star-delta-goodrem-swap-covers-of-each-others-songs-ahead-of-the-eurovision-song-contest-asia-2026-final).

## How the cover swap came about

Goodrem posted a video on the contest's official social media channels in which she sings "DEEP DIVE", the track JUNHEE is performing in the competition. According to allkpop, she took on the song after watching his competition stage, keeping the mood of the original while adding her own musical colour.

JUNHEE replied by covering "Eclipse", the self-written song Goodrem took to Eurovision. The outlet says fans responded warmly, with comments such as "Fantastic covers" and "I want to hear the full versions".

The exchange is a small gesture, but it links two artists from different countries and different Eurovision contests days before the Asian final.

## Who is Delta Goodrem in this story?

Goodrem is an Australian singer-songwriter and actress with a career of more than 20 years, known for hits such as "Innocent Eyes" and "[Born To Try](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_to_Try)", according to allkpop. She represented Australia at the 70th Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna, Austria, where "Eclipse" placed 4th in the grand final.

That result is the reason allkpop calls her a "Eurovision star" and the reason her video carries weight with the Asia contest's audience. A fourth-place finish in Vienna gives her a credible view of what a finalist faces on a big stage.

## JUNHEE's road to the final

JUNHEE earned the right to represent South Korea by taking first place in the national selection "Korea's Next Wave", allkpop says. He then released "DEEP DIVE" as an official single in response to global interest in the song.

He plans an upgraded performance to suit the larger venue of the final. "I'm preparing this performance with all my heart, carrying a sense of responsibility as an artist representing Korea rather than a simple mindset," JUNHEE said, as quoted by the outlet. He added that he will pour everything he has onto the stage so that everyone who chose him can be proud.

## What happens next

The Eurovision Song Contest Asia 2026 grand final is at One Bangkok Forum in Bangkok on 14 November. The broadcaster ENA will show it live from 10:30 pm KST, which is 8:30 pm local time in Thailand, according to allkpop.
