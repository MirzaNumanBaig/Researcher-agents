**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) told [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) that returning as Thor for *Avengers: Doomsday* felt "oddly uncomfortable at first".
- He said he now feels like one of the "elder statesmen" on a set full of newer faces.
- He described acting opposite [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) as "oddly unsettling, in the best way".
- MensXP reports the film opens on December 17 in India and a day later in international markets.

Chris Hemsworth has opened up about walking back onto a Marvel set for *Avengers: Doomsday*, and the Australian actor admits it did not feel the way he expected. Speaking to Entertainment Weekly this week, he said he felt out of place among so many unfamiliar faces, and that sharing scenes with Downey was strange in a way he quite enjoyed. The comments were reported on 25 November by [MensXP](https://www.mensxp.com/entertainment/hollywood/186957-chris-hemsworth-calls-working-with-rdj-again-oddly-unsettling-says-he-felt-like-odd-one-out-on-avengers-doomsday-set.html) and [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/english/news/2026/Nov/25/chris-hemsworth-on-returning-as-thor-was-oddly-uncomfortable-at-first).

## Why Hemsworth felt like the odd one out

The [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) has added a large number of new heroes in recent years, and the *Doomsday* ensemble reflects that. According to MensXP, Hemsworth said he genuinely did not know who many of his castmates were, and that he felt like the "odd one out".

The feeling mirrors what viewers have seen in the trailer. In it, Thor addresses the assembled heroes and says he does not recognise half of them. MensXP notes that behind the scenes the situation was much the same.

Hemsworth put it in terms of age and experience. "Being on set and now feeling like one of the elder statesmen, or one of the elders, was oddly uncomfortable at first," he said, as quoted by Cinema Express. He first played the character in the 2011 film *Thor*, which makes him one of the longest-serving original Avengers, the outlet points out.

## A conversation that changed his view

The discomfort did not last. Hemsworth said he talked it over with his co-star [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)) and with directors [Anthony Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers) and [Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers). That exchange left him seeing his place differently.

"What it led to is, well, no, we are experienced," he said. "We started this journey over a decade ago." Cinema Express reads this as Hemsworth recognising how much experience he and his fellow original cast members have built up across more than ten years in the franchise.

For a franchise that has been criticised by some fans for introducing too many characters too quickly, as MensXP puts it, the remark is a neat way of turning a weakness into a strength. Thor, like his actor, is a veteran in a crowded room.

## Facing Robert Downey Jr as Doctor Doom

The more striking part of the interview concerns Downey. The actor, long associated with [Iron Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man) and Tony Stark, now plays [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), the new threat the heroes must confront, according to Cinema Express.

Hemsworth said the strangest detail was that Downey rarely took off his mask on set. Having spent years on the same side as Stark, he found himself opposing him. "That's not Iron Man, that's not Tony Stark, that's not even Robert Downey," he said, calling the experience "oddly unsettling, in the best way".

The rest of the returning cast named by Cinema Express includes Evans as Steve Rogers, [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) as Sam Wilson, [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd) as Ant-Man, [Tom Hiddleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hiddleston) as Loki and [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh). Readers who want the earlier footage can see our post on [Hemsworth sharing the new *Avengers: Doomsday* trailer](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-avengers-doomsday-trailer/).

## What happens next

MensXP reports that *Avengers: Doomsday* arrives in India on December 17, a day before it reaches international cinemas on December 18. It also says the film will compete with *Dune: Part Three* around the same time. Neither outlet gave an Australian release date, so check local cinema listings closer to the day.

## Frequently asked questions

### What did Chris Hemsworth say about returning to Marvel?

He told Entertainment Weekly it was "oddly uncomfortable at first" to feel like one of the elders on set. After talking with Chris Evans and the Russo brothers, he decided the group is simply experienced.

### Who plays Doctor Doom in Avengers: Doomsday?

Cinema Express reports that Robert Downey Jr plays Doctor Doom, the new villain the heroes face. Hemsworth said working against him was unsettling because Downey often kept the mask on.
