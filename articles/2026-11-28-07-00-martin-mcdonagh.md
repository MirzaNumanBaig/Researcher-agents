**Quick summary**

- [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) had taken US$19.03 million in the US and Canada and US$39.94 million worldwide as of 25 November, according to figures cited by Startefacts.
- That domestic total is already above the US$10.58 million lifetime figure of The Banshees of Inisherin.
- Startefacts forecasts a domestic finish of US$23 million to US$25 million, well short of the US$54.51 million made by Three Billboards.
- Union VFX handled the film's invisible effects, and the Rolling Stones' "Out of Time" plays through the credits.

[Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh)'s 1973-set comedy thriller is doing solid business abroad and a more modest one at home. The Wild Horse Nine box office stood at US$39.94 million worldwide on 25 November, according to a Startefacts analysis that uses figures from The Numbers. Just over US$19 million of that came from North America.

## How the Wild Horse Nine box office compares

Startefacts puts the film's worldwide takings behind The Banshees of Inisherin, which finished on US$52.34 million, by about US$12.41 million. At home, though, the new film has already gone past the US$10.58 million that Banshees made in the US and Canada.

[Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing%2C_Missouri) remains the benchmark. It made US$54.51 million domestically and US$161.16 million worldwide. To match the domestic number, Wild Horse Nine would need roughly US$35.49 million more, almost double what it has earned so far.

The analyst's own call is a domestic total of about US$24 million and a worldwide total near US$51 million, within a range of US$47 million to US$55 million. That is a forecast from one outlet, not a studio figure.

## A shrinking theatre count

The film opened to US$5.91 million in 1,100 locations. A week later it was in 2,175 cinemas and took US$4.53 million. By the weekend of 20 to 22 November it was averaging US$1,231 a location, and Startefacts reports that about 1,000 theatres have since dropped it.

The outlet points to Three Billboards as the way back. After its Oscar nominations that film added 503 locations and doubled its weekend takings from US$1.92 million to US$3.84 million. John Malkovich, who won best actor in Venice for Wild Horse Nine, was quoted at the BFI London Film Festival saying an Oscar is the only way to get people into a theatre.

## The effects nobody is meant to notice

Union VFX returned to work with McDonagh on the film, according to trade title [Broadcast](https://www.broadcastnow.co.uk/post-and-vfx/post-uncovered-union-vfx-crafts-invisible-effects-for-mcdonaghs-wild-horse-nine/5222858.article). The 1973-set story follows CIA agents Chris, played by Malkovich, and Lee, played by [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell), on a mission from Santiago, Chile to Easter Island. [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits) are also in the cast, and Blueprint Pictures and Searchlight Pictures produced it.

Union's creative director, Simon Hughes, spent two weeks advising on VFX needs during the location recce across Easter Island, Santiago and New Mexico. The studio's work covered clean-up, wire removal, CG plane builds, face replacements and enhanced violence. Hughes said the brief demanded invisible effects and that the team kept a vintage look. For more on this, see our related post on [Wild Horse Nine VFX](https://moduscarry.com/wild-horse-nine-vfx-union/).

## A closing song with a sting

There is no post-credits scene. According to Startefacts, the roughly five-minute credits run over the Rolling Stones' "Out of Time", sung by [Mick Jagger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mick_Jagger), straight after a final hillside scene where the characters sing a [Hank Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hank_Williams) song. Critic [Justin Chang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Chang) found the choice painfully obvious in his New Yorker review, while Startefacts reads it as a deliberate dark joke. Composer [Carter Burwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carter_Burwell) scored the film.

## What happens next

The film's holiday-week numbers will show whether the theatre cuts hurt or whether stronger cinemas held on. Awards nominations could change that picture. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much has Wild Horse Nine made?
As of 25 November it had made US$19.03 million in the US and Canada and US$39.94 million worldwide, per Startefacts citing The Numbers.

### Has it beaten The Banshees of Inisherin?
Only domestically. Banshees made US$10.58 million in North America but still leads worldwide on US$52.34 million.

### When did Wild Horse Nine open in cinemas?
Broadcast reports it was released in cinemas on 4 November.
