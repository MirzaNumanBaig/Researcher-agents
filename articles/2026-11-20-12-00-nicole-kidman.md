[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) has been named Icon of the Year at marie claire's 2026 Women of the Year Awards. The winners were announced at a red carpet event in Sydney on Thursday 19 November, according to [Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/nicole-kidman-tina-arena-among-marie-claire-s-2026-women-of-the-year). It is the latest honour for the Australian actor in a year that has also included a new Practical Magic film.

## What marie claire announced

The awards are presented each year by the Are Media title to recognise Australian women who, in the magazine's words, are challenging norms and building a brighter future. The 2026 ceremony was held at The Lands by Capella in Sydney, and [Little Black Book](https://lbbonline.com/news/nicole-kidman-tina-arena-mary-fowler-marie-claire-women-of-the-year) reports that more than 180 winners, nominees, guests and media attended.

Awards were handed out across nine categories: The Powerhouse, Creative of the Year, Designer of the Year, Champion of the Year, Entrepreneur of the Year, Advocate of the Year, Icon of the Year, Rising Star and Entertainer of the Year. Kidman took Icon of the Year. Singer-songwriter Tina Arena won Entertainer of the Year.

The Powerhouse of the Year was a tie between [Allegra Spender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegra_Spender) and [Zali Steggall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zali_Steggall), two independent voices in Australian politics. Footballer Mary Fowler is also among the winners named in the LBB report.

## Who chose the winners

Editor Georgie McCourt said the winners showed what happens "when women turn conviction into action". She added that each one has a story worth celebrating, for what she has achieved and for the possibilities she is creating for the women who follow.

The judging panel included Chanel Contos, founder and CEO of the advocacy group Teach Us Consent, SBS World News presenter [Janice Petersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janice_Petersen), and Volvo marketing director Julie Hutchinson. Volvo presented the awards, with support from Clé de Peau Beauté, Palmolive Luminous Oils, STRAND and FIGUR.

## A career built on risky choices

The honour lands as Kidman is also back in the conversation about her older roles. In an interview recalled by [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/2001-nicole-kidman-begged-to-be-fired-from-movie-role-she-wants-to-play-again/), she said she begged to be released from the 2001 ghost story The Others, directed by [Alejandro Amenabar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alejandro_Amen%C3%A1bar). She had just finished Moulin Rouge! with [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), and said she wanted only to make love stories and musicals at the time.

She went on with the film anyway. According to Far Out, The Others earned a Golden Globe nomination for her and took about US$210 million against a budget of US$17 million. That is roughly twelve times its cost. Kidman has said she feels the film is overlooked and that she loved the character, and she has expressed interest in returning to it.

The same piece notes that she also tried to pull out of The Hours, the film that later won her an Oscar, saying she felt overwhelmed. Both stories show how often her best-known work began with doubt.

## Practical Magic 2 in the background

Kidman's year has also included Practical Magic 2, which opened on 26 August 2026. Directed by Susanne Bier, it reunites her with Sandra Bullock. Wikipedia lists a budget of US$75 million and a box office of about US$106 million, a return of roughly 1.4 times the budget, and a 35 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score. The critics' consensus quoted there singles out the sisterly rapport between Bullock and Kidman as the film's main strength.
