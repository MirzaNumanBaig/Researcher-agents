**Quick summary**

- Netflix released a new story trailer for *Ray Gunn* on 12 November 2026, with a poster and first-look images.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) voices Venus Nova, the biggest star in the film's city of Metropia.
- [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) voices the title detective in Brad Bird's animated noir.
- The film opens in select cinemas, including limited 70mm screenings, on 4 December, then streams on Netflix from 18 December.

A fresh trailer for *Ray Gunn* has put [Scarlett Johansson](https://moduscarry.com/tag/scarlett-johansson/) front and centre as megastar Venus Nova. According to [Netflix's Tudum site](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/ray-gunn-trailer), the trailer, released on 12 November, shows a murder and corruption plot winding through Brad Bird's retro-futuristic city.

## What the new Scarlett Johansson Ray Gunn trailer shows

Unlike the teaser from September, which [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/ray-gunn-gets-its-most-revealing-trailer-yet-plus-a-new-poster-and-first-looks-with-a-pixar-easter-egg/) says was set to Sneaker Pimps' "6 Underground", this one lays out the story.

Rockwell plays Ray Gunn, described as an ex-cop turned private eye. Metropia is a city where humans and aliens live side by side, and tiny "pry bots" now do most detective work. Business is so slow that Ray has given up his apartment and sleeps in his office.

Johansson's Venus Nova is described by Netflix as a controversial star linked to scandal. Her fourth husband, Arnold Dom, hires Ray to find out whether she is having an affair. That job leads Ray through her circle of handlers and hangers-on, from the city's glittering heights to its seedy underworld.

## The cast around her

Rockwell and Johansson lead a voice cast that, according to What's on Netflix, also includes:

- Bobby Cannavale as Arnold Dom
- Tom Waits as Eye-Ra, Ray's cyclopean alien partner
- Michelle Rodriguez as police captain Roz Dexler, who is also Ray's ex
- Matt Berry as the mob boss Slugsy

Bird, whose previous films include *The Incredibles* and *Ratatouille*, told Netflix his pitch was "*The Maltese Falcon* meets *Buck Rogers*". He also said animation is "too fun a medium, and too interesting, to limit what kind of stories can be told". He cited Bogart-era detective films, the noir classic *Out of the Past* and the Philip Marlowe and Sam Spade stories as influences.

## A Pixar nod and early reviews

What's on Netflix points to one detail in the new footage: the hero's particle blaster is engraved "A113", a reference to Bird's old CalArts classroom that appears across Pixar films. The weapon also has its own green energy that no other gun in the film shares.

Early reaction is strong but not unanimous. The same outlet reports that the film debuted at 91% on Rotten Tomatoes after its London Film Festival premiere and has since reached 94%, Certified Fresh. Peter Bradshaw of The Guardian gave it five stars, while The Hollywood Reporter and Screen International were among the critics who were less convinced. Our earlier report on [Johansson singing in Ray Gunn](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn/) covers the film's score of 94%.

The film runs 1 hour 59 minutes and is rated PG for violence, action, language, some suggestive material and thematic elements.

## What happens next

*Ray Gunn* opens in select theatres on 4 December, including limited 70mm engagements, before landing globally on Netflix on 18 December. Viewers in Australia should check local cinema listings for the 70mm dates, as neither source lists specific Australian screenings.
