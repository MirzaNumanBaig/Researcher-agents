**Quick summary**

- Dead Man's Bones, the duo of Ryan Gosling and Zach Shields, posted twice from a recording studio on Thursday, 26 November (US Pacific time).
- The caption promised "More soon. Sooner than later."
- The band has not announced a song, an album, a tour or a release date.
- The posts come a month after "Lost in the Night", released on 27 October, the duo's first new song in 17 years.

[Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s band [Dead Man's Bones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Man's_Bones) has hinted that its comeback will not stop at a single song. According to [89 A Rádio Rock](https://www.radiorock.com.br/en/news/dead-mans-bones-ryan-gosling-studio-new-music), the group's verified Instagram account shared a photo and a short video from inside a studio on Thursday, with a promise of more Dead Man's Bones new music "sooner than later".

## What the band posted

The first post went up at 10am Pacific time. It is a blue-lit photo of a studio hallway, with a sign reading "Quiet, Recording Area, Observe Recording Lights" and a door marked "Studio A". The caption read: "Thanks for sticking with us. More soon. Sooner than later."

About four hours later the band shared a short, dark and slightly blurry studio video captioned "Can't let you down". It tags Capitol Studios, the recording facility inside the Capitol Records Tower in Hollywood. By early Friday the photo had more than 18,000 likes and the video more than 12,000, the report says.

The wording echoes the band's message on 27 October, when "Lost in the Night" came out: "Thanks for sticking with us all these years. New single, Lost In The Night. More coming soon."

## What is confirmed and what is not

Instagram labels the video's audio "Dead Man's Bones • Blue Light". Fans on X have taken that as the title of a new song. The band has not announced any track by that name, so for now it is fan speculation backed by a label on the platform.

Several basic questions remain open, according to the report:

- Whether "Blue Light" is a new song, and when it might be released.
- Whether the session is for a single, an EP or a second album.
- Whether any live shows are planned. No tour has been announced.
- Who is producing. Tim Anderson produced both "Lost in the Night" and the 2009 album.

## How the comeback began

The return started in October with an Instagram video in which a talking ghostly head announced the 27 October date. Actress Eva Mendes, Gosling's partner, joined in and said she was trying to "summon" new music from the band.

"Lost in the Night" was released on ANTI-, the label behind the first album. Quoting the press release as carried by Stereogum, the report says the duo tried several Los Angeles studios without finding the sound they wanted. They then took two guitars, two drum machines and two synthesisers to a farm outside the city and recorded the song in a day. The black-and-white video stars Noah Jupe and Rain Spencer. The sound moves away from the haunted-lullaby feel of 2009 towards synths and a more new wave, post-punk edge.

## Where it started

Gosling and Shields met in 2005 and bonded over ghosts, monsters and Disneyland's Haunted Mansion ride, with the idea of writing love songs about those creatures. Their only album, "Dead Man's Bones", came out on 6 October 2009 on ANTI-.

It is best remembered for the Silverlake Conservatory Children's Choir, from the Los Angeles music school co-founded by Red Hot Chili Peppers bassist Flea. Shields told Rolling Stone in 2009 that the songs were first written with the children's choir in mind for the vocals.

## What happens next

The band has said only that more is coming. Nothing official has been announced about a title, format or date. Given the studio posts and the Instagram audio label, a "Blue Light" release is plausible, but that remains unconfirmed until the band says so.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Ryan Gosling releasing new music?

Yes, with his band Dead Man's Bones. "Lost in the Night" came out on 27 October, and the band's Instagram posts on 26 November promised more soon.

### Is "Blue Light" a confirmed new Dead Man's Bones song?

Not officially. Instagram shows "Blue Light" as the audio label on the band's studio video, and fans have picked up the title, but the band has not announced a song by that name.

### Has Dead Man's Bones announced an album or tour?

No. As of 27 November the band had announced no album, no tour and no release date.
