**Quick summary**

- London studio Union VFX has released a breakdown reel of its effects work on *Wild Horse Nine*, according to Animation World Network.
- The film stars [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) and John Malkovich as CIA agents in 1973.
- The effects include CG period aircraft, face replacements and enhanced on-screen violence.
- The film opened in theatres on 4 November after a festival run from Venice to TIFF.

Union VFX has pulled back the curtain on the quiet effects work behind *Wild Horse Nine*, the new thriller from [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) starring [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell). The London studio published a breakdown reel this week, [Animation World Network reported](https://www.awn.com/news/union-vfx-breaks-down-martin-mcdonaghs-wild-horse-nine), showing how its team built period aircraft, replaced faces and sharpened the film's violence without drawing attention to any of it.

## What *Wild Horse Nine* is about

[*Wild Horse Nine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) is described as a darkly funny thriller set in 1973. Rockwell plays CIA agent Lee opposite John Malkovich as Chris, and the pair travel from Santiago, Chile, to Easter Island on a mission that tests how far they trust each other.

The supporting cast includes [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits). Blueprint Pictures and Searchlight Pictures produced it. After a festival run that took in Venice and TIFF, it reached cinemas on 4 November, per the same report.

## What Union VFX actually did

The brief to the studio was for "invisible" effects, meaning work the audience should never notice. According to Animation World Network, that covered cleanup, wire removal and plate fixes, plus CG period aircraft, face replacements and enhanced on-screen violence.

Creative director Simon Hughes led the work. This is Union's third film with McDonagh, after *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri* and *The Banshees of Inisherin*. Hughes spent two weeks on the production's location scout across Easter Island, Santiago and New Mexico, advising on what the effects shoot would need.

"The vfx brief demanded invisible effects and we feel we achieved this well and in turn added to the aesthetic of the film," Hughes said. He added that the team kept a vintage look and feel and helped the filmmakers pull off technically difficult shots, from recreating period aircraft to making violence look realistic.

## The studio behind it

Union was founded in 2008 by Tim Caplan and Adam Gascoyne. It is an independently owned, BAFTA-winning and Emmy-nominated studio in London's Soho. Its credits include work for [Danny Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Boyle), [Sam Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Mendes) and McDonagh, according to the report.
