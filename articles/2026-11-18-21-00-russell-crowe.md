**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) told *The Howard Stern Show* he bought a dinosaur skull from Leonardo DiCaprio for about US$35,000, the price DiCaprio had paid.
- Crowe said the sale happened around 2008 or 2009 and that "a bunch of vodka" was involved, according to Netflix Junkie.
- In the same interview he said he turned down a role in *The Lord of the Rings* that came with 10 per cent of the gross, which Stern put at roughly US$100 million.
- Crowe's Netflix film *Unabomber* premiered on 25 September 2026 and has a 25 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score.

Russell Crowe has revealed that one of the more unusual items in his past collection came from Leonardo DiCaprio's house: a dinosaur skull, bought for about US$35,000 during a night that, by his own account, involved plenty of vodka. The Oscar winner shared the story, along with a tale of a role he refused, in a recent conversation with [Howard Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Stern), as reported by [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-bunch-of-vodka-involved-russell-crowe-recalls-buying-a-thirty-five-thousand-dollar-dinosaur-skull-from-leonardo-dicaprio) and [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/that_my_instinct_russell_crowe_reveals_why_he_rejected_a_100_million_the_lord_of_the_rings_deal/s1_17785_44573739).

## How Crowe ended up with the dinosaur skull

According to Netflix Junkie, the deal took place at DiCaprio's home around 2008 or 2009. Crowe paid the same amount DiCaprio had paid, roughly US$35,000. DiCaprio was reportedly selling because another dinosaur he wanted was about to come on the market.

"I bought it for my kids," Crowe told the show, before adding that "there was a bunch of vodka involved in that transaction." He did not haggle. He also could not name the species when Stern asked, and said it was not a particularly popular one.

Stern remarked on how few complete dinosaur heads are held privately. The skull became a centrepiece of the children's playroom, Netflix Junkie reports, before it was sold in the memorabilia auction linked to Crowe's divorce, which the outlet says raised more than US$4 million in total.

## The Lord of the Rings role he turned down

The same interview covered a far bigger number. Crowe said he was offered a part in *The Lord of the Rings* with 10 per cent of the gross attached, a figure Stern calculated at about US$100 million, according to Yardbarker.

Crowe said he declined because of a feeling about the director. "My instinct was that he had somebody else in mind, which turned out to be Viggo," he said, referring to [Viggo Mortensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viggo_Mortensen). He also said that [Peter Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Jackson), a fellow New Zealander, seemed to have been pushed into making the call, and that "he should be allowed to hire the actor that he wants."

Crowe said he rarely thinks about the missing fortune, except when interviewers do the sums. Stern joked that the money would have kept the skull out of the auction. Yardbarker adds that Crowe also said no to *The Wolverine*.

## Where Crowe's career stands now

The interview lands while Crowe has a streaming title in the spotlight. *Unabomber*, directed by [Janus Metz Pedersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janus_Metz_Pedersen), premiered on Netflix on 25 September 2026. Yardbarker, citing Collider, says it drew about 16 million views in its first week and roughly 40 million in its first two weeks.

The film follows Ted Kaczynski, the mathematician behind a mail-bombing campaign that lasted nearly two decades, and the FBI manhunt that ended it. [Jacob Tremblay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Tremblay), [Shailene Woodley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shailene_Woodley) and [Annabelle Wallis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabelle_Wallis) also star.

Critics have been less warm than viewers. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) lists the film at 25 per cent (Yardbarker quoted 24 per cent at the time of its report), and Metacritic gives it 46 out of 100 from 18 critics, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unabomber_(film)). Reviewers have questioned its approach to the story, and The Hollywood Reporter's Richard Lawson described Crowe's performance as phoned in. No budget or box office figures are on record yet.
