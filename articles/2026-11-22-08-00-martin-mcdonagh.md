**Quick summary**

- [Martin McDonagh's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) new film [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) has been removed from 175 theatres, according to Collider.
- The film has grossed about US$30 million worldwide, roughly US$15 million of it from domestic cinemas, on a reported US$15 million budget.
- That is the weakest result of McDonagh's three Searchlight Pictures releases.
- Critics are strongly behind it, with a 93% score on Rotten Tomatoes, and Oscar nominations could still extend its run.

[Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), the new black comedy from writer-director [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh), has been pulled from 175 theatres, [Collider reported on 21 November](https://collider.com/wild-horse-nine-john-malkovich-removed-175-theaters/). It is a sharp reversal for a film that arrived on the back of strong festival reviews and a lot of awards chatter.

## What the numbers show

Collider says the film has taken about US$30 million worldwide so far, with roughly US$15 million of that from domestic theatres. Its reported budget is also about US$15 million, so it has earned back around twice its cost at the box office, though that figure does not count marketing or the share cinemas keep.

The outlet frames the result against McDonagh's earlier work with Searchlight Pictures. [Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Billboards_Outside_Ebbing%2C_Missouri) (2017) grossed more than US$160 million worldwide on a budget of about US$15 million. *The Banshees of Inisherin* (2022) made more than US$50 million against a reported US$20 million. By Collider's reckoning, the new film has the weakest box-office performance of the three.

Collider also stresses that the run is not over. The film will probably keep adding to its total in the coming weeks, particularly if it collects Oscar nominations.

## Why cinemas drop a film like this

[The Hype Magazine](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2026/11/21/wild-horse-nine-john-malkovich-sam-rockwell-removed-theaters/), which credits Collider as its original source, describes the cut as a recalibration. Specialty distributors regularly trim screen counts when turnout falls below what exhibitors want, it says, and mid-budget adult dramas compete with franchise films and family titles for the same weekend slots.

It adds that Searchlight, which sits under the Walt Disney Studios umbrella, often builds a film's reputation in cinemas before moving it to premium video on demand and streaming. That is the outlet's general reading of the industry rather than a statement from the studio, and neither report says when Wild Horse Nine will reach those platforms.

## What the film is about

The story follows two disillusioned CIA officers, played by John Malkovich and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell), who are sent from Santiago to Easter Island (Rapa Nui) on the eve of the September 1973 Chilean coup. According to Collider, one of them is supposed to kill the other. Their bureau chief is played by [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), and the pair cross paths with rebellious local students played by Mariana di Girolamo and Ailín Salas.

[Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits) round out the ensemble. The film played at the Venice, Telluride and Toronto festivals before its Searchlight release. Collider notes it shares a premise with [In Bruges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Bruges) and is McDonagh's third film with Rockwell.

## Critics are on its side

Reviews are the film's strongest card. Collider reports a "Certified Fresh" 93% critics' score and a "Verified Hot" 92% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The site's consensus line, as Collider quotes it, singles out Malkovich's performance as a highlight and calls the film an uproariously funny espionage comedy.

McDonagh's Oscar record adds weight to the awards case. Collider notes that Three Billboards earned seven nominations and two wins, for [Frances McDormand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_McDormand) and Rockwell, while The Banshees of Inisherin earned nine nominations. Both were nominated for [Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_common_film_awards_categories). McDonagh was also nominated for [Best Original Screenplay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Original_Screenplay) for his debut feature, In Bruges.

## What happens next

The screen count will keep shrinking unless awards attention changes the picture. Nomination announcements are the next big test, and Collider's view is that a run of nods is what could keep the film earning. ## Frequently asked questions

### How many theatres has Wild Horse Nine lost?

Collider reports that the film has been removed from 175 theatres. It remains on select screens.

### How much has Wild Horse Nine made?

About US$30 million worldwide, with roughly US$15 million from domestic cinemas, against a reported budget of US$15 million, according to Collider.

### Who stars in Wild Horse Nine?

John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell lead, with Steve Buscemi, Parker Posey, Tom Waits, Mariana di Girolamo and Ailín Salas in the cast.

### Is it still in the Oscar race?

Yes. Both Collider and The Hype Magazine describe it as an awards-season contender, and its reviews are very strong.
