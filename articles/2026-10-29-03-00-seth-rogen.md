**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) stars in Sephora's "We All Sephora" holiday campaign, which launches on 29 October in the US and Canada.
- The ads show him as a beauty novice shopping for gifts, with a store in Los Angeles briefly renamed "Sethphora".
- Rogen will join a panel for the animated film Tangles at the ASIFA-Showcase in Los Angeles on 14 and 15 November.
- He also says he and his co-stars "overtly pressured" Olivia Wilde to lead The Invite, now streaming on ZEE5.

Seth Rogen is the face of Sephora's holiday push, with the retailer's "We All Sephora" campaign set to roll out on 29 October across connected TV, linear television, streaming and social, according to [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/sephora-casts-seth-rogen-for-holiday-ads-targeted-at-gift-givers/872877/). The ads play on Rogen as a shopper who knows little about beauty and needs rescuing.

## A gift-giver who needs help

Sephora built the campaign around people who want to buy thoughtful presents but lack beauty knowledge, rather than its core base of regular shoppers, Marketing Dive reports. In the 30- and 60-second spots, Rogen arrives at a store and relies on an advisor named Charlotte. He misreads the use of setting sprays and calls pimple patches "face stickers" before she steers him towards gifts that suit the people on his list.

The closing joke involves matching red light LED masks that he wears with his mother. [Muse by Clio](https://musebyclios.com/fashion-and-beauty/seth-rogen-visits-sephora-for-holidays-gifts-and-he-seems-a-bit-confused/) names Jason Woliner as the director and says agency Gut developed the work. Marketing Dive reports it will run in the US and Canada, and that Rogen's curated gift list will sit on Sephora's app and website.

Sephora's chief marketing officer, Zena Srivatsa Arnold, said in a statement quoted by [Brand Innovators](https://brand-innovators.com/seth-rogen-stars-in-sephoras-holiday-spot/) that Rogen brings an "unexpected perspective" on beauty gifting. Sam Bennett, the retailer's SVP of brand content, told Muse the funny moments are "rooted in a relatable gifting experience".

## Why Sephora is betting on the holidays

The numbers explain the focus. Brand Innovators cites the National Retail Federation's forecast that November and December retail sales will reach US$1.013 trillion, up 4.2% on 2025. It adds that Sephora took in nearly US$9 billion in US retail sales across 2025, with the fourth quarter a key period.

Marketing Dive notes that LVMH reported 6% organic revenue growth for its selective retailing segment, which includes Sephora, over the first nine months of the year. Last year Sephora paired Mariah Carey with Billy Eichner for its holiday ads, so a celebrity-led comedy spot is a familiar play.

The campaign was teased first. A Sephora store at The Grove in Los Angeles was temporarily renamed "Sethphora", and the final shot of the ad shows the same storefront.

## Tangles keeps Rogen busy

The ads land in a busy fortnight. According to [Animation Scoop](https://www.animationscoop.com/inaugural-asifa-showcase-at-infinity-festival-brings-brooks-bird-and-rogen/), ASIFA-Hollywood has added Rogen to the first ASIFA-Showcase, held during the ninth Infinity Festival in Los Angeles on 14 and 15 November. He will sit on a panel for the animated feature Tangles with [Lauren Miller Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Miller_Rogen), director Leah Nelson and co-writer [Sarah Leavitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Leavitt), followed by an evening screening.

The same lineup includes [Mel Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mel_Brooks) and [Brad Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Bird), who will present his film Ray Gunn with Netflix.

[Hollywood PQ](https://hollywoodpq.com/en/pedro-pascal-seth-rogen-and-mark-ruffalo-have-a-blast-at-afi-fest-2026/) reports that Rogen attended AFI Fest 2026 to support a Tangles screening. It describes the film as an adaptation of Leavitt's autobiographical graphic novel about Alzheimer's disease and family relationships, with Rogen lending his voice alongside Julia Louis-Dreyfus, Bryan Cranston and Abbi Jacobson.

## The Invite and The Studio

Rogen has been talking up The Invite, an A24 comedy now streaming on ZEE5. In an interview with the Associated Press, quoted by [Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/international/the-invite-arrives-on-zee5-seth-rogen-says-he-and-cast-overtly-pressured-olivia-wilde-to-star-in-the-film/), he said the cast kept a side text chain to plan how to talk [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde) into starring as well as directing. He joked that he dismissed every other actor she suggested.

Wilde called it "the best experience of my career". The film follows a married couple, played by Wilde and Rogen, who host their upstairs neighbours, played by Edward Norton and Penélope Cruz, for dinner. Marketing Dive says Rogen is riding awards buzz for the film and that the second season of "The Studio", which he co-created and produces, is building excitement.

## What happens next

The Sephora campaign begins on 29 October, with Rogen's gift picks available in the app and online. The Tangles panel follows on 14 or 15 November in Los Angeles, and ASIFA-Hollywood says advance registration is required. No date for the second season of The Studio appears in the sources.
