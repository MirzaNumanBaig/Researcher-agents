**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie)'s 2016 film The Legend of Tarzan reached No. 4 on HBO Max's most-watched movies chart in the US as of 15 November, according to MovieWeb.
- The film took about US$356 million worldwide on a reported US$180 million budget, and was widely seen as a disappointment.
- Critics were lukewarm, with a Rotten Tomatoes score in the mid-30s, while audiences rated it noticeably higher.
- MovieWeb says it also reached Netflix's global top ten in 2021.

Margot Robbie's The Legend of Tarzan is having another moment. As of 15 November, the 2016 adventure sat at No. 4 on HBO Max's most-watched movies chart in the US, according to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/the-legend-of-tarzan-november-2026/), nearly ten years after it left cinemas.

## A box office letdown finds a streaming audience

The film was directed by [David Yates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Yates) and released by Warner Bros. MovieWeb reports it earned roughly US$356 million worldwide against a reported production budget of US$180 million. That is a large gross in absolute terms, but it was still widely judged a disappointment once the cost of making and marketing it was counted.

The outlet also points to the timing of its July 2016 release. It opened against the later weeks of Finding Dory's run, and the outlet argues that the vine-swinging period adventure had little theatrical appetite left by then.

## Critics were cool, audiences warmer

On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the film settled in the mid-30s, according to MovieWeb, while the audience score ran notably higher. The outlet reads that gap as a typical sign of a film that does better at home than it did in cinemas.

Its pitch to streaming viewers, as MovieWeb frames it, is a 110-minute adventure with Alexander Skarsgård in the title role, Robbie as a Jane written with more spine than is usual for the character, and [Christoph Waltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Waltz) as the villain. Samuel L. Jackson also appears, as George Washington Williams.

## Why the cast matters now

MovieWeb notes that the stars' careers look very different from 2016. It describes Robbie as one of the biggest stars in the world after Barbie, and says Skarsgård's profile has risen through The Northman, Succession and Murderbot. In 2016, it says, Skarsgård was a rising TV actor and Robbie was fresh from Suicide Squad.

The outlet's view is that this pairing helps explain why an older film keeps resurfacing. It adds that this is not the first streaming revival: The Legend of Tarzan reached Netflix's global top ten in 2021 and has spiked periodically on whichever platform carries it.

Where it sits on the HBO Max chart is also worth noting. MovieWeb places it ahead of The Hunger Games and behind Matilda, The Pout-Pout Fish and The End of Oak Street. A single chart position does not change the film's box office record, and MovieWeb says as much.

## What happens next

Streaming charts move daily, so the ranking is a snapshot rather than a trend. The reported figures show where the film stands on 15 November, and nothing in the sources says whether it will hold the spot.
