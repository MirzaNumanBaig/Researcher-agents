**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) headed the European premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth at London's BFI IMAX on Monday 23 November.
- [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) withdrew on the day. Organisers said he had flu and was advised not to travel.
- Debicki plays Hollywood agent Karen Crosby in the Netflix film, directed by [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher).
- Headlines quote her saying she was sad Pitt couldn't be there.

Elizabeth Debicki carried the London premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth on Monday night after her co-star Brad Pitt dropped out with flu. The Australian actress walked the red carpet at BFI IMAX Waterloo without the film's lead, in a gown that fashion writers and the tabloids described in different colours.

## Why Brad Pitt missed the premiere

According to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16316157/Elizabeth-Debicki-Mis-Adventures-Cliff-Booth-premiere-Brad-Pitt-flu.html), Pitt was due to attend with Fincher but was ruled out earlier in the day. Organisers said he had "come down with flu and has been advised not to travel".

Pitt reprises the stuntman he played in Quentin Tarantino's [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood). The new film is a spin-off for Netflix. No new date for a Pitt appearance has been reported in the sources we read.

Entertainment headlines carried Debicki's own reaction, with one quoting her as saying she was sad he wasn't there. The video behind it could not be read in full, so treat the wording as a headline quote, not a transcript.

## What Debicki wore

The Mail said Debicki wore a low-cut teal gown with a cowl neckline and a flowing train. [Yahoo's fashion column](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/elizabeth-debicki-takes-centre-stage-211605469.html) saw a powder-blue Valentino Spring 2026 Haute Couture gown with a softly draped neckline, plus Glenn Spiro gold and diamond earrings.

The two accounts disagree on the shade, so take the colour with a pinch of salt. The Yahoo writer noted she had worn a lot of black lately and welcomed the change. The look suited the occasion: with the male lead absent, she was the main draw for photographers. More of our red carpet coverage sits under [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/).

## The role: Karen Crosby

[IndieWire's interview](https://www.indiewire.com/features/interviews/cliff-booth-elizabeth-debicki-slapping-brad-pitt-rom-coms-1235229895/) says the film is set nearly a decade after the 2019 original. Cliff has drifted into new work, including film projecting and bartending, before a meeting with Hollywood agent Karen Crosby, played by Debicki.

Karen's marquee client is actor Charlie Nelson, played by Scott Caan. IndieWire says the script was written by Tarantino and that the chemistry between Pitt and Debicki gives the film its emotional weight. It also notes Karen wins Cliff over by slapping him, and that Debicki gets to use her own Australian accent this time.

## How she landed it

Debicki told IndieWire she didn't audition. Fincher emailed to ask whether she would consider a "very tricky part" in a film he hoped to shoot in July, and she called him straight away. He insisted on explaining the project in full, she said, because he and Tarantino were still reworking the script.

A two-week rehearsal period followed, with Tarantino there throughout. She recalled Fincher, Tarantino and Pitt turning to ask what she thought, which unnerved her at first. She also said she saw the 2019 film at a cinema, probably alone, and chose not to rewatch it before filming.

## What happens next

Netflix has not changed its plans in anything we read, and the premiere went ahead without Pitt. Whether he joins any later appearances is unconfirmed. For now the film's promotion rests on Debicki, who is also in line for more attention after the IndieWire interview raised the idea of a Karen Crosby follow-up, a point the outlet made half-jokingly.
