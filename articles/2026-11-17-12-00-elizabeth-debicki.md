**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) attended a small Netflix screening of *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* in Los Angeles on Thursday, 12 November.
- Director [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher) and actor [Peter Weller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Weller) joined her for a Q&A, according to BroadwayWorld.
- The film opens in IMAX on 25 November 2026 and reaches Netflix on 23 December 2026.
- Separately, Go Fug Yourself lists Debicki among the women in Elle's 2026 Women in Hollywood coverage, tied to *Wicker* and the Fincher film.

Elizabeth Debicki was among the cast and crew who took part in a Netflix screening of *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* in Los Angeles last week, and she now appears in Elle's Women in Hollywood line-up for two of her upcoming projects.

## A small screening with a crowded guest list

According to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Photos-David-Fincher-Elizabeth-Debicki-and-Peter-Weller-Attend-Netflix-Screening-of-THE-FURTHER-MIS-ADVENTURES-OF-CLIFF-BOOTH-20261116), Netflix hosted the event at the Ross House in Los Angeles on Thursday, 12 November. Fincher, Debicki, Weller and film editor [Kirk Baxter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirk_Baxter) all attended. A Q&A followed, moderated by [Jonathan Groff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Groff), who starred in Fincher's series *Mindhunter*.

The guest list read like a gathering of working directors. BroadwayWorld names [Zach Cregger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Cregger), [Cameron Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cameron_Crowe), [Scott Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Cooper_(director)), [Gore Verbinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gore_Verbinski), [Craig Gillespie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Gillespie) and [Kemp Powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kemp_Powers), along with Steven Zaillian, Ti West, Emile Hirsch, Frances Fisher, Kelvin Harrison Jr., Candy Clark, Luke Davies and Beverly D'Angelo.

The report does not say what Debicki or anyone else said during the Q&A, so there is nothing yet on her character or her scenes.

## What is known about the film

BroadwayWorld describes the film as a return for Brad Pitt as Cliff Booth, working from a screenplay by Quentin Tarantino, and says it is set in Hollywood in 1977. The character first appeared in [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood).

The release plan is unusual. Per BroadwayWorld, the film gets a two-week exclusive run in IMAX theatres worldwide from 25 November 2026, then moves to Netflix on 23 December 2026. That gives cinema audiences a window of about four weeks before the streaming debut.

For earlier reaction to the film, see our post on [Debicki's early buzz in Fincher's Cliff Booth](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-cliff-booth/).

## Elle's Women in Hollywood line-up

Debicki also turns up in coverage of Elle's 2026 Women in Hollywood issue. Fashion blog Go Fug Yourself says the group is largely tied to the awards conversation, or to films that could be part of it. It pairs Debicki with *Wicker* and the Fincher film.

The same post lists [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) for *Dune 3*, [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) for *Obsession*, [Carrie Coon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Coon) for *The Liberation*, [Samantha Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Morton) for *The Odyssey*, [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore) for *The Debut* and [Penelope Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen%C3%A9lope_Cruz) for *The Invite*. Go Fug Yourself's account is a commentary on the covers. It is not Elle's own announcement, and it does not say which cover Debicki appears on.

## What happens next

The next date is 25 November, when the film opens in IMAX. Netflix's streaming release follows on 23 December. Elle's issue and further coverage of the Cliff Booth cast are likely to keep Debicki in the awards-season conversation, though nothing in these reports confirms a nomination or campaign.
