**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) attended the Christmas lights switch-on ceremony on the Champs-Élysées in Paris over the weekend.
- The evening was rain-soaked, according to Red Carpet Fashion Awards and Yahoo.
- She wore Louis Vuitton: a camel coat, a yellow top and black leather trousers.
- Her jewellery came from the Le Damier de Louis Vuitton Fine Jewelry collection.

[Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) stepped out in Paris this weekend for the Christmas lights switch-on on the Champs-Élysées, and she chose a Louis Vuitton outfit that suited a wet November night. Fashion coverage of the event focused on how she paired a classic camel coat with a bright yellow top.

## What Cate Blanchett wore on the Champs-Élysées

According to [Red Carpet Fashion Awards](https://www.redcarpet-fashionawards.com/2026/11/23/cate-blanchett-lights-up-the-champs-elysees-christmas-celebrations/), the Australian actress wore Louis Vuitton from head to toe. The centrepiece was a straight-cut camel coat, layered over a fitted yellow top and black pointelle leather trousers.

The site's writer singled out the yellow as the most striking part of the outfit, saying it gave a familiar coat a fresher feel. The same text was carried by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/cate-blanchett-lights-champs-lys-182603163.html).

## The jewellery and styling

The accessories came from the same house. Blanchett wore white gold and diamond earrings, plus a necklace and rings, all from the Le Damier de Louis Vuitton Fine Jewelry collection, the report says. Her hair was swept up in an updo, which the reviewer described as the finishing touch.

The coverage did not give prices for the jewellery or name a stylist, so those details are not confirmed.

## A rainy night for the lights

The report describes the evening as rain-soaked. That made the outfit's practical layers, a long coat and trousers, a sensible choice for the weather, though the sources do not say whether she planned it that way. Fashion writers framed the look as proof that a wet evening can still work as a style moment.

The sources we could read are fashion write-ups. They do not detail the ceremony itself, such as the running order, the crowd or what Blanchett said on stage, so we have left those out rather than guess.
