**Quick summary**

- Season 2 of the Netflix spy thriller *Black Doves* is now streaming, with Ben Whishaw and Keira Knightley both returning.
- Knightley says she enjoyed the stunt work, including kicking down a door, even though rehearsal time was short this year.
- An art gallery fight was rehearsed in pieces, then filmed in one continuous shot at the director's request.
- Whishaw describes the story of his character Sam and Knightley's Helen as, in a way, a love story.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) and [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) are back as spies-for-hire in the second season of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves), now streaming on Netflix. In a new interview reported by the Arizona Daily Star on 12 November, the pair explain what made the new episodes physically demanding and emotionally messy.

## Knightley and the door she had to kick down

Knightley plays Helen Webb, a politician's wife who has spent years leading a double life as a member of the Black Doves, a spies-for-hire organisation. Asked what she enjoyed most about the role, she pointed to something simple. "I got to kick a door down," she told the paper, adding that she had not realised it was what was missing from her life.

She said she was good at it, and joked that she might start a club where people can kick doors in. The character itself gets less affection. Knightley said she would not want to meet Helen or be her, but loves how "weird" she is, and described the show's people as morally complicated creatures living strange lives.

That mix of the ordinary and the lethal is the show's engine. According to the Arizona Daily Star, Helen kills dozens of enemies, guards her secrets and keeps up appearances as the prime minister's wife, all at once.

## A stunt-heavy season on a tight schedule

Season 2 asks a lot of its leads physically. The actors rehearsed with stunt coordinators, but Knightley said they had less preparation time than in the first season, so the work was "a little touch and go". The moves had been choreographed, she explained, but the cast had not had time to learn them properly.

One sequence set in an art gallery shows how that played out. It was rehearsed in pieces, yet the director wanted to shoot it in a single take. "And we did it," Knightley said.

## Ben Whishaw on Sam, and why he wanted him to survive

Whishaw plays Sam Young, a fellow Black Dove who reunites with Helen when her secrets are at risk of coming out. According to the report, he tracks her detractors and becomes tangled up in her life.

Whishaw said he felt great sympathy for Sam, who is down on his luck. "I really wanted him to get out of this alive," he said, before admitting it might also be interesting if he did not. He acknowledged that it is a strange thing to wish for a mass murderer, but called it good drama.

He also pointed to Episode 2, where Sam has to kill an extra person after following Helen's "very bad advice" and going snooping. Whishaw said the conflict between the two is taken to an extreme: both want to be loyal, but Sam is in pain and tormented and traumatised by what he has done. "In a way," Whishaw said, "this is a love story between them."

For more from the actor on the series, see our earlier piece, [Ben Whishaw on Black Doves, Bond and a Spy Grandfather](https://moduscarry.com/ben-whishaw-black-doves-spy-grandfather/).

## Two spies who owe each other

Whishaw described Helen as a whirlwind who sucks Sam into her chaos, while Sam has chaos of his own. Knightley agreed, with a caveat: it is "maybe a little bit her fault".

She framed the bond as a debt. There is an "owing" between them, she said, a kind of understanding that each will save the other's skin, but the consequences keep rising. Meanwhile, Helen's husband is pulled into the drama, and the report says it is clear she cannot keep a double life and expect to hold on to her marriage.

Knightley said that tension is what makes the series exciting: how can people be loving and also horrific killers who lie the whole time?

## What happens next

Season 2 of *Black Doves* is available on Netflix now. The interview does not say whether a third season is planned, so there is no confirmed news on that.
