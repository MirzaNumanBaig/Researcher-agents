**Quick summary**

- A scooper called Cartier Films says [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) "might" play Daniel Ketch in Marvel's *Ghost Rider*, according to SuperHeroHype.
- This is a rumour. Marvel Studios has not announced any role for Storrie.
- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) is set to star as the title hero, with [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) directing.
- *Ghost Rider* is listed for release on 28 July 2028.

A fresh rumour is adding detail to the casting talk around Ryan Gosling's *Ghost Rider*. According to [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/movies/703350-ghost-rider-rumor-connor-storrie-playing-ryan-gosling-mcu-movie), a scooper now claims Connor Storrie, the 26-year-old star of [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), may be playing Daniel Ketch. Nothing has been confirmed.

## Where the Daniel Ketch rumour comes from

The claim comes from Cartier Films, which wrote in a blog post that it was "hearing" Storrie "might be taking on the role" of Ketch. SuperHeroHype notes that the scooper does not fully commit to the report, which suggests the detail is not settled.

That caution matters. Earlier talk had Storrie reportedly in discussions with the studio about a villain role, and fans had speculated along those lines. The new rumour points in a different direction. In Marvel Comics, Ketch is another Spirit of Vengeance and the brother of Johnny Blaze, the first Ghost Rider, according to the same report.

Marvel Studios has not announced anything about Storrie or his part in the film. Until it does, treat the casting as talk and nothing more.

## What is confirmed about the film

Far less is on the record. Gosling will play the title character, and Levy will direct. SuperHeroHype says the director was announced at Comic-Con earlier this year.

Levy is no stranger to the studio. He directed 2024's *Deadpool & Wolverine*, and he has worked with Gosling before on *Star Wars: Starfighter*, which the report says arrives next year. Fans can find more of our coverage in the [Ryan Gosling tag](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-gosling/), including an earlier piece on the [Ghost Rider rumour and the band return](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-ghost-rider/).

The film is also expected to sit just after a major milestone. SuperHeroHype reports it will be one of Marvel Studios' first projects following 2027's *Avengers: Secret Wars*, with a release date of 28 July 2028. No other plot or cast details have been revealed.

## Which Ghost Rider will Gosling play?

That question is still open. The report says it is unclear which version of the character he will portray.

There are precedents on screen. [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage) played Johnny Blaze in two films, and [Gabriel Luna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Luna) played Robbie Reyes in *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* According to SuperHeroHype, the MCU has not yet acknowledged Luna's version of the character.

If Gosling were to play Blaze, a Ketch appearance would fit the comics, since the two are brothers. That is an inference from the comic history in the report, not a confirmed plan.

## What happens next

Marvel Studios has yet to comment on the Storrie talk, so any real casting news would have to come from the studio itself. With a July 2028 release date, official announcements may still be some way off. We will update this story if Marvel confirms a cast list.
