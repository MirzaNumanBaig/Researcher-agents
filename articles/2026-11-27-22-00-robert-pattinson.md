**Quick summary**

- Florence Pugh is now the 3/1 second favourite with William Hill to play opposite Robert Pattinson in the next James Bond film, up from 6/1 a week earlier, according to Geo News.
- Margaret Qualley leads the market at 5/4, and Suki Waterhouse sits third at 4/1.
- No co-star has been announced, and no release date has been set. Denis Villeneuve is directing the Amazon film.
- A separate report, citing sources quoted by The Sun, says Pattinson and Waterhouse are house-hunting in the Cotswolds. That is unconfirmed.

The Robert Pattinson Bond girl race has a new second favourite. The contest to play opposite [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) in the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film is tightening. Bookmaker William Hill has shortened [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) to 3/1, according to [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/692803-florence-pugh-gets-ahead-in-race-to-be-robert-pattinsons-bond-girl), days after she said she had texted the director to put herself forward.

## Why Florence Pugh's odds moved

Pugh was 6/1 and sixth in the betting only a week ago. The move followed her remarks at the Elle Women in Hollywood event, where Villeneuve had just presented her with an award.

She said she messaged him that morning to congratulate him on "finding your Bond". She then asked whether he had any villains "with the size of five foot four, female" and added that she was "just reminding" him.

A William Hill spokesperson told Geo News that things were "likely to chop and change" before any decision is announced. Odds reflect betting and publicity, not casting news, so this is a market story rather than a confirmed one.

## Who else is in the market

Ahead of Pugh is Margaret Qualley, who has firmed from 2/1 to 5/4. Third, at 4/1, is [Suki Waterhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suki_Waterhouse), the actress and singer. Geo News notes that casting her would put Pattinson opposite his own fiancée. It reports the pair became engaged in December 2023 and had a daughter in July 2024.

Presenter Maya Jama and Anya Taylor-Joy are also named as contenders, though without quoted odds. Pattinson was confirmed as 007 just over a week ago, and Geo News says no casting decision has been announced.

## A reported Cotswolds house hunt

Separately, Plataforma Media reports that Pattinson and Waterhouse are looking for a country home in the Cotswolds, citing sources quoted by The Sun. It says they want space for their two-year-old daughter and expect a heavy UK-based production schedule.

The article adds that the area is also home to [Meghan Markle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meghan%2C_Duchess_of_Sussex) and Prince Harry, who it says returned to the UK earlier this year. None of this has been confirmed by Pattinson or his representatives, and the sourcing is anonymous. Treat it as a rumour.

The same report says the couple currently own property in Los Angeles. It repeats Pattinson's statement on the casting: "To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement."

## A Bond line that now looks prescient

The casting has also revived an old Twilight moment. Director [Catherine Hardwicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Hardwicke) told The Kyle Buchanan Show that a "Bond, James Bond" line was added after Pattinson stepped out of a car in sunglasses as [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen), as [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/movies/a74539624/robert-pattinson-twilight-james-bond-scene/) reports. "I guess we were seeing the future," she said. We covered that in more detail in [Twilight Director Says Robert Pattinson Foretold His Bond Role](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-twilight-line/).

Digital Spy adds that Pierce Brosnan and George Lazenby have welcomed him, and that [Judi Dench has backed the choice](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-judi-dench/).

## What happens next

Nothing official has been said about who will star opposite Pattinson, so the odds will keep moving with each interview and rumour. Everything we publish on the actor is on our [Robert Pattinson page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).

According to William Hill odds reported by Geo News, Margaret Qualley is the early favourite at 5/4. Florence Pugh is second at 3/1 and Suki Waterhouse third at 4/1.

No. Geo News says no casting decision has been announced and no release date has been set for the film.

Denis Villeneuve is directing the forthcoming Amazon instalment, according to Geo News.

It is a report only. Plataforma Media cites anonymous sources quoted by The Sun, and nothing has been confirmed.
