**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) is the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), with a debut likely in 2028, according to The Guardian.
- It will be the longest gap between Bond films since the character reached the screen in 1962.
- Amazon paid US$1 billion last year for creative control of the franchise, The Guardian reports.
- Netflix has announced 15 January as the premiere date for his heist film The Take.

Robert Pattinson has spent the week at the centre of the biggest casting story in cinema, and The Guardian's weekend newsletter has now laid out what he is walking into. His 007 debut is likely to arrive in 2028, which would mean a wait of about seven years since Daniel Craig's last outing, and he has a heist thriller to release first.

## Why the gap is so long

The Guardian's Sian Cain points out that [No Time to Die](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2026/nov/20/robert-pattinson-and-james-bonds-baggage-problem) reached cinemas five years ago. By the time Pattinson steps into the role, she writes, it will be the longest stretch without a Bond film since the series began in 1962.

The audition process also played out in public, which helped feed the buzz. People hold strong opinions about who Bond should be, Cain notes, and the franchise is now in new hands. Amazon paid US$1 billion last year for creative control, taking the character away from the Broccoli family, who had steered the films for 63 years.

## Amazon, Bezos and the product placement

According to The Guardian, Jeff Bezos said this week that he personally watched several rounds of auditions and sensed "something special" in Pattinson. Some fans worry about spin-off rumours, including a teenage Bond and films for Moneypenny and Q. Those remain rumours.

The money is part of the story. The Guardian says one executive bidding for product placement in the first Pattinson film told The New York Times that a seven-figure offer is needed just to start a conversation. Watches, cars, suits and phones all carry a price tag in a Bond frame.

## A blockbuster actor with an indie streak

Cain argues Pattinson can straddle both worlds. His credits span [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter), Twilight, Batman and Dune, alongside smaller films such as The Lighthouse, Cosmopolis and [Die My Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_My_Love). His recent film Primetime, directed by Lance Oppenheim, holds an 86 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score, and reviews singled out his performance.

Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who is making the next Bond film, has just worked with him on Dune Part III. The Guardian quotes Villeneuve saying he wanted an actor who could be believed as "a killer", with "that kind of anger deep in the eye". Our earlier report on [why Villeneuve picked Pattinson](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-villeneuve-2/) covers that in more detail.

The newsletter also repeats a report that Josh O'Connor was an early favourite but stepped back because he did not want the "baggage" of the role, and that Pattinson joined late and negotiated a three-film deal instead of four. The Guardian presents both points as reports, not confirmed facts. We have not seen Amazon confirm either.

## The Take arrives first

Before Bond, Pattinson is in The Take. Netflix has announced a 15 January premiere, according to [Digg's summary](https://digg.com/entertainment/hwdo213w) of the streamer's posts. The cast includes [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones). [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles) directs a script by [Simon Kinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Kinberg).

Netflix describes a bank guard, a teller and a master thief caught in a heist full of cons and double crosses. It has not said which actor plays which part. The announcement also does not state a year, though film databases list the release as 2027. A separate report says a limited cinema run will open the same day, but Netflix's own posts do not mention one. For more, see our post on [Pattinson leading The Take](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-the-take/).

## What happens next

The first Pattinson Bond film is expected around 2028, with Villeneuve directing. Until then, The Take on 15 January is the next chance to see him on screen, and our [Robert Pattinson tag](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/) will follow the casting news as it develops.
