**Quick summary**

- [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), a 21-year-old Australian actor, plays the young Haymitch Abernathy in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- The film reaches cinemas on 20 November, with early screenings from 19 November, according to Biography.com.
- Zada was born in Sydney and says he beat 2,700 other actors to the part.
- His recent credits include *We Were Liars* and Netflix's *East of Eden*.

A relatively new face is about to become very familiar. Joseph Zada, a 21-year-old actor from Sydney, takes on the role of young Haymitch Abernathy in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opens in cinemas today after early screenings began on 19 November, according to [Biography.com](https://www.biography.com/actors/a74481307/joseph-zada-facts).

## What is Sunrise on the Reaping about?

The film is the latest prequel in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) franchise, which made stars of [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) and [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson). It is set in Panem 24 years before Katniss and Peeta's first Games and follows the 50th annual Hunger Games, also called the second Quarter Quell. That edition has 48 tributes instead of the usual 24.

It adapts the 2025 book of the same name by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins). Zada plays the 16-year-old Haymitch, the character [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) played in the original films. Biography.com notes that Haymitch wins the contest, but tragedy follows him home.

The supporting cast is large. [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin) and [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) play younger versions of characters first played by [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks), [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci) and [Philip Seymour Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Seymour_Hoffman).

## Who is Joseph Zada?

Zada was born Joseph Cumpston on 21 July 2005 in Sydney. Last year he took the surname Zada from his maternal grandmother. He told Vanity Fair in October that the change gave him "a bit of power", as in "I am an actor!", according to Biography.com.

Acting runs in the family. His father, [Jeremy Cumpston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Cumpston), is a doctor who also acted, including a doctor's role on the Australian drama *All Saints*. His mother, Jessica Brentnall, is a film producer, and his brother Hal is an actor.

Zada credits his father with his work ethic. "You work as hard as you can, and then you let it go because there's nothing else that you can worry about," he said, as quoted by Biography.com.

## His career so far

His first credit came in 2019 with *Bilched*, and his biggest parts have been in book adaptations. Last year he played Johnny Sinclair Dennis in the Amazon Prime Video series *We Were Liars*, based on the young adult thriller by E. Lockhart.

He then starred as Cal Trask in Netflix's seven-episode *East of Eden* (2026), drawn from John Steinbeck's [1952 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_of_Eden_(novel)). In it he follows [James Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Dean), who played Cal in an earlier film adaptation.

## Winning the part and learning from Woody Harrelson

Zada says the role came out of a field of 2,700 actors. He described the audition process to Vanity Fair as an "unnecessary hell". Once he had it, the parallels with Harrelson began to show. The two were born in July, two days apart, as is the character Haymitch. Neither is keen on technology. Zada called phones "an epidemic", while Harrelson does not own a mobile at all and congratulated Zada through a text sent by his niece.

To prepare, Zada told The Hollywood Reporter in November that he spent a month studying everything Harrelson had made. He did not want to copy him. He told Rolling Stone AU/NZ the aim was to keep "some of Woody's energy" while building his own performance. In a Lionsgate teaser he said sharing that space with Harrelson was "so special".

## What happens next

The film is now in cinemas worldwide, according to Biography.com, so audience and critic reaction is the next test for the young cast. Zada told People the film "delivers on everything audiences distinctly love" about the franchise. No figures on its opening are available yet.
