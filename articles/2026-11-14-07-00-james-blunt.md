**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has the UK's number one album with his eighth studio record, *Airplane Mode*, according to the Official Charts Company.
- It is his first chart-topper in 19 years, since *All The Lost Souls* in 2007, and his third overall.
- He had promised to change his name to Ryan Air if it reached the top, and he signed off his celebration post that way.
- Royal Blood's *Dead Company* is second, with Olivia Rodrigo third.

*Airplane Mode* has landed at number one on the Official Albums Chart, giving [James Blunt](https://moduscarry.com/tag/james-blunt/) his first UK chart-topper in 19 years. The result was announced on Friday 13 November, a week after the album's release, and it comes with a joke promise attached: a legal name change to Ryan Air.

## How James Blunt's number one album came together

The Official Charts Company [confirmed](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/james-blunt-airplane-mode-number-1-album/) that the 12-track record gives the singer his third number one album. His last was *All The Lost Souls* in 2007. His debut, [*Back To Bedlam*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_Bedlam), spent 10 weeks at the summit after its 2005 release, as [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/music/news/james-blunt-new-album-charts-b3084716.html) notes.

Blunt, 52, thanked everyone who bought, downloaded or streamed the record. "From the bottom of my heart, thank you so much," he told Official Charts. Asked how he would celebrate, he said he planned to get "absolutely pissed". Metro [adds](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/13/james-blunt-changes-name-budget-airline-album-lands-number-1-29791820/) that it is his ninth top 10 album.

The win was not guaranteed. Official Charts describes a "fiercely contested race" for the top spot. Royal Blood's *Dead Company* debuted at number two, after the band had an unbroken run of four number ones.

## The Ryan Air promise

Blunt has a record for chart-related stunts. Official Charts says he made a similar pledge in 2024, when a reissue of *Back To Bedlam* peaked at number seven. This time he said that if *Airplane Mode* reached number one, he would legally become Ryan Air, a nod to the budget airline.

The [Daily Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/james-blunt-ryan-air-chart-37876283) reports that a man known on X as Joe Bangles, who asks celebrities for their favourite cheese, offered to change his own name to James Blunt if the album hit number one. He also offered £1,000 to Blunt's chosen charity if the singer named his cheese. Metro's account of the exchange shows Blunt checking the terms, then answering "Deal. People, let's get to work."

When the chart came out, Blunt posted that his charity was the Blue Marine Foundation and that his favourite cheese is Delice de Bourgogne. He added: "And you're no longer called Joe." He signed off as Ryan Air. The man behind the account replied that he could "hang up my celeb cheese fact finding gloves" and called the album "amazing".

Earlier in the week Blunt had refused to answer the same question on *This Morning*. He told presenters Dermot O'Leary and [Alison Hammond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Hammond) the viewer was "a plant", according to the Mirror.

## The Celebrity Traitors effect

The record arrived while Blunt was in the spotlight for [*The Celebrity Traitors*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors). Reports from [Radio News Hub](https://www.radionewshub.com/articles/showbiz/James-Blunt-bags-first-number-one-album-in-almost-20-years-after-Traitors-stint) and the [Manchester Evening News](https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/showbiz-news/james-blunt-lands-major-career-34909954) say he reached the BBC show's final and was banished by Sebastian "Bash" Croft and Romesh Ranganathan. They won the game and £83,000 for their chosen charities, Choose Love and the Refugee Council.

Blunt has said he joined the series to promote his new music rather than to play the game to the end. The chart result suggests the plan worked, though none of the sources attribute the sales to the show directly.

## What else is on the chart

According to [Hits Daily Double](https://www.hitsdailydouble.com/news/charts/uk-charts-james-blunt-2026-11-13), the top five also includes four new entries. Olivia Rodrigo's *You Seem Pretty Sad For A Girl So In Love* is third. Skepta's *The Fork and Knife*, his first solo album in seven years, is fourth. Kings of Leon's self-produced *O My Beloved* is fifth.

[Steven Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Wilson) takes his fifth solo top 10 at number six. The late George Michael's *The Faith Tour*, drawn from his 1988 Paris concerts, is seventh. On the singles chart, Jamie Miller's "Babydoll" holds number one for a fourth week.

## What happens next

The name change is not yet official. Metro reports it has not been made official by deed poll, and the singer's wife, Sofia Blunt, wrote on Instagram: "So proud of you. I'm not changing my name though." Whether Blunt follows through legally has not been confirmed.
