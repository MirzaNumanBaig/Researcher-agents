**Quick summary**

- Ryan Reynolds Deadpool 4 is reportedly in early development at Marvel Studios, according to The Hollywood Reporter, with [Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) expected to return as Wade Wilson.
- No writer or director is reportedly attached, and Marvel has not made an official announcement.
- The reported project follows *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which grossed more than US$1.3 billion worldwide in 2024.
- At Fanatics Fest 2026, Reynolds said "there eventually will be another Deadpool".

Marvel Studios has reportedly started early work on a fourth film, and [Ryan Reynolds](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-reynolds/) is said to be heading back into the red suit. The Ryan Reynolds Deadpool 4 news comes from The Hollywood Reporter, as relayed by several outlets this week. The studio has not confirmed it.

## What has been reported about Deadpool 4

According to [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/movies/709501-ryan-reynolds-another-deadpool-movie-marvel), which cites The Hollywood Reporter, Reynolds has another Deadpool movie in the works after the success of *Deadpool & Wolverine*. [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-ryan-reynolds-is-reportedly-suiting-up-for-another-deadpool-movie-at-marvel-studios) adds that the project is at an early stage and that no screenwriter or director has officially boarded it.

That is a long way from a green light. [Times Now](https://www.timesnownews.com/entertainment-news/hollywood/deadpool-4-in-works-ryan-reynolds-to-make-mcu-return-as-next-installment-enters-early-development-report-article-156570489) notes that details of the plot, supporting cast and creative team have not emerged, and that Marvel has stayed quiet. Treat the casting as a report, not a confirmed deal.

## Why Marvel would want another one

The numbers explain the interest. *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which brought Hugh Jackman back as Wolverine, earned more than US$1.3 billion worldwide and became the highest-grossing R-rated film of all time, according to SuperHeroHype. The first two films, released in 2016 and 2018, each took roughly US$780 million, per Netflix Junkie. So the third film made about 1.7 times what either earlier one did.

Netflix Junkie also says the 2024 film laid groundwork for *Avengers: Doomsday*. Reynolds has played Wade Wilson in all three films so far.

## What Reynolds has said

The actor was not always so keen. After 2024, Netflix Junkie reports, he questioned whether another solo outing might feel "a little iterative and redundant", and earlier this year argued that the character works best on "scarcity and surprise".

His tone shifted at Fanatics Fest 2026. As quoted by [Odisha TV](https://odishatv.in/entertainment/deadpool-4-on-the-cards-ryan-reynolds-set-to-return-as-marvel-begins-work-on-fourth-film-report-12844046), he said: "There's a few really deep cuts I think are missing from the movies... There's stuff upcoming. There eventually will be another Deadpool." Netflix Junkie says he named stories by Deadpool co-creator Fabian Nicieza and writer Gerry Duggan as untapped material. He did not reveal a storyline.

Our earlier coverage looked at how [Deadpool plans stayed open](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-publicist-deadpool-future/) when Reynolds changed publicist.

## A lighter note from Reynolds this week

Away from Marvel, Reynolds marked Thanksgiving with an Instagram post on 25 November. He paired a still from *Planes, Trains and Automobiles*, released 39 years earlier, with an image from *Deadpool & Wolverine*. He wrote that he has "memorized every word" of the John Hughes comedy and that the Marvel film contains some "very obvious homages" to it, according to [Jang](https://jang.com.pk/en/76906-ryan-reynolds-breaks-silence-after-blake-lively-dragged-in-taylor-swifts-controversy-news).

Separately, a 2022 GQ interview resurfaced this week. [David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour), who starred in [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things), said he rang Reynolds on the opening day of the 2019 *Hellboy* reboot to ask whether an actor could survive a big flop, per [Giant Freakin Robot](https://www.giantfreakinrobot.com/ent/david-harbour-called-ryan-reynolds-after-hellboy.html). Harbour called Reynolds's reply "sweet" but gave no detail.

## What happens next

Expect little for now. With no writer or director attached, an official announcement, a release window and a cast list are all still to come. Marvel has not commented. Any confirmation will most likely arrive through the studio or Reynolds himself. Follow [our Movies coverage](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) for updates.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Deadpool 4 confirmed?

No. The Hollywood Reporter reports that Marvel Studios has begun early development, but the studio has not made an official announcement.

### Will Ryan Reynolds return as Deadpool?

He is reportedly expected to. Reynolds has played Wade Wilson in all three films and said at Fanatics Fest 2026 that another Deadpool will eventually happen.

### Who is writing or directing Deadpool 4?

Nobody has been named. Reports say no writer or director has been attached yet.

### How much did Deadpool & Wolverine make?

It grossed more than US$1.3 billion worldwide, making it the highest-grossing R-rated film ever, according to the outlets covering the story.
