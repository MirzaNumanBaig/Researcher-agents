**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) has released "Too Much to Ask", a song she performs in the animated film [Ray Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn).
- The song was written by two-time Grammy winner Kimbra and is available on streaming platforms, according to Music Mundial.
- Netflix has submitted it for consideration in the Best Original Song category at the Oscars.
- Ray Gunn opens in select cinemas on 4 December and reaches Netflix on 18 December.

Scarlett Johansson has released "Too Much to Ask", an original song she sings in Netflix's animated feature Ray Gunn. The track, written by Kimbra, is now on all major streaming platforms, [Music Mundial reports](https://www.musicmundial.com/scarlett-johansson-releases-too-much-to-ask-original-song-by-kimbra-for-netflix-s-ray-gunn), a few weeks before the film arrives.

## Who wrote and sings "Too Much to Ask"

Kimbra, the New Zealand artist who has won two Grammys, wrote the song for the film. Johansson performs it as Venus Nova, a huge multimedia star inside the story's own world. Music Mundial describes the track as one of the most emotional moments on the soundtrack.

The outlet also says the pairing has drawn praise, with Johansson's vocal range and Kimbra's knack for cinematic hooks singled out. That is the outlet's characterisation, and it did not quote specific reviews, so treat it as early reaction rather than a critical verdict.

## What Ray Gunn is about

Ray Gunn is a Skydance Animation film for Netflix, directed by [Brad Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Bird), the filmmaker behind The Incredibles and Ratatouille. It is set in Metropia, a vast retro-futuristic city imagined as it looked from the vantage of 1939.

According to Music Mundial, the plot follows a private detective pulled into a case that involves aliens, a murder and the mysterious Venus Nova. That makes Johansson's character central to the mystery as well as to the music.

The voice cast is led by Johansson, [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) and [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits). We covered the film's story trailer earlier this month in [Scarlett Johansson's Ray Gunn Gets a Story Trailer](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn-trailer-2/).

## The Oscar angle

Music Mundial says Netflix has formally submitted "Too Much to Ask" for the Best Original Song category at the Oscars. A submission is only the first step. Studios put forward many songs each season, and only a small number reach the nominations list, so nothing is decided yet.

## What happens next

Ray Gunn opens in select cinemas on 4 December and begins streaming on Netflix on 18 December, according to the same report. Cinema listings for Australia have not been confirmed in the sources we read, so local viewers should expect the Netflix date to be the reliable one.
