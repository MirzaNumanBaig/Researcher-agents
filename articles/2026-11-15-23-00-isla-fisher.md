**Quick summary**

- [Isla Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_Fisher) has joined the cast of the thriller *Captivated*, led by Al Pacino, according to an exclusive report dated 14 November 2026.
- The film is inspired by the 1973 kidnapping of John Paul Getty III in Italy.
- Blake Ritson and Jackson Kelly are also in the cast, and Dito Montiel is directing.
- Filming is under way in Calabria and Rome. No release date has been announced.
- Fisher's role has not been disclosed.

Isla Fisher is the latest name attached to *Captivated*, a crime thriller built around the 1973 kidnapping of John Paul Getty III. The Australian-born actress joins Oscar winner [Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino) and [Blake Ritson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Ritson) in the project, which is now in production in Italy, according to [JournalArta](https://journalarta.com/en/2026/11/15/isla-fisher-al-pacino-captivated-getty-kidnapping-film/).

## What we know about Isla Fisher's role in Captivated

Reports so far say little about the part she will play. [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/691132-isla-fisher-lands-major-role-alongside-al-pacino-for-crime-thriller-captivated) describes her role as still a secret, and none of the other outlets name her character.

Coverage instead leans on her back catalogue. [Zoom TV](https://www.zoomtventertainment.com/hollywood/isla-fisher-blake-ritson-join-al-pacino-in-captivated-film-article-156508556) points to her 2005 comedy *Wedding Crashers* and her voice work in animated films such as *Rango* and *Rise of the Guardians*. Geo News singles out *Now You See Me*. For readers of our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) coverage, it is a sharp turn from comedy into a dark true-crime setting.

## The story behind the film

The real case is among the best-known abductions of the twentieth century. John Paul Getty III was a teenager when he was taken in Italy in 1973. He was the grandson of oil billionaire J. Paul Getty, and Geo News reports that his grandfather refused to pay the US$17 million the kidnappers demanded. The kidnappers later cut off the teenager's ear and sent it to a Rome newspaper, and he was released in December 1973.

The makers say their version takes a different angle from earlier screen treatments. Zoom TV notes the film aims to explore parts of the story not covered in Ridley Scott's *All the Money in the World* or the FX series *Trust*.

## Who else is in the cast

Pacino plays an older version of Saro Mammoliti, according to [Mathrubhumi English](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/movies-music/news/isla-fisher-blake-ritson-join-al-pacino-captivated-ptihb4gqtq8), which credits the entertainment outlet Deadline for the news. Geo News describes the character as a mafia boss from Calabria. It also reports that [Katie Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Holmes) will play Gail Getty, the kidnapped teenager's mother, and that the story centres on an unexpected bond between her and Mammoliti. That detail comes from a single outlet in the material reviewed.

Ritson is known for *The Gilded Age*, while Jackson Kelly appeared in *The Pitt* and *The Body*, per JournalArta.

## Behind the camera

[Dito Montiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dito_Montiel) directs. His earlier credits include *A Guide to Recognizing Your Saints* and *Fighting*. The cinematographer is Stefan Ciupek, whose work includes the Oscar-winning *Slumdog Millionaire*.

The producers are Michael Mammoliti and [Manasvi Mamgai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manasvi_Mamgai) of 32Red Entertainment, [Andrea Bucko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Bucko) and Nicky Weinstock of Sweetheart Entertainment, and [Cassian Elwes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassian_Elwes). Mammoliti is also a co-writer. He called the project "deeply personal" because his family has a direct connection to the events, and said he grew up with a very different understanding of the story from the one audiences have seen on screen.

Bucko said the team was proud to finally share the film "after a decade of work". Voltage Pictures is handling international sales, while CAA Media Finance and WME are handling domestic distribution.
