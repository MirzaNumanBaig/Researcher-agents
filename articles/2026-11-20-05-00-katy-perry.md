**Quick summary**

- A Daily Mail report, relayed by Yahoo's Reality Tea, says [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) is in "active discussions" about a Katy Perry book and a streaming series. Perry has not confirmed it.
- The report says her team has been in touch with Netflix and Hulu.
- Separately, Los Angeles police arrested two people after break-ins that included a Mulholland Drive home reportedly once owned by Perry.
- Perry is not linked to the burglary in any way beyond the former ownership.

[Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) is reportedly considering telling her side of her short marriage to Russell Brand in a book and a streaming series, according to a Daily Mail report relayed on 19 November. Nothing has been announced, and Perry has not commented. Separately, police in Los Angeles have arrested two people after a burglary at a Hollywood Hills house she once owned.

## What the report says about a book and series

According to Yahoo's republication of a [Reality Tea](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/katy-perry-plans-expose-dark-175609545.html) article, which in turn cites the Daily Mail, the 42-year-old singer is weighing a Netflix-style series and an autobiography. An unnamed source told the Mail that she and her team have been in "active discussions", and that her team has reportedly spoken with Netflix and Hulu.

The source also said Perry had received several offers over the years but would not consider any until Russell Brand's trial was over. That is a single anonymous account. Treat it as a report, not a decision.

Perry and Brand married in October 2010, and the marriage lasted about 14 months. In a 2013 Vogue interview, she described him as "very controlling" and said he told her by text message on 31 December 2011 that he was ending it, according to the same article.

## Why the timing matters

Brand was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, according to the Reality Tea report. The offences were alleged to have taken place between 2004 and 2009.

The report says Perry did not give evidence during the four-week trial at Southwark Crown Court, and that Brand's lawyers reportedly stopped prosecutors from raising the marriage. She has not publicly addressed the conviction. For more on how her family has responded, see our earlier post on [Katy Perry's father declining to discuss the Russell Brand verdict](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-father-russell-brand/).

## Police arrest two after break-in at her former home

In unrelated news, the Los Angeles Police Department says it arrested two suspects following a pursuit of nearly 30 miles, according to [FOX 11 Los Angeles](https://www.foxla.com/news/news-katy-perry-former-home-us-open-champ-la-burglary-arrests).

Around 10pm on Tuesday, two masked men reportedly broke into a Sherman Oaks home belonging to a former US Open tennis champion, who was not there. Her mother and her mother's husband were inside, and the intruders fled. A few hours later, near midnight, the same suspects allegedly struck a Mulholland Drive house near Runyon Canyon that was reportedly once owned by Perry.

Officers already had the group under surveillance. When they arrived, the suspects were loading a car and drove off, leading police down the 110 Freeway South to Gardena, where the car crashed. Three men were detained, two of them teenagers. One teen was released to his parents. The other teenager and an adult were formally arrested. Police found suitcases of items they believe were stolen.

LAPD Chief Jim McDonnell said the group is a South Los Angeles burglary crew with prior burglary arrests. Detectives are checking whether it is also linked to two break-in attempts in Encino. Police have not named the suspects or the tennis player.

## What happens next

Neither Perry nor her representatives have confirmed any book or series, so there is no release date, publisher or platform. Any real deal would normally be announced by the company involved.
