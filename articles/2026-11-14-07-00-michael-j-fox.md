**Quick summary**

- TVLine published a piece on 13 November 2026 calling *Spin City* a forgotten 1990s gem that is worth watching again.
- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) played Deputy Mayor Mike Flaherty in the New York political comedy.
- According to TVLine, Fox left after Season 4 following his Parkinson's disease disclosure, and Charlie Sheen took over as the lead.
- The show ran for six seasons before it was cancelled.

A new TVLine feature has put Michael J. Fox's *Spin City* back in the spotlight, arguing that the 1990s sitcom is sharper and funnier than its reputation suggests. [TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2310945/michael-j-fox-political-sitcom-spin-city-worth-watching-today/) published the piece on 13 November 2026, describing the show as one of the best of its decade that "nobody talks about today".

## What Spin City is about

[*Spin City*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_City) follows the staff of New York City Mayor Randall Winston, played by [Barry Bostwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Bostwick). The people who keep him out of trouble are led by Deputy Mayor Mike Flaherty, Fox's character. TVLine says the show's central joke is the chaos of getting a politician elected and then keeping a term free of scandal, especially when that politician is not the most focused person in the room.

The supporting cast, according to TVLine, was packed with character actors, including [Richard Kind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Kind), [Alan Ruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ruck) and [Connie Britton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connie_Britton). TVLine also credits the writers' room as one of the strongest of the decade, and says the show's fast-talking style anticipated comedies such as *Veep* by about 15 years.

## The people behind the show

The series was created by [Gary David Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_David_Goldberg), who also created Fox's earlier hit [*Family Ties*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties). The other co-creator and writer was [Bill Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Lawrence_(TV_producer)). He left the series to create *Scrubs* and has since helped develop shows including [*Ted Lasso*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso) and *Shrinking*, TVLine notes.

That Lawrence connection is the reason Fox fans keep finding the show again. TVLine says Fox and Lawrence later reunited on *Scrubs* and, more recently, on *Shrinking*. ## Why Fox left

According to TVLine, *Spin City* was a steady ratings hit for its first four years, with a top-20 finish in its debut season. During Season 3, Fox revealed that he had been living with Parkinson's disease for several years and that his symptoms were worsening. TVLine says that disclosure ultimately led to his departure after Season 4.

The same season also marked the exit of Lawrence and several ensemble members, so the producers had to rebuild the show. TVLine reports that [Charlie Sheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Sheen) replaced Fox and brought a more sardonic tone than Fox's quick-witted delivery. Ratings never returned to their earlier level. Even Fox's return for three episodes in Season 6 did not help, and the show was cancelled after a weak final year.

## What happens next

TVLine's verdict is that all six seasons reward a rewatch, though it says the show was at its best with Fox in the lead and Lawrence in the writers' room. The piece does not announce any revival or new release, so this is a retrospective rather than a news development. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who played the lead in Spin City?
Michael J. Fox played Deputy Mayor Mike Flaherty for the first four seasons. Charlie Sheen replaced him after Fox left the series, according to TVLine.

### Why did Michael J. Fox leave Spin City?
TVLine reports that Fox revealed during Season 3 that he had lived with Parkinson's disease for several years and that his symptoms were getting worse. He left after Season 4.

### How many seasons did Spin City run?
Six. TVLine says Fox returned for three Season 6 episodes, but the show was cancelled after a poor year in the ratings.
