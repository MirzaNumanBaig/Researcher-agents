**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and Elle Fanning's Paramount+ legal thriller began filming in downtown Dallas this month.
- The Dallas Film Commission says shooting continues through the end of October.
- The eight-episode series is due on Paramount+ in 2027, and A24 is producing.
- Kidman also attended the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles on Sunday 18 October, wearing Jean Paul Gaultier.

Nicole Kidman's next television project is now shooting on the streets of Dallas. The Paramount+ legal thriller she leads with Elle Fanning began filming in downtown Dallas in October and will run until the end of the month, according to the Dallas Film Commission, as reported by [The Dallas Morning News via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/nicole-kidman-elle-fanning-led-192227041.html).

## What is Nicole Kidman's Dallas show about?

The series is set at a prestigious Dallas law firm. Fanning plays a summer associate who clashes with a partner, played by Kidman, after uncovering what the synopsis calls "a web of NDAs masking a dark truth". It runs to eight episodes and is due on Paramount+ in 2027.

Paramount+ has called the show "Discretion". The Dallas Film Commission told The Dallas Morning News on Monday that the production has been using a different working title, "The Summers". The streamer announced in August that production was underway.

Dallas-born author Chandler Baker is an executive producer and writer. The series is based on a short story of hers, and A24 is producing it.

## Who else is in the cast?

Alongside Kidman and Fanning, the show stars [Josh Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Lucas), [Stephan James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephan_James_(actor)) and [Colton Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colton_Ryan). [Zazie Beetz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zazie_Beetz), [Ben Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Foster_(actor)), [Stella Baker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_Baker) and John Hoogenakker are listed as recurring cast members.

The Dallas shoot is one of several big productions in North Texas right now. The Dallas Morning News notes that Taylor Sheridan's *Landman* is filming in Fort Worth, including a recent stop at Texas Christian University, and that the Christian series *The Chosen* recently finished shooting in Midlothian.

## Kidman at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show

Away from the set, Kidman was in the front row at the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show at Sony Pictures Studios in Los Angeles on Sunday. According to [WWD](https://wwd.com/pop-culture/celebrity-news/nicole-kidman-jean-paul-gaultier-victorias-secret-show-1239374801/), it was the first time the show has been held in Los Angeles since 2007. The brand revived the runway event in 2024 after a six-year break.

Kidman wore a look from Jean Paul Gaultier's fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection: a white collared shirt, a high-waisted pencil skirt with garter-inspired seams and a black and pink striped tie. She added pointed-toe pumps and a Chanel handbag, and WWD says stylist Jason Bolden put the outfit together. The outlet linked the suited look back to her "Babygirl" era.

The collection was designed by Jean Paul Gaultier creative director Duran Lantink and debuted in March during Paris Fashion Week.

## The song that caught her attention

The moment that drew the most attention came from the soundtrack. [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/10/19/nicole-kidman-blushes-adela-runway-song/) reported that Slovak singer Adéla's song "Nicole Kidman" played while models walked, and that the 59-year-old was seen laughing and covering her face. The song name-drops her films *Moulin Rouge!* and *Babygirl*, according to TMZ.

TMZ also says Kidman had acknowledged the 22-year-old singer's music before. It reports she sat beside Vanessa Bryant during the show.

The runway itself featured Adriana Lima, Alessandra Ambrosio, Candice Swanepoel, Gigi and Bella Hadid, Anok Yai and Alex Consani, WWD reports. Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Megan Moroney and Katseye performed.

## What happens next

Filming in Dallas is scheduled to continue through the end of October, per the Dallas Film Commission. The series is due on Paramount+ in 2027, though the sources give no premiere date.
