**Quick summary**

- Puck's Matthew Belloni reported on Friday 13 November 2026 that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) looks like the frontrunner to play the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- The report says Pattinson, 40, would commit to three films, while two rivals were asked for four, and that he was offered considerably more money.
- None of this has been confirmed by Amazon MGM, which has not named its Bond.
- IGN reports that an announcement is expected before the end of the year.

Robert Pattinson has been described as the leading candidate to succeed Daniel Craig as 007, based on a Puck report that outlets including the Daily Mail and IGN picked up on 13 November. The claim rests on anonymous sources and the studio has said nothing, so it is best read as informed speculation rather than a decision.

## What the Puck report says

According to [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/robert-pattinsons-james-bond-deal-offers-higher-pay-for-fewer-films-than-rivals), Belloni reported that five actors on the shortlist signed contracts for Bond 26 with options for sequels: Pattinson, [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Josh O'Connor and [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner). IGN notes that this is standard practice on Bond films and was also done when Craig auditioned for Casino Royale.

The difference, Belloni wrote, is in the terms. Citing three sources, he said O'Connor and Lowden had to sign up for four films if chosen, while Pattinson lowered his commitment to three and secured "a whole lot more money than the others". The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16290137/james-bond-robert-pattinson-casting-role-race-frontrunner.html) says Belloni argued the package suggests the studio is "willing or even eager" to give up a little to land a name star for its most important franchise.

Belloni also doubted Amazon would choose its Bond on price. Pattinson is the oldest of the five and, as IGN puts it, the biggest star, which makes him the most expensive option.

## Why the deal was reportedly hard to close

[The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/films/news/james-bond-robert-pattinson-movies-007-b3084626.html) reports that Pattinson's test deal was described as "difficult to close", possibly because he also plays Batman, another major franchise role. Belloni's sources said some involved wondered why the filmmakers would push so hard if they did not truly want him.

IGN adds that Pattinson and Hoult reportedly tested after the three lesser-known actors, which might suggest the producers were not fully convinced by the earlier auditions. That is an inference in the report, not a confirmed fact. Belloni also wrote that he was surprised Pattinson agreed to test at all, given his track record and his history with the director, which he took as a sign the actor wants the part.

## What the other contenders have said

Lowden told GQ the attention was flattering but that he was "not really going to add to the noise", according to Digital Spy and The Independent. O'Connor told The Hollywood Reporter that being mentioned alongside the role was a "pinch me moment" and that he had nothing more to add.

Director Denis Villeneuve was asked about the Pattinson rumours and declined to answer directly. As [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/movies/a74426162/robert-pattinson-james-bond-deal/) quotes him, he should not comment because everything he says will be scrutinised, but he is "having a great time". Digital Spy also reminds readers that all of this remains reports.

## The case against

Not everyone wants it. In a Substack column, critic Vince Mancini argues that Pattinson is better placed as one of Hollywood's most adventurous role-choosers. He points to collaborations with [Robert Eggers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Eggers), [David Cronenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cronenberg), [Claire Denis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Denis) and the [Safdie brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safdie_brothers), and to this year's The Drama, The Odyssey and Primetime. His worry is that a long Bond shoot would crowd out that kind of work. It is an opinion piece, but it captures a debate fans are having.

## What happens next

IGN says all signs point to an official announcement before the end of 2026, which would sit close to the release of Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three, in which Pattinson also stars. The new film will be the 26th Bond picture and follows Craig's last outing, No Time To Die, released in October 2021. Amazon bought the franchise's longtime backers at MGM in January 2025, according to The Independent.

Until Amazon MGM speaks, the Pattinson frontrunner label is one reporter's reading of leaked deal terms. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has Robert Pattinson been cast as James Bond?

No. Amazon MGM has not announced its choice. The reports about Pattinson come from Puck's Matthew Belloni, citing unnamed sources.

### Who else is reportedly in the running?

Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner are named as the other four actors who screen-tested.

### Who is directing the next Bond film?

Denis Villeneuve, known for the Dune films, is directing the 26th Bond film.

### When will the new Bond be announced?

IGN reports that signs point to an announcement before the end of 2026, but there is no official date.
