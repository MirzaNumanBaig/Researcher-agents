**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) shared a deed poll screenshot on Instagram on 16 November, switching his name from James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air.
- He said the application cost £53.05.
- The promise followed his album Airplane Mode reaching No. 1 on the UK albums chart on 13 November, his first chart-topper in 19 years, according to the Official Charts Company.
- A separate cheese bet has raised £4,500 for the Blue Marine Foundation, and new 2027 tour dates have been announced, including Lisbon.

James Blunt has gone ahead with the joke that fans dared him to finish. The singer posted a screenshot on 16 November showing a deed poll application to become Ryan Bel Air, and captioned it: "This cost me £53.05." The move came three days after Airplane Mode, his eighth studio album, reached the top of the UK albums chart.

## How the Ryan Bel Air promise came about

On 11 November, Blunt posted an Instagram video explaining the deal. Two years earlier, he said, he had promised to change his name to Blunty McBluntface if his album hit No. 1, and it did not happen. This time he offered a second chance: get Airplane Mode to the top and he would become Ryan Air, a nod to the budget airline, [according to E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1438185/james-blunt-says-he-changed-his-name-to-ryan-air-after-album-success).

The earlier pledge was tied to the anniversary edition of Back to Bedlam. [The London Evening Standard reports](https://www.standard.co.uk/showbiz/james-blunt-name-change-b1305185.html) that it stalled at No. 7, so he was spared. Airline Ryanair joined in with a reply on X, writing "At least people would know you then."

The final name has an extra twist. [Digital Spy notes](https://www.digitalspy.com/tv/a74455406/celebrity-traitors-finalist-james-blunt-confirms-bizarre-name-change/) that the middle name "Bel" reads as a nod to The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air, which ran from 1990 to 1996. Fans filled the comments, with one calling him an "absolute legend". Ryanair had not responded to the change when Digital Spy published.

## Is it official yet?

Not quite, going by the wording of the coverage. [Hotpress](https://www.hotpress.com/music/james-blunt-has-started-process-to-change-name-to-ryan-air-23169541) says he has "started the process" and "requested" the change by deed poll, and the Standard says he "appears" to have changed it. What the public has seen is a screenshot of the application, not a certificate.

A skit he posted the Saturday before adds to the doubt. In it, the "real" Ryanair phones him to warn of "consequences" if he goes ahead, which led some fans to assume the stunt was off. Treat the name as filed rather than confirmed.

## A chart win 19 years in the making

The numbers behind the joke are real. Airplane Mode came out on 6 November and went to No. 1 a week later. The Official Charts Company says it is his first UK number one album in 19 years. His last was All The Lost Souls in 2007, while his 2005 debut Back To Bedlam spent 10 weeks at the top, according to the Standard.

Blunt told the Official Charts Company he was grateful to everyone who downloaded, bought or streamed the record. His plan for celebrating, he added, was "to get absolutely pissed". The campaign also included airport skits posted on social media, and he recently finished as a runner-up on The Celebrity Traitors, where he said he signed up partly to promote the music.

## The cheese bet that came with it

[VICE reports](https://www.vice.com/en/article/james-blunt-files-to-legally-change-his-name-after-losing-bizarre-bet-with-fans/) a second wager sits on top. Writer Alan Pender, who posts as Joe Bangles, had spent about six years asking celebrities for their favourite cheese and could never get an answer from Blunt. After the chart win, Pender offered to give £1,000 of his own money to a charity of Blunt's choice and to change his own name to James Blunt if the singer simply named his cheese.

As of 17 November, Pender and his followers had raised £4,500 for the Blue Marine Foundation. Blunt's answer, according to VICE, is Délice de Bourgogne, a soft cheese.

## What happens next

Blunt will tour Airplane Mode in 2027. [Hotpress lists](https://www.hotpress.com/music/james-blunt-has-started-process-to-change-name-to-ryan-air-23169541) Dublin's 3Arena on 30 March and Belfast's The O2 on 31 March, part of a UK and European run in early 2027. [The Portugal News adds](https://www.theportugalnews.com/news/2026-11-17/british-musician-james-blunt-returns-to-portugal/1117986) Lisbon's Campo Pequeno on 1 July and the Douro & Porto Wine Festival in Lamego on 2 July. Lisbon tickets go on sale on 20 November, with prices not yet announced. No Australian dates appear in the coverage so far.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has James Blunt legally changed his name to Ryan Bel Air?

He has shared a screenshot of a deed poll application showing the change from James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air. Some outlets describe it as filed or started rather than completed.

### How much did the name change cost?

Blunt said it cost him £53.05.

### Is Airplane Mode really his first No. 1 in 19 years?

Yes, according to the Official Charts Company. His previous UK number one album was All The Lost Souls in 2007.

### When does Blunt tour Airplane Mode?

Confirmed dates include Dublin on 30 March 2027, Belfast on 31 March, Lisbon on 1 July and Lamego on 2 July, with more UK and European shows planned.
