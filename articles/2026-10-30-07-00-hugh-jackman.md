**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) has paid tribute to his long-time "X-Men" co-star [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart), who died on Wednesday aged 86, according to Variety.
- Stewart died after what was described as a "brief battle" with Alzheimer's and Lewy Body Dementia.
- Jackman called Stewart "a tremendous friend and man" and said he learned a great deal from him.
- Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige and "Star Trek" actor William Shatner were among those who also paid tribute.
- Separately, Jackman's 2004 film "Van Helsing" has climbed the Prime Video charts, MovieWeb reports.

Hugh Jackman is mourning Patrick Stewart, the actor he worked beside for nearly two decades on the "X-Men" films. Stewart died on Wednesday at the age of 86, Variety reported, and Jackman's tribute was among the first from the film world. His words, as quoted in the outlet's headline, were: "A tremendous friend and man. I learned so much from him."

## A partnership across five films

Jackman and Stewart played Wolverine and Professor Charles Xavier, the volatile fighter and the telepathic teacher who anchored the franchise. According to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/patrick-stewart-dies-hugh-jackman-mourns-x-men-actor-1236934490/), they shared the screen in "X-Men" (2000), "X-Men: The Last Stand" (2006), "X-Men Origins: Wolverine" (2009), "The Wolverine" (2013) and "Logan" (2017). That is a run of 17 years for one on-screen friendship.

Stewart's version of [Professor Charles Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X) was the calm centre of that world. Jackman's Wolverine was its rough edge. The contrast was part of why the pairing worked for audiences, and why the two actors stayed close off screen as well.

## "Logan" as the favourite

Stewart himself singled out the last of those films. Speaking to Variety in 2020, he said he and Jackman were thrilled that their final "X-Men" project was "Logan". He called it the best experience either of them had on the series, because the characters were the same but their world had been "blown apart".

It is a telling choice. "Logan" stripped the superhero formula back to two ageing men trying to look after each other, and Variety's report makes clear Stewart saw it as the high point of the partnership with Jackman.

## Tributes from Marvel and "Star Trek"

Jackman was one of many well-known names to react. Kevin Feige, president of Marvel Studios, described Stewart as "brilliant, charming, warm, generous, with a quiet strength and gravitas". Feige recalled the first time he saw Stewart on the set of the original "X-Men", and said his legacy lived on in "the many, many people he mentored".

Stewart's other defining role was Captain Jean-Luc Picard in "Star Trek". William Shatner, who played Captain Kirk, said Stewart "brought intelligence, grace, and a deep sense of humanity to everything he did". Shatner added that his heart went out to Stewart's wife, Sunny, and the family.

Stewart also appeared in Marvel's 2022 film [Doctor Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Strange) sequel "Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness". Variety notes he is due to reprise Xavier in "Avengers: Doomsday" this December, alongside his close friend Ian McKellen as Magneto.

## Meanwhile, "Van Helsing" finds a new audience

In lighter Jackman news, MovieWeb reports that his 2004 gothic action film "Van Helsing" is having a second life on streaming. The film arrived on Prime Video on 1 September 2026 with little fanfare, but as Halloween approaches it has reached the tenth spot on the service's popularity chart at the time of writing.

The film's history is mixed. MovieWeb says it holds a 24 per cent critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 57 per cent audience score, with the audience figure improving as the film gained a cult following. It grossed more than $300 million worldwide against a reported $160 million budget, which the outlet says was probably still not enough to break even once marketing and cinema cuts are counted.

No funeral or memorial details were included in the Variety report. "Avengers: Doomsday" is due in December, and Variety says it is unclear whether Wolverine will appear in it.
