**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays two characters, Henry Park and Logan Casey, in the new Canadian series Yaga.
- Yaga is a Crave Original and a supernatural mystery based on a play by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler), who adapted it herself.
- US viewers can watch it on AMC+, not HBO Max, according to Yahoo.
- The cast includes [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) and a former Schitt's Creek star.

Hudson Williams has followed his breakout in Heated Rivalry with a double role in Yaga, a spooky Canadian mystery series that Yahoo describes as hot, magical and always close to a darker twist. He plays both a missing billionaire heir and the lookalike who shares his face, in a show built around a large Canadian ensemble.

## What Yaga is about

Yaga is a Crave Original, the same Canadian streamer behind Heated Rivalry. [Yahoo reports](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/hudson-williams-isn-t-only-213531969.html) that the show is a feminist retelling of the Baba Yaga figure from Slavic folklore, set in the fictional coastal town of Whittock.

The story starts when a chatty, flirtatious private detective arrives to look into the disappearance of a rich fishery heir. A local sergeant with a soft spot for romantasy novels is assigned to the same missing persons case.

The series is based on a play by Sandler, who wrote the screen version of her own work. Yahoo says the tone sits somewhere between cosy mystery and something stranger.

## Williams' two characters

Williams plays Henry Park, the billionaire playboy fishery heir who goes missing, and Logan Casey. Logan is Henry's overalls-loving roommate and, by an odd coincidence, his doppelgänger. Yahoo describes Logan as dorky, and he has an on-and-off girlfriend, a student named Pam.

For Williams, it is a quick step from his first big role. Yahoo calls him half of 2025's most impressive Cinderella stories after Heated Rivalry. Before that, it says, he appeared in an episode of CBS' Tracker, in the CBC series Allegiance and in several short films.

Yahoo adds that he can be seen later this year in the Netflix limited series The Altruists, and as himself in the docu-series The Assembly. It also says he turned up in a Laufey video and a Peloton ad this year.

## The rest of the cast

Williams is not the only familiar name. Moss plays Dr Katherine Hargrove, a mysterious professor who teaches a course on bones. Yahoo notes she is best known as Trinity opposite [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) in the Matrix films, and that she won an [Independent Spirit Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_Independent_Spirit_Awards) in 2000 for Memento.

Another lead, according to Yahoo, will be known to most viewers as Patrick from [Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek). That actor also appeared this summer in The Vampire Lestat on AMC, and made his Broadway debut in 2022.

The ensemble also includes a veteran of Letterkenny and The Handmaid's Tale as the sergeant, and an actor best known to many as Anne in the Anne of Green Gables adaptations, who plays a diner owner called Geena. A Canadian award-winner from Women Talking and The Umbrella Academy plays Olena, a local Ukrainian woman whose nickname is, as Yahoo puts it, curious.

## Why the US release matters

Heated Rivalry streamed on HBO Max in the United States. Yaga takes a different route, landing on AMC+, per Yahoo. For Williams, who is now recognisable well beyond Canada, that gives the series a clear North American audience from the start.

## What happens next

Yaga is available now on Crave in Canada and AMC+ in the US. Later this year Williams is due in The Altruists on Netflix and The Assembly. Neither source gave an Australian release date for Yaga, so local viewers should check streaming listings.

He plays two characters: Henry Park, a billionaire fishery heir who goes missing, and Logan Casey, Henry's roommate and lookalike.
