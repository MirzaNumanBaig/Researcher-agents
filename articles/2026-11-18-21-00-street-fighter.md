**Quick summary**

- The new live-action Street Fighter film has passed US$100 million worldwide, about US$100.7 million after 31 days, according to Koimoi.
- That makes it the highest-grossing film in the franchise, ahead of the 1994 version's US$99.4 million.
- It arrives on digital HD through Amazon Video and iTunes on 1 December 2026, according to Wrestlezone.
- Its US theatre count fell from 1,760 to 814 in a week, and the budget was reportedly US$82 million, so profit looks unlikely.

The **Street Fighter movie box office** run has reached a milestone and a warning sign at the same time. The video game adaptation has crossed US$100 million worldwide, becoming the franchise's biggest earner, yet it is losing cinemas quickly and is set for a digital release on 1 December.

## A franchise record with an asterisk

Koimoi reports that the film had earned US$100.74 million worldwide after 31 days in cinemas, including US$41.7 million overseas. That total edges past the 1994 film, which collected US$99.4 million and had held the franchise record until now.

Livemint puts the US share at about US$59.05 million, which adds up to roughly US$100.75 million globally. The two outlets agree on the overall figure, and Wrestlezone, citing Box Office Mojo, also says the film made a little over US$100 million.

The film opened in the US on 16 October 2026 after a world premiere at Grauman's Chinese Theatre on 12 October. It took the top spot that first weekend with US$30.77 million, according to figures from The Numbers quoted by Livemint, the strongest domestic start for any live-action Street Fighter film.

## Why cinemas are letting it go

The start did not last. Livemint reports that takings fell 72% in the second weekend to about US$8.59 million. By the fourth weekend the film had slipped to No. 12 in the weekly domestic rankings.

In its fifth week it played in 814 US theatres, down from 1,760 the week before, a loss of 946 locations. Exhibitors routinely hand screens to newer releases when a film's ticket sales soften, Livemint notes. Koimoi puts the fifth-weekend total at US$754K, with US$217K on the Sunday.

## The budget problem

Reported production costs were US$82 million. The film is co-produced by Legendary Pictures and Capcom and distributed by Paramount Pictures, according to Koimoi.

Gross takings are not the same as studio income, because cinemas keep a share of each ticket and marketing adds to the bill. Koimoi applies an industry rule of thumb that a film must gross about 2.5 times its budget to profit, which it works out at around US$205 million. On that basis it calls the film a likely box office failure. That is Koimoi's own analysis rather than a studio statement.

Reviews did not help. Wrestlezone reports a 42% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), and the film's Wikipedia entry lists 43 out of 100 on Metacritic from 28 critics. The aggregated verdict, in short, praises the film's affection for the games and its energetic style but faults the muddled story and juvenile humour.

## The film and its cast

Director [Kitao Sakurai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitao_Sakurai) wrote the screenplay with T. J. Fixman, from a story by [Dalan Musson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalan_Musson) and [Gary Dauberman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Dauberman). Set in 1993, it follows estranged fighters Ryu, played by Andrew Koji, and Ken Masters, played by [Noah Centineo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Centineo). [Callina Liang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callina_Liang) plays Chun-Li, who pulls them back into the World Warrior Tournament and a conspiracy behind it.

WWE stars [Roman Reigns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Reigns) and Cody Rhodes play Akuma and Guile. The cast also includes [David Dastmalchian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Dastmalchian) as M. Bison, [Vidyut Jammwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vidyut_Jammwal) as Dhalsim, [Orville Peck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orville_Peck) as Vega and [Andrew Schulz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Schulz) as Dan Hibiki. Wrestlezone says that despite the mixed reviews, the wrestlers' performances drew praise.

## What happens next

The film lands on digital HD from Amazon Video and iTunes on 1 December 2026, per Wrestlezone. For viewers who missed it in cinemas, that is the next chance, and home viewing is where many game adaptations find a second audience. No streaming or disc dates were in the sources.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Street Fighter movie available on digital?

It arrives on digital HD from Amazon Video and iTunes on 1 December 2026, according to Wrestlezone. The cinema release was 16 October 2026 in the US.

### How much has the Street Fighter movie made?

About US$100.7 million worldwide after 31 days, roughly US$59 million in the US and US$41.7 million overseas, according to Koimoi and Livemint.

### Did Street Fighter make a profit?

Not on current figures. Its reported budget was US$82 million, and Koimoi estimates it needs about US$205 million worldwide to turn a profit.

### Who stars in the new Street Fighter film?

Andrew Koji plays Ryu, Noah Centineo plays Ken Masters and Callina Liang plays Chun-Li. Roman Reigns and Cody Rhodes also appear.
