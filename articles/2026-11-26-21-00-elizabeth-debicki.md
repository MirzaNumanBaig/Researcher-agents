**Quick summary**

- Australian actress [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) attended the London premiere of The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth on 24 November 2026 at BFI IMAX Waterloo.
- She plays talent agent Karen Crosby and told Collider that a long balcony scene with Brad Pitt's character is "an extraordinary piece of writing".
- She wore Valentino couture to the premiere and an Altuzarra animal-print set to a screening the next evening.
- The film opens in IMAX theatres before reaching Netflix on 23 December 2026.

Elizabeth Debicki has spent the past two days at the centre of the London promotion for The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, first on the red carpet on 24 November and then in an interview with Collider, where she spoke about working with director [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher). The film arrives on Netflix on 23 December 2026, after a run in IMAX cinemas.

## What Debicki said about working with Fincher

In the Collider interview, published on 25 November and summarised by [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/debicki-valentino-london-premiere), Debicki described Fincher's way of working as freeing for actors. She said he arrives already knowing the performances he wants, and that this clarity takes pressure off the cast. She also said the set was enjoyable, though she admitted to nerves before the most demanding scenes.

Her co-star [Scott Caan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Caan), who plays television star Charlie Peters, spoke about the same nerves. He said joining a project written by [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) and directed by Fincher made him anxious, and that he wanted the filmmakers to be glad they had cast him. He recalled a friend who fights for a living telling him the one time he was beaten badly was the time he felt no nerves. Debicki reassured him about his casting, and Caan said the worry faded once filming began.

## The balcony scene

The most specific detail came from Debicki herself. She called the balcony confrontation between her character, Karen Crosby, and Cliff Booth "an extraordinary piece of writing". By her account the scene runs to roughly eight or 10 pages of script, which makes it an unusually large piece of material for one sequence.

In it, Karen slaps Booth. Fincher uses several camera positions to cover the exchange, including a shot from inside the apartment as Booth strikes the glass. According to [bsinews.id](https://bsinews.id/en/debicki-valentino-london-premiere), the crew had already filmed the preceding apartment sequence, in which a character named Gus is asleep, before moving on to the balcony.

## The film and its cast

The story picks up eight years after [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood). [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) returns as Booth, now working as a bartender and theatre projectionist while still acting as a fixer for Hollywood figures. Karen Crosby calls on him over a problem involving her client Peters, a television star known for doing his own stunts. The wider cast includes [Carla Gugino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Gugino), [Yahya Abdul-Mateen II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Abdul-Mateen_II) and [Timothy Olyphant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Olyphant).

Pitt did not attend the London premiere. UA News, as relayed by bsinews.id, reported that he had the flu.

## Two looks in two nights

The fashion coverage was as busy as the interviews. At the premiere, Debicki wore a turquoise-blue gown from Valentino's Spring/Summer 2026 haute couture collection, made under creative director [Alessandro Michele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Michele). It had a plunging draped neckline, side cutouts and a long train. She added gold flat sandals by Essen and diamond-accented gold earrings by Glenn Spiro. We covered that look in more detail in [our Valentino premiere report](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-valentino-cliff-booth-premiere/).

The following evening, at a special screening at the same venue, she changed direction. Red Carpet Fashion Awards [described](https://www.redcarpet-fashionawards.com/2026/11/25/elizabeth-debicki-takes-another-walk-on-the-wild-side/) a zebra-print, two-piece Altuzarra outfit from the Spring 2027 collection, with black Jimmy Choo Love pumps. Other outlets called the print tiger. [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/elizabeth-debicki-altuzarra-animal-print) said it followed another animal-print outfit she wore in New York, and noted that it departs from the minimalist style she was known for during The Crown press tour. Go Fug Yourself, quoted in the same report, thought a black blouse would have set off the skirt better. That is one critic's view, not a settled verdict.
