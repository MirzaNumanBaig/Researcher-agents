**Quick summary**

- [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) was celebrated at a Cultured magazine cover launch party in Los Angeles on Tuesday, November 10.
- He appears on the magazine's "Artists on Artists" cover, according to Just Jared.
- Co-star [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) attended, as did Mamie Gummer and Maxwell Jenkins.
- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, in which Zada plays Haymitch Abernathy, opens in cinemas on November 20.

Joseph Zada was the guest of honour at a Cultured magazine cover launch in Los Angeles on Tuesday night, just over a week before he leads [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel *Sunrise on the Reaping* in cinemas. The dinner, held at Duke's at Sunset Tower Hotel, marked his place on the magazine's "Artists on Artists" cover.

## What happened at the Cultured cover launch

According to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/11/mckenna-grace-wears-fully-see-through-dress-to-support-joseph-zadas-cultured-cover/), the event was an intimate cocktail and dinner party hosted by the magazine's founder, Sarah Harrelson, in partnership with Spotify and ATM Collection. The outlet reported that the guest list included Zada's *We Were Liars* co-star Mamie Gummer and Maxwell Jenkins, who is due to appear alongside Grace in a future *Scooby-Doo* project.

Grace drew the most attention for her outfit, a sheer dress, which Just Jared described as one of her boldest looks yet. Her appearance fits a pattern. NYLON notes that her red carpet looks on the current press tour have leaned heavily on references to the franchise.

## The role behind the attention

The cover arrives at a busy moment. Just Jared describes *Sunrise on the Reaping* as following sixteen-year-old Haymitch Abernathy of District 12 from the morning of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games, the Second Quarter Quell, which has twice as many tributes as usual. Zada plays Haymitch, who forms an alliance with fellow tribute Maysilee Donner (Grace) and joins a coalition against the Capitol under President Snow, played by Ralph Fiennes.

Just Jared reported that the film is due in theatres on November 20, and that the party took place one week before that date.

## A friendship that made it into the soundtrack

Zada also features in a smaller way on the film's music side. In an [exclusive interview with NYLON](https://www.nylon.com/entertainment/mckenna-grace-half-alive-hunger-games-soundtrack-premiere), Grace said she wrote "Half Alive" from the perspective of her character, Maysilee, with Haymitch in mind. She said that when she listened back, it also reminded her of the cast who stood by her during filming, naming Zada and co-star Ben Wang.

Grace, who is 20, told NYLON she pitched the song to director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) near the end of shooting, and that he promised to place it on the soundtrack. She said the closing seconds of the track include voices of the cast recorded on her digital camera during production, among them the group singing "Happy Birthday" to Zada and him telling her to go away. The song sits on a soundtrack that, per NYLON, also lists [Stevie Nicks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stevie_Nicks), [Lizzy McAlpine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzy_McAlpine), [Kacey Musgraves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kacey_Musgraves), The Smashing Pumpkins and The Cure.

## What happens next

The press run continues until the film opens on November 20. Grace told NYLON she was heading to Mexico City for a fan event, after stops in Paris, Sydney, London and Madrid.
