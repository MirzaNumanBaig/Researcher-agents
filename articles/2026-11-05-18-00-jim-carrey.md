**Quick summary**

- [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey), 64, has married his girlfriend Min Ah in a small, private ceremony in Los Angeles.
- TMZ reported the wedding first, and a spokesperson for the actor confirmed it to People, E! News and Entertainment Weekly, according to Global News.
- The couple made their public debut at the César Awards in Paris seven months earlier.
- Neither Carrey nor Min Ah has shared details of the ceremony publicly.

[Jim Carrey](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jim-carrey/) has married Min Ah. A spokesperson for the actor confirmed the news at the start of October 2026, after TMZ reported that the couple exchanged vows in a small, private ceremony in Los Angeles.

## What has been confirmed about the wedding

According to [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12083977/jim-carrey-marries-min-ah/), TMZ broke the story, and Carrey's spokesperson then confirmed the marriage to People, E! News and Entertainment Weekly. [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/entertainment/jim-carrey-marries-longtime-girlfriend) also cites People as confirming it.

The reports describe a small, private event. They do not give the exact wedding date, and no guest list, venue or photographs have been released. Anything beyond that is unconfirmed, so it is left out here.

## How Carrey and Min Ah got here

The couple were first photographed together in February 2022, Global News reports. Their first major public appearance followed in February 2026 at the César Awards in Paris, where Carrey received an honorary award.

Carrey gave his acceptance speech entirely in French. Khaleej Times reports he thanked Min Ah publicly and called her his "sublime companion", and that he credited his late father with teaching him the value of love, generosity and laughter. According to Global News, he also told his family, "I love you now and forever."

The wedding arrived seven months after that night in Paris.

## A private life, kept that way

Carrey has generally kept his personal life out of the spotlight, and Khaleej Times notes he has said he enjoys a quieter life away from it. That fits with a wedding announced by a spokesperson and not by the couple.

The marriage is his third. He was married to Melissa Womer from 1987 to 1995, and they share a daughter, Jane. He then married his *Dumb and Dumber* co-star Lauren Holly, from 1996 to 1997, according to the same coverage. He was also linked with *Kidding* co-star Ginger Gonzaga in 2019, a relationship that Us Weekly reported had ended by October of that year, per Khaleej Times.

## Where his career stands

In 2022 Carrey said he was "fairly serious" about stepping away from acting, though he allowed that he might return for a script he considered important, as both outlets recall.
