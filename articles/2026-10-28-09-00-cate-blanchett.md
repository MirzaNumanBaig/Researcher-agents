**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) is a 2026 Praemium Imperiale laureate in the Theatre/Film category.
- The Japan Art Association announced the winners on 8 September 2026.
- The award ceremony takes place in Tokyo on 28 October.
- Each laureate receives 15 million yen, a certificate and a medal.

Australian actor Cate Blanchett is being honoured in Tokyo on 28 October with the Praemium Imperiale, one of the world's major international arts prizes, which often gets called the "Nobel of the arts". The Japan Art Association named her its Theatre/Film laureate for 2026 in September, and the formal presentation now follows.

## What the Praemium Imperiale is

According to [Finestre sull'Arte](https://www.finestresullarte.info/en/art-and-artists/the-2026-oscars-of-the-arts-praemium-imperiale-winners-announced), the Japan Art Association established the prize in 1988 and this is its 37th edition. It was created to honour Prince Takamatsu, who wished to contribute to the appreciation and promotion of the world's cultures and arts. The association itself, per [Webull's report](https://www.webull.com/news/15541931972617216) on the announcement, was founded in 1887 and is described as the oldest cultural foundation in Japan. It also manages the Ueno Royal Museum.

One laureate is chosen in each of five disciplines. With the 2026 class, the total number of artists honoured reaches 190, according to Finestre sull'Arte.

## Who else is on the 2026 list

Blanchett is joined by four other laureates:

- Jenny Holzer (United States), painting
- Andy Goldsworthy (United Kingdom), sculpture
- Daniel Libeskind (United States, born in Poland), architecture
- Herbert Blomstedt (Sweden, born in the United States), music

A separate Grant for Young Artists went to La Source Garouste, a French association that develops new talent. It receives 5 million yen.

## How Blanchett is described

Finestre sull'Arte describes her as an Australian actress working across film, theatre and television, with international fame dating from the 1998 film *Elizabeth*. It notes her two Academy Awards, her reputation for versatility, and her role as a global ambassador for UNHCR, the United Nations refugee agency. The reports we read carry no direct statement from Blanchett or the organisers about her selection.

Webull's report quotes Hillary Rodham Clinton praising the group of recipients for "the kind of artistry that shapes culture in ways that remind us of our shared humanity". The quote is about the laureates as a whole, not Blanchett specifically.

The prize money is the same for everyone: 15 million yen each, plus a certificate and a medal. Currency conversions vary by outlet, from about 81,000 euros to about US$95,000, so the figure depends on the exchange rate used.

## What happens next

The ceremony is in Tokyo on 28 October 2026. Finestre sull'Arte says it will be presided over by Prince Hitachi, the association's Honorary Patron; Webull's account names Princess Hitachi as attending. Neither report says whether Blanchett will speak or has confirmed travel plans, so check back for coverage after the event.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Praemium Imperiale?
It is an international arts award given by the Japan Art Association since 1988, with one laureate each in painting, sculpture, architecture, music and theatre/film.

### When was Cate Blanchett's win announced?
The 2026 laureates were announced on 8 September 2026, simultaneously in several cities including Tokyo, Paris, Rome, Berlin, New York and London.
