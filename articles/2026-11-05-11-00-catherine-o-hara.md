**Quick summary**

- [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara) died of a pulmonary embolism, according to her death certificate.
- The certificate, released by the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner's Office, lists rectal cancer as the underlying cause.
- She was 71 and had not spoken publicly about any health struggles, ABC News reports.
- Her manager confirmed her death on Friday, 30 January, and tributes followed from her *Schitt's Creek* and *The Studio* colleagues.

The cause of Catherine O'Hara's death has been made public. The actress died of a pulmonary embolism, with rectal cancer named as the underlying cause, according to her death certificate released by the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner's Office, as [reported by ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/catherine-ohara-cause-death-revealed/story?id=129786941). She was 71.

## What the death certificate says

The document gives two entries. The immediate cause is a pulmonary embolism, which the U.S. National Library of Medicine describes, as quoted by ABC News, as a sudden blockage in a lung artery caused by a blood clot that travels to the lungs. The underlying cause is rectal cancer.

ABC News adds context on the disease, citing the National Cancer Institute: colorectal cancer, in which cells in the colon or rectum grow out of control, is the second leading cause of cancer-related deaths in the United States.

O'Hara had never publicly revealed any health struggles, ABC News notes. For many fans, the certificate was the first sign she had been unwell.

## How the news unfolded

O'Hara's manager first confirmed her death on Friday, 30 January. The Los Angeles Fire Department told ABC News it received a call for medical aid at 4:48 a.m. that day, at an address connected to the actress. She was taken to hospital in serious condition, according to the department.

At the time, she was starring in the award-winning Apple series *The Studio*. The certificate's release came days later, and ABC News reported that tributes from the entertainment world had been arriving since the news broke.

## Tributes from Schitt's Creek and The Studio

[Dan Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Levy_(Canadian_actor)), who played her son David on [*Schitt's Creek*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek), posted a tribute on Instagram. "What a gift to have gotten to dance in the warm glow of Catherine O'Hara's brilliance for all those years," he wrote. He added that he had spent more than fifty years collaborating with his father, [Eugene Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Levy), so that O'Hara "was extended family before she ever played my family."

The cast and crew of *The Studio*, including [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Ike Barinholtz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ike_Barinholtz), [Chase Sui Wonders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chase_Sui_Wonders) and [Kathryn Hahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hahn), also responded. A representative for Rogen shared a statement on the show's behalf, calling her "classy, warm, and hilarious all at the same time" and saying they pinched themselves daily that they got to work with her.

## A career of more than 100 roles

ABC News counts more than 100 roles across television and film in O'Hara's career. Her best-known recent work was Moira Rose, the grandiose, wonderfully detached actress in *Schitt's Creek*. The role earned her a Primetime Emmy Award in 2020 and a Golden Globe in 2021.

The series ran for six seasons from 2015 to 2020. It followed a formerly wealthy family forced to live in a small town, and its four leads, O'Hara, Dan Levy, Eugene Levy and [Annie Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Murphy), each won an Emmy for the final season, [ABC News reported in an earlier story](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/schitts-creek-reunion-dan-levy-catherine-ohara-reunite/story?id=97658751). The show also took the top prize for outstanding comedy series.
