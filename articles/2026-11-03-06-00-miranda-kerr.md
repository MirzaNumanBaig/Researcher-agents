**Quick summary**

- [Miranda Kerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Kerr) has spoken about the death of her first love, Christopher Middlebrook, who died in a car accident as a teenager.
- She shared the story on the "Not Skinny But Not Fat" podcast, hosted by Amanda Hirsch, in an episode due on Tuesday, 3 November.
- Us Weekly published an exclusive preview of the interview.
- Kerr says she still speaks with Middlebrook's parents at least once a month.

Miranda Kerr has given a rare account of losing her high school boyfriend, Christopher Middlebrook, and of how that grief still shapes her family today. The Australian model and KORA Organics founder, 43, made the comments on the "Not Skinny But Not Fat" podcast, in an episode due out on 3 November, according to an [Us Weekly preview](https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/miranda-kerr-makes-rare-comment-about-ex-boyfriends-death/).

## The day her world stopped

Kerr told host Amanda Hirsch that the two families were close, and that she and Middlebrook had been together for two years as high school sweethearts. She recalled being told the news by her uncle.

"I just remember my whole world just stopped," Kerr said, according to Us Weekly. She described seeing a man mowing a lawn outside and thinking how strange it was that other people's lives carried on while hers had halted.

She also described the circumstances of the accident. By her account, Middlebrook's car had been taken off the road the week before because of a headlight problem, so he was driving without headlights. On a night with a full moon, a plough was on the side of the road, and Kerr said he died instantly when he saw it.

## A promise written in a letter

Kerr said she went to her grandmother's house soon afterwards and wrote Middlebrook a letter to be buried with him. In it, she promised that her firstborn son would carry his name.

That promise was kept. Her eldest son, Flynn, 15, whom she shares with ex-husband [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom), is named Flynn Christopher. Kerr said she feels Middlebrook is "really watching over" Flynn, who also wears a St. Christopher pendant.

Kerr is also mother to Hart, 8, Myles, 7, and Pierre, 2, with her husband, Evan Spiegel, Us Weekly reports.

## Staying close to his family

Kerr said staying in touch with Middlebrook's parents has been central to how she has coped. She speaks to them at least once a month, and described the connection as healing for both his family and herself.

"We grew up together. He was my first love," she said. She sends his mother a sunflower emoji as a private tribute, because the flower is closely tied to his memory. She recalled him arriving at her house with an enormous sunflower he had pulled out of the farm, roots and all.
