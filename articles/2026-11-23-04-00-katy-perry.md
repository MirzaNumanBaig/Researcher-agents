**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) and boyfriend [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) were photographed holding hands on arrival in Venice on Friday, 20 November, according to IndulgExpress.
- Old court papers from a 2025 deposition of her ex-husband [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) have resurfaced, and in them he struggles to say whether he was married during a 2010 film shoot.
- Brand was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison after being convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
- Perry has not publicly commented, and reports that she is weighing a book or streaming series come from unnamed sources.

## Katy Perry arrives in Venice with Justin Trudeau

The singer and the former Canadian prime minister were seen arriving in Venice on Friday, hand in hand, IndulgExpress reported. Perry wore a strapless black dress with gold buttons under a long black coat and sunglasses, while Trudeau chose a dark jacket, button-down shirt and denim.

The trip is part of a longer run through Europe. The same outlet says the couple were seen leaving a Milan hotel on Thursday and later at the Ritz Paris. On Tuesday they were in Spain, where Trudeau was among the speakers at Atresmedia's Metafuturo conference.

The pair were first linked in July 2025, when they were seen walking together in Montreal. Before that, Perry was in a relationship with actor [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) from January 2016 until June 2025, according to the same report. Trudeau announced his separation from his wife Sophie Grégoire Trudeau in August 2023 after 18 years of marriage.

## What the Russell Brand deposition says

The timing matters because newly circulated court documents have put Perry's first marriage back in the news. TMZ, which says it obtained the papers, reported that Brand was questioned in a deposition connected to a civil lawsuit from a woman identified as Jane Doe. She alleges an incident during the 2010 filming of *Arthur*. Brand denies the allegations.

Asked whether he was married when the film was shot, Brand said he could look it up online. According to the transcript quoted by LADbible and The Mirror US, he added that he was in a relationship with "a very high-profile woman" and was "sort of drowning in love" even while married to her. The Mirror US notes that the alleged incident was said to have taken place about three months before the wedding.

The allegations in the lawsuit are unproven claims in a civil case. TMZ also reports that a New York judge recently refused to throw out negligence claims against Warner Bros. and other production companies named in it.

## The marriage and the criminal case

Perry and Brand met while working on *Get Him to the Greek* in 2009. The Mirror US says they married in Rajasthan, India, on 23 October 2010, and that Brand filed for divorce on 30 December 2011. LADbible and IndulgExpress describe the marriage as running from 2010 to 2012, so exact end dates differ between outlets.

Brand, 51, was convicted in a separate UK criminal case of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women, offences dating from 2004 to 2009. The Mirror US reports he is now held at HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire after a transfer from HMP Wandsworth. TMZ says he has released a message through his wife saying he is "not guilty."

None of the outlets suggests Perry is involved in either case. LADbible notes that mention of her was forbidden during his recent trial.

## Reports of a book or series remain unconfirmed

The Irish Star, citing the Daily Mail and the Mirror US, says Perry has been in "active discussions" about telling her story in a book or a streaming series. The claim rests on an unnamed source, and the outlet itself says she has stayed publicly silent. Nothing from Perry or her representatives confirms it.

The same article quotes PR commentator Chad Teixeira, who guessed at deal values of US$3 million to US$5 million for a book and US$3 million to US$7 million for a series. Those figures are one adviser's speculation, not reported offers, and they should be read that way.

## What happens next

Perry's travel schedule beyond Venice has not been announced. Any decision about a book or series would have to come from her or her team, and so far there is none on record. Further court filings in the *Arthur* lawsuit could bring more of Brand's testimony into public view.
