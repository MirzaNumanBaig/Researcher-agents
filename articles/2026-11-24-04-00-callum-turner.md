**Quick summary**

- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) was reportedly the early favourite among Amazon MGM's team for the role of [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to a Variety report relayed by Celebitchy.
- Amazon MGM announced [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the next 007 on 16 November.
- Insiders told Variety that Pattinson's "shorthand" with director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) tipped the decision.
- Turner, 36, was photographed at Madison Square Garden on Sunday, his first public outing since the news, according to the Daily Mail.

Callum Turner was reportedly the person Amazon MGM's Bond team wanted first, before two late screen tests changed the picture and Robert Pattinson won the part. Variety published the account on Saturday 21 November, five days after the studio announced Pattinson as the next 007, and it has since been picked up by other outlets.

## What the report says about the screen tests

According to [Celebitchy's summary of the Variety piece](https://www.celebitchy.com/1008491/callum_turner_was_the_amazon_teams_early_favorite_for_james_bond/), the round of screen tests cost Amazon MGM between US$300,000 and US$500,000, and was meant to be secret. Sources told Variety that Turner was the early favourite on the Amazon side, but that he came under scrutiny once more established names entered the process.

Those names were Pattinson and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult), who both tested at the last minute. Variety's sources said Villeneuve's voice became the most trusted in the room on the casting question. Villeneuve has worked with Pattinson on "Dune: Part Three", and insiders described their working shorthand as the factor that made the choice feel inevitable.

None of this has been confirmed on the record by Amazon MGM. The studio has not published a full list of who reached the final stage, as [inkl's write-up of the Variety story](https://www.inkl.com/news/robert-pattinsons-shorthand-with-denis-villeneuve-secured-james-bond-over-three-rivals-insiders-say) points out.

## The other names in the mix

Turner was never the only name in circulation. [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), the star of [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses), and Josh O'Connor featured heavily in earlier speculation, according to inkl. Variety had also reported earlier this month that [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) and [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James) were in talks, which Celebitchy described as an unusually public list of candidates for a tentpole film.

The same report says paparazzi footage of Lowden fumbling a motorcycle stunt on a set circulated about two weeks before the casting was confirmed. Celebitchy characterised that as a suspected sabotage campaign. That is the outlet's framing of Variety's reporting, and no one has offered evidence for it.

## Turner's first public appearance since the announcement

The Daily Mail reported on Monday 23 November that Turner was seen courtside at Madison Square Garden on Sunday, watching the New York Knicks beat the Indiana Pacers to extend their winning streak to five games. The paper said he wore a leather jacket and light-wash jeans.

According to the Daily Mail, Turner is in New York filming "The Comebacker", a sports comedy that also stars Tom Hanks and Patricia Clarkson. The paper's headline claims he "failed to impress" in a final audition. That is the tabloid's reading of the reports, and Turner has not commented publicly on the Bond process.

## Where Turner's career stands

Turner's most recent release was the romantic comedy "One Night Only", which opened on 7 August. It was made on a reported budget of US$25 million and has grossed about US$25.2 million, roughly break-even on those figures. Its Rotten Tomatoes score sits at 44 per cent, and Metacritic lists 42 out of 100 from 36 critics. Reviewers praised the appeal of the leads but found the film's world-building hard to follow. Our earlier post covers [where One Night Only is streaming](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-one-night-only-peacock/), and everything else is on the [Callum Turner tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/callum-turner/).

## What happens next

Pattinson's first image as Bond, in a tuxedo and holding a pistol, was released by Amazon MGM the morning after he was told he had the role. Reports suggest his Batman future is now uncertain after leadership changes at DC Studios, though that is analysis rather than an announcement. For Turner, the near-term picture is "The Comebacker".
