**Quick summary**

- Johnny Flynn plays London drug boss Frankie Cooper in season 2 of MobLand, and viewers are comparing him to Heath Ledger's Joker.
- Episodes 7 and 9, both built around Frankie, are tied at 8.7 on IMDb, the highest of any episode in the series, according to Startefacts.
- The season 2 finale arrives on Paramount+ on Friday, November 20.
- Paramount+ has already ordered season 3, and Flynn is also set to play Lucius Malfoy in HBO's Harry Potter series.

[Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) has become the talking point of MobLand's second season, with fans likening his character Frankie Cooper to Heath Ledger's Joker days before the finale. According to [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/mobland-s-new-villain-has-fans-dubbing-him-the-next-heath-ledger-joker), the season wraps on Paramount+ this Friday, November 20, and the conversation is less about the show's big names than about the man on the scooter.

## Why Frankie Cooper is dominating the conversation

Frankie is a London drug boss who first appears in the closing seconds of the season 2 premiere, known only as "Beast". Startefacts reports that [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer)'s Kat McAllister needs someone to take on Harry and the Harrigans, and [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones), playing a corrupt ex-cop named Colin Tattersall, brings her Frankie.

The numbers are what set him apart. Episode 9, "Nervous Breakdown", sits at 8.7 on IMDb, tied with episode 7, "Ace of Spades", as the best-rated episode of the whole series, season 1 included. Both are centred on Frankie. That is a notable result for a show that also features [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy), [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren).

## What Frankie has done so far

Startefacts lists a run of showpiece moments. Frankie burns down the Harrigans' London headquarters, stages a coordinated attack on the family to The Prodigy's "Firestarter", and crashes a black-tie diplomatic party dressed as a waiter. In episode 9 he breaks down in tears over his dead son in the middle of a threat.

His look has drawn comment too. The report cites Show Snob describing it as a history lesson running from 1960s mod culture to Britpop, which helps explain the scooter that the outlet links to Quadrophenia. It also notes that TechRadar called him "the best new villain Paramount+ could have asked for" after episode 2, and that Ready Steady Cut said Flynn takes every scene he is in. Flynn told TechRadar that his big episode 2 scene was shot on his first day: "That was my first day, so I was terrified."

## Is he really the next Joker?

The comparison began with Show Snob's recap of episode 9, which said Ledger's Joker was "strong in this one". Fans picked it up, and the Startefacts piece points to the parallels: a villain who crashes a high-society event, treats crime as theatre and makes those around him look slow. In episode 4, according to the report, Harry even warns Frankie that his showmanship makes him look like a clown.

Startefacts argues the likeness only goes so far. Ledger's Joker famously offered a different story about his scars each time, while Frankie has a specific loss, a dead son, that surfaces in the middle of his menace. The writer's view is that "the next Joker" undersells him. That is an opinion of one outlet and its fans, not a verdict from the show's makers.

## What Flynn has coming up

Beyond MobLand, Startefacts reports that he is playing Lucius Malfoy in HBO's [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) series. Flynn is also in the cast of Elsinore, directed by Simon Stone and released on September 4, 2026. Wikipedia's entry on the film lists Rotten Tomatoes at 89% and Metacritic at 75 out of 100 from 19 critics, with early reviews focusing mostly on Andrew Scott's lead performance.

The season 2 finale lands on Friday, November 20. Startefacts says Paramount+ has already ordered season 3 and that Tom Hardy is confirmed to return. Whether Frankie survives the finale has not been confirmed in the material available, so the finale itself is the next real answer.
