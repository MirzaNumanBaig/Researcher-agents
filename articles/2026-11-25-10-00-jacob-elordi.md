- InStyle published a round-up on 24 November of the off-duty outfits worn by [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner).
- The magazine says the pair have been romantically linked since April 2026.
- The looks come from Tokyo (2 June), New York and Paris (28 September).
- InStyle describes the common thread as clean silhouettes, elevated basics and understated luxury.

[Jacob Elordi](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/) and Kendall Jenner are being celebrated for their everyday wardrobes. On 24 November, InStyle published a feature on the couple's best off-duty moments, calling their style "on another level". The magazine says the two have been romantically linked since April 2026, and the story is built around what they wear when they are not working.

## Why the pair's style is getting attention

InStyle opens by pointing to couples with no "swag gap", naming [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) with Zoë Kravitz and [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner) with Timothée Chalamet as earlier examples. Its case for Elordi and Jenner is that, for two people whose jobs regularly involve runway looks and red-carpet tailoring, their casual outfits are just as interesting.

The magazine's verdict is that neither seems to dress for the other. Their tastes still sit well together, it argues, with a preference for clean lines and basics over logos and excess.

## The looks InStyle picked

### Paris Fashion Week, 28 September

According to InStyle, the couple left L'Oréal's Paris Fashion Week show on 28 September looking like a 1990s fashion editorial. Jenner wore a polished grey dress with pointed black kitten heels. Elordi kept it simple with a white button-down shirt and black trousers.

### A Paris dinner the same night

For dinner that evening, Jenner added a lightweight coat and a vintage-style shell clutch to the same grey dress. InStyle notes that Elordi kept accessories to a minimum, though it adds that he is known to have an eye for a good handbag.

### A walk through New York

For a stroll in New York, InStyle says both leaned on The Row. Jenner wore an olive-green chore jacket from the label with bootcut jeans, brown leather boots, an oversized red tote and a leopard-print scarf. Elordi went more relaxed in loose indigo denim, a white tee, a grey hoodie and a black beanie, with slim sunglasses, his crossbody bag and The Row's Clement sneakers.

### Pizza in Tokyo, 2 June

The earliest look in the round-up is from a 2 June pizza date at Seirinkan in Tokyo, where the couple posed with chef-owner Susumu Kakinuma. Elordi wore an unbuttoned powder-blue shirt over a white tee, dark denim and a backward cap. Jenner chose a butter-yellow silk outfit with small front ties and a low ponytail.
