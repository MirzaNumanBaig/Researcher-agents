**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) wore a robin's egg blue Valentino couture gown to the London premiere of "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth" on Monday 24 November.
- The dress comes from [Alessandro Michele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Michele)'s spring 2026 couture collection, which first appeared in Paris in January, according to WWD.
- Stylist Elizabeth Saltzman put the look together, with Glenn Spiro earrings and Essen sandals.
- [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) missed the event with the flu, UA.NEWS reports.
- WWD says the film, directed by [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher), opens in theatres on Wednesday.

Elizabeth Debicki arrived at the BFI IMAX Waterloo in London on Monday night in a sleeveless Valentino couture gown, for the premiere of "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth". The Australian actress wore a robin's egg blue dress with a plunging neckline, side cutouts and a long train, [WWD reported](https://wwd.com/pop-culture/celebrity-news/elizabeth-debicki-valentino-couture-cliff-booth-london-1239463709/). It was the kind of entrance that put the dress, not the jewellery, at the centre of the photographs.

## What Elizabeth Debicki wore

According to WWD, the gown has a mostly fitted shape, draped fabric at the base and a crisscross back. The skirt runs in a clean line before pooling into a train that followed her along the arrivals carpet. The outlet described the mood as Old Hollywood refinement with a strong dose of sensuality.

[UA.NEWS](https://ua.news/en/culture/elizabet-debiki-vrazila-biriuzovoiu-sukneiu-valentino-na-premieri-v-londoni) calls the colour turquoise and blue, which shows how much the shade shifts between photographs. Both outlets agree on the main details: a deep neckline, cutouts at the sides and a train that gave the look its drama.

The rest of the styling was kept quiet. She wore gold and diamond earrings by Glenn Spiro and Essen sandals, and UA.NEWS adds that her hair was curled inward at the ends, with soft blush and a pink-brown gloss on her lips. WWD credits stylist Elizabeth Saltzman with the look.

## The couture behind the gown

The dress is from Alessandro Michele's spring 2026 couture collection for Valentino. WWD says it first appeared on the runway during Paris Couture Week in January 2026, and that Debicki's version pared back the original design, which included a gold lamé sequined coat.

That collection carried extra weight. WWD notes it was shown as a tribute to the label's founder, [Valentino Garavani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentino_(fashion_designer)), who died aged 93 earlier that month. In its review, WWD's Joelle Diderich wrote that the show was "so close in spirit to the label's founder, he couldn't have planned a better tribute", and said Michele drew on silent film iconography and Art Deco glamour.

## The film and the missing co-star

"The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth" follows Brad Pitt's character, a Hollywood fixer, in a return to the role that won him an Oscar in [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood). [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) wrote the screenplay and David Fincher directed. UA.NEWS lists Debicki as talent scout Karen Crosby.

Pitt was not on the carpet. UA.NEWS says he skipped the London event because of the flu. WWD did not mention his absence, so that detail rests on one outlet.
