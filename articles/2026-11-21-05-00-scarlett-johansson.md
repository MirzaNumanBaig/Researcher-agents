**Quick summary**

- Slate has reviewed James Gray's film Paper Tiger, which stars [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson), [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) and [Miles Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Teller).
- Johansson plays Hester, the wife of Teller's character Irwin, in a story set in 1980s Queens.
- The reviewer compares the film's mood to Martin Scorsese's mob movies, but with a Jewish, middle-class setting.
- Teller's and Johansson's characters were inspired by Gray's own parents, according to Slate.

Scarlett Johansson is drawing attention for her role in Paper Tiger, the ninth feature from director James Gray. In a review published on 20 November 2026, [Slate](https://slate.com/culture/2026/11/paper-tiger-adam-driver-scarlett-johansson-miles-teller-movie.html) describes the film as a family tragedy dressed as a gangster picture, and says Johansson's part is far from the usual background wife.

## What Paper Tiger is about

According to Slate, the film follows two brothers, Irwin and Gary Pearl, played by Miles Teller and Adam Driver. Gary is a former NYPD officer who works as a security consultant and fixer. He believes the government-funded cleanup of Brooklyn's Gowanus Canal is their way to wealth.

Irwin is an engineer and a family man who lives in a modest brick house in Queens. Gary wants to pair his City Hall connections with his brother's expertise and start a consultancy that helps Russian companies win permits for dredging work. Slate says those companies have moved into a business once controlled by the Mafia.

The trouble starts when Irwin sees something the Russians did not want him to see. Slate says that sets off a run of reprisals that put Irwin, his two teenage sons and, in time, his wife in danger.

## Johansson's role as Hester

Johansson plays Hester, Irwin's affectionate and practical wife. The reviewer notes she is a stay-at-home parent who supervises her sons' homework and pushes back when her husband drives the boys to a deserted industrial area on a school night.

Slate also points to a subplot in which Hester keeps a serious health scare from her family. The review says it gives her story, and the brothers' story, a tragic dimension. The sons are played by Gavin Goudey and Roman Engel. The review text available to us cuts off partway through its comment on Johansson's wider career, so this article does not go beyond what it states.

## A personal film with a thriller edge

Slate says Paper Tiger is not a sequel to Gray's 2022 drama [Armageddon Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_Time), but it is just as personal. Both are set in a Jewish family's life in 1980s Queens, where Gray grew up. The characters played by Teller and Johansson were inspired by Gray's parents, according to the review.

This time the stakes are higher. Slate singles out a violent scene in which Driver's Gary fights Russian thugs armed only with a belt, and a late chase through marshland outside New York. Even so, the reviewer argues the film stands apart from standard action fare because of the care Gray gives the domestic drama beneath the crime plot.

The film was shot on grainy 35mm, and the review says its fatalistic melancholy owes a debt to Scorsese. It opens with a line from Aeschylus' tragedy Agamemnon about wealth without tears.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is in Paper Tiger?

Per Slate, the cast includes Miles Teller as Irwin Pearl, Adam Driver as his brother Gary, and Scarlett Johansson as Irwin's wife Hester. Gavin Goudey and Roman Engel play their sons.

### Who directed Paper Tiger?

James Gray directed it. Slate calls it his ninth film and links its family setting to his earlier film Armageddon Time.

### What role does Scarlett Johansson play?

She plays Hester, a stay-at-home mother in 1980s Queens. Slate says she is drawn into danger when her husband crosses Russian business operators, and that Hester also hides a serious health scare.
