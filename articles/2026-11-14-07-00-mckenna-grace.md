**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) has premiered "Half Alive", a new song on the soundtrack of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, with its music video debuting on NYLON.
- She wrote it mainly from the point of view of her character, Maysilee Donner, and pitched it to director Francis Lawrence during filming.
- The film opens on November 20, according to CinemaBlend.
- Grace also stepped out in a sheer black dress in Los Angeles this week, a change from her Hunger Games-themed looks.

Mckenna Grace has released "Half Alive", a song she wrote for the Sunrise on the Reaping soundtrack, and the music video premiered exclusively on NYLON on November 13. The 20-year-old actor plays Maysilee Donner in the latest [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) film, which reaches cinemas on November 20.

## How the song ended up on the soundtrack

According to [NYLON](https://www.nylon.com/entertainment/mckenna-grace-half-alive-hunger-games-soundtrack-premiere), Grace told director Francis Lawrence throughout the shoot that she would eventually pitch him a song. She says she is nervous about sending it because Lawrence is, in her words, the franchise's head and "the coolest music video director ever". She wrote it anyway and texted it to him without asking for a place on the album. Lawrence liked it enough to promise a spot on the soundtrack straight away.

The track sits alongside names such as The Smashing Pumpkins, Stevie Nicks, Lizzy McAlpine, Kacey Musgraves and The Cure, NYLON reports. Grace is not new to this. She has been releasing music for five years, her latest single "Bones and All" came out in September, and she has made songs for earlier projects including Scream 7 and Ghostbusters: Afterlife.

## What the song is about

Grace says the lyrics lean toward Maysilee's view, especially her bond with Haymitch, and touch on how the character sees herself, her fellow tributes and her sister. While writing the chorus she pictured the two in the arena. On playback, though, it reminded her of the people who supported her during a demanding shoot, including co-star Joseph Zada, filming the Games sequences in Spain, and [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), who she says had dinner with her every night in Berlin.

Those friends are in the track itself. Over the end of the instrumental, Grace layered voices she recorded on a digital camera during production: Zada, Wang, Iona Bell and Molly McCann, including a round of "Happy Birthday" for Zada. It is a small detail that listeners can only catch by staying to the final seconds.

## A busy press tour

NYLON describes a press run that has taken Grace through Paris, Sydney, London and Madrid, with a fan event in Mexico City still ahead when it spoke to her from the back of a car in Los Angeles at 6:45 a.m. Over the past month, the outlet says, she has fielded questions about Maysilee, her history of making Hunger Games fan edits and her references to the story on the red carpet. We have followed those outfits in [Mckenna Grace's Hunger Games looks](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-looks-2/) and [her tribute tour](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-tribute-looks/).

She has also been playing games with her co-stars. In a video for Mashable's Say More, Grace and Wang, who play District 12 tributes Maysilee Donner and Wyatt Callow, [drafted their own revolutionary squads](https://mashable.com/entertainment/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-choose-your-squad-mckenna-grace) from characters across the original trilogy, Sunrise on the Reaping and The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes.

## A sheer black dress, for a change

Her wardrobe took a different turn this week. [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/sheer-black-dresses-new-celebrity-trend-hailee-steinfeld-mckenna-grace) reports that Grace wore a sheer black dress to the launch of CULTURED Magazine and Joseph Zada's "Artists on Artists" issue in Los Angeles. Her stylist, Molly Dickson, identified the dress as Annie's Ibiza, and Grace added a matching lace choker and black pumps.

The outlet called it a departure from her recent method dressing for the film. It pointed to [Hailee Steinfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailee_Steinfeld), who wore a sheer black leather and lace dress to the Los Angeles premiere of Disney's Hexed two days later, as another example. It also mentioned [Joey King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joey_King) at the Practical Magic 2 premiere and [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry) earlier this year as other recent wearers of the look. Whether that adds up to a trend is the writer's opinion, not an established fact. More of our coverage is on the [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) page.

## What happens next

Sunrise on the Reaping opens on November 20, and the soundtrack with "Half Alive" is part of that release. NYLON also says Grace hinted at ending her retirement from fan edits, although it did not report a date or detail in the portion of the interview we could read.
