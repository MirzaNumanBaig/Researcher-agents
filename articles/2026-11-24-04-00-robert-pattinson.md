**Quick summary**

- Industry insiders say [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)'s reported "shorthand" with director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) helped him win the role of [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) ahead of several rivals.
- Variety carried the account on Saturday 21 November, five days after Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting.
- Speculation has now turned to who will play the female lead, and Amazon MGM Studios has not named anyone.
- A 88-year-old former Bond stunt double told the BBC the new 007 "looks different" to his predecessors.

## Insiders credit a Villeneuve connection

Robert Pattinson's working relationship with Denis Villeneuve was a factor in his selection as the next James Bond, according to industry insiders cited in a [report relayed by inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/robert-pattinsons-shorthand-with-denis-villeneuve-secured-james-bond-over-three-rivals-insiders-say). The account first ran in Variety on Saturday 21 November, five days after Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Pattinson as the new 007.

The insiders say that shorthand put him ahead of several other actors who went through a reported audition process. [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and Josh O'Connor had all featured prominently in earlier speculation. The studio has not published a full list of the actors who reached the final stage, so the detail remains an insider account rather than something Amazon MGM Studios has confirmed.

## What Pattinson has said

In a statement after the announcement, quoted by [MEAWW](https://meaww.com/who-is-the-next-bond-girl-james-bond-robert-pattinson-amanda-seyfried-instagram-comment-zendaya-hunter-schafer-sophie-turner-amazon-mgm), Pattinson called James Bond "one of the most legendary characters in cinema" and said being entrusted with 007 was "the honour of a lifetime". He thanked Villeneuve, along with Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios, for their belief in him.

The official 007 Instagram account posted a picture of the actor in a classic Bond pose with the caption "Robert Pattinson is James Bond". Amazon MGM Studios replied: "Identity confirmed."

Our earlier coverage looks at the [big expectations that come with the role](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-baggage/) and the [reported Pinewood Studios plans](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-pinewood/).

## Who will star opposite him?

With the lead settled, fans have started guessing at the female lead. On 17 November Variety shared a list on Instagram under the caption "WHO COULD BE THE NEXT BOND GIRL?", according to MEAWW. It named [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Gemma Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Chan), [Hunter Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunter_Schafer), [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy), [Sophie Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Turner) and [Adria Arjona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adria_Arjona), among others.

One reply stood out. [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) commented, "Am I too old?" MEAWW notes she is 40, the same age as Pattinson. It was a joke, not an audition announcement, and the list is a fan-style guessing game rather than a studio shortlist.

## A veteran's view from the Bond set

Not everyone is focused on the cast list. Jenny Le Fre, 88, spent 15 years as a stunt performer and doubled on early Bond films, including the 1967 picture You Only Live Twice. She told BBC Radio Surrey, in a [BBC report](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cjn502pxyv22o), that Pattinson "looks different to the others, but I guess they've done that on purpose".

Le Fre worked with [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) and [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore). She remembered Connery as a "very dour Scot" and Moore as a "great practical joker". She was fair about the new casting, saying he may be very good and the script may be too, but "we don't know because we haven't seen it". She added that ideas of what counts as handsome change, and the franchise has to "move with the times". The role passes from [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) to Pattinson.
