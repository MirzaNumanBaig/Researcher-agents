**Quick summary**

- The trailer for the action thriller *Om: Chapter 1, Udhiram: The Blood Wood* has been released, according to Gulf News.
- [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) plays IPS officer Nithya Reddy, who leads an anti-smuggling operation in Andhra Pradesh.
- Dhanush plays an undercover officer and Mammootty a shadowy figure believed to control the red sandalwood trade.
- The film opens worldwide on 20 November in Tamil, with dubbed versions in four other languages.

The first trailer for *Om: Chapter 1* gives Sai Pallavi a very different role from the one audiences have been talking about lately. She appears as a senior police officer running a joint operation against red sandalwood smugglers, opposite Dhanush and Mammootty, in a film due in cinemas on 20 November, Gulf News reported.

## What the trailer shows

Directed by [Rajkumar Periasamy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajkumar_Periasamy), the film follows a joint operation by the police forces of Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu to break up a smuggling network in the Seshachalam forest region, per [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/south-indian/dhanush-mammootty-and-sai-pallavi-face-off-in-intense-om-chapter-one-trailer-1.500747699).

Dhanush plays an officer from Tamil Nadu's Organised Crime Intelligence Unit who infiltrates the syndicate disguised as a woodcutter. The trailer shows him working alongside smugglers and slowly earning their trust. Some scenes, however, show his character apparently fighting the police, which leaves his true position in the operation unclear.

Pallavi's Nithya Reddy heads the Exclusive Special Task Force in Andhra Pradesh and leads what the trailer calls Operation Mole, a name that appears linked to the film's abbreviated title. Mammootty plays Siya, a character whose hold over the trade is hinted at throughout. The trailer suggests he could be the mastermind, though the film itself will have to confirm that.

## The trade behind the story

Red sandalwood, also called red sanders, grows mainly in the Seshachalam hills of Andhra Pradesh's Kadapa district. Gulf News notes it is in high demand in markets such as China and Japan, which makes it a target for organised smuggling.

The report adds that the trade has drawn in labourers from poorer communities in northern Tamil Nadu, and that their involvement has led to several fatal incidents over the years. Periasamy told Galatta Plus that the emotional core of the film was partly inspired by a real 2014 incident. He recalled a 30-second video of a girl of about two and a half at a ceremony, an image that stayed with him for more than ten years.

## A release date that moved twice

The film was first set for 16 October. Dhanush, who also produces, postponed it so it would not open a day after Rajinikanth's *Jailer 2*, which was scheduled for 15 October. In a note on X he said he made the call "out of immense respect" for the superstar. The makers also avoided the Diwali window to stay clear of Suriya's *Scene*.

Periasamy wrote and directed the film. It is his latest project after *Amaran*, for which he won the National Award for Best Director. Dhanush, Shraddha Agrawal and Azmat Jagmag produce it under [Wunderbar Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wunderbar_Films) and RTake Studios. Sreeleela also has a pivotal role, and the makers have kept the details of the other characters under wraps.

## What happens next

*Om: Chapter 1* is scheduled for a worldwide theatrical release on 20 November. It will play in Tamil, with dubbed versions in Telugu, Hindi, Malayalam and Kannada. More of the film's detail, including what the other characters are doing in the story, has not been revealed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Sai Pallavi play in Om: Chapter 1?
