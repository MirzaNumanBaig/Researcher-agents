**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) faced her first [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) dance-off last weekend against [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) and was saved by the judges.
- She had scored 36 points for Blackpool week, fourth on the leaderboard.
- On Friday's *It Takes Two*, former contestants [Sara Davies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sara_Davies) and JB Gill told her to put the dance-off out of her mind.
- Delta and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) dance a Couple's Choice to AC/DC in week 10, live on Saturday 28 November.

Delta Goodrem walks into week 10 of Strictly Come Dancing carrying an unusual burden for someone near the top of the table: the memory of a dance-off she did not expect. According to the [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/tv/strictly-star-makes-tough-admission-37924175) and [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/strictly-star-admits-it-must-34979083), two former contestants used Friday's *It Takes Two* to tell her how to handle it.

## What happened in the dance-off

Delta and her professional partner Kai Widdrington landed in the bottom two last weekend, facing Jaime Winstone and her partner [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu). The judges chose to keep Delta and Kai, and Winstone and Gu went home.

The result was a surprise on paper. Delta had scored 36 points for Blackpool week, which put her fourth on the leaderboard. [Cach Mercer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cach_Mercer) and [Maddie Ingoldsby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddie_Ingoldsby), John Nellis and [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones), and Tabby Stoecker and [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita) were joint first on 39, according to both outlets.

Afterwards Delta wrote on social media that the pair had made it through their first dance-off and that "the nerves were definitely high". She said she was relieved to stay another week but sad to see Winstone and Gu leave, adding that everyone at the show feels like a family.

## The advice from Sara Davies and JB Gill

Asked by host Ellie Taylor how the couple could come back "bigger and better", Davies said Delta would be fine as long as she did not let the dance-off get to her. Davies pointed out the oddity of being in the bottom two while near the top of the leaderboard. In her view, viewers may have assumed Delta did not need their votes because she had danced so well, so the result could leave her feeling that nobody had backed her.

JB Gill spoke from his own time on the show, when his professional partner Amy Dowden was injured and [Lauren Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Oakley) stepped in. He called the experience tough and said mindset matters. His advice was to treat the new week as a new dance and a fresh chance, and he added that Delta "has been shining".

The comments carry some weight because Delta has had a bumpier run than most. According to [Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/tv/entertainment/delta-goodrem-strictly-come-dancing-2026-contestant-age-instagram-explained/), she picked up a rib injury in week four, the pair were given a bye week, and in week seven she briefly danced with Aljaž Škorjanec while Kai was ill. Even so, her scores have climbed: 30 for her American Smooth in week two, 33 for the Viennese Waltz in week six and 35 with Škorjanec in week eight.

## Who is Delta Goodrem?

For viewers who know her only from the ballroom, Radio Times describes an Australian singer-songwriter who signed her first record deal at 15. Her 2003 debut album *Innocent Eyes* was certified 23 times platinum and is among the best-selling albums in Australian history. The same profile credits her with more than nine million albums sold worldwide, five number one albums, nine number one singles and 12 ARIA Awards.

She began on screen in 2002 as Nina Tucker in the soap *Neighbours*, a role she returned to in 2015 and 2022. Radio Times also says she represented Australia at Eurovision 2026 and finished fourth with 287 points.
