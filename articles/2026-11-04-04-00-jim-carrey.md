**Quick summary**

- [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey)'s Maui beachfront home is now "pending", meaning a buyer has been found, according to Realtor.com.
- The house was listed at US$35 million less than a month earlier.
- Records show he paid US$16.5 million for it in 2016.
- The listing came soon after Carrey's marriage to partner Min Ah, which a representative confirmed to People.
- It is not clear whether he will keep a home in Hawaii.

Jim Carrey's Hawaii retreat appears to be sold. Realtor.com says it can exclusively reveal the actor's Maui estate has moved to "pending" status, less than a month after it was listed for US$35 million (roughly A$53 million at recent exchange rates). The sale has not been completed, and the buyer has not been named.

## The Jim Carrey Maui home sale in numbers

The asking price was more than double what Carrey paid. Realtor.com cites records showing he bought the property for US$16.5 million in 2016, so the listing aimed to roughly double that over a decade of ownership. The final sale price has not been reported, and a pending status does not guarantee the deal will close at the asking figure.

The listing agent is Blake Hill of Hawaii Life. According to Realtor.com, the property is offered fully furnished.

## Inside the beachfront estate

Built in 2002, the house has three bedrooms and 4.5 bathrooms across 4,717 square feet. It sits on nearly an acre on Keawakapu Beach, where South Kihei Road ends and Wailea begins, with about 170 feet of water frontage.

The listing describes a great room that rises into the trusses and opens onto a covered stone lanai. The kitchen has two large islands and white cabinetry. The primary suite has a swinging bed and floor-to-ceiling windows, while outside a freeform pool winds through natural rock, with a grotto, a slide and an elevated spa.

The architecture firm Ike Kligerman Barkley designed it. The listing calls it a "bohemian luxury unicorn" and stresses how uncommon oceanfront land of this kind is on Maui.

## A quieter chapter for the Ace Ventura star

The sale follows a personal milestone. Realtor.com reports that Carrey, 64, married Min Ah in a small ceremony in Los Angeles, and that a representative for the actor confirmed the marriage to People. The report says the couple had rarely appeared together in public before a César Awards ceremony in Paris in February, where Carrey accepted an honorary award. From the stage he thanked his daughter, Jane, and his grandson, Jackson, and called Ah his "sublime companion".

The Maui house suited the low-profile life Carrey has described. Realtor.com says reports have him reading, writing, walking, painting and sketching on the island. In 2018 he described his Maui life as "an isolated life". In 2022 he said he was "fairly serious" about stepping away from acting and that he really liked his quiet life.

That history gives the sale extra weight for fans of the star of [Ace Ventura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ace_Ventura) and The Truman Show. Realtor.com notes it is unclear whether he plans to keep a base in Hawaii or return to California.

## What happens next

The property now has to move from pending to closed, and Realtor.com has not said when that might happen. Neither Carrey's representative nor the agent was quoted on the buyer or the final price in the report.
