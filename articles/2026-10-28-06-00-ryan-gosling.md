**Quick summary**

- Dead Man's Bones, the band of [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) and screenwriter Zach Shields, released the single "Lost in the Night" on 27 October 2026.
- It is the group's first new music in 17 years, according to VICE, MovieWeb and FLOOD Magazine.
- The video stars [Noah Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Jupe) and [Rain Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_Spencer).
- The band's Instagram caption said "More coming soon", but no album has been announced.

Ryan Gosling's spooky indie project Dead Man's Bones has come back from the dead. The band, which Gosling runs with Zach Shields, put out a new single called "Lost in the Night" on 27 October, four days before Halloween, ending a gap of 17 years since its only album.

## What the band has released

The song arrived with a black-and-white music video starring Noah Jupe and Rain Spencer. [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/ryan-gosling-dead-mans-bones-new-song/) describes it as a moody short film in which a young couple leaves a cemetery and wanders through a city.

[Tim Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Anderson_(musician)) produced the track. According to [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/ryan-gosling-resurrects-his-spooky-rock-band-after-17-years-releases-surprise-new-song-just-in-time-for-halloween/), he also produced the band's 2009 debut. [FLOOD Magazine](https://floodmagazine.com/235751/watch-dead-mans-bones-lost-in-the-night/) reports that the pair shopped the idea around several Los Angeles studios before settling on a farm outside the city. VICE calls it a barn. Both say they brought two guitars, two drum machines and two synthesisers, and FLOOD adds, with a "reportedly", that the song was recorded in a single day.

MovieWeb says Gosling handles vocals, piano and bass, while Shields plays drums and guitar. The children's choir that featured on the debut has been swapped for retro synths this time.

## How Eva Mendes set the scene

The release followed a small public nudge. FLOOD notes that Eva Mendes, Gosling's wife, posted a clip from the band's song "In the Room Where You Sleep" on Instagram last week. Her caption ended, "Sure wish they had new music coming out...". Days later, she had her wish.

## A band built on ghost stories

Dead Man's Bones released its self-titled debut in 2009 through ANTI-Records. VICE describes it as indie rock mixed with theatrical goth and folk, and says the idea grew from the pair's shared love of Disneyland's Haunted Mansion. The album featured the Silverlake Conservatory Children's Choir, which belongs to the music school founded by Flea of the Red Hot Chili Peppers.

Shields is a screenwriter, producer and director. VICE says he often works with [Michael Dougherty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dougherty), co-wrote Krampus (2015) and has contributed to the recent Godzilla films. The outlets disagree on when the band formed: VICE says 2005, MovieWeb says 2007. Both agree the group has been quiet since 2010, after a US tour in autumn 2009.

VICE draws a comparison with [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp), another actor who dabbled in music on the side. For Gosling, the contrast with his screen career is sharp. MovieWeb points to his run from The Notebook and Drive to The Fall Guy and, most recently, Project Hail Mary, plus his Oscar-nominated turn singing "I'm Just Ken" in Barbie.

## What happens next

Nothing further is confirmed. The band's Instagram post on 27 October read, "Thanks for sticking with us all these years. New single, 'Lost In The Night'. More coming soon." VICE notes that no sophomore album has been announced and it is unclear what comes next.

Both men are busy elsewhere. MovieWeb reports that Gosling is preparing for Shawn Levy's 2027 film Star Wars: Starfighter, while Shields has been hired to write Gladys, a prequel to Zach Cregger's Weapons.
