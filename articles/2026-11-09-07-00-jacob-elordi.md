**Quick summary**

- [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) was photographed with noticeably lighter hair in New York on Saturday 7 November 2026, according to E! News and Vogue.
- The pictures, obtained by the gossip account DeuxMoi, show him with [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner) before a Broadway performance of Paranormal Activity.
- E! News says his eyebrows also appear to have been lightened.
- Neither outlet reports a reason. Vogue notes he is currently filming the A24 movie Scapegoat.

[Jacob Elordi](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/) has swapped his dark waves for a much lighter shade, based on photos from a New York night out on Saturday 7 November. The Australian actor, 29, was seen with Jenner, 31, on a date night that ended at a Broadway performance of Paranormal Activity, as E! News reported.

## What the photos show

The images were obtained by DeuxMoi, and both E! News and Vogue built their reports on them. E! News describes one shot of Elordi sitting across from Jenner as they shared drinks before the show. He wore a black long-sleeved top and a matching black beanie, and Jenner wore a black trench coat.

The beanie keeps the full result hidden. What peeks out from under it is a sandy blonde, according to Vogue, which calls it a significant change from his usual chocolate-brown hair. E! News adds that the colour seems to extend to his eyebrows, which suggests a deliberate, full change rather than a few highlights.

Because the pictures come from a single evening and a single source, the exact shade and length are still unclear. Treat any detail beyond what the two outlets describe as guesswork.

## A habit of changing his look

Vogue points out that Elordi rarely sticks with one style. It lists a shaggy, bushy beard shown at the Marrakech International Film Festival in 2024, mutton chops for his turn as Heathcliff in [Wuthering Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights), and more recently a moustache and a 1990s-style mullet.

E! News adds one more entry. In September, a Los Angeles lunch outing with Jenner showed him with a goatee, which he has since shaved off.

The bigger transformations came on screen. Guillermo del Toro told E! News last year that Elordi spent around 10 hours a day in the chair to play the Creature in the 2025 adaptation of Frankenstein, with about 42 pieces of makeup and prosthetics. Elordi told Netflix's Tudum that the process changed his face and its structure, and that the costume's leather, furs and weight were all real.

## Could it be for a role?

Vogue offers a possible explanation, and it is careful to call it that. Elordi is shooting Scapegoat, an A24 film from [Ari Aster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Aster), the director of Hereditary and Midsommar, with [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) and [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law).

According to Vogue, the film reportedly follows a surgeon whose career comes under scrutiny after a social media influencer dies during a routine operation. Details are tightly guarded, and E! News also says it remains to be seen whether the makeover is tied to a new part.

Vogue also repeats a report that Elordi is in the running to be the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond). That is an unconfirmed report, and nothing in either article links it to the new hair.

## What happens next

No studio, representative or stylist has commented on the change in the coverage we reviewed. The next clear view will probably come from a full-face photo without the beanie, or from official Scapegoat material, if and when A24 releases any.

For now, the confirmed facts are small: a date night, a black beanie and a lighter shade of hair.
