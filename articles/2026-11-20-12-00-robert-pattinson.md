**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was officially unveiled as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) on 16 November.
- [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby) have both posted messages of support on Instagram, according to USA Today.
- Pattinson will debut as 007 in a film from [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve). No title or release date has been announced.
- Attention has turned to the female lead, and Amanda Seyfried joked online that she might be "too old" for the part.

Two former James Bond actors have welcomed Robert Pattinson to the franchise within days of his casting being confirmed. Brosnan posted his message on 18 November, while Lazenby sent a video, according to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2026/11/19/robert-pattinson-james-bond-pierce-brosnan-reaction/92857817007/).

## Robert Pattinson James Bond: what the former 007s said

Brosnan shared an edited photo of Pattinson as Bond on Instagram. "Welcome to the world of Bond," he wrote. "You are going to be magnificent."

The two men know each other. Brosnan played the father of Pattinson's character in the 2010 drama *Remember Me*, and he said he has "fond memories" of making it. He added that it has been "a joy to watch you create a remarkable body of work."

Brosnan played Bond in four films between 1995 and 2002, from *GoldenEye* to [*Die Another Day*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Another_Day). He took over from [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton) and was followed by [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig).

Lazenby, who played Bond in the 1969 film *On Her Majesty's Secret Service*, is the oldest living Bond actor. His video was addressed to Pattinson directly. "Welcome to the most exclusive club in the world," he said, before urging him to enjoy himself and hoping for a "this never happened to the other fella" moment.
Brosnan's support has history behind it. When he promoted *Remember Me* in 2010, he told IrishCentral that Pattinson, then deep in the *Twilight* films as [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen), had handled the "vortex of fame" gracefully. Earlier this month he told Vanity Fair he was "just an audience member now" with no inside knowledge of who would be chosen.

## Co-stars and friends join in

[Rami Malek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rami_Malek) has a double connection to the story. He worked with Pattinson on *The Twilight Saga: Breaking Dawn – Part 2* and played the villain Safin in [*No Time to Die*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Time_to_Die). He told People magazine that Pattinson will be "quite extraordinary" in the role. "I've known Rob for quite some time and I could not be more proud," Malek said.

Pattinson's own statement called Bond "one of the most legendary characters in cinema" and said being entrusted with 007 is "the honour of a lifetime." The announcement came with a photo and a video of him in a tuxedo holding a gun, according to USA Today.

## The Bond girl guessing game starts

With the lead settled, fans and bookmakers have moved on to the female lead. Yahoo, republishing a Men's Journal report from 18 November, says Zendaya, Anya Taylor-Joy, Margaret Qualley and Margot Robbie are among the names circulating on social media and betting sites. These are fan and bookmaker suggestions, not casting news.

Seyfried added her own voice under a Variety Instagram post that ranked possible picks. Her comment was short: "Am I too old?" According to the report, she is 40 and turns 41 next month, which makes her only a few months older than Pattinson, who is also 40.

The report points out that Bond leading women have often been a decade or more younger than the man playing 007. It notes that Monica Bellucci, who is four years older than Craig, is one of the few exceptions in the Craig films.

## What happens next

The film itself is still a blank. USA Today reports that Villeneuve, the director of *Dune*, will direct Pattinson's debut, but no title or release date has been announced.

Pattinson has other work arriving first. Wikipedia lists the Fernando Meirelles film *The Take*, with Daisy Edgar-Jones and Danai Gurira in the cast, for release on 15 January 2027. For more on the star's screen work, see our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) coverage.
