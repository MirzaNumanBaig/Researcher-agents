**Quick summary**

- A new [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/johnny-depp-jack-sparrow-panicked-disney-execs-pirates-1236756163/) account says [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) asked Disney for a live-action role at a meeting in late 2000.
- The studio's then chairman, [Dick Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dick_Cook), had only a one- or two-page outline titled Pirates of the Caribbean.
- Depp's new film Ebenezer opened at No. 1 worldwide with an estimated US$42 million, according to Variety and the Associated Press.
- Critics are cooler than audiences, and his next film, Day Drinker, is due in 2027.

Johnny Depp walked into Disney's Burbank lot in late 2000 hoping for a family-friendly part, and walked out as the rogue pirate of a film that did not yet exist. The Hollywood Reporter has now published the story of that meeting, and it lands in the same week Depp's Ebenezer won the global box office.

## How the Pirates meeting happened

According to the Hollywood Reporter, the call came from agent [Tracey Jacobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracey_Jacobs) and [United Talent Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Talent_Agency) chairman [Jim Berkus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Berkus). They wanted Depp in a room with Cook, who had worked his way up from a ride operator at Disneyland to head of the Walt Disney Motion Picture Group.

It was an odd match. Depp's next release was From Hell, in which he played an opium-fuelled [Scotland Yard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland_Yard) detective hunting Jack the Ripper. The report says that film took US$74 million on a budget of about US$35 million, respectable but nowhere near Disney numbers. His breakthrough role, [Edward Scissorhands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scissorhands), was about as far from the studio's usual fare as it gets.

Cook recalled that Depp looked like "just a normal guy", with a couple of extra rings. Depp explained his reason for coming: he was living outside Paris with Vanessa Paradis and their young daughter, and the family spent its time watching Disney films. He told Cook that none of his own movies were really suitable for children.

## A one-page idea and a rogue

Cook offered voice work in an animated film. Depp said he could do that, but wanted real acting in a live-action Disney picture. Cook had almost nothing, only a short outline for a movie based on the Pirates of the Caribbean ride.

The idea was far from a sure thing. Cook told the magazine he had received enough emails calling it the dumbest idea ever to fill an office, since pirate films had not been popular since the days of Errol Flynn. Disney was also about to release The Country Bears, another ride-style property, which the report says earned only US$18 million against an estimated US$35 million budget.

Depp did not care about the numbers. When Cook described two pirates and a love interest, with one of the pirates a rogue, Depp leaned forward and asked, "With a sword?" Then he said he wanted to be the rogue pirate. Cook pointed out that there was no script, director or cast. The report is cut short at that point, so how the part became Jack Sparrow is not covered in what we have read.

## Ebenezer's opening weekend

The timing is neat. According to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/johnny-depp-ebenezer-ramayana-godzilla-minus-zero-global-box-office-1236948618/), Paramount's Ebenezer won the global box office this weekend, edging out the Indian epic Ramayana: Rise of a Legend, while Toho's [Godzilla Minus Zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla_Minus_Zero) passed US$100 million.

[Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/entertainment/johnny-depps-ebenezer-comeback-has-scrooge-brought-hollywood-back-on-his-side) reports an estimated US$19 million in North America and US$23 million overseas, for US$42 million worldwide, against a reported production budget of US$38 million. The Associated Press, as relayed by [UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/johnny-depp-has-returned-to-the-top-of-the-box-office-for-the-first-time-in-8-years-the-new-film-has-already-grossed-dollar42-million), says it played in 3,535 North American theatres and 63 international markets. Reports describe it as Depp's first studio film to top the North American chart in eight years.

Audiences are warmer than critics. Open Magazine cites an A- CinemaScore and a 90% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, while the critics' score sits in the low-to-mid 50s (UNN puts it at 52%). A PostTrak survey found 51% of moviegoers named Depp as the main reason they bought a ticket.

## What the numbers do and don't prove

Directed by Ti West, the film also stars Rupert Grint, Daisy Ridley, Sam Claflin and Andrea Riseborough. Open Magazine notes it is Depp's first major Hollywood production since the 2022 defamation trial involving Amber Heard, and cautions that an opening weekend cannot show whether the film will be profitable once marketing costs and later weeks are counted.

Read side by side, the two stories make a useful contrast. In 2000 Depp was a cult favourite pitching himself to a cautious studio. In 2026 he is a name that 51% of ticket buyers say they came for.

## What happens next

Ebenezer's second weekend will show whether the US$42 million start has legs. Depp's next film, the thriller Day Drinker, is expected in 2027, with UNN giving March 2027.
