**Quick summary**

- Twilight director Catherine Hardwicke says Robert Pattinson looked so suave in sunglasses on set that he inspired the "Bond, James Bond" line in the 2008 film.
- She made the comments on The Kyle Buchanan Show, in an episode dated 24 November, according to Complex.
- Pattinson was confirmed earlier this month as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig, with Denis Villeneuve directing.
- Hardwicke said she "almost fell out of" her bed when she heard the news.

[Catherine Hardwicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Hardwicke) has explained how a throwaway prom joke in Twilight ended up predicting the future of [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson). The director told The Kyle Buchanan Show that his look in one scene was what prompted the line "Bond, James Bond", nearly two decades before he was named the new 007.

## The Twilight scene fans resurfaced

When Pattinson's casting was announced, Twilight fans quickly dug up a moment from the 2008 film. According to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/twilight-director-robert-pattinson-james-bond-1236956867/), [Justin Chon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Chon)'s character, Eric Yorkie, hands out flyers announcing that the Forks High School prom theme is Monte Carlo. He promises "gambling, tuxedos and Bond. James Bond." The camera then cuts to Pattinson's [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen).

Hardwicke said the cut was no accident. "That's the one scene where I was like, 'Let's put the sunglasses on that day,'" she told the show, as quoted by [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/twilight-director-robert-pattinson-inspired-james-bond-line-1236763521/). Pattinson stepped out of a car and looked so suave, she said, that it inspired the line. She added that Chon "kind of ad-libbed" it, and that the crew did think he looked like Bond. "So I guess we were seeing the future," she said.

## "I almost fell out of my bed"

Hardwicke was candid that she had not expected the casting. She told the show she did not think producers would choose him because of The Batman and his fame, adding that they "like to get the unknown". [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/twilight-director-reaction-robert-pattinson-james-bond-1237233564/) reports she then joked that it was the same bed where Pattinson and Kristen Stewart first auditioned together and did their kissing scene.

According to Variety, Hardwicke has told the story before. On a 2022 podcast she said Pattinson arrived at the audition with black dyed hair and "wacky bangs", looking a little dishevelled. She said he got so absorbed in the scene that he fell off the bed. Stewart's verdict afterwards, Hardwicke recalled, was that it had to be Pattinson.

Complex adds that Hardwicke said she was "thrilled" for him, and that she saw "electricity" between him and Stewart at that audition.

## How Pattinson got to Bond

Pattinson, now 40, is replacing Daniel Craig, whose run ended with No Time to Die in 2021. Complex reports he beat Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden to the part in the Amazon MGM Studios film, which Villeneuve is directing.

His route there was unusual for a blockbuster star. The Art Journal notes that after Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire in 2005 and Twilight, he chose smaller films with directors such as David Cronenberg, Claire Denis and Robert Eggers rather than a standard multiplex career. The publication says that choice is why critics have called the casting "weird" in a positive sense.

Hardwicke made a similar point about the actor himself. "He's intense and interesting," she said. "He does not fit the mold."

## What the bookmakers expect

Betting markets have already moved. OLBG reports that William Hill make his first Bond film 4/9 to be released in 2028, and 8/13 to take more than US$1 billion worldwide. Skyfall is currently the only Bond film to pass that mark, according to the same report. These are betting odds, not forecasts from the studio, and no release date has been announced in the sources reviewed.

## What happens next

Neither Amazon MGM nor Villeneuve has set out a start date for filming in the reports we reviewed, so the timing of Pattinson's first Bond film remains open. Pattinson also has [The Take](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Take_(2027_film)), directed by Fernando Meirelles, listed for 15 January 2027. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who said Robert Pattinson inspired the "Bond, James Bond" line?
Twilight director Catherine Hardwicke said it on The Kyle Buchanan Show. She said his look in sunglasses on set prompted the line, which Justin Chon then delivered.

### Who is directing the new James Bond film?
Denis Villeneuve is directing, according to Complex and The Hollywood Reporter. [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) producers include Amy Pascal and David Heyman.

### Who did Pattinson beat for the role?
Complex names Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden as the other actors in contention.

No official date has been given in the reports reviewed. Bookmakers favour 2028, but that is a betting market and not an announcement.
