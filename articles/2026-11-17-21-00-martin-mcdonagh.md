**Quick summary**

- Two new reviews of [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), the latest film from [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh), were published on 17 November 2026.
- Both single out John Malkovich and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) as CIA agents who bicker their way through the story.
- Comic Book Resources calls it less bleak than The Banshees of Inisherin but says it spirals out of control at the end.
- AZ Family goes further, calling it a "masterpiece" and tipping Malkovich for a Best Actor run.

The first critics' verdicts on *Wild Horse Nine* are landing, and they lean warm. Reviews from [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) and AZ Family on 17 November describe a funny, talky film in which John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell carry most of the weight, with McDonagh back on the awards radar four years after *The Banshees of Inisherin*.

## What the film is about

According to the [Comic Book Resources review](https://www.cbr.com/wild-horse-nine-review/), the story follows two CIA agents, Chris Carlisle (Malkovich) and Lee Chesterfield (Rockwell), working in Chile before the 1973 coup. Despite the setup, the reviewer says it is not a film of gunfights and action. Most of it unfolds on Easter Island, where the pair go on a holiday to fulfil Chris's final wishes after a fatal diagnosis.

AZ Family's critic describes the same premise in darker terms. Its [review](https://www.azfamily.com/2026/11/17/wild-horse-nine-review-john-malkovich-sam-rockwell-give-career-best-performances-martin-mcdonaghs-latest-masterpiece/) says Carlisle is slipping mentally because of cancer and dementia, which pushes him to reflect on a life of toppling governments and risks exposing old secrets.

## The double act

Both reviewers spend most of their space on the leads. Comic Book Resources says Malkovich swings between teasing and barking at Rockwell's character in an instant, while hints of Chris's pain show in his face. It calls Rockwell's turn as Lee compelling, a man torn between loyalty to a friend and loyalty to the agency.

AZ Family writes that both men may be giving the best performances of their careers, and says Malkovich deserves to be in the [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actor) conversation this awards season. It also highlights Chris's recorded monologues, which wander into questions such as whether dinosaurs had souls.

The supporting cast gets credit too. Comic Book Resources says [Steve Buscemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Buscemi), as the boss known as MJ, becomes comic relief, and that [Parker Posey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parker_Posey) builds a layered character as Lee's wife, Marge, in only two phone scenes. Both outlets single out Chilean actor Mariana di Girolamo, who plays student Monica. AZ Family calls her the only sympathetic figure in the film and the source of its heart.

## How it compares with McDonagh's earlier work

Comic Book Resources notes that The Banshees of Inisherin earned nine Oscar nominations and three Golden Globes, and that Rockwell won an Oscar for *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*. It says the new film reuses much of the Banshees formula but feels fresh because of its spy-conspiracy backdrop.

AZ Family sees an echo of McDonagh's debut, [In Bruges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Bruges), another story of two killers in a foreign place, but argues the similarities end quickly. It adds that the characters are, in its words, genuinely terrible people responsible for killings, torture and imprisonment, so audiences should not expect anyone to cheer for.

## Where the critics split

The praise comes with caveats. Comic Book Resources says that when so much of a film is two people talking, you can almost forget little is happening, and it warns that the ending gets out of hand. AZ Family also notes the final stretch turns dark and depressing, though it means that as a compliment. Neither review is a final word, and these are two voices from a single day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in Wild Horse Nine?
John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell play the CIA agents Chris Carlisle and Lee Chesterfield. The reviews also name Steve Buscemi, Parker Posey and Mariana di Girolamo.

### What is Wild Horse Nine about?
It follows two CIA agents in 1973, mostly on Chile's Easter Island, as one reflects on his past while seriously ill. Politics is the backdrop, and the reviewers describe a comedy about mortality, friendship and loyalty.

### Is Wild Horse Nine as bleak as The Banshees of Inisherin?
Comic Book Resources says it is a lot less bleak than that film. AZ Family says it is hilarious but turns dark near the end.
