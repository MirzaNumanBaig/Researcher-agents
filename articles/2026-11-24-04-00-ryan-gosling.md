**Quick summary**

- Ryan Gosling Ghost Rider news: [Rosamund Pike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosamund_Pike) is reportedly being considered for the female lead opposite [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) in Marvel's film.
- The claim comes from a newsletter by journalist [Jeff Sneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Sneider). Neither Pike nor Marvel Studios has confirmed it.
- A separate report on 19 November named Pike and [Betty Gilpin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betty_Gilpin) as possible names for a female role.
- The film is slated for cinemas in 2028, with [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) directing.

Ryan Gosling Ghost Rider casting talk has a new name attached. According to a report by Speaks Media, Rosamund Pike has been linked to the female lead in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) project, though nothing has been confirmed by the studio.

## Where the Rosamund Pike report comes from

The Speaks Media piece, published on 23 November, says the idea first surfaced in the InSneider newsletter run by Jeff Sneider. He reportedly wrote that Pike was under consideration to play the romantic interest of Johnny Blaze, the character most widely expected to be Gosling's role.

The outlet is open about the limits here. It says the source's reliability "has not been firmly established", that neither actress has said anything publicly, and that Marvel has made no casting announcement. Treat it as talk, not news of a signing.

A second report, dated 19 November, floated Pike and Betty Gilpin together as names in the mix for a female part, according to the same article.

## What we know about the film

Marvel has announced that Gosling will headline a live-action Ghost Rider film, per Speaks Media. [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy), who made *Deadpool & Wolverine*, is developing it as director, and [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper) is attached to write the screenplay. The release is currently pencilled in for 2028.

Which version of the character Gosling plays is still officially open. Johnny Blaze is the common assumption. In the comics he is a motorcycle stunt rider who becomes Ghost Rider after a supernatural bargain, and the same source notes that his story is tied to Roxanne Simpson, which is why a Pike casting would make sense if the film follows the classic storyline.

The character has been on screen before. [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage) played him in *Ghost Rider* (2007) and *Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance* (2012), so Gosling's version will be a fresh start.

## Why Pike makes the rumour stick

Pike earned an Oscar nomination for Best Actress for playing [Amy Dunne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Dunne) in *Gone Girl* (2014), and her credits include *The Wheel of Time*, *I Care a Lot* and the [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film [Die Another Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Another_Day). That record of complicated, high-stakes roles is why fans took the report seriously, according to Speaks Media.

## Gosling's other screen moments this week

Gosling is also in the conversation for other reasons. ComingSoon.net reports that *Blade Runner 2099*, a series set after his film *Blade Runner 2049* (2017), is among Prime Video's releases for the week of 23 to 29 November. Created by Silka Luisa, it is set in a Los Angeles where Replicants hold power and humans are second-class citizens, with Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer in the cast.

Meanwhile, Montenegrin broadcaster TV Vijesti is screening [Only God Forgives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_God_Forgives), the 2013 Bangkok revenge thriller directed by Nicolas Winding Refn, according to vijesti.me. It stars Gosling alongside [Kristin Scott Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristin_Scott_Thomas), played in competition at Cannes in 2013 and drew mixed reviews. It also won Best Film at the Sydney Film Festival that year, per the outlet.
