**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) presented an award at the 15th Hamilton Behind the Camera Awards on Friday 13 November 2026, according to WWD.
- He honoured prosthetic makeup designer Joe Dunckley in the Hair and Makeup category for his work on *Wicker*.
- He skipped black tie for a dusty pink liquid satin shirt from COS, the brand he fronts as global ambassador for its Spring/Summer 2026 collection.
- *Wicker* premiered at Sundance in January and reached cinemas in October.

Alexander Skarsgård swapped the usual black tie for pink satin on Friday night, presenting the Hair and Makeup award at the 15th Hamilton Behind the Camera Awards in Hollywood. Fashion outlet [WWD](https://wwd.com/pop-culture/celebrity-news/alexander-skarsgard-satin-cos-mens-shirt-hamilton-awards-1239416644/) reports that he used the stage to recognise the artist who helped turn him into the Wicker Husband.

## Why Skarsgård was on stage

Skarsgård handed the award to prosthetic makeup designer Joe Dunckley, whose work on *Wicker* earned the honour. According to WWD, he gave a speech about Dunckley's contribution. The film is the reason the two share a connection: Skarsgård wore heavy prosthetics to play the Wicker Husband, so Dunckley's craft is central to how the character looks on screen.

The ceremony was presented with Variety and held at Avalon in Hollywood, based on the photo caption carried by [Yahoo News Singapore](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/alexander-skarsg-rd-glistens-liquid-191312831.html), which republished the WWD report.

## The look: pink satin and a cummerbund

WWD describes a deliberately loose take on formalwear. The shirt was a dusty pink, blouse-like COS piece in a liquid satin finish, worn with the top three buttons undone. The sleeves were long and a little oversized, which gave the top a puffy, almost nautical shape.

Below the chest the outfit tightened. A cummerbund sat at the waist and led into high-waisted black trousers, creating a sharp block of contrast between pink and black. WWD says the lights picked up the sheen of the fabric as he spoke.

The brand choice was not a surprise. Skarsgård is COS's global brand ambassador for Spring/Summer 2026, and the label is Swedish, like the actor. Our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section tracks more red carpet looks like this one.

## A pattern of unconventional red carpet choices

WWD notes that Skarsgård is known for unusual styling. At the 70th BFI London Film Festival earlier this year, where *Wicker* screened on 8 October, he wore a metallic gold jacket and matching trousers with Saint Laurent's clear lace-up shoes. The outlet also points back to his role in HBO's *Succession*, where his character was often seen walking around barefoot.

## About Wicker

*Wicker* is based on a short story by Ursula Wills. It follows a lonely woman who asks a basket weaver to make her a husband out of wicker. According to WWD, the film premiered at Sundance in January, opened in cinemas this October, and drew praise for its storytelling and its special effects. That reception is the reason Skarsgård was asked to present the makeup award.
