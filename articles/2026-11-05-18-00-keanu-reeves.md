**Quick summary**

- DC Studios has hired Francis Galluppi to write a new John Constantine film, according to a Deadline exclusive.
- The project is in very early development, with no other attachments announced.
- It is separate from the long-delayed sequel to Keanu Reeves' 2005 film, which is still said to be in development.
- DC Studios has not commented publicly, and it is unclear whether the film belongs to James Gunn's DC Universe or an Elseworlds banner.

A second big-screen Constantine is on the way, and it does not star Keanu Reeves. DC Studios has hired writer-director [Francis Galluppi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Galluppi) to script a new John Constantine movie, [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/704199-john-constantine-new-movie-not-keanu-reeves-sequel) reports, citing Deadline. It is the first creative hire on the project, which is at a very early stage.

## Who is Francis Galluppi?

Galluppi directed *The Last Stop in Yuma County*, a debut feature that premiered to critical acclaim at [Fantastic Fest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Fest), according to SuperHeroHype. He also wrote the upcoming [Evil Dead Wrath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evil_Dead_Wrath), which Warner Bros. will release on 7 April 2028.

Filmmaker [Sam Raimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Raimi) praised his work, saying Galluppi "knows when to keep us waiting in simmering tension and when to hit us with explosive violence". That suits a character built on dread and the occult.

## What happens to the Reeves sequel?

[Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) played the Hellblazer in Warner Bros.' 2005 film *Constantine*, and fans have waited for a follow-up ever since. [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/john-constantine-new-dc-movie-development/) notes that the sequel was recently described as still in development after the Warner Bros. Discovery and Paramount Skydance merger.

Reeves has said the creative team is still pushing it forward. According to SuperHeroHype, director Francis Lawrence and writer [Akiva Goldsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiva_Goldsman) planned to bring it to DC Studios as an "Elseworlds" title, and Lawrence blamed "regime changes" at Warner Bros. and DC Studios for the delays. Reeves has also said it would be set in the "same world" as the original and that Constantine would be "tortured even more".

DC Studios co-head [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) gave a more cautious picture in an October 2025 interview with BobaTalks. "I've discussed it on and off. I discussed it with Keanu," he said, adding that he had not read a script even though Reeves had said a new draft existed.

So the two films are not the same project. Neither outlet reports that Reeves is attached to Galluppi's film, and neither reports that his sequel has been cancelled.

## Why Constantine, and why now?

The character has a crowded history for a cult favourite. Constantine first appeared in *The Saga of the Swamp Thing* #37 in 1985, created by [Alan Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Moore), [Steve Bissette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_R._Bissette) and John Totleben as a working-class British detective who deals in magic and con artistry. [Matt Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Ryan_(actor)) played him in NBC's *Constantine*, which ran for one season from 2014, and later returned to the role in The CW's Arrowverse, according to ScreenRant.

ScreenRant also recalls an earlier attempt at a series. During J.J. Abrams' Justice League Dark plans, Guy Bolton was reported by Variety in 2022 to have written four scripts for a Constantine show, but that project was scrapped once Reeves' sequel was announced.

The timing may also reflect the studio's recent horror push. ScreenRant points out that the news comes weeks after James Watkins' *Clayface* opened. The film has grossed $90.5 million worldwide against a reported budget of $30 million to $44 million. It holds a 78 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score from 218 reviews and a 94 per cent audience score. ScreenRant suggests, as its own view, that this success could have encouraged more DC horror. It adds that James Mangold's *Swamp Thing* is still being written. That is speculation, not a DC Studios statement.

## What happens next

DC Studios has yet to comment, and Deadline has not said whether Galluppi's film sits inside Gunn's DC Universe or under the Elseworlds label that already houses Matt Reeves' Batman films. ScreenRant argues the two Constantine projects could coexist, but that remains its reading rather than anything announced.

In the meantime, Reeves has other work on his slate. [Collider](https://collider.com/keanu-reeves-bill-and-ted-excellent-adventure-arcade/) reports that he and Alex Winter voice the 16-bit-style game *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure: Arcade*, with a BackerKit crowdfunding campaign planned for February 2027 and no release date yet. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Keanu Reeves in the new Constantine movie?
Not according to current reports. Neither SuperHeroHype nor ScreenRant says he is attached to Francis Galluppi's film.

### Is the Constantine sequel with Keanu Reeves still happening?
ScreenRant says it was recently described as still in development. It has been delayed for years, and Gunn said in October 2025 that he had not read a script.

### Who is writing the new Constantine film?
Francis Galluppi, who directed *The Last Stop in Yuma County* and wrote the upcoming *Evil Dead Wrath*, according to Deadline as reported by SuperHeroHype.

### When will the new Constantine movie be released?
There is no release date. The project is in very early development.
