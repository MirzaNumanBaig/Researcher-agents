**Quick summary**

- Netflix's *Unabomber*, with [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) in a supporting role, has now spent a full week outside the platform's Top 10, according to ScreenRant.
- The film collected nearly 53 million views in its first few weeks, based on figures ScreenRant attributes to FlixPatrol.
- Critics were unkind: it holds a 25% "Rotten" score on Rotten Tomatoes.
- Crowe still has other titles performing on streaming charts, including *Nuremberg* and *The Get Out*.

*Unabomber* has run out of road on Netflix's charts. [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/russell-crowe-unabomber-end-era-netflix-streaming-chart-drop-october-2026/) that the crime thriller, in which Crowe plays Harvard professor Henry Murray, has now been out of the streamer's Top 10 for a full week, ending a run of close to a month.

## How the film fared on Netflix

According to ScreenRant, citing FlixPatrol, the film first dropped off the chart on 16 October, climbed back in at No. 10 on 20 October, and has now been absent for seven straight days. That makes a return unlikely unless Netflix releases something that pairs naturally with it.

The numbers tell a more interesting story than the final position. *Unabomber* opened with 16.4 million views, a solid but modest start for the service. Its second week was stronger, rising 52% to 24.9 million. Then came the third week, when Ben Affleck's *Animals* and Tyler Perry's *Doing Life* took the top spots and Crowe's film slid to No. 7 with 7.3 million views.

Across its run, ScreenRant puts the total at nearly 53 million views. For a film that critics largely rejected, that is a respectable audience.

## What the film is about

Directed by Emmy nominee [Janus Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janus_Metz_Pedersen), the 100-minute thriller retells the story of Ted Kaczynski, the American domestic terrorist, across two timelines. One follows the FBI investigation, centred on a fictional agent, Joane Miller, who works to decode his manifesto. The other goes back to his time at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he took part in psychological experiments run by Murray.

[Jacob Tremblay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Tremblay) plays Kaczynski, [Shailene Woodley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shailene_Woodley) is Miller, and [Annabelle Wallis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabelle_Wallis) appears as Harvard professor Christiana Morgan. Crowe plays Murray, described by ScreenRant as a polarising figure.

The reception was cool. ScreenRant notes a 25% "Rotten" rating on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). By contrast, *The Weight*, the Ethan Hawke historical drama Crowe also appears in, sits at 94% and is described as his best-reviewed film of the year, with a "[Certified Fresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes)" badge.

## Crowe's other streaming results

ScreenRant says the actor's year has been busy after a quiet 2025. It began with the sports drama *Beast*, which he helped co-write. The outlet adds that *The Get Out*, another crime thriller, has stayed in Paramount+'s Top 10 Movies chart for well over a month, and that *Nuremberg* has been climbing global charts on several platforms.

His Oscar-winning *Gladiator* has also returned to Netflix, according to the same report, after its sequel was added to the platform. ## What happens next

ScreenRant considers a comeback possible but unlikely. It points to the lasting appetite for true crime, and to viewers who may go looking for something in the vein of *Mindhunter*, written by [Joe Penhall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Penhall) with David Fincher. That is the outlet's speculation, not a Netflix announcement.
