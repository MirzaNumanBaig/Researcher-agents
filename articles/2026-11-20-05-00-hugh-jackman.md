**Quick summary**

- Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige says an old interview with [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) helped inspire the X-Men crossover in *Avengers: Doomsday*.
- Jackman said in 2013, around *The Wolverine*, that Marvel's biggest heroes could share one film.
- *Avengers: Doomsday* is due in cinemas on 16 December 2026.
- Jackman is also among more than 150 Australian artists urging the government to reject a gas project near Scott Reef.
- *The Prestige*, his 2006 thriller with Christian Bale, leaves Netflix in the UK on 11 December.

Kevin Feige has pointed to a 13-year-old Hugh Jackman interview as part of the reason the original X-Men appear in *Avengers: Doomsday*, according to reports published on 19 November. It is a neat piece of Marvel history, and it lands in a busy week for the Australian actor, who is also lending his name to an environmental campaign at home.

## What Feige said about Jackman and the X-Men

Around the release of *The Wolverine* in 2013, Jackman said he believed there had to be a way to bring Iron Man, the Avengers, Wolverine, the X-Men and Spider-Man together in one place. [Chosunbiz](https://biz.chosun.com/en/en-entertainment/2026/11/19/RDHX7M2LSREUNPCVN6VSIQQGRA/) and [SBS News](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008854084) both resurfaced that quote this week, and both are working from translated material.

Feige, in an interview those outlets cite, described bringing in the X-Men as a fun way to close a story that has run for 25 years. He added that he remembered seeing Jackman talk about the two teams meeting one day, and that it "just felt right".

The reports say the film brings back a large group of original X-Men cast members, who share the screen with the Avengers and the Fantastic Four. None of the sources we read confirms which individual actors appear, so this article does not either.

## The film they are talking about

According to [Star News Korea](https://www.starnewskorea.com/en/movie/2026/11/19/2026111916013742948), *Avengers: Doomsday* sees heroes from different worlds unite against the villain Doctor Doom, who threatens to destroy every reality. It opens in cinemas on 16 December 2026.

Chosunbiz adds that Chris Evans and Chris Hemsworth will travel to Korea with Feige for a press conference and a green carpet event before release. Evans has not made a Marvel promotional trip there in 11 years, the outlet says. ## Jackman joins a Scott Reef campaign

Away from Marvel, Jackman is one of more than 150 Australian artists who signed an open letter asking Federal Environment Minister Murray Watt to reject Woodside Energy's proposed Browse gas development, according to [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/rest-of-world/hugh-jackman-and-more-than-150-australian-artists-have-joined-a-campaign-to-protect-scott-reef-from-a-gas-project-proposing-up-to-57-wells-near-endangered-whales-and-vulnerable-turtles-with-a-government-decision-pending/articleshow/135984588.cms). That report draws on The Guardian and Greenpeace.

Other signatories named include Rebel Wilson, Simon Baker, Richard Roxburgh, Mia Wasikowska, Jessica Mauboy, Tina Arena, Paul Kelly and Missy Higgins. The project would drill up to 57 wells near Scott Reef, roughly 270 to 300 kilometres off the Kimberley coast in Western Australia.

Greenpeace says the reef supports more than 1,500 species, including endangered pygmy blue whales and vulnerable turtles, and estimates the project could produce about 1.6 billion tonnes of climate pollution over its life. Woodside says the reef itself would not be drilled, mined or built on, and describes Browse as a natural gas and condensate project. The federal assessment is still running, with the timeline paused while more technical advice is considered, The Guardian reported.
