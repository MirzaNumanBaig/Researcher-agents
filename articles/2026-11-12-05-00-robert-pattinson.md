**Quick summary**

- Director Denis Villeneuve was asked by GQ whether the next James Bond has been cast and replied that it is "in process".
- Asked to blink once for Robert Pattinson or twice for Callum Turner, he covered his eyes instead, according to FandomWire.
- No official casting announcement has been made.
- Vogue reports that Pattinson apparently faced Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Nicholas Hoult in the final round of screen tests.

The race to play 007 is still open, and a single gesture has fuelled the latest round of speculation. [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who is directing the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film, declined to name his lead when GQ put the question to him, and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) is one of the names at the centre of it.

## What Villeneuve actually said

According to [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/james-bond-denis-villeneuve-reaction-robert-pattinson/), GQ approached the director at a #GQMOTY screening and asked whether a Bond had been chosen. His answer was short: "It's in process."

The interviewer then pushed, asking him to blink once if the actor was Pattinson and twice if it was [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner). Villeneuve covered his eyes. GQ posted the clip and left viewers to interpret it.

That is the whole of the evidence. A hand over the eyes is not a confirmation, and FandomWire itself reads it as a sign that the part has not been filled. No studio, producer or representative has said otherwise.

## Who is reportedly in the running

The shortlist comes from a different outlet. In a column arguing for Pattinson, [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/the-case-for-robert-pattinson-as-bond) says he "apparently" competed in the final round of screen tests against Turner, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Josh O'Connor and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult). The wording matters: Vogue presents the list as reported, not confirmed.

FandomWire adds that several actors have been screen-tested and that Turner, known for Masters of the Air, still has a real chance. Both outlets are describing a process that remains unannounced, so the names should be treated as reports.

## The case being made for Pattinson

Both pieces are opinion as much as news, and they make different arguments.

FandomWire points to the cold, controlled intensity he brought to The Batman, his spy-thriller turn in Christopher Nolan's Tenet and the range shown in The Lighthouse. It also notes that he has worked with Villeneuve on Dune: Part Three, so the director already knows how he performs.

Vogue's writer takes a broader view. They describe a star who balances franchise work, from [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) and Twilight to The Batman, with unusual choices such as Mickey 17, Primetime and The Drama. The column argues that his slightly offbeat profile is a strength, and that the least obvious pick can pay off.

## The Craig precedent

Vogue leans on history. When [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) took over from Pierce Brosnan, the column recalls, he was criticised for being blond and under six feet tall, labelled bland by tabloids and the subject of a petition that gathered hundreds of thousands of signatures. Casino Royale followed a year later, and many fans rate it the best Bond film.

The column also notes the shift behind the camera. Amazon now controls the franchise, and the next film is being produced by Amy Pascal and David Heyman rather than the long-time team of [Barbara Broccoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Broccoli) and Michael G. Wilson. Steven Knight, creator of Peaky Blinders, is writing it. Vogue sees Villeneuve's involvement as a sign the series may loosen up.

## What happens next

There is no confirmed date for a casting announcement. Villeneuve's "in process" suggests a decision is still being made. Until Amazon or the producers say so, Pattinson, Turner and the other reported finalists remain candidates only.
