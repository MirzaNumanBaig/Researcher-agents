**Quick summary**

- [Renate Reinsve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Reinsve) has reportedly met Marvel about the role of Moira MacTaggert in its X-Men reboot.
- The claim comes from scooper Caleb Williams of Knight Edge Media, as relayed by GeekTyrant on 27 October 2026.
- Neither Marvel nor Reinsve has confirmed it, so treat it as an unconfirmed report.
- The untitled X-Men film is scheduled for cinemas on 5 May 2028.

Renate Reinsve, the Oscar-nominated star of *Sentimental Value*, has reportedly been in talks with Marvel about joining its rebooted X-Men film as geneticist Moira MacTaggert. The report surfaced on 27 October 2026 and, so far, rests on a single scooper's claim rather than an announcement from the studio.

## What the report says

According to [GeekTyrant](https://geektyrant.com/news/actress-renate-reinsve-in-talks-with-marvel-about-joining-x-men-as-the-character-moira-mactaggert), which cites Caleb Williams of Knight Edge Media, Reinsve met with Marvel about playing MacTaggert. The outlet describes her as an Academy Award nominee and lists *Sentimental Value*, *Backrooms* and *Fjord* among her credits. It is a meeting, not a signed deal, and the report does not say an offer has been made or accepted.

One detail makes the story more interesting. GeekTyrant notes that Reinsve told [Josh Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Horowitz) in an interview in December 2025 that she did not want to join a Marvel movie. The outlet's own take is that "things can change". That is commentary, not a statement from Reinsve, and she has not publicly addressed the new report in anything we have seen.

## Who is Moira MacTaggert?

In the comics, Moira MacTaggert is a Nobel Prize-winning human geneticist and a leading authority on mutants, GeekTyrant explains. She is a close ally and former partner of [Professor Charles Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X). She founded the Muir Island Mutant Research Center and mentored characters including Rahne Sinclair, also known as Wolfsbane. Her troubled, reality-warping son is Kevin MacTaggert, who becomes the mutant Proteus.

That makes her a scientist and a mentor figure rather than a costumed headliner, which is a different kind of part from the ones most associated with the franchise.

## The cast so far

GeekTyrant lists the actors already attached to the film. They are [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) as Rogue, [Sadie Sink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadie_Sink) as Jean Grey, [Kit Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kit_Connor) as [Scott Summers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclops_(Marvel_Comics)), [Christopher Abbott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Abbott) as Xavier, [Samara Weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samara_Weaving) as [Emma Frost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Frost) and [Maya Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Boyd) as Storm.

[Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) has been cast as the main villain, Nathaniel Milbury, better known as [Mister Sinister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Sinister). For Australian readers, Weaving is the one local name on that list.

Two further actors are reportedly in talks, according to the same article: [Asa Germann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asa_Germann) for Warren Worthington III, also known as [Angel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Worthington_III), and Cailee Spaeny for Kitty Pryde. Like Reinsve's meeting, those are reports rather than confirmed casting.

## Where it sits in Marvel's schedule

GeekTyrant points out that the studio's immediate focus is *Avengers: Doomsday* and *Avengers: Secret Wars*, described as the biggest films since *Endgame*. The X-Men reboot follows shortly after, with the untitled film dated for 5 May 2028.

## What happens next

Nothing official has been announced. Marvel usually confirms major casting itself, so the next signal to watch is a studio statement or a trade report that backs up the scooper's claim.
