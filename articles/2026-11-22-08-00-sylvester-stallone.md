**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone)'s 2025 spy thriller Alarum is climbing streaming charts, according to Comic Book Resources.
- FlixPatrol had it at No. 9 on Starz in the US on 20 November.
- On Paramount+ in Austria, Germany and Switzerland it reportedly jumped from No. 10 to No. 1 in a day.
- The film holds a 0% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 14% audience score.
- Stallone is also in Tulsa King season four, with season five already confirmed.

Sylvester Stallone Alarum viewing is rising on streaming, even though critics all but wrote the film off. The 2025 thriller has become a surprise performer on the charts this month, according to [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/sylvester-stallones-alarum-action-starz-success-november-2026/), which cited FlixPatrol rankings as of 20 November.

## Where Sylvester Stallone's Alarum is ranking

The numbers are modest but telling. Comic Book Resources reports that Alarum sat at No. 9 on Starz in the United States on 20 November. In Austria, Germany and Switzerland, the film reportedly rose from No. 10 to No. 1 on Paramount+ within a single day.

That is a notable swing for a film that, by the same report, did not do well on release. Alarum opened in select cinemas and on video on demand on 17 January 2025 through Lionsgate. Streaming has given it a second life.

The report says it can be streamed on Starz, and through Hulu, YouTube and Prime Video with a Starz subscription. Those are US options; Australian availability was not covered.

## What the film is about

Directed by [Michael Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_brothers) and written by Alexander Vesha, Alarum follows two married spies who live off the grid. Agents Joe Travers, played by [Scott Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Eastwood), and Lara Travers, played by [Willa Fitzgerald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willa_Fitzgerald), have a quiet retreat at a winter resort. It falls apart when members of the old guard suspect they have joined a team of rogue spies known as Alarum.

The cast also includes [Mike Colter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Colter), Ísis Valverde, D.W. Moffett and Abigail Spear, among others.

## Critics were harsh

The reviews were severe. The film sits at 0% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), with a 14% audience score, according to Comic Book Resources. Radio Times critic David Parkinson called it "not unwatchable, but all rather dispiriting", while Leslie Felperin of The Guardian wrote that it was a film so bad it shames American cinema. Comic Book Resources' own reviewer gave it 3 out of 10, saying a good action film was in there but buried under a convoluted plot.

A low score and a strong chart position can sit side by side, and a one-day jump may not mean lasting demand.

## Stallone's busy run on television

Stallone is not relying on Alarum. He stars as Dwight Manfredi in the [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan) crime dramedy Tulsa King, whose fourth season premiered on 16 October and runs through 18 December, according to Comic Book Resources. Paramount+ has already renewed it for a fifth season. InStyle reports that production on season five began in New York in October.

On the day of the season four finale, the first spinoff, Frisco King, debuts with Samuel L. Jackson. Stallone is set to reprise Manfredi in the eight-episode series, which runs through 29 January.

Rocky is back in the conversation too. The original film arrived in December 1976, and InStyle marked its 50th anniversary with a look at where the cast is now. Rocky took three Oscars, including Best Picture, at the 1977 ceremony. For more on the anniversary, see our coverage of [I Play Rocky](https://moduscarry.com/i-play-rocky-sylvester-stallone-story/) and our look at [what The Guardian said of Stallone in 1977](https://moduscarry.com/sylvester-stallone-rocky-1977-review/).
