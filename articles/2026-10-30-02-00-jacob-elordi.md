**Quick summary**

- Ridley Scott's post-apocalyptic thriller *The Dog Stars*, led by [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi), is No. 3 on Apple's iTunes movie chart, according to ComingSoon.net, which cites PopVortex.
- Only *Verity* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* rank higher on that digital list.
- The film opened in the United States on 28 August with $8 million and has made about $39 million worldwide.
- Its reported budget was $70 million to $110 million, and critics were mixed.

A film that struggled in cinemas is finding an audience at home. *The Dog Stars*, starring Jacob Elordi, has reached No. 3 on Apple's iTunes movie chart, [ComingSoon.net reported](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2214783-the-dog-stars-jacob-elordi-digital-streaming-hit) on 29 October, citing the tracking site PopVortex. Two titles sit above it: *Verity* and *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*.

## From weak opening to digital top three

The climb stands out because of how the cinema run went. According to ComingSoon.net, the film took just $8 million in its US opening weekend and has since grossed $39 million worldwide. The outlet puts the reported production budget at $70 million to $110 million, so even the top end of the box office total sits well below the low end of the cost.

The release also arrived late. The film premiered at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square in London on 20 August 2026 and opened in the US on 28 August, after moving from its original March 2026 date.

Critics were lukewarm. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) lists a 41% approval rating from 222 reviews, Metacritic gives it 49 out of 100, and audiences polled by CinemaScore gave it a "C+", the same outlet says.

## What the film is about

Elordi plays Hig, a civilian pilot living in rural Colorado years after a devastating flu pandemic. He shares a homestead with Bangley, a former Marine turned survivalist, and now and then flies into an emptied Denver to collect supplies. A mysterious radio signal eventually pushes Hig to leave the safety of his home.

The cast also includes [Josh Brolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Brolin), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley) and [Guy Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Pearce). Scott directed from a screenplay by [Mark L. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_L._Smith), adapted from Peter Heller's 2012 novel. 20th Century Studios produced it with [Scott Free Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Free_Productions). Filming took place at several locations in Italy, including Cinecittà Studios in Rome and the Cansiglio plateau.

## A role that changed hands

Elordi was not the first name attached to the lead. ComingSoon.net says [Paul Mescal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Mescal) was originally set to star, and that Elordi replaced him in January 2025 because of Mescal's scheduling conflicts with [Sam Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Mendes)' Beatles biopic series.

## Price of watching at home

On iTunes the film is listed at $19.99 to rent and $24.99 to buy, according to the report. That is a premium price, which makes a No. 3 ranking more notable. The report does not give download numbers, and a chart position reflects recent sales rather than total audience.

## What happens next
