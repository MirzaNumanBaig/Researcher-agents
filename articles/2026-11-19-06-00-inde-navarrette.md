**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), 25, says her main aim after Obsession is to be a good role model for her younger siblings.
- She told Extra she is the second oldest of seven children.
- She plays Nikki in Curry Barker's horror hit Obsession and is set to play Rogue in Marvel's upcoming X-Men film.
- She was recently honoured at the 2026 ELLE Women in Hollywood event.

Breakout success usually brings talk of bigger roles. For Inde Navarrette, the 25-year-old star of Obsession, the ambition she keeps returning to is closer to home: she wants her younger sisters and brothers to be able to look up to her. She shared the goal in an interview with Extra, as reported by [Yahoo Style Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/inde-navarrette-reveals-simple-goal-190000350.html).

## A big family and a clear goal

Navarrette described herself to Extra as "a child of seven" and the second oldest. She said she wants to make her parents proud, and herself too, and to be "a good role model" for her little sisters and brothers.

The comments come as her profile rises quickly. She plays Nikki in Curry Barker's horror hit Obsession, the part that made her a breakout name, and she is set to play Rogue in Marvel's upcoming X-Men movie. Our earlier coverage looks at [how Obsession arrived on Netflix](https://moduscarry.com/obsession-netflix-inde-navarrette/) and at [her preparation for Rogue](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-rogue-training/).

## Honoured, and feeling the weight of it

Navarrette was recently honoured at the 2026 ELLE Women in Hollywood event, and she says the attention brings a sense of responsibility. She spoke of a "level of trust" she gained on Obsession that she wants to keep earning in herself.

Her message to other women, younger and older, was to trust themselves: in her words, people are capable of more than they think. We covered the night in more detail in [Inde Navarrette Honoured at ELLE Women in Hollywood](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-women-in-hollywood/).

She also said Nikki left a lasting mark. According to Navarrette, the character taught her to stop judging in order to understand, and to build empathy in herself and in others. A related interview is covered in [Inde Navarrette Says Obsession Role Nikki Comforted Her](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-obsession-nikki/).

## "You just do the work"

Navarrette also spoke to ELLE magazine about how her family shaped her. She said both sides of her family did not treat failure as an option: "You just do the work." That mindset, she said, helps her take moments in rather than wish she had done everything differently. She added that having little Hollywood in her family is "really nice".

## The turning point at 24

Her advice for anyone chasing their goals comes from personal experience. She said her life changed when she began doing the things she had been putting off. Until 23, she described herself as a big people-pleaser who cared how she was seen and acknowledged. At 24, she said, she did not feel good and knew something had to change.

That shift shaped her approach to Obsession. She told herself she was not there to make friends, and said it was the first set where she arrived as an actor and left as an actor. Friendships still formed once production wrapped, she said, and some are now among the best she has had.

## What happens next

Her next high-profile project in the sources is the X-Men film, in which she is set to play Rogue. No release date was given in the interview coverage we read.
