**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, wore a black Carolina Herrera dress with feather detailing at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards.
- Her strongest colour was a muted, glossy berry-red lip rather than a bright cherry red.
- Grazia Magazine says softer, creamier reds can flatter mature lips; the exact shade Anderson wore has not been identified.
- Meanwhile, model [Brooks Nader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooks_Nader) has revived the original Baywatch red swimsuit for a reboot due in early 2027.

Pamela Anderson turned up to the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards with a lipstick that says as much about where her style is now as the dress did. The 59-year-old paired a black Carolina Herrera gown with a glossy, muted berry-red lip, and beauty editors have taken note.

## The look at the CFDA Fashion Awards

According to a report published by cwibc.co.id, Anderson's dress had feather detailing around the neckline. She kept the rest of the face soft: curled hair, a touch of blush and mascara.

The lipstick did the work. Rather than the bright cherry red many people associate with old-school Hollywood glamour, she chose a berry-red with a glossy finish. The same report notes that her exact lipstick brand and shade have not been identified, so any product named as "her" lipstick is a guess.

## A quieter kind of glamour

The look fits a shift that began in 2023. Since appearing at Paris Fashion Week that year, Anderson has leaned towards minimal makeup and a more natural face, according to cwibc.co.id. That is a long way from the heavily styled appearance that made her famous in the 1990s.

The CFDA look shows that "natural" does not have to mean plain. One strong feature, here the lip, carries the whole face. For readers who follow her style, we have also covered her [red wrap coat](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-red-coat/) this month.

## Why softer reds suit mature lips

The report points to Grazia Magazine, which cites makeup specialists quoted by Women.com. Their view is that highly pigmented shades can sometimes sharpen fine lines around the mouth or bleed past the natural lip line.

Makeup artist Caroline Hernandez recommends satin, cream and balm-like textures over matte formulas, which can feel drier on lips that lose moisture with age. A berry tone with gloss fits that advice.

## Three alternatives Grazia suggests

Grazia named three products as close options:

- Westman Atelier Squeaky Clean Liquid Lip Balm in Raspberry Lolly
- Glossier Ultralip in Cranberry
- Clinique Almost Lipstick in Black Honey

These are Grazia's picks, not products Anderson has been confirmed to use.

## Where Baywatch comes in

Anderson's name keeps coming up for a different reason. The Blast reports that Nader, 29, filmed beach scenes in the red lifeguard swimsuit this summer for a Baywatch television reboot that also stars [Olivia Dunne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livvy_Dunne) and is set for release in early 2027.

Reaction online was mixed. One commenter wrote that "there will only be one 90's Pamela Anderson", while another called Nader a "Temu Pamela Anderson". Those are reader comments, not a verdict from the production or from Anderson, and neither source says she has commented. Nader told People that wearing the red suit was "such a full-circle moment", noting she wore a red one-piece on her [Sports Illustrated Swimsuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Illustrated_Swimsuit_Issue) cover years ago.

Neither source says Anderson is involved in the reboot.
