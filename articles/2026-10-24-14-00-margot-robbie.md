**Quick summary**

- A sheer, crystal-embellished Armani Privé Spring 2025 gown worn by [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) is being debated online again, according to Fashion Times UK.
- She wore it to the UK premiere of A Big Bold Beautiful Journey at Odeon Luxe Leicester Square on 11 September 2025.
- The photographs resurfaced on the r/fashionporn forum this week and split fans over the beadwork, the lining and the naked dress trend.
- Fashion Times UK reports that the criticism targets the styling, not Robbie herself.

A gown from more than a year ago has become the latest flashpoint in the argument over see-through red carpet dressing. [Margot Robbie](https://moduscarry.com/tag/margot-robbie/) wore a sheer Armani Privé design to a London premiere in September 2025, and its reappearance online this week has fans asking whether the naked dress still shocks or has simply become routine, Fashion Times UK reported on 23 October.

## What Robbie wore

According to [Fashion Times UK](https://www.fashiontimes.co.uk/margot-robbie-sheer-armani-prive-gown-naked-dress-trend-1766183), the halter-neck gown is covered in hand-stitched crystal beadwork at the front, while the back is sheer and low-cut. She wore it on 11 September 2025, a week after the death of Giorgio Armani, which some coverage read as a quiet tribute.

Online, the dress picked up a joke that has outlasted the premiere itself: that it was "lined with Margot Robbie". The outlet says few put-downs of the naked dress trend have been repeated as often.

## Where fans split

The craftsmanship drew near-universal praise, particularly the patchwork-style beading and a jewelled rosette at the back. The complaint was about contrast. Without a contrasting underlayer, the outlet says, flash photography flattens the beadwork into bare skin and much of the silver detail fades. Many fans suggested a deep blue or nude lining.

Some commenters also took aim at the proportions, arguing that the narrow bodice and the placement of the front straps flattened her silhouette. Even so, Fashion Times UK says the harshest critiques stop short of faulting Robbie, and phrases such as "beautiful as always" recur through the discussion.

## A trend with a long history

The outlet points out that sheer dressing is far from new. Jane Birkin wore a completely sheer minidress in 1969, and Cher's barely-there gown by [Bob Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Mackie) at the 1974 Met Gala arguably made it a trend. Rihanna kept it going in 2014 with a CFDA Awards gown encrusted with 230,000 Swarovski crystals.

Fashion Times UK argues that the difference is intent. Those earlier looks used transparency as provocation, whereas today it works more like red carpet protocol, and when nearly every premiere offers a variation the surprise wears off. That is the outlet's opinion rather than an established fact, and fans are not unanimous.

## Robbie's styling history

The article also sets the gown against her Barbie press tour, when she and stylist Andrew Mukamal recreated vintage doll outfits, including a black glittering Schiaparelli gown in Los Angeles and a custom Vivienne Westwood gown in London. Kate Young had styled her before Mukamal took over for that tour. For the A Big Bold Beautiful Journey premiere, Marie Claire reports she reunited with Mukamal.

Fashion Times UK notes that, unlike the Barbie looks, which each had a clear archival story, the Armani gown had no stated theme, so attention moved to the execution. ## What happens next

Nothing official is scheduled: the debate is a reaction to older photographs, and neither Robbie nor Armani has commented in the source material. The next test of the trend will be the next major premiere, where, as the outlet puts it, a fresh concept would give a sheer gown more to say.
