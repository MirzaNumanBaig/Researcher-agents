**Quick summary**

- UK tax filings show Disney spent about $533.7 million making *Deadpool & Wolverine*, according to Dark Horizons, which cites a Forbes report.
- Tax credits and reimbursements of about $104.7 million cut the net cost to roughly $429 million.
- The film earned about $1.34 billion worldwide, and IGN India estimates Disney's share of ticket sales at around $670 million.
- Marketing and other costs are not included, so the true profit is still unclear.

The Marvel comeback that brought [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) back to Wolverine cost far more than the $200 million figure most fans have long repeated. Disney's own filings in the United Kingdom put the gross production bill at about $533.7 million, a number that is back in the headlines this week through fresh coverage from [IGN India](https://in.ign.com/deadpool-wolverine/275765/deadpool-wolverine-disney-reveals-ryan-reynolds-and-hugh-jackman-starrer-cost-nearly-half-a-billion).

## What the filings show

According to [Dark Horizons](https://www.darkhorizons.com/?p=188329), the UK tax filings cover the period through 31 October 2024 and show gross spending of $533.7 million. The site credits a Forbes report, published on 27 November 2025, as the origin of the analysis.

That is not the whole story. The same filings list roughly $104.7 million in tax credits and reimbursements, which brings the net figure down to about $429 million. IGN India describes the cost as nearly half a billion dollars, which sits between the net and gross numbers.

For context, the earlier benchmark was very different. In April 2025, [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/1964523-deadpool-and-wolverine-profit-marvel-disney-400) relayed a Deadline calculation that used a production cost of about $200 million. Even the lower net figure now on the table is more than double that.

## The box office side of the ledger

The film's takings were enormous. IGN India reports a worldwide total of $1.34 billion. ComingSoon.net's summary of the Deadline report adds that it opened above $211 million, the biggest start for an R-rated film, and went on to pass *Joker* as the highest-grossing R-rated release.

Cinemas keep a large share of ticket sales, though. Working on an estimated 50-50 split, IGN India puts Disney's cut at about $670 million. Take away the net production cost and you reach an estimated $223.9 million, according to that outlet. Dark Horizons, using the $429 million net cost, arrives at a similar box-office profit of roughly $241 million.

Both outlets stress the same limit: these sums leave out the cost of selling the film.

## Marketing, and why the profit is disputed

Dark Horizons says marketing ran to at least $100 million and possibly about double that, given the size of the worldwide campaign. Once that is subtracted, the theatrical profit shrinks sharply, which is why the same film can be described as a runaway hit and as only modestly profitable.

The Deadline estimate reported by ComingSoon.net took a very different route. It put total expenses at about $580 million, including about $160 million for advertising, and total revenue at $980 million, made up of $620 million from cinemas, $150 million from home entertainment and $210 million from television and streaming. Its conclusion was a $400 million profit for Disney. That model rested on the $200 million production figure, so it cannot be compared directly with the new filings.

Dark Horizons adds that home video income is effectively pure profit for the studio, which helps explain why estimates keep moving.

## What happens next

No single, audited profit number is public. Disney has not published a full breakdown of marketing, residuals or streaming income for the film, so any final figure remains an estimate. A second report, from IMDb, framed the profit as under $250 million, but that page could not be read in full, so it is not relied on here.

For fans of Jackman and co-star Ryan Reynolds, the practical point is simpler. The film was a major financial success by any measure, and the new cost data suggests Marvel paid heavily to get there. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Deadpool & Wolverine cost to make?

UK tax filings, as reported by Dark Horizons, show a gross production cost of about $533.7 million. After roughly $104.7 million in tax credits and reimbursements, the net cost was about $429 million.

### How much money did the film make?

It earned about $1.34 billion worldwide, according to IGN India. Disney's estimated share of ticket sales is around $670 million.

### Did Deadpool & Wolverine make a profit?

Yes, but the size is disputed. Box-office estimates run from about $224 million to $241 million before marketing, and Deadline's earlier model, based on a lower cost, claimed $400 million.
