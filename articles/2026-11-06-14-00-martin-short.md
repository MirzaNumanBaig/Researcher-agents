**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) stars as an elf in "Holiday Workshop", a new Verizon holiday ad, reported by MediaPost on 6 November 2026.
- He is joined by [Meg Stalter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Stalter) and [Marsai Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marsai_Martin), who also wear pointy ears.
- The ad was made in-house and promotes Verizon's Simplicity plan, which offers one line for $30.
- Verizon is also running separate campaigns aimed at Hispanic audiences.

The American carrier Verizon has cast Martin Short as an elf in its holiday campaign, according to a report published by MediaPost on 6 November 2026. The ad, "Holiday Workshop", puts the comedian in Santa's workshop alongside two much younger co-stars.

## Who is in the Martin Short Verizon ad

MediaPost describes the cast as Short, Meg Stalter (known for "Hacks") and Marsai Martin (known for "Black-ish"). All three have been given pointy elf ears. The report calls them an "overworked trio", and the setting is the North Pole.

Verizon leans on Short's comedy reputation. Ricardo Aspiazu, the company's senior vice president of brand creative, said in the announcement: "When you put a comedy titan like Martin Short in a room with comedic dynamos like Meg Stalter and Marsai Martin, you basically just get out of the way and let the lightning strike."

The ad was created in-house rather than by an outside agency, MediaPost notes.

## What happens in "Holiday Workshop"

According to the report, the elves toss around "money snow" while they talk up Verizon's Simplicity plan. That plan offers one line for $30. No full script or release date for the ad appears in the report.

Aspiazu set out the goal in the same announcement: "We set out to build a holiday campaign that people would genuinely smile at, not just scroll past."

## The rest of Verizon's holiday push

Short's ad isn't the only one. MediaPost reports that Verizon is also debuting campaigns for Hispanic audiences. In "Hot Holidays", Colombian singer [Manuel Turizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Turizo) details the carrier's iPhone deals. In "Dos Vidas, Un Destino", Argentine-Mexican actor [Sebastian Rulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebasti%C3%A1n_Rulli) stars in a Verizon-themed novela.

The company is also offering promotional pricing on the latest Apple, Google, Motorola and Samsung phones, with no trade-in needed. Customers who pick a 36-month device payment plan can get a smartwatch and earbuds.

## Why phones are the holiday battleground

MediaPost frames the campaigns against a crowded market. Rival T-Mobile is offering special pricing on the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 17, plus up to $1,200 off the new iPhone Duo and $200 off 5G Home Internet.

The Consumer Technology Association's holiday gift survey, cited in the same report, ranks smartphones No. 1 on shoppers' wish lists and fourth on the gift list. About 53% of respondents are considering a phone as a present, and of those, 63% are eyeing a foldable or flip model. Some surveys also suggest consumers feel financially strapped, which can make practical gifts feel more meaningful, the report says.
