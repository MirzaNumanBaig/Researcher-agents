**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan)'s fragrance and lifestyle house TSU LANGE YOR is launching in Malaysia.
- The full collection goes on sale from 13 November, exclusively at TROVE, according to Malaysian outlet Hype.MY.
- The debut range has six Eau de Parfums: Luca, By Your Side, Sage's Rose, Pool, TLY 5755c and Sala.
- The brand's name comes from a Yiddish phrase meaning "to long years".

The Troye Sivan TSU LANGE YOR fragrance range is about to get its first Malaysian stockist. The independent lifestyle and fragrance house, founded by the artist [Troye Sivan](https://troyesivan.com), goes on sale in the country from 13 November, exclusively at TROVE, [Hype.MY reported](https://hype.my/tsu-lange-yor-by-troye-sivan-makes-its-malaysian-debut-at-trove/) on 12 November.

## What TSU LANGE YOR is

According to Hype.MY, TSU LANGE YOR describes itself as an independent lifestyle and fragrance house. Its name is taken from a Yiddish phrase meaning "to long years". The outlet says the brand is built around intimate moments, personal spaces and cherished memories, which it translates into scent.

That framing matters for how the range is pitched. The brand is not selling a single celebrity perfume. It presents a small collection of fragrances, each meant to evoke a particular kind of atmosphere.

## The six fragrances in the Malaysian launch

The debut collection is made up of six Eau de Parfums, as listed by Hype.MY:

- Luca
- By Your Side
- Sage's Rose
- Pool
- TLY 5755c
- Sala

The outlet says each scent is meant to feel personal, so that wearers can find a signature that fits their own story. Hype.MY does not give prices, scent notes or sizes, so shoppers will need to check with the retailer for those details.

## What Troye Sivan said about the launch

The article quotes Sivan on the Malaysian arrival. "It means so much that TLY now has a real home in KL, with a team who take the time to help people find their scent," he said. He added that fragrance is "one of the most personal things you can give someone, or give yourself", and that he could not wait for people to experience the brand "up close".

His comments point to the approach TROVE will take: in-person guidance on choosing a scent rather than a purely online sale. Whether that guidance extends beyond the Kuala Lumpur store was not stated in the report.

## What happens next

The next date to watch is 13 November, when the full collection becomes available in Malaysia for the first time. Hype.MY names TROVE as the only place to buy it at launch. No other markets or retailers were mentioned in the source, so any wider rollout would need to be confirmed by the brand.
