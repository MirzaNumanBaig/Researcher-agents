**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) sat front row at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney in an ivory silk slip set.
- Beside her, [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) wore black double-breasted tailoring, according to Grazia.
- The show at The Cutaway at Barangaroo was the first full Chanel replica presentation on Australian soil, Grazia reports.
- Separately, Robbie's 2016 film The Legend of Tarzan is the most-watched title on HBO Max, Collider reports.

[Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) sat side by side at Chanel's Sydney show, and their outfits could hardly have been more different, according to [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/articles/margot-robbie-and-nicole-kidman-wore-chanel-in-sydney-but-their-looks-couldnt-have-been-more-different/). The two Australian actresses, both global Chanel ambassadors, wore pieces from artistic director Matthieu Blazy's Spring/Summer 2027 collection.

## Margot Robbie Chanel Sydney: the ivory silk look

Robbie chose a translucent ivory silk slip set. Grazia describes accordion pleating falling from a bralette, with a single handmade fabric camellia at the bust. Rather than lace, she wore high-waisted jersey briefs in a vintage style.

The jersey is a deliberate nod to Gabrielle Chanel, who in the 1910s used fabric associated with men's underwear to free women from corsetry, the magazine notes. Robbie paired the outfit with a 1960s-inspired flipped blonde bob, dewy makeup and a black quilted flap bag.

## Nicole Kidman in black tailoring

Kidman went the other way. She wore a double-breasted jacket and trousers in matte black grain de poudre, layered over a silk-cotton base and a white collared shirt buttoned to the throat. Pointed patent pumps and loose strawberry-blonde waves finished the outfit, per Grazia.

Grazia's verdict is that Robbie was soft and breezy while Kidman was structured and severe. It reads the pairing as a sign of the range Blazy is building at the French house.

## Why Sydney, and why these two

The Cruise 2026/27 replica show was staged in the sandstone space of The Cutaway at Barangaroo. Grazia calls it the first time an international luxury house has mounted a full replica presentation in Australia. Kylie Minogue provided the evening's music and Baz Luhrmann was seated nearby.

Both women have long ties to the brand. Kidman starred in Luhrmann's Chanel No. 5 campaign in 2004 and was named a global house ambassador in 2025, according to the magazine. Queensland-born Robbie was chosen by Karl Lagerfeld in 2018 as his final global ambassador, Grazia reports.

## The Legend of Tarzan finds a second life

Away from the runway, Robbie's older work is getting attention. [Collider](https://collider.com/the-legend-of-tarzan-streaming-hit-hbo-max-november-2026/) reports that The Legend of Tarzan, released in 2016, is the most-watched movie on HBO Max worldwide at the time of writing.

That is a turnaround for a film that struggled on release. Collider says it earned about $350 million worldwide against a reported $180 million budget and scored 35% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). The cast included [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd), [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson), [Djimon Hounsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djimon_Hounsou) and [Christoph Waltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Waltz). We covered the chart climb earlier in [Margot Robbie's The Legend of Tarzan Climbs HBO Max Chart](https://moduscarry.com/margot-robbie-legend-of-tarzan/).

Collider notes that interest in Robbie's back catalogue comes as she is announced to star opposite [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper), who is also directing, in an [Ocean's Eleven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean's_Eleven) prequel scheduled for 25 June 2027.
