**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) has said he badly wanted to play Max in the Mad Max revival in 2010, according to Far Out Magazine.
- He told Entertainment Weekly he could not get "a call or a meeting" for the part at the time.
- The role went to Tom Hardy in Fury Road, released in 2015.
- In 2024 Hemsworth joined the franchise as the villain Dementus in Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga.

The Australian actor Chris Hemsworth chased the lead in the Mad Max revival back in 2010 and could not even secure a meeting, according to [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/2010-chris-hemsworth-auditioned-play-mad-max/). Fourteen years later he walked into the same franchise as its villain. The Chris Hemsworth Mad Max story is a neat example of how a career can circle back on itself.

## A Mad Max fan before he was famous

Hemsworth was born in 1983, so he grew up in an Australia that already treated the original Mad Max films as a local legend. Far Out notes that he was watching what director [George Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Miller_(filmmaker)) did on screen as a young fan. Speaking to [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), he put it simply: "I grew up on this shit."

That background explains why the 2010 casting search mattered so much to him. Miller was returning to the world he created for the first time in about 30 years, and the big open question was who would take over from the original star, Mel Gibson. Far Out, citing Entertainment Weekly, says Gibson was considered too old to come back.

## Why the 2010 audition went nowhere

Hemsworth told the magazine he wanted the role badly. The timing, though, worked against him. In 2010 he was not yet a household name, and Far Out points out that his best-known credit then was the Australian soap opera Home and Away. That was not enough to open doors for a film of that size.

"I couldn't even get a call or a meeting or anything," he said, adding that he simply had not done enough to earn one. The part went instead to [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) in [Fury Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Max%3A_Fury_Road), which arrived in 2015. By that point Hemsworth was far better known, but the job was filled. Far Out describes the film as a major hit and a modern action standout, even though Hardy's Max says relatively little.

## The villain role that came 14 years later

Miller returned once more in 2024 with Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga. The story centres on a younger version of the character played by [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron) in Fury Road, with [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy) in the title role. Max himself was no longer the focus, but the film still needed an antagonist strong enough to stand against her.

That was Hemsworth's way back in. Far Out reports that when he first saw Fury Road he was "completely absorbed" by it and told his agent he had to work with Miller, whom he called a genius. By 2024 he had more than a decade of Thor and Avengers films behind him, plus less celebrated work such as In the Heart of the Sea. Playing Dementus, the ruler of Gastown, meant stepping away from heroes entirely.
