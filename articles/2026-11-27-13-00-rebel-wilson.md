**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) posted a photo of her six-month-old daughter Rose on Instagram Stories on Thursday 26 November.
- Rose wore a fuzzy reindeer onesie, and the caption read simply "Happy Thanksgiving".
- It is Rose's first Thanksgiving. She was born in May to Wilson and her wife, Ramona Agruma.
- The couple also have a four-year-old daughter, Royce.

The Australian actress marked the American holiday with a single picture. According to [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/692815-rebel-wilson-shares-sweet-photo-of-baby-rose-on-her-first-thanksgiving), the 46-year-old shared a shot of baby Rose in a reindeer onesie on Thursday 26 November, with the words "Happy Thanksgiving" laid over the image. [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/rebel-wilson-shares-adorable-thanksgiving-211521000.html), republishing a PEOPLE report, describes the outfit as Christmas-ready.

## A short post for a first holiday

Wilson kept it brief. There was no long message and no group photo, just the baby in her festive outfit. Both outlets point out that the post went to Instagram Stories, which disappear after a day, rather than to her main grid.

The Pitch Perfect star has shared glimpses of family life this year without turning it into a constant stream. This was the kind of small update her followers have come to expect.

## How the family grew

Wilson and Agruma were introduced by mutual friends and went public with their relationship in 2022, according to both reports. They got engaged at Disneyland the following year and married in Sardinia, Italy, in September 2024.

Wilson's older daughter, Royce, arrived via surrogate in 2022, before the wedding. Agruma has helped raise her as the relationship developed and has been present for several milestones, the reports say.

The couple announced in December 2025 that Agruma was expecting. Rose was born the following May. Announcing the birth on Instagram, Wilson wrote that she and Agruma felt "incredibly grateful and blessed" to grow their family.

In a statement to PEOPLE after the pregnancy news, the couple spoke about the long road to this point, including Wilson's IVF journey. They said they were thinking of others on a similar path, and described Agruma's pregnancy as high risk with some scares along the way, before the birth brought relief.

## Family moments since May

AOL notes that the couple have shared a handful of family moments online since Rose arrived. In July they posted their first picture as a family of four, taken during a day at the beach.

That same month Royce made her screen debut beside her mother in a commercial for Noom. Wilson told PEOPLE that acting with Royce was "a dream come true" and that her daughter is her reason for staying healthy. The ad was shot in Malibu, where Royce spent the breaks visiting horses on the property and playing on a swing.

Readers who follow Wilson's other news can find more in our [Rebel Wilson](https://moduscarry.com/tag/rebel-wilson/) archive, including a recent report on the [costs fight over The Deb](https://moduscarry.com/rebel-wilson-the-deb-costs-contempt/). Geo News also points to a separate delay in her legal dispute over that film, though the Thanksgiving post itself has no link to it.

## What happens next

Nothing formal is scheduled. Wilson has not said whether more family pictures are planned, and the Thanksgiving image was a Stories post that has no set shelf life.
