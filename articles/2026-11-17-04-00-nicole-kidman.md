**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) sat front row at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 replica show in Sydney, wearing black tailoring, according to Grazia.
- Grazia reports it was the first full replica presentation by an international luxury house on Australian soil.
- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) sat beside her in an ivory silk slip set.
- Separately, the Off-Broadway comedy A Kidman Carol, which casts Kidman as a character, runs at New World Stages from 16 November to 30 December, per New York Theatre Guide.

Nicole Kidman turned the front row of Chanel's Sydney show into a study in black tailoring, sitting beside Margot Robbie at an evening presentation inside The Cutaway at Barangaroo, according to [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/articles/margot-robbie-and-nicole-kidman-wore-chanel-in-sydney-but-their-looks-couldnt-have-been-more-different/). The same week, a new stage comedy built around the actor opened in New York.

## Nicole Kidman at the Chanel Sydney show

Grazia describes the event as Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 replica show, staged in the sandstone space of The Cutaway. It calls it the first time an international luxury house has mounted a full replica presentation in Australia. The set recreated the coastal feel of Biarritz inside Sydney's rock walls.

Kidman wore a double-breasted jacket and trousers in matte black grain de poudre, according to the magazine. Underneath sat a stiff white collared shirt buttoned to the throat. She finished the look with pointed-toe patent pumps and loose strawberry-blonde waves. Grazia reads the outfit as a nod to the house's long flirtation with androgynous codes.

Both women wore pieces from Matthieu Blazy's Spring/Summer 2027 collection for the house, the outlet says. The tailoring was about as far as a look could get from what Robbie chose.

## A very different look beside Margot Robbie

Robbie wore an ivory silk slip set with accordion pleating, a single fabric camellia at the bust and high-waisted jersey briefs. Grazia says the jersey was a deliberate reference to Gabrielle Chanel's use of men's underwear fabric to free women from corsetry in the 1910s. Robbie paired it with a flipped blonde bob and a black quilted flap bag.

The magazine's verdict is that the soft, translucent Robbie and the severe, structured Kidman made a monochrome pair. It argues the contrast shows how wide Blazy's range is, from light romance to sharp suiting.

## Two Australians, two eras of Chanel

Grazia traces Kidman's link to the brand to 2004, when she starred in Baz Luhrmann's Chanel No. 5 campaign. It says she was named a global ambassador in 2025. Luhrmann sat nearby, and Kylie Minogue provided the music, the outlet reports.

Robbie's connection is more recent. In 2018, Karl Lagerfeld picked the Queensland-born actor as his final global ambassador, Grazia notes. Placing both women side by side, it says, was a statement about the bridge between Paris and Australia.

## A Kidman Carol: a stage spoof of the actor

Away from the runway, Kidman is also the subject of a satire. A Kidman Carol, written by Kevin Zak and directed by Skylar Fox, is a spin on Charles Dickens's Christmas story. [New York Theatre Guide](https://www.newyorktheatreguide.com/theatre-news/interviews/marla-mindelle-and-josh-sharp-bring-awards-season-to-the-holiday-season-in-a-kidman-carol) says it plays at New World Stages in Manhattan from 16 November to 30 December.

[Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle) plays three spirits of Kidman who visit [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett), played by [Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp), on Oscars eve. Blanchett takes the Scrooge role. According to Mindelle, the spirits stand for different eras: Moulin Rouge!, The Hours (the film that won Kidman her Oscar) and the viral AMC Theatres ad.

Mindelle told the publication the show is not a straight impression. She said Kidman's films show a sense of humour and a self-aware campiness that suits the material. Sharp said the real Blanchett is not cynical, but is one of the few people who seems more interested in the work than the circus around it. The cast also includes Ashby Florence, Richard Perez and Phillip Taratula.

## What happens next

A Kidman Carol continues at New World Stages until 30 December, with tickets listed through that date. The Chanel presentation was a one-night event, though Grazia's coverage suggests Kidman and Robbie remain central to the house's Australian story. Neither source mentions any comment from Kidman on the play.
