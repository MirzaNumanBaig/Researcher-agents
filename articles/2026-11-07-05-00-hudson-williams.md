Hudson Williams has fans talking twice in one week. Photos and videos of the actor posing in underwear outdoors surfaced online, and a scene from his new series Yaga has gone viral on X, Attitude reported on 6 November 2026. Neither moment comes with an official explanation, so here is what is actually known.

## What the Hudson Williams underwear shoot shows

According to [Attitude](https://www.attitude.co.uk/culture/hudson-williams-sparks-underwear-campaign-speculation-539307/), the images show Williams in black briefs with red piping, posing against the outside of a building and on its fire escape. Several photos capture him from different angles, and video clips show him moving around the location.

The outlet says the shoot has not been officially released. Fans have suggested it could be for Rihanna's Savage X Fenty, based only on the style of underwear. That is speculation. No brand has confirmed a deal, and Attitude says fans will have to wait to learn what the shoot was for.

The reaction online was predictable. One user asked how many people were filming that morning and said they wanted every angle, while another joked about the view. A third asked how fans would cope with season two. For context on his existing fashion work, see our earlier piece on [Hudson Williams' brand deals with Balenciaga and Bulgari](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-brand-deals/).

## Why Yaga is back in the conversation

The second thread comes from episode four of [Yaga](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-yaga/), titled "Dance with the Devil". Attitude [reports](https://www.attitude.co.uk/culture/hudson-williams-kisses-himself-yaga-539243/) that Williams plays two characters: the missing heir Henry Park and Henry's lookalike roommate, Logan Casey. The episode pushes their relationship into adult territory, and a scene in which the two characters kiss has been widely shared on X.

It is not the first time he has gone viral for the show. Attitude says an episode two party scene, in which his character wears a horned pup mask, also drew heavy attention online. One fan wrote that "Hudson Williams kissing Hudson Williams" was an actual sentence they could now say.

The series is created by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) and reimagines the Baba Yaga legend as a modern mystery thriller built around the question "What makes a witch?" The eight-part cast includes [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo) as local detective Carson and [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) as Katherine, a charismatic university professor. [Patrick Gilmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Gilmore_(actor)) and [Sheila McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheila_McCarthy) also appear, with Georgia Acken and Christin Park. UK viewers can watch on Sky Atlantic and NOW. We have also covered how [Yaga set an AMC+ launch record](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-yaga-amc-record/).

## Where Heated Rivalry fits in

Most of the attention around Williams traces back to [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), where he plays [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander). The series adapts Rachel Reid's Game Changers novels and follows two professional hockey players whose rivalry turns into a secret relationship. His co-star [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) plays [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov).

Season one also featured François Arnaud, Robbie G.K., [Christina Chang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christina_Chang), [Dylan Walsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dylan_Walsh) and [Ksenia Daniela Kharlamova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ksenia_Daniela_Kharlamova), among others. Production on season two began in Toronto in August, with Williams and Storrie returning. New cast members include [Justice Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_Smith), [Charlie Gillespie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Gillespie), [Daniel Diemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Diemer) and [Shaheen Jafargholi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaheen_Jafargholi). Everything we publish on the actor is gathered on our [Hudson Williams page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hudson-williams/).
