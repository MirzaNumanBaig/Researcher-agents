**Quick summary**

- New reports say [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) is among actors invited to screen-test for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) is reportedly not among those auditioning.
- It is unclear whether Elordi declined or simply was not chosen to advance.
- Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed any casting or shortlist.

Jacob Elordi is reportedly not taking part in the latest James Bond screen tests, according to a report published on 13 November by [AOL.com](https://www.aol.com/articles/callum-turner-reportedly-screen-testing-224721000.html), which draws on earlier coverage from OK! and TheWrap. The same report says Callum Turner is moving forward in the process. None of it has been confirmed by the studio.

## Who is reportedly screen-testing

According to the AOL report, Turner, 36, is among a small group of actors invited to test for the role Daniel Craig left after 2021's *No Time to Die*. It names [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor, both 36, as other contenders reportedly offered auditions.

The report cites TheWrap as saying the tests were scheduled to begin on 23 October. Producers are said to be assessing stunt work, camera presence and dialogue delivery. Turner's credits include [Fantastic Beasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Beasts) and *The Boys in the Boat*, and the report notes he is engaged to [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa).

## Where Elordi fits in

The Elordi detail is the surprise. The report says OK! earlier reported that Amazon executives had narrowed their search to three leading contenders, with Elordi and Lowden among the most discussed names, and that Elordi was emerging as a favourite. The studio never confirmed that shortlist.

The new reporting indicates the *Euphoria* actor is not in the screen-test group. Nothing in it explains why. As the report puts it, it remains unclear whether he passed on the opportunity or was not selected to move ahead. Treat both the earlier favourite talk and this latest claim as unconfirmed.

You can follow everything else we have published on him through our [Jacob Elordi tag](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/), including his casting as a killer in [The Executioner's Song](https://moduscarry.com/jacob-elordi-gary-gilmore/).

## The wider Bond search

The AOL report notes that several other actors have been linked to the franchise over the years, among them [Aaron Taylor-Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Taylor-Johnson), [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill) and [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James). [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) is set to direct the next film, but the filmmakers have stayed quiet about who will take over.

The report also quotes former Bond casting director Debbie McWilliams, who said the character needs more than looks and charm. In her words, "part of his job description is license to kill," so audiences must believe he could pick up a gun and shoot you.

## What happens next

No announcement has been made. Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed that Turner or anyone else has the role, and the report does not say when a decision is due. Until the studio speaks, the names in circulation are reported speculation. We will update this story if Amazon MGM or the actors comment.
