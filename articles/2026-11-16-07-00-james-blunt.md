**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has his first UK number one album since 2007 with Airplane Mode, according to Wales Online.
- A fan, Alan Pender, says he is legally changing his name to James Délice de Bourgogne Blunt as a result of a bet, Metro reports.
- Pender had pledged £1,000 to a charity of the singer's choice and the name change if the album hit the top.
- Blunt also told the Sunday Times that watching his Celebrity Traitors episodes left him "less positive" about the show.

James Blunt's new album has reached number one in the UK, and it has had an unusual knock-on effect. A fan who spent six years trying to find out the singer's favourite cheese says he will legally take Blunt's name this week, according to a first-person piece in [Metro](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/15/i-legally-changed-my-name-to-james-blunt-just-so-i-could-find-out-his-favourite-cheese-29793718/). It caps a busy few weeks for the singer, who also spoke candidly about his time on reality television.

## The bet behind the name change

Alan Pender, who posts online as Joe Bangles, wrote in Metro that he had told Blunt a simple deal: if the new album reached number one, he would give £1,000 of his own money to a charity of the singer's choosing and change his name to Blunt's. In return, Blunt promised that he would finally reveal his favourite cheese.

Pender did not expect it to happen. "As much as I am a fan of his music, I didn't expect him to get the top position," he wrote. His new legal name, he says, will be Mr James Délice de Bourgogne Blunt, effective from Wednesday.

The quest began in 2020, he writes, when he started a Twitter account during the pandemic and asked [Lord Sugar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Sugar) for his favourite cheese. Since then he says he has sent 152,000 posts and collected answers from [Dawn French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dawn_French) (Yarg) and [Alex Horne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Horne) (Babybel). [Matt Lucas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Lucas), by his account, "loathes all cheese".

## Six years of cheese teasing

Blunt, by Pender's telling, has kept the answer to himself. In 2024 the singer posted a teaser photo in front of a cheese board, but the video cut off before any reveal. More recently, a viewer question about his favourite cheese was put to him on This Morning. Blunt told the camera the questioner was a plant, named Joe Bangles, and that he would never say.

At an HMV album signing, Pender says, the singer saw him in the queue, nodded and signed his copy with the line "You'll always be Bangles to me". The Metro text available to us is cut short, so it does not say whether Blunt has now revealed the cheese. We have not confirmed that either way.

## A number one that Celebrity Traitors may have helped

The chart result is a milestone in its own right. Wales Online reports that Airplane Mode is Blunt's first UK number one since 2007, and that it was released shortly after the second series of [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) finished airing. Blunt has acknowledged that promoting the album was one reason he took part.

Taking part was not his idea, according to the same report. His wife, lawyer Sofia Wellesley, signed him up through her friendship with host [Claudia Winkleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claudia_Winkleman). He told the Guardian he had sworn off reality shows and "really wish[ed]" she hadn't done it.

## Blunt on the edit

Speaking to the Sunday Times, the 52-year-old said he felt upbeat about the experience until he watched the episodes. The heavy editing, he said, removed a lot of context and was "frustrating". He lost out to [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan) and [Sebastian Croft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Croft), though Wales Online says he came close to winning a share of the prize fund for his chosen charity.

He also joked that he asked whether [Chris Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Martin) might appear. His manager's reply, reportedly: "James, you're not Chris Martin." For more on the show, see our earlier report, [James Blunt Says Celebrity Traitors Edit Left Out Key Context](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-celebrity-traitors-editing/).

His career began with the 2004 debut Back to Bedlam, which Wales Online describes as the biggest-selling debut album by a British artist. Its hit single was [You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful).

## What happens next

Pender says the name change takes effect on Wednesday. Whether Blunt keeps his side of the deal on the cheese is the open question, and neither source confirms an answer.
