**Quick summary**

- *Stuntnuts*, an action-comedy with [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell), will get its first market screening at the American Film Market (AFM).
- It is the first film from UR MARV, the company launched by footballer [Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo) and filmmaker [Matthew Vaughn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Vaughn).
- [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon) has a cameo, according to ComingSoon.net, which cites Deadline.
- Disney Korea says Hemsworth, [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)) and [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) will visit South Korea on 7 December.
- *Avengers: Doomsday* is due in cinemas on 16 December.

Two pieces of news landed for Chris Hemsworth within a day of each other. His stunt-world comedy *Stuntnuts* is about to be shown to international buyers, and the actor has been confirmed for an official promotional trip to South Korea. Here is what each report says, and what is still to come.

## Chris Hemsworth Stuntnuts: what the film is about

According to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2219503-cristiano-ronaldo-hollywood-matt-damon-chris-hemsworth-stuntnuts), which cites Deadline, *Stuntnuts* follows two stunt performers who take a big film job to save their community gym. The job does not go smoothly. Dangerous stunts and the unpredictable behaviour of the film's leading stars pile up complications for the pair.

Hemsworth and Rockwell play fictionalised versions of themselves, which gives the story a Hollywood-insider angle. Damon appears in a cameo, again as reported by ComingSoon.net.

The cast leans heavily on real stunt professionals. ComingSoon.net lists [Bobby Holland Hanton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Holland_Hanton), Greg Townley, Paul Lowe and Haruka Oshima. Holland Hanton has worked extensively with Hemsworth. Townley has doubled for [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) in the *Spider-Man* films, and Lowe's credits include *Harry Potter* and *Game of Thrones*. Oshima has worked on *No Time to Die* and the *Matrix* films.

One detail comes from real life. Townley broke his back in three places in an accident during production, and the filmmakers have his permission to use the experience in the film, the outlet reports.

## Who is behind it

[Damien Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Walters), a stunt performer by background, directs. ComingSoon.net says his earlier work includes *Skyfall*, *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, the *Kingsman* films and [*Jurassic World*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World): *Dominion*.

The film is the first production from UR MARV, which pairs Ronaldo's move into entertainment with Vaughn's filmmaking experience. The report describes it as Ronaldo's debut as a film producer. Legendary's sales label 193 is handling international sales, and the AFM screening is aimed at international distributors. No release date has been given in the sources we read.

## Hemsworth's first official Seoul visit

The second story comes from South Korea. [MoneyToday](https://www.mt.co.kr/index.php/en/entertainment/2026/11/06/2026110608161626615) reports that Walt Disney Company Korea said on Friday that Hemsworth, Evans and Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige will visit the country on 7 December, nine days before *Avengers: Doomsday* opens.

Evans is returning for the first time in 11 years for a Marvel release, after promoting *Avengers: Age of Ultron* there in 2015. Hemsworth's trip has drawn more attention. The outlet says his absence from earlier official events had led some people to suspect he was avoiding the country. It adds that he did visit last year to film the second season of the Disney+ series *Limitless with Chris Hemsworth*.

During the visit the trio will meet local media and fans, with a press conference and a red carpet event planned. MoneyToday says further schedule details will follow. The article notes it was translated with AI and may differ slightly from the original.

## What happens next

Buyers at the AFM will see *Stuntnuts* first, so sales and release news may follow in the coming weeks. For Hemsworth, the Seoul events on 7 December lead straight into the *Avengers: Doomsday* opening on 16 December.
