**Quick summary**

- *Ebenezer* won its opening weekend with US$19 million in North America and US$23 million overseas, a US$42 million worldwide total, according to Business Insider.
- Paramount says 51% of surveyed moviegoers named [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) as their main reason for buying a ticket.
- Women made up 52% of the opening audience, and the film took the top spot in 63 international markets.
- Critics are split: [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) showed a 90% audience score but a 53% critics' score at the time of writing.

Paramount's Christmas gamble has paid off at the box office, and the studio's own audience data suggests the reason is simple: people wanted to see Johnny Depp. His Scrooge film *Ebenezer* opened at No. 1 over the weekend, giving the actor his first studio-backed lead role since 2018.

## Half the audience came for one actor

The most striking figure is not the takings but the survey. According to [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/ebenzer-box-office-numbers-johnny-depp-comeback-2026-11), Paramount said 51% of moviegoers it surveyed over the weekend named Depp as the main draw. An industry insider told the outlet that an actor typically drives attendance for around 20% of a film's audience, so this is more than double the usual share.

The crowd also leaned female. Women accounted for 52% of weekend ticket buyers, the report said. Paramount argued that Dickens was not the attraction; the anticipation of seeing Depp in the role was.

Some of that interest was built months earlier. Business Insider reports that Depp's surprise in-character appearance at San Diego Comic-Con generated more than 152 million social media impressions, and the first trailer drew over 225 million views in its first seven days, both figures supplied by the studio.

## The numbers behind the opening

At US$42 million worldwide, the debut is far from the scale of the *Pirates of the Caribbean* films, as Business Insider notes. Context matters, though. The film cost US$38 million to make, so the opening weekend has already covered its production budget.

[Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/johnny-depp-s-first-studio-movie-in-8-years-tops-the-box-office) cites Variety in reporting that marketing cost about US$50 million, which means the film still has ground to cover before it is clearly profitable. The same report says Depp was paid US$5 million up front, with bonuses tied to box office, compared with a reported US$55 million for *On Stranger Tides*.

Overseas, the strongest market was the UK, where the film held its world premiere and took US$5.4 million, according to Business Insider.

## A cast built around the lead

Director [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West), best known for horror, keeps the story at a PG-13 level, per Startefacts. The supporting cast includes [Sir Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) as Marley, Tramell Tillman as the Ghost of Christmas Present, [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint) and [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley). The story is a fresh take on the classic by [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens).

A BBC report from the set adds some texture. Colin Fraser, a member of the choir London Voices, told the BBC that his group sang carols live on camera, including *Silent Night* in a prison-yard scene with Depp present. He said the sets were physical rather than CGI and that night shoots in February brought real rain.

## The comeback context

Business Insider describes the release as part of Depp's effort to rebuild his image after the defamation trial involving his ex-wife [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard). Startefacts notes that courts in the UK and the US reached different conclusions in separate cases, and that both rulings still stand. Readers can find more of our coverage in the [Johnny Depp tag](https://moduscarry.com/tag/johnny-depp/) and in our earlier look at [the opening weekend](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-weekend/).

Separately, Page Six reports on an excerpt from Mark Seal's forthcoming biography, *The Many Faces of Johnny Depp*, due on 24 November, in which Depp recalls how Disney executives reacted to his early *Pirates* look. The *Pirates* franchise has earned over US$4.5 billion across five films, per the report. Our earlier post covers [how Depp pitched himself for the role](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-pirates-of-the-caribbean-pitch/).

The next test is the second weekend, when holiday films usually show whether word of mouth holds. Startefacts reports a claim from Cosmic Book News that Disney wants Depp back for a sixth *Pirates* film; that is unconfirmed. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much did Ebenezer make on opening weekend?
It earned US$19 million in North America and US$23 million internationally, for US$42 million worldwide, according to Business Insider.

### How much did Ebenezer cost to make?
Its production budget was US$38 million, per Business Insider and Startefacts. Startefacts also cites Variety on roughly US$50 million in marketing.

### Did critics like Ebenezer?
Reviews are mixed. Business Insider reported a 53% critics' score and a 90% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes at the time of writing.
