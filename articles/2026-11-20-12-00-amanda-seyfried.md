**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) joked about being a Bond girl, commenting "Am I too old?" on a Variety Instagram post about who could play the next Bond girl.
- The post followed news that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been officially cast as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- Seyfried is 40 and turns 41 next month, only a few months older than Pattinson, who is also 40.
- No Amanda Seyfried Bond girl casting exists: the female lead is unannounced and the names circulating are fan and bookmaker talk.

Amanda Seyfried has put her name into the unofficial race to play the next Bond girl, with a one-line joke on Instagram this week. Under a Variety post listing the actresses its writers and editors would like to see opposite Robert Pattinson's 007, she wrote: "Am I too old?"

The Amanda Seyfried Bond girl comment was reported by Men's Journal on 18 November and republished by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/amanda-seyfried-asks-shes-too-230337709.html).

## What Seyfried actually said

According to that report, Seyfried posts under the handle @mingey, a nod to her favourite British word, which she also has tattooed. Her comment sat beneath Variety's list of suggested Bond girls, which included Zendaya, [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), Pugh, Nyong'o and Anya Taylor-Joy, along with Sydney Sweeney, Hunter Schafer and Adria Arjona.

It was a light remark, not a statement of interest from her team, and nothing in the report suggests she has been approached for the film.

## Why the joke lands

The question is a little pointed once the numbers are lined up. Per the same report, Seyfried is 40 and will turn 41 next month. Pattinson is also 40, so she is only a few months older than the actor who has just been confirmed as the new Bond.

The younger names on Variety's list sit well below that. The report puts Zendaya, Taylor-Joy and Pugh at 30, Qualley at 32, Arjona at 34, Sweeney at 29 and Schafer at 27. Several of them are a decade or more younger than the man they would be paired with.

That gap is a long-running criticism of the franchise. The report notes that feminist film fans have objected to Bond girls often being much younger than the actor playing 007. It adds that some Daniel Craig-era films did better, citing Monica Bellucci, who is four years older than Craig, but still calls the pattern a key example of Hollywood ageism.

## The Bond girl role and the speculation

The Bond girl, a female ally or romantic interest for 007, is one of the franchise's best-known roles. The report lists [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry), [Eva Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Green), [Ana de Armas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Armas), [Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh), [Rosamund Pike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosamund_Pike) and [Jane Seymour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Seymour_(actress)) among the best-remembered.

After Pattinson's casting followed a weeks-long, widely publicised search, attention moved quickly to his co-star. The report says bookmakers and fans have been trading names this week, with [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), Zendaya, Taylor-Joy and Qualley among the most mentioned on social media and betting sites. These are guesses from fans and oddsmakers, not confirmed casting.

## What happens next

No female lead has been announced for the new Bond film, so for now Seyfried's comment is a joke in a comment thread. Any real casting would come from the producers and studio.
