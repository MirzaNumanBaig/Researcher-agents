**Quick summary**

- Julia Stiles danced a tribute to [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger) on the US *Dancing With the Stars* Dedication Night episode.
- She told Extra TV she asked [Michelle Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Williams_(actress)) whether the tribute was acceptable and Williams said yes, according to Rova.
- Several *10 Things I Hate About You* co-stars joined her on the floor at the end.
- David Krumholtz was absent because he was working on a show in New York, Rova reports, and he later praised the tribute on social media.

Julia Stiles has said she sought Michelle Williams' blessing before honouring Heath Ledger on *Dancing With the Stars*, and that Williams agreed. The performance aired during the show's Dedication Night, and it reunited much of the *10 Things I Hate About You* cast.

## What Stiles danced

Stiles and her professional partner Ezra Sosa performed to Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off You", according to [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/pop-culture/julia-stiles-reunites-with-10-things-i-hate-about-you-cast-for-emotional-heath-ledger-tribute-on-dwts-c-23057725). The song is the one Ledger's character serenades her with in the 1999 film, and the routine recreated that scene, including some of his signature moves.

The routine closed with a photo from the film's set and audio of the poem Stiles' character reads in the story, [Rova](https://www.rova.nz/articles/julia-stiles-asked-michelle-williams-for-permission-before-honouring-heath-ledger-with-dance) reports. Stiles then placed a single red rose on a set of steps in Ledger's honour.

## The co-stars who joined her

After the rose, Stiles was joined by Andrew Keegan, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Larisa Oleynik, Gabrielle Union and Susan May Pratt, and they held hands on the floor, according to both outlets. It was a rare on-screen gathering of the cast of a film that has stayed popular for more than 25 years.

Krumholtz was the notable absence. Rova reports he could not attend because he was filming in New York, and that he praised the tribute online afterwards.

## Asking Michelle Williams first

Before the show, Stiles contacted Williams, who shares a daughter, Matilda, with Ledger, The Nightly reports. Speaking to Extra TV, Stiles said: "I did ask Michelle if this was OK and she said yes."

Sosa, in a joint Instagram post with Stiles quoted by The Nightly, said the pair had never approached a performance "with more care, sensitivity, and intention than this one". He added that they wanted it made with "the utmost respect" and close guidance from people closest to Ledger.

Ledger, who was born in Perth, met Williams while filming *Brokeback Mountain* in 2004, according to The Nightly. Matilda was born in 2005.

## How viewers reacted

Online reaction was largely warm. The Nightly quotes one fan on X who wrote that Stiles "wrecked me with that tribute to Heath Ledger". Not every response was positive, and some commenters questioned reality-television tributes in general, though the outlets we read did not detail those criticisms, so we have not reported them.

## What happens next

Stiles remains in the competition, and further episodes will show how far she goes. We have not seen any announcement that the cast will appear together again.
