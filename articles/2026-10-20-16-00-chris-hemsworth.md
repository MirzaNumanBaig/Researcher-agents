**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) hosted Thai Muay Thai star [Rodtang Jitmuangnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodtang_Jitmuangnon) on the set of Netflix's *Extraction 3* in Ratchaburi, Thailand.
- Rodtang posted a video of the meeting on 19 October; Hemsworth shared a photo on Instagram the next day.
- Rodtang gave the actor personalised gloves, which Hemsworth said were headed for his trophy room.
- Rodtang fights Nong-O Hama on 13 November in Bangkok, according to Thaiger.

Australian actor Chris Hemsworth has welcomed one of Thailand's best-known fighters to the set of *Extraction 3*, closing the loop on a social media friendship that began about two years ago. Muay Thai star Rodtang Jitmuangnon visited the production in Ratchaburi, and both men posted about it this week.

## How the meeting played out

According to [Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/news/national/rodtang-finally-meets-chris-hemsworth-his-hollywood-hero), Rodtang shared a video of the encounter on 19 October. It shows the pair trading warm embraces, chatting and joking. The outlet says the meeting came roughly two years after Hemsworth first followed the fighter on Instagram, which prompted a delighted Rodtang to record a greeting for the actor.

[Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/featured/2026/10/20/chris-hemsworth-welcomes-thai-boxing-star-rodtang-to-extraction-3-set/) reports that Hemsworth later posted a photo of the two on his own account, where he has more than 58 million followers. The 43-year-old wrote that he was honoured to have the "Muay Thai legend" visit the *Extraction 3* set. He called Rodtang "all class, absolute champion" and thanked him for a specially made, signed pair of gloves that he said were "straight to the trophy room".

## Who Rodtang is

Rodtang, nicknamed The Iron Man, is a former ONE Flyweight Muay Thai world champion, Thaiger says. The outlet adds that he has long admired Hemsworth's films, best known for the actor's role as Thor in the Marvel series.

Hemsworth has been leaning into the sport himself. Thaiger reports that earlier this month he shared footage of himself practising Muay Thai at The St. Regis Bangkok. Rodtang was among the fighters who reacted, alongside several UFC stars.

## What the visit says about the production

Both outlets confirm that Hemsworth is in Thailand to film *Extraction 3*, the Netflix action sequel. Neither report gives a release date, a plot outline or details of any role for Rodtang, and the visit is described only as a set visit. Khaosod English frames it as a meeting between one of Hollywood's best-known action stars and a prominent figure in Thai combat sports.

## What happens next

Rodtang returns to the ring on 13 November at Lumpinee Boxing Stadium in Bangkok. Thaiger says he will face fellow Thai fighter Nong-O Hama in the opening round of the 2026 ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Grand Prix. The two were due to meet at ONE 173 last November, but the bout was postponed after Rodtang fell ill during fight week.
