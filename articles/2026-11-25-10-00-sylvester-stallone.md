**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) floated a Stallone Tulsa King spinoff starring Arnold Schwarzenegger in a Cigar Aficionado interview.
- The Hollywood Reporter says no such project has been announced at any stage of development.
- Stallone also doubted he would return as Rocky Balboa, and floated a prequel instead.
- I Play Rocky, the film about his breakthrough, took second place at the US box office last weekend with US$9.32 million.
- It beat Johnny Depp's Ebenezer for the weekend, though Ebenezer still leads on total earnings.

A Stallone Tulsa King spinoff starring Arnold Schwarzenegger is a wish, not a plan. Sylvester Stallone raised it in a Cigar Aficionado cover story, reported on 24 November by The Hollywood Reporter, about a show built off [Tulsa King](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King). There is no deal, script or announcement.

## What Stallone said about Schwarzenegger

"I would love in a perfect world to do an extension of Tulsa King starring [Schwarzenegger] in his own show," Stallone told the magazine, according to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/sylvester-stallone-tulsa-king-spinoff-arnold-schwarzenegger-1236762273/). He called them "comrades in the fantasy world" whose characters could meet, as in The Expendables. His closing line was blunt: "I hope he agrees."

The trade outlet noted that Paramount+ and Tulsa King producer 101 Studios had no immediate comment, and that this could simply be Stallone floating a concept.

There is a precedent, however. Tulsa King, a Nielsen ratings hit, already has one spinoff, Frisco King, which stars Samuel L. Jackson, according to the same report.

## Rocky II, and a possible prequel

With I Play Rocky in cinemas, Stallone also described a clash with John G. Avildsen, director of the original Rocky, over the sequel. He said he wrote the version where Rocky is married, which was eventually made. In his telling, Avildsen wanted Rocky drinking and partying, and quit when Stallone refused.

On the question of playing Rocky Balboa again, he was cautious. "It would be hard," he said, saying a prequel is more likely, with Mickey at 30 and Rocky at 16. No prequel has been announced.

## The box office: I Play Rocky versus Ebenezer

The film itself is having a good run. According to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/ebenezer-i-play-rocky-november-2026/), I Play Rocky expanded nationwide on 20 November after a limited release that began on 6 November in seven theatres and grew to 29. It beat its US$7 million projection, finishing second with US$9.32 million.

That placed it ahead of Ebenezer, Johnny Depp's gothic take on Charles Dickens's A Christmas Carol, even though it played in about 1,000 fewer theatres. Ebenezer opened on 13 November with US$19 million, in line with forecasts of US$18 million to US$20 million, and took first place. In its second weekend it fell 59.5 percent to third place with US$7.79 million, facing The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

I Play Rocky does not star Stallone. [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) plays the young writer fighting to get his script made. Audiences gave it an A+ CinemaScore, and it averaged US$3,763 per theatre, more than 70 percent above Ebenezer's US$2,197, according to MovieWeb.

## Why the totals still favour Ebenezer

The weekend win is not the whole picture. Ebenezer has earned US$32.5 million in the United States and US$78.4 million worldwide against a US$38 million budget, per MovieWeb. I Play Rocky stands at US$10.4 million domestically and US$15.1 million globally, and still has ground to cover to recoup its US$30 million budget.

Both films now face a crowded Thanksgiving period, with Disney's Hexed and the comedy sequel Focker-In-Law arriving. MovieWeb argues I Play Rocky is better placed because two-week booking minimums should protect its screens.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Tulsa King spinoff with Arnold Schwarzenegger confirmed?

No. Stallone said he would love it and hopes Schwarzenegger agrees, but The Hollywood Reporter says nothing has been announced at any stage of development.

### Does Sylvester Stallone star in I Play Rocky?

No. Anthony Ippolito plays the young Stallone in the film, which chronicles the making of the original 1976 Rocky, according to MovieWeb.

### Did I Play Rocky beat Ebenezer at the box office?

For last weekend, yes: US$9.32 million against US$7.79 million. Ebenezer still leads on total earnings, with US$78.4 million worldwide against US$15.1 million.

### Will Stallone play Rocky Balboa again?

He said it would be hard and suggested a prequel is more likely than a return. No project has been announced.
