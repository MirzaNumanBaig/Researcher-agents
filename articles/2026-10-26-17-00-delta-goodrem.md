**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) returned to [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) after a fractured rib and danced a rumba with [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington).
- The pair scored 34 out of 40, including the first 10 awarded to any contestant this season, according to Who.
- They finished second for the night; Tabby and Mark led with 37 points, per Yahoo Lifestyle Australia.
- Some viewers called the top mark too generous, while others praised her comeback.

Delta Goodrem came back to the Strictly dance floor on Saturday night in London with a fractured rib, and the judges rewarded her with the first perfect mark of the series. The score has since split viewers, with some questioning whether the 10 was earned and others pointing to how hard the return looked.

## The score that started the argument

Goodrem and Widdrington danced a rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back To Me Now", according to [Yahoo Lifestyle Australia](https://au.lifestyle.yahoo.com/delta-goodrem-makes-triumphant-return-to-strictly-come-dancing-as-fans-express-concern-for-stars-health-212840647.html). The four judges gave 7, 9, 8 and 10, a total of 34 out of a possible 40, [Who reported](https://www.who.com.au/entertainment/delta-goodrem-strictly-come-dancing-return/).

That 10 was the first of the season. Who says it came from Anton Du Beke, and that the crowd erupted when it went up. Goodrem and Widdrington hugged on the floor.

The 34 was good for second place on the leaderboard. Tabby and Mark finished first with 37 points and two 10s of their own, Yahoo reported. So the series now has more than one perfect mark, but Goodrem's was the first.

## Why some viewers pushed back

Who says the reaction online was mixed. One viewer wrote that the routine was "very over marked", and another called the score "ridiculous". A third suggested she was the "judge's favourite" and that the injury had tilted the marking in her favour.

Those views are opinions from social media, not findings, and the judges gave no sign of anything beyond scoring what they saw. Other viewers defended the 10. One said it was good to see her dancing again and that she had done an incredible job coming back while still recovering.

The disagreement is a familiar one for a show where scores mix technique, performance and story. It is hard to separate a dancer's comeback from the dance itself.

## What the return cost her

Yahoo says some viewers were worried about her health as she danced. One noted she held a rail on the stairs and that Widdrington was gentle with her. Another said the injury seemed to affect how upright she stood. These are viewer impressions, not medical statements.

Hours before the show, the 41-year-old posted footage of her recovery on Instagram. Who reports she spent days on the couch and felt nauseous from the pain. Yahoo adds that she shared clips of being assessed in hospital and became emotional when she spoke about whether she could return.

"I was gutted when the injury happened as I've been truly loving this experience so much," she wrote, according to Who. She added that she was being patient and "learning to appreciate the small steps".

The timeline was tight. Yahoo says that by the Monday, eight days after she first went to hospital, she was back in the studio learning the routine. Who notes she had topped the leaderboard in two consecutive weeks before she stepped away.

## What happens next

Goodrem is still recovering, and the pair will have to keep building routines around the injury. Neither Who nor Yahoo reported any change to her plans to stay in the competition. The Sydney Morning Herald also ran a piece on Sunday headlined that she had shared a raw insight into her recovery, but the page was not readable for this report, so nothing from it is used here.

## Frequently asked questions

### What score did Delta Goodrem get on Strictly?
She and Kai Widdrington scored 34 out of 40, with marks of 7, 9, 8 and 10 from the four judges, according to Who.

### Was it the first perfect mark of the series?
Yes. Who reports the 10 was the first given to any contestant this season. Yahoo says Tabby and Mark later scored two 10s in the same round.

### What injury does Delta Goodrem have?
She has a fractured rib. She stepped away from the competition for a week to recover and returned for the rumba, according to Who and Yahoo.

### Why are some fans unhappy with the score?
Some viewers said it was over-marked or suggested the judges favoured her because of the injury, Who reports. Others said the mark was deserved.
