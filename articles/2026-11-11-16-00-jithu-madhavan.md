**Quick summary**

- *Scene*, the first Tamil film from director [Jithu Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jithu_Madhavan), opened in theatres on 6 November 2026 over the Diwali weekend.
- The makers say it earned ₹145 crore worldwide in its first three days, as reported by Hindustan Times.
- [Mahesh Babu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahesh_Babu) and director Atlee both praised the film publicly.
- Reviewers call it a quirky cop-and-killer comedy, though Asian Movie Pulse flags some narrative conveniences.

Jithu Madhavan's Tamil debut is turning into a talking point. *Scene*, a dark action comedy starring Suriya, reached cinemas on 6 November, and within days two of south Indian cinema's biggest names had posted their approval. The makers also report a ₹145 crore worldwide gross after three days, according to [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/tamil-cinema/mahesh-babu-is-all-praise-for-suriya-in-his-review-of-scene-calls-the-jithu-madhavan-film-a-must-watch-101794237853140.html).

## What the Jithu Madhavan Scene release means for him

Madhavan made his name in Malayalam cinema with *Romancham* and *Aavesham*, and *Scene* is his first film in Tamil, [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/tamil/movies/news/mahesh-babu-praises-suriyas-scene-calls-him-fabulous-as-inbaraj-says-jithu-madhavans-film-is-must-watch/articleshow/135681952.cms) reports. He wrote and directed it. [Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/south-cinema/mahesh-babu-reviews-suriya-starrer-scene-calls-the-jithu-madhavan-directorial-cool-smart-and-so-much-fun/) describes his usual approach as mixing humour with unconventional characters and situations.

The film is produced by Suriya and Jyotika's 2D Entertainment with the newly formed Zhagaram Studios. [Sushin Shyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sushin_Shyam) composed the music. [Nazriya Nazim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazriya_Nazim) returns to Tamil cinema after 12 years, and Malayalam actor Naslen makes his Tamil debut.

## The film's premise, according to reviewers

Suriya plays DSP Inbaraj, called Inba, a decorated officer. Asian Movie Pulse says he faces two problems at once, one rooted in a disability and the other in a deranged killer, and decides to deal with both outside the force. The result, it says, is a cat-and-mouse game that gives Madhavan room to play with eccentric characters.

[High On Films](https://www.highonfilms.com/scene-2026-movie-review/) is warmer still. Its reviewer calls the film a "deliciously quirky" comedy and says it turns a serial-killer story into something darkly funny. Naslen's killer, Eeswaran, sucks a lollipop and treats murder as a business transaction. Inba's parents, played by Anandraj and Selvi, are both criminologists, but each carries a condition that makes them an unlikely investigative team. Nazriya's Maya is described as far more than a romantic interest.

Asian Movie Pulse gives a more measured verdict. It calls the film "an absolute blast" by the time the credits roll, but notes the first half takes time to settle. It also points to characters whose quirks become convenient solutions, and says a few performers, Lal among them, don't always seem at ease in Madhavan's first Tamil setting. It credits cinematographer Vineeth Augustin with giving a modest budget a larger look.

## Mahesh Babu and Atlee weigh in

On 9 November, Mahesh Babu posted on X that *Scene* is "cool, smart and so much fun" and called it a "must watch". He singled out Suriya as "fabulous", praised Nazriya and Naslen, and credited Madhavan's "brilliant writing and excellent execution", according to Bollywood Hungama.

Director Atlee followed a day later. The Times of India reports he called the film a "paisa vasool" entertainer, meaning good value for money, and said he has long been a fan of Madhavan's work. He described Naslen as a superstar in the making and said he wanted to see more of Nazriya in Tamil films.

## A third hit for Suriya in 2026

Hindustan Times calls *Scene* Suriya's third release of the year. Asian Movie Pulse names the other two as *Vishwanath and Sons* and *Karuppu*, and says all three were made by directors who understand his appeal. Suriya thanked fans in a statement quoted by Hindustan Times, writing that the love shown to the film "across languages and borders" meant the world to the team.

## What happens next

The box office figure comes from the makers' own Instagram update, so independent tallies will show whether the opening holds beyond the first week. For the people praising it, work continues elsewhere. Mahesh Babu's next release is SS Rajamouli's *Varanasi*, due in 2027, and Atlee is preparing *Raaka* with Allu Arjun. Madhavan has not announced a next project in the sources reviewed.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Scene released?
*Scene* opened in theatres on 6 November 2026, during the Diwali weekend, according to Hindustan Times and The Times of India.

### Who directed Scene?
Jithu Madhavan wrote and directed it. It is his first Tamil film after the Malayalam titles *Romancham* and *Aavesham*.

### How much has Scene earned?
The makers say it grossed ₹145 crore worldwide in three days. That figure is their own and has not been independently verified in the sources reviewed.

### Who stars in Scene?
Suriya plays the lead, DSP Inbaraj, with Nazriya Nazim, Naslen, Anandraj and Lal in the cast.
