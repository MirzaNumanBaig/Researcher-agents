**Quick summary**

- [Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary) has spent exactly 100 days in the Apple TV Store's VOD Top 20, according to data dated 25 November 2026.
- That ties it with 2018's The Grinch as the longest-running film currently on the chart.
- The film reached digital release in May 2026, so it has been in the Top 20 for about half of its time on the market.
- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) next appears in Star Wars: Starfighter, due on 28 May 2027.

[Ryan Gosling](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-gosling/)'s space survival drama Project Hail Mary has stayed in the Apple TV Store's Top 20 on-demand chart for 100 days, a streak that now matches The Grinch for the longest on the list. The figure comes from data for 25 November 2026, reported by [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/project-hail-mary-ryan-gosling-apple-tv-milestone-november-2026/), roughly eight months after the film reached cinemas.

## What the Apple TV milestone means

Most films fade from rental and purchase charts within weeks of their digital debut. Project Hail Mary has not. ScreenRant reports that it arrived digitally in May 2026 and has been in the Top 20 for about half the time since.

The only other title level with it is The Grinch, released in 2018, which makes the comparison a useful one. A family animation built around the Christmas season has a natural reason to keep returning to the chart. A science-fiction film released in spring has no such help, and the 100-day count says viewers are still finding it by word of mouth.

## The story behind the film

The film is based on Andy Weir's novel of the same name. Gosling plays Dr Ryland Grace, a molecular biologist who wakes aboard a spacecraft far from Earth with no memory of how he got there. As he pieces things together, he learns he was sent to find a way to stop the sun from dimming, a threat that could end humanity. Along the way he teams up with a rock-like alien.

ScreenRant describes it as Gosling's best film of 2026, and puts it alongside earlier high-profile roles such as the replicant K in Blade Runner 2049 and Ken in Barbie.

## The reviews and the box office

The critical response backs up the staying power. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) lists a Certified Fresh score of 95% across 430 reviews, and audiences give it a Verified Hot 95% from more than 25,000 verified ratings, according to ScreenRant.

The money was strong too. The report says the film earned US$685.1 million worldwide against a gross budget of US$248 million, which is about 2.8 times its cost. Those are the figures as reported by ScreenRant, and the outlet does not say which tracker supplied them.

## What happens next

The streaming run arrives as Gosling lines up two much bigger franchise jobs. Star Wars: Starfighter, the first film in the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) saga set after the Skywalker era, opens in cinemas on 28 May 2027. He is also due to play Johnny Blaze in Ghost Rider for the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe), scheduled for 28 July 2028.
