**Quick summary**

- Chanel made up three of the six main outfits [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) wore on the *Practical Magic 2* press tour, according to Red Carpet Fashion Awards.
- At the Los Angeles premiere she debuted Pieter Mulier's first Versace red carpet look, a black lace dress.
- Other stops included a Chanel Haute Couture gown in London, tailoring on *The Tonight Show* and a floral Erdem dress at a surprise LA screening.
- A separate beauty feature says Kidman credits a scalp serum from Vegamour for bringing her natural curls back.

Nicole Kidman spent the *Practical Magic 2* press tour moving between classic Chanel glamour and darker, witch-ready black, and the standout moment came in Los Angeles, where she wore the first Versace look designed by Pieter Mulier on a red carpet. Red Carpet Fashion Awards published its round-up of the six best outfits on 25 November.

## Chanel runs the wardrobe

The French house accounted for half the tour's headline looks, the fashion site reports. For the London premiere, Kidman chose Chanel Haute Couture, the most formal of the set. At the London photocall the same label appeared in a more understated form. Her appearance on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon* then brought menswear-inspired tailoring, again from Chanel.

The variety is the point of the round-up: the writer notes that despite the Chanel-heavy lineup, each outfit offered something different, from familiar Kidman glamour to a darker and more seductive side of her red carpet style.

## The Versace debut in Los Angeles

The biggest talking point, according to the same report, was the black lace dress at the LA premiere. It marked the first Versace red carpet look from Pieter Mulier, and the site says the witchy lace suited a film built around witchcraft.

The film nods continued elsewhere. Kidman wore a black Colleen Allen dress at CinemaCon, while a floral Erdem dress at the LA surprise screening gave a softer, more romantic take on the theme.

## The film behind the fashion

*Practical Magic 2* is the sequel to the [1998 original](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic). Susanne Bier directed, and the cast includes Sandra Bullock, Kidman, Stockard Channing, Dianne Wiest and Joey King, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic_2). The same entry records a budget of about US$75 million and a box office of about US$106 million, with a 35% score on Rotten Tomatoes. Its critics' consensus describes the film as nostalgic but mechanical, while praising the sisterly rapport between Bullock and Kidman.

## Kidman's hair routine

Separately, a beauty feature from [Ethos](https://the-ethos.co/the-serum-nicole-kidman-uses-religiously-goes-on-her-scalp/) revisits comments Kidman made to Beauty Matter. She said years of dye, wigs, clips and tight ponytails left her hair weak and prone to breakage, particularly at the front. The recovery began at the scalp with a serum she used "religiously", and her natural curls returned over time. She liked the brand, Vegamour, enough to invest in it and become its wellness advocate. The comments are Kidman's own account, and the feature uses them to illustrate a wider trend in scalp care.

## Frequently asked questions

### What did Nicole Kidman wear to the Practical Magic 2 Los Angeles premiere?

She wore a black lace dress that was the first Versace red carpet look from designer Pieter Mulier, according to Red Carpet Fashion Awards.

### Which designers appeared most on the press tour?

Chanel, with three of her six featured looks: Haute Couture in London, a subtler outfit at the London photocall and menswear-style tailoring for *The Tonight Show*.

### Who stars in Practical Magic 2?

Sandra Bullock and Nicole Kidman lead, alongside Stockard Channing, Dianne Wiest and Joey King. Susanne Bier directed.
