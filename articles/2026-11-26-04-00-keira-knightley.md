**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) and [Joe Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Wilkinson) reprise their roles in Waitrose's Christmas advert, The Perfect Gift 2.
- [Alex Hassell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Hassell) joins the sequel as Hugo Bunty-Griffin, a rival for Wilkinson's character.
- The trio spoke to Stylist about nativity plays, Christmas dinner and the best presents they have received.
- Knightley said her mother once gave her a toolbox and a Black & Decker power tool.

The Keira Knightley Waitrose Christmas ad is back. The Perfect Gift 2, a sequel to last year's festive romcom, reunites Knightley with Joe Wilkinson and adds Alex Hassell to the cast, according to a [Stylist interview](https://www.stylist.co.uk/entertainment/keira-knightley-waitrose-christmas-interview/1129417) published on 25 November.

## What happens in The Perfect Gift 2

Knightley and Wilkinson play characters named Keira and Phil, as they did in last year's advert. Hassell plays Hugo Bunty-Griffin, Phil's romantic rival, described by Stylist as a man whose one true love may be his own reflection. A beef wellington sits at the centre of the chaos, and the writer calls the result a festive love triangle.

Knightley said she always felt last year's love story deserved another chapter. She said she enjoyed revisiting the relationship, though she did not expect to be caught between two men and a beef wellington.

## The toolbox present

Asked about the best Christmas present she has received, Knightley named a toolbox and a Black & Decker power tool from her mother. She told Stylist she read it as a passive-aggressive gift, since she is not very handy and had a habit of phoning her mother whenever a shelf fell down. She is still undecided whether it was her worst present. It did prove useful in the end, because she used it to build her daughter's dollhouse.

## Nativity memories

Long before roles such as Elizabeth Bennet in Pride & Prejudice and Helen in [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves), Knightley played the angel Gabriel in her school nativity. She said she was annoyed to learn the part is usually a boy, so she set out to show a girl could do it. She also recalled playing a witch, without knowing why one was in the nativity.

Hassell remembers little beyond a photo of himself with a tea towel on his head, which suggests he may have been a wise man. Wilkinson does not think he was ever in a nativity, but he remembers playing a sailor in a school play and feeling at his most comfortable there.

## Who runs Christmas dinner

Hassell described himself as an all right cook, and both Wilkinson and Knightley promptly volunteered as his assistants. Wilkinson said he is happy with a clear task such as laying the table. Knightley called herself a good sous chef and asked to be given the brussels sprouts.

The beef wellington did not win everyone over. Wilkinson stays loyal to it, Hassell prefers a traditional turkey, and Knightley sees no reason not to serve both. On presents, Knightley and Wilkinson both open them as soon as they wake. Knightley, who has children, has one condition: she must have a cup of coffee first.
