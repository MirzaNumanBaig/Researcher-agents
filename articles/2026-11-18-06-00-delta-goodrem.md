**Quick summary**

- *Strictly Come Dancing* professional [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) returns to training on Monday with [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem), according to the Daily Mail.
- He had missed Saturday's live show because of an illness the paper calls a mystery.
- [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in at the last minute and danced an Argentine Tango with Goodrem.
- The pair spent Monday practising their routine for the Blackpool special.

Kai Widdrington returns to training with Delta Goodrem after a scare, looking in good spirits on Monday, days after illness forced him out of Saturday's *Strictly Come Dancing* live show. The Australian singer, 42, and her 31-year-old dance partner were photographed after a full day of rehearsals for the Blackpool special, according to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16298983/Strictly-pro-Kai-Widdrington-returns-training-Delta-Goodrem-rushed-hospital-ambulance-forced-pull-show.html).

## Kai Widdrington returns to training: what the pair were seen doing

The Daily Mail says Widdrington high-fived Goodrem as they finished for the day. He wore a black jacket, joggers and a green cap, and was seen waving her off as she climbed into a chauffeur-driven car. Goodrem chose a dark padded jacket with brown leggings and brown trainers.

The paper describes him as looking back on form. That is its own reading of a few photographs, and neither he nor Goodrem has said how he is feeling in the material we read.

## How Saturday's show unfolded

Widdrington could not take part in the live show because of what the Daily Mail calls a mystery illness. The report says Goodrem had recently shared a picture of him in the back of an ambulance as he was taken to hospital. No cause or diagnosis is given in the source, so none should be assumed.

Fellow professional Aljaz Skorjanec took over with very little notice. Goodrem and Skorjanec performed an Argentine Tango to "Missing" by Everything But The Girl. For a show built on a full week of drilling one routine, a late change of partner is a big ask, which is why Monday's return matters to viewers following her progress.

We covered the fallout from that night in [Delta Goodrem's Strictly curveball](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-strictly-curveball/).

## Why Blackpool raises the stakes

The Daily Mail refers to the coming episode as the Blackpool special, and says the pair spent Monday working on that routine. The source does not say what dance they will perform or which music they have chosen, so those details are still unknown. The report also gives no word on whether Widdrington has been cleared to dance on the night, only that he was able to train.

## What happens next

The next milestone is the Blackpool episode. Anything beyond that, including the dance style, the song and the scores, has not been announced in the reporting we have seen.
