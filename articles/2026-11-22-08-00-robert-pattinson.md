**Quick summary**

- Robert Pattinson The Take: Netflix's heist thriller arrives on 15 January, with a limited cinema release the same day, according to [Metro Mode](https://metromode.se/en/film-och-tv/denzel-washington-och-robert-pattinson-ranar-bank-i-netflix-the-take/).
- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) stars with [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones).
- Netflix has released a first poster and stills, but no trailer yet.
- The news lands days after Pattinson was announced as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) on 16 November.

Robert Pattinson The Take now has a date: Netflix has confirmed the bank heist premieres on 15 January, with Denzel Washington and Daisy Edgar-Jones alongside him, and has put out a first poster. It arrives in the week Pattinson was named the next 007.

## What Netflix has revealed about The Take

The streamer's official description is short: a bank guard, a cashier and a master thief are pulled into a deadly game of deception and double-dealing. Metro Mode reports that the film will stream worldwide on the day it opens in a limited run in cinemas.

The poster sets the tone. Pattinson and Washington sit across a table in a dark room, hands clasped, eyes locked. The tagline reads "Time to get what you deserve."

Netflix has not released a trailer. Metro Mode notes that clips circulating on YouTube under the name "The Take Trailer" are fan-made, and that What's on Netflix expects a first teaser in December. That is a forecast, not a confirmed date.

## The plot and the cast around Robert Pattinson

Metro Mode, citing What's on Netflix, which in turn refers to [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), says Pattinson plays Noah, a former army officer struggling to settle back into civilian life. Washington plays master thief Christian Carter, who recruits Noah to rob Manhattan's richest bank.

There is a complication. Noah's girlfriend Rose, played by Edgar-Jones, works as a cashier at that very bank. The whole story is told from Noah's point of view, according to the same report.

The supporting cast includes [Danai Gurira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danai_Gurira) as Oaklynn, [Sean Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Harris) as Logan, Moisés Arias as Gabriel and [Justin Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kirk) as Anderson. For Washington and Edgar-Jones, it is their first film made directly for Netflix. Pattinson has worked with the service before, on *The King* and *The Devil All the Time*.

Asked about the offer, Pattinson reportedly needed little persuading. "Getting to make a heist film in New York with Denzel Washington and Daisy Edgar-Jones, what more is there to say?" he said, as quoted by Metro Mode from Kinocheck.

## Behind the camera

[Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles) directs. The Brazilian filmmaker was Oscar-nominated for *City of God* and has made *The Two Popes* for Netflix. [Simon Kinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Kinberg) wrote the screenplay and produces through [Genre Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genre_Films) with Audrey Chon.

The project has been a long time coming. Netflix bought Kinberg's script in 2020 under the title *Here Comes the Flood*, and that was the working title when cameras rolled between November 2025 and January 2026. Locations included New York, Jersey City, Hoboken, Newark and Atlanta. Wikipedia's entry for the film lists a 15 January 2027 release.

## A big week for Pattinson

The Take news sits alongside the Bond story. According to [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/amazon-mgm-spent-up-to-a-staggering-500-000-testing-actors-for-james-bond), Pattinson's casting was announced on Monday, 16 November, ending years of speculation over who would follow [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig). The same report's headline says Amazon MGM spent up to $500,000 testing actors for the part. For more on how that decision came together, see our coverage of [Pattinson's Bond screen test](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-screen-test-2/) and [what made his casting work](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-quirky/).

*The Guardian* also ran a cartoon by Sam Lau on Saturday about Pattinson's presence across cinema, a sign of how much attention he is getting. Metro Mode describes it as an unusually intense year in film for him, with a career that has run from *Twilight* to *The Batman*.

## What happens next

Mark 15 January for the premiere. A teaser could arrive in December, though Netflix has not said so. Treat unofficial trailers with caution.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does The Take come out?

Netflix lists 15 January for its worldwide release, with a limited cinema run on the same day, according to Metro Mode.

### Who stars in The Take?

Robert Pattinson plays Noah, Denzel Washington plays master thief Christian Carter and Daisy Edgar-Jones plays Rose. Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk also appear.

### Is there an official trailer for The Take?

Not yet. Netflix has released a poster and stills only. Videos titled "The Take Trailer" on YouTube are fan-made.

### Who directed The Take?

Fernando Meirelles, the Brazilian director of *City of God*, directed it from a script by Simon Kinberg.
