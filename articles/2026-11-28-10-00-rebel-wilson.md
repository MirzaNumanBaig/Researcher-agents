**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) attended publicist Lee Caulfield's fourth annual Christmas party in London on Thursday 26 November.
- The event was held at St. Clement, Nick Jones' new hotel on the Thames, with a "Christmas Glam" dress code.
- Guests took home goody bags worth more than £2,000, with gifts from over 50 brands.
- Other guests included Rob Minkoff, Richard Gadd, Pixie Lott and Dame Kelly Holmes.

[Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) was one of the headline names at a Thames-side Christmas party in London on Thursday 26 November, hosted by celebrity fixer and Circle PR founder Lee Caulfield, according to [The Luxe Review](https://theluxereview.com/2026/11/27/rebel-wilson-real-housewives-and-a-2000-goody-bag-inside-one-of-londons-most-glamorous-christmas-parties/). It was Caulfield's fourth annual festive gathering, and this year it took place at St. Clement, the new hotel from Nick Jones.

## Who joined Rebel Wilson at the party

The Luxe Review says Wilson was there alongside *The Lion King* director [Rob Minkoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Minkoff) and his wife, *The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills* star [Crystal Kung Minkoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_Kung_Minkoff). The wider list leaned British: *[Baby Reindeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_Reindeer)* creator [Richard Gadd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Gadd), model [Adwoa Aboah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adwoa_Aboah), [Munroe Bergdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munroe_Bergdorf), singer [Pixie Lott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pixie_Lott), [Joel Dommett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Dommett), [Mo Gilligan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Gilligan) and [Dame Kelly Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_Holmes).

The outlet also names actor [Mathew Horne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathew_Horne), The Kooks' Hugh Harris, Royal Ballet principal [Reece Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reece_Clarke), author Roxie Nafousi and nutritionist [Emily English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_English) among the crowd.

## What the evening looked like

The dress code was "Christmas Glam". The report describes free-flowing drinks, canapés and live music, plus a cocktail bar called "Clementini" with an ice luge. Festive ice sculptures and personalised calligraphy by Montblanc completed the setting.

The Luxe Review does not quote Wilson or say what she wore, so there is little to add about her own evening beyond her place on the guest list.

## The £2,000 goody bag

The take-home bag drew the most attention. According to the report, it was worth more than £2,000 and held products from over 50 brands, including Clé de Peau Beauté, Valmont, [Charlotte Tilbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Tilbury), Monica Vinader, Montblanc, Kayali, Vyrao and Elemis. It also contained Krude Olive Oil from [Gordon Ramsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Ramsay).

## The host behind the party

Caulfield grew up in Walthamstow, East London, and began at 17 working backstage on the West End musical *We Will Rock You*. He later became a personal assistant to stars including George and Amal Clooney, and by 25 he had co-founded the London cocktail bar The Parrot with Idris Elba.

He launched his communications agency in 2022. At 34, The Luxe Review says, he runs an international business with offices in London, New York, Miami, Dubai and Paris. Of the party, Caulfield said: "This party is my way of saying thank you to everyone who has picked up the phone for me along the way."
