**Quick summary**

- Director [Jithu Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jithu_Madhavan) says no sequel to Scene or Aavesham is in the works.
- He told a press meet that Romancham 2 is the only sequel he has announced.
- He called the talk of Scene 2 and Aavesham 2 "assumptions", according to The Hollywood Reporter India.
- Scene has reached Rs 200.60 crore worldwide gross after seven days, The Times of India reports, citing Sacnilk.

Jithu Madhavan has shut down the sequel chatter around his two latest hits. Speaking at a press meet, the Malayalam director said he has announced only Romancham 2, and that Scene 2 and Aavesham 2 exist only in the minds of fans, according to [The Hollywood Reporter India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/amp/story/features/insight/director-jithu-madhavan-says-no-sequels-in-the-works-for-scene-or-aavesham) and [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/entertainment/mollywood/jithu-madhavan-rules-out-scene-2-and-aavesham-2-2004022).

## What he actually said

Madhavan's words, as quoted by The Hollywood Reporter India, were blunt: "Aavesham 2 and Scene 2 are just assumptions. I haven't announced them." He separated what he has confirmed from what audiences have inferred.

On Aavesham, the 2024 action-comedy starring [Fahadh Faasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fahadh_Faasil) as the Bengaluru gangster Ranga, he accepted that a sequel was discussed at one stage. Nothing serious followed, both outlets report, and there are no confirmed plans. He added that several factors must be weighed before any follow-up, and that it would have to be taken up at the right time.

## Why the endings felt open

Deccan Chronicle reports that Madhavan also explained why some of his films end on an open note. He wanted viewers to feel the characters carry on living beyond the final frame. That, he said, was not a hint that a sequel was being kept in reserve.

The same report reads his stance as a preference for returning to a story only when there is something worth telling, not simply because the first film was a hit. That is a notable position when, as Deccan Chronicle points out, audiences now expect a second part for almost every successful film.

## Rumours about his next film

The Hollywood Reporter India adds that Madhavan also responded to reports linking him with [Dulquer Salmaan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dulquer_Salmaan) and [Mamitha Baiju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mamitha_Baiju) for his next project. He called them rumours and said he has not planned anything for the next film yet. We covered the earlier denial in [Jithu Madhavan Denies Next Film Rumours After Scene Hit](https://moduscarry.com/jithu-madhavan-next-film/).

## Scene's first-week numbers

The comments come while Scene, his Tamil crime-comedy thriller, is still pulling crowds. The film stars Suriya, [Nazriya Nazim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazriya_Nazim) and Naslen, and follows an eccentric police officer, DSP Inbaraj, who is made to reopen a long-forgotten case.

According to The Times of India, which cites Sacnilk, Scene earned Rs 6.10 crore in India on its seventh day, Thursday. That took its India net total to Rs 111.55 crore and its India gross to Rs 129.60 crore. Worldwide, the gross stands at Rs 200.60 crore.

The Thursday figure came from two versions: the Tamil release added Rs 4.50 crore from 3,643 shows, and the Telugu version added Rs 1.60 crore from 1,419 shows.

The Times of India also reported a lighter moment from a press conference in Kochi. Asked whether he is arrogant, Naslen replied with a question of his own. Madhavan joked that an arrogant person would never admit it and told the reporter to ask others. Nazriya called her co-star innocent and harmless.
