**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) stars in the new eight-episode series Yaga, which began streaming in the US on AMC+ and in Canada on Crave.
- Cinemablend reviewer Danielle Bruncati says co-star Noah Reid, as private detective Charlie Rapp, is the early standout.
- Williams plays Henry Park, the missing heir to a commercial fishery, who has not yet appeared on screen after two episodes.
- The third episode is due on Friday, 30 October.

The first reviews of Yaga are in, and one of them argues that the actor who came for Hudson Williams may stay for someone else. In a piece published on 26 October, [Cinemablend](https://www.cinemablend.com/streaming-news/hudson-williams-new-show-yaga-noah-reid-stole-show) says Noah Reid's turn as a charming but unsettling detective has pulled focus after two episodes.

## What Yaga is about

According to Cinemablend, the story is set in a small coastal town. Reid's Charlie Rapp arrives to use his private detective skills and help local Sergeant Carson, played by [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo), investigate the sudden disappearance of Henry Park. Henry is the young heir to a commercial fishery, and he is the role Williams plays.

The reviewer notes that [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) appears as university professor Dr. Katherine Hargrove. Cinemablend also says the series is adapted from a play, though the reviewer admits to not knowing the source material.

## Why Noah Reid is getting the attention

Reid is best known for playing Patrick in [Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek), a role Cinemablend ties to one of television's most loved couples. Charlie Rapp is a different proposition. The review describes him as charming and flirtatious, yet mysterious and "a bit of a walking red flag", with every woman in town seemingly drawn to him.

The darker thread is what hooked the writer. After shooting a chicken that Carson accidentally ran over, Charlie begins to hallucinate and is haunted by the dead bird. The first episode ends with someone burning a rooster outside his hotel room door, and the visions continue in episode two. Cinemablend says that storyline gives Reid strong material to work with.

## Where Williams fits in

Williams has not appeared yet. The reviewer says Henry Park is still to be met after two episodes, and expects that to change the balance once he does. The piece also points to the contrast with his earlier work: it calls his Logan Casey endearing and fun, and sets that against the uptight hockey star [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander) in [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry). The writer also says they are excited to see him play a second character in Yaga, though the review does not say who that is.

This is one critic's opinion rather than a verdict on the series. The reviewer concedes their view could flip by the end of the eight episodes.

## What happens next

The next episode of Yaga drops on Friday, 30 October. Cinemablend only lists the US (AMC+) and Canada (Crave) as streaming homes, and its sources do not mention an Australian release, so local viewers should check with their usual services. Heated Rivalry and Schitt's Creek are both available on HBO Max, according to the same piece.
