**Quick summary**

- Papa Salt Coastal Gin, co-founded by [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), has appointed Richard Pilbeam as its first Global Marketing Director, according to Drinks Trade on 10 November 2026.
- The role is newly created and covers brand strategy and marketing in Australia, the UK and new markets.
- Pilbeam has eight years in drinks marketing, including time at Monster Energy and Goodrays.
- CEO Charlie Maas called the role an important investment in the next stage of the business.

The Byron Bay gin brand behind the Margot Robbie Papa Salt Gin story has hired its first global marketing chief. [Drinks Trade](https://www.drinkstrade.com.au/news/margot-robbies-papa-salt-gin-appoints-first-global-marketing-director/) reports that Richard Pilbeam has been named Global Marketing Director at Papa Salt Coastal Gin, a position the company created for the first time.

## Who is Richard Pilbeam?

According to Drinks Trade, Pilbeam brings eight years of drinks marketing experience. He was most recently Director of Marketing for Great Britain and Ireland at Monster Energy, and before that Head of Marketing at the functional beverage brand Goodrays.

He will lead brand strategy and marketing, with responsibility for awareness, consumer engagement and growth in Australia, the UK and new markets. The report does not give a start date.

Pilbeam told the trade title that the real excitement is in what the brand can become globally. That framing matters because the hire is aimed at markets beyond the home base, not only the Australian shelf where Papa Salt already sells.

## Why the new role matters

Charlie Maas, co-founder and CEO, said creating the first global marketing role is an important investment in the next stage of the business, as quoted by Drinks Trade.

The numbers in the report show why the company may feel ready for that step:

- More than 46,000 bottles sold last year, roughly one every 11 minutes.
- Stock in more than 265 Dan Murphy's stores across Australia.
- An initial production run that sold out in under 48 hours.
- First-year sales more than four times the original forecast.

Drinks Trade says the brand has recently entered the top 10 of its category and aims to become Australia's number one super-premium gin.

## The brand behind the name

Papa Salt was co-founded in 2023 by Josey McNamara, Robbie, Tom Ackerley, Charlie Maas and Regan Riskas Maas. The range includes the gin in 50ml, 700ml and 1L glass bottles, plus two ready-to-drink cans in a 250ml slimline format: a Gin Paloma and a Blood Orange Gin Spritz.

## What happens next

The sources do not say when Pilbeam starts or what the first campaigns will be.
