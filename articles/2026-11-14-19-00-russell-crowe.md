**Quick summary**

- Collider argues that [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s performance in Michael Mann's *The Insider* (1999) is still his greatest, 27 years later.
- The role of tobacco whistleblower Jeffrey Wigand earned Crowe his first Oscar nomination, according to Collider.
- A new trailer for the MMA film *Beast*, released by Lionsgate, shows Crowe training a fighter played by [Daniel MacPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_MacPherson).
- Crowe also co-wrote *Beast*, according to JoBlo.

Russell Crowe's work in *The Insider* is back in the conversation this week, with Collider calling the 1999 thriller his best film and arguing it feels sharper in 2026 than it did on release. At the same time, a fresh trailer for *Beast* puts Crowe in a very different corner: a hard-nosed trainer in a fight drama he helped write.

## Why Russell Crowe's The Insider is being rewatched

Collider's piece, published on 14 November 2026, says Crowe became an international name between 1997 and 1999. Roles in *Virtuosity*, *The Quick and the Dead* and *L.A. Confidential* often had him playing slick, violent men. Then came Jeffrey Wigand.

In *The Insider*, directed by Michael Mann, Crowe plays the scientist who blew the whistle on tobacco company Brown & Williamson and took his story to CBS's *60 Minutes*. Collider describes his Wigand as pasty, paranoid and almost aggressively plain, the opposite of the characters that had made his name. The film is based on a 1996 Vanity Fair article, and Collider notes it has been dramatised.

That contrast matters because *Gladiator* came right after it. Collider calls Maximus an almost entirely physical performance, and Wigand the mirror image: all tension in the face, with every twitch visible.

## The cast and the true story behind it

[Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino) plays CBS producer [Lowell Bergman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_Bergman), who fights his own network to get Wigand's story on air. Collider singles out [Michael Gambon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Gambon) as the tobacco chief who threatens Wigand's family in an unnervingly polite scene. [Christopher Plummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Plummer) appears as [Mike Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Wallace), and [Phillip Baker Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Baker_Hall) as producer [Don Hewitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Hewitt).

The film runs about two and a half hours. Collider says it shows the personal cost of financial and physical threats on Wigand, and the internal battle Bergman fought at CBS.

Crowe met the real Wigand three times, Mann said in a later interview quoted by Collider. Both men wanted to avoid what Mann called a "Xerox" of the real person. Mann praised the awkwardness of Wigand, a scientist who joined a tobacco firm for the money, as the thing that makes his stubbornness heroic.

## A box office miss that critics loved

Collider notes that the film arrived to an unremarkable box office but unanimous critical acclaim. It also ties the story to the present, pointing to public anger over corporate and political interests and to leaked reports of arguments inside CBS over what airs on *60 Minutes* today. Those are Collider's observations rather than confirmed facts about the network.

## Crowe in the corner: the Beast trailer

On the other side of his career, Lionsgate has released a new trailer for *Beast*, according to JoBlo. Crowe trains a once-feared MMA champion, played by MacPherson, who is pulled out of retirement. The official synopsis describes a fighter drawn back into the cage when his younger brother is put in danger, with a title-holder waiting for him.

Crowe co-wrote the script with [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio), and Tyler Atkins directs. The cast includes [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth), [Bren Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bren_Foster) and [Amy Shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Shark), along with Mojean Aria and Kelly Gale.

MacPherson told People that *Beast* is an underdog story with heart, and said that working with Crowe on a third film, after *Poker Face* and *Land of Bad*, still teaches him something every day.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did Russell Crowe get an Oscar nomination for The Insider?

Yes. Collider says the film earned him his first Oscar nomination, for playing Jeffrey Wigand.

### Who directed The Insider?

Michael Mann directed it. Collider compares its energy to his crime films such as *Heat*, *Manhunter* and *Public Enemies*.

### What is Beast about?

It follows a retired MMA champion who returns to fight when his younger brother is in danger. Crowe plays the trainer who made him a legend, according to JoBlo.

### Did Russell Crowe write Beast?

He co-wrote it with David Frigerio, and he also stars in it.
