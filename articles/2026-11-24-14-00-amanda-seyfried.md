**Quick summary**

- The Amanda Seyfried SNL host date is 5 December: [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) debuts on [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live).
- Rapper [Don Toliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Toliver) is the musical guest and also makes his SNL debut, according to Rolling Stone.
- [Robert De Niro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_De_Niro) hosts on 12 December and [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet) on 19 December, which closes the show's 2026 run.
- The booking follows strong reviews for Seyfried's work in the documentary series *You Can See Everything*.

Amanda Seyfried will make her *Saturday Night Live* hosting debut on 5 December, with Don Toliver as musical guest, as NBC's sketch show announced the final three episodes of 2026. According to [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/snl-december-hosts-timothee-chalamet-amanda-seyfried-1235663031/), it is the first time on the show for both of them.

## Why this booking stands out

Hosts usually arrive with a film or series to promote. Rolling Stone points out that Seyfried's slot is different: it does not sit in the middle of a traditional press tour. Instead, it lands while she is collecting strong reviews for a surprise turn in *You Can See Everything*, from Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim.

In that project, per Rolling Stone, she appears as herself and also reprises her role as the documentary's subject, Elizabeth Holmes, in several recreations. Playing a real figure she has already portrayed, while also appearing as herself, is the sort of material that suits a live sketch show built on impressions and heightened versions of real people. That is our reading, not something either outlet says.

Fans looking for more on her recent run of work can follow everything we publish on [Amanda Seyfried](https://moduscarry.com/tag/amanda-seyfried/), including our look at [her Tiffany & Co. campaign ahead of the SNL debut](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-tiffany-ambassador/).

## Don Toliver arrives with a busy few weeks

Toliver's episode falls the day after his next album. Rolling Stone reports that his upcoming LP, *Nitrous*, is due on 4 December, following the hit album *Octane*. He was recently nominated for five Grammy awards, according to the same report, including Best New Artist, Best Rap Album for *Octane* and Best Rap Song for "E85".

## The rest of the December lineup

The Hollywood Reporter's [season 52 schedule](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/snl-season-52-hosts-musical-guests-list-1236695508/) lays out how the year ends:

- 5 December: Amanda Seyfried, with Don Toliver.
- 12 December: Robert De Niro, with Slayyyter making her debut as musical guest.
- 19 December: Timothée Chalamet, with Charli xcx.

Rolling Stone says De Niro has hosted three times before, though not since 2010, and that he made a run of cameos in 2018 and 2019 as Robert Mueller. He stars in the *Meet the Parents* sequel *Focker-In-Law*. The Hollywood Reporter lists this as his fourth turn at hosting.

Chalamet's Christmas-week appearance is also his fourth as host, and Rolling Stone says he is promoting *Dune: Part Three*. His last visit was in January 2025, when he was host and musical guest while promoting the Bob Dylan biopic *A Complete Unknown*. Charli xcx, also on her fourth appearance, last featured in November 2024 as host and musical guest.

## How the season has shaped up

The Hollywood Reporter notes the cast is steadier than it was before season 51. Chloe Fineman left in July after seven seasons, and writer Martin Herlihy left after five. The rest of last season's cast returns, with Saidah Belo-Osagie and Grace Reiter joining as featured players.

Earlier in the season, hosts include [Dakota Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_Johnson) and [Shane Gillis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Gillis), each in their third turn, and [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) on Halloween. ## What happens next

Seyfried's episode is on 5 December, with Toliver's *Nitrous* due the day before. De Niro follows on 12 December and Chalamet on 19 December. The Hollywood Reporter says its schedule will be updated as more shows are announced. Neither outlet says what Seyfried will perform in her sketches, and no sketches have been confirmed.
