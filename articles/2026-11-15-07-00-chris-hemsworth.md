**Quick summary**

- Four masked people reportedly entered [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s home at Broken Head, near Byron Bay, early on 11 November.
- Three vehicles were stolen: a BYD, a Ram truck and a [Ford Raptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Raptor) pickup.
- NSW Police say no one was injured, and all three vehicles have since been recovered.
- Police are examining the vehicles for forensic evidence and looking for the suspects.

Four masked intruders broke into Chris Hemsworth's home in northern New South Wales in the early hours of 11 November and drove away three vehicles, according to a report by Korea's [Sportschosun](https://www.sportschosun.com/en/celebrity/2026-11-14/202611140100096310009009). The paper cites People, ABC News and Australia's 7NEWS. NSW Police said no one was hurt.

## What reportedly happened at the Hemsworth home

The house is at Broken Head, near Byron Bay. Sportschosun, citing 7NEWS, says [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky), Hemsworth's wife, woke around 2 am to the dogs barking. She saw nothing unusual and went back to sleep.

The next morning she checked the home's CCTV. According to the report, it showed four men with their faces covered entering the property, and two of them appeared to be carrying weapons. They are said to have climbed a security fence and gone straight to the garage.

Keys were reportedly still in the vehicles parked there. The intruders took a BYD, a Ram truck and a Ford Raptor.

The report does not say whether Hemsworth himself was home. It describes only Pataky as being inside.

## What police have said

NSW Police told media that four unidentified people are believed to have entered the property and stolen three vehicles. The force added that, fortunately, no one was injured, as quoted by Sportschosun.

The recovery came quickly. Two of the vehicles were found on the same day, in Coraki and Tabulam, both inland from the coast. The third turned up the next day in Lennox Head, a short drive from Byron Bay. According to the report, police now hold all three and are carrying out forensic examinations while they track down those responsible.

No arrests had been reported in the source material at the time of writing.

## The property and the family

The home has drawn attention partly because of its reported value. Sportschosun puts it at about A$50 million and says locals call it "Fortress Hemsworth". That figure is a media estimate and has not been confirmed by the family. The report also describes a large clifftop-style property overlooking the sea, with six bedrooms, an infinity pool, a gym and a tennis court.

Hemsworth, 43, married Pataky in 2010. They have a daughter and twin sons. The report says the family left Los Angeles to settle in Australia for a quieter life, and that Hemsworth has said on a podcast that the move was the best decision for his family.
