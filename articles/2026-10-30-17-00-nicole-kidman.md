**Quick summary**

- Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, 18, fronts the November 2026 issue of Vogue Germany, shot in Louis Vuitton, with the issue on newsstands from October 24.
- She is the eldest daughter of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban) and made her runway debut for Miu Miu in October 2024.
- In the cover interview she addressed nepotism directly and named film directing as her long-term goal.
- She is in her final year of high school in Nashville, according to the report.

Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, the 18-year-old daughter of Nicole Kidman and Keith Urban, is the cover star of Vogue Germany's November 2026 issue. CSP Times, citing Just Jared, reports that she was photographed in Louis Vuitton and that the magazine reached newsstands on October 24.

## What she told Vogue Germany

The interview does not dodge the obvious question. "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have," she told the magazine, according to [CSP Times](https://www.csptimes.com/post/what-to-know-about-nicole-kidman-s-model-daughter-sunday-rose-kidman-urban-now-a-vogue-germany-co). She said she tries to repay that by giving every job her best, staying positive when travel has worn her out, and treating everyone she works with respectfully.

She also said her interest in fashion began at 14, and that her mother made her wait two years before taking work. Her mother's influence shows up in smaller ways too. "Half my wardrobe now consists of her things," she said, laughing that Kidman has always been generous with her clothes.

## From Miu Miu to Dior in two years

Sunday Rose was born in Nashville in July 2008, the first child of Kidman and Urban, who married in 2006. Her runway debut came at Miu Miu's Spring/Summer 2025 show in Paris in October 2024, and the label then cast her in its campaign for the season, ABC News reported, as relayed by CSP Times.

Since then the list has grown to include Dior, Calvin Klein and Omega. Per the same report, she walked in Jonathan Anderson's first Dior show on October 1, 2025. Her first magazine cover was Nylon in August 2025, followed by Elle Australia in March 2026, and she attended this year's Met Gala with her mother, who co-chaired it.

Vogue Germany is the third cover in 15 months. The timeline is worth noting because it ran alongside her parents' public separation: CSP Times says the Dior show fell the day after Kidman filed for divorce.

## Two rules and a different ambition

Her mother set two conditions, Sunday Rose told Nylon: no fashion work before she turned 16, and school always comes first. She admitted she resented the second rule at first. It "keeps me in a good mindset," she said, as reported by Today. She says the school routine keeps her grounded between shows.

Modelling may not be the destination. Her stated ambition is to direct. As a child on her mother's sets she watched the monitors rather than the cameras. "I was always staring at the preview monitor because I was so captivated by the work," she told Vogue Germany, naming Osgood Perkins and Emerald Fennell among her favourite directors. She has already had brief cameos in her mother's series *Big Little Lies* and *The Undoing*, and told Nylon she hopes to study in a big city such as New York.

Kidman has backed the modelling work from a distance. This spring she described it as her daughter's "own, whole world", according to Page Six. The best advice she has passed on, by Sunday Rose's account, is simply: "Always be on time."

## A wider family picture

The cover arrives as another strand of the Kidman family drew attention this week. E! News looked back at what Kidman and her former husband [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise) have said about their adult children, Isabella (Bella) and Connor, after Connor was seen at Game 4 of the 2026 World Series on October 27.

Sunday Rose's two older half-siblings were adopted during Kidman's marriage to Cruise, which ended in 2001. Kidman has long kept those relationships private. "I have to protect all those relationships," she told Who in 2018, as quoted by [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437638/what-tom-cruise-nicole-kidman-have-shared-about-kids-bella-connor). Sunday Rose's younger sister Faith, 15, joined their mother at Chanel's Paris Fashion Week show this month, according to CSP Times.
