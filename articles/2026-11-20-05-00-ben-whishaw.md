**Quick summary**

- The Young Vic released first-look production photos of *Eurotrash* on 19 November 2026.
- [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) plays Christian and [Kathryn Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hunter) plays his Mother.
- The play adapts Christian Kracht's novel and runs in the Main House until 9 January 2027.
- Walter Meierjohann directs, using an adaptation by [Colin Teevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Teevan).

The Young Vic has shared the first images from *Eurotrash*, the stage adaptation starring Ben Whishaw and Kathryn Hunter, according to [WhatsOnStage](https://www.whatsonstage.com/news/ben-whishaw-and-kathryn-hunter-in-eurotrash-stage-adaptation-first-look-photos-released_1737738/). The show is already playing in London and runs until 9 January 2027.

## What the Eurotrash photos show

[BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/westend/article/Photos-Kathryn-Hunter-Ben-Whishaw-and-More-in-EUROTRASH-at-the-Young-Vic-20261119) published a large gallery by photographer Ellie Kurttz. Most frames pair Whishaw as Christian with Hunter as his Mother, and several add Glyn Pritchard as The Janitor and Deanna H. Choi as The Sound Engineer.

The cast list also includes understudies Annabel Arden and Chris Jared. Choi is credited on the production for composition and co-sound design as well as appearing on stage, which is an unusual double duty for a cast member.

## The story behind Ben Whishaw's Eurotrash role

The play follows a blocked novelist who is called to Zurich by his elderly mother. She is barely holding herself together with vodka, pills and Ferragamo sweaters, according to the Young Vic's description as reported by BroadwayWorld.

Her fortune comes from arms investments and dairy products. The pair decide to give it away, stuff the cash into a plastic bag and set off on a chaotic road trip through the snowy mountains and valleys of Switzerland. BroadwayWorld describes the result as a darkly comic travelogue and a search for redemption and connection, as mother and son try to come to terms with their own history and Europe's.

The source is Christian Kracht's international best-seller, adapted for the stage by Colin Teevan. Walter Meierjohann directs.

## Who is behind the production

The design team is led by Lizzie Clachan, who handles set and costume. Richard Howell designs the lighting, and Jozef Houben is the movement director. Charlie Smith co-designs the sound with Choi, and Kim Kasim looks after wigs, hair and make-up.

WhatsOnStage notes that Romana Meyrat is associate director, Lydia McKinley is the Jerwood assistant director, and the production is supported by the Charlotte Aitken Trust.
