**Quick summary**

- [Adam Croasdell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Croasdell) says he expected [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) to be chosen as the next James Bond.
- Amazon MGM Studios instead named [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) on Monday 16 November 2026.
- Croasdell played Pattinson's on-screen father in The Odyssey and still backs the final choice.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct the next Bond film.

An actor who played Robert Pattinson's father on screen says Callum Turner looked like the favourite for 007 until Pattinson arrived late in the race. Adam Croasdell shared the view with BANG Showbiz, in an interview published by [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/celebrity/odysseys-adam-croasdell-shocked-screen-son-robert-pattinson-named-next-james-bond-1454018.html) this week, after Amazon MGM Studios announced its new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).

## Why Croasdell tipped Callum Turner

Croasdell appeared in Christopher Nolan's adaptation of The Odyssey as the father of Pattinson's character, Antinous. He told BANG Showbiz that the casting seemed to him to run in about three phases.

In the first, he said, big film stars were put into contention, and he rated [Aaron Taylor-Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Taylor-Johnson) as a strong contender because he is physically imposing. As the process went on, he thought Turner was the best fit.

His reasoning was Ian Fleming's original description of the character. "I thought he was more like the description of Fleming's Bond. Tall, dark," Croasdell said, according to the interview. He then said he thought it might go Turner's way, before [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) and Pattinson came in "right at the end".

For readers following Turner's career, it adds to the picture of how close he was reported to be. Turner has been named in earlier coverage on this site, including [Callum Turner Misses Out as Pattinson Is Named Bond](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-bond-pattinson/), and you can find everything we have published on him under the [Callum Turner tag](https://moduscarry.com/tag/callum-turner/).

## Who was reported to be in the running

According to Female First, the casting process reportedly included Turner, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Hoult and Josh O'Connor, who were all said to have taken part in screen tests. The outlet describes Pattinson as a late contender whose name surprised fans.

That list comes from reports, not from a studio statement. Amazon MGM Studios has not, in the source we read, confirmed who tested or in what order.

## Why Croasdell still backs Pattinson

Surprise did not turn into doubt. Croasdell said that of the two finalists he mentioned, both were "phenomenal actors", but that Pattinson was "the right way to go".

He described Pattinson as warm and kind in person, and as an actor who can bring a "mechanical edge" and a "crystalline edge" to a role. That quality, he suggested, could suit a darker or rebooted tone, though he added that nobody yet knows what tone the new films will take. He also pointed to the menace Pattinson can bring alongside the action and the debonair side of the character.

Croasdell has a small Bond history of his own. He performed motion capture for [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig)'s Bond in the 2011 video game 007: Blood Stone.

## What the studio and Pattinson said

In a statement quoted by Female First, Pattinson called the role an "honour of a lifetime" and said it was "a dream come true". He thanked Villeneuve and the Amazon MGM Studios team for their belief in him.

Villeneuve thanked the actors who auditioned and said "there can only be one 007". He added that Pattinson has "the timeless soul of 007" and brings a spirit that is "completely new". Villeneuve directed Pattinson in Dune: Part Three, according to the report.

Pattinson becomes the seventh actor to officially play Bond in the film series. He follows [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery), [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore), [George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby), [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton), [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and Craig, who last played the spy in 2021's No Time to Die.

## What happens next

The next step is production on Villeneuve's Bond film, which Pattinson will lead. No start date, release date or plot details appear in the source we read, so none are given here.
