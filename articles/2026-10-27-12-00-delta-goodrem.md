Delta Goodrem has spoken about what she thinks caused the rib injury that kept her out of Strictly Come Dancing last week. According to [SoaplandTV](https://soaplandtv.co.uk/article/delta-goodrem-strictly-injury-never-encountered-first-10), the Australian singer believes the repeated lifts in her routines with partner Kai Widdrington are the likeliest explanation, and says she has no single moment to blame.

## What Goodrem said about the injury

Goodrem described the problem as "a new injury that I've never encountered before", per SoaplandTV. She did not name one incident. Instead she pointed to the cumulative effect of lifts across rehearsals and live shows.

That fits with what was known earlier. [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/10/19/delta-goodrem-strictly-come-dancing-injury-bbc-2026/) reported that doctors advised her to rest and recover, but its report did not say how the injury happened, so the lifts explanation currently rests on the SoaplandTV account.

## The week out and the all clear

Goodrem and Widdrington missed Icons Week, the same stretch in which, according to SoaplandTV, their salsa had put them top of the leaderboard. PinkNews reported that she was granted a bye, which let the pair advance automatically to the next live show. She had topped the leaderboard when the first two weeks were combined and again in week three, and averaged 30.7 per dance across her first three routines.

On 19 October the BBC said on Instagram that Goodrem had "been given the all clear to dance again this weekend", PinkNews reported.

## A rumba and the first 10

Goodrem returned on 24 October with a rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back To Me Now". SoaplandTV reports it scored 34 out of 40 and left her second on the leaderboard. Craig Revel Horwood gave a 7, Motsi Mabuse a 9, Shirley Ballas an 8 and Anton Du Beke a 10, the first 10 of the 2026 series. Du Beke called it his favourite dance of the series so far.

For context, Tabby Stoecker and Mark Karmalita topped the board that night with 37 points, according to SoaplandTV, and Goodrem's 34 compares with the 30.7 average she held before the injury. She and Widdrington advanced after the results show on 25 October.

## What happens next

The Halloween routines air on Saturday 31 October at 6.30pm on BBC One and BBC iPlayer, SoaplandTV reports. Whether any lifts are scaled back has not been stated in the sources we reviewed.
