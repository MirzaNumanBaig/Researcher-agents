**Quick summary**

- Nicole Kidman's 2001 horror film The Others reached the Top 10 on AMC+ in the United States on 2 November 2026, according to FlixPatrol data cited by Comic Book Resources.
- In the United Kingdom, where it streams on Canal+, the film topped the chart on the same day.
- The Others earned $210 million worldwide on a $17 million budget and has an 85% Certified Fresh score on Rotten Tomatoes.
- Kidman's recent sequel Practical Magic 2 grossed $106 million worldwide against a $75 million budget.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) has a new streaming hit, and it is 25 years old. Her Gothic horror film [The Others](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Others_(2001_film)) entered AMC+'s US Top 10 at number ten on 2 November 2026 and went to number one in the UK on the same day, according to [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) (CBR), which cites FlixPatrol figures.

The timing is the interesting part. The climb comes just after Halloween, when horror viewing usually fades, and it lands while Kidman's newest spooky release has had a far harder time.

## How is The Others performing on streaming?

The Others reached tenth place on AMC+'s overall US chart on 2 November, per CBR. In the UK, where the film is available on Canal+, it took the top spot on that date.

CBR reports that the film's Halloween-season audience is still talking about it. It quotes a Rotten Tomatoes reviewer who said the film may have been the favourite of the season because its twist is so carefully built, rather than because it was the scariest. Another viewer wrote that they screamed out loud a few times.

That is a long way from a new release. The film first reached cinemas in 2001, and the question is why a quarter-century-old ghost story still pulls viewers in. The numbers from its original run offer a clue.

## What was The Others about, and how did it do originally?

Kidman plays Grace Stewart, a devoutly religious mother who moves her family to the English coast during World War II. Her children, Anne (Alakina Mann) and Nicholas ([James Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bentley_(actor))), are photosensitive, so sunlight is dangerous to them. Grace is waiting for news of her missing husband when new servants arrive and the children begin to say they see ghosts.

The film made $210 million worldwide on a budget of about $17 million, CBR reports, roughly twelve times its cost. Kidman earned a Golden Globe nomination for [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actress) in a drama for the role.

[Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) gives the film an 85% Certified Fresh score from more than 175 critic reviews, and a 77% audience score from more than 250,000 ratings. The site's critics' consensus, quoted by CBR, says the film shows a thriller does not need expensive effects to be creepy.

That low-effects approach is part of why the film dates well. It also makes the contrast with Kidman's latest release sharper.

## How did Practical Magic 2 compare?

Kidman's recent Halloween-season release has not matched that run. According to CBR, [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic) sequel Practical Magic 2, which reunites Kidman with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock), opened in September to mostly middling reviews and a weak box office.

It grossed $106 million worldwide against a $75 million production budget. That is a gross of about 1.4 times its budget, against roughly twelve times for The Others. Critics gave the film a 36% score on Rotten Tomatoes, with the plot named as the main complaint.

Fans were kinder. The film holds a 90% Popcornmeter from more than 2,500 verified audience reviews, and the audience consensus describes it as a nostalgic celebration of family and tradition.
