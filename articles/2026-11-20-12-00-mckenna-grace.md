**Quick summary**

- Mckenna Grace wore a half-up braided bun with two dangling braids to the New York premiere of *Sunrise on the Reaping*, according to Teen Vogue.
- Her stylist, Glen Coco, says he likes hiding braids so fans can go looking for them.
- Grace plays District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner in the Hunger Games prequel.
- In a virtual press conference, she called slapping Glenn Close in a scene "terrifying".

At the New York premiere of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, the dress and the Mockingjay wings drew the attention. The hair carried a smaller secret. [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) wore a half-up style with tiny braids tucked in, and her stylist says that was the point, according to [Teen Vogue](https://www.teenvogue.com/story/mckenna-grace-hid-braids-in-hair-for-hunger-games-fans-to-find).

## The hairstyle behind the Mockingjay gown

Grace arrived in a navy strapless Pamela Rolland gown with silver eye makeup and full Mockingjay wings, a tribute to the earlier films and to [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen). Teen Vogue reports that, because the outfit was already a statement, her hair was kept softer.

Stylist Glen Coco, who has worked with her throughout the press tour, described the look as a half-up braided bun with two dangling braids beneath it, then bouncy, shiny lengths. He told the magazine it echoed the style she wore in Paris, adjusted so it would not compete with the gown.

Coco used L'Oréal products throughout, from a glossing shampoo, conditioner and lamination mask to an oil on the mid-lengths and a strong-hold hairspray to set the finish, as listed by Teen Vogue.

## Braids as a running Easter egg

The braids are not a one-off. Teen Vogue says small braids have appeared in many of Grace's looks on this tour, similar to the plaits worn across the films. Grace and Coco joked about how many they have done, and she revealed she keeps a Pinterest board just for braids.

"I like hiding the braids and having people find them," Coco said. "The fans will be looking for it."

The same thinking runs through her wardrobe. Teen Vogue points to a Katniss-style side braid worn around New York City, a teal mini that paid tribute to Rue from the first film, and a dress that resembled the Games arena. We covered more of those outfits in our [guide to her press tour looks](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-looks-3/) and in the story of how [the Mockingjay wings were built](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-mockingjay-wings-designer/).

"It's been so special with the Hunger Games fan base," Grace told Teen Vogue. "I've met so many wonderful people along the way."

## Playing a character whose fate is known

Away from the carpet, Grace spoke about the acting side in a virtual press conference with co-star Joseph Zada, reported by [Mama's Geeky](https://mamasgeeky.com/2026/11/mckenna-grace-joseph-zada-hunger-games.html). Asked how she approaches a role whose ending is already written, she said she does not play the outcome. She plays a character trying to live who does not know what happens next. She added that the cast tried hard to make audiences wonder whether another twist might be possible, even for viewers who have read the book.

Zada, who plays a young Haymitch Abernathy, described the long, daunting work of holding Woody Harrelson's accent and staying in it every day on set, including through emotional scenes.

## The Glenn Close slap

Grace was most animated about a scene in which she slaps Glenn Close. She called it "terrifying" and said she was full of nerves that day, imagining Close might want to go method. She described the scene as the moment audiences decide whether they believe her as Maysilee, so she committed to it. She called the actor a legend and said the scene was wild and fun to shoot.

The press tour continues as the film nears release, and the sources do not give further appearances. Expect more franchise nods in her outfits, since both Grace and her stylist say the fans are paying close attention.

She plays Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute, in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, according to Teen Vogue.
