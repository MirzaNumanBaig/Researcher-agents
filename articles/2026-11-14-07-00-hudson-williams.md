Hudson Williams and Carrie-Anne Moss have explained how they prepared for the most talked-about scene in *Yaga*, the folklore thriller in which Williams plays two roles. In interviews published on 13 November, Moss said the pair rehearsed it repeatedly before cameras rolled, according to [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/yaga-carrie-anne-moss-hudson-williams-erotic-scene-breakdown-12249728).

## How the pair prepared

Williams and Moss share only one scene in the series, which appears in episode five, "The Horns". Moss plays Kathryn Hargrove, a university professor. Williams plays Henry Park, the missing son of a wealthy family, who records his own encounters.

"We did it lots of different ways. Lots," Moss told Entertainment Weekly. The rehearsals took place in a hotel screening room with directors [David Frazee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frazee) and [Rachel Talalay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Talalay). Moss said the importance of the scene to showrunner and writer [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) and the directors was obvious in the room.

She also described the creative puzzle of how to begin. There were "so many ways to start", she said, including whether Kathryn should be in pursuit from the first moment, and she wanted the scene to have an arc. [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/13/entertainment/hudson-williams-25-and-carrie-anne-moss-59-share-insight-into-initimate-scene/) reported the same comments and noted the actors' ages, 25 for Williams and 59 for Moss.

## Where Williams found Henry

Williams told Entertainment Weekly that the scene is the first time viewers hear Henry in extended dialogue, and the first real look at the power balance between the two characters. "You think it's one way," he said, praising Moss for how well she played that shift.

For Henry's mindset, Williams pointed to the 1999 film *The Talented Mr. Ripley*, directed by Anthony Minghella. He was drawn to the gap between posturing as wealthy and actually being wealthy, and said Henry believes he holds the upper hand even as the situation moves against him. Moss added that Kathryn "very much enjoys watching that".

Moss also said she started reading Sandler's original *Yaga* play, which became the series, before putting it down. Henry on the page, she said, is more timid and earnest than the version Williams plays on screen.

## A double role fans keep talking about

The attention is not limited to episode five. [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/11/13/hudson-williams-yaga-threesome/) reported that a clip from episode four, "Dance with the Devil", went viral on X because Williams plays both Henry and his doppelgänger roommate, Logan Casey, in the same flashback. In a clip released by HBO Max, he joked that two people in the scene are him, so "the narcissist in me" was happy with the day's work.

According to PinkNews, episode two also drew attention for a scene in which Williams wore a horned mask. The outlet noted that the double-casting twist was teased in September 2026, and that UK viewers can watch on [Sky Atlantic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Atlantic) and NOW.

Show Snob's recap of episode five says Henry has a far bigger presence this week than in earlier instalments, after fans spent weeks waiting for his arrival. Its reviewer describes the show as now halfway through its run, with investigators still working out who posted the video of Logan's death from Henry's account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Henry Park in Yaga?
Hudson Williams plays Henry Park, the missing heir of a wealthy family. He also plays Henry's lookalike roommate and podcast producer, Logan Casey.

### What is Yaga about?
Created by Kat Sandler, *Yaga* is an eight-part series that reimagines the Baba Yaga legend from Slavic folklore as a modern mystery thriller.
