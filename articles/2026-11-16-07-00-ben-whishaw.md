**Quick summary**

- The second series of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) is now streaming on Netflix, with [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) back as the former contract killer Sam.
- It runs for six episodes, which one reviewer says are easy to watch in a single sitting.
- TodoAlicante rates it sharper and better written than the first series.
- The first series reportedly passed 63 million views when it launched.

Ben Whishaw Black Doves fans have a warm early review to read: season 2 of the Netflix spy thriller, again led by Ben Whishaw and [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley), runs six episodes. TodoAlicante, a Spanish outlet with an English-language section, says it is "sharper and better written" than the first series and calls it one of the platform's most entertaining spy dramas.

## What Black Doves season 2 is about

Whishaw plays Sam and Knightley plays Helen, partners the review describes as bound to each other to the death. According to [TodoAlicante](https://www.todoalicante.es/english/black-doves-season-2-20261114111101-nt.html), Helen is now married to the British Prime Minister. That makes her second life as an undercover operative, a "black dove", harder to hide. She moves between the school run at 10 Downing Street and gunfights.

Sam is trying to leave his old trade as a contract killer behind. The review says circumstances will not let him. A run of violent deaths points to a deliberate vendetta against Helen's secretive handlers, and the two are pulled into a long-running conspiracy.

## The cast around Whishaw

The review singles out the supporting players. [Ella Lily Hyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Lily_Hyland) and [Gabrielle Creevy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabrielle_Creevy) play a pair of hitwomen who trade banter about their relationships mid-shootout. TodoAlicante compares them with the duo in [Baby Assassins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baby_Assassins).

[Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire), known for Happy Valley, plays the missing spymaster whose disappearance Helen investigates. [Ambika Mod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambika_Mod), from One Day, joins as Laila, a new assassin the reviewer says would give John Wick a run for his money.

## Tone, writing and real-life roots

The review says the series leans further into comedy than before while keeping its bloody action. It also says Sam and Helen's willingness to risk everything for each other makes the emotional side stronger than the spy plotting.

Joe Barton, who wrote Giri/Haji, remains head writer. TodoAlicante notes the show's loose starting point: the British scandal over undercover police officers who took long-term false identities to infiltrate activist groups. Reports said such operations dated back as far as 1968, and some officers formed relationships and had children while undercover. The drama moves well beyond those events, the review adds.

The reviewer places Black Doves within a run of hitman thrillers, alongside [Kill Jackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Jackie), which stars [Catherine Zeta-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Zeta-Jones). The piece also mentions the return of MobLand, with [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), as another recent series built on its characters.

This is one critic's view, and the review does not give a release date or viewing figures for season 2. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Sam in Black Doves?
Ben Whishaw plays Sam, a former contract killer trying to leave his past behind. Keira Knightley plays his partner Helen.

### How many episodes does Black Doves season 2 have?
The second series has six episodes, according to TodoAlicante.

### Is Black Doves based on a true story?
Not directly. The review says the series draws loosely on a British scandal over undercover police officers and then moves well beyond it.
