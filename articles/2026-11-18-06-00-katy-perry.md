**Quick summary**

- Katy Perry's father, pastor Keith Hudson, was asked about Russell Brand's conviction on Sunday, 15 November, outside a church in Montecito, California.
- He did not criticise his former son-in-law and said, "You're better off talking about the Lord; it does more for you."
- Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison, according to Yahoo Lifestyle Canada.
- Perry has not commented publicly on the verdict.

[Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry)'s father has been asked about the conviction of her former husband, and he chose to steer the conversation to faith. Keith Hudson was approached on Sunday, 15 November, as he headed to a service in Montecito, California, according to [Yahoo Lifestyle Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/youre-better-off-talking-lord-151833333.html).

## What Keith Hudson said

Asked about [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand), Hudson did not condemn or defend him. His reply, as reported, was: "You're better off talking about the Lord; it does more for you."

The outlet noted that the remark carried extra weight because Hudson is a pastor and Brand has publicly embraced Christianity. Brand did so in early 2024, after earlier exploring Eastern spirituality, New Age beliefs and recovery practices. That shift came a few months after allegations against him became public in September 2023, Yahoo reported.

One social media user described the comment as a "jab" at Brand's conversion. That is one person's reading. Hudson himself offered no explanation beyond the single line.

## The conviction behind the question

Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months, according to Yahoo's report. It said a trial ran from 12 October to 3 November, and a jury of 12 considered allegations involving incidents between 1999 and 2009. He was convicted on one count of forced intimacy and three counts of other misconduct involving women, and has begun his sentence at HMP Littlehey.

Yahoo reported that Brand maintained the encounters were consensual. It also quoted Justice Joel Bennathan, who said Brand had shown a "casual arrogant disregard for any woman's right to say no".

Perry herself has remained silent as the sentence begins. For earlier coverage of that silence, see our report on [Katy Perry staying quiet as Russell Brand was jailed](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-russell-brand-docuseries/).

## How Perry and Brand's marriage unfolded

The two met in June 2009, when Perry made a cameo in the comedy *Get Him to the Greek*, which starred Brand. They reconnected at the MTV Video Music Awards later that year. They married in a Hindu ceremony in Rajasthan, India, in 2010, and the marriage lasted a little over a year. Brand filed for divorce in December 2011, citing irreconcilable differences.

Perry has spoken about the split before. In a 2013 Vogue interview, she called Brand "very controlling" and said he did not like "the atmosphere of me being the boss on tour", as quoted by Yahoo. She also said she had learned "the real truth" about the relationship but kept it private.

## Perry's reported project and current relationship

Yahoo cited the Daily Mail as saying that unnamed insiders describe a planned documentary that would go deeper than her earlier film, *Katy Perry: Part of Me*. According to those sources, Brand would feature but not be the centrepiece. This is a claim from anonymous sources and has not been confirmed by Perry or her team.
