**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) are midway through The Best of Steve Martin and Martin Short Tour, according to the New York Post.
- Eight shows remain on the 2026-27 calendar, running from December 2026 to April 2027 across North America.
- The Post found tickets from US$90 to US$231 including fees on resale site Vivid Seats.
- Season 6 of Only Murders in the Building starts on Hulu on 8 December, with the pair heading to London.

Martin Short and Steve Martin have eight shows left on their North American live tour, and fans can still buy seats for every one of them. The New York Post reported on 18 November that the run continues from December 2026 through April 2027, just as the pair's TV series returns for a sixth season.

## Where the tour goes next

The two comedians are playing what the Post describes as a mix of resorts, performing arts centres, palaces, domes and auditoriums. The paper says tickets are available for all eight remaining dates, and it publishes a full calendar with venues and links.

On price, the Post's reporter found the cheapest seat for any single show at US$90 including fees, for the Hollywood, Florida date on 15 January 2027. Other nights started between US$96 and US$231, again with fees included. Those are resale-market figures from one site on one day, so they may move.

## What the show is like

The format is not a standard stand-up set. Reviewing a March 2020 London performance, The Guardian described it as a variety show that weaves together stand-up and conversation, music, song and "affectionate insults". The review, quoted by the Post, also said Martin's introverted deadpan finds a foil in Short's extrovert, attention-seeking style.

The duo have long worked together on screen too, appearing in Father of the Bride and [Three Amigos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Amigos), as the Post notes. Their live act leans on that history, with banter and banjo between the jokes.

## Only Murders Season 6 arrives on 8 December

The tour sits alongside the return of [Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building). A trailer for Season 6 appeared online on 17 November. It shows Martin's Charles Haden-Savage and Short's Oliver Putnam travelling to London to find the killer of podcaster Cinda Canning, played by [Tina Fey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tina_Fey).

In the trailer, Martin's character says: "Where do we even start here? The killer has home court advantage." The official synopsis, as reprinted by the Post, says the trio face their hardest case yet, set in the home of the cosy murder mystery and full of English eccentrics.

The guest list is large. Returning names include Fey, [Meryl Streep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meryl_Streep) and [Da'Vine Joy Randolph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Da'Vine_Joy_Randolph). British newcomers are [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman), [David Tennant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Tennant), [Nicola Coughlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Coughlan), [Martin Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Freeman) and [Jodie Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Whittaker).

## What happens next

Season 6 opens on 8 December with two episodes on Hulu. Episodes 3 to 10 follow weekly on Tuesdays, starting with Episode 3 on 15 December, the Post says. The tour continues through April 2027.
