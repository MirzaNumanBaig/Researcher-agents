**Quick summary**

- Billy Evans, the partner of Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, has given his first print interview about *You Can See Everything*, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- He disputes a scene showing him holding up production over a share of profits, saying the money was agreed two days before filming began.
- He also answers Amanda Seyfried, who told Rolling Stone her opinion of him "only got worse" after their filmed dinner.
- The film, directed by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and Lance Oppenheim, was released on 6 September 2026.

Billy Evans has hit back at the people who shaped how audiences see him in *You Can See Everything*. In a Q&A published on 17 November, [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/billy-evans-amanda-seyfried-nathan-fielder-see-everything-1236756656/) reports, he rejected parts of the film's portrayal and replied directly to [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried), who plays Holmes in the film. It is the latest turn in a story we have followed in [our Nathan Fielder coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/nathan-fielder/).

## Why Evans took part

Evans, 34, is the partner of Elizabeth Holmes, 42, who is serving an 11-year sentence for defrauding investors. He told the magazine the aim of taking part was to change how the world sees her. Filming was first discussed in February 2023, he said, when the couple's young children were already being uninvited from playdates.

He said the film was meant to let viewers "just see her" and decide for themselves, and that this was why they chose Fielder, whom he called a genius filmmaker. The magazine notes that since the release, strangers have been picking over details of the couple's private life, down to how they make scrambled eggs.

## The scenes he disputes

Evans says he did not enjoy watching himself. He told the magazine it is hard to see small moments, such as helping his wife cook, recast as a comment on controlling behaviour. He said "control" is not a word he associates with their relationship.

On the egg scene, he said Holmes did not cook during her years running Theranos, while he grew up working in kitchens and cooked for the family throughout her pregnancy and the trial.

The bigger dispute is over money. The film shows Evans holding up production over a cut of the profits. He told the magazine the financial terms were settled two days before filming began and that appearance releases were signed soon after.

## Reply to Amanda Seyfried

Seyfried, 40, told Rolling Stone she became "this weird Billy Evans detective" because of the strong negative feelings she picked up from his footage, and that her view of him worsened after their filmed dinner. The Hollywood Reporter says Evans responds to this in the interview, along with the much-discussed bag of turkey jerky and what Holmes thought of the shower scene.

Before this, Evans had spoken on X on 2 November and given an interview to CBS Sunday Morning. There he said he loved the film, had not consented to his nudity being shown and had no current plans to sue studio A24. We covered that earlier in [Billy Evans on the shower scene consent](https://moduscarry.com/billy-evans-consent-nathan-fielder/).

## How the film has fared

According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything), it has earned about US$86.5 million against a budget of roughly US$7 million. It holds 96% on Rotten Tomatoes from 226 reviews and 97/100 on Metacritic from 42 critics. Audiences surveyed by PostTrak gave it a 98% positive score. Wikipedia lists the cast as Elizabeth Holmes, Fielder and Christopher Abbott.

## What happens next

Evans told CBS he does not plan to sue A24 for now. Beyond that, the article does not describe any further legal steps. The Hollywood Reporter notes his latest business venture, Haemanthus, is also drawing scrutiny. For earlier reaction to the film, see [Nathan Fielder and Elizabeth Holmes: Billy Evans speaks out](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-billy-evans/).
