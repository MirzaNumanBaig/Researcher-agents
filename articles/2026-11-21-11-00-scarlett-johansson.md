**Quick summary**

- Glam has ranked [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson)'s best hairstyles of 2026, and the top spot goes to beachy waves.
- She wore that look to the "Paper Tiger" red carpet at the New York Film Festival in September.
- The ranking also covers two London Film Festival appearances in October and a November TV spot.
- The new look is a blunt bob, which Johansson debuted this year while promoting "Paper Tiger" and "Ray Gunn".

Scarlett Johansson's 2026 has been defined by a haircut as much as by her films. According to a ranking published by Glam on 20 November, the actor's best look of the year was the tousled, beachy lob she wore to the "Paper Tiger" premiere at the 64th New York Film Festival in September.

## The new blunt bob

Glam says Johansson debuted a snappy bob with blunt ends this year, and that she has styled it several ways while promoting her two latest films, "Paper Tiger" and "Ray Gunn". The outlet describes the cut as one that suits her features and works across very different looks.

The publication's criteria were simple. It judged how well each style suited the actor and the cut, and whether it would appeal to a stylish, modern woman. Looks that felt dated or overly trendy ranked lower, while those that felt modern and effortless, even with a retro reference, placed higher.

## The ranking, from fourth to first

At number four, Glam placed the blunt waves Johansson wore to the "Ray Gunn" gala at the 70th BFI London Film Festival in October. She voices Venus Nova in the animated film, which [Ray Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn) fans can expect in December. The outlet called the clavicle-length lob with a middle part and easy barrel waves a low-maintenance cut, though not a groundbreaking one.

Third went to a French bob. Johansson wore it in bright blond on a November episode of "Today with Jenna & Sheinelle". Glam liked the volume and face-framing waves but felt the style was slightly too trendy, and suggested a darker shade might have scored higher.

Second place belongs to her "Paper Tiger" appearance at the same London festival. Glam says the sleek blond lob with dramatically curled ends and a deep side part gave her an Old Hollywood air, helped by a statement necklace with a single centre stone.

## Why the beach waves won

Top spot went to the September New York look. Glam describes a piece-y, undone version of the blunt lob, with disconnected strands that added structure without losing movement. Its verdict was that the style was polished but easy, modern without chasing trends.

The outlet also took a light-hearted swipe at the past, recalling the crimped hair Johansson wore to the 2004 Tony Awards, which it calls a notable misstep. The comparison is Glam's opinion, and a ranking like this is subjective by nature.
