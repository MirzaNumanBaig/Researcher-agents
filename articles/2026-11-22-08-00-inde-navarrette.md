**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) has been named as a possible Oscar contender for her role as Nikki in the horror film Obsession.
- She told The Playlist she is not setting her expectations high and is focused on the work.
- Obsession was made for US$1 million and grossed US$524 million, according to Screen Rant.
- CinemaBlend reports that Julianne Moore, for The Debut, leads most Best Actress predictions, with Navarrette also high on the lists.
- The Oscars are set for Sunday, 14 March 2027, and Obsession is streaming on Netflix in the US.

[Inde Navarrette](https://moduscarry.com/tag/inde-navarrette/) has heard the Oscar talk, and she is trying not to let it change how she works. The star of the breakout horror Obsession told The Playlist she is keeping her expectations in check, even as the film's huge box office and strong reviews push her name into awards-season predictions.

## What Inde Navarrette said about the Oscar buzz

According to [Screen Rant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Rant), which quoted the interview, Navarrette said she approaches the attention the same way she approached the film: by concentrating on the work itself. She described herself as a very sensitive person and said she did not want to raise her hopes too far.

She also admitted to being a lifelong Oscars viewer. Some acceptance speeches she knows by heart, and a few she watches before big events because they inspire her. In her view, the whole awards circuit is inspiring for an actor, since it rewards doing your best.

On the question of a win, she was relaxed. If it does not happen, she said, it does not happen. Being part of the conversation, she added, makes her want to do the work she believes she is capable of.

## Praise from the industry

The most meaningful result of the film's reception, Navarrette said, has been the reaction from other performers. She told The Playlist she can now call [Colman Domingo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colman_Domingo) a friend, and she spoke warmly about meeting [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron), who has also been recognised this season.

Hearing "you did a good job" from people of that standing, she said, is what actors are ultimately looking for. She described the team behind Obsession as being, in her words, obsessed with showing skills they had not shown before, and said she wanted to do justice to Nikki.

## Why Obsession became a talking point

Directed by [Curry Barker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curry_Barker), Obsession follows Nikki, who is the object of her friend Bear's affection. Bear is played by [Michael Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnston_(actor)). When his wish that she would love him above everyone comes true, Nikki becomes a distorted version of herself, fixated on him. The cast also includes [Cooper Tomlinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooper_Tomlinson), [Megan Lawless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Lawless) and [Andy Richter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Richter). The film was released by [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features).

The numbers explain the attention. Screen Rant reports a budget of US$1 million against a worldwide gross of US$524 million. It also cites a 93 per cent critics' score and a 94 per cent audience score. Reviewers often singled out Navarrette's unsettling performance as the reason the film worked.

## The Best Actress race

Variety placed Navarrette among its early [Actor Awards predictions](https://variety.com/lists/2027-sag-actor-awards-predictions/), alongside [Andrew Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Garfield), in what it called an unsettled film race.

CinemaBlend writer Sarah El-Mahmoud wrote that Navarrette is widely expected to land a nomination in the [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actress) category, but that Julianne Moore is consistently listed ahead of her for The Debut. According to the same piece, Moore has won an Oscar before and has been nominated five times in total. The Debut, directed by Jesse Eisenberg, is a dark comedy in which Moore plays Mona, a woman who joins a community play and is guided by a director played by Paul Giamatti.

The writer still said they would like to see Navarrette win, calling her work a generational horror performance. They also noted that it is early in the season and that Navarrette's charm could win over voters. Horror performances have broken through before, they pointed out, citing Amy Madigan's win for Weapons at the 2026 Oscars.

## What happens next

The 2027 Oscars take place on Sunday, 14 March, according to CinemaBlend, so there are about four months of campaigning ahead. The Debut opens widely in cinemas on Christmas Day.
