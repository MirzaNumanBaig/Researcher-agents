**Quick summary**

- All six episodes of the second series of Netflix spy thriller Black Doves are available to watch now.
- Co-star Keira Knightley told the BBC World Service the show is "utterly ridiculous" yet has depth, humour and seriousness, according to The Mirror.
- Ben Whishaw plays hitman Sam Young, who is at the heart of the show's friendship with Knightley's character.
- Whishaw, who played Q in three Bond films, says his own grandfather secretly spied for the British in Germany.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) is back on screens as the melancholy hitman at the centre of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves), and the cast are talking up how far the show will go. With the second series now streaming on Netflix, [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) has described the spy-action drama as "utterly ridiculous", according to [The Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/keira-knightley-ben-wishaw-doves-37862430), and Whishaw has shared a family story that sounds like it was written for the series.

## Keira Knightley on a "romp" with real depth

Speaking on the BBC World Service, Knightley said the show is ridiculous but also carries a depth, a lot of humour and a real seriousness. She pointed out that her character is blown out of a window twice in the first series, and said it is "meant to be a romp", as quoted by The Mirror.

Knightley plays Helen Webb, who appears to live a privileged, ordinary life in London with her politician husband and two children. In truth she works as an agent for the shadowy spies-for-hire group that gives the show its name. Early in the new series her husband becomes Prime Minister and the family moves into Downing Street, which raises the stakes for her double life. [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire) also stars as Reed, Helen's long-term handler.

The Mirror reports that Knightley, 41, particularly enjoys the stunts, including kicking in doors. She said a piece of balsa wood rigged with a small explosive made the effect satisfying, and called it "just the best day at work ever".

## Whishaw's Sam Young is the show's anchor

The Mirror describes the friendship between Helen and her old hitman friend Sam as the heart of the thriller. Whishaw plays Sam with what the paper calls "mournful menace". Critics quoted in the piece say the on-screen chemistry between the two leads is "vibrant and believable", mixing poignancy with fun. The show and its stars have been nominated for a number of awards, according to the same report.

For a fuller look at how the new run has been received, see our earlier report on [Ben Whishaw's Black Doves season two reviews](https://moduscarry.com/ben-whishaw-black-doves-season-2/).

## From Q to a real-life spy in the family

Whishaw is no newcomer to espionage. Before playing Sam, the 46-year-old played Q in three [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) films, supplying gadgets and weapons to [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig)'s 007. Craig left the role after No Time to Die.

According to The Mirror, there is a real spy in the family too. Whishaw's grandfather, Jean Stellmacher, spoke seven languages and had a photographic memory. He joined the German Army and acted as a mole for the British during the Second World War. His wife, Olga, only told the family about it some 50 years later, as he lay dying. "We don't know very much," Whishaw said. "He kept it a secret, as you have to."

Whishaw has said in the past that the Bond franchise needs to be "reinvented", adding that he does not know where a new story should go. His request, as quoted by the paper: "surprise me." Knightley, meanwhile, has her own suggestion for the next Bond. She would like to see the tiny baby-blue velour swimsuit return, in a nod to Sean Connery's swimwear in Goldfinger.

## What happens next

The sources do not say whether a third series has been ordered, and neither Netflix nor the cast have confirmed anything on that front in the material we reviewed.
