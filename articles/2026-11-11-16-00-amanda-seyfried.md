**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) says she feels "relieved" and "excited" now that her surprise role in the documentary *You Can See Everything* is public.
- She told People on 8 November, at the New York premiere of Netflix's *Steps*, that she looks for work that scares her.
- She reprises Elizabeth Holmes, the character that won her an Emmy and a Golden Globe for *The Dropout*, in staged reenactments.
- The A24 film opened on 16 October and, according to BroBible, has passed US$80 million in North America.
- *Steps*, in which she voices Cinderella, arrives on Netflix on 20 November 2026.

Amanda Seyfried has spoken publicly for the first time about the biggest secret of this year's film season. The actor told People at the 8 November New York premiere of *Steps* that she is "relieved" and "excited" that audiences now know she plays Elizabeth Holmes again in *You Can See Everything*, the A24 documentary from Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim.

## What Seyfried said about the secret

Asked how it felt to finally talk about the role, Seyfried answered in two words: "Relieved. Excited." She then explained why the job appealed, in a quote carried by BroBible, Yardbarker and The A.V. Club.

"It's important for me as an actor, an artist, I mean, a mom, of course, but as a person in the world who loves to work, to find things that I haven't done before and to do things that scare me in a specific way," she said. When a frightening choice turns out to have "mostly positive impact," she added, it feels good because you took a risk.

Her involvement was left out of the film's marketing. According to Mezha, it was revealed at a surprise premiere at the Telluride Film Festival in September, ahead of the October release. BroBible reports that her reaction to a question about Holmes, in the weekend the film reached cinemas, went viral on social media.

## How the documentary uses her

The film starts 34 days before Holmes reports to federal prison in May 2023, according to Popviewers. Fielder films her at home with partner Billy Evans and their two young children. Mezha says she was sentenced to 11 years on fraud charges and reported to a prison in Bryan, Texas.

Once Holmes was behind bars, the filmmakers lost access. BroBible says Fielder had earlier shown Holmes scenes from *The Dropout*, and the film also shows Seyfried watching footage of Holmes reacting to her performance. The Playlist describes the second half as a way around the lack of cameras: Seyfried plays Holmes again in recreations of real conversations between Holmes and Fielder.

Popviewers adds that Christopher Abbott plays Evans in the reenactments, giving the film a second version of its central relationship. The A.V. Club notes that Seyfried also appears as herself, an expert on Holmes after the earlier series, who decides to have another go at the character.

## The Oscar conversation and the box office

The film is doing well commercially. BroBible puts its total at more than US$80 million after four weekends, which it says makes it the second-highest-grossing documentary ever in North America. For a documentary, that is a rare figure.

Awards talk has followed. Mezha reports that A24 has been building Oscar buzz for Seyfried, and that a nomination would be the first for a documentary reenactor in an acting category. BroBible adds that a Best Picture nomination would also be a first for a documentary. None of this is confirmed, since nominations are months away.

## Steps: a very different role

Seyfried was speaking at the premiere of *Steps*, an animated musical comedy that retells Cinderella from the point of view of the stepsisters. She voices Cinderella. The A.V. Club lists Ali Wong, Daniel Radcliffe and Bette Midler among the cast.

She told the Associated Press, as reported by Yardbarker, that embodying someone so iconic is "really special," but also "a little stressful." The A.V. Club suggests a voice role in a fairy tale is a gentler risk than being the secret co-star of a film this widely discussed.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Amanda Seyfried's role in You Can See Everything?

She plays Elizabeth Holmes in staged reenactments of conversations the filmmakers could not film, and also appears as herself. She won an Emmy and a Golden Globe for playing Holmes in *The Dropout*.

### Who directed You Can See Everything?

Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim directed it, and A24 is behind the film. It opened in cinemas on 16 October.

### When can I watch Steps?

Netflix releases *Steps* on 20 November 2026. Seyfried voices Cinderella in the animated musical comedy.
