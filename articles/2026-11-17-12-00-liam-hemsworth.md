Liam Hemsworth Hunger Games fans got a small reunion on Monday as Jennifer Lawrence marked the lead-up to the next film. On Monday, 16 November, she shared three never-before-seen throwback photos of herself with [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) and [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson), according to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/16/jennifer-lawrence-shares-never-before-seen-throwback-photos-with-hunger-games-co-stars-liam-hemsworth-josh-hutcherson/).

## Liam Hemsworth Hunger Games photos: what they show

The pictures appeared on Lawrence's Instagram Story. In the first, she stands between Hemsworth and Hutcherson on an airport tarmac with a plane behind them. Her caption reads "Girls just wanna have fun."

The second is a black-and-white shot of the three walking together, with Hemsworth and Hutcherson both wearing panda-themed items. The third, also in black and white, shows all three on a private plane.

Just Jared did not say when or where the photos were taken. The images look like off-set moments from the years the trio spent making the films, but that is an impression from the descriptions, not a confirmed detail.

## Why now

The timing is hard to miss. *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, the franchise prequel, opens in US cinemas on 20 November. Just Jared reports that Lawrence and Hutcherson are reprising their roles as Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark, a casting detail announced in October. The same page notes that Lawrence and Hutcherson reunited at the film's New York premiere.

Hemsworth, who played Gale Hawthorne in the original films, is not mentioned in the report as having a role in the new film. The photos work as a reminder of the original [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) ensemble rather than a casting announcement.

## What happens next

The prequel reaches US cinemas on Friday. Australian release details were not in the source material, so check local listings. Lawrence's post is an Instagram Story, which disappears after 24 hours unless saved, so the pictures are most likely to travel through entertainment sites in the meantime.
