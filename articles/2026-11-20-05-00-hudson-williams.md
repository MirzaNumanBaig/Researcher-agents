- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays Duncan Rheingans-Yoo in Netflix's crypto drama The Altruists.
- He appears in three of the eight episodes: 4, 5 and 7, according to TV Insider.

- Yahoo reports he will next appear in the upcoming Tyrant alongside his The Altruists co-star Julia Garner.

[Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) has a role in Netflix's new crypto drama The Altruists, and fans of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) can find him in three of its eight episodes. He plays Duncan Rheingans-Yoo, an investor caught up in the collapse of Sam Bankman-Fried's empire, as the series arrived on the streamer this week.

## Who Hudson Williams plays in The Altruists

According to [TV Insider](https://www.tvinsider.com/1304554/the-altruists-hudson-williams-scenes-episodes/), Williams' character is the boyfriend of Lily Zhang, played by Marianna Phung. The two are cofounders of Modulo Capital, a crypto trading firm, and both invest with [Sam Bankman-Fried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Bankman-Fried) and Caroline Ellison. Their involvement comes at the worst possible time, just as everything they have built begins to unravel.

The series follows the relationship between Bankman-Fried, played by [Anthony Boyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Boyle), and Ellison, played by [Julia Garner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Garner). TV Insider describes it as a story of two people whose belief in effective altruism led them toward greed and betrayal. Yahoo's cast guide describes Williams' character as the cofounder of a trading firm with murky connections to FTX.

## Which episodes feature Hudson Williams

Williams is in Episodes 4, 5 and 7 of the eight-episode run. TV Insider noted that he was not as famous as he is now when he filmed the show, so his screen time is modest. He does share scenes with Boyle and other cast members.

The outlet also listed approximate timestamps for viewers who want to find him quickly:

- Episode 4: his first appearance comes at roughly 30 minutes and 52 seconds.
- Episode 5: he appears at about 22 minutes and 16 seconds, and again at 30 minutes and 48 seconds.
- Episode 7: his biggest share of screen time, with scenes at around 5 minutes and 33 seconds and 30 minutes and 33 seconds.

TV Insider said Williams was cast before the Crave and HBO Max series Heated Rivalry took off. That timing explains why a supporting part now draws so much attention.

## The wider cast and the real story

Yahoo's guide says the ensemble mixes television, film and Broadway names. Boyle is described there as an Olivier Award winner who received a Tony nomination for playing Scorpius Malfoy in Harry Potter and the Cursed Child. Garner is known for Ozark and the 2025 film Weapons, per the same guide. Williams is also credited as a Canadian actor best known as Shane Hollander in Heated Rivalry.

The drama draws on a real scandal. TV Insider notes that Bankman-Fried and Ellison were prosecuted in 2023 over the theft of more than eight billion dollars from investors through the cryptocurrency exchange FTX, founded in 2019. Yahoo adds that the company collapsed in 2022 and was a major news story at the time.

## What happens next

Williams' next project is already lined up. Yahoo reports that he will appear in Tyrant, which also stars Garner, Demi Moore, Charlize Theron and Paapa Essiedu. No release details were given in the sources we read, so we have none to share yet.
