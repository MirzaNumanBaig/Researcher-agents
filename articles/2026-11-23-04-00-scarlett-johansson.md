**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) turned 42 on 22 November 2026; she was born in New York City in 1984.
- She began acting as a child and broke through with Lost in Translation in 2003, which won her a BAFTA for Best Actress, according to Open Magazine.
- She earned two Oscar nominations in 2020, for Marriage Story and Jojo Rabbit, and won a Tony in 2010.
- Her directing debut, Eleanor the Great, premiered at Cannes in 2025. Her next projects include Paper Tiger.

Scarlett Johansson turns 42 today, 22 November, and the milestone has prompted a round of career retrospectives. Open Magazine's profile traces a path from a child actor in the 1990s to a global blockbuster name, a Tony winner and now a film director.

## From child actor to Lost in Translation

Johansson was born in New York City on 22 November 1984, and began acting while still a child. Open Magazine says her film debut was North in 1994. She drew further notice in Manny & Lo (1996) and The Horse Whisperer (1998), roles that marked her as a young performer to watch.

The same profile argues those early films were more than a stepping stone, because they built her reputation for emotionally nuanced work. The big turning point came in 2003 with Sofia Coppola's Lost in Translation, in which she played Charlotte, a young woman adrift in Tokyo, opposite Bill Murray. Open Magazine reports the performance won her the BAFTA Award for [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actress). She also appeared in Girl with a Pearl Earring that year.

## Black Widow and the Marvel years

Johansson joined the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) as [Natasha Romanoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Widow_(Natasha_Romanova)), also known as Black Widow, in Iron Man 2 in 2010. The character returned in The Avengers, Captain America: The Winter Soldier, [Avengers: Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Infinity_War) and Avengers: Endgame. In 2021 she headlined the standalone film Black Widow, which Open Magazine credits with making her one of the franchise's most recognisable faces.

## Oscars, a Tony and a range of roles

Both of her Academy Award nominations came in 2020. She was nominated for Best Actress for Marriage Story, playing a woman going through a painful divorce, and for Best Supporting Actress for [Jojo Rabbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jojo_Rabbit), where she played a mother protecting her son in Nazi Germany. She did not win either award.

Her stage work has been recognised too. Johansson won a Tony in 2010 for Best Featured Actress in a Play for A View from the Bridge on Broadway, opposite [Liev Schreiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liev_Schreiber). Open Magazine adds that she has also worked in music, lending her voice to soundtracks and collaborating with musicians.

## Directing and business

Her directorial debut, Eleanor the Great, premiered at the Cannes Film Festival in 2025. The film follows an elderly woman who starts telling a story that is not hers, and explores grief, identity and the pull of storytelling, per Open Magazine. In 2022 she also launched a skincare brand, The Outset.

## What happens next

On screen, Johansson starred in [Jurassic World Rebirth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World_Rebirth) in 2025 as Zora Bennett and appeared in Wes Anderson's [The Phoenician Scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Phoenician_Scheme). She is starring in James Gray's crime thriller Paper Tiger, in which she plays a woman whose family is drawn into danger involving the Russian mob. Open Magazine also reports, with the caveat that these are reported plans, that she is preparing for Scapegoat, a project from Hereditary director Ari Aster, and is involved in Mike Flanagan's upcoming Exorcist film. ## Frequently asked questions

### How old is Scarlett Johansson?

She turns 42 on 22 November 2026. She was born in New York City on 22 November 1984.

### Which film made Scarlett Johansson famous?

Lost in Translation (2003), directed by Sofia Coppola, is widely seen as her breakthrough. It won her a BAFTA for Best Actress, according to Open Magazine.

### Has Scarlett Johansson won an Oscar?

No. She was nominated twice in 2020, for Marriage Story and Jojo Rabbit, but did not win. She did win a Tony Award in 2010.

### What is Scarlett Johansson doing next?

She is starring in the crime thriller Paper Tiger from director James Gray. Other projects, including Scapegoat and an Exorcist film, are reported but not confirmed.
