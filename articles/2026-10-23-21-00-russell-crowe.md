**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s historical thriller The Weight reached premium video on demand (PVOD) in the US on 20 October, according to Collider.
- On the Apple TV Store chart it rose two places to No. 6, ahead of Alan Ritchson's Runner at No. 7, Comic Book Resources reported.
- The film is Certified Fresh at 94% on Rotten Tomatoes, based on 143 reviews, but took only about $4 million at the US box office.
- Crowe's Netflix film Unabomber has stayed in the streamer's global top 10 for a full month, Collider says, despite poor reviews.

Russell Crowe has two films competing for attention on screens at home this week. The Weight, a gold-smuggling thriller co-starring [Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke), is climbing the US rental and purchase charts after reaching PVOD on 20 October. Unabomber, his Netflix crime film, is still in the global top 10 a month after release.

## The Weight finds its audience at home

The film had a quiet cinema run. [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/the-weight-defeats-runner-pvod-success-october-2026/) reports that distributor Vertical released it in the United States on 18 September and that it grossed just $4 million. The critics were far kinder than the ticket buyers. The Weight sits at 94% on Rotten Tomatoes from 143 reviews, with an audience approval rating of 84%.

Home viewing is changing that picture. Citing the tracking site FlixPatrol, CBR says The Weight moved up two spots on the Apple TV Store top 10 to reach No. 6. Runner, the Ritchson action film, slipped three places to No. 7 after peaking at No. 3 on 18 October. [Collider](https://collider.com/russell-crowe-the-weight-streaming-digital-october-2026/) adds that The Weight debuted in the top 10 of the iTunes chart and the top 20 on Amazon, and landed alongside the true-crime film By Any Means, starring [Mark Wahlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Wahlberg) and [Yahya Abdul-Mateen II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahya_Abdul-Mateen_II).

## What the film is about

Hawke plays Samuel Murphy, a recently jailed man. Crowe is Warden Clancy, who offers him freedom if he can smuggle gold out of a remote mine with a crew of dangerous prisoners, according to CBR. Julia Jones, Austin Amelio, Avi Nash and Lucas Lynggaard Tønnesen also star. Matthew Booi and Shelby Gaines wrote the script.

It is also the feature directing debut of Padraic McKinley. The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival earlier this year to strong reviews, Collider says, and McKinley has since been hired to direct Sicario 3.

## Unabomber holds on at Netflix

Crowe's other current release is a different kind of success. Directed by Janus Metz, Unabomber has been in Netflix's global top 10 for a full month, Collider reports, even though its reviews were poor. Shailene Woodley and Jacob Tremblay appear in it alongside Crowe.

The context matters. Collider says Netflix's 2026 slate of mid-budget genre films began with The Rip, Apex, Thrash and War Machine, and each passed 100 million views. Ben Affleck's thriller Animals has topped 50 million views in two weeks. Unabomber sits a tier below those hits, according to the site, which is still a solid result for a film the critics did not rate.

Collider also notes that the film arrives at a time when fans are asking for more of the kind of psychological crime drama that Netflix's Mindhunter offered. David Fincher has ruled out a third season of that show.

## What happens next

The Weight is available to rent or own on PVOD in the US now, so its chart position over the coming week will show whether the critical praise is turning into viewers. McKinley's Sicario 3 is the next project attached to his name. For Unabomber, Netflix's next weekly viewership report will show whether it holds its top 10 place into a second month. This article could not confirm Australian release dates for either title from the sources reviewed. Check local streaming and rental listings.
