**Quick summary**

- A court filing dated 21 October asks to move [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson)'s trial over *The Deb* from 19 January 2027 to 22 March 2027.
- The reason given is that her lead lawyer, Allyson K. Thompson, has been diagnosed with breast cancer.
- Both sides agreed in principle to the new date during a call on 16 October.
- The court still has to approve the change, and a later date is possible.

The January trial between Rebel Wilson and the producers of *The Deb* looks set to slip by more than two months. According to a 24-page filing submitted on Wednesday 21 October and reported by Deadline, the Australian actor's lead attorney is facing medical treatment, and both sides want the schedule reset.

## What the filing says

The application was lodged by Allyson K. Thompson, who leads Wilson's legal team. As quoted by [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/687940-rebel-wilson-faces-fresh-delay-over-the-deb-legal-fight) and [rova](https://www.rova.nz/articles/rebel-wilson-trial-pushed-back-to-march-2027), the document states that Thompson "was recently diagnosed with breast cancer".

It adds that she has been placed on medical leave for surgery from 30 November 2026 until 4 January 2027. She is also working reduced hours because of the side effects of the illness. The trial is currently listed for 19 January 2027, only two weeks after that leave ends.

That timing explains the request. A lead lawyer returning from surgery with little preparation time before a trial date is a problem for both parties, and the filing treats it as a shared scheduling issue rather than a tactical move by one side.

## How the new date was reached

The filing says the lawyers held a scheduled phone conference on Friday 16 October to deal with this and other discovery matters, after all counsel had been told about Thompson's diagnosis. During that call, the parties "agreed in principle" to move the trial to 22 March 2027, or to a later date that suits them and the court.

Agreement in principle is not a ruling. Geo News notes the proposed date still needs the court's approval, which means January is unlikely to hold but March is not yet locked in. That leaves a gap of about nine weeks between the old and proposed dates.

## The dispute behind the trial

The legal fight pits Wilson, who directed *The Deb*, against the film's producers [Amanda Ghost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Ghost), Gregor Cameron and Vince Holden. Both outlets describe the dispute as lengthy and complicated. The sources reviewed for this article do not set out the individual claims, so those details are not covered here. Earlier developments are collected in our post on how [Rebel Wilson won a second court case over The Deb](https://moduscarry.com/rebel-wilson-wins-second-court-case-over-the-deb/).

## A busy run for Wilson's film career

The filing became public just after Wilson received positive reviews at the Toronto Film Festival for her latest film, *Girl Group*, according to both reports. The contrast is stark: a well-received premiere on one side, and a court calendar that keeps shifting on the other.

## What happens next

The court must now decide whether to accept 22 March 2027 or set another date. Until it does, the 19 January trial date technically stands. Thompson's medical leave is scheduled to begin on 30 November, so a decision is likely to be needed before then to give both sides time to plan.
