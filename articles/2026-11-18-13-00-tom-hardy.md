**Quick summary**

- Episode 9 of MobLand season 2, "Nervous Breakdown", scored 8.7 on IMDb, according to ScreenRant.
- That equals the series-high mark set by season 2, episode 7.
- The episode streamed on Paramount+ on Friday, November 13; the season 2 finale follows on Friday, November 20.
- A third season has already been confirmed.

The Tom Hardy MobLand rating has matched its best-ever mark just days before the season 2 finale of the crime drama starring [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy). Its penultimate episode holds an 8.7 rating on IMDb, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mobland-season-2-tom-hardy-guy-ritchie-mob-thriller-peaks-before-finale/), level with the show's highest-rated episode so far.

## Tom Hardy MobLand rating: a series high with one episode to go

The ninth episode of the second season, titled "Nervous Breakdown", arrived on Paramount+ on November 13. Its 8.7 IMDb score ties it with episode 7 of the same season, which ScreenRant describes as the highest-rated MobLand episode on the platform.

The season began on September 18, so the show has built toward this point over about two months. ScreenRant also reports that the series' [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) audience score has climbed to 75% during the second run, and that MobLand is currently the most-watched show worldwide on Paramount+'s viewership charts. That figure is the outlet's own reading of the charts rather than an official Paramount+ statement.

The momentum fits a pattern we have followed on the site. Earlier this week MobLand was reported as the top show on Paramount+ in the US, and you can find everything we have published on the actor in our [Tom Hardy](https://moduscarry.com/tag/tom-hardy/) archive.

## What happens in "Nervous Breakdown"

Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, a fixer working for the London-based Harrigon crime family. ScreenRant says the episode pushes him into a bind between loyalty to that family and loyalty to his own: his wife and daughter.

According to the review, Harry's marriage has been under strain all season and his bond with his daughter has been tested. The episode hands him a chance to put some of that right, and the usually polite, reserved character is pushed past his limit. ScreenRant says he walks into dangerous territory prepared to sacrifice himself for the people he loves. We have left out the specifics here to avoid spoilers.

## The villain driving the season

The wider story this season, as ScreenRant tells it, is a lethal contest between the Harrigons and a new antagonist, Frankie Cooper. The outlet credits the character with lifting the series, and says the scale has widened from the turf war of season 1 into a larger operation with several players involved. That is one critic's view, and the audience score of 75% suggests viewers are not unanimous.

## Who is behind the camera

Guy Ritchie directs several episodes and is an executive producer alongside Hardy and co-creator [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth). Episode 9 was directed by Lawrence Gough, who is also set to direct the tenth and final episode of this run, according to ScreenRant.

## What happens next

The season 2 finale is due on Friday, November 20, on Paramount+. ScreenRant notes the high score creates an obvious challenge: an episode that lands at or above 8.7 would set a new bar, while anything lower will feel like a step back to rating watchers. A third season is already confirmed, so the finale is a chapter break rather than an ending.
