**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) will play Rogue in Marvel Studios' next X-Men film.
- Asked by Deadline how she is preparing, she said she loves to read and stretch, but is "not a working out type of a girl".
- She says she wants to build stamina for the role and calls the gym "very important" for fitness and health.
- Separately, she is one of the actresses on Elle's 2026 Women in Hollywood covers, tied to her film Obsession.

Inde Navarrette has given an unusually relaxed answer about getting ready for Rogue. In a recent interview with Deadline, reported by SuperHeroHype on 16 November 2026, the Obsession star said her preparation so far leans towards books and stretching rather than heavy gym sessions.

## What Navarrette said about training for Rogue

Asked whether she had started working out or reading specific comics, Navarrette kept it light. "I love to read, and I'll say I love to stretch," she said, according to [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/news/706232-new-x-men-rogue-inde-navarrette-surprising-training-confession). She then added that regular workouts are not really her thing.

She did not wave the physical side of the job away, though. SuperHeroHype reports that she wants to improve her stamina so she can keep up with the demands of the part, and that she described the gym as "very important" for both fitness and health.

The answer fits a pattern from her recent press rounds, where she has been playful about the role without giving much away. A clip from the Governors Awards, shared by the outlet, shows her playing coy on the same question.

## Who Rogue is, and why the role matters

Rogue, known off screen as Anna Marie, can drain another person's life force and absorb their memories, according to SuperHeroHype's summary of the character. The power first surfaced when she kissed a boy she cared about, Cody Robbins. It pushed her to cover up from head to toe so no one else would be hurt.

Anna Paquin played the character across several earlier X-Men films. Navarrette is the first actress to pick up the part since Marvel Studios gained the franchise.

## The film around her

The new movie is Marvel Studios' first X-Men project since Disney bought 20th Century Fox and its properties in 2019. Per the same report, Jake Schreier, who directed Thunderbolts, is leading it. Beef creator Lee Sung Jin and The Bear co-creator Joanna Calo are writing the script, according to Deadline as relayed by SuperHeroHype.

The reported ensemble is growing:

- Sadie Sink as Jean Grey
- Kit Connor as Scott Summers
- Samara Weaving as Emma Frost
- Christopher Abbott as Professor X
- Maya Boyd as Storm
- Adam Driver as Mister Sinister
- Asa Germann as Angel

Ben Wang has entered talks to play Iceman, so that casting is not yet final. No release date appears in the sources we read.

## An Elle cover for Obsession

Navarrette's profile is rising on the awards-season side too. The style site Go Fug Yourself reports that she is among the women on Elle's 2026 Women in Hollywood covers, listed alongside her film Obsession. The site groups the cover stars as people linked to the awards conversation, or to a film that could be part of it.

Others named include [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) for Dune 3, [Carrie Coon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Coon) for The Liberation, [Samantha Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Morton) for The Odyssey, [Rosalind Eleazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Eleazar) for [Misty Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misty_Green), [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore) for The Debut, [Penelope Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pen%C3%A9lope_Cruz) for The Invite, and [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) for Wicker and The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. Go Fug Yourself notes that early buzz has Moore following in the footsteps of [Jessie Buckley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jessie_Buckley) this season. That is the site's observation, not a confirmed forecast.
