**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has reportedly come aboard Maelstrom, a Netflix-bound action thriller directed by [Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra), according to Deadline as relayed by Empire.
- He replaces [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson), whose exit has not been explained.
- The film is described as "Die Hard in a hurricane", built on a script idea dating back to 1993.
- No release date has been reported.

Keanu Reeves is set to star in Maelstrom, a hurricane-bound action thriller for Netflix, after taking over the lead role from Alan Ritchson. Deadline broke the news, and Empire picked it up on 26 October 2026, noting that Jaume Collet-Serra will direct.

## What Maelstrom is about

Deadline's synopsis, quoted by Empire, has the film following "a Federal Marshal assigned to protect the fiancée of an arms dealer turned witness". The marshal must keep her alive through one brutal night as a hurricane cuts off communication, strands them without reinforcements and brings the dealer's men to their safe house.

Empire sums it up as [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard) in a hurricane. The current script is credited to Mark Bianculli, the writer of How To Rob A Bank, according to the outlet.

## A script that has waited since 1993

The idea is far older than the casting. Empire traces it to an initial draft by the late Gregory Fitzpatrick that first surfaced in 1993. Back then, the spec script was reportedly bought with [Steven Seagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Seagal), [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) or [Clint Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Eastwood) in mind.

The project was revived in recent months with Ritchson, the star of Reacher, attached. Empire says his departure was not explained, and that it opened the door for Reeves. Neither Netflix nor the filmmakers' statements were part of the coverage we could read, so the casting stands as a trade report rather than a formal announcement.

## Reeves' crowded slate

Maelstrom joins a long list of projects on Reeves' horizon, per Empire. They include Shiver, described as a pirate-and-shark time-loop action film, Sonic The Hedgehog 4, Ruben Östlund's The Entertainment System Is Down, and an untitled LEGO movie from [Josh Cooley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Cooley) and [Patty Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patty_Jenkins).

The outlet also notes that, only five days earlier, Reeves was reported to be eyeing James Wan's remake of The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil. That is a report of interest, not a confirmed deal.

For Collet-Serra, the job follows the airport thriller Carry-On, with [Taron Egerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taron_Egerton), and last year's The Woman In The Yard. Empire adds that he is finishing post-production on his Cliffhanger remake, another film with roots in 1993.
