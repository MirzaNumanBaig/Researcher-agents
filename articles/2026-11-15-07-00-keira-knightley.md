**Quick summary**

- The green dress worn by [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) in the 2007 film *Atonement* will be sold at Christie's in December, according to The Telegraph.
- The guide price is £100,000 to £120,000.
- The gown was created by costume designer [Jacqueline Durran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacqueline_Durran).
- Knightley, 41, also debuted a new bob at the press night party for her West End return in *The Lives of Others*, Grazia reports.

The **Keira Knightley green dress** from *Atonement* is heading to auction. Christie's will offer the emerald gown in December with a guide price of £100,000 to £120,000, [The Telegraph reported on 14 November](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2026/11/14/keira-knightleys-green-dress-is-a-bargain-at-120k/). Writer Hannah Betts argues that is a modest figure for one of cinema's best-loved costumes.

## Why this dress still matters

In Ian McEwan's 2001 novel, Cecilia Tallis chooses a backless green halterneck for a dinner that changes her family's fortunes. Joe Wright's 2007 film put the garment on screen, with Knightley in the role, and it has been tied to the story ever since. The Telegraph says it was voted "Best Costume of All Time" in a poll by [Sky Movies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Cinema) and *InStyle* magazine. That result placed it ahead of Audrey Hepburn's little black dress in *Breakfast at Tiffany's* and [Marilyn Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Monroe)'s billowing white dress in *The Seven Year Itch*.

Almost two decades after the film, the gown is a piece of fashion history as much as film history. You can browse more of our [fashion coverage](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) for similar stories.

## How the gown was made

Despite its simple look, the construction was elaborate. The Telegraph notes that it is not a single dress but a bodice, skirt and sash. Durran blended 1920s and 1930s styling with a more fluid, modern feel, and hand dyed acres of white silk to reach a green that shifts between shades depending on the angle.

The fabric was fragile, and the piece had to work under hard conditions on set. As a result, according to the paper, several versions were made and frequent repairs were needed during filming.

## Where it could end up

Betts hopes the buyer will not lock the gown away. Her wish is that it goes to the V&A, where visitors queued to see it during the 2012 *Hollywood Costume* exhibition. That is her opinion, not an announcement, and nothing in the sources says who will bid or where it may land.

She also points to the controversy around Kim Kardashian wearing the dress Monroe wore to sing for President Kennedy, to the 2022 Met Gala, as a cautionary example for historic costume. The Telegraph describes that dress as worth $4.8 million.

## A new look for the West End

Knightley is in the news for a second reason. Grazia reports that she showed off a fresh haircut at the press night after-party for *The Lives of Others* at Brasserie Zédel in London on 29 October. Her stylist, Luke Hersheson, founder of the Hershesons salons, combined a chin-length French bob with soft, waterfall-style layers and a deep side part.

Hersheson told Grazia the style suits Knightley's offbeat, cool personal taste. He created a gentle flat wave using a large-barrel waver, then added a light spray to keep the layers separate. ## What happens next

The Christie's sale is scheduled for December, though the sources do not give an exact date. The guide price is an estimate, and the final price depends on bidding on the day. Knightley's run in *The Lives of Others* continues in London.
