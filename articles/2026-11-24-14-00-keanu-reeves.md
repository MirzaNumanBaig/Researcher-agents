**Quick summary**

- Director Francis Lawrence says a Constantine sequel script exists and that DC executives were the obstacle, according to Cinemablend, which quotes his interview with Collider.
- The comments followed news that James Gunn and Peter Safran are leaving DC Studios.
- Jeremy Latcham is reported to be stepping in as DC Studios CEO.
- No studio has announced a sequel, and DC has not revealed its next plans.

Talk of Keanu Reeves Constantine 2 has returned. [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has been linked to a sequel to the 2005 supernatural thriller for years, and director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) now says the leadership change at DC could finally open the door, according to [Cinemablend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/james-gunn-dc-exit-has-people-asking-about-keanu-reeves-constantine-sequel).

## What Francis Lawrence said

Lawrence spoke to Collider while promoting *Sunrise on the Reaping*. He said he did not want anyone to lose their job, and he did not treat the news with glee. But he said the team has been trying to make Constantine 2 for "20-something years", and that it has an idea and a script.

In his telling, the people running DC at the time stood in the way. After the news about the studio's leaders, he said, the team felt more hopeful that a new opportunity might appear.

He was also careful about what he does not know. He said the obstacle was never himself, Reeves or screenwriter [Akiva Goldsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiva_Goldsman), and that J.J. Abrams is attached too. The decision, he said, now sits with executives at Paramount, Warner and whoever runs DC next.

## A long-running project

Cinemablend notes that a sequel has technically been in the works since 2022, when Goldsman was reported to be writing it. Lawrence has stayed committed to directing, and he previously said Reeves was "excited" about the idea. Cinemablend says a positive update arrived in 2025, but news has been scarce since.

The 2005 film has built a cult following over time. Reeves was not fans' first pick for the role, Cinemablend writes, because his version departs so much from the comics. The character has since appeared elsewhere. [Matt Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Ryan_(actor)) played John Constantine in a short-lived NBC series from 2014, then in The CW's Arrowverse, and voiced him in animated projects through 2024.

Lawrence does not see a conflict. He said he would be fine if the studio wanted a different John Constantine for a shared universe, as long as he could also make a sequel to his own version with Reeves. In his view, the two can exist side by side.

## The DC leadership change

Cinemablend reports that [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) and Peter Safran are leaving DC, and that it is unclear whether the DC universe they built will continue or be rebooted again. [Jeremy Latcham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Latcham), a Marvel Studios alumnus who worked with Gunn on *Guardians of the Galaxy*, is named as the new DC Studios CEO.

That uncertainty cuts both ways. A new regime could revive long-stalled projects, or it could clear the slate. For now, DC has not said what comes next. ## What happens next

Nothing is confirmed. Lawrence says the script is ready and the creative team is willing, so the next step is a decision from the studio side. DC Studios has not yet shown any plans following the departures of Gunn and Safran, so a sequel announcement, if one comes, would depend on how the new leadership shapes its slate.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Constantine 2 officially happening?

No. Lawrence says a script exists and the team wants to make it, but no studio has announced a production or a release date.

### Who is involved in the Constantine sequel?

Reeves, Lawrence, Goldsman and J.J. Abrams are named as attached, according to Lawrence's comments reported by Cinemablend.

### Why are fans talking about it now?

The announcement that Gunn and Safran are leaving DC prompted fans to ask whether stalled projects could return, and Lawrence said he felt more hopeful after the news.
