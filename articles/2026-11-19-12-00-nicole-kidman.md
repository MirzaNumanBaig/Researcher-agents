## Nicole Kidman's Posh Grandpa outfit in the West Village

Nicole Kidman has handed fashion editors a new name for an old idea. InStyle reported on 18 November that the actress was seen in the West Village in New York wearing an outfit it describes as the clearest example yet of "Posh Grandpa", the latest label in a long line of internet-born style trends.

The Australian star's look began with baggy trousers and a blazer, which InStyle says had distinctive lapels. Under the jacket she wore a silky blouse. She finished with a Chanel bag and black leather loafers. At first glance, the magazine writes, it reads like an ordinary office suit.

## What "Posh Grandpa" means

InStyle's writer describes the trend as a mix of vintage-inspired menswear and tailoring with comfortable, lived-in pieces. The mental picture offered is a grandfather in his study with a cigar and a vintage fountain pen. It follows earlier named looks such as Balletcore, Gorpcore and Coastal Grandma.

By that definition, the magazine argues, Kidman's outfit qualifies. Her trousers are tailored but roomy, and her loafers look worn in rather than box-fresh. The writer admits the pairing is not groundbreaking on its own. What gives it the "grandpa" feel, according to InStyle, is the overall mood of easy, slightly old-fashioned polish.

## How to wear it this winter

The practical advice in the piece is simple. Keep the trousers and loafers, and change the top to suit the weather. InStyle suggests an oversized argyle sweater in place of Kidman's silky blouse for colder days. It also lists shopping picks in the style, including loafers with a lug sole and pleated trousers, though those are the magazine's own recommendations and not what Kidman wore.
