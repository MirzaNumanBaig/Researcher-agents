**Quick summary**

- [Justine Lupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Lupe) and [Timothy Simons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Simons) spoke to The AU Review about their characters Morgan and Sasha in *Nobody Wants This* season 3.
- Season 3 is now streaming on Netflix.
- Sasha is newly single after separating from Esther, while Morgan has found a new professional ambition.
- Lupe said the pair fall for each other and for who they get to be together; Simons said they are at their best around one another.

[Justine Lupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Lupe) says the pull between Morgan and Sasha in *Nobody Wants This* is probably two things at once. In an interview published this week by The AU Review, the actor and her co-star Timothy Simons discussed how their characters' connection deepens in season 3, which is now available on Netflix.

## A different kind of romance in season 3

According to [The AU Review](https://www.theaureview.com/watch/interview-nobody-wants-this-season-3/), the new season sees Joanne ([Kristen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Bell)) and Noah ([Adam Brody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Brody)) settle into domestic bliss. Around them, the relationship between Morgan and Sasha offers what the outlet describes as a different romantic possibility, built on friendship, mutual understanding and the freedom to be entirely themselves.

Sasha is navigating single life after his separation from Esther ([Jackie Tohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Tohn)). Morgan, played by Lupe, has found a new sense of professional ambition. The interviewer, Peter Gray, asked whether the pair are falling for each other or for the people they get to be in each other's company.

## What Lupe and Simons said

Lupe's instinct was that the answer is both. She said Morgan sees what is valuable in Sasha and has her eyes open about who he is. She also agreed there is something Morgan is learning about herself, and that Morgan may want to hold on to what she gets from the relationship.

Simons pointed to what the two characters share. Both, he said, occupy a similar place in their families as second children. He noted that Sasha is the older brother but is in some ways the overlooked one, who gets less attention. When they are together, he said, there is an understanding of each other's lives, and each is the best version of themselves around the other.

## Rom-com couples who might not last

The conversation then turned to classic romantic comedies. Asked whether any famous screen couple probably isn't together today, the two answered with some humour.

Simons picked *[Reality Bites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality_Bites)*. He said that at a certain age you realise [Ben Stiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stiller)'s character is the better choice, and that he doubts the characters played by [Winona Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winona_Ryder) and [Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke) made it.

Lupe chose *Notting Hill*, saying she has little faith in a romance between a wildly famous movie star ([Julia Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Roberts)) and a bookshop owner played by Hugh Grant. Simons added *[Pretty Woman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pretty_Woman)*, with Roberts and [Richard Gere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Gere), calling it too long a bridge to cross. Lupe then offered *You've Got Mail*, with [Tom Hanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks) and [Meg Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meg_Ryan), joking about where Hanks's character might stand politically today. Both laughed that they were pessimistic about these couples.
