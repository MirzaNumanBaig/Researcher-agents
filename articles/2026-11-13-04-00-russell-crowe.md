**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and [Priyanka Chopra Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra) star in the sci-fi action thriller *Bluefly*.
- Highland Film Group has confirmed distribution deals across all media in major international territories, AOL reports.
- Nimród Antal directs, and production is under way in Queensland, Australia.
- The story follows a special-operations team hunting a missing military aircraft in the Congo.

Russell Crowe is heading into the jungle again, this time on home soil. The Oscar winner and Priyanka Chopra Jonas lead *Bluefly*, a sci-fi action thriller now filming in Queensland, and Highland Film Group announced this week that it has locked in distribution deals across all media in major international territories, according to [AOL.com](https://www.aol.com/articles/russell-crowe-priyanka-chopra-jonas-164343000.html).

## What is Bluefly about?

The film starts with a secret military mission that goes wrong. A high-tech military aircraft vanishes in the Congo, and a special-operations team is sent to find it.

Crowe plays Benny, a former military pilot with a mysterious past. Chopra Jonas plays Myla Reese, a U.N. interpreter who speaks the local language and knows the region well. They work under John Broyles, a U.S. government official leading the operation, played by [Michael Chiklis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chiklis).

The brief is blunt: find the aircraft and destroy every trace of the incident, because it has to stay secret. As the team pushes deeper into the Congo, it crosses ground controlled by mercenaries and oil pirates. Then comes the twist the title hints at. Something mysterious and powerful is waiting further into the jungle, and Myla begins to suspect that Benny knows more about it than he is letting on.

## Who is in the cast and crew?

*Bluefly* is directed by Nimród Antal. According to AOL, Crowe is billed as the 2000 Academy Award winner for *Gladiator*, and his other credits include *Nuremberg*, *A Beautiful Mind*, *Robin Hood* and *Unhinged*.

Chopra Jonas' listed credits include *The Bluff*, *Heads of State* and *The White Tiger*. [Daniel MacPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_MacPherson), the Australian actor, also joins the cast alongside Chiklis.

The producers named in the announcement are Michael Jefferson, [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio) and RuthAnne Frigerio of Broken Open Pictures, Deborah Glover of G2 Dispatch, Arianne Fraser and Delphine Perrier of Highland Film Group, Vanessa Yao Guo and Petr Jákl.

## Why the Queensland shoot matters

Production is currently under way in Queensland, which keeps a high-profile international thriller on Australian soil even though the story is set in central Africa. The sources do not give a start date for filming, a wrap date or a release window, so none of those are confirmed yet.
