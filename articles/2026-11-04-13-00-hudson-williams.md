**Quick summary**

- Yaga creator Kat Sandler says Hudson Williams' casting brought "so many eyes" to the supernatural thriller.
- She told Hindustan Times that the attention gave the series a platform to discuss women's issues.
- Yaga premiered on Crave on 23 October and reaches India on Lionsgate Play on 6 November.
- Williams plays a young man whose disappearance sets the plot in motion.

The Hudson Williams Yaga casting has done more than add a famous name to a poster, according to the show's creator. [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) told Hindustan Times that [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams), the star of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), pulled a much larger audience towards a series built around women's stories. The interview ran ahead of the show's India launch.

## What is Yaga about?

Yaga is a supernatural thriller adapted from Sandler's own play, which retells the Slavic legend of Baba Yaga, the figure said to eat children's bones. The story is set in a small town where two detectives try to work out why a young man has vanished, according to [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/web-series/yaga-creator-kat-sandler-thanks-hudson-williams-for-getting-eyeballs-to-show-helped-womens-issues-get-a-bigger-platform-lionsgate-play-heated-rivalry-101793693956290.html).

Williams plays that missing man. The series stars [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss), [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) and [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo) alongside him.

So how much did one casting change things?

## Hudson Williams Yaga casting: what Sandler said

Sandler says Williams signed on at the point when it was becoming clear that Heated Rivalry, and he himself, were about to be very big. "Hudson signed on right when it was starting to become clear that Heated Rivalry and Hudson were going to be so huge," she told the outlet.

The effect showed up in the original material too. Sandler said many more people have read the play since the teaser appeared, and Hindustan Times notes reports that stagings of the play sold out after Williams was announced. She says she is "thrilled" he joined, because the extra attention meant the team had a platform to discuss bigger women's issues.

That is a notable claim for a thriller adapted from a stage play, and it comes from the person with the most to gain from saying it.

## A star who sends the focus back to the story

Sandler also described how Williams handled his rising profile on set. She says he never overshadowed the narrative and "is so good at redirecting the story back to women". She called the relationship between star and series "symbiotic", adding that he would not have gone to TIFF if Yaga had not been accepted there.

On his behaviour, she was blunt. "He's never been a diva even once," she said, adding that he made the show "sexier, bolder, and weirder". These are the creator's views, offered in a promotional interview, so they read as praise rather than independent assessment.
