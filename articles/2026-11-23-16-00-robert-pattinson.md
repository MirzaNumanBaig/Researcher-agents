**Quick summary**

- A journalist's chat with ChatGPT about missing out on the James Bond role has gone viral, according to UNILAD.
- The joke is part of a wider meme wave since Robert Pattinson was announced as the next 007.
- The UK Government also joined in, with a post about Pattinson applying for 90,000 youth jobs.
- UNILAD names Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner among the other actors who were in the mix for Bond.

[Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has not said a word about it, but the internet has already found a new sport: pretending he is coming for everyone's job. The latest Robert Pattinson Bond memes include a chat with ChatGPT that spread widely over the weekend, reported by [UNILAD](https://www.unilad.com/film-and-tv/man-asks-chatgpt-why-robert-pattinson-got-bond-role-over-him-450756-20261122) on 22 November.

## The ChatGPT conversation

Journalist Tom Scibelli shared screengrabs on his Instagram page. According to UNILAD, he told the chatbot that Pattinson had just been cast as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) and that he had wanted the part himself.

The bot's reply was sympathetic but blunt. It said Pattinson had "several unfair advantages", listing an established acting career, international name recognition and, presumably, an audition. It added that Scibelli was not necessarily wrong to see himself in the running.

Scibelli then complained that he had never been given the chance to audition. The chatbot called that the most frustrating part and joked that Hollywood overlooks "a 30-year-old Italian-American blogger with limited acting experience and acid reflux", before calling the system "fundamentally broken". UNILAD says the exchange later turned darker, with Scibelli threatening to kidnap the actor, which the bot firmly advised against. It ended with a question about what he should eat for dinner.

## A wave of Pattinson jokes

The chat is one piece of a larger pile of memes. UNILAD reports that social media users have joked that Pattinson has also landed the role of the Pope, the chief executive of Nvidia and the voice of Snoopy. Others said they were more worried about Pattinson taking their job than about artificial intelligence.

The joke works because of his résumé. UNILAD points to Batman, [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen) in Twilight, Cedric Diggory in Harry Potter and Antinous in The Odyssey, and asks whether there is any job he has not had.

## Even the British Government joined in

The [British Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_the_United_Kingdom) took part too. UNILAD says it recently announced a youth employment initiative and shared a post reading that Pattinson would apply for all 90,000 jobs created for young people through the scheme. An official account making a gag about a casting announcement is unusual, and it shows how far the Bond news travelled beyond film fans.

## Who else was in the running

UNILAD says Pattinson replaces [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) as 007 and beat out Josh O'Connor and [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) for the role. It also repeats a report that he was paid a large sum to audition, though it gives no figure and we have not confirmed it elsewhere, so treat that detail with caution.

For more on the casting, see our earlier coverage of [why his quirky edge won him the role](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-quirky/) and [the expectations that come with the job](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-baggage/). Everything we publish on the actor is gathered on the [Robert Pattinson](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/) tag page.

## What happens next

The sources do not give a start date for the Bond film. Our earlier report says the production [is eyeing Pinewood Studios](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-pinewood/). Before that, Pattinson leads the Netflix heist film [The Take on 15 January](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-the-take/), directed by Fernando Meirelles. Expect the memes to keep coming until then.
