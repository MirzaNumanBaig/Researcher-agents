**Quick summary**

- Fans of the Paramount+ crime drama MobLand are comparing villain Frankie Cooper, played by [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn), to the [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger) version of the Joker.
- Season 2 ends on Friday, 20 November, according to Startefacts.
- Episodes 7 and 9, both built around Frankie, are rated 8.7 on IMDb, the highest of the series.
- Paramount+ has already ordered season 3, with Tom Hardy confirmed to return.

The Heath Ledger Joker is back in the conversation, this time because of a scooter-riding gangster in a London crime series. As MobLand season 2 closes on Paramount+ this Friday, 20 November, viewers are pointing to Frankie Cooper, the drug boss played by Johnny Flynn, as the character who has stolen the show, according to [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/mobland-s-new-villain-has-fans-dubbing-him-the-next-heath-ledger-joker).

## Why the Heath Ledger Joker comparison started

The label did not come from nowhere. Startefacts traces it to a recap of episode 9 by Show Snob, which said Ledger's Joker was "strong" in the episode. Fans then picked it up, with one comment quoted via CosmicBook saying Frankie "gives the joker vibes."

Startefacts lays out the parallels. In *The Dark Knight*, the Joker crashes a fundraiser for Harvey Dent. In MobLand, Frankie turns up to a black-tie diplomatic party disguised as a waiter. Both characters treat crime as a performance, and both leave the people around them looking slow. Startefacts also notes that in episode 4, Harry Da Souza warns Frankie that his showmanship makes him look like a clown.

## Who Frankie Cooper is

Frankie first appears in the final seconds of the season 2 premiere, known only as "Beast." Kat McAllister, played by [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer), needs someone to take on Harry and the Harrigans. Colin Tattersall, the bent ex-cop played by [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones), brings her Frankie.

Since then, Startefacts reports, he has burned down the Harrigans' London headquarters and staged a coordinated attack on the family set to The Prodigy's "Firestarter." His look, described by Show Snob as a run from 1960s mod culture to Britpop, explains the scooter, which Startefacts compares to *Quadrophenia*.

## The numbers behind the buzz

The ratings back up the noise. Episode 9, "Nervous Breakdown," sits at 8.7 on IMDb. It is tied with episode 7, "Ace of Spades," as the highest-rated episode of the whole series, season 1 included. Both episodes centre on Frankie.

That is a notable result in a cast that includes [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy), [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren). Critics have noticed too. TechRadar called him "the best new villain Paramount+ could have asked for" after episode 2, and Ready Steady Cut said Flynn walks off with every scene he is in.

## Where the comparison breaks down

Startefacts argues the Joker label undersells him. Ledger's character gave a different story about his scars each time he was asked, so he had no fixed past. Frankie has one: a dead son. Episode 9 has him break down in tears over the boy in the middle of threatening people, and the outlet says that scene is why the episode rates so highly.

Its argument is that Ledger's Joker frightened audiences because he was empty, while Frankie is frightening because he is not. That is the outlet's view, not a settled critical verdict, but it explains why viewers keep reaching for the comparison and then qualifying it.

## What happens next

The season 2 finale lands on Friday, 20 November. Startefacts says Paramount+ has already ordered season 3, with Hardy confirmed to return. Flynn also has another high-profile villain role ahead: he is set to play Lucius Malfoy in HBO's [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) series, per the same report.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Frankie Cooper really like Heath Ledger's Joker?
Fans and some reviewers see a likeness in his theatrical, chaotic approach to crime. Startefacts argues the key difference is that Frankie has a painful backstory, which Ledger's Joker deliberately lacked.

### When does MobLand season 2 end?
The finale airs on Paramount+ on Friday, 20 November, according to Startefacts.

### Will there be a MobLand season 3?
Yes. Startefacts reports that Paramount+ has already ordered a third season and that Tom Hardy is confirmed to return.
