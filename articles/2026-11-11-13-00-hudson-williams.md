**Quick summary**

- Crave has released new photos and a synopsis for episode 5 of the mystery-thriller *Yaga*, titled "The Horns".
- The episode debuts Friday, November 13, on Crave in Canada and AMC+ in the United States.
- The stills mostly feature [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) and [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams), who plays Henry Park.
- Crave teased on Instagram that it is "time to learn a little bit more about Henry".

New photos from **Hudson Williams Yaga episode 5** have landed, and they point to a story that turns toward his character. Crave released the stills and a synopsis this week, ahead of the episode's debut on Friday, November 13, according to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/10/yaga-episode-5-photos-synopsis-revealed-see-new-looks-at-hudson-williams-carrie-anne-moss-in-crave-series/).

## What the Yaga episode 5 photos show

The new stills focus mostly on Moss, who plays Katherine, and Williams, who plays Henry Park. Crave captioned the images on Instagram with a line that hints at where the episode is heading: "I think it's time to learn a little bit more about Henry."

That teaser matters because Henry is the character at the centre of the mystery. Just Jared describes him as the young heir to a powerful fishery whose disappearance sets the whole story in motion.

## The synopsis for "The Horns"

The fifth episode is titled "The Horns". Its official synopsis is short: Rapp, played by [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid), draws close to Katherine while circling one question, who is Baba Yaga?

So the episode appears to put Moss and Reid at the front, while the photos suggest Williams still has a prominent place. The outlet does not say how much screen time Henry gets, and neither the synopsis nor the stills answer that.

## Where Yaga stands after the viral episode

*Yaga* is based on the play by [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler), who is also the writer, showrunner and an executive producer on the series. The show follows Rapp, a private investigator who arrives in a small coastal town to look into Henry's disappearance. He runs into a wary local detective named Carson, played by Backo, along with Katherine, described as a charismatic university professor with a taste for younger men, and a web of suspects, secret lives and ancient magic.

Just Jared also notes that the previous episode produced a scene that spread widely on social media. It featured Williams as Henry and Logan alongside [Ezra Franky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Franky) as Pam. We covered that instalment in more detail in [Hudson Williams Plays Two Roles in Yaga Episode 4](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-yaga-episode-4/), and the show's start was also covered in [Hudson Williams' Yaga Sets an AMC+ Launch Record](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-yaga-amc-record/).

New episodes have been arriving weekly for the past few weeks, so this Friday's instalment continues a steady run rather than a one-off drop.
