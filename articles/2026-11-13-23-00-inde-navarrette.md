**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) attended the GQ Men of the Year party in Los Angeles on Wednesday night, according to Reality Tea on Yahoo.
- She wore an all-black Jil Sander outfit: a $650 silk faille bandeau top and a $2,690 lightweight taffeta skirt.
- Yahoo's coverage of the party also lists [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon) and [Chris Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Rock) among the guests.
- The 25-year-old is best known as the star of *Obsession*.

[Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) arrived at the GQ Men of the Year party in Los Angeles on Wednesday night in a head-to-toe black Jil Sander look, and the photos have been circulating widely since. The 25-year-old *Obsession* actor chose a minimalist outfit with a dramatic skirt, which made it one of the more talked-about red carpet looks of the evening.

## What Inde Navarrette wore to the GQ party

According to [Reality Tea, via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/inde-navarrette-jil-sander-high-060937401.html), the outfit came straight from the Jil Sander runway. It paired a bandeau top with a high-low skirt, and both pieces are listed at retail prices.

The top is Jil Sander's Silk Faille Top, priced at $650. The outlet describes it as a black bandeau in wool and silk faille, with fine shoulder straps, a folded-front detail and an open back.

The skirt is the Lightweight Taffeta Skirt at $2,690. It has a high waist, a voluminous draped shape and an asymmetrical opening at the front. Because the hem is cut high at the front and longer behind, it reads as a micro miniskirt from the front, Reality Tea reports. Navarrette was photographed with both hands tucked into the side-seam pockets.

Together, the two pieces cost $3,340 at the listed prices. The skirt alone costs about four times as much as the top, which shows how much of the look rests on its shape.

## The accessories and beauty details

The rest of the outfit stayed in the same palette. [Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/11/13/inde-navarrette-tiny-bandeau-top-bare-midriff-gq-men-of-the-year/) says she added sheer black stockings and black pointed-toe patent leather pumps.

Her jewellery was the one point of contrast: a chunky twisted diamond necklace worn close to the neck, with matching dangling earrings.

For hair and make-up, she wore her black hair half up and half down, with loose strands framing her face. The outlet describes lightly defined eyes, rosy pink lips and a touch of blush.
