**Quick summary**

- Connor Storrie, star of Heated Rivalry, is in talks to join Ryan Gosling in Marvel Studios' Ghost Rider, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Deadline says Storrie is "circling" a role opposite Gosling. The part has not been named, and Just Jared reports it is a villain.
- Ghost Rider is set for release on 28 July 2028, with filming planned in New York in 2027.
- Separately, Gosling's band Dead Man's Bones released a new single, "Lost in the Night", on 27 October.

[Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is in talks to join [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) in Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider*, according to reports on 27 October. The Hollywood Reporter broke the news, and Deadline says the actor is circling a role opposite Gosling. Neither outlet has confirmed which character he would play.

## Who is Connor Storrie, and what role is he in talks for?

Storrie is 26 and best known for the Canadian series [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), according to Just Jared. The site reports that The Hollywood Reporter describes the part as a villain, though which one is undecided.

That leaves a long list of candidates. Bloody Disgusting points to Mephisto, the devil figure who tricks Johnny Blaze into selling his soul, as the obvious name. Just Jared lists others from the comics, including Blackheart, Zarathos, Blackout and Nightmare. Mephisto has appeared once in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, played by [Sacha Baron Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacha_Baron_Cohen) in the series *Ironheart*.

Storrie has a small comic-book credit already, having appeared briefly in *Joker: Folie à Deux*, Just Jared notes. He is currently filming the second season of *Heated Rivalry*. His other projects include *Peaked*, *Turpentine*, *Please* and *The Bell Jar*, plus his own indie film [Transaction Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transaction_Planet), which he wrote and directed.

## What do we know about the Ghost Rider film?

Gosling plays Johnny Blaze, a stunt motorcyclist who makes a deal with the devil and is bonded to a demon, as Bloody Disgusting describes the comic character. He was announced as the lead at San Diego Comic-Con this year, after years of showing interest in the part, according to Just Jared.

[Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy), who directed *Deadpool & Wolverine*, is directing, and Jonathan Tropper is writing. Levy also directed Gosling in the upcoming [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars): Starfighter, per Just Jared.

Bloody Disgusting, citing The Hollywood Reporter, adds that *Dune: Part Three*'s Linus Sandgren is the director of photography and that the film will shoot on film stock. Marvel boss Kevin Feige produces alongside Louis D'Esposito, Stephen Broussard, Levy and Gosling.

Johnny Blaze has been on screen before. Nicolas Cage played him in *Ghost Rider* (2007) and *Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance* (2011), and Gabriel Luna played a different Ghost Rider, Robbie Reyes, in *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.* A Luna spinoff series at Hulu never went ahead.

## Gosling's other news: Dead Man's Bones return

The casting report landed on the same day Gosling's other creative project resurfaced. Dead Man's Bones, his band with Zach Shields, released "Lost in the Night" on Tuesday 27 October, according to Yahoo's report from Reality Tea. It is the duo's first new music since their self-titled debut album in 2009, a gap of 17 years.

The pair worked with longtime collaborator Tim Anderson. After trying several Los Angeles studios, they recorded the song in a day on a farm outside the city, a press release said. The video stars Rain Spencer and Noah Jupe. The band wrote on Instagram that more music is coming soon, though no release details have been given. ## What happens next

Nothing has been signed off publicly. Studio confirmation of Storrie's casting would normally come first, and no announcement date has been given. Filming is planned for New York in 2027, and *Ghost Rider* opens in cinemas on 28 July 2028.
