**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) told Vogue's podcast The Run-Through that he is "okay if people are upset" about his buzzcut.
- He has kept the shaved look since he was first spotted with it in August.
- Storrie says he never styled his hair much, with or without the curls.
- He added that he would do it "again and again and again", according to Yahoo's report of the interview.

Fans hoping [Connor Storrie](https://moduscarry.com/tag/connor-storrie/) will grow his curls back have just been given a clear answer: not a chance. The [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) star said on Vogue's podcast The Run-Through that he enjoys letting people down, and that the backlash to his buzzcut does not bother him.

## What Storrie told Vogue about the buzzcut

According to a [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/connor-storries-not-sorry-hate-150252189.html) report on the conversation with Vogue's Chloe Malle, Storrie was asked directly about the strong reaction to his new look. His reply was blunt: "I love disappointing people." He also admitted he can be contrarian, adding that if people dislike something he is doing, he is fine with that.

Yahoo reports that Storrie has kept the shaved style since he was photographed with it in August. That sighting set off a wave of reaction among fans of the hockey romance, because his tousled golden curls had become closely tied to his role as [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov) and, in Yahoo's words, were arguably part of why viewers fell so hard for both the actor and the series.

## No grand grooming routine either way

The interview also touched on whether the cut had changed how he dresses. Storrie said it shifts the focus of his overall look, but it has not changed what he does with his hair, because he never did much to begin with. "I've never done anything to it," he said of his longer styles. On the odd occasion, he told Malle, he would use a little curl cream or moisturiser and let it air dry.

That is a useful detail for anyone who assumed the curls were the product of a styling team and a long routine. By his own account, the change is about the shape of his silhouette, not about maintenance.

## Why the haircut is still news

Storrie's profile has risen quickly over the past year, and his appearance now draws as much commentary as his acting. We recently reported that he was named in [Vogue's Best Dressed of 2026](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-vogue-best-dressed-2026/), and fan attention has also followed him on trips abroad, as in our story on [his meeting with CORTIS fans in Paris](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-cortis-fans/). The buzzcut sits inside that wider conversation about how much a young actor's look belongs to the audience.

Yahoo notes that the fan reaction has been passionate, and that some viewers are still mourning the curls. Storrie's answer suggests that mourning will continue. He said he is fine with people being upset and would repeat the change "again and again and again".

## What happens next

The source report does not say whether Storrie plans another change of look, and it gives no timing for any new project tied to the haircut. What it does make clear is his attitude: the shaved head is staying for now, and complaints are unlikely to move him.
