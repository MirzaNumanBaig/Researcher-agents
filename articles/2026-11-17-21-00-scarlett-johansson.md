**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) voices pop star Venus Nova in [Ray Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn), an adult animated film directed by [Brad Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Bird).
- It opens in cinemas on 4 December 2026 and streams on Netflix from 18 December, according to MovieWeb.
- Johansson sings five songs in the film and worked with recording artist Kimbra on Venus's pop hits.
- After its BFI London Film Festival premiere on 10 October, MovieWeb reports a 94% Rotten Tomatoes score.

Scarlett Johansson is about to add a singing role to her action résumé. In Ray Gunn, the animated sci-fi noir from Brad Bird, she voices Venus Nova, a famous pop star whose past catches up with her. The film reaches cinemas on 4 December 2026, then Netflix two weeks later, according to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/ray-gunn-upcoming-tease/).

## What Ray Gunn is about

[Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) plays the title character, Raymond Gunn, one of the last human private detectives in Metropia. The city is an alternate future imagined from the viewpoint of 1939, and machines have largely taken over the work of police officers and investigators.

Ray takes a job as bodyguard to Venus Nova, played by Johansson. A vicious murder pulls her devious past into the open, and Ray is soon caught in a web of conspiracy and corruption as he tries to clear her name. MovieWeb describes the story as adult-oriented, mixing classic film noir with science fiction.

The script is by Bird and [Matthew Robbins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Robbins_(screenwriter)). It is an original story, not based on an existing comic, book, game or series, which MovieWeb calls a risk for Netflix and Skydance Animation because there is no ready-made fanbase.

## Johansson's role and the songs

Johansson told People that Venus is a complex character who feels boxed in by fame, something she found easy to relate to. She also sings five songs in the film. According to MovieWeb, she worked with recording artist Kimbra to shape the tone of Venus's commercial pop hits.

Johansson said she knew songs were part of the job and that doing every side of the character was the point of taking it on. In her words, it let her "live the whole experience".

The film adds to a long run of genre roles for her. MovieWeb points to The Island in 2005, Lucy, Ghost in the Shell and, most recently, [Jurassic World Rebirth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World_Rebirth). It also lists The Batman: Part II, The Gauntlet and an untitled Jurassic World sequel as upcoming projects. For more on her recent work, see our [Scarlett Johansson page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/scarlett-johansson/) and our report on [Paper Tiger](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-paper-tiger/).

## A 30-year passion project

Bird has been trying to make Ray Gunn for more than 30 years, MovieWeb reports, citing Variety. The idea grew from a TV trailer for [Blake Edwards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Edwards)'s series [Peter Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Gunn) and the B-52s song "Planet Claire". Studios long saw it as too strange, too expensive and too mature. Bird first pitched it as The Maltese Falcon meets Buck Rogers.

It is also made differently from most big animated films today. Rather than leaning on computer-generated, motion-capture methods, the team used handmade keyframe animation to build its own look. MovieWeb adds that Bird walked away from making The Incredibles 3 at Pixar to do this film instead.

## Early reviews

Ray Gunn premiered at the BFI London Film Festival on 10 October 2026. MovieWeb reports near-unanimous praise and a 94% Certified Fresh score on Rotten Tomatoes. Critics singled out the visual style, the humour, the pace and the blend of science fiction and noir. Some compared the retro-cyberpunk world to [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott)'s [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner). The score is MovieWeb's figure and may move as more reviews arrive.
