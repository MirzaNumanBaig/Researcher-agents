**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) played [Johnny B. Goode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_B._Goode) with John Mayer and [Brad Paisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Paisley) on Saturday 14 November in New York City.
- It closed his annual gala raising money for Parkinson's research, according to Guitar World.
- Fox played a Les Paul rather than the Gibson ES-345 from Back to the Future.
- Fox and Mayer also played the song together in 2013.

## Fox picks up the guitar again

[Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) closed his annual Parkinson's fundraiser with a performance of "Johnny B. Goode" alongside John Mayer and Brad Paisley, according to [Guitar World](https://www.guitarworld.com/artists/guitarists/john-mayer-michael-j-fox-johnny-b-goode). The gala, titled "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson's", was held in New York City on Saturday 14 November. Fox lives with the condition, and the evening raises money for research into it.

Mayer, one of the guests, played a set of his own songs first. He then invited Paisley and Fox up for the finale, a cover of the Chuck Berry classic. Guitar World reports that footage from the night has appeared online, though there is not a lot of it.

## Why this song belongs to Fox

"Johnny B. Goode" is tied to Fox's best-known film role. In Back to the Future, his character Marty McFly plays it on a Gibson ES-345 at the Enchantment Under the Sea dance. Guitar World notes that the scene pushed many viewers to take up the instrument.

This time Fox did not bring out the ES. According to the report, he played a Les Paul, while Mayer used one of his Silver Sky prototypes from the Advanced Research Division.

## A scene that shaped Mayer

Mayer is one of those viewers. He told MSNBC in 2015 that the scene was "the big Rocky moment for a lot of kids who were into music", adding that it was the moment "the nerd gets revenge".

Fox has spoken about this too. He told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) that Mayer had said he started playing guitar because of the Back to the Future scene, and that Mayer is now regarded as one of the best guitarists in the world. Seeing the two share a stage for the song, as Guitar World puts it, completes a circle.

## Not their first duet

The pairing has history. Guitar World says Mayer and Fox played "Johnny B. Goode" together in 2013 as well. Mayer is not the only musician to bring Fox on stage for it. In 2016, Fox joined Coldplay at MetLife Stadium for a song, according to the same report.

Earlier in the week, Fox looked back on another Coldplay moment, saying his cameo during their Glastonbury headline set "scared the hell out of" him, Guitar World reports.
