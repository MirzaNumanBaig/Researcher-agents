**Quick summary**

- [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) plays CIA agent Lee Chesterfield in [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), Martin McDonagh's new period film.
- The story is set largely on Easter Island in the last days before the 1973 Chilean coup, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- McDonagh says he wanted his most improvisational film yet, with at least one unscripted line in most scenes.
- Rockwell says the director has matured and that the film carries real emotional depth.

Sam Rockwell says he could not have made Wild Horse Nine a decade ago. In a new interview feature, The Hollywood Reporter quotes the Oscar winner praising director [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) for a looser, more emotional approach to a film about two CIA agents on Easter Island.

## A different kind of McDonagh film

According to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/martin-mcdonagh-interview-making-wild-horse-9-1236753454/), the film is a period piece set largely on Easter Island just before the 1973 Chilean coup d'état. It still carries the director's usual marks: darkly funny dialogue, profane but lovable characters and sudden bursts of violence.

What has changed is the method. McDonagh, the director of [In Bruges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Bruges) and The Banshees of Inisherin, says he wanted the most improvisational feel of any film he has made. Earlier in his career he stuck to the script "word for word, comma for comma". This time, he says, each scene probably has at least one line that is not his.

He compares the cast to jazz musicians who know the tune but are free to wander around it.

## Rockwell's role

Rockwell has worked with McDonagh for decades and won his Oscar for Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri. In Wild Horse Nine he plays Lee Chesterfield, described as an even-keeled protégé with a big appetite and a conflicted conscience.

His partner on the trip is John Malkovich as Chris Carlisle, a veteran agent with terminal cancer and a failing memory. Malkovich, a two-time Oscar nominee, is the newest member of the company McDonagh likes to build around himself.

Rockwell told the magazine that McDonagh "has matured as a filmmaker", adding that life experience gives the film its depth. "These are two grown men going through it," he said.

## An island worth getting right

McDonagh first visited Easter Island about 15 years ago and has thought about filming there ever since. He dropped several earlier ideas, including a father-and-son bucket-list trip, before settling on this more politically charged story.

Casting director [Sarah Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Halley_Finn) says she studied the island's political history and culture and sought Chilean actors, because she wanted the country represented authentically. Cinematographer Ben Davis says the film has the most dynamic visuals of any McDonagh movie, and that the director now storyboards the whole film before shooting.

Malkovich, for his part, called the part rare. He said he has played at least 50 cameos or underwritten roles, so a character this rich does not come along often.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Wild Horse Nine about?
It follows two CIA agents, a dying veteran and his protégé, on a trip to Easter Island in the last days before the 1973 Chilean coup, according to The Hollywood Reporter.

### Who does Sam Rockwell play in Wild Horse Nine?
He plays Lee Chesterfield, the younger agent and protégé of John Malkovich's Chris Carlisle.

### Is Wild Horse Nine improvised?
Not entirely. McDonagh says he wanted an improvisational feel and that most scenes include at least one line he did not write.
