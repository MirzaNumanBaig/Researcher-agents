## Martin Short joins Santa's workshop

Martin Short has swapped the stage for Santa's workshop in Verizon's latest holiday campaign. According to [Muse by Clios](https://musebyclios.com/film-tv/martin-short-elfs-himself-for-verizon-and-were-so-there-for-it/), which covered the launch on 6 November 2026, the veteran comedian plays a loud, eccentric elf. It is the centrepiece of the Martin Short Verizon ad push this season.

The site has already looked at the casting in [Martin Short Plays an Elf in Verizon's Holiday Ads](https://moduscarry.com/martin-short-verizon-ad/). This piece covers the details that came with the full campaign report.

## A skit built for two generations

Short does not work alone. Muse by Clios says he shares the spot with Megan Stalter and Marsai Martin, two younger stars, in a skit designed to appeal across generations. The look is Dr. Seuss-style, with a storybook feel to the workshop.

Short's lines reference Verizon's low fees and cash-back offers. The cash-back idea is shown as falling "money snow", a visual gag that turns the offer into part of the set.

## Who made it

The spot was directed by Jocelyn DeBoer and Dawn Luebbe, according to the report. The article does not name an outside agency. Ricardo Aspiazu, Verizon's senior vice president of brand creative, credited an in-house crew.

Aspiazu said the aim was "to build a holiday campaign that people would genuinely smile at, not just scroll past."

## Two Latino campaigns alongside it

Verizon is not running a single ad. Muse by Clios reports two Latino-focused campaigns, produced with The Community:

- **"Hot Holidays":** Colombian singer Manuel Turizo parties on the beach.
- **"Dos Vidas, Un Destino":** Sebastián Rulli stars in a spoof of the telenovelas he is known for.

The report does not give a run schedule or list the channels where the spots will air. Holiday campaigns of this kind usually build toward Black Friday and Christmas, but that is general context, not something the source states. We will update this article if Verizon shares dates.
