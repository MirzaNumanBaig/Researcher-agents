**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) and [Alex Winter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Winter) are set to voice Bill and Ted in a new video game, *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure: Arcade*.
- A trailer shows the pair in 16-bit graphics, with local co-op and a single-player mode.
- It is planned for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch, with no release date yet.
- A crowdfunding campaign is due to begin in February 2027.

Keanu Reeves is going back to Bill and Ted, this time as a voice in a video game. According to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/games/trailers/2217156-bill-and-ted-drop-surprise-new-trailer), a new trailer has announced *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure: Arcade*, with Reeves and Winter returning as the original film's heroes. The announcement came on 3 November 2026 and confirms that the next chapter of the franchise is a game rather than a movie.

## What the Bill & Ted arcade game is

The game retells the story of the 1989 comedy in a retro style. [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/bill-ted-new-video-game/) reports that players control Bill and Ted as they travel through time, with local co-op on offer. MovieWeb credits ScreenRant with the exclusive first look.

ComingSoon.net adds that the trailer shows the Grim Reaper approaching the pair before they are whisked off on another time-travelling trip. The footage uses 16-bit graphics and shows the characters causing mayhem across historical periods. It also promises catchphrases from the films.

Players will move through iconic moments from the original movie. Along the way, they take on mini-games to earn respect, according to ComingSoon.net. A single-player mode lets one person control both characters. MovieWeb says the adaptation will bring in new twists and gameplay mechanics.

## Who is making it

Both outlets say Art of Play is developing the game. MovieWeb names the studio as Art of Play Interactive and says it is working in partnership with Roditeli Productions. ComingSoon.net reports that Kevin Roditeli and Roditeli Productions are overseeing production.

Reeves and Winter are both expected to lend their voices. The development team has also teased that more movie stars will be announced later in 2026, per ComingSoon.net. No names have been given.

## How the franchise reached games

The original film followed two high school friends whose band dreams are at risk when they near failing history. MovieWeb notes that [George Carlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Carlin) played Rufus, the traveller from the future who hands them a time machine.

A PC game followed the film in 1989 and reached the NES and other consoles in 1991, MovieWeb reports. The franchise has since added two more movies, a comic book and a short-lived 1990s breakfast cereal. ComingSoon.net says earlier games ran on PC, NES and Game Boy, and that this will be the duo's first appearance on current-generation consoles.

MovieWeb describes *Bill & Ted* as one of Reeves' first major breakout roles. It is also a rare case where the actor's earliest hit is coming back in a brand-new format. For more on his recent projects, see our [Keanu Reeves coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/keanu-reeves/) and our earlier post, [Keanu Reeves Returns for Bill & Ted Arcade Game](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-bill-ted-game/).

## What happens next

The game does not have a release date. Both outlets say a crowdfunding campaign opens in February 2027. ComingSoon.net reports it will run on BackerKit with rewards at different tiers. MovieWeb adds that backers can choose digital or physical editions, plus merchandise and apparel.

The planned platforms are PC through Steam, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Fans of the actor can browse more in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Keanu Reeves in the new Bill & Ted game?

Yes. Both MovieWeb and ComingSoon.net report that Reeves and Alex Winter will reprise their roles by lending their voices to *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure: Arcade*.

### When is the Bill & Ted arcade game released?

There is no release date yet. A crowdfunding campaign is due to start in February 2027, and the game is planned for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch.

### Is this a new Bill & Ted movie?

No. ComingSoon.net says the duo are not returning for another film. The new project is a video game based on the 1989 movie.
