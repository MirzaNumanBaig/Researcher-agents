**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) confirmed on the Happy Sad Confused podcast that Madonna appears in Season 2 of *The Studio*.
- He called her storyline "really wonderful and very meta" and described her as the hardest working person he has met.
- It would be Madonna's first live-action acting role in 24 years, according to ComingSoon.net.
- Season 2 is expected in March 2027, per co-star Ike Barinholtz, but Apple TV+ has not announced a date.

Seth Rogen Madonna news: the star has confirmed she is in the second season of his Apple TV+ comedy *The Studio*, with a storyline that plays on her own history. He made the comments on the Happy Sad Confused podcast, hosted by [Josh Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Horowitz), in an episode that also covered *This Is the End*, *The Invite* and *The Green Hornet*.

## What Seth Rogen said about Madonna

Asked whether viewers should expect Madonna in Season 2, Rogen answered with a plain "Yeah", [ComingSoon.net reported](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/news/2224113-madonna-return-live-action-acting-24-years-meta-update-seth-rogen). Pressed on what it was like to direct her, he said the show is physically demanding, with many takes and constant movement. He said he admires people who work hard, then called her the "hardest working person" he had ever met.

He described her arc as "really wonderful and very meta in a lot of ways". He also admitted the idea felt "very scary", because a big chunk of the season depended on Madonna agreeing to take part.

The role would mark her return to scripted live-action acting after 24 years. Her last such part was in *Swept Away*, according to ComingSoon.net. The same report cites Variety saying she will appear in a two-episode arc inspired by the behind-the-scenes saga around her long-delayed autobiographical biopic, which was eventually scrapped. Her character's details have not been confirmed.

## How the casting came to light

Interest began when Madonna shared an image from Venice on social media, holding a script and hinting at a new project. She was later spotted filming with Rogen, who created and stars in the series. The photo also showed returning cast members Bryan Cranston, Ike Barinholtz, Chase Sui Wonders and Kathryn Hahn, while Michael Keaton and Julia Garner were also seen during production, ComingSoon.net said.

On the Smartless podcast, Barinholtz said Season 2 is expected to premiere in March 2027. Apple TV+ has not yet given an official date, so that timing is a cast member's account rather than a studio announcement.

## Stories from This Is the End and The Invite

The same podcast conversation produced two behind-the-scenes stories. Rogen said *This Is the End* and the drama *12 Years a Slave* were shooting in New Orleans at the same time. The tone on the two sets could hardly have been more different, and he recalled [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) and Steve McQueen wandering over to the comedy to "sulk in". "We'd give them a few drinks and they would kind of perk up," he said, according to [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-sulk-in-seth-rogen-recalls-brad-pitt-and-steve-mcqueen-escaping-12-years-a-slave-gloom/amp/).

Those visits also led to his first meeting with [Michael Fassbender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Fassbender), who later worked with him on *Steve Jobs*. Rogen said Fassbender was relaxed and happy to chat between takes, which helped him settle into a film that rehearsed for six weeks.

On *The Invite*, directed by [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde), Rogen said he made fun of [Edward Norton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norton) straight after Norton's emotional backstory speech. "The look on Edward Norton's face when I did that was like of true shock and betrayal," he said, per [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-true-shock-and-betrayal-seth-rogen-recalls-derailing-edward-norton-emotional-moment-in-the-invite/amp/). He asked Wilde to keep the scene running, and she liked it enough to rewrite the next five pages.

## Looking back at The Green Hornet

[The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/seth-rogen-green-hornet-recalls-abandoned-stephen-chow-version-20261116/) reports that Rogen also reflected on *The Green Hornet*, the Sony reboot he co-wrote and starred in. He said he "hated" the comedic limits of a PG-13 rating, and he spoke about an earlier version that Hong Kong filmmaker Stephen Chow had tried to put together after *Kung Fu Hustle* and *Shaolin Soccer*.
