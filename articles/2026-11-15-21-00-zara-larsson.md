**Quick summary**

- Makeup artist Sophia Sinot told MEGA that Zara Larsson's halo-halo-inspired look for the Manila show came from requests by Filipino fans.
- It was the first food-inspired look she has created for the singer on the Midnight Sun Tour.
- Sinot asked her Instagram followers on the day of the show, and many suggested the Filipino dessert again.
- Fans arrived at the Smart Araneta Coliseum in their own Midnight Sun-inspired glam, according to MEGA.

[Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson)'s Manila concert produced one of the tour's most talked-about beauty moments: a makeup look based on halo-halo, the Filipino dessert of shaved ice, bright toppings and sweet extras. In an interview with MEGA, artist [Sophia Sinot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_Sinot) explained that the idea came from the fans themselves.

## How the Zara Larsson halo-halo makeup idea started

Sinot told MEGA that fans in the Philippines had been sending her direct messages for some time, asking for a look inspired by the dessert. She kept the idea in her notes in case the [Midnight Sun Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midnight_Sun_Tour) ever reached the country. It did.

On the day of the show, she asked her Instagram Story what look to do. According to the artist, halo-halo came up again and again, so she committed to it. She said the dessert offered plenty of bright colours to work with.

The show took place the night before Halloween at the Smart Araneta Coliseum, and MEGA described the crowd as a sea of colour, embellishment and shimmer. Sinot, who is 28 according to the magazine, said she looks out at the front rows before every show and sees fans arriving in full glam, gems on their faces. She called it a fun girls' night out that starts with getting ready.

## A first for the tour

Sinot said Manila was the first time she had done a food-inspired look for Larsson. Until then, her only look that was not nature-based was a Hello Kitty design for the Japan show. She had been working with nature themes that audiences loved, but wanted something different for the Asia and Australia legs of the tour.

The details carried the concept. Sinot said the gems on the face stood in for pinipig, the crisp rice topping often added to halo-halo. She does not sketch looks in advance, preferring to work from a reference and see where it goes.

## The strawberry slip

Sinot admitted to a small mistake. After she posted the look, followers pointed out that halo-halo does not normally contain strawberries. She worried she had picked the wrong reference photo. Other followers then noted that the dessert can be made however a person likes, so, in her words, she was not entirely wrong.

## What went on Larsson's skin

Sinot described the skin preparation as minimal. She cleanses with micellar water, then applies only a [Charlotte Tilbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Tilbury) eye cream and moisturiser, which doubles as a primer. After that she goes straight to foundation, concealer, powders and cream blush. She keeps the layers light so the makeup lasts through a full performance, she told MEGA.
