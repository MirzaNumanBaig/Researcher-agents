**Quick summary**

- [Julia Stiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Stiles) dedicated her dance on *Dancing With the Stars* to late co-star [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger).
- The routine aired on Dedication Night, Tuesday 20 October (US time), and recreated a scene from *10 Things I Hate About You*.
- Several castmates of the 1999 film were in the audience, according to Yahoo.
- The show's next live episode, on 27 October, was set to include a double elimination.

Julia Stiles used Dedication Night on *Dancing With the Stars* to pay tribute to Heath Ledger, her co-star in the 1999 teen comedy [10 Things I Hate About You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Things_I_Hate_About_You). Partnered with professional dancer [Ezra Sosa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ezra_Sosa), she performed on Tuesday 20 October, according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/julia-stiles-dedicates-dwts-dance-183759303.html).

## A famous scene, danced

The pair danced to Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off You". Yahoo reports that the routine was an homage to the scene in which Stiles's character, Kat Stratford, is at soccer practice and Ledger's Patrick Verona serenades her.

Partway through, a photo of Stiles and Ledger together appeared on screen. Over it played audio of Kat's well-known "I Hate You" speech from the film.

## What Stiles said

Speaking to Variety, as quoted by Yahoo, Stiles said she was "bawling" through the choreography and that it brought back "a ton of great memories".

Ledger died in January 2008, aged 28, from an accidental overdose of prescription pills, according to the same report. His final film role was in [The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Imaginarium_of_Doctor_Parnassus).

## The cast turns up

The audience included several of Stiles's castmates. [Joseph Gordon-Levitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gordon-Levitt), [Gabrielle Union-Wade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabrielle_Union) and [Andrew Keegan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Keegan) were there, and the show shared a recap on its Instagram account, Yahoo says. [Upworthy](https://www.upworthy.com/julia-stiles-honors-the-late-heath-ledger-with-10-things-i-hate-about-you-routine-on-dwts/) also reports that the cast reunited to cheer on Stiles and Sosa.

For context, the film grossed more than US$60 million worldwide against a budget of about US$13 million, Yahoo notes. That is roughly a fivefold return on a modest teen comedy, which helps explain why it still has such a loyal audience.

## A second nostalgic week

This was not Stiles's first dip into her film past on the show. Upworthy says that the week before she performed a routine inspired by her 2001 film *Save the Last Dance*, which earned strong reviews.

A separate headline on IMDb says Stiles asked for Michelle Williams' permission before the Ledger dedication. The page could not be read in full, so the details of that conversation are not confirmed here.

## What happens next

The next live episode of *Dancing With the Stars* airs on Tuesday 27 October at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+, with a double elimination planned, according to Yahoo. Whether Stiles and Sosa go through will be clear after that broadcast.
