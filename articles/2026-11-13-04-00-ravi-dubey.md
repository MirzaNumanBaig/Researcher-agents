**Quick summary**

- [Ravi Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey) plays Lakshman in *Ramayana*, and Thai viewers have been praising his looks on social media.
- [Times Now](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Times_Now) reported on 12 November 2026 that one fan called him "damn handsome".
- The film is in its second week, a week after its worldwide release.
- Sacnilk's live estimate put worldwide earnings above Rs 934.55 crore by Day 8.
- News18 and other Indian outlets are also reporting that Dubey has shared behind-the-scenes moments with Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi.

Ravi Dubey has picked up an unexpected following in Thailand. A week after *Ramayana* opened in cinemas worldwide, Thai fans have been posting about the actor who plays Lakshman, and Times Now reported on 12 November that the reaction has spread across social media.

## Thai audiences discover the cast

According to Times Now, the epic has found an enthusiastic audience across Southeast Asia, with Thailand a standout. Viewers there have been sharing screenshots and reactions to the cast, and two actors have become the centre of the attention: Dubey and Kunal Kapoor.

Kapoor, who plays Devraj Indra, has been getting love poems and invitations to visit Thailand, the outlet said. He replied to some of the messages, saying his sister's family lives there and that the country is "like a second home". Dubey's reception has been simpler. Times Now describes fans complimenting his appearance, with one standout post calling him "damn handsome".

## Who Dubey is to Indian audiences

For viewers in India, Dubey is not a newcomer. Times Now notes that he has enjoyed considerable popularity on Indian television, in particular for his stint on *Jamai Raja*. The Thai response, the outlet says, adds another chapter to a career that was built on the small screen.

Dubey's Lakshman has been a talking point locally too. ## The box office picture

The overseas interest arrives as the film's numbers keep climbing. Times Now cited Sacnilk's live estimates, recorded at 7:03 PM IST on 12 November 2026:

- India net collections of Rs 547 crore by Day 8.
- India gross collections of Rs 653.41 crore by Day 8.
- A worldwide total above Rs 934.55 crore, with overseas earnings counted through Day 7.

These are live estimates rather than final figures, and they can shift as distributors report. A separate headline from News18 says the film is nearing Rs 1,000 crore worldwide. We could not open that article, so we have not relied on it for any figures.

## Behind-the-scenes posts with the cast

The same headline, which also ran on The Indian Express, NDTV and NDTV Profit, says Dubey has shared behind-the-scenes moments with co-stars Ranbir Kapoor and [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi). We could not read those pages, so we can't say what the posts show. It does suggest Dubey is using the film's momentum to stay close to his audience, but that is a reading of the headlines, not a confirmed plan.

## What happens next

The next marker is the Rs 1,000 crore mark worldwide, which the film appears close to if the Day 8 estimate holds. Updated collection figures should follow as the second weekend approaches.
