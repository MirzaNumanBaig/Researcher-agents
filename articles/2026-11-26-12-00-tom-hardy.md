**Quick summary**

- [Gregg Sulkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregg_Sulkin) has joined the MobLand Season 3 cast in an undisclosed recurring role, according to Deadline, as reported by Collider.
- He is the first new addition announced for the Paramount+ crime series starring [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy).
- Production on Season 3 began last week, and no release date has been announced.
- Hardy has also told PopDrop that a second season of his period drama Taboo is being written.

[Gregg Sulkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregg_Sulkin) is the first new name confirmed for the third season of MobLand, the Paramount+ crime thriller led by [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy). Collider, citing Deadline, reports that the British actor will play an undisclosed recurring role. Filming on the new season started only last week, which makes this the earliest look at who is arriving.

## Who is Gregg Sulkin?

Many viewers will know Sulkin from Disney Channel's Wizards of Waverly Place, where he played the werewolf Mason Greybeck opposite [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez). Collider notes he recently returned to that role in Wizards Beyond Waverly Place.

His recent work is far darker. He has appeared in two Second World War dramas, World on Fire and Tyler Perry's The Six Triple Eight, and moved into horror earlier this year with Spiders. He is also reported to be joining the third season of the period drama The Forsytes.

## What we know about MobLand Season 3

The series was created by Ronan Bennett and is executive produced by [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie). Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, the fixer who cleans up after the Harrigan crime family. [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) plays Conrad Harrigan and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) plays Maeve Harrigan. Paddy Considine, Anson Boon and Joanne Froggatt complete the main Harrigan and Da Souza line-up.

Spoilers for Season 2 follow. According to Collider, the finale leaves Harry with good reason to question his loyalty. Eddie Harrigan arranged a hit on him that may have killed his wife, Jan, instead. Where Sulkin's character fits into that fallout has not been revealed.

The first two seasons are streaming on Paramount+. Our earlier report covers how [MobLand reached No. 1 on Paramount+ worldwide](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-paramount-number-one/) after the Season 2 finale.

## Hardy on returning to Taboo

While MobLand films, Hardy has also been talking about Taboo. BGR reports that the BBC and FX series, which ended in 2017 and now streams free on Pluto TV, was co-created with his father, Chips Hardy, and Steven Knight. Hardy played James Delaney, an explorer who comes home from Africa to find he has inherited land that the East India Company wants.

Speaking to PopDrop, Hardy said a second season is "happening" and that he and his collaborators are "scribbling away". He added that the long gap has created pressure to do something epic, and that the first season is now a special project he worries about messing with. No timeline has been given. We looked at the same show in our piece on [Taboo and The Revenant as a Tom Hardy watchlist](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-the-revenant-taboo-watchlist/).

## A throwback look at Hardy's transformations

Hello! reports that the 49-year-old shared a throwback Instagram photo on Wednesday showing him as an ageing Al Capone in the 2020 film Capone, in a yellow pinstripe suit with prosthetics. The magazine recalls his earlier physical changes, including gaining 42 lbs for Bronson and losing 30 lbs for [Mad Max: Fury Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Max%3A_Fury_Road).

Hardy told Men's Journal that such changes take a toll as he gets older, saying his joints click more than they used to. Good Housekeeping separately singled out his one-man drama Locke as "Tom Hardy at his best" in a list of psychological thrillers to stream.
