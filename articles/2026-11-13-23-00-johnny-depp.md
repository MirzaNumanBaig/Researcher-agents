**Quick summary**

- Ebenezer, a darker take on A Christmas Carol directed by Ti West, stars Johnny Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge.
- The first reviews, from the Los Angeles Times and The Arts Desk, are unimpressed by the film as a whole.
- Critics single out Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley and Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit as the cast's best moments.
- The film is due for release on 14 November, according to Yahoo.

Johnny Depp's first major leading role in a Christmas release is getting a cool reception. The first reviews of *Ebenezer*, published on 13 November, describe a bleak, gruesome retelling of Charles Dickens' ghost story that struggles to find its audience. This Johnny Depp Ebenezer review roundup looks at what the critics actually said.

## A horror director takes on Dickens

[Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West), known for the horror films *Pearl* and *MaXXXine*, adds jump scares, a cemetery outside Scrooge's window and a crowd of ghoulish figures to the familiar story, according to the [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/movies/story/2026-11-13/ebenezer-review-johnny-depp-rupert-grint-daisy-ridley-ian-mckellen-ti-west). The paper's critic also notes that West has never made a PG-13 film before.

The script opens with Scrooge's late partner [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) as Jacob Marley, shown alive and even meaner than Scrooge. The Los Angeles Times says the film then invents new cruelty for Scrooge's past, including an abusive father and the stabbing of his mentor Fezziwig, played by [Ian Midlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Midlane).

The Arts Desk takes a different view of how much has changed. Its critic calls the film a "fairly faithful rendition" of a story everyone knows, lightly "tickled up" with spooky effects, and questions why it exists at all.

## What reviewers say about Depp

Both reviews focus on how Depp looks and plays the part. The [Arts Desk](https://theartsdesk.com/film/ebenezer-review-needless-remake-starring-unrecognisable-johnny-depp) says he is almost unrecognisable under a top hat, grey hair, sideburns and jowls, and that this is distracting. It also describes a curmudgeonly growl and a permanently sullen glare.

The Los Angeles Times is more pointed. It says Depp's eyes are "empathetic and gooey" and that he makes Scrooge too endearing. It argues the actor's visible need to be loved "hijacks the movie". The same review notes that Depp has spent recent years dealing with his public court cases, and suggests a redemption story may suit him. That reading is the critic's interpretation, not something Depp has said.

## The supporting cast gets better notices

The praise goes mostly to the ensemble. The Los Angeles Times calls [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint) well cast as the underpaid clerk Bob Cratchit. The Arts Desk calls him suitably put-upon and says the supporting cast is generally likeable.

The same outlet reserves its warmest words for McKellen, whose ghostly Marley it describes as a hint of "a more worthwhile film". It also mentions [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough) as the Ghost of Christmas Past, Tramell Tillman as the Ghost of Christmas Present and [Ellie Bamber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellie_Bamber) as Belle.

## Who is it for?

The Los Angeles Times asks the question directly: it finds the film too dark for most children and not frightening enough for adults. It suggests a slice of preteens might be the right audience. That is a problem for a title that opens in the holiday season, when studios usually aim for families.

Yahoo, in a separate piece, describes the film as Depp's return to a lead studio role, and says it opens on 14 November. It reports that his last big studio film was *Fantastic Beasts: The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in Ebenezer?

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) plays Ebenezer Scrooge. The cast includes Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley, Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit, Andrea Riseborough, Tramell Tillman, Sam Claflin and Ellie Bamber.

### Who directed Ebenezer?

Ti West directed it. He is best known for horror films including *Pearl* and *MaXXXine*.

### When is Ebenezer released?

Yahoo reports a release date of 14 November. Check local listings for Australian session times.

### Is Ebenezer suitable for children?

The Los Angeles Times calls it too dark for most kids, citing violence and a bleak tone. Parents should read a classification before booking.
