**Quick summary**

- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) film, with [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as 007, according to an Amazon MGM Studios announcement reported by Power FM SA.
- [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), creator of Peaky Blinders, is writing the script.
- Production is expected to start next year, with a 2028 release currently anticipated.
- Reaction is mixed: some fans want a boycott, while commentators point to Pattinson's varied film work.

Denis Villeneuve has become the face of the most debated casting in recent franchise history. Amazon MGM Studios has announced that the Oscar-nominated filmmaker will direct Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond, and the reaction on Tuesday 17 November was as divided as the news was big, according to Power FM SA and two film commentators.

## What has been confirmed about the Denis Villeneuve Bond film

Amazon MGM Studios named the 40-year-old British actor as the new 007, taking over from Daniel Craig, [Power FM SA reports](https://www.powerfmsa.com.au/trending/entertainment/robert-pattinson-is-the-new-james-bond-but-not-everyone-is-happy-about-it/). Villeneuve directs, and Knight writes the screenplay. The same outlet says production is expected to begin next year, with a 2028 release anticipated.

Villeneuve has publicly backed his lead. Power FM SA quotes him describing Pattinson as having the timeless soul of 007 while bringing something new to the character.

The two men already know each other's work habits. Pattinson plays the villain Scytale in [Dune: Part Three](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune%3A_Part_Three), which Villeneuve directed, as critic Simon Dillon [notes on Substack](https://simondillon.substack.com/p/robert-pattinson-as-james-bond-ok). Miss Flicks also lists that earlier collaboration as a likely factor in the decision.

## Why fans are split

Social media reaction was loud. Power FM SA reports that some critics called Pattinson the worst choice ever, many pointed back to his years as [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen) in the Twilight films, and a few threatened to boycott the franchise.

Supporters point to his range. The same report cites The Batman, The Lighthouse and Tenet as proof he can carry a big film.

Not every sceptic is hostile. Writing for Miss Flicks, one reviewer called the choice a surprise but said the combination of Pattinson, Villeneuve and Knight "fills me with confidence". Their main complaint was that the field had narrowed to five names (Pattinson, Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner) and they had hoped for a curveball.

## The "weird movies" question

A recurring worry is what Bond means for the rest of Pattinson's career. Miss Flicks notes four releases this year: The Drama, The Odyssey, Primetime and Dune: Part Three. It also lists indie work such as The Lighthouse, High Life, Good Time and Mickey 17, and hopes he still finds room for smaller films between Bond outings.

Dillon traces the same range further back. He cites roles for [David Cronenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cronenberg) in Cosmopolis and Maps to the Stars, plus The Lost City of Z and The Devil All the Time, alongside mainstream turns in Tenet and The Batman. His reservations are about the studio's priorities. He suggests Amazon MGM wanted a bankable star rather than an unknown, and he says he would have preferred a less famous face.

The scheduling is another talking point. Miss Flicks says The Batman: Part II is due in February 2028 and that rumours place the Bond film in 2028 as well. That is a rumour, and no release date for the Bond film has been confirmed in the reports we read.

## What happens next

Filming is expected next year, so expect further casting and crew announcements in the coming months. Those will show how much of Villeneuve's usual creative team follows him from Dune to Bond. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who is directing the next James Bond film?

Denis Villeneuve, the Oscar-nominated director of the Dune films, according to the Amazon MGM Studios announcement reported by Power FM SA.

### Who is playing James Bond?

Robert Pattinson, 40, replaces Daniel Craig as 007.

### Who is writing the script?

Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders.

A 2028 release is currently anticipated, with production expected to start next year. No official release date has been given in the reports we read.
