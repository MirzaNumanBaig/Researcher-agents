**Quick summary**
- *The Nice Guys*, the 2016 action comedy starring [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling), is No. 1 on Apple TV's list of most popular movies in the United States, according to MovieWeb.
- The film grossed $62.8 million at the box office against a $50 million production budget.
- It holds a 91% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and is Certified Fresh.
- MovieWeb reports that a sequel has not moved forward, which it links to the modest theatrical result.

*The Nice Guys* has reached the top of Apple TV's US movie chart, a decade after it left cinemas. [Russell Crowe](https://moduscarry.com/tag/russell-crowe/) and Ryan Gosling lead the 2016 buddy comedy, and [MovieWeb reported on 5 November](https://movieweb.com/the-nice-guys-stream-november-2026/) that it now sits ahead of every other title on the platform's list of popular movies in the United States.

It is the latest step in a run for the film. We have already covered how [The Nice Guys climbed the Apple TV charts](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-the-nice-guys-apple-tv/) and how [Body of Lies reached the Apple TV top 10](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-body-of-lies-apple-tv/). Top spot is a clear step up.

## What is The Nice Guys about?

Directed by Shane Black, who co-wrote the script with Anthony Bagarozzi, [the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice_Guys) is set in 1970s Los Angeles. Gosling plays Holland March, a private detective who is not having much success. Crowe plays Jackson Healy, an enforcer with an unpredictable streak.

The two are hired to solve different mysteries, and both lead back to a suspicious death and the disappearance of a young woman. The trail takes them through the city's crime and corruption, and they agree to work together. March's teenage daughter Holly, played by [Angourie Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angourie_Rice), joins the search and, as MovieWeb puts it, proves an invaluable asset.

Joel Silver, the producer behind *The Matrix* and [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard), produced it. The supporting cast includes [Matt Bomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bomer), [Kim Basinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Basinger), [Keith David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_David) and [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley).

## A box office disappointment that critics loved

The numbers tell two different stories. Released in May 2016, the film took $62.8 million worldwide by the end of its theatrical run. With a production budget of $50 million, MovieWeb describes that as an unsuccessful return.

Reviews were far kinder. The outlet says many critics called the Gosling and Crowe pairing brilliant, and others praised the balance of tones. The film now carries a 91% critics' score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) and is Certified Fresh. MovieWeb compares the partnership to the genre-defining duos of [Lethal Weapon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Weapon) and 48 Hours, saying that kind of chemistry had been missing from cinemas for some time.

## How the chart looks

According to the latest update cited by MovieWeb, the film ranks above recent streaming favourites *Prisoners* and *It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown*, as well as newer releases *Matchbox* and *Mayday*. The chart covers the United States only, and the source does not give viewing figures, so it is not possible to say how many people have watched it.

## What happens next

MovieWeb notes that the story left room for a follow-up, but the cool box office response seems to have kept the studio from going ahead. No sequel has been announced in the source material. Whether a streaming revival changes that is, for now, only a question.
