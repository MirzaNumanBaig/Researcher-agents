**Quick summary**

- Netflix's *Unabomber*, starring [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe), has passed 50 million views in its first month, according to Collider.
- The film is sitting at 24% on Rotten Tomatoes after poor reviews.
- Crowe's Western *The Weight* has moved to premium video on demand after a theatrical run of about $3.5 million.
- Collider calls *The Weight* his best-reviewed film in nearly three decades.

Two Russell Crowe films are heading in opposite directions this week. *Unabomber*, a Netflix thriller that critics did not like, is losing momentum after a month on the service, while the Western *The Weight* is picking up viewers on premium video on demand, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/unabomber-russell-crowe-netflix-top-10-drop-october-2026/) on 24 October.

## A hit that critics did not buy

*Unabomber* has gathered more than 50 million views over a month on Netflix, according to the figures Collider cites from the streamer. That makes it a solid result, even though the film was poorly reviewed across the board. Collider says its Rotten Tomatoes score has settled at 24%.

The film also stars [Jacob Tremblay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Tremblay) and [Shailene Woodley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shailene_Woodley). It runs on two timelines: one follows Ted Kaczynski as a troubled college student, the other follows the FBI manhunt for him decades later.

## How it compares with Netflix's other 2026 films

Fifty million views is not a runaway number this year. Collider points out that several Netflix films have reached 100 million views in 2026, naming *The Rip*, *War Machine*, *Thrash*, *Apex* and *Swapped*. *The Last House* has topped 90 million and *The Whisper Man* has passed 80 million.

The clearest comparison is *Animals*, the political thriller directed by and starring [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck). Collider says it is on track to pass 100 million views. It also reports that *Unabomber* needed four weeks to reach a viewing benchmark that *Animals* passed in less than two.

## The Weight finds a second life

The timing helps Crowe. *The Weight*, a Western drama, ran briefly in cinemas and took around $3.5 million before moving to premium video on demand. Collider describes it as his best-reviewed title in almost 30 years and says it is already among the more popular films on several PVOD platforms in the United States, with a larger audience likely in the coming weeks.

We have also covered [how The Weight has climbed the streaming charts](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowes-the-weight-climbs-the-streaming-charts/), and you can follow everything else on the [Russell Crowe tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/russell-crowe/).

## What happens next

Collider expects *Unabomber* to drop down Netflix's rankings as its run winds down, while *The Weight* should keep gaining ground on PVOD.
