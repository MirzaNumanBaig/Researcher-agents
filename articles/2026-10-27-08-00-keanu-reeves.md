**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is set to star in Netflix's action thriller *Maelstrom*, according to Deadline, which cited sources.
- He takes over the lead from [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson), who left the project for reasons that have not been disclosed.
- [Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra), who directed Netflix's *Carry-On*, is behind the camera.
- The script first sold to Warner Bros. in 1993 and was pitched as "Die Hard in a hurricane".
- Netflix declined to comment and no release date has been announced.

Keanu Reeves has signed on to lead *Maelstrom*, a Netflix siege thriller set during a hurricane, after Alan Ritchson stepped away from the role. The news broke on 26 October (US time) and was confirmed by [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/10/keanu-reeves-to-star-maelstrom-netflix-alan-ritchson-1237187931/), which credited Page Six Hollywood with announcing the casting first. Representatives for Netflix declined to comment.

## What Maelstrom is about

According to Deadline, the film follows a Federal Marshal assigned to protect the fiancée of an arms dealer who has turned witness. Over one brutal night, a hurricane cuts off communication and strands the pair without reinforcements while the dealer's men close in on their safe house.

[Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/keanu-reeves-replaces-alan-ritchson-netflix-maelstrom/) reports that the project has been described as "Die Hard in a hurricane", a nod to the 1988 action classic [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard). The same outlet says Reeves would play the Marshal.

## A script with a 33-year history

*Maelstrom* is not a new idea. [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/keanu-reeves-maelstrom-netflix/) reports that first-time writer Greg Fitzpatrick sold it to Warner Bros. in 1993, during a spec script boom in which studios stockpiled action vehicles for stars such as Sylvester Stallone, Steven Seagal and Clint Eastwood. Renny Harlin was reportedly eyed to direct, but the film was never made.

The price is reported differently depending on the outlet. [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/maelstrom-keanu-reeves-alan-ritchson/) puts the sale between $225,000 and $625,000, roughly $500,000 to $1.4 million in today's money. Comic Book Resources gives the $625,000 figure. TheWrap notes that contemporary reports said the script sold for less than the $1 million some had predicted.

Producer Jeff Robinov, who sold the script as an agent at ICM and later ran Warner Bros. film, is now producing it, according to TheWrap. [World of Reel](https://www.worldofreel.com/blog/2026/10/26/keanu-reeves-to-star-in-netflixs-maelstrom-directed-by-jaume-collet-serra-a-die-hard-style-spec-script-dusted-off-from-1993) says it is the first big title under his Netflix deal. Deadline adds that Tabitha Shick of Talking Pictures is producing alongside him.

The current draft is by Mark Bianculli, working from Fitzpatrick's original. Fitzpatrick has since died. TheWrap says Netflix executive Ori Marmur, who also revived *Carry-On*, is behind the resurrection of *Maelstrom*.

## Why Ritchson left

No reason has been given. [The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/keanu-reeves-netflix-maelstrom-20261026/) speculates that Ritchson's move into Sony's *Helldivers* film, where he replaced Jason Momoa, could be a factor, but that is the outlet's guess rather than a confirmed explanation. TheWrap says he may instead be focusing on a sequel to Netflix's *War Machine*, which he led earlier this year.

The Playlist adds that Ritchson is still expected to return for that sequel. Comic Book Resources notes that his series *Reacher* is filming its fifth season and has already been renewed for a sixth.

## Reeves' crowded slate

The casting adds to a busy run for the 62-year-old. Deadline says he recently entered talks to lead James Wan's take on the South Korean thriller *The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil* for Paramount, where he is also set to voice Shadow again in *Sonic the Hedgehog 4*. World of Reel reports that the Wan film is in final negotiations and shoots in January.

Deadline also reports that Reeves has set up an adaptation of Oliver Lowe's upcoming novel *Kill Current: A Jon Hendricks Thriller*, the first project under his new company, Two Dragonflies, with partners The Arena and Zando. Other titles include the Warner Bros. sci-fi film *Shiver* and Ruben Östlund's *The Entertainment System Is Down*.

## What happens next

No release date or start of production has been announced for *Maelstrom*. Netflix has not commented on the casting, so any further supporting cast, a shooting schedule and the reason for Ritchson's exit remain to be confirmed.
