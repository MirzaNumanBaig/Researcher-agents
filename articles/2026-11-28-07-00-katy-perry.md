**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) and [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) travelled from Paris to Venice on the Simplon-Orient-Express, according to Page Six.
- They stayed in the private suite L'Observatoire, a standalone carriage on the Orient Express.
- The Times reports a one-way ticket costs around US$105,000, per Page Six.
- Perry documented the trip on Instagram and joked about the "Euro debut" of her new bob.

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau have spent a night aboard one of the world's most famous luxury trains. [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/27/celebrity-news/inside-katy-perry-and-justin-trudeaus-80k-romantic-overnight-train-ride-through-europe/) reported on 27 November that the pair rode the Simplon-Orient-Express from Paris to Venice in a private suite, on a journey that reportedly cost more than US$100,000.

## What the Orient Express suite includes

The couple booked L'Observatoire, a relatively new private carriage run by the Orient Express brand, which is known for restored vintage carriages and fine dining. Page Six describes a bedroom with a retractable skylight, a lounge with a curated vinyl collection that converts into a dining space, an en-suite marble bathroom, a hidden library and a small tea room.

The fare also covers a personal steward on call around the clock, private meals served in the suite, champagne, wine and premium spirits, loungewear, toiletries, gifts and private transfers to and from the station. Page Six cites The Times for the price, saying a one-way ticket costs about US$105,000. That figure is the newspaper's, relayed second-hand, and neither the singer nor the train operator has confirmed what this particular booking cost.

## How Katy Perry shared the journey

Perry posted the trip to Instagram, according to Page Six. One clip shows her in the suite's standalone bathtub, which has a stained-glass window and a view of the passing scenery. The singer, 42, and Trudeau, 54, also passed the time with card and board games.

Both dressed up for the occasion. Perry wore a champagne-coloured silk and lace minidress with white pointed-toe heels, and joked that the journey was the "Euro debut" of her new bob. Trudeau, who was Canada's prime minister until 2025, wore a white tuxedo.

## How the relationship got here

Page Six traces the timeline back to July 2025, when the two were first linked, shortly before Trudeau went to Perry's Lifetimes Tour concert in Montreal. Three months later they were photographed kissing on her yacht off the California coast. They then confirmed the relationship by holding hands in Paris while celebrating her birthday.

Paris is also where this latest trip began, which gives the train ride a certain symmetry. Page Six does not say whether the journey marked a particular anniversary, and we are not suggesting one.
