**Quick summary**

- Free tickets for the Druski New Year's Eve Chicago show at Navy Pier go live on November 25 at 10 a.m. Central, according to AllHipHop.
- A second batch is released at the same time on Wednesday, December 2.
- Each person can reserve up to two seats through the city's event page, and tickets go in the order they are claimed.
- Doors open at 6 p.m. on December 31 at Crystal Gardens, an hour before the music starts.
- Fans without a ticket can still watch screens at the pier and see a free fireworks and drone show at midnight.

[Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) will host Chicago's New Year's Eve celebration at Navy Pier, and the free tickets for the indoor show are being released in two rounds. The first round opened on November 25 at 10 a.m. Central, according to [AllHipHop](https://allhiphop.com/news/druski-hosts-chicagos-new-years-eve-show-when-free-tickets-drop/). The second follows on December 2.

We have already covered the [announcement that Druski will host ABC's Chicago special](https://moduscarry.com/druski-chicago-new-years-eve/). This piece covers the practical side: how to get in, what the night looks like and where to go if you miss out.

## How the free tickets work

The city is handing out seats through its event page, and there is no payment step. AllHipHop reports a limit of two seats per person, with tickets released on a first-come, first-served basis.

Anyone under 18 must attend with a parent or legal guardian. Ticket holders can enter Crystal Gardens, at 600 E. Grand Ave., from 6 p.m. on December 31.

The city is also setting aside a raised viewing area for guests who use seated mobility devices. It asks for at least 72 hours' notice for access requests, so anyone who needs that space should ask well ahead of the night.

## What is on the bill

The audience inside Crystal Gardens may appear on camera when Druski hosts Chicago's live segments of ABC's Dick Clark's New Year's Rockin' Eve with [Ryan Seacrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Seacrest) 2027. Mayor Brandon Johnson said in announcing the event: "Chicago knows how to put on a show, and this New Year's Eve will be no exception."

The indoor stage lineup, as listed by AllHipHop, includes DJ Mike P, Opal Staples and [Isaiah Sharkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaiah_Sharkey). The 911 Mambo Orchestra and house producer Steve "Silk" Hurley are also booked.

One slot is still a secret. The city says a Grammy winner will headline, but the name has not been announced.

## If you miss out on a ticket

A ticket is not the only way to be at the pier. Local acts start playing at 6 p.m., and screens will carry the indoor set with captions and sign language interpretation.

At midnight the pier will put on a free drone and fireworks show over Lake Michigan. A separate free fireworks display will go off over the Chicago River from bridges between Franklin Street and Columbus Drive.

Last year the city's first ABC countdown was led by Chance the Rapper from a stage beside the river, and it drew fans to the Riverwalk without tickets. This year the ticket rules apply only to the indoor part of the night at the pier, per AllHipHop.

## What happens next

The second ticket round opens on December 2 at the same time as the first, so anyone who misses today's batch has one more chance. The broadcast then starts at 7 p.m. Central and 8 p.m. Eastern on December 31 on ABC. The headline act is still to be named.
