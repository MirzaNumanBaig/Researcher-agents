**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) reportedly attended the Broadway opening night of "Downstate" on 16 November (US time) to support her estranged husband, [Thomas Sadoski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Sadoski).
- Page Six, citing a source, says she brought a bouquet of flowers and stayed at the after-party until about 11 p.m.
- The couple announced their separation in September after nine years of marriage and say they remain on good terms.
- Seyfried skipped a Los Angeles awards-season event to be in New York.

## Amanda Seyfried and Thomas Sadoski: a supportive night out

Amanda Seyfried turned up to the Broadway opening of "Downstate" carrying flowers for Thomas Sadoski, who stars in the play, according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/16/celebrity-news/amanda-seyfried-supports-ex-husband-thomas-sadoskis-broadway-opening/). The outlet cites a source for the account, so the details come from an insider rather than an on-the-record statement from either actor.

The same source told Page Six that Seyfried worked the room before curtain. Just seconds before the show began, she reportedly hurried to Alec Baldwin's seat for what looked like an intense catch-up.

## Choosing New York over Los Angeles

Seyfried reportedly gave up another big event to be there. Page Six says she skipped the Governors Awards, the pre-Oscars gathering in Los Angeles where [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim) were among the guests.

The timing is notable because Seyfried has been in the headlines for her part in "You Can See Everything", the documentary about Elizabeth Holmes. She is also [due to host Saturday Night Live](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-snl/) on 5 December, per our earlier report.

## A heavy play, a light after-party

"Downstate" deals with four convicted sex offenders who share a state-supervised group home after serving their prison sentences. Page Six notes the subject is heavy, yet reports that Seyfried kept spirits high at the after-party at Michael's, beaming as she posed for photos with Sadoski.

## What the pair have said about their split

The two announced their separation in September, after nine years of marriage. They have two children. At the time, they told People: "We have been separated for a while, and it's proven to be the right move for us and our family unit. We've got each other forever — and it's GOOD," as quoted by Page Six.

Seyfried also told WWD that she is being selective about work, sticking to projects in her hometown of New York and nearby New Jersey so the family can stay close to each other, according to the same report.

Last week she posted a black-and-white photo to Instagram Stories showing herself and Sadoski smiling with their hands wrapped around each other. She then shared a quote from clinical hypnotherapist Olivia Howell about making divorce "boring" and normal, Page Six reports.
