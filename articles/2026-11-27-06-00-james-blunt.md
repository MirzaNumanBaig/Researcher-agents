**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) is releasing a charity Christmas single called "Another Year".
- Profits from CD sales will go to Médecins Sans Frontières UK, with a minimum of £3 donated per sale.
- The video was filmed at his London pub, The Cock and Bottle, with many stars from The Celebrity Traitors.
- The CD is due on 11 December and can be pre-ordered on his website now.

[James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has brought a crowd of familiar faces from [The Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) into his London pub to film a video for a new charity Christmas single, "Another Year". According to [Rayo](https://www.hellorayo.co.uk/forth/entertainment/music/james-blunt-christmas-single-another-year), the song will raise money for the medical charity Médecins Sans Frontières UK, also known as Doctors Without Borders, and the CD is due on 11 December.

## Who turned up to film

The "[You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful)" singer shared footage from the filming day at The Cock and Bottle. Rayo reports that [Jerry Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Hall), Richard E Grant and King Kenny were among those who joined him, along with [Davina McCall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davina_McCall), Dame Prue Leith, [Spencer Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Matthews), [Denise Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denise_Welch), [Cat Deeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_Deeley) and [Ben Shephard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Shephard).

Stars from the first series of the show also appeared, including [Clare Balding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_Balding) and [Kate Garraway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Garraway). Several of the guests posted about the day themselves. Welch wrote that it was a Monday morning "up there" with the best, and joked about Matthews' attempts to become Blunt's best friend.

## A game of Pass The Phone

Blunt also posted a lighter clip in which he tries to play "Pass The Phone" with friends who pretend to be too busy enjoying a pint to take part. He says he is passing the phone to his favourite Traitor, and Kenny appears on screen. Kenny passes it straight back. Deeley then tells him to "put a headband on and jog on", a nod to the sweatbands he wore through the series, as Rayo describes it.

Blunt has become one of the most talked-about faces of the show this year. We have covered that side of the story in [James Blunt's wife thought he won Celebrity Traitors](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-celebrity-traitors/) and [James Blunt touched by Romesh's Celebrity Traitors donation](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-romesh-ranganathan/).

## The song is not a jolly one

Despite the party atmosphere of the shoot, the single has a serious subject. In a video about the track, Blunt said Christmas is happy for some but, for others, a time to remember people who have died, who cannot be there, or whom they would rather not see. "Another Year", he explained, is about a father telling his children he will not be home for Christmas because he is leaving their mother.

Blunt's caption invited fans to "have yourself a James Blunt kind of Christmas" by buying the song from his website. CD sale profits will be donated to MSF UK, with at least £3 from each sale.

## What happens next

The CD is set for release on 11 December and is available to pre-order now on Blunt's website. Rayo says it will initially be sold only as a CD. The sources do not say when, or whether, the song will reach streaming services, so that remains unconfirmed.
