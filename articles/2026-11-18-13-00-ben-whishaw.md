**Quick summary**

- [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) season 2 arrived on Netflix on Thursday 5 November and opened with 3.8 million views, according to Startefacts.
- Season 1 opened with 10.8 million views, so the new run started about 65 per cent lower.
- The second season landed at No. 4 on Netflix's English-language TV list for 2 to 8 November.
- Critics quoted by the outlet rate season 2 as good as, or better than, the first.
- Netflix's chart for 9 to 15 November will show the first full week of season 2.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) and [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) are back in the Netflix spy thriller, and the early numbers for the second season are softer than the first. [Black Doves](https://moduscarry.com/black-doves-season-3/) season 2 released all six episodes on Thursday 5 November and opened with 3.8 million views and 19.9 million viewing hours, according to a report by Startefacts.

## How the opening compares

On Netflix's English-language TV chart for 2 to 8 November, the season placed fourth. It sat behind a Natalee Holloway docuseries, a documentary about [Matthew Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Perry) and the third season of Nobody Wants This, as Startefacts tells it.

Season 1 also premiered on a Thursday and opened with 10.8 million views. Startefacts puts the drop at roughly 65 per cent, and says the Natalee Holloway title alone drew 12.9 million views, more than three times Black Doves. The report adds that season 2 ranks 13th among 14 comparable Netflix thrillers on opening numbers. Those comparisons are the outlet's own and Netflix has not been quoted on them.

## Reviews are warmer this time

The dip is unusual because the notices are positive. Startefacts cites Inverse, which calls season 2 even more of a blast, and TheWrap, whose headline says the show gets even better. It also cites Tom's Guide, which gave the season four out of five stars and described it as an answer to [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses) withdrawal.

The returning cast includes Whishaw, Knightley and [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire), who still runs the Doves. New names are [Neve Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Campbell), [Ambika Mod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambika_Mod), [Sam Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Riley) and [Sylvia Hoeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvia_Hoeks). Netflix renewed the series in August 2024, before season 1 had aired.

## Why the numbers may be low

Startefacts points to three factors. The first is the wait: season 1 premiered in December 2024 and the second run came almost two years later. The second is timing, since season 1 was a Christmas thriller released at Christmas, while season 2 opened in early November without that seasonal hook. The third is competition from Netflix's own true crime and documentary titles in the same week.

These are the outlet's reading of the figures, not findings from Netflix. The report also notes that interest has not vanished in Britain. It says Black Doves was the country's most-searched series on 8 November, ranked No. 2 on Netflix's UK list for the week and debuted at No. 1 in Germany.

## What happens next

Season 1 grew in its second week, rising from 10.8 million views to 14.6 million and reaching No. 1, according to the same report. Startefacts says season 2 would need to nearly quadruple to match that second-week figure, which makes a repeat look unlikely.

Netflix has not announced a third season. Our earlier coverage of [creator Joe Barton's comments on season 3](https://moduscarry.com/black-doves-season-3/) says he is already working on ideas with the streamer's support, though nothing has been confirmed.
