**Quick summary**

- *A Kidman Carol*, a comedy built on Charles Dickens's *A Christmas Carol*, opens off-Broadway at New World Stages on 30 November, according to Broadway.com.
- [Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle) plays [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp) plays [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett).
- Kevin Zak wrote the play, and Skylar Fox directs a limited run that Broadway.com says continues to 30 December.
- Vogue spoke to the writer and both stars on 26 November about why the two Australian actors were the obvious pair.

Nicole Kidman is the ghost of awards season past, present and future in a new stage comedy. *A Kidman Carol* begins its off-Broadway run at New World Stages this week, with Mindelle as Kidman and Sharp as Blanchett, and Vogue has now published a conversation with the people behind it.

## The idea started as a meme

Zak is the playwright behind last year's *Ginger Twinsies*, a satire of *The Parent Trap*, and he is better known online for pop culture memes. He told [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/marla-mindelle-josh-sharp-and-kevin-zak-a-kidman-carol) that the play began as a joke: a Dickens spin in which Blanchett is visited by Kidman to learn the true meaning of awards season.

A friend who runs the Alleyway Theatre in Buffalo, New York, offered him a few nights in 2024 to workshop anything he liked, and this was the show he wanted to see. According to Broadway.com, a developmental run last year sold out.

## How the premise works

Broadway.com describes the setup this way: Blanchett, a self-described Scrooge, is visited on Oscar's Eve by three spirits of Kidman. They walk her through her past in the Australian outback, a chaotic present in Hollywood and a frightening future.

Sharp told Vogue that Blanchett works as the curmudgeon because she is known for intensity and is harder to impersonate than Kidman. In his view, Kidman is the one who can agree the world could be better while staying more hopeful about it.

## Two stars, two kinds of performance

Zak offered a simple split. Kidman's characters, he said, tend to get hurt, as in *Big Little Lies*, while Blanchett's tend to get angry, as in *Tár*, *Carol* and *Notes on a Scandal*. He also noted that the two have never made a film together.

Mindelle said she rewatched [*To Die For*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Die_For) while preparing and now ranks it her favourite Kidman film, ahead of *Moulin Rouge!*. She described Kidman as having about 10 per cent more camp than Blanchett: someone who knows what she is serving and gives it a wink. Asked when they realised Kidman was a star, Sharp and Zak both pointed to her turn as Dr Chase Meridian in *Batman Forever*.

The trio also named their favourite surprise Oscar wins. Sharp chose *Moonlight*, and Zak picked [Youn Yuh-jung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youn_Yuh-jung)'s *Minari* win, remembering her acceptance speech, in which she scolded [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt), a producer on the film, for never visiting the set.

## Who is behind the show

Both leads come from New York's alternative comedy scene. Vogue notes that Mindelle's hit *Titanique* and Sharp's A24-backed film *Dicks: The Musical* both began in underground comedy clubs. Mindelle joked that the pair texted each other when Zak first asked them to join a five-performance workshop, agreeing to do it if the other did. Sharp's reply to what could go wrong: a full six-week run at what he called the off-Broadway mall, a nod to the five-theatre layout of New World Stages.

## What happens next

Broadway.com lists opening night as 30 November, with performances starting on 16 November and the limited engagement running through 30 December. Further casting is still to be announced, and tickets were listed from about US$53 on the show's page. Neither Kidman nor Blanchett has commented on the play in the sources we could read.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is A Kidman Carol?

It is an off-Broadway comedy by Kevin Zak that reworks Dickens's *A Christmas Carol* around Nicole Kidman and Cate Blanchett. Blanchett plays the Scrooge figure and is haunted by three versions of Kidman.

### Who stars in A Kidman Carol?

Marla Mindelle plays Nicole Kidman and Josh Sharp plays Cate Blanchett. Skylar Fox directs, and more cast members are yet to be announced.

### Where and when can I see it?

It plays at New World Stages in New York, with opening night on 30 November and a run to 30 December, according to Broadway.com. Check the venue for current ticket availability.

### Is Nicole Kidman involved?

The sources we read do not say so. The show is a parody performed by other actors.
