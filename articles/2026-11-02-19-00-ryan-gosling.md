**Quick summary**

- Three children's books starring Kade Auberon, the character [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) plays in *Star Wars: Starfighter*, are listed for 20 April 2027.
- The film is due on Memorial Day weekend, 28 May 2027, so the books give readers a first look more than a month early.
- Other tie-ins include a colouring book, sticker book, visual dictionary and a novelisation, according to The Direct.
- Separately, Scott Speedman has praised Gosling's early comic work, and Gosling's band Dead Man's Bones released a new single in October.

## Ryan Gosling Starfighter books: what has been announced

Fans will meet Ryan Gosling's new *Star Wars* hero on the page before they see him on screen. According to [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/star-wars-starfighter-ryan-gosling-character-lucasfilm-spin-offs-movie), Penguin Random House has listed three children's books about Kade Auberon, the lead character in [*Star Wars: Starfighter*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars%3A_Starfighter), all due on 20 April 2027.

That is roughly five weeks ahead of the film's 28 May release, directed by Shawn Levy. The official synopses describe Kade as "a rogue with a mysterious past" who is swept into a dangerous, galaxy-spanning adventure.

The three titles are pitched at different ages:

- *Rumble with the Rock Pirate*, a Little Golden Book hardcover for ages two to five.
- *Risk and Reward*, a full-colour paperback storybook for ages three to seven.
- *Rock Pirate Adventures*, a Step 3 Step into Reading book for ages five to eight.

Two of the three mention a "Rock Pirate", and the title of the first suggests Kade will clash with whoever or whatever that is. The Direct notes the books may offer backstory for Kade that the film does not, though that is the outlet's reading of the listings rather than anything confirmed.

## The wider Starfighter publishing push

The children's stories are only part of the plan. The Direct lists a run of other releases:

- *Art of Coloring: Star Wars: Starfighter*, a 128-page book with 100 images, on 23 March 2027.
- *Outer Rim Outcasts*, a Step 2 Step into Reading book for ages four to six, on 20 April 2027.
- *Star Wars Starfighter Ultimate Sticker Book* from DK, with more than 200 stickers, on 4 May 2027.
- *DK Super Readers Level 3 Star Wars Starfighter*, a 48-page reader for ages eight to 10, on release day.
- *Star Wars Starfighter Visual Dictionary*, 176 pages, by Pablo Hidalgo, featuring new art and behind-the-scenes images.
- An official novelisation by Delilah S. Dawson on 22 June 2027.

The Direct says Matt Smith and Mia Goth are expected to play the film's antagonists. It also reports that Kade will help protect his young nephew in the story, which fits the family-friendly tone of the books.

## Why Lucasfilm wants an early start

The timing matters for the franchise. The Direct describes the return of [*Star Wars*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) to cinemas earlier this year, with *The Mandalorian and Grogu*, as a disappointment for many fans, and says the live-action Disney+ output appears to be winding down after *Ahsoka* season two. That is the outlet's assessment, but it explains why a new generation of young readers is being courted early.

A trailer exists but has only been shown to a limited audience at D23 in August, according to The Direct. It leaked online and was quickly removed. An official public release has not been announced.
