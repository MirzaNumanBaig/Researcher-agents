**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) believes someone knows where the cherry-red Gibson ES-345 from *Back to the Future* is and is staying silent, according to Guitar Player.
- The guitar has not been seen in more than four decades.
- Gibson launched a global search in June 2025 but has not found the original instrument.
- A documentary about the hunt, *Lost to the Future*, is in the works, though Fox doubts it will arrive soon.

The guitar Marty McFly played in *Back to the Future* has been missing for more than 40 years, and its owner on screen thinks he knows why. In his memoir, Michael J. Fox suggests that someone saw the instrument's future value, took it, and has kept quiet since, [Guitar Player reports](https://www.guitarplayer.com/guitarists/michael-j-fox-thinks-he-knows-what-happened-to-the-back-to-the-future-gibson).

## What Fox says happened to the guitar

In his memoir, *Future Boy: Back to the Future and My Journey Through the Space-Time Continuum*, Fox writes that he wishes he could claim the guitar but never had the foresight to see how valuable it would become. According to the extract quoted by Guitar Player (via Parade), he adds that someone else did see it coming and took it.

Fox says that person may be holding it in an active collection or have it stashed in an attic or home studio, and is not speaking up. His own guess at the motive is wry: they may be waiting for the statute of limitations on vintage guitar theft to run out. That is his theory, not an established finding, and nothing in the report says the guitar was stolen.

## Where the guitar came from

Norman's Rare Guitars loaned the instrument to the production, according to Guitar Player. The props team had planned to use a Stratocaster or a Gretsch with a Bigsby, then changed course at the last minute. The switch also produced an onscreen mistake that has bothered guitar fans ever since.

The scene still left its mark. The magazine notes that John Mayer and Coldplay's Chris Martin have both cited Marty's [Johnny B. Goode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_B._Goode) performance as an influence on their decision to pick up the guitar. Fox and Mayer have since played the song together, which we covered in [Michael J. Fox and John Mayer Play Johnny B. Goode Again](https://moduscarry.com/michael-j-fox-johnny-b-goode-john-mayer/).

## The search so far

In June 2025, ahead of the film's 40th anniversary, Gibson started a worldwide hunt for the ES-345. It brought in the team behind the Lost Bass Project, which helped recover [Paul McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_McCartney)'s stolen Höfner bass after 51 years.

The effort drew headlines and plenty of tips. It did locate the second guitar used in *Back to the Future Part II*. The original from the 1985 film is still missing.

One possible clue is the guitar's block parallelogram inlay at the 12th fret, which differs from the more familiar split parallelogram. Guitar Player says that detail could help separate the real instrument from lookalikes and rule out false leads.

## Why that scene mattered to Fox

Fox prepared for the role at night after long days on the set of [Family Ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties), according to the report. For the "Enchantment Under the Sea" dance, he says he asked the cinematographer and choreographer to let him riff through his favourite players: [Jimi Hendrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimi_Hendrix) playing behind the head, [Pete Townshend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Townshend)'s windmill and [Eddie Van Halen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Van_Halen)'s hammer-on. He says the team was open to it.

## What happens next

Gibson is making a documentary, *Lost to the Future*, about the search. Fox is doubtful about timing. As he puts it, the release will apparently come after the guitar is found, "if that ever happens."

## Frequently asked questions

### What guitar did Marty McFly play in Back to the Future?

A cherry-red Gibson ES-345, loaned to the production by Norman's Rare Guitars, according to Guitar Player.

### Has the original guitar been found?

No. Gibson's 2025 search found the second guitar used in *Back to the Future Part II*, but not the one from the 1985 film.

### Does Michael J. Fox own it?

No. He writes that he wishes he did, and believes someone else took it.

### Is there a documentary about the search?

Gibson has begun work on *Lost to the Future*. Fox says its release will follow the guitar's discovery, if that happens.
