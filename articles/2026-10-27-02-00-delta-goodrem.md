**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) is reportedly being lined up to front BBC One's New Year's Eve concert on 31 December, according to wheretowatch.tv.
- The report is unconfirmed. Neither the BBC nor Goodrem's representatives have confirmed the schedule.
- The proposed show would mix her biggest hits, new cover versions and guest duets.
- Goodrem is currently competing on the 2026 series of [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing).

Australian singer-songwriter Delta Goodrem may be heading into the BBC's biggest night of the year. Entertainment site wheretowatch.tv reports that discussions are under way for her to headline a New Year's Eve concert on BBC One on 31 December. The broadcaster has not confirmed it.

## What the report says

According to wheretowatch.tv, which describes the story as an exclusive, Goodrem would front her own prime-time special as the clock strikes midnight. The outlet says the format would pair a set of her greatest hits with new cover versions and high-profile duets with guest stars.

The site stresses that talks are only "apparently" under way. Treat it as a rumour until the BBC or Goodrem's team says otherwise.

## A big year on British television

If the plan goes ahead, it would cap a busy year for Goodrem in Britain. wheretowatch.tv notes that the 41-year-old represented Australia at the Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna earlier this year, where her ballad "Eclipse" finished fourth in the Grand Final. The outlet describes that as one of Australia's best results in the contest.

She also performed at BBC Radio 2's Radio 2 in the Park festival. Now she is on Strictly, where the same report calls her the bookmakers' favourite to win the Glitterball Trophy.

Her run has not been smooth. She rested during rehearsals with a painful rib injury, then returned to earn the first 10 of the 2026 series. ## The BBC's New Year's Eve tradition

The BBC has handed this slot to a music name for several years running. wheretowatch.tv lists the recent hosts:

- 2025-26: [Ronan Keating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Keating)
- 2024-25: [Sophie Ellis-Bextor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Ellis-Bextor)
- 2023-24: [Rick Astley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Astley)
- 2022-23: [Sam Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ryder)
- 2021-22: Years & Years, with guests Kylie Minogue and the Pet Shop Boys

The outlet calls the concert one of the most-watched entertainment broadcasts on British television each year.
