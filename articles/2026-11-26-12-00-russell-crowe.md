**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) plays the coach in *Beast*, a mixed martial arts drama directed by Tyler Atkins, and co-wrote the film's story, according to The Canberra Times.
- [Daniel MacPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_MacPherson) stars as Patton James, a retired MMA fighter from Port Kembla who returns to the cage to save his brother.
- The story was first set in Buffalo, New York, before the team moved it to the New South Wales Illawarra region.
- The Canberra Times headline says MacPherson broke his pelvis for the role.

Russell Crowe has a hand in more than the performance in *Beast*, the new Australian fight film. The Oscar winner plays a cantankerous coach and is credited with co-writing the story, The Canberra Times reported on 25 November 2026, in a feature that follows the production from its Port Kembla locations to its lead actor's training.

## What Beast is about

The film follows Patton James, played by MacPherson, a mixed martial arts star who has become a fisherman and recluse. He is lured out of retirement to save his brother. The Canberra Times describes it as a gritty workman's tale in the shadow of *Rocky*, and says the trailer promises an exciting if formulaic fight film with plenty of bone-breaking action.

Crowe's role is the coach who guides him. MacPherson told the paper that the character is defined by his relationships with the men played by Crowe and [Mojean Aria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojean_Aria), and also by the women in his life.

## From Buffalo to Port Kembla

The origin of the film is a small piece of Crowe-and-MacPherson history. MacPherson said that [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio), who worked with both actors on the earlier film *Land of Bad*, came to them with an MMA script he thought suited the pair. That version was set in Buffalo, New York.

MacPherson said neither he nor Atkins knew upstate New York, but they did know what it was like to grow up on Australia's east coast. The story moved to Port Kembla, a steel town south of Sydney. Atkins told the paper he fought hard to shoot there, and wanted a tough, old-school Australian hero who still works hard and lives outside Sydney. The opening shots glide over the steel mill and suburban streets to set that scene.

## The training behind the fights

The Canberra Times reports that MacPherson, an experienced endurance athlete, spent several years in MMA gyms around the world to prepare. He described the physical side as completely alien, saying he had to teach his hips to move in directions he did not know they could go, and start again from the beginning. The paper's headline says the star broke his pelvis for the role, though the portion of the feature available to us does not detail how or when.

MacPherson also said the character is meant to be a strong, upstanding provider and protector. His greatest strength, in his words, is also his greatest weakness: a moral compass and a sense of responsibility for the people he loves.

## Why MMA on screen now

The feature places the film in a wider trend. Mixed martial arts has moved from the shock-value spectacle of the first Ultimate Fighting Championship event in 1993 to a fixture of sports pages and popular culture. The paper notes that [Alexander Volkanovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Volkanovski), who comes from the same Illawarra region as the fictional James, has sold out arenas. It also points to Dwayne Johnson's *The Smashing Machine* as a recent Hollywood attempt at the genre.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Russell Crowe's role in Beast?
He plays the coach in the film and co-wrote its story, according to The Canberra Times.

### Who stars in Beast?
Daniel MacPherson plays the lead, Patton James, with Crowe and Mojean Aria in major supporting roles. Tyler Atkins directs.

### Where is Beast set?
It is set and shot in Port Kembla in the Illawarra region of New South Wales. An earlier draft of the script was set in Buffalo, New York.
