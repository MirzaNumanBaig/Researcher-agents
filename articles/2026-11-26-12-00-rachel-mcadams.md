**Quick summary**

- Searchlight has announced "The Families Stone", a sequel to the 2005 Christmas film "The Family Stone".
- [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams) is among the original cast set to return, according to ABC News.
- The story picks up 20 years after the death of matriarch Sybil Stone, played in the original by the late Diane Keaton.
- No release date has been announced.

## Rachel McAdams joins The Families Stone reunion

A sequel to the holiday favourite "The Family Stone" is moving forward, and Rachel McAdams is part of the returning cast. [ABC News reports](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/family-stone-sequel-announced-sarah-jessica-parker-rachel/story?id=136390805) that Searchlight announced "The Families Stone" on Instagram, in a joint post with the original film's official account and one of its stars, [Sarah Jessica Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Jessica_Parker). The post used a video graphic of the returning cast arranged in the shape of a Christmas tree, with the caption "Coming Soon".

The reunion comes more than two decades after the first film reached cinemas in 2005. For fans of McAdams, it is a return to one of the ensemble comedies that built her early career, which also gives this announcement a nostalgic pull beyond the holiday season.

## Who is coming back

According to ABC News, the returning cast also includes [Claire Danes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Danes), [Dermot Mulroney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dermot_Mulroney), [Craig T. Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_T._Nelson), [Luke Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Wilson), [Tyrone Giordano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrone_Giordano), [Elizabeth Reaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Reaser), [Paul Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Schneider_(actor)) and [Brian J. White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_J._White). That is ten names from the original ensemble in total, with Parker and McAdams among them.

ABC does not say which characters each actor will play in the sequel, so this article does not guess.

## What the sequel is about

The official logline, as relayed by ABC News, sets the new film 20 years after the death of Sybil Stone, the family's matriarch. Sybil was played by [Diane Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Keaton), who died in October 2025 at age 79.

In the new story, the family gathers for Christmas and receives surprising news from its father, Kelly Stone, played by Nelson. He has fallen in love and is bringing the woman in his life home to meet his children.

The original film followed a close-knit family as it came together for the holidays, juggling complicated relationships while dealing with Sybil's cancer diagnosis. Keaton was at the centre of that story, which makes her absence a defining part of the sequel's premise.

## How the sequel came about

[Thomas Bezucha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Bezucha), who wrote and directed the original, had said in a November 2025 interview with CNN that he was working on a follow-up. According to ABC, he was already writing the script when he learned of Keaton's death. The report does not say whether Bezucha will direct the new film, so that remains unconfirmed.

## What happens next

A release date for "The Families Stone" has not been announced. Searchlight's "Coming Soon" message is the only timing given so far.
