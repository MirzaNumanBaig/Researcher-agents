**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) are listed under "Additional Literary Material" for Avengers: Doomsday in an updated Writers Guild of America (WGA) listing, according to Yahoo.
- The credit does not confirm either actor will appear on screen.
- The film is scheduled for release on December 18, 2026.
- Marvel Studios has not officially confirmed their casting.

Hugh Jackman and Ryan Reynolds have both received writing credits on Avengers: Doomsday, an updated Writers Guild of America listing shows. [Yahoo reports](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/ryan-reynolds-hugh-jackman-avengers-181127984.html) that the pair appear under "Additional Literary Material", which adds weight to speculation that Wolverine and Deadpool will turn up in Marvel's next Avengers film.

For more on the actor, see everything we publish about [Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hugh-jackman/).

## What the writing credit does and does not mean

The WGA uses the "Additional Literary Material" credit for people who contributed writing to a project without earning the main writing credit. Yahoo notes that Jackman and Reynolds sit alongside several other writers on the listing. The main credit goes to [Stephen McFeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Markus_and_Stephen_McFeely) and [Michael Waldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Waldron).

The credit shows the two actors contributed material. It is not a casting announcement, and Yahoo is clear on that point. Until Marvel says otherwise, their involvement on screen remains an inference.

## Two public pitches that now look different

Both actors have spent the year joking about joining the film. According to Yahoo, Reynolds appeared at San Diego Comic-Con in July in a grey Deadpool costume and pitched Marvel Studios president [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) for a role.

Jackman followed at the D23 Expo in August. He asked to join the already crowded cast while Reynolds encouraged him from off camera. Yahoo says both moments seemed to be jokes, especially as the pair were told the film had already been shot. A writing credit suggests there may have been more to it, though that is a reading of the listing, not something Marvel has said.

## A very large cast

The film already has a long list of names. [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) returns to Marvel as [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom). [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), Australian actor [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal) and [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) are also named by Yahoo.

Several actors from the original Fox X-Men films are returning too: [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart), [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) and [James Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Marsden). [Hayley Atwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayley_Atwell), [Kathryn Newton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Newton), [Mabel Cadena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mabel_Cadena) and [Alex Livinalli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Livinalli) have joined as well. The Russo brothers, including [Anthony Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers), are directing. Yahoo describes the story as bringing heroes from three universes together against a major threat.
