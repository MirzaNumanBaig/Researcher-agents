**Quick summary**

- MobLand has passed 321 million minutes watched on Paramount+ this year, according to Collider.
- The crime drama stars [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) as the fixer Harry Da Souza.
- Season 2 began in September and wraps up this week.
- A third season has already been ordered.

[Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)'s crime series MobLand has reached 321 million minutes watched on Paramount+ this year, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/tom-hardy-crime-thriller-mobland-season-2-minutes-watched-streaming/) on 22 November. The milestone arrives as the second season finishes its run, and the show regularly appears in the top 10 of most-watched streaming originals.

For Tom Hardy MobLand is now one of the more reliable draws on his résumé. The series was created for television by [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie) and only premiered in 2025, which makes the numbers notable for a show still in its second year.

## What the 321 million minutes mean

Streaming services measure viewing in minutes watched rather than ticket sales, so the figure is a measure of time spent, not of how many people tuned in. Collider does not say how many viewers that represents or how the total splits between seasons. It says only that the total is for this year and that MobLand keeps landing among the most-watched streaming originals.

Collider puts the show's pull down to fast-paced storylines and a criminal underworld where something is always at stake. It also credits morally grey characters who are easy to follow even when their actions are hard to defend. The outlet adds that no character feels safe, which keeps the series easy to binge.

## Who is in the MobLand cast

Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, the right-hand man and fixer for the London-based Harrigan crime family. Collider describes him as a protagonist with a strange but firm set of ethics.

The family is led by Conrad Harrigan, played by [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan), whose role is a long way from his [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) years. His wife Maeve is played by [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren), and Collider singles her out as the most ruthless figure in the household.

Their sons Brandon and Kevin are played by [Daniel Betts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Betts) and [Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine). [Mandeep Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeep_Dhillon) plays Seraphina, Conrad's secret love child, and all three compete for control of the family.

The supporting players include [Joanne Froggatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Froggatt) as Harry's wife, Jan, and [Lara Pulver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Pulver) as Kevin's wife, Bella. Collider also names Janet McTeer and [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones) as villains who win through intelligence rather than muscle.

## How season 2 has played out

Season 1 centred on a gang war between the Harrigans and the rival Stevenson clan. According to Collider, season 2 began in September and puts the family under pressure from outside forces as well as its own internal splits. The finale lands this week.

Viewers who have followed the story do not face a long gap. Collider notes that MobLand has already been renewed for a third season.
