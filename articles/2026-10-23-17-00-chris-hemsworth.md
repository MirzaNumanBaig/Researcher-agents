**Quick summary**

- Sydney distiller Archie Rose has relaunched its whisky range with a new identity, bottle and packaging, reported on 21 October 2026.
- Co-owner [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) worked on the design with Glasgow agency Thirst.
- The three-whisky range reaches US shelves from Friday 23 October, according to The Whiskey Wash.
- The rebrand follows August's announcement that Hemsworth had joined founder Will Edwards as co-owner.

Archie Rose, the Sydney distillery now co-owned by [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth), has unveiled a redesigned whisky range built around a flask-shaped bottle, timed to its first US sales on 23 October. The actor helped shape the new identity and produced a campaign film, [Mediaweek](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/archie-rose-and-chris-hemsworth-unveil-new-look-ahead-of-us-launch) reported.

## A bottle meant to look "forged from the earth"

Per Mediaweek and [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/chris-hemsworth-stars-in-archie-rose-camapaign-ahead-of-us-launch/), the new bottle is flask-shaped, and its idea is that it was "forged from the earth". The glass is textured to suggest the soil where the barley and rye are grown, while the labels and boxes draw on the Australian landscape.

Hemsworth worked with the Archie Rose team and Glasgow-based creative agency Thirst on the identity, bottle, packaging and global creative direction. Design Week also covered the Thirst-designed bottle, but that page was not readable for this article.

There is a practical side too. The lighter glass and redesigned box cut packaging weight and improve freight efficiency, which the coverage ties to export plans and Archie Rose's B Corp certification.

## The range and the prices

Three core whiskies carry the new look. According to [The Whiskey Wash](https://thewhiskeywash.com/whiskey-news/archie-rose-australian-whiskies-hit-us-with-chris-hemsworth/), the US line-up is:

- Straight Rye Malt Whisky, $49.99, bottled at 46% ABV.
- Single Malt Australian Whisky, $59.99, bottled at 46% ABV.
- Australian Original Whisky, $39.99, bottled at 40% ABV.

The first two keep the same liquid and production methods; the rye was previously sold as Rye Malt Whisky. Australian Original Whisky is the new addition to the core range, described as an approachable all-malt whisky that marries the rye and single malt.

All three are made at the Sydney distillery from Australian-grown grains. The Whiskey Wash and B&T cite 12 years of distilling and more than 50,000 casks under bond.

## The campaign and the Hemsworth brothers

A global campaign launches alongside the range, filmed across eight locations in Australia. Hemsworth produced the film, and his brother Luke Hemsworth stars with him. Justin McMillan directed. The Whiskey Wash says the film premiered on 20 October on Hemsworth's YouTube channel and the Archie Rose website.

Edwards said the new chapter stays true to making whisky in Australia from Australian-grown ingredients, with a sense of place. Hemsworth called the result "Australian distilling at its absolute peak" and said he had been a fan for years. Master distiller Dave Withers said great whisky starts in the field.

## What happens next

The whiskies go on sale in the US from 23 October. None of the coverage names states or retailers, so where they appear first is unconfirmed.
