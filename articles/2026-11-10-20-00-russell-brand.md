**Quick summary**

- A column published on 10 November 2026 by The Sydney Morning Herald and The Age looks at how [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) has described his past.
- In his 2007 memoir, Brand implied that [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) was partly to blame for his sacking from MTV in 2001, according to the column.
- The columnist says the likelier reason is that the day was 12 September 2001 and Brand arrived dressed as Osama bin Laden.
- Brand has been sentenced to 10 years and six months after a jury found him guilty of rape and sexual assault.

[Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) told one story about losing his MTV job, and an opinion column in The Sydney Morning Herald and The Age says it left out the detail that mattered most. The piece, published on 10 November 2026, uses the comedian's sentencing to look back at how he has recast his own history.

This is commentary, not a court report, and the claims below are the columnist's reading of events and Brand's own published words.

## What Brand's memoir said about MTV

According to the [Herald and Age column](https://www.smh.com.au/world/europe/russell-brand-is-a-convicted-creep-women-the-world-over-have-reason-to-feel-relief-20261110-p61csl.html), Brand's 2007 memoir described the day that led to his sacking six years earlier. He wrote that, while still using drugs, he brought his dealer and the dealer's eight-year-old child to the set of the MTV show. The column says the two adults smoked crack in a bathroom before touring the studio.

[Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), the Australian singer, was on the show as a guest. In Brand's telling, she had an "awkward chat" with the dealer. He then wrote: "I thought: 'It don't get any better than this.' And it didn't, cos they sacked me about two days later."

The columnist argues that this account points the finger sideways, with Minogue's awkward moment playing a part. Nothing in the column suggests Minogue did anything other than appear as a guest.

## The detail the column says he skipped

The columnist writes that a far likelier reason for the sacking is the date. The day in question was 12 September 2001, the day after the 11 September attacks. Brand, the column says, had turned up dressed as Osama bin Laden, on top of using crack cocaine at work.

Asked about the firing later on a US talk show, Brand said that when he was "a crack and heroin addict, I made some unusual decisions". On the costume, he said it was "the worst time to do it in many ways", according to the column.

## A pattern of reframing, says the columnist

The piece argues the MTV story is one example of a habit. It points to a long interview with US commentator [Megyn Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megyn_Kelly) earlier this year, in which Brand discussed a past relationship with a 16-year-old when he was 30. The column quotes him calling himself "an immature 30-year-old". That matter was aired publicly in 2023 and was not part of the criminal trial, the column notes. The columnist says Brand's version left out context that the woman involved has described.

The column also notes that Brand told the court last month that friends and colleagues had abandoned him, while two former girlfriends and his wife, Laura Brand, gave evidence that spoke warmly of him. Laura Brand told the jury it had been "difficult for him to be monogamous" when they first dated, the column says. The couple reconciled about 10 years ago and have three young children.

The columnist also writes that further allegations against Brand are now being examined by UK police. Those are allegations only, and no finding has been made on them.

## What happens next

The column opens with the sentence: 10 years and six months, handed down after a jury found Brand guilty of rape and sexual assault. The two newspapers' piece does not cover any appeal or the next steps in his case. Readers wanting the court details should look to the reporting of the trial itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why was Russell Brand sacked from MTV?

In his memoir, Brand implied the episode with his dealer and Kylie Minogue was part of it. The Herald and Age columnist says it was more likely because he came to work on 12 September 2001 dressed as Osama bin Laden.

### What sentence did Russell Brand receive?

He was sentenced to 10 years and six months, according to the column, after a jury found him guilty of rape and sexual assault.

### Was Kylie Minogue involved in the sacking?

Only as a guest on the show that day, according to Brand's own account as quoted in the column. The column does not suggest she was responsible for the outcome.
