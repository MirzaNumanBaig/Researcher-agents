**Quick summary**

- HELLO! reports that The Revenant, Tom Hardy's Oscar-nominated Western, leaves Netflix at the end of November, with 30 November the last day to stream it.
- Hardy plays the villain John Fitzgerald opposite Leonardo DiCaprio, and earned his first and only Oscar nomination for the role, according to HELLO!.
- ScreenRant says Hardy's period drama Taboo is the natural follow-up for viewers missing MobLand, whose second season has now finished.
- MobLand season 3 is already in production with Hardy set to return, and Hardy has recently said a second season of Taboo is still happening, per ScreenRant.

[Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) fans have a short window and a long wait on their hands. His most ruthless screen villain is about to leave Netflix, and the series many say best matches his work in MobLand is still waiting for a sequel.

## Why The Revenant is leaving at the worst time

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/film/936859/time-running-out-to-watch-tom-hardy-villainous-role-the-revenant-netflix/), the 2015 survival Western The Revenant drops off Netflix at the end of the month, with 30 November the final day to watch. The outlet calls John Fitzgerald Hardy's most brutal villain, a notable claim given that his résumé includes Bane in The Dark Knight Rises, the Kray twins in Legend and Alfie Solomons in Peaky Blinders.

The film follows explorer [Hugh Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Glass), played by Leonardo DiCaprio, in 1823. After a grizzly bear mauls him, Glass is left for dead by a member of his hunting party and sets out for revenge, crossing winter wilderness towards Fort Kiowa on the Missouri River. HELLO! describes it as inspired by true events and directed by Alejandro G. Iñárritu.

## The cast and the awards

Hardy's Fitzgerald sits in an ensemble that includes [Domhnall Gleeson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Domhnall_Gleeson) as Andrew Henry and [Will Poulter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Poulter) as [Jim Bridger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Bridger). Hardy's Peaky Blinders colleague [Paul Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Anderson_(actor)) also appears, alongside [Forrest Goodluck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forrest_Goodluck) and [Kristoffer Joner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristoffer_Joner), among others.

HELLO! notes that the film won three Golden Globes, five BAFTAs and three Oscars in 2016. DiCaprio finally took home his Oscar after six earlier nominations, while Hardy received his first and only nomination, for Best Supporting Actor. Our earlier note on the departure is [here](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-the-revenant-leaving-netflix/).

## Taboo as the MobLand stand-in

With MobLand season 2 over, ScreenRant argues that Hardy's period thriller Taboo is the ideal stopgap. Both shows are set in London, though Taboo goes back to 1814. In MobLand, Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, a fixer who stays ahead of dangerous syndicates, with [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) in the cast. In Taboo he is James Delaney, who returns from twelve years in Africa after his father's death to claim an inheritance.

ScreenRant's view is that Delaney gives Hardy more room than Da Souza does. It calls the character feral, intimidating and unpredictable, and compares him to a mix of [Sherlock Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes) and Hannibal Lecter. That is the outlet's opinion, but it explains the pairing. Both shows treat crime as grim rather than glamorous, and both dig into dark family secrets.

## What happens next

The ScreenRant piece says MobLand season 3 is already in full swing, with Hardy returning, despite past behind-the-scenes conflicts. It adds that Taboo, a passion project for Hardy and his father Chips Hardy, was developed with co-creator Steven Knight, and that Hardy recently confirmed a second season is still happening, a comment ScreenRant attributes to Pop Drop. A decade after the first series, no date has been announced.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does The Revenant leave Netflix?

HELLO! reports that the last day to stream The Revenant on Netflix is 30 November. Availability can differ by country, so check your own Netflix library.

### Who does Tom Hardy play in The Revenant?

He plays John Fitzgerald, the hunter who abandons Hugh Glass after the bear attack. The role brought Hardy his first Oscar nomination, for Best Supporting Actor.

### Is there a second season of Taboo?

Not yet. ScreenRant reports that Hardy has said it is still happening, but no release date has been given.

### Will there be a MobLand season 3?

Yes. ScreenRant says production on season 3 is under way and Hardy is set to return.
