**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), 20, wanted to wear a look based on Peeta's rock camouflage on the *Sunrise on the Reaping* press tour.
- Her stylist, Molly Dickson, told Bustle on 24 November that she could not make it look elevated rather than costume-like.
- Dickson says the idea "might be the one that got away."
- The film opened in US theatres on 20 November and reached $225 million worldwide in its first weekend, according to EL PAÍS English.

The Mckenna Grace Hunger Games look she most wanted never reached a red carpet. Her stylist, Molly Dickson, told Bustle on 24 November that an outfit based on Peeta's rock camouflage defeated her, even though the actress asked for it.

## The Mckenna Grace Hunger Games look that got away

The scene in question comes from the first [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) film, released in 2012, in which [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson)'s Peeta disguises himself as a rock in the arena. According to [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/style/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-press-tour-stylist-interview), Grace has long called herself a Team Peeta fan and made fan edits as a teenager, so the moment sat near the top of her vision board.

"We explored the idea, but I just couldn't find a way to translate it into a fashion look that felt elevated rather than costume-y," Dickson said, as reported by Bustle and [People](https://people.com/mckenna-grace-stylist-reveals-hunger-games-look-impossible-to-recreate-sunrise-on-the-reaping-12277188). She added that, as much as she wanted to realise every one of Grace's ideas, "Peeta's rock might be the one that got away."

Dickson told Bustle she does not usually dress clients in literal method style, but it felt right here because it matched the actress's excitement for the project.

## What did make it onto the carpet

The homages that worked were plenty. People reports that Grace wore the wedding dress from [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire) and a custom Pamela Rolland gown with human-size mockingjay wings. Bustle adds that Dickson also dressed her as Rue and Lucy Gray, and that the press run was bookended by [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen)'s wedding gown and mockingjay dress. The link to Katniss was deliberate: Grace's character, Maysilee Donner, was the original owner of the mockingjay pin.

Two of the deeper cuts came with stories. The white dress Grace wore to SiriusXM before the premiere was a vintage Givenchy piece, a nod to Johanna Mason's white gown in the Capitol portraits from *Mockingjay: Part 1*. Dickson said she had spotted that dress back in 2011.

The hardest one was Johanna's lumber look. According to Dickson, Grace texted one night to say she needed to be a tree. After a long search through vintage houses and dealers, the team found an archival Céline piece by Phoebe Philo, which looked like wood, on The RealReal.

## A lighter finish to the tour

Red Carpet Fashion Awards noted that Grace appeared on the morning show Despierta América in Florida wearing a lilac Miu Miu mini dress with crystal embroidery and white platform sandals. The outlet suggested the colour and white collar might echo Maysilee's reaping dress, but called it a stretch. That is its reading, not something Grace's team has confirmed.

## Why the fashion matters to the film

The costumes have kept the film in the conversation. EL PAÍS English reports that *Sunrise on the Reaping* took $56 million on its first day in the US and $225 million worldwide over its opening weekend, well above early estimates.

In an October interview with People, Grace said fashion had once been a source of anxiety because of scoliosis, and that spinal surgery in 2022 changed how she felt about fittings. She described dressing up for films she loves as "fun and exciting."

## What happens next

The film is in cinemas now, so the press tour is largely done.
