**Quick summary**

- Netflix has not officially confirmed a third season of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves).
- Creator Joe Barton says the writers room is already working on ideas for Season 3 and that Netflix is supportive.
- Season 2 arrived on 5 November 2026 and opened to about 65% of Season 1's first-week viewing, according to The Economic Times.
- If it is renewed and made at the same pace as Season 2, a third season could land around 2028.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) and [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) may get another round as spies Sam Young and Helen Webb. Black Doves has no official Season 3 order yet, but its creator says the next chapter is already being sketched out. [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/magazines/panache/black-doves-season-3-will-the-keira-knightley-netflix-spy-series-return-with-new-season-heres-what-joe-barton-has-revealed/articleshow/135889950.cms) reports that Joe Barton told [Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times) the writers are developing ideas and are "waiting to be officially greenlit".

## What Joe Barton has said about Black Doves Season 3

Barton, who created the series and runs the writers room, has been open about where things stand. According to The Economic Times, he told Radio Times that Netflix is supporting the early work, even though no renewal has been announced. He added that he would love to carry on with Helen and Sam's adventures.

The same report says Barton also spoke to Variety and the Pilot TV podcast. In those conversations he said Netflix was keen to continue, but that a decision depends on several factors. The article does not spell those factors out, so it is fair to treat them as unknown for now.

That is a careful position rather than a promise. A writers room working on ideas is not a green light, and Netflix has said nothing publicly.

## How Season 2 performed

The numbers explain why the answer is not a simple yes. The Economic Times says Season 2 recorded 3.8 million views in its first week, which it describes as a sharp fall from the debut season, at roughly 65% of that earlier opening. It also reports that the show reached only number 4 on the English-language TV chart at first.

The picture improved afterwards. Citing Forbes, the report says the series climbed from number 10 to number 2 on the US chart. Streaming performance is often judged over weeks rather than a single opening, so the later climb may matter as much as the debut figure. Netflix has not said how it weighs either.

## When could Season 3 arrive?

There is no release date. The first season premiered in December 2024 and the second on 5 November 2026, a gap of almost two years. The Economic Times notes that Season 2 was produced faster once filming began in November 2025.

If a third season were ordered and made along the same lines, the report suggests it could arrive in the autumn of 2028 (northern hemisphere). A quicker writing and production schedule could bring that forward. That is the outlet's estimate, not a Netflix schedule.

## Where to watch Black Doves

Both seasons are streaming on Netflix. All six episodes of Season 2 were released together on 5 November. The Economic Times adds that UK viewers can also reach the show through Sky TV or [Virgin Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virgin_Media) subscriptions. ## What happens next

Nothing is certain until Netflix speaks. Barton says the team is waiting for an official decision, and viewing figures over the coming weeks are likely to shape it. Whishaw and Knightley have not commented on a third season in the sources reviewed here.
