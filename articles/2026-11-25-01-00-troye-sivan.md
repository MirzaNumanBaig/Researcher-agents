**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) hosted an intimate dinner for 20 guests at a private Toorak home in Melbourne.
- Winemaker Penfolds staged the evening to celebrate Holiday Designed by Troye Sivan, his latest project with the brand.
- The night also introduced Field Study: Magill, a candle made with his fragrance house, Tsu Lange Yor.
- RUSSH reported on the dinner on 24 November 2026.

[Troye Sivan](https://moduscarry.com/tag/troye-sivan/) has come home to Melbourne and thrown a dinner party. According to [RUSSH](https://www.russh.com/inside-troye-sivan-penfolds-holiday-dinner-melbourne/), Penfolds opened a private Toorak residence this spring for a small dinner hosted by the singer, who is the winemaker's creative partner, to mark his holiday collection for the brand.

## A small guest list in a private home

Only 20 guests attended, among them Michelle Bañares, Yasmin Suteja and singer and actor [Bryn Chapman Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Chapman_Parish), RUSSH reported. The venue was chosen on purpose. Penfolds Creative Director Luke Anton said the brand wanted the evening to feel intimate and unprecedented, and to resemble the homes people return to over the holidays to see loved ones.

Anton told the outlet that Penfolds is often part of those occasions, helping make gifts feel more meaningful. The dinner was built around that idea rather than a large launch event, which is why the guest list stayed so short.

## What was on the table

The menu was seasonal. RUSSH reports it was bookended by creations from Chris Yuille before Ellie Bouhadana, a family friend of Sivan, took over the kitchen. Sivan told the room he considers her the best chef in the world.

Glasses were filled with Penfolds wines, including Sivan's personal favourite, the St Henri Shiraz. The finish was a giant cherry bombe Alaska, which RUSSH described as gloriously excessive. Sivan, a keen home cook by the outlet's account, spoke about his love of bringing people around a table and about what the holiday season means to him: returning home, generosity and small gestures that say "I thought of you".

## The holiday collection and the new candle

That sentiment runs through Holiday Designed by Troye Sivan. RUSSH says the bottles are dressed in vibrant colour and tactile textures, with handwritten messages, borrowing from the nostalgia of wrapping paper and personal notes. The idea is a gift that looks special before it is opened.

The evening also introduced Field Study: Magill, a candle developed with Sivan's fragrance house, Tsu Lange Yor. It is inspired by the vineyards, cellars and surrounding landscape of the Penfolds Magill Estate. Penfolds Director of Winemaking Steph Dutton spoke about the pairing of wine and scent, which she felt also captured the spirit of the holidays.
