**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) returned to [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) on Saturday 24 October after missing week four.
- Teesside Live reports an MRI scan the day after a hospital visit showed a fractured rib.
- She shared clips from the past two weeks on Instagram, saying there would be progress "even on the frustrating days".
- She and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) dance a rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back To Me Now".

Delta Goodrem has opened up about the fortnight that kept her off the Strictly Come Dancing floor, posting a series of Instagram clips that begin in hospital and end on show day. The 41-year-old Australian singer and her professional partner Kai Widdrington were due to perform on Saturday night, with the BBC One broadcast starting at 6.30pm.

## From hospital visit to MRI

According to [Teesside Live](https://www.gazettelive.co.uk/news/tv/injured-strictly-star-delta-goodrem-34812994), the footage starts with a hospital visit last Sunday, where Goodrem admitted the pain was making her feel nauseous. An MRI scan followed the next day, after which she relayed that doctors had told her she had a fractured rib.

Later clips show her trying to hold back tears while she waited for a decision on her place in the competition. Teesside Live says there were fears at one point that her run could end early. Further footage shows a video call with Widdrington and her first walk after the injury, on day six.

## What she said

Goodrem wrote that she filmed small moments to remind herself that "even on the frustrating days, there would be progress". She said she was "gutted when the injury happened" because she had been "truly loving this experience".

[The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/arts-entertainment/tv/news/strictly-delta-goodrem-rib-injury-b3072713.html) quotes her saying it is hard to be patient when you want to be at full strength, but that she had been listening, taking things carefully and learning to appreciate the small steps. She closed by addressing "all my warriors out there in your own battles" and signed off with the hashtag #Teamdeltakai.

In a training room clip earlier in the week, quoted by Teesside Live, she said she "was not going to go down without a fight" and had to process her emotions quickly. She also praised Widdrington, saying he had been "so amazing".

## The one-week rule

The Independent notes that BBC rules allow each contestant to skip only one week before having to withdraw. That makes this return significant: another absence would have ended her run.

While she was out, Widdrington danced to "Jolene" with fellow professional [Amy Dowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Dowden), a tribute to [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton), who died in August aged 80, according to The Independent.

## Week five lineup

All 12 remaining contestants are back on the floor this week. [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) and [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin) dance a salsa to Rosalia's "Despecha". Actress Bethany Antonia, who narrowly avoided elimination last week after landing in the bottom two, performs an Argentine tango with [Nancy Xu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Xu) to "All The Things She Said" by Harrison.

Goodrem's name will also be familiar to Eurovision followers. Eurovisionworld lists her as Australia's 2026 entry with the song "Eclipse", though that page carries lyrics rather than any account of her Strictly run.
