**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show at Sony Pictures Studios in Los Angeles on 18 October, according to The Times of India.
- She wore a Jean Paul Gaultier Fall 2026 look: white shirt, black pencil skirt and a black-and-pink striped tie.
- The outfit was styled by Jason Bolden and drew comparisons with her Babygirl press tour wardrobe.
- Separately, her 18-year-old daughter Sunday Rose told Vogue Germany she hopes to move into filmmaking after finishing school next year, as reported by HELLO! Magazine.

Nicole Kidman skipped the usual evening gown for the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles on 18 October, choosing office tailoring from Jean Paul Gaultier instead. The Times of India described the look as a nod to the confident style she wore while promoting Babygirl. Days later, her eldest daughter was in the news for a different reason: she wants to direct.

## What Kidman wore to the show

The Oscar winner arrived at Sony Pictures Studios in a crisp white button-down shirt and a high-waisted black pencil skirt, according to [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/life-style/fashion/celeb-style/nicole-kidman-brings-back-her-babygirl-energy-in-a-striking-suit-look-at-the-victorias-secret-fashion-show/photostory/135111687.cms). A black-and-pink striped tie sat at the collar. The skirt's garter-inspired seams were the one unexpected detail.

She finished the outfit with pointed-toe pumps and a Chanel handbag. Stylist [Jason Bolden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bolden) kept the accessories plain, and the outlet said that left the clothes to carry the look.

It's an unusual choice for an event built around lingerie and spectacle. The outlet noted that the borrowed-from-menswear shape, softened with a playful edge, recalled what Kidman wore around the release of Babygirl.

## The Gaultier collection behind the look

The pieces came from Jean Paul Gaultier's Fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection, overseen by creative director [Duran Lantink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duran_Lantink). It debuted at Paris Fashion Week in March and, per the report, explored how tailoring, lingerie and exaggerated silhouettes meet.

Where the runway version reworked classic suits with unusual proportions, Kidman's take was more restrained. The plain shirt and fitted skirt kept things understated, while the tie and seaming supplied the personality. Fans of the [fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) coverage on this site will recognise the pattern: a strong designer piece worn simply.

## A Los Angeles return for Victoria's Secret

The 2026 show was the brand's first in Los Angeles since 2007, The Times of India reported. Victoria's Secret revived the annual event in 2024 after a six-year break, and this edition continued that run.

Models on the runway included [Adriana Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adriana_Lima), [Alessandra Ambrosio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandra_Ambrosio), [Candice Swanepoel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Swanepoel), [Gigi Hadid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gigi_Hadid) and [Bella Hadid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bella_Hadid), alongside Anok Yai and Alex Consani. Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Megan Moroney and Katseye performed. Kylie Jenner modelled the returning Fantasy Bra, with Amina Muaddi and Swarovski as footwear and crystal partners.

Among that crowd, a shirt and tie was a quiet statement. That was the outlet's reading, not a comment from Kidman, who has not publicly discussed the outfit in the sources reviewed.

## Sunday Rose looks beyond the runway

Kidman's elder daughter with [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban), Sunday Rose Kidman-Urban, has her own plans. HELLO! Magazine reports the 18-year-old already works as a model, walking for brands including Dior, Miu Miu and Schiaparelli. Her younger sister Faith, 15, leads a more private life.

Sunday told Vogue Germany that filmmaking, and directing in particular, has long fascinated her. She said that on her mother's sets, others wandered off to watch other things while she stayed fixed on the preview monitor. She named [Greta Gerwig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greta_Gerwig), [Emerald Fennell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerald_Fennell) and [Osgood Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osgood_Perkins) among her favourite directors. Her favourite film is her mother's 1995 Golden Globe-winning [To Die For](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Die_For), directed by [Gus Van Sant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gus_Van_Sant).

She hopes to start once she graduates next year, perhaps "as a filmmaker in Paris", though she added "you never know". Her biggest worry, she said, is how friendships will change when classmates scatter after school.
