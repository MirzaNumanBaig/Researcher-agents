**Quick summary**

- Former Bond actor Pierce Brosnan has publicly welcomed the Robert Pattinson Bond casting, writing "You are going to be magnificent".
- Brosnan and Pattinson worked together on the 2010 drama "Remember Me", according to Deadline and Variety.
- George Lazenby, who played Bond in 1969, also sent congratulations and told Pattinson to enjoy himself.
- The UK government joined the online jokes about Pattinson's busy career with a post about a youth employment scheme.
- Denis Villeneuve will direct the film for Amazon MGM, according to Variety.

The [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) Bond announcement on Monday has drawn a warm welcome from Pierce Brosnan. Brosnan, who played 007 in four films between 1995 and 2002, posted a short message on social media on Wednesday, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/pierce-brosnan-robert-pattinson-james-bond-advice-1237228669/).

## What Brosnan said to Pattinson

"Dear Robert, Welcome to the world of Bond," Brosnan wrote. "You are going to be magnificent." He added that he has "fond memories" of making "Remember Me" together and that it has been "a joy" to watch Pattinson build "a remarkable body of work". He closed with a Gaelic phrase meaning "great love" and signed off "Enjoy the ride."

The two have history. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/robert-pattinson-pierce-brosnan-james-bond-1236952519/) notes that "Remember Me" was a 2010 romantic drama that also starred Emilie de Ravin and Meghan Markle. Variety adds that Brosnan joins Pattinson's "Tenet" co-star Elizabeth Debicki and former Bond villain Rami Malek among the well-wishers. See also [Elizabeth Debicki Calls Robert Pattinson 'Perfect' for Bond](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-james-bond/).

Brosnan had stayed neutral before the announcement. Variety reports that he told The Times in September he would not pick anyone, and that he expected the new Bond to be "someone that none of us know about".

## Lazenby's advice from the "most exclusive club"

George Lazenby, who played Bond once, in "On Her Majesty's Secret Service" in 1969, also posted a message. Deadline reports he called the Bond actors "the most exclusive club in the world" and gave Pattinson almost the same advice as Brosnan: remember to enjoy yourself.
## The internet's favourite joke: Pattinson has every job

Online, the reaction has been less formal. According to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/global/robert-pattinson-trolled-by-uk-government-after-bond-reveal-1236951991/), the star already plays Batman, was recently in "The Odyssey", is on screens in "Primetime" and will soon appear in "Dune: Part Three". That has led to a wave of memes about him taking everyone's job.

On Wednesday the UK government's official account used Pattinson's Bond reveal photo to promote a new youth employment scheme expected to help more than 90,000 young people into work. Its headline said Pattinson would "apply for all 90,000 jobs". Variety also points to a resurfaced remark from a 2025 Vanity Fair interview with Jennifer Lawrence, in which he said he likes "taking jobs off younger actors".

## A Bond audition of sorts in "Primetime"

The casting news has also changed how people read his current film. In A24's "Primetime", Pattinson plays Chris Hansen of "To Catch a Predator". Deadline spoke to cinematographer David Bolen and editor Daniel Garber about the film. Bolen described a staircase entrance, backlit with direct sunlight, as Hansen's "superhero shot". Garber said Pattinson studied Hansen as an online meme and built specific physical moments that he expected to stick.

"Primetime" opened on 5 September 2026 with a US$14.5 million budget and about US$62 million at the box office, a return of roughly 4.3 times its cost. It holds an 86% Rotten Tomatoes score and 68/100 on Metacritic from 54 critics, according to the film's Wikipedia entry.

## What happens next

Villeneuve is directing the Bond film for Amazon MGM, according to Variety.
