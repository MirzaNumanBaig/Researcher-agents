**Quick summary**

- Julia Stiles dedicated her Dedication Night dance on *Dancing With the Stars* to her late co-star Heath Ledger.
- She and pro partner Ezra Sosa danced to "Can't Take My Eyes Off You", the song Ledger's character sings in *10 Things I Hate About You* (1999).
- Five castmates from the film joined her on the floor, according to The Nightly and Boston 25 News.
- Boston 25 reports the judges gave four 9s, a total of 36 out of 40.

A red rose, a photo and a song that fans have not forgotten in more than 25 years. On Dedication Night of *Dancing With the Stars*, Julia Stiles paid tribute to Perth-born [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger), her co-star in the 1999 teen comedy *10 Things I Hate About You*, and brought much of the film's cast along with her. The performance aired on Tuesday 20 October, and the tributes have kept circulating since, including a Yahoo video clip that fans shared this week.

## A routine built around one scene

Stiles, who is competing on the US version of the show, danced with Sosa to Frankie Valli's "Can't Take My Eyes Off You". It is the song Ledger's character, Patrick Verona, serenades her character with in the film's marching-band scene, according to [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/pop-culture/julia-stiles-reunites-with-10-things-i-hate-about-you-cast-for-emotional-heath-ledger-tribute-on-dwts-c-23057725).

The outlet reports that the choreography recreated that moment and worked in some of Ledger's signature moves. Afterwards, the poem that Stiles's character, Kat Stratford, reads about Patrick played in the ballroom.

Boston 25 News adds that Stiles placed a rose in front of a photo of Ledger while her speech from the film played. That was the cue for the rest of the cast.

## The castmates who joined her

Stiles was joined on the dance floor by five film co-stars: Gabrielle Union, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Larisa Oleynik, Susan May Pratt and Andrew Keegan. Both outlets list the group, though Boston 25 spells two of the names differently.

Stiles said she had asked every one of them personally. "I begged them, each and every one of them, and they all rallied," she told the programme, as quoted by [Boston 25 News](https://www.boston25news.com/news/trending/julia-stiles-honors-heath-ledger-10-things-i-hate-about-you-cast-reunites-dwts/7RIGMMXN3JB3DJ3WMBZYNLRJ3M/?outputType=amp). Of the rehearsals she said simply, "It's a lot." Of Ledger, she said: "He was so authentic."

## Asking permission first

The Nightly reports that before choosing the tribute, Stiles contacted Michelle Williams, the mother of Ledger's daughter, Matilda, to ask for her blessing. The outlet does not quote Williams directly, and Boston 25 does not mention the approach.

Sosa wrote in a joint Instagram post with Stiles, as quoted by The Nightly: "I don't think we've ever approached a performance with more care, sensitivity, and intention than this one."

## The score and the reaction

Boston 25 reports that the judges awarded four 9s, a total of 36 out of 40. That is a strong mark on a night when many of the dances were emotional rather than technical showpieces.

Fans responded online. The Nightly quotes one viewer on X who said the whole cast appearing at the end was "such a beautiful way to end tonight's beautiful dancing", and another who wrote that Stiles "wrecked me with that tribute to Heath Ledger".

For Australian readers, the tribute lands close to home. Ledger grew up in Perth, and *10 Things I Hate About You* was the film that introduced him to a wide US audience. We covered the first reports of the plan in our earlier post, [Julia Stiles Honours Heath Ledger on Dancing With the Stars](https://moduscarry.com/julia-stiles-honours-heath-ledger-on-dancing-with-the-stars/), and you can find everything else in our [Heath Ledger archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/heath-ledger/).

## What happens next

Neither source says how the tribute will affect Stiles and Sosa's run in the competition, and no future plans for the cast reunion have been reported. The show continues weekly, and results will follow its usual elimination format.
