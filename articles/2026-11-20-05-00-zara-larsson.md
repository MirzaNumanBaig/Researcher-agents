**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) has been named Changemaker of the Year 2026 at the Anders Carlberg Memorial Awards.
- The award is presented on Thursday, 19 November 2026, at Fryshuset in Stockholm.
- The jury cites her public stance on gender equality and women's rights, and her decision to buy back her music catalogue and build her own label.
- Three other people share the 2026 honours, across four categories.

Swedish pop star Zara Larsson has been named Changemaker of the Year 2026 at the Anders Carlberg Memorial Awards, which are presented on 19 November at Fryshuset in Stockholm. According to the organisers' [press release on Mynewsdesk](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/fryshuset/pressreleases/zara-larsson-receives-anders-carlberg-memorial-award-for-her-courage-to-speak-out-3479222), the jury chose her for using her international platform to encourage young people, particularly young women, to speak their minds and claim their place.

## Why the jury picked Zara Larsson

The citation, quoted in the release, points to a long record of public engagement. As a teenager she was already writing and speaking about feminism, gender equality and women's rights. The jury says she has kept those positions despite hostility, abuse and fierce criticism.

The honour is not only about what she says. The jury highlights how she works: she has bought back the rights to her music catalogue and developed her own record label, and she chooses her collaborators deliberately, from producers and songwriters to band members and dancers. The citation also credits her with challenging power and influence inside the music industry, and quotes the line that values are measured by actions, not statements.

[Popzine](https://popzine.se/news/zara-larsson-named-changemaker-of-the-year-at-anders-carlberg-memorial-awards) reports the same reasoning and notes that the jury sees her voice as resonating more strongly this year, in Sweden and with audiences worldwide.

## What she said about the award

Larsson responded in the press release. She said the award makes her happy because she has not only been making music for 15 years but has also been outspoken about her political views and human rights. She traced that to a love of people and a wish to stand up for those who cannot do so right now.

She also admitted that speaking out can feel frightening, and thanked the people who supported her when things were difficult. Her message to others was to "dare to stand up for one another when you don't always have the strength to stand up for yourself."

## The award and the other winners

The prize was first presented in 2016 and is named for Fryshuset founder [Anders Carlberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Carlberg), who lived from 1943 to 2013. It honours people whose actions help build a more inclusive society. Fryshuset, founded in 1984, is an independent non-profit and one of Sweden's largest youth organisations, working through education, music, sport and social projects.

There are four categories, and according to the [organisers' announcement of all four recipients](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/se/fryshuset/pressreleases/chidi-jansson-chukwuka-zara-larsson-aron-anderson-and-cecilia-udden-receive-the-2026-anders-carlberg-memorial-award-3479227), the 2026 winners are:

- **Young Role Model of the Year:** Chidi Jansson Chukwuka, 23, recognised for changing his life after a difficult upbringing and using music to offer hope to other young people.
- **Changemaker of the Year:** Zara Larsson.
- **Resilience Award:** [Aron Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aron_Anderson), a para-athlete and adventurer who lost the use of his legs after childhood cancer. He has climbed Kebnekaise and Kilimanjaro and skied to the South Pole, raising funds for the Swedish Childhood Cancer Fund.
- **Lifetime Achievement Award:** Cecilia Uddén, a Swedish radio correspondent who has reported from the Middle East and elsewhere for nearly four decades.
