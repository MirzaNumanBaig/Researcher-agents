**Quick summary**

- Veteran actor [Jaya Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaya_Prada) shared a throwback picture from her 1997 film Lav Kush, in which she played Sita, according to Cinema Express.
- The post arrived while [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi)'s Sita in Ramayana: Part 1 is the subject of debate over her looks, clothing and action scenes.
- Cinema Express reports the new film has passed Rs 1000 crore worldwide in its first eight days.
- Its India gross stands at Rs 686.55 crore so far, the same report says.

[Jaya Prada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaya_Prada) has reminded fans of her own time as Sita, posting a still from the 1997 film [Lav Kush](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lav_Kush) just as audiences argue over how [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) plays the same figure in Ramayana: Part 1. [Cinema Express reported](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/hindi/news/2026/Nov/13/jaya-pradas-sita-memories-resurface-amid-sai-pallavi-buzz) the throwback on 13 November.

## What Jaya Prada posted

The picture carried a short caption, "Throw back pic Lav Kush". Fans filled the comments with praise, according to Cinema Express. One called her their favourite heroine and "always beautiful". Another, a self-described 90s kid, said nobody could replace her. A third called the film a perfect choice and a great performance as Mother Seeta.

The post is a nostalgic one. Cinema Express does not say Jaya Prada commented on the new film or on Sai Pallavi, and she is not quoted on either.

## The 1997 film behind the picture

Lav Kush follows the closing part of the epic and centres on Ram and Sita's sons, Lav and Kush. The cast named by Cinema Express includes Jeetendra as Lord Ram, Jaya Prada as Sita, [Arun Govil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arun_Govil) as Lakshman, [Dara Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_Singh) as Hanuman and Pran as Valmiki. Madhusudhana Rao directed it, and Dilip Kanikaria produced it under the Devyank Arts banner.

In an earlier interview, Jaya Prada said she has played Sita three times in her career and called it a blessing. The other two films were Telugu titles, Sita Kalyanam and Sita Rama Vanavasam. She also spoke about the responsibility that comes with depicting such a character, the report says.

## The debate around Sai Pallavi's Sita

Cinema Express says Sai Pallavi's portrayal in Ramayana: Part 1 has set off an ongoing discussion about several aspects, including her "beauty", her clothing and the action scenes. The report describes the debate but does not name who is making these points or give figures for how widespread they are.

The film also stars [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) as Rama and Yash as Ravana. We covered the opening-week run in more detail in [Sai Pallavi's Ramayana Passes Rs 1000 Crore in Eight Days](https://moduscarry.com/sai-pallavi-ramayana-box-office/).

## Numbers so far

According to Cinema Express, Ramayana: Part 1 crossed Rs 1000 crore worldwide within eight days of release. Its India gross collection has reached Rs 686.55 crore. For context, that means India accounts for roughly two thirds of the global figure, going by the two numbers in the report.

The debate has not visibly slowed ticket sales. A film can draw heavy argument and strong business at the same time, and so far the reported numbers fit that pattern.

Further box-office updates are likely as the film moves into its second week. Whether Jaya Prada or other actors who have played Sita will comment directly on the new performance is not known.
