**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) plays Frankie Cooper, a London drug lord, in Season 2 of Paramount+'s MobLand.
- Frankie first appeared in the closing minutes of the 18 September 2026 premiere, according to FanBolt.
- Flynn says the character has no line he will not cross, and describes the part as "completely demented" in an interview cited by SSBCrack.
- Episode 9 streams on 13 November and the finale, "The Queen Is Dead", on 20 November 2026.
- Flynn is also due to play Lucius Malfoy in HBO's Harry Potter series.

Johnny Flynn has become the most unsettling presence in MobLand Season 2. As Frankie Cooper, a polite but ruthless London drug lord, the English actor spends the season picking at the Harrigan family from the edges, and with two episodes left, nobody on screen appears safe from him.

## Who is Frankie Cooper in MobLand?

According to [FanBolt](https://www.fanbolt.com/188598/mobland-frankie-cooper-johnny-flynn/), Frankie arrives as the man sent to dismantle the Harrigans. [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones), playing the bent ex-detective Colin Tattersall, brings him to Kat McAllister ([Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer)) as an enforcer who could wipe out the family and hand her their London business. What Kat does not know, FanBolt reports, is that Colin was already working with Frankie, and that Frankie has plans of his own.

He does not go straight for Harry Da Souza ([Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)) or Conrad Harrigan ([Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan)). He targets the people around them. Episode 2 ends with Frankie holding a gun on Bella, played by [Lara Pulver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Pulver), before he picks up Conrad's phone and says "Bang". Bella turned out to be alive, having been kidnapped, and was back with her husband Kevin by Episode 3.

## What Flynn says about playing him

Flynn told ScreenRant, as quoted by FanBolt, that writer [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth) wanted "an agent of chaos to come in and mess with the family". He drew on an earlier stage role set in the mid-1960s, and he borrowed the look. "I had a similar haircut, because I was kind of referencing" [Brian Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Jones) of the Rolling Stones, he said.

Asked by Collider whether Frankie has a line he will not cross, Flynn answered: "Frankly, no". He added that realising this as the season goes on is "what's so terrifying", and compared the way Frankie treats Tattersall to "a cat with a mouse".

[SSBCrack](https://news.ssbcrack.com/johnny-flynn-embraces-the-chaos-as-frankie-cooper-in-mobland-season-2/) reports that in an interview with British GQ, Flynn spoke of the pleasure of playing a "completely demented" character, and of balancing humour with darker material. Frankie often outsmarts others, the outlet says, until he comes up against Hardy's character.

- Dickie Greenleaf in Netflix's Ripley (2024), the wealthy American Andrew Scott's Tom Ripley is sent to bring home.
- A young [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie) in Stardust (2020).

- Dylan in Lovesick, which ran from 2014 to 2018.
- Rory Lomax, a photographer, in The Dig (2021).

SSBCrack adds his turns as a young Albert Einstein in the first season of Genius, and as a gangster named Francis in the 2022 thriller The Outfit. Flynn is also a musician and records and tours with his band, Johnny Flynn and the Sussex Wit, per FanBolt. ## How MobLand Season 2 is performing

Season 2 has ten episodes, with a new one released on Paramount+ each Friday. Episode 8, "We Don't Talk Anymore", arrived on 6 November 2026. FanBolt says the season held a 90 per cent Tomatometer score from 21 reviews on Rotten Tomatoes as of 10 November 2026. Ronan Bennett created the show, Butterworth writes and executive produces, and Guy Ritchie, who directed the first two episodes of Season 1, is an executive producer.

Paramount+ renewed the series for a third season on 3 September 2026, with Hardy, Brosnan and Helen Mirren returning. There is no premiere date yet.

## What happens next

Episode 9, "Nervous Breakdown", is due on 13 November, and the finale, "The Queen Is Dead", streams on 20 November 2026. Flynn's next big role is already confirmed: HBO announced on 9 June 2025 that he will play Lucius Malfoy in its Harry Potter series, alongside newcomer Lox Pratt as Draco. Jason Isaacs, who played Lucius in the films, publicly gave his blessing and called Flynn a "brilliant actor", according to FanBolt. That casting came well before MobLand Season 2 aired, so Frankie was not what won him the job, though it is a handy preview of a well-dressed man who threatens people politely.
