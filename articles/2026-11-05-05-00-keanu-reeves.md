**Quick summary**

- A ComicBook.com feature published on 4 November 2026 argues that [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has reinvented action cinema three times.
- The three films it names are Speed (1994), The Matrix (1999) and John Wick (2014).
- Each one, the outlet says, restarted a different phase of his career and set off a wave of imitators.
- The piece is an opinion feature, not a new announcement, so no fresh project is confirmed.

Few leading men can point to one film that changed how action movies get made. [Keanu Reeves](https://moduscarry.com/tag/keanu-reeves/) has three, according to a [ComicBook.com feature](https://comicbook.com/movies/feature/keanu-reeves-has-reinvented-action-movies-3-different-times-and-everyone-is-still-copying-him/) published on 4 November 2026, and the outlet says Hollywood is still borrowing from all of them.

## How Keanu Reeves action movies got started

ComicBook.com traces Reeves' rise through comedy first. He broke out in Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure and Parenthood, then moved into action with [Point Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Break) in 1991. The outlet calls that film a classic of the genre.

It was Speed in 1994 that proved the move was no one-off, the feature says. Reeves played an LAPD officer on a bus that will explode if it drops below 50 miles per hour. The outlet describes it as a ride that never calms down, and says it let him show off his likeability as a heroic lead and shed the image of a comic actor.

## The Matrix and the long gap

Five years later, in 1999, Reeves led The Matrix. ComicBook.com calls it the high point of sci-fi action and says its sequences were copied for years afterwards. His stoic Neo, the outlet adds, became one of his best-known characters.

The film also gave him a franchise to lead. According to the feature, its success allowed him to try smaller projects, and then he slipped out of major movies for a while. He kept acting, the outlet notes, just not in anything especially prominent.

## John Wick and the third reset

That changed in 2014 when Reeves took the title role in John Wick. The feature credits the film with a new emphasis on close-quarters combat, world-building and stunts that Reeves often performed himself. It also notes that he played a lead who barely speaks, letting the physical work carry the performance.

Few actors get a second major action franchise, the outlet points out. Since then, it says, he has landed big roles in sequels such as The Matrix Resurrections and Bill & Ted Face the Music, plus voice work in Toy Story 4 and Sonic the Hedgehog 3.

## Who has copied the formula

The feature contrasts Reeves with stars such as [Liam Neeson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Neeson) and [Jason Statham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Statham), whose films it describes as close to interchangeable in plot, style and action. Reeves, it argues, keeps changing approach.

On the imitators, ComicBook.com lists several examples:

- After Speed: Chill Factor, Air Force One and Turbulence, all built around high stakes on a vehicle. Unstoppable followed a similar idea years later.
- After The Matrix: Equilibrium, Wanted and much of the work of [Zack Snyder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zack_Snyder), which used bullet time and wire-fu.
- After John Wick: Nobody, The Beekeeper and Monkey Man, which lean on actors doing their own training and stunts.

These are the outlet's comparisons, and readers may weigh them differently.
