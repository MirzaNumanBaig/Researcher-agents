**Quick summary**

- Good Housekeeping UK has named Tom Hardy's 2014 film The Drop as a crime drama to watch tonight for viewers missing MobLand.
- Hardy plays Bob Saginowski, a quiet Brooklyn barman pulled into a robbery investigation.
- The cast includes James Gandolfini, Noomi Rapace and Matthias Schoenaerts.
- The magazine gives the film 9/10 and also points readers to Capone and Taboo.

[Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) is best known on screen for hard men, but a film from 2014 is being recommended for the opposite reason. According to [Good Housekeeping UK](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/uk/lifestyle/a74454798/tom-hardy-the-drop/), The Drop shows the actor's gentler side, and it is the one to stream tonight if you are looking for a crime drama to fill the gap left by MobLand.

## Why The Drop is being pitched as a MobLand replacement

The comparison is easy to see. Hardy has played the Kray twins in Legend and, more recently, the fixer Harry Da Souza in MobLand, as the magazine notes. Those are men who run the room. In The Drop, he plays someone who barely registers in it.

Good Housekeeping calls the film outstanding and rates it 9/10. It describes the ending as one you are unlikely to see coming. That is the outlet's own verdict, and it reads as a recommendation rather than a critics' consensus, so treat it as one view. If you have been following the show's run, our report on [Tom Hardy's MobLand hitting its highest IMDb rating yet](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-rating/) covers how the series is landing with audiences.

## The plot, without spoilers

Hardy plays Bob Saginowski, a reserved bartender in the outer reaches of Brooklyn. The bar was once run by his cousin Marv, played by [James Gandolfini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gandolfini), who has since handed it over to Chechen gangsters. The bar is used as a drop point, a place where the mob temporarily stashes envelopes of cash.

Bob keeps his head down and looks away. That changes when armed men rob the bar at closing time and take a load of the Chechen mob's money. The gangsters hold both Bob and Marv responsible, and the pair have to find a way out of a very dangerous position.

A second thread runs alongside it. Bob finds a wounded pit bull puppy in a bin outside the home of Nadia, played by [Noomi Rapace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noomi_Rapace), and the two nurse the dog back to health. Their growing closeness is interrupted when Nadia's abusive ex, Eric Deeds, played by [Matthias Schoenaerts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthias_Schoenaerts), returns. He is also the dog's original owner and tries to blackmail Bob into handing it back. According to the review, the two storylines converge in a dramatic climax, and Bob is not quite who he seems at the start.

## How Hardy approached the role

Good Housekeeping quotes Hardy as telling the Boston Herald that Bob is the kind of man people overlook. He said he thought of Lennie from [Of Mice and Men](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Of_Mice_and_Men) as inspiration. "We underestimate him as 'poor old Bob.' He's supposed to be harmless, the loyal dog that you have that shuffles alongside with the big guy," he said.

The rest of the cast, as listed by the magazine, includes John Ortiz as Detective Torres and Elizabeth Rodriguez as Detective Romsey.

## Two more Hardy dramas it suggests

The article also points viewers to two other Hardy titles. In Capone, he plays the gangster in his final years, living in seclusion at his Florida estate and suffering from dementia, with the story told through flashbacks and hallucinations as he tries to recall where a large sum of money is hidden. The magazine says it is available on Netflix.

The other is Taboo, set in London in 1814. Hardy plays James Delaney, who was long presumed dead and returns from Africa after 12 years to take on the powerful East India Company. It lists BBC iPlayer and Netflix as places to watch. Streaming availability changes by region and over time, so check your own service before settling in. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is The Drop about?

It follows a lonely Brooklyn barman, Bob, whose mob-run bar is robbed, leaving him and his cousin Marv answerable to Chechen gangsters. A rescued pit bull puppy and a dangerous ex complicate his life.

### Who stars in The Drop?

Tom Hardy leads, with James Gandolfini, Noomi Rapace and Matthias Schoenaerts. John Ortiz and Elizabeth Rodriguez play the detectives.

### When was The Drop released?

The film was originally released in 2014, according to Good Housekeeping.

### What other Tom Hardy crime dramas can I watch?

The magazine suggests Capone and Taboo. It lists Netflix for Capone, and BBC iPlayer and Netflix for Taboo.
