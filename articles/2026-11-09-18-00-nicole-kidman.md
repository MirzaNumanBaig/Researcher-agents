**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) appeared barefoot on stage at Chanel's Sydney after-party on 5 November 2026, according to Fashion Times UK.
- She joined director [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) to introduce [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) before Minogue's set.
- Social media users disagreed over whether bare feet suited a formal fashion event.
- Kidman has not explained the choice, and the footage does not show why she took her shoes off.

Nicole Kidman walked onto the stage at Chanel's Sydney after-party without shoes on 5 November 2026, and the internet has been arguing about it since. The 59-year-old actor stood beside Baz Luhrmann to introduce Kylie Minogue, and it was her bare feet, not the speech, that people picked up on, according to [Fashion Times UK](https://www.fashiontimes.co.uk/nicole-kidman-barefoot-chanel-sydney-after-party-1767227).

## What happened at the Chanel after-party

Kidman had attended Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 presentation earlier that day in a tailored black outfit and heels. Later footage showed her crossing the stage at the after-party with her shoes off.

The party was held at Pier 2/3 in Walsh Bay. Kidman and Luhrmann, who worked together on *Moulin Rouge!* in 2001, introduced Minogue together. Kidman referred to Minogue's hit "Can't Get You Out of My Head" in her remarks, and Luhrmann added a nod to "Padam Padam", Minogue's 2023 British top-ten single.

Minogue's set then ran through "Love at First Sight", "Can't Get You Out of My Head", "On a Night Like This" and "Padam Padam", Fashion Times UK reported.

## Why the barefoot moment divided social media

One user asked who walks barefoot in the 21st century. An X user replied, "Australians do." Others questioned whether it suited a formal Chanel event where a performer was being introduced. Some defended Kidman's decision to take her heels off.

A few commenters guessed she did it to look closer in height to Luhrmann. That is speculation. Kidman has not confirmed it, and Fashion Times UK notes the footage does not establish her reason.

## A first for Chanel in Australia

The party followed a landmark day for the brand. Chanel staged two presentations of its Cruise 2026/27 collection at The Cutaway in Barangaroo on 5 November. Matthieu Blazy had first shown the collection in Biarritz, France, on 28 April 2026, and the Sydney event was Chanel's first official runway show in Australia.

Guests included [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), Minogue, [Joel Edgerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Edgerton) and Olympic champion [Cathy Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathy_Freeman). Kidman went to the runway show with her teenage daughter, and the pair wore coordinated black-and-white looks. We covered the look in more detail in [Nicole Kidman and her daughter match in Chanel in Sydney](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-chanel-sydney-daughter/).

Kidman has been a Chanel House Ambassador since 2025. Her link to the house goes back further: she was announced as the face of Chanel No. 5 in 2003 and starred in the Luhrmann-directed film *No. 5 the Film* in 2004, alongside [Rodrigo Santoro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigo_Santoro). The advertisement reportedly cost $33 million, and Kidman was reportedly paid $3 million, per Fashion Times UK.

There is also a Minogue connection to the brand. Her 2003 video for "Come Into My World", directed by [Michel Gondry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Gondry), inspired the recent CHANEL 25 campaign with Robbie. Minogue makes a cameo in it, and Gondry directed the commercial too.

## Not the first time Kidman has gone shoeless

Fashion Times UK points to two earlier occasions. At the 2003 Cannes premiere of *Dogville*, she appeared without shoes after, she said, director Lars von Trier had asked her to do so if he attended the festival. "I am a woman of my word, so I took my shoes off," she said.

In November 2024, at the Academy's Governors Awards, she removed her heels at the lectern. She explained that the lectern was too low for her to reach the microphone comfortably in them.
