**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) was photographed in Manhattan's West Village on her way to a Screen Actors Guild nominating screening of her Paramount+ drama *Lioness*, according to Grazia.
- She wore a dark grey two-piece suit from Sarah Burton's Autumn/Winter 2026 collection for Givenchy.
- Under the jacket was a white lace blouse with a high collar and ruffled front, a nod to Regency-era costume.
- She finished the outfit with a quilted black Chanel 25 handbag and flat black leather loafers.

Nicole Kidman turned a screening appearance in New York into a lesson in mixing sharp tailoring with period romance. Grazia reports that the Australian actor arrived in the West Village for a [Screen Actors Guild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screen_Actors_Guild) nominating screening of *Lioness*, wearing a Givenchy suit over a lace blouse that looked lifted from a [Jane Austen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen) adaptation.

## The Givenchy suit by Sarah Burton

The foundation of the look was a dark grey two-piece from [Sarah Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Burton)'s Autumn/Winter 2026 collection for Givenchy, as described by [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/articles/nicole-kidman-paired-a-jane-austen-inspired-blouse-with-a-chanel-bag-in-new-york/). The magazine says it is cut from a heavyweight grain de poudre wool, a dense, finely textured fabric.

The jacket avoids the classic double-breasted shape. Its lapel is reversed, with the notch dropped towards the waist, which leaves an open geometric frame across the torso. Grazia quotes Burton, speaking to The Business of Fashion, saying her studio team "cut the jacket open, folded the collar forward and laid it flat against the body".

Kidman wore it with high-waisted balloon trousers that have deep front pleats. Burton spent years at Alexander McQueen before moving to the French house, and Grazia ties the sculptural cut to that background.

## A Regency blouse under the wool

The contrast came from the blouse. Grazia describes a white lace piece with a high pie-crust collar and a ruffled bib front, the sort of detail associated with Regency costume. Set against heavy grey wool, the lace reads as a deliberate counterweight rather than a costume, the magazine argues.

That is the publication's take, and it is a fair reading of the pairing: one soft, fine fabric under a structured, masculine one. The same idea works with a plain blazer for anyone wanting to borrow it.

## Chanel bag and flat loafers

Kidman skipped heels. Grazia notes she chose flat black leather loafers, with the hems of the pleated trousers falling over the front of the shoe.

Her bag was a quilted black leather Chanel 25. Grazia describes her as a longtime ambassador for the house and says she often carries its bags in daytime looks. Using one piece from another label, rather than a head-to-toe runway outfit, is the point the magazine draws from the styling.

## What happens next

The outing points to a busy awards period for *Lioness*, since nominating screenings are held while Screen Actors Guild members consider their picks. No nomination has been confirmed in the sources we reviewed, so treat that as something to watch rather than a result.
