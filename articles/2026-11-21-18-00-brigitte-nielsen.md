**Quick summary**

- [Brigitte Nielsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigitte_Nielsen) is named as a host on Vice City FM, the 80s station in Grand Theft Auto VI, according to The A.V. Club.
- She shares the station with [Weird Al Yankovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%22Weird_Al%22_Yankovic), whose role was confirmed by Rockstar Games to Kotaku on 18 November 2026.
- The A.V. Club, published on 19 November, describes her as "Madame Brigitte" and links her to the Reagan-era theme of the station.
- Kotaku's report does not mention Nielsen, so her role rests on one outlet so far.

## Brigitte Nielsen GTA VI role: what has been reported

The actress best known for *Red Sonja*, *Rocky IV* and *Cobra* is listed as the second voice on Vice City FM, the "non-stop 80s" radio channel in Grand Theft Auto VI, according to [The A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/weird-al-yankovic-radio-dj-grand-theft-auto-iv-gta). The outlet published its piece on 19 November 2026 and calls her "Madame Brigitte", pairing her with Yankovic as another figure from the Reagan era.

The detail is thin. The A.V. Club does not say how much airtime she has, whether she plays a character or herself, or how she was cast. Rockstar Games has not been quoted on her involvement in the sources we could read, so treat the report as a single-outlet account until it is confirmed.

## Weird Al is the confirmed host

The firmer part of the story is Yankovic. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/yes-thats-weird-al-hosting-a-radio-station-in-gta-6-2000758140) reported on 18 November that he hosts Vice City FM, saying writer Zack Zwiezen recognised the voice while driving in the game and then confirmed it with Rockstar. Kotaku says the station has two hosts and that it plays 1980s hits.

The A.V. Club adds that Yankovic confirmed the job himself in a post on Bluesky, describing himself as a longtime fan of the series. It also says he wrote some of the in-game commercials and voices the Burger Shot ads. Kotaku describes his on-air persona as loud and exaggerated, and notes that his own songs and parodies do not appear to be on the playlist.

## A station built on 80s nostalgia

Vice City FM is built around the 1980s, and The A.V. Club presents Nielsen as another Reagan-era figure beside Yankovic. The film credits it cites for her, *Red Sonja*, *Rocky IV* and *Cobra*, are all titles from that decade's action cinema.

The same article lists a long line-up of other voices and musicians tied to the game's stations, including Lana Del Rey, Keith Richards, Bad Bunny, Jeremy Irons and Robyn. Readers who follow the soundtrack side of the franchise can find more coverage in our [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) section.
