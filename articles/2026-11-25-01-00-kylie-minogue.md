**Quick summary**

- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) will release *Fever (XXV Edition)* through Parlophone on 27 November.
- The reissue comes on crystal-clear vinyl and as a two-CD set that adds B-sides, bonus recordings and remixes.
- A limited 12-inch of "Can't Get You Out Of My Head" pairs the original with a Peggy Gou remix.
- A matching anniversary merchandise line is on sale, and the formats are open for preorder.

[Kylie Minogue](https://kylie.com) is marking 25 years of her landmark album *Fever* with an expanded reissue, *Fever (XXV Edition)*, due on 27 November through Parlophone. According to [Big Country News](https://www.bigcountrynewsconnection.com/kylie-minogue-celebrates-25-years-of-fever-with-expanded-anniversary-edition/), the release includes several limited-edition formats and a new collection of anniversary merchandise, with everything now open for preorder.

## Why Fever still matters

The original album came out on 1 October 2001. Big Country News reports that it topped the charts in the United Kingdom, Australia and several European markets, and has sold more than 6 million copies worldwide. It carried "Love At First Sight", "In Your Eyes" and "Come Into My World", but one single towers over the rest.

"Can't Get You Out Of My Head" reached No. 1 in more than 40 countries and spent four weeks at the top of the UK chart, according to the same report. The song is also having a strong year. It has recently passed 1 billion streams on Spotify, which gives the anniversary a hook that has nothing to do with nostalgia alone.

## What is in the XXV Edition

The vinyl version is pressed on crystal-clear wax and keeps the original artwork, with an alternate back cover. It comes in a reflective mirror-board sleeve and includes a previously unseen contact sheet from the album's Vincent Peters photo shoot. A separate Arctic Pearl vinyl pressing will be sold only through Minogue's online store.

The expanded two-CD edition pairs the original 12-track album with B-sides, bonus recordings and remixes. The listed extras include "Can't Get Blue Monday Out Of My Head", "Boy", "Tightrope" and "Rendezvous At Sunset". There are also mixes by [Roger Sanchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Sanchez), Twin Master Plan and Fischerspooner.

## The 12-inch and the packaging

Collectors get a limited 12-inch single of "Can't Get You Out Of My Head". It pairs the original recording and its extended mix with "Can't Get Blue Monday Out Of My Head" and a "Midnight Remix" by Peggy Gou.

The standard two-CD package arrives in a three-panel digipak with a 12-page booklet. The store-exclusive version stretches the booklet to 20 pages and adds the mirror-board packaging. Alongside the music, Minogue has launched a *Fever (XXV Edition)* merchandise line of anniversary-themed collectibles and accessories. Details are on her official store.

## What happens next

The reissue is on shelves and in inboxes on 27 November, and preorders are open now. The report does not mention new live dates or a new interview tied to the anniversary, so nothing beyond the release itself is confirmed.
