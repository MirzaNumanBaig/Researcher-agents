**Quick summary**

- You Can See Everything, co-directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, has opened in UK cinemas about a month after its US release.
- British critics quoted by HuffPost UK describe the film as "unmissable", with several calling it a masterpiece.
- The documentary runs just under three hours and follows Elizabeth Holmes in her final month before prison.
- Wikipedia records a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score and a US$93 million worldwide gross on a US$7 million budget.

[Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder)'s documentary about Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes has reached UK cinemas, and the first British reviews are glowing. According to [HuffPost UK](https://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/entry/nathan-fielder-elizabeth-holmes-documentary-reviews_uk_6b0074a1e4b0edd18eddb88c), critics agree that *You Can See Everything* was worth the wait. US audiences saw it a month earlier.

## What British critics are saying

HuffPost UK gathered a selection of recent reviews, and the language is strong. One reviewer called the film "unblinking, unhinged and unmissable". Another said the more you see, the less you truly know, and described it as a "staggering docufiction odyssey" about the unknowable nature of truth.

Not every line is pure praise for the format. One critic said that calling it a documentary at all "feels reductive", comparing its mischief to Orson Welles's *F For Fake*. Another called it the best film of the year, while a third said Fielder "has made a landmark work".

There was one note of caution. A reviewer felt the slower, fluffier opening could test patience a little, before the film turns into "one of the most audacious, narrative-bending films" they had seen. HuffPost UK does not name the outlets behind each quote in the text we could read, so we have attributed them to the round-up rather than to individual publications.

## What the film is about

The premise is unusual. A camera crew follows Elizabeth Holmes in the last month before she goes to prison for fraud. Fielder, best known for the deadpan comedy series [Nathan For You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You) and *The Rehearsal*, co-directs with [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim). One review quoted by HuffPost UK also points to Holmes's husband, Billy Evans, as a central figure.

A24 is the distributor, according to one of the reviews. The same review says the film is tipped to dominate the Oscars race, which is a critic's prediction and not a confirmed outcome. We covered the awards angle in more detail in [our look at the Oscar directing-duo question](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-oscar-directing-duo/).

## The numbers so far

The scores back up the UK mood. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything) lists a 97% approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes from 229 reviews and a Metacritic score of 97 out of 100 from 43 critics. It also records a 98% positive score from PostTrak audiences.

The commercial picture is just as striking. Wikipedia gives a budget of about US$7 million and a worldwide gross of about US$93 million. That is roughly 13 times what the film cost, a rare result for a nearly three-hour documentary. The page lists a release date of 6 September 2026.

Manohla Dargis of The New York Times is quoted there saying few movies speak so uncannily to the confusions of the present moment. That puts the British verdict in line with the American one.

## What happens next

The film is in UK cinemas now. Its length means many screenings will be limited to a handful of sessions a day, so checking local times first is sensible.
