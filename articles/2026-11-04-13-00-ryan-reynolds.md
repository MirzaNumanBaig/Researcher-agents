**Quick summary**

- Social media posts this week claim [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) and [Blake Lively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Lively) are heading for divorce. No named, on-the-record source has confirmed this.
- The rumours are tied to reports about the couple living apart during Lively's legal dispute with [Justin Baldoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Baldoni), according to The Tab.
- Reynolds told [Willie Geist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Geist) he has "never in my life been more proud" of his wife.
- The couple married in 2012 and have four children. Lively has joked about divorce talk before.

Ryan Reynolds has spoken warmly about Blake Lively, just as divorce rumours about the couple went viral again this week. The Tab reported on 3 November that the claims are spreading on social media, and it gathered what both actors have said publicly about their marriage. Nothing in that coverage shows a split.

## Where the divorce rumours came from

According to [The Tab](https://thetab.com/filmshrine/2026/11/03/what-ryan-reynolds-has-said-about-wife-blake-lively-as-bizarre-divorce-rumours-go-viral/), the talk picked up after reports that the pair had been spending time apart. One of them, attributed by The Tab to International Business Times, said Reynolds was in New York while Lively was in Los Angeles, and that her legal fight with Baldoni had strained the marriage.

A second claim came from a newsletter run by Rob Shuter, which suggested Reynolds was unhappy about how much attention the dispute was drawing. It relied on an anonymous source. Neither actor has confirmed any of this, and we have not repeated the unnamed source's remarks here.

These are unverified reports, not facts. The Tab itself concludes it is safe to assume the couple are not divorcing.

## What Reynolds has said on the record

The clearest evidence comes from Reynolds himself. In an appearance on Sunday Sitdown Live with Willie Geist, he was asked how his family had coped during the legal battle. He said you "really see kind of the illusion behind so much of this stuff", describing the gap between digital life and real life.

He went further. "Without getting into too much, I have never in my life been more proud of my wife," he said, adding that "people have no idea what's really going on". He praised her integrity and said he was proud of how she carries it into everything she does.

On family, he was just as direct. "My wife and kids are everything," he said, according to The Tab.

## Not the first time

Divorce talk has followed the couple before. In July 2024, a fan commented on one of Lively's Instagram posts that rumours had been going around that the two had split, and said they did not believe it. The Tab reports that Lively responded to the comment at the time.

Since then she has kept sharing family moments. The Tab says she posted a picture of Reynolds in June and called him the "father of all of my favourite children". She also posted photos from a family trip to England in March, thanking Wrexham AFC and its players for what she called a performance of a lifetime in front of 10,000 people. Reynolds co-owns the Welsh club, and we have covered that side of his life in [Ryan Reynolds at 50: Wrexham, Mayday and a £295m fortune](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-at-50-wrexham-mayday-and-a-295m-fortune/).

The couple have been married since 2012. The Tab lists their four children as James, Inez, Betty and Olin, and notes they have generally kept family life private.

## What happens next

Nothing in the sources points to an announcement of any kind. The legal dispute between Lively and Baldoni is the backdrop to the speculation, and further court developments are likely to keep the couple in the headlines.
