**Quick summary**

- *The Nice Guys*, the 2016 crime comedy starring [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling), is No. 4 on Apple TV's Top 10 Movies chart, according to FlixPatrol figures cited by Comic Book Resources.
- It entered the chart at No. 7 and rose three places in a day after Apple TV added it to its film library at the start of November.
- The film earned about $63 million worldwide on a $50 million budget, and a sequel has never been confirmed.
- Gosling is due to begin shooting *Ghost Rider* for Marvel Studios next year, with a 2028 release planned.

A film that struggled in cinemas ten years ago is now doing well at home. [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) reports that [The Nice Guys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice_Guys), which stars Gosling opposite [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe), has climbed to No. 4 on Apple TV's Top 10 Movies chart after being quietly added to the streamer's library on 1 November.

## How The Nice Guys is performing on Apple TV

The figures come from FlixPatrol, the chart tracker CBR cites. The film debuted at No. 7 and moved up three places within a day. Setting aside the Peanuts specials, CBR says it is currently the most popular acquired movie on the service, ahead of *Prisoners* and *Interstellar*.

That is a modest slice of the market, since it is one chart on one service, but it is a clear sign of life for a title that is a decade old. It also fits a familiar pattern, where a film that missed its audience in cinemas finds one once it is easy to press play on.

## What the film is about

Set in Los Angeles in 1977, the story follows Holland March, a down-on-his-luck private investigator played by Gosling, and Jackson Healy, a hired enforcer played by Crowe. The two are thrown together to look into a missing girl and the apparently unrelated death of a porn star. The supporting cast includes Australian actress [Angourie Rice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angourie_Rice), [Matt Bomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bomer), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Keith David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_David) and [Kim Basinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Basinger).

[Shane Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Black) directed it, his third feature as director, and co-wrote the script with Anthony Bagarozzi. Black created the [Lethal Weapon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Weapon) franchise almost 40 years ago, and his writing credits also include *The Last Boy Scout*, [Last Action Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Action_Hero) and *The Long Kiss Goodnight*.

## A cinema flop with a loyal following

The box office numbers explain why the film is remembered as a near miss. It took about $63 million worldwide against a $50 million budget, according to CBR. Reviews were far kinder. [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) lists a 91% approval rating from critics and 79% from audiences.

Gosling has his own theory about the weak opening. Speaking in 2024, as quoted by CBR, he said so much of a sequel's chances are decided by the opening weekend, and that the film opened against *Angry Birds*, which went on to get a sequel of its own. Black said in 2018 that he wanted to make a second film but that the first had not earned enough to justify one.

## Why a sequel is still talked about

Interest in *The Nice Guys 2* has not gone away. In May 2024, producer [Ken Kao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Kao) told ScreenRant that any follow-up would have to be made "on a budget", noting that streamers are no longer writing blank cheques and that studios need films that can pay their way. Nothing in CBR's report suggests that has changed, and no sequel has been announced.

Gosling is not short of work. CBR describes him as busier than ever, with *Project Hail Mary* a big hit earlier this year and a role in Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider* to follow. ## What happens next

For now, the film's future depends on how long it stays in Apple TV's chart. A strong run could add to the case for a sequel, but only the producers and studio can decide that, and none has said a second film is in development. Filming on *Ghost Rider* is due to begin next year, with the film slated for cinemas in 2028.
