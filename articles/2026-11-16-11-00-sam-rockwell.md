**Quick summary**

- Wild Horse Nine, starring Sam Rockwell, grows to 2,175 US theatres for its second weekend, according to MovieWeb.
- That is 1,075 more screens than its opening run.
- Deadline's figures, cited by MovieWeb, show a US$5.9 million opening weekend and a projected US$4.6 million second weekend.
- Critics score the film 94% on Rotten Tomatoes, and audiences give it 92%.

Searchlight Pictures is putting more than 1,000 extra screens behind **Sam Rockwell's** new spy comedy. [MovieWeb reports](https://movieweb.com/wild-horse-nine-box-office-update/) that Wild Horse Nine will play in 2,175 domestic theatres this weekend, up 1,075 from its first run, after strong reviews and audience scores.

## What is Wild Horse Nine?

The film is written and directed by Martin McDonagh, and it reached cinemas on 6 November 2026. It is rated R and sits somewhere between drama, comedy, crime and thriller. It follows two CIA men sent to Easter Island in 1973, in the days before the Chilean coup.

[Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) plays Lee. John Malkovich plays Chris, with Steve Buscemi as MJ and Parker Posey as Marge, according to MovieWeb.

It is McDonagh's fifth feature and his first since The Banshees of Inisherin. MovieWeb notes that his last two films together earned seven acting nominations and two wins. Rockwell has worked with the director before, and we have covered [what McDonagh has said about him](https://moduscarry.com/sam-rockwell-martin-mcdonagh-praise/).

## How the box office is shaping up

MovieWeb cites Deadline for the numbers. The film opened to US$5.9 million. Its second weekend is projected at about US$4.6 million, a drop of 22%, which is a gentle fall for a new release.

The per-theatre average tells the expansion story. It fell from US$5,369 to a projected US$2,115, which is what you would expect when a film moves from a limited run to more than twice as many screens.

Deadline reports a budget of US$13 million to US$15 million. After ten days, the worldwide total stands at US$28 million, MovieWeb says. Against that budget, the film has already passed its reported cost at the worldwide box office.

## What audiences and critics say

The reception is the reason for the wider release. MovieWeb lists a 94% critics score on Rotten Tomatoes and a 92% Verified Hot audience score. On PostTrak, audiences gave it an average of 5 out of 5, and 72% said they would definitely recommend it.

Reviewers have praised the cast. David Rooney of The Hollywood Reporter called John Malkovich's performance "the real revelation". Ian Freer of Empire wrote that "Malkovich and Rockwell make a double act for the ages." Malkovich also won the Volpi Cup for Best Actor at the Venice Film Festival for the role, MovieWeb notes.

## What happens next

The next test is how the film holds on those 2,175 screens. A drop of around 22% would suggest word of mouth is working.
