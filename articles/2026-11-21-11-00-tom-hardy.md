**Quick summary**

- Production on MobLand Season 3 has started, with [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) back as fixer Harry Da Souza, according to Variety.
- [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) return as Maeve and Conrad Harrigan.
- The news landed on 20 November, the day the tenth and final episode of Season 2 arrived on Paramount+.
- No premiere date has been announced for Season 3.

Tom Hardy MobLand Season 3 filming is under way. Cameras began rolling this week on the Paramount+ crime drama, [Variety reports](https://variety.com/2026/tv/global/mobland-season-3-tom-hardy-helen-mirren-1236954158/), with Hardy returning as Harry Da Souza alongside Mirren and Brosnan. The announcement came on Friday, 20 November, just as the Season 2 finale started streaming.

## Who is back for MobLand Season 3

Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, the loyal fixer caught between two warring crime families. Mirren and Brosnan play Maeve and Conrad Harrigan, who head one of those families.

The supporting cast also returns, according to Variety: Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt, Lara Pulver, Anson Boon, Mandeep Dhillon, Jasmine Jobson, Teddie Allen, Emmett J. Scanlan, Johnny Flynn, Ophelia Lovibond, Janet McTeer and Toby Jones.

Ronan Bennett created the series, and [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth) is showrunner, as [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-production-start/) notes. Executive producers include Guy Ritchie, [David C. Glasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_C._Glasser) and Hardy himself.

## How the firing reports fit in

Earlier this year, reports claimed Hardy had been fired from the show after clashes with Glasser and Butterworth. Variety says those reports were premature, and that Ritchie and Paramount+ were keen to keep him. [Mezha.net](https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-25a32b256d084efc6798616e_mobland_season_3/), repeating Variety's account, says the parties patched things up soon afterwards.

Digg's summary of Deadline's reporting adds that Paramount confirmed the Season 3 renewal in September, after a London meeting involving Hardy, Butterworth and Glasser. We looked at how that story unravelled in [our earlier piece on the firing reports](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-firing/).

Brosnan addressed the talk before Season 2 launched. He called it "a storm in a teacup" and said of Hardy, "I love the dude," according to Comic Book Resources. At the Season 2 premiere in London, Variety says, Glasser introduced Hardy on stage as the "captain of our ship" who "stayed with it to the bitter end".

## How MobLand has performed

Season 1 earned a 76 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score, per Comic Book Resources, and Season 2 rose to 90 per cent. ScreenRant puts that second-season figure at 21 reviews, and says Episode 9 reached 8.7 out of 10 on IMDb, the highest rating of any episode so far.

On audience numbers, Comic Book Resources says the first season set a Paramount+ series premiere record with 2.2 million viewers, and that total viewership reached 26 million before Season 2 was ordered. Both figures come from that outlet and we have not verified them elsewhere.

Season 2 also brought in a new rival for the Harrigans, the mobster Frankie Cooper, ScreenRant says. For where Season 3's own storyline may head, see our post on [Harry's new rival](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-rival/).

## What happens next

Neither Variety's nor Deadline's production report gives a Season 3 premiere date, according to Digg. ScreenRant suggests a late 2027 release is possible, but that is the outlet's reading of the signs, not an announcement from Paramount+.

Australian viewers can catch up on earlier episodes on Paramount+. Comic Book Resources says all previous episodes, plus the Season 2 finale, are available to stream globally on the service. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tom Hardy in MobLand Season 3?

Yes. Variety reports that Hardy is back on set as Harry Da Souza, and that filming began this week.

### Was Tom Hardy fired from MobLand?

Variety says the reports of his axing were premature. Ritchie and Paramount+ wanted to keep him, and Season 3 is now in production with Hardy in the cast.

### When is MobLand Season 3 coming out?

There is no confirmed date. Filming has only just started, and the production reports from Variety and Deadline do not name a premiere window.

### Who else is returning for Season 3?

Helen Mirren and Pierce Brosnan return as Maeve and Conrad Harrigan. Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt, Lara Pulver, Anson Boon and Toby Jones are among the other cast members listed.
