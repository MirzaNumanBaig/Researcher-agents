**Quick summary**

- South African singer Tyla sang a snippet of ADÉLA's "Nicole Kidman" at her Boston concert, according to InMusic Blog.
- The show was at MGM Music Hall at Fenway, on the North American leg of her A*POP World Tour.
- The song has reached the US top 10 and No. 2 on the Billboard Global 200.
- Kidman has publicly praised the track and joined in a sing-along, the same report says.

Tyla gave her Boston crowd a surprise this week by working a few lines of ADÉLA's "Nicole Kidman" into her set, and clips of the moment quickly spread online, [InMusic Blog reports](https://inmusicblog.com/news/tyla-adela-nicole-kidman-boston-concert/). The song, named after the Australian actor [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), has become one of the standout pop hits of the year.

## What happened in Boston

The moment came at MGM Music Hall at Fenway, during the North American leg of the A*POP World Tour. The tour supports Tyla's second studio album, A*POP, which was released on 24 July 2026 through FAX and Epic. According to InMusic Blog, the 14-track record blends pop and R&B with South African amapiano and Afrobeats, and it debuted at No. 1 on Billboard's World Albums chart.

Tyla did not perform the full song. It was a short snippet, which is part of why the clip travelled: a tweet showing the moment drew plenty of attention from fans.

## How the song rose

"Nicole Kidman" is on ADÉLA's debut album, Prima, released on 4 September. The outlet describes it as glossy, high-energy pop. Its lyrics draw on Kidman's screen image, with nods to her work in Moulin Rouge! and Babygirl.

Its chart run has gone well past the album release. InMusic Blog says it has reached the US top 10 and climbed to No. 2 on the Billboard Global 200. The same report says Kidman herself has praised the song and joined a sing-along, though it does not say when or where.

That puts the actor in an unusual spot. Her name is now the hook of a global pop single, and a second major artist has paid tribute to it from a stage.

## Kidman's name keeps turning up in pop culture

The song is not the only place Kidman is cited as a style reference this week. InStyle's shopping feature on ballet sneakers notes that Vivaia's slim Sneakerina shoe has been worn by Kidman, Bella Hadid and Julia Roberts, and that Laura Dern has now joined the trend. That is a retail article rather than news about Kidman, and it does not say when she wore them.

## What happens next

Tyla's tour continues across North America, with dates listed for Toronto, Brooklyn, Washington, D.C., Tampa, Atlanta, Texas, Phoenix, San Diego, Los Angeles and Las Vegas through mid-December. It then moves to South Africa, with homecoming shows in Cape Town, Pretoria and Johannesburg in early January 2027, according to InMusic Blog.

Nobody has announced whether Tyla will perform the song again, or whether ADÉLA will appear on the tour.
