**Quick summary**

- Slovak pop singer Adéla, whose viral single "Nicole Kidman" peaked at No. 6 on the Billboard Hot 100, says she has withdrawn from the 2027 Grammys race.
- She made the comments in a Who What Wear cover story published on 11 November (US time).
- Grammy nominations will be announced on Monday, 16 November, and the ceremony is set for 7 February 2027 in Los Angeles.
- Adéla's representative told Entertainment Weekly she "has no comment at this time".

The Adéla Nicole Kidman story has a twist few pop breakouts offer: the 22-year-old behind the song says she does not want a Grammy nomination yet. Speaking to Who What Wear in a cover story dated 11 November, the Slovak singer said she is stepping out of consideration for the 2027 awards, as reported by [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/music/2026/11/11/adela-grammys-nominations-who-what-wear/92725995007/) and [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) via [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/new-albums/articles/viral-nicole-kidman-pop-star-195225649.html).

## Why Adéla says no to a Grammy nomination

"I think it's a nomination you need to earn," she told the magazine. She added that she has "so much more work to do" first, pointing to her tour and her plan to start writing a second album.

Her timeline is modest. According to USA Today, she said she needs to do all the work she can this year and next, and that "maybe in 2028" she will feel she truly deserves to be in the room.

The timing matters. Nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards are due on Monday, 16 November, and USA Today notes the ceremony on 7 February 2027 coincides with the final dates of her Red Bottoms Tour in Europe and Australia. Her representative declined further comment when Entertainment Weekly asked.

## How "Nicole Kidman" became a hit

The song is a playful anthem of self-confidence that nods to the Oscar-winning actress and her roles in *Babygirl* and *Moulin Rouge!*, according to USA Today. It comes from Adéla's debut album *Prima*, which USA Today says arrived in September; Entertainment Weekly describes the release only as earlier this year.

The numbers explain the buzz. "Nicole Kidman" reached No. 6 on the Billboard Hot 100 on 24 October. The follow-up, "Ain't in LA", peaked at No. 24 on the same chart, per Entertainment Weekly, and recently entered the top 20 of Billboard's Pop Airplay chart, per USA Today. Both songs spread through short video clips on Instagram and TikTok.

For context, a top-10 Hot 100 placing is rare for an artist whose first full-length album is only weeks old, which is why a break from the awards conversation stands out.

## The star-studded clip and a seal of approval

The song picked up an endorsement from its namesake. [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) has embraced it, and USA Today reports her daughter Sunday Rose did too. Entertainment Weekly adds that Kidman appeared at the end of a clip posted by [Kristen Wiig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Wiig), the [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) alumna, in which Wiig, [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal) and [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa) lip-synced the lyrics before the video cut to Kidman dancing along.

## Who is Adéla?

Born Adéla Jergová in Bratislava, she is a musician and dancer who first drew attention in 2023 on the reality series *Dream Academy*, which followed the making of the girl group Katseye, according to USA Today. She was eliminated in the first round. Entertainment Weekly adds that she released a debut EP, *The Provocateur*, in 2025 before this year's breakout.

*Prima* blends electropop and R&B with brash lyrics, USA Today says, and she told Who What Wear she had not expected the response. "I honestly wasn't even expecting it all," she said.

## What happens next

After that she returns to the Red Bottoms Tour, which, per USA Today, runs through Europe and Australia into early February, and to writing her next record.
