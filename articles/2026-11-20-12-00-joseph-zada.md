Two of the young leads of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping have described the work behind the film's most demanding moments. In a virtual press conference reported by [Mama's Geeky](https://mamasgeeky.com/2026/11/mckenna-grace-joseph-zada-hunger-games.html), Zada and Grace talked about a famous voice, a known ending and a slap that Grace says she dreaded.

## Living inside Haymitch's accent

Zada plays the young Haymitch, a character audiences already know through [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson)'s earlier performance. Asked how he captured that voice and manner, Zada called it a long and daunting process. He said any accent has to be worked at until the actor stops thinking about it, because audiences can tell when the effort shows.

His method was blunt: he stayed in the accent every day on set. That mattered most in emotional scenes, he explained, because feeling can push an accent off course and cost a take. He also described tracking how Haymitch's body changes as the character grows drunken, tired and worn down, and finding that later physicality.

Zada's preparation has been covered before on this site. See our report on how he [studied a month of Woody Harrelson films](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-woody-harrelson/).

## Playing a character whose fate is known

Grace was asked how she approaches a role when the ending is already written. She said she does not play the ending. In her words, she tries to play someone who is fighting to live and does not know what comes next, even though viewers who have read the book will.

She added that the cast tried hard to make the audience wonder whether a twist might change the outcome. Her answer shows how the film treats its own story: familiar events, played as if nothing were certain.

## The Glenn Close slap

The most vivid answer came when Grace was asked what it was like to slap Close. She called it terrifying and said she arrived full of nerves. She imagined Close might want to go method, and said she feared tripping or hitting the veteran actress by accident, because the take was repeated.

Grace said she treated it as the scene where viewers decide whether they believe her version of her character. She went for it, and called the day crazy, fun and wild. She praised Close as "so good" and said her villain was "hissable" from the first moment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Haymitch in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping?

Joseph Zada plays the young Haymitch Abernathy. Woody Harrelson played the older character in earlier films.

### What did Mckenna Grace say about slapping Glenn Close?

She called the scene terrifying and said she was very nervous, but committed to it because she saw it as the moment viewers decide whether they believe her character.

### How did Joseph Zada prepare Haymitch's accent?

He said he worked on it for a very long time and stayed in the accent every day on set so it would hold up in emotional scenes.
