**Quick summary**

- The first five short films made under the Displacement Film Fund screened at the Tokyo Film Festival, according to Deadline.
- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) joined fund directors Shahrbanoo Sadat and Mohammad Rasoulof for a Q&A after the screening.
- Separately, Uniqlo announced "stories she tells", a literary magazine for women writers with Blanchett chairing the essay selection committee.
- The magazine's first issue is planned for autumn 2027, and the Tokyo Film Festival runs until 4 November.

Cate Blanchett used the Tokyo Film Festival on 30 October to present the first five short films backed by her Displacement Film Fund, then announced a second Japanese project the same day: a Uniqlo-backed literary magazine for women writers. Together they put her work behind the camera and on the page in the spotlight, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/10/cate-blanchett-shahrbanoo-sadat-mohammad-rasoulof-tokyo-1237209297/) and a [Fast Retailing press release](https://www.fastretailing.com/eng/group/news/2610301000.html).

## The first five films

The two-time Oscar winner appeared with the fund's directors, Afghan filmmaker [Shahrbanoo Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrbanoo_Sadat) and Iranian filmmaker [Mohammad Rasoulof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Rasoulof), for a Q&A after the screening, Deadline reported.

The programme was *Rotation* by [Maryna Er Gorbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryna_Er_Gorbach), Sadat's *Super Afghan Gym*, *Allies in Exile* by Hasan Kattan, Mo Harawe's *Whispers of a Burning Scent* and Rasoulof's *Sense of Water*.

Two of the films were described in some detail. Rasoulof's follows an exiled Iranian writer struggling to learn the language of his adopted home. Sadat's follows Afghan women in Kabul who meet at a gym during the only hour of the day set aside for women, where they train and talk about body norms and daily life.

Sadat said her film grew out of a feeling of "not being enough", and the pressure to correct something in order to be accepted. "We carry our first home with us: our bodies," she said.

## Why the fund exists

Blanchett called the fund a long journey made possible by a "very passionate group" of people. She cited a statistic to explain the aim: one in 70 people around the world is displaced. Her group asked why their stories were not more visible in the mainstream.

Short films, she said, are a space where filmmakers can take risks and experiment freely.

She also pushed back on a label. Quoting Sadat, she said being displaced or a refugee is not a defining characteristic of a person. "Although I haven't personally been displaced," Blanchett said, she connects deeply with the films as an audience member and a film artist.

A surprise guest joined the stage: *Perfect Days* actor Kōji Yakusho. He praised the selection and said he expected a second, third and many more editions of the fund.

## Who is behind the fund

Blanchett launched the Displacement Film Fund in 2025, Deadline reports. The Rotterdam Film Festival's Hubert Bals Fund is its Management Partner. Amahoro Coalition, Droom en Daad, Master Mind, the Tamer Family Foundation and Uniqlo are Founding Partners.

A second cohort has already been announced at Cannes. It includes Mo Amer, Annemarie Jacir, Akuol de Mabior, Rithy Panh and Bao Nguyen. The fund also held a screening in New York earlier, covered in our report on [Cate Blanchett hosting Displacement Film Fund shorts in New York](https://moduscarry.com/cate-blanchett-hosts-displacement-film-fund-shorts-in-new-york/).

## A literary magazine with Uniqlo

Hours earlier, Uniqlo announced "stories she tells", an initiative backed by Blanchett, its Global Brand Ambassador. Its centrepiece is the retailer's first literary magazine, built from essays chosen through an open call.

The first issue is due in autumn 2027 and its theme is "Connection". Blanchett will chair the selection committee. Chosen contributors will also receive support for their writing and mentorship, and Uniqlo plans to include academic research papers and literary works on the theme.

The University of Tokyo is the launch partner. Faculty from its Center for Coproduction of Inclusion, Diversity and Equity will advise on themes and editing. A reading event for 50 students was held at the university's General Library the day before the announcement, according to Fast Retailing.

Submissions open this winter, with details expected around December. ## What happens next

The Tokyo Film Festival continues until 4 November. Uniqlo says the magazine may expand to university partners outside Japan, with more editions to be announced. Essay submission details are due around December.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Displacement Film Fund?
It is a fund Blanchett launched in 2025 to support short films by filmmakers who have experienced displacement. Its Management Partner is the Rotterdam Film Festival's Hubert Bals Fund.

### Which films screened in Tokyo?
*Rotation*, *Super Afghan Gym*, *Allies in Exile*, *Whispers of a Burning Scent* and *Sense of Water*.

### When will the "stories she tells" magazine come out?
Uniqlo plans the first issue for autumn 2027. Submission details are expected around December.
