**Quick summary**

- *Prisoners*, the 2013 thriller starring [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Jake Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Gyllenhaal), joins HBO Max's US library on 1 December.
- The date comes from TV Guide, as reported by ComingSoon.net. The report refers only to the US service.
- Directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), the film runs 153 minutes.
- It holds an 82% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and made about US$122.1 million worldwide on a reported US$46 million budget.

*Prisoners* is about to get a new streaming home. According to TV Guide, as cited by [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2232093-hugh-jackman-jake-gyllenhaal-prisoners-coming-to-hbo-max), the crime thriller arrives on HBO Max in the United States on 1 December, giving viewers another chance to see Hugh Jackman in one of the darkest roles of his career.

## What the film is about

Jackman plays Keller Dover, a father whose six-year-old daughter, Anna, disappears with her friend Joy. Gyllenhaal plays Detective Loki, who is handed the case. Police arrest a suspect, Alex Jones, played by [Paul Dano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dano), but a lack of solid evidence means he is released.

Dover then starts his own search. That choice puts him at odds with Loki, who has to follow the evidence to learn what really happened. The screenplay is by [Aaron Guzikowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Guzikowski).

The supporting cast includes [Viola Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viola_Davis), [Maria Bello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Bello), [Terrence Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrence_Howard) and [Melissa Leo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_Leo).

## How critics and audiences rated it

ComingSoon.net puts the film's [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) critics' rating at 82% from 249 reviews, with an 87% audience score from more than 100,000 ratings. On IMDb, users give it 8.2 out of 10 from about 967,000 votes. Those are strong numbers for a bleak, slow-burning story that runs for more than two and a half hours.

The awards record is solid too. Cinematographer Roger Deakins earned an Oscar nomination. The [National Board of Review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Board_of_Review) singled out the cast as one of the year's best ensembles, the Saturn Awards recognised its makeup work, and Gyllenhaal won a Hollywood Film Award for supporting actor, according to the same report.

## The money it made

ComingSoon.net reports a budget of about US$46 million. The film took roughly US$122.1 million worldwide, including about US$61 million in North America. That is close to three times its cost, and it means the film earned about half of its total outside North America.

## What happens next

The film joins the HBO Max US library on 1 December. The sources do not say when, or whether, it will reach Australian streaming services, so check your local platforms before planning a viewing. Nothing in the report points to a sequel or a new project from the cast.
