**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) leads *Star Wars: Starfighter*, which Lucasfilm has dated for May 28, 2027.
- Marvel's *Ghost Rider*, also with director Shawn Levy, is scheduled for July 28, 2028.
- *Project Hail Mary* returns to cinemas on December 11, 2026, with IMAX and select 70mm screenings, after grossing more than US$685 million worldwide.
- Aaron Pierre, who co-stars in *Starfighter*, has been talking about how he landed the part, according to IndieWire.

Ryan Gosling has three big films lined up, and the dates are now clear. According to a November 19 report from [cwibc.co.id](https://cwibc.co.id/en/ryan-gosling-star-wars-marvel), he is attached to a [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) film due on May 28, 2027, a Marvel Studios film due on July 28, 2028, and a theatrical return of his space hit *Project Hail Mary* on December 11, 2026.

## Project Hail Mary heads back to the big screen

The nearest date is the rerelease of [Project Hail Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary). Citing a Yahoo report from November 18, cwibc.co.id says the film will play again on December 11, including IMAX and selected 70mm sessions. The science-fiction film has taken more than US$685 million at the worldwide box office.

Gosling plays Ryland Grace, a middle-school science teacher who wakes aboard a spacecraft with no memory of who he is or why he is there. As his memory returns, he learns that a mysterious substance is dimming the Sun and that he may be the one to stop a global catastrophe. His unlikely partner is Rocky, an alien engineer performed and voiced by [James Ortiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ortiz).

The cast also includes Sandra Hüller as Eva Stratt, [Lionel Boyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Boyce), [Ken Leung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Leung), [Milana Vayntrub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milana_Vayntrub) and [Priya Kansara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priya_Kansara). [Phil Lord and Christopher Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lord_and_Christopher_Miller) directed the adaptation of Andy Weir's novel, and [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard) wrote the screenplay. We have also covered the [December 11 return to cinemas](https://moduscarry.com/project-hail-mary-rerelease/) and the [IMAX 70mm screenings](https://moduscarry.com/project-hail-mary-imax-return/) in earlier posts.

## Star Wars: Starfighter gets a pirate-flavoured logo

*Star Wars: Starfighter* is the next to arrive. Lucasfilm has set the premiere for May 28, 2027. The report, which cites The Direct's coverage from November 16, says a new logo appeared at [New York Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Comic_Con). It shows an inverted Rebel Alliance symbol that looks like a skull, with two blasters crossed beneath it like bones. That is a clear change from earlier promotional artwork and brings pirate imagery into the film's identity.

Shawn Levy directs, which makes this another collaboration with Gosling. [Flynn Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flynn_Gray) and Aaron Pierre are in the cast. Behind-the-scenes photos have shown Gosling with Gray near a large body of water and beside a new landspeeder.

Lucasfilm also plans three tie-in books after the premiere: an art book by Phil Szostak, a visual dictionary by [Pablo Hidalgo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Hidalgo) and an expanded novelisation by [Delilah Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delilah_S._Dawson).

## How Aaron Pierre ended up in the cast

[Aaron Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Pierre_(actor)) spoke about the film on IndieWire's "Screen Talk" podcast this week. According to [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/features/podcast/aaron-pierre-lanterns-dc-shakeup-starfighter-1235229615/), he told co-host Anne Thompson how he came to co-star with Gosling in Levy's film. The same conversation covered his HBO series *Lanterns* and his role in Rachel Morrison's *Love of Your Life*. The summary we could read does not give the details of how he was cast, so those are best left to the episode itself.

## Ghost Rider and the Marvel casting view

Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider* reunites Gosling with Levy and is scheduled for July 28, 2028, following the announcement at this year's San Diego Comic-Con. Marvel casting director Sarah Finn told Collider at the Newport Beach Film Festival that his passion for the character mattered in the decision. She called him an "unbelievably talented and versatile actor" and said she felt good about the pairing with Levy. Our earlier post on the [Ghost Rider skull debate](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-ghost-rider-skull-debate/) has more on the character's look.

## A Barbie memory from Kingsley Ben-Adir

In an Esquire interview, [Kingsley Ben-Adir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsley_Ben-Adir) recalled Gosling as the Ken who made the cast laugh most on *Barbie*. He described a Jeep scene with Margot Robbie that had to be shot again and again through the day, and said he found himself wondering what on earth was going on as Gosling performed it.

## What happens next

The first date to mark is December 11, when *Project Hail Mary* returns to cinemas. *Star Wars: Starfighter* follows on May 28, 2027, and *Ghost Rider* on July 28, 2028. All dates come from the reports above, and studios can move release dates.
