**Quick summary**

- USA Today rated "I Play Rocky" 3.5 stars out of four in a review published on 19 November 2026.
- The film is directed by Peter Farrelly and stars Anthony Ippolito as a young Sylvester Stallone.
- It is rated PG-13 and opens in US theatres nationwide on 20 November.
- The story follows how Stallone wrote "Rocky" and fought to play the lead.

The first review of the [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) origin story "I Play Rocky" is in, and it is warm. According to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/reviews/2026/11/19/i-play-rocky-movie-review/92792971007/), the film earns 3.5 stars out of four and "exudes that familiar magic" of the original. It opens in US theatres on 20 November.

## What I Play Rocky is about

The film is directed by [Peter Farrelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Farrelly), the filmmaker behind "Green Book". [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) plays Stallone, who is known as Mike in his early acting years, at a time when he struggles to find work.

USA Today's reviewer says the picture starts as a Stallone biopic and then turns into a Hollywood comedy-drama about the rough, low-budget effort to make "Rocky", the best picture winner of 1977. The critic describes the film as emotional and funny in its own right.

## The road to the screenplay

According to the review, the young Stallone takes odd jobs, including work as a cinema usher. That is where he meets his future wife, Sasha, played by AnnaSophia Robb. She suggests he write his own script and give himself the best part, which prompts a move to Los Angeles.

There he meets producers [Irwin Winkler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Winkler) and [Robert Chartoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Chartoff), played by P.J. Byrne and [Toby Kebbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Kebbell). The film shows Stallone being inspired by watching [Chuck Wepner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuck_Wepner) fight [Muhammad Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali) on a bar television. He then finishes the "Rocky" screenplay in three and a half days, according to the review.

The producers like the writing but doubt him as an actor. The title comes from his repeated line to them: "I play Rocky". A fictional studio head, played by [Tracy Letts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Letts), tries to push for name actors such as [Burt Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burt_Reynolds). Stallone does not budge, and the film is finally approved for under US$1 million.

## The cast and the boxing

USA Today singles out Ippolito's performance, saying he captures Stallone's voice and mannerisms so well that viewers forget it is not the real man on screen. [Jay Duplass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Duplass) is praised as "Rocky" director John Avildsen. [Stephan James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephan_James_(actor)) plays [Carl Weathers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Weathers), and the reviewer says the film could use more of him.

There is little boxing for a film about a boxing classic. Some of the finale appears in the credits, but the review notes careful recreations of moments from the 1976 original, including the neighbourhood run and the climb up the famous Philadelphia steps.
