Chris Hemsworth has produced and starred in a humorous new campaign for Archie Rose, the Australian distiller he now co-owns, as the brand prepares to enter the United States. The film launched on 21 October, according to [Marketing-Interactive](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/chris-hemsworth-gives-archie-rose-global-push-some-star-power).

## An over-the-top delivery mission

The campaign follows Hemsworth on an increasingly absurd journey to get Archie Rose whisky to the world. Marketing-Interactive reports that the sequences show him dragging barrels through a desert on camels, hauling them up cliff faces, and pushing through snowstorms and rough seas. In one scene he swims across an ocean while towing a barge loaded with barrels.

The film plays on his action-hero image and his willingness to poke fun at himself. His brother, Luke Hemsworth, also features.

It was directed by Justin McMillan and filmed across eight Australian locations. It is available through Hemsworth's YouTube channel and the Archie Rose website.

## From co-owner to creative partner

The campaign comes after Hemsworth was appointed co-owner alongside founder Will Edwards in August. Marketing-Interactive says his role goes beyond the film. He is also involved in the brand's creative direction, including a new identity, custom bottle and packaging developed with Glasgow-based agency Thirst.

The redesign brings three core whiskies together under one look. According to the report, it draws on the idea of being "forged from the earth", with textured glass and packaging inspired by the landscape and by the locally grown ingredients used at the Sydney distillery.

## Rollout plans

The refreshed brand is designed to support the US launch and wider international growth. The rollout is planned across Australia, the US, New Zealand, Singapore and selected Asian markets, per the same report.

Edwards said the distillery has spent twelve years refining its whiskies and now has a portfolio and brand identity that reflect its Australian heritage, giving it "a strong platform to introduce Australian whisky to more people around the world".

Hemsworth said he was "incredibly proud" of what the team had created and could not wait for more people in Australia and overseas to discover the whiskies.

## What happens next

With the film now live, attention turns to the US launch and the staged rollout across the other markets. No specific launch dates were given in the report.
