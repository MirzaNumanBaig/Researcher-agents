**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) has said in the past that he wanted to play Jason Voorhees, the hockey-masked killer of the Friday the 13th films, according to SlashFilm.
- The claim appears in the 2013 documentary Crystal Lake Memories and in an old Tonight Show appearance that is no longer available online.
- Separately, The Direct reports that Avengers: Doomsday draws on the X-Men universe known as Earth-10005, the world tied to Jackman's Wolverine in Deadpool & Wolverine.
- Jackman's own part in Doomsday is not confirmed in the reports we read.

Hugh Jackman built a career on playing Wolverine, but a report published on 23 November says the Australian star once had a very different character in mind: the silent, machete-wielding Jason Voorhees. [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2309073/hugh-jackman-dream-role-horror-movie-villain-jason-voorhees/) revisited the story, which comes from a 2013 documentary and a late-night interview.

## What Hugh Jackman said about Jason Voorhees

The source is [Daniel Farrands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Farrands)' 2013 documentary [Crystal Lake Memories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_the_13th_(franchise)), a roughly 400-minute history of every film in the franchise. SlashFilm says Jackman is shown being asked about the idea and replies, "Oh man, you've done your research."

SlashFilm also cites the fan site Fridaythe13thFilms, which says Jackman raised the topic on The Tonight Show with [Jay Leno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Leno). That interview is no longer available. In it, he reportedly said he loved the films as a teenager and that wanting to play Jason helped push him towards acting.

Farrands repeated the point in a 2013 interview with The New York Times' ArtBeats blog, using it as evidence of how far the character has travelled into popular culture. He described Jason as an outsider who speaks to people who feel alienated, and he said Jackman had told people that wish was why he became an actor. That second-hand account is Farrands' own, so it is best read as his recollection rather than a direct quote from the actor.

## Would he have suited the mask?

SlashFilm's writer weighs it up with some humour. Jackman stands about 6ft 2in, only slightly shorter than [Kane Hodder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kane_Hodder), who played Jason in four films from 1988's The New Blood to 2001's Jason X. The catch, the article says, is bulk: Jason is usually a lumbering mass, while Jackman, even at his most muscular, looks lean and quick. The writer still thinks he could have nailed the body language.

The part has passed through many hands. [Ari Lehman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Lehman) appeared in the first film, [Richard Brooker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Brooker_(actor)) in the third, [Ken Kirzinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Kirzinger) in Freddy vs. Jason and [Derek Mears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Mears) in the 2009 remake. [Callum Vinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Vinson) now plays him in the prequel series Crystal Lake.

## How Jackman got here instead

According to SlashFilm, Jackman's first screen credit came in 1994 on the Australian series Law of the Land, with his film debut five years later. His breakthrough followed when he replaced [Dougray Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dougray_Scott) as Wolverine in Bryan Singer's 2000 film X-Men. He has since returned to the role many times, most recently in the 2024 hit Deadpool & Wolverine, and has also worked across musicals, dramas, romances and comedies.

## The Avengers: Doomsday link

Jackman is also in the news because of Marvel's next big crossover. The Direct says Empire Magazine's coverage of Avengers: Doomsday listed the realities threatened by Incursions as the main Marvel universe, Earth-828 and "the X-Men's arbitrarily numbered Earth-10005."

Deadpool & Wolverine had already established that designation as the home of Ryan Reynolds' Wade Wilson. The Direct argues that most of the returning mutants in Doomsday come from that revised Fox timeline, and that Jackman's new Wolverine, Dafne Keen's X-23 and Channing Tatum's Gambit ended up there after the last film. The headline mentions appearance reports for Reynolds and Jackman, but the part of the article we could read does not confirm either actor is in Doomsday, so treat that as unverified.

## What happens next

No Friday the 13th project involving Jackman has been announced, and the stories do not suggest one. On the Marvel side, more detail on the X-Men's place in Avengers: Doomsday is likely as the film nears release. ## Frequently asked questions

### Did Hugh Jackman really want to play Jason Voorhees?

According to SlashFilm, yes. He is shown acknowledging the wish in the documentary Crystal Lake Memories and reportedly discussed it on The Tonight Show.

### Will Hugh Jackman appear in a Friday the 13th film?

There is no report of any such plan. The stories describe a past wish, not a current project.

### Is Hugh Jackman confirmed for Avengers: Doomsday?

Not in the reports we read. The Direct links his Wolverine's universe to the film, but the article text available to us does not confirm his appearance.
