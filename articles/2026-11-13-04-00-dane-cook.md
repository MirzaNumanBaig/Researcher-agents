**Quick summary**

- Comedian [Dane Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dane_Cook) has described an early-career show where the audience threw hot dogs at him before he performed.
- He told the story on the podcast Dinner's On Me, hosted by [Jesse Tyler Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Tyler_Ferguson).
- Cook says about 15 hot dogs flew past his head, and none hit him.
- He briefly said he was quitting, then decided that a clean miss was a reason to carry on.

Dane Cook has shared one of the stranger stories from his early stand-up days: a crowd that threw hot dogs at him because it did not want him on stage. He told the tale on a recent episode of Jesse Tyler Ferguson's podcast Dinner's On Me, according to [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-i-am-not-gonna-quit-dane-cook-recalls-bizarre-hot-dog-incident-that-tested-his-resolve/amp/).

## What Dane Cook said happened

Cook told Ferguson he was in the early, difficult stretch of his comedy career when the audience turned hostile before his set had properly begun. In his telling, the crowd did not want him to perform and started launching hot dogs at the stage.

"I remember all the hot dogs flying. I'm jumping over hot dogs," Cook said, putting the count at 15 hot dogs sailing over his head, as quoted by Netflix Junkie. The publication did not say where or when the show took place, and Cook's account of the date and venue was not included in the report.

## Why he did not quit

Cook says he left the stage and, humiliated, announced that he was done with comedy. Then he changed his mind. "I am not gonna quit cause not one hot dog hit me," he said.

That is the whole turning point as he tells it: a rowdy room, a lot of flying food, and the small fact that he had dodged every piece. Netflix Junkie framed it as one of the odder tests of his early career, and a story he has clearly never forgotten.

## The podcast host's own news

The same report notes that Ferguson has been talking about his old show. He said on Dinner's On Me that the cast of [Modern Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_Family) would "love to get back together and do it again". The sitcom ended in 2020 after 11 seasons and 250 episodes.

According to Netflix Junkie, Ferguson has previously discussed a proposed spinoff following his character Mitchell Pritchett and Eric Stonestreet's Cameron Tucker in Missouri, which ABC passed on. He has stayed in touch with Stonestreet, [Ty Burrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ty_Burrell), [Julie Bowen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julie_Bowen) and Sofía Vergara. No revival or reunion special has been announced.
