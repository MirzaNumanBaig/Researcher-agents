**Quick summary**

- A new review in The Daily Free Press, the Boston University student paper, welcomes [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) back with [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine).
- John Malkovich and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) play two CIA agents in Chile on the eve of the 1973 military coup.
- The reviewer says the film mixes absurdist comedy, period thriller and a friendship story without feeling disjointed.
- Malkovich's performance as a dying agent is singled out for praise.

The Daily Free Press has reviewed [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), the latest film from writer-director [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh), and its verdict is warm. The paper's critic describes it as a return to form after the Academy Award-nominated The Banshees of Inisherin, built around double-crossing spies, American imperialism and an awkward but heartfelt friendship.

## What Wild Horse Nine is about

According to the review, the film follows two CIA agents, Chris and Lee, played by John Malkovich and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell). They are stationed in Chile just before the 1973 military coup. Their journey takes them to the remote Rapa Nui, also known as Easter Island, where they meet two optimistic leftist students, Monica and Alicia. Chris keeps trying to befriend them.

As tension builds on the island, Chris begins to doubt Lee, and the review says that doubt puts their friendship at risk in life-or-death circumstances.

## A familiar McDonagh setting, with higher stakes

The reviewer sees plenty of overlap with The Banshees of Inisherin: an isolated island, two cranky companions and constant bickering. The difference, the review argues, is that war sat quietly in the background of Banshees. Here the characters stand in the middle of events and are directly affected by them.

McDonagh began as a playwright, and the review says it shows. The script is described as full of symbolic motifs and his trademark banter, with long, circular exchanges that turn funny through repetition. One scene has Malkovich and Rockwell repeating the phrase "Venice, Italy" over and over. The critic adds that McDonagh is also strict about economy, so even throwaway jokes pay off in the final act.

## Malkovich at the centre

Most of the praise goes to Malkovich. Chris is an ageing agent with a violent past, and Lee claims he has killed at least 68 people. Yet he spends his time wondering whether there will be dinosaurs in heaven and befriending the island's wild horses. The review says it becomes clear early on that Chris is dying, and that his habit of humanising everything from moths to dinosaurs reads as a man reassessing his life.

The reviewer calls one later scene especially chilling. Chris describes the coming coup to Monica in blunt, objective terms before it happens, and hearing it spoken with such certainty is, in the critic's words, haunting. The critic credits Malkovich with moving smoothly between absurd humour and quiet reflection.

## The friendship that holds it together

The review calls the relationship between Chris and Lee the heart of the film. It says their bond is clear without much exposition, thanks to the chemistry between the characters, and that it lands in the more honest moments even though both men are, as the critic puts it, horrible people. Lee's irritation at Chris, who keeps revealing that they are CIA agents and refuses to cooperate, drives a steady run of sharp remarks.

The verdict is that the film is at once an absurdist comedy, a dramatic period thriller and an emotional friendship story, and that the three tones come together in a satisfying finish. For viewers who like McDonagh's offbeat dialogue, the critic calls it an unexpectedly ideal mix of dark humour and sincerity.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in Wild Horse Nine?
John Malkovich and Sam Rockwell play Chris and Lee, two CIA agents, according to The Daily Free Press review.

### Where and when is Wild Horse Nine set?
It is set in Chile and on Rapa Nui (Easter Island) on the eve of the 1973 military coup.

### Is Wild Horse Nine similar to The Banshees of Inisherin?
The reviewer notes shared traits, including an island setting and bickering friends, but says the war is much closer to the characters this time.
