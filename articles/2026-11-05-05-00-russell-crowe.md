**Quick summary**

- Body of Lies, the 2008 spy thriller starring [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and Leonardo DiCaprio, is No. 8 on Apple TV's US chart, according to data from 4 November 2026.
- The film arrived on the platform on 2 November, so it reached the Top 10 within days.
- It now ranks above Interstellar and Brad Pitt's F1 on that chart, ScreenRant reports.
- Critics were lukewarm on release, but audiences have been kinder over the years.

Eighteen years after it left cinemas, a Russell Crowe spy film many viewers had forgotten is back in the spotlight. Body of Lies has entered Apple TV's US Top 10, sitting at No. 8 on 4 November 2026, just two days after it landed on the service, [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/russell-crowe-leonardo-dicaprio-body-of-lies-apple-tv-streaming-success-november-2026/) reports.

## What is Body of Lies about?

The film was directed by [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott). It follows the American CIA and Jordan's intelligence service, the GID, as they try to capture a terrorist named Al-Saleem in the Middle East.

Crowe plays Ed Hoffman, the chief of the CIA's Near East Division. Leonardo DiCaprio plays Roger Ferris, a field officer who reports to him. Much of the story is their effort to uncover the man's plots and track him down, with the two men working from very different positions: one directing operations from a distance, the other on the ground.

For readers following Crowe's recent work, ScreenRant points to The Pope's Exorcist, a supernatural horror film it describes as an underrated box office performer. DiCaprio's recent credits include Killers of the Flower Moon, a critical success and awards favourite directed by [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese). Body of Lies, by contrast, is the one the pair made together years earlier.

## How is it doing on Apple TV?

According to the 4 November data cited by ScreenRant, Body of Lies stands at No. 8 in the United States. That places it ahead of Christopher Nolan's sci-fi epic Interstellar and the Brad Pitt racing film F1.

The climb is quick. The film only appeared on the platform on 2 November, so the chart position reflects strong early interest rather than a long build. We have not seen Apple's own figures, and ScreenRant does not say how many viewers sit behind the ranking, so the placement is the only number available.

## A mixed reception that softened over time

The chart move sits oddly against the film's first reception. ScreenRant notes the response at release was lukewarm. Reviews gave it 58% from critics across 208 reviews. Audiences were more generous, scoring it 62% from more than 100,000 ratings.

Neither figure makes it the best work of Crowe or DiCaprio, and ScreenRant does not claim otherwise. It does suggest steady interest in the pairing that has lasted well past the film's cinema run.

## Where it sits among Ridley Scott's films

ScreenRant also compares the film with the rest of Scott's career. It says Body of Lies does not match the cultural reach of [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner), which spawned a wider franchise, or The Martian, the acclaimed adaptation starring [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon). Even so, the outlet says viewers of both leads have found it worth watching.

This is not the first time one of Crowe's older films has found new life on a streaming chart. ## What happens next

Body of Lies has only just reached the chart, so it is too early to say how long it will stay. ScreenRant says it remains to be seen how the film performs over the coming days and weeks. Whether it climbs higher or drops out will show if this is a short burst of curiosity or a real second life.
