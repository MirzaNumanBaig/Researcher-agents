**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) has reached Blackpool Week on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing), where she will dance a Quickstep to an Eternal song.
- Body language expert Judi James says a repeated habit after each dance may hurt her chances of winning the Glitterball.
- The comments were reported by Wales Online and the Daily Express, with James speaking to Paddy Power.
- Her partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) is back after missing a show through illness.

Delta Goodrem has made it to Blackpool Week on Strictly Come Dancing, but one commentator believes a small post-dance routine could stand between the Australian singer and the Glitterball Trophy. According to [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/strictlys-delta-goodrem-risks-glitterball-34940429), body language expert Judi James thinks Goodrem's polished image may be keeping viewers at arm's length.

## What the body language expert said

James told Paddy Power that Goodrem's body language is "perfect" for her career and celebrity status, but that it could still be "fatal" for a Strictly win. Her argument is about emotional investment. Viewers, she said, have grown attached to the other contestants, while Goodrem's professional image looks "hewn out of concrete".

The specific habit James points to comes after each routine. When Goodrem climbs to the balcony for the judges' chat, she reportedly turns away from her dance partner and heads for host [Johannes Radebe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Radebe), clutching at his arm and hands. Last week, James said, Goodrem even stepped away from stand-in professional Aljaž Škorjanec to talk to Radebe one-to-one.

In James's reading, this leaves the professional partner "standing like spare parts" and makes the moment look like Goodrem's interview rather than a shared one. She added that Radebe has the star profile and charisma Goodrem may find magnetic, and that contestants usually stay close to their partners because of the bond a routine creates. The [Daily Express](https://www.express.co.uk/showbiz/tv-radio/2267856/strictly-delta-goodrem-s-weekly-mistake-could-ruin-her-chances-winning) carried the same comments.

It is one expert's interpretation of a few seconds of television, not a finding about Goodrem or her partners. Neither outlet reports any comment from Goodrem, Widdrington or Radebe in response.

## A turbulent run to Blackpool

Both reports describe a bumpy road for the 42-year-old former Neighbours actor and singer. She has already missed a week through injury, and last week she had to learn and perform an Argentine tango with Škorjanec at very short notice after Widdrington fell ill.

Widdrington later explained on Instagram that he was on his way to hospital when Škorjanec took the call at about 5.30pm on Friday. According to Widdrington, Škorjanec rehearsed with Goodrem that evening and again the next morning. Learning a routine with a new partner in roughly 24 hours is, in his words, "absolutely unheard of" on the show, and he said he watched from home "gleaming with pride".

Despite the disruption, the reports say Goodrem has kept earning impressive scores, which is why the question of viewer connection matters. Strictly results depend on the judges' marks and the public vote, and a popularity gap is the kind of thing that can show up late in the series.

## Blackpool Week line-up

Goodrem and Widdrington are set to dance the Quickstep to Eternal's "I Wanna Be the Only One". She will share the Blackpool floor with [Lacey Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacey_Turner), [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) and Olympian Tabby Stoecker, among others. Both reports say Widdrington's return to the dance floor was confirmed in recent days.
