**Quick summary**

- [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) and [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) said on a live SiriusXM chat that they got matching ladybug tattoos.
- According to BroadwayWorld, the tattoos are inspired by a pivotal scene in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping and symbolise the bond between their characters.
- Zada also said Woody Harrelson invited him to smoke weed the night before, and that he was too nervous to say yes.
- The cast appeared at the film's New York premiere, and Nine.com.au says the film reaches cinemas this week.

The cast of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping spent Monday in New York doing what finished press tours do best: sharing secrets. Zada and Grace told SiriusXM host Andy Cohen they had matching ladybug tattoos done the day before, a keepsake from a film that is about to reach cinemas.

## The ladybug tattoos

The reveal came during a live SiriusXM Front Row session, reported by [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwmusic/article/Mckenna-Grace-Says-Playing-Britney-Spears-in-Biopic-Would-Be-a-Dream-Cast-of-THE-HUNGER-GAMES-SUNRISE-ON-THE-REAPING-Talk-SiriusXM-20261116). Zada said the pair got the tattoos "yesterday, actually". According to the report, the design comes from a pivotal scene in the film and stands for the bond between their two characters.

The sources do not say where the tattoos are or what the scene involves, so those details stay unconfirmed for now. Grace plays Maysilee Donner, described by Nine.com.au as a fan favourite among the tributes. Zada plays the younger version of Haymitch, a role he has discussed in [an earlier interview](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-haymitch/).

## A Woody Harrelson invitation

Cohen pressed the cast on whether they had spent time with Woody Harrelson, who played Haymitch in the earlier films. Zada said Harrelson had asked him the night before whether he smokes weed. He turned it down, he said, because it makes him paranoid and he did not want to embarrass himself in front of an actor he clearly admires. "He's so cool," Zada told Cohen, per the BroadwayWorld transcript.

Zada has talked about stepping into Harrelson's old role before. Our report on [Zada following Woody Harrelson](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-haymitch-woody-harrelson/) covers that side of the job.

## Britney Spears and a Halloween detour

Zada was also vocal about a fan campaign for Grace to play Britney Spears in a biopic. He said he had been campaigning for her himself, and Grace joked that his mother was too. Grace said she has loved Spears' music since childhood, when a greatest-hits CD was the only one she had on drives from outside Los Angeles to auditions. Playing the singer would be a dream, she said, but only if Spears were comfortable with it.

The chat also took in Jesse Plemons, who said he was flattered that Heated Rivalry stars Hudson Williams and Connor Storrie dressed as Bugonia characters for Halloween. [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) admitted he had not seen Heated Rivalry. After Cohen explained it, Fiennes called it "Brokeback Mountain with hockey".

## The New York carpet

The cast also walked the New York premiere carpet. Yahoo carried a [video of Zada on the carpet](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/videos/joseph-zada-shines-hunger-games-003906037.html), and a Deadline gallery listed Zada, Grace and [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)) among the arrivals.

Grace drew most of the style coverage. [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/lifestyle/fashion-beauty/mckenna-grace-style-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-red-carpet-looks-20261117-p61eso.html) reports she wore a blue Pamella Roland organza gown with feathered wings, a nod to the Mockingjay dress [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) wore as [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) in [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire). The outlet says stylist Molly Dickson has built the tour's looks around tributes to Katniss's costumes. For more on that side of the tour, see our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) coverage.
