**Quick summary**

- Connor Storrie told Vogue on 23 November 2026 that he is living with co-star Hudson Williams while season 2 of Heated Rivalry is filmed.
- He said the move came after a hotel stay left him wanting a washing machine and a kitchen.
- Vogue named the 26-year-old to its 2026 Best Dressed digital cover.
- He also addressed the online reaction to his buzzcut in the interview and in a separate Vogue video.
- Season 2 is expected in spring 2027, though no exact date has been announced.

[Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) has revealed that he shares a home with his [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) co-star [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) while the show's second season is in production. The actor made the comment in a Vogue interview published on Monday 23 November, tied to his place on the magazine's 2026 Best Dressed cover.

## What Connor Storrie said about living with Hudson Williams

Asked how often he and Williams keep in touch, Storrie answered plainly. "Right now we're filming, so I literally live with him, which is amazing," he told [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/connor-storrie-best-dressed-2026-digital-cover), as quoted by [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/23/connor-storrie-living-with-hudson-williams-heated-rivalry-season-2-vogue-interview/).

The reason was practical rather than dramatic. According to [Attitude](https://www.attitude.co.uk/culture/connor-storrie-living-with-heated-rivalry-hudson-williams-540415/), the pair had first stayed in a hotel. Storrie called it a brilliant hotel but said he needed a washing machine and a kitchen, so the two moved into a shared place.

That fits what Williams has said about the friendship. In comments to Wonderland, reported by [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/11/23/connor-storrie-hudson-williams-heated-rivalry-season-2/), Williams said their friendship "doesn't need to be public" and that the two avoid appearing together too often because it can start to feel like branding.

## Style, a buzzcut and the Best Dressed cover

The living arrangement was one part of a wider conversation about fashion. Storrie credited his stylist, James Yardley, with helping him enjoy the work behind red carpet looks, according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/23/style/undressed-connor-storrie-lands-vogue-best-dressed-2026-cover/). Page Six described him as a Saint Laurent ambassador and listed his sheer mesh turtleneck at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party and a silk halter top at the Met Gala among the year's talking points.

He said he and Williams do not plan their red carpet outfits together, yet their taste lines up. "We are very cut from the same cloth," Storrie said, while admitting he envies the street-style edge in Williams' wardrobe.

The buzzcut came up too. Storrie said he does not have social media apps on his phone and only learned fans had nicknamed him "Buzznor" when people approached him in person, per Just Jared. In a separate Vogue "Now Serving" video, he said he feels like a different person every six months and tries to honour that. He added that he likes the haircut even though many people did not.

## A Texas childhood and a cooking video

The Now Serving episode shows a lighter side. [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com/article/now-serving-connor-storrie) says he mixed a mezcal, soda and lime drink and cooked a minimalist burger with grass-fed butter, provolone and arugula. He also traced his style from growing up in Odessa, Texas, where he wore long cargo shorts and band tees, to sitting front row at Saint Laurent beside Madonna and [Kate Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Moss). The brand's creative director is [Anthony Vaccarello](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Vaccarello), who designed the beige sweater Storrie wore in the video.

According to [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/i_literally_live_with_him_connor_storrie_drops_a_major_heated_rivalry_season_2_filming_update/s1_17785_44606403), the Vogue piece also touches on a YouTube channel he ran at age 12 from his bedroom in Odessa, which he can no longer delete after losing access to the account.

## Where Heated Rivalry season 2 stands

Storrie plays Russian hockey player Ilya Rozanov opposite Williams as Shane Hollander. Attitude reports that season 2 is expected in spring 2027 and that creator Jacob Tierney told CBS News in October he expected a "late spring" release. The new episodes draw mainly on Rachel Reid's novel The Long Game.

The Express Tribune reports that production began in Toronto in August 2026. Attitude adds that reports say seasons 2 and 3 are being filmed back to back, and that a third season has not been officially confirmed, although both leads are said to have signed a three-season deal. Those details come from earlier reports and have not been confirmed by the network in the sources reviewed.

## What happens next

Filming continues, and no premiere date has been set. Fans can expect more promotion closer to release, and Storrie's fashion choices are likely to stay part of the story.
