**Quick summary**

- Horror hit *Obsession* began streaming on Netflix on 17 November 2026, according to [Ready Steady Cut](https://readysteadycut.com/2026/11/17/obsession-netflix-streaming/).
- The site reports Netflix has the film on loan from Peacock until September 2027.
- *Obsession* has earned more than $521 million worldwide on a reported $1 million budget, per The Numbers, as cited by [MotorBiscuit](https://www.motorbiscuit.com/obsession-star-inde-navarrette-ranks-the-original-fast-furious-movies-by-their-cars/).
- Star [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) is due to play Rogue in Marvel's X-Men reboot.

The horror film that made Inde Navarrette a name is now on the biggest streaming service in the world. *Obsession* landed on Netflix on 17 November, and the timing is hard to miss: Navarrette has spent the past week at industry events, talking about her next roles and her taste in car movies.

## Why the Netflix arrival matters

Curry Barker's film has already had a long life. According to Ready Steady Cut, it premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival a year earlier, opened in cinemas in May, moved to digital in June, and reached physical release and Peacock in July. Netflix is the fourth stage of that run.

The reviewer argues the move should push the film in front of a much larger audience than Peacock reached, given Netflix's global subscriber base. That is an opinion, not a measured result, and no viewing figures have been published yet.

The deal has a clock on it. Ready Steady Cut reports that Netflix negotiated a ten-month window with NBCUniversal, so the film returns to Peacock in September 2027.

## A $1 million film with a $521 million return

The numbers explain the attention. MotorBiscuit, citing The Numbers, says *Obsession* has taken more than $521 million worldwide on a reported $1 million budget. For comparison, Barker's next film, *Anything but Ghosts*, is set in the same universe and is due in May 2027 on a budget Ready Steady Cut puts at $5 million.

Ready Steady Cut also notes the film beat bigger names in a strong year for horror, including *Scream VII*. The same piece cautions that Navarrette does not carry the film alone, and credits co-star Michael Johnston and Barker too.

## Navarrette on Rogue, Bond and her family

Speaking to *Extra* at the 2026 Elle Women in Hollywood event, where she was honoured alongside [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), Penélope Cruz and [Julianne Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julianne_Moore), Navarrette kept details of her Rogue role under wraps. She did say she is "really excited to play her". We covered that night in [Inde Navarrette Honoured at ELLE Women in Hollywood](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-women-in-hollywood/).

Asked about playing a Bond girl opposite [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), she joked, "Give me a gun!" She called Pattinson "a phenomenal actor" who has disappeared into every role. Our earlier story, [Inde Navarrette Named in Bond Girl Wishlist After Obsession](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-bond-girl/), has the background.

She also spoke about being the second oldest of seven children and wanting to be a role model for her younger siblings.

## The Fast & Furious fan

In a BAFTA interview published on 14 November, Navarrette ranked the early Fast & Furious films by their cars, as MotorBiscuit reports. Her order is *2 Fast 2 Furious*, then *Fast & Furious*, then *The Fast and the Furious*. She says she still owns the original DVDs, "really beaten up", and names *Fast Five* as her favourite for its soundtrack.

That BAFTA link is no accident. BAFTA North America named her on Tuesday to its 2026 Breakthrough US cohort of 14 creatives, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/bafta-north-america-breakthrough-us-cohort-list-1237226911/). The list also includes Heated Rivalry's Connor Storrie and Backrooms creator [Kane Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kane_Parsons). More in [Inde Navarrette Joins BAFTA Breakthrough US Class of 2026](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-bafta-breakthrough/).

## What happens next

Viewers will now find out whether Netflix can repeat the film's cinema run. Ready Steady Cut expects it to climb the most-watched charts, though that is a forecast. Barker's *Anything but Ghosts* follows in May 2027, and Navarrette's Rogue debut comes with the X-Men reboot, which the site says follows *Avengers: Doomsday* and *Avengers: Secret Wars*.
