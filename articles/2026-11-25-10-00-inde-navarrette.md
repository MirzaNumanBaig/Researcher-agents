**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) joked in a Focus Features video that she is "deeply afraid" of [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) taking her roles.
- She said she picks parts he is unlikely to chase, and added that she was "the second choice for Bond".
- Outlets including Daily Times stress she was joking; it is not a casting revelation.
- Navarrette is next set to play Rogue in Marvel's X-Men reboot.

The Inde Navarrette Robert Pattinson joke is the week's lightest Hollywood story. In a video shared by Focus Features this week, the *Obsession* breakout said she is "deeply afraid" the newly confirmed James Bond will snap up her next job.

## What Navarrette actually said

Asked whether she worried about Pattinson taking roles, Navarrette played along. "I'm deeply afraid," she said, according to [AOL's republication of a Parade report](https://www.aol.com/articles/inde-navarrette-jokes-avoiding-roles-160621000.html). "Every single day, I wake up, and I decide that I have to choose the roles that he's not gonna do."

She then pushed the gag further. As [Complex reported](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/tracewilliamcowen/inde-navarrette-robert-pattinson-jokes), she said director Denis Villeneuve "will never admit to this", but that she was "actually the second choice for Bond", and that Pattinson only got the part because she was busy.

[Daily Times](https://dailytimes.com.pk/1586342/inde-navarrette-jokes-robert-pattinson-could-steal-her-acting-roles/) was explicit that this was not a genuine casting claim. Nothing in the sources suggests she was ever offered the role.

## Where the joke comes from

The punchline leans on a remark Pattinson made in a 2025 Vanity Fair lie detector interview with Jennifer Lawrence. Discussing a possible return as Edward Cullen, he said he liked "taking jobs off younger actors", AOL reported.

That line has resurfaced since his Bond casting was confirmed. Complex notes he has delivered four widely acclaimed performances this year, which has fuelled memes about him collecting roles. His past credits include Cedric Diggory in *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire*, Batman in *The Batman* and Antinous in *The Odyssey*.

## Bond chatter follows her too

The same AOL piece says the search for the next Bond girl is reportedly under way, with publications floating dream casts that place Navarrette alongside Margaret Qualley, Zendaya, Sydney Sweeney, Florence Pugh and Anya Taylor-Joy. That is speculation, not an announcement.

Asked by Extra how she felt about joining the franchise, she replied: "Oh, give me a gun!"

## A breakout year in numbers

Navarrette plays Nikki Freeman in Curry Barker's horror film *Obsession*. Sweety High says the film was made for $750,000 and has earned more than $250 million domestically, which it calls one of the biggest box office success stories of all time. The 25-year-old from Tucson has been working for years, with credits including *13 Reasons Why*, *Superman & Lois* and *Wander Darkly*.

Complex recently named her among the best actors currently in their twenties, alongside Dominic Sessa and Chase Infiniti. She is also set to play Rogue in the X-Men reboot, as Sweety High and AOL both note. For more on her recent run, see our earlier posts on [her Knicks courtside appearance](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-knicks-courtside/) and [her plans for GTA 6](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-gta-6/).

That Knicks night, on Nov. 22, also drew attention to a courtside exchange with tennis player Ben Shelton, according to the [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/11/24/sports/ben-shelton-courtside-exchange-inde-navarrette-trinity-rodman-breakup/). The Post said it was unclear how the two know each other and that nothing suggests a romance.

## What happens next

Navarrette's next big screen job is Rogue in the X-Men reboot. Pattinson's Bond debut is further off, and no Bond co-star has been announced.
