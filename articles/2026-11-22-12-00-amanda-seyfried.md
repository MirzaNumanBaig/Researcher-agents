**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) voices Cinderella in Netflix's animated film *Steps*, which arrived on 20 November 2026.
- She sings one of the four original songs, "Smile and a Song", a musical theatre number.
- BLACKPINK's JENNIE performs "Someone to Hold", her first song for an animated film.
- All four songs were written and produced by Justin Tranter, according to What's on Netflix.

Amanda Seyfried is back in a musical role, this time as a voice. She plays Cinderella in *Steps*, the Netflix Animation Studios film that landed on the streaming service worldwide on 20 November 2026, and she sings on its soundtrack alongside a new original track from BLACKPINK's JENNIE.

## What Steps is about

*Steps* is a Cinderella spin-off that puts the stepsisters at the centre. According to [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/news/steps-trailer-drop-blackpink-jennie-soundtrack-news/), Lilith, voiced by Ali Wong, gets hold of the Fairy Godmother's wand and the familiar story comes apart. Lilith then teams up with Cinderella, and the pair face biker trolls and a trip through the Screaming Woods, according to [Netflix Tudum](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/amanda-seyfried-cinderella-steps?inapp=true).

The film is directed by Alyce Tzue and John Ripa. Netflix says the animation was done at its Sydney and Vancouver studios, which gives the release a local angle for Australian viewers. What's on Netflix describes it as the first feature produced entirely in-house by Netflix Animation Studios.

## Seyfried's role and song

Seyfried's Cinderella likes to sing, and What's on Netflix notes the casting suits a project that began life as a full musical. Her number, "Smile and a Song", runs 2 minutes 8 seconds on the album, according to [Film Music Reporter](https://filmmusicreporter.com/?p=144332). The outlet's track list places it fourth, after the three other original songs.

Netflix quotes the directors on why she fits. Tzue praised her ability to make characters specific and relatable, while Ripa said her experience and passion push the team to dig deeper into the character. Seyfried has sung on screen before, and fans of the actress will find more in our [Amanda Seyfried tag archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/amanda-seyfried/).

## JENNIE and the rest of the soundtrack

JENNIE's "Someone to Hold" was released as a digital single on 6 November, according to Film Music Reporter. What's on Netflix says the song represents the sisterhood between Lilith, Cinderella and Margot. The singer said she was excited to join the *Steps* family and enjoyed making her first song for an animated film.

Songwriter Justin Tranter, a Grammy nominee, wrote and produced all four originals. He described JENNIE's track as "pure danceable joy" inspired by vintage organic dance pop. The other two songs are:

- "The Understudy" by EMEI, tied to Lilith's underdog anger
- "Meet Me on the Dance Floor" by Rose Gray, which belongs to the villain Priscilla

The score is by Kris Bowers, whose credits include *Bridgerton*. The album carries 47 tracks in all and is released by Netflix Music.

## The cast around her

Beyond Wong and Seyfried, What's on Netflix lists Stephanie Hsu as Margot, Nikki Glaser as Priscilla and Bette Midler as the Fairy Godmother. Daniel Radcliffe, Young Mazino and Peter Dinklage also voice roles. Amy Poehler is among the producers.
