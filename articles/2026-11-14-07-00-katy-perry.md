**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) did not respond when a TMZ reporter asked about ex-husband Russell Brand's sentence, according to Yahoo's report on the video.
- Brand was sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault, Yahoo reports.
- Separately, Novara Media reports Perry gave a private show for staff of the cybersecurity company Wiz in Thailand on 7 November.
- Perry had not commented publicly on either matter at the time of the reports.

Katy Perry has kept quiet on two fronts this week. On one, she walked past questions about [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand)'s prison sentence while out with boyfriend [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau). On the other, a private concert she reportedly played for a tech company's staff in Thailand has prompted criticism online. Neither has drawn a statement from the singer.

## The question she did not answer

According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/katy-perry-seemingly-ignores-russell-192106516.html), which republished a report from Mandatory, TMZ posted a video on X showing its team approaching Perry as she stepped out with Trudeau. The caption described it as her first outing since Brand's conviction.

In the footage, a reporter asks how she feels about Brand's reported 10-year sentence and whether she has a message for women. Perry does not reply to either question. Trudeau, the former Canadian prime minister, holds her hand and helps her into a car.

Yahoo reports this was the first time anyone had publicly tried to put a question about Brand to her since the verdict. Her silence leaves her position unclear, and it should not be read as a comment either way.

## What the court decided

The report says London's Southwark Crown Court sentenced Brand to 10 years and six months in prison. A jury found the 51-year-old guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted of three other charges, according to the same account.

Perry's name surfaced around the case, but not as part of it. Yahoo says there were reports that she might share private details about Brand and was waiting for the trial to end. It adds that she has made no official statement, and that sources close to her have reportedly told media she is discussing a book and documentary that could cover her marriage. Those claims are unconfirmed, and nothing has been announced.

## A private show in Thailand

The second story comes from [Novara Media](https://novaramedia.com/2026/11/13/katy-perry-performs-for-tech-firm-founded-by-ex-idf-soldiers/), which cites Israeli outlet Israelim. It reports that Perry arrived by surprise on Saturday 7 November to perform for employees of Wiz, an American-Israeli cybersecurity company, during a five-day staff getaway in Khao Lak, Thailand.

Novara notes that Wiz was founded in 2020 by four people who served together in the Israeli army, and it cites the Times of Israel for that background. It also says Alphabet, Google's parent company, acquired Wiz in March 2026 for $32 billion. Novara is a left-leaning outlet and its article takes a critical line on the company and its ties to Israel, so readers should treat its framing as that outlet's view rather than settled fact.

The outlet says Perry has yet to respond to the online backlash over the performance. It points to a post she shared in May 2024 from Unicef calling for a ceasefire and condemning the killing of children in Rafah. Private corporate shows are a common part of the touring business, and neither report says what Perry was paid or how the booking came about.

## What happens next

Perry has not announced any response, and nothing in the sources suggests a statement is scheduled. If the book and documentary mentioned in the Yahoo report are real, details would have to come from her or her team. Until then, the facts are limited to a silent exit on camera and a reported surprise gig.
