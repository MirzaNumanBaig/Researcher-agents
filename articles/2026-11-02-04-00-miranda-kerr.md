**Quick summary**

- [Miranda Kerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Kerr) walked in [Stella McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_McCartney)'s Spring/Summer 2027 show at Paris Fashion Week on 30 September 2026.
- It was her first catwalk appearance since June 2017, when she modelled for Moschino, according to Hello! magazine.
- It was her third McCartney show, after appearances in October 2011 and September 2013.
- Reports describe the walk as a surprise, and it is not clear whether she will model again this season.

The Australian model Miranda Kerr has returned to the runway after nearly a decade away, closing a long gap by walking for Stella McCartney in Paris. The show, part of the Spring/Summer 2027 womenswear schedule, took place on 30 September 2026 and caught many in the audience off guard.

## What Kerr wore

According to [Hello!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/926469/miranda-kerr-makes-bold-runway-return-in-ab-baring-dress/), Kerr wore a seafoam green jersey dress knotted at the waist, with large cutouts that left her midriff bare. The piece also had a high neckline, oversized shoulder pads and ruched detail on the skirt.

[Comic Basics](https://www.comicbasics.com/love-advent-star-miranda-kerr-43-makes-a-surprise-runway-comeback-in-paris-after-nearly-a-decade/) adds that the gown suited the collection's under-the-sea theme, and that she paired it with metallic silver sandals and a small silver clutch. The two outlets describe her hair and make-up differently, so we have left that detail out. The same report says the show was staged on a rain-soaked runway in the gardens of the British ambassador's residence.

## How long she had been away

Hello! reports that her last catwalk before this was Moschino in June 2017. Comic Basics places that show at Milk Studios in Los Angeles. That makes the gap a little over nine years, which the outlets call "nearly a decade".

The McCartney link is not new. Kerr first walked for the British designer in October 2011 and again in September 2013, per Hello!. She last appeared in the Victoria's Secret show in 2011, the same report notes.

Kerr is now 43, according to Comic Basics. Away from fashion, she is the founder and CEO of the skincare brand Kora Organics, which she launched in 2009, and a mother of four boys, Hello! says.

## Reaction so far

Comic Basics says Page Six called the appearance a surprise return, and that online fans praised the cameo. It does not quote specific comments, so the strength of the reaction is hard to measure. The report also says a social post about the walk passed 10,000 views on X.

## What happens next

Nothing in the coverage we could read says Kerr has signed on for more shows. Comic Basics notes she had been stepping back from modelling "by most accounts" and says the walk does not necessarily signal a full comeback. The same report says she also appeared at the Dior show the day before, on 29 September, but gives no detail on that.

For now, treat this as a confirmed one-off return rather than a new chapter. If more bookings follow, they are likely to be announced by the brands involved or by Kerr herself.
