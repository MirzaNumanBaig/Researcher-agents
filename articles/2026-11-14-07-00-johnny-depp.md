**Quick summary**

- *Ebenezer*, Paramount's horror-tinged take on *A Christmas Carol*, made $1.5 million from Thursday previews in North America, according to The Hollywood Reporter and Variety.
- The studio projects about $20 million domestically and another $20 million from 63 international markets.
- It is [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s first major Hollywood release since 2018, and the film has a 52 per cent critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes.
- The budget was $38 million. Depp's next release, *Day Drinker*, is due in March.

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s bid for a studio comeback has its first number. *Ebenezer* grossed $1.5 million from Thursday previews ahead of its Friday release in more than 3,500 North American theatres, [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/johnny-depp-ebenezer-comeback-box-office-previews-rob-bank-1236754682/) reported. The film, directed by [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West), casts Depp as Ebenezer Scrooge in a darker version of the [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens) classic.

## What the Johnny Depp Ebenezer box office numbers say

Paramount expects roughly $20 million from North America over the opening weekend and a further $20 million from 63 markets abroad, according to The Hollywood Reporter. That would put the combined opening near the film's $38 million production budget.

Previews are only a first reading. The same report says Amazon MGM's *How to Rob a Bank* made $900,000 in previews and is expected to open in the high single-digit millions. Sony's *Ghost Soldier* took $3.1 million across its first two days and is projected at $6 million for a five-day opening. *Ebenezer* is clearly the bigger release of the three, but nothing here shows yet how audiences will respond over the weekend.

## A first big studio film in eight years

[Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/box-office-ebenezer-previews-johnny-depp-1236946391/) says *Ebenezer* is Depp's first major Hollywood film in eight years, since *[Fantastic Beasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Beasts): The Crimes of Grindelwald* in 2018. Warner Bros. replaced him in the third *Fantastic Beasts* film with Mads Mikkelsen.

In the gap, Variety notes, he kept working outside the studio system. He appeared in *Minamata* (2020) and *Jeanne du Barry* (2023) and directed the 2024 biopic *Modì, Three Days on the Wing of Madness*. The Hollywood Reporter links the break to the 2022 defamation trial involving his ex-wife [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard) and abuse allegations that Depp has denied.

Variety adds that his next film is Lionsgate's revenge thriller *Day Drinker*, with Madelyn Cline and Penelope Cruz, set for March.

## The cost of the bet

[Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-movie-hollywood-comeback-pr-analysis-2026-11) says the deal was cheap by his old standards. It cites The New York Times as reporting that Depp took a salary of about $5 million in exchange for a larger share of the proceeds if the film performs. For comparison, Business Insider says he once received $25 million up front for 2017's *Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales*.

Paramount has still backed the film with a visible campaign. Business Insider reports Depp appeared in full Scrooge costume at promotional events, including New York's Bryant Park holiday market and San Diego Comic-Con. Crisis PR specialist Tara Goodwin told the outlet that one film does not rebuild a reputation, but that it can shift the conversation from his personal story to his professional work.

## How critics have reacted

The Hollywood Reporter puts the film at 52 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes. Its own critic, Frank Scheck, wrote that it "fulfills its ostensible goal of restoring the actor to A-list status."

Others are cooler. Vanity Fair called Depp's performance mediocre and the film plodding. Third Coast Review found his Scrooge "a bit too troll-like" but praised moments where he reacts with real emotion, and said the grey, dirty look is hard to enjoy. TheOnlyCritic was warmer, calling it a spirited gothic retelling, though it noted there is little holiday cheer and that the film is not suited to younger viewers.

The supporting cast includes Rupert Grint as Bob Cratchit, Ian McKellen as Jacob Marley, Sam Claflin, Daisy Ridley, Andrea Riseborough as the Ghost of Christmas Past and Tramell Tillman as the Ghost of Christmas Present.

## What happens next

The first real test is the opening weekend, when Paramount's $20 million domestic projection will be measured against actual ticket sales. International results from the 63 markets follow. Depp's *Day Drinker* arrives in March.

Producer [Jerry Bruckheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Bruckheimer) said in 2025 that he had talked to Depp about a sixth *Pirates of the Caribbean* film, according to Vanity Fair. Nothing beyond that conversation has been confirmed in the sources reviewed here.
