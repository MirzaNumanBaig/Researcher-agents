**Quick summary**

- [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault.
- Page Six, citing The Sun, reports he was transferred on Friday to HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire, a lower-security prison.
- The details of his cell and first days come from an unnamed insider and have not been confirmed by the prison service.
- Brand denied wrongdoing throughout the trial. A representative has not responded to new allegations from six other women.

[Russell Brand](https://moduscarry.com/tag/russell-brand/) has reportedly been moved to a new prison a week into his sentence. According to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/15/celebrity-news/inside-the-low-security-prison-where-russell-brand-is-serving-10-year-rape-sentence/), which cited The Sun, the 51-year-old was taken to HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire on Friday. Australian outlet The Sydney Morning Herald also reported the transfer on Monday morning, Australian time.

## What is reported about HMP Littlehey

Page Six says Littlehey mainly holds an estimated 1,200 male sex offenders. The Sun's source claimed Brand was placed in a single cell, asked for blankets on his first night and has had extra officers watching over him. The same source said he had not yet mixed with other prisoners.

These accounts come from one unnamed insider and are not confirmed by the Prison Service. They should be read as reported detail, not established fact.

The outlet also describes the activities on offer at the prison: a football league, board games, origami classes, choir sessions, indoor bowling, two gyms and several sports halls. Brand's wife, Laura, 39, can reportedly visit once a week, though conjugal visits are not allowed. The couple has three children.

## Why not a harsher prison?

Page Six reports that Brand was at one point nearly sent to HMP Wakefield, a high-security jail nicknamed "Monster Mansion". Former Metropolitan Police detective Mick Neville told The Sun that Wakefield was likely judged too dangerous, pointing to the 2025 killing there of [Ian Watkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Watkins). Neville said some people would see Littlehey as a soft option, but that keeping Brand safe at Wakefield would have drained resources.

That is one commentator's view. No official reason for the choice was included in the reporting we could read.

## The conviction and the new allegations

Brand was sentenced on 9 November after a trial lasting almost four weeks. He was convicted of rape and sexual assault over incidents dating from the 2000s, and he denied any wrongdoing during the trial. We covered the judge's remarks in our report on [what the judge said on consent](https://moduscarry.com/russell-brand-verdict-consent/).

Page Six also reports that last Thursday six more women accused him of sexual misconduct, with the alleged incidents from around the same period. These are allegations only. A representative for Brand had not responded when Page Six published.

The case has spread beyond one man. NBC News ran an analysis under the headline that the Brand and [David Walliams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Walliams) scandals "provoke a reckoning for British media", while The Guardian's Ella Baron published a cartoon on his first days behind bars. We could not read either piece in full, so we do not summarise their arguments here.

The next confirmed steps are not clear from the sources. Any response to the six new allegations, and any decision on police action, would need to come from Brand's team or the authorities.
