**Quick summary**

- The Robert Pattinson Bond deal is reportedly three films with a bigger fee, and he is said to be the favourite to become the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), per Matthew Belloni in Puck on 13 November.
- Belloni says [Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)'s test deal covers three Bond films, while Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden were asked to commit to four.
- Pattinson is also reported to have negotiated a much larger fee. The amount has not been disclosed.
- Amazon MGM has not announced its choice. Nothing here is confirmed.

The Robert Pattinson Bond deal is the talk of the casting race to succeed Daniel Craig as 007. Puck's Matthew Belloni reported on 13 November that Pattinson was offered a shorter Bond commitment and a bigger fee than his rivals. The Guardian described Belloni as "a credible film-industry source", but Amazon MGM Studios hasn't confirmed anything.

## What does the Robert Pattinson Bond deal reportedly include?

According to [The Guardian's summary of the Puck piece](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/13/robert-pattinson-reportedly-leads-james-bond-contenders), the five finalists were each offered terms before screen tests began. Belloni calls these "test deals". Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden were required to sign for four Bond films if chosen. Pattinson's was cut to three, with a much larger, unspecified fee.

[Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/13/robert-pattinsons-james-bond-deal-is-different-from-the-other-finalists-heres-why-what-that-suggests/) adds a detail from the same report: Pattinson's deal was reportedly so hard to close that "some involved were left wondering why the filmmakers would bother if they didn't really want him". The terms for Nicholas Hoult and Callum Turner were not disclosed.

Belloni reads the pattern as a studio "willing or even eager to sacrifice a bit" to get a name star for its biggest franchise.

## Who else is in the running?

The casting hunt has played out in public over recent weeks. The Guardian says reports first put Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor on the producers' shortlist. Two weeks later Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult were reported to be testing as well.

Comics Gaming Magazine adds that [the Daily Mail has reported](https://www.cgmagonline.com/news/james-bond-new-robert-pattinson-amazon/) Josh O'Connor has since turned down the role. We haven't seen that confirmed elsewhere.

[ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/james-bond-robert-pattinson-casting-conditions-report/) says Pattinson was a late addition to the final screen tests, and that director Denis Villeneuve already knows him from Dune: Part Three, due on 18 December. We covered the earlier test reports in [Robert Pattinson Bond Screen Test Puts Him in 007 Race](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-screen-test/), and the betting side in [Robert Pattinson Bond Odds Close In on Callum Turner](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-odds/).

## Why would Amazon want a star?

Bond 26 is the first film under Amazon MGM, which took creative control of the franchise in a 2025 deal with the previous producers, Eon. The Guardian lists David Heyman and Amy Pascal as producers, Villeneuve as director and Steven Knight, of Peaky Blinders, as writer.

Pattinson is the best-known name on the list, with Twilight, Harry Potter, The Batman and Dune behind him, plus dramas such as The Lighthouse and The Lost City of Z. The Guardian notes that would probably have counted against him under Eon, whose credo was that Bond is bigger than any actor. Belloni argues that has changed: "No franchise is bulletproof any more."

## What does a three-film deal change?

[Den of Geek](https://www.denofgeek.com/movies/james-bond-robert-pattinson-rumors-new-status-quo/) argues a trilogy could suit the series. Craig's run took 16 years to tell a connected story, which Den of Geek ties to the actor tiring of the role, strikes and COVID. Three films would still give audiences a continuing arc, it says, without locking a star in for a decade.

ScreenRant raises one wrinkle: the future of The Batman: Part II. It says the delays could leave Pattinson's place in that franchise open to change, though that is the outlet's speculation. For his other recent work, see [Robert Pattinson's Netflix Thriller The Take Fuels Bond Talk](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-the-take-netflix/).

## What happens next

Amazon MGM hasn't said when it will name its Bond. ScreenRant says an announcement is expected before the end of 2026. Until then, Pattinson can be seen in cinemas in Primetime, and Dune: Part Three arrives on 18 December. Everything on [our Robert Pattinson page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/) will be updated as the story moves.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Robert Pattinson confirmed as the next James Bond?

No. The Guardian reports he is the favourite, citing Puck's Matthew Belloni, but Amazon MGM Studios hasn't announced a casting.

### How many Bond films would Robert Pattinson sign for?

Reportedly three. Belloni says Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden were asked to commit to four.

### Who are the other Bond finalists?

Reports name Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner. The Daily Mail has reportedly said O'Connor turned the role down.

### Who is directing Bond 26?

Denis Villeneuve, who directed the Dune films, according to The Guardian. Steven Knight is writing the script.
