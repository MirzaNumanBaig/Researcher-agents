**Quick summary**

- In 1995, [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) wanted a part in the French period drama *The Horseman on the Roof*, according to Far Out Magazine.
- The director chose French actor Olivier Martinez for the role of Angelo instead.
- Reeves has said he cried when the rejection letter arrived.
- The same year he took a role in *A Walk in the Clouds*, which Roger Ebert reviewed warmly.

Keanu Reeves was one of Hollywood's busiest action stars in 1995, yet he was close to tears over a film he did not get. [Far Out Magazine reports](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/keanu-reeves-was-desperate-cast-french-period-drama-cried-didnt-get-role/) that he went after a role in Jean-Paul Rappeneau's *The Horseman on the Roof*, lost it, and has said the letter telling him so made him cry.

## Why a French period drama?

By the mid-1990s Reeves was a fixture on action posters. Far Out notes that *The Matrix* and *Speed* grossed a reported $467 million and $350 million respectively, which put him at the centre of the era's biggest hits.

He did not want to stay there. The magazine quotes him saying he tends to be cast in heroic roles in the classic sense and that he tries to move away from that a little, because he likes variety. Far Out says that outlook shaped several decisions, including turning down the Val Kilmer part in *Heat* to play Hamlet on stage, passing on the Charlie Sheen role in *Platoon* because it was too violent, and skipping the *Speed* sequel.

## The role he missed

*The Horseman on the Roof* is an adaptation of the 1951 adventure novel by [Jean Giono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Giono). Reeves wanted the part of Angelo, a casting that would have been a sharp turn away from American blockbusters and into a European costume picture.

The director went another way and chose [Olivier Martinez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Martinez). Far Out says Reeves was inconsolable. It quotes him recalling that when the letter arrived telling him he had not been chosen to play Angelo, he cried.

It is a small, human detail about an actor who is now known for calm, unflappable screen roles, and it shows how much he wanted the change.

## The consolation prize

The setback did not slow him for long. The same year, according to Far Out, he took a role in *A Walk in the Clouds*, directed by Alfonso Arau. The film is an English-language remake of the 1942 Italian film *Four Steps in the Clouds*.

Reviews were mixed. Far Out says some newspapers were unkind, but [Roger Ebert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Ebert), a critic known for being tough, gave it a glowing review. For an actor chasing serious recognition, the magazine suggests, that was about the best endorsement available.

## What happens next

The Far Out piece is a look back rather than a new announcement, so there is nothing fresh to report on a *Horseman on the Roof* revival. Reeves's recent work has stayed close to the action genre that he once tried to escape.
