**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) posted a new set of workout photos and video on Instagram this week, according to CinemaBlend.
- The clips show him on cardio machines from his fitness company, Centrfit.
- *Avengers: Doomsday* opens in cinemas on 18 December, with Hemsworth returning as Thor.
- Whether Thor survives the film has not been confirmed, and Marvel has revealed little.

The new Chris Hemsworth workout post, a fresh photo and video set from his training, has landed a month before *Avengers: Doomsday* reaches cinemas. The post, reported by [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/doctor-doom-strong-chris-hemsworth-abs-workout-post-stronger) on 19 November, was published to his [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/chrishemsworth/) account, which CinemaBlend puts at 58.2 million followers.

## What the post shows

According to CinemaBlend, the images and clip show the actor working out on cardio equipment made by Centrfit, his own fitness company. The outlet's writer described the actor as looking in excellent shape and called him 43 years old. The post itself carries no announcement and no film news. It is a training update, and nothing in it ties it to a specific role.

CinemaBlend also notes that Hemsworth has spoken openly about how his body weight changes depending on whether he is about to play Thor again. That context matters because the current photos do not show him mid-production. The outlet says filming on *Doomsday* ran from April to September 2025, so the new post comes well after the cameras stopped.

## Where Thor stands in Doomsday

Hemsworth is due back as Thor in *Avengers: Doomsday*, which CinemaBlend says arrives on 18 December as part of the 2026 release slate. The film pits the Avengers against [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), played by [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), who was previously [Iron Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man) in the franchise.

Some fans worry Thor will not make it through the film. CinemaBlend points out that the last two Avengers films saw several major characters die, among them Iron Man, Black Widow, Gamora, Heimdall and Loki, and that Thor has featured heavily in the marketing so far. The outlet is clear that its reading of the new photos as a hopeful sign for Thor is speculation. It adds that Marvel keeps tight control over plot details and that very little has leaked.

That caution is worth repeating. A workout post says nothing about a character's fate, and the studio has not said what happens to Thor.
