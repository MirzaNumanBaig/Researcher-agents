**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett), Kew Wakehurst's Ambassador, will narrate this year's Glow Wild lantern trail in Sussex.
- Twelve other actors, including Olivia Colman, Daniel Craig and Jude Law, have recorded readings for the trail.
- Glow Wild runs from 26 November 2026 to 2 January 2027, and tickets are on sale now.
- The words were written by Kew Wakehurst volunteers and community groups, according to SussexWorld.

[Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) will guide visitors through this year's Glow Wild, the winter light trail at Kew Wakehurst, with a new soundscape voiced by a cast of well-known actors. SussexWorld [reported on 3 November](https://www.sussexexpress.co.uk/your-world/cate-blanchett-leads-a-cast-of-legendary-star-voices-at-kew-wakehursts-glow-wild-winter-light-trail-9326869) that the trail opens on 26 November 2026 and closes on 2 January 2027.

## Cate Blanchett Glow Wild narration: who is in the cast

Blanchett, who is an Ambassador for Kew Wakehurst, narrates "In Search of Nature's Gifts", the title of this year's trail. She is joined by [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom), [Priyanga Burford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanga_Burford), [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman), [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), [Nick Frost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Frost), [Richard E. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Grant) and [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law).

The list continues with [Lesley Manville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesley_Manville), [Carey Mulligan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carey_Mulligan), [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci), [Dame Harriet Walter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harriet_Walter) and [Naomi Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Watts). That makes 13 voices in total, all recorded for the trail and, according to the report, heard only there.

The scripts are not Hollywood writing. SussexWorld says the words came from Kew Wakehurst's Glow Wild volunteers and community groups, inspired by Kew's mission to research, conserve and restore nature. The actors voice reflections the report describes as sometimes profound and sometimes funny, all carrying the message that nature is a gift worth protecting.

## What visitors will see on the trail

Now in its 13th year, Glow Wild is billed as the most ambitious edition yet. The theme takes visitors "pole to pole", according to the report. Along the way they pass a nearly five-metre illuminated iceberg, which is the tallest lantern the event has produced, and a colony of handmade penguins.

An alpine flower carpet made from thousands of handmade petals sits further along. It points to the work of Kew scientists safeguarding Arctic plants. The trail ends with a bespoke projection across Wakehurst's historic Mansion.

Bespoke artworks and thousands of handmade lanterns fill the gardens, and the installations were created exclusively for Kew Wakehurst.

## Blanchett's hands-on role

Blanchett did more than record a voiceover. In July she joined one of Wakehurst's lantern-making workshops to help build part of an installation of more than 200 handcrafted lanterns made by volunteers. Students from Peacehaven Community School took part with her, as part of the 100 Mile Classroom project, a Weald to Waves initiative led by the Knepp Wildland Foundation.

Weekly workshops run through the year ahead of the event, and the volunteers' work is central to the lanterns on show.

"What a joy to join one of Wakehurst's workshops and create a lantern for the magical Glow Wild this year," Blanchett said. She added that the event is "a not-to-be-missed date in our family diary".

Kew Wakehurst Director Susan Raikes said the event depends on the "vision, care and passion" of artists and volunteers. She said she was delighted to have Blanchett narrating alongside the other actors for tens of thousands of visitors.

## A link to Kew's seed conservation work

Wakehurst is a wild botanic garden in Sussex and home to Kew's Millennium Seed Bank, described as the world's largest store of seeds from wild plant species. For the bank's 25th anniversary in 2025, Blanchett joined His Majesty The King in conversation for Kew's podcast, Unearthed: The Need for Seeds, discussing the bank's achievements and conservation work around the world.
