**Quick summary**

- The UK government posted a joke on Instagram about [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) applying for all 90,000 jobs in its youth employment scheme, according to The Guardian.
- The post riffs on a wave of "job stealing" memes that followed his casting as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- It had more than 117,000 likes and 1,400 comments when The Guardian reported on 19 November 2026.
- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) has said he wants a Bond who is a killer first, ScreenRant reports.

The Robert Pattinson James Bond memes have now reached Westminster. The UK government used its official Instagram account to promote its youth jobs guarantee with a picture of the actor as 007 and a caption saying he would apply for all 90,000 jobs created through the scheme, [The Guardian reported](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/19/robert-pattinson-james-bond-uk-government-youth-employment-scheme) on 19 November.

## How the government joined the joke

The scheme was first announced in August, according to The Guardian. The Department for Work and Pensions shared the Instagram post, which borrows the tone of the memes that appeared after Pattinson was named as Bond following what the paper describes as a seemingly late intervention in the process.

The paper says the post appears to have worked. At the time of writing it had passed 117,000 likes and 1,400 comments, a strong result for an official government account promoting an employment programme.

## Why the "job stealing" memes took off

The jokes centre on the idea that Pattinson keeps winning roles other people wanted. One post, "AI won't take my job. Robert Pattinson will", has reportedly collected millions of likes.

Ralph Fiennes added to it. After saying his first reaction to the casting was that he was "still in love with Daniel Craig", he joked that the new Harry Potter TV series might offer its Voldemort role to Pattinson too, The Guardian reports.

An older clip has resurfaced as well. In a 2025 Vanity Fair lie detector interview with Jennifer Lawrence, Pattinson was asked whether he would make another Twilight film. He answered, not entirely seriously, that he likes taking jobs from younger actors and wants to play 17 again.

The memes have a kinder side. Social media users have been commiserating with Nicholas Hoult, who like Pattinson was apparently a late addition to the shortlist and has missed out on other major roles, including Superman, according to the same report.

## What Villeneuve wants from his Bond

ScreenRant reports that Pattinson beat several high-profile names to the part, among them [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor. The film is being made by Amazon Studios with Villeneuve directing.

The pair have worked together before. Pattinson plays the shape-shifting villain Scytale in Dune: Part Three, which ScreenRant says opens next month. The outlet notes he is 40, previously led the Twilight films and is the current screen Batman, though it is unclear whether he will keep that role alongside Bond.

Villeneuve told GQ what he is looking for. "The first quality is that I need to believe that this man is a killer," he said, adding that Bond is more an assassin than a spy and that he needs "that kind of anger deep in the eye." ScreenRant argues that Pattinson's darker work, including Good Time, The Lighthouse and The Devil All the Time, fits that brief.

Pattinson's most recent film, Primetime, directed by Lance Oppenheim, opened on 5 September 2026. Rotten Tomatoes lists it at 86 per cent, and Wikipedia's entry puts its box office at about US$62 million against a US$14.5 million budget. His next listed release is The Take, directed by Fernando Meirelles, due on 15 January 2027.

## A passing mention elsewhere

The actor is also now a shorthand for a certain kind of star. LaineyGossip this week asked whether Hunger Games lead [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) and his We Were Liars co-star [Esther McGregor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_McGregor) could become the Pattinson and [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart) of their generation. That is an opinion piece, not news, but it shows how closely his Twilight-era rise is still tied to his name.
