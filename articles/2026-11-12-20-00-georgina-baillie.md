**Quick summary**

- [Georgina Baillie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgina_Baillie) wrote a character reference for [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) that was put before the judge at his sentencing hearing on Monday.
- The Press Association has now obtained the letter, according to LBC and The Independent.
- She wrote that the two had around 12 "casual sexual encounters" over about a year after meeting in 2006, and that he "understood and respected boundaries".
- Brand was sentenced to 10 and a half years in prison after a jury found him guilty of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
- The Metropolitan Police says it is assessing further complaints with the Crown Prosecution Service.

The granddaughter of the late actor [Andrew Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Sachs) has described Russell Brand as "always lovely" in a letter handed to the judge before he was jailed. The Press Association obtained the document on 12 November 2026, and it was reported by [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/russell-brand-andrew-sachs-georgina-baillie-5HjdmHb_2/) and [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/crime/russell-brand-georgina-baillie-andrew-sachs-b3083616.html). The letter was presented to Mr Justice Bennathan at Brand's sentencing hearing.

## What Georgina Baillie wrote

Baillie, an artist and art therapist, said she met Brand in 2006 and that they had around 12 "casual sexual encounters" over the following year, according to the reports. She described her own experience only. "Russell never behaved aggressively, coercively or without regard to my wishes," she wrote.

She added that she never knew him to be dishonest with her and still regarded him as "truthful and reliable". The Daily Mail, which first reported the letter, quoted her saying that "in my experience" he understood and respected boundaries.

Her account of the relationship was personal and warm in places. She wrote that she "grew fond" of him, called him a "genuinely nice person to me" and described a man who was quick-witted but also "thoughtful, reserved and contemplative". She was also critical. In her words, he could be "immature" and had "a large ego", and his comedy was often "cutting, shocking or crass".

## The Sachsgate connection

Baillie was at the centre of the 2008 "Sachsgate" scandal. As LBC and The Independent recount, Brand and [Jonathan Ross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Ross) left four voicemails for Sachs about Brand's sexual relationship with his granddaughter. The recording was later aired on Brand's BBC Radio 2 show, and he was sacked from the programme.

Baillie told the court Brand had later left her a voicemail "suggesting I go to my grandfather's house to delete the message". She also complained of being "vilified in the press and cast as some sort of immoral witch". Brand later paid for her to go to rehab while she was battling alcohol addiction, she said.

She closed the letter by acknowledging the history. "I have perhaps just as good a reason as anyone not to support Russell, but I still choose to support him because of the person I know him to be," she wrote.

## The verdict and the sentence

A jury found Brand guilty of four offences dating from 2004 to 2009. According to LBC, he was convicted of rape and three counts of sexual assault, and was sentenced on Monday to 10 and a half years in prison. The offences concerned four women and are not connected to Baillie.

The judge called Brand "staggeringly arrogant" and said he had assaulted women believing his fame and status would let him get away with it. The Daily Mail reported that the plea for leniency did not appear to sway the court, and that some legal experts thought the sentence harsher than expected. That second point is the paper's reporting of expert opinion, not a finding of the court.

Baillie's letter was one of six character references used by Brand's legal team. The Independent reports that the Press Association has obtained the other five too, with names redacted by his lawyers. A friend of Brand said he had paid around £400,000 to send at least 20 people through rehab for alcohol and substance addiction.

## What happens next

The Metropolitan Police has said it has received more complaints about Brand since the trial began, according to The Independent. Two allegations of rape were also made before the trial, and the force is assessing with the Crown Prosecution Service whether to bring further charges. No decision has been announced.
