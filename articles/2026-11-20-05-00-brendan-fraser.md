**Quick summary**

- [Brendan Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Fraser) says director [Terrence Malick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrence_Malick) offered him a role in The Thin Red Line (1998).
- According to Far Out Magazine, Malick then forgot he had made the offer, so the part never happened.
- Fraser says Malick was a fan of his 1992 comedy Encino Man.
- The war drama earned seven Oscar nominations, including Best Picture and Best Director.

Brendan Fraser came close to appearing in one of the most admired war films of the late 1990s. According to a report published by [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/brendan-fraser-supposed-to-be-in-one-of-best-movies-1998-director-forgot/) on 19 November 2026, the actor was offered a role in The Thin Red Line by Terrence Malick, who then lost track of the offer altogether.

## What Fraser says happened

Fraser, now an Academy Award-winning Best Actor for The Whale, recalled meeting Malick for the project. "He actually offered me something," he said, as quoted by Far Out. The reason, he explained, was Malick's fondness for [Encino Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encino_Man), the 1992 comedy that first made Fraser a name.

By Fraser's account, Malick asked where that performer had come from and compared him to silent-film greats. "He just came down from the Moon or something," Fraser quoted the director as saying, adding that Malick likened him to [Charlie Chaplin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Chaplin) and [Buster Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buster_Keaton). Fraser said he had thought the same thing about the character himself.

Far Out says Fraser took the mix-up in good humour. The outlet's reading is that the role simply slipped Malick's mind, though the quotes it carries do not spell out how Fraser found out. The article does not say when Fraser gave the interview, and the details come from his own recollection.

## Where it sat in Fraser's career

The timing makes the story more striking. Fraser was already on the rise in 1998. Far Out notes he drew decent reviews for James Ford Robinson's low-budget drama Still Breathing, then took second billing behind [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) in Bill Condon's Gods and Monsters, which earned McKellen an Oscar nomination for Best Actor.

The following year, according to the same report, The Mummy made him a major name. [Stephen Sommers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Sommers)' adventure is still one of the most rewatched blockbusters of its era. It was successful enough that Dudley Do-Right, which arrived three months later and struggled at the box office, did not slow his climb.

Far Out also points out that [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan) counts The Thin Red Line among the great films, which is why a role in it would have carried weight for a rising actor.

## A film famous for its missing names

Fraser is in good company. The same report lists several actors who were hired for the film and shot scenes that were later cut or reduced, among them [Mickey Rourke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Rourke), [Bill Pullman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Pullman), [Lukas Haas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lukas_Haas) and [Billy Bob Thornton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Bob_Thornton). [Adrien Brody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrien_Brody) filmed the whole production believing he was the lead, and, as Far Out puts it, he was not.

Even so, the film landed seven Oscar nominations, including Best Picture and Best Director. In awards season it was largely overshadowed by [Saving Private Ryan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saving_Private_Ryan), another 1998 war film. Far Out's point is that the missed part cost Fraser a credit on a celebrated picture rather than a place in a hit that lost out.

## Frequently asked questions

### Was Brendan Fraser in The Thin Red Line?

No. Fraser says Terrence Malick offered him a part, but he does not appear in the finished 1998 film.

### Why did Terrence Malick forget the offer?

Far Out Magazine reports that the director forgot he had made it. The report gives no further explanation.

### Which films was Brendan Fraser in during 1998 and 1999?

According to Far Out, he appeared in Still Breathing and Gods and Monsters in 1998, then The Mummy in 1999.
