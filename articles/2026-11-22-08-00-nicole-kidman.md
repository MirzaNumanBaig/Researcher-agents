**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) told Entertainment Tonight that her late mother, Janelle, helped her decide to let daughter Sunday Rose model.
- Sunday Rose, now 18, opened Miu Miu's Spring/Summer 2025 show in Paris in October 2024, a month after her grandmother's death.
- She is Vogue Germany's November 2026 cover star and is in her final year of high school in Nashville.
- Separately, Grazia says Kidman wore sculptural trousers in New York, a look it ties to the Spring/Summer 2027 runways.

The story of how **Nicole Kidman Sunday Rose** reached the catwalk turns out to start with a grandmother's reply. According to [Country Thang Daily](https://www.countrythangdaily.com/sunday-rose-kidman-urbans-modeling-began-with-her-late-grandmothers-parting-gift/), the actress put the question of her eldest daughter's modelling to her own mother, and the answer decided it.

## The answer that settled it

Sunday Rose told Vogue Germany she was 14 when she worked out what modelling was, and that she raised it with her mother from then on. Kidman told her to wait until she was at least 16, Country Thang Daily reports.

When that age arrived, Kidman turned to her mother. She told [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) she asked Janelle whether she should let her granddaughter do it. The reply, as Kidman recalled it, was that Kidman had done it herself and so had to allow it. Kidman calls that answer "my mom's parting gift to Sunny".

Janelle Kidman died in September 2024, aged 84. A month later her granddaughter walked her first runway.

## From Paris to a Vogue cover

That first show was a big one. Sunday Rose opened Miu Miu's Spring/Summer 2025 presentation at Paris Fashion Week in October 2024. Alexa Chung, Cara Delevingne and Willem Dafoe also walked, according to the report.

Since then, NYLON notes campaigns for Miu Miu and Omega, and Vogue Germany adds shows and campaigns for Dior and Calvin Klein. She went to the 2026 Met Gala with her mother on 4 May, and Getty Images photos show her at the Valentino show in Paris on 4 October 2026. She is Vogue Germany's cover star for November 2026.

She has also acknowledged her position. "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have," she told the magazine, adding that she is grateful to take them.

## A final year of school in Nashville

Between fashion weeks, Sunday Rose lives in Nashville and has started her last year of high school, with college applications ahead. Country Thang Daily reports that filmmaking has been her ambition since she was five and that she hopes to study it. She has already had cameos in her mother's series Big Little Lies and The Undoing.

Before modelling, the report says, the family kept the children largely out of public view. Kidman told Vogue in 2017 that she was protective of her daughters' identities. Sunday Rose told NYLON in 2025 that meeting new people who know nothing about her family is "sort of wonderful".

The report also states that her parents' divorce was finalised in January, after Kidman filed in Nashville in September 2025, and that Kidman was named primary residential parent. Her father is [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban). Some things still pass between mother and daughter: "I take everything from my mom's closet," she told NYLON.

## Kidman's own look: sculptural trousers

Kidman's wardrobe is making news too. Grazia reports she was seen in New York in a grey suit with voluminous trousers, a blouse with a pie-crust collar and a velvet Chanel handbag. The magazine ties the look to sculpture-inspired trousers shown at Balenciaga and Celine for Spring/Summer 2027.

Grazia's styling advice is to keep the top half tailored so the fabric below can do the talking. It is the publication's reading of a trend, not a statement from Kidman. ## What happens next

No new modelling dates were given in the reports. Sunday Rose's school year and college plans come first, and the Vogue Germany issue with her cover has been on sale since 24 October. Everything we publish on the actress sits on our [Nicole Kidman page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/nicole-kidman/).
