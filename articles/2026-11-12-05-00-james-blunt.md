**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) says he will legally change his name to Ryan Air if his album Airplane Mode reaches Number 1 on the UK Official Albums Chart.
- The chart is announced on Friday 13 November, and Official Charts reports Blunt was at Number 2 in the midweek update, behind Royal Blood.
- He made a similar pledge in 2024, promising to become "Blunty McBluntface", but the Back to Bedlam reissue stopped at Number 7.
- Airplane Mode is his first studio album since 2023, and he has added a second Oxford launch show.

James Blunt has promised to legally become Ryan Air if Airplane Mode takes the top spot on the UK albums chart this week. The singer made the pledge in an Instagram post, according to [Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/james-blunt-vows-to-legally-change-his-name-to-ryan-air-if-he-makes-number-1/), with the final result due on Friday 13 November.

## The pledge, and the 2024 precedent

The post is classic Blunt: self-deprecating and aimed squarely at fans. He reminded followers that in 2024 he vowed to change his name to Blunty McBluntface if a reissue of his debut album Back to Bedlam hit Number 1. It peaked at Number 7. "Your fault," he told them, before offering a "second chance" with the new record.

His closing line sums up the tone. Stream it, buy it, he said, or he will "have to carry on being James Blunt. And nobody wants that."

The name he picked is a pun on the budget airline, a nod to the album's aeronautical title. Official Charts notes that the stunt is a legal name change, not just a social media joke, if it comes off.

## Where the chart race stands

Airplane Mode is his eighth studio album. According to Official Charts, it is fighting for the top spot with Royal Blood's fifth album, Dead Company, which led the midweek figures. Blunt sat at Number 2 in that update. Midweek positions can shift as the week's sales and streams are finished, so the Friday chart is the one that counts.

The numbers give him a respectable case. Blunt has two Number 1 albums already: Back to Bedlam and All the Lost Souls, released in 2007. Six more of his albums have reached the Top 10. A third chart-topper would end a long wait, since his last Number 1 came almost two decades ago.

We covered the midweek position in more detail in [James Blunt's Airplane Mode Heads for Number 2 on UK Chart](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-airplane-mode/).

## What Airplane Mode is about

The Oxford Mail reports that the album takes its name from the phone setting that cuts off calls, messages and online distractions. Blunt has described it as a reflection on switching off from the pressures of modern life, and as his strongest work in 20 years.

Opening track "Circles" is about the late-night loop of worries that keeps people awake, according to [Songfacts](https://www.songfacts.com/facts/james-blunt/circles). It leans on 1990s Britpop and early 2000s indie guitar sounds. "This is what I wanted my music to sound like," Blunt said there. He also described losing his way and finding it again "in a studio in Banbury".

He co-wrote the song with George Tizzard and Rick Parkhouse, the production duo known as Red Triangle. They also worked on the lead single, [Tastes Like Summer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tastes_Like_Summer), and have credits with [Cheryl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl_(singer)), [Louis Tomlinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Tomlinson) and [James Arthur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Arthur). Songfacts places the record as his first since 2023's Who We Used To Be, and as a fresh chapter for the man behind the 2005 hit "[You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful)".

## Launch shows add to the push

Fans can also see him play live. The Oxford Mail says promoters added a 5pm matinee at O2 Academy Oxford on Tuesday 1 December after heavy ticket demand, alongside the 7.30pm evening show. Both are acoustic sets of new songs and past hits, open to ages eight and over, with under-14s accompanied by an adult.

The launch campaign also includes appearances at Circuit in Kingston upon Thames, Prospect Building in Bristol and Union Chapel in London. At Union Chapel he will take part in an in-conversation event with [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) co-star [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan).

## What happens next

The Official Albums Chart is published on Friday 13 November. If Blunt beats Royal Blood, Official Charts says his "new era as Ryan Air" begins. If not, he stays James Blunt, and the 1 December Oxford shows go ahead either way.
