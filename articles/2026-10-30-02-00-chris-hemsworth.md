**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) posted several shirtless gym photos to his Instagram stories this week, according to CinemaBlend.
- The 43-year-old plays Thor, and the site reads the photos as a hopeful sign for the character beyond Avengers: Doomsday.
- Nothing about Thor's fate has been confirmed; the idea is fan speculation.
- Avengers: Doomsday opens on December 18, with Secret Wars currently expected a year later, on December 17, 2027.

A fresh set of gym photos from Chris Hemsworth has Marvel fans asking a familiar question: does Thor survive the next two Avengers films? The actor shared the images on his Instagram stories, and [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/chris-hemsworth-thor-fit-swole-pics-feel-like-secret-wars-hint) said on October 29 that his physique looks like a hint about his role in Secret Wars.

## What Hemsworth posted

According to CinemaBlend, the 43-year-old shared a run of shirtless photos that show him looking noticeably muscular. The writer calls them proof that he is not slowing down at the gym. The outlet is careful to say the pictures are not a real confirmation of anything. Hemsworth did not, in the material reviewed, tie the posts to any film.

You can follow his account directly on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/chrishemsworth/). Our full coverage of the actor sits on the [Chris Hemsworth tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/chris-hemsworth/).

## Why fans are reading so much into it

Hemsworth has played Thor since 2011. CinemaBlend counts nine films with the character so far, plus a few cameos, and notes that he has kept his superhero build through that whole stretch.

The speculation matters because of how the franchise treats its heroes. CinemaBlend recalls that the first Avengers film lost Coulson and that Pietro died in Age of Ultron. [Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Infinity_War) then removed Gamora, Vision, Loki, Heimdall and half of all life. Those who were turned to dust returned in Endgame, but Black Widow and [Iron Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Man) did not. With that record, the outlet says, fans expect more deaths in Doomsday.

## What is known about Thor in Doomsday

Hemsworth was confirmed in the Doomsday cast announcement and appears set for a sizeable part, CinemaBlend reports. It adds that the Russo Brothers have hinted Thor will return to the fallen-warrior mindset he had after Infinity War. The Doomsday trailer, per the same outlet, shows Thor facing the title villain alone, which is why many viewers sense high stakes for him.

The villain is [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), played in the film by [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.). The big open question, CinemaBlend says, is not Doomsday itself but whether Thor appears in Secret Wars. Marvel has not said, and little is known about the Russo Brothers' plans for that film.

## Worth keeping in proportion

A gym photo is not a casting announcement. A strong physique tells us nothing about a script. The CinemaBlend piece is an opinion column that frames the pictures as a "hopeful update", and readers should treat it that way.

## What happens next

Avengers: Doomsday reaches cinemas on December 18, part of the 2026 release slate. Secret Wars is currently set for December 17, 2027. Until then, Marvel's own announcements, not Instagram stories, will decide whether Thor has a future past Doomsday.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did Chris Hemsworth confirm he is in Secret Wars?

No. CinemaBlend says the photos are not confirmation of his role in Secret Wars. He was confirmed for Avengers: Doomsday only.

### When does Avengers: Doomsday come out?

It is scheduled for December 18, 2026, according to CinemaBlend. Secret Wars is expected on December 17, 2027.

### Does Thor die in Avengers: Doomsday?

Marvel has not said. CinemaBlend reports that fans fear it, since the trailer shows Thor facing Doctor Doom alone, but that is speculation.
