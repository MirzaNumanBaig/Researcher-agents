**Quick summary**

- [Jim Carrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Carrey) listed his Maui, Hawaii, beachfront estate in early October for US$35 million.
- Less than a month later the home was marked "pending", according to a November 3 Realtor listing update reported by El Cronista.
- He paid US$16.5 million for it in 2016, so the asking price was just over double that.
- The final sale price has not been disclosed, and Carrey has not said why he is selling.

[Jim Carrey](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jim-carrey/) has found a buyer for his Maui estate in under a month. The actor, 64, put the Keawakapu Beach property on the market in early October at US$35 million, and by November 3 the listing showed a "pending" status, [El Cronista reports](https://www.cronista.com/en/today/in-2016-jim-carrey-bought-a-home-in-maui-hawaii-for-165-million-now-he-listed-it-for-35-million-and-found-a-buyer-in-weeks/). No final price has been made public.

## What he paid and what he asked

Records cited by El Cronista show Carrey bought the home in 2016 for US$16.5 million. The October asking price of US$35 million was therefore more than twice that figure. If the sale closes near the list price, he will have more than doubled his money over roughly a decade of ownership.

A pending status means an offer has been accepted, not that the deal is done. Until the sale closes, the real number stays private. Public records usually show it only after settlement.

## Inside the beachfront estate

The property sits on close to an acre, with 170 feet of ocean frontage along Keawakapu Beach. It was built in 2002 and designed by the architecture firm Ike Kligerman Barkley, according to the report.

The main house has three bedrooms, a chef's kitchen with two large islands, and a primary suite with a swinging bed and floor-to-ceiling windows. A separate guest house adds two more bedrooms. Between the two buildings are a pool with a hidden grotto, a slide and an elevated spa.

The estate is being sold fully furnished, with woven bamboo interiors and Douglas fir framing. Those details come from the listing as described by El Cronista.

## A quiet life away from Hollywood

The sale closes a long chapter in which Maui served as a retreat. El Cronista notes that in 2018 Carrey described his life there as "an isolated life", and that reports have described days filled with reading, writing, walking and painting. In 2022 he said he was "fairly serious" about stepping away from acting, adding: "I really like my quiet life."

He has not explained the decision to sell, so any theory about his plans is speculation. What is on record is that this is not his first large property sale. He bought a Brentwood estate in Los Angeles in 1994 for US$3.8 million, listed it in 2023 for US$28.9 million, and sold it in 2024 for US$17 million after several price cuts. The Maui sale looks far smoother by comparison.

The sale now has to close before the price becomes known. Once it does, property records should show what the buyer paid, and that will settle whether the estate went for the full US$35 million or for less. Carrey has made no public comment on the sale, and we will update this story if he does.
