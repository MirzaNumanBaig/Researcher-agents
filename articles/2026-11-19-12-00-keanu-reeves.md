**Quick summary**

- Point Break, the 1991 action thriller starring Keanu Reeves, is due to leave Netflix on 1 December 2026, according to What's on Netflix as cited by ComingSoon.net.
- The report refers to viewers in the United States. Availability elsewhere, including Australia, was not covered.
- Kathryn Bigelow directed the film, which pairs Reeves with Patrick Swayze.
- Rotten Tomatoes lists a 69 percent critics' score from 78 reviews and a 79 percent audience score.

Fans of [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) have a few weeks left to watch one of his best-known early films on Netflix. [Point Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Break) is scheduled to be removed from the service on 1 December 2026, according to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2226124-keanu-reeves-point-break-leaving-netflix), which attributes the date to the tracking site What's on Netflix.

## When is Point Break leaving Netflix?

The 1991 film is due to leave on 1 December 2026. ComingSoon.net frames the departure as a United States matter, saying American viewers have until next month to watch it. The report does not say whether the film is leaving Netflix in other countries, so Australian readers should check their own catalogue before planning a viewing.

Streaming libraries rotate constantly, and titles often disappear with little notice. A confirmed date gives fans a clear deadline.

## What is Point Break about?

[Kathryn Bigelow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Bigelow) directed the film from a script by W. Peter Iliff. It opened in cinemas on 12 July 1991.

Reeves plays Johnny Utah, an FBI agent sent to investigate a string of bank robberies carried out by a gang known as the Ex-Presidents. Working alongside fellow agent Angelo Pappas, Utah follows a lead into a local surfing community and goes undercover.

With help from a surfer named Tyler, he gets to know Bodhi and his tight-knit group. Utah becomes friends with Bodhi, and the surfer's lifestyle and beliefs start to rub off on him. Then he begins to suspect that Bodhi and his crew are the robbers he has been chasing.

## Who is in the cast?

[Patrick Swayze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Swayze) plays Bodhi Bodhisattva opposite Reeves. [Gary Busey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Busey) appears as Pappas, and [Lori Petty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lori_Petty) plays Tyler Ann Endicott. John C. McGinley is FBI Director Ben Harp, and James LeGros plays Roach.

## How did critics rate it?

ComingSoon.net describes the response as generally positive. On Rotten Tomatoes, the film holds a 69 percent critics' score across 78 reviews, while its audience score sits at 79 percent. The gap suggests viewers have been a little warmer on it than reviewers. Loud and Clear Reviews, quoted in the report, called it a full-throttle action showcase that makes the most of its 1990s flair.

## What happens next

If the date holds, Point Break will be gone from Netflix's US catalogue after 1 December 2026. The source does not say where the film will stream next.
