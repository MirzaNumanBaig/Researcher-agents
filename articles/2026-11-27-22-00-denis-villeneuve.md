**Quick summary**

- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) describes *Dune: Part Three* as "the most epic and yet the most personal film" he has made, according to Korean outlets MK and SBS.
- The film opens first in South Korea on 16 December, ahead of the rest of the world.
- Advance bookings there passed 100,000 tickets in two days, and it led reservation rankings four hours after sales opened.
- Separately, Geo News reports that Villeneuve is directing the next James Bond film, with no casting or release date announced.

Denis Villeneuve has called *Dune: Part Three* the biggest and most personal film of his career, in publicity material reported on 27 November by South Korea's MK and SBS. The final chapter of the trilogy reaches cinemas in South Korea on 16 December, the first market in the world to see it.

## What Villeneuve says about the final chapter

According to [MK](https://www.mk.co.kr/en/hot-issues/12220164), Villeneuve described the film as "the most epic and yet the most personal film I've made". He also said it is more suspenseful and emotionally intense than the one before it. The outlet reads that as a promise to spend as much time inside the characters' heads as on spectacle.

The story follows Paul, played by Timothée Chalamet, who has taken absolute power and turned into a tyrant. Zendaya's Chani, a desert warrior, stands against him. [SBS](https://news.sbs.co.kr/english/article.do?news_id=N1008866877) describes a war that shakes the universe alongside a psychological clash between the two.

Both reports stress that Villeneuve carries the whole trilogy. He adapted and directed a project he had wanted to make for years, and he is responsible for both its start and its end.

## The cast's view of the director

The same publicity material quotes three of the cast. Chalamet said he was deeply inspired by watching Villeneuve guide a film of such scale with confidence and precision. [Jason Momoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Momoa), who returns as the revived character Hayt, said no one else could make *Dune* the way Villeneuve does and called him one of the greatest directors working today.

[Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy), who plays Paul's sister Alia, said he "brings out the best in everyone". These are promotional quotes, so they show how the film is being sold more than how it plays on screen.

## The numbers behind the hype

MK and SBS both say the first film and *Dune: Part Two* together won eight Academy Awards and passed US$1.1 billion at the worldwide box office. That is the benchmark the finale has to meet.

Early signs from Korea are strong. The outlets report that *Part Three* took first place in overall advance-booking rates just four hours after tickets went on sale, and passed 100,000 advance tickets within two days. Advance sales are not final box office, and the reports do not give figures for other countries.

## A career built across genres

Both Korean reports place the trilogy in the context of Villeneuve's earlier work, from *Incendies* and *Sicario* to *Arrival* and *Blade Runner 2049*, the sequel to [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner). Our earlier coverage of the sequel's influence is in [Blade Runner 2099 Borrows From Denis Villeneuve's Sequel](https://moduscarry.com/blade-runner-2099-denis-villeneuve/).

## Bond casting talk keeps his name in the headlines

Villeneuve is also in the news for another reason. Geo News reports that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was confirmed as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) a little over a week ago, with Villeneuve directing the Amazon film. No casting decision on the lead female role has been announced, and there is no release date.

The outlet says [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) has moved to 3/1 second favourite with William Hill after saying she texted Villeneuve to ask about a villain role. She sent the message on the day he presented her with an award at the Elle Women in Hollywood event. [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley) is favourite at 5/4, and [Suki Waterhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suki_Waterhouse) is third at 4/1. [Maya Jama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Jama) and Taylor-Joy are also mentioned, without quoted odds. William Hill itself warned that the market is likely to change before any announcement, so treat the odds as speculation. More is in [Denis Villeneuve's Bond Film: Casting Talk Heats Up](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-film-2/).

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Dune: Part Three come out?
It opens in South Korea on 16 December, which MK and SBS describe as the first release worldwide. Dates for other countries were not in the reports we read.

### Who stars in Dune: Part Three?
Timothée Chalamet plays Paul and Zendaya plays Chani. Jason Momoa returns as Hayt and Anya Taylor-Joy plays Alia, according to the Korean reports.

### Is Denis Villeneuve directing the next James Bond film?
Geo News reports that he is, with Robert Pattinson confirmed as Bond. No release date has been set.
