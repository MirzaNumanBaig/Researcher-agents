**Quick summary**

- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) told Time he is "not here to change the DNA of James Bond".
- The interview was recorded about a week before [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was named as the new 007, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Villeneuve predicted a "hurricane" of reaction to the casting and compared it with the backlash when Daniel Craig took the role.
- Bond 26 has no release date. Steven Knight is writing the script.

Denis Villeneuve has promised that his Bond film will keep the character's traditions, in his first public comments on the franchise since Robert Pattinson was announced as the next 007. Speaking to Time, the director said he wants to embrace Bond rather than reinvent him, and that he already expects a loud reaction to his choice of lead.

## What Villeneuve told Time

Villeneuve was careful not to give much away. "I don't want to talk too much about Bond because it's still in the oven cooking," he said, according to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/robert-pattinson-bond-movie-denis-villeneuve-1236764148/). He called this stage his favourite, "because everything is possible".

What he did offer was a statement of intent. "I love traditions. I love the unapologetic way the character behaves, the panache," he said. "I'm not here to change the DNA of James Bond. I'm here to embrace it, to bring it to the world of today."

He also described himself as a "hardcore" Bond fan who wants other fans to enjoy the film, while admitting it will be "tainted by my sensibility". The Hollywood Reporter noted that the remarks land in the middle of a long debate about how a character from the 1960s should behave today, a debate the later Daniel Craig films tried to answer.

## A hurricane before the first frame

The more striking part of the interview, reported by [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/movies/denis-villeneuve-defends-casting-james-bond-robert-pattinson/), is Villeneuve's expectation of backlash. He recalled that Craig was dismissed as too blond and too short when he was cast, and said the actor's interpretation is what defines Bond for many people now. That was in 2006, he noted, "before social media".

"I expect a hurricane when we bring our choice to the world. Some people will be happy. Some will be mad," he said. "At the end of the day, the truth will be on the screen." He also said he reads nothing about the betting markets that formed before the announcement and cannot "listen to the rumors".

Because the conversation took place before the reveal, none of these remarks were a direct answer to the reaction that followed. Some of that reaction has been noisy. PinkNews reports that a GB News presenter recently claimed Pattinson was "not even British" and was corrected live on air. Pattinson is British.

## What is confirmed about Bond 26

Not much beyond the casting. According to India Today, Amazon MGM Studios announced on 16 November that Pattinson, 40, would become the seventh actor to play Bond in the Eon Productions series. He succeeds Craig, whose run ended with *No Time to Die* in 2021. Villeneuve directs from a script by *Peaky Blinders* creator Steven Knight, and the film is his first project outside the *Dune* universe since *Blade Runner 2049* in 2017, TheWrap reports.

In the studio announcement, quoted by India Today, Pattinson called the part "the honour of a lifetime" and Villeneuve said his star "has the timeless soul of 007". AOL notes that earlier reports suggested Pattinson may have signed for three films. That figure has not been confirmed in the sources we read, so treat it as a report only.

## The villain question

With the lead settled, speculation has moved to the antagonist. The Hollywood Reporter says oddsmakers rate Tom Hardy as a strong chance. Danny Boyle, who was originally attached to direct *No Time to Die*, has said he is "beginning to talk about" Jack O'Connell as a possible villain, and that O'Connell and Pattinson share a quality that makes audiences want to keep watching. None of this is official.

Pattinson's casting has also pulled in political commentary. PinkNews reports that US President Donald Trump, asked about the news in the Oval Office, wished the actor luck. It is background noise rather than news about the film.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is directing the next James Bond film?

Denis Villeneuve, the Canadian director of *Dune* and *Blade Runner 2049*, is directing Bond 26 from a script by Steven Knight.

### Will Villeneuve change James Bond?

He says no. In his Time interview he said he is "not here to change the DNA of James Bond" and wants to bring the character to the world of today, though he admits his own style will show.

### When is the Robert Pattinson Bond film out?

No release date has been announced for the 26th Bond film.

### Has the villain been cast?

No. Talk of Tom Hardy and Jack O'Connell is speculation, not confirmation.
