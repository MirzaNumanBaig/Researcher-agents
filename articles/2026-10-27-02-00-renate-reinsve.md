**Quick summary**

- Norwegian actress Renate Reinsve has reportedly met Marvel Studios about playing Moira MacTaggert in its X-Men reboot.
- The reports surfaced on 26 October 2026 and come from online scoopers, not from Marvel or Reinsve.
- Epicstream and Murphy's Multiverse both say no deal has been reported.
- Reinsve said in December 2025 that a Marvel superhero role didn't feel right for her.
- Murphy's Multiverse describes the film as an untitled 2028 release.

[Renate Reinsve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Reinsve), the Cannes-winning star of *The Worst Person in the World*, has reportedly met Marvel Studios about joining its X-Men reboot as Dr Moira MacTaggert. The reports appeared on 26 October 2026 and remain unconfirmed. Marvel hasn't commented, and no outlet has reported that Reinsve agreed to anything.

It would be an unusual match. Just ten months ago, according to Epicstream, she said she didn't want to be in a Marvel film.

## Who is reporting the Renate Reinsve meeting?

The claim comes from film-news scoopers, and the two outlets that covered it name different ones. [Epicstream](https://epicstream.com/article/x-men-reboot-reportedly-eyes-backrooms-star-renate-reinsve-for-moira-mactaggert) credits Daniel Richtman. [Murphy's Multiverse](https://www.murphysmultiverse.com/renate-reinsve-moira-mactaggert-mcu-x-men-reboot-casting/) credits Caleb Williams. Both say she met the studio to discuss the part, and neither says she has been offered it.

IMDb also ran a story on the same day, under a headline calling her an Oscar nominee who reportedly met Marvel. Its page couldn't be read, so this article doesn't rely on it.

Treat it as a rumour until Marvel or Reinsve says otherwise.

## What did Reinsve say about Marvel?

Epicstream points to an interview on Josh Horowitz's *Happy, Sad, Confused* podcast in December 2025. In it, the outlet says, Reinsve explained that playing a Marvel superhero wasn't right for her.

Epicstream notes that the comments are now several months old and that only time will show whether she has changed her mind. A meeting isn't a commitment, and studios often talk to many actors for a single role.

The reported casting isn't a superhero part in the usual sense, though. Moira is a human scientist for much of her history, which may matter to an actress who has built her career on character-driven drama.

## Who is Moira MacTaggert?

Moira MacTaggert is a geneticist and an ally of the X-Men. Epicstream describes her as a Nobel Prize-winning scientist who founded the Mutant Research Center and worked with Charles Xavier on the school that became the X-Mansion.

On screen, she has been played twice in Fox's X-Men films: Olivia Williams in *X-Men: The Last Stand* and Rose Byrne in *X-Men: First Class*. Murphy's Multiverse adds that Byrne also played her in *Apocalypse*.

The comics changed her story. In Jonathan Hickman's *House of X* and *Powers of X*, she turned out to be a mutant who is reincarnated and remembers her earlier lives. Murphy's Multiverse reports that director Jake Schreier has said Hickman's Krakoan run will influence the 2028 film.
