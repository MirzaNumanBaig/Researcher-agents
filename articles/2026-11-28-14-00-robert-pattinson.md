**Quick summary**

- Twilight director Catherine Hardwicke says Robert Pattinson looked so much like James Bond on set that he inspired the film's "Bond, James Bond" line.
- She made the comments on The Kyle Buchanan Show, according to Star Mackay and the Whitsundays.
- The line was ad-libbed by Justin Chon, and the sunglasses in the scene were Hardwicke's idea.
- It comes weeks after Amazon MGM Studios confirmed Pattinson as the next 007, with Denis Villeneuve directing.

The most quoted moment of the Bond casting fever may not be a coincidence after all. [Catherine Hardwicke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_Hardwicke), who directed the 2008 film Twilight, says [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) looked so much like [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) when he stepped out of a car during filming that it prompted the line fans have been sharing for two weeks, according to [Star Mackay and the Whitsundays](https://www.star1019.com.au/trending/entertainment/we-were-seeing-the-future-how-twilight-foreshadowed-pattinson-as-007/).

## The Twilight scene behind the Bond meme

The moment is brief. In a conversation about the school prom, Justin Chon's character, Eric Yorkie, says the night will have a Monte Carlo theme and promises "gambling, tuxedos, and Bond, James Bond." The film then cuts to Pattinson's Edward Cullen getting out of a car in dark sunglasses.

Speaking on The Kyle Buchanan Show, Hardwicke said the sunglasses were her call for that day of shooting. She told the host that Pattinson looked so suave that he inspired the line, and that Chon ad-libbed it. "We did think he looked like James Bond," she said. "So I guess we were seeing the future."

The clip has been circulating since the Bond announcement earlier this month. Fans have paired it with the franchise's theme music, and the director's account suggests the link was there from the start.

## Hardwicke on the casting

Amazon MGM Studios has confirmed Pattinson as the new 007, succeeding [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig). [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct, with [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) and [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman) producing, as reported by the same outlet.

Hardwicke said the news stunned her. "I almost fell out of my bed," she recalled, explaining that she had not expected the studio to pick him because of The Batman and his fame, given that producers often prefer an unknown. She added that the bed she was lying in is the one where Pattinson and [Kristen Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Stewart) did their chemistry read and filmed their first kiss for Twilight.

She described Pattinson as "intense and interesting", with "a lot of stuff going on in there", and called him "weird and original" and someone who "does not fit the mould".

## From vampire to spy

After Twilight made him one of the world's best known young actors, Pattinson spent years on smaller and more demanding projects before returning to big franchise work with The Batman, the same report notes.

He had also raised the idea of playing Bond long before the casting. In a 2012 interview, while the Twilight films were still being made, he said it would only work once he had "some baggage", and that a "fresh-faced Bond" would be a mistake.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did Robert Pattinson say the Bond line in Twilight?

No. According to Hardwicke, Justin Chon ad-libbed it as Eric Yorkie. Pattinson's character appears on screen right after it.

### Who is directing the next Bond film?

Denis Villeneuve, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing, per Star Mackay and the Whitsundays.

### Had Pattinson talked about playing Bond before?

Yes. In a 2012 interview he floated the idea, saying he would need "some baggage" first.
