**Quick summary**

- [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) plays Eddie Carbone in a new Off-Broadway revival of Arthur Miller's *A View from the Bridge*, and is also a producer.
- Performances at La MaMa's Ellen Stewart Theatre begin on November 27, with opening night on Sunday, December 13.
- The run is strictly limited to 13 weeks and is directed by Neil Pepe.
- Rehearsal photos released on November 18 show Rockwell with Carla Gugino and Alfred Molina.

Sam Rockwell is back on the New York stage this month. Rehearsal photos released on November 18 give a first look at his Off-Broadway revival of *A View from the Bridge*, which starts performances on November 27 at La MaMa, according to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/Photos-Sam-Rockwell-Carla-Gugino-and-Alfred-Molina-in-A-VIEW-FROM-THE-BRIDGE-Rehearsals-20261118).

## Who is in the cast

Rockwell takes the lead role of Eddie Carbone. [Carla Gugino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Gugino) plays his wife Beatrice, and [Alfred Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Molina) plays the lawyer Alfieri. [Fred Hechinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hechinger) is Rodolpho, Tom Pecinka is Marco and Riley Prior is Catherine.

The company also includes Erick Betancourt, [Lou Liberatore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lou_Liberatore), Benj Mirman, Jordan Lage, Matthew J. Harris, Taylor Dunbar, Allen Tedder and Carly Zien. Casting is by Taylor Williams, per [Playbill](https://playbill.com/article/photos-in-the-rehearsal-room-for-a-starry-off-broadway-a-view-from-the-bridge).

## A story that has not aged

[Arthur Miller's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Miller) tragedy follows Eddie, a Brooklyn longshoreman in the 1950s. His world starts to come apart when his wife's cousins, Marco and Rodolpho, arrive from Italy hoping for a new life in America. Catherine, the niece Eddie has raised as his own, falls for Rodolpho. Eddie's protectiveness turns to jealousy, and that leads him to an act of betrayal that splits his family and community, BroadwayWorld reports.

The play was first staged on Broadway in 1955. Playbill notes that this will be its first major Off-Broadway staging since a 1965 production starring Robert Duvall and Jon Voight.

## Rockwell as star and producer

Rockwell is not only acting. Playbill reports he is producing the engagement with Mark Berger and Play Hooky Productions. The two hope the show can act as a proof of concept for a new model, using commercial theatre to send meaningful resources back to nonprofit institutions such as La MaMa. It is not a full partnership with the theatre. Tickets will be offered to La MaMa members at special rates.

Playbill describes Rockwell as best known for his Oscar-winning role in *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*, alongside work in *The Green Mile*, *Charlie's Angels* and *Confessions of a Dangerous Mind*. His stage record is substantial too. He made his Broadway debut in *A Behanding in Spokane* in 2010, later appeared in revivals of *Fool for Love* and *American Buffalo*, and earned a Tony nomination for the latter in 2022.

Film fans can also catch up on his recent screen work: see our coverage of [Sam Rockwell and John Malkovich in *Wild Horse Nine*](https://moduscarry.com/sam-rockwell-wild-horse-nine-review/) and [the reviews that followed](https://moduscarry.com/wild-horse-nine-reviews/).

## The creative team

Neil Pepe directs. Scott Pask handles scenic design, Sarah Laux costumes, John Torres lighting and Palmer Hefferan sound. Baseline Theatrical is general manager, Jason Hindeland is production stage manager and Bethany Weinstein Stewart is production manager, according to Playbill.

## What happens next

Performances begin on November 27 at La MaMa's Ellen Stewart Theatre. The official opening is Sunday, December 13, and the run is limited to 13 weeks. Tickets are listed at ViewFromtheBridgePlay.com.
