**Quick summary**

- Valda Marshall, 102, wrote early scripts for Kylie Minogue Neighbours fans know as Charlene, and says she shaped the tomboy role for her.
- She told Woman's Day, via Now To Love, that Minogue was "the exact fit" the moment she saw her.
- Neighbours was cancelled by Seven after four months, then took off at Network Ten after 1986. Its last free-to-air episode aired in December 2025.
- Minogue has also been in the news for her tribute to Molly Meldrum, who died aged 83 and will receive a Victorian state funeral.

The writer behind the [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) breakout role of Charlene in Neighbours has spoken about the casting, 40 years on. Valda Marshall, now 102, told Woman's Day, in a story published by [Now To Love](https://www.nowtolove.com.au/news/real-life/neighbours-writer-valda-marshall/), that Minogue was "perfect" for the part from the first time she saw her.

## How Valda Marshall came to write Neighbours

Marshall says she always wanted to write. Her first job was as a journalist at News Limited in Adelaide, and she later worked overseas in Canada and New York. She also raised five children, writing at the kitchen table late at night after the household had gone to bed.

After years writing a TV column, she turned to scripts. In November 1984, [Grundy Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reg_Grundy_Organisation) approached her about a new five-nights-a-week show, and she left with the first 10 episodes to write. She says her brief was likeable characters in warm, recognisable Australian families, with "no Hollywood-type people".

She credits the team led by producer and screenwriter [Reg Watson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reg_Watson) as the happiest 10 years of her life.

## Why Charlene became a tomboy for Kylie Minogue Neighbours fans

Marshall says she wanted a tomboy with a very Australian name. She adapted an earlier character called Raeleen, played in the 1970s by [Victoria Nicolls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Nicolls), into Charlene.

"The minute I saw Kylie she was the exact fit," she told Woman's Day.

According to Now To Love, the show made global stars of Minogue and [Jason Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Donovan), who played Scott Robinson. Marshall says Donovan was not the first choice for the role, after a problem with the original casting. The wedding of Charlene and Scott, the outlet reports, became one of the most-watched television events of the 1980s.

## The rocky start, Bouncer and Harold's return

Neighbours was not an instant hit. Seven cancelled it after four months, but it began rating at home after Ten took it on in 1986, and then overseas.

Marshall also recalls the storylines viewers loved. A romance involving Bouncer the dog was, in her words, "the most ridiculous storyline" yet a favourite. She also remembers killing off [Harold Bishop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Bishop), only for the writers to realise he was the show's most popular character. Their fix was to have him walk out of the water as the cast mourned him.

She says she pitched a Neighbours movie at the time. Watson later told her not pursuing it was the biggest regret of his life.

## A week of looking back for Minogue

The story lands as Minogue is part of a wider wave of tributes. Music broadcaster [Molly Meldrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Meldrum), host of Countdown, died aged 83 in Richmond, Melbourne, his family said. According to [7NEWS](https://7news.com.au/entertainment/kylie-minogue-elton-john-and-king-charles-remember-molly-meldrum-as-melbourne-funeral-details-announced-c-23204513), Victorian Premier Ben Carroll offered a state funeral and the family accepted.

Minogue wrote that she was in the Countdown audience once and was on the show by her late teens, adding that Meldrum visited the Neighbours set. "Vale Molly. I'm going to turn the music up in your honour," she wrote. 7NEWS also reports tributes from Sir Elton John and from King Charles, through a palace aide, who recalled the King's 1977 Countdown interview.
