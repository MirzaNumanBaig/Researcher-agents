**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) paid tribute on Instagram on 29 October to [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart), who died on 28 October aged 86.
- Stewart died at his Los Angeles home after a brief illness with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia, according to his spokesperson.
- Jackman wrote that he would miss Stewart "all of my days" and called him a "tremendous friend and man".
- The pair worked together from the first X-Men film in 2000 to *The Sheep Detectives* this year.

Hugh Jackman has said he learned "so much" from Patrick Stewart, the co-star he shared the screen with for a quarter of a century. The Australian actor posted his tribute on Instagram on 29 October, a day after Stewart died in Los Angeles at 86.

"We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today," Jackman wrote, according to [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/entertainment/patrick-stewart-dead-co-star-statements-9.7402078). "And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man. I learned so much from him. I will miss you, Patrick Stewart, all of my days." The post paired a backstage photograph with an image of the two men embracing during *Logan*.

## What we know about Stewart's death

Stewart died on Wednesday 28 October at his home in Los Angeles. His spokesperson said he had been cared for by his wife, [Sunny Ozell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunny_Ozell), during a brief period of illness, according to [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/hugh-jackman-mourns-patrick-stewart-183050632.html), which cited Reuters. CBC reports the causes as Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia.

Stewart was best known as Captain Jean-Luc Picard, a role he began in *Star Trek: The Next Generation* in 1987, and as [Professor Charles Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X) in the X-Men films.

## Twenty-five years of Wolverine and Xavier

Jackman and Stewart first appeared together in *X-Men* in 2000. StyleCaster lists the films that followed: *X2*, *X-Men: The Last Stand*, *X-Men Origins: Wolverine*, *The Wolverine*, *Days of Future Past* and *Logan* in 2017. That is seven further films across 17 years after the first.

Their most recent credit together is *The Sheep Detectives*, released this year. Jackman plays a shepherd, George Hardy, while Stewart voices Sir Ritchfield, one of the sheep in his flock.

## The Logan premiere: "we were both crying"

*Logan* gave the pairing its most personal chapter. In James Mangold's film, an ageing Wolverine cares for a frail Xavier while protecting a young mutant, Laura, played by [Dafne Keen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dafne_Keen).

In a joint interview with ABC Australia's *Lateline* on 1 March 2017, Stewart said the two had been "preparing for this movie for 17 years". He recalled sitting beside Jackman at the Berlin premiere. Near the end, Jackman took his hand and raised his other hand to his face, and Stewart realised they were both crying.

Jackman has described filming a seven-and-a-half-minute scene with Stewart. When Mangold showed them the cut, the two actors embraced, and someone photographed the moment. Jackman also remembered Stewart urging the then 11-year-old Keen to promise she would work in theatre, because on stage a good scene can be played again night after night.

Stewart told Variety in 2020 that it was the best X-Men experience the pair had shared, according to Yahoo's account. The film also earned an Academy Award nomination for adapted screenplay.

## Other tributes

Stewart's death prompted a wave of messages from his colleagues. [William Shatner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shatner), who played Captain Kirk opposite Picard in *Star Trek Generations*, said that "Captain Picard stood for the best of us, and so did the man who played him."

[Seth MacFarlane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_MacFarlane), who worked with Stewart on *Family Guy*, *American Dad!* and *Ted*, recalled that Stewart once told him he always walked onto a new set feeling like the biggest novice there. Mangold called Stewart "the beating heart of our film", according to the Los Angeles Times as reported by Yahoo.
