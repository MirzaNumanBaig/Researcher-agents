**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) introduced [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) on stage at Chanel's Sydney after-party on Thursday, 5 November.
- The party followed Chanel's first official runway show on Australian soil, held in two sittings at 2.30pm and 6pm.
- The collection was Matthieu Blazy's first as Chanel's creative director, according to Nine.
- Kidman also attended with her 15-year-old daughter, in matching black and white looks, per People.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) kicked off her heels and walked onto a Sydney stage barefoot on Thursday night to help introduce [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) at Chanel's harbourside after-party, the closing act of the label's first official runway show in Australia. The Nicole Kidman Chanel party drew a crowd that included [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), and a set of Minogue hits that, by Nine's account, sent the room wild.

## How the Nicole Kidman Chanel party unfolded

The after-party was held at the Pier 2/3 Event Space in Walsh Bay, according to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/chanel-sydney-fashion-show-which-celebrities-attended-everything-to-know-20261105-p61bol.html). Few official details were released, so most of what the public has seen came from photos and videos shared on social media.

Once the party was in full swing, Kidman and Luhrmann, who made Moulin Rouge together 25 years ago, took the stage to announce the entertainment. Luhrmann joked that his head had been "spinning" since he learned who was performing. Kidman added that she could not get "her" out of her head, a nod to Minogue's hit, and Luhrmann followed with a line about hearts going "padam padam" before bringing out the singer.

Minogue then performed a medley that included On A Night Like This, Can't Get You Out of My Head, Padam Padam and Love At First Sight, backed by dancers dressed in Chanel, Nine reported. Robbie arrived later than most guests in a sheer black Chanel dress.

## The runway behind the party

The party followed two runway showings at The Cutaway, the Barangaroo venue on Sydney Harbour. Nine reported that the show was a replica of Chanel's Resort 2027 collection, first unveiled in Biarritz, France, and the first time the house has staged an official runway in Australia.

It was also the first collection from Blazy, described by Nine as an old friend of both Kidman and Minogue, and it nodded to the roots of Mademoiselle Chanel in Biarritz. First Nations models walked the catwalk, which Nine called a watershed moment for representation on an Australian runway.

The scale was large. Nine reported about 150 international guests, many of whom arrived on Monday for activities such as private Australian Ballet performances and surfing lessons, and three Sydney hotels were fully booked. The runway was one part of a week-long Chanel programme in the city, ahead of the reopening of the brand's refurbished Melbourne boutique on Flinders Lane next week.

## Who was in the front row

[Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/05/nicole-kidman-margot-robbie-lead-star-studded-guest-list-at-chanel-fashion-show-in-sydney/) listed Joel Edgerton and his partner Christine Centenera, Olivia DeJonge, K-pop singer Soobin and Minogue among the guests. Nine added DJ [Dom Dolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom_Dolla) and model Mia Hewitt, daughter of Lleyton and Bec Hewitt.

Kidman wore a black suit with tails and a white waistcoat, according to Nine, while Robbie, a Chanel ambassador, chose a sheer white dress with a feathered flower at the bust. Kidman's own link to the house is long. Nine noted that Luhrmann directed the 2004 No.5 film campaign starring her, and that Blazy named her a Chanel ambassador in 2025.

## A mother-daughter outing

Kidman brought her 15-year-old daughter, who wore an ivory or white skirt suit with a brown handbag, according to [Yahoo News](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/nicole-kidman-lookalike-daughter-faith-212253016.html) and [People](https://people.com/nicole-kidman-daughter-faith-attend-2027-chanel-cruise-show-12237889). The two outlets describe the outfits slightly differently in detail, but agree on the black-and-white theme. Kidman wore a black suit with a white top underneath.

The pair were also at Chanel's Spring/Summer 2027 show in Paris on 5 October, where both wore shades of grey and charcoal, People reported. When Chanel named her ambassador in October 2025, Kidman told WWD she admired the house's habit of "shining a smart and gracious light on women."
