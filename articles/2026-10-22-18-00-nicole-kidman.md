Nicole Kidman has drawn plenty of online attention for her outfit at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, where she chose a buttoned-up "office chic" look rather than anything resembling lingerie. Separately, her 18-year-old daughter, Sunday Rose, has been talking about how she balances modelling with her final year of school.

## An office look at a lingerie show

According to [The Handbook](https://www.thehandbook.com/nicole-kidman-office-chic/), the Australian actor arrived in a crisp white collared shirt, a striped pink tie and a fitted black pencil skirt by Jean Paul Gaultier. The outlet reports that a garter-inspired detail on the skirt offered a small nod to the show's lingerie theme, while the rest of the styling stayed polished and minimal.

The choice of label was a fitting one, The Handbook notes. Gaultier has long been known for putting a twist on classic menswear, and the outlet says Kidman was famously the house's first couture client.

The outlet also reports that the show paid a direct homage to her by playing a new song as part of its soundtrack. Reaction on social media was warm, with fans quoted by The Handbook calling her "such an icon" and suggesting she "should have walked the show".

## Sunday Rose on school, college and modelling

In an interview with Vogue Germany, reported by [HELLO! Magazine](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/930330/nicole-kidmans-daughter-reveals-future-plans-amid-model-status/), Sunday Rose said she wants to enjoy her senior year rather than rush ahead. "I definitely have more challenging classes this year, plus the college applications," she said, adding that she would like to "take it slow" and spend the final year with her friends.

She said school helps keep her grounded while she models. By her account, she initially wasn't sure how to cope with the contrast between the two worlds, but she has since found a way to enjoy travelling and working, then returning to the routine of school.

Sunday Rose also addressed questions about nepotism. "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have, and I'm truly grateful," she said, according to HELLO!. She said she always tries to give her best at work and to treat everyone with respect, however tired or jet-lagged she may be.

Asked whether she had ever deliberately kept her parents' identity quiet, she said she had not, adding that few people live in her area and most already knew when she started school.

## A busy season on the runway

HELLO! reports that September was a big month for Sunday Rose, who made her runway debut for both Max Mara and Marni in Milan and also walked for Coach in New York. The magazine says she first appeared on the runway at Miu Miu's Spring/Summer 2025 show during Paris Fashion Week in October 2024, has since become a muse for Jonathan Anderson at Dior, and most recently walked for Valentino.

## What's next

Neither report mentions new projects for Kidman herself. Sunday Rose's college plans remain open, with applications still ahead of her according to her own comments.
