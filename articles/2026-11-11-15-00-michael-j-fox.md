**Quick summary**

- [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) appears in the Season 3 premiere of Apple TV's *Shrinking*, according to SSBCrack.
- He plays a fellow Parkinson's patient who meets [Harrison Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Ford)'s character in a doctor's office.
- The role is Fox's first acting job since a break that began in 2020, SSBCrack reports.
- Fox told Vanity Fair that Ford's performance of the condition felt true to him.
- The series has been renewed for a fourth season.

Michael J. Fox has returned to acting with a guest role in the Season 3 premiere of Apple TV's *Shrinking*, sharing a scene with Harrison Ford, [SSBCrack reported](https://news.ssbcrack.com/michael-j-fox-makes-heartfelt-return-to-acting-in-apple-tvs-shrinking-alongside-harrison-ford/) on 11 November. It is the first time Fox has acted since 2020, and it came about because he asked for it.

## How the cameo came together

*Shrinking* follows a grieving therapist, played by [Jason Segel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Segel), who is raising a daughter while trying out unconventional methods with his patients. Ford plays his mentor, a fellow therapist who is living with Parkinson's disease.

Fox, who has lived publicly with Parkinson's for years, watched Ford's portrayal and was struck by it. According to SSBCrack, he contacted co-creator [Bill Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Lawrence_(TV_producer)), who had worked with him on [Spin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_City), and asked, in blunt terms, why he was not in the show. Those talks led to the guest spot.

## What happens on screen

In the premiere, Ford's character visits a doctor and meets Fox, who plays another patient with the same condition. SSBCrack describes the scene as a kind of mentorship that hints at a friendship growing through the season.

The humour is dry. When Ford says he has come in for a haircut, Fox answers with a line about falling every day. The report says the exchange is funny and affectionate at once, rather than solemn.

## On set and in his own words

Cast members felt the weight of the pairing. Michael Urie, a co-star, said the moment stirred nostalgia and emotion among the group, per the report.

Fox spoke to Vanity Fair about watching Ford work. "I recognized Parkinson's in his eyes," he said, as quoted by SSBCrack. He called Ford an underrated actor who is at his best with subtlety. Ford, for his part, described Fox as an extraordinarily powerful person.

## What happens next

*Shrinking* is streaming on Apple TV, and the show has already been renewed for a fourth season, SSBCrack says. The report suggests Fox's character and Ford's will keep developing their friendship through Season 3, but it does not say how many episodes Fox appears in. We will update this story if Apple or the producers confirm more.
