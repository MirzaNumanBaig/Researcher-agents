**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) missed Icons Week on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) after an injury, reported as a rib injury.
- The BBC confirmed on Monday 19 October that she had been given the all clear to dance again.
- She and partner Kai Widdrington will dance a Rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back To Me Now" on Saturday 24 October.
- Goodrem says her physio is continuing and she is taking the week "slow and steady".

Delta Goodrem will be back on the Strictly Come Dancing floor this Saturday, 24 October, after the BBC confirmed she had been cleared to dance following a week out with a rib injury. The Australian singer and her professional partner, [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington), will perform a Rumba on BBC One at 6.30pm.

## How Delta Goodrem's week out unfolded

Goodrem was forced to miss week four, the new Icons Week special, because of the injury. According to the [Manchester Evening News](https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/tv/delta-goodrem-gives-candid-glimpse-34799793.amp), doctors told her to rest, and she was expected to pay tribute to fellow Australian [Olivia Newton-John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Newton-John) that night. Instead she watched from home, filming herself in tears as the hosts sent her a shoutout.

She later thanked fans on Instagram, as [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/strictlys-delta-goodrem-set-suffer-34791729) reported, calling it "not the start to the week I'd hoped for". [Dannii Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dannii_Minogue) wrote that she was "absolutely gutted", and fellow contestant [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) told her to rest up.

Whether she would return was not certain. Widdrington told [Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/tv/entertainment/delta-goodrem-strictly-come-dancing-2026-contestant-age-instagram-explained/) at the Pride of Britain Awards that it was "touch and go for a second" while they waited to hear from the medical team.

## What did the BBC say about her return?

On Monday 19 October, the show's social media channels carried a statement. "Following her bye last week due to injury, we're delighted to confirm Delta has been given the all clear to dance again this weekend," it read.

Widdrington described her attitude as that of a fighter. "She wants to be on the dancefloor, and she's enjoyed it so much," he told Radio Times. At the Pride of Britain red carpet he said her recovery was coming in "baby steps", but that she was a pro.

The all clear does not mean the recovery is finished. On Wednesday 21 October, Goodrem posted a photo of herself on a physio bed, writing that she was "well looked after and focusing on healing every day", according to the Manchester Evening News. She added that the week was "slow and steady".

## A Rumba to Celine Dion

The routine itself is a deliberate choice for a body still healing. Speaking on the BBC's It Takes Two, as [Digital Spy](https://www.digitalspy.com/tv/reality-tv/a74209314/strictly-come-dancing-delta-goodrem-tears-comeback/) reported, Widdrington called the Rumba the slowest of the Latin dances, but one of the most difficult, especially for the female partner.

Goodrem named the song as one of her favourites of all time. "We've just gotta take this week gently but it's the ballroom, we've gotta go out there and deliver," she said. She also admitted she had been "going in waves of tears all week" over the messages of support.

Her mum, watching from Australia, has opinions too. "She wasn't thrilled with my arms in the American Smooth," Goodrem said, so strong arms and a power walk are on this week's to-do list. Radio Times lists that American Smooth, from week two, as scoring 30 points.

## Who is Delta Goodrem?

Goodrem signed her first record deal at 15, according to Radio Times. Her 2003 debut album, Innocent Eyes, went 23 times Platinum and is one of the highest-selling albums in Australian history. The paper says she has sold more than nine million albums worldwide, has five number one albums, nine number one singles and 12 ARIA Awards.

She joined Neighbours in 2002 as Nina Tucker, and returned to the role in 2015 and 2022. Most recently she represented Australia at Eurovision 2026, finishing fourth with 287 points. She has also toured or performed with artists including [Shania Twain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shania_Twain), [Tony Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Bennett) and [Ricky Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Martin).

Away from the ballroom, the Manchester Evening News says she is finishing her next album and is due to appear on The Voice Germany this Friday, in a segment filmed in advance in Berlin.
