**Quick summary**

- The Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research raised a record US$6 million at its gala on 14 November, according to Town & Country.
- More than 950 guests filled the Waldorf Astoria New York for the evening, hosted by Denis Leary.
- Tracy Pollan told People the money goes "directly into the scientists' pockets", as reported by HELLO!.
- Pollan also said she and Michael J. Fox have very different tastes in books and television but share a love of good meals.

The [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) Foundation has set a fundraising record. Its annual New York gala brought in US$6 million for Parkinson's research on 14 November, [Town & Country reports](https://www.townandcountrymag.com/the-scene/parties/a74456623/michael-j-fox-foundation-gala-2026/), the biggest total for the event so far.

## A record night at the Waldorf Astoria

More than 950 guests attended the evening, called "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson's", at the Waldorf Astoria New York. Comedian [Denis Leary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Leary) hosted and opened the night by playing with his band, the Enablers.

The guest list leaned toward comedy and music. Town & Country names [Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Colbert), [David Letterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Letterman) and [Willie Geist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willie_Geist) among the performers and speakers, alongside comedians Josh Johnson and Kylie Vincent. Musical sets came from John Mayer, [Brad Paisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Paisley), [Tom Dumont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Dumont) and [Shoshana Bean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoshana_Bean).

Fox and his wife, [Tracy Pollan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Pollan), spoke during the evening. The magazine's photo gallery also shows guests including [Terry Crews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Crews), though it does not detail what each attendee did on the night.

For more on the musical side of this year's event, see our earlier post on [Michael J. Fox and John Mayer playing Johnny B. Goode again](https://moduscarry.com/michael-j-fox-johnny-b-goode-john-mayer/).

## Where the money goes

Pollan spoke to People at the gala, and [HELLO! carried her comments](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/935661/michael-j-fox-tracy-pollan-very-different-tastes-40-year-marriage/). She said the evening celebrates everyone who has worked with the foundation for more than 20 years.

She was direct about where the funds end up. "It's really unbelievable and unprecedented how much money goes directly to scientists," she said, adding that the scientists can then do the work that is needed. She described the discoveries made with that funding as exciting and said they give the family "so much hope and optimism".

She also thanked the supporters who have stood with the family for 25 years, calling the gala a chance to celebrate with them and show gratitude.

## What keeps the marriage going

The same interview turned personal. Fox is 65 and Pollan is 66, HELLO! notes. They met on the set of Family Ties and married in 1988, which makes 38 years of marriage and about four decades together.

Pollan said the pair differ on a lot. "We have very different tastes in books and very different tastes in what we watch," she said, "but we both always love a great meal." She named shared meals as the thing that makes them happiest.

On balancing public life, Fox's Parkinson's and family, she said they take it "day by day".

## The family behind the foundation

The couple have four children: Sam, Aquinnah, Schuyler and Esmé. In an earlier interview with the same outlet, Pollan said Fox was diagnosed before the girls were born and very early in Sam's life, so the condition is "kind of all they know".

She was candid that it has not been easy. In her words, it is very difficult for the family, the caregivers and the patient, and it is important to acknowledge that it is not all easy. Her children, she said, "show up and they're there for us", and she leans on them a lot.

## What happens next

Neither report says how the US$6 million will be allocated. Pollan's comments point to research funding, but no specific projects were named in the coverage we read. The foundation's own website, [michaeljfox.org](https://www.michaeljfox.org), is the place to look for updates on the studies it supports.
