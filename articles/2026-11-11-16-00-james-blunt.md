**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt)'s eighth studio album, Airplane Mode, is heading for a Number 2 debut on the UK Official Albums Chart, according to the Official Charts midweek update.
- Royal Blood's Dead Company holds the early lead for Number 1.
- A Top 10 finish would be his ninth in a row, an unbroken run that began with 2004's Back to Bedlam.
- On Good Morning Britain he said his wife signed him up for [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) after his manager turned the offer down.

James Blunt is on course for another high chart finish. His new album, Airplane Mode, is flying towards a Number 2 debut in the midweek Official Albums Chart, and the singer has spent the week explaining, with typical self-deprecation, how he ended up on one of British television's biggest shows.

## Where Airplane Mode is sitting on the chart

[Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/royal-blood-dead-company-james-blunt-airplane-mode-albums-chart/) reports that Royal Blood's fifth studio album, Dead Company, has taken an early lead in the race for Number 1. The Brighton duo have topped the chart with all four of their previous studio records, so they are chasing a perfect run.

Blunt sits just behind them. Midweek positions can shift before the final chart, and the Official Charts piece describes Number 2 as a prospective placing rather than a result.

The context matters. If Airplane Mode lands in the Top 10, it will be his ninth consecutive Top 10 album, a streak that stretches back to Back to Bedlam in 2004. He has reached Number 1 twice: with that debut and with its 2007 follow-up, All the Lost Souls.

The week is crowded. Official Charts lists 19 new entries and returns in the midweek chart, including Steven Wilson at Number 3, Kings of Leon at Number 4 and the late George Michael's live set The Faith Tour at Number 5.

## A trending single and a Graham Norton slot

Streaming and sales of individual tracks are moving too. In the [Official Trending Chart](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/trending-songs-uk-10-november-2026/) for 10 November, Blunt's track Circles lands at Number 16. That chart ranks songs showing week-on-week growth, using the first four sales days of the chart week. The same list is led by [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo)'s Christmas song, and features [Noah Kahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Kahan) at Numbers 3 and 4.

Blunt is also booked as the musical guest on The Graham Norton Show. [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Photos-Dame-Judi-Dench-Alan-Cumming-Nicola-Coughlan-Tramell-Tillman-and-James-Blunt-Set-for-THE-GRAHAM-NORTON-SHOW-20261109) says the episode, which also features [Dame Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench), [Alan Cumming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Cumming), [Nicola Coughlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Coughlan) and [Tramell Tillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramell_Tillman), debuts on BBC America on Thursday 12 November. That date is the US listing, so UK and Australian airing times may differ.

## The Celebrity Traitors story

The talking point away from the charts is how Blunt got into the castle. Speaking on Good Morning Britain, he said his manager rejected the approach from the programme's team without bringing it to him. [The Tab](https://thetab.com/2026/11/10/james-blunt-reveals-the-hilarious-way-he-was-roped-into-appearing-on-celebrity-traitors) quotes him saying his wife then signed him up, telling him one day that Claudia Winkleman had been in touch.

Co-host Susanna Reid asked whether he had watched the show. He had not, he admitted, and said it showed in his play. He described his approach as "blundering through and apologising profusely", and called the game a survival and guessing exercise rather than a detective one.

He also said he told no one he was taking part. Watching the final episode with his wife, he recalled, she told him she had thought he would win, and was disappointed. The Tab notes he got surprisingly far in the competition, and that he had already written and recorded the album before the Traitors offer arrived.

The same piece says he is promoting Airplane Mode with airport-themed stunts and humour aimed at himself, which suits the title.

## What happens next

The final albums chart will confirm where Airplane Mode lands. Whether Royal Blood hold off Blunt for Number 1 will not be clear until the full week's sales are counted. Headlines this week also point to a 2027 UK and Ireland tour linked to the album, which is tracked on the [Airplane Mode Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airplane_Mode_Tour) page.
