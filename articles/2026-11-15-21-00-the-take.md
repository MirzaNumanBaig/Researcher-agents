**Quick summary**

- The Take is a Netflix heist film starring [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington) and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson).
- It is directed by [Fernando Meirelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernando_Meirelles) and written by [Simon Kinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Kinberg).
- It arrives on Netflix on 15 January 2027, with a limited cinema run, according to Yahoo.
- The film was previously known as Here Comes the Flood.

The first pairing of Denzel Washington and Robert Pattinson is close. The Take, a Netflix heist thriller that brings the two actors together for the first time, arrives on 15 January 2027, according to a [Yahoo Entertainment preview](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/denzel-washington-robert-pattinson-know-193356556.html) published on 14 November.

## What is The Take about?

Netflix's official logline, as quoted by Yahoo, describes the film as an unconventional heist movie. The story centres on a bank guard, a teller and a "master thief" who end up in a deadly game of cons and double crosses.

Washington plays the master thief, Christian Carter. Pattinson plays Noah, who is recruited by Carter to help carry out a high-stakes robbery. Noah's girlfriend, Rose, works as a teller at a prominent Manhattan bank. She is played by [Daisy Edgar-Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Edgar-Jones).

The film was originally titled Here Comes the Flood before it became The Take.

## Who is making it?

Fernando Meirelles directs. He is the Brazilian filmmaker behind City of God and The Constant Gardener. Simon Kinberg, who wrote Mr. & Mrs. Smith, is the screenwriter.

The supporting cast includes [Danai Gurira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danai_Gurira), [Sean Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Harris), Moisés Arias and [Justin Kirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Kirk). Wikipedia and Wikidata list the release date as 15 January 2027.

## Why Washington signed on

Washington has been selective about his next projects. He told [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) that he is focused on doing the best work with the best filmmakers, and that what he does from here and who he does it with matters to him both spiritually and artistically, as quoted by Yahoo.

He has not appeared on the big screen in 2026. His most recent film listed in the Yahoo preview is Spike Lee's Highest 2 Lowest, which also starred ASAP Rocky.

## A busy year for Pattinson

Pattinson, 40, has had a heavy 2026. Yahoo counts The Drama, The Odyssey and Primetime among his releases this year, all of which drew widespread praise. Dune: Part Three is still to come in December. Yahoo also notes another Batman film on his horizon.

At one point it was widely assumed The Take might land before the end of 2026, which would have given him five films in a single year. The January date moves it into 2027 instead. Yahoo describes Pattinson as a likely nominee for next year's Oscars.

The Take reaches select cinemas and then Netflix on 15 January, according to Yahoo. No budget, box office or review scores exist yet, because the film has not been released. A trailer and any festival or premiere details have not been confirmed in the sources reviewed.
