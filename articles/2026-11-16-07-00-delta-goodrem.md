**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) released her new single Telescope on Friday, 13 November 2026.
- Her album PURE is due on 5 February 2027, her first studio album in five years.
- The PURE World Tour starts in Glasgow in February and finishes with eight Australian shows, including Adelaide on 19 April 2027.
- On Saturday's live show of [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing), Goodrem danced without her partner Kai Widdrington, who was unwell.

Delta Goodrem has released Telescope, the newest single from her upcoming album PURE, and fans now have a clear run of dates to look forward to in 2027. The song came out on Friday, 13 November, according to [Glam Adelaide](https://glamadelaide.com.au/delta-goodrem-launches-new-music-ahead-of-huge-2027-world-tour/), and it lands in the middle of a busy week for the singer.

## What Telescope is about

Telescope is a song about having someone in your corner when life feels uncertain. Goodrem explained the idea in her own words: "I loved the idea that a telescope doesn't change what's out there, it helps you see it."

She added that someone who knows you well can spot possibilities you've lost sight of. The aim, she said, was to hold that feeling while keeping "the lift and energy of looking forward."

The single follows Eclipse, Hologram and Warrior, and comes after a year in which Goodrem represented Australia at the 2026 Eurovision Song Contest, Glam Adelaide reports. The outlet also notes she has sold more than nine million records.

## PURE and the world tour

PURE is scheduled for 5 February 2027. It will be her first studio album in five years.

The PURE World Tour opens in Glasgow in February and moves through the UK and Europe, with stops in London, Paris, Berlin, Amsterdam and Vienna. She then returns home for eight Australian shows. The Adelaide date is Monday, 19 April 2027, at 7pm at the Adelaide Entertainment Centre Arena in Hindmarsh, and it falls between the Melbourne and Perth concerts.

Tickets for Adelaide are on sale now. VIP packages, including meet-and-greet and soundcheck options, are also available. The show is billed as a mix of new songs and hits from more than two decades.

## Strictly: a week without Kai

Goodrem is also in the middle of the UK's Strictly Come Dancing. The Daily Mail reported that her professional partner, [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington), could not take part in Saturday's live show because of illness, and that fellow professional Aljaz Skorjanec stepped in for the week.

It was announced on Friday that Widdrington was not well enough to perform. On the show, Goodrem said it had been "an extremely crazy week" and that he had become so unwell the day before that he had to go to hospital, according to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16294311/Strictly-Kai-Widdrington-tears-Delta-Goodrem-quit-hospitalised.html).

Widdrington, 31, watched from home and filmed himself getting emotional during the routine. He also shared clips of the performance and said how proud he was, and Goodrem replied that he was with them "in spirit". The Daily Mail says there are fears he may have to leave the competition, but no outlet in our sources has confirmed that.
