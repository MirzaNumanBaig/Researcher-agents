**Quick summary**

- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) is reportedly one of a small group of actors invited to screen-test for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to a report published by AOL on 13 November 2026.
- The same report says [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) is not among those auditioning.
- The tests were reportedly scheduled to begin on 23 October and to cover stunt work, camera presence and dialogue.
- Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed who has been tested or who has the role.

Callum Turner has reportedly moved into the next stage of the search for the new James Bond, with a report published on 13 November saying the 36-year-old has been invited to screen-test for the role. Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed it, and the identity of the next 007 remains officially unannounced.

## What the report says about Turner

According to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/callum-turner-reportedly-screen-testing-224721000.html), which relayed reporting first attributed to TheWrap, Turner is among a small group of actors reportedly offered auditions. The Masters of the Air star is named alongside [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor, both also 36.

AOL says the screen tests were scheduled to start on 23 October. Producers were reportedly weighing stunt work, how each actor looks on camera and how they deliver dialogue before making a final choice. The article notes that Turner, whose credits include [Fantastic Beasts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantastic_Beasts) and The Boys in the Boat, has been linked to the franchise for months.

None of this is a casting announcement. The studio has not said who was invited, who attended or when a decision will arrive.

## Where Jacob Elordi fits in

The more surprising detail is who is reportedly missing. AOL says the Australian actor Elordi is not taking part in the latest auditions, after months in which he was one of the most discussed names.

It points back to an earlier OK! report claiming Amazon executives had narrowed their search to three leading contenders, with Elordi and Lowden drawing the most attention. The studio never confirmed a shortlist. AOL says it is unclear whether Elordi passed on the chance or simply did not advance.

For Australian readers, that makes the Elordi question the one to watch. Until Amazon speaks, his absence from the tests is a reported detail, not a fact about his casting.

## A crowded field

Turner is far from the only name in play. AOL lists [Aaron Taylor-Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Taylor-Johnson), [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill) and [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James) among actors who have been mentioned over the years.

The Daily Mail reported the same day that Puck's Matthew Belloni named Nicholas Hoult, Lowden, O'Connor and Turner as in the running, and called [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), 40, the frontrunner. Belloni's claims about contracts and money are his own reporting, and Turner's terms were not described in the piece. For more on that angle, see our earlier story, [Callum Turner Bond Audition Questioned After Pattinson Deal Report](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-bond-audition/).

The sources do not agree on who is ahead. One has Turner moving forward, the other puts Pattinson in front. Both are reports from named outlets, not statements from Amazon.

## The franchise context

Daniel Craig left the role after 2021's No Time to Die, and the hunt for a successor has run since. The next film will be the 26th to feature Ian Fleming's spy and is being directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), the Dune director. The filmmakers have so far stayed quiet about casting.

AOL also quotes former Bond casting director Debbie McWilliams, who said the character needs credibility as a man who could "pick a gun up and shoot you", not only charm and good looks.

## What happens next

Nothing is official until Amazon MGM announces it. Casting reports around Bond have shifted often, so each new name should be read as reported, not settled.
