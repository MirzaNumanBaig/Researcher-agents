**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) and his *The Invite* co-stars will receive the Vanguard Award at the 2027 Palm Springs International Film Awards.
- The honour goes to director and star [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde), Rogen, Penélope Cruz and [Edward Norton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norton).
- The award will be presented on 7 January at the Palm Springs Convention Center, as the festival opens.
- The film picked up four Gotham Award nominations this week, according to Gold Derby via Yahoo.

The cast of *The Invite* has booked the first big awards-season date of 2027. Seth Rogen, Olivia Wilde, Penélope Cruz and Edward Norton will be given the Vanguard Award at the Palm Springs International Film Awards, [Gold Derby reported on Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/invite-stars-olivia-wilde-seth-170000938.html) on 28 October (US time).

## What the Vanguard Award is for

The ceremony takes place on 7 January at the Palm Springs Convention Center. The festival then runs from 7 to 18 January, so the event opens the programme.

Festival Chairman Nachhattar Singh Chandi described the film as both comedic and deeply human. He said each of the four actors brings "something irreplaceable" to the story and that the film has stayed with audiences long after the credits roll, according to the Gold Derby report.

The Vanguard Award has a decent record as an awards-season marker. Past recipients named in the report include Oscar Best Picture winners *Green Book* and *The Shape of Water*. Best Picture nominees on the list include *Belfast*, *Emilia Pérez*, *Killers of the Flower Moon*, [*La La Land*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_La_Land), [*Little Miss Sunshine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Miss_Sunshine), *The Fabelmans*, *The Trial of the Chicago 7* and, most recently, *Hamnet*. Several of those titles went on to win or contend for Best Picture.

## The film behind the honour

*The Invite* centres on a dinner party between two neighbouring couples. The evening, the report says, spirals into desire and unexpected connection. Wilde directs and stars alongside Rogen, Cruz and Norton. The screenplay is adapted by [Will McCormack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_McCormack) and [Rashida Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rashida_Jones).

The same week, the film earned four [Gotham Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotham_Awards) nominations. They cover Best Feature, Best Adapted Screenplay, Outstanding Supporting Performance for Cruz, and the Gotham Audience Award. The report names Cruz as the only cast member singled out in those categories.
