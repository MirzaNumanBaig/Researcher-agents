**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, posted a short autumn video on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/pamelaanderson/) showing her Vancouver Island home, according to Geo News.
- She called the season the "season of the soul" and filmed her dogs, her garden and a lake dock.
- She also showed wild ground cherries and her Wild Groundcherry Basic Balm, made with the skincare brand Sonsie.
- She moved back to the family estate in 2020.

Pamela Anderson has shared a quiet look at autumn at her six-acre Vancouver Island home. The Baywatch star, 59, posted a short Instagram clip of a rainy morning on the property, which she described as the "season of the soul", [Geo News reported](https://www.geo.tv/latest/689972-pamela-anderson-shares-autumn-glimpse-of-vancouver-island-home) on 6 November.

## What the video shows

The clip opens at sunrise with Anderson walking one of her dogs through her sunroom. She then tours her backyard garden in the rain.

Later she sits down with a book, *Amor and Psyche: The Psychic Development of the Feminine*. "I've read this book so many times," she says in the video, according to Geo News. She also films the garden again once the rain has stopped.

The video closes with a selfie beside a large pine tree and a walk along a lake dock. Bright pink and light orange roses are in bloom in the footage.

## Wild ground cherries and a skincare product

In her Instagram Stories, Anderson showed wild ground cherries she had picked. She paired them with her Wild Groundcherry Basic Balm, a product she developed with the skincare brand Sonsie.

Geo News notes that Anderson spoke to People in October about how gardening keeps her grounded. We could not open the People piece, so that detail rests on Geo's account.

## A family property she came back to

Anderson returned to the family's longtime estate on Vancouver Island in 2020. She told Better Homes & Gardens in September 2024 that the land was once a motel owned by her grandmother, and that she was taken there after she was born, according to Geo News.

When she came back, she has said, the garden had decayed, and she lived in a small beach house on the property. She has described expanding the garden as a way of putting her life back together.
