**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) is one of five actors named on a fan-style "Bond Girl" wishlist published by Hindustan Times after Robert Pattinson was announced as the next 007.
- The list is the outlet's own opinion. No casting for the female lead has been announced.
- Navarrette wore a champagne-gold Dior gown to the Governors Awards on Sunday, 15 November, and online reaction was mixed, according to Fashion Times UK.
- She also spoke about the hardest scene in her horror hit Obsession and is due to play Rogue in Marvel's next X-Men film.

Inde Navarrette has found herself in a casting conversation she has not started. After Amazon MGM Studios announced [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/hollywood/zendaya-margaret-qualley-or-inde-navrette-opposite-robert-pattinsons-james-bond-the-ultimate-bond-girl-wishlist-101794843774083.html) published a wishlist of actors who could play the [Bond Girl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bond_girl), and her name is on it.

## Where the Bond Girl talk comes from

The newspaper's list is a columnist's pick, not a report of talks. It names Zendaya, Navarrette, [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy) and [Anna Sawai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Sawai) as contenders worth discussing. Nothing in it suggests any of them has been approached.

On Navarrette, the writer says she has become a fan favourite since her debut in the horror film Obsession, and argues a short role as Rogue in a crowded Marvel film will not be enough to hold the attention she has gained. The columnist is not sure she suits the classic heartbreaker role of an [Eva Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Green) type. They see her more as an [Ana de Armas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Armas) sidekick, someone who joins Bond in the action and brings some lightness to the film.

For context, the report says the franchise began in 1962 with Dr. No and has earned more than US$7 billion at the global box office across more than two dozen films. The next film will be the first made under Amazon MGM Studios, which took creative control in 2025 through a joint venture with the longtime rights holders. Denis Villeneuve is directing from a screenplay by Steven Knight, according to the same report.

## The Governors Awards gown

Navarrette's red-carpet look has been a separate talking point. According to [Fashion Times UK](https://www.fashiontimes.co.uk/inde-navarrette-dior-gown-governors-awards-criticism-1767838), she attended the 17th Governors Awards at the Ray Dolby Ballroom in Hollywood on Sunday, 15 November, in a floor-length champagne-gold Christian Dior gown. It had a sleeveless cut, a draped neckline and cut-outs at the waist, and she paired it with Tiffany & Co. jewellery and her long dark hair worn down.

Reaction online was divided. Some commenters said the dress looked too big or disliked the colour and fabric, and one compared it to curtains. Others praised the fabric and said it suited her. A recurring complaint was about styling, with one user writing that the team keeps dressing her as though she were older. The outlet notes the same debate followed her Schiaparelli couture gown at the Academy Museum Gala in October. These are social media opinions, not a verdict from fashion critics.

## What she says about Obsession

Away from the carpet, Navarrette has been talking about the craft behind Obsession. In a video interview on 13 November, reported by [Creepy Catalog](https://creepycatalog.com/inde-navarrette-says-playing-the-real-nikki-in-obsessions-trivia-bar-scene-was-harder-than-she-expected/), she said the hardest scene was the trivia bar sequence, where she plays the ordinary Nikki who exists before the wish. She had spent so long as "wish Nikki" that returning to the original character was harder than expected. The scene was shot last, as a reshoot of the film's opening.

In Curry Barker's film, a music-store worker named Bear wishes that his coworker Nikki will love him, which strips her of her agency. Navarrette has said she has only about 15 minutes to show who the real Nikki is, and Barker has said she added qualities he had not originally written.

No Bond casting beyond Pattinson has been confirmed, so the wishlist remains speculation. Navarrette's confirmed next step is playing Rogue in Marvel's upcoming X-Men film, and awards season is only beginning, which means more public appearances and more fashion debate are likely.
