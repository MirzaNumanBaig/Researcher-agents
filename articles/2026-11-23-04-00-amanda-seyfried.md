**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) is the new House ambassador for Tiffany & Co., according to trade title B&T.
- She stars in "You & Me", a two-minute holiday film directed by Academy Award nominee Derek Cianfrance.
- She hosts *Saturday Night Live* for the first time on Saturday 5 December, with Don Toliver as musical guest.
- Her Netflix animated film *Steps* has been streaming since 20 November.

Amanda Seyfried has a busy end to 2026. The actress is now the face of Tiffany & Co. as its newest House ambassador, and in a few weeks she steps onto the *Saturday Night Live* stage for the first time as host.

## Amanda Seyfried's Tiffany & Co. role

The luxury house unveiled Seyfried as ambassador with its global "Love is a Gift" holiday campaign, reports [B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/tiffany-co-rewinds-to-90s-new-york-with-amanda-seyfried-for-global-christmas-campaign/). The agency American Haiku created it. Tiffany's chief marketing officer, Andrea Davey, described Seyfried as the brand's "new House ambassador", per the same report.

The centrepiece is "You & Me", a two-minute film directed by Cianfrance. It will run in cinemas, on paid and organic social media, on digital out-of-home screens, on the Tiffany website and through the brand's own channels, B&T says.

## What happens in the film

The film moves between present-day New York and the 1990s. It follows a mother and daughter through one December day that takes in a ballet rehearsal and a walk past Tiffany's holiday windows. Seyfried plays a New York choreographer, and B&T notes that the character has a craft and an identity of her own rather than serving only as a source of festive warmth.

The emotional turn comes when the daughter sees her mother as a person in her own right for the first time. A custom ornament inspired by Jean Schlumberger's Bird on a Rock, a design made for Tiffany in 1965, acts as the keepsake that returns each year.

Two creative choices stand out. The music is a reworking of The Wannadies' 1994 track "You and Me Song", turned from a romantic number into one about a mother and daughter. Production designer Karen Murphy also dressed an Upper East Side home with genuine Tiffany archive pieces, according to B&T.

## Seyfried's first night hosting SNL

NBC announced the booking live during John Mulaney's 14 November episode, [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/tv-shows/snl-taps-amanda-seyfried-to-host-next-season-52-episode/) reports. Seyfried will be the fourth first-time host of Season 52, after Jalen Brunson, Inde Navarrette and Druski. Rapper and singer Don Toliver is the musical guest and will also perform on the show for the first time.

[TVLine](https://tvline.com/2317069/saturday-night-live-amanda-seyfried-host-snl-december-5-2026) says she is the first host announced for December. Episodes on 12 and 19 December are expected, though hosts for those have not been named. TVLine also notes that she is an Emmy winner whose television credits include *Big Love* and *The Dropout*.

## A crowded few weeks

*Steps*, Netflix's animated take on Cinderella, began streaming on 20 November, according to TVLine. For more on that project, see our coverage of her [song in the film](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-steps-netflix-soundtrack/) and our earlier piece on her [SNL booking and Oscar buzz](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-snl-host-oscar/).

She also appears in *You Can See Everything*, the Elizabeth Holmes documentary directed by Lance Oppenheim and [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder). TheWrap says she plays Holmes in dramatised reenactments of scenes the filmmakers could not capture. TVLine adds that she returns to Peacock's *Ted: The Animated Series*, due on 17 December.
