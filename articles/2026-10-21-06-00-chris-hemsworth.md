**Quick summary**

- Sydney's Archie Rose Distilling Co. begins selling three whiskies in the United States on Friday 23 October, its first US release.
- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) is a co-owner, after joining founder Will Edwards in an August announcement, according to The Whiskey Wash.
- Hemsworth wrote, directed and produced the launch campaign film, which premiered on 20 October.
- The range is priced from $39.99 to $59.99 and uses Australian-grown grains.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://www.instagram.com/chrishemsworth/) is taking his Australian whisky business abroad. Archie Rose, the Sydney distillery he co-owns, will put its first bottles on United States shelves from Friday 23 October, [The Whiskey Wash](https://thewhiskeywash.com/whiskey-news/archie-rose-australian-whiskies-hit-us-with-chris-hemsworth/) reports.

The launch comes only a few months after the actor's involvement became public, and it is the first time the brand has sold outside Australia, according to that report.

## What is launching in the US

Three core whiskies make up the American debut: a Single Malt Australian Whisky, a Straight Rye Malt Whisky and a reworked Australian Original Whisky. Every one is made at the Sydney distillery from Australian-grown grain.

The Whiskey Wash lists the line-up this way:

- **Straight Rye Malt Whisky, $49.99:** made from Australian-grown rye, bottled at 46% ABV and matured in charred virgin American oak. The outlet says it has twice been named World's Best Rye Whisky.
- **Single Malt Australian Whisky, $59.99:** made with six Australian barley malts, also at 46% ABV. It has been named Australia's Best Single Malt Whisky five times, the report says.
- **Australian Original Whisky, $39.99:** a blend of the rye malt and single malt expressions, bottled at 40% ABV.

The distillery ferments, distills and matures each malt separately before combining them. Casks include Australian fortified wine (apera) barrels alongside European and American oak. The company cites 12 years of distilling and more than 50,000 casks under bond.

## Hemsworth's role in the rebrand

According to The Whiskey Wash, Hemsworth has had a leading hand in reshaping the brand's look. That includes a new flask-shaped bottle in textured glass, which is meant to evoke the land where the grains are grown. The glass is lighter and the packaging more streamlined, which the report says cuts weight and improves freight efficiency. Archie Rose is a certified B Corp, and the outlet ties the change to those sustainability commitments.

Hemsworth also made the marketing. A global campaign film, written, directed and produced by him, premiered on 20 October on his YouTube channel and the Archie Rose website. His brother, [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth), appears in it.

"Archie Rose embodies the very best of Australia," Hemsworth said in the launch statement quoted by the outlet, adding that he has been a fan of the whisky for years.

## What the distillery says

Founder Will Edwards framed the US release as a new chapter built on the same idea: making whisky in Australia from Australian ingredients. "We've spent the last twelve years refining our whiskies," he said, adding that the range and branding now reflect that heritage.

Master distiller Dave Withers said great whisky starts in the field, and that working with Hemsworth lets the company take its craft to a global audience.

Two other outlets, The Australian and Parade, also covered the launch. Their pages could not be read for this article, so nothing here relies on them.
