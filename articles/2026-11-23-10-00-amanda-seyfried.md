**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried)'s 2011 sci-fi thriller *In Time* joins the Paramount Plus catalogue on 1 December, according to MovieWeb.
- Her 2011 fantasy horror *Red Riding Hood* starts streaming free on Tubi the same day, Comic Book Resources reports.
- Both announcements come from US platforms, so check your own local services for availability.
- Seyfried also voices Cinderella in Netflix's animated *Steps* and has been talking about the contrast with her recent Theranos project.

Two of Amanda Seyfried's early-2011 leading roles are about to become far easier to watch. On 1 December, *In Time* arrives on Paramount Plus and *Red Riding Hood* lands on the free platform Tubi, according to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/in-time-cillian-murphy-streaming-november-2026/) and [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/red-riding-hood-horror-fantasy-tubi-december-2026/). Both films are 15 years old, and both were divisive on release.

## In Time: a thriller where hours are currency

Directed by [Andrew Niccol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Niccol), [*In Time*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/In_Time) opened in cinemas on 28 October 2011. [Justin Timberlake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Timberlake) plays Will Salas, a working-class man in a future where people stop ageing at 25 and then get one more year unless they earn, borrow or steal extra time. A countdown on each person's forearm tracks what is left.

Seyfried plays Sylvia Weis. After Will inherits more than a century of life from a stranger, played by [Matt Bomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bomer), he is framed for murder and goes on the run with her. Cillian Murphy plays Raymond Leon, the Timekeeper cop chasing them. The cast also includes [Olivia Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Wilde), [Vincent Kartheiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Kartheiser) of [*Mad Men*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Men) and [Johnny Galecki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Galecki).

The numbers tell a mixed story. MovieWeb says the film took US$174 million at the box office on a US$40 million budget, so it earned more than four times its cost. Yet it holds only 35% from critics on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), against 51% from audiences. MovieWeb describes it as a cult favourite for its premise and world-building.

## Red Riding Hood: a darker fairy tale

Catherine Hardwicke, the director behind *Twilight*, made *Red Riding Hood*, and Tubi has listed it among its December horror additions. Comic Book Resources says it will sit alongside the original *Silent Hill*, *Underworld* and *The Mist*, all starting on 1 December.

Seyfried plays Valerie, a young woman in a medieval village haunted by a werewolf. She loves a poor woodcutter, Peter (Shiloh Fernandez), but is promised to the wealthy Henry (Max Irons). When her sister is killed, a werewolf hunter played by Gary Oldman tells the villagers the beast can look human by day, so anyone could be the killer.

Critics were not kind. Comic Book Resources notes that Roger Ebert gave it one star out of four. Audiences were warmer but still split, and the film made nearly US$90 million on a US$42 million budget. The outlet adds that even some negative reviews singled out Seyfried's performance.

Seyfried has stood by the film. In an earlier interview with HeyUGuys, quoted by Comic Book Resources, she called it "special in its own way" and said she is proud of it.

## Cinderella after Elizabeth Holmes

The streaming news lands as Seyfried promotes a very different role. She voices Cinderella in Netflix's animated *Steps*, which tells the fairy tale from the stepsisters' side, and we have covered [her singing in the film](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-steps-netflix-songs/).

Speaking to People, as reported by [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/692042-amanda-seyfried-reeasl-why-she-needed-break-from-elizabeth-holmes), the 40-year-old said it was a relief to play someone so "pure and guileless" after revisiting Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes in the documentary *You Can See Everything*. She admitted that project carried a sense of risk, saying she wondered if she might "destroy my career in some way". She also said Cinderella could use a bit more edge.
