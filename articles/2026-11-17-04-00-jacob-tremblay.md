**Quick summary**

- [Jacob Tremblay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Tremblay) attended the Governors Awards gala on 15 November 2026, according to LaineyGossip.
- His year centres on playing Ted Kaczynski in the Netflix film Unabomber, which opened on 25 September.
- Because it premiered directly on Netflix, the film is not eligible for the Oscars.
- Critics have been mixed: Rotten Tomatoes lists 25%, and Metacritic gives 46/100 from 18 critics.

Jacob Tremblay was among the guests at the Governors Awards on 15 November, a night that put actors known for television and streaming alongside the film crowd. [LaineyGossip](https://www.laineygossip.com/kate-oflynn-jacob-tremblay-charles-melton-and-all-the-tv-people-at-the-governors-awards/) photographed him there as part of a round-up of guests who split their time between film and TV.

## Tremblay at the Governors Awards

The site's write-up groups Tremblay with other attendees whose year has been shaped by screen work outside traditional cinema. [Charles Melton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Melton) was also there, and the same weekend he took part in a For Your Consideration event for Beef alongside [Cailee Spaeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cailee_Spaeny) and [Lee Sung Jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Sung_Jin). [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) and [Hoon Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoon_Lee), both linked to the limited series East of Eden, were photographed at the gala too.

LaineyGossip notes that Tremblay voiced a character in Wildwood, but says this year is really about his performance as Ted Kaczynski in Netflix's Unabomber. It adds that the film is not eligible for the Oscars, since it went straight to the streamer. That matters at an event run by the Academy, because it explains why his appearance reads as a profile-raising night out rather than a campaign stop for a film nomination.

## What Unabomber is and how it has landed

Unabomber was directed by Janus Metz Pedersen and has a cast that also includes Russell Crowe, Shailene Woodley, Annabelle Wallis and Alexander Ludwig. Its listed release date is 25 September 2026, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unabomber_(film)).

Reviews have been tough. The same Wikipedia entry records a 25% score on Rotten Tomatoes and 46/100 on Metacritic, based on 18 critics. The Rotten Tomatoes consensus line says the acting cannot make up for a thesis that fails to convince. It also reports that Robert Daniels of RogerEbert.com awarded two out of four stars and felt the film leaned on biopic habits and looked like a television movie. Richard Lawson of The Hollywood Reporter was critical of both Crowe's and Tremblay's performances.

No budget or box office figures are on record for the film, which is normal for a title that debuted on a streaming service rather than in cinemas.

## Why the appearance counts

For Tremblay, who first drew wide attention as a child actor, a role as one of America's most notorious criminals is a sharp turn into adult drama. Awards-season events like the Governors Awards give actors a chance to be seen by the industry voters and filmmakers in the room, even when their project sits outside the Oscar race. LaineyGossip makes a similar point about several TV-focused guests, who it says were there to network as much as to celebrate.

## What happens next

Nothing in the sources announces a new project or awards campaign for Tremblay. Viewers can find Unabomber on Netflix. Awards season is only starting, so any recognition for the film or its lead would most likely come from television or streaming categories rather than the Oscars, though no nominations have been reported.
