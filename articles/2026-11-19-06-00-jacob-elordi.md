**Quick summary**

- A source close to the couple told People that [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner) have adopted a Dalmatian puppy together.
- The report was published on 18 November 2026 and picked up by Just Jared, Page Six and others.
- Neither Elordi, 29, nor Jenner, 31, has publicly confirmed the adoption or their relationship.
- No name for the puppy has been reported.

The Jacob Elordi Dalmatian story broke on 18 November 2026: the Australian actor has reportedly adopted a puppy with the American model Kendall Jenner, according to a source who spoke to People. It is the first report of the two sharing a pet, and it arrives about eight months after they were first linked.

## Jacob Elordi Dalmatian: what the report says

The source told People the pair are "huge dog lovers" and that it is "something they've bonded over". The same insider said both are "already in love with their new puppy". [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/18/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-adopt-dalmatian-together-already-in-love/) noted that there is no word yet on what the dog has been called.

That is the full extent of the confirmed detail. The account rests on a single unnamed source, and the outlets covering it, including [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2226581-kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-family-addition) and [Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/11/18/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-relationship-official-puppy/), are all repeating the People report rather than adding their own. Neither star has posted about a new dog.

## How the relationship got here

Reports differ slightly on when it started. Just Jared traces the first rumours to the Vanity Fair Oscar Party in March 2026, then a sighting at Coachella in April. [RelRules](https://www.relrules.com/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-adopt-dalmatian-together/), citing Page Six, says they were first linked in April, followed by a trip to Hawaii the next month.

The sightings since then span several countries. In June the pair were photographed taking Elordi's dog for a hike in Byron Bay, in his home country of Australia, a few days before his 29th birthday. They were later seen in Japan and Montecito, according to Just Jared. In September, Elordi was in the front row at the Bottega Veneta Spring/Summer 2027 show, and ComingSoon.net reports that the two shared a candid moment at the Academy Museum Gala in October.

Page Six, as relayed by RelRules, reported that Justin and Hailey Bieber introduced them. The same outlet said last month that the couple had not moved in together, though Elordi had been house hunting in Los Angeles and staying with Jenner often.

## The dogs already in the picture

Jenner is not new to dog ownership. [StyleCaster](https://stylecaster.com/entertainment/celebrity-news/1234973540/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-dog/) says she has a Doberman Pinscher named Pyro and, reportedly, a dachshund named Finn, while Elordi has a golden retriever named Layla. RelRules adds that Pyro has been with Jenner since 2018. The Dalmatian would join a household that is already busy.

Jenner has been around dogs since childhood. ComingSoon.net recalls that she and her sisters raised a Chihuahua named Dolce and a black Labrador named Gabbana, and that she and [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner) were given two dogs, Louis and Vuitton, at Christmas in 2011.

## What it does and does not mean

Some outlets read plenty into the news. [Perez Hilton](https://perezhilton.com/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-adopt-dog-together/) called it a sign the couple are serious, while conceding that a puppy "doesn't come with a relationship contract". That is opinion, not reporting.

Other claims in the coverage are thinner still. StyleCaster cited an October report from In Touch about marriage talk, and ComingSoon.net quoted an earlier People source saying Jenner is "very into" Elordi and that her family has welcomed him. These are anonymous accounts of private matters, and none has been confirmed by either person.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. The next confirmation would have to come from Elordi or Jenner themselves, for instance through a social media post introducing the puppy.
