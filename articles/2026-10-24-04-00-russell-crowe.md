**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) historical epic *Gladiator* is among the films newly added to Netflix in October 2026.
- Tom's Guide named it a top pick for the weekend of 23 to 25 October.
- The film won five Oscars, including Best Picture and Best Actor for Crowe.
- Netflix has also added the 2024 sequel, *Gladiator II*, so viewers can watch both back to back.

Russell Crowe's *Gladiator* is now streaming on Netflix, and Tom's Guide has made it the headline recommendation in its list of new movies to watch over the weekend of 23 to 25 October. The site's October 2026 round-up describes the film as one of the most acclaimed historical epics ever made.

## Why Tom's Guide leads with Gladiator

According to [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/entertainment/netflix/3-new-to-netflix-movies-to-watch-this-weekend-including-russell-crowes-iconic-historical-action-epic-oct-23-25), the film won five Oscars, including [Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_common_film_awards_categories) and [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actor) for Crowe. The writer points to the brutal Colosseum fights and the often-quoted lines, among them "Are you not entertained?", as the reasons it still holds up. The film was directed by [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott), whom the piece credits with delivering a movie that "reigns supreme" in cinema history.

That is a strong endorsement from a site that covers streaming every week, and it comes as Netflix's October slate fills with familiar titles for the colder months.

## The story behind the film

Crowe plays [Maximus Decimus Meridius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gladiator_(2000_film)), a celebrated Roman general who serves Emperor Aurelius, played by [Richard Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Harris). When Aurelius's son Commodus, played by [Joaquin Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joaquin_Phoenix), murders his father and seizes control of Rome, Maximus's family is killed. He is taken by slave traders and sold to a gladiator trainer.

Thrown into the Colosseum, Maximus vows revenge on Commodus and fights with what Tom's Guide calls ferocity and purpose. It is the plot that gave the film its best-known line about vengeance "in this life, or the next".

## A double bill with the sequel

Tom's Guide notes that Netflix has also added the 2024 sequel, *Gladiator II*. The site calls the pairing a respectable double bill, though it adds that the follow-up was perhaps not worth the long wait of almost 25 years. That is the reviewer's view rather than a measured verdict, but it gives a sense of how the two films are being framed for new viewers.

## What else is on the list

*Gladiator* is one of three Netflix picks in the round-up. The others are *The Fate of the Furious*, the eighth film in the Fast and Furious franchise, which features [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron) as the cyberterrorist Cipher, and *Emily*, a period drama about Emily Brontë starring [Emma Mackey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Mackey). Tom's Guide describes the last as a semi-fictional account of the author's life that takes many creative liberties.

## What happens next

The recommendation covers the weekend of 23 to 25 October. Tom's Guide did not say how long the titles will stay on the service, so viewers who want to catch both *Gladiator* films should check Netflix directly. Netflix and Crowe had not commented on the listing in the source we read.
