**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) has built his red carpet style around Saint Laurent tailoring and Tiffany & Co. jewellery, according to a Socialite Life roundup published on 26 November.
- He is now an ambassador for both brands.
- His Met Gala debut paired a black smoking jacket with a polka-dot top that fell into a long train.
- At Saint Laurent's Spring/Summer 2027 men's show in Paris he wore latex stockings under a PVC raincoat.
- He debuted a buzzcut at the Creative Arts Emmys, with his mother as his date.

Connor Storrie did not set out to become a fashion figure, but a year of award shows, the Met Gala and a Paris runway front row has made him one. A [Socialite Life](https://socialitelife.com/connor-storrie-best-fashion-moments-saint-laurent/) roundup published on 26 November traces how the [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) star's red carpet look took shape, and the thread running through it is Saint Laurent.

## From a reluctant start to a signature look

Storrie has been open that clothes were never his first interest. "Fashion was not on my radar, but the moment you start trying on really nice clothes, you understand why people love it so much," he told VMAN, as quoted by Socialite Life.

The first template came at the Golden Globes. He wore a classic black suit with a skinny tie and sunglasses, and pinned on a Tiffany & Co. Bird on a Rock brooch, a design made famous by the jeweller's longtime designer [Jean Schlumberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Schlumberger_(writer)).

The Actor Awards on 1 March sharpened the approach. Storrie paired Saint Laurent tailoring with small silver hoop earrings and a platinum and diamond Tiffany Victoria Mixed Cluster necklace, worn as a choker.

Two weeks later he and co-star [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) arrived at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party in matching sheer tops. Their stylists later said the match was unplanned, according to the roundup.

## The Met Gala train

The biggest swing came at the Met Gala, where Storrie made his debut as an ambassador for Tiffany & Co. and Saint Laurent. He climbed the steps in a black smoking jacket, then took it off.

He explained the plan to GQ in plain terms: "Smoking jacket, super-wide shoulder, very black, very, very classic YSL." Underneath sat a sleeveless polka-dot lavallière top in silk muslin. "It creates this waterfall train that's underneath the blazer," he said. "So you get kind of a two-beat moment."

Stylist James Yardley told Marie Claire Australia that he saw discipline in the look. "There's a confidence in that kind of restraint," he said.

## Paris, then the buzzcut

Paris loosened things up. At Saint Laurent's Spring/Summer 2027 men's show on 23 June, Storrie removed a black PVC raincoat to show a ribbed vest, tailored shorts and a tie knotted around his bare neck. Black latex stockings and laceless patent mules finished the outfit.

By September's Creative Arts Emmys the curls were gone. He debuted a buzzcut with a charcoal-grey Saint Laurent suit and an unbuttoned blue shirt. His mother, Linda Storrie, was his date. He had given fair warning in a Teen Vogue interview: "I'm ready to cut this stuff off if y'all want me to be completely honest."

The haircut has divided fans, and Storrie has since said he is happy to disappoint them, as we reported in [Connor Storrie Isn't Sorry Fans Hate His Buzzcut](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-buzzcut/).

## Why it matters

Storrie links the red carpet to a craft he already knew. "It feels like an extension of photography," he said. "It feels like live photography." For an actor whose career took off with one television series, that framing explains why his looks read as deliberate rather than dressed-for-the-cameras.

For readers who follow style, the year offers a clear lesson in consistency: one fashion house, one jeweller, and a willingness to change the plan on the night. ## What happens next

The roundup does not announce any new appearances or campaigns. Given his ambassador roles with Saint Laurent and Tiffany & Co., more joint appearances are plausible, but none is confirmed in the sources. Watch for the next awards season for his next red carpet statement.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which designer does Connor Storrie wear most?
Saint Laurent. The roundup shows him in the house's tailoring at the Actor Awards, the Met Gala, its Paris show and the Creative Arts Emmys.

### What did Connor Storrie wear to the Met Gala?
A black Saint Laurent smoking jacket over a sleeveless polka-dot silk muslin lavallière top whose bow fell into a long train, as he described to GQ.

### Is Connor Storrie a brand ambassador?
Yes. Socialite Life describes him as an ambassador for both Tiffany & Co. and Saint Laurent.
