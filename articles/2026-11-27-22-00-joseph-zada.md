**Quick summary**

- Arab News has reviewed *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* and calls [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) a "likable lead" as the teenage Haymitch Abernathy.
- The reviewer says the film lacks the bite of the original trilogy and tones down the violence in the arena.
- The supporting cast, including Mckenna Grace, Elle Fanning and Jesse Plemons, fares better than the script, according to the review.
- The film opened on 28 October 2026 and, per Wikipedia, has earned about US$237.2 million against a US$150 million budget.

Joseph Zada's turn as the young Haymitch Abernathy has earned a mixed notice from Arab News, whose critic praised the actor but felt the prequel had lost the sharp edges of the franchise that made it a phenomenon. The review of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* was published on 27 November 2026.

## What the review says about Joseph Zada

The film is set 24 years before the original trilogy. It follows a 16-year-old Haymitch, who is picked for the 50th Hunger Games and ends up caught in a plot to sabotage the event. In the later films, the character is the heavy-drinking mentor to [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen), played by [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson).

According to [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/entertainment/review-the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-lacks-bite-of-originals-3014228), Zada is likable, but his version of Haymitch has little of the roguish charm Harrelson brought to the part. The reviewer put much of the blame on the screenplay, which they said reduces the character's growing political awareness to blunt, on-the-nose dialogue.

That is a fair-minded verdict: the criticism is aimed mainly at the writing, not the performance. For more on how Zada prepared, see our earlier piece on [Joseph Zada's road to Haymitch](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-haymitch-invisible-boys/).

## The supporting cast gets stronger marks

The reviewer singled out [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) as the abrasive tribute Maysilee Donner and [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) as a younger Effie Trinket. [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons) was called excellent as the young games designer Plutarch Heavensbee, with glimpses of how his ideas of resistance take shape and how quietly he manipulates the young tributes.

## Where the film loses its bite

The main complaint concerns tone. Arab News argues the story should have been the most emotionally devastating in the series, yet the arena violence has been dialled back. The dangers are reduced to stinging butterflies, rats and poisonous pollen, and deaths barely register before the plot moves on.

The reviewer also said director Francis Lawrence seems reluctant to let viewers sit with the cruelty described in Suzanne Collins' novel. The final stretch is rushed, and a reassuring epilogue undercuts the tragedy. The verdict: audiences should leave inconsolable, but this critic left disappointed.

## How the film is faring

Arab News is not alone in holding reservations. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), it holds a 68% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 55 out of 100 from 43 critics. The critics' consensus there says the prequel retreads familiar territory but does so sturdily, and benefits from spotlighting beloved characters.

On the numbers, the film has taken about US$237.2 million worldwide against a US$150 million budget, roughly 1.6 times its cost. That sits at the modest end for a blockbuster of this size, and the sources do not say whether it is still in cinemas in every market.
