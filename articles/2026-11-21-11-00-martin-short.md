**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short), 76, was teased about [Meryl Streep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meryl_Streep) on *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* on Thursday, 19 November.
- [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) started the joking, and host Jimmy Kimmel kept it going.
- Short has said before that he and Streep are "just very close friends"; his representative has said the same.
- Season six of *Only Murders in the Building* premieres on 8 December.

Martin Short found himself on the receiving end of the jokes when he appeared on *Jimmy Kimmel Live!* with Selena Gomez on 19 November. The pair were there to talk up the sixth season of [Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building), and the conversation soon turned to Meryl Streep, who has long been rumoured to be close to him.

## How the Meryl Streep teasing started

According to [Yahoo Lifestyle Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/selena-gomez-jimmy-kimmel-tease-153410008.html), Kimmel asked Gomez whether she had shared scenes with Streep. She said she had, and that she gets "geeked out" around her. Short chimed in that it is Streep's fourth season on the show.

Gomez then said she did not know why Streep keeps coming back, glancing at Short as she spoke. Short answered that he did not know either. Gomez added that working with Streep makes her unbelievably happy, and that if Streep is one of your heroes, she lives up to it.

## Kimmel keeps pushing

The talk-show host did not let it go. When Gomez said she did not want to "creep out" Streep in case they were all going to dinner, Kimmel jumped on the slip and asked whether Streep was joining them that night. Gomez quickly backed away from the idea.

[Yahoo News Canada](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/martin-short-cornered-meryl-streep-165813997.html) reports that Short answered the dinner question with a joke about In-N-Out. Kimmel also remarked on the role reversal: people are usually curious about Gomez's personal life, yet on this occasion the host was the one being "cagey" about Short's.

Short got his own back as the interview wound up. He asked Kimmel what he would do after the segment ended, then guessed he would head to the buffet. Kimmel replied that he would sit at the next table and take photos on his phone to sell to TMZ.

## What Short and his team have actually said

Nothing said on the show confirmed a relationship, and the exchange was played for laughs. The outlets point to an earlier, direct statement. In January 2024, Short told [Bill Maher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Maher) on *Club Random*: "We're not a couple, we are just very close friends," according to Yahoo News Canada. Yahoo Lifestyle Canada adds that his representative said at the time, "They are just very good friends, nothing more."

The speculation has not gone away. Yahoo Lifestyle Canada says the two first drew attention in August 2024, when they were seen holding hands at the season four premiere after-party in Los Angeles, after sitting together at that year's Golden Globes. A viral photo shared on X on 17 May this year, reportedly taken at a London restaurant, revived the talk. Both outlets describe it as speculation, and neither Short nor Streep has said anything to change the 2024 statements.
