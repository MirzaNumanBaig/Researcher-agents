**Quick summary**

- *You Can See Everything*, the documentary co-directed by Nathan Fielder, has earned US$102.4 million worldwide, according to Variety as cited by Yahoo.
- That total is made up of US$93.4 million in the United States and Canada and US$9 million overseas, on an estimated US$7 million budget.
- It holds a 97% score on Rotten Tomatoes from 248 reviews as of 23 November.
- On Thursday, UCLA students held a Fielder and Elizabeth Holmes lookalike contest, reported by the Daily Bruin.

A documentary made largely in secret has become one of the biggest of all time. [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder)'s *You Can See Everything*, which he co-directed with [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim), has passed US$100 million at the global box office, according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/article/nathan-fielders-elizabeth-holmes-movie-you-can-see-everything-hits-rare-box-office-milestone-130406253.html), which cites Variety. The film has also started to inspire fan events, including a lookalike contest at a Los Angeles university.

## How the numbers add up

Yahoo reports a worldwide gross of US$102.4 million. Most of that, US$93.4 million, came from the domestic market, with the remaining US$9 million earned overseas. The film's estimated production budget was US$7 million, so it has returned more than 14 times its cost.

Documentaries rarely get near that territory. For comparison, Yahoo notes that Michael Moore's *Fahrenheit 9/11* took US$222 million worldwide in 2004, and *March of the Penguins* made US$127 million in 2005. Neither figure is adjusted for inflation. That places Fielder's film among the highest-grossing theatrical documentaries on record.

Our earlier report covers its [record UK documentary opening](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-you-can-see-everything-box-office/).

## What the film is about

*You Can See Everything* follows Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes through the weeks before she entered federal prison in 2023 and the years after. Holmes was convicted of defrauding investors in her blood-testing company. The story starts 34 days before she was due to begin an 11-year sentence.

Holmes and her partner, Billy Evans, invited Fielder and a small crew into their rented home in Del Mar, California. Fielder moved in with the couple and their two young children. The project took nearly four years and stayed secret until a surprise screening at the Telluride Film Festival in September, according to Yahoo. Holmes, who remains in federal prison, has not commented on the film.

Fielder is best known for *Nathan for You* and *The Rehearsal*, both built on awkward encounters and elaborate social experiments.

## Critics are almost unanimous

The film holds a 97% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) from 248 reviews as of 23 November, per Yahoo. The [film's Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything) lists a Metacritic score of 97 out of 100 from 43 critics.

The Guardian gave it five stars and called it a "modern masterpiece of deceit". [Manohla Dargis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manohla_Dargis) of The New York Times made it a Critic's Pick. Vulture called it "the movie of the year".

Not every reviewer was fully won over. Some critics had reservations about the runtime of nearly three hours and about how the film treats Holmes. The Washington Post's Shane O'Neill said that even for fans of cringe comedy, it is "a tough watch".

## UCLA students stage a lookalike contest

The film's reach now extends to campus. According to the [Daily Bruin](https://dailybruin.com/2026/11/23/they-can-see-everything-ucla-club-hosts-elizabeth-holmes-nathan-fielder-lookalike-competition), about 20 people attended a Holmes and Fielder lookalike contest in Dodd Hall on Thursday, roughly a month after the film's release. The event was hosted by ACM Teach LA at UCLA, a club that gives free coding classes to schools around Los Angeles.

Co-president Javier Castaneda said he wanted to build community among members and was inspired by YouTuber Anthony Po's 2024 Timothée Chalamet lookalike contest in New York. Castaneda dressed as Fielder from *Nathan for You*.

The contest had five stages. They covered a quiz on Fielder quotes, absurd business pitches, a staring contest borrowed from a scene in the film, scene re-enactments and a dance-off to Lil Wayne's "How to Love". That song is a nod to *The Dropout*, in which Amanda Seyfried played Holmes.

Law students Luke Glaze, entering as "Prime Nathan", and Alina Bozhko, as "Liz", won the most votes and a US$25 AMC gift card each. Bozhko beat Glaze in the staring contest in 4.92 seconds. Another contestant, "Beth", pitched a start-up called "Urinos", a joke on Theranos.

A similar event was covered earlier in our post on [another Fielder lookalike contest](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-lookalike-contest/).

## What happens next

The sources do not say how much longer the film will stay in cinemas or when it will reach streaming. Yahoo's critic Brett Arnold argues it deserves to be the first documentary nominated for Best Picture at the Oscars, but that is his opinion, not an announcement.
