**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) shared a set of boxing workout photos on Instagram on November 16, according to HELLO!.
- His caption joked that he trains against smaller opponents to lift his self-esteem.
- His trainer, Luke Zocchi, has described a routine of full-body strength work and circuit-style cardio.
- Hemsworth is due on the green carpet at EVENT Cinemas Pacific Fair on the Gold Coast on December 4.
- Avengers: Doomsday opens in Australian and New Zealand cinemas on December 17.

Chris Hemsworth has given fans a look at his current training, posting a series of boxing photos on Instagram on November 16 with a caption that poked fun at himself. The images arrive a few weeks before the actor returns home for an Avengers: Doomsday event on the Gold Coast.

## The boxing post and the joke behind it

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/935478/chris-hemsworth-looks-more-ripped-than-ever-in-new-photos/), the pictures show Hemsworth mid-workout, topless and in black shorts. The caption set up the gag: some people spar with bigger, stronger opponents to sharpen their skills, while he prefers smaller ones to improve his self-esteem.

He thanked the sparring partner, tagged as @zocobodypro, and wrote that his ego "has never been healthier". He then added that the partner would win a real fight but has to keep his hands off the actor's face because Hemsworth needs to be on camera.

Fans filled the comments with praise. One wrote that he was in great shape, while another joked that their wife was on the app too.

## A family habit

Boxing is not new to the household. HELLO! notes that Hemsworth previously posted a clip of himself practising the sport with his two sons in the family's backyard. In that caption he wrote that training with his own father shaped some of his fondest childhood memories, and that sharing the same moments with his kids felt like life coming full circle.

## What his trainer says about the routine

HELLO! also quotes Luke Zocchi, the actor's personal trainer, from an earlier interview. Zocchi said the programme in the lead-up to filming Furiosa ran three to four sessions a week and closely resembled the Power Shred plan on Centr, the fitness platform Hemsworth is associated with.

Those sessions were full-body workouts built on bench presses, chin-ups, kettlebell deadlifts and lunges, plus machine work for every muscle group. They were followed by circuit-style cardio, including running, SkiErg and farmer's carries, with minimal rest.

Zocchi added that recovery is a large part of the plan. On the Furiosa shoot the team had access to a hotel sauna and a cold plunge pool, and in remote locations the focus was on stretching and slowing his breathing after a session. These details describe the Furiosa period, so they are not a confirmed account of what he is doing now.

## The Doomsday connection

The timing matters because Hemsworth is a headline name in Marvel Studios' Avengers: Doomsday. [HEAVY Magazine](https://heavymag.com.au/chris-hemsworth-to-appear-at-event-cinemas-pacific-fair/) reports that he will walk the green carpet and take part in a Q&A at EVENT Cinemas Pacific Fair on December 4, before invited guests see an exclusive first screening.

The film is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo and produced by Kevin Feige, Louis D'Esposito and Jonathan Schwartz. The cast includes [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) and [Tom Hiddleston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hiddleston), among many others. The story sets heroes from three universes against what HEAVY describes as an existential threat.
