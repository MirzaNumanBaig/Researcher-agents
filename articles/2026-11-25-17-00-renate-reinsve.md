**Quick summary**

- Two films starring [Renate Reinsve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renate_Reinsve) are in the European Film Festival Malaysia, which opens in Kuala Lumpur on Thursday, 26 November.
- Cristian Mungiu's Fjord opens the festival, and Joachim Trier's Oscar-winning Sentimental Value is also on the programme, according to Scandasia.
- The festival runs over three weekends to 13 December, with tickets at RM8.
- Separately, Foro3D reports that Vogue placed Reinsve ninth on a list of 45 possible Bond casting candidates. No casting has been announced.

Norwegian actor Renate Reinsve has two films in the line-up of the European Film Festival Malaysia, which begins in Kuala Lumpur on Thursday, 26 November, [Scandasia reports](https://scandasia.com/renate-reinsve-stars-in-two-films-at-malaysias-european-film-festival/). Fjord, the recent Palme d'Or winner, opens the event, and the Oscar-winning Sentimental Value sits alongside it in the programme.

## Reinsve's two films at the festival

The festival is screening 20 films from 20 countries at TGV Pavilion Kuala Lumpur, over three weekends ending on 13 December. Scandasia says the line-up draws on 17 European Union member states, plus Norway, Switzerland and Ukraine. The EU Delegation to Malaysia and Blackflag present this year's edition.

In Fjord, Reinsve stars opposite [Sebastian Stan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Stan). The story follows a family that settles in a Norwegian village, where local authorities begin to look closely at how the parents raise their children. It is a co-production between Romania, France, Norway, Sweden and Denmark. According to Scandasia, it won the Palme d'Or at Cannes in May, which is Mungiu's second win of the festival's top prize.

That gives Reinsve a lead role in a film that has already won the biggest prize in European cinema this year. For Malaysian audiences, it is also the film that starts the festival.

## Sentimental Value and the Oscar

[Sentimental Value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sentimental_Value) is the second Reinsve title in the programme. She plays Nora Borg, a stage actor who turns down a part in her estranged father's comeback film. Her father, played by Swedish actor Stellan Skarsgård, casts a young Hollywood star in the role instead. [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) plays that star, and [Inga Ibsdotter Lilleaas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inga_Ibsdotter_Lilleaas) plays Nora's sister.

The film won the Grand Prix at Cannes in 2025. In March it became the first Norwegian film to win the Oscar for [Best International Feature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_International_Feature_Film), according to Scandasia. It also reunited Reinsve with director Joachim Trier, who made The Worst Person in the World with her. That earlier film earned her the best actress award at Cannes in 2021.

Put together, the festival offers a Cannes-winning actor in two films that each took a top prize on the Croisette within about a year of each other.

## Also on the programme

Scandasia notes one more title worth knowing: Finnish director Lauri-Matti Parppei's debut, A Light That Never Goes Out, which won seven Jussi Awards in Finland, including Film of the Year.

Tickets cost RM8 and are sold through the TGV website, its app and cinema kiosks. The organisers plan to make selected films available in Malaysia through Astro First from January 2027, and say they will announce those titles later this year.

## A Vogue Bond list names Reinsve

In a separate report, Foro3D says British Vogue placed Reinsve ninth on a list of 45 candidates to be the new Bond girl. The report says the list is headed by [Saoirse Ronan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saoirse_Ronan), Zendaya and [Nicola Coughlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Coughlan), and that it mentions [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the actor she would join. Foro3D says Vogue pointed to her indie profile and a possible fit with a Scandinavian plot.

This is a magazine's guess, not a casting call. Foro3D notes that the franchise has not announced anything and that such lists come out every few months and rarely come true. It also quotes the critic Morten Ståle Nilsen, who welcomes the mention but wants a substantial role rather than a decorative one. We have not seen the Vogue list itself, so treat the ranking as secondhand.

Reinsve's recent work also includes the film Butterfly, directed by Itonje Søimer Guttormsen, which was released on 25 September 2026, according to Wikidata. No box office figures are available for it yet.

## What happens next

The festival opens on 26 November and closes on 13 December. After that, the organisers say selected titles will reach Malaysian viewers through Astro First from January 2027, with the list still to come. On the Bond question, nothing is confirmed.
