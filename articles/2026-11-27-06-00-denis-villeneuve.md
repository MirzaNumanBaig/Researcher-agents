**Quick summary**

- Dune: Part Three reportedly cost about US$200 million to make, excluding marketing, according to The New York Times as cited by AOL.
- That would make it the most expensive of [Denis Villeneuve's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) three Dune films.
- A new 60-second teaser gives a first proper listen to [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the villain Scytale.
- The film opens in Australian cinemas and IMAX on 16 December 2026, two days before the US release.

The final chapter of Denis Villeneuve's Dune trilogy is reportedly its most expensive, with a production budget of around US$200 million, and a fresh teaser is giving audiences their first real taste of its new villain. With the film due in Australian cinemas on 16 December, the numbers and the footage are arriving together.

## How the Dune: Part Three budget compares

According to [AOL's report](https://www.aol.com/articles/dune-part-three-reported-budget-160328000.html), which cites The New York Times, [Legendary Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legendary_Entertainment) and Warner Bros. spent about US$200 million on the film, not counting marketing. These are reported figures rather than studio-confirmed ones.

The same report puts the 2021 original at about US$165 million and Dune: Part Two at US$190 million. That leaves the third film US$35 million above the first and US$10 million above the second, a rise of roughly 21% across the trilogy.

The earlier films give the studio something to point to. AOL, citing The Numbers, says the first film earned around US$430 million worldwide. Dune: Part Two collected roughly US$715 million, about US$285 million more, and opened to US$82.5 million in North America against the original's US$41 million. That is more than three times its reported budget.

## What the new teaser shows

The 60-second teaser, which debuted through IGN according to [Screen Realm](https://screenrealm.com/dune-part-three-teaser-robert-pattinson-voice/), centres on Pattinson as Scytale, a Face Dancer of the Bene Tleilax who is among those plotting against Emperor Paul Atreides, played by Timothée Chalamet.

In the clip, Scytale probes Chani (Zendaya) about rumours of Paul's children. She waves it off as desert legend and children's stories. His reply, "I love children's stories," is delivered in a soft, almost playful voice that has already drawn reaction online. Screen Realm notes it is another distinctive vocal turn from Pattinson, following his work in Mickey 17 and The Odyssey.

The film adapts [Dune Messiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_Messiah), Frank Herbert's 1969 novel, and picks up 17 years after Part Two, with Paul facing the fallout of the holy war fought in his name. Florence Pugh's Princess Irulan is now his wife. [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy) returns as Alia, and the cast also includes Jason Momoa, Rebecca Ferguson, Javier Bardem and Charlotte Rampling.

## A crowded release week

Warner Bros. has set 16 December for Australian cinemas and IMAX, and 18 December in the US. According to AOL, that is also the opening date of Avengers: Doomsday, and fans have nicknamed the pairing "DunesDay" in the spirit of 2023's Barbenheimer. ## Villeneuve and the Bond question

Villeneuve's next job also surfaced in the same news cycle. Screen Realm states that Pattinson has been officially cast as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) and that Villeneuve is directing that film. Foro3D, which describes Villeneuve as directing the next Bond, says he has pointed to Casino Royale and Skyfall as references, along with childhood viewing of [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) films with his father. Those two films belong to the [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) era. Foro3D reads that as a mix of Craig-style drama and Connery-style wit, which is its interpretation rather than a confirmed plan. Our earlier piece, [Denis Villeneuve's Bond Film: Casting Talk Heats Up](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-film-2/), tracks how that story developed.
