Hudson Williams and the groomer who has styled his hair through a breakout year say they are now more like family than colleagues. In an interview published on 18 November, [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/lifestyle/style/hudson-williams-aika-flores-interview-behind-looks-1236746322/) describes how Aika Flores went from a single-day job to a constant presence beside the 25-year-old Canadian actor.

## How Hudson Williams and Aika Flores met

According to The Hollywood Reporter, the two first worked together in early December 2025. Flores had been hired by a close friend, the editor-in-chief of Timid Magazine, to do grooming for a cover shoot. [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), the Canadian hockey romance series that made Williams a star, had then been out for just over a week. Flores recalls that her friend told her the show was already getting people talking.

Williams remembers the session as an easy one. He says he was exhausted that day but that the pair quickly swapped stories about where they grew up. He describes Flores as "grounding, chill, funny", and says that early in the morning, after late nights, a quiet presence matters to him.

His team was watching too. Flores says they wanted to see whether the two got along, and booked her for the rest of the press tour while the shoot was still under way. A job meant to last a day turned into trips around the world for media appearances and red carpets.

## A year that moved fast

The magazine sets out how crowded the months afterwards were. Once Heated Rivalry finished airing around Christmas, Williams's calendar filled with late-night talk show tapings, a Golden Globes presenting turn, carrying the Olympic torch and walking a runway in Milan. He also became a brand ambassador for the Korean skincare company Skin1004, which the report links to his genuine interest in skincare.

Flores was there for it, along with his CAA branding agent Leilani Houston and stylist Anastasia Walker. Williams says the four call their group chat the "quad squad". The day before the interview, he says, they stayed up until 3 a.m. after a 12-hour shoot day simply because they enjoy each other's company.

## "Uncle Hudson" and the lighter side

The interview also shows a softer side. Williams says he has become "Uncle Hudson" to Flores's young son, and he describes taking bedtime stories seriously, doing voices for each character. Flores jokes that she and her wife do not go to the same lengths. In the green room of the magazine's New York studio, the pair were joking around between video takes, with Williams dodging Flores's hands as she tried to get him ready.

Not everything is play. Williams says he leans on the group when online commentary turns harsh, and that the four laugh off speculation and made-up claims because the alternative is letting them weigh on him.
