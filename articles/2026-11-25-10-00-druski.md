**Quick summary**

- Comedian and creator [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) is ranked No. 7 on Forbes' Top Creators list, with earnings of $20 million, according to The Root.
- A multi-year overall deal with Netflix was announced in November 2026 and covers original programming and access to his YouTube catalogue.
- The first project under the deal, the semi-scripted series Collect & Praise, premieres on December 1, 2026.
- He owns his production company outright and holds equity in consumer brands rather than only taking sponsorship fees.

Druski's Netflix deal is the headline move in a year that has turned the YouTube comedian into a business owner as much as a performer. [The Root](https://www.theroot.com/laughing-all-the-way-to-the-bank-druski-s-top-5-bigges-2000136841) published a breakdown of his five biggest financial decisions this week, pointing to a $20 million Forbes ranking and a deal that starts on December 1.

## A year of firsts

According to The Root, Druski has hosted the BET Awards and became the first digital creator to host [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live). The outlet argues that his real edge is keeping creative control, and that the money follows from it. You can follow our wider coverage on the [Druski tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/druski/).

## Building 4lifers Entertainment

The Root says Druski launched 4lifers Entertainment in 2023. The company handles his content, touring, licensing and merchandise. The outlet compares the model to Adam Sandler's Happy Madison Productions.

He used money from commercial sponsorships to fund several unscripted YouTube series himself: Coulda Been Records, Coulda Been House, Coulda Been Love and Coulda Been Daddy. According to the report, he made them after television networks and streaming platforms passed on his ideas. Because he kept 100 per cent ownership, he also controlled distribution, sponsor placements and long-term licensing rights.

## The Netflix deal

The Root reports that the multi-year overall deal with Netflix was announced in November 2026. It includes original programming and access to his full YouTube catalogue, which the outlet says has already passed a billion views without a studio behind it.

The first project is Collect & Praise, a semi-scripted comedy series due on December 1, 2026. For reaction to it, see our post on [how Druski's Netflix series Collect & Praise splits opinion](https://moduscarry.com/druski-collect-and-praise-netflix-2/).

## Equity over endorsements

Rather than only being paid to promote products, The Root says Druski takes ownership stakes in fast-growing consumer companies. A multi-year partnership with Gopuff folds Coulda Been Records and 4lifers into the delivery company's commerce and distribution network. He also holds equity in Happy Dad Hard Seltzer, which appears in his sketches, podcasts and arena tours.

He still works with large brands too. The Root lists Amazon, Beats by Dre, Bud Light, EA Sports, Fanatics, Google Pixel, Nike, PrizePicks and Raising Cane's among them, through TV spots, custom sketches and live event sponsorships.

## From viral characters to ticket sales

The fourth move is turning online personas into live revenue. The Root says his 2023 debut, the 30-city Coulda, Woulda, Shoulda Tour, grossed more than $2.5 million, with Happy Dad as co-sponsor. Coulda Fest has since sold out Atlanta's State Farm Arena and London's Wembley Arena, according to the report.

## What happens next

Collect & Praise arrives on Netflix on December 1, 2026, the first test of whether his online audience follows him to the streamer. Further projects under the overall deal have not been detailed in the sources we read.
