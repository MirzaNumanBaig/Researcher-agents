**Quick summary**

- Matt Damon says [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck) strongly praised Robert Pattinson's work in Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey", according to Variety.
- Pattinson plays Antinous, the antagonistic ringleader of Penelope's suitors.
- Damon agreed, saying Pattinson made the character as cowardly and hateable as possible.
- Pattinson has four major films this year, including "Primetime" and "Dune: Part Three", due on 18 December.

[Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has a new fan on the Nolan set's guest list. Matt Damon says Ben Affleck was so taken with Pattinson's performance in Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" that he described it as "like watching a guy run into an avalanche", according to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/ben-affleck-robert-pattinson-the-odyssey-1236948199/), which reported the exchange on 15 November 2026.

## What Affleck said about Robert Pattinson in The Odyssey

Variety reports that Affleck saw the film for the first time and then talked it over with his close friend Damon, who stars in it. Their focus was Pattinson's turn as Antinous, the antagonistic ringleader of Penelope's suitors.

Damon relayed Affleck's verdict this way, as quoted by Variety: Pattinson "had all of the weight and heft and power of Christopher Nolan's storytelling coming down on him", and the way he handled it was "like watching a guy run into an avalanche". An Entertainment Weekly headline on the same story says Affleck spent 20 minutes raving about the performance. We could not open that article, so treat the length of the conversation as EW's claim.

Damon backed his friend up. He said that in any moment where Pattinson could make Antinous more cowardly or more hateable, you could see the actor's mind working: "How could I make this even worse for myself?" He added that it was fun to watch because Pattinson is such a dynamic and interesting actor.

## A heavy year for Pattinson

Variety notes that Pattinson is starring in four major movies this year, which it says puts his star power to the test. Besides "The Odyssey", the list includes Lance Oppenheim's "Primetime", the dark romantic comedy "The Drama" and "Dune: Part Three", which opens on 18 December.

"The Odyssey" is not his first Nolan project. Pattinson played the mysterious operative Neil in the 2020 sci-fi thriller "Tenet", as Variety points out.

## How Primetime is doing

"Primetime" is the film where the critics have been loudest. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film)), it opened on 5 September 2026 and carries an 86% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 68 out of 100 from 54 critics. The same entry lists a budget of about US$14.5 million and a box office of about US$62 million, roughly four times its cost. Those figures come from a crowd-edited source, so check them against trade reports before relying on them.

The entry also quotes the BBC's Nicholas Barber, who gave the film five stars and called Pattinson's work "extraordinary". A separate review from the site Micropsia, which we could not read in full, carries the headline "Robert Pattinson Gets Lost in the Dark Side of Reality Television".
