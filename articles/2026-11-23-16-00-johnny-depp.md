**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) plays Ebenezer Scrooge in *Ebenezer*, a Gothic retelling of *A Christmas Carol* directed by Ti West.
- Editor David Kashevaroff spoke to Awards Radar on 23 November about cutting the film with West.
- The film holds an 89% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes' Popcornmeter, according to Awards Radar.
- Latination, citing The Wall Street Journal, says Depp spent about eight years away from major productions and relaxes on his private island in the Bahamas.

Johnny Depp is back in cinemas as Ebenezer Scrooge, and the people who built the film are now explaining how it came together. *Ebenezer*, a Gothic reimagining of [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens)' *A Christmas Carol*, is directed by [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West) and is already playing, with its editor and its star both in the news this week.

## Who is in Ebenezer?

According to [Awards Radar](https://awardsradar.com/2026/11/23/interview-ebenezer-editor-david-kashevaroff/), Depp leads a supporting cast that includes [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen), [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint), [Sam Claflin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Claflin), [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) and [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough). The outlet reports an audience score of 89% on the [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) Popcornmeter. That figure reflects viewer ratings, not critics, so it says little about how reviewers received the film.

The film reunites West with editor David Kashevaroff. Awards Radar notes Kashevaroff's two decades of work on shows such as [Project Runway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Runway) and [Top Chef](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Top_Chef), and on the horror series *Them*. Their partnership began in 2022 when they co-edited the slasher film *X*.

## How Ebenezer was cut

In the interview, Kashevaroff describes an unusual arrangement. West has always edited his own movies, so on *X* he brought Kashevaroff in as a co-editor. West was also preparing *Pearl*, which he shot about three weeks after *X*, and the extra pair of hands let the work on the first film continue while the second was filming.

Kashevaroff says the method relies on surprise. West would trade sequences back and forth with him, sometimes sending a scene back with changes he had not mentioned. Kashevaroff presents that as a strength, because each of them kept seeing the footage in a new way.

He also says West usually knows what he wants and rarely shoots many takes. The exception is a complicated camera move. Kashevaroff points to the opening shot of *Ebenezer*, which includes a big drop, a wraparound into a window and Depp's first line, as one that took numerous takes. He adds that the film went from shooting to finish in nine months, which is why two editors who knew the footage well were needed.

The interview also covers the balance of Depp's performance and the editor's role in making a jump scare work. The version published by Awards Radar was cut off in our source text, so those answers are not summarised here.

## Depp's life away from the spotlight

A separate report from [Latination](https://latination.com/johnny-depp-private-island-bahamas-hollywood-escape/) draws on an interview Depp gave to The Wall Street Journal. We have not read the original, so these details come second-hand.

According to Latination, Depp bought a wooden house built in the 1940s on a private island in the Bahamas in 2004. He later added a yurt, a painting studio and a music space. He reportedly told the Journal that nobody points a camera at him there. Latination says his days involve reading, painting and playing guitar.

The same report says *Ebenezer* ends roughly eight years away from major film productions for Depp. It also recalls how he arrived in Los Angeles in the 1980s with his band and met [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage), who encouraged him to try acting. That led to roles such as [Edward Scissorhands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Scissorhands) and later [Jack Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Sparrow).
