**Quick summary**

- [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon) is reportedly set for a "fun cameo" in *Stuntnuts*, according to a Deadline report cited by The Direct.
- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) play exaggerated versions of themselves.
- The film is the first in a new trilogy set in the *Kick-Ass* universe, directed by stuntman-turned-filmmaker [Damien Walters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Walters).
- It debuted at a screening in November, is looking for a distributor and has no worldwide release date.

Matt Damon is reportedly joining Chris Hemsworth for a cameo in *Stuntnuts*, an action-comedy that puts four stunt doubles in the lead roles. The report comes via Deadline, as relayed by [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/matt-damon-chris-hemsworth-action-movie) on 28 November. If confirmed, it would be only the third time the two actors have shared a project.

## What Stuntnuts is about

*Stuntnuts* is the first film in a new trilogy set in the *Kick-Ass* world. [Matthew Vaughn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Vaughn), who directed the original *Kick-Ass*, is attached as a producer. The director's chair belongs to Damien Walters, a stuntman who moved into directing.

According to The Direct, the story follows gifted stunt performers who join a huge blockbuster in a bid to save their community gym. The production then spirals out of control. One of the leads is Hemsworth's regular stunt double, [Bobby Holland Hanton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Holland_Hanton), which makes the film a nod to a part of the industry audiences rarely see.

## Hemsworth plays himself

Hemsworth and Rockwell are described as playing "hilariously heightened versions of themselves", with "dueling egos" that help push the fictional production off the rails. The Direct notes that Hemsworth has only played himself once before, as a hologram cameo in 2019's *Jay and Silent Bob Reboot*. This role looks far bigger, because he drives the story and pays tribute to his own stuntman.

Damon's part has not been described in detail. Reports call it a "fun cameo", and The Direct suggests he may also play a version of himself, though that is its guess, not a confirmed detail.

## How Damon and Hemsworth have worked together before

Their earlier collaborations were small. Damon appeared in Marvel's *Thor: Ragnarok* in 2017 as an unnamed Asgardian actor playing Loki in a stage version of an earlier Thor story. He returned in *Thor: Love & Thunder*, directed by [Taika Waititi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taika_Waititi), with Hemsworth's brother, [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth), again playing an actor playing Thor.

Damon is also in the spotlight this year for his lead role in Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey*, which The Direct says could bring him another [Best Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actor) nomination. He has five Oscar nominations and one win, for co-writing [*Good Will Hunting*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Will_Hunting).

Footballer [Cristiano Ronaldo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cristiano_Ronaldo) is also involved as a producer, through the UR MARV production company he co-founded with Vaughn. There is no sign yet that he will appear on screen.

## What happens next

*Stuntnuts* had its first screening in November and is now on the market looking for a distributor. No worldwide release date has been set, so audiences, including those in Australia, will have to wait for a deal before they can see it.

Hemsworth's schedule is already full. The Direct lists *Avengers: Doomsday*, *Avengers: Secret Wars* and *Extraction 3* among his upcoming projects.
