**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) told the Los Angeles Times she sometimes tells studios a project "seems irresponsible to make".
- The interview came as her film Paper Tiger, with Adam Driver and Miles Teller, reached US theatres on 13 November.
- She is lined up for The Exorcist: Martyrs (March 2027), The Batman Part II (February 2028) and Ari Aster's Scapegoat.
- In the same interview she called herself the "CEO" of her household with husband Colin Jost.

Scarlett Johansson says she has no problem telling Hollywood studios when a film should not exist. In an interview with the Los Angeles Times published this week, the actress described how she weighs her roles, just as her new film Paper Tiger opened in US cinemas on 13 November.

## What Scarlett Johansson said about studio offers

Johansson framed her choices around the audience rather than herself. "Am I making something that other people want to see? Is there an audience for this movie? I'm not making a movie just for me," she told the paper, as quoted by [The A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/scarlett-johansson-irresponsible-studio-movie-offers).

She also pushes back on the people sending her scripts. "Sometimes I get studio projects that I have to say, 'I don't think you should make this. I don't know who you're making this for. It seems irresponsible to make this,'" she said. According to [Yahoo's Parade report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/scarlett-johansson-reveals-shes-rejected-180257123.html), she did not name any of the films she has turned down.

The comments carry weight because of her record. The A.V. Club notes that most of her box-office success comes from playing Natasha Romanoff, also known as Black Widow, in the Marvel films, including Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame. It adds that Jurassic World: Rebirth earned $869 million worldwide in 2025, which briefly made her the highest-grossing actor ever, before Tom Holland and Zoe Saldaña passed her.

## Scarlett Johansson Paper Tiger and what comes next

Paper Tiger puts Johansson opposite [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) and Miles Teller. She attended its premiere at the 64th New York Film Festival on 25 September, according to Parade. Australian release details were not in the sources, so check local listings.

Her calendar is full. Parade reports she spent most of 2026 on two big productions: The Exorcist: Martyrs, due in March 2027, and The Batman Part II, expected in February 2028. She is also preparing to film Scapegoat for A24, directed by Midsommar's Ari Aster. The A.V. Club says Jacob Elordi and Jude Law co-star.

Johansson told the paper she is still working out the story of Scapegoat, but the script grabbed her because it was unlike anything she had read. For more on that project, see our earlier post on [Scarlett Johansson and Ari Aster's Scapegoat](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-scapegoat/), and on the same interview, [Scarlett Johansson on bad studio offers and Paper Tiger](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-studio-offers/).

## A voice role in Netflix's Ray Gunn

Johansson also voices Venus Nova in Brad Bird's animated Netflix film Ray Gunn, which she discussed at a press conference in the last week of October. Speaking there, as reported by [Solzy at the Movies](https://solzyatthemovies.com/2026/11/12/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn/), she described Venus as a diva with a performative public voice and a more mysterious, intimate one when she talks to Ray.

She said she and Bird recorded facing each other in the booth, so his notes were specific and she reacted to his performance as well. Ray Gunn's cast also includes Sam Rockwell, Tom Waits and Patton Oswalt. Our post on the [Ray Gunn Netflix trailer](https://moduscarry.com/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn-trailer/) has more on the film.

## Life at home with Colin Jost

The same Los Angeles Times interview, dated Wednesday 11 November, touched on family life. As [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/scarlett-johansson-makes-candid-admission-230000813.html) reports, she called herself "the CEO of our household" and said she is a hands-on parent.

Johansson and [Colin Jost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Jost) of [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) married in October 2020. She has a daughter from a previous marriage and a son with Jost. She said the couple coordinate their schedules so the children's routines stay as normal as possible. In an earlier InStyle interview she said her children's anonymity is "very precious" to her, so we have not named them.
