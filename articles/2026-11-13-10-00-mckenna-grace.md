**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), 20, plays District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- Working with stylist Molly Dickson, she has worn a different franchise reference at most stops of the press tour, according to Yahoo.
- Looks so far nod to Katniss Everdeen, Lucy Gray Baird, Rue and Caesar Flickerman.
- Grace told Entertainment Tonight the outfits are a homage to the people in the franchise before her.

Mckenna Grace has turned the press run for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* into a game of spot-the-reference. Since late October, the 20-year-old, who plays Maysilee Donner, has stepped onto carpets from New York to Los Angeles in gowns that echo costumes from earlier films in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series, [Yahoo reports](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/article/mckenna-grace-is-the-new-girl-on-fire-a-look-at-her-hunger-games-tributes-during-the-sunrise-on-the-reaping-press-tour-170619176.html).

## Why Grace is dressing as the franchise

Yahoo says Grace has worked with stylist Molly Dickson to pay tribute to the stars who came before her, including [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence), [Rachel Zegler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zegler) and [Elizabeth Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Banks), while keeping her own point of view.

She explained the idea to [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight). Grace said she is a huge fan of the series and that fashion had felt hard to figure out while growing up, so playing with it this way has been fun. "Everything is a reference lately," she said, calling the looks a homage to everyone involved in the franchise before her.

## The Katniss looks

The most faithful nod so far came at a Los Angeles screening on 9 November. Yahoo says Grace wore a custom Elisabetta Franchi gown inspired by the Capitol party dress [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) wears in *The Hunger Games: [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire)*, with deep red feathers and fringed beadwork on the shoulders.

It was the second time she had reached for that dress. In late October in New York, she wore a Chloé design from fall/winter 2000 with pink sequined shoulder details that resembled wings.

Two other red carpets referenced Lawrence directly:

- **Berlin, 2 November:** a custom Cong Tri gown in sparkling black with flashes of red, orange and yellow, and a pleated skirt made to look engulfed by flames. Yahoo links it to the tribute-parade chariot scene in *Catching Fire*.
- **Madrid, 5 November:** a Miss Sohee spring 2024 couture gown with sculptural sleeves and three-dimensional floral appliqué. Yahoo says it echoes the beaded Givenchy gown Lawrence wore to the 2026 Golden Globes.

## Lucy Gray, Rue and Caesar

Further back on the tour, the references moved to other characters. On 28 October in London, Grace wore an archival Gucci fall 2016 gown by [Alessandro Michele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alessandro_Michele) with vibrant tiered ruffles. Yahoo says it recalls the dress Lucy Gray Baird, Zegler's character, wears for her performances in *The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes* (2023).

She changed for the London premiere the same day, choosing a handcrafted gown from Almée Couture's spring 2027 collection. The brand described it as adorned with blooming florals and colours inspired by nature. Grace told Entertainment Tonight she was "going for second Quarter Quell" and the arena, adding that she was "just missing a Cornucopia".

On 26 October, for an appearance on the *Today* show, she wore an archival Betsey Johnson satin minidress from spring 2015. Yahoo says the pale blue colour recalls the chiffon dress that Rue, played by [Amandla Stenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amandla_Stenberg), wears in her pre-games interview with Caesar Flickerman, played by [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci).

Flickerman's flamboyant, theatrical style inspired another outfit. Yahoo says Grace was spotted in Gucci in New York on 23 October with that host in mind.

## What happens next

The Yahoo list was still running when its text was captured, and Grace has not said which characters are left. Yahoo describes her as not finished yet with the method dressing, so more references are likely while the promotion continues.
