**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) reportedly drove Max Verstappen, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri to the podium ceremony after the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
- Malay Mail, citing The Guardian, says the limousine took the top three to the Bellagio on race night.
- Verstappen won by 0.639 seconds from Norris, with Piastri third.
- Reeves had already been seen in the paddock on Friday 20 November and again on Saturday 21 November.

The actor best known as John Wick has added a new job to his Las Vegas weekend: limousine driver. [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/showbiz/2026/11/22/john-wick-at-your-service-keanu-reeves-chauffeurs-f1-stars-verstappen-norris-and-piastri-to-las-vegas-podium/244315) reports that Reeves, 62, drove Formula One's top three finishers to their podium celebration at the Bellagio after the Las Vegas Grand Prix, a Keanu Reeves Las Vegas Grand Prix moment that has quickly become the talking point of the weekend.

## The chauffeur cameo

According to Malay Mail, Reeves took the wheel of the limousine carrying Verstappen, Norris and Piastri on race night. The outlet credits The Guardian for the account of him ferrying the podium finishers to the Bellagio. We could not open a second report that describes the drive in detail, so the finer points, such as how long the trip took, remain unconfirmed.

The cameo suited the occasion. The race itself was a tight one: the Red Bull driver crossed the line just 0.639 seconds ahead of the McLaren of Norris over 50 laps, with Piastri, Norris's teammate, completing the podium. In other words, the winner's ride to the party was far calmer than his drive to victory.

## A two-day paddock presence

The chauffeur duty was not Reeves's first appearance of the weekend. Speaks Media reports that he walked through the paddock on Friday afternoon, 20 November, and returned on Saturday, 21 November, when the race was held. Malay Mail adds that he was seen chatting with Verstappen in the paddock.

He was in busy company. Speaks Media lists Colin Farrell on Friday, and Chris Pratt, Anthony Mackie, Kim Kardashian and Mark Wahlberg among Saturday's celebrity guests. [Demokratis](https://demokratis.co.id/en/keanu-reeves-las-vegas-grand-prix), citing Sporting News, also mentions NFL quarterback Russell Wilson at the event.

We covered his first sighting at qualifying in [Keanu Reeves Spotted at the F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-las-vegas/).

## Not new to Formula One

Malay Mail describes Reeves as a longtime motorsport enthusiast and notes that he presented the documentary series *Brawn: The Impossible Formula 1 Story*, which looks back at the 2009 title win by the underdog Brawn GP team. Demokratis adds, citing an Esquire interview, that his love of riding goes back to childhood in Toronto, where he watched motorcycle groups gather in Yorkville each summer.

That background helps explain why the Las Vegas organisers, or the drivers, may have been happy to see him in the paddock. The race has become as much a celebrity showcase as a sporting event. Speaks Media notes that the fourth edition ran from 19 to 21 November on a 3.8-mile street circuit around the Strip, and that it has become one of the sport's most recognisable destination races.

## The wider weekend

The Grand Prix had its own storylines. Speaks Media reports that Verstappen took pole position after Friday's qualifying, that George Russell qualified second, and that Kimi Antonelli, who had already secured the 2026 drivers' championship, was due to start sixth. Demokratis, citing Sporting News, says Antonelli received a five-place grid penalty. Lewis Hamilton qualified outside the top 10.

## What happens next

Nothing further has been announced about Reeves's motorsport plans. The Formula One season has now moved past its Las Vegas round, so any repeat appearance would have to wait for another race weekend.
