**Quick summary**

- The Revenant, with [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) as the villain, leaves Netflix UK after 30 November, according to Digital Spy.
- The 2015 film earned 12 Oscar nominations, including one for Hardy as Best Supporting Actor.
- It holds a 78% critic score on Rotten Tomatoes from more than 400 reviews.
- Separately, ELLE is pointing viewers to London Road, a true-crime musical in which Hardy plays a taxi driver.

Viewers in the UK have about a week left to watch Tom Hardy's turn as a menacing fur trapper in The Revenant, which comes off Netflix UK after 30 November, [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) reports. A second British outlet, ELLE, has meanwhile put another Hardy film, London Road, back in front of readers as a streaming pick.

## What The Revenant is about

Set in the 1823 Dakota territories, The Revenant follows frontiersman [Hugh Glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Glass) as he tries to survive a brutal winter. Leonardo DiCaprio plays Glass. Hardy plays John Fitzgerald, a fellow fur trapper who becomes Glass's nemesis, and the two end up in a violent fight for survival, according to Digital Spy.

Alejandro González Iñárritu directed the film and co-wrote it with Mark L Smith. Digital Spy describes it as inspired by a real story.

## The awards record

The film was nominated for 12 Oscars, including Best Picture and Best Supporting Actor for Hardy. It won DiCaprio his first Academy Award for Best Lead Actor. Iñárritu took home his second Best Director award, following Birdman in 2014, and cinematographer Emmanuel "Chivo" Lubezki earned a third win, per Digital Spy.

## What critics said

On Rotten Tomatoes the film is Certified Fresh, with a 78% critic score across more than 400 reviews, Digital Spy notes. The outlet gathered several reactions:

- The Financial Times called the locations in Argentina, Canada and the US, along with Lubezki's camerawork, "wow-inducing".
- The Observer described DiCaprio's performance as "barnstorming" and Glass's quest as a "Herzogian odyssey".
- Detroit News wrote that the film is "both draining and rewarding", and Rolling Stone called it a "brutal, badass epic".
- The Arizona Republic said Iñárritu was aiming high and never lets viewers forget the story is based on true events.

## Hardy in London Road

ELLE has also highlighted London Road, a film about the community affected by the 2006 Ipswich murders, in which five women were killed by Steve Wright. It is based on the National Theatre musical first staged in 2011. Rather than focusing on the killer, it follows the residents of the street.

The dialogue comes from interviews with people who lived there. Writer Alecky Blythe and composer Adam Cork set those speech patterns, hesitations included, to music. ELLE quotes The Guardian calling it "an addictive forensic thriller set to music".

Olivia Colman plays Julie, a resident who helps organise a neighbourhood event. Hardy has a smaller part as Mark, a taxi driver. Anita Dobson, Kate Fleetwood and Claire Moore round out the ensemble. ELLE says the film can be rented on Prime Video and Apple TV, and notes that Hardy and Colman also appeared in Steven Knight's 2013 drama Locke.

## What happens next

The Revenant stays on Netflix UK until 30 November, so the window to watch it there is short. Availability can differ by country, and Digital Spy's report refers only to the UK catalogue. London Road remains a rental on the platforms ELLE names.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does The Revenant leave Netflix UK?

According to Digital Spy, 30 November is the last day to watch it on Netflix UK.

### Was Tom Hardy nominated for an Oscar for The Revenant?

Yes. Hardy was nominated for Best Supporting Actor for playing John Fitzgerald. The film received 12 nominations in total.

### Who does Tom Hardy play in London Road?

Hardy plays Mark, a taxi driver, in a smaller role. Olivia Colman has the larger part of Julie, a resident of the street.
