**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) joked at the global premiere of season six of Only Murders in the Building that the King and Queen had already "declined" a guest role.
- Season six takes the show to London for the first time and premieres on Disney+ on December 8, according to the Press Association.
- The cast includes Olivia Colman, Geri Halliwell-Horner, Martin Freeman, David Tennant and Nicola Coughlan.
- A separate Deadline video headline says the stars want season seven to return to New York.

Martin Short has a guest-star wish list for [Only Murders in the Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Murders_in_the_Building), and it starts at Buckingham Palace. Speaking at the global premiere of season six, the 76-year-old said he would like the King and Queen to appear, then quipped that they had already said no. The new season, set largely in London, reaches Disney+ on December 8.

## What Short said about the royal cameo

The comments came in an interview with the Press Association, reported by [Express and Star](https://www.expressandstar.com/whats-on/showbiz/martin-short-jokes-king-and-queen-declined-only-murders-guest-roles-9369563) and [The Independent](https://www.the-independent.com/arts-entertainment/tv/only-murders-in-the-building-charles-camilla-martin-short-b3090217.html). Asked who he would like to see on the show in future, Short answered: "I'd like the King and Queen."

He then supplied the punchline himself. "They wanted script approval," he said. A line was added, which the outlets attribute to the interview, that the royals were "busy helping others". The Independent's version has the pair joking that the royals had "declined their call". It is a joke, not a report that anyone approached the palace.

## Why London matters for Only Murders

Season six is the first time the Emmy-winning comedy leaves its New York setting. Short plays one of three neighbours, alongside [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) and Selena Gomez, who investigate killings and record a true-crime podcast about them.

Short told the Press Association that filming overseas "certainly was fun". He praised the city's architecture and said the production worked with "the greatest British actors imaginable". Pressed for a favourite guest from the new run, he declined to pick one, saying "everyone was a delight".

Short also co-created the series with John Hoffman, who executive produces with Short and Gomez, according to both reports.

## The guest list so far

British names fill out the new season. The reports list Olivia Colman, Geri Halliwell-Horner, Martin Freeman and David Tennant. Irish actor Nicola Coughlan, known for Bridgerton, plays Gemma Joyce, described as an up-and-coming fashion designer who is falsely accused of murder.

Her Bridgerton co-star Simone Ashley also appears. Coughlan said the two filmed very little together and shared one scene where their storylines overlap. "It's always really nice to be in such big ensemble cast," she added.

The show already has a record of star cameos. Earlier seasons featured Oscar winners Meryl Streep, Da'Vine Joy Randolph and Renee Zellweger, along with Paul Rudd, Shirley MacLaine and Tina Fey. For readers who follow the pair's off-screen bond, we also looked at [a real friendship ritual written into the show](https://moduscarry.com/martin-short-only-murders-easter-egg/).

## A role Short nearly played

In a separate story, Far Out Magazine revisited a piece of Short trivia. In February 1989 it was announced that he would play the title role in an adaptation of F Scott Fitzgerald's The Curious Case of Benjamin Button, with [Frank Oz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Oz) directing and production pencilled in for April that year.

That version never went ahead. The film that finally reached cinemas had [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) in the lead and [David Fincher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fincher) directing. According to Far Out, it earned more than US$335 million at the box office and received 13 Academy Award nominations, winning three, for art direction, makeup and visual effects. Almost two decades separate the 1989 announcement from that release.

## What happens next

Only Murders in the Building season six premieres on Disney+ on December 8. Beyond that, Deadline shared a video whose headline quotes Short and Martin saying they are open to filming in new cities but that "right now, our goal is to head back to New York City" for season seven. We could not read the full video, so treat that as a headline-level detail until more is published.

### Did the King and Queen turn down Only Murders in the Building?

No evidence suggests they were asked. Short was joking at the season six premiere, saying they "wanted script approval" and were "busy helping others".

### When does Only Murders in the Building season six start?

It premieres on Disney+ on December 8, according to the Press Association reports carried by Express and Star and The Independent.
