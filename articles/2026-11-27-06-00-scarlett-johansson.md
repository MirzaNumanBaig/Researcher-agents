**Quick summary**

- Jurassic World Rebirth, Scarlett Johansson's 2025 dinosaur film, leaves Netflix on 26 December, according to ScreenRant.
- The film earned more than US$869 million worldwide.
- Johansson also voices pop star Venus Nova in the Netflix animation Ray Gunn, directed by Brad Bird.
- Netflix has submitted the film's song "Too Much to Ask" for Best Original Song, and Johansson says she is "way, way underprepared" to sing it at the Oscars.

Two pieces of Scarlett Johansson news landed on the same day, and both involve Netflix. Her blockbuster [Jurassic World Rebirth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World_Rebirth) has a departure date from the streamer, while her animated film Ray Gunn is edging into awards season. Here is what [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) fans need to know.

## When does Jurassic World Rebirth leave Netflix?

[ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/scarlett-johansson-jurassic-world-rebirth-leave-netflix-december-2026/) that Jurassic World Rebirth will be removed from Netflix on 26 December. The film opened in July 2025 and, per the same report, has taken more than US$869 million at the global box office.

That figure matters for context. ScreenRant argues the film restored faith in the Jurassic brand after two weaker sequels, calling it the strongest entry since [Jurassic World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_World), though it does not rate it above the 1993 original, [Jurassic Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jurassic_Park). That is the outlet's opinion rather than a measured result.

Viewers who want to catch it have until Boxing Day. Netflix catalogues can differ between countries, so Australian viewers should check their own listings.

## Johansson's role in the Jurassic sequel

Johansson plays Zora Bennett, who is sent to a dinosaur-infested island to collect DNA from three huge creatures. The sample could lead to a new drug. The cast also includes [Jonathan Bailey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Bailey) and [Mahershala Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahershala_Ali).

ScreenRant notes that the film's plot is deliberately simple and that it brought in new creatures, including the Aquilops and a mutated Distortus Rex. It also singles out a river scene with a Tyrannosaurus Rex that comes from Michael Crichton's original novel.

Johansson is also known for her long run in the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) as Black Widow, a character who first appeared in 2010's Iron Man 2. She has two Oscar nominations, for Jojo Rabbit and Marriage Story.

## A sequel is in development

According to ScreenRant, a follow-up to Rebirth is being written by David Koepp, with Johansson, Bailey and Ali all expected to return. No release date has been confirmed. The same report says Gareth Edwards has recently left the director's chair, so the project still has open questions.

## Ray Gunn and a possible Oscar song

The second story comes from an interview with [Collider](https://collider.com/scarlett-johansson-ray-gunn-oscar-too-much-to-ask-song-brad-bird-interview/), where Johansson and director Brad Bird talked about [Ray Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn). Collider describes the Netflix sci-fi noir as thirty years in the making. It is one of a record 38 animated features eligible for Oscar consideration this year.

Sam Rockwell voices the title private detective, with Tom Waits as his partner Eyera. They are hired to protect global pop star Venus Nova, voiced by Johansson, from her own reckless behaviour, and Collider says the job leads Ray to a darker mission.

Asked what she would do if the film's song "Too Much to Ask", which Netflix has submitted for Best Original Song, earned a nomination, Johansson laughed and said nobody had raised it before. "I'm definitely not prepared," she said. "Underprepared. Way, way underprepared."

## What Johansson says about fame

Johansson told Collider she can relate to Venus Nova's struggle, but she does not claim to know life as a global pop star. In her view, an actor always has the screen between themselves and the audience. A singer facing 30,000 people carries a heavier burden, she said.

Bird used the interview to argue that Hollywood rarely backs adult-oriented animation with big budgets. He said the West has embraced it less than Japan has.
