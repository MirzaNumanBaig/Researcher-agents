**Quick summary**

- Reviews for *Ebenezer*, [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s first major studio lead in years, landed on 12 November, ahead of its opening this Friday.
- [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) showed about 51 per cent positive, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Critics are sharply divided on Depp's Scrooge, but the supporting cast and the film's atmosphere are drawing more consistent praise.
- The film is directed by [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West) and is backed by Paramount, which is hoping it restores Depp's standing.

The first wave of *Ebenezer* reviews is out, and they split almost down the middle. Johnny Depp's turn as Scrooge in Ti West's horror-tinged take on *A Christmas Carol* has been called both a welcome comeback and a scenery-chewing misstep, days before the film opens on Friday.

## Where the scores stand

According to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/ebenezer-reviews-johnny-depp-1236754299/), the film held a 51 per cent positive score on Rotten Tomatoes when its roundup ran, and even the favourable notices were tempered. [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/knew-ebenezer-reviews-would-focus-johnny-depp-didnt-see-muppets-comparison-coming) put the figure at around 50 per cent fresh.

The Hollywood Reporter noted that nearly every critic was circling the same question: how to feel about Depp leading a major studio film again. It also suggested the audience score could prove noticeably higher than the critics' number, since reviewers may feel differently about the actor than fans do. That is the outlet's speculation, not a reported result.

## What the critics liked

Not everyone was unkind. The Guardian called Depp "rather good", praising his bad-tempered, petulant take on the unredeemed Scrooge, though it felt the redeemed version at the end lacked pathos. Empire's Helen O'Hara wrote that he leans into the prosthetics and sneers for all he is worth, while Deadline's Pete Hammond said he finds more humanity in the character than earlier versions did, according to CinemaBlend.

[Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/reviews/ebenezer-review-johnny-depp-1236945154/) called him a "perfectly solid, by-the-book Scrooge" with occasional moments of ripe inspiration. It described the film as spookily atmospheric but redundant, and credited production designer Eve Stewart's dank re-creation of Dickensian London.

## What the critics did not

The harsher notices focused on Depp himself. Vulture called him the worst part of the film, while The New York Times said he appears to be playing an agreeable version of himself and compared the result to a seasonal advertising campaign. The Boston Globe went furthest, calling it the worst Scrooge the reviewer had seen, as quoted by The Hollywood Reporter.

Comparisons to earlier versions ran through the coverage. The Times of London placed the 1951 Alastair Sim film, the 1988 Bill Murray comedy and the 1992 Muppet musical well above this one. The Independent said Depp's "unmediated shtick" cheapens the story, and Consequence went as far as saying he was acting like a Muppet in a film that already has a famous Muppet version, according to CinemaBlend.

## The cast and the context

The supporting cast is the film's safest ground. [Rupert Grint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rupert_Grint) plays Bob Cratchit, [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) is Jacob Marley, [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough) is the Ghost of Christmas Past, [Tramell Tillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramell_Tillman) plays the Ghost of Christmas Present and [Daisy Ridley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daisy_Ridley) appears as Clara, with Sam Claflin as Fred. Variety singled out Riseborough's unsettling ghost, and several reviewers praised her.

Variety also framed the stakes plainly: Depp returns to studio cinema after eight years on what it called the A-list blacklist, and the film is a PG-13 release that stops short of outright horror. CinemaBlend said he took a big pay cut for the role.

For a sense of what a divided reception can mean, CinemaBlend pointed to Depp's own record: *Alice in Wonderland* was dismissed by critics but earned more than a billion dollars, while *Alice Through the Looking Glass* was widely disliked.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Rotten Tomatoes score for Ebenezer?
It was about 51 per cent positive when The Hollywood Reporter published its roundup, and around 50 per cent according to CinemaBlend. Scores usually move as more reviews arrive.

### Who directed Ebenezer?
Ti West, known for horror films such as *X* and *Pearl*, directed it from a script by Nathaniel Halpern, according to Variety.

### Is Ebenezer a horror film?
Not entirely. Variety describes it as a PG-13 release with horror styling that stays faithful to Dickens' redemptive story, and says it may be too frightening for many children.
