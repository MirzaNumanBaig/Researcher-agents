**Quick summary**

- The [Street Fighter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Fighter_(2026_film)) film is playing in 325 US cinemas in its sixth weekend, 489 fewer than a week earlier, according to ScreenRant.
- It has passed $100 million worldwide, a franchise high, but its reported budget is $82 million.
- Box Office Mojo figures cited by Koimoi put its North American total at $59.3 million.
- [Godzilla Minus Zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla_Minus_Zero) sits about $8 million behind that domestic figure after 14 days.

Street Fighter is fading from American cinemas quickly. In its sixth weekend, the video game adaptation is on 325 screens across the United States, down 489 from the weekend before, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/street-fighter-2026-box-office-theater-removal-week-6/), which cites figures from The Numbers. That is a loss of roughly 60 per cent of the cinemas that still carried it, and it comes just as a rival import closes in on its domestic total.

## A franchise record with a short run

The film opened on October 16, with Noah Centineo as Ken Masters and Andrew Koji as Ryu. The cast also includes Callina Liang, Don Lee, 50 Cent, Roman Reigns, Cody Rhodes, David Dastmalchian, Orville Peck and Jason Momoa. ScreenRant reports that its opening beat the debuts of the two earlier live-action films combined.

Its worldwide gross has since reached about $101 million, which makes it the highest earner in the franchise before adjusting for inflation. The film's Wikipedia entry lists a similar figure of roughly $100.8 million.

The screen count tells a different story. ScreenRant says the film lost 750 screens in its third weekend, then 1,065, then 946, before this latest cut of 489. For comparison, the Elizabeth Holmes documentary You Can See Everything, which opened the same weekend, is still on 1,252 screens. Our earlier report on [Callina Liang's Street Fighter passing $100M](https://moduscarry.com/callina-liang-street-fighter-box-office/) covered the first signs of that squeeze.

## The budget problem

The reported budget is $82 million, the highest in the series. That puts the $101 million worldwide total at about 1.2 times the cost of making the film. ScreenRant points out that Paramount Pictures is only responsible for distribution costs, so the studio probably saw a healthy return when the film crossed $100 million. The outlet adds that the film is still well short of its break-even point and may need $200 million or more to count as a success.

Critics have not helped. ScreenRant reports a 42 per cent critics score on Rotten Tomatoes against 78 per cent from audiences. The film's Wikipedia page quotes Metacritic at 43 out of 100 from 28 critics, and summarises the critical view as affection for the games and plenty of style, undercut by weak storytelling. A sequel was teased in the ending, but ScreenRant says the numbers make that doubtful. Home video, video on demand and streaming could still narrow the gap.

## Godzilla Minus Zero is catching up

Pressure is also coming from Japan. Koimoi and Zoom TV report that Godzilla Minus Zero, released on November 6, had collected $51.9 million in North America after 14 days. Its second Thursday brought in $383,000, down 73.3 per cent on the previous Thursday.

Using Box Office Mojo's $59.3 million figure for Street Fighter, the gap is about $8 million. Wisevoter puts the Japanese film's worldwide total at $107 million, including $55.1 million overseas, which means it is already ahead of Street Fighter globally. Both outlets note that it ranks among the top 10 highest-grossing foreign-language films in North America. The R-rated sequel is a story about postwar Japan rebuilding itself, which is a long way from a tournament fighting film.

## A sad note from the games world

Separately, the wider Street Fighter family has lost a creator. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/street-fighter-ex-endless-ocean-and-tetris-99s-akira-kurabayashi-has-died-at-56-2000759170) reports that developer [Akira Kurabayashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Kurabayashi) died of heart failure on November 14 at the age of 56. He worked at studio Arika on the 3D Street Fighter EX games, the Tetris: The Grand Master series, Tetris 99 and the Endless Ocean games. Arika president Akira Nishitani said a memorial is being arranged with the family.

## What happens next

Street Fighter's domestic total is the number to watch. If Godzilla Minus Zero holds even modestly, it should pass $59.3 million in the coming weeks. How the film does on home video and streaming will decide whether the sequel tease goes anywhere.
