**Quick summary**

- [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) voices a character called "Richard Bastion" on the radio station WKTT in Grand Theft Auto 6, according to the game's credits.
- Rockstar Games published the full credits after the game's launch on 19 November 2026, according to AXS TV.
- Other newly confirmed radio names include Keith Richards, Lindsey Buckingham and Gloria Estefan.
- Separately, Apple TV has renewed *Ted Lasso* for a fifth season, with no dates announced yet.

Jason Sudeikis has turned up in an unexpected place this week: the radio dial of Grand Theft Auto 6. The credits Rockstar Games released after the game's 19 November launch list the actor as the voice of "Richard Bastion" on WKTT, according to [AXS TV](https://axs.tv/news-story/gta-6-radio-host-lineup-features-keith-richards-lindsey-buckingham-jason-sudeikis-and-more/).

## What the credits say about Sudeikis

AXS TV reports that Sudeikis shares WKTT with Stavros Emmanuel Halkias, who plays "Dominic 'The Brick' Brizzolato". The credits do not, in the coverage we could read, say what kind of programming WKTT carries, and nothing in the sources describes how large or small the role is. For now the confirmed facts are the character name, the station and his co-host.

It is a notable credit for a performer best known for *Saturday Night Live* and for playing the title role in *[Ted Lasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ted_Lasso)*. Neither Sudeikis nor Rockstar had commented in the material we read.

## A radio lineup that mixes music and comedy

The wider list is long. AXS TV says the credits mix musicians, actors and comedians, and add new names to hosts announced before launch.

- [Keith Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Richards) appears as "DJ Keith" on Gloriana Blues Radio, next to blues veteran [Buddy Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buddy_Guy) as "DJ Buddy". Richards also contributes his own take on Jimmy Reed's "Bright Lights, Big City".
- [Lindsey Buckingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsey_Buckingham) is "The Buck" on the classic rock station V-Rock, paired with Michael McDonald as "Skipper McD".
- [Gloria Estefan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloria_Estefan) hosts Radio La Ola with her husband Emilio Estefan.
- [Bad Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Bunny) voices "El Martillo" on Cocoteo FM, and [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey) plays "Delta Dawne" on Back Country Radio. Both roles were confirmed before release.

The game also carries five episodic podcasts with guest voices, including Chloe Fineman and Sarah Sherman, who host a show of their own, and rapper Freddie Gibbs.

## Where Sudeikis stands on Ted Lasso

The GTA credit lands in the same week as the renewal of his best-known role. Apple TV has confirmed a fifth season of *Ted Lasso*, according to [Diario AS's MeriStation](https://en.as.com/meristation/news/its-official-ted-lasso-will-have-a-fifth-season-and-jason-sudeikis-will-once-again-lead-afc-richmond-f202611-n/), which says no production or premiere dates have been announced. The outlet notes talk of filming starting in April 2027, but that is speculation rather than an official schedule.
