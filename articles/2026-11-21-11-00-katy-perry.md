**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) and former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) were photographed leaving the Ritz in Paris on 19 November.
- The pair spent the week in Europe, with reported stops in Barcelona and Milan before Paris.
- A gossip account claimed a December wedding, but the claim is unverified and no representative has confirmed it.
- Separately, unsealed court papers about Perry's ex-husband Russell Brand have been reported by Page Six and Yahoo.

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau have spent the past week travelling across Europe together, and on 19 November they were photographed outside the Ritz hotel in Paris. According to [HOLA](https://www.hola.com/us/celebrities/20261120936388/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-paris-ritz-hotel/), Perry, 42, wore a brown faux-fur jacket with oversized cuffs, a matching beret and dark sunglasses.

## Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau in Paris: what the photos show

HOLA describes Trudeau in a grey jacket over a navy sweater, with a black baseball cap and sunglasses. Yahoo Canada's account differs slightly on the outfits, which is common with paparazzi shots, but both agree on the location and the day.

The Paris sighting came a few days after a rare red carpet outing in West Hollywood, according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-were-174223568.html). The couple then crossed the Atlantic. That same report places them at Atresmedia's Metafuturo conference in Barcelona on 17 November, where Trudeau was a speaker.

## A week across three countries

Photographers also caught them leaving the Four Seasons hotel in Milan on the same day as the Paris sighting, [Yahoo Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/are-katy-perry-and-justin-trudeau-getting-married-rumours-swirl-as-the-couple-look-loved-up-in-paris--but-theres-a-catch-192243659.html) reports. On Friday Trudeau posted a set of photos on social media captioned "Back on the road", thanking people he met in Barcelona, Milan and Paris. The first image was a selfie Perry took with Trudeau and French President Emmanuel Macron.

The same post included pictures of Trudeau speaking on stage and meeting politicians, including former Italian prime minister Paolo Gentiloni and the president of the Government of Catalonia, Salvador Illa. It is a reminder that this was a working trip for him as much as a getaway.

## How Perry has described the relationship

Perry has spoken warmly about Trudeau in recent months. In a September interview with People, quoted by Yahoo, she called him the love of her life and said the Lifetimes tour had brought her that "greatest gift". HOLA notes she told Entertainment Tonight in August that he supports her "on a different level" from past partners, and that "Like the Movies" is his favourite of her songs.

Earlier this month she shared that they spent her 42nd birthday together on a separate holiday. Yahoo Canada reports they began dating in the summer of 2025, after Perry's split from her ex-fiancé Orlando Bloom. HOLA adds that Perry and Bloom had a daughter, Daisy Dove, in August 2020.

## The wedding rumour

Some outlets have asked whether a wedding is coming. The talk traces back to the gossip account DeuxMoi, which posted the Paris photos along with an anonymous message from someone who said they overheard a stranger at a New York bar mention "Katy Perry's wedding in December".

Yahoo Canada is careful to point out that DeuxMoi shares largely unverified, crowdsourced tips, and that the account itself joked about how many people share the singer's name. There is also a legal question. Trudeau and Sophie Grégoire Trudeau married in 2005 and announced their separation in August 2023, and Yahoo Canada says no official update on a divorce has been reported. Neither Perry nor Trudeau has announced engagement or wedding plans.

## Brand court papers, briefly

Perry's name has also surfaced in coverage of [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand). Page Six and Yahoo report that newly unsealed deposition transcripts from a New York civil case, first obtained by the Daily Mail, show Brand could not recall if he was married to Perry when an alleged incident occurred in July 2010. Perry and Brand wed that October and divorced in 2012. Perry is not accused of anything and is not a party to the case. Brand was recently convicted in the UK and has maintained his innocence. For earlier coverage of that marriage, see our related post, [Katy Perry Reportedly Weighs Book and Series on Brand Marriage](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-book-series-report/).

## What happens next

Neither Perry nor Trudeau has said where they head next. Perry has not yet posted her own pictures from the Europe trip, according to Yahoo. ## Frequently asked questions

### Where were Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau photographed?

They were photographed leaving the Ritz hotel in Paris on 19 November. Earlier that day they were seen leaving the Four Seasons in Milan, and they attended a conference in Barcelona on 17 November.

### Are Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau getting married?

There is no confirmation. The December wedding claim comes from an anonymous message shared by a gossip account, and Yahoo Canada says such tips are largely unverified.

### When did Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau start dating?

Yahoo Canada reports they began dating in the summer of 2025, shortly after Perry ended her engagement to Orlando Bloom.
