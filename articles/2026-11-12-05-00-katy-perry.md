**Quick summary**

- Russell Brand, the ex-husband of [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry), was sentenced this week after being convicted of rape and sexual assault.
- Old comments from Perry about the marriage, including a 2013 Vogue interview, are circulating again online.
- At a July concert at Blenheim Palace she joked that an unnamed ex "should be in jail", according to The Mirror. She did not name Brand.
- Perry has not publicly commented on the verdict. A source told People she is focused on her relationship with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau).

Katy Perry has said nothing in public about Russell Brand's sentencing, but her past words about the marriage are doing the talking for her. Over the past few days, clips and quotes from her years with the comedian have resurfaced, according to [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/katy-perrys-comment-ex-should-jail-resurfaces-after-russell-brands-prison-sentencing), after Brand was sentenced for rape and sexual assault convictions in the UK.

For readers who want the wider entertainment picture, our [Celebrities](https://moduscarry.com/category/celebrities/) and [Music](https://moduscarry.com/category/music/) sections follow the story as it develops.

## What Perry has said about the marriage before

Perry and Brand met while filming *Get Him to the Greek* in 2009, according to [Mezha.net](https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-2db6abdf8835fa78a28d6059_katy_perry-s_-he/). They announced their engagement in January 2010 and married in India on 23 October that year. The marriage lasted about 14 months, and Brand filed for divorce in December 2011.

In a 2013 Vogue interview, Perry described his behaviour during the marriage as "controlling". She said he disliked "the atmosphere of me being the boss on tour", and called the experience hurtful and upsetting. She also said he told her by text message that he wanted a divorce. That moment was filmed and appeared in her 2012 documentary *Katy Perry: Part of Me*.

Our earlier post on [Katy Perry's 2013 Russell Brand comments](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-russell-brand-comments/) covers the interview in more detail.

## The Blenheim Palace remark

The line drawing the most attention came from a concert this year. During an audience game about reacting to seeing an ex, Perry said at Blenheim Palace in Oxfordshire in July: "I hope my ex isn't here. He should be in jail," The Mirror reported, as cited by Fox News.

Two points matter here. Perry did not name Brand, and the remark came months before his conviction. Any link between the joke and Brand is made by commentators, not by Perry.

Another clip is also back in circulation: a 2010 Vanity Fair Oscar party interview in which Brand appeared to criticise Perry for swearing on camera and showed a badge reading "Obey". One social media user wrote on X that he "didn't like her getting more attention than him", a comment that reflects online opinion rather than established fact.

## A source says Perry is looking ahead

According to a [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/katy-perry-focuses-future-justin-224944096.html) report originally published by InTouch Weekly, Brand was found guilty on 5 November of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. The report says Perry has stayed silent since.

An unnamed source told People that Perry "thought she knew the person she married" and realised afterwards that he was not who she believed. The same source said the pair have not spoken since the December 2011 divorce text, and that she is grateful for her career, loves raising her daughter and is happy with Trudeau.

Perry and the former Canadian prime minister first drew romance rumours in July 2025, when they were seen at dinner during the Montreal stop of her Lifetimes Tour, according to the same report. The report says things turned serious that October, and that she confirmed the relationship at a concert days later, telling a fan who proposed that she was "dating someone else". The comments about her state of mind come from an anonymous source, not from Perry or her team.

## Other voices are coming forward

Brand's past is also under fresh scrutiny from others. After the sentencing, journalist and producer [Jemima Goldsmith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jemima_Goldsmith), who dated Brand from 2013 to 2014, accused him in a statement on X of "violence, coercive control and abuse", Mezha.net reported. She said she had called police to remove him from her home during a violent incident in 2014, and chose not to pursue charges or speak out at the time because she feared the media attention would affect her teenage children. Brand's side of that allegation is not given in the sources reviewed.

## What happens next

None of the sources reviewed reports a statement from Perry or her representatives, so the next development is whether she chooses to speak. Further detail on the sentencing itself will come from outlets covering the UK case.

For now, the verifiable record is the one Perry created herself: a 2013 interview, a 2012 documentary and a July concert joke. Everything else is commentary.
