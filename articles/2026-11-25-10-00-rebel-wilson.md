**Quick summary**

- [Rebel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Wilson) posted an Instagram Stories video on 24 November showing her having a Xerf skin treatment.
- She said she had the session at Harley Street Injectables in London, along with a light-based treatment she called "BBL".
- Xerf is a non-invasive, radio-frequency treatment that aims to boost collagen and elastin in the skin.
- Wilson said she expected to be "looking beautiful" in about a week.

## Rebel Wilson shares her Xerf session

Rebel Wilson has given her followers a look at a cosmetic skin treatment, posting a behind-the-scenes video to her [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/rebelwilson/) Stories on 24 November. According to [AOL.com](https://www.aol.com/articles/rebel-wilson-shares-behind-scenes-174832000.html), which carried a report first published by People, the 46-year-old actress was filmed during a Xerf session after a visit to Harley Street Injectables in London.

Wilson also said she had "BBL" done at the same time. The AOL report, citing Good Housekeeping, explains that term as the Broad Band Light category of treatments. Her own verdict on the results was upbeat but patient: "In about a week I'll be looking beautiful," she said in the video.

## What Xerf actually does

The AOL piece quotes dermatologist Dr Suchismita (Tia) Paul, speaking to Women's Health, who describes Xerf as using radio frequency to deliver controlled heat into the deeper layers of the skin. The aim is to encourage the body to produce more collagen and elastin, which is why it is marketed as skin tightening without surgery.

Nothing in the report suggests Wilson has described any side effects or specific results beyond her own comment about the coming week. As with any cosmetic procedure, anyone considering it should speak to a qualified practitioner.

## A treatment that keeps turning up in celebrity feeds

Xerf has become a talking point in recent weeks. The report notes that interest rose after [Barry Keoghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Keoghan) was shown last month having the treatment with Dermaspa founder Nilam Holmes. In a clip posted to Holmes's page, Keoghan said he found each visit relaxing and that he felt better about himself afterwards.

AOL, citing Elle, adds that [Priyanka Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra), [Martha Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martha_Stewart) and Cardi B are among the other well-known names to have tried the laser. That is a short list, but it helps explain why a single Instagram Story from Wilson was enough to make news.

## Wilson's public approach to her appearance

The Australian star of [Pitch Perfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitch_Perfect) has spoken openly about her looks and health in recent years, particularly since what she called her "year of health" in 2020. In a March Instagram post, she said she had accepted that she would never have a "bikini body" and added that her body had carried her through life and she was grateful for it, according to the same report.

She has also shared parts of her routine online, including daily steps, a Pilates class once or twice a week, some strength training and trying to eat as healthily as she can. That candour is part of why her posts tend to travel quickly.
