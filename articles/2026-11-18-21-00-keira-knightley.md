**Quick summary**

- [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) season 2 climbed from fourth to second place on Netflix's chart in its second week, according to Comic Book Resources.
- It drew 6.6 million views that week, more than 50% fewer than season 1's second week.
- Season 1 reached No. 1 in its second week with 14.6 million views.
- Netflix has not yet decided whether the show will return, and creator Joe Barton says a writers' room is hoping.

Season 2 of [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley)'s spy thriller Black Doves moved up to No. 2 on Netflix's weekly chart in its second week, but with 6.6 million views it still trails its first season by a wide margin, [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) reports.

## How the two seasons compare

The first season arrived in early December 2024 and entered the chart in second place with 10.8 million views and 59.4 million hours viewed. A week later it reached No. 1 with 14.6 million views and 80.1 million hours viewed, according to the report, which cites Netflix's Tudum figures.

Season 2 premiered on 5 November, almost two years later. Its first week brought 3.8 million views and 19.9 million hours viewed, good for fourth place behind Taken: Natalee Holloway, The One About Matthew Perry and Nobody Wants This.

In the second week it rose to No. 2, behind only Taken: Natalee Holloway. The 6.6 million views that week are more than 50% below the 14.6 million season 1 recorded in the same stage of its run.

## Reviews have improved even as viewing fell

The drop is not tied to poor reception. CBR notes that the series holds a 94% critics' score overall and 66% from audiences. Season 2 is rated [Certified Fresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) at 96% from critics and 67% from audiences, ahead of season 1's 92% and 64%.

CBR places the numbers inside a wider Netflix pattern. It points to a Bloomberg report earlier this year on viewers leaving shows after a single season, and says Beef lost more than 70% of its audience in season 2, while The Night Agent lost 50% in its second season and a further 35% in its third. The outlet also notes that [Squid Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid_Game) and [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things) have both finished, which leaves the service leaning on titles such as Bridgerton and Wednesday.

## What the show is about

Created by Joe Barton, the series follows Helen Webb, the wife of the UK's Secretary of State for Defence. She also works for the Black Doves, a secret society that extracts state secrets and sells them to the highest bidder. In season 2, CBR says, the stakes rise as her husband, Wallace, prepares to become Prime Minister.

## What happens next

Netflix has not announced a decision on a third season. CBR quotes Barton telling Variety: "I'm in a writers' room for it at the moment, and we're hoping." Netflix usually weighs viewing across a longer window than two weeks, so the chart positions in the coming weeks will matter, though the company has not said how it will decide.
