**Quick summary**

- Cody Rhodes says he has another acting project lined up for this year, and it has not been announced.
- He made the comments on Stephanie McMahon's "What's Your Story" podcast, as reported by Wrestlezone.
- Rhodes played Guile in the 2026 Street Fighter film, which opened on 12 October.
- The film's box office and reviews have been modest so far, according to Wikipedia's recorded figures.

Wrestler [Cody Rhodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Rhodes) has hinted that his Hollywood run is not over. After playing Guile in the 2026 Street Fighter film, he told a podcast this week that another acting project is set for this year, with an announcement hoped for soon.

## What Cody Rhodes said about his next project

Speaking on Stephanie McMahon's "What's Your Story" podcast, Rhodes described the project as an "unannounced fun project", according to [Wrestlezone](https://www.wrestlezone.com/news/1675335-cody-rhodes-new-unannounced-acting-project). He gave no title, no format and no release window.

He also drew a line between the work and his day job. By his account, it is not wrestling, "but it is" storytelling. That framing runs through the whole interview as reported.

Rhodes also said he had just signed his "final deal", and that he was excited about telling stories in the acting space. Wrestlezone does not say what the deal covers beyond his quote, so it is best read as his own description rather than a confirmed contract detail.

## Why acting felt familiar on the Street Fighter set

The most useful part of the interview is how Rhodes compared the two crafts. He said acting and pro wrestling share the same need for nuance and for choices about a character.

On the Street Fighter set, he said, he kept asking the questions he would ask when building a wrestling storyline. Where is this moment in the script or the canon? Has this part of the story developed yet? Why does a character act this way?

That last question matters with a video game adaptation. Guile is a long-standing character from the fighting game series, and the film's fans arrive with strong views on how the characters should behave. Rhodes said the overlap made the experience more enjoyable for him.

Wrestlezone adds that Rhodes has said in earlier comments that wrestling remains his main priority.

## How the Street Fighter film has fared

The film, directed by Kitao Sakurai, was released on 12 October 2026. Its cast includes Andrew Koji, Noah Centineo, Callina Liang, Orville Peck and Rhodes, according to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_Fighter_(2026_film)).

The recorded figures point to a soft result. Wikipedia lists a budget of about US$82 million and a worldwide gross of about US$102.5 million, which is roughly 1.25 times the budget. A film usually needs to earn well beyond its production cost to turn a profit once marketing and cinema shares are counted, so that ratio is on the weak side.

Critics were not warm either. The film holds 42 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes and 43 out of 100 from 28 critics on Metacritic. The Rotten Tomatoes consensus, paraphrased, says the film shows real affection for the games and plenty of flashy style, but is let down by messy storytelling and juvenile humour.

A separate IMDb box office item this week, which could not be read in full, was headlined as saying Clayface had moved ahead of Street Fighter in the worldwide race. That suggests the film is now trailing newer releases.

For Rhodes, the reaction to the film has not dented his enthusiasm for acting, at least in his own telling.

## What happens next

Nothing has been confirmed about the new project. Rhodes said he hopes to announce it soon, and he said it would land this year, so a reveal could come within weeks. Until it does, the title, studio and his role all remain unknown.
