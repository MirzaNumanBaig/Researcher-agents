**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) is set to star in *The Week of Pain*, a new A24 film written and directed by Kristoffer Borgli, Deadline reported on 13 November 2026.
- A24 will finance and produce, alongside Square Peg, Dilemma Films and Robbie's company LuckyChap.
- Plot details have not been disclosed, and no release date has been set.
- Robbie has committed to shoot the film sometime in 2027, according to Deadline.

[Margot Robbie](https://moduscarry.com/tag/margot-robbie/) has lined up her next project before her current one has even finished. [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/margot-robbie-kristoffer-borgli-the-week-of-pain-a24-1237222997/) reported exclusively on 13 November that the Australian actor will lead *The Week of Pain*, the next film from writer-director [Kristoffer Borgli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristoffer_Borgli), with A24 behind it.

## What we know about The Week of Pain

The film is being financed and produced by A24, according to Deadline and [IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/news/business/margot-robbie-teams-kristoffer-borgli-director-a24-movie-1235227777/). It is made with Square Peg, the company run by Lars Knudsen and [Ari Aster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ari_Aster), plus Dilemma Films and Robbie's own label, LuckyChap Entertainment. Borgli also wrote the script.

Nothing has been shared about the story. IndieWire notes that only the title is public, and that it seems to suit Borgli's taste for putting his characters through the wringer. That is the outlet's reading, not a confirmed plot point.

Deadline says Robbie has admired Borgli's work for some time. She began talking with him about starring in his next project a while ago and has now committed to a 2027 shoot. World of Reel, a film blog, reads that timeline as pointing to a 2028 release. That is an estimate, and no date has been announced.

## Why Borgli is in demand

Borgli's most recent film, *The Drama*, opened in April 2026 and starred Zendaya and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson). IndieWire puts its worldwide gross at $134 million. [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/the-week-of-pain-margot-robbie/) says that makes it A24's fourth highest-grossing film worldwide, with $48.1 million in the United States and a 76 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score from 302 reviews.

Before that he made [*Dream Scenario*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dream_Scenario) in 2023 with [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage), and earlier *Sick of Myself*. *Dream Scenario* and *The Drama* both came through A24 and Square Peg, so *The Week of Pain* would be his third straight A24 feature, according to World of Reel. IndieWire says that run has made him a go-to director for stars who want a stranger, riskier part.

The Drama's run also fits a good year for the studio. MovieWeb reports that A24 is having its best year at the box office and took a bigger share of the summer takings than Warner Bros. or Paramount.

## Robbie's busy 2026 and 2027

Robbie's year opened with *Wuthering Heights*, Emerald Fennell's adaptation of Emily Brontë's [novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights), which also starred fellow Australian [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi). It opened on 13 February and grossed about $240 million worldwide, with MovieWeb citing Variety for a $40 million profit. Deadline calls it a strong start to her year.

Her next release is the *Ocean's* prequel for Warner Bros., which she is making opposite [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper), who also directs. IndieWire says it is set in Monte Carlo during the 1962 Monaco Grand Prix and shares a universe with Steven Soderbergh's *Ocean's* films. The supporting cast includes Monica Barbaro, Wagner Moura, Josh Gad, Vicky Krieps, George MacKay and Omar Sy. MovieWeb reports that production has recently wrapped and gives a release date of 25 June 2027, and that Robbie and Cooper play the parents of Danny and Deborah Ocean.

MovieWeb also notes that reports of a possible *Barbie* follow-up are unconfirmed and that there is no official word. That is worth treating as speculation for now.

## What happens next

A casting announcement for the rest of the ensemble is the obvious next step, though none has been reported. Plot details are being kept under wraps, as with most Borgli and Square Peg projects, according to Deadline. For Robbie, the *Ocean's* prequel arrives first, with *The Week of Pain* following into production in 2027.
