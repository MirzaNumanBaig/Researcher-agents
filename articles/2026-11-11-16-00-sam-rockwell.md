**Quick summary**

- Producers announced the full cast on 10 November for the off-Broadway revival of Arthur Miller's *A View From the Bridge*, led by [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) as Eddie Carbone.
- Performances begin on 27 November, with opening night on Sunday 13 December, in a 13-week limited run.
- Neil Pepe directs, and Rockwell is also a producer alongside Mark Berger.
- In a Backstage interview, Rockwell says theatre is "the only way" for actors to develop range.

Oscar winner Sam Rockwell has a full company around him for his return to the stage. According to BroadwayWorld, producers announced the complete cast of the off-Broadway revival of *A View From the Bridge* on 10 November, with performances starting on 27 November and opening night set for 13 December.

## Who is in the cast

Rockwell plays Eddie Carbone, the Brooklyn longshoreman at the centre of Miller's drama. [Alfred Molina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Molina) plays the lawyer Alfieri, Carla Gugino is Beatrice, Fred Hechinger is Rodolpho, Tom Pecinka is Marco and Riley Prior is Catherine. Those six were announced earlier.

The latest additions, per Playbill and BroadwayWorld, are Erick Betancourt as Mike, Lou Liberatore as Louis and Benj Mirman as Tony. Jordan Lage and Matthew J. Harris play the two immigrant officers, and Taylor Dunbar, Allen Tedder and Carly Zien complete the company. Casting is by Taylor Williams.

Behind the scenes, the creative team includes scenic designer Scott Pask, costume designer Sarah Laux, lighting designer John Torres and sound designer Palmer Hefferan.

## Dates, venue and a first since 1965

The production plays the Ellen Stewart Theatre at La MaMa, and New York Theatre Guide lists the limited engagement as running from 27 November to 27 February. BroadwayWorld notes that the play first appeared off-Broadway in 1965 at the Sheridan Square Playhouse, with Robert Duvall and Jon Voight in the cast. Playbill calls this the first major off-Broadway staging since that production, and BroadwayWorld describes it as the play's first off-Broadway return since 1965.

Miller's story follows Eddie as his protective love for his niece Catherine turns to jealousy when she falls for Rodolpho, one of two cousins who arrive from Italy. New York Theatre Guide recommends the show for ages 12 and over because of mature content and violence, including gunshots.

## A producer's model for nonprofit theatres

Rockwell is not only the lead. Playbill reports he is producing with Mark Berger and Play Hooky Productions. Although the run is not a full partnership with La MaMa, the producers hope it will show that commercial theatre can send meaningful resources back to nonprofit institutions. Tickets will be offered to La MaMa members at special rates, according to the same report.

It is a familiar stage for the actor. Playbill notes that he made his Broadway debut in *A Behanding in Spokane* in 2010 and later appeared in revivals of *Fool for Love* and *American Buffalo*, the last of which earned him a Tony nomination in 2022. His film credits include an Oscar-winning role in *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*.

## Why he says theatre matters

The casting news landed the same week as a Backstage interview in which Rockwell discusses [*Wild Horse Nine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine), the 1973-set black comedy-thriller from [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) in which he co-stars with John Malkovich as CIA agents sent to Easter Island. He told Backstage the "only way" to build range is to do theatre and take on great parts, then named Stanley Kowalski and *American Buffalo* among his own, and said he is "about to do" *A View From the Bridge*.

He added that film leads, especially McDonagh's, can stretch an actor too, but that theatre "stretches you probably even more". He also said he could not have played the *Wild Horse Nine* role five years ago, let alone 14 years ago when he first worked with McDonagh in 2012. For acting lessons, he credited colleagues from [Tom Hanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks) to [Frances McDormand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_McDormand), and praised [George Clooney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Clooney) for thanking the crew even after a bad night's sleep.

## What happens next

The run starts on 27 November and opens officially on 13 December. Tickets are sold through ViewFromtheBridgePlay.com, according to Playbill, and New York Theatre Guide says all sales are final, with no late seating.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Sam Rockwell's A View From the Bridge open?

Opening night is Sunday 13 December. Performances begin on 27 November, according to BroadwayWorld and Playbill.

### Where is the production being staged?

At the Ellen Stewart Theatre at La MaMa in New York's East Village, at 66 East 4th Street, according to New York Theatre Guide.

### Who plays which role?

Rockwell is Eddie Carbone, Alfred Molina is Alfieri, Carla Gugino is Beatrice, Fred Hechinger is Rodolpho, Tom Pecinka is Marco and Riley Prior is Catherine.

### Who directs it?

Neil Pepe directs, according to all three theatre outlets.
