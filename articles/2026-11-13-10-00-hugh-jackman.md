**Quick summary**

- The Writers Guild of America directory lists 10 writers for *Avengers: Doomsday*, including Hugh Jackman and Ryan Reynolds.
- Jackman and Reynolds are credited under "Additional Literary Material", not the main "Written By" credit.
- Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron hold the main screenwriting credit, according to ScreenCrush.
- The film opens in cinemas on December 18, and Marvel has not confirmed what the pair will do in it.

[Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) has been credited as a writer on *Avengers: Doomsday*. The Writers Guild of America (WGA) directory lists him alongside [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) among 10 writers on Marvel's next big crossover, which arrives on December 18.

## What the Hugh Jackman Avengers Doomsday credit means

The entry surfaced when fans went through the guild's online database, according to [The Hype Magazine](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2026/11/12/avengers-doomsday-ryan-reynolds-hugh-jackman-writers/). [ScreenCrush](https://screencrush.com/ryan-reynolds-and-hugh-jackman-avengers-doomsday-writers/), citing a Variety report, explains that the database was expanded a few years ago to include writers whose work was not large enough to earn the main "Written By" title on screen. Those writers receive an "Additional Literary Material" credit instead.

That is the category covering Jackman and Reynolds. It points to writing contributions, such as dialogue or scene work, rather than a full screenplay. Neither outlet says exactly what the two wrote.

## Who else is on the list

The main screenwriting credit goes to [Stephen McFeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Markus_and_Stephen_McFeely) and [Michael Waldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Waldron). ScreenCrush notes that McFeely co-wrote *Captain America: Civil War*, [Infinity War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Infinity_War) and *Endgame*, while Waldron created *Loki* and wrote *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*.

The additional credits go to several writers with Marvel history:

- Jeff Loveness, who wrote *Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania* and was the original writer when the film was titled *Avengers: The Kang Dynasty*.
- Andrew Lanham, a co-writer on *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*.
- Destin Daniel Cretton, who directed *Shang-Chi* and was the intended director of *The Kang Dynasty*.
- Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers, who have written all four of Tom Holland's Spider-Man films.

The long list reflects how much the project has changed. The Hype Magazine says it moved from its earlier form into a story built around [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) facing [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom).

## Why fans are reading it as a sign

The Hype Magazine argues the credits are the clearest signal yet that Wade Wilson and Logan, played by Reynolds and Jackman, have a real role. ScreenCrush goes a similar way, saying the credits strongly suggest the pair will appear as [Deadpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool) and Wolverine, though that is the outlet's reading, not a confirmation.

There is precedent for Reynolds. The Hype Magazine notes he co-wrote *Deadpool 2* and *Deadpool & Wolverine*, the 2024 film that paired him with Jackman. It adds that the directors, the Russo brothers, are known for rehearsal-driven work and on-set improvisation on big ensemble films.

A writing credit does not prove an on-screen appearance, and Marvel Studios and directors Anthony and [Joe Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers) have kept cameos and plot details private. Treat the speculation as speculation until the studio speaks.
