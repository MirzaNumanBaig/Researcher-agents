**Quick summary**

- A new Netflix documentary, "Russell Brand: Open Secret", includes a claim from his ex Jordan Martin about why he married Katy Perry, according to Page Six.
- The claim is Martin's allegation alone. Page Six says it asked a representative for Brand for comment.
- Court papers reported by Yahoo, citing Variety, show prosecutors agreed to leave Perry out of their opening arguments at Brand's trial.
- Perry was never interviewed by police or asked for a witness statement, according to Yahoo.
- Brand was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison.

The Katy Perry and Russell Brand story has returned this weekend through two separate reports. A new Netflix documentary features an ex-girlfriend's claim about why Brand married the singer, and newly disclosed court papers explain why Perry's name stayed out of the prosecution's opening at his London trial. Perry is not accused of anything in either report.

## What the Netflix documentary claims

Page Six reports that Jordan Martin, who dated Brand in 2007, appears in "Russell Brand: Open Secret". She alleges that he "always wanted" to marry someone famous. "He's doing exactly what he always wanted to do and be married to someone famous, which he always said he would be," Martin says in the film, as quoted by [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/14/entertainment/russell-brands-ex-makes-bombshell-claim-about-why-he-married-katy-perry-in-new-netflix-doc/).

Martin also alleges that Brand asked her to join him in Hawaii, where he was filming [Forgetting Sarah Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgetting_Sarah_Marshall), so that someone would be there to stop him "trying it on" with people on set. She says he told her he should be dating "somebody more famous". According to Page Six, the documentary also draws on anonymous industry figures, former roommates and journalists.

These are one woman's allegations. Page Six says it contacted a representative for [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) and had not published a response.

## How the marriage unfolded

[Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) and Brand met in 2008 while he was working on "Get Him to the Greek", according to Page Six. Yahoo dates the start of their relationship to 2009. They married in a Hindu ceremony in Rajasthan, India, in 2010, separated in December 2011, and their divorce was finalised in 2012.

Perry has spoken about the split before. In a 2013 interview with Vogue, quoted by Page Six, she described the relationship as "controlling" and said he did not like "the atmosphere of me being the boss on tour". She said Brand told her by text on 31 December 2011 that he was divorcing her, and that she had not heard from him since. She also said she had "moved on" from it.

## Why Perry was left out of the trial openings

Yahoo, citing Variety, reports that prosecutors had planned to refer to the marriage but dropped it after Brand's lawyers objected to personal-life detail unrelated to the charges. Lead prosecutor Riel Karmy-Jones KC wrote in pretrial submissions that matters after 2009 involving Perry "may be sensitive" and the prosecution was "content to remove these from the opening".

The timeline helps explain the decision. The offences Brand was convicted of took place between 2004 and 2009, before the marriage. Brand did raise Perry himself while giving evidence, describing the marriage as part of a period of "staggering fame". Yahoo says Perry was never interviewed by police or asked to provide a statement.

## Where Brand's case stands

Brand, 51, was convicted on 5 November of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women, according to Yahoo. Jurors acquitted him on two further rape charges and one count of attempted indecent assault. On 9 November Judge Joel Bennathan sentenced him at Southwark Crown Court to 10 years and six months, and he will stay on the sex offenders' register for life.

Page Six reports that Brand is held at HMP Wandsworth in south London and that six more women made allegations of sexual misconduct against him on Thursday. Yahoo adds that Metropolitan Police investigators are examining two further rape allegations.

## What happens next

No statement from Perry about the documentary had been reported at the time of writing. Brand has not publicly answered Martin's claims in the sources we read. The additional police inquiries are continuing, and any further charges would be a matter for prosecutors.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does the Netflix documentary say about Katy Perry?

It does not accuse her of anything. Jordan Martin alleges in the film that Brand always wanted to marry someone famous, and Perry's marriage to him is cited as part of that claim, according to Page Six.

### Why was Katy Perry not mentioned in the trial openings?

Brand's lawyers objected to personal details unrelated to the charges, and prosecutors agreed to remove them, according to court papers reported by Yahoo. The offences predated the 2010 marriage.

### When were Katy Perry and Russell Brand married?

They married in 2010 in Rajasthan, India, separated in December 2011 and finalised their divorce in 2012, according to Yahoo.
