[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) turned heads at the LACMA Gala on 7 November with a new Nicole Kidman haircut on show: soft, wispy bangs and a high ponytail. Women.com reports that the look takes one of 2026's most talked-about hair trends and loosens it up, and that the cut itself is brand new.

## A ponytail with a ballerina feel

According to [Women.com](https://www.women.com/2311807/nicole-kidmans-cool-girl-spin-on-curtain-bangs-haircut-trend-2026-lacma-gala-2026/), the actor's strawberry blonde hair was pulled into a high ponytail, with the bangs mostly swept to one side. She paired it with a Chanel dress, described as a modest, neutral-toned bodice joined to a leg-baring tulle skirt.

The outlet says the fringe is less structured than a classic curtain bang or an elevated shag, two styles it counts among the year's big hair trends. The high ponytail adds what it calls a ballerina note, which sits neatly against the tulle skirt.

It is also a change of pace. Women.com notes that for most of 2026 Kidman has worn her hair loose around her shoulders or in a polished updo.

## The cut behind the look

The writer argues the real work was done before the gala. Kidman debuted a fresh cut at the first Chanel runway show held in Australia, shortly before the LACMA event.

The stylist responsible is Adir Abergel. Women.com says he aimed for versatility and ease, so the hair can be worn as true curtain bangs, a straight-across fringe or swept to the side. He described the bangs to Vogue, as quoted by Women.com, as soft and wispy, long enough to graze the brow and broken up so they move. He then blended them into her long layers so everything falls naturally around her face.

## One cut, two finishes

At the Australian show, Kidman wore the same cut differently: loose waves with the feathery bangs parted in the middle. Abergel told Vogue there is a real overlap between Australian ease and French-girl nonchalance, a line that fits a Chanel show in Australia as well as a gala.

Women.com's reading is that the two appearances show the cut doing its job: one haircut, two very different finishes.

## What happens next

Neither Women.com nor Abergel's quoted comments point to a specific next appearance. Given how the cut has already been styled in at least two ways, expect the bangs to keep changing shape.
