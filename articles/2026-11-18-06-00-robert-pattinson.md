**Quick summary**

- Robert Pattinson James Bond casting is confirmed: [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), 40, takes over as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), announced on Monday 16 November (US time), according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) told GQ he needs to believe Bond is "an assassin" first and a spy second.
- The 26th Bond film is untitled and set for a 2028 release. No other casting has been announced.
- Old interviews show Pattinson once said he'd be "pretty bad" as Bond.

The casting is official, and the man who picked him has now said what he wants from the character. In a GQ interview published a day after the casting was announced, Villeneuve described a 007 who is first of all dangerous, according to [Consequence of Sound](https://consequence.net/2026/11/denis-villeneuve-james-bond-robert-pattinson/).

## What Villeneuve wants from Robert Pattinson as Bond

"I need to believe that this man is a killer, because more than a spy, Bond is an assassin," he told GQ's Zach Baron. He asked for "that kind of anger deep in the eyes" and "unapologetic panache".

He also wants sensuality, wit and dark humour, plus someone "strong, muscular" with "a deep intelligence in the eyes" who is always ahead of the audience.

The interview was recorded while the casting was still under way. Villeneuve said he has "massive" input but that the choice has to be accepted by a long list of people, a list he joked included the Pope and his mother. He also pointed to history: when Daniel Craig was cast, he said, the actor drew heaps of negative comments for being too short and too blond.

## Why it took so long

Villeneuve blamed his own schedule. He told the studio that if they wanted him, they would have to wait many months because he would be fully focused on Dune: Part Three. "I'm sorry for everybody," he said, but the casting took longer because he was busy.

Puck's Matthew Belloni added a behind-the-scenes account on his podcast The Town, as reported by Consequence. Belloni said early favourite Josh O'Connor had reservations about even testing, unsure he wanted to be "that type of commercial actor". Belloni described it as Villeneuve's call, backed by producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman. That is Belloni's account, not something Amazon MGM has confirmed.

## Pattinson's old jokes about the role

Pattinson's own history with the question is the lighter side of this story. Back in 2010, while promoting Remember Me, he told the BBC that playing 007 would be "quite big shoes to fill". Pierce Brosnan, a former Bond, played his father in that film. Pressed on how good he'd be, Pattinson laughed: "If I did it right now, probably pretty bad. Pretty embarrassing."

Two years later he told Shortlist he would consider it "definitely", but more like 20 years away than 10. A "fresh-faced Bond" would be "the worst idea in the world", he said, adding that after Craig the character needs "some baggage". Nine.com.au notes the 2028 release lands 16 years after that interview, so he wasn't far off.

Fans have also dug up a 2008 Twilight scene in which a prom flyer promises "Gambling, tuxedos and Bond, James Bond", Nine reports.

## A safe choice, or a risk?

Reaction online has been mixed. Writing in The Conversation, one academic called Pattinson the first Bond who was once a teen idol and the first to have worked across several major franchises: Harry Potter, Twilight, The Batman and Dune. The piece also cited his independent work, such as The Lighthouse (2019) and Mickey 17 (2025), and called the casting a calculated risk by Amazon.

Primetime, released on 5 September, holds an 86% Rotten Tomatoes score, according to its Wikipedia entry.

ABC News noted Pattinson will likely be 41 or 42 by the time his first Bond film arrives.

## What happens next

The untitled film is slated for 2028, with no leading lady, villain or release date confirmed. Before then, Pattinson is due in Fernando Meirelles' The Take, listed for 15 January 2027, and in Dune: Part Three, which Nine places in cinemas in 2027.

### Who is the new James Bond?

Robert Pattinson, 40, was announced as the next 007 on Monday 16 November (US time), according to The Hollywood Reporter as cited by Consequence of Sound.

### When is the next Bond film released?

The 26th Bond film is untitled and set for 2028. No exact date has been announced.
