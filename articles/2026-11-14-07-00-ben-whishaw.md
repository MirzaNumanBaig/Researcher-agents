**Quick summary**

- Black Doves Season 2 premiered on Netflix on 5 November 2026 with six new episodes.
- As of 13 November, it is the No. 1 series on Netflix worldwide, according to FlixPatrol figures cited by Men's Journal.
- Season 2 holds a 96% Rotten Tomatoes score, ahead of the 94% for the show overall.
- Separately, Ben Whishaw has spoken to The Sunday Times about dating a fellow actor, as reported by Yahoo.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) is part of the biggest show on Netflix this week. [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) Season 2 reached the No. 1 spot on the streamer's global series chart on 13 November 2026, one week after its debut, according to a report from Men's Journal republished by [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/keira-knightley-stars-global-hit-155932000.html).

## How Black Doves reached the top

Season 2 arrived on 5 November with six episodes. The AOL report, which cites the tracking site FlixPatrol, says the series first climbed to No. 2 and then took the top place on 11 November. It widened its lead the following day and still held the spot on 13 November.

The report notes a substantial gap over the next most-watched series, Taken: Natalee Holloway. That true-crime title drew 12.9 million views worldwide in the week ending 8 November, according to the same article.

FlixPatrol rankings are daily snapshots of what is most watched on each service, not audited audience figures. The AOL piece itself says it is "very likely" that Black Doves is the biggest show in the world right now, or very close to it. That is an inference, and it is worth treating as one.

## The cast and the story

The thriller was created by Joe Barton and stars [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley), Whishaw and [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire). Knightley plays an undercover spy who leads a double life as a devoted wife and mother. Whishaw plays an assassin who comes out of hiding to protect her after she is targeted.

Knightley earned a Golden Globe nomination in 2025 for best actress in a television drama for the role. On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the series sits at 94%, and AOL reports Season 2 scores higher at 96%. One recent Pajiba review called it "the most enjoyable binge in ages".

For more on the pair's work on the new run, see our earlier piece on [Ben Whishaw and Keira Knightley on Black Doves Season 2](https://moduscarry.com/ben-whishaw-black-doves-season-2-2/).

## Why the numbers matter

The scale helps explain the result. AOL, citing FlixPatrol, puts Netflix at roughly 325 million subscribers worldwide. Its nearest rival, Prime Video, is put at 200 million. For comparison, the top non-sports programme on US television in the week ending 8 November, ABC World News Tonight, drew just over 9.5 million viewers, according to Nielsen data quoted in the same report.

Those figures measure different things, since Netflix counts views worldwide and Nielsen counts US viewers. They are still a useful reminder of how far a streaming hit can travel.

## What Whishaw has said about his private life

Whishaw is known for keeping his personal life quiet, but he has opened up a little. Yahoo reports that he spoke to The Sunday Times in 2026 about being in a relationship with a fellow actor. He told the paper that it "makes sense" to date someone in the industry because the job is so unusual, and that partners can support each other and forgive each other when work makes them stressed or absent.

He added that he is "guilty of neglecting" his partner when he is working. Yahoo identifies the partner as [Kadiff Kirwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadiff_Kirwan), his co-star in the BBC drama This Is Going to Hurt and a performer who has also appeared in [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses). Yahoo also reports that Whishaw was previously in a civil partnership with composer Mark Bradshaw, which ended amicably in 2022.

## What happens next

If viewing holds, Black Doves may stay near the top of the chart through the coming weekend, though FlixPatrol rankings can shift daily.
