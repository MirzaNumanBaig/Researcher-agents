**Quick summary**

- BAFTA North America named [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) to its 2026 BAFTA Breakthrough U.S. cohort on 17 November.
- The class has 14 rising creatives across film, television and games, and Navarrette is listed for *Obsession*.
- Members get a year of mentoring, full BAFTA voting membership and access to screenings and events.
- The news follows her ELLE Women in Hollywood award speech and a Ralph Lauren suit look at the same event.

BAFTA has added Inde Navarrette to its 2026 Breakthrough class in the United States, recognising the *Obsession* lead as one of 14 emerging creatives to watch. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/bafta-us-breakouts-2026-connor-storrie-inde-navarette-1236756614/) reported the full list on 17 November, and [Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/bafta-breakthrough-cohort-connor-storrie-inde-navarrette/) carried the same announcement. It lands in a busy week for the actress, who was also honoured by ELLE.

## Who else is in the BAFTA Breakthrough cohort

The group mixes screen and games talent. According to The Hollywood Reporter, it includes [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) from [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), *Backrooms* filmmaker Kane Parsons, *Union County* writer-director Adam Meeks and *Bedford Park* writer-director Stephanie Ahn. Games names include the lead developer of *Despelote*, Julián Cordero, and the writer of *South of Midnight*, Zaire Lanier.

Navarrette is the only member listed for *Obsession*, the film written and directed by Curry Barker. Gold Derby describes her as that film's breakout.

BAFTA Breakthrough is now in its seventh year, is supported by Apple TV and is billed as the arts charity's flagship talent initiative.

## What the programme offers

Members take part in a year-long scheme. Gold Derby lists one-on-one meetings and career guidance, full BAFTA voting membership, entry to BAFTA events and screenings, and networking in the US and abroad.

Courtney LaBarge Bell, executive director of BAFTA North America, said the cohort shows imagination, ambition and distinct perspectives. She added that emerging creatives face growing difficulty finding opportunity and mentorship, which is why BAFTA wants to give them a supported space.

The Hollywood Reporter says the selection was made by a jury. Film and television jurors included Adolpho Veloso, Alysa Nahmias and Sonia Kennebeck, while a separate group chose the games names.

## An awards-week speech that set the tone

The BAFTA news arrives a day after Navarrette accepted an award at the 2026 ELLE Women in Hollywood event in Beverly Hills. According to [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a74454476/inde-navarrette-elle-2026-women-in-hollywood-speech/), she said playing Nikki in *Obsession* meant more to her than a first leading role. She described needing to push through every barrier set by school, peers, society and herself.

She thanked Barker for writing the part and thanked the actresses who came before her. ELLE says she named Jessica Lange, Natalie Portman for *Léon: The Professional*, Sally Field and Bryce Dallas Howard, who presented the award. She closed by saying those women had given her permission to be free.

We covered the night in more detail in our post on [Inde Navarrette at ELLE Women in Hollywood](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-women-in-hollywood/).

## The suit that made the red carpet pages

Her outfit drew its own coverage. [WWD](https://wwd.com/pop-culture/celebrity-news/inde-navarrette-ralph-lauren-suit-elle-women-in-hollywood-1239420872/) reported that she wore look three from Ralph Lauren's spring 2027 collection: a pinstripe vest with wide-leg trousers, a white collared shirt, a blue tie and a pocket chain. A cream pinstripe train ran down one trouser leg.

The label showed the design on the runway at New York Fashion Week in September. WWD credits stylist Enrique Melendez, hair by Marc Mena and makeup by Kayli Rachelle, with Bulgari rings finishing the look.
