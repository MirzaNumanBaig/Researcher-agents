**Quick summary**

- Unnamed sources quoted by UK media claim [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) kept difficult parts of her marriage to Russell Brand private. None of this is confirmed.
- [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was convicted on November 5 and sentenced on November 9, according to AOL.
- Metro reports that Perry was never approached by police or asked for a witness statement, and says it has asked her representative for comment.
- Separately, Vogue says Perry and Justin Trudeau were seen arriving together at Venice's Marco Polo Airport.

New claims about the Katy Perry Russell Brand marriage have surfaced in the UK press, days after Brand was sentenced. Anonymous insiders say Perry carried a lot privately. Perry has not responded publicly, and her representative had not replied when Metro published on 23 November.

## What the insiders are saying

Metro quotes a source who spoke to The Sun. The source said Brand "tried to control" Perry and was verbally abusive, and that she "needed a lot of support" to leave. The same person said Perry kept much of it private. AOL carries the same account and adds that the source believes Brand was suspicious of her behaviour throughout.

These are claims from people who are not named, and neither outlet has verified them. Perry has not confirmed them.

The singer, 42, married Brand, 51, in October 2010 in a private Hindu ceremony in Rajasthan, according to AOL. He filed for divorce on 30 December 2011. Perry has said she learned of it through a text message.

## What Perry has said herself

In a 2013 Vogue cover story, Perry gave her own account. She said Brand disliked her being the boss on tour, and called his reaction "very controlling, which was upsetting," as quoted by Metro and AOL.

She also said she later learned a "real truth" about the split that she would not disclose, and that she had moved on. That is the only first-hand account in these reports, and it is more than a decade old.

## The court case and the possible book

Per AOL, a jury at Southwark Crown Court convicted Brand of sexually assaulting four women between 2004 and 2009. He had pleaded not guilty to all seven charges. Judge Joel Bennathan sentenced him on 9 November, citing his "casual and arrogant disregard" for women who refused him. Metro reports a 10-and-a-half-year term at HMP Littlehey.

Metro adds a point about Perry's name. Legal submissions made public after the verdict said a victim believed others were in the room during a 2009 assault, "possibly including" Perry. Brand's lawyers argued the marriage was irrelevant, prosecutors agreed not to mention her, and the Crown Prosecution Service confirmed she was never approached by police. Nothing in the reports says she witnessed anything.

The Mail on Sunday reported, via an unnamed source, that Perry's team has held "active discussions" about a series or autobiography, including with streamers. That is unconfirmed. Our earlier coverage looked at the [reported Brand book and series](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-russell-brand-book/).

## A different picture in Venice

Vogue says Perry and Trudeau, the former Canadian prime minister, were seen arriving at Venice's Marco Polo Airport together over the weekend. It describes her in a camel cashmere-style set with a beige scarf and a Polo Ralph Lauren cap, and him in a slogan T-shirt reading "Will yap about books." The pair went public with their relationship last year, per Metro. For more, see [everything we publish about Katy Perry](https://moduscarry.com/tag/katy-perry/) and our report on [Perry focusing on Trudeau](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-brand-headlines/).

## What happens next

Whether Perry comments on the claims, or on any book or series, is unknown. Brand's legal team is reported to be weighing an appeal, according to a headline linked by Metro. Any statement from Perry's team would be the first confirmed response.
