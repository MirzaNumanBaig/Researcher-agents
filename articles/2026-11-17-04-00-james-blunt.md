**Quick summary**

- On the James Blunt Panettiere memoir rumours, [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt), 52, told The Sunday Times Magazine it is "deeply troubling" to be linked to a story he says he has nothing to do with.
- The story comes from the memoir of the late actress [Hayden Panettiere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayden_Panettiere), who does not name the British singer she describes.
- Some social media users guessed Blunt was the singer. He denies it, and a journalist's Substack article argued the dates do not fit.
- Separately, Blunt was announced on 16 November as a headliner for CarFest 2027 at Silverstone, with tickets on sale on 19 November.

The James Blunt Panettiere memoir story took a new turn on 16 November 2026, as the singer publicly rejected online speculation that he is the unnamed "famous British singer" in Hayden Panettiere's book. He told The Sunday Times Magazine it is "deeply troubling", as reported by the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16296305/It-deeply-troubling-caught-James-Blunt-weighs-claims-famous-British-singer-actress-Hayden-Panettiere-bed-18.html) and [Music-News.com](https://www.music-news.com/news/UK/192315/James-Blunt-on-deeply-troubling-claims-he-was-famous-singer-in-Hayden-Panettiere-s-memoir).

## James Blunt Panettiere memoir: what he said

Panettiere's memoir, *This Is Me: A Reckoning*, was published in May. According to the Daily Mail, she wrote that at 18 a friend took her to meet a British singer-songwriter, then in his 30s, on a yacht, and that she found him naked in a cabin. The book does not name him. Panettiere died in August, aged 36, of an accidental drug overdose, as reported by Music-News.com.

After the book came out, some internet users suggested Blunt was the man. He has denied it. "It's deeply troubling to be caught up in something you have nothing to do with, and no control over," he told the magazine. He added: "In the Wild West of the internet, there's no interest in detail or truth."

Asked whether that was why he chose to speak rather than ignore the rumours, he answered: "Yes, or else you're entirely dependent on others."

## The timeline argument

Blunt had already pointed to a defence in August, according to [The Blast](https://theblast.com/886112/james-blunt-breaks-silence-troubling-allegations/). He shared an article by journalist Dean Piper on Substack, titled "Hayden Panettiere and James Blunt, An Internet Lynching".

Piper argues that photographs of the two together were taken in May 2009, when Panettiere was 19, almost 20. If the incident happened when she was 18, he writes, Blunt "is not your guy". He also says her account, in which she fled the room, does not sit with the pair being seen together several times afterwards. Both points are Piper's reasoning, not a ruling by any authority.

## CarFest 2027: a Silverstone headline slot

The same week brought lighter news. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ck4gn204qjjxo) reports that Blunt will headline CarFest at Silverstone from 27 to 29 August 2027, alongside [Jess Glynne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jess_Glynne) and DJ Pete Tong. Melanie C, Kaiser Chiefs, Rick Parfitt Jnr and The Amy Winehouse Band are also on the bill.

[Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/carfest-2027-lineup-james-blunt-melanie-c-kaiser-chiefs-tickets-dates-venue/) notes that Blunt's album *Airplane Mode* reached Number 1 on the Official Albums Chart on Friday 13 November. NewsHub says his Sunday set will mix new material with "You're Beautiful" and "1973".

CarFest, run by radio presenter Chris Evans, raises money for UK children's charities. The BBC says it has raised about £29 million since 2012 and drew more than 30,000 people a day when it first moved to Silverstone in 2026.

## The Celebrity Traitors edit

Blunt has also been talking about his run on [The Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors). He told The Times, as reported by [HuffPost UK](https://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/entry/james-blunt-celebrity-traitors-edit_uk_6afaf6ede4b0e2644ec8ae70), that the broadcast was "so heavily edited" that a lot of context was removed. ## What happens next

CarFest tickets go on general sale at 8am UK time on Thursday 19 November. A three-day adult camping pass costs £251 and single-day entry £95, according to NewsHub. More acts are still to be announced.
