**Quick summary**

- [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan) began his European tour on October 17 in Oslo and has kept adding older songs to the show.
- On Saturday in Salzburg he played "Honest with Me" for the first time since December 2019, according to Jambands.com.
- On November 7 in Casalecchio di Reno, Italy, he played "Sugar Baby" for the first time since November 2012.
- The tour is scheduled to run through December 8.

Bob Dylan is treating his European tour as a chance to dust off songs that have sat unplayed for years. The latest to return is "Honest with Me", which he performed at the Salzburg Arena in Austria on Saturday, its first appearance in his sets since December 8, 2019, [Jambands.com reports](https://jambands.com/news/bob-dylan-revisits-another-love-and-theft-track).

## A tour built on bustouts

The run began on October 17 in Oslo, and the setlist has kept shifting ever since. According to Jambands.com, Dylan opened the tour by bringing back "Tangled Up in Blue" for the first time since 2018, and he added extra verses when he played it.

At the next show, in Gothenburg, Sweden, he played "Girl From the North Country", a song he had last performed in 2019. That one has a fan on the other side of the rock family tree. In a recent piece, [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/1963-neil-young-fell-in-love-with-bob-dylan/) says it is [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young)'s favourite Dylan song. The magazine quotes Young describing its chords as "sideways" and calling the narrator a sympathetic character for the respect he shows the woman in the song. The same article says the track has now passed 200 million streams.

## Covers and Time Out of Mind

Dylan has also reached outside his own catalogue. Jambands.com lists three song debuts on the tour: Randy Newman's "Louisiana 1927", [Jerry Lee Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Lee_Lewis)'s "I'll Make It All Up to You" and Guy Clark's "Dublin Blues".

He has also gone back to 1997's Time Out of Mind. The outlet says "Not Dark Yet", "Million Miles" and "Tryin' to Get to Heaven" were all played earlier in the run. Its notes on how long each had been away differ: "Not Dark Yet" and "Tryin' to Get to Heaven" were last heard in 2019, and "Million Miles" in 2014.

## Two Love and Theft returns

The 2001 album Love and Theft has supplied the most recent surprises. In Casalecchio di Reno, at the Unipol Arena, Dylan played "Sugar Baby" for the first time in just over 14 years, since November 14, 2012. On Saturday in Salzburg it was "Honest with Me", the album's ninth track, back after almost six years.

We covered the Austrian show separately in [Bob Dylan Revives Honest With Me in Salzburg](https://moduscarry.com/bob-dylan-honest-with-me-salzburg/). JamBase also reported the Salzburg performance, but its page could not be read for this article, so the details here rely on Jambands.com.

## The band on stage

Jambands.com lists the current lineup as Julian Lage and Joel Paterson on guitars, Tony Garnier on bass and Anton Fig on drums.
## What happens next

The tour continues through December 8, which leaves a few weeks of shows in which more songs could resurface. Given how often the setlist has changed since Oslo, there is no way to predict which ones.
