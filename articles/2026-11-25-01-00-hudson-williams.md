**Quick summary**

- Canadian streamer Crave announced on 23 November that the supernatural thriller Yaga will return for a second season.
- Carrie-Anne Moss is the only cast member confirmed so far; Hudson Williams' return has not been announced.
- The eight-part first season launched on 23 October and became Crave's number one series over its opening weekend.
- The two-episode season 1 finale airs on 27 November, and no season 2 premiere date has been set.

The Hudson Williams thriller Yaga has been renewed for a second season, just over a month after it debuted. The announcement came on 23 November, according to [Attitude](https://www.attitude.co.uk/culture/film-tv/yaga-renewed-for-season-2-hudson-williams-540464/), and it lands while Williams is also filming the next season of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry). What the news does not settle is whether [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) himself is coming back.

## What has been confirmed for Yaga season 2

Crave confirmed the renewal through its official Instagram account, [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/news/2229531-hudson-williams-tv-show-renewed-heated-rivalry-season-2) reports. [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss), who plays university professor Katherine Hargrove, is the first returning cast member named. Both outlets say further casting news is still to come.

Behind the camera, [Kat Sandler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kat_Sandler) stays on as creator, writer and showrunner, according to ComingSoon.net. Crave and Blink49 Studios will produce the new season, and Moss will serve as an executive producer. There is no production schedule yet.

The finale of season 1 is still ahead. The last two episodes arrive on 27 November, so viewers will see how the first story ends before any new details emerge.

## Why Williams' role is the open question

Williams plays two parts in the series. He is Henry Park, a missing university student and heir to a powerful fishing business, and Logan Casey, a lookalike roommate. Attitude notes that Logan was created for the screen and does not appear in Sandler's original stage play, which the series adapts.

Crave has not said whether Williams, [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid) or [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo) will return, ComingSoon.net says. Reid plays private investigator Charlie Rapp, and Backo plays local detective Fran Carson. Because the plot of season 1 turns on Henry's disappearance, how the story continues is unclear, and none of the reports describes the plot of season 2. Anything about Williams' future in the show is speculation until Crave says more.

## How season 1 performed

The numbers behind the renewal are strong. Per Attitude, Yaga was Crave's number one series over its opening weekend and reached number one on AMC+ in the US, the platform's biggest exclusive series launch to date. It also topped the charts on [Lionsgate Play](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionsgate%2B) in India, on BINGE and Foxtel in Australia, and on Neon in New Zealand.

Justin Stockman, Bell Media's vice-president of global content, said the response had "blown us away". Pancho Mansfield, president of global scripted television at Blink49 Studios, praised Sandler's "bold and distinctive vision". For Australian viewers, the chart result on BINGE and Foxtel is the local signal that the show found an audience here.

The series reimagines Baba Yaga, the witch of Slavic folklore, as a modern mystery set in the fictional coastal town of Whittock. In the UK and Ireland it streams on Sky and NOW.
