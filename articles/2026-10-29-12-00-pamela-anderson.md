**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) was photographed in September 2026 leaving her New York hotel with a sheer white-to-cream "French fade" manicure, according to Women.com.
- The look is part of the ombré nail trend, which blends several shades into a soft gradient.
- Latination reports that her recent red carpet appearances, including Venice, have centred on minimal makeup.
- Both reports focus on style, not on any new film or television project.

Pamela Anderson has become an unlikely reference point for two beauty trends at once: soft, blended nails and a nearly bare face. [Women.com](https://www.women.com/2294469/pamela-anderson-is-jumping-on-the-ombre-manicure-trend-aka-the-french-fade/) reported on 28 October that the actress wore a sheer ombré French manicure when she left her hotel in New York City in September 2026, while [Latination](https://latination.com/pamela-anderson-rediscovers-beauty-without-makeup/) says her red carpet style has shifted towards a natural look.

## What is the "French fade" manicure?

According to Women.com, the ombré manicure is defined by a diffused colour gradient, a smooth fusion of more than one shade. In Anderson's case, the outlet describes healthy natural nails with a white-to-cream palette, a light, sheer application and a glossy finish. It also notes her rounded nail tips.

The outlet says the colours matched the rest of her outfit, from her coat to her luggage. That detail is the outlet's observation, and it reads as a styling point rather than anything Anderson herself has said.

Women.com places the look in what it calls an era of minimalism, one of several nail trends it expects to dominate in 2026. It quotes Susan Carroll, founder of the London medispa Young LDN, who told Elle that a softer French ombré is "both effortless and expensive" and suits minimal makeup, linen dresses and swimwear.

## How to get the look at home

The Women.com piece treats the manicure as achievable without a salon visit. Its steps, drawn from Carroll's advice to Elle, are:

- Buff the nails and look after the cuticles, since the look relies on a healthy base.
- Choose a sheer white or nude polish as the base colour.
- Paint a stripe of nude and a stripe of white side by side on a makeup sponge, overlapping slightly where they meet.
- Dab the sponge onto the nail with a light touch until you can no longer see where one colour ends and the other begins.
- Finish with a top coat.

The outlet adds that the technique takes a steady hand and practice, and points to ombré lip gloss nails as the latest variation.

## A bare-faced approach on the red carpet

Latination describes a broader change in how Anderson presents herself in public. The outlet says she has appeared at high-profile events, among them the Venice Film Festival and Los Angeles galas, with minimal makeup, and links the choice to self-acceptance and personal confidence.

The same report says the shift is also a tribute to her late makeup artist, Alexis Vogel. This is Latination's account, and the article does not show a full quotation from Anderson on the subject, so it is best read as that outlet's interpretation.

## The outfits behind the look

Latination points to two specific appearances. In Venice, it says she wore a pastel blue lemon-print dress to the Place to Be photocall. At the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles, it reports a white shirt with a sweeping green Carolina Herrera skirt.

The outlet also highlights her jewellery. It says she wore lab-grown diamonds, including pieces from Pandora, and personalised charm bracelets given to her by her sons. It contrasts this with her Baywatch years, when it says her style was heavier and more extravagant. The report includes embedded Instagram posts from Anderson and [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight), which we could not view in full.

## What happens next

Neither report announces a new project or appearance. Nail trend coverage tends to move quickly, so expect the French fade to keep circulating through the season.
