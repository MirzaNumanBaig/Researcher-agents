**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) and composer [Bill Conti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Conti) appeared at a 50th anniversary screening of *Rocky* at Kings Theatre in Brooklyn earlier this month, according to Gold Derby.
- Stallone said the ice rink date was meant to feature 250 extras, but none turned up on the day.
- He also described how producers cut the lights on the film's key "go the distance" scene.
- The event doubled as a promotion for Peter Farrelly's film *I Play Rocky*, which Gold Derby says dramatises Stallone's struggle to get the original made.

Fifty years after *Rocky* opened in New York over Thanksgiving 1976, Sylvester Stallone has been telling the stories behind its most tender scene. At an anniversary screening in Brooklyn, the writer and star explained that the famous ice rink date only exists in its quiet form because the crowd he had planned never arrived, [Gold Derby reported](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/rocky-at-50-sylvester-stallone-bill-conti-behind-the-scenes-stories/) on 26 November.

## The ice rink date that was meant to be very different

In the finished film, [Rocky Balboa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocky_Balboa) takes pet store worker Adrian Pennino, played by [Talia Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talia_Shire), skating on Thanksgiving night. The rink is almost empty, and the two of them wobble around it before heading back to his apartment, where the night turns intimate.

Stallone told the Kings Theatre audience that the plan was far busier. According to Gold Derby, the script called for 250 extras and a skilled skater who spun around the rink and flirted with Adrian while Rocky could not even step onto the ice. The idea was that Rocky's heart would be broken.

Then the extras did not show. Stallone said he asked director John G. Avildsen where everyone was, and the answer was that they had one: a heavyset man by the ice-resurfacing machine. The team improvised, and the lonely rink became the setting people remember. Stallone described it as a frozen wasteland for two people who cannot really skate, and said you learn a great deal about them after a few laps. He called it "divine intervention" and said the production had about five such moments.

## The scene producers tried to switch off

The second story concerned the night-before scene in which Rocky tells Adrian he cannot beat champion [Apollo Creed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_Creed), played by [Carl Weathers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Weathers). All he wants, he says, is to go the distance and still be standing after 15 rounds.

Stallone said he fought to keep that admission because an honest hero who knows he will lose is one an audience cheers harder. He quipped that, for the record, it took Rocky only three years to beat Creed.

Gold Derby quotes him saying that producers [Irwin Winkler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irwin_Winkler) and [Robert Chartoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Chartoff) told him the shoot was running late and they were "pulling the plug". He had assumed it was a figure of speech. Instead the lights went out and he was left in the dark, unable to see Adrian. He said he feared being fired if he got it wrong, but they shot the scene anyway. The next day, he recalled, the producers told him it was the best scene so far.

## A film about the making of the film

The anniversary night was also a promotional stunt for *I Play Rocky*, a new awards hopeful from director Peter Farrelly. Gold Derby describes it as a dramatisation of the trials Stallone went through to bring his vision to the screen. Stallone met his younger screen counterpart, [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito), at the event and publicly gave the portrayal his blessing.

The original film went on to win Best Picture, and Rocky and Adrian's story ran for a quarter of a century across five films, according to the same report. ## Frequently asked questions

### What did Sylvester Stallone say about the Rocky ice rink scene?
He said the scene was originally written with 250 extras and a skater flirting with Adrian. When no extras arrived, the crew improvised the near-empty rink seen in the film.

### When did Rocky first open?
According to Gold Derby, it debuted in New York City over the Thanksgiving holiday in 1976 and opened nationwide in December of that year.

### What is I Play Rocky?
It is a new film from director Peter Farrelly that dramatises what Stallone went through to get *Rocky* made. Anthony Ippolito plays the younger Stallone.
