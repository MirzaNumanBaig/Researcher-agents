**Quick summary**

- Dame Judi Dench, who played MI6 boss M in the Bond films, has praised Robert Pattinson's casting as 007, according to inkl.
- She added that her eye condition will make the film difficult for her to watch.
- Amazon MGM Studios named Pattinson the seventh Bond on Monday 16 November; Denis Villeneuve directs.
- A 2025 joke about "taking jobs off younger actors" has resurfaced since the announcement, Yahoo reports.

A veteran of the franchise has given [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) her blessing. [Dame Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench), who played M opposite [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), has welcomed his casting as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), but said her eye condition means she will struggle to watch the film, according to a report on [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/judi-dench-shares-heartbreaking-caveat-while-giving-verdict-on-robert-pattinsons-james-bond-casting) published on 25 November.

## What Judi Dench said about the casting

The inkl report describes Dench's response as praise for Pattinson with a sad caveat. She is on record as approving of the choice, yet she expects watching the finished film to be difficult because of her eyesight. The piece does not set out her full comments in the portion we could read, so we are limiting this account to what the outlet states.

Dench's view carries weight because she knows the job from the inside. She played the head of MI6 across several Bond eras, working with both Brosnan and Craig. Her opinion of the next 007 is that of someone who has watched the character change hands before.

## How Pattinson became 007

Amazon MGM Studios named Pattinson the seventh actor to play Bond in the franchise's 26th film on Monday 16 November, according to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/robert-pattinsons-jokes-taking-jobs-235049633.html). [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) directs. It is the studio's first Bond film since it took over the series. Pattinson takes over from Craig, who led five films.

Pattinson called the part "the honor of a lifetime". Villeneuve acknowledged the other actors who reportedly auditioned, including Josh O'Connor, [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult). "We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007," the director said.

## The age joke that came back

Since the announcement, an older clip has been recirculating. In 2025, Pattinson took a Vanity Fair lie detector test with [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) while promoting [Die My Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_My_Love). Lawrence asked whether he would make a new Twilight film if [Benny Safdie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benny_Safdie) and his brother Josh directed it. "I like taking jobs off younger actors," he answered. "I want to play 17 again."

Pattinson is 40, which Yahoo notes is on the older side for a Bond debut. By its count, [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) was 32 when his first film came out, [George Lazenby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lazenby) 30, Craig 38, [Timothy Dalton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timothy_Dalton) 41, Brosnan 42 and [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore) 45. He is older than Craig was, but younger than three of his predecessors.

## A busy year on screen

Pattinson already leads The Batman series, and 007 adds a second franchise. Yahoo lists The Drama with Zendaya, Primetime and The Odyssey among his 2026 releases. Primetime, directed by Lance Oppenheim, opened on 5 September. Wikipedia records a budget of US$14.5 million and a box office of about US$62 million, roughly four times the cost. It also lists an 86% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic average of 68 from 54 critics, with reviews singling out his performance.

## What happens next

Next on his slate is Dune: Part Three, which Yahoo says opens on 18 December. He also stars in The Take, directed by Fernando Meirelles, which Wikipedia lists for 15 January 2027. No release date for the Bond film appears in the sources we read.
