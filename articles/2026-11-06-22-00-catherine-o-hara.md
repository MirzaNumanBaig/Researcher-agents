**Quick summary**

- The writers of *Beetlejuice Beetlejuice*, Al Gough and Miles Millar, say a third film is harder to make after Catherine O'Hara's death.
- They told ScreenRant that any new sequel is "really up to Tim" Burton, according to Comic Book Resources.
- Burton has said publicly that he does not necessarily see a third film happening.
- The 2024 sequel earned about $452 million at the box office.

Writing a third Beetlejuice film has become a harder task since the death of [Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara), according to the screenwriters of the last one. In an interview with ScreenRant, reported by [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) on 6 November 2026, [Al Gough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Gough) and [Miles Millar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Millar) said her absence is a major hurdle for a [Beetlejuice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beetlejuice) threequel. O'Hara died in January, aged 71.

## What the writers said about Catherine O'Hara Beetlejuice 3

Asked about a third film, Gough kept the door open but did not push it. "You never say never, but that's really up to Tim," he said, adding that O'Hara's passing makes it "certainly harder". Millar agreed, saying the answer is simply that it is harder.

Comic Book Resources describes two obstacles. The first is the loss of O'Hara, who was central to the films. The second is that [Tim Burton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Burton) would first need to want to make another one. Neither writer announced a script, a pitch or a plan.

For readers who want the background on her career and passing, our earlier coverage is here: [Catherine O'Hara Dies at 71: Her Career in Review](https://moduscarry.com/catherine-ohara-dies-at-71/).

## Burton is not counting on a third film

Burton has been cautious in public. Speaking to The Hollywood Reporter last year, as quoted by Comic Book Resources, he pointed out that the second film took 35 years to arrive, joking that he would be 105 by the time a third one came around. He said the odds were not good.

He also said he loved making the sequel and that Warner Bros. did not really want to do it. He described working the same way as on the first film, with the actors improvising. He compared the idea of a follow-up to trying to re-create the Wednesday dance scene, adding that he loves the characters but does not necessarily see it happening.

Comic Book Resources notes that these comments came before O'Hara's death, so the loss adds a further complication rather than being the only one. We covered the question in more depth in [Beetlejuice 3 Looks Unlikely Without Catherine O'Hara](https://moduscarry.com/beetlejuice-3-chances/).

## How the last film performed

[Beetlejuice Beetlejuice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beetlejuice_Beetlejuice) reached cinemas in 2024 and took about $452 million at the box office, with mostly positive reviews, according to Comic Book Resources. It has since found more viewers on streaming services, which keeps interest in a third chapter alive.

The film brought back [Michael Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Keaton) as the title character, alongside O'Hara and [Winona Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winona_Ryder). [Jenna Ortega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Ortega) joined as Lydia's daughter, Astrid. The cast also included [Monica Bellucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Bellucci), [Justin Theroux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Theroux), [Willem Dafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willem_Dafoe), [Burn Gorman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burn_Gorman) and [Arthur Conti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Conti).

The title itself sets up a trilogy, a point Comic Book Resources makes, and that is part of why fans keep asking. Still, nothing in the interview suggests a third film is in development.

## What happens next

Nothing has been confirmed. Any third film depends first on Burton's interest, and then on the writers finding a story that works without one of its central performers. Until Burton or Warner Bros. says otherwise, a third film remains a possibility rather than a plan.
