## A reunion at the Parkinson's gala

Christopher Lloyd has spoken about what Michael J. Fox means to him after the pair were reunited at a fundraising evening in New York. According to a report by [KarloBag.eu](https://karlobag.eu/en/entertainment/christopher-lloyd-on-michael-j-fox-a-lifelong-friendship-born-during-the-back-to-0i5rc/), which cites People, Lloyd called their connection a "lifelong love" and said the bond formed on the Back to the Future set has never broken.

The occasion was the 26th annual gala of The Michael J. Fox Foundation, held on 14 November at the Waldorf Astoria New York. The event, titled "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson's", raises money for Parkinson's research. Lloyd reportedly said that the part that mattered most to him was simply seeing his friends again and spending the evening with them.

The foundation's fundraising has been in the news recently. Our earlier report on how the [Michael J. Fox Foundation passed $1.75bn in a $2.5bn drive](https://moduscarry.com/michael-j-fox-foundation-campaign/) covers the scale of that effort.

## From Hill Valley to a real friendship

Lloyd and Fox starred together in the 1985 film Back to the Future and its sequels in 1989 and 1990. The report says Fox once told Variety, at the 2023 Sundance Film Festival, that it took several films before he felt he knew Lloyd beyond the character. According to that account, the two grew closer during Back to the Future Part III.

That slow start makes the later warmth more notable. Fox is also reported to have praised Lloyd's "exceptionally youthful spirit" at a charity event in Nashville in April 2026.

## A birthday message online

In October, Fox marked Lloyd's 88th birthday on Instagram. The post showed a photo of the two at a seaside restaurant dinner, an image first shared in March. Lloyd answered in the comments, according to the report, saying the pair looked timeless and that he loved Fox as a friend.
