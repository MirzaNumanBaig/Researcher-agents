**Quick summary**

- [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) says she messaged director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) on the morning [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was announced as the new [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- She joked at the Elle Women in Hollywood event that she is available if the film has a villain "the size of a five-foot-four female".
- Pugh worked with Villeneuve on Dune: Part Two and returns for Dune: Part Three, which opens on 18 December.
- No release date is confirmed for the Bond film, and CinemaBlend reports current estimates point to 2028.

Florence Pugh has put her name forward for Denis Villeneuve's James Bond film, and she did it by text message. Speaking at the Elle Women in Hollywood event, the Oscar-nominated actress said she contacted the director the same morning Robert Pattinson's casting as 007 became public, according to [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/one-of-the-first-people-text-denis-villeneuve-after-007s-casting-florence-pugh-robert-pattinson).

## What Pugh said to Villeneuve

Pugh told the audience she congratulated Villeneuve on "finding your Bond" and called it a nice catch. Then came the pitch: she asked whether there were any villains the size of a five-foot-four woman, and added that she was only reminding him in case he needed it.

It was a joke, and nobody has suggested a role has been offered. CinemaBlend notes that the cast beyond Pattinson is still unknown, and fans are already guessing who will play the next Bond girl and who will take on the villains.

## Why the pairing is plausible

Pugh and Villeneuve already have history. She first worked with him on Dune: Part Two, playing a role she will repeat in Dune: Part Three. Pattinson is part of that third film as well, playing Scytale, according to CinemaBlend. That means all three could be on the same press tour in a matter of weeks.

CinemaBlend suggests the Dune connection is part of why Villeneuve chose Pattinson for Bond. That is the outlet's reading rather than a statement from the director, so treat it as a view, not a confirmed reason.

## Reaction to the casting

Pugh was not the only famous voice. The report says [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) also had a notable reaction, though it does not spell out what he said. Fans have been vocal too. Some are pleased to see a new face follow Daniel Craig, while others say they have seen a lot of the 40-year-old actor lately.

The outlet points out that Pattinson appeared in The Drama, The Odyssey and Primetime this year, and is currently filming [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves)' The Batman: Part Two. Online jokes have fixated on his run through big franchises: [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter), Twilight, Batman, Dune and now Bond.

For more on how audiences have responded, see our earlier coverage of [how Villeneuve's Bond choice split fans and critics](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-film-reaction/) and [what Villeneuve wants from 007](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-villeneuve-vision/).

## What happens next

The next date on the calendar is Dune: Part Three, which CinemaBlend says reaches cinemas on 18 December. Our piece on [Villeneuve's own verdict on Dune: Part Three](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-dune-part-three-2/) has more on that film.

The Bond film itself has no confirmed release date. CinemaBlend says current estimates suggest 2028. Until the studio announces more casting, Pugh's message stays a friendly pitch.
