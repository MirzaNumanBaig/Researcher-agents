**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) plays District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner, and her twin Merrilee, in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opens in theatres on November 20.
- The film holds a 71% Rotten Tomatoes score from 59 reviews, according to ScreenRant, placing it third among the six films.
- Co-star [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) told Extra that Grace "carried our entire press tour".
- Grace told SiriusXM she would happily play Britney Spears in a planned biopic, if the singer was comfortable with it.

[Mckenna Grace](https://moduscarry.com/tag/mckenna-grace/) has spent the past week on the final stretch of the press tour for the new [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel, and the 20-year-old has plenty to say about a franchise she has loved since childhood. The film opens on November 20, and its first reviews landed on November 17.

## Why Mckenna Grace Hunger Games fans have noticed her

Grace is one of the series' most devoted followers, according to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2026/11/17/mckenna-grace-interview-hunger-games-maysilee-donner/92642682007/). She says she fell for the books while making the 2019 horror film *Annabelle Comes Home*, putting up Katniss posters in her on-set school room and carrying a Nerf bow and arrow.

That knowledge showed in her audition. The novel describes Maysilee wearing six necklaces at once, including a perfume bottle, a locket and a braided piece with daisies. Grace wore all six. She learned she had the part over a Zoom call in her trailer while shooting the romantic drama *Regretting You*, and says she was "weeping".

On set she kept pushing for book details, according to the same interview. She asked for Maysilee to be shown with coffee, because the novel mentions her love of caffeine, and she insisted on the mockingjay pin that Katniss later inherits. She also suggested bangs and a braid to director Francis Lawrence, a nod to a popular fan film. "Francis was probably so sick of me," she told USA Today.

## What the cast said at the New York premiere

At the New York premiere, Zada was generous about his co-star. "The film wouldn't be the same thing without her," he told Extra. Grace said she would miss touring with him and called the fan base "incredible".

She also explained her red-carpet look. Speaking about the Mockingjay gown, she said it began as a Capitol attempt to turn Katniss into an attraction and became a moment of rebellion. For more on the outfits, see our earlier posts on her [Mockingjay gown](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-mockingjay-gown/) and her [premiere wings](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-premiere-wings/).

Extra also spoke to [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke) and [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons). Zada plays young Haymitch Abernathy, the role [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson) made famous, and said the part let him support the younger actors around him.

## How critics have reacted

According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-rotten-tomatoes-score/), the prequel edges out both *Mockingjay* films on Rotten Tomatoes. *Catching Fire* still leads the franchise at 90%, and *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes* ranks lowest.

Reviewers credited the tighter pacing and clearer tone compared with the earlier prequel. They described Grace's performance as gutsy and resilient, with notable chemistry alongside Zada. Complaints centred on too many cameos and some franchise fatigue. ScreenRant's own review gave the film 6 out of 10. Some pundits expect an opening weekend as high as US$100 million, the outlet reports, though that remains a forecast.

## The Britney Spears question

Away from Panem, [Bustle](https://www.bustle.com/entertainment/mckenna-grace-britney-spears-fan-casting) reports that fans have campaigned for Grace to play Britney Spears. Universal is developing a biopic based on Spears' memoir *The Woman in Me*, with Jon M. Chu attached to direct.

Asked on SiriusXM's *Andy Cohen Live*, Grace said "of course", adding that a Britney greatest-hits CD was the only one she owned during her long drives to Los Angeles as a child actor. She said it would be a dream, "but only if she was OK with it". Chu has said he is not yet thinking about casting.

## What happens next

The film opens on November 20, and opening-weekend figures will show whether audiences agree with the critics. There is no word yet on whether more *Hunger Games* films will follow, as Suzanne Collins has not published another book since *Sunrise on the Reaping*.
