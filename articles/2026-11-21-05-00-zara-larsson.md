**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) has two songs tied to Grand Theft Auto VI, which launched on 19 November on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- Her version of "Midnight Sun" with PinkPantheress, titled "Midnight Sun (Girls Trip)", plays on the in-game radio station Flash FM.
- A brand-new Larsson song, "Fast Emotion", is on the companion release, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album.
- The album has 34 new songs and was made by Rockstar Games with Atlantic Records, according to Swedish outlet Metro Mode.

Zara Larsson is one of several Swedish artists heard in Grand Theft Auto VI, which reached players on 19 November. The pop singer appears in two places: on the game's pop radio station and on the new soundtrack album released the same day, according to [Metro Mode](https://metromode.se/en/noje/robyn-zara-larsson-och-tove-lo-har-ar-svenskarna-i-gta-6/).

## Where Larsson's songs appear

On Flash FM, the pop station in the game's Vice City, Larsson's "Midnight Sun (Girls Trip)" is in the rotation. Metro Mode describes it as her take on the title track of her album Midnight Sun, recorded with British artist PinkPantheress. The "Girls Trip" tag matches the title of the 2017 comedy [Girls Trip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_Trip), though the source does not explain the link, so it is best treated as a version name only.

Her second contribution is new. "Fast Emotion" is listed on [Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI%3A_The_Album), which Metro Mode says carries 34 newly written songs. It is available on Spotify, Apple Music and the other major streaming services.

## The Swedish line-up

Larsson is not alone. Metro Mode lists [Tove Lo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tove_Lo), whose song "Blood in Your Eyes" came out on 10 November and was heard in Rockstar's short video before the game opened for pre-load. It was written with Swedish hitmakers Ludvig Söderberg and [Ilya Salmanzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Salmanzadeh).

[Yung Lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yung_Lean) also has a prominent slot. His track "That's It", with Future and [Metro Boomin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Boomin), is the second song on the album and was among the first six released in September. Robyn hosts Flash FM, alongside American model [Alex Consani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Consani), the same report says.

## What else is on the pop station

Flash FM mixes older pop with current names. Metro Mode, citing the fan site GTA Base, says it pairs classics from [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), Madonna and [Mariah Carey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariah_Carey) with newer acts such as [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa), [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan), Charli xcx and [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish). For Australian listeners, that puts Larsson in the same playlist as Minogue.

Other stations carry big names too. [Bad Bunny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Bunny) runs the Latin channel, while [Lana Del Rey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lana_Del_Rey) and [Morgan Wallen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Wallen) lead the country station, according to the report, which credits GamesRadar for those details.

## What happens next

The game's original soundtrack, made by the French duo Justice, arrives on streaming services, CD and vinyl on 11 December. Justice also wrote "Flower in a Hurricane", the song in the launch trailer, which features [Tame Impala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tame_Impala), SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.
