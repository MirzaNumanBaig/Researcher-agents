**Quick summary**

- *The Northman*, the 2022 Viking revenge film starring Alexander Skarsgård, is set to leave Netflix in December 2026, according to ScreenRant.
- Robert Eggers directed it, and the cast includes Nicole Kidman, Ethan Hawke, Claes Bang and Anya Taylor-Joy.
- The film reportedly cost about US$70 million and earned roughly US$75.9 million worldwide, per Box Office Mojo as cited by ScreenRant.
- Critics praised it, but it struggled to find a big cinema audience.

[Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd)'s Viking epic *The Northman* is leaving Netflix in December, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/alexander-skarsgard-the-northman-leave-netflix-december-2026/), which leaves viewers only a few weeks to watch the film on the streaming service. For anyone who missed the brutal 2022 revenge story, or wants to see it again, the window is closing.

## What The Northman is about

[Robert Eggers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Eggers) directed the film after *The Witch* (2015) and *The Lighthouse* (2019). He wrote the screenplay with Icelandic novelist and poet Sjón. It draws on the Scandinavian legend of Amleth, the medieval revenge tale that also sits behind Shakespeare's *Hamlet*, as ScreenRant notes.

Skarsgård plays Prince Amleth. His childhood is destroyed when his uncle Fjölnir, played by [Claes Bang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claes_Bang), murders Amleth's father, King Aurvandil ([Ethan Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethan_Hawke)), and takes his mother, Queen Gudrún ([Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman)). The boy flees, swears vengeance and returns years later as a fearsome Viking warrior.

ScreenRant says Eggers kept up the focus on historical accuracy that marks his earlier films, working with historians, archaeologists and specialists in Norse mythology. The outlet also points to the long-take action sequences and the horror elements woven into the fantasy.

## Skarsgård's physical performance

The role is one of the Swedish actor's most demanding. ScreenRant reports that he went through an extensive physical transformation, and that he built Amleth's movement around the character's spiritual link to wolves and bears. Speaking to TIME in 2022, according to the outlet, he said the warrior identity Bjǫrnúlfr, meaning bear-wolf, shaped how he moved. He wanted the physical weight of a bear and the instincts of a wolf.

The film's emotional contrast comes from Olga, played by [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy), who offers Amleth a glimpse of a life beyond violence.

Skarsgård has long been tied to Norse imagery. His breakthrough international role was Eric Northman, a centuries-old Viking vampire, in HBO's [True Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Blood). He later played the title role in *The Legend of Tarzan* in 2016. ScreenRant says an Icelandic-saga Viking film was a long-held ambition, and *The Northman* gave him the chance to make it.

## Why it struggled at the box office

Despite strong reviews, ScreenRant says the film did not draw a large enough theatrical audience. It reportedly cost about US$70 million, and some estimates put the final figure higher after pandemic delays and added safety costs. By comparison, Eggers' earlier films were small independent productions.

ScreenRant cites Box Office Mojo for a worldwide gross of about US$75.9 million, which includes a later UK re-release. That figure sits only slightly above the reported budget, which helps explain why the film is remembered as a commercial disappointment even as its reputation has grown among film fans.

## What happens next

ScreenRant gives December 2026 as the departure month. The report available to us does not name an exact date, so check Netflix's own listing if you plan to watch. Films often move to other streaming services or rental platforms after leaving a catalogue, but no such plan has been reported for *The Northman*.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is The Northman leaving Netflix?

ScreenRant reports that it will be removed in December 2026. An exact day was not given in the report we read.

### Who stars in The Northman?

Alexander Skarsgård plays Amleth. The cast also includes Nicole Kidman, Ethan Hawke, Claes Bang and Anya Taylor-Joy.

### Did The Northman make money?

According to Box Office Mojo as cited by ScreenRant, it earned about US$75.9 million worldwide on a reported budget of around US$70 million, and the outlet describes its theatrical run as a struggle.
