## The Mockingjay wings Mckenna Grace wore in New York

The Mckenna Grace Mockingjay wings seen on the New York red carpet on Monday, 16 November, were built in roughly three days, according to the designer who made them. Claudia Marin, a full-time costume designer based in Los Angeles, [told People](https://people.com/designer-behind-mckenna-grace-mockingjay-look-breaks-down-cost-of-creating-wings-exclusive-12262544) in an exclusive interview that tight turnarounds are normal in her industry.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) wore a custom Pamela Rolland gown of liquid organza, set with gemstones in a diamond pattern. The wings were designed separately and added to complete the outfit. They were meant to recall the wings worn by Jennifer Lawrence as [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) in *[Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire)*.

Marin said the idea came from Grace's stylist, Molly Dickson, who planned the actor's looks for the press tour and brought Marin in. "Very little gets planned far ahead in this industry; it's pretty much week to week," Marin told the magazine.

## How the wings were built

Marin described a hands-on process. She began by patterning the base of the wings in foam and fabric, shaped around Grace's arm measurements. She then sewed the structure together and added boning channels so she could thread wire through for extra support.

The feathers came next. Marin said she used goose and silver pheasant feathers from a wholesaler she regularly works with, and dyed batches by hand in different shades of blue. She worked from a still of Lawrence in the original look, with the reference photo beside her as she sewed and glued.

One problem was that the film's wings were computer-generated, so an exact copy was not possible. Marin said she put her own spin on the design. She also feared running out of feathers, and each one had to sit in the right place so both sides matched.

## What custom wings cost

People reports that Marin estimates about 20 per cent of her business income goes on materials alone. Custom wings like these can cost customers between US$1,500 and US$3,500, she said, depending on the design, the materials and the timeline. Her shop, Angel Wings Los Angeles, also makes bird-style and angel designs.

Marin describes herself as a big fan of the series and said she read all the books growing up, calling the commission "surreal". She started out as a photographer who built props and sets for her shoots, then made a pair of wings and began renting them out on Instagram. That grew into a full-time career.

Her clients now include Madonna, [Heidi Klum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidi_Klum), [Addison Rae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addison_Rae), [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) and [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo), according to People.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who made Mckenna Grace's Mockingjay wings?

Claudia Marin, a 27-year-old Los Angeles costume designer who runs Angel Wings Los Angeles, made them. Grace's stylist, Molly Dickson, commissioned the piece.

### How long did the wings take to make?

Marin says she had about three days to design and finish them, which she describes as typical for the industry.
