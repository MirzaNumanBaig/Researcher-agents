**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is set to star in Maelstrom, a Netflix action thriller set during a hurricane, according to a report by Deadline on 26 October 2026.
- He replaces [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson), who left the project for reasons that have not been disclosed.
- [Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra) is directing, and no release date has been announced.
- Separately, Reeves's Toy Story 5 role as Duke Caboom has helped the film reach fourth place among the most-watched movies on Disney+ worldwide, per Collider.

Keanu Reeves has stepped into one of Netflix's more intriguing action projects. Deadline reported on 26 October 2026 that the actor will lead Maelstrom, a hurricane-set siege thriller, taking over from Alan Ritchson. The details below come from a recap by 25YearsLaterSite.com of Deadline's report, and Netflix representatives declined to comment to Deadline.

## What Keanu Reeves Maelstrom is about

According to that recap, the story follows a Federal Marshal assigned to protect the fiancée of an arms dealer who has turned witness. The task is to keep her alive through a single violent night. A hurricane cuts off communication and leaves them without backup, and then the dealer's men close in on their safe house.

Men's Journal, cited in the same piece, reported on 28 October that the film has been pitched as "Die Hard in a hurricane". The comparison is apt. [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard) (1988) set the template for the single-location siege, and [Under Siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Under_Siege) (1992) moved it onto a battleship.

The script is also older than many of its fans. Deadline reported that the spec was set up at Warner Bros. in 1993. The late Gregory Fitzpatrick wrote the first draft and Mark Bianculli wrote the current one. Jeff Robinov is producing with Tabitha Shick and Talking Pictures.

## Why the casting swap matters

Ritchson is best known as Jack Reacher on the Reacher series, where the character wins by sheer size. Reeves brings a different kind of screen presence. His best-known action work, from John Wick onwards, leans on precision and fatigue rather than bulk, and we have looked at [how Keanu Reeves reshaped action movies three times](https://moduscarry.com/keanu-reeves-action-movies/).

No report has said how the script treats the marshal, so how that difference plays out is only speculation. What is on record is that Ritchson's reasons for leaving remain undisclosed, and that is where the sources stop.

## A busy calendar for Reeves

Collet-Serra is a logical fit for a contained thriller. Just Jared, via Yahoo, pointed to his earlier films Carry-On, Non-Stop and The Shallows, all built around confined spaces and rising pressure.

The open question is timing. According to Deadline, Reeves's slate already includes the Warner Bros. sci-fi film Shiver and Ruben Östlund's The Entertainment System Is Down. Empire's October report added Sonic the Hedgehog 4 and an untitled LEGO movie, and said he is also eyeing James Wan's The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil. MovieWeb noted that no production date has been set for Maelstrom.

On the streaming side, he is already busy. We recently covered [Keanu Reeves's animated John Wick film](https://moduscarry.com/animated-john-wick-movie/) and the [continued streaming strength of John Wick: Chapter 4](https://moduscarry.com/john-wick-chapter-4-streaming/).

## Toy Story 5 lifts Reeves on Disney+

Collider reports that Toy Story 5, in which Reeves again voices Duke Caboom, has become a streaming hit. At the time of writing it was the fourth most-watched movie on Disney+ worldwide, behind the live-action Moana, which arrived on the platform late last month.

The numbers back that up. Collider says the film holds a "certified fresh" 92 per cent critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes and 94 per cent from audiences, the second-highest audience score in the franchise's history. Tom Hanks, Tim Allen and Joan Cusack also return in the film.

Collider adds that Reeves is due back as Shadow in Sonic the Hedgehog 4, directed by Jeff Fowler, on 19 March 2027.

## What happens next

The next beat for Maelstrom is the casting of the witness's fiancée, which MovieWeb flagged as the obvious step, along with a production start date. Neither has been announced.
