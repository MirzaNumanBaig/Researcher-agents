**Quick summary**

- [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan) has said guitarist Mike Bloomfield was the best player he ever heard, according to Far Out Magazine.
- Dylan told Playboy he wrote "Desolation Row", the 11-minute closer on Highway 61 Revisited, in the back of a New York taxi.
- American Songwriter recounts a tense card game at a Louisiana party during the 1976 Rolling Thunder Revue tour.
- A tribute night to Dylan, Neil Young and Van Morrison is scheduled for Saturday 28 November at Draíocht in Dublin.

This week's Bob Dylan stories revisit his admiration for guitarist Mike Bloomfield, the taxi ride behind one of his longest songs and a close call on the 1976 Rolling Thunder Revue tour. A Dublin venue has also listed a tribute show.

## Why Dylan still talks about Mike Bloomfield

Far Out Magazine says Dylan never practised scales; the guitar was a foundation for his lyrics, as it was for folk figures such as [Pete Seeger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Seeger) and [Woody Guthrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Guthrie).

When Dylan moved towards rock in the mid-1960s, he wanted something bluesier for the first studio sessions. That is where Bloomfield came in. The outlet notes that Dylan grew up on blues records, though he was never going to become a modern-day [Robert Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Johnson). Bloomfield's playing on Highway 61 Revisited, it says, cemented him as one of the best musicians Dylan worked with.

Dylan's own words, as quoted by Far Out, are blunt. He said he first heard Bloomfield at a Chicago club called The Bear and called him "the best guitar player I ever heard on any level". He also said he always felt Bloomfield should have stayed with him instead of joining [Paul Butterfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Butterfield).

## The taxi ride behind "Desolation Row"

A second Far Out piece looks at how Dylan wrote "Desolation Row". By its count, Dylan released nine albums in the 1960s, and this song closes Highway 61 Revisited, which came out in 1965.

Asked by Playboy where he wrote it, Dylan answered that he was in the back of a taxi cab. The outlet links the song's crowded, surreal imagery to Allen Ginsberg's poem Howl, and Dylan described that period as one of "city songs".

Mick Jagger, quoted by Far Out from The Guardian, called the street unreal and imagined an unforgiving place of strange characters. He also called it "a really lovely song, which shouldn't work with the imagery but does".

## A card game that went wrong in Louisiana

American Songwriter tells a darker story from 1976. After a 6 May Rolling Thunder Revue date in Lake Charles, Louisiana was cancelled because of poor ticket sales, Dylan and his crew went to the home of musician Bobby Charles for a Louisiana-style barbecue.

Roger McGuinn of The Byrds later told Mojo there were no plates or utensils, only alligator eaten by hand and washed down with beer. The man who caught it, known as Bird, carried a large Bowie knife.

Later, Bird played cards for money against guitarist Mick Ronson. Ronson's partner Suzi Ronson told Mojo that Bird threw the knife past Mick's face into the wall behind him, then laid a gun on the table. According to her, road manager Bob Neuwirth led Mick outside and away from the table. Everyone left the bayou unharmed, American Songwriter reports.

Away from the archive stories, the Journal of Music in Ireland lists a show called The Songs of Neil Young, Bob Dylan & Van Morrison, performed by The Muddy River Band. It is on Saturday 28 November at 8pm at Draíocht in Blanchardstown, West Dublin.

The listing promises the songs of [Neil Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_Young), Dylan and [Van Morrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Morrison) for fans of all ages. Listings are added by third parties, so check details with the venue.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who did Bob Dylan call the best guitarist he ever heard?
Dylan named Mike Bloomfield, whom he first heard at a Chicago club called The Bear. Far Out Magazine reports he said Bloomfield could flat pick and fingerpick.

### Where did Bob Dylan write "Desolation Row"?
Dylan told Playboy he wrote it in the back of a taxi cab. The song closes the 1965 album Highway 61 Revisited and runs about 11 minutes.

### What happened at the Rolling Thunder Revue party in Louisiana?
After a cancelled show in Lake Charles on 6 May 1976, the band visited Bobby Charles. A card game involving guitarist Mick Ronson turned frightening, but nobody was hurt, according to American Songwriter.

### When is the Dylan, Young and Morrison tribute show in Dublin?
It is on Saturday 28 November at 8pm at Draíocht, Blanchardstown, performed by The Muddy River Band.
