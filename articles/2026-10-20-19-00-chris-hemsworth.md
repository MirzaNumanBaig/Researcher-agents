**Quick summary**

- Muay Thai fighter Rodtang Jitmuangnon visited Chris Hemsworth on the set of Extraction 3 in Ratchaburi, Thailand, this week, according to ONE Championship.
- Rodtang gave Hemsworth a pair of personalised gloves, and both men shared photos and video on social media.
- Hemsworth is due to return as Thor in Avengers: Doomsday, which ONE Championship says is scheduled for this coming December.
- Rodtang fights Nong-O Hama on Friday, 13 November, in Bangkok.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) took a break from filming Extraction 3 this week to meet one of the biggest names in Muay Thai. [Rodtang Jitmuangnon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodtang_Jitmuangnon), the former ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Champion known as "The Iron Man", turned up unannounced on the Thailand set, ONE Championship reported on 20 October.

## A surprise visit in Ratchaburi

According to [ONE Championship](https://www.onefc.com/news/thor-meets-the-iron-man-chris-hemsworth-gets-surprise-visit-from-rodtang-while-filming-in-thailand/), the Australian actor is currently based in Ratchaburi, in Western Thailand, where he is shooting the third film in the Extraction action series. The promotion says Rodtang had long been a fan of Hemsworth's screen work, while Hemsworth follows combat sports and has supported Rodtang's career.

The pair documented the meeting online. Rodtang posted a video of the two men hugging. In it, he mimics the thunder grab associated with Hemsworth's Thor, and Hemsworth answers with Rodtang's trademark Muay Thai head shake, ONE Championship said.

## What each of them wrote

Hemsworth shared photos on Instagram. He thanked Rodtang for the personalised gloves and said they were going "straight to the trophy room", calling the fighter "all class" and an "absolute champion".

Rodtang, whom ONE Championship describes as 29, wrote that he was grateful for the chance to meet Hemsworth and added that he would "always love and respect" him.

## Rodtang's next fight

The visit came as Rodtang finishes a training camp. ONE Championship says he meets former ONE Bantamweight Muay Thai World Champion Nong-O Hama at The Inner Circle 38 on Friday, 13 November, at Lumpinee Stadium in Bangkok. The bout is a quarterfinal in the 2026-2027 ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Grand Prix, an eight-man bracket.

Other quarterfinals listed by the promotion include reigning champion Asadula Imangazaliev against Aslamjon Ortikov, and [Shimon Yoshinari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimon_Yoshinari) against Ramadan Ondash. The winner takes a silver Grand Prix belt and a shot at the undisputed flyweight title. The card streams to members of The Inner Circle at live.onefc.com.

## What happens next

After Extraction 3 wraps, Hemsworth is due to play Thor again in Avengers: Doomsday, which ONE Championship says arrives in cinemas this coming December. The role is part of the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe). The source gives no release date for Extraction 3 itself.
