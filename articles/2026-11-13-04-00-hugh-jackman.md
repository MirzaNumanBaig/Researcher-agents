**Quick summary**

- An updated Writers Guild of America listing credits [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) with "Additional Literary Material" on *Avengers: Doomsday*.
- The credit was reported on 12 November 2026 by MovieWeb and Toy People, and it is the strongest sign yet that both actors are involved in the film.
- Neither is a main screenwriter: Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron are the credited co-writers.
- A writing credit does not guarantee an on-screen appearance, and Marvel has not announced their roles.

Hugh Jackman has written his way into *Avengers: Doomsday*, at least on paper. According to an updated Writers Guild of America listing reported on 12 November 2026, Jackman and Ryan Reynolds are both credited with "Additional Literary Material" on Marvel's next big team-up film. It is the clearest sign so far that Wolverine and Deadpool will be part of it.

## What the credit actually says

The listing, as [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/avengers-doomsday-ryan-reynolds-hugh-jackman/) describes it, names [Stephen McFeely](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Markus_and_Stephen_McFeely) and [Michael Waldron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Waldron) as the film's official co-writers. Jackman and Reynolds sit in a second tier of "additional" credits, alongside [Destin Daniel Cretton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destin_Daniel_Cretton), [Jeff Loveness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Loveness), [Andrew Lanham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Lanham), Chris McKenna, Erik Sommers and Eric Pearson.

Toy People, which also reported the listing, notes that neither actor is a primary screenwriter. Credits of this kind, it says, generally point to actors contributing dialogue or material for their own characters. That is an interpretation rather than something Marvel has confirmed, and the studio has not commented in the sources we reviewed.

Cretton's name drew its own attention. He directed *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* and *Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings*, and Toy People suggests he may have helped shape material involving those characters.

## The campaign that led here

The credit follows a long-running public pitch. MovieWeb recounts that Reynolds turned up at San Diego Comic-Con in July dressed as a cosplayer in a grey Deadpool suit and asked [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) for a role. In August, Jackman picked up the joke at the D23 Expo, telling the crowd he could "help round out the cast" while Reynolds heckled from off-stage.

Treat the campaign as a running gag with a real payoff. The listing marks only the second Marvel Studios project for the pair, after their official franchise debut together in *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

## A very crowded cast

*Avengers: Doomsday* is already enormous. MovieWeb says the five-hour chair-reveal livestream last year announced 27 characters, including members of the Fantastic Four, the Thunderbolts and the returning Fox X-Men, with Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom. Chris Evans, Hayley Atwell, Kathryn Newton, Mabel Cadena, Alex Livinalli and Sandra Hüller have since been added.

Toy People adds that [Anthony Mackie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Mackie) let slip on 11 November that [Paul Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bettany) will return as Vision. MovieWeb also points to the post-credits scene of the *VisionQuest* finale, which brought back Elizabeth Olsen as Wanda Maximoff. Against that backdrop, two more returning names would hardly stand out.

The news lands while Jackman is in Australia. PerthNow reported this week that the 58-year-old was seen in the surf at Bondi, his former neighbourhood, during his first visit to Sydney with partner Sutton Foster. Nothing in that report links the trip to the Marvel film. Marvel has not said whether Wolverine or Deadpool will appear, or how large either role would be. MovieWeb stresses that Writers Guild credits do not automatically mean cameos. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hugh Jackman confirmed for Avengers: Doomsday?

Not officially. The Writers Guild listing credits him with additional literary material, which MovieWeb and Toy People both read as strong evidence he is involved, but Marvel Studios has not announced a role.

### What is "Additional Literary Material"?

It is a Writers Guild credit for people who contributed writing beyond the main screenplay credit. Here the main credited co-writers are Stephen McFeely and Michael Waldron.

### Who else received the same credit?

Destin Daniel Cretton, Jeff Loveness, Andrew Lanham, Chris McKenna, Erik Sommers and Eric Pearson, alongside Jackman and Reynolds, according to MovieWeb.
