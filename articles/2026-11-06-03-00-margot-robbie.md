**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) wore a sheer, pleated ivory Chanel slip to the brand's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on Thursday, according to W Magazine.
- She sat in the front row next to [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), and both are Chanel ambassadors, reports The West Australian.
- Under the dress she wore high-waisted jersey briefs, a nod to Coco Chanel's use of sportswear fabric.
- Her blonde bob was shorter and brighter than earlier this year, Yahoo reports.
- Robbie has been filming an Ocean's Eleven prequel, so public appearances have been rare lately.

Margot Robbie made one of her few public appearances of the season at Chanel's first runway shows in Australia, arriving in Sydney on Thursday in a sheer ivory slip and a freshly lightened bob. The look opened the brand's Cruise 2026/27 presentation, and it quickly became the most discussed outfit of a star-heavy day.

## What Margot Robbie wore to the Chanel Sydney show

According to [W Magazine](https://www.wmagazine.com/fashion/margot-robbie-chanel-cruise-show-sheer), the slip came straight from Chanel's spring 2027 runway. It was covered in tiny translucent pleats, with a built-in sheer bralette finished by a fabric flower. A black Chanel flap bag completed the outfit.

The detail that drew the most attention sat underneath. Instead of lace, Robbie wore high-waisted jersey briefs. W Magazine points out that Chanel made its name by turning jersey, once a fabric for men's underwear, into women's sportswear in the 20th century. The briefs, in other words, were a deliberate reference to the house's own history.

CinemaBlend's Riley Utley also praised the dress, singling out the sparkles along its border and the large flower at the neckline. She noted that Robbie has worn Chanel to a cruise show before: in 2023, the year of Barbie, she chose a black bikini top, a gold vest and bell-bottom jeans.

## A front row full of Australian names

The show took place at The Cutaway at Barangaroo. The West Australian reports that Chanel staged two shows that day, its first ever runway presentations in Australia. Kidman wore a black blazer and trousers. Surfer Jack Robinson, who partnered with Chanel earlier this year to promote its J12 watch, sat beside the pair.

Other guests included Olympic champion Cathy Freeman, singer Kylie Minogue, director Baz Luhrmann and DJ Dom Dolla. Bruno Pavlovsky, Chanel's president of fashion, told The Australian the brand had wanted to do something special in Australia and was now ready. The shows coincided with the opening of a new Chanel flagship store in Sydney, and a renovated Melbourne store is due to be unveiled later this month.

## The bob gets a brighter update

Hair got nearly as much coverage as the dress. Yahoo reports that Robbie has worn a bob since spring 2026, first with choppy bangs at an earlier Chanel show, and that it turned darker and warmer over the summer. In Sydney it was lighter again, with layers of baby blonde, butter blonde and pale gold, and it sat a few inches above her collarbone. The outlet says she styled it straight with a slight flick at the ends.

Yahoo describes the cut as a "power bob", one of its top hair trends for winter 2026, and says her colour is unusually summery for November. That is the outlet's view rather than anything Robbie has said.

## Why we have seen so little of her

CinemaBlend notes that Robbie has been largely out of the spotlight since her method-dressing run for [Wuthering Heights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights) earlier in 2026, apart from the Met Gala in spring. The reason, it says, is work. She has spent the summer and autumn filming an Ocean's Eleven prequel with [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper), and People saw the pair filming in France in September. Josh Gad and Monica Barbaro have also joined the cast.

W Magazine adds that Robbie has been busy with her production company, LuckyChap, which is backing the 2027 psychological sports drama Nasty, starring [Jenna Ortega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Ortega), and a live-action adaptation of The Sims. W Magazine puts her age at 36.

Her arrival was styled too. Who What Wear reports she landed at Sydney Airport earlier in the week in black capri leggings, a three-quarter-sleeve top, ballet flats, a bandana, sunglasses and an east-west Chanel bag.

## What happens next

No new Robbie appearances have been confirmed in the sources. The next project CinemaBlend expects her to dress for is the untitled Ocean's prequel, which is still filming. In the meantime, Chanel's Melbourne store reopening later this month is the next date on the brand's Australian calendar.
