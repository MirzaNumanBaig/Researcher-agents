**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) and Evan Goldberg are reportedly attached to write a new Home Alone film for Disney, according to trade reports on Friday, 20 November 2026.
- [Macaulay Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macaulay_Culkin) is said to be in talks to return as Kevin McCallister, now a father.
- The story is based on Culkin's own pitch, and the project is in early development with no official announcement yet.
- Rogen and Goldberg will first direct an original horror comedy for Universal, according to Toy People.

Seth Rogen is reportedly writing a new Home Alone film, with Macaulay Culkin set to come back as Kevin McCallister. Multiple outlets reported on Friday, 20 November, that Rogen and his longtime partner [Evan Goldberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evan_Goldberg) have been brought in to script a Disney project built on an idea Culkin pitched himself. Disney has not confirmed the news.

## What has been reported about the Seth Rogen Home Alone project

According to [Us Weekly](https://www.usmagazine.com/entertainment/news/macaulay-culkin-to-star-in-home-alone-sequel-by-seth-rogen/), Culkin, 46, is in talks to return to the franchise in a sequel developed by Disney and 20th Century Studios. Rogen and Goldberg are reportedly attached to write the script. Us Weekly says it contacted Disney for comment.

[Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/tracewilliamcowen/home-alone-new-movie-macaulay-culkin-seth-rogen) notes that both Variety and Deadline carried the news, and that the trades disagree on the label: Variety calls it a reboot, Deadline a sequel. Complex also credits Puck's [Matthew Belloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Belloni) with breaking the story first. It describes the film as in early development.

Toy People adds that Rogen and Goldberg will produce through their Point Grey Pictures banner. They are also expected to share writing duties while working with Culkin on the story.

## Culkin's pitch: Kevin as a single father

The plot reportedly stays close to what Culkin floated publicly last year. In that version, Kevin is a widower or divorcee raising a son and working too much to spend time with him. The boy, furious, locks his father out of the house and sets traps to keep him away.

Culkin also described the house as a metaphor for the relationship, with Kevin forced to win back his child's love, according to Toy People. Deadline reported on Friday that the pitch does centre on Kevin as a father and that he is the story's main point of view.

Culkin once compared the tone to a "Liar, Liar kind of thing", Us Weekly reports, quoting an earlier interview. In July, Belloni said on his podcast that Culkin had been seen on the Disney lot in Burbank and that the studio was keen on his idea.

## Why the franchise needs a reset

Home Alone, directed by [Chris Columbus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Columbus_(filmmaker)), launched in 1990, and Culkin returned for 1992's Home Alone 2: Lost in New York. Us Weekly puts the combined worldwide gross of those two films at US$836.3 million.

The sequels without him did far less. The 1997 threequel, with [Alex D. Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_D._Linz) and directed by [Raja Gosnell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Gosnell), made US$79.1 million. Later made-for-TV entries followed, along with Home Sweet Home Alone on Disney+ in 2021.

Columbus was blunt about the follow-ups. He told The Hollywood Reporter in December 2025 that the franchise had been revisited with "really bad sequels", according to Us Weekly. Complex makes the same point more gently, saying none of the post-Culkin material has had a fraction of the impact of the first two films.

## Where Rogen is at right now

The Home Alone news lands in a busy stretch for Rogen. He and Goldberg co-wrote Superbad and later co-wrote and co-directed This Is the End, and their series The Studio has drawn acclaim, per Toy People. The pair are due to direct an original horror comedy for Universal before turning fully to Home Alone.

In a separate interview, Rogen, 44, told the Happy Sad Confused podcast that casting directors often assumed he was a messenger when he went to auditions early in his career, [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/seth-rogen-started-auditioning-young-195658000.html) reports. He said he rarely won parts by auditioning, and found his footing in comedy by working with Judd Apatow. ## What happens next

No director, release date or formal casting has been announced. Disney is yet to comment, so the details above remain reports from trade outlets and not studio confirmation. Rogen and Goldberg's Universal horror comedy is reportedly first in line. Watch for an official announcement from Disney and 20th Century Studios, and for any word on who directs.
