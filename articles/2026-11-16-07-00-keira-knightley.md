**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley)'s Netflix spy thriller [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) is No. 3 on IMDb's most popular TV shows chart, according to Comic Book Resources.
- Season 2 premiered on 5 November and holds a 7.2 user rating on IMDb from 81,000 reviews as of 14 November.
- Critics have given it 96% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), and it is rated Certified Fresh.
- A third season has not been confirmed.

Black Doves Season 2 has settled into the top three of IMDb's most popular TV chart, just over a week after it arrived on Netflix. Comic Book Resources ([CBR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources)) reported on 15 November that the show sits behind MobLand and Nocturne, with Knightley's spy drama the third most-watched title on the list.

## Where Black Doves Season 2 stands

The chart is built from the activity of IMDb users, so it measures interest rather than raw viewing hours. Still, the climb follows a strong opening week. As of 14 November, CBR says the series carries a 7.2-star rating from 81,000 reviews, a count it expects to grow the longer Season 2 is available.

That sits alongside our earlier report that the show reached [No. 1 on Netflix worldwide](https://moduscarry.com/black-doves-netflix-number-one/). Netflix released every episode at once, as it did for the first season, and each season runs to six episodes of just under an hour.

## What the critics are saying

CBR puts the Season 2 Rotten Tomatoes score at 96%, matching the Certified Fresh rating of the first season. It quotes TheWrap's Liam Matthews, who described the show as "like James (or Jane) Bond by way of Quentin Tarantino". The [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) and [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino) comparison has followed the series since it debuted in December 2024.

Anie Han of The Hollywood Reporter, also quoted by CBR, singled out the tone as the show's "not-so-secret weapon", saying it carries the series over narrative stumbles. Todo Alicante's review makes a similar point, calling it one of Netflix's sharpest spy thrillers and praising its mix of violence and wit.

## The story so far

Created by Joe Barton, Black Doves follows a spies-for-hire organisation that sells secrets to the highest bidder. Knightley plays Helen, an operative whose cover is a life as a politician's wife. [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) plays Sam, her partner and a contract killer.

According to Todo Alicante, Helen is now married to the British Prime Minister. That puts the school run at 10 Downing Street alongside her undercover work. Sam is trying to leave his old trade behind, but a string of violent deaths among former agents pulls both of them into an older conspiracy.

[Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire) plays the spymaster whose disappearance Helen investigates. [Ella Lily Hyland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Lily_Hyland) and [Gabrielle Creevy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabrielle_Creevy) are the pair of hitwomen the review singles out for their banter in the middle of shootouts. Ambika Mod joins as Laila, a new and deadly adversary.

Todo Alicante also notes the show's origin in a real scandal: the revelation that undercover police officers in Britain had spent years under false identities inside activist groups, with some forming relationships and starting families. It says the series has moved well beyond that inspiration.

## Awards and audience

Season 1 earned Knightley a Golden Globe nomination for best actress in a television drama in 2025, according to CBR. She was also nominated at the Critics' Choice Awards, Whishaw won the Gotham TV Award for outstanding supporting performance in a drama series, and the show received a GLAAD Media Awards nomination for outstanding new series.

Todo Alicante says the first season drew more than 63 million views on its debut. CBR describes the show as one of Netflix's bigger sleepers, since it has stayed relatively quiet in the conversation despite its numbers.

## What happens next

Netflix has not announced a third season. CBR says the show's popularity with fans and critics suggests a renewal may not be far off, but that is the outlet's reading, not a statement from the streamer. Both seasons are streaming now on Netflix.
