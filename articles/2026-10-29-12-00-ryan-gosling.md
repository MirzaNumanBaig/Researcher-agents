**Quick summary**

- A report from Knight Edge Media claims Eva Mendes will appear in *Star Wars: Starfighter* alongside her partner Ryan Gosling.
- The outlet says the part is minor: an intergalactic cantina owner.
- Lucasfilm has not confirmed the casting, so it remains a rumour.
- The film is scheduled to reach cinemas on 28 May 2027.

A new report says [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) may not be travelling to a galaxy far, far away alone. According to entertainment site GeekTyrant, which is relaying a claim from Knight Edge Media, [Eva Mendes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_Mendes) will have a small role in *Star Wars: Starfighter*, which would mark a rare return to acting for her.

## What the report says

Knight Edge Media told its readers it had "exclusively confirmed" that Mendes appears in the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) film in "just a minor role" as an intergalactic cantina owner, [GeekTyrant reports](https://geektyrant.com/news/star-wars-starfighter-rumor-reveals-eva-mendes-role-alongside-ryan-gosling). The detail is a cameo-sized part, not a co-lead.

The talk is not new. GeekTyrant says rumours about Mendes joining the production have circulated for about a year, with some reports suggesting she was seen on set while filming took place in the summer of 2025. None of that has been confirmed by the studio.

One thing is clear: Lucasfilm has not officially announced Mendes's involvement. GeekTyrant itself tells readers to treat her casting and the character details as rumour for now.

## Why it would matter

If the report holds, it would be Mendes's first live-action film role since *Lost River* in 2014, according to GeekTyrant. That film was also Gosling's directorial debut, and she played a cabaret performer in it.

Since then she has stepped back from acting to raise her family. GeekTyrant says her only credit in the years after was a voice part as a yoga instructor in the animated series *Bluey* in 2021.

Mendes has been open about why she left. In a 2022 interview quoted by GeekTyrant, she said she grew tired of "fighting for the good roles" and decided to build her own opportunities as a producer instead. She added that she was "keeping it in the home with my kids" for now, while allowing that "who knows what I will do in the future".

She has also said before that working with Gosling was the one thing that could bring her back to acting, GeekTyrant notes. That context makes the rumour plausible, though plausible is not the same as confirmed.

## A Ghost Rider footnote

The same Knight Edge Media update touched on another Gosling project. The outlet reported that Mendes is not currently expected to return as reporter Roxanne Simpson from 2007's *Ghost Rider*, in the Marvel adaptation Gosling is attached to lead. The outlet added: "That could change, but it's not expected at this time."

## What happens next

*Star Wars: Starfighter* is due in cinemas on 28 May 2027, per GeekTyrant. Until Lucasfilm releases a cast list or trailer, the Mendes casting rests on a single outlet's claim. We will update this page if the studio responds.
