**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) is in Sydney with girlfriend [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster), her first visit to the city.
- They are staying at his Bondi penthouse, valued by one industry source at about $15m to $15.5m.
- Jackman told The Daily Telegraph it was "great" to be home and that the couple are "doing great".
- Some online users criticised the choice of home, which the Daily Mail says he kept in his divorce settlement.

Hugh Jackman has brought Sutton Foster to Sydney, and the couple spent this week at his Bondi penthouse, a trip that has drawn fresh attention to the property itself. The Hugh Jackman Bondi penthouse, a top-floor apartment at the northern end of the beach, is where the pair have based themselves during a short break in Australia.

## A morning at North Bondi

According to [realestate.com.au](https://www.realestate.com.au/news/inside-hugh-jackmans-15m-bondi-beach-lovenest/), the couple were seen walking hand in hand at Bondi on Wednesday. The site cites The Daily Telegraph for the detail that they began with an early session at North Bondi Surf Club before heading along Campbell Parade and nearby Hall Street.

They later set off on the Bondi to Bronte coastal walk. Jackman, who has owned a home at Bondi for more than a decade, told the Telegraph it was "great" to be back. Asked about Foster's first trip to Sydney, he said: "We're doing great."

The Daily Mail reports the pair landed in Australia on Tuesday and went straight to the apartment, where Jackman, 58, pointed out the sights from the balcony to Foster, 51. According to realestate.com.au, it is thought to be his first stay in the Bondi home since December 2024.

## What the Bondi apartment is worth

The 125 square metre, three-bedroom apartment sits in the Ben Buckler block. Realestate.com.au says Jackman bought it for $5,925,000 in 2015 with help from his friend and agent Paul Dainty. Its figures put the price at nearly three times that today, although the valuation is an informal one: "Probably about $15m or $15.5m", one industry source said.

The comparison comes from the neighbours. Film producer [Rebel Penfold-Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebel_Penfold-Russell) paid $12m for a ground-floor three-bedroom unit in February 2023, and agents quoted by the site say a top-floor apartment would sell well above that. The block has only six homes.

The apartment was listed for permanent rent at $10,000 a week, but realestate.com.au says it has not been on that market since February 2022. Insiders told the site Jackman plans to keep it as his Australian base.

## The divorce and the reaction online

Jackman's long marriage to [Deborra-Lee Furness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborra-Lee_Furness) ended in a divorce finalised in 2025, according to the Daily Mail and realestate.com.au. The Mail says he kept the Bondi home in the settlement, while the couple's New York penthouse was listed for sale.

The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16282743/hugh-jackman-sutton-foster-marital-home-deborralee-furness.html) says some people online were unimpressed that he took Foster there, quoting one commenter who called it "bit tacky". That is the view of social media users, not a statement from either of them, and neither Jackman nor Foster has been reported as responding to it.

## An old career lesson resurfaces

Separately, Far Out Magazine this week revisited a story from early in Jackman's career. In 1996 he turned down the lead in the musical The Boy From Oz, the story of singer-songwriter [Peter Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Allen_(musician)), because he wanted to be taken seriously for film work. By his own account, he then did not work in film for two years.

[Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/1996-hugh-jackman-turned-down-dream-role/) says he vowed never to "disobey my heart" again. When producer [Robert Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Fox_(producer)) later called about a Broadway production, Jackman said he cut in and accepted. The role earned him a Tony Award for best actor in a musical, eight years after he first said no.

## What happens next

No dates have been given for how long Jackman and Foster will stay in Sydney, and the reports describe the visit as brief downtime. Realestate.com.au says the apartment is expected to remain his Australian base.
