**Quick summary**

- [Yahoo Lifestyle Australia](https://au.lifestyle.yahoo.com/keanu-reeves-replace-alan-ritchson-100000061.html) reports, citing Deadline, that Keanu Reeves will lead the Netflix film Maelstrom.
- He replaces Alan Ritchson, who dropped out for reasons that haven't been disclosed.
- Jaume Collet-Serra is directing. Netflix declined to comment on the casting.
- The story is a hurricane-night thriller built on a 1993 spec script.
- Reeves also closed a Dogstar tour in Minneapolis on 23 October.

Keanu Reeves is reportedly set to star in Maelstrom, a Netflix thriller that has lost its original lead. Sources told Deadline that the actor will take over from [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson), who left the project for undisclosed reasons, according to a report by [Yahoo Lifestyle Australia](https://au.lifestyle.yahoo.com/keanu-reeves-replace-alan-ritchson-100000061.html) published on 27 October.

Netflix hasn't confirmed anything. Reps for the streamer declined to comment to Deadline about the casting, so for now this is a trade-press report and not an official announcement.

## What is Maelstrom about?

Netflix hasn't released an official logline, but Yahoo's account of the Deadline report describes a story that plays out over a single night. A US Federal Marshal is assigned to protect the fiancée of a dangerous arms dealer. She plans to testify against her partner in exchange for witness protection.

Then a hurricane makes direct landfall. Outside communication goes down and police reinforcements can't get through. The Marshal and the fiancée have to survive both the storm and a stream of armed henchmen sent to kill her.

[Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra) is directing. The script has an unusual history: it is a resurrected 1993 spec script by Gregory Fitzpatrick, recently revised by Mark Bianculli.

## A busy run of projects for Reeves

[Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) has been linked with several projects lately, and Maelstrom is the latest. According to The Hollywood Reporter, as relayed by Yahoo, he is in talks to join the cast of The Gangster, The Cop, The Devil. [James Wan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Wan) is set to direct Paramount Pictures' reboot of the 2019 Korean action-crime thriller, which follows a police officer and a gangster who reluctantly team up to hunt a serial killer.

In June, Deadline reported that Reeves would join a new Lego movie. It would reunite him with his Toy Story 4 director [Josh Cooley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Cooley) on a live-action and animation hybrid, built around Reeves' character.

Reeves was most recently seen in the comedy drama Outcome, which streamed on Apple TV+. Next year he will voice the fan-favourite character Shadow in Sonic the Hedgehog 4, per the same report.

## Dogstar close their tour in Minneapolis

Away from film, Reeves was on stage this month. According to [Glide Magazine](https://glidemagazine.com/332097/dogstar-keanu-reeves-pay-tribute-to-minneapolis-alt-rock-history-at-the-fillmore-photos/), Dogstar finished their tour at the Fillmore Minneapolis on 23 October. The band, with Reeves on bass, played The Replacements' "Can't Hardly Wait", and singer Bret Domrose paid tribute to the Minneapolis band, the magazine said.
