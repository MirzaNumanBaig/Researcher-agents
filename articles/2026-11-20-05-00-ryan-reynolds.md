**Quick summary**

- Former Premier League striker Kevin Phillips says Wrexham's owners can do little more than keep backing manager Phil Parkinson, according to the Daily Star.
- Wrexham sit 15th in the Championship with 18 points from 15 matches.
- The club drew 1-1 with Wolves earlier this month, a day after chief executive Michael Williamson left.
- Club director Shaun Harvey has taken over as interim chief executive. Wrexham host Lincoln City on Saturday.

[Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) and [Rob Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney) have been told there is a limit to what Hollywood money and star power can do for Wrexham. Speaking to the Daily Star, former Premier League Golden Boot winner Kevin Phillips said the co-owners can mostly keep supporting the manager as the club tries to climb the Championship table.

## What Kevin Phillips said about Wrexham's owners

According to the [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/sport/football/ryan-reynolds-rob-mac-told-37895395), Phillips gave his view through BestBettingSites.co.uk. The former Sunderland striker said he does not think there is much more the pair can do. In his view, the job is to keep backing Phil Parkinson, which they have done.

Phillips pointed to the money behind the manager. He said Parkinson has been given funds when he asked for them and was allowed to bring in several players over the summer. Off the pitch and commercially, he said, the owners do everything right. After that, he argued, it is up to the manager and players to deliver on the field.

He also had a blunt word about the division itself. The Championship is a hard league to escape, he said, and a team needs some luck as well as quality. Even so, he believes Wrexham are still in a good position and that others in the league admire what the club is doing.

## Where Wrexham stand in the table

The numbers explain the caution. The Daily Star reports that Wrexham host Lincoln City on Saturday sitting 15th with 18 points from 15 matches. That is a long way from the quick climbs the club made in the divisions below.

The paper describes this as the first time in the owners' tenure that ambition and commercial strength cannot guarantee an immediate rise. Reynolds and Rob Mac were at the Racecourse Ground earlier this month to watch a 1-1 draw with Wolves. That result followed an impressive win over Middlesbrough, one of the sides chasing promotion.

## Why the results have stalled

Phillips blamed a mix of injuries and small errors rather than a lack of quality. He said key defenders have been missing, and the team has made mistakes at set pieces. He added that Wrexham have probably drawn too many games and have lacked an edge going forward, which he linked to injuries among their attacking players.

His outlook was still positive. He said the team can perform against the best in the league and, once players return and the errors stop, should be challenging for the play-offs by the end of the season.

## A boardroom change off the pitch

The Wolves draw came less than 24 hours after a boardroom reshuffle. According to the Daily Star, chief executive Michael Williamson left after two years in the role, and club director Shaun Harvey stepped in on an interim basis.

Phillips said he does not expect the change to reach the dressing room. He said it will be mentioned, but an experienced manager like Parkinson will tell his players it cannot affect them. In his words, it is above their level and the squad should focus on results.

## What happens next

Wrexham's immediate test is Lincoln City on Saturday. The club have not announced a permanent replacement for Williamson, so Harvey's interim role is the detail to watch. For the owners, the plan outlined by Phillips is simple: stay patient and keep backing Parkinson.
