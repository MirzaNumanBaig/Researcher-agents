**Quick summary**

- A scooper called Cartier Films says [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) "might" play Daniel Ketch in Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider*, according to SuperHeroHype on 2 November 2026.
- The claim is an unconfirmed rumour, and the scooper does not fully commit to it.
- Marvel Studios has not announced anything about Storrie or his role.
- *Ghost Rider* stars [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) and is due on 28 July 2028.

A new rumour links [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) to a specific part in Marvel's *Ghost Rider*: Daniel Ketch, a character from the comics. The report, covered by [SuperHeroHype](https://www.superherohype.com/movies/703350-ghost-rider-rumor-connor-storrie-playing-ryan-gosling-mcu-movie), is unconfirmed, and Marvel Studios has said nothing publicly about his involvement.

## Where the Daniel Ketch rumour comes from

The claim comes from Cartier Films, a scooper who posted it in a blog entry. The wording is hedged. According to SuperHeroHype, the post says Storrie "might be taking on the role of Daniel Ketch", which suggests the information is not settled.

Storrie, the 26-year-old star of [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), had already been linked to the film. SuperHeroHype notes he was reportedly in talks with the studio about a villain role, and that fans had been guessing he was up for that part. This new report points somewhere different, which is one more reason to treat both as speculation.

## Who Daniel Ketch is in Marvel Comics

In the comics, Ketch is described as another Spirit of Vengeance. He is also the brother of Johnny Blaze, the first Ghost Rider. That family link is what makes the rumour interesting for fans, since it would tie a new character directly to the franchise's best-known name.

None of this has been confirmed for the film. Whether the movie would use that relationship at all is unknown.

## What is known about the Ghost Rider film

Ryan Gosling is set to play the title hero. [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) will direct, a detail announced at Comic-Con earlier this year. Levy has already worked with Marvel Studios on 2024's *Deadpool & Wolverine*, and he directed Gosling on [*Star Wars*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars): Starfighter, which is due next year.

The film is listed for 28 July 2028, and SuperHeroHype says it will be one of Marvel's first projects after 2027's *Avengers: Secret Wars*. It is still unclear which version of the character Gosling will play. [Gabriel Luna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Luna) previously played Robbie Reyes in *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*, though the MCU has not acknowledged that character yet. Before him, [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage) played Johnny Blaze in two films.

## What happens next

Nothing is official until Marvel Studios or Storrie's representatives say so. With the release more than a year and a half away on the current schedule, casting news could arrive at any point, or not for some time. We will update this story if either side confirms or denies it.
