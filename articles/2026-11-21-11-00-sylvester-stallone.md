**Quick summary**

- *I Play Rocky*, a film about how [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) wrote *Rocky* and fought to star in it, opened wide in theaters on November 20, according to CultureMap Austin.
- Peter Farrelly directed it from a script by Peter Gamble, and Anthony Ippolito plays the young Stallone.
- CultureMap Austin's reviewer called it an old-fashioned crowd-pleaser and praised Ippolito's physical and vocal resemblance to Stallone.
- LIFE magazine marked the release with a photo gallery of Stallone alongside co-stars and fellow action stars.

The film that retells how Sylvester Stallone fought to play Rocky Balboa is now in cinemas. *I Play Rocky* opened wide on November 20, and the first review in our sources is a warm one. Around the release, LIFE has also dug into its archive for pictures of Stallone with the famous people he worked and socialised with over the decades.

## What I Play Rocky is about

The story starts in the early 1970s, when Stallone was a struggling actor living on bit parts. CultureMap Austin says the film shows him agreeing to a softcore film just to pay the bills. His life begins to turn after he meets his future wife, Sasha, played by AnnaSophia Robb, who urges him to keep chasing his writing.

The couple move from New York to Los Angeles. Stallone writes several screenplays, and one catches the eye of producers Irwin Winkler (P.J. Byrne) and Robert Chartoff (Toby Kebbell). They pass on it but tell him to keep going.

The spark for *Rocky* comes from a real fight, the bout between [Muhammad Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad_Ali) and Chuck Wepner, in which the underdog Wepner lasted far longer than anyone expected. Stallone writes the script quickly. The producers like it, but they do not like his insistence on playing the lead. That standoff is the heart of the film.

## How the first review sees it

CultureMap Austin's reviewer says the film works as an old-fashioned crowd-pleaser, and singles out Ippolito. According to the review, his physical and vocal imitation of Stallone becomes "scarily believable" within the first five minutes. It adds that Ippolito is starring in a film for the first time, which echoes Stallone's own position when he wrote himself the lead in *Rocky*.

The review says the film is accessible even if you have not memorised *Rocky*, and that Farrelly and Gamble keep it light with a fair amount of humour. It also points to two behind-the-scenes details that may surprise casual viewers: how rare it was for a writer with no starring credit to write himself a lead role, and the arrival of the Steadicam, a smooth camera-operating system that was only beginning to be used in the year *Rocky* was made.

The supporting cast named in the review includes Tracy Letts, Jay Duplass, Matt Dillon and Stephan James. The reviewer describes the film as heartwarming, funny and nostalgic and puts its running time at about two hours.

## Stallone and his famous company

LIFE's gallery, titled "He Played Rocky: Sylvester Stallone and Friends", shows how wide his circle became. It includes a 1981 party photo with the Brazilian footballer Pele for their film *Victory*, and pictures with [Arnold Schwarzenegger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Schwarzenegger) and [Bruce Willis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Willis) at a 1992 event for Planet Hollywood, the movie-themed restaurant chain all three backed.

Co-stars feature heavily. There is [Dolly Parton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolly_Parton), his partner in the 1984 comedy *Rhinestone*, a film LIFE says Stallone recently called his favourite besides *Rocky*. The 1997 premiere of [*Cop Land*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cop_Land) brings together [Harvey Keitel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Keitel), [Ray Liotta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Liotta) and [Annabella Sciorra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabella_Sciorra). [Talia Shire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talia_Shire), who played Adrian in the *Rocky* films, is also pictured.

The most unexpected image, LIFE says, is Stallone backstage at the musical *Dreamgirls* in 1982 with [Loretta Devine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loretta_Devine) and [Sheryl Lee Ralph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheryl_Lee_Ralph). The magazine notes the Philadelphia link: Stallone became a star playing a Philadelphia boxer, and Ralph now stars in the Philadelphia-set sitcom [*Abbott Elementary*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbott_Elementary).

## A small *Tulsa King* link

Stallone's name also turned up in sports coverage this week. Sherdog profiled Branden Smith, a stuntman on Stallone's series [*Tulsa King*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King), who is chasing a bare-knuckle title and faces Braxton Smith at BKB Burbank. It is a side note, but it shows how far the Stallone name travels in boxing circles.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is I Play Rocky about?
It dramatises how Sylvester Stallone, then a struggling actor, wrote *Rocky* and refused to sell the script unless he could play the lead, despite pressure from producers.

### Who directed I Play Rocky and who stars in it?
Peter Farrelly directed it from a script by Peter Gamble. Anthony Ippolito plays Stallone and AnnaSophia Robb plays Sasha, with a supporting cast that includes Tracy Letts, Jay Duplass and Matt Dillon.

### When did I Play Rocky open?
CultureMap Austin reports a wide US theatrical release on November 20, 2026.
