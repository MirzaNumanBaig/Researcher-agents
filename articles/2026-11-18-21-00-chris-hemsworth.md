## Chris Hemsworth's Avengers: Doomsday return

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) is back in the Marvel fold. A new trailer for *Avengers: Doomsday* has put him alongside [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) and [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), a trio that [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/entertainment/movies/avengers-doomsday-reunites-robert-downey-jr-chris-hemsworth-and-chris-evans-but-this-is-the-hero-i-cant-wait-to-see-in-marvels-biggest-crossover-yet) calls the "Big Three" of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

The outlet's writer says the reunion is only part of the appeal. They single out a moment from the first trailer in which Thor's Stormbreaker axe is halted with two fingers, and a "Captains America" gag in the latest one. They also expect Downey's Doctor Doom to be central to the story. That is one reviewer's reaction, not a studio statement.

## What the trailer tells us, and what it does not

The column is an opinion piece, and most of it is about [Paul Rudd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Rudd)'s Ant-Man, so it reveals little about Thor's storyline. It does show how the film is being sold: as a crossover that pulls together heroes from different eras of the franchise. The writer also mentions a returning Captain America.

Plot details, running time and Thor's exact role are not covered in the sources we have read. We will update this story when Marvel or the cast confirm more.

## The MMA question

A second story doing the rounds comes from Men's Health Australia, which asked whether Hemsworth is preparing for an MMA fight. We could not open that page, so we cannot report what it claims.

What we can verify comes from an earlier interview. According to [Boss Hunting](https://www.bosshunting.com.au/entertainment/movies/chris-hemsworth-mma-future), published on 19 February 2026, Hemsworth was promoting *Crime 101* with Complex when he said he would love to try MMA at some point, though he guessed he might end up on the floor. He said he would not get into a ring right now, but part of him is tempted.

He pointed to years of training and sparring, with the aim of not seriously hurting anyone. He named UFC featherweight champion Alex Volkanovski, described as a friend, as a possible opponent, then joked about fighting a long-retired star and simply running until they tired. Boss Hunting treats the whole exchange as a joke.

## Why the two stories feel connected

Fans have long linked Hemsworth's training with his Thor work, and the MMA chatter tends to flare when he posts gym footage or a new trailer lands. Neither source here shows a link between the two. There is no confirmed fight, date, opponent or promotion, and treating a training clip as a fight announcement would be a stretch.

## What happens next

Expect more footage and cast news as *Avengers: Doomsday* approaches. On MMA, the only thing on record is Hemsworth's own half-joking comment that he is curious. Any real announcement would come from him or his team, and we will report it when it does.
