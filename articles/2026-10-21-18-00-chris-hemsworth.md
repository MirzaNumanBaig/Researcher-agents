**Quick summary**

- Archie Rose Distilling Co. has launched a global campaign and a new look for three core whiskies, according to Drinks Digest.
- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) became co-owner of the Sydney distillery in August 2026 and worked on the bottle, packaging and creative direction.
- The campaign was shot across eight Australian locations and stars Hemsworth with his brother [Luke Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Hemsworth).
- The whiskies are priced from $79.99 to $119 (RRP) and will sell in Australia, the US and other markets.
- Separately, Hemsworth is training for Netflix's Extraction 3, which has no official release date.

Chris Hemsworth has put his name on the label. On 21 October, Sydney's Archie Rose Distilling Co. unveiled a redesigned three-whisky core range and a global campaign built around its new co-owner, who joined founder Will Edwards in August, as Drinks Digest reports.

## What has changed at Archie Rose?

The packaging is new. The whisky is not. According to Drinks Digest, the Single Malt Whisky and the Straight Rye Malt Whisky (formerly Rye Malt Whisky) keep the same liquids and production methods. They are joined by Australian Original Whisky, described as an approachable all-malt whisky and the newest addition to the core range.

The Australian Women's Weekly says the labels now carry Hemsworth's name and signature alongside the line "Distilled Down Under". The flask-shaped bottle has textured glass meant to evoke the soil where the barley and rye grow. Drinks Digest adds that the lighter bottle and redesigned box cut packaging weight and improve freight efficiency, which matters for a brand preparing to export.

The design was developed with Glasgow-based agency Thirst, and Hemsworth worked with the distillery team across the identity, packaging and creative direction.

## The campaign with Luke Hemsworth

The launch film is set against eight locations around Australia. Hemsworth produced it with director Justin McMillan and stars alongside Luke Hemsworth, according to Drinks Digest.

Hemsworth said in the announcement that he has been a fan of the distillery "for years" and is proud of what the team created. The Women's Weekly quotes him from the August announcement saying an Archie Rose whisky takes him straight back to a campfire in the Australian bush with his mates.

## Prices, markets and the US push

Drinks Digest lists the recommended retail prices as follows:

- Archie Rose Single Malt: $119
- Straight Rye Malt: $99.99
- Australian Original Whisky: $79.99

The three whiskies launch in Australia and the US, plus markets including New Zealand, Singapore and selected parts of Asia. Drinks Trade's headline frames the rebrand as Hemsworth backing the distillery's US expansion, though that page was not readable for this article, so the detail here comes from Drinks Digest and the Women's Weekly.

## Who is behind the whisky?

Archie Rose was founded in Sydney in 2014 by Will Edwards and his family. The Women's Weekly describes it as the first whisky distillery to open in Sydney since 1853 and says it has won more than 450 awards, including World's Best Rye Whisky twice and Australia's Best Single Malt Whisky five times. It also gained B Corp certification earlier this year.

Edwards said the distillery has spent 12 years refining its whiskies. Master Distiller Dave Withers said Hemsworth's involvement lets the team take Australian craft to a global stage. Grain comes from local farmers and maltsters, including Voyager Craft Malt in New South Wales, and the whiskies are matured in Australian fortified wine barrels and European and American oak.

The venture is a step outside the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) work Hemsworth is best known for, as the Women's Weekly puts it.

## What happens next

The new range is on sale in Australia and heads to the US as part of the distillery's next phase of growth, according to Drinks Digest. No US launch date was given in the sources.

On screen, Hemsworth is back in training for Extraction 3. News of Bahrain reports he shared Instagram footage of boxing, kicks and knee strikes with trainer Jack Boonsanong, while filming continues in Thailand. Sam Hargrave is returning to direct, with Anthony and Joe Russo producing. Netflix has not announced a release date, though The Manual says the film is due next year.
