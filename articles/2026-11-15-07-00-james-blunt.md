**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt)'s eighth album, Airplane Mode, landed at Number One on the UK chart on Friday 13 November 2026.
- He had promised to change his name to "Ryan Air" if fans got it there, and he marked the result with a spoof phone call to the airline.
- Ryanair replied with its own comedy video, according to Euro Weekly News.
- His wife, Sofia Wellesley, congratulated him on Instagram and said she is not changing her name.

James Blunt has a Number One album and an awkward promise to keep. Airplane Mode topped the UK chart on Friday 13 November, and the singer's pledge to become "Ryan Air" has turned into a running joke between him and the budget airline. It has also pulled his wife into the banter.

## How the Ryanair joke started

Blunt challenged fans to push Airplane Mode to the top of the UK charts and said he would legally take the airline's name if they did, [Euro Weekly News reported](https://euroweeklynews.com/2026/11/14/james-blunts-promise-to-change-name-to-ryan-air-takes-hilarious-turn-after-album-reaches-no-1/). Ryanair had already answered on X when he first made the dare, with the line "at least people would know you then".

The album did reach Number One, so the singer posted a staged phone call on Instagram. In it, a supposed Ryanair representative congratulates him, then warns that he has a lovely family. The voice settles the matter with "You keep your name, we keep ours" and a final threat to seat his family apart.

Euro Weekly News stressed that the exchange is a comedy sketch rather than a real customer-service call.

## Ryanair hits back

The airline did not let the clip stand alone. According to the same report, it released its own video featuring an animated aircraft with a talking face. The plane jokingly threatens to sue Blunt so thoroughly that he could not afford one of its flights.

Fans joined in. One commenter wrote that the Ryan Air gag "ain't gonna go away", while another asked whether the family could be sued together or if that costs £39.99 extra per person.

## What the chart win means

The [Daily Mail reported](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16292275/James-Blunt-wife-Sofia-Wellesley-number-one-album-Celebrity-Traitors.html) that Blunt, 52, has secured his third Number One album on the Official Albums Chart. It is his first since All the Lost Souls in 2007, a gap of 19 years.

His wife, Sofia Wellesley, shared a photo on Instagram captioned "Number 1. So proud of you. I'm not changing my name though." The paper says they married in 2014 and have two children. It adds that the win follows his run on [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors).

## Blunt on perspective, Ibiza and Pacha

In a Guardian interview published on 15 November, Blunt reflected on the years when his hit You're Beautiful made him a target for critics. He said his time as a captain in the British army gave him perspective. When someone raises a bad review, he thinks of the 15,000 people who paid to see his latest concert, [according to The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/nov/15/james-blunt-10-chaotic-questions-interview).

He also described living in Ibiza, where he bought a house once his first album, Back to Bedlam, became a hit. His favourite club is Pacha, which opened in 1973, and he wrote the song 1973 about it. He said [Ed Sheeran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ed_Sheeran) is a godparent to one of his children.

On humour, he told the paper that he once hired a helicopter and dropped 100,000 rose petals on his wife in the garden last Valentine's Day. She thought it was "OK". Asked who should play him in a biopic, he said his mother wanted [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise), but he suspects [Alan Partridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Partridge) is more likely.

## What happens next

Blunt has not said whether he will start any paperwork. Neither the airline nor the singer has suggested the name change is real, and the sources describe the exchange as a joke. Expect more social media replies. The chart position will be the first measure of how long Airplane Mode stays in the top spot.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did James Blunt really change his name to Ryan Air?

No. The sources describe the pledge and the phone call as a comedy bit, and Ryanair's reply as a joke too. No name change has been reported.

### Which James Blunt album reached Number One?

Airplane Mode, his eighth studio album, reached Number One in the UK on 13 November 2026. The Daily Mail says it is his third chart-topping album and his first since 2007.

### What did Sofia Wellesley say about the name change?

She congratulated him on Instagram and wrote that she was "so proud" but is "not changing my name though".
