**Quick summary**

- The New York Post says [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), 40, has become one of the most in-demand actors in the world after a run of risky hits this year.
- Puck News reported this week that he did a surprise screen test for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond). This is a report, not an announcement.
- Puck also says his reported deal is the best of the contenders: three films instead of four, with higher pay.
- Next up is "Dune: Part III" next month, then a sequel to "The Batman" next year.

[Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) is having the kind of year that studios plan careers around. According to a column in the [New York Post](https://nypost.com/2026/11/14/entertainment/robert-pattinson-a-james-bond-favorite-is-suddenly-the-biggest-star-in-hollywood/) published on 14 November, the 40-year-old Briton has gone from cult favourite to one of the most popular actors anywhere, and the talk of him as the next 007 is growing with every release.

## What Puck reported about the Bond role

The Post column points to a report from Puck News this week. Puck said Pattinson took a surprise screen test to play the spy and holds the most favourable terms of all the names in contention, which it lists as Josh O'Connor, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner).

The reported difference is striking. The others would have to commit to four films, while Pattinson would sign for three and be paid considerably more. Puck reads that as a sign that Amazon and MGM want him badly. None of this has been confirmed by the studios, so it should be treated as a report. We have followed the story closely, including [the reported three-film deal](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-deal/) and [his status as a front-runner](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-front-runner/).

There is an odd link behind the scenes. The Post notes that [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who directs "Dune: Part III", is also the filmmaker set to direct the 26th Bond film.

## A year of risky hits

The Post argues that Pattinson's arthouse instincts and mainstream fame finally met this year. It cites three results:

- "The Drama", a film about wedding jitters co-starring Zendaya, grossed US$134 million.
- "The Odyssey", in which he plays the villainous suitor Antinous, has taken US$1.8 billion so far and is heading back to cinemas to boost its Oscar chances.
- "Primetime", in which he plays "To Catch a Predator" host [Chris Hansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hansen), earned US$62 million.

The last figure is the most telling. According to [Wikipedia's film entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film)), "Primetime", directed by Lance Oppenheim, opened on 5 September on a budget of about US$14.5 million. A US$62 million gross is more than four times that. The same entry says critics responded warmly, with a Rotten Tomatoes score of 86 per cent and a Metacritic score of 68 out of 100 from 54 critics.

## From vampire to Batman to auteur favourite

Many fans first met him as [Edward Cullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Cullen) in "Twilight" and as Cedric Diggory in the [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) series. The Post calls him the most successful young actor to emerge from the Potter films.

He then turned toward auteurs. The column lists [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan), [Werner Herzog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Herzog), [David Cronenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cronenberg), [Bong Joon Ho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bong_Joon_Ho), [Brady Corbet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brady_Corbet) and [Robert Eggers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Eggers) among his collaborators. It also notes his voice work as the title bird in Hayao Miyazaki's "The Boy and the Heron", a performance the writer says is hard to recognise as his.

The Post credits that range with winning over Gen Z viewers, who it says are now the largest moviegoing group and are active on Letterboxd. Mainstream audiences know him best as [Bruce Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman) in 2022's "The Batman", and he is due back in the sequel next year.

## Why some doubt the Bond fit

The columnist is not wholly convinced on paper. Pattinson's profile, and his Batman credit, might count against him, since Bond actors are often relatively unknown at the start. The Post also says there was talk of a younger Bond, and notes he is two years older than [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) was in "Casino Royale".

Even so, the writer argues his strangeness is an asset. Every Bond, from [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) on, has been distinctive, and Pattinson is described as private enough that audiences still know little about him. For more of our coverage, see [everything we publish about Robert Pattinson](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).

## What happens next

"Dune: Part III" is due next month, with Pattinson playing a spy. "The Take", directed by Fernando Meirelles with Daisy Edgar-Jones and Danai Gurira in the cast, is listed for release on 15 January 2027, as we covered in [our report on the heist film](https://moduscarry.com/the-take-netflix-heist-film/). The Post says an official Bond announcement could come by the end of the year, though that is its expectation, not a confirmed date.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Robert Pattinson been cast as James Bond?

No. Puck News reported a screen test and favourable terms, but neither Amazon nor MGM has announced a decision.

### How much did Primetime make?

The New York Post puts the gross at US$62 million. Wikipedia lists a budget of about US$14.5 million.

### When is Dune: Part III released?

The New York Post says it arrives next month, which means December. Pattinson plays a spy in it.

### Will Robert Pattinson play Batman again?

The Post says he will star in the sequel to "The Batman" next year.
