**Quick summary**

- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) told GQ that his [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) must be, above all, a believable killer.
- ScreenRant reports that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been cast as the next 007 for Amazon Studios.
- Pattinson reportedly beat Callum Turner, Jack Lowden and Josh O'Connor to the part.
- The two have already worked together on Dune: Part Three, which ScreenRant says opens next month.

Denis Villeneuve has said he wants a James Bond who is an assassin before he is a spy, and a new analysis argues that is exactly why Robert Pattinson got the job. ScreenRant [reported on 19 November](https://screenrant.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-casting-denis-villeneuve-comments/) that Amazon Studios and the director have settled on Pattinson after months of speculation.

## What Villeneuve said about his Bond

In an interview with GQ, quoted by ScreenRant, the Canadian filmmaker set out the quality he cares about most. "The first quality is that I need to believe that this man is a killer," he said. "Because more than a spy, Bond is an assassin. I need that kind of strength. I need that kind of anger deep in the eye. It's not everybody who has that."

That is a clear departure from some earlier eras. ScreenRant points out that [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) and [Roger Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Moore) played the character as a spy first and a killer second, while Daniel Craig's version was stern and serious, with villains and a world to match. Villeneuve appears to want to push the dial further in the darker direction.

## Why Pattinson fits the brief

According to ScreenRant, Pattinson beat several high-profile names, including [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor. The outlet describes the choice as unexpected only a few weeks ago, but sensible on closer inspection.

Its argument rests on three points:

- **Age:** at 40, ScreenRant says, Pattinson is at a good age for the role.
- **Franchise experience:** he led the Twilight films for several years and is currently DC's Batman, playing [Bruce Wayne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman). The outlet says it is unclear whether he will keep the cape while committed to Bond.
- **Dark roles:** ScreenRant points to Good Time, The Lighthouse and The Devil All the Time as proof he can play characters who are not afraid to get their hands dirty. It also cites his most recent part in Primetime, as the television journalist [Chris Hansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hansen), whom it calls unhinged as the film goes on.

This is one outlet's reading, and it is an opinion piece rather than a studio statement. Still, the link between Villeneuve's "anger deep in the eye" remark and Pattinson's filmography is hard to miss.

## A second film together

The pairing is not new. ScreenRant notes that Pattinson and Villeneuve first teamed up on Dune: Part Three, where the actor plays Scytale, a shape-shifting villain who wants to bring down Paul Atreides's regime. The outlet says that film premieres in cinemas next month, which means audiences will see the pair's first collaboration before the Bond project takes shape.

Villeneuve's track record gives that some weight. ScreenRant lists Prisoners and Blade Runner 2049 among his credits, the second a sequel to the 1982 [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner). For more on the director's recent work, see our earlier piece, [Denis Villeneuve Says Dune: Part Three Is His Best Yet](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-dune-part-three-2/).

## What happens next

The first real test is Dune: Part Three, where viewers can judge how Pattinson handles a menacing role under Villeneuve. After that, attention turns to the Bond production itself. The sources read for this article give no start date for filming, no release date and no word on the rest of the cast, so those details remain unconfirmed.
