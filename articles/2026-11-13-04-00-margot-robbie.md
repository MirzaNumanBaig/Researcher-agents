**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) told People she did not see Barbie and Ken never kissing in the 2023 film as a win, according to the Daily Star.
- She said her friends asked why she made a whole film with Ryan Gosling and never kissed him.
- Gosling said he was glad the attempts at Barbie and Ken kissing were cut.
- Separately, ComicBook.com argues her 2022 film Babylon is one of her best performances despite a weak box office run.

Margot Robbie has admitted she was not thrilled that Barbie and Ken never shared a kiss on screen. Speaking to People, the actor and producer was asked whether she was relieved to skip the moment with co-star Ryan Gosling. Her answer, as reported by the [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/showbiz/margot-robbie-admits-shes-gutted-37861274), was blunt: "Uh, no! That didn't feel like a win for me."

## What Robbie said about the missing kiss

The comment is a look back at the 2023 hit directed by Greta Gerwig, in which Robbie played Barbie and Gosling played Ken. The two characters travel from Barbie Land into the real world, yet the film never gives them a kiss.

According to the Daily Star, Robbie said her friends were baffled. "All of my girlfriends were like, 'Well, you did a whole movie with him and you don't kiss?'" she recalled. She said they also asked what was wrong with her, given she was producing the film and, in their view, in charge. Her reply, in her words, was that she could not check that one off her list.

Gosling took a different view. He told the same publication it was "so funny trying to figure out what their idea of kissing might be", adding that he was glad all of that was cut. The Daily Star does not say what those scenes looked like or why they were removed, so the reason behind the cut remains unexplained.

## The numbers behind Barbie

The film arrived in 2023 alongside Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer in the "Barbenheimer" double bill. The Daily Star reports that Barbie grossed $1.448 billion (about £1.08 billion), and that fans dressed head to toe in pink for cinema visits.

The same outlet says Robbie and Gosling each received an upfront salary of $12.5 million, and that her total earnings rose to around $50 million after box-office bonuses and producer profits. The Daily Star frames those figures as "understood" rather than confirmed, so treat them as unverified. Gosling received a Best Supporting Actor nomination for playing Ken.

For more on where the franchise stands, see our report on the [Barbie 2 pay dispute](https://moduscarry.com/barbie-2-pay-dispute/), which covers reported talks over a sequel.

## Babylon gets a second look

Robbie is back in the conversation for a different reason. ComicBook.com published a feature calling Babylon, her 2022 film, one of her most overlooked roles and one of her most committed performances.

The film comes from [Damien Chazelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Damien_Chazelle), the filmmaker behind [La La Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_La_Land). Set in 1920s Hollywood, it follows Mexican immigrant Manny Torres, played by [Diego Calva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diego_Calva), who meets Robbie's Nellie LaRoy, a New Jersey newcomer who has already decided she will be a star. [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) also features, playing a leading man trying to hold on as the industry changes.

ComicBook.com says the film left critics deeply divided and did not draw audiences back to cinemas still wary after the pandemic, partly because it runs over three hours. It also credits Chazelle with making an $80 million budget look far more expensive, through large party scenes and location shoots with hundreds of extras.

The writer argues Nellie asks for singing, dancing, comedy and vulnerability, and that Robbie holds the centre of a very large cast. The piece adds that the film's themes about technology changing the movie business feel more relevant in 2026 than they did on release, with Hollywood now dealing with artificial intelligence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Did Barbie and Ken kiss in the Barbie movie?

No. The Daily Star reports that the 2023 film never shows the two characters kissing, and Robbie said that did not feel like a win to her.

### How much did Barbie earn at the box office?

The Daily Star puts the worldwide gross at $1.448 billion, about £1.08 billion.

### What is Babylon about?

It is a 2022 Damien Chazelle film set in 1920s Hollywood, following Manny Torres and Nellie LaRoy as they chase success in the film industry. Robbie plays Nellie.
