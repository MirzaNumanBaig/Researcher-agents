**Quick summary**

- *The Legend of Tarzan*, starring [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd), has reached third place on HBO Max's global top 10, Collider reported on 21 November 2026.
- Only *The End of Oak Street* and the classic *Matilda* sit above it on that list.
- The film earned US$357.2 million worldwide on a US$180 million budget but drew mixed reviews on release.
- Critics gave it 36% on Rotten Tomatoes, while audiences were kinder at 58%.

About a decade after it reached cinemas, the Alexander Skarsgård Tarzan film is having an unexpected second life. According to [Collider](https://collider.com/alexander-skarsgard-the-legend-of-tarzan-streaming-success-hbo-max-november-2026/), *The Legend of Tarzan* has climbed to third on HBO Max's global top 10, a result that suggests viewers are catching up with a blockbuster that never quite won over critics.

## Where Tarzan sits on the HBO Max chart

Collider says the film trails just two titles: Anne Hathaway and Ewan McGregor's *The End of Oak Street* and the family favourite *Matilda*. The same list also includes *The Hunger Games* films, *How to Make a Killing* and *Reminders of Him*, so Tarzan is sharing space with some very current company.

The report does not give viewing hours or say how long the film has held the spot, so it is best read as a snapshot rather than a long-running trend.

## What the film is about

Directed by [David Yates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Yates), the film reimagines the classic hero created by [Edgar Rice Burroughs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Rice_Burroughs). Skarsgård plays John Clayton, the man known as Tarzan, who has moved to London. He is persuaded by [George Washington Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_Williams), played by [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson), to return to the African jungle and investigate claims of slavery.

Once there, Tarzan discovers he is a pawn in a larger scheme. [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) plays his wife, Jane Clayton, [Christoph Waltz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Waltz) plays the villain Leon Rom, and [Djimon Hounsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djimon_Hounsou) appears as Chief Mbonga. Collider notes that the story weaves in real historical events.

## A hit at the box office, a miss with critics

The numbers tell a split story. According to Collider, the film took US$357.2 million worldwide against a US$180 million budget, roughly double its cost. Reviews were less generous. The [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) score landed at 36%, with criticism aimed at the CGI, an underused cast and a serious tone.

The critics' consensus, as quoted by Collider, said the film has more on its mind than many earlier Tarzan movies but cannot overcome a generic plot and sluggish pace. Audiences scored it 58%, which helps explain why fans are now pushing it back into view.

## Skarsgård's wider run

The renewed interest lands while the actor is busy. Collider points to his latest role in *Wicker*, where he plays a magically constructed husband to [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman), and to *Pillion*, in which he plays the charismatic leader of a queer motorcycle club. It also recalls his turn as the Viking prince Amleth in *The Northman* and his erratic tech founder in *Succession*.
