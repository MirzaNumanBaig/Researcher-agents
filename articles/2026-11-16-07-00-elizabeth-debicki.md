**Quick summary**

- David Fincher's Cliff Booth film screened in Los Angeles on the night before 15 November 2026, according to World of Reel.
- Early word singles out Elizabeth Debicki, with one account saying she "just about stole the show".
- The film opens exclusively on about 200 IMAX screens on 25 November 2026, over US Thanksgiving weekend.
- Reviews can be published from Thursday at 10 a.m. Pacific time.

The first audience reaction to David Fincher's Cliff Booth film has landed, and [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) is at the centre of it. After a Los Angeles screening, [World of Reel](https://www.worldofreel.com/blog/2026/11/15/finchers-cliff-booth-screens-in-la-tarantino-on-speed-and-elizabeth-debicki-steals-the-show) reported on 15 November that early word on Elizabeth Debicki's Cliff Booth performance is strong, ahead of an IMAX release on 25 November.

## What happened at the Los Angeles screening

The film, titled "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth", was shown for the Directors Guild of America at the old Academy theatre at Wilshire and La Peer, according to World of Reel. A Q&A followed, moderated by Elvis Mitchell, with Fincher, Debicki, Scott Caan and Peter Weller. Quentin Tarantino and Brad Pitt did not attend.

The site quoted a Hollywood Elsewhere source who called the film "a violent madcap comedy" and a love letter to late-1970s Los Angeles. This is one viewer's account, not a published review, and critics have not yet weighed in.

## Why Elizabeth Debicki is drawing the attention

The same source said Debicki "just about stole the show" and described her as a "new kind of screen goddess". Early word also suggests her storyline carries as much weight as Pitt's, according to the report.

That matters because the story belongs to Cliff Booth, the stuntman Pitt played in [Once Upon a Time in Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Once_Upon_a_Time_in_Hollywood). A co-lead role in a Fincher film is a notable step for an actor who has built her reputation on distinctive, often towering supporting performances.

## A Tarantino world through Fincher's lens

The new film is set in 1977, eight years after Booth's encounter with the Manson family in Tarantino's alternate version of 1969. World of Reel's source described the result as a blend of the two filmmakers, calling it "Tarantino on speed".

Fincher spoke about the script at the Q&A. He said he received a 230-page screenplay and had to decide which half to shoot, which he described as essentially two movies. The dialogue was reportedly sped up, so a film that could have run close to three hours came in at about two. The one reservation in the report was that some cut material might have deepened the third act.

Pitt plays Booth. The cast also includes Scott Caan, Carla Gugino and Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, according to the report. The article does not name the studio.

## What happens next

The film opens on about 200 IMAX screens on 25 November 2026, over Thanksgiving weekend. The social media embargo lifts on Monday at 9:30 p.m. Pacific time, and reviews can be published on Thursday at 10 a.m. Pacific time. Those reviews will show whether the early praise for Debicki holds up outside a guild audience.
