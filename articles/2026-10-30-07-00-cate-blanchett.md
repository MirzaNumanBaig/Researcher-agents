**Quick summary**

- Game designer Hideo Kojima shared three photos with [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) on X on 29 October 2026.
- His post, written in Japanese, read "With Cate Blanchett" and tagged her production company, Dirty Films.
- It did not announce a project, a collaboration or any casting.
- Fans have long suggested she could play The Boss in a Metal Gear Solid adaptation, but that remains speculation.

Hideo Kojima has posted photos of a visit from Cate Blanchett, and the Metal Gear Solid community has done the rest. The creator of the stealth-game series shared three pictures with the Oscar winner on X, according to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/cate-blanchett-kojima-productions-visit-180901000.html), republishing a ComingSoon.net report. Nothing in the post says why she was there.

## What the post actually says

[Hideo Kojima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hideo_Kojima) wrote only "With Cate Blanchett" in Japanese. He tagged [Dirty Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Films), the company Blanchett co-founded with her husband, [Andrew Upton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Upton). The tag is the one concrete clue, and it points to a meeting between two creative businesses rather than a casual fan visit.

AOL's report is clear on the limits: the post "didn't announce a collaboration", and neither side has confirmed a Metal Gear Solid project or any casting plans.

## Why fans keep naming her for The Boss

The speculation is old. According to the same report, fans have discussed Blanchett as a possible choice for The Boss since at least January 2007. The Boss appears in Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater as a legendary soldier and mentor to Naked Snake. Her short, light hair, sharp features and serious presence are what fans point to when they compare the two.

There is a catch. Kojima has named British actress [Charlotte Rampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Rampling) as the model for the character's look, so Blanchett was not the inspiration. The fan casting, in other words, has run for close to two decades without any sign she was approached.

Interest has stayed high because of Metal Gear Solid Delta: Snake Eater. That remake of the 2004 game arrived in August 2025 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, introducing the story to a new generation of players.

## A busy week in Japan for Blanchett

The photos land in the middle of a Japan trip. As we reported earlier, Blanchett was in Tokyo for the Praemium Imperiale ceremony, and on 29 October she appeared at a press conference at the University of Tokyo. According to WWD, as summarised in [our earlier report](https://moduscarry.com/cate-blanchett-to-chair-uniqlos-new-literary-magazine/), she will chair the selection committee for a new Uniqlo literary magazine, "Stories She Tells", with a first issue planned for autumn 2027. Readers can follow everything on her in our [Cate Blanchett](https://moduscarry.com/tag/cate-blanchett/) archive.

That context matters. A Tokyo visit by a star in town for other engagements fits a courtesy call as easily as a business meeting. No source reviewed for this article links the Kojima photos to a project.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. If Kojima Productions or Dirty Films has something to announce, it would most likely come from the studio's own channels or from Kojima's X account. Until then, the Metal Gear link is a fan theory, not a development. More on her film work sits in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.

## Frequently asked questions

### Has Cate Blanchett been cast in Metal Gear Solid?
No. Neither Blanchett nor Kojima has announced a Metal Gear Solid collaboration, and the post confirms no casting.

### Who is The Boss in Metal Gear Solid?
The Boss is a legendary soldier and mentor to Naked Snake in Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater. Kojima has said Charlotte Rampling was the model for her look.

### What is Dirty Films?
It is the production company co-founded by Blanchett and Andrew Upton, which Kojima tagged in his post.
