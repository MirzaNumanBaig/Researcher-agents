**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) appears in the music video for "She's the Best", the title track of [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan)'s fourth album, released on 9 October.
- In an interview with W Magazine, Sivan says he was "completely dazzled" by working with her.
- Kidman plays the owner of a burlesque bar, and the two share a slow dance in the video.
- Sivan says the pair texted for weeks beforehand about her styling and her character.

Troye Sivan has described the experience of filming with Nicole Kidman as dazzling, in a new interview with W Magazine published on 26 October. The Australian singer-songwriter, 31, spoke about the music video for "She's the Best", the title track of his fourth record, which came out on 9 October.

## What Sivan says about working with Kidman

According to [W Magazine](https://www.wmagazine.com/culture/troye-sivan-interview-shes-the-best), Sivan was asked directly what it was like to slow dance with Kidman in the video. His answer was warm. He said Kidman "does everything in her power" to make people forget who she is, because she is trying to disarm them, and that he appreciated it. He added that he imagines people act strangely around her all the time.

The preparation began well before the shoot. Sivan says the two were texting weeks earlier about glam and about how to understand her character. By the time the slow dance was filmed, he says, he felt comfortable, because she was fully committed to the role.

## The story of the video

Sivan sets the scene as a burlesque nightclub. In his telling, Kidman is the owner of a bar, possibly a former showgirl herself, who now runs the place. When she walks in, the staff react as if the boss has arrived. She has come to check on the new entertainment, which is Sivan's character, performing at the bar for the first time alongside the queens.

From there, he says, her character trains him up and sees something in him that he cannot yet see himself. She puts him in drag for the first time, and the encouragement she gives him leads to what he calls an ultimate freedom by the end of the video. He describes her as someone with an exterior that people may fear at first, but with deep love for "her girls" underneath.

## The song and the album

The video accompanies a track Sivan wrote with George Daniel, Jack Antonoff, Styalz Fuego and Leland. Sivan told W he knew the song was interesting the moment it was written, and he is clear that it is not "Rush", his best-known recent single. He says the aim was different from the usual approach of leading with a pop song that the whole world will understand.

He has said the album is an ode to the glamorous women who have been a constant in his life. He told the magazine he did not want to attempt to sum up womanhood, so he zoomed in on specific people instead, such as a friend who DJs and a woman he once watched walking down a street.
