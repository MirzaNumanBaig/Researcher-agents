**Quick summary**

- [Molly Meldrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molly_Meldrum), the Countdown host and music journalist, died in Melbourne on Monday 23 November at the age of 83.
- Victorian Premier Ben Carroll said the government offered a state funeral and Meldrum's family accepted, according to ABC reporting cited by Music Times.
- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) led the tributes, thanking him for his "unwavering love and support".
- A date and location for the funeral had not been confirmed at the time of the reports.

Molly Meldrum, the man who sent Australian pop into the nation's lounge rooms through Countdown, will be farewelled with a state funeral after his death on Monday aged 83. Kylie Minogue was among the first to respond, and her message has set the tone for a wave of tributes from Australian and international artists, as reported by [Music Times](https://www.musictimes.com/articles/113366/20261123/kylie-minogue-jimmy-barnes-remember-molly-meldrum-australia-plans-state-funeral.htm).

## The state funeral offer

Music Times, citing the ABC, reports that Meldrum died after a long illness. Premier Ben Carroll said the Victorian government offered a state funeral and that the family said yes. No date or venue had been verified when the reports were published.

[AsiaOne](https://www.asiaone.com/entertainment/kylie-minogue-leads-tributes-australian-music-legend-molly-meldrum) says he died in hospital in Melbourne. His longtime friend and manager, Mark Klemens, confirmed the news to the Guardian, calling him "a true Australian icon". A statement from his family and partner, Yan Wongngam, described him as "the heartbeat, the champion and the spirit of Australian music". The family also thanked the staff at Epworth Richmond hospital for their care.

## What Kylie Minogue wrote

Minogue posted on Instagram that she watched Countdown every weekend as a child, "dreaming of how music might become part of my life". She said she later sat in the audience once, and by her late teens she was on the show. Meldrum also visited the set of Neighbours, she added.

She finished with a promise: "Vale Molly. I'm going to turn the music up in your honour." The same words were carried by [The News International](https://www.thenews.com.pk/latest/1424747-kylie-minogue-mourns-beloved-music-journalist-molly-meldrums-death), which also noted his signature "do yourself a favour" line.

## Jimmy Barnes and the other voices

The most unexpected memory came from [Jimmy Barnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Barnes). Music Times says the Cold Chisel singer first saw Meldrum as an obstacle, because Countdown expected bands to mime and Cold Chisel wanted to be taken seriously as a live act. The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16314431/molly-meldrum-death-tribute-kylie-minogue-jimmy-barnes.html) quotes him calling Meldrum "the enemy" at the time. Barnes later recognised how much the exposure did for the band.

Others described a gentler debt:

- [Jason Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Donovan) called him "one of this country's greatest pioneers and ambassadors for music and entertainment" and recalled the day Meldrum appeared on Neighbours alongside Minogue, [Guy Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Pearce) and himself.
- [Peter Andre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Andre) said he would not have had his chances without Meldrum, who "believed in me from day one".
- US band Blondie said he was "instrumental" in their success in Australia, and Leo Sayer said Countdown introduced him to local audiences in 1974.
- Music Times adds that [Kate Ceberano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Ceberano) and Icehouse's Iva Davies also thanked him.

## Why Countdown carried so much weight

Countdown ran on the ABC from 1974 to 1987. According to Music Times, a spot on the program could change the fortunes of a band trying to reach audiences outside its home city. Meldrum also worked on records, producing Russell Morris's 1969 single "The Real Thing", and he was inducted into the ARIA Hall of Fame in 2014 alongside the show.
