**Quick summary**

- [Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari), director of *Ramayana*, is No. 1 on IMDb India's Trending People list this week, according to ETV Bharat.
- He sits ahead of Ranbir Kapoor, Yash and Shah Rukh Khan; five of the top ten names come from the film.
- ETV Bharat reports *Ramayana: Part One* earned Rs 663 crore worldwide in its first five days.
- Critics are split, with published ratings running from 2.75 to 4.5 out of 5, per The Tribune. Part Two is slated for Diwali 2027.

Directors rarely top a popularity chart in a country that celebrates its film stars, but this week Nitesh Tiwari has done it. IMDb India's latest Trending People list puts the *Ramayana* filmmaker first, ahead of the actors he directed, according to [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/entertainment/nitesh-tiwari-claims-no-1-spot-on-imdb-trending-people-in-india-list-beats-shah-rukh-khan-ranbir-kapoor-and-yash-enn26111001038), published on 10 November.

## Nitesh Tiwari IMDb trending: why the director is top

IMDb's list is not a critics' ranking. ETV Bharat explains that it follows page views from more than 250 million people who visit the site each month, so a person climbs when large numbers of readers look them up.

The pull this week is *Ramayana*, which ETV Bharat describes as the most expensive film in Indian cinema, planned as a two-part release and mounted on a reported budget of Rs 4,000 crore. Part One reportedly collected Rs 663 crore worldwide over its first five days. Those are the outlet's figures, and they are worth treating as reported rather than audited.

The list also shows how much of the chart the film owns. Tiwari is No. 1, Sai Pallavi is No. 4, Yash is No. 7, Ranbir Kapoor is No. 8 and Ravi Dubey is No. 10. In other words, half of the top ten belongs to one production.

## From advertising to India's biggest film

Tiwari did not come up through a film family. ETV Bharat notes that he was born in Itarsi, Madhya Pradesh, studied metallurgy at IIT Bombay and spent close to 18 years in advertising before directing.

His first feature, the children's film *Chillar Party* in 2011, won a National Award. *Dangal* followed in 2016, starring Aamir Khan. ETV Bharat says it still holds an 8.3 rating on IMDb and earned more than Rs 2,000 crore worldwide. *Chhichhore* in 2019, with the late Sushant Singh Rajput, carries the same 8.3 rating. That back catalogue explains why a search for the *Ramayana* director leads to a long list of well-loved titles.

## A divided reception

Opinion on the film itself is far from uniform. Veteran actor Hema Malini posted on X that she went in with the lowest expectations and came out surprised, as [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/entertainment/hema-malini-reviews-ramayana-part-1-praising-nitesh-tiwaris-special-effects-casting-and-technical-innovation/) reported. She called the presentation "fabulous" and the effects "mind blowing", and praised the casting. The Tribune lists Ranbir Kapoor as Rama, Sai Pallavi as Sita, Yash as Ravana and Sunny Deol as Hanuman, and says the film was shot for IMAX.

A more reserved view appears in a reader review on [Baradwaj Rangan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baradwaj_Rangan)'s blog. The writer, who saw the Tamil 3D version, found the pacing uneven and the visual effects inconsistent, with some shots looking photorealistic and others like rough green screen. They also noted a score with contributions from composers including [James Newton Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Newton_Howard), Hans Zimmer and A.R. Rahman, and said the story felt like setup for the second film. The same review praised Yash's presence as Ravana and described a packed cinema reacting loudly to Ravi Dubey's Lakshman. Their reading of the cost was that both parts together come to nearly half a billion dollars.

Choreography is one of the film's talking points as well. [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/23626/shiamak-davar-choreographs-shiv-tandav-and-jai-jai-ram-sequences-for-nitesh-tiwaris-ramayana) reports that Shiamak Davar created the Shiv Tandav associated with Yash's Ravana and the devotional Jai Jai Ram, conceived as an aarti for Rama, with large temple sequences.

## What happens next

Part Two is slated for Diwali 2027, per The Tribune, so the conversation around the director is likely to continue for a year. Whether the IMDb surge lasts will depend on how Part One holds at the box office in the coming weeks. ## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Nitesh Tiwari?
He is an Indian film director best known for *Dangal*, *Chhichhore* and *Chillar Party*, and now for *Ramayana*. He studied at IIT Bombay and worked in advertising before moving into films, according to ETV Bharat.

### How does IMDb decide its Trending People list?
IMDb's list is based on page views from the site's audience of more than 250 million monthly visitors, not on critic scores or studio input, as ETV Bharat describes it.

### When is Ramayana Part Two released?
The Tribune reports that Part Two is slated for Diwali 2027.
