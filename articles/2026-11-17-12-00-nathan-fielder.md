**Quick summary**

- [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) was the most approached guest at the Academy's Governors Awards, according to The Guardian's report published on 16 November.
- Guests praised his Elizabeth Holmes documentary, *You Can See Everything*, which he co-directed with [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim).
- The honorary awards went to [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close), [Floyd Norman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floyd_Norman), [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott), [Christine Vachon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christine_Vachon) and [Pamela Koffler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Koffler).
- Wikipedia lists the film's box office at US$86.5 million against a US$7 million budget.

Nathan Fielder walked into the Academy's Governors Awards as one of Hollywood's most unlikely draws and, by The Guardian's account, left as its most popular guest. The paper's reporter spent the evening in the Ray Dolby Ballroom and found that the comedian-turned-documentarian was the person everyone wanted to talk to, ahead of an audience of about 700 film-industry figures.

## A room full of stars, and one awkward magnet

According to [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/film/2026/nov/16/governers-awards-honorary-oscars), the evening had a school-like feel, with cliques and a clear pecking order. [Timothée Chalamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoth%C3%A9e_Chalamet) was the last guest standing as Academy president [Lynette Howell Taylor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynette_Howell_Taylor) tried to get the room seated. He was due to sit beside [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner), opposite [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland) and Zendaya.

Others the reporter placed in the room included [Tom Cruise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Cruise), [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon), [Willem Dafoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willem_Dafoe), [David Byrne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Byrne), [Edward Norton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Norton) and [Robert Eggers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Eggers). Yet it was Fielder who kept being stopped.

## What Fielder was like in person

The Guardian describes him as friendly and stopping to talk to practically everybody, with a social ease that "almost seemed a betrayal" of his on-screen persona. Several people told him his documentary was "genius". He accepted the praise graciously, the reporter wrote, before drifting back into the familiar television figure: hands clasped, walking away with a timid, wobbly gait.

He was often alongside co-director Lance Oppenheim, who also made *Primetime*. When executives told Oppenheim how much they loved his film, he asked which one. [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) later joined them and was seen wiping away a tear as the group laughed.

## Why the film is getting so much attention

*You Can See Everything*, about former Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, was released on 6 September, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything). The same page lists a budget of about US$7 million and a worldwide gross of US$86.5 million, roughly 12 times its cost. It also records a 96 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score from 226 reviews and a Metacritic score of 97 from 42 critics.

For an independent documentary, those are unusual numbers. They help explain why a film about one of the most scrutinised figures in Silicon Valley was the one people raised at an event meant to honour careers in cinema. ## The honorary awards themselves

The Governors Awards are not televised and, unlike the Oscars, carry no suspense about who wins. This year's recipients, per The Guardian, were Glenn Close, who has eight competitive Oscar nominations and no win; Floyd Norman, described as Disney's pioneering first Black animator; Ridley Scott, who could not attend; and the independent producing partners Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler of [Killer Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Killer_Films).

## Frequently asked questions

### What are the Governors Awards?

They are the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences' annual, non-televised ceremony honouring careers and contributions to cinema. Winners are known in advance.

### Who was honoured this year?

Glenn Close, Floyd Norman, Ridley Scott, and the producers Christine Vachon and Pamela Koffler, according to The Guardian.

### What is *You Can See Everything*?

It is Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's documentary about Elizabeth Holmes, released on 6 September, according to Wikipedia.
