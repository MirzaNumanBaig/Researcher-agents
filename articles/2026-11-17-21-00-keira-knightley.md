**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) made her big-screen debut in 1999's *Star Wars: Episode I, The Phantom Menace*.
- She auditioned at 12, after a casting agent suggested she try out.
- Her parents were reluctant because they feared a bad experience on set, according to an interview she gave the Reading Eagle.
- Knightley has said the one thing she clearly remembers from filming is a headache caused by a heavy headdress.

Long before she led Netflix thrillers and period dramas, Keira Knightley nearly missed the job that started her film career. According to a [Yahoo Entertainment report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/keira-knightleys-parents-didnt-want-144500592.html) published this week, her parents did not want her to audition for *The Phantom Menace*, the 1999 prequel in the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) series. She went anyway.

## A casting call her parents didn't love

Knightley had worked on a handful of minor television films and shows before the franchise came calling. Yahoo reports that a casting agent recommended she try out for the prequel, and that she was 12 at the time.

Her parents hesitated. They believed the experience would turn out badly for a child, Knightley told the Reading Eagle, according to Yahoo. She was not persuaded. By her own account she told them she had to do it, adding that it was a Star Wars film, "for heaven's sake."

That detail is easy to miss in a career that now spans decades. A 12-year-old pushing back on her parents is a long way from the polished lead performances that followed, and it shows how early her determination was visible.

## The handmaiden who looked like the queen

In the film, Knightley plays Sabé, one of the handmaidens to Queen Padmé Amidala, played by [Natalie Portman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Portman). Sabé works as a decoy. Because she looks so much like the queen, she can wear Padmé's clothes and take her place in dangerous moments while the real queen dresses as a handmaiden. The two use subtle signals to communicate during the swap, and the switch happens several times as Padmé's life is under threat.

The resemblance was not just a story device. Yahoo notes that Knightley's own parents reportedly could not tell the two actresses apart once hair and make-up had been applied.

## What she remembers about filming

Knightley has been candid that the memories are thin. Yahoo points to earlier reports that she does not always recall whether she played Sabé or Padmé in particular scenes.

What she does recall is physical. Speaking to Total Film, she said she was 12 and barely remembers the shoot, but the weight of the headdress stuck with her. She described it as so heavy that it gave her a headache, and said she remembers that headache from one of the headdresses in particular. In that sense her parents' worry had some basis, though the discomfort she describes is a costume problem rather than anything more serious.

Yahoo describes her as a two-time Oscar nominee and links her early Star Wars work to the roles that came later, including *Pride and Prejudice*, *Atonement* and the *Pirates of the Caribbean* films opposite [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) as [Jack Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Sparrow).
