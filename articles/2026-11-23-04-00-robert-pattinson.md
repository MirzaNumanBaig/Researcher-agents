**Quick summary**

- Robert Pattinson was applauded at a special London screening of The Odyssey on 20 November.
- It was his first public appearance since being announced as the next James Bond.
- Tom Holland, Himesh Patel and Samantha Morton were at the event, according to Cinema Express.
- Denis Villeneuve is set to direct the Bond film, with Steven Knight writing the script.

[Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) walked into a room of well-wishers on 20 November, when a London screening of The Odyssey turned into an impromptu celebration of his casting as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond). The 40-year-old, who plays a role in the film, answered the applause with a bow of the head and a quiet thank you, [Cinema Express reports](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/english/news/2026/Nov/21/robert-pattinson-receives-warm-welcome-as-james-bond-at-the-odyssey-screening).

## A humble answer to a loud welcome

The screening was meant to focus on the film. Pattinson attended alongside co-stars including [Tom Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Holland), [Himesh Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himesh_Patel) and [Samantha Morton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Morton). According to Cinema Express, the host paused the programme to congratulate him on the 007 role and called for a round of applause for what the host described as his "amazing news this week".

The host also said they hoped there would be a chance to talk about Bond properly in future, but that the night was for celebrating the work on the film. Pattinson smiled, bowed slightly and said thank you. Holland, seated nearby, joined in the applause.

The outlet describes the moment as unexpectedly warm, coming while opinion online is still divided over whether he is the right pick. That debate is covered in our look at [the baggage and expectations of his Bond era](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-baggage/).

## How the Bond job came about

The role has been open since [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) stepped down in 2021, and many names were floated over the years, per Cinema Express. The new film will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, known for the Dune films. Steven Knight, creator of Peaky Blinders, is writing the screenplay. The report says the villain and the lead Bond girl are still undecided.

Why Villeneuve chose him is explored in [our piece on the director's reasoning](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-villeneuve-2/), and the audition story is in [his Bond screen test](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-bond-screen-test-2/).

## A career that led here

Cinema Express lists the credits that brought him to this point: The Twilight Saga, The Lighthouse, The Batman, Tenet, The Drama, Mickey 17 and now The Odyssey. Former Bond [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) has also publicly welcomed him, the outlet notes, and [Ben Affleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Affleck) has praised his menace in the new film.

More recent work includes Primetime, released on 5 September 2026. According to its Wikipedia entry, it earned about US$62 million against a budget of US$14.5 million, and holds an 86% Rotten Tomatoes score.

## What happens next

Bond will not arrive for a while, and Pattinson's schedule is already full. Cinema Express lists Dune: Part 3, The Batman Part II and The Take among his upcoming films. The Take, a thriller directed by Fernando Meirelles with Daisy Edgar-Jones in the cast, is due on 15 January 2027, according to Wikipedia. We have more on [the Netflix release date](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-the-take/), and everything we publish on him is gathered on [his tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).

No filming date or villain has been confirmed for Bond. Until then, the screening offered the first look at how Pattinson handles being the centre of that conversation: modestly.
