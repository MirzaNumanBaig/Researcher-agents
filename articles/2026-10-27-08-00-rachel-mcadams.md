**Quick summary**

- Searchlight has announced a sequel to the 2005 holiday film The Family Stone, titled The Families Stone.
- [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams) is among ten original cast members set to return, according to ABC News.
- The story picks up 20 years after the death of matriarch Sybil Stone, played by the late Diane Keaton.
- No release date has been announced.

[Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams) will rejoin the Stone family for a sequel to the 2005 Christmas film The Family Stone. Searchlight announced the project, titled The Families Stone, on Instagram on Saturday, with the line "Coming Soon" and no release date, [ABC News reported](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/family-stone-sequel-announced-sarah-jessica-parker-rachel/story?id=136390805).

## Who is coming back

The announcement was a collaborative post with the film's official account and one of its stars, [Sarah Jessica Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Jessica_Parker). It used a video graphic of the returning cast, with their photos arranged in the shape of a Christmas tree.

According to ABC News, the returning cast also includes [Claire Danes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claire_Danes), [Dermot Mulroney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dermot_Mulroney), [Craig T. Nelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_T._Nelson), [Luke Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Wilson), [Tyrone Giordano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyrone_Giordano), [Elizabeth Reaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Reaser), [Paul Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Schneider_(actor)) and [Brian J. White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_J._White). With McAdams and Parker, that makes ten names from the original film, more than two decades after it was released.

## The story picks up after Sybil

The sequel is set 20 years after the death of Sybil Stone, the family matriarch played by [Diane Keaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diane_Keaton). Keaton died in October 2025 at the age of 79, ABC News noted, and she was at the centre of the original film.

The 2005 story followed a close-knit family gathering for the holidays while dealing with complicated relationships and Sybil's cancer diagnosis. The new film brings the family together for Christmas again.

This time the surprise comes from their father, Kelly Stone, played by Nelson. According to the official logline cited by ABC News, he has fallen in love and is bringing the woman in his life home to meet his children.

## The writer-director behind it

[Thomas Bezucha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Bezucha), who wrote and directed the original, told CNN in a November 2025 interview that he was working on a follow-up, ABC News said. He added that he was already writing the script when he learned of Keaton's death. The sources read for this article do not say whether he is directing the sequel.

## What happens next

Searchlight has confirmed the title and the returning cast, but not when the film will arrive or where it will screen. ABC News says a release date has not yet been announced. Details on a start of production, a director and any new cast members, including the woman Kelly brings home, have not been made public in the report we read.
