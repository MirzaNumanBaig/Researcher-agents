**Quick summary**

- Netflix's animated Cinderella spin-off *Steps*, with [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) voicing Cinderella, was the No. 2 film on Netflix worldwide on 23 November, according to FlixPatrol figures cited by Comic Book Resources.
- The film arrived on 20 November and tops the chart in nine countries.
- It holds a 79% score on Rotten Tomatoes.
- Seyfried also went viral this week with an ABBA cover, and she hosts *Saturday Night Live* for the first time on 5 December.

Amanda Seyfried has a hit on her hands. *Steps*, the Netflix animated comedy that retells Cinderella from the stepsisters' side, climbed to second place among films on the platform worldwide just three days after its 20 November release, according to [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/netflix-amanda-seyfried-cinderella-steps-streaming-success-november-2026/). It is a busy week for the actress, who has also gone viral for a song and is days away from a new career milestone.

## How big is Amanda Seyfried's Steps on Netflix?

FlixPatrol's tracker had *Steps* at No. 2 among Netflix films worldwide as of 23 November, behind only the Christmas romance *Guarding Stars*, Comic Book Resources reported. It sits at the top of the chart in nine countries.

Three days is a short window, so this is a launch-week snapshot and not a measure of how long the film will last. Streaming charts reward a strong opening weekend, and the holiday period tends to keep family titles in demand. The figures come from a third-party tracker, not from Netflix itself.

## What is Steps about?

The film follows Lilith, voiced by Ali Wong, who is tired of living in Cinderella's shadow. She steals the Fairy Godmother's wand, voiced by Bette Midler, and with her sister Margot, voiced by Stephanie Hsu, crashes the royal ball. The stunt changes Cinderella's story and lets a bigger villain rise. Priscilla, voiced by Nikki Glaser, moves to grab the throne.

Lilith then joins forces with Cinderella herself, voiced by Seyfried. Their adventure takes in biker trolls, henchmen and a trip into the Screaming Woods, with a damaged sisterly bond to mend along the way. The cast also includes Daniel Radcliffe, Young Mazino and Peter Dinklage, according to Comic Book Resources.

## What are critics and viewers saying?

Reviews have been generally positive. The film has a 79% rating on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), where both critics and audiences call it an enjoyable family watch, though some say it falls short of Disney's animated classic.

Audience reaction is split in the way these films often are. One viewer compared it to *Wicked* for telling a familiar story from another character's point of view and gave it four stars out of five. Another dismissed it as a cheap attempt to cash in on Disney's princesses. A third said they normally dislike the "villain rehab" trope but enjoyed this one.

## Why is Seyfried in the news this week?

*Steps* is not the only reason. Parade, via Yahoo, reported that Seyfried joined music creators Jonathan Tilkin and Anthony Gargiula for a cover of ABBA's "The Winner Takes It All". The clip passed one million likes on both Instagram and TikTok and, according to *Good Morning America*, more than 20 million views in two days.

The song is tied to her *Mamma Mia!* films, and one fan suggested she should sing it in a third instalment. In the 2008 film the number is performed by Meryl Streep's character, Donna. Tilkin and Gargiula are known for their kitchen cover series, which has featured guests including Teddy Swims and Natasha Bedingfield, [according to Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/music/articles/amanda-seyfried-goes-viral-abba-160115295.html).

Her older work is finding new viewers too. Collider notes that *Red Riding Hood*, the 2011 fantasy directed by Catherine Hardwicke in which she plays Valerie opposite [Shiloh Fernandez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiloh_Fernandez) and [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman), is now free to stream. The film scored just 10% on Rotten Tomatoes at release, although Seyfried's performance drew praise. We covered that move in [Amanda Seyfried's In Time and Red Riding Hood Hit Streaming](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-streaming-in-time-red-riding-hood/).

## What happens next?

Seyfried makes her *Saturday Night Live* hosting debut on 5 December, with Don Toliver as musical guest, NBC announced on 23 November, as reported by Yahoo News UK. The show airs at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and streams on Peacock. [Robert De Niro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_De_Niro) hosts on 12 December and Timothée Chalamet closes the year on 19 December.
