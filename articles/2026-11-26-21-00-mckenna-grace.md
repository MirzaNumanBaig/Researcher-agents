**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) is scheduled to appear on ABC's daytime talk show "The View" on Friday, December 4.
- She is booked to discuss "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping", according to the guest lineup reported by What's On Disney Plus.
- [Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh) is listed for Tuesday, December 1, and Ava DuVernay for Thursday, December 3.
- The lineup is subject to change, and the episodes air in the United States on ABC.

Mckenna Grace is due to sit at the "The View" table on Friday, December 4, to talk about her role in "The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping". The booking comes from the show's guest lineup for the week ahead, as reported by [What's On Disney Plus](https://whatsondisneyplus.com/michelle-yeoh-mckenna-grace-to-appear-on-the-view/).

## Who else is on the guest list

Disney's announced schedule for the week names three headline guests. Michelle Yeoh is listed for Tuesday, December 1, to talk about "Blade Runner 2099", a project linked to the [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner) franchise. Director Ava DuVernay follows on Thursday, December 3, for "14th", the same day the show begins its "12 Days of Holidays" run.

Grace closes the week on Friday, December 4, which is also part of the holiday series. What's On Disney Plus notes that every booking is subject to change, so the date is a plan rather than a promise.

## Why The View matters for a film promotion

"The View" has just started its 30th season. According to What's On Disney Plus, it is the most-watched daytime talk show in the United States and regularly ranks first among daytime networks, syndicated talk shows and news programs. That reach explains why studios and publicists line up their stars for it.

The show is executive produced by [Brian Teta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Teta) and directed by Sarah de la O. Episodes air on ABC and ABC News Live, which also streams on Disney+, and new episodes reach Hulu and Disney+ the next day for a limited time.

## How the film has performed so far

"Sunrise on the Reaping" opened on October 28 and is directed by Francis Lawrence. The cast includes Joseph Zada, Whitney Peak, Ben Wang and Grace. According to [Wikipedia's film entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), it has taken about US$237.2 million at the box office against a reported budget of US$150 million, a return of roughly 1.6 times the cost.

Critics have been mixed. The same entry lists a 68 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic average of 55 out of 100 from 43 reviews. The critics' consensus describes a prequel that leans on much-loved characters and treads familiar ground, while a negative review in The New York Times called it bloated and limited.

## What happens next

Grace's appearance is pencilled in for Friday, December 4, ahead of the holiday season. The source does not say which topics she will cover beyond the film, and no clip has been released yet.
