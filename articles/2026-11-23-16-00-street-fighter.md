**Quick summary**

- Paramount's *Street Fighter* has been removed from more than 3,000 US theaters, according to Collider.
- The film has grossed about $100 million worldwide against a reported $82 million budget.
- A film usually needs roughly twice its budget to break even in cinemas, so a theatrical profit looks unlikely.
- A digital release is close, and the studio is hoping home audiences find it.

The *Street Fighter* reboot has effectively finished its run in US cinemas. [Collider reports](https://collider.com/street-fighter-removed-from-3000-theaters-box-office-digital-debut/) that Paramount's video game adaptation has been pulled from more than 3,000 theaters, with a digital debut around the corner. Its worldwide gross sits near $100 million, against a reported budget of $82 million.

## Why the numbers fall short

Collider cites the usual rule of thumb: a film of this size needs to gross about twice its budget to break even in theaters. For *Street Fighter* that would mean roughly $164 million. The film is a long way from that, and with almost no screens left it has little room to add to its total.

The film, directed by Kitao Sakurai and headlined by Noah Centineo and [Andrew Koji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Koji), opened in October. Wikipedia lists its gross at about $100.8 million, a return of about 1.23 times its budget. Critics were not kind. The film holds 42% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) and 43 out of 100 on Metacritic from 28 reviews. The Rotten Tomatoes summary credits the film with affection for the games and plenty of style, but faults its muddled storytelling and juvenile humour.

## How it compares with other game adaptations

Collider places the film against a crowded field of recent releases. Capcom's *Resident Evil* arrived shortly beforehand and has grossed more than four times as much as *Street Fighter*, despite costing about $5 million less. It is already on premium video on demand, where Collider says it has done well.

*Mortal Kombat II* took about $130 million worldwide on a reported $80 million budget this year. On a similar budget, that is a better return than *Street Fighter* managed. DC's *Clayface* also finished ahead. It has made around $120 million on a reported $40 million and holds a "Certified Fresh" 78% score. Both *Clayface* and *Street Fighter* gave the newly merged Skydance Corporation back-to-back number-one openings.

The most awkward comparison is with the past. Collider notes that the new film has barely outgrossed the 1994 adaptation starring [Jean-Claude Van Damme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Claude_Van_Damme). That film made $99 million worldwide on a reported $35 million budget, and it sits at 20% on Rotten Tomatoes.

## What fans are still talking about

Interest in the film has not disappeared with the screens. Comic Book Resources ran a list of the strongest game characters missing from the movie. [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) says the 2026 film features Ryu, Akuma and Blanka, while the franchise's most powerful fighters stayed off screen. It names the android Seth, the veteran Gen, the Illuminati leader Gill, the hermit Oro and Ingrid, who it calls the strongest entity in the series. The list shows how much of the game's lore is still untouched if the studio ever returns to it.

Cody Rhodes appears in the film as Guile, according to Collider's photo captions. Wikipedia's cast list also names Callina Liang and Orville Peck. For more on the film's slide, see our earlier reports on [how Street Fighter lost 60% of its screens](https://moduscarry.com/street-fighter-box-office-screens-godzilla/) and [Callina Liang's Street Fighter passing $100M](https://moduscarry.com/callina-liang-street-fighter-box-office/).

## What happens next

The next step is the digital release. Collider says the hope is that the film will follow *Resident Evil* and find a bigger audience at home, though it has not said when the film will arrive. Whether a strong streaming and rental run is enough to change the studio's view of a sequel has not been reported. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much has Street Fighter made at the box office?
About $100 million worldwide, according to Collider, against a reported budget of $82 million.

### Is Street Fighter still in theaters?
Mostly not. Collider reports it has been removed from more than 3,000 US theaters and from virtually every cinema in the country.

Collider describes the digital debut as around the corner but does not give a date. Check Paramount's announcements for the exact day.
