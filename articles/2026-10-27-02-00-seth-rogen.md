- Rogen's comedy The Invite reached No. 1 on HBO Max's movie chart, according to a SlashFilm report on 24 October.

[Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) is having a busy week. Sephora named him the face of its holiday campaign on 26 October, and a few days earlier his R-rated comedy The Invite landed on HBO Max and shot to the top of its movie chart.

## Sephora's "We All Sephora" campaign

According to the [Sephora Newsroom](https://newsroom.sephora.com/sephora-partners-with-seth-rogen-to-celebrate-the-beauty-gifter-in-everyone-this-holiday-season/), the campaign was unveiled in the US and Canada on 26 October. It casts Rogen as an unlikely beauty shopper hunting for holiday presents, with a Sephora Beauty Advisor guiding him through the aisles.

The spot was directed by [Jason Woliner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Woliner) and created with the agency GUT New York. In it, Rogen picks out setting spray for his producer and a fragrance for his cousin, among other gifts for family and friends.

"Holiday gifting can be stressful, so anything that makes it easier is a win in my book," Rogen said in the release. Zena Arnold, Sephora U.S. chief marketing officer, said he brings "warmth, curiosity and humor" that makes beauty feel approachable.

To tease the launch, Sephora turned its store at The Grove in Los Angeles into a "Sethphora", swapping its own name on the facade for his. The retailer says the campaign goes to air on 29 October across connected TV, linear television, streaming and social.

## What Rogen picked

E! News published an exclusive first look at the gifts. [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437419/seth-rogen-is-sephoras-new-holiday-star-and-hes-sharing-his-gift-picks) reports the list includes a red and near-infrared LED face mask for his mum and a sweet-smelling fragrance for his wife, [Lauren Miller Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Miller_Rogen). There is also a men's cologne, a brightening skincare routine, a long-wearing setting spray and a 3-in-1 hair cream.

Sephora says shoppers can buy the same picks on Sephora.com and in its app. The campaign is US and Canada only, so Australian readers should not expect to see the "Sethphora" storefront locally.

## The Invite climbs the HBO Max chart

The more durable news for Rogen's fans is on the screen. According to a [Yahoo report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/r-rated-comedy-starring-seth-175001020.html), originally published by Men's Journal, The Invite reached No. 1 on HBO Max after arriving on 23 October. It cites SlashFilm's report of 24 October.

Olivia Wilde directs and stars alongside Rogen. He plays Joe and she plays Angela, a couple whose shaky marriage is tested when they invite their upstairs neighbours, Hawk and Piña (Edward Norton and Penélope Cruz), to dinner. The script is by Rashida Jones and Will McCormack, adapted from Cesc Gay's 2020 Spanish film The People Upstairs.

The A24 release premiered at Sundance and received positive reviews before reaching cinemas over the summer. Deadline reported worldwide ticket sales above US$55 million. SlashFilm critic Nina Starner called it "one of the year's standout films".

Most of the story plays out in a single apartment. That keeps the focus on conversation and awkward silences rather than big set pieces, which suits a film now being watched on the couch.

## A red carpet appearance with Lauren

Rogen was also photographed on the red carpet with his wife. [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/seth-rogen-looks-completely-smitten-185346000.html), republishing a Parade story from 25 October, says the pair attended a premiere at the 2026 AFI Fest at the TCL Chinese Theatre in Hollywood.

The piece quotes the couple speaking to People in September about working in the same industry. "It doesn't feel like we're not spending time together even when we're working," Rogen said. The article also notes he stars in the Apple TV+ comedy series The Studio.

## What happens next

The Sephora spot begins airing on 29 October, and the retailer says the featured products are available now. The Invite is streaming on HBO Max, and chart positions there change daily, so its No. 1 spot may not last the week.
