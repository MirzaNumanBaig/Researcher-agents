**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, walked the 2026 CFDA Awards red carpet in New York on Monday, 2 November.
- She wore a black halter gown, a platinum bouffant and a berry-toned lip, a step away from her recent bare-faced style.
- Outlets differ on the exact lip shade, calling it raspberry, cherry or magenta, but agree the look was the talking point.
- Her nails were a deep red that ELLE UK labelled "winter cherry".

Pamela Anderson swapped her signature bare-skin look for a dose of Old Hollywood glamour at the 2026 CFDA Awards at the American Museum of Natural History in New York on Monday night. The actress, 59, arrived with a teased platinum bouffant, a bold lip and a long black halter gown, and fashion and beauty outlets spent the next day dissecting each element.

## What Pamela Anderson wore to the CFDA Awards

According to [WWD, via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/pamela-anderson-channels-old-hollywood-164716568.html), Anderson chose a black halter dress with a feather-embellished neckline. She paired it with a white satin polka-dot clutch and feathery black heels.

[Yahoo News Canada](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/pamela-anderson-swaps-no-makeup-155551338.html) describes the dress as form-fitting and ankle-length, with glittering beads along the halterneck. It also reports bejewelled pointed-toe slingbacks, a cream silk clutch dotted with black polka dots and fire-engine red nails.

The two reports differ slightly on the details of the neckline and shoes. They agree on the overall picture: a sleek black column, with the playful polka-dot bag as the one light touch.

## The hair and makeup behind the look

The hair drew the most attention. WWD says Anderson's short platinum hair was styled into a voluminous updo, with the curls swept back from her face and lifted at the crown. It credits hairstylist Jacob Rozenberg and assistant Michaella Garfinkel, who used Biolage products.

Yahoo News Canada compared the shape to a pompadour and said she appeared to take a page from Marilyn Monroe's beauty playbook.

Makeup is where the story gets interesting. WWD notes that Anderson said in 2023 she would stop getting fully glammed for high-profile appearances, after her longtime makeup artist Alexis Vogel died in 2019. Since then she has added the occasional touch, such as eye makeup designed by [Pat McGrath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pat_McGrath) in September.

On Monday, WWD reports, she wore little more than mascara, light pink blush and a glossy raspberry lip. WWD suggested she likely did her own makeup, possibly with products from Sonsie, the skincare brand she joined as cofounder and owner in January 2024.

Yahoo News Canada saw a bolder face, listing matte magenta lipstick, black eyeliner and a berry flush. Good Housekeeping UK settled on a cherry "bitten" lip with light mascara and otherwise pared-back skin. Without an official makeup credit, the exact shade remains a matter of interpretation.

## Her own words on makeup

WWD quoted Anderson from an interview earlier in the month about a limited-edition Sonsie lip balm line, with shades and flavours inspired by crops she grows in her backyard. She told the outlet: "It's not like I'm against makeup. I just feel like in my personal life, I prefer not to wear so much."

She also said that good skin gives her the confidence to try small changes. That fits the pattern of her recent appearances rather than reversing it.

## Nails and the Academy Museum Gala

ELLE UK picked out her manicure, calling it "winter cherry", a deeper and richer take on classic red, and set it against her black gown. The magazine quoted Susan Carroll, founder of the Young LDN salon in Notting Hill, on why careful nail prep matters for dark reds.

Yahoo News Canada also pointed back to the 2026 Academy Museum Gala on 17 October in Los Angeles. There she wore a white collared shirt, a trailing mint green chiffon skirt and soft Shirley Temple-style curls with new curly bangs. Our own coverage of that night is in [Pamela Anderson at the Academy Museum Gala in Carolina Herrera](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-academy-museum-gala/). Two red carpets within a fortnight, with two very different hairstyles, show a clear willingness to experiment.

For more on the same event, see [Pamela Anderson Wears Carolina Herrera to CFDA Awards](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-cfda-fashion-awards/).

## What happens next

No new red-carpet dates for Anderson have been confirmed in the sources. Readers can follow her appearances on our [Pamela Anderson](https://moduscarry.com/tag/pamela-anderson/) page and in the [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

She is also tied to a busy year of screen work. WWD describes her as the star of The Last Showgirl and the [Naked Gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Naked_Gun) reboot. Whether Monday's glamour becomes her new default or another one-off experiment is not something any of the outlets could say.
