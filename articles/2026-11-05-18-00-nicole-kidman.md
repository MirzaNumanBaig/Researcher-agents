**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on 5 November, the first runway the French house has staged in Australia.
- The show was held at the Cutaway in Barangaroo and re-staged the collection first shown in Biarritz in April.
- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) and [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) were also among the guests, according to The Age.
- Separately, the 1998 film Practical Magic is among the ten most-watched titles on HBO Max in the US, Collider reports.

Nicole Kidman was among the biggest names at Chanel's first Australian runway show in Sydney on Thursday 5 November, a night The Age described as one of the biggest moments in the country's fashion history. The actor flew in for the Cruise 2026/27 presentation, which gives the Nicole Kidman Chanel Sydney story a local twist: a global ambassador coming home for a global brand.

## A first for Chanel in Australia

The show took place at the Cutaway in Barangaroo. According to [The Age](https://www.theage.com.au/lifestyle/fashion/kylie-minogue-dom-dolla-and-more-attend-chanel-runway-in-sydney-20261105-p61bbx.html), it was a re-staging of creative director Matthieu Blazy's debut cruise collection, first presented in Biarritz, France, on 28 April. The Australian Women's Weekly reported two showings on the day, at 2:30pm and 6pm.

The Sydney set borrowed from the French original, with sand-coloured carpet, mirrored walls and a maze of rooms. Biarritz matters to the house because Gabrielle "Coco" Chanel opened her first atelier there in 1915.

Chanel's fashion president, Bruno Pavlovsky, told The Age the collection is the same everywhere, seen "through a new angle". He added that the Sydney version should bring a different mood and energy. Chanel opened its first Australian boutique in Sydney in the 1980s, and a major renovation of its Flinders Lane store in Melbourne is due to open next week.

## Who came, and how Kidman arrived

Beyond Kidman, The Age lists Robbie, Minogue, Luhrmann and producer [Dom Dolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dom_Dolla) among the guests. Models [Julia Nobis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Nobis), [Gemma Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemma_Ward), [Catherine McNeil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_McNeil) and [Charlee Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlee_Fraser) walked the runway, and artist [Atong Atem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atong_Atem) was in the audience.

The Women's Weekly says Kidman landed in Australia with her 15-year-old daughter. She wore a Chanel cap and carried a Chanel crocodile Maxi Flap bag, which the magazine puts at nearly $16,000. Robbie, a Chanel ambassador too, travelled to Sydney on her own, the same report says. Jacob Elordi, 29, who recently became the face of Bleu de Chanel, was also listed as returning for the event.

The outlet notes that Kidman rejoined Chanel as an ambassador last October, more than two decades after her No5 campaign. It describes that advertisement as the most expensive TV commercial ever produced, at $33 million. The full guest list had not been confirmed when the Women's Weekly published.

## Why Chanel chose Sydney

Pavlovsky said the decision was not about the beach or surf, even though Biarritz and Sydney share both. It was about what Biarritz means to the brand, where Chanel began designing haute couture. He called the collection "very meaningful" and said the house sees Australia as a market it wants to grow.

The Age reports that the guests who mattered most were Chanel's top clients, whom the house hopes will visit a boutique when the collection goes on sale on 6 November.

## Practical Magic finds a second life on streaming

Away from the runway, Kidman's back catalogue is getting attention. Collider reports that the 1998 film Practical Magic is among the ten most-watched movies on HBO Max in the US.

That comes as the sequel, Practical Magic 2, nears the end of its cinema run. Collider says it earned about $105 million worldwide against a $75 million budget, and that critics and fans responded poorly. Kidman and Sandra Bullock returned as sisters Gillian and Sally Owens. The site's reviewer called the follow-up "painfully forgettable".

## What happens next

The Cruise 2026/27 collection reaches Chanel boutiques the day after the show, according to The Age. The renovated Melbourne boutique opens next week. The Women's Weekly says more names may be confirmed, so the guest list could still grow.
