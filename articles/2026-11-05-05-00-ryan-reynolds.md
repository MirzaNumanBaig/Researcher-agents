**Quick summary**

- An old behind-the-scenes clip of a Deadpool 2 makeup prank has resurfaced online, according to Yahoo Style Canada.
- It shows [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) tearing off prosthetics that took about seven to eight hours to apply.
- Some viewers call it harmless mischief, while others have described it as bullying.
- The footage was reportedly released as promotional material ahead of the film's 2019 home-video release.

The **Ryan Reynolds Deadpool 2 prank** is drawing fresh criticism after an old behind-the-scenes video went round social media again this week. According to [Yahoo Style Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/ryan-reynolds-hit-furious-backlash-161917278.html), the clip shows the actor destroying a long day of prosthetic work on his makeup crew, and many viewers have questioned whether the joke was worth it.

## What the clip shows

The footage reportedly sat among the official Deadpool 2 behind-the-scenes material put out through the film's social media account before its 2019 home-video release. In it, Reynolds sits in the makeup chair for roughly seven to eight hours while lead makeup designer [Bill Corso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Corso) and his team turn him into the heavily scarred [Wade Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool).

Yahoo reports that the process involved detailed silicone prosthetics across his face and neck. Once the application was finished, Reynolds studied himself in the mirror, said it was all wrong and told the crew they would have to start again. He then began pulling the pieces off his face.

The artists appear stunned in the video. Some reportedly walked out of frame, and one crew member is seen throwing a brush and swearing. Yahoo adds that, according to some reports, Reynolds had already learned that filming for the day was cancelled but let the team finish before revealing the joke.

## How viewers have reacted

Reaction has been divided. Some fans see the moment as typical of Reynolds' mischievous humour. Others, quoted by Yahoo, called it bullying. One critic asked what was funny about making hardworking crew members believe their efforts had been wasted. Another suggested the makeup team deserved overtime and a week off.

A different commenter said a prank should be funny rather than cruel, and pointed to the crew member's reaction as evidence it was not fun for the people on the receiving end. Not every response was hostile: one viewer joked that the artists deserved a raise for their patience. These are social media comments, not statements from anyone who worked on the film, and no member of the crew is quoted in the report.

## Why the timing is drawing attention

Yahoo says the renewed scrutiny arrives after the legal dispute involving Reynolds' wife, [Blake Lively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Lively), and her It Ends With Us co-star and director [Justin Baldoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Baldoni). The report says that case ended in May 2026 with an out-of-court settlement between the two parties. It also states plainly that the makeup prank has no connection to that dispute, and that its timing has simply contributed to the cold reception online.

## What happens next

Neither Reynolds nor his representatives are quoted in the Yahoo report, and there is no sign of a response so far.
