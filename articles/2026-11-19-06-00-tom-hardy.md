**Quick summary**

- A May report said Paramount had dropped Tom Hardy from MobLand after clashes with producers; the Hollywood Reporter said he had not been fired.
- Co-stars Helen Mirren and Pierce Brosnan publicly backed him over the summer.
- Hardy told AP there was "no firing" and "no arguments in that aspect".
- On September 3, Paramount+ renewed MobLand for a third season with Hardy returning as Harry Da Souza.

A report that [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) had been shown the door at MobLand dominated entertainment news for months this year, and it ended with the actor named first in the show's season three renewal. A look back at the saga, published by [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/bts-drama-that-nearly-got-tom-hardy-fired-from-mobland), shows how a single newsletter item grew into a Hollywood row and then faded.

## How the firing story started

On May 22, [Matthew Belloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Belloni) wrote in his Puck newsletter that Paramount had opted not to pick up Hardy for a third season after clashes with producers [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth) and David Glasser. According to Puck, as summarised by Startefacts, Hardy was often late to set, kept reworking dialogue and sending script notes to Butterworth and creator [Ronan Bennett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronan_Bennett), and was unhappy that the show was becoming an ensemble rather than a Hardy vehicle.

Butterworth, who co-wrote every episode of season one, reportedly threatened to quit, and Paramount was said to favour the writer over the star. Those details are Puck's reporting, and Hardy has denied the premise.

Anonymous insiders quoted in tabloid coverage went further, claiming he kept co-stars waiting on set. Those claims are unverified.

## Fired, or "in limbo"

Within a day the picture changed. The Hollywood Reporter said Hardy had not been fired and that his future was "very much in limbo". Season two had wrapped in March, so the dispute concerned a third season that had not yet been ordered.

Per Deadline, Hardy, Butterworth and Glasser met in London in June to agree a way forward, with executive producer [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie) involved in the talks. Hardy was also reportedly on a three-year contract, which made a return simpler than the headlines suggested.

## The co-stars stepped in

[Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) was the first to respond, six days after the Puck report. She posted a photo of Hardy in character on Instagram on May 28, with comments switched off and the caption "Love you now and always, Helen." In June she said she would work with him again without hesitation. By September she told [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) that "Tom is very much in".

[Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) told The Hollywood Reporter he was "blindsided" and learned of the story from a text his son sent while he was on holiday. He called it "a storm in a teacup" and said that without Hardy there is no MobLand.

Hardy stayed quiet until the season two press tour in September. He told AP: "There was no firing, there was no walking away and there was no arguments in that aspect." Asked by Extra about Mirren's support, he said "I love Helen. She's my friend," and called the saga "an interesting few weeks".

## The renewal that settled it

On September 3, two weeks before season two premiered, Paramount+ renewed MobLand for a third season. The announcement confirmed Hardy's return as fixer Harry Da Souza, alongside Brosnan and Mirren as Conrad and Maeve Harrigan. That ran against the early trade reading that the studio had chosen Butterworth over its star.

Startefacts also notes the irony that the ensemble approach Hardy reportedly disliked helped season two. The standout character, it says, is Frankie Cooper, a scooter-riding drug lord played by [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) who did not appear in season one. The two episodes built around him are tied for the show's highest IMDb rating, according to that report. [Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine) also stars.

## What happens next

Season three is confirmed but, based on the sources read, no release date has been announced. Hardy, Butterworth and Glasser have reportedly settled their differences, so attention now turns to production and how the ensemble expands. We will update this story when Paramount+ shares dates.
