**Quick summary**

- Russell Crowe's 2026 action thriller The Get Out is the ninth most-watched movie on Paramount+ in the US, according to FlixPatrol.
- The film had earlier reached No. 2 on the same chart in October.
- It was not a box office hit, and it holds 48% from critics and 49% from audiences on Rotten Tomatoes.
- Crowe's next releases include The Last Druid in 2027, with Billion Dollar Spy and a Highlander reboot still undated.

[Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s action thriller The Get Out has climbed back into the top 10 on Paramount+ in the United States, giving the Russell Crowe The Get Out story a second wind after a quiet theatrical run. According to [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/russell-crowe-the-get-out-action-paramount-success-november-2026/), the film now sits ninth on the streamer's chart, based on FlixPatrol data.

## A second run on the streaming charts

The film is a streaming story more than a cinema one. Comic Book Resources reports that it did not perform at the box office, but found an audience once it reached Paramount+. In October it rose as high as No. 2 on the platform's US list, and its return to the top 10 suggests viewers are still discovering it.

That pattern is common for mid-budget thrillers, which can build an audience at home long after a muted cinema release. The figures here come from a single chart tracker, so they show relative popularity on one service rather than total viewing.

## What The Get Out is about

[Derrick Borte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Borte) directed the film and co-wrote it with Daniel Forte. Crowe plays Marco Kapak, an Albanian nightclub owner whose venue doubles as a front for money laundering. Kapak wants to leave that life behind and plans to sell the club.

His plans unravel when two amateur thieves, played by [Nina Dobrev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Dobrev) and [Aaron Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Paul), rob him and leave him short of money. A mysterious buyer then turns up, played by [Luke Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Evans), and Kapak is pulled into a mix of mystery and action as he tries to get out.

The supporting cast also includes [Teresa Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa_Palmer), Danny Zovatto, [Josh McConville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_McConville) and Kartiah Vergara. Palmer and McConville are Australian, which gives local viewers a reason to look twice.

## How critics and audiences responded

Reviews were mixed. On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) the film holds a 48% critics score and a 49% audience score, according to Comic Book Resources. Some viewers compared it favourably with John Wick and Heat, but the outlet says that was not enough to win broad praise.

Where reviewers were positive, they tended to single out Crowe. Comic Book Resources notes he is usually seen as an action-forward star, and that some enjoyed watching him take on a more serious thriller role. The same notes say the action could not lift a story they found weak.

## Crowe's busy 2026 and what comes next

The Get Out was one of several Crowe films this year. He also appeared in The Weight and Beast, and in Netflix's Unabomber, which holds a 26% critics score on Rotten Tomatoes despite praise for his performance, per Comic Book Resources.

Looking ahead, Crowe is due to star in William Eubank's historical action drama The Last Druid in 2027. It follows a Celtic elder who takes up arms to protect his family from an invading Roman army. He is also attached to Amma Asante's Billion Dollar Spy and has a role in Chad Stahelski's Highlander reboot. Neither project has an official release date yet.
