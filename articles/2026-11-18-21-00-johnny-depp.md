**Quick summary**

- [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) stars as Ebenezer Scrooge in *Ebenezer*, a straight retelling of Charles Dickens's *A Christmas Carol*, now in cinemas.
- Yahoo's weekly new-movies guide calls it a "mixed bag": competent and serviceable, but uninspired.
- The guide praises the supporting cast and director [Ti West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ti_West)'s spooky sequences, and finds Depp's performance too broad.
- Critics quoted in the guide are split, with TheWrap unimpressed and The Guardian mildly positive.

*Ebenezer*, the Christmas ghost story that puts Johnny Depp in Scrooge's nightshirt, is now playing in cinemas. A weekend viewing guide from Yahoo describes the film as a competent but unremarkable adaptation, and says the lead performance is the least convincing part of it.

## What Yahoo's guide says about Ebenezer

The verdict comes from "Trust Me, I Watch Everything", Yahoo's weekly roundup of notable films opening or arriving on streaming each Friday. It files *Ebenezer* under "mixed bag" and describes it as a "totally competent, perfectly fine adaptation".

The reviewer's main complaint is that the film's marketing promises "the story you haven't seen", while the movie itself is a completely straight version of one of the most adapted stories ever told. In this telling, Depp plays Ebenezer Scrooge, an elderly miser who is visited by the ghost of his former business partner and then by the Spirits of Christmas Past, Present and Yet to Come. They push him to face his history and fight for a second chance at happiness.

## The Depp performance

The guide is blunt about the lead. It calls Depp's Scrooge a big, broad turn full of mugging, "like a cartoon character". It also argues the role is less interesting than a Depp creation such as [Captain Jack Sparrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Sparrow), simply because audiences have seen Scrooge played this way so many times.

The reviewer also reads the film as tied to Depp's wider career story, saying it is hard not to hear the character's lines about no longer being the man he was as a comment on the actor. That is the guide's interpretation, not something the film or its makers have said.

## The supporting cast and the direction

The same review is warmer about everyone around Depp. It singles out [Tramell Tillman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tramell_Tillman) as the Ghost of Christmas Present and [Andrea Riseborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Riseborough) as the Ghost of Christmas Past, saying both give committed, earnest performances.

Ti West also gets credit. The guide says the director, known for lower-budget horror, has made a smooth step up to bigger studio work and delivers some "genuinely spooky sequences". Its closing judgement is that the film is not better than what the Muppets have already done with the material, but is serviceable.

## How other critics are reacting

The guide quotes two other reviews to show how split the response is. Amelia Emberwing of TheWrap wrote that there have been successful retellings of *A Christmas Carol* and there will be more, but that far more attempts end up on a long list of forgotten bids to cash in on the holiday box office, and that West's film joins that list. Peter Bradshaw of The Guardian was kinder, calling it "a decent enough new version" that avoids big revisionist ideas which might have clunked.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Johnny Depp in Ebenezer?

Yes. Depp plays Ebenezer Scrooge in the film, according to Yahoo's weekend guide.

### Who directed Ebenezer?

Ti West directed it. The guide notes he is moving from smaller horror projects to bigger studio films.

### Is Ebenezer a new take on A Christmas Carol?

Not according to the reviewer. Despite the marketing line about "the story you haven't seen", the guide calls it a completely straight retelling of the Dickens story.

### How have critics reviewed Ebenezer?

Reviews are mixed. TheWrap's critic was unimpressed, while The Guardian's Peter Bradshaw called it a decent enough new version.
