**Quick summary**

- Bhanushali Studios Limited has announced *Captivated* as its first international production, according to reports published on 19 November 2026.
- The true-crime drama stars [Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino), [Isla Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_Fisher) and [Blake Ritson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Ritson).
- [Dito Montiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dito_Montiel) directs, and the film is made in association with Los Angeles company 32Red Entertainment.
- The story is inspired by the 1973 kidnapping of John Paul Getty III and is set in 1970s Italy.
- No release date has been announced in the sources.

Isla Fisher's Getty kidnapping drama has picked up a new production partner. The Captivated Bhanushali Studios announcement says the company, founded by producer Vinod Bhanushali, is making *Captivated* as its first international production, according to [Bollywood Hungama](https://www.bollywoodhungama.com/news/features/bhanushali-studios-makes-international-production-debut-with-al-pacino-and-isla-fisher-starrer-captivated/) and [Culture Crossroads](https://culturecrossroads.ca/posts/bhanushali-studios-limited-enters-global-arena-with-al-pacino-isla-fisher-starrer-captivated/). The announcement came on 19 November.

## What is Captivated about?

The film draws on the 1973 abduction of John Paul Getty III, a case both outlets describe as one of the most notorious kidnappings of the twentieth century. It is set in 1970s Italy and, per the reports, looks at the events from a fresh angle. The focus is an unexpected emotional connection that makes an already tense situation harder to control.

Cast and crew details come from the announcement. Pacino is the Academy Award winner known for *The Godfather* and *Scarface*. Fisher is credited for [Wedding Crashers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_Crashers) and *Now You See Me*, and Ritson for *The Gilded Age*. Fisher was first linked to the project in our earlier report, [Isla Fisher joins Al Pacino in a Getty kidnapping thriller](https://moduscarry.com/isla-fisher-captivated-al-pacino/).

## Who is making it?

Montiel, known for *A Guide to Recognizing Your Saints* and *Fighting*, directs. The cinematographer is Stefan Ciupek, whose credits include [Slumdog Millionaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slumdog_Millionaire) and *Rumours*.

Producers named are Michael Mammoliti and [Manasvi Mamgai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manasvi_Mamgai) of 32Red Entertainment, [Andrea Bucko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Bucko) and Nicky Weinstock of Sweetheart Entertainment, and [Cassian Elwes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassian_Elwes), whose producing credits include [Dallas Buyers Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dallas_Buyers_Club). Mammoliti also co-wrote the film.

## A personal link to the story

Mammoliti told the outlets the project is deeply personal because his family has a direct connection to the events. He said he grew up with an understanding of the story that differs from the one audiences have seen on screen, and that the film is a chance to add a new dimension to a case people think they know.

Vinod Bhanushali said his company has always believed in stories that cross borders, and called working with global talent a natural next step. Mamgai said the partnership is a step towards more collaboration between Indian cinema and Hollywood.
