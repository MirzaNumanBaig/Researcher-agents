**Quick summary**

- Black Doves season 2 landed on Netflix on Thursday, 5 November, with all six episodes.
- It opened with 3.8 million views and 19.9 million hours viewed, ranking No. 4 on Netflix's English-language TV list for 2 to 8 November.
- Season 1 opened with 10.8 million views, so the new run started about 65 per cent lower, according to Startefacts.
- Critics have been warmer on season 2 than on the first run, and Netflix's chart for 9 to 15 November will show its first full week.

[Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) season 2 has opened to roughly a third of the audience that watched the first season, even though reviewers are rating it higher. The Netflix spy thriller starring [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) posted 3.8 million views in its opening window, according to a [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/keira-knightley-s-black-doves-season-2-doesn-t-seem-to-be-working-out-at-all) analysis of Netflix's weekly figures, and it did so against a crowded week of true crime and documentaries.

## How the numbers compare

All six episodes arrived on Thursday, 5 November, and the series logged 19.9 million hours viewed. That placed it fourth on Netflix's English TV chart for 2 to 8 November. Ahead of it were a docuseries on Natalee Holloway, a documentary about [Matthew Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Perry) and the third season of *Nobody Wants This*.

Season 1 also premiered on a Thursday and opened with 10.8 million views. Startefacts puts the fall at about 65 per cent on a like-for-like basis. The Holloway docuseries pulled 12.9 million views, more than three times the Black Doves figure. The same report says the season 2 opening ranks 13th out of 14 comparable Netflix thrillers, though it does not say which titles make up that group.

## Reviews are better, not worse

The drop is notable because the notices have been strong. Startefacts cites Inverse calling the new run even more of a blast, TheWrap saying it gets even better, and Tom's Guide giving it four stars out of five and pitching it as a cure for [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses) withdrawal.

[Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) returns alongside Knightley, and [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire) is back running the Doves. New cast members include [Neve Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Campbell), [Ambika Mod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ambika_Mod), [Sam Riley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Riley) and [Sylvia Hoeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvia_Hoeks). Netflix had faith in the show, renewing it in August 2024, before the first season had aired.

## Why viewers may have stayed away

Startefacts argues the problem is not Knightley and offers three explanations. These are the outlet's reading, not Netflix's:

- **The wait.** Season 1 arrived in December 2024, so fans waited almost two years for the follow-up.
- **The calendar.** The first run was a Christmas thriller released at Christmas. Season 2 came in early November without that seasonal pull.
- **The competition.** It launched in a week dominated by Netflix's own true crime titles.

There are signs of interest elsewhere. The report says Black Doves was the most-searched series in the UK on 8 November, ranked No. 2 on Netflix's UK list for the week and debuted at No. 1 in Germany.

## What happens next

The first full week will give a clearer picture. Netflix's chart for 9 to 15 November covers it. Season 1 grew in its second week, climbing from 10.8 million to 14.6 million views and reaching No. 1. Startefacts notes that season 2 would need to nearly quadruple to match that second-week figure, so it calls a rebound a long shot.

The outlet also points to What's on Netflix, which describes the show's renewal as uncertain. Netflix has not announced a third season. ## Frequently asked questions

### How many people watched Black Doves season 2?

It recorded 3.8 million views and 19.9 million hours in its first window, 2 to 8 November, according to Startefacts' reading of Netflix's data.

### Is Black Doves season 2 doing worse than season 1?

On opening numbers, yes. Season 1 started with 10.8 million views, and season 2 is about 65 per cent lower. Critics, however, have rated the new season more highly.

### Has Black Doves been renewed for a third season?

Not as of this report. Netflix has made no announcement, and What's on Netflix, as cited by Startefacts, describes the renewal as uncertain.
