**Quick summary**

- [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner) were photographed on two dates in New York over the weekend of 7 and 8 November 2026.
- On Saturday they attended a performance of *Paranormal Activity* at the August Wilson Theatre on Broadway.
- On Sunday they were seen with coffee on a stoop in the West Village, according to reports citing TMZ.
- Elordi wore a beanie at both outings, and fans noticed bleached blond hair underneath.
- Neither of them has publicly discussed the relationship in these reports.

Jacob Elordi and Kendall Jenner spent the weekend in New York, where photographers caught them at a Broadway show on Saturday and over coffee in the West Village on Sunday. The Australian actor also drew attention for what he was hiding: a new, much lighter hair colour.

## A Broadway night out

The pair were photographed at *Paranormal Activity* on Saturday, 7 November, according to [ELLE](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a74378773/jacob-elordi-hair-change-hiding-kendall-jenner-nyc-dates-2026/). The show is billed as an immersive stage take on the horror franchise, as [The Cut](https://www.thecut.com/article/jacob-elordi-is-blond-now.html) describes it. The Cut also noted that both wore black, which suited the theme, and that photos of the night were published by Deuxmoi.

## The hair under the hat

Elordi, who is naturally brown-haired, wore a beanie to both outings and added a hoodie on Sunday for extra cover, ELLE reports. It did not fully work. A strip of blond hair showed at the back of his hat in the theatre photos.

Elordi has not explained the change. ELLE points to *Outer Dark*, the dark fairy-tale film he is set to star in with Lily-Rose Depp. It cited a May 2025 Deadline report that the project was being lined up to shoot in 2026. The Cut offers a different guess: it suggests the change is linked to *Scapegoat*, an Ari Aster film it says Elordi is shooting in New York. It describes the plot only as rumoured, and no outlet in this batch has confirmed either theory. We have covered the look separately in [Jacob Elordi Goes Blonde on Date Night With Kendall Jenner](https://moduscarry.com/jacob-elordi-blonde-hair/).

## Coffee on a stoop

On Sunday, 8 November, the couple were seen buying coffee in the West Village and sitting on the steps of a building, as reported by [UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/kendall-jenner-and-jacob-elordi-went-on-a-romantic-date-in-new-york), citing TMZ. [BollywoodShaadis](https://www.bollywoodshaadis.com/articles/kendall-jenner-and-jacob-elordi-get-closer-spotted-enjoying-coffee-dare-in-new-york-city-86490) adds the outfit details: Elordi in a light grey zip-up hoodie over a white tee, Jenner in a forest green field coat with a leopard-print silk scarf, both in black sunglasses.

Both outlets say the pair also went to Jenner's Halloween-themed birthday party at the Chateau Marmont the weekend before. They add that the two have been seen together in places from London to Hawaii.

## How the story got here

ELLE's [relationship timeline](https://www.elle.com/culture/celebrities/a71340974/kendall-jenner-jacob-elordi-relationship-timeline/) traces the pair back to a February 2022 photo of them in Paris with a group of friends. They were photographed talking at the Vanity Fair Oscar Party on 15 March 2026. Dating rumours began at Coachella in April, when unconfirmed reports said they were seen kissing at an after-party. ELLE notes that no photos of that moment surfaced.

Photos from a private Hawaii trip followed in May. Sources then told People and [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight) that they had been spending time together for months. ELLE also reports that in February 2026 a source told Entertainment Tonight that Elordi and [Olivia Jade Giannulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Jade) had ended their on-off relationship for good.

ELLE reports that in July, an unnamed source told Entertainment Tonight that Jenner had been telling close friends she was in love. Treat that as an anonymous claim: neither star has confirmed it.
