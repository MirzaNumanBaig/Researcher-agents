**Quick summary**

- Apple TV's adaptation of William Gibson's *Neuromancer* is due to premiere on 22 January 2027, according to Winter is Coming.
- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) starred in 1995's *Johnny Mnemonic*, based on a Gibson short story set in the same world.
- The new series is expected to start afresh rather than follow the Reeves film, the outlet reports.
- The 1995 film took about US$19 million against a US$26 million budget.

The Keanu Reeves Johnny Mnemonic film of 1995 is being pulled back into the spotlight as Apple TV prepares *Neuromancer*, which is due to premiere on 22 January 2027, according to [Winter is Coming](https://winteriscoming.net/keanu-reeves-sci-fi-flop-stealth-sequel-apple-tv-neuromancer). The site argues the old box office disappointment sits in the same fictional universe as the new show, even though the series will not acknowledge the movie.

## How the two projects connect

William Gibson's *Neuromancer* came out in 1982 and is widely credited as the book that set the template for cyberpunk. Gibson also wrote short stories in the same world, and [Johnny Mnemonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Mnemonic) is one of them. Winter is Coming describes that story as a prequel to the novel, which is why it sees the 1995 film as the first live-action visit to Gibson's universe.

Earlier attempts to bring *Neuromancer* itself to the screen collapsed before production, the outlet says. Apple TV is the first to get a series moving.

## What happened to the film

On a budget of US$26 million, *Johnny Mnemonic* earned about US$19 million at the box office, according to figures Winter is Coming attributes to Box Office Mojo. That means it did not recover its cost in cinemas. Critics were no kinder: the outlet cites a [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) score of 20 per cent, with audiences a little higher at 32 per cent.

Gibson wrote the screenplay himself and made notable changes to suit the screen. The biggest was swapping the short story's Molly Millions for a new character, Jane, played by [Dina Meyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dina_Meyer). Molly also appears in *Neuromancer*, and the Apple TV series will give her an on-screen debut with Brianna Middleton in the role, according to the same report.

## The Matrix and Cyberpunk 2077

Winter is Coming is careful to say that *The Matrix* is not part of the *Neuromancer* canon. It does argue that the idea of a human mind plugged into a shared digital space comes straight from Gibson's work, and that Reeves' turn as Neo in 1999 would not exist without it. The outlet also points to Reeves' role as Johnny Silverhand in the video game *Cyberpunk 2077* as further proof that the 1995 film was an exception rather than a sign the material could not work.

Its view is that the weak result came down to execution, not Gibson's ideas. That is the outlet's opinion, not a fact on record, but it explains why the show's arrival is drawing fresh attention to an overlooked Reeves title.

## What happens next

*Neuromancer* lands on Apple TV on 22 January 2027. Gibson wrote two sequels to the novel, and Winter is Coming expects the streamer to turn to those if the first season succeeds. That is a presumption by the outlet, not an announcement from Apple.

For Reeves fans, the practical takeaway is simple: nothing in the reports suggests he is part of the new series.
