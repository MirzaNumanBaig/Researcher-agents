**Quick summary**

- Horror film [Other Mommy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Other_Mommy) opened on 8 October 2026 and has grossed about US$119.4 million, according to figures compiled on Wikipedia.
- It cost an estimated US$18 million to make, so it has earned roughly 6.6 times its budget.
- Critics were unkind: it holds 19% on Rotten Tomatoes and 39/100 on Metacritic.
- ScreenRant lists it among 2026's horror films that made money despite bad reviews, and it is now available to rent or buy.

Other Mommy is the kind of film critics dislike and audiences keep buying tickets for. The horror starring Jessica Chastain, released on 8 October 2026, has grossed about US$119.4 million worldwide against an estimated US$18 million budget, and ScreenRant has now named it among the year's biggest box office winners with poor Rotten Tomatoes scores.

## What the numbers say

The figures come from the film's Wikipedia entry, which lists the budget at US$18 million and the gross at US$119.4 million. That is a return of about 6.6 times the cost, a strong result for a mid-budget release.

Decider goes further, describing the film as Chastain's biggest box office hit in years. The same piece notes that Chastain is also leading Heartland, a Netflix mystery-thriller in which she plays a washed-up country singer, so she has two high-profile titles in front of audiences at once.

The film was directed by Rob Savage. The cast also includes Jay Duplass, Dichen Lachman and Karen Allen. Chastain plays both the real mother and the unsettling, grinning double of the title, according to Decider.

## Why critics were not convinced

The reviews are poor. The film sits at 19% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) and 39/100 from 16 critics on Metacritic. The Rotten Tomatoes critics' consensus praises Chastain's committed performance as a smothering parent but says the film is too silly for its jump scares to build into real dread.

Other reviewers took a similar line. The New York Times called it a "sometimes-creepy, sometimes-hilarious schlockfest". Benjamin Lee of The Guardian gave it 2 out of 5, saying it begins as a tense thriller and slides into an increasingly absurd fight with a special effect.

## A strong year for divisive horror

ScreenRant argues that 2026 has been a rich year for the genre, with audiences turning out in large numbers even when reviewers do not. It points to filmmakers such as [Kane Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kane_Parsons), who has drawn praise from [Steven Spielberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Spielberg), and to online fame as a launching pad for cinema releases.

Its list of poorly reviewed earners includes Scream 7, which opened to US$63.6 million and grossed close to US$122 million domestically, according to Box Office Mojo as quoted by ScreenRant. It also names Lee Cronin's The Mummy, which holds 45% on Rotten Tomatoes, and Iron Lung from YouTuber [Mark Fischbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markiplier), known as Markiplier.

Other Mommy sits alongside those titles in the same list. The pattern is hard to miss: reviews were harsh, and the tickets sold anyway.
