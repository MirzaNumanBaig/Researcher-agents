**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) sat front row at the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles on Sunday 18 October (US time).
- She wore a white shirt and tie with a black pencil skirt that has garter-belt-style details, according to Harper's Bazaar UK.
- Her feathered fringe, dubbed "Gucci bangs", was singled out by Cosmopolitan UK as the hair look of the night.
- Harper's Bazaar reports she was expected to bring her daughter Sunday Rose, who did not appear.

Nicole Kidman turned a runway night into a fashion talking point by showing up in a tie. The Oscar winner took a front-row seat at the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show at Sony Pictures Studios in Los Angeles on Sunday 18 October (US time), and two of the biggest style outlets have since written about her hair and her nails.

## What Kidman wore

According to [Harper's Bazaar UK](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/beauty/make-up-nails/a74174746/nicole-kidman-victorias-secret-fashion-show/), Kidman arrived in a crisp white shirt and tie tucked into a black pencil skirt trimmed with garter-belt-like details. The outlet called that a "very VS touch". She added pointed black pumps and carried a burgundy Chanel clutch, with what the magazine described as sleek, sultry make-up.

It is a deliberate contrast. Tailoring at a lingerie show is an unusual choice, and it is the detail that tabloid and entertainment headlines have fastened on since.

## The hair and nails getting attention

Cosmopolitan UK named her "Gucci bangs" as the standout hair of an evening that also featured [Kris Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Jenner) in a bob and Timothée Chalamet in soft curls. The magazine describes the style as a shaggy, wispy, face-framing fringe blended into the rest of the hair, rather than a blunt cut.

The name comes from a look that celebrity hairstylist Dimitris Giannetos created for Demi Moore's role in Gucci's fall 2025 short film The Tiger, Cosmopolitan reports. It also cites figures from the salon booking platform Fresha, which says searches for Gucci bangs grew 555% year on year, making it one of that platform's top five hair trends.

That number comes from a company with a commercial interest in booking appointments, so it is best read as a signal of interest rather than a measure of how many people are getting the cut.

Harper's Bazaar also covered her manicure, a sheer, milky coconut shade with a glassy finish. The magazine labelled it "cashmere coconut" and called it an understated autumn option. Yahoo Life UK carried both beauty pieces.

## Where was Sunday Rose?

Kidman sat next to Vanessa Bryant, whose daughter Natalia made her runway debut, according to Harper's Bazaar. The magazine says Kidman was expected to attend with her 18-year-old daughter, Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, but the young model was not seen. No reason was given in the sources we reviewed, and we have not speculated on one.

Sunday Rose has built a modelling career quickly. Harper's Bazaar says she debuted on the Miu Miu runway in Paris in October 2024, walked for the brand again in 2025, and this year travelled to walk for Dior, Calvin Klein and Valentino during fashion month.

The pair were last seen together on a major red carpet at the 2026 Met Gala in New York. ABC News reported that Kidman, a co-chair of the event and a Chanel brand ambassador, wore a red sequined Chanel gown with feathers, while her daughter wore a pink Christian Dior Haute Couture gown. It was Sunday Rose's Met Gala debut. "For me, fashion is art," Kidman told Vogue, as quoted by ABC News.
