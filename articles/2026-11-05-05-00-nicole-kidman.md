**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) arrived at Sydney airport on Wednesday morning wearing a full Chanel look, according to Harper's Bazaar UK.
- She is home for Chanel's first runway show in Australia, set for Thursday 5 November 2026.
- The show re-stages the house's Resort (Cruise) 2027 collection, first shown in Biarritz, France, in April.
- The venue had not been announced in the coverage we could read.

Nicole Kidman touched down in Sydney on Wednesday morning, and she did it in Chanel from head to toe. [Harper's Bazaar UK](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/uk/fashion/shopping/a74332008/nicole-kidman-airport-trousers/) reports that the Oscar winner travelled with her 15-year-old daughter and has come home for the French house's first-ever runway show in Australia, which takes place on 5 November.

## What she wore through arrivals

Harper's Bazaar describes a sleek trouser-and-shirt set worn with slip-on loafers. Kidman carried a suede duffle bag from Chanel's Cruise 2027 collection, the same range the house is about to show in Sydney. She kept her hair under a black baseball cap carrying the brand's emblem, but still waved to onlookers as she left the terminal.

The magazine's angle is practical. It points to relaxed, lightweight trousers as the sensible choice for a flight of eight hours or more that can still pass at dinner once you land. It also notes that silk trousers have appeared on recent catwalks, including at Ferragamo and Schiaparelli. That is the outlet's fashion commentary, and the piece carries affiliate links, so treat the shopping advice as a sales pitch rather than news.

The news in the story is simpler: Kidman chose to arrive in the collection she is here to watch.

## Why Chanel is in Sydney

According to [Pedestrian.tv](https://www.pedestrian.tv/style/chanel-bringing-resort-2027-collection-sydney-australia/), the Sydney event is a re-staging of the Resort 2027 collection, which Chanel describes as its first Cruise collection under creative director Matthieu Blazy. The collection debuted in Biarritz on 28 April, with seaside themes such as nautical stripes, raffia skirts and swim caps. Pedestrian adds that no other luxury house has staged a show of this scale in Australia.

[RUSSH](https://www.russh.com/chanel-resort-2027-sydney-show-announcement/) calls it a major moment for the local fashion industry and expects international press, clients and industry guests. It also ties the show to the house's history with Australian talent, naming Kidman, director Baz Luhrmann, Margot Robbie and Jacob Elordi among the people who have worked with Chanel over the years.

## A long relationship with the house

Neither of those reports gives Kidman a formal role in the Sydney show, so there is no confirmed programme for her beyond arriving for it. What the coverage does establish is the long association. Luhrmann directed her in Chanel's celebrated No. 5 film in 2004, a connection RUSSH points to when placing Australia in the brand's story.
