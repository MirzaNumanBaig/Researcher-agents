**Quick summary**

- Julien's Auctions and Turner Classic Movies will hold *A Week of Hollywood Legends* from December 14 to 18, 2026, with more than 1,000 screen objects.
- Catherine O'Hara's day, titled "A Life in Character", is Tuesday, December 15, at 10 a.m. Pacific time.
- Lots span *SCTV*, *Schitt's Creek*, *Home Alone*, *Beetlejuice* and her Tim Burton work, with estimates from US$1,000 to US$25,000.
- Bidding is open online, by phone, by absentee bid or in person at Julien's in Gardena, California.

[Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O%27Hara) is about to see her wardrobe, scripts and puppets go under the hammer. According to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Photos-Juliens-Auctions-and-TCM-to-Auction-Kirk-Douglas-Catherine-OHara-and-Rob-Lowe-Memorabilia-in-December-20261118), Julien's Auctions and TCM will sell items from across her career on December 15, as one day of a five-day sale of Hollywood memorabilia.

## What is in the Catherine O'Hara auction

The sale is called "Catherine O'Hara: A Life in Character" and covers her television and film work, from *SCTV* to *Schitt's Creek* and *Home Alone*.

The *Schitt's Creek* collection is the largest, with more than 60 items listed. Among them is a Moira Rose gown from the episode "Smoke Signals", estimated at US$3,000 to US$5,000, and a Moira's Rose's Garden plaque estimated at US$1,000 to US$2,000.

From *Home Alone*, the auction lists production scripts, including a revised partial script with handwritten notes. A leather-bound personalised *Home Alone 2* script and an outtake VHS tape are also offered, sold separately. BroadwayWorld did not publish estimates for these in the portion of the page we read.

## Beetlejuice, Frankenweenie and puppets

Her work with director Tim Burton is well represented. A screen-used *Beetlejuice* jacket is estimated at US$2,000 to US$3,000, and an ensemble worn as Delia Deetz at US$3,000 to US$5,000. A Burton-signed copy of the *Handbook for the Recently Deceased* prop book carries the same US$2,000 to US$3,000 estimate as the jacket.

The priciest lots are puppets. Three stop-motion figures from *Frankenweenie*, Weird Girl, Gym Teacher and Susan Frankenstein, are each estimated at US$10,000 to US$20,000. The "Lost Leader" puppet from *Fraggle Rock: Back to the Rock* is estimated at US$15,000 to US$25,000.

## The rest of the week

O'Hara's session sits in a busy programme. According to the same report, the week opens on Monday, December 14, with the Anne and Kirk Douglas archive of nearly 300 items. Its highlights include a 1967 Golden Globe Cecil B. DeMille Award, estimated at US$8,000 to US$12,000, and a sword from the London premiere of *Spartacus*. All proceeds from that day go to The Douglas Foundation.

On Wednesday, December 16, the "Modern Masters" session features Rob Lowe in the morning and Patricia Arquette in the afternoon. Thursday and Friday are given to "Hollywood Legends: Past & Present", including the estate of Sidney Poitier and, in an online-only session on Friday, items from Rudolph Valentino.

Other headline lots across the week include Yoda's gimer stick from *Star Wars: The Phantom Menace*, estimated at US$60,000 to US$80,000, and Dumbledore's Elder Wand prop from *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire*, estimated at US$50,000 to US$70,000.

## How to bid

The in-person sessions take place at Julien's Auctions in Gardena, California, with online bidding available throughout. Bidders can also use a phone representative or submit an absentee bid. Julien's says it accepts several cryptocurrencies as payment. All times are Pacific, so Australian bidders should check the local conversion before the sale day.

## What happens next

The auction runs December 14 to 18, and O'Hara's lots go on Tuesday, December 15. Final prices will show how collectors value the characters she has played, particularly Moira Rose. It is on Tuesday, December 15, 2026, at 10 a.m. Pacific time, as part of a week of sales that runs from December 14 to 18.
