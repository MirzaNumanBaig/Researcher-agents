**Quick summary**

- [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) hosted the fifth episode of Season 52 of [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) on Saturday, with The Strokes as musical guest.
- Nielsen Live+Same Day data show 5.6 million total viewers and 1.5 million adults aged 18 to 49, the show's largest audience in two years, according to LateNighter.
- A strong college football lead-in helped, and the cold open with Texas senator-elect [James Talarico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Talarico) went big on YouTube.
- The Guardian's reviewer called the episode low-energy, so the numbers and the verdict did not match.

The Druski SNL ratings are the biggest story from the episode: the comedian's night as host drew the largest audience Saturday Night Live has had in two years, according to the ratings site LateNighter. Per Nielsen Live+Same Day figures, the episode averaged 5.6 million total viewers and 1.5 million adults aged 18 to 49.

## How the Druski SNL ratings compare

The jump is clear against recent weeks. LateNighter reports an 8% rise in total viewers and an 18% gain in the 18 to 49 demographic over the previous Saturday's Halloween episode. That show, hosted by [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), had itself been the strongest of the season.

The year-on-year picture is sharper. Against the fifth episode of last season, hosted by [Nikki Glaser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikki_Glaser), Druski's night was up 39% among total viewers and 105% in the demo. In other words, the younger audience more than doubled.

To find a bigger crowd you have to go back to 2 November 2024, when John Mulaney hosted with [Chappell Roan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chappell_Roan) as musical guest. That episode averaged 6.8 million total viewers and 1.7 million in the demo. It also featured a surprise cameo from then-Vice President [Kamala Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamala_Harris) three days before the presidential election.

## The football factor

Druski had some help, and so did Navarrette a week earlier. LateNighter says SNL's college football lead-in has more than doubled its audience over the past two weeks compared with the first three weeks of the season. Saturday's Miami and Notre Dame game averaged 8.5 million total viewers, including 2.4 million adults aged 18 to 49. The Ohio State and USC game a week earlier drew 6.6 million viewers and 1.8 million in the demo.

That context matters. A bigger lead-in does not guarantee viewers stay for the show, but it puts the programme in front of more people at the start of the night.

## A cold open that travelled online

The episode also had a post-election surprise. Talarico, who had beaten Republican Ken Paxton four days earlier, appeared in the cold open alongside James Austin Johnson's [Donald Trump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Trump). According to The Guardian, he teased the show over its repeated use of the same Trump-themed openings.

LateNighter says the clip became the most-watched video of Season 52 on YouTube, passing 11 million views by Tuesday afternoon. Those online views are not counted in the Live+Same Day figures, which also leave out live viewing on Peacock.

## What the critics made of it

Strong numbers did not mean strong reviews. The Guardian called the episode low-energy and questioned whether Druski's style of viral comedy, which includes cursing and whiteface, suits the show's format. It still credited him with the energy and comedic chops to host, and said he was better in the pre-recorded sketches than the live ones.

The same review noted that Druski is filming a movie alongside Emma Stone, who joined him on stage for the opening banter. ## What happens next

These figures are early. LateNighter notes that Nielsen's Live+3 ratings, which count three days of delayed viewing, will push them higher. The Halloween episode, for example, added 1.3 million viewers that way and finished at 6.5 million.

Next up is a test of whether the momentum holds. John Mulaney returns for his seventh hosting stint this Saturday, with [Phoebe Bridgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Bridgers) as musical guest. He fronted the last episode to draw a bigger audience than Druski's.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many people watched Druski host SNL?

The episode averaged 5.6 million total viewers and 1.5 million adults aged 18 to 49, based on Nielsen Live+Same Day data reported by LateNighter. Those totals are expected to rise once delayed viewing is counted.

### Was it the biggest SNL audience in years?

Yes. According to LateNighter, it was the show's largest audience in two years. The last bigger one was the 2 November 2024 episode hosted by John Mulaney, with 6.8 million viewers.

### Who was the musical guest?

The Strokes performed on the night Druski hosted.

### Did the Druski SNL ratings come from the comedy alone?

Not entirely. The college football lead-in drew much larger audiences than earlier in the season, which likely helped. Reviews were also mixed, with The Guardian calling the episode low-energy.
