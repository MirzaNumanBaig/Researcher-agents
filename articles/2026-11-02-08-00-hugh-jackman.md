**Quick summary**

- [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart) died in Los Angeles on a Wednesday at age 86, according to the Atlanta Journal-Constitution.
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) posted a tribute on Instagram, saying "we lost one of the greats of stage and screen".
- Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige, William Shatner and George Takei also paid tribute.
- Stewart's friend [Sir Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen) has also paid tribute, Variety reports, though we could not read the text of it.

Hugh Jackman has led the tributes to his X-Men co-star Patrick Stewart, who has died at 86. The Australian actor shared the news of the loss on Instagram, calling Stewart one of the greats of stage and screen and a close friend.

## What Jackman said

According to [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/patrick-stewart-dies-hugh-jackman-mourns-x-men-actor-42101/), Jackman described Stewart as a friend from whom he learned a great deal, and said he would miss him "all of my days". His opening line, reported by the [Atlanta Journal-Constitution](https://www.ajc.com/news/2026/10/hugh-jackman-william-shatner-and-george-takei-among-those-paying-tribute-to-patrick-stewart/), was: "We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today."

The two men worked together across nearly two decades. Jackman played Wolverine and Stewart played Professor Charles Xavier in several X-Men films, from the 2000 original through to 2017's Logan, according to Variety. For more on that last film, see our post on [Hugh Jackman remembering Patrick Stewart and the Logan premiere](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackman-remembers-patrick-stewart-and-the-logan-premiere/).

## The wider tributes

Stewart's death drew responses from across film and television, as listed by the AJC:

- **William Shatner**, a fellow Star Trek captain, said: "Captain Picard stood for the best of us, and so did the man who played him."
- **George Takei** wrote: "Rest in the heavens, Captain."
- **Kevin Feige**, president of Marvel Studios, praised Stewart's talent and his mentoring of others. Variety quotes him calling Stewart "brilliant, charming, warm, generous".
- **Seth MacFarlane**, **Ricky Gervais**, **Minnie Driver**, **Cary Elwes** and **Rebecca Romijn** also posted messages, as did SAG-AFTRA and Marvel Studios.

The AJC reports that Stewart's spokesperson said he had Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia. Variety describes it as a brief illness.

## Ian McKellen and the Marvel connection

Variety describes McKellen as Stewart's good friend. It says the pair were due to appear together in Avengers: Doomsday this December. Stewart had also appeared in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness in 2022. Variety says it is unclear whether Wolverine will return in the new film, so any link between Jackman and that project remains unconfirmed.

We could not read McKellen's tribute in full, so we have not quoted it here.

For more of our coverage, see [everything we publish about Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hugh-jackman/) and our earlier report on [the X-Men cast paying tribute to Patrick Stewart](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackman-and-x-men-cast-pay-tribute-to-patrick-stewart/).

## What happens next

No funeral or memorial details appeared in the reports we read. Further statements from the X-Men cast and from Marvel are likely as the release of Avengers: Doomsday approaches, but nothing has been announced.
