**Quick summary**

- Streaming guide JustWatch has published two viewing lists on [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder), timed to his first feature documentary, *You Can See Everything*.
- One lists his shows in order, from a 2011 sitcom to *The Curse*. The other covers five cameos, including *The Simpsons* and *Rick and Morty*.
- JustWatch calls *The Rehearsal* the best preparation for the film.
- Wikipedia lists the film's release as 6 September 2026, with a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score from 229 reviews.

Nathan Fielder's name is everywhere this month, and viewers who only know him from the film are being pointed back to his television work. On 20 and 21 November, streaming guide JustWatch published two lists: his shows in order, and five cameos that many fans may have missed. Together they give a quick map of a career built on deadpan awkwardness.

## Why the film sent people back to the TV shows

JustWatch describes *You Can See Everything* as Fielder's debut documentary and says it has made the Canadian comedian and filmmaker a name "everyone is talking about". Its viewing guide argues that his methods only make sense once you have seen the earlier series.

Critics have been warm. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything), it holds a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score from 229 reviews and 97 out of 100 on Metacritic from 43 critics. It lists Fielder and Lance Oppenheim as directors and gives a budget of about US$7 million against a gross of about US$93 million. Those figures come from Wikipedia and Wikidata, not from the studio. For more, see our [review of You Can See Everything](https://moduscarry.com/you-can-see-everything-review/) and our report on [how UK critics received it](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-you-can-see-everything-uk-critics/).

## Every Nathan Fielder show in order

According to JustWatch, the path starts in 2011 with *Jon Benjamin Has a Van*, a 10-episode sketch-style sitcom created by and starring H. Jon Benjamin, the voice of Bob Belcher in *Bob's Burgers*. The guide calls it far from essential, but a useful look at Fielder as an actor early on.

His career took off in 2013 with [Nathan For You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You). In it he pitches strange business ideas to struggling American shops, and, JustWatch notes, most owners go along with them. The guide's examples include a parody of the Starbucks logo and a deal offering cheap TVs to customers who walk through a room of live alligators.

In 2016 came *David*, a scripted miniseries directed by Dean Fleischer Camp, who also made *Marcel the Shell With Shoes On*. Fielder plays a lonely, recently divorced man told by a psychic that he has five weeks to live. JustWatch calls it dark, surreal and existential.

## The Rehearsal and The Curse

JustWatch's pick for the best way to prepare for the film is *The Rehearsal*. In season one, Fielder helps a woman test whether she is ready for children by hiring child actors and building a live-in set-up, then becomes oddly attached himself. Season two has him training to fly a commercial jet, and the guide says it also covers an autism diagnosis and an intense fixation on pilot Chesley Sullenberger.

Between those two seasons sits *The Curse*, a scripted ten-episode series made with Emma Stone and Benny Safdie. Fielder and Stone play newly married partners who fear a curse is derailing plans for a child and a new TV show. JustWatch says its satire touches on white saviour narratives, gentrification and the dangers of reality TV.

## Five cameos worth hunting down

The second list follows Fielder through smaller roles. In *The Disaster Artist* (2017), directed by [James Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Franco), he plays Kyle Vogt, the actor who appears as Peter in *The Room*. The cast also includes [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Alison Brie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alison_Brie), [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson) and [Dave Franco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Franco), and the film is about [Tommy Wiseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Wiseau).

His other cameos, per JustWatch:

- *Bob's Burgers* (2012): a creepy Wagstaff student named Nathan who briefly dates Tina.
- *Bob's Burgers* (2015): Jordan Cagan, another of Tina's short-lived boyfriends, who turns out to be the "Ghost Boy" graffiti artist.
- *The Simpsons* (2015, ["Sky Police"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Police)): casino owner Doug Blattner. The guide is lukewarm about this one.
- *Rick and Morty* (2015, "The Ricks Must Be Crazy"): teenage scientist Kyle, alongside [Stephen Colbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Colbert) as Zeep.
- *Marcel the Shell With Shoes On* (2021): the voice of Marcel's brother Justin, in a film voiced by [Jenny Slate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_Slate) and [Isabella Rossellini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabella_Rossellini).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Nathan Fielder show should I watch first?

JustWatch recommends *The Rehearsal* as the best preparation for *You Can See Everything*. For his earliest style, it points to *Nathan For You*.

### What is You Can See Everything?

It is Fielder's first feature documentary, which JustWatch calls highly anticipated. Wikipedia lists him and Lance Oppenheim as directors.

### Did Nathan Fielder appear in The Simpsons?

Yes. In the 2015 episode "Sky Police" he voiced casino owner Doug Blattner, according to JustWatch.
