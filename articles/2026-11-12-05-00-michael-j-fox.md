**Quick summary**

- Michael J. Fox, 65, and Tracy Pollan have four adult children: Sam (37), twins Aquinnah and Schuyler (31) and Esmé (25 this month).
- None of the four has followed their parents into acting, according to DoYouRemember?, published on 11 November 2026.
- Sam works as a producer and married Molly Milstein in New York in October 2025.
- Fox thanked his family at the Actor Awards in Los Angeles on 1 March 2026.

The Michael J. Fox kids have grown up out of the spotlight their parents once lived in. [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox) and [Tracy Pollan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Pollan) both made their names as actors, yet [a round-up from DoYouRemember?](https://doyouremember.com/241361/michael-j-fox-kids) published on 11 November 2026 says their son Sam is now 37, their twin daughters are 31 and their youngest, Esmé, has just turned 25.

## How a Family Ties romance became a family of six

The couple met on the set of [Family Ties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Family_Ties), where Pollan played Ellen, the art student who caught the eye of Fox's character Alex P. Keaton in 1985. They married on 16 July 1988 at the West Mountain Inn in Arlington, Vermont, according to DoYouRemember?.

Sam arrived the following spring. Fox was diagnosed with young-onset Parkinson's disease in 1991, at 29, when Sam was still a toddler. The twins were born in 1995 and Esmé in 2001. The outlet reports that all four grew up with a famous father who kept his health largely private, and quotes remarks Fox made in 2024, reported by Hello!, saying his children simply "make the transition" when he reaches for something and carry on.

Privacy mattered to the children too. When the documentary *Still: A Michael J. Fox Movie* came out in 2023, Fox told People the family had agreed to appear, then joked that they "retracted their permission" at various points.

## Sam Fox: the producer in the family

Sam Michael Fox was born on 30 May 1989. DoYouRemember? notes that photographers have long remarked on his resemblance to the young Marty McFly. He works behind the camera: People and Hello! list his producing credits as including the short film *Good Grief* and Ben Barnes's music video "11:11".

He and Molly Milstein announced their engagement in February 2024 and married in New York in October 2025. His three sisters held up the chuppah over the couple, the outlet reports.

At the Actor Awards on 1 March 2026, Fox thanked Pollan for "four gifts", naming Schuyler, Aquinnah, Esmé and Sam. He then pointed out his son at the table with a joke, as reported by Hello! and OK!: "He's not an actor. He's just my date."

## Aquinnah and Schuyler Fox: two different paths

The twins were born on 15 February 1995. Aquinnah is named after the town at the western tip of Martha's Vineyard. She studied psychology, visual and media studies and art history at Duke University and danced in its ballet programme. When she graduated in 2018, her father wrote online, "You beat me to it!", a nod to the fact that he left high school early.

She later moved to Los Angeles and worked as an assistant at United Talent Agency. According to People and The List, she now works in television development at Annapurna Pictures, which puts her closer to the family trade than her siblings, though on the side that chooses projects rather than stars in them.

Schuyler attended Pomona College in California, where she ran track, and graduated with a psychology degree in 2017. The source text available for this article cuts off after that point, so her later career and Esmé's are not covered here.
