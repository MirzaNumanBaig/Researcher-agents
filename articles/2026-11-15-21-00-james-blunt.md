**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) says the broadcast of [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors) was "heavily edited" and removed context.
- He made the comments to The Sunday Times magazine, as reported by Female First on 15 November 2026.
- He says a cut puzzle task would have shown he was faithful, and that viewers did not see an intense interrogation before a fellow contestant confessed.
- He calls the experience "deeply unpleasant" and says he will not do it again.

James Blunt has said the version of Celebrity Traitors that reached screens did not match what he lived through, telling The Sunday Times magazine that "a lot of context is removed", according to [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/tv/news/james-blunt-reveals-frustration-celebrity-traitors-editing-1453713.html). The singer, 51, reached the final three before being banished.

## What James Blunt said about the editing

Blunt filmed the series in spring and watched it go out last month. Before seeing the broadcast he felt upbeat about how it had gone, Female First reports. Afterwards, he said, the edit left out material that changed how his game looked.

He described the show as "deeply unpleasant, but great to have done it. Never again." He also said his wife signed him up and compared the experience to running a marathon. He called it a "social experiment" and said he should stay at home rather than join similar programmes.

## The puzzle task that was cut

His main example was a puzzle mission. According to the report, Blunt finished his shield early and then helped other players. He says that scene would have shown viewers he was faithful, and it did not make the final cut.

## The interrogation viewers did not see

Blunt also pointed to James Acaster. He said the audience did not see the "very effective, intense interrogation" Acaster faced before he confessed, which he believes left a misleading picture of events.

## Why he forced the final vote

Blunt said he forced the final vote because he believed Romesh Ranganathan was a Traitor. He added that part of his reasoning was that Ranganathan had watched him behave as a faithful. Female First reports that Ranganathan and Sebastian "Bash" Croft, named as the winners, banished him.

## What happens next

Nothing further has been announced. Blunt's comments came in a single interview, and the production has not been quoted in the report we could read.
