**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) A24 drama *The Death of Robin Hood* reached No. 1 on HBO Max's US movie chart on 18 October 2026, according to FlixPatrol rankings cited by FandomWire.
- It displaced *The Housemaid*, which fell to second place after seven days on the chart.
- The film earned about US$9.9 million worldwide against a reported US$20 million budget in cinemas.
- The figures are US chart positions, not confirmed viewing totals.

Hugh Jackman's bleak take on the Robin Hood legend has found an audience at home that it never reached in cinemas. *The Death of Robin Hood* entered HBO Max's US movie chart at No. 1 on 18 October 2026, pushing the hit thriller *The Housemaid* down to second, according to [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/hugh-jackman-death-of-robin-hood-tops-sydney-sweeney-the-housemaid-hbo-max/), which cited FlixPatrol's daily rankings.

## A chart win, with caveats

FandomWire is careful to say what the result does and does not show. FlixPatrol tracks chart positions on a platform, so the ranking reflects the US HBO Max list only. It does not confirm viewing figures, and it is not a worldwide streaming victory.

The film arrived on the chart as a new entry. *The Housemaid*, by contrast, had held a place for seven days before slipping one spot.

## What the film is

*The Death of Robin Hood* was written and directed by Michael Sarnoski, whose earlier work includes *Pig* and *A Quiet Place: Day One*. FandomWire describes it as a 122-minute A24 drama set in medieval England. Jackman plays an ageing Robin, haunted by years of violence, who is gravely wounded and meets Sister Brigid, played by [Jodie Comer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Comer). Her compassion offers him a possible route to redemption.

The supporting cast includes Bill Skarsgård, [Murray Bartlett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murray_Bartlett) and [Noah Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Jupe), according to [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), as relayed by FandomWire.

FandomWire draws a comparison with Jackman's weary Wolverine in *Logan*, since both are ageing fighters weighed down by what they have done. It argues that this film goes further by asking whether its hero was ever heroic at all.

## Mixed reviews

Critics have been divided. The film holds a 69 per cent Tomatometer score and a 56 per cent Popcornmeter score, FandomWire reports. Its own review gave the film 6 out of 10, praising Jackman's performance and the cinematography while finding the pacing ponderous.

## The box office gap

The theatrical numbers explain why the streaming result stands out. FandomWire reports that *The Death of Robin Hood* took roughly US$9.9 million worldwide against a reported US$20 million production budget. That is about half of what it cost to make.

*The Housemaid*, directed by Paul Feig and adapted from the novel by [Freida McFadden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freida_McFadden), earned about US$400.5 million worldwide on a reported US$35 million budget. It carries a 74 per cent critics' score and a 92 per cent verified audience rating, per the same report. It reached HBO Max earlier in October after its cinema and digital runs.

The outlet's reading is that a subscription removes the cost of a cinema ticket, which may help a film that struggled to sell them. That is interpretation rather than data, and the sources do not show how many people actually watched.

## What happens next

Chart positions on streaming services move daily, so the No. 1 spot may not last. Whether the film holds its place over the coming days will be the better test of whether this is a brief bump or a lasting second life.

*The Housemaid* is not going away. [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) confirms a sequel, *The Housemaid's Secret*, is scheduled for December 2027, with Sydney Sweeney returning alongside new cast member [Kirsten Dunst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirsten_Dunst), FandomWire says.
