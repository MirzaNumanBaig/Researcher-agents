**Quick summary**

- The Hollywood Reporter says [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is joining Marvel's "Ghost Rider" as a villain opposite [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling).
- One outlet describes the deal as done, another says he is still "in talks", so treat the casting as not yet officially confirmed.
- Cinematographer Linus Sandgren will shoot the whole film on film stock, with production expected in New York in early 2027.
- "Ghost Rider" is scheduled for cinemas on 28 July 2028.

Connor Storrie, the breakout star of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), has reportedly landed a villain role in Marvel's "Ghost Rider" reboot, according to reports that trace back to The Hollywood Reporter on 27 and 28 October 2026. The same report says the film will be shot on film, with Linus Sandgren behind the camera.

## What has been reported about Storrie's role

Toy People, summarising the Hollywood Reporter story, [says Storrie is joining Gosling](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=115833) and will play a villain, though his character is still under wraps. World of Reel, writing a few hours earlier, [puts it more cautiously](https://www.worldofreel.com/blog/2026/10/27/linus-sandgren-to-dp-shawn-levys-ghost-rider-on-film-connor-storrie-in-talks-to-co-star): Storrie is "in talks" to star opposite Gosling.

That gap matters. Neither Marvel nor Storrie has been quoted in the material we could read, so the role should be read as reported, not announced.

## A fast rise from waiting tables

World of Reel describes Storrie as someone who was waiting tables to pay the bills until recently. He has since shot three films this year, all slated for 2027: Sarah Polley's "The Bell Jar", Craig Zobel's "Turpentine" and Molly Gordon's "Peaked", which is an A24 title.

If the Marvel role is confirmed, it would sit alongside those three. That is a lot of screen time for an actor whose name was little known before "Heated Rivalry" went viral.

## Why the film-stock detail stands out

The other headline is the format. [The Hollywood Reporter's news](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=115833) is that Sandgren, the cinematographer on "Dune: Part Three", will serve as director of photography on "Ghost Rider", and that the entire movie will be shot on film.

World of Reel notes that Sandgren is a known advocate for 35mm and 65mm, and says he pushed [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) to shoot the third "Dune" chapter on film, including IMAX 65mm, even though the first two instalments were digital. His credits include [La La Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_La_Land), which won him an Oscar, plus "Saltburn", "Babylon", "No Time to Die" and Noah Baumbach's [Jay Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Kelly).

The site's writer adds that Marvel films have long drawn criticism for a flat, digital look, and calls this the most promising creative choice the studio has made in a while. That is an opinion, but it shows how the news is being received.

## The team around the film

[Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy), the "Deadpool & Wolverine" filmmaker, will direct. He was announced three months ago, and Marvel revealed the project at San Diego Comic-Con this summer, according to World of Reel. His other credits include "Night at the Museum", [Free Guy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Guy) and [Real Steel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Steel).

The idea grew out of Levy's work with Gosling on [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars): Starfighter, which Levy recently wrapped. [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper), who wrote "Starfighter" and has worked with Levy on several films, is writing the screenplay.

## What happens next

Production is expected to begin in New York in early 2027, with the film due in theatres on 28 July 2028. Storrie's character, and whether his casting is finalised, are the main open questions. For Australian audiences, local release dates are usually set closer to the US date.
