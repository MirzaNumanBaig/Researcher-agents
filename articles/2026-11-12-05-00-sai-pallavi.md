**Quick summary**

- Lyca Productions has set a worldwide release date of 29 January 2027 for the Tamil romantic drama Inji Vellam.
- The film stars [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) and [Vijay Sethupathi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_Sethupathi) in their first on-screen pairing, and is directed by [Mani Ratnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mani_Ratnam).
- It is set in Kolkata, with music by AR Rahman, who says shooting is finished and the songs are still to be scored.
- It is Pallavi's first project with Ratnam.

## Sai Pallavi and Vijay Sethupathi's Inji Vellam gets a date

Sai Pallavi's next big release now has a date. Lyca Productions announced on 11 November that Inji Vellam, the new romantic drama from Mani Ratnam, will reach cinemas worldwide on 29 January 2027, according to [The Hollywood Reporter India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/features/insight/inji-vellam-mani-ratnams-vijay-sethupathi-sai-pallavi-romance-drama-locks-january-2027-release) and [Pinkvilla](https://www.pinkvilla.com/entertainment/south/inji-vellam-release-date-vijay-sethupathi-and-sai-pallavis-romantic-film-to-hit-big-screens-on-january-29-2027-1406396).

The banner shared the date alongside a motion poster. It shows Sethupathi and Pallavi holding two young girls who appear to be twins, standing against a busy city backdrop. The caption was brief: "Save the date", followed by the film's title and "Jan 29th, Worldwide".

## What we know about the film

The Hollywood Reporter India describes Inji Vellam as a Tamil romantic drama and says it is the first time Sethupathi and Pallavi have shared the screen. The story is set in Kolkata. Both the first-look poster, released in September, and the first glimpse video feature the city's Howrah Bridge, and the poster shows the two leads in a close embrace in the rain.

An earlier teaser was built around Kolkata streets, trams and crowds before closing on close-ups of the two actors. It gave away nothing about the plot, and the makers have not yet shared a synopsis. Until they do, the story itself remains unknown.

Before it had a title, the project was listed as Madras Talkies Production No. 23, the publication reports.

## A new pairing for the director

The film brings together several first and second collaborations. According to The Hollywood Reporter India, it is Ratnam's second film with Sethupathi after the 2018 sibling action drama Chekka Chivantha Vaanam. It is also the first project in which Pallavi works with the veteran director.

Inji Vellam is produced by Subaskaran, Ratnam and Siva Ananth under the Lyca Productions and Madras Talkies banners. The music is by AR Rahman. In a recent interview with the publication, Rahman said Ratnam had finished shooting and that the songs were about to be scored.

That detail suggests the film is still in post-production, with the music the main piece left to complete. The January date leaves roughly twelve weeks from the announcement for that work.

## Sai Pallavi's busy run

The date lands while Pallavi is also in the spotlight for Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, in which she plays Sita. Outlook Luxe reports that the film's jewellery for her character was created by Tribe Amrapali with costume designers Rimple and Harpreet Narula, and that nearly 4,000 handcrafted pieces were made for the production overall, according to Amrapali's creative director Tarang Arora.

## What happens next

Next in line is the film's marketing. A trailer and music release have not been announced, and neither the Hollywood Reporter India nor Pinkvilla gave a date for either. With Rahman still to score the songs, the soundtrack is likely to arrive closer to release, though that is an expectation rather than anything the makers have confirmed.
