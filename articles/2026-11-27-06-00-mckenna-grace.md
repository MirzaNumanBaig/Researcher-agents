- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, with [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) as Maysilee Donner, passed US$150 million at the US box office in six days, according to ScreenRant.
- Lionsgate confirmed an estimated US$15.15 million for Wednesday, taking the domestic total to US$163.3 million.
- The previous prequel took 36 days to reach the same mark.
- Grace appears on ABC News Live Prime with Linsey Davis on Friday 4 December.

[Mckenna Grace](https://moduscarry.com/tag/mckenna-grace/) has a hit on her hands. *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, in which she plays Maysilee Donner, crossed US$150 million at the domestic box office in just six days, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office-150-million-domestic-milestone/). That is a pace the franchise has not managed since its early years.

## How fast is the Sunrise on the Reaping box office run?

Lionsgate confirmed an estimated US$15.15 million on Wednesday, ScreenRant reports, which lifted the cumulative domestic total to US$163.3 million. The film opened to US$119.3 million over its three-day weekend. That is the second-best domestic debut for a prequel, behind only [Rogue One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rogue_One) at US$155.1 million.

The comparison with the last film in the series is stark. *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes* (2023) needed 36 days to pass US$150 million and finished with US$166.3 million in the US. The new film has nearly matched that final total in under a week.

Inside the franchise, it ties *Mockingjay – Part 1* as the third-quickest to reach the mark. Only the first two films got there faster, both by the Sunday of their opening weekends.

The film also sits 13th among 2026's highest-grossing US releases after six days. ScreenRant notes it has already passed Grace's earlier release, the slasher sequel *Scream 7*, which took US$121.9 million domestically.

## What do the reviews and the budget say?

The numbers are backed by decent scores rather than rave ones. ScreenRant cites a 67% Fresh rating from critics on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), close to the 70% for *Mockingjay – Part 1*. Audiences are warmer: a 94% "Verified Hot" Popcornmeter score, against 71% for that earlier film. ScreenRant reads that gap as a sign of word-of-mouth strength.

On cost, ScreenRant says the film reportedly cost about US$150 million. If it follows the *Mockingjay – Part 1* path, the outlet projects it could pass US$300 million domestically and US$750 million worldwide. That is a projection, not a result. ScreenRant also points out that Lionsgate tends to offset budgets with international pre-sales, so its real break-even point may be lower than the usual estimate of around US$375 million.

## Why Maysilee matters to the story

The film is the second prequel after 2023's release and follows the young Haymitch Abernathy, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada). It co-stars [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)), [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) and [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close). It adapts the novel by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins), part of the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series.

Grace's Maysilee starts out icy toward Haymitch but becomes his ally in the 50th Games. ScreenRant describes her death as one of the franchise's most tragic. It also reports that a more graphic version was planned to match the book, and that Grace shared a behind-the-scenes image of the original make-up design. According to ScreenRant, director Francis Lawrence said it "killed" him to scale the scene back to keep the PG-13 rating.
