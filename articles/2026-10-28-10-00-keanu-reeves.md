**Quick summary**

- [Kyle MacLachlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_MacLachlan) has released a comedy clip about auditioning for the role [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) played in *Bram Stoker's Dracula* (1992).
- The video is part four of his social series "Parts Kyle Didn't Get", shared exclusively with People on 27 October 2026.
- MacLachlan, 67, ends the sketch with the line "Oh man...that sucks", according to People.
- The clips promote his memoir, *Fictional Selves*, which covers other roles he missed.

Kyle MacLachlan has turned a 34-year-old Hollywood disappointment into a joke. In a new sketch released on 27 October 2026, the actor dresses as a vampire, lies in a coffin and learns, on camera, that the part he tried out for in *Bram Stoker's Dracula* went to Keanu Reeves.

## What happens in the clip

The video is the fourth instalment of "Parts Kyle Didn't Get", a social media series produced by Full Picture Productions. [People](https://people.com/kyle-maclachlan-auditioned-for-keanu-reeves-role-in-bram-stoker-s-dracula-exclusive-12188059) published it exclusively, and [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/movies/articles/kyle-maclachlan-almost-took-one-154455715.html), via Reality Tea, also covered it.

It opens with MacLachlan in Dracula-style clothing, typing at a laptop and talking as though he had worked on the film. He praises [Francis Ford Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Ford_Coppola), the director, for creating an atmosphere that let actors lose themselves in a character. "I remember stepping onto that set and thinking, 'I am him,'" he says.

Then the camera finds him in a coffin, wearing vampire makeup and fangs, and he growls, "I am Dracula." A female narrator cuts in. "Kyle, you weren't even in Dracula," she says.

MacLachlan protests that he did audition. The narrator agrees, but adds that the role he read for was "the Keanu part", which he did not get. He gives up the act, says "Oh man...that sucks" and lies back down in the coffin.

## Where the joke comes from

The sketch plays on a real near-miss. According to both outlets, MacLachlan did audition for the part that Reeves eventually took in Coppola's 1992 film. Neither report says how close the audition came to a casting decision, so the clip should be read as a comic reminder rather than a detailed account of what happened in the room.

The tone matters here. MacLachlan is not complaining about Reeves or the film. The gag is aimed at himself, and the narrator's deadpan corrections do most of the work.

## A career built on other roles

People notes that MacLachlan has not lacked success since. His credits include [Twin Peaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Peaks), *Sex and the City*, [Desperate Housewives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desperate_Housewives), *Portlandia*, *How I Met Your Mother*, *The Good Wife* and *Fallout*. He is also known for the film *Dune*.

The video series is tied to his memoir, *Fictional Selves*. In the book, People reports, he writes about other parts that slipped away. [Oliver Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Stone) told him he was the top choice to lead the Vietnam War film *Platoon*. MacLachlan says his agent warned that he "really needs a success after Dune", and he had reservations about the ending, so he turned it down. [Charlie Sheen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Sheen) took the role.

*Platoon* went on to win four Oscars. MacLachlan wrote that he instantly regretted it: "I kick myself, having missed a chance to be in something so zeitgeist-defining." He added that he cannot dwell on what could have been, because "it would simply take too much out of me."
