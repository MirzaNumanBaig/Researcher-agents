**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) posted an autumn video to Instagram showing her garden on Vancouver Island, Canada, captioned "Fall is the season of the soul."
- HELLO! reports the clip shows her gardening in the rain, reading on her veranda and watching a sunset.
- Cosmopolitan UK noticed the manicure in the same reel: glossy tomato-red polish on short, squoval-shaped nails.
- The garden is part of Arcady, her family property in Ladysmith, British Columbia, which she returned to in 2020.

Pamela Anderson's latest Instagram reel is a quiet one: a garden after rain, a book on the veranda and a sunset on her doorstep. The video, shared with her 5.4 million followers, has drawn warm reactions from fans and a second round of attention for the bright red manicure on show.

## What the video shows

According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/homes/932167/pamela-anderson-peaceful-garden-video/), the 59-year-old is seen tending her garden on Vancouver Island once the rain has passed, then settling in with a book. She pulls a card from it that reads "There's nothing to worry about." At the start she tugs a brown jumper over a milkmaid dress, a small sign of the turning season.

Fans responded in kind. "What a life!" wrote one, while another called her "our peace amongst the chaos," HELLO! reported.

## The tomato-red nails

[Cosmopolitan UK](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/uk/beauty-hair/celebrity-hair-makeup/a74293628/pamela-anderson-squoval-tomato-nails/) focused on her hands. It describes a glossy, tomato-red shade on short squoval nails, a departure from the cloudy French manicure she is usually seen with.

The magazine's advice for copying it is practical. Trim the cuticles, file the nails and round the corners for the square-meets-oval shape, then choose a warm red with subtle orange undertones and finish with a high-shine top coat.

It is the second nail story about her in a matter of days. [SSBCrack](https://news.ssbcrack.com/pamela-anderson-embraces-the-ombre-manicure-trend-with-a-chic-french-twist/) wrote about a soft white-to-cream ombré French manicure she wore while leaving a New York City hotel, and quoted Susan Carroll, founder of the London medispa Young LDN, who called the look "effortless and expensive." We covered that style in [Pamela Anderson's Soft French Fade Nails and Bare-Faced Style](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-andersons-soft-french-fade-nails-and-bare-faced-style/).

## Arcady and the "wild gardener"

The garden belongs to Arcady, the property in Ladysmith that she has described as linked to her childhood and grandparents. HELLO! says she moved back during the pandemic in 2020 and, with Vancouver interior designer Francesca Albertazzi, renovated a house that had been largely untouched for 25 years. The process was shown on the HGTV series Pamela's Garden of Eden.

The Economic Times reports that she told Homes & Gardens in October 2026 that she is a "wild gardener" who lets self-seeding plants grow where they land. "I go into my garden and I come out a better person every time," she said, according to the outlet. It lists tomatoes, garlic, pumpkins and roses among her crops, plus flowers chosen to attract bees and butterflies.

The Economic Times also cites a 2025 ELLE Decor interview in which she called the garden her "happy place," and notes her skincare brand Sonsie made a Wild Groundcherry lip balm after she found the fruit growing there. Her sons, Brandon and Dylan, help. "Brandon has taken it to another level, of course," she once told Architectural Digest, according to HELLO!. More in our earlier piece, [Pamela Anderson's Storybook Garden and Her Simple Growing Rules](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-andersons-storybook-garden-and-her-simple-growing-rules/).

It links the shift to the loss of her long-time makeup artist. At the gala she wore a white shirt with a green Carolina Herrera skirt and lab-grown diamond jewellery, the outlet reports.
