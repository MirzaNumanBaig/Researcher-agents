- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has confirmed he will return to [Forest Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Live) for two shows at Westonbirt next June, according to Swindon Link.

James Blunt Westonbirt plans are set: the singer is heading back to the forest stage. The singer-songwriter has confirmed two Forest Live shows at Westonbirt next June, according to [Swindon Link](https://swindonlink.com/lifestyle/westonbirt-james-blunt/), which describes him as a chart-topping artist returning to the summer concert series.

## What we know about the Westonbirt shows

The report is short. It confirms that Blunt will play two nights, that they fall in June next year, and that this is a return to Forest Live rather than a first visit. It does not give exact dates, ticket prices or an on-sale time in the text we could read, so those are not covered here. Check the official Forest Live listings before planning a trip.

Forest Live is a long-running British summer series of outdoor concerts in woodland settings, and a double-date booking suggests demand for Blunt's live show remains strong. That is our reading, not a claim from the report.

## Fame after Celebrity Traitors

The announcement arrives as Blunt is very visible in the UK. In an episode of Dish from Waitrose, covered by [PodcastingToday](https://podcastingtoday.co.uk/james-blunt-discusses-music-pubs-and-fame-on-dish-podcast/), he talked about the reaction to his time on [Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors).

He said he stepped off a train at Paddington Station recently and could not get out, because so many people wanted selfies and to ask about the show. His laces were undone and he could not stop to tie them. He texted his wife to say he was "so famous right now".

He also said he kept his result on the show secret. He spoke about his dealings with fellow contestants, including comedian Romesh Ranganathan and actor [Richard E. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Grant). We have also covered [how Blunt felt about Romesh's donation](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-romesh-ranganathan/) after the show.

## A melancholic album and a saved pub

On his new album, *Airplane Mode*, Blunt said the idea is to switch off the noise of the world and be left with one voice, "the voice you spend your life arguing with". He admitted to writing a more melancholic record on purpose. His reasoning was blunt: nobody hires him to play a wedding, he said, but they do hire him for a funeral.

He also reflected on the pressure of writing for radio. According to the podcast summary, he moved from personal storytelling towards meeting industry expectations, then returned to writing songs that move him.

Away from music, he spoke about his efforts to save his local pub, which he sees as an important community space at a time when many venues struggle. Owning it, he said, has become a source of enjoyment and connection.

## Old stories and a famous song

Blunt said he still has affection for *You're Beautiful*, his best-known song. He recounted making its video, which included jumping off a cliff in Mallorca soon after he left the army. He also mentioned living with [Carrie Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Fisher) in Los Angeles, and a trip to New Mexico for hot wings that involved flying in, eating, and flying straight back to Los Angeles.
