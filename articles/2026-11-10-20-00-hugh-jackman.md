**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and his girlfriend [Sutton Foster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sutton_Foster) landed at Sydney Airport on Tuesday, the Daily Mail reports.
- It is described as Foster's first trip to Australia with the actor, and they went to his North Bondi penthouse.
- Separately, the New York Post reports that his West Village triplex shared with [Deborra-Lee Furness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborra-Lee_Furness) is back on the market at $29.9 million.
- Jackman has not confirmed whether he will appear in [Avengers: Doomsday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Doomsday), due on December 18.

Hugh Jackman has brought Sutton Foster to Australia for the first time, with the couple arriving in Sydney on Tuesday, according to the Daily Mail. The pair, who went public with their relationship in January 2025, were photographed holding hands in the terminal before a chauffeured car took them away. For readers following [everything we publish about Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hugh-jackman/), it is a quiet but notable personal milestone.

## Hugh Jackman and Sutton Foster in Australia

The Daily Mail says Jackman, 58, and Foster, 51, looked relaxed and affectionate as they walked through Sydney Airport. Their first stop was reportedly the North Bondi penthouse, which the outlet values at $15 million and says he kept in his 2025 divorce settlement with Furness.

According to the report, Jackman showed Foster the seaside view from the balcony and pointed out local landmarks. The outlet says she appeared comfortable in the home and keen to learn about his Australian life. The details come from a single tabloid account, and neither Jackman nor Foster has commented publicly on the trip.

The visit also has some symmetry. Jackman and Furness announced their separation in 2023 after nearly three decades together, per The Times of India. Bringing a new partner to the home is, on the Daily Mail's telling, a sign of how settled things now are.

## The New York penthouse back on the market

Across the Pacific, a different property is making headlines. The Times of India, citing the New York Post, reports that the 11,000-square-foot triplex at 176 Perry Street in Manhattan's West Village is listed again at $29.9 million. It first came to market in 2022 at $38.9 million, a cut of roughly $9 million.

The listing was pulled in April 2024 after several price reductions and has now returned. The report says property records show Jackman and Furness still jointly own it.

The home occupies the eighth, ninth and tenth floors of a building designed by architect Richard Meier. It has five bedrooms, a double-height great room with floor-to-ceiling glass facing the Hudson River, terraces on each level, a sauna and a separate catering kitchen. The Times of India notes it has not independently verified the details, and that the report is not confirmation from Jackman, Furness or their representatives.

It adds that the former couple are dividing their property holdings, including a Chelsea penthouse that recently changed hands in an intra-family transaction.

## Where Wolverine stands ahead of Doomsday

Jackman's professional future is also under discussion. Gulf News notes that his return as Wolverine in Deadpool & Wolverine (2024), alongside [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds), earned $1.3 billion worldwide. That came after the character's apparent farewell in Logan (2017). The Wolverine in the later film was a variant from another timeline.

At Marvel Studios' D23 presentation, Jackman and Reynolds appeared in a comic video in which the two characters jokingly competed for a place in Avengers: Doomsday, according to the outlet, citing The Playlist. Asked about it on The View, Jackman said he had nothing to add. Marvel has not confirmed his involvement.

Gulf News argues, as opinion, that Wolverine's healing ability could counter [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.)'s [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom). That remains speculation until the film arrives.

## What happens next

No itinerary has been reported for the Australian trip, so how long the couple stay is unknown.
