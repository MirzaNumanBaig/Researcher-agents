**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays two lookalike characters, Henry Park and Logan Casey, in the Crave paranormal mystery *Yaga*.
- Episode 4, released this week, includes a flashback scene in which the two characters kiss.
- Queerty and Out Magazine both report that clips spread quickly on social media.
- *Yaga* streams through AMC+ in the United States, according to Queerty.

[Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) has made his first big post-[*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) move a double act. In the fourth episode of *Yaga*, released this week, the actor plays two characters who share a face, and a flashback has them share a kiss. Fans noticed straight away.

## What Yaga is about

*Yaga* is a Canadian paranormal mystery produced by Crave, and [Queerty](https://www.queerty.com/hudson-williams-makes-out-with-hudson-williams-in-wild-yaga-scene-20261106/) notes it streams through AMC+ in the United States. It draws on the Baba Yaga myth, the witch of Slavic folklore who lives in the woods, and is set in a small coastal town.

The case at its centre is a disappearance. Henry Park, the heir to a fishing fortune, has gone missing. Private investigator Charlie Rapp, played by [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid), takes the job and works alongside local detective Sergeant Carson, played by Clark Backo.

Per Queerty, the suspects include Katherine, a professor who specialises in the occult, played by Carrie-Anne Moss, and Henry's girlfriend Pam, played by Ezra Franky. The roommate, Logan, is the second Williams role.

## Two characters, one face

Williams was cast as Henry in what Queerty describes as his first role since *Heated Rivalry*. He is best known there as [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander). The second part only became clear once the series began.

According to [Out Magazine](https://www.out.com/gay-tv-shows/hudson-williams-yaga-threesome-scene), Logan Casey is Henry's best friend and doppelgänger, and Williams wears a prosthetic nose to play him. Queerty adds that Logan wears glasses and overalls. Neither outlet reports an explanation for the resemblance. Queerty says the two are not twins as far as viewers know, and suggests a reveal could still come.

What the show does establish is the dynamic. Logan is fixated on Henry, and Henry, in Queerty's words a narcissist, has come to depend on that attention.

## What happens in episode 4

Episode 4, titled "Dance With The Devil", is where that fixation is pushed furthest. Queerty reports that Rapp and Carson go undercover at a party at Logan's home. It ends with Logan found dead on the beach.

With Pam now a suspect in both cases, the detectives bring her in for questioning. She describes the odd triangle between herself, Henry and Logan, and the show cuts to flashback. Henry kisses Pam, then draws Logan in and kisses him too. Out Magazine says the scene continues in a more explicit way, and that Henry watches.

Both outlets say the moment spread quickly online. Queerty quotes fans enjoying the idea of two Williams characters on screen at once. Out Magazine says viewers have been cheering the star's recent run of bold roles.
