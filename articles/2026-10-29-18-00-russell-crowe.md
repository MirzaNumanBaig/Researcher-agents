**Quick summary**

- Anna Baker, now a baby sleep consultant, says her first serious job was as a nanny for [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and his then-wife Danielle Spencer.
- She was 20 and looked after the couple's sons, Charles and Tennyson, when they were seven and five.
- Baker described the work to PerthNow on Thursday as "insane", with first-class flights, top hotels and bodyguards.
- She says she still sees the actor's sons and his ex-wife today.

A nanny who cared for Russell Crowe's two sons has described what it was like to start a career inside one of Australia's best-known households. Anna Baker, who went on to build a business around the sleep habits of babies, told PerthNow on Thursday that working for the actor and his then-wife, Danielle Spencer, at age 20 was "insane".

## A first job with first-class travel

According to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16248275/russell-crowe-nanny-anna-baker-danielle-spencer-sons.html), Baker looked after the couple's boys, Charles and Tennyson, when they were seven and five. The Daily Mail gives their current ages as 22 and 20.

"A 20-year-old flying first class, staying in top hotels, with bodyguards. Yes please!" Baker told [PerthNow](https://www.perthnow.com.au/lifestyle/anna-baker-celebrity-baby-whisperer-reveals-how-elite-parents-raise-kids-offering-free-perth-seminars-c-23087648). She said the job taught her what fine dining was and what it felt like to hold a Rolex or a designer handbag.

She was clear that the experience was a good one. "It was lush, and I'm so glad I was welcomed by the Crowe family," she said, adding that she still sees his sons and his ex-wife.

## From the Crowe household to a sleep business

Baker has since worked with a string of high-profile clients. PerthNow lists fashion designer Camilla Franks, ModelCo founder Shelly Sullivan, Real Housewives of Sydney's Martine Chippendale and Red Wiggle Caterina Mete among them, along with several chief executives.

She is also the founder of SleepBaker, a baby sleep consultancy based in Sydney, and has launched an organic infant clothing label, Dream with Baker. PerthNow describes her as one of the country's top baby trainers who tours the nation offering advice on bedtime routines and sleep.

The publication also reports that Baker, a mother of two, has fielded some unusual offers over the years. On one trip, a parent handed her an Amex card at 2am and offered to cover her own children's school fees in return for some peace. The children were jet-lagged, she recalled, and everyone ended up laughing over room service before finally getting to sleep.

## How wealthy families really parent

Baker pushed back on the idea that rich parents spend less time with their children. In her telling, they do spend time with their kids, because they are spared the tedious jobs most families cannot outsource. "People are probably just jealous," she said of critics.

Her own role, she said, was to keep up appearances when things were less polished, such as a child's meltdown at an airport. "I hold everything together with a smile when really I'm dying inside," she told PerthNow, adding that she did not mean it as a criticism.

## What happens next

Baker is holding free masterclasses for parents on Thursday and Friday this week at Milestones Early Learning centres in Middle Swan, Edgewater and Canning Vale, according to PerthNow. Her advice for Western Australian families is to treat anything before 6am as a night waking, keep the room dark with blackout blinds, and stick to the same bedtime, routine and wake-up time every day.
