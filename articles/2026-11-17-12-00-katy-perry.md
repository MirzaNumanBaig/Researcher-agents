Katy Perry's plan to build a permanent youth camp near Santa Barbara is meeting resistance from some of the neighbours who would live beside it. The Santa Barbara News-Press reported on 16 November that residents of Winchester Canyon fear the project will be too big, too noisy and too busy for the area.

## What Katy Perry wants to build at Gratitude Canyon

The singer, who attended Dos Pueblos High School, wants the site at 183 Winchester Canyon Road to become the permanent base for Camp Firework, the overnight program run by her Firework Foundation. She co-founded the foundation with her sister, Angela Lerche, to give underserved young people access to the arts and the outdoors.

According to the News-Press, the plans include cabins, a dining hall, a gymnasium, a pool and a recording studio. Summer sessions would give way to rentals and occasional events for the rest of the year.

County planner Soren Kringel told the paper the requested limits would allow up to 10 week-long summer sessions with 160 campers. Off-season rentals could host up to 208 overnight guests, and four annual special events could draw as many as 400 people each. The project team says weddings and concerts are excluded.

## Why neighbours are pushing back

Winchester Canyon resident Jen Lilienstein started an online petition against the proposal. She raised concerns about noise, traffic and evacuation, and said people living nearby should have a meaningful say when wealthy landowners buy local land.

She also wants clearer answers on how any commercial venue activity would work under a nonprofit structure, and argues that such users should help pay for the roads and services they rely on.

The project team responds that campers would mostly arrive by bus and most counsellors would stay on site, which would cut daily vehicle trips. Principal planner Steve Welton said the goal is "a meaningful community purpose while respecting the rural neighborhood in which it sits".

## Where the county review stands

Kringel said the county deemed the application complete on 21 September. A month later, planners found issues with how the proposal meets county standards, and the applicant is working on them.

Traffic and acoustic studies have been submitted. Fire Marshal Fred Tan said the county's fire planning section has required a Fire Protection Plan covering fire code compliance, emergency vehicle access and public evacuation routes. The project team proposes vegetation management, defensible space and fire-resistant construction. Fire protection and evacuation plans are still at an early stage, according to county officials.

Camp Firework already serves local young people. Girls Inc. sent 23 students and two chaperones to the October camp, its interim executive director Lauren Hudspeth said, though the group has taken no official position on the development.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Gratitude Canyon?

It is the proposed site at 183 Winchester Canyon Road, in California's Goleta Valley, for a permanent Camp Firework. The program serves underserved young people through the arts and outdoor activities.
