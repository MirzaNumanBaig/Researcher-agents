**Quick summary**

- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, with [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) as Maysilee Donner, is projected to take more than US$120 million in its three-day US and Canadian opening, according to ScreenRant.
- That would be the fourth-best opening in the franchise and the best since *Mockingjay – Part 1* in 2014.
- Critics are cooler than audiences: a 67% Rotten Tomatoes score against a 92% audience score.
- The film's reported budget is about US$200 million.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) has helped carry a prequel to one of the franchise's strongest starts in more than a decade. [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office-opening-weekend-domestic-result/) reports that *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* was heading for more than US$120 million at the domestic box office by the end of its opening weekend, based on Saturday morning figures. Sunday could push the total higher.

## Mckenna Grace box office: how the opening compares

The sixth film in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series is directed by [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) and based on [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)'s prequel novel. Grace stars alongside [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)), [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) and [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close).

ScreenRant says Thursday night previews took roughly US$21 million. That is the second-best preview figure in the series, behind the US$25.2 million made by *Catching Fire*. The site adds that the film is one of only 16 releases to open above US$100 million in November. It passes the debuts of *Zootopia 2* (US$100.3 million) and *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire* (US$102.7 million).

The contrast with the last prequel is stark. *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes*, starring [Tom Blyth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Blyth) as young [President Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow), opened to US$44.6 million in 2023, the weakest start in the series. On ScreenRant's numbers, the new film is on course to earn about two and a half times that. The first two films both opened above US$150 million, so those records look safe.

## Critics and audiences split

Reviews are a mixed picture. ScreenRant notes the film holds a 67% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the fifth-lowest of the six films. Audiences like it far more: its 92% Popcornmeter score is the highest in the franchise, ahead of the 89% for *Catching Fire*. The best-reviewed film remains *Catching Fire* at 90% with critics.

ScreenRant suggests the audience score points to a longer run closer to *Catching Fire* than to *Ballad*. It offers a wide range for the worldwide total, from roughly US$600 million to about US$898 million, and judges the middle ground most likely. Against a reported budget of about US$200 million, either outcome would count as a solid hit. The franchise has grossed US$3.3 billion worldwide so far.

## The role behind the numbers

Grace plays Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute in the 50th Hunger Games, set 24 years before [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen)'s first Games. In an interview with [The AU Review](https://www.theaureview.com/watch/interview-mckenna-grace-hunger-games/), the actress said the character's defiance is about refusing to lose herself. "So much of her character is trying to be determined to not let her personality and her character and her humanity be stripped away from her," she said.

She also stressed that Maysilee is not fearless. The tributes are 16, Grace said, and being terrified of dying is an inherently human reaction given how dire their situation is.

That idea carried into the press tour. According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/mckenna-grace-honors-katniss-everdeen-064322835.html), republished from Parade, Grace wore franchise-inspired looks throughout, including a cobalt blue Pamella Roland gown with feathered wings at the New York premiere on 16 November, a nod to [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence)'s Mockingjay moment. Grace told Variety that Maysilee's style "isn't a vain thing" and is her form of defiance against the Capitol. We covered the thinking behind those looks in [Mckenna Grace's Hunger Games Method Dressing, Explained](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-method-dressing/).
