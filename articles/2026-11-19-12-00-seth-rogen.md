**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen) says he shot *This Is the End* in New Orleans while *12 Years a Slave* was filming in the same area.
- He told the podcast Happy Sad Confused that the drama's cast and crew often came to his set because it was a cheerful place.
- He named [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt), [Chiwetel Ejiofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiwetel_Ejiofor) and director Steve McQueen as visitors.
- The story came up in a wider chat about his role as Steve Wozniak in *Steve Jobs*, reported by [Entertainment Weekly](https://ew.com/seth-rogen-helped-perk-up-brad-pitt-12-years-a-slave-cast-12263705).

Seth Rogen has revealed how his comedy set became an unofficial retreat for the cast of *12 Years a Slave*. The star of *The Studio* said this week that the two productions were filming in New Orleans at the same time, and that the drama's actors kept turning up to spend time with his crew.

## Seth Rogen and 12 Years a Slave: two sets in one city

Rogen was making *This Is the End*, released in 2013, in New Orleans while Steve McQueen's film was shooting in the same area. The slavery drama later won the Best Picture Oscar, but Rogen's memory is of a heavy mood on its set.

"Understandably, the cast and crew of *12 Years a Slave* was in a bummed out headspace a lot of the time," he said on Happy Sad Confused, as quoted by Entertainment Weekly. His explanation for why they came over was simple. His own set was in "a great mood all the time", he said.

According to Rogen, the visits were not limited to the workplace. The two groups went to bars together, and the drama's cast and crew kept coming back to hang out.

## The visitors he remembers

Rogen singled out Ejiofor, the lead of *12 Years a Slave*, and Pitt, who both acted in the film and produced it. He also named McQueen. His description was good-humoured: his own group was "having the time of our lives", and then the three would "sulk in". A few drinks later, he said, they would "kinda perk up".

It is Rogen's own account of events, told years afterwards on a podcast. Entertainment Weekly did not report any comment from Pitt, Ejiofor or McQueen, so the story rests on his recollection alone.

## How it led to Steve Jobs

The anecdote arose during a longer discussion of *Steve Jobs*, Danny Boyle's biopic, in which Rogen played Apple cofounder [Steve Wozniak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Wozniak) opposite Michael Fassbender as Steve Jobs. Rogen said he first met Fassbender in New Orleans, when Fassbender was working on *12 Years a Slave*. He got to know him properly once rehearsals for the Boyle film began.

He said he was unsure what Fassbender would be like to work with, so he asked about his process. His example was whether Fassbender minded talking about [A Tribe Called Quest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Tribe_Called_Quest) half a minute before the cameras rolled. Fassbender, he said, loved the idea. Rogen found him casual and said he put him at ease.

Rehearsals, he added, ran for about six weeks in total, because the cast rehearsed for two weeks before each act of the film. He credited that process with teaching him a lot about blocking a scene.

## Why he auditioned

Rogen also talked about being asked to join the project. By then he did not audition often, but he was glad to be asked to read for the part. His reasoning was that Boyle would see exactly what he could do before filming started, and would not regret hiring him on set.

Auditioning, he said, gave him more confidence on the first day, because he knew the director had seen his work and liked it.
