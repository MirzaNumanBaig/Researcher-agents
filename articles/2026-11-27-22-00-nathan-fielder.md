[Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) style is getting its own close reading. In a piece published on 27 November 2026, British GQ asks whether the comedian's low-effort clothes in *You Can See Everything* are a joke, and whether he wants the audience in on it.

## What Fielder wears in the film

GQ describes the look seen in the trailer: a ribbed black knit over a white T-shirt, tapered navy trousers and [New Balance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Balance) 990v5 sneakers. The writer admits the film does not need costume design, since its themes of deceit and high-level scheming already pull focus. Even so, the outfit stayed on the writer's mind.

Elsewhere in the film, according to GQ, Fielder sticks to plain grey zip-ups, dark T-shirts and sweaters in neutral shades. The accessory that stands out is the vintage-looking HamiltonBuhl headphones, which GQ credits a Reddit user with identifying.

## From costume to character

GQ traces the idea back to *Nathan For You* on Comedy Central. There, Fielder played a deadpan consultant who persuaded business owners to try odd ideas, and the chinos, button-down shirts and collared jackets worked as costume. The magazine says they served his comic timing in the same way as his line delivery. The famously oversized suit from that series, it adds, turned office wear into a caricature.

*The Rehearsal* is where GQ sees his personal taste. In that documentary and reality hybrid he builds sets and hires actors so participants can rehearse real-life moments. The clothes there are crewneck jumpers, shirts worn open over T-shirts and dad-style trainers, with the odd bit of forest green. GQ compares the effect to the "normcore" trend of around 2014, when bland clothing signalled a kind of ironic cool.

## Why the ordinary look works

The magazine's reading is that Fielder's clothes suggest a man dressing for comfort and nothing else, which suits a persona the audience is always trying to decode. GQ adds that The Cut wrote about his "strange sex appeal" in 2022, and that the look does not change when he is off-screen. It cites an appearance on stage with his friend Mac DeMarco in the same kind of outfit.

## The film behind the fashion talk

The style debate sits alongside strong reviews. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything), *You Can See Everything* holds 97% on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 97 from 52 critics. The same entry lists a budget of about US$7 million and a box office of around US$105 million, roughly 15 times its cost. For a film pitched as an indie, that is an unusual result.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Nathan Fielder wear in You Can See Everything?

According to British GQ, mostly plain knits, grey zip-ups, dark T-shirts, tapered navy trousers and New Balance 990v5 sneakers, plus a pair of vintage-looking HamiltonBuhl headphones.

### Who directed You Can See Everything?

Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder directed it. A24 produced the film, which follows Elizabeth Holmes a month before her prison sentence starts.

### Is Nathan Fielder's style deliberate?

GQ thinks so, calling his ordinary look part of his persona. That is the magazine's reading, not a statement from Fielder.
