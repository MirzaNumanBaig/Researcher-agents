**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman)'s West Village triplex at 176 Perry Street went into contract in the week of 16 to 22 November, according to 6sqft and People.
- The final price has not been made public. The last asking price was US$28.75 million, down from US$38.9 million in 2022.
- Jackman and ex-wife [Deborra-Lee Furness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborra-Lee_Furness) bought the home for US$21 million in 2008.
- Separately, an unconfirmed online claim says Jackman's Wolverine filmed extra scenes for *Avengers: Doomsday*, which opens on 18 December 2026.

After more than four years on the market, Hugh Jackman's Manhattan home has found a buyer. The 11,000-square-foot triplex at 176 Perry Street in the West Village is now in contract, according to [6sqft](https://www.6sqft.com/hugh-jackman-sells-28-75m-west-village-triplex-after-four-years-and-10m-price-cut/), which cited CityRealty. The listing is held by the Deborah Grubman Team at The Corcoran Group, [People](https://people.com/hugh-jackman-finds-a-buyer-for-his-usd29m-n-y-c-home-that-he-shared-with-ex-deborra-lee-furness-before-divorce-12275794) reports.

## What we know about the Hugh Jackman West Village triplex sale

The asking price fell by roughly US$10 million over the life of the listing. It first went up at US$38.9 million in June 2022, People notes, was relisted at US$34.8 million in April 2023, and was withdrawn that November. It came back this year at US$28.75 million. [PerthNow](https://www.perthnow.com.au/entertainment/hugh-jackman-and-ex-deborra-lee-furness-finally-land-buyer-for-lavish-ny-triplex-after-four-years-c-23205641) converts the latest figure to about A$39.7 million.

People says the sale price is not yet public, and Yahoo, citing the Daily Mail, says the sum is undisclosed. That means any headline claiming the home sold for the asking price is guessing. A contract is also not a closed sale, so the deal is not final until it completes.

The week's market data adds some scale. 6sqft, citing The Real Deal, says 28 Manhattan homes asking US$4 million or more went into contract between 16 and 22 November, down from 34 in the previous period. PerthNow reports that agents described this one as the priciest of them.

## Inside the Perry Street home

The residence sits across the eighth, ninth and tenth floors of a 13-unit tower finished in 2002 and designed by architect [Richard Meier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Meier). Listings describe five bedrooms, a library, three terraces, double-height ceilings and Hudson River views, joined by a spiral staircase. The tenth floor holds the primary suite, with its own gym, sauna and dressing rooms.

Famous neighbours have come and gone. According to 6sqft, residents have included [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and fashion designer Calvin Klein, and chef [Jean-Georges Vongerichten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Georges_Vongerichten) opened a restaurant on the ground floor in 2005.

## The divorce backdrop

Jackman and Furness announced their separation in September 2023 after 27 years of marriage, and People says the divorce was finalised in June 2025. Yahoo reports the couple rented the triplex out at US$125,000 a month after the split, and that it returned to the market in March 2026. The same outlet says it is unclear how proceeds will be divided. 6sqft reports that the pair agreed to split the property between them as part of the divorce proceedings.

The property was not the only shared asset. People reports that Furness bought out Jackman's share of a Chelsea penthouse in May 2025, based on property records its reporters reviewed.

## A relaxed Sunday online

Jackman himself kept things light over the weekend. The Daily Express US reports that he posted two outdoor selfies on X on 22 November, wearing an olive-green shirt and captioning them "Today's vibe. Well, mine anyway. #Sunday." Fans replied warmly, and the post made no mention of the property.

## The Wolverine rumour

A separate item is doing the rounds among Marvel fans. GeekTyrant reports that an X account known for Marvel scoops claimed the directors [Joe Russo and Anthony Russo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo_brothers) shot a last-minute scene with Jackman's Wolverine for [*Avengers: Doomsday*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Doomsday). Marvel Studios has not confirmed it, so treat it as a rumour.

GeekTyrant points out that neither Jackman nor [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) has been officially announced in the cast, though both have joined promotion for the film and, the site says, hold "Additional Literary Material" writing credits. Returning X-Men names mentioned include James Marsden, Kelsey Grammer and Ian McKellen. The sale now needs to close, and the buyer and final price should surface once city records are updated. ## Frequently asked questions

### Has Hugh Jackman sold his West Village home?

Not completely. The triplex at 176 Perry Street is under contract, according to People and 6sqft, but the sale has not been reported as closed and the price is undisclosed.

### How much did Hugh Jackman pay for the home?

Jackman and Deborra-Lee Furness bought the unit for US$21 million in 2008, according to city records cited by 6sqft.

### Is Hugh Jackman in Avengers: Doomsday?

Marvel has not officially announced him in the cast. An unconfirmed report says Wolverine filmed extra scenes, but nothing has been verified by the studio.
