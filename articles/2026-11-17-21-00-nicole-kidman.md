**Quick summary**

- [Naomi Watts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Watts) has been photographed in cutout beige jazz shoes, according to InStyle, which calls them the hottest shoe of fall 2026.
- InStyle says the trend was put on the map by an earlier sighting of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) in the style this fall.
- The look blends a sneaker, a ballet flat and a loafer, and usually has a slim, lace-up shape.
- Separately, [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban) will headline a SiriusXM and Pandora holiday show in New York on December 8.

Naomi Watts has stepped out in jazz shoes, and the fashion press is crediting Nicole Kidman with starting the moment. InStyle reports that Watts wore a pair of cutout beige lace-ups this week, which it describes as the latest celebrity endorsement of a style that Kidman helped popularise earlier in the season.

## What exactly are jazz shoes?

If you took after-school dance classes, you already know them. Jazz shoes are slim, fitted and usually flat, with a lace-up front that follows the shape of the foot. InStyle describes the trend as a three-in-one: part sneaker, part ballet flat, part loafer.

That mix is the selling point. The writer argues the style will make you reconsider both your ballet flats and your sneakers, because it sits neatly between the two. Some versions, such as those from Jeffrey Campbell, add a small heel for extra height.

The magazine admits the shoe is polarising. Even so, its pitch is that it wins people over once they try a pair, and it lists lookalikes from about US$110 to US$149 for readers who want the style without hunting for a specific designer pair.

## How Naomi Watts wore them

According to InStyle, Watts paired the shoes with breezy striped trousers, a white button-down shirt and a collarless leather jacket. The beige cutout pair kept the outfit light, and the dance-shoe shape gave a relaxed finish to otherwise tailored pieces.

InStyle's reasoning is simple: when two of Hollywood's most stylish women have been seen in the same trend, it is probably worth trying. The outlet does not say which designer made Watts's pair or give details of where Kidman wore hers, so those specifics remain unconfirmed.

## Keith Urban's holiday show

Kidman's former husband is also in the news. Yahoo reports that Urban, 59, will headline "SiriusXM and Pandora Present Keith Urban" at Hammerstein Ballroom in New York City on December 8. A press release promises hits such as "Blue Ain't Your Color", ["Somebody Like You"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somebody_Like_You) and "The Fighter", along with holiday classics. The show will air in full on SiriusXM's The Highway on December 18 at 8pm EST.

Yahoo notes this will be Urban's first holiday season since the divorce was finalised in January. The pair separated in August 2025. Yahoo adds that Kidman has kept a public profile, including regular appearances with their daughters Sunday and Faith, while Urban has stayed more low-key.

Urban's album *Flow State* arrived in June 2026. He told the Associated Press it was a challenging record to make and that he remains "very, very protective" of his family. In an earlier interview with guitarist and producer [Dann Huff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dann_Huff), he spoke about wanting to simplify his life.

One Australian magazine has run claims from an unnamed insider that the former couple have been in touch more often. Those claims are unconfirmed, and neither Kidman nor Urban has commented in the pieces we read, so we are not treating them as fact.
