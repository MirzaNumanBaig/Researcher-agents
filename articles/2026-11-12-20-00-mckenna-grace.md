- The film opens in Australian cinemas on 19 November, according to Refinery29.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) and Joseph Zada have been answering fan questions about *Sunrise on the Reaping* ahead of its Australian release. In an interview published on 12 November, they told [Refinery29 Australia](https://www.refinery29.com/en-au/joseph-zada-mckenna-grace-hayffie-sunrise-reaping) about the film's central friendships, its themes and a physically demanding shoot.

## Is there a "Hayffie" romance?

The question fans keep asking is whether Haymitch and Effie are a couple. Zada said the film stays close to the books, where the two are never romantically involved, according to Refinery29. He described Effie as the first person Haymitch meets in the Capitol who means well, even though she sees the world differently.

On any spark between them, Zada's answer was that there is "a little bit of attraction", but that it is more on her side. He said he played Haymitch as having eyes only for Lenore Dove, played by Whitney Peak. Elle Fanning plays Effie.

## What Grace and Zada say the film is about

Zada named standing up for what you believe in as the film's main theme, and said it takes time. Grace picked up the thread. Speaking about the film's message, she said that speaking up now, even when you see no difference, may pave the way for a future generation.

## Spain locations and real stunts

Refinery29 reports that the arena scenes were shot in Spain. Grace said she did most of her own stunts and came away bruised. Zada also did many of his own, though the report says he had a stunt double.
