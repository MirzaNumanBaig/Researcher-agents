**Quick summary**

- Black Doves Season 2 premiered on Netflix on 5 November 2026 with six new episodes.
- As of 13 November, FlixPatrol lists it as the No. 1 series on Netflix worldwide, according to Men's Journal.
- It first reached the top spot on 11 November, after earlier climbing to No. 2.
- Season 2 holds a 96% Rotten Tomatoes score, and the series overall sits at 94%.

[Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley)'s spy thriller [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) has become the most-watched series on Netflix worldwide, one week after its second season arrived. The ranking comes from FlixPatrol's daily charts, as reported by Men's Journal and republished by [AOL](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/keira-knightley-stars-global-hit-155932000.html).

## How Black Doves reached No. 1 on Netflix

Season 2 landed on 5 November 2026. According to the report, the show first slipped into the No. 2 position, then took the top place on 11 November. A day later it widened its lead over the next most-watched title, the true-crime series Taken: Natalee Holloway, and it held the No. 1 spot on 13 November.

FlixPatrol publishes daily lists of the most-viewed titles across the big streaming services, so the figure measures ranking, not total viewing hours. Men's Journal puts the series well ahead of its nearest rival on the day.

For context, the same report notes that Taken: Natalee Holloway drew 12.9 million views worldwide in the week to 8 November. By comparison, the top non-sports show on US network television that week, ABC World News Tonight, had just over 9.5 million viewers, according to Nielsen. The two measures are not identical, so the comparison is a rough guide only.

## What the show is about

Black Doves is a British spy action thriller created by Joe Barton for Netflix. Knightley plays an undercover operative who lives a double life as a devoted wife and mother. [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) plays an assassin who comes out of hiding to protect her after she is targeted, and [Sarah Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Lancashire) completes the main cast.

Knightley was nominated for a Golden Globe in 2025 for Best Performance by a Female Actor in a Television Series, Drama, for the role. The reviews have stayed strong: the series holds 94% on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), and Season 2 scores higher at 96%. One Pajiba review called it "The Most Enjoyable Binge in Ages."

## Why the global ranking matters

Men's Journal points to Netflix's reach. It cites FlixPatrol's estimate of roughly 325 million Netflix subscribers worldwide, against about 200 million for Prime Video. On that basis, the outlet argues Black Doves is probably the most-watched show in the world right now, or very close to it. That is the outlet's inference, not a confirmed viewing figure from Netflix.

## A long way from the Pirates years

The success lands at a different point in Knightley's career from her blockbuster start. She was 18 when Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl came out in 2003. The following years brought Bend It Like Beckham, [Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually) and an Oscar nomination for Pride & Prejudice.

In a 2018 interview with The Hollywood Reporter's Awards Chatter podcast, summarised by [Startefacts](https://startefacts.com/news/pirates-of-the-caribbean-did-something-to-keira-knightley-that-took-years-to-undo), she described the cost of that fame. She said the paparazzi attention of the mid-2000s was relentless, and that she took a year off after 2007 and went into therapy. She told the podcast she was diagnosed with post-traumatic stress disorder. Since then she has leaned towards smaller projects and period dramas such as The Imitation Game and Colette.
