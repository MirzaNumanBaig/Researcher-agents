**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) wore a deep red manicure to the 2026 CFDA Awards, according to ELLE and Woman & Home.
- ELLE calls the shade "winter cherry", a richer take on classic cherry red.
- She paired it with a black gown and a black-and-white clutch, per ELLE.
- Woman & Home notes the look is a change from her usual sheer or milky rose nails.

Pamela Anderson's manicure at the 2026 CFDA Awards has turned into the beauty story of the week. The actor and model arrived with freshly done deep red nails, and two publishers have since named the shade as a pick for the holiday season.

## What Pamela Anderson wore

[ELLE](https://www.elle.com/beauty/makeup-skin-care/a74326105/pamela-anderson-winter-cherry-nails/) describes the colour as "winter cherry", a nuanced alternative to the familiar cherry red. The magazine says it has more depth and dimension, and that its slightly darker tone suits a room with a fire more than a picnic blanket.

The nails sat against a sleek black gown and a crisp black-and-white clutch. ELLE says the contrast shows how much a statement manicure can do for an otherwise simple outfit.

[Woman & Home](https://www.womanandhome.com/beauty/beauty-news/pamela-anderson-winter-berry-nails/) saw the same look slightly differently. Its writer calls it berry red, compares it to holly berries and notes the polka-dot clutch that completed the outfit. The publication also points to the almond shape as a detail that makes the colour look more luxe.

Both outlets agree on the main point: a rich red, glossy finish and a formal black outfit.

## A change from her usual style

ELLE says the look departs slightly from Anderson's usual clean, minimalist and barely-there manicure. Woman & Home goes further, saying she often chooses a subtle milky rose or sheer polish and that almond appears to be her preferred shape.

That context is why the red drew attention. Neither outlet suggests a lasting change in her style. Woman & Home frames it as a way for people who usually wear neutrals to try something bolder while keeping a classic shape.

For more on her appearance at the event, see our coverage of her [feathered Herrera look](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-cfda-awards-feathers/) and her [Old Hollywood glam](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-cfda-awards-glam/).

## Why red is trending for winter

ELLE quotes Susan Carroll, founder of the Notting Hill beauty destination Young LDN, who previously told the magazine that reds are becoming deeper and richer. Carroll's advice for a shade this strong is careful preparation: neatly shaped nails, well-prepped cuticles and a completely clean nail plate before the base coat. She also recommends a glossy top coat for a high-shine finish, similar to Anderson's.

Woman & Home makes a simpler case. Red goes with everything, it says, from jeans and a cream knit to a black dress, and it brings a pop of colour to winter outfits. The outlet admits it is early to talk about Christmas, but points to the arrival of John Lewis' Christmas advert and the end of Halloween as signs the season is close.

## How to get the look

Both sources focus on practical steps rather than any named salon or nail artist for Anderson herself. Neither says who did her nails, so that detail is unconfirmed.

Woman & Home suggests an essie cherry-red polish for under £10, which it says delivers a classic result in two or three coats. It also recommends OPI's Big Apple Red, and a clear top coat to boost shine. Prices there are in British pounds, so check local Australian retailers before buying.
