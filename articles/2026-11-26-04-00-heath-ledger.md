**Quick summary**

- [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger) signed his last will in Australia in 2003, aged 24, before his daughter Matilda was born, according to The Times of India.
- The will was never updated, so it left Matilda nothing.
- After his death on 22 January 2008, his family confirmed they were passing the whole estate, put at US$16.3 million to US$20 million, to her.
- Matilda is now 21 and lives in New York City, the paper reports.

The Times of India has revisited how [Heath Ledger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heath_Ledger)'s estate, valued at about US$16.3 million, ended up with his daughter even though his will never mentioned her. The will was signed in 2003, years before Matilda was born, and the Ledger family handed her the inheritance after his death in 2008.

## What did Heath Ledger's will say?

The paper cites the Associated Press in reporting that Ledger signed the document in Australia at 24. It listed modest personal assets and cash of roughly US$118,000. The estate was to be split equally between his parents, Kim Ledger and Sally Bell, and his three sisters, Kate, Olivia and Ashleigh.

A year later, in 2004, he met [Michelle Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Williams_(actress)) on the set of [Brokeback Mountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brokeback_Mountain). Their daughter, Matilda Rose Ledger, was born in New York City on 28 October 2005. According to The Times of India, he never revised the will to include Williams or Matilda.

## Why did the will cause questions?

Ledger died on 22 January 2008, aged 28. His death was ruled an accidental overdose of prescription drugs at his New York apartment, the paper says. He had been dealing with insomnia, anxiety and back pain, and had split from Williams in October 2007.

In March 2008, probate filings in Manhattan Surrogate's Court made the 2003 will public. They listed about US$145,000 in local assets, including bank accounts, furniture and a Toyota Prius. The larger wealth, which came from *Brokeback Mountain*, *The Dark Knight* and Australian trusts, was estimated at between US$16.3 million and US$20 million.

Speculation followed about possible legal claims under Western Australia's Inheritance (Family and Dependants Provision) Act 1972. It did not come to that. The paper reports that Kim Ledger told Australia's Sunday Times and the Associated Press the family had transferred their inheritance to Matilda, and that there was never any doubt about who the estate belonged to.

## How did his final film help?

When Ledger died he was midway through [The Imaginarium of Doctor Parnassus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Imaginarium_of_Doctor_Parnassus). Director [Terry Gilliam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Gilliam) rewrote the story so that other actors could play different versions of his character. [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp), [Colin Farrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Farrell) and [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law) all worked on the film, and The Times of India reports they donated their salaries to Matilda.

## Where is Matilda now?

The paper describes Matilda as a 21-year-old college student who has stayed largely out of the public eye. She lives in New York City with her mother, her stepfather [Thomas Kail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Kail) and her younger half-siblings, and keeps in touch with the Ledger family in Australia. Her godparents are [Jake Gyllenhaal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Gyllenhaal) and [Busy Philipps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Busy_Philipps).

Ledger spoke about fatherhood to InTouch in December 2005, calling it the most remarkable experience of his life, according to the paper. In a 2013 New Yorker interview, Williams said Matilda had inherited her father's love of the movies.
