**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) reportedly screen tested for [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) late last month, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Nothing has been confirmed by the studio or the filmmakers.
- The same report named Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Nicholas Hoult as other actors who tested.
- Bookmaker William Hill lists Turner at 5/4, Lowden at 7/4 and Pattinson at 3/1.
- Denis Villeneuve is directing the next Bond film.

A Robert Pattinson Bond screen test is the latest twist in the race to play 007. The Hollywood Reporter says the 40-year-old tested for the part late last month, as reported by [The Nightly](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/robert-pattinson-would-be-a-magnificent-james-bond-but-for-his-sake-and-ours-he-shouldnt-do-it-c-23134104) in Australia. It is a reported audition, not an offer.

## Beyond the Robert Pattinson Bond screen test

For years the Bond guessing game barely mentioned Pattinson. The Nightly says that changed this past weekend, when the Hollywood Reporter placed him alongside the names most people expected: [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden). It also listed a more surprising entry, Josh O'Connor, and a newly revealed one, [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult).

The Nightly adds that Vanity Fair has alleged [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) and [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James) are both still in the frame. That is one outlet's claim, relayed by another.

The Nightly notes that [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) was 38 when Casino Royale premiered and was best known for theatre and independent film. It describes Pattinson at 40 as a little older and more famous than the usual profile for a new Bond.

## What the bookmakers think

Betting site OLBG, in a [market round-up](https://www.olbg.com/news/next-james-bond-odds-bookmakers-make-callum-turner-5-4-robert-pattinson-joins-007-race), reports William Hill's prices: Turner 5/4, Lowden 7/4 and Pattinson 3/1, with O'Connor at 6/1. That puts Pattinson in the top three after entering the race only last week, the site says.

OLBG also notes reports that the shortlist has narrowed to Turner, Lowden and O'Connor, with Turner described as the presumptive frontrunner. Some reports claim he is already negotiating terms. OLBG stresses that none of this has been confirmed, and the odds are a reading of the market, not inside knowledge.

## Why Pattinson fits, and why it might not suit him

The Nightly's columnist argues Pattinson would be the best pick among the known names. The case rests on his range across comedy, drama and action, and on Tenet in 2020, where his character Neil had the polish of a British spy. The piece jokes that no one fully understood that film, including Pattinson, according to co-star [Himesh Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himesh_Patel).

There are also working links to the Bond team. Pattinson appears in Dune Part 3, directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who is making the next Bond film. One of the producers, [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman), was closely involved with the [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) films, where Pattinson made his feature debut as Cedric Diggory.

Yet the same column argues he should say no. Signing on would reportedly lock an actor in for 10 to 15 years, and Pattinson has built a career on unusual projects, including The Lighthouse, Good Time, The Rover and Mickey 17. This year he has four films out, among them Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey and The Drama with Zendaya. OLBG adds that his commitment to the next Batman film could complicate any Bond deal.

## The pressure on the film

Villeneuve told GQ for its Men of the Year issue that expectations are enormous, saying: "We cannot f*** it up." OLBG notes Amazon MGM took full creative control of the franchise in early 2025 and turned to Villeneuve, known for Arrival, Blade Runner 2049 and the Dune films. Whoever wins the role is likely to carry several films, so producers are expected to favour someone in their late twenties or thirties.
