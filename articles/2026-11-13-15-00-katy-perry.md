**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) was photographed holding hands with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) in Beverly Hills on Wednesday, 11 November.
- It is her first reported public appearance since ex-husband [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand) was sentenced on 9 November.
- Brand received 10 years and six months in prison after a jury at Southwark Crown Court found him guilty on 5 November.
- Perry has not commented publicly on the conviction or sentence.
- An unconfirmed report says she is considering a book and a docuseries about the marriage.

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau left a Beverly Hills restaurant hand in hand on the evening of Wednesday, 11 November, two days after the singer's former husband was jailed. According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/934755/katy-perry-42-steps-out-hand-in-hand-with-justin-trudeau-following-ex-russell-brands-rape-conviction/), which cited photos shared by Page Six, it was her first reported outing since the sentencing.

## What was seen in Beverly Hills

HELLO! reports that the pair, aged 42 and 54, left the celebrity hotspot E. Baldi after dinner. Perry wore an off-the-shoulder black top with light-wash jeans, a statement belt and black heeled boots, her dark hair in a sleek bun. Trudeau, Canada's prime minister from 2015 to 2025, was in a navy sweater and dark jeans.

The magazine describes the couple as relaxed and in good spirits, with Trudeau opening the car door for her. Nothing in the coverage suggests the dinner was anything other than a private evening out.

## The Russell Brand verdict and sentence

The timing is what drew attention. Brand, 51, was found guilty at Southwark Crown Court in London on 5 November of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others. [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/katy-perry-reportedly-planning-tell-003019162.html) reports the offences covered the years 2004 to 2009. He was sentenced on Monday, 9 November.

Perry and Brand married in October 2010, a year after she had a cameo in his film Get Him to the Greek. According to Yahoo, he filed for divorce about 14 months later, in December 2011, and HELLO! notes the divorce was finalised in 2012. Perry has not publicly addressed the case. Her earlier comments have resurfaced, though. In a 2013 Vogue interview she called his behaviour during the marriage "very controlling" and said he told her by text message on New Year's Eve 2011 that he wanted a divorce.

## A reported book and docuseries

Separately, Yahoo, citing The Mail on Sunday, says Perry is considering an autobiography and a streaming series about the marriage. An unnamed insider told the paper she had received offers over the years but wanted to wait until the trial was over, adding that the marriage was "a very dark period in her life."

This is a single anonymous source. Neither Perry nor her representatives have confirmed it, and Yahoo notes it is unclear who would publish a book or where a series might air, although Netflix and Hulu have reportedly been mentioned. It should be read as a report, not a plan.

## How the Perry and Trudeau relationship got here

HELLO! traces the relationship back to July 2025, when the two were seen at dinner in Montreal shortly after Perry's split from [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom). They made their red-carpet debut as a couple at the Tribeca Festival in June 2026, then appeared together at the opening of the [Lucas Museum of Narrative Art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucas_Museum_of_Narrative_Art) in Los Angeles in September.

In a September interview with People, quoted by HELLO!, Perry called Trudeau the "love of my life" and said he had been supportive throughout her [Lifetimes Tour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Lifetimes_Tour). Perry shares six-year-old daughter Daisy Dove with Bloom. Trudeau has three children with his former wife Sophie Grégoire; the two separated in 2023 after 18 years of marriage.
