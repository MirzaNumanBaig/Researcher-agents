**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) is now a co-owner of Australian spirits brand Archie Rose and stars in its first global campaign, reported on 20-21 October 2026.
- The 1 minute 45 second film casts his brother Luke Hemsworth as a bewildered stranger at the Archie Rose bar.
- The campaign launches the whisky in the US, according to Little Black Book.
- The desert, snow, ocean and clifftop scenes were made with AI because Hemsworth was available for only two shooting days.

Chris Hemsworth has swapped Asgard for a Sydney whisky bar. This week Archie Rose, the Australian distiller he now co-owns, released a tongue-in-cheek global ad in which he tells his brother Luke an increasingly unbelievable story about taking Australian whisky to the world. The film is the brand's push into the United States.

## What the film shows

According to [Campaign Brief](https://campaignbrief.com/chris-hemsworth-spins-a-tall-tale-about-whisky-to-brother-luke-in-archie-roses-new-global-campaign-directed-by-justin-mcmillan/), the hero film runs 1 minute 45 seconds and is set inside the Archie Rose bar. Two strangers talk: Chris, recounting how he took whisky to the world, and a customer played by Luke.

[Little Black Book](https://lbbonline.com/news/chris-hemsworth-archie-rose-citadel-brand-campaign) describes it as a parody of a whisky commercial that plays it straight while the tale gets wilder. Chris sails a raft through dangerous water, rides a camel across a desert and takes on snow, ocean and clifftops. The story runs from the Southern Ocean to the New York skyline.

## Two days, a hybrid shoot and some AI

The live-action shoot covered several Sydney locations, Little Black Book reports: the Archie Rose bar and original distillery in Rosebery, a beach sequence at Kurnell, the Blue Mountains and the CBD. Chris was free for only two shooting days.

Citadel executive producer Hamish Roxburgh told the outlet that time limit is why the extreme-weather scenes were built with AI. "There simply wasn't time to capture those elements on film in those environments," he said, adding that the hybrid approach "became the solution, not a workaround". Citadel hired Berlin studio Lipstick for the AI work. Campaign Brief lists [Sebastian Strasser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastian_Strasser) and Kai Stocker as its managing directors.

Roxburgh said he sees the model working for other celebrity campaigns with tight availability. He was careful to add that it was not about one method being better or cheaper than the other.

## Casting Luke

Director Justin McMillan told Little Black Book the brothers improvised, with two cameras on one actor at a time while the other stood behind the lens. It took a little while to find a rhythm, he said, and the pair had not worked opposite each other like this before.

Luke is cast as a stranger rather than a sibling, McMillan said, because the role is the audience's way into the joke. Chris, he said, would not hand it to just anyone. Keeping it in the family meant someone he trusted.

## The business behind it

Chris worked with the Archie Rose team and Glasgow agency Thirst on the identity, bottle, packaging and creative direction, Little Black Book says. Archie Rose head of marketing Victoria Tulloch told Campaign Brief the film will go to Chris's "100 million plus" social audience, plus other channels in Australia and the US.

Trade outlet Mumbrella ran a related piece headlined "We have absolutely no brand awareness", on Archie Rose taking on the US, and Mi-3 covered the launch as a global brand push. Neither page was readable for this article, so only their headlines are cited here.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in the Archie Rose campaign?
Chris Hemsworth and his brother Luke Hemsworth, according to Campaign Brief. Chris is a co-owner of the brand.

### Is the footage real or AI?
Both. The bar and city scenes were shot live in Sydney. The desert, snow, ocean and clifftop sequences were created by Lipstick in Berlin using AI, per Campaign Brief and Little Black Book.

### Where will the ad run?
Archie Rose says it will go to Chris Hemsworth's social audience and other channels in Australia and the US.
