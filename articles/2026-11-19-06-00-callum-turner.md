**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been cast as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), announced on 16 November 2026 by Amazon MGM Studios and the official 007 account.
- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) had been widely named as a favourite, alongside [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor.
- Denis Villeneuve is directing a script by Steven Knight, according to Time Out. Production is expected to start in 2027.
- No release date has been confirmed.

The role Callum Turner was tipped to win has gone to someone else. Amazon MGM Studios announced on 16 November that Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond, closing a casting race in which Turner, Lowden and O'Connor had all been named as leading contenders.

## What was announced

Time Out [reported the news](https://www.timeout.com/news/its-official-robert-pattinson-is-the-new-james-bond-111626) on Monday 16 November, embedding a post from the official @007 account that confirmed the casting. Hello! magazine says the [announcement](https://www.hellomagazine.com/film/515735/robert-pattinson-announced-as-new-james-bond/) came from Amazon MGM Studios and the same account, with the line "Robert Pattinson is James Bond."

Pattinson succeeds Daniel Craig. Time Out says the film, working title *Bond 26*, will be directed by Denis Villeneuve and written by Steven Knight, the creator of *Peaky Blinders*. Pattinson called the job a dream come true, and said that phrase was an understatement. Villeneuve was quoted as saying there can only be one 007.

## Where Callum Turner fitted in

Turner's name came up again and again in the months of speculation. Hello! notes that when he was asked about the role he said, "I genuinely know nothing," and declined to say more. Time Out lists him with Lowden and O'Connor as previous favourites who were beaten to the part.

Lowden was also described as a strong contender. Hello! reports that his co-star Gary Oldman said Lowden was among the names in the frame but that he had no inside information.

## His most recent film

Turner's latest release is *One Night Only*, directed by Will Gluck, which opened on 7 August 2026. According to [Wikipedia's film entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Night_Only_(2026_film)), it was made on a budget of about US$25 million and has grossed about US$25.2 million, roughly break-even before marketing costs. It holds 44 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes and 42 out of 100 on Metacritic from 36 critics.

Reviewers generally liked its leads, including Monica Barbaro and Turner, but many found the premise harder to accept than the performances. The film's reception does not appear to have been linked to the Bond decision, and no outlet has suggested it was.

## What happens next

Production on the Bond film is expected to begin in 2027, according to Time Out. Hello! says a 2028 release is anticipated, but no date has been confirmed by the studio. The outlets also disagree on whether this is the 26th or 27th Bond film, so the numbering is not settled in the coverage we read.

For Turner, the immediate picture is simple: the role is filled. His other projects and any new announcements will be covered in our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section.
