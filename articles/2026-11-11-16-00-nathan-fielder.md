**Quick summary**

- *You Can See Everything*, the A24 documentary directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, opens in Australian cinemas on November 12, according to The Sydney Morning Herald.
- The film follows Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes in the 34 days before she entered prison in May 2023.
- Amanda Seyfried's surprise return as Holmes was revealed at Telluride in September, and she told People she felt "relieved" and "excited".
- Billy Evans, Holmes's partner, has said on social media that he wrestled Fielder during filming.
- Fans are now staging Fielder lookalike contests, including one in Toronto on November 17.

[Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder)'s *You Can See Everything* arrives in Australian cinemas on November 12, after a month of hype that has already turned the documentary into a pop culture event. The three-hour film takes viewers inside the home of disgraced Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes in the weeks before her prison term began, and it has Oscar talk attached.

## What the documentary covers

According to [The Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/culture/movies/nathan-fielder-s-elizabeth-holmes-doco-could-make-oscars-history-here-s-what-to-know-before-seeing-it-20261110-p619qq.html), the film captures intimate moments with Holmes, her children, her parents and her partner before her sentence started. The paper says more than 20 million people watched the trailer in the month after it dropped.

Holmes reported to Federal Prison Camp Bryan in Texas on May 30, 2023. She was convicted on four felony counts of fraud and conspiracy and sentenced to more than 11 years, the Herald reports. Her sentence has since been cut by two years and four months, with an official release date set for February 2030.

The film also changes shape once Holmes is behind bars. Popviewers reports that the filmmakers lose direct access to her, so Fielder turns to staged reenactments of conversations the cameras never caught. That choice is where the film's most talked-about surprise comes in.

## Amanda Seyfried's secret cameo

[Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) returns as Holmes, the role that earned her an Emmy and a Golden Globe in Hulu's *The Dropout*. Mezha.net reports that her involvement was revealed at a surprise Telluride premiere in September, and that A24 has been building Oscar buzz for her. A nomination would be the first for a documentary reenactor in an acting category, the outlet adds.

Speaking to People at the New York premiere of Netflix's *Steps* on November 8, Seyfried said she was "relieved" and "excited" that the secret was out. She also told the magazine she looks for work that scares her. *Steps*, an animated musical comedy in which she voices Cinderella, premieres on Netflix on November 20.

Christopher Abbott plays Evans in the reenactments, according to Popviewers, which gives the film a second version of the central relationship.

## Billy Evans responds

The release has drawn a public reply from Evans. The Independent reports that he claimed on social media he wrestled Fielder "Borat-style" during production, and that the filmmakers shot the couple's lives over three years. He argued the final cut shaped the story selectively.

Evans also denied viewer speculation that he controls Holmes, calling her fiercely independent and describing his power of attorney as a legal tool while she is in prison, per the same report. He said he does not regret taking part. These are his own claims, and the filmmakers' account may differ.

## A film critics keep unpacking

Commentators have found plenty to chew on. An essay in The Conversation uses Aristotle's idea of imitation to ask whether Holmes was ever playing a role, drawing on Fielder's HBO series *The Rehearsal*. A rabbi writing for Kveller compares his questioning style to Talmudic reasoning. Whatever the angle, the film keeps pulling in new readers and viewers.

Fans are also turning it into a party. Toronto.com reports a Fielder lookalike contest at the Paradise Theatre on November 17, presented by Mubi and hosted by Hannah Spear and Andrew Phung. It will include a screening of the first episode of *The Curse*, the series Fielder stars in and directs alongside Emma Stone and Benny Safdie, which arrives on Mubi on November 19.

Australian audiences get their first look from November 12, and the Oscar conversation around Seyfried will likely grow as awards season builds. ## Frequently asked questions

### When does You Can See Everything open in Australia?
The Sydney Morning Herald says it is in Australian cinemas from November 12. It runs about three hours.

### Who directed You Can See Everything?
Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim directed it, and Fielder also appears in the film. It is released by A24.

Yes. She plays Elizabeth Holmes again in staged reenactments, a role first revealed at Telluride in September.
