**Quick summary**

- Robert Pattinson has been named the next James Bond, according to The Sydney Morning Herald and Mediaweek.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct the 26th Bond film, which is still untitled.
- It is the first Bond film made since Amazon MGM took over the franchise, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing and Steven Knight writing.
- Villeneuve and Pattinson will also appear together in Dune: Part Three, which opens in December.

The Denis Villeneuve Bond film now has its star: [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) has his 007. Amazon MGM has confirmed that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) will play [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) in the director's film, ending years of guessing about who would follow [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig). The franchise's official account made the announcement overnight, according to Mediaweek.

## How the casting was announced

The reveal was short: a video of Pattinson in a tuxedo holding a gun, set to the words "James Bond will return", Mediaweek reported. Pattinson, 40, becomes the seventh actor to play the spy and the first since Amazon MGM took charge of the series, according to [The Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/culture/movies/robert-pattinson-cast-as-next-james-bond-20261117-p61eod.html).

Villeneuve said in a statement that the field was strong. "We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007," he said, adding that Pattinson "has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new."

Pattinson called the role "the honour of a lifetime" and thanked Villeneuve, the producers and Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in him. Mediaweek also noted a message from the Royal Family: "Welcome to His Majesty's Secret Service, Mr Bond."

## Why the Denis Villeneuve Bond pick makes sense

The two have worked together already. Mediaweek reports that Pattinson plays the villain Scytale in Villeneuve's Dune: Part Three, due in December. That puts him on the other side of the table in one film and at the centre of the next.

Pattinson's recent run is busy. Mediaweek lists four films this year, including A24's The Drama opposite Zendaya, Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey in July and Primetime, in which he plays Dateline NBC host Chris Hansen. The Sydney Morning Herald adds that he is currently shooting The Batman Part II, and that his villainous turn in The Odyssey drew rave reviews.

His name only entered the Bond conversation recently, after word that he had screen-tested. The Sydney Morning Herald says others reportedly in the mix included [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner), Josh O'Connor, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult). Mediaweek had reported on 3 November, citing a column by Roger Friedman, that Turner had the job. That report, based on what Friedman described as "word on the street", turned out to be wrong.

## A new team behind the franchise

This is a different Bond operation. Amazon bought MGM in 2022 for US$8.5 billion (A$12.6 billion), and producers [Barbara Broccoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Broccoli) and [Michael G. Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_G._Wilson) gave up creative control in 2025. The Sydney Morning Herald says it is the first Bond film since 1962's Dr. No in which a Broccoli did not choose the lead.

That job went to producers [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) and [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman). [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), known for Peaky Blinders, is writing the script. Mike Hopkins, head of Prime Video and Amazon MGM Studios, has promised to protect the franchise's legacy.

The gap is also long. It will be five years since 2021's No Time to Die, which ended with the character's death, and the Herald calls it the longest break in Bond history. Some fans are sceptical of the Amazon-run chapter, the paper adds.

## What happens next

No title or release date has been confirmed in the sources we reviewed. Villeneuve's statement ended with "I cannot wait to begin", so production planning is expected to follow, though neither outlet gave a start date. Pattinson's schedule includes The Batman Part II, which he is still filming.
