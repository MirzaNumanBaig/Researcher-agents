**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) attended the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday night at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood.
- She wore a look from Antonio Grimaldi's spring 2025 couture collection: a metallic minidress over a floor-length champagne skirt.
- According to Yahoo News, she walked the carpet with her boyfriend, former Canadian prime minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau).
- Gold in varied textures was the theme of the night, with guests including Kerry Washington, Rachel Zoe and Jenna Dewan.

Katy Perry was among the stars who leaned into gold at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday night, arriving in a layered couture outfit that mixed a short silver-toned dress with a long champagne skirt. The event was held at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood, and according to a [Yahoo News report](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/katy-perry-jackie-tohn-olandria-053417443.html), she was accompanied by Justin Trudeau.

## What Katy Perry wore to the Baby2Baby Gala

Perry chose a piece from Antonio Grimaldi's spring 2025 couture collection, according to Yahoo News. The outfit combined two silhouettes. On top sat a strapless metallic minidress with a straight neckline and a heavily textured, silver-toned finish. Underneath was a softer, gold-coloured skirt that reached the floor.

Long panels extended from the skirt and trailed behind her on the carpet. The contrast between the hard shine of the short dress and the softer fabric beneath was the centrepiece of the look.

The accessories were kept simple. Her stylist, named in the report as Denagia, paired the outfit with a Forever Diamonds ring and earrings by Azature, and tan-gold Giuseppe Zanotti sandals to match the skirt.

Perry has been in the news in recent days for other reasons, including the [Russell Brand documentary](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-russell-brand-documentary/) coverage. Everything we have published about her is collected on our [Katy Perry page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/katy-perry/).

## Who joined her on the carpet

The Yahoo report says Perry attended with Trudeau, whom it describes as her boyfriend. The two were photographed together at the gala. Neither has been quoted in that report about the evening.

Other guests listed alongside her were actress [Kerry Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerry_Washington), designer and Baby2Baby board member [Rachel Zoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Zoe), actress [Jenna Dewan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenna_Dewan) and journalist [Taylor Rooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Rooks).

## How the other guests handled the gold theme

Gold appeared in many forms across the evening, from cascading fringe and shimmering fabric to sculpted peplums and three-dimensional florals.

- [Jackie Tohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Tohn), who stars in "Nobody Wants This", wore the Laila gown from Markarian's resort 2025 collection. It is a gold brocade design with a scooped neckline, fitted corset bodice and full skirt.
- [Olandria Carthen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olandria), known from "Love Island USA", wore a Pajtim Raci gown with overlapping gold embellishments that resemble leaves, layered over a sheer champagne base.
- Washington chose a fringed Oscar de la Renta gown from the label's fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection, with layers of gold fringe in varying lengths, plus Gianvito Rossi sandals and a bronze clutch.
- Dewan wore a strapless Monique Lhuillier gown with a structured peplum at the hips and shimmering embellishment concentrated around the bodice and waist.
- Zoe went for draping instead of beadwork: a floor-length gold gown with a high neckline, a crinkled shimmering finish and a long train.

Yahoo News also noted that Cardi B, Billy Ray Cyrus and Elizabeth Hurley were at the event, each covered in separate reports.
