**Quick summary**

- Director [Jithu Madhavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jithu_Madhavan) says the twist about Suriya's character in *Scene* was leaked news, not a fan theory.
- He told the Cineulagam YouTube channel that the leak came before the film's first look was released.
- The film's first half is built around the reveal, so the leak worried him.
- *Scene* opened on 6 November and earned about ₹175 crore worldwide in five days, according to Sacnilk.

Spoiler warning: this article discusses a major plot point from *Scene*.

Jithu Madhavan, the Malayalam filmmaker making his Tamil debut with *Scene*, has said the film's biggest surprise was out in public well before audiences sat down to watch it. Speaking on the Cineulagam YouTube channel after the 6 November release, he said the talk that Suriya plays a blind police officer was not guesswork. Someone had leaked it, he said, and it frightened him.

## What Jithu Madhavan said about the leak

According to [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/tamil-cinema/jithu-madhavan-says-it-was-scary-when-news-of-suriyas-character-being-blind-in-scene-was-leaked-even-before-his-1st-look-was-out-101794381570399.html), the host asked how he felt when fans appeared to have guessed that Suriya's DSP Inbaraj is blind. Jithu corrected the premise. "It was actually not an assumption or fan theory; the news was leaked by someone," he said.

He explained that the first half is organised around that moment, with the story clearly split into before and after the reveal. For that reason, he said, it was alarming to see something so central circulate before the makers had shown the first look. He added that the team had been careful on set, and that audiences started to question the report after the teaser came out.

[Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/tamil/news/2026/Nov/11/jithu-madhavan-i-was-scared-when-key-detail-about-scene-got-leaked-before-release) adds a detail on timing. It quotes the director as saying the unit guarded the secret during filming, but that it got out during post-production. The same outlet notes that dialogue writer Dravid Selvam had earlier told it the speculation was wrong, yet the film's release showed it to be correct.

## Why the first song surprised fans

The director also answered criticism of the first single, *Aathi Iva Yarraa*, released in October. The song centred on Nazriya's character rather than on Suriya, and some fans had hoped for a "mass" hero-introduction number, especially as Suriya was coming off *Karuppu* and *Vishwanath & Sons*, as Hindustan Times reports.

Jithu said that style is not his genre and that the negative reaction left him confused. He called the approach risky, since audiences expected one thing and received another. His plan, he said, was to introduce Naslen first, then [Nazriya Nazim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazriya_Nazim) and the rest of the cast, and to show Suriya only about a week before release.

## The story and the box office

In the film, Suriya plays Inbaraj, a celebrated DSP whose quirks irritate a colleague. When a contract killer named Easwaran, played by Naslen, leaves a trail of bodies, an old case reopens. Inbaraj's secret about his blindness comes out, and he turns to Maya, played by Nazriya, and his parents to find the killer, according to Hindustan Times.

Trade site Sacnilk puts the worldwide gross at ₹174.40 crore over five days, which Cinema Express rounds to about ₹175 crore. Both outlets say critics and audiences have responded well, so the leak does not appear to have dented the film's run.
