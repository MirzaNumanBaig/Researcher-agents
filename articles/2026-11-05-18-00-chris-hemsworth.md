**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) posted photos on Instagram of himself teaching his children boxing.
- He wrote that training with his own father is among his fondest childhood memories.
- He called fatherhood "a blessing" and said life had come full circle.
- His next big screen projects include the thriller Crime 101 and Avengers: Doomsday.

Chris Hemsworth has shared a string of Instagram photos showing him teaching his children to box, and the caption turns the lesson into a reflection on his own childhood. Reports published on 4 and 5 November say the actor now finds himself in the position his father once held.

## What Chris Hemsworth said in the post

According to [Social News XYZ](https://www.socialnews.xyz/2026/11/04/chris-hemsworth-relives-childhood-memories-with-father-as-he-enjoys-training-sessions-with-his-own-kids/), the post opens with the line "Kids club. Keep 'em moving!" Hemsworth then recalls how keen he was to learn as a boy, constantly asking his father to show him one thing or another. "Feels like yesterday," he wrote.

He went on to describe the shift in roles. "Now, all these years later, I find myself in his shoes, sharing those same moments with my own kids," the caption reads. He added that it is funny how life comes full circle, that time flies, and that fatherhood is a blessing. The post also tags a name, @evanpaterakis, at the end of the caption. The reports do not say who that account belongs to.

The agency copy carried by [Lokmat Times](https://www.lokmattimes.com/entertainment/chris-hemsworth-relives-childhood-memories-trains-kids-like-his-father-did-a001-w02/) describes the photos as showing the actor teaching his children boxing. Neither report describes the pictures in more detail, and neither says where they were taken.

Worth noting: the Lokmat Times page carries a disclaimer that the story came from a syndicated agency feed, was refined with artificial intelligence and was published without human editorial review. The quotes match across both outlets, which is why they are repeated here.

## Family and career background

Both outlets place the post in the context of the actor's family life. Social News XYZ reports that Hemsworth married Spanish model and actress [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky) in 2010. The couple have a daughter, born in May 2012, and twin sons, born in March 2014. Hemsworth often shares glimpses of family life on social media, according to Lokmat Times.

His career began on Australian television. He rose to prominence as [Kim Hyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Hyde) in the soap Home and Away before moving into Hollywood films. His breakthrough as Thor came in the 2011 film of the same name inside the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe), a run that Social News XYZ says made him one of the world's highest-paid actors.

Other credits listed in the report include [Red Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Dawn), [A Perfect Getaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Perfect_Getaway), [The Huntsman: Winter's War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Huntsman%3A_Winter's_War), Extraction and Extraction 2, Furiosa: A Mad Max Saga and [Transformers One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformers_One). For more on the Mad Max connection, see our piece on [how Chris Hemsworth wanted Mad Max in 2010 and got Furiosa](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-mad-max/).

## The work ahead

Social News XYZ lists Crime 101 as his latest film. The crime thriller is written and directed by [Bart Layton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bart_Layton) and based on a 2020 novella by [Don Winslow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Winslow). Hemsworth stars with [Mark Ruffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Ruffalo), [Barry Keoghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Keoghan) and [Halle Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Berry). The cast also includes [Monica Barbaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monica_Barbaro), [Corey Hawkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corey_Hawkins), [Jennifer Jason Leigh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Jason_Leigh) and [Nick Nolte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Nolte).

He is also preparing for Avengers: Doomsday, described as the sequel to Avengers: Endgame and the 39th film in the Marvel franchise. It is directed by Anthony and Joe Russo, according to the report, with [Robert Downey Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) as [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom). Lokmat Times says Hemsworth is expected to return as Thor.
