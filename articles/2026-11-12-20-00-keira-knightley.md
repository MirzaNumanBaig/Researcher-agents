**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) told Heart she has not watched [Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually) since its 2003 premiere, when she was 18.
- She says she can tell when fans are quoting the film to her, but the lines "mean nothing" to her.
- The second season of [Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves) began streaming on Netflix on 5 November, with all six episodes available.
- Knightley describes the show's violence as "almost like a video game" and calls the series a "romp", according to The i Paper and The Mirror.

Keira Knightley has a confession for anyone who greets her with a line from a Christmas classic: she has no idea what you are talking about. In an interview with Heart published on 9 November, the actress said she has not seen Love Actually since its premiere, and she made the admission while promoting the new run of Netflix's Black Doves.

## What Keira Knightley said about Love Actually

Knightley, 41, appeared alongside co-star Ambika Mod for the Heart conversation with Lindsey Russell, according to [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/keira-knightley-hasnt-watched-love-173058618.html). She said people give her a certain look and then say something, and she knows from experience that they are quoting the film. "It means nothing to me," she said.

She estimates she worked on the movie for about a week, and she was 17 during filming. She still likes the idea of the film's long life. "I quite like that it has this life, such a big life out there," she said, as quoted by [Parade via Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/keira-knightley-reveals-she-doesnt-152441133.html).

For context, the same report says the film took more than $252 million at the box office against a $45 million budget and earned a Golden Globe nomination for Best Motion Picture. Knightley played Juliet, the bride whose wedding opens one of the film's linked stories. She is not new to being wary of her own work: in 2021 she told Entertainment Weekly that watching films you are in is "just very odd".

## A shared take with Daniel Radcliffe

Knightley is not the only star who draws a blank. [Daniel Radcliffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Radcliffe), 37, told People that unless a fan picks an obvious line, a deep-cut [Harry Potter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Potter) quote will go straight over his head, according to [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/daniel-radcliffe-keira-knightley-same-take-fans-quoting-movies-harry-potter-love-actually). The one he would recognise, he said, is "You're a wizard, Harry".

CinemaBlend notes that Radcliffe now stars in the comedy series [The Fall and Rise of Reggie Dinkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Fall_and_Rise_of_Reggie_Dinkins) and voices a lead in the Netflix animated film Steps, due on 20 November. Both actors have lived with fame since childhood, which may explain why neither keeps their old dialogue on call.

## Keira Knightley Black Doves season two

The reason Knightley is doing the rounds is the return of her spy thriller. She plays Helen Webb, who passes secrets to a covert organisation while married to a politician, Wallace, played by Andrew Buchan. In season two his promotion to the prime minister's office moves the family into Downing Street and raises the risk of her double life, per Yahoo. Ben Whishaw returns as hitman Sam, Sarah Lancashire is back as handler Reed, and Mod joins as rogue agent Laila.

The i Paper describes the show as a gleeful mix of gloss and gunfire that still carries real feeling. Knightley told the paper the violence is "almost like a video game" until creator Joe Barton brings one death into focus, which she called the joy of the series. She also noted that Barton found the second season harder to write than expected, rewriting the first episode several times.

The Mirror reports that she called the series "utterly ridiculous" yet layered with humour and depth, and that she loved the stunts. Speaking on the BBC World Service, she said kicking in a door was the "best day at work ever". The same piece notes she is also the face of a new Waitrose Christmas ad, which we covered in [Keira Knightley Returns in Waitrose Christmas Ad Sequel](https://moduscarry.com/keira-knightley-waitrose-ad/).

## Bond swimwear and other asides

Asked about the future of James Bond, Knightley told the BBC she would like the next actor to bring back Sean Connery's tiny baby-blue velour swimsuit from Goldfinger. Whishaw, who played Q in three Bond films, has said the franchise needs to be reinvented, per The Mirror.
