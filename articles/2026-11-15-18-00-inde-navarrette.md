**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) says she would like to make a father-daughter comedy with [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon).
- She made the comment in an interview with [Entertainment Tonight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Tonight), when asked about a romantic comedy co-star.
- She wants Damon to play her father, citing his reputation as a devoted dad.
- No project has been announced, and there is no word on whether Damon has heard the idea.

Asked who she would pick as a romantic comedy co-star, Inde Navarrette turned the question around and named Matt Damon as the father she would like to play opposite. According to [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/inde-navarette-matt-damon-next-comedy-movie/), the *Obsession* breakout made the remark in a short interview with Entertainment Tonight that was reported on 15 November 2026.

## What Inde Navarrette actually said

The question was about romance. Her answer was not. "Instead of a romantic comedy, I want like father-daughter comedy," she told Entertainment Tonight, as quoted by FandomWire. "I would love to be a Matt Damon's daughter. He seems like a phenomenal father."

The reason she gave is personal rather than professional. She was not naming Damon for his box office pull or a particular film. She was naming him because of how he comes across as a parent.

## Why Matt Damon's family life fits the pitch

FandomWire points to Damon's public record to explain the choice. He has four daughters, and the outlet notes that his family attended the New York premiere of *The Instigators*, an event Damon said they do not usually go to.

Damon has also been candid about the cost of his job. While promoting *The Odyssey*, he told GQ that he thinks often about being present as his children get older, and that it is hard for him because the film business is "uncertain" and "pretty ruthless", according to the quote FandomWire reproduced. FandomWire adds that he has long followed a two-week rule for filming away from home.

There is humour in the picture too. FandomWire reports that Damon once told [Seth Meyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Meyers) that his teenage daughter likes to tease him and avoids his films when she thinks they might be good. That kind of gentle ribbing is the sort of material a father-daughter comedy could be built on.

## A change of tone for Navarrette

The wish would be a sharp turn from her recent work. FandomWire describes *Obsession* as a horror hit, although [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features) submitted it in the comedy and musical categories at the Golden Globes. That means comedy is already part of the film's awards story.

Navarrette has also said she admires Damon. Earlier this year she told Complex, as cited by FandomWire, that he has been in the industry "for a long time in such an elegant way". The same report says she wants to show that *Obsession* was not "lightning in a bottle", and a warm family comedy would show a different side of her.

On Damon's side, FandomWire notes he has comedy credits, from *Stuck on You* to the thriller-comedy *The Instigators*.

## What happens next

Nothing is confirmed. FandomWire says plainly that this is only a wish: no project has been announced, and there is no word on whether Damon has heard the pitch. Neither actor's representatives have commented in the sources reviewed. Any real development would need to come from the actors, a studio or a trade announcement.
