**Quick summary**

- Cate Blanchett will host the New York premiere of five short films backed by the Displacement Film Fund on 11 November 2026.
- The screenings are at Film Forum in Manhattan, at 6 pm and 7 pm, according to AmNews Curtain Raiser.
- The directors come from Ukraine, Somalia-Austria, Syria, Iran and Afghanistan; Maryna Er Gorbach and Mo Harawe will attend in person.
- The films premiered at the 2026 International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) and were later invited to festivals in London, Sao Paulo and Tokyo.

[Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) will host the New York premiere of the first films funded by the Displacement Film Fund, a body she co-founded and leads, at Film Forum on 11 November. Five shorts will screen, according to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Cate-Blanchett-to-Host-NY-Premiere-of-Displacement-Film-Fund-Shorts-20261020), in partnership with IFFR's Hubert Bals Fund. So what is the fund, and what will audiences see?

## What is the Displacement Film Fund?

The Displacement Film Fund (DFF) backs displaced filmmakers, as well as filmmakers with a proven record of telling authentic stories about displaced people. Its stated aim is to bring those stories into the mainstream and show how varied life after displacement can be.

The idea was first raised at the 2023 Global Refugee Forum, hosted by UNHCR, the UN Refugee Agency. Blanchett built the fund with film industry figures who have a personal connection to forced displacement or a strong interest in it, and it launched at IFFR in 2025, according to BroadwayWorld.

The scale of the problem is the backdrop. One in every 70 people on earth is forcibly displaced by conflict, war or persecution, per the same report. The Hubert Bals Fund is the management partner, and UNHCR is a strategic partner.

## Which films are screening?

The slate was directed by [Maryna Er Gorbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryna_Er_Gorbach), [Mo Harawe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Harawe), Hasan Kattan, [Mohammad Rasoulof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Rasoulof) and [Shahrbanoo Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrbanoo_Sadat). The sources describe four of the five in detail.

- Rotation (12 minutes, Gorbach): a young Ukrainian woman who has moved from civilian life into military service goes through a therapeutic hypnosis ritual. Gorbach won the World Cinema Dramatic Directing Award at Sundance in 2022 for Klondike.
- Super Afghan Gym (14 minutes, Sadat): women in a Kabul gym use the one hour a day reserved for them to train and talk behind closed doors. Sadat's latest feature, No Good Men, opened the 2026 Berlin Film Festival.
- Allies in Exile (40 minutes, Kattan): two Syrian filmmakers, Kattan and Fadi Al-Halabi, who have worked together for 14 years, film their wait inside a UK asylum hotel. Kattan was cinematographer on the Oscar-winning The White Helmets.
- Whispers of a Burning Scent (27 minutes, Harawe): a wedding musician's private life is exposed to public scrutiny on the day of a court hearing.

The fifth film is Rasoulof's, and the published coverage does not describe it.

## What has Blanchett said?

Blanchett framed the programme as a response to the moment. "I am privileged to share the inaugural life-affirming films supported by the Displacement Film Fund with Film Forum audiences in New York," she said. "In bewildering times like these where forces seek to separate us from our very humanity, visionary filmmakers matter more than ever."

IFFR managing director [Clare Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_Stewart) and Hubert Bals Fund head Tamara Tatishvili said in the same release that the fund had accompanied the filmmakers "from idea to completion".

The backers are listed too. The Amahoro Coalition, MASTERMIND, the Tamer Family Foundation, UNIQLO and Droom en Daad were founding partners, and the SP Lohia Foundation joined as main partner in 2026.

## What happens next

The screenings take place at Film Forum, 209 West Houston Street, on 11 November, with Gorbach and Harawe attending each programme. AmNews Curtain Raiser lists the sessions at 6 pm and 7 pm, New York time.
