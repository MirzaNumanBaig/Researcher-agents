**Quick summary**

- [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), a producer on the Darkwing Duck reboot, says a Scrooge McDuck voice actor has just been cast.
- He called it "the most exciting casting I could ever imagine" but did not name the actor.
- [Mark Hamill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Hamill) is confirmed as Drake Mallard, and Rogen voices Launchpad McQuack.
- Disney has not set a release date, according to Comic Book Resources.

The [Darkwing Duck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkwing_Duck) reboot has landed a new voice for Scrooge McDuck, and Seth Rogen says it is a big one. Speaking to Variety, as reported by [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/darkwing-duck-reboot-update-seth-rogen/), Rogen teased the casting without revealing who it is, which suggests a formal announcement is close.

## What Rogen said about the Scrooge McDuck casting

Rogen is a producer on the series, and he told Variety the cast is "really great". According to Comic Book Resources, he said the team had just cast a Scrooge McDuck who "might be the most exciting casting I could ever imagine", and added that he would reveal the name afterwards.

No name has been confirmed. Comic Book Resources reads the comment as a sign that Disney will make the announcement before long, but that is the outlet's interpretation, not a statement from the studio.

Rogen also spoke about how long the project has taken. He said the team has worked on Darkwing Duck "for years and years" and got caught up in corporate restructurings along the way. He said he has been watching finished episodes and finds them "amazing", praising the animation as beautiful and inventive.

## The confirmed Darkwing Duck cast

Mark Hamill voices the hero, Drake Mallard, the masked crime-fighter of the title. Rogen is not only producing: he also voices Launchpad McQuack.

Comic Book Resources lists these other confirmed cast members:

- [Lizzy Caplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lizzy_Caplan)
- Lilimar
- [Dan Stevens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Stevens)
- [Tiffany Shepis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tiffany_Shepis)
- [Kathryn Newton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Newton)
- [Teo Briones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teo_Briones)
- Tyler Dean Flores
- [Katie Rich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katie_Rich)
- [Nat Faxon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nat_Faxon)

The outlet did not say which role each of these performers will voice, apart from Hamill and Rogen.

## Why Rogen cares about Darkwing Duck

Rogen has said he grew up a fan of the original cartoon, which first aired in 1991 and 1992. He told Variety that he loved how meta it was, with a habit of breaking the fourth wall and commenting on other shows from the same era that he was watching at the same age.

He also tied it to his taste in comics. Rogen said he recently reread Calvin and Hobbes and realised it shaped a lot of his personality. Comic Book Resources takes his comments to suggest the new series will lean on that self-aware humour, though the outlet does not cite any confirmed plot details.

## What happens next

Disney has not announced a release date for the new series. Rogen's remark that he will "tell you after" points to a casting reveal in the near future, but no date or format for that announcement has been given. Until then, the Scrooge McDuck voice remains unconfirmed.
