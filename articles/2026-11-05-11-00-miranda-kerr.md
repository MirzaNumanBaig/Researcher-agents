**Quick summary**

- [Miranda Kerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Kerr) did not walk in this year's Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles, which marked 20 years since her first show.
- She said she was invited back but felt it was right "to cherish this chapter as it was", according to The Nightly.
- Kerr was an angel from 2007 and left the brand in 2013 to focus on family and her skincare label, KORA Organics.
- Other returning names walked, and Kylie Jenner made a surprise debut in a Fantasy Bra.

Miranda Kerr has explained why she stayed off the runway at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in October 2026. The Australian model, who was the brand's first Australian angel, said she was asked back for the show's 20th anniversary year but chose to leave her time with the label as it was, [The Nightly reported](https://thenightly.com.au/culture/pop-culture/miranda-kerr-snubs-victorias-secret-return-20-years-after-debut-kylie-jenner-steals-show-in-fantasy-bra-c-23043842).

## What Miranda Kerr said about the Victoria's Secret show

Kerr shared her reasoning on social media after the Los Angeles show. She noted it had been 20 years since her first Victoria's Secret show and wrote that she "ultimately felt in my heart it was right to cherish this chapter as it was".

She also posted a short Instagram Story in which she pretended to walk a runway, captioned to say she was on her way to put her kids to bed. The tone was light, and she did not suggest any dispute with the brand.

Kerr is 43, according to The Nightly. She has four sons: one with actor Orlando Bloom and three with Snapchat co-founder Evan Spiegel.

## Her history with the brand

Kerr joined Victoria's Secret as an angel in 2007 and left in 2013, the outlet reported, to focus on her family and her skincare brand, KORA Organics. That is a six-year run, and this year's anniversary falls 13 years after she stepped away.

Her absence has been a topic before. In 2013 she told Fashionista she could not attend that year's show because she had to be in Asia that week, [according to the NZ Herald](https://nzherald.co.nz/lifestyle/miranda-kerr-reveals-reason-for-victorias-secret-no-show/BJIMGSS7AY4A3YE45TSMBVWS6M). That report also said her only earlier absence was in 2010, when she was pregnant with her first son, Flynn. Reports at the time that she had been dropped were denied by the brand's then president, Ed Razek.

For more on how she has talked about the label, see our earlier piece on [why Miranda Kerr turned down Victoria's Secret](https://moduscarry.com/miranda-kerr-victorias-secret/).

## Who did walk, and the Fantasy Bra

The Nightly said returning models Rosie Huntington-Whiteley and Alessandra Ambrosio both walked this year. Performers and guests included Tate McRae, Gigi and Bella Hadid and Barbara Palvin, with Nicole Kidman in the front row.

The headline moment went to Kylie Jenner, who made a surprise debut in a Fantasy Bra by Jacob & Co. The piece is valued at $71.7 million and carries 4,410 natural diamonds around a 75.54-carat fancy vivid yellow diamond, which alone is worth about $32 million, according to the report. It was the first Fantasy Bra shown since 2018, the last before the show's five-year hiatus. Timothée Chalamet and Kris Jenner cheered Jenner on, while Kendall Jenner, who modelled for the brand in 2015, 2016 and 2018, did not appear.

## What happens next

Kerr has given no sign of a return. Her own words point to a closed chapter, and her focus remains on family and KORA Organics. If that changes, it will most likely come from her own channels first.
