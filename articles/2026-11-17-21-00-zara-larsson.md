**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) is nominated for Best Dance Pop Recording at the 2027 Grammy Awards for "Blue Moon".
- She is up against Madonna, RAYE, Slayyyter and Tinashe, according to the Recording Academy list reported by Metro Mode.
- The ceremony is on Sunday, 7 February at the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.
- Separately, KAYOU is giving away 10 Larsson-signed Winx Club trading cards in a fan contest.

Zara Larsson has her first Grammy nomination to talk about this week. When nominations for the 2027 ceremony were announced on Monday, the Swedish pop star appeared in Best Dance Pop Recording for "Blue Moon", according to [Metro Mode](https://metromode.se/en/noje/zara-larsson-grammy-nominerad-tavlar-med-blue-moon-mot-madonna/), which cited the Recording Academy's full list.

## The Zara Larsson Grammy nomination, and who she is up against

"Blue Moon" is a drum 'n' bass-driven track from the album Midnight Sun. Larsson has described it as a sister song to the title track, and it is about a love that never cools, Metro Mode reports.

The category is crowded. The other nominees are Madonna with "Danceteria", RAYE with "Life Boat.", Slayyyter with "DANCE..." and Tinashe with "Too Easy". The award goes to the artist along with the track's producers and mixers. For "Blue Moon" that means producers Margo XS, MNEK and Zhone, with a mix by [Tom Norris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Norris_(musician)).

There is a small twist in the numbers. Norris also mixed Tinashe's "Too Easy", so he is on two of the five nominated records in the same category.

## A big year behind the nomination

The nod follows a busy stretch for Larsson. According to Metro Mode, the Midnight Sun tour has taken her around the world, and a remix album, Midnight Sun: Girls Trip, arrived in spring with guests including Shakira and Robyn. A related song, "Eurosummer - Girls Trip", on which she sings with Shakira, is also listed among the works behind a Songwriter of the Year nominee.

She has another big date lined up. Larsson is confirmed for Coachella next year, a few months after the Grammys and in the same Los Angeles region. We covered her other live plans in our report on the [Abu Dhabi Grand Prix concert](https://moduscarry.com/zara-larsson-abu-dhabi-concert-tickets/).

## Swedish names across the Grammy lists

Larsson is not the only Swedish name to be recognised. Max Martin and [Ilya Salmanzadeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Salmanzadeh) are nominated in Song of the Year as songwriters, alongside [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande), for her "hate that i made you love me". Metro Mode notes that Larsson holds Martin in high regard.

Across the wider field, RAYE leads with eleven nominations, followed by ROSALÍA with nine. [Ella Langley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ella_Langley), [Miranda Lambert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Lambert), [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo) and [Phoebe Bridgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Bridgers) have seven each, while [Bruno Mars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Mars), [Leon Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Thomas) and [Olivia Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Dean) have six. Awards will be handed out in 100 categories across twelve fields.

## Winx Club cards signed by Larsson

In lighter news, trading card maker KAYOU has released a [Winx Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winx_Club) card line in the United States, and Larsson features in it, The Geekiary reports. The Magic Edition Series 1 has 123 cards across 14 rarities. Packs are sold at Best Buy for US$3.99, and five-pack boxes cost US$19.99. GameStop and Target are also due to stock the range.

The signed card is inspired by the Winx Club version of "Midnight Sun", showing a Winx-style Larsson with Bloom and her friends from the Netflix series. KAYOU is giving away 10 signed cards through a fan-art contest on social media using #WinxifyWithKayou. It closes on 30 December 2026. A second contest, open to anyone who pulls the ultra-rare anniversary card, runs until 30 January 2027, but that prize is digital artwork rather than a signed card.

## What happens next

The Grammy Awards air on Sunday, 7 February at the Crypto.com Arena, on ABC in the United States and on Disney+ and Hulu. The broadcast starts at 7pm US Eastern Time. Going by that start time, Australian viewers would be watching on Monday morning, 8 February, though local broadcast details have not been confirmed in the sources.
