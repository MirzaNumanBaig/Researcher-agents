**Quick summary**

- Netflix released the first poster and stills for heist thriller The Take on 9 November 2026.
- Robert Pattinson and Denzel Washington lead, with Fernando Meirelles directing.
- The film arrives on Netflix on 15 January 2027, with a limited cinema run reported.
- Entertainment Weekly published the first images exclusively; no trailer was out at the time.

Netflix has shown the first images from *The Take*, an unconventional bank-robbery thriller starring [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) and [Denzel Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denzel_Washington). The poster and stills appeared on 9 November, first through [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly), and the film is set to land on Netflix on 15 January 2027, according to [Kinocheck](https://kinocheck.com/news/vphdd7/robert-pattinson-and-denzel-washington-team-up-for-netflix-heist-thriller-the-take-first-look-revealed).

## What The Take is about

The story follows Noah, played by Pattinson, a former Army captain who is struggling to settle into civilian life. A master thief named Christian Carter, played by Washington, recruits him to rob the richest bank in Manhattan.

There is a complication. Noah's girlfriend, Rose, works at that bank as a teller, and she is played by Daisy Edgar-Jones. Noah has to decide whom he can trust if he wants to get through the job alive, as [Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/first-look-at-netflixs-the-take-starring-denzel-washington-robert-pattinson-and-daisy-edgar-jones/) outlines the premise.

Netflix describes it as an unconventional heist movie about a bank guard, a teller and a master thief caught in a deadly game of cons and double crosses, per Kinocheck. Director Fernando Meirelles has called it a psychological drama with a love story at its centre rather than only a heist film.

## Cast and filmmakers

Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk fill out the supporting cast. Meirelles, the Brazilian filmmaker behind *City of God* and *The Constant Gardener*, directs from a script by Simon Kinberg, who also wrote *X-Men: Days of Future Past*, Kinocheck reports.

According to the same report, Meirelles prepared by watching *Heat*, *Point Break*, *Dog Day Afternoon* and *The Killing*. That list points to a film more interested in tension and character than in gadgets.

## What the stars said

Speaking to Entertainment Weekly, as quoted by Kinocheck, Washington said that at this point in his life he is focused on doing the best work with the best filmmakers. Pattinson said that getting to make a New York heist film with Washington and Edgar-Jones left little else to say, and that it made joining an easy decision.

Pattinson has been busy. Kinocheck lists Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* among his recent work and notes he is in *Dune: Part Three*. We covered the earlier Netflix announcement in [Robert Pattinson's Netflix Thriller The Take Fuels Bond Talk](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-the-take-netflix/).

## What happens next

The next milestone is a trailer, which had not been released when the first look appeared. The film's Wikipedia entry lists a 15 January 2027 release, matching the Netflix date. Australian release details and cinema screenings have not been confirmed in the sources we read, so check local listings closer to the date.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does The Take come out?
Netflix has set 15 January 2027, according to the reports on the first look. A limited cinema release has also been reported.

### Who stars in The Take?
Robert Pattinson plays Noah and Denzel Washington plays Christian Carter. Daisy Edgar-Jones plays Rose, with Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk in support.

### Who directs The Take?
Fernando Meirelles directs, from a screenplay by Simon Kinberg.
