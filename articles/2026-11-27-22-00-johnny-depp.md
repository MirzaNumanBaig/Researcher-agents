**Quick summary**

- A forthcoming biography, "The Many Faces of Johnny Depp" by Mark Seal, describes the actor's behaviour on the set of *21 Jump Street*.
- The book claims he tried to get out of the show's second season through a series of protests, according to Page Six.
- The excerpts were reported by The Times of India, which cites Page Six; these are claims from the book, not confirmed facts.
- Depp, now 63, is currently promoting his new film *Ebenezer*.

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) has a new chapter of his early career coming under the spotlight. Excerpts from an upcoming biography describe how the star behaved on the set of the late-1980s police drama *21 Jump Street*, according to Page Six as relayed by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/johnny-depps-on-set-antics-during-21-jump-street-exposed-in-new-biography/articleshow/136207943.cms) on 27 November.

## What the Johnny Depp 21 Jump Street book claims

Author Mark Seal's book, "The Many Faces of Johnny Depp", lists several incidents. Seal writes that Depp turned up to film dressed as Gandhi, and that he set fire to his underwear on set to protest that his trailer had not been cleaned. He also reportedly wore a Band-Aid across his face to hide his most recognisable feature from the camera.

The book also alleges that Depp was arrested and charged with assault after a confrontation with a security guard at a party in a Vancouver hotel. According to the report, he kept his role on the show despite the incident. The Times of India only repeats this account from the book, and the outlet gives no further detail on the case.

## Why he wanted out

*21 Jump Street* ran from 1987 to 1991 and followed young-looking undercover officers working in high schools. It made Depp a teen heartthrob and one of the early faces of the Fox network.

His pay rose with that fame. The report says his salary went from US$6,000 an episode in the first season to US$12,000 in the second, and then to US$45,000 in the third. That is a more than sevenfold increase in two seasons.

Depp, however, was reportedly uneasy about the show's premise. In a 1990 interview with Sky Magazine he said he did not agree, morally, with the idea of police officers in high schools, calling it "borderline fascism".

## A co-star pushes back

Seal writes that only one co-star directly confronted him. Actress Holly Robinson, who was delighted to be on the show, is quoted as finding it hard to work every day with someone who was so dismissive of it. She reportedly went to his trailer to say so, and the book says Depp trashed the trailer soon afterwards.

Robinson also gave a kinder view. She told Seal that Depp has "a really great heart" but was frustrated, because he hated the thought of ending up "on a lunch box or some teenage girl's wall."

## Where Depp is now

The stories land as Depp returns to the cinema in *Ebenezer*, a version of the Dickens tale directed by Ti West that opened on 12 November. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebenezer_(2026_film)), it had taken about US$79.6 million against a US$38 million budget at the time of writing. Reviews have been mixed, with a Rotten Tomatoes score of 55%.
