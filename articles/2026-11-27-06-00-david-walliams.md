**Quick summary**

- Wrexham Council and Gwella, which runs Flintshire's libraries, say they have no plans to withdraw David Walliams' children's books.
- The same applies to his 2012 autobiography, *Camp David*, according to Herald.Wales.
- In Flintshire school libraries, the decision belongs to each school.
- Walliams denies all allegations of sexual misconduct and abuse of power. No charges have been brought.

Two north Wales library services have said they will keep [David Walliams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Walliams)' books on their shelves for now, days after allegations against the author and comedian were aired in an ITV documentary and published by the Guardian. [Herald.Wales reports](https://herald.wales/north-wales/wrexham-north-wales/david-walliams-books-to-remain-on-council-library-shelves-for-now/) that Wrexham Council's library service and Gwella, the body that operates Flintshire's libraries, have no plans to remove his titles, either temporarily or permanently.

## What the councils have said

According to Herald.Wales, Walliams denies all allegations of sexual misconduct and abuses of power. No charges have been brought against him, and he has denied breaking the law.

Kate Leonard, Head of Libraries and Compliance at Gwella, said the starting point for libraries is that books should be available to readers unless their content is illegal. She added that collections are refreshed all the time in response to what people want to borrow.

A Wrexham Council spokesperson took a similar line. Collections are reviewed regularly, the spokesperson said, taking in reader demand, the condition of stock and changing tastes.

## How many copies are on the shelves

The numbers give a sense of how widely the books are held. Gwella has 375 physical copies of Walliams' children's titles, including several Welsh translations, along with three copies of his autobiography. It also holds one copy of *David Walliams: A Comic Genius*, a biography for teenagers that he did not write.

Wrexham lists 376 copies of his books in English or Welsh, plus three copies of the autobiography, according to the same report.

Both services pointed to falling demand. Leonard said Gwella understands that borrowing of Walliams' books has dropped significantly in recent years, and that it expects the number of copies in circulation to shrink naturally as newer titles replace them. Wrexham Council made the same point about its own stock.

## Schools make their own decisions

School libraries are a separate matter. Flintshire County Council's Chief Officer for Education and Youth, Claire Homard, said whether to keep his books is a decision for individual schools. Herald.Wales says Wrexham Council's education department was approached for comment and declined.

## A career built on children's books

Walliams came to wider attention through the BBC sketch show *Little Britain* before moving into children's fiction for readers aged 5 to 12. Herald.Wales says titles such as *Gangsta Granny*, *Billionaire Boy* and *Mega Monster* have sold more than 50 million copies between them.

The report also says he has recently been dropped by his long-time publisher, HarperCollins. Gwella's comments about the future of its stock refer to him being without a publisher.

## What happens next

The councils describe their position as current rather than permanent. Gwella and Wrexham both expect stock to fall gradually through normal replacement. Other library services and school bodies have not been covered in the source report, so it is not clear whether any will take a different approach.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will David Walliams' books be removed from Wrexham and Flintshire libraries?

Not at present. Both Wrexham Council's library service and Gwella say they have no plans to withdraw them, though copies may decline over time as stock is refreshed.

### Who decides whether the books stay in Flintshire schools?

Individual schools. Flintshire's Chief Officer for Education and Youth said the choice is a matter for each school.

### Has David Walliams been charged with anything?

No. According to Herald.Wales, no charges have been brought, and he denies all allegations of sexual misconduct and abuses of power.
