**Quick summary**

- Marvel Studios has unveiled a new logo called the "Worthy Star" that pairs Thor with Steve Rogers for *Avengers: Doomsday*.
- The emblem is a star split by a lightning bolt, one half blue and one half red, according to The Direct.
- It first appeared on a Hot Toys figure of Steve Rogers and was named on a Box Lunch T-shirt.
- *Avengers: Doomsday* is due on December 18.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s Thor now has a symbol to share with an old friend. Marvel Studios has revealed the "Worthy Star", a logo that joins Thor and Steve Rogers, both able to lift Mjolnir, as *Avengers: Doomsday* heads towards its December 18 release, [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/marvel-studios-steve-rogers-thor-worthy-star-logo-avengers-doomsday) reports.

## Where the Worthy Star appeared

The logo surfaced on merchandise rather than in a trailer. According to The Direct, a new Hot Toys Special Edition figure of [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor))' Steve Rogers includes a life-sized metal ring bearing the design. The name "Worthy Star" was then confirmed on Box Lunch merchandise, a T-shirt with the emblem at its centre.

The design is a star divided by a lightning bolt, one half blue and the other red. On the shirt, Thor sits in the blue half holding Stormbreaker, while Steve stands in the red half with a lightning-charged Mjolnir. The colours nod to Captain America, even though Steve is no longer carrying that title.

## Why a star for a man who gave up the shield

The star is a curious choice, because The Direct notes Steve will not wear any star-shaped costume in the film. He passed the Captain America mantle to Sam Wilson. This is the second logo Marvel has made for his return. The first, which described him as "Mjolnir Worthy", set the hammer against a shield-like backdrop. Thor has his own logo too, with Mjolnir at its centre.

Steve's return has been teased for some time. The film's first teaser showed a bearded Steve arriving at a farmhouse, wearing a wedding band and holding a newborn, before closing on the line "Steve Rogers will return", The Direct says. That followed more than a year of denials and speculation. The first full trailer then showed Thor holding out Mjolnir and Steve catching it, to Thor's evident surprise.

## The worthiness thread in the Marvel films

Mjolnir's enchantment has been a recurring story device. Thor lost his powers and was sent to Earth by Odin, and had to earn the hammer back through humility and sacrifice. Steve gave the hammer a small nudge at the party in *Avengers: Age of Ultron*, and in *Endgame* he lifted it against Thanos, according to The Direct's recap.

The outlet argues that the marketing points to Thor and Steve fighting as a pair. That is the publication's reading of the promotional material, not a confirmed plot detail. It adds that [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.) plays [Doctor Doom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Doom), who threatens the Marvel multiverse and, in the trailer, stops a Stormbreaker strike from Thor with two fingers. The Direct also cites unconfirmed reporting that Steve's choice to stay in the past caused an incursion that may have affected Doom's life. Treat that as a rumour until Marvel says otherwise.

## What happens next

*Avengers: Doomsday* opens on December 18, with the Avengers, the X-Men and the Fantastic Four all assembling, per The Direct.
