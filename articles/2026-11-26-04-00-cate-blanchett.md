**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) leads the cast of voices on this year's Glow Wild light trail at Kew Wakehurst in West Sussex, according to Secret London.
- The trail switches on this Friday, 27 November, with the theme "In Search of Nature's Gifts".
- Thirteen actors recorded narration, including Olivia Colman, Daniel Craig and Jude Law.
- All the words were written by Wakehurst's Glow Wild volunteers and community groups.

Cate Blanchett is the lead voice of this year's Glow Wild, the lantern trail at Kew Wakehurst that opens on Friday, 27 November. Secret London reports that the Oscar winner, who is also a Kew Wakehurst ambassador, introduces visitors to a walk themed around nature's gifts and helps tell the story of the trail.

## Who is narrating Cate Blanchett's Glow Wild trail

The woodland soundscape was specially recorded for the event. Secret London lists 13 voices: Blanchett, Orlando Bloom, [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman), Richard E. Grant, [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), [Jude Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jude_Law), Nick Frost, Lesley Manville, Carey Mulligan, Stanley Tucci, Dame Harriet Walter, Priyanga Burford and Naomi Watts.

The scripts did not come from a writers' room. According to the report, Wakehurst's Glow Wild volunteers and community groups wrote every word. Grant, for example, reflects on nature whispering secrets through rustling leaves, while Colman's lines credit the silver moon as a light that helps you find your way in the dark.

## Blanchett's hands-on role

Blanchett's involvement goes beyond the recording booth. Earlier this year she joined a lantern-making workshop with young people from Peacehaven Community School, Secret London says. The session fed into an installation of more than 200 handmade lanterns made by volunteers, each one a personal reflection on nature's gifts.

In a quote carried by the outlet, she praised "the artistry, care and energy" behind the event and called it a chance to reconnect with the natural world.

## What else is on the trail

The 2026 theme takes visitors from pole to pole, inspired by the work of Kew's scientists in remote parts of the world. Secret London highlights several new pieces:

- The tallest lantern the trail has had so far: a five-metre iceberg by artist Kerith Ogden, part of a polar landscape with 68 penguins and 1,000 fish.
- An Alpine flower carpet by Michelle Dufaur made from 2,500 handmade petals.
- A light commission from The Colour Project recreating the Aurora Australis, the southern lights.
- Pyro Studio's Moonbeam Gatherer, a half-hare, half-human sculpture that floats on the lake.

There is also a Christmas Corner with winter warmers and seasonal street food beneath the giant redwood.

## What happens next

Glow Wild opens on 27 November. Kew Wakehurst is a botanic garden in West Sussex. Secret London says it is about an hour by train from London Victoria, followed by a bus or taxi, or reachable by car via the M25. The source does not give ticket prices or closing dates, so check Kew's official booking page before planning a visit.
