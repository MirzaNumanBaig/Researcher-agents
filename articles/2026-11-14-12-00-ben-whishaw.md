**Quick summary**

- [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw) returns as Sam, a former hitman, in season 2 of the Netflix spy thriller *Black Doves*.
- Season 2 premiered on Netflix on 5 November 2026, according to Netflix's own Tudum site.
- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) is back as Helen, and [Neve Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neve_Campbell) is among seven new cast members.
- The story picks up with Helen's husband preparing to become prime minister, which makes her double life riskier.

Ben Whishaw is back in *Black Doves*. The second season of the Netflix thriller arrived on 5 November 2026, and IMDb has published a cast-and-characters guide that puts Whishaw, Keira Knightley and newcomer Neve Campbell at the front of it. Here is who plays whom, based on Netflix's listing and cast coverage from What's on Netflix.

## Ben Whishaw's role as Sam

Whishaw plays Sam, whom Netflix describes as Helen's best friend and a former hitman. What's on Netflix gives the character's full name as Sam Young. When the new season opens, Sam has fallen on hard times. According to the series logline reported by What's on Netflix, he is drinking alone in Soho and taking low-paid hits.

That is a long way from where the character began. Season 1's success is one reason Netflix ordered more, and our earlier report on [Ben Whishaw's Black Doves hitting No. 1 on Netflix worldwide](https://moduscarry.com/ben-whishaw-black-doves-netflix-number-one/) covers how the show performed. You can find everything else we have published about the actor on the [Ben Whishaw tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ben-whishaw/).

## The returning cast

Keira Knightley returns as Helen Webb, a spy who passes her country's secrets to the Black Doves, according to Netflix. Sarah Lancashire is back as Mrs Reed, Helen's handler. Andrew Buchan plays Wallace, Helen's husband, who is preparing to become prime minister.

The rest of the returning group, as listed by Netflix, is Kathryn Hunter as Lenny Lines, Ella Lily Hyland as Williams, Gabrielle Creevy as Eleanor, Agnes O'Casey as Dani and Molly Chesworth as Marie.

## Who is new in season 2

Netflix lists seven additions:

- Neve Campbell as Cecile Mason. No character description has been released.
- Ambika Mod as Laila, described as an acerbic, anarchic Black Doves agent sent to help Helen on a mission.
- Babou Ceesay as Mr Conteh, a Black Doves executive with suspicious motives.
- Sam Riley as Patrick, an emissary from a mysterious organisation who offers Sam a lifeline.
- Sylvia Hoeks as Katia Chernov and Goran Kostic as Alexi Chernov.
- Samuel Barnett as Jerry.

Campbell is best known to many viewers for the *Scream* films, so her part, kept deliberately vague, is the one to watch.

## What the new season is about

Per Netflix's synopsis, Helen keeps betraying state secrets to the Black Doves while her husband's political career rises. That makes her position more precarious. Mrs Reed is caught in a scheme to undermine Helen inside the organisation. Helen reunites with Sam, and the two investigate. As loyalties shift and trust wears thin, they must protect the people they love, and the search leads them back to the heart of the Black Doves.

Joe Barton is the creator, writer and executive producer. Julian Farino and Kieron Hawkes direct, Netflix says. What's on Netflix reported that filming began in the United Kingdom in late September 2025.
