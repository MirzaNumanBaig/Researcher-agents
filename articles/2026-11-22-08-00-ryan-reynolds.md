**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds), co-owner of Wrexham, was seen at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Saturday 21 November 2026.
- Tottenham beat Ipswich 2-0 in the Premier League, according to Nation.Cymru.
- Wrexham were playing at Lincoln, about 140 miles away, at the same time.
- Reynolds was pictured talking with Spurs chief executive Vinai Venkatesham and actor Sir Kenneth Branagh before kick-off.

Ryan Reynolds was in north London on Saturday, not at his own club's away game. The Deadpool star and Wrexham co-owner took a seat at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium for the 2-0 win over Ipswich, while Wrexham were at Lincoln.

## Who Ryan Reynolds sat with at Tottenham

Nation.Cymru reported that a star-studded group joined [Roberto De Zerbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Zerbi) in the West Stand. De Zerbi could not use the touchline. He was serving a ban after a third yellow card of the season in the defeat at Leeds before the international break, so he watched near technical director Johan Lange.

A few seats along, chief executive Vinai Venkatesham was photographed in conversation with Reynolds and [Sir Kenneth Branagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Branagh), the *Hamlet* actor and a Spurs fan. The two actors also appear together on screen this year. Branagh is in the cast of *Mayday*, the film released on 4 September 2026, according to its [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayday_(2026_film)). That page records an 83 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and 60 out of 100 on Metacritic, and no box office figures yet.

## Why the choice drew attention

Reynolds is a Wrexham co-owner, so where he is on a matchday gets noticed. Nation.Cymru said his appearance in London "may have raised eyebrows" given that Wrexham were 140 miles away at Lincoln. Headlines in The Sun and talkSPORT also pointed out the clash, although those pages could not be read in full for this article, so only Nation.Cymru's account is relied on here. Neither report says why he chose that match, and nothing in them suggests a problem at Wrexham.

## What he saw on the pitch

Reynolds did get a notable afternoon. Eighteen-year-old academy graduate [Luca Williams-Barnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luca_Williams-Barnett), wearing number 68, made his full debut. He scored after 15 minutes and, six minutes into the second half, set up [Dominic Solanke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Solanke) for the second goal. He left to a standing ovation in the 64th minute.

The ground also paused for a moment of remembrance. Five minutes after Williams-Barnett went off, supporters applauded for a minute in memory of Danny Kelly, the former NME and Q magazine editor and talkSPORT presenter, who died aged 69 on 11 November. A video of Kelly listing his 100 reasons for supporting Spurs had been shown at half-time.
