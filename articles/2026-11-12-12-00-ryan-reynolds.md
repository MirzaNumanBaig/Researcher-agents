**Quick summary**

- An unconfirmed online rumour links [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) to an Alpha Flight movie at Marvel Studios, reportedly as a writer.
- Two 1979 issues of The Uncanny X-Men, which introduced Alpha Flight, ranked third and fifth on Covrprice's weekly chart for the week ending 8 November 2026.
- Wrexham AFC confirmed chief executive Michael Williamson has left, with director Shaun Harvey acting in the role.
- Neither Marvel nor Reynolds has commented on the Alpha Flight talk in the sources reviewed.

A rumour about a possible Alpha Flight film has sent collectors hunting for 47-year-old X-Men comics, and it has landed in the same week that Ryan Reynolds and [Rob McElhenney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney) lost the chief executive of their football club. Here is what is confirmed, and what is not.

## The Alpha Flight rumour

According to [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources), the online scooper CartierFilms posted a run of unverified claims about Marvel Studios earlier this month. One of them was an Alpha Flight movie in development, with the Deadpool star involved as a writer.

CBR is clear that none of this has been confirmed. Nor is it certain that the rumour explains what has happened to comic sales. It reads the connection as possible, not proven.

## Why collectors are paying attention

The numbers are what make the story interesting. Covrprice's top ten for the week ending 8 November 2026 included The Uncanny X-Men #120 and #121 from 1979 at third and fifth place. Those issues carry the first appearance of Alpha Flight, the Canadian team created by John Byrne.

Another X-Men title sat in sixth place, The New Mutants #100 from 1991, which has no link to Alpha Flight. It is the debut of X-Force and was co-written by [Rob Liefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Liefeld), who also drew it, and [Fabian Nicieza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fabian_Nicieza), the creators of Deadpool. A version of X-Force appeared in Deadpool 2 in 2018. The chart was topped by Halloween: The Game Comic #1, so the surge is not purely a Marvel story.

## Canada, Wolverine and a possible explanation

Alpha Flight and Deadpool share a home country, and Reynolds is Canadian. CBR suggests that may be where the rumour started. In issues #120 and #121 the X-Men clash with Alpha Flight in Canada after the team holds Wolverine and Nightcrawler captive.

The original line-up includes Guardian, Sasquatch, Snowbird, Shaman, Northstar and his twin sister Aurora. Byrne, who also helped create Kitty Pryde and Sabretooth and co-led the [Dark Phoenix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Phoenix_Saga) saga, launched the team's own Marvel title in 1983.

## Wrexham loses its chief executive

Away from Marvel, Reynolds and McElhenney are dealing with a leadership change at Wrexham. According to [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/sports/international-sports/wrexham-a-f-c-owners-ryan-reynolds-and-rob-mcelhenney-lose-another-key-figure-as-fresh-questions-emerge-over-the-football-teams-leadership/articleshow/135735162.cms), the club announced in a short statement that Michael Williamson has left, without saying why or whether the decision was mutual.

[Shaun Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaun_Harvey) will act as chief executive. The owners thanked Williamson for two years of work taking the club from League One to the Championship, and said they remain focused on making the Wrexham community proud. Williamson joined in May 2024.

He is the second senior departure in recent months. [Humphrey Ker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humphrey_Ker) stepped down from his board role in September.

## A tougher season on the pitch

The timing is awkward. After three straight promotions, Wrexham sit 16th in the Championship after 14 matches, with four wins, five draws and five defeats. The club went out of the EFL Cup in the first round against Middlesbrough, and the Times of India notes thin depth at centre-back and centre-forward despite seven summer signings.

In October the EFL also fined the club over two eligibility breaches, involving Issa Kaboré and Callum O'Hare. The club said the right-to-work documents were in order for Kaboré, but paperwork had not been correctly submitted to the league.

Earlier coverage on the club is on our site, including [Ryan Reynolds says Wrexham's best is ahead as the Kop Stand rises](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-wrexham-kop-stand/).

## What happens next

On the film front, the next step is simple: either Marvel or Reynolds addresses the rumour, or it fades. Until then, treat it as speculation.

At Wrexham, the club faces Wolverhampton Wanderers next, with [Phil Parkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Parkinson) still in charge of the team and Harvey steering the boardroom.
