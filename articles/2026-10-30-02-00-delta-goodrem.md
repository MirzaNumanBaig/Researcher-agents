**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) danced a rumba to [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion)'s "It's All Coming Back to Me Now" in Week 5 of [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing).
- It was her first show after missing the previous week with a rib injury.
- [Anton Du Beke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Du_Beke) gave the first 10 of 2026, and the pair scored 34 out of 40 for second place on the night, according to The Music Man.
- Goodrem co-wrote "Eyes on Me" for Dion's 2007 album, which gives the song choice a personal edge.

[Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) made her return to the Strictly floor count. Dancing with professional partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington), the Australian singer performed an emotional rumba in Week 5 and left with 34 out of 40, second place on the night and the series' first perfect mark, according to [The Music Man](https://www.themusicman.uk/delta-goodrem-strictly-return/).

## A routine built around the injury

Goodrem had missed the previous week's show with a rib injury. The Music Man reports that Widdrington shaped the choreography around her recovery, avoiding strenuous lifts and backbends. Instead the couple relied on technique, measured hip action and storytelling.

The result, in the outlet's account, was a dance that did not hide behind tricks. It was staged with a metallic blue dress and a wind machine.

For more on how the week unfolded, see our earlier report, [Delta Goodrem's Strictly Return: First 10 and Halloween Week](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrems-strictly-return-first-10-and-halloween-week/).

## What the judges said

[Anton Du Beke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Du_Beke) called it his favourite routine of the series and awarded the first 10 of 2026, according to The Music Man. [Motsi Mabuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motsi_Mabuse) described it as one of the strongest rumbas she had seen on the programme, the same report says.

The 34 out of 40 put the pair second for the night. That is a strong number for a dancer returning from a missed week, and it follows an early run in which Goodrem and Widdrington topped the combined leaderboard across the first fortnight. The outlet adds that they earned the series' first nine for a Week 3 salsa.

## Why the song mattered

"It's All Coming Back to Me Now" was written by Jim Steinman, but Goodrem has her own history with Dion. She co-wrote "Eyes on Me", a track on Dion's 2007 album [Taking Chances](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taking_Chances), and, according to The Music Man, also sang backing vocals on the final recording. The outlet says she has named Dion as a formative inspiration.

That link helps explain why the performance landed with viewers. The Music Man reports nearly 200,000 YouTube views of the dance by 28 October 2026. One viewer's comment praised the lack of lifts, tricks and smoke machine, calling it a simple and elegant routine. That is one viewer's reaction, not a measure of the whole audience.

## A seasoned performer in the ballroom

The same report points to Goodrem's stage experience, which includes coaching on The Voice Australia and, it says, placing fourth at the 2026 Eurovision Song Contest in Vienna. Those claims come from a single outlet in this article, so treat the Eurovision result as reported rather than independently confirmed here.
