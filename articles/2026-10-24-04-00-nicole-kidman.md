**Quick summary**

- Slovak pop singer Adéla made her late night television debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on Thursday, performing "Nicole Kidman".
- She also sang the chorus of "Ain't in LA" and taught Fallon her "Swaggerina" dance, according to Consequence.
- Her debut album, PRIMA, came out on 4 September, and the song reached No 1 on Spotify earlier this month, The Guardian reports.
- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) was filmed laughing at the song during a Victoria's Secret show in Los Angeles this week.

Adéla brought a song named after one of Australia's best-known actors to American late night on Thursday, performing "Nicole Kidman" on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. The Slovak singer's first US talk show slot came just days after Kidman herself reacted to the track from the front row of a runway show.

## What happened on The Tonight Show

According to [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/10/adela-nicole-kidman-aint-in-la-fallon-watch/), Adéla performed with a group of dancers. The choreography mixed ballet movements, a nod to her album title PRIMA, with standard pop routines. There were also playful references to Kidman's film Moulin Rouge!, including dress lifts and a cabaret-style set.

The outlet noted that the song doesn't demand much vocally, so the performance leaned on dance, building to a fast finish. After the last note, Adéla said, "I can't believe I'm on Jimmy Fallon! This song changed my life." She then sang the chorus of "Ain't in LA", another track from the album.

## The interview and the Swaggerina

Adéla also sat down with Fallon before the performance. Consequence says she spoke about growing up in Slovakia, learning English through Hannah Montana and The Tonight Show, and being inspired by Kidman. She also described getting Kidman's approval of the song.

[BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/ADLA-Reacts-to-Nicole-Kidman-Mentioning-Her-Teaches-Swaggerina-Moves-20261023) reports that she told Fallon how she reacted on learning Kidman had mentioned her by name. The segment ended with Adéla teaching the host the Swaggerina, the signature dance she says she created. Neither outlet gives details of what Kidman said, so that part remains in the clip itself.

## How the song became a hit

The track has had a quick run. Writing in The Guardian's weekly column, Emma Brockes says the song, from Adéla's debut album PRIMA, became a minor TikTok sensation and went to No 1 on Spotify earlier this month. She adds that Madonna gave it a discreet nod in a TikTok video.

Brockes also reports that the song was inspired by a corset Adéla wore to the Golden Globes, and that Kidman has accepted the compliment graciously. Her column began with her 11-year-old asking who Kidman was, a sign of how the song has introduced the actor to a much younger audience. For earlier coverage, see our report on how the [Nicole Kidman song lifted Adéla into the top 10](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-song-lifts-slovak-star-adela-into-top-10/).

## Kidman's reaction

The Guardian says that when the song played at a Victoria's Secret runway show in Los Angeles this week, Kidman, seated in the front row, laughed and put her head in her hands. It is a light-hearted moment, and the only direct reaction from Kidman in the sources we reviewed. We have not seen a statement from her or her representatives.

## What happens next

PRIMA has been out since 4 September, and the Fallon booking suggests the song's run isn't over. None of the sources we read announced further US appearances or a tour, so any next dates will need to come from Adéla's team. We will update this story if they do.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Adéla?

Adéla is a Slovak pop singer whose debut album, PRIMA, was released on 4 September. Her song "Nicole Kidman" reached No 1 on Spotify earlier this month, according to The Guardian.

### Did Nicole Kidman respond to the song?

Yes, in two ways according to the sources. The Guardian reports she laughed at it during a Victoria's Secret show in Los Angeles, and Adéla told Fallon she received Kidman's approval of the song.

It is Adéla's signature dance. She explained its origins on The Tonight Show and taught the steps to Jimmy Fallon, BroadwayWorld reports.
