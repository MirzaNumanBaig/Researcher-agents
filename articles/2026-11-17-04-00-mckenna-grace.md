**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), 20, visited a Hunger Games pop-up in Midtown Manhattan on Sunday, 15 November 2026.
- She wore a two-piece leather outfit that Yahoo News says recalls Katniss Everdeen's hunting gear.
- The look was vintage Roberto Cavalli, styled by Molly Dickson, with a side-swept Dutch braid.
- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* opens in cinemas in the United States on Friday, 20 November.

Mckenna Grace has turned a casual pop-up visit into one of the most talked-about looks of her Hunger Games press tour. The 20-year-old actor stopped by the franchise's pop-up in Midtown Manhattan on Sunday, 15 November, dressed in a nod to Katniss Everdeen's famous braid and hunting wardrobe, according to [Yahoo News Singapore](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/mckenna-grace-channels-katniss-everdeen-172122307.html).

## What Mckenna Grace Katniss styling looked like

The outfit was a two-piece leather ensemble that Yahoo describes as reminiscent of the hunting gear worn by Katniss in the original films. It was vintage Roberto Cavalli, styled by Molly Dickson. Grace added tinted sunglasses and slouchy knee-high boots.

Her hair did much of the storytelling. Yahoo reports waist-length blond curls swept into a Dutch braid over her right shoulder, with loose tendrils and curly bangs on the left. The report says that shape echoes Katniss's hair before she enters the arena. Makeup was kept soft: a glowy, skin-forward base with peach-pink blush and a matching lip.

Grace did not credit a hair or makeup team for this appearance. Yahoo notes that on the wider press tour she has often worked with hairstylist Glen Oropeza and makeup artist Melissa Hernandez.

## A shift from full-scale recreations

The pop-up look differs from what Grace has done so far on the tour. According to Yahoo, she has mostly recreated well-known scenes, including Katniss's tribute parade from the first film and her Capitol appearance in the second. Yahoo calls the pop-up outing her first casual example of "method dressing", where an actor borrows from a character for an everyday appearance rather than a staged homage.

Jennifer Lawrence played Katniss in the original films, which is the reference point for every one of these fashion callbacks. Grace stars in the new franchise entry, a dystopian story that has kept fans engaged through a run of premieres and events.

## The press tour so far

The New York stop follows a busy schedule. Yahoo lists a Madrid premiere on 5 November and a Los Angeles fan event on 9 November. Those earlier looks leaned on direct references to the series, which makes the relaxed leather-and-braid outfit stand out as a quieter wink at the same source material.

Other outlets also covered the appearance. Teen Vogue published its own piece on the braid, and a Hunger Games quiz video with Grace and her co-stars Joseph Zada and Whitney Peak was circulating the same day.
