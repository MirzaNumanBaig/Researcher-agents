**Quick summary**

- [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart) died at 86 at his home in Los Angeles on Wednesday, according to his longtime spokesperson, Kelly Bush Novak.
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) called him "one of the greats of stage and screen" in an Instagram post.
- Ian McKellen, Rebecca Romijn, Famke Janssen, Kelsey Grammer and Marvel's Kevin Feige have also paid tribute.
- Stewart is set to appear in Marvel's Avengers: Doomsday, due in December.

Hugh Jackman has led a wave of tributes from the X-Men cast after the death of Patrick Stewart, who died at 86 at his home in Los Angeles on Wednesday. Jackman, who played Wolverine opposite Stewart's Professor Xavier, wrote on Instagram that he had "learned so much" from his co-star and would miss him "all of my days".

## How Stewart's death was announced

Stewart's longtime spokesperson, Kelly Bush Novak, announced the death. She said the actor had experienced "a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia", according to a report from [Tempo.co](https://en.tempo.co/read/2122645/x-men-stars-remember-patrick-stewart-from-hugh-jackman-to-kelsey-grammer) that cites The Hollywood Reporter.

Bush Novak said he was surrounded by family and friends in his final moments, with live music, prayer, tears and laughter. She described a "peaceful ease and reflection" on his face that she called "an enduring reminder of his invincible grace".

## Jackman's message

Jackman posted his tribute on Instagram. "We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today. And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man," he wrote. He added that he had learned a great deal from Stewart.

The two worked together across the X-Men series. Stewart first played [Professor Charles Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X) in the 2000 film, and his run in the role stretched through to Logan in 2017, according to Tempo's list of his appearances.

## What the rest of the cast said

[Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen), who played Magneto against Stewart's Xavier, posted a tribute on Thursday. He spoke of their shared Yorkshire roots and called Stewart "a man to rely on, to admire and in my case, love".

[Rebecca Romijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Romijn), who played Mystique, shared a video about a friendship of 25 years. She called him hilarious and mischievous and praised his voice and classical training.

[Famke Janssen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Famke_Janssen), who played Jean Grey, told The Hollywood Reporter that Stewart was "a legend". She recalled working with him on Star Trek: The Next Generation and on the X-Men films.

[Kelsey Grammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelsey_Grammer), who played Beast, singled out Stewart's guest turn on the sitcom Frasier, calling it one of the greatest ever by a guest.

[Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige), the head of Marvel Studios, said he first met Stewart on the set of the original X-Men film, after years of watching him as Captain Picard. He praised Stewart as a generous collaborator and pointed to the many people he mentored. Marvel Studios also put out its own statement, crediting Stewart with bringing Xavier to life with grace, intelligence and humanity.

## Stewart's X-Men legacy

After the 2000 film, Stewart returned for X2: X-Men United (2003), X-Men: The Last Stand (2006), X-Men: Days of Future Past (2014) and Logan (2017). He also appeared in X-Men Origins: Wolverine (2009) and The Wolverine (2013), the two films built around Jackman's character.

That adds up to seven films across 17 years, from 2000 to 2017. Our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) section has more on the films.

## What happens next

Stewart is still due to appear in Marvel's Avengers: Doomsday, which is scheduled for release in December. According to Tempo, several former X-Men cast members are also in the film, including McKellen, [James Marsden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Marsden), Grammer, [Alan Cumming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Cumming) and Romijn.

McKellen noted that audiences have yet to see Stewart's last performance as Xavier. "His farewell performance in Avengers: Doomsday is yet to be seen," he wrote.
