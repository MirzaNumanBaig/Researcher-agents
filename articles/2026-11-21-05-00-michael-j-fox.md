**Quick summary**

- [Tracy Pollan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy_Pollan), the wife of [Michael J. Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_J._Fox), spoke about family life with his Parkinson's at the foundation's 26th charity gala.
- She said the family lives "day by day" and called the illness hard on the family, caregivers and patient, according to El Mundo America.
- The couple married in 1988 and have four children, three of whom joined them on the red carpet.
- Separately, 3DVF revisits how a new camera system let Fox pass himself a glass of water in Back to the Future Part II.

## What Tracy Pollan said at the gala

Tracy Pollan has described how her family copes with Michael J. Fox's Parkinson's, speaking at the 26th edition of the charity evening "A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Cure Parkinson's", according to [El Mundo America](https://www.mundoamerica.com/celebrity/2026/11/20/6b00353921efa035258b45be.html). Fox was there with his family.

Her approach is plain. "I think you just have to live day by day," she said, adding that you have to look at what is in front of you and stay in the present moment.

She did not play down the difficulty. More than 30 years after the diagnosis, she told the outlet: "It's very difficult for the family, for the caregivers, and obviously, for the patient. It's not easy." Fox was diagnosed in 1991, the same report notes.

## The small pleasures that hold the family together

Pollan also spoke about what the couple enjoy most. Their tastes in books and screen entertainment do not always match, she said, but food is common ground. "We both always enjoy a good meal. So I would say that our meals together make us happy," she explained.

It is a modest detail, and a useful one. It shows how a family living with a long-term condition builds routine around ordinary comforts rather than around the illness itself.

## A family affair on the red carpet

The couple married in 1988. Their four children are Sam, 37, twins Aquinnah and Schuyler, 31, and Esmé, 25, according to the same report. Three of the daughters joined their parents on the red carpet at an evening that drew many people from the entertainment industry.

The gala supports [The Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Michael_J._Fox_Foundation), which Fox established to advance research and treatments. For more on its fundraising, see our earlier report on the [foundation gala's record $6 million](https://moduscarry.com/michael-j-fox-foundation-gala-record/).

## Behind the camera: one actor, three McFlys

A different Fox story is circulating among film fans too. It is not new reporting: 3DVF is retelling an account that cinematographer [Dean Cundey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Cundey) gave on the ILM podcast *Lighter Darker* in an episode published on 10 December 2024.

In the 2015 family dinner scene, Fox plays Marty Sr., Marty Jr. and Marlene. Cundey suggested having one version of Fox hand a glass of water to another. Director Robert Zemeckis wanted the camera to move through the scene rather than sit still, which ruled out the simpler split-screen approach, according to [3DVF](https://3dvf.com/en/ilm-invented-a-new-camera-so-michael-j-fox-could-hand-himself-a-glass-of-water/).

To make that work, ILM developed VistaGlide, a motion-control dolly with video playback that could record a camera move and repeat it exactly for each pass. Fox wore different makeup and costume each time. The film's production notes, cited by 3DVF, say becoming the older Marty took more than four hours in the makeup chair.

He also acted against his own earlier performances, hearing recorded lines through a hidden earpiece to time his replies. Cundey had a small marker placed on the table so the glass landed in the same spot in both takes, which he called "a little foot".

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Michael J. Fox diagnosed with Parkinson's?

He was diagnosed in 1991, according to El Mundo America. He later set up The Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson's Research.

### What did Tracy Pollan say about living with the diagnosis?

She said you have to live day by day and focus on the present moment. She also said the illness remains very hard on the family, caregivers and patient.

### How did Back to the Future make one actor play three family members in one shot?

ILM's VistaGlide system repeated the same camera move for each pass while Fox changed roles, according to 3DVF. A tabletop marker kept props like the glass of water in the right place.
