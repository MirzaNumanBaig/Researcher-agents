**Quick summary**

- [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) and [Carla Gugino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carla_Gugino) spoke to TODAY about working with director David Fincher and writer Quentin Tarantino, according to BroadwayWorld.
- Some scenes on *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth* took "upwards of 100" takes, the report says.
- The film has a two-week IMAX run from 25 November 2026 and arrives on Netflix on 23 December.
- Debicki plays talent agent Karen Crosby, according to Netflix Junkie.

Elizabeth Debicki and Carla Gugino have been talking about what it was like to work with two exacting filmmakers. In an interview with TODAY reported by [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/Elizabeth-Debicki-Carla-Gugino-on-Working-With-Tarantino-Fincher-20261120) on 20 November, the pair discussed making *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth*, a Netflix crime comedy-drama directed by David Fincher from a Quentin Tarantino screenplay. The film reaches cinemas five days after that report.

## What the film is

The film follows *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*, which opened on 9 August 2019. It is set in Los Angeles in 1977, eight years after the events of that film, according to both BroadwayWorld and [Netflix Junkie](https://www.netflixjunkie.com/hollywood-news-a-glorious-time-elizabeth-debicki-looks-back-on-making-brad-pitts-the-further-mis-adventures-of-cliff-booth/).

Brad Pitt returns as Cliff Booth. BroadwayWorld's earlier coverage describes him as having become a studio fixer after his stuntman days, while Netflix Junkie says he now also works as a bartender and a cinema projectionist. Tarantino wrote the script, and Fincher took over as director. Netflix Junkie says Pitt helped bring that collaboration together.

The cast also includes Scott Caan, Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, Peter Weller and Timothy Olyphant, along with Debicki and Gugino.

## What Debicki and Gugino said

The headline detail from the TODAY interview is the number of takes. According to BroadwayWorld, some scenes took "upwards of 100". The report does not say which scenes needed that many.

Debicki has been consistently warm about the shoot. Netflix Junkie, on 6 November, quoted her calling it "a glorious time" and "a total joy". She said she loved working with Pitt and "just adore[s]" Fincher. The same report says she told PEOPLE at Sundance in February 2026 that the film was a dream to make, and that filming ran for nearly six months in Los Angeles.

Debicki also took part in a Q&A with Fincher and Weller after a Netflix screening in Los Angeles, BroadwayWorld reports.

## Debicki's part in the story

According to Netflix Junkie, Debicki plays Karen Crosby, a talent agent who draws Booth back into Hollywood's orbit. She approaches him on behalf of her client, TV star Charlie Peters, played by Caan. It puts Debicki opposite Pitt in one of Netflix's highest-profile releases of the season.

Gugino's next stage work is also in the report. She plays Beatrice in Arthur Miller's *A View from the Bridge* at La MaMa, with performances beginning on 27 November.

## What happens next

The film opens in a two-week IMAX engagement starting 25 November 2026. It then arrives on Netflix on 23 December 2026, according to both outlets.
