**Quick summary**

- Australian critic Jim Schembri has given [Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s new film *Ebenezer* a strongly positive review for Starts at 60.
- He calls it a "hands-down winner" and describes it as Depp's first big studio movie in eight years.
- Depp plays Scrooge in a faithful adaptation of Charles Dickens's *A Christmas Carol*.
- Schembri's column is one critic's view; other verdicts on the film have been more mixed.

## A review that welcomes Depp back

The Johnny Depp Ebenezer review from Jim Schembri, published on 20 November by [Starts at 60](https://startsat60.com/media/news/the-screen-critic-johnny-depps-christmas-gift), opens with a simple idea: the film is a Christmas present. Schembri writes that Depp returns "triumphantly" with *Ebenezer*, which he describes as the actor's first big studio movie in eight years.

The critic ties the comeback to the end of Depp's court fight with ex-wife [Amber Heard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber_Heard), saying the actor is now "free and clear" of that litigation. That is Schembri's framing, and it is worth reading as context rather than as a measure of the film itself.

## What the critic liked about the performance

*Ebenezer* retells the 1843 story by [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens), in which a miserly man who hates Christmas is visited by a series of ghosts. According to Schembri, Depp "eats up" the role and gives the skinflint some soul as the visitations slowly change his sneering outlook.

He describes the production as handsome, with a few good gags and some spooky moments. In his view, the faithful approach lets this version stand proudly beside earlier screen takes, including those from [Bill Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Murray), the Muppets and Mickey Mouse.

## How it sits with other coverage

Schembri's praise is on the warm end of the early response. This site has already covered [a mixed weekend verdict on Ebenezer](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-weekend-verdict/) and [a report that gave the film a C grade from an unnamed insider](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-grade/). Those pieces are separate reports and are not part of this review, so readers should weigh the opinions side by side.

The Starts at 60 column is a weekly round-up, and Depp's film is only one of several titles in it. Schembri also recommends the documentary *You Can See Everything* about Elizabeth Holmes, and the biopic *Chopin*, while panning *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*. None of that bears on Depp, but it shows the column's tone: direct, and happy to name a favourite.

## What happens next

The review lands as Christmas approaches, which is when Schembri says audiences are most likely to want a familiar story. Further reviews and box office figures will show whether his enthusiasm is shared. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is Ebenezer?
It is a new screen version of Charles Dickens's *A Christmas Carol*, with Johnny Depp as the miser Ebenezer Scrooge.

### Is Ebenezer Johnny Depp's first studio film in years?
According to critic Jim Schembri, it is Depp's first big studio movie in eight years.

### Did the critic like it?
Yes. Schembri calls it a "hands-down winner" and praises Depp's performance, though other reports on the film have been more mixed.
