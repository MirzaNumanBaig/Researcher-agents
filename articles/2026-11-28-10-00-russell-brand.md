**Quick summary**

- Metro reported on 26 November 2026 that [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand)'s wife, Laura, took their three children to see him at HMP Littlehey.
- It is believed to be the first time the children have seen him since he was jailed, according to Metro.
- Brand, 51, is serving a 10-year, six-month sentence for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault, Metro says.
- A Ministry of Justice investigation is examining whether he broke prison rules by passing a message to be read on his YouTube channel.

Russell Brand's family has visited him in prison for the first time since his sentencing, according to a report from [Metro](https://metro.co.uk/2026/11/26/russell-brands-wife-takes-children-visit-cushy-jail-first-time-29823187/) published on 26 November. Laura Brand, 39, brought the couple's three children to HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire for a supervised visit.

## What the visit looked like

Metro, using pictures first published by the Daily Mail, says the family was escorted into the prison by Joe McCann, the bodyguard who was also seen at Southwark Crown Court during the trial. Laura carried the youngest child, who is three, on her hip. Another child held McCann's hand, and a third was accompanied by a woman whose identity is not known.

Visits take place in a centre run by Invisible Walls, a community interest company that says it supports families and helps prisoners strengthen relationships. Metro describes the space as decorated for Christmas, with refreshment machines, cushioned seating and toys. A source quoted by The Mirror said Laura wanted the visit to go as smoothly as possible, because the children had not seen their father for two weeks.

## Where Brand is serving his sentence

Metro reports that Brand, 51, is serving 10 years and six months, of which he is expected to serve about seven years. The sentence relates to one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault between 2004 and 2009. He was first held at HMP Wandsworth in south-west London, in solitary confinement, and was later moved to Littlehey, a Category C prison near Huntingdon for men convicted of sex offences, for his own safety.

The paper says the prison has been called "cushy" because of its football pitch, two gyms and yoga classes, and that Brand has his own cell. Metro also reports that other women have since come forward with allegations, including Mia Latter, 37, who has waived her anonymity. Those claims are allegations, and the report does not say they have been tested in court.

## Family and the court

Metro recalls that Brand's former barrister, Ian Winter KC, asked for a short release so he could say goodbye to his children. Mr Justice Bennathan refused, citing the risk of flight, and said the hardship to the children had been caused by serious criminal offending by their father. Winter later told the court the family faced "total devastation" and that the eldest child, who recently turned 10, was finding it hardest to understand. Laura attended the trial and gave character evidence for her husband.

## The prison rules inquiry

Separately, Metro says the Ministry of Justice is looking into whether Brand broke prison rules shortly after sentencing. Laura read a message from him on the Sunday Service series on their YouTube channel, which has about six million subscribers. In it, she repeated his claim that he is "not guilty", although a jury convicted him. According to the report, she has since promised to adhere to the restrictions that were set after the backlash.

The case also continues to prompt commentary. A [Zeteo](https://zeteo.com/p/mehdi-and-owen-on-their-russell-brand) podcast description says two presenters, Mehdi and Owen, answered criticism from the political right over interviews they did with Brand more than ten years ago, when he had not been charged with any crime.
