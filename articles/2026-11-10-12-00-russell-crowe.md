**Quick summary**

- [Russell Crowe's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) 2026 crime drama *The Get Out* ranks ninth among the most-watched movies on Paramount+ in the US, according to Collider.
- The film is directed by [Derrick Borte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derrick_Borte) and based on Thomas Perry's 2010 novel *Strip*.
- Crowe plays a nightclub owner named Manco who is pulled into a world of crime.
- Crowe's next releases are the *Highlander* remake and the historical action drama *The Last Druid*.

Russell Crowe has a quiet streaming hit on his hands. *The Get Out*, his 2026 crime drama, is currently number nine on the Paramount+ top ten in the United States, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/russell-crowe-the-get-out-streaming-hit-paramount-plus-november-2026/), which described it as one of the ten most-watched films on the service.

## What The Get Out is about

The film is directed by Derrick Borte, whose earlier work includes *The Joneses*. It is adapted from *Strip*, a 2010 novel by Thomas Perry. Crowe plays Manco, a nightclub owner who gets caught up in a criminal underworld.

The supporting cast includes [Luke Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luke_Evans), [Teresa Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teresa_Palmer), [Daniel Zovatto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Zovatto) and [Aaron Paul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Paul). Palmer is an Australian actress, which gives the film a local connection for readers here.

We have followed the film's run on the chart before. See our earlier posts on how [The Get Out climbed the Paramount+ chart again](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-the-get-out/) and how [Crowe had two films on streaming charts at once](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-streaming-hits/).

## What else is on the Paramount+ chart

Collider says the list is an unusual mix. The top two places belong to a pair of connected action titles, *One Mile: Chapter One* and *One Mile: Chapter Two*. Further down, the Eddie Murphy comedy *Norbit* is described as the most surprising entry. Luc Besson's *Dracula*, the buddy-cop favourite *Rush Hour*, the 1996 disaster film *Twister* and the comedy *We're the Millers* also made the top ten.

That puts a recent crime drama alongside older catalogue titles. Ninth place is not a headline-grabbing spot, but it keeps the film in front of subscribers at a time when few of the others on the chart are new.

## Highlander is next

Collider notes that Crowe is due back on the big screen in the remake of the 1986 fantasy *Highlander*, which starred [Christopher Lambert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Lambert) and Sean Connery. John Wick director Chad Stahelski is behind the new version, and Crowe plays Juan Sánchez-Villalobos Ramírez.

The announced cast is large. It includes Henry Cavill, [Dave Bautista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Bautista), [Karen Gillan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_Gillan), [Jeremy Irons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Irons), [Djimon Hounsou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djimon_Hounsou) and [Marisa Abela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marisa_Abela).

## The weight-loss rumour

Photos of a dramatic physical change in Crowe circulated recently, and some reports suggested he had slimmed down or got into peak shape for *Highlander*. Collider says that was not the case. Crowe used social media to say he had already finished filming *Highlander* before the photos were taken. The report links the change instead to his following project, *The Last Druid*, a historical action drama directed by [William Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Eubank).

This detail comes from Collider's account of Crowe's own comments. We have not seen the original post.

## What happens next

No release dates for *Highlander* or *The Last Druid* appear in the source we read, so we are not guessing.
