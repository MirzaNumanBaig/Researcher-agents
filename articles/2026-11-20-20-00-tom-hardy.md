**Quick summary**

- Executive producer David Glasser says MobLand Season 3 will bring in someone to "challenge Tom Hardy", who plays fixer Harry Da Souza.
- Helen Mirren told Radio Times on 13 November that work on the third season starts "next week".
- Paramount+ renewed the crime drama on 3 September 2026, before Season 2 had premiered.
- No premiere date or production schedule has been announced, and the rival's identity is still under wraps.

[Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)'s Harry Da Souza is about to meet an opponent he has not faced before. According to [FandomWire](https://fandomwire.com/mobland-season-3-tom-hardy-new-rival-update/), MobLand executive producer David Glasser has confirmed that Season 3 will introduce a new challenger for the Harrigan family's fixer, while the cast says cameras are about to roll.

## What the producer has said

Glasser made the comment at Paramount UK's Upfront event in London in October, FandomWire reports, citing Esquire. He said viewers would see "someone challenge Tom Hardy" in the next run. He did not say who that person is, or whether it will be an existing character or a new face.

That is all that has been confirmed. Everything about the rival's identity is guesswork for now, and Paramount+ has not added detail.

## Work on the third season begins

Helen Mirren gave the clearest sign of timing. Speaking to [Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times) at the premiere of her thriller A Talent for Murder on 13 November, she said the team was "starting work on a third season" and would begin the following week. She declined to share story details when asked.

Her remark matches an earlier indication from [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) that filming would start in November, according to FandomWire. Paramount+ has not published a formal schedule or a premiere date, so viewers should treat the timing as the cast's word rather than an official calendar.

The renewal itself was announced on 3 September 2026, two weeks before Season 2 premiered. It confirmed that Hardy, Brosnan and Mirren would all return.

## The doubts about Hardy's future

The speed of the Season 3 start is notable given the mood in May. Reports of creative disagreements between Hardy and the production fuelled talk that he might leave. A production source told Variety at the time: "Tom was not fired, the door is not closed for Season 3 and things are being worked through creatively," as quoted by FandomWire. The renewal and the confirmed return have since settled the question. For the background, see our earlier piece, [Tom Hardy and MobLand: How the Firing Story Unravelled](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-firing/).

## Where Season 2 left Harry

FandomWire's analysis of the Season 2 finale suggests the threat may come from inside the family's own circle. Spoilers follow.

By the end, Harry has seemingly dealt with his main enemies: [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn)'s Frankie Cooper is in captivity and Kat McAllister has been forced to resign. But FandomWire says Maeve, Mirren's character, enlisted Eddie to have Harry killed, and Eddie's botched attempt left Jan's survival uncertain after an exploding Maserati.

FandomWire argues that makes Maeve a strong candidate for Harry's next adversary. It points to Mirren's own comments to TV WEEK, where she described Maeve as someone who feels "dead" if she is out of the game. The site also raises another route: Mad Dog, the son of a man Harry killed, is pursuing Eddie, and a misplaced blame for the blast could send Harry after the wrong person. These are the outlet's theories, not anything the producers have said.

## What happens next

Filming is the next step, and Paramount+ has yet to announce when Season 3 will arrive. Glasser's comment is the only confirmed clue to the story so far. Until the network names the new rival, the Maeve theory remains speculation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who will challenge Tom Hardy in MobLand Season 3?

Nobody has been named. Executive producer David Glasser only said someone would challenge Hardy's character, Harry Da Souza.

### When does MobLand Season 3 start filming?

Helen Mirren said on 13 November that work would begin the following week. Paramount+ has not published an official production schedule.

### Is Tom Hardy returning for MobLand Season 3?

Yes. Paramount+'s renewal announcement on 3 September 2026 confirmed that Hardy, Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren will return.
