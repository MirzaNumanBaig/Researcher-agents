**Quick summary**

- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) was interviewed in character by a Nottinghamshire history content creator who performs as "Robyn Hood of Wakefield".
- The creator, Craig, told the Mansfield and Ashfield Chad that Jackman was "an absolute top bloke".
- The interview was linked to *The Death of Robin Hood*, which the Chad says reached cinemas earlier this year.
- Craig says a talent representative approached him to script and conduct the interview.

Hugh Jackman has swapped the Wolverine claws for a longbow on screen, and recently met a man who lives the Robin Hood legend online. According to the [Mansfield and Ashfield Chad](https://www.chad.co.uk/news/people/hugh-jackman-was-top-bloke-says-robin-hood-of-wakefield-after-interview-9318281), a 36-year-old North Nottinghamshire creator known as Robyn The Hooded Man was invited to interview the actor, and came away calling him a "top bloke".

## Who is Robyn The Hooded Man?

The Chad reports that the creator, who gives his name as Craig, has built an audience of more than 1.4 million followers across four platforms, including 191,000 on Facebook alone. He presents himself as "Robyn Hood of Wakefield", an outlaw placed in the year 1326, when Edward II was on the English throne, rather than in the era of Richard the Lionheart and Prince John.

That may sound unusual, but the paper notes that many experts have argued Robin Hood, if he existed, belonged to a later period than the ballads suggest. Craig says he draws on historians and scholars including J. C. Holt, John Bellamy and David Baldwin, and that a documentary by [Tony Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Robinson) first sparked his interest in the real history.

## How the Hugh Jackman interview came about

Craig said a talent representative contacted him and asked if he would script and conduct an interview with Jackman. "Of course, I said yes," he told the Chad. The interview was tied to *The Death of Robin Hood*, which the paper says was written and directed by [Michael Sarnoski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Sarnoski) and also stars [Jodie Comer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jodie_Comer). It follows an ageing Robin, haunted by his past killings and living in self-imposed exile.

Craig described the actor as respectful and curious about the subject. "He was absolutely buzzing to see me in my medieval kit and fully committed to me being in character," he said. He added that he was nervous about performing a few feet from a world-renowned performer, and that he felt Jackman enjoyed a format that was quite different from the usual press round.

## Why a historical Robin Hood?

Craig argues there is no single "real" Robin Hood. In his telling, the early stories feature lowborn yeoman outlaws in Barnsdale under a King Edward, while later versions turn him into a disinherited nobleman in Sherwood under King Richard. He chose to portray one historical figure whose exploits inspired the tales, which lets him speak as a Robin Hood while also commenting on the fictional versions from the outside.

His kit, weapons and equipment are all made to suit 1326. He also appears at medieval festivals with a green tent and a full yeoman archer set-up, and says he is Archery GB qualified and can offer coaching.
