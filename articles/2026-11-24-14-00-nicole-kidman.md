**Quick summary**

- A Kidman Carol, a new comedy by Kevin Zak, is playing at New World Stages in New York and runs through 30 December 2026.
- The plot sends three spirits of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) to visit an awards-chasing Cate Blanchett, in a Hollywood take on Dickens.
- Skylar Fox directs, and [Marla Mindelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marla_Mindelle) and [Josh Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Sharp) star, according to BroadwayWorld.
- Kidman herself was also in the news this week for a sculptural grey trouser look in Manhattan, reported by Grazia.

Nicole Kidman has become the ghost in somebody else's Christmas story. A Kidman Carol, a new comedy now playing at New World Stages in New York, reworks the Dickens classic around Oscar season, and it runs through 30 December 2026, according to [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/article/Video-Marla-Mindelle-Josh-Sharp-Are-Having-a-Very-Unhinged-Christmas-in-A-KIDMAN-CAROL-20261123).

## What A Kidman Carol is about

Writer Kevin Zak describes the premise in simple terms. Cate Blanchett takes the Ebenezer Scrooge role as an Oscar-season miser, and she is visited by three spirits of Kidman who are there to teach her what awards season is really for.

Zak told BroadwayWorld that the idea took the Scrooge legend and gave it a Hollywood spin with something to say about the state of the industry. He also pointed to a more serious thread underneath the impressions: what happens when a dream comes true and turns out not to be what you expected, and how a person keeps hope alive for themselves and others.

That mix of parody and sincerity is the pitch.

## Who is making it

Skylar Fox directs. The two leads are Mindelle, an American musical theatre actress, and Sharp, an American actor and writer. Zak praised the unusual chemistry between them and said audiences have not seen either of them do anything quite like this before.

Fox said the cast and creative team have all worked in what he called the "psycho gay fever dream" space, so this show is a chance to collaborate on one together. He promised plenty of stage magic as well: smoke, trap doors, ghosts and heavy makeup for the performers.

The production has been building buzz for about a week. BroadwayWorld ran a first-look piece on 16 November, followed by a video interview with the company on 23 November, and NBC New York has also run a segment asking who is haunting the cast. The NBC page could not be read in full, so nothing here relies on it beyond its headline.

## Kidman's own week: trousers and a film follow-up

The parody lands while the real Kidman is getting her own attention. Grazia reports she was seen on the streets of Manhattan in slate-grey trousers cut with sculptural volume, worn with a tailored blazer, a ruffled Edwardian-style blouse, black loafers and a quilted velvet Chanel bag. The magazine ties the look to Spring/Summer 2027 runway shapes at Balenciaga and Celine, and compares the proportions to the work of [Dame Barbara Hepworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Hepworth). We covered the outfit in more detail in [Nicole Kidman Wears Sculptural Grey Trousers in New York](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-sculptural-trousers/), and more fashion stories sit in our [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/) section.

On screen, Kidman reunited with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock) for Practical Magic 2, the follow-up to the 1998 film [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic). Wikipedia lists a release date of 26 August 2026, a US$75 million budget and about US$106 million at the box office, which works out to roughly 1.4 times its cost. It also records a 35% Rotten Tomatoes score and 44 out of 100 on Metacritic.
