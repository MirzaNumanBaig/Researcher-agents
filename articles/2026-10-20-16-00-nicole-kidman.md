**Quick summary**

- The new Sydney leads of Moulin Rouge! The Musical, Erin Clare and Nigel Huckle, have publicly invited Nicole Kidman to opening night, according to Nine's Front Row column.
- Kidman played Satine in Baz Luhrmann's 2001 film, 25 years ago, opposite Ewan McGregor.
- Her new Paramount+ series Discretion has moved from New Jersey and New York to Dallas, with West Texas filming due next.
- Discretion is an eight-episode series and is due to premiere on Paramount+ in 2027.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) has a standing invitation to a Sydney opening night, and the people issuing it are the performers stepping into her most famous musical role. At the Moulin Rouge! The Musical cast reveal at the Sydney Lyric Theatre, new leads Erin Clare and Nigel Huckle asked her, on the record, to turn up. Meanwhile, her next screen project is in the middle of a busy week of filming in Texas.

## The cast reveal at the Sydney Lyric

According to [Nine's Front Row column](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/front-row-aussie-theatre-moulin-rouge-cast-reveal-country-music-star-stunt-20261020-p616ur.html), the room was full of speculation before the announcement. Names floated in the lobby included [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) as Harold Zidler and [Samantha Jade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samantha_Jade) as Satine.

Comedian Susie Youssef then announced Clare as Satine and Huckle as Christian. The pair performed [Your Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Your_Song), and Front Row described them as strong vocalists with obvious chemistry. Comedian and drag artist [Reuben Kaye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuben_Kaye) will play Zidler.

The reveal came 25 years after Kidman and [Ewan McGregor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewan_McGregor) played Satine and Christian in Luhrmann's film. The stage production is returning to Sydney, and no Hollywood name was attached to the cast.

## The invitation

After the performance, Front Row spoke to the new leads, and the conversation turned straight to Kidman.

"Nicole, we're speaking directly to you," Clare said. "We would love if you could come and bring those incredible bangs to opening night in Sydney!" Huckle followed with what he called a formal invitation: "Nicole, we come to this place for magic." Clare added: "You've never been more culturally relevant!"

Neither Kidman nor her representatives had responded in the source material, so whether she will attend is unknown. Front Row's own verdict was that a home-grown cast taking over Sydney is a boost for Australian theatre, which it said has been hit by a run of tour cancellations.

## Discretion moves to Texas

Kidman's current work is a different kind of story. According to a [Yahoo report](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/tv/articles/nicole-kidman-dallas-set-legal-202917738.html), Discretion is an eight-episode Paramount+ series in which she and Elle Fanning play lawyers at a Dallas firm. It is based on a short story by Dallas-raised author Chandler Baker, who drew on her own time as a corporate lawyer. The series was first announced in October 2025.

Filming began in mid-August in New Jersey, with additional photography in New York. This week the production shifts to Dallas, then to West Texas.

Local casting calls, posted under the working title "The Summers", give a sense of the scenes being shot in Dallas. They include business people on 20 October, a Mexican restaurant scene and crime scene investigators on 21 October, and firefighters, gun club members and a crime scene crowd on 22 October. Pay listed ranges from $110 for 10 hours to $250 for 12 hours for photo doubles. Those figures are in US dollars. KMID-TV reported that driving scenes will be filmed on 27 October along South County Road 1160 in Midland County.

## What the series is about

The logline describes Fanning's character, Lenny, as a summer associate who finds a web of non-disclosure agreements hiding a dark truth. She then realises she signed the same agreement. That puts her at odds with Sharon, the firm's most powerful female partner, played by Kidman, and tests their mentor and protégé relationship. The question the logline poses is who gets to keep secrets, and at what cost.

The cast also includes Josh Lucas, Colton Ryan, Stephan James, Zazie Beetz, Ben Foster, Stella Baker and John Hoogenakker.

## What happens next

Discretion has its West Texas shoot next, including the 27 October driving scenes, and is due on Paramount+ in 2027. No opening night date for the Sydney Moulin Rouge! season was given in the sources, and Kidman has not publicly answered the invitation.
