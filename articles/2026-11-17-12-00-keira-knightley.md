**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) says she has seen [Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually) only once, at its premiere when she was 18.
- She made the comments in a video interview with Heart, as reported by Ireland's Classic Hits Radio.
- She says her part took about a week to film when she was 17, so she remembers very little of it.
- Knightley says she is happy the film has such a long life with audiences.

One of the most-watched Christmas films of the century has an unlikely gap in its audience: its own co-star. Keira Knightley has told Heart that she has not watched Love Actually since its premiere, when she was 18, according to a report from [Ireland's Classic Hits Radio](https://www.classichits.ie/news/showbiz/keira-knightley-says-shes-only-watched-love-actually-once/).

## Keira Knightley and Love Actually: a single viewing

Knightley played Juliet in the 2003 romantic comedy. Speaking in a video interview, she said that viewers often look at her in a certain way and quote lines from the film, and she knows that is what they are doing. The references, she said, do not land. "It means nothing to me," she told the station, adding that she is probably the person who knows the film least.

Her distance from the film comes down partly to timing. Knightley described her involvement as short: about a week of work when she was 17. As she put it, she does not really remember it.

## A small role in a large ensemble

The film was directed by [Richard Curtis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Curtis) and follows several linked stories set in London in the run-up to Christmas, each looking at a different kind of love. Over the years it has become a seasonal fixture, according to the Classic Hits Radio report.

Knightley's character sits at the centre of one of its best-known threads. Juliet has just married Peter, played by [Chiwetel Ejiofor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiwetel_Ejiofor). She then learns that his best friend Mark, played by [Andrew Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Lincoln), has secretly been in love with her.

That storyline builds to the cue-card scene on Christmas Eve, in which Mark declares his feelings without saying a word. The sequence has since become one of the best-remembered moments in modern romantic comedy.

## Why the cue-card scene needed care

The report notes that Knightley has said in the past that she found the idea of such a declaration creepy. According to that account, a good deal of thought went into making sure the scene did not read the wrong way. The outlet does not say where she made that earlier comment, and it is not repeated in her latest remarks.

## A film that outgrew its cast

Knightley did not sound bitter about the film's reach. Even though she has stayed away from it, she said she likes that it has "such a big life out there". For an actress who was a teenager when she shot it, that is a generous way to describe a film she has not revisited in more than 20 years.

It also fits her wider career. The report describes her as a Pirates of the Caribbean star, and her recent work has moved well beyond that period.
