**Quick summary**

- Sony Pictures has set Ridley Scott's *Treasure Island* for cinemas on 11 November 2027, per Variety and The Hollywood Reporter.
- Hugh Jackman plays the pirate Long John Silver; Owen Cooper and Hugh Grant also star, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- Scott directs and produces with Michael Pruss. Jack Thorne, the Emmy-winning writer of *Adolescence*, wrote the script.
- Nothing has been reported yet on an Australian release date, a trailer or the filming schedule.

[Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman)'s turn as Long John Silver now has a date. Sony Pictures has scheduled [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott)'s *Treasure Island* for cinemas on 11 November 2027, according to [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/24-jump-street-hugh-jackman-treasure-island-release-dates-40874/), which means the Australian actor's pirate film is roughly a year away.

## Who is in the cast?

Jackman leads. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/24-jump-street-ridley-reasure-island-helldivers-release-1236717471/) says [Owen Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Cooper) and [Hugh Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Grant) also star in the film, which follows a young boy who finds a map to buried treasure. In a conversation with [Collider](https://collider.com/ridley-scott-treasure-island-release-date-november-2027-hugh-jackman-blackbeard/) during the press tour for his latest film, Scott said he was eyeing Cooper and Grant for key roles, so The Hollywood Reporter is the first of these sources to list them as cast.

The story itself is old and well travelled. [The New Daily](https://www.thenewdaily.com.au/life/entertainment/2026/06/12/jackmans-treasure-island-role) notes that [Robert Louis Stevenson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Louis_Stevenson)'s novel has sold more than 100 million copies and been translated into more than 50 languages since it appeared in 1883. It follows Jim Hawkins, a boy who sets out on a dangerous sea voyage for a fortune and ends up in a battle of wits with Silver.

## How did the project come together?

The package surfaced in June. The New Daily, citing Deadline, reported that it was about to be shopped to studios, and that 20th Century, the Disney division behind Scott's *The Dog Stars*, had a first look and passed. The reason given was that Disney already treats *Pirates of the Caribbean* as a priority and did not want a competing pirate project. Sony has since put a date on it.

It is a quick turnaround. Variety says the Jackman and Scott pairing was revealed in June, so the film went from announcement to a dated release slot in about four months.

## Why does the date matter for Scott?

Collider describes 2026 as a slow year for the 88-year-old director. His sci-fi film *The Dog Stars* took about US$39 million at the box office despite a cast that includes [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi), [Josh Brolin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Brolin) and [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley), according to the outlet, which counts it among Scott's lowest-grossing releases. Collider also lists *Gladiator II* and *Napoleon* in what it calls a string of misfires. That is the outlet's view, not an official figure, but it explains why a new Scott release with Jackman in the lead gets attention.

For Jackman, the film follows *The Sheep Detectives*, which The New Daily describes as a surprise box-office hit.

## What else is opening around it?

Sony has placed the film a month ahead of *24 Jump Street*, which opens on 10 December 2027 with [Jonah Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonah_Hill), [Channing Tatum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channing_Tatum) and [Ice Cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_Cube) returning, per The Hollywood Reporter. The studio also moved the video game adaptation *Helldivers* from 10 November 2027, the day before *Treasure Island*, to 9 June 2028.
