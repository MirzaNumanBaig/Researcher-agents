**Quick summary**

- An arbitrator dismissed all eight claims brought by the former nanny of [Jason Sudeikis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Sudeikis) and Olivia Wilde, according to TMZ.
- The ruling also found she willfully breached her confidentiality agreement, TMZ reports.
- The couple was awarded more than $1.27 million in damages, legal fees and costs.
- They have asked a New York judge to confirm parts of the ruling, but are not seeking to enforce the money because of the nanny's bankruptcy.

A long-running employment dispute involving Jason Sudeikis and Olivia Wilde appears to be nearing its end. [TMZ reported on 11 November](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/11/jason-sudeikis-olivia-wilde-nanny-arbitration-results/) that the former couple won a private arbitration against their ex-nanny, Ericka Genaro, and have now gone to a New York court to make key parts of the decision official.

## What the arbitrator decided

According to court documents obtained by TMZ, the arbitration ruling went overwhelmingly in favour of Sudeikis and Wilde. It denied all of Genaro's requests for damages and dismissed each of her eight claims.

The arbitrator also found that Genaro willfully breached a confidentiality agreement, TMZ says. On that basis, the couple was awarded more than $1.27 million in damages, legal fees and costs.

That headline figure comes with a catch. TMZ reports that the pair are not trying to enforce the monetary award in their latest filing because of Genaro's bankruptcy. The petition is about confirming the ruling, not collecting the money.

## How the dispute began

The case dates back to February 2023, when Genaro sued the pair in California. As TMZ describes it, she alleged she was wrongfully terminated after the couple's breakup increased her childcare duties and caused her significant stress and anxiety. She also claimed Sudeikis dismissed her after she asked for three days of medical leave.

These were allegations in a lawsuit, and the arbitrator's ruling, as reported, rejected them.

## Why it went to arbitration

Sudeikis and Wilde argued that Genaro had signed a confidentiality agreement requiring employment disputes to be settled privately, through binding arbitration in New York rather than in a California courtroom. According to TMZ, a California judge agreed and ordered the dispute into private arbitration in New York.

That is where the couple ultimately prevailed. The detail matters because arbitration is normally confidential, so much of what is known about the process comes from the court filings now being made public.

## What happens next

The petition asks a New York judge to formally confirm portions of the arbitration ruling. If the judge grants it, the key findings become official, which TMZ says could bring the legal fight closer to a close. At the time of writing, neither Sudeikis nor Wilde had been quoted publicly on the filing in the source coverage.
