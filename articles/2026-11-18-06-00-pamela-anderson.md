## Pamela Anderson's red coat dress, explained

Pamela Anderson stepped out in New York on Monday wearing a ruby red coat as a dress, finished with sheer mesh heels, on her way to a Pandora dinner. WWD's Footwear News reports that the look was photographed on the Upper East Side.

The outerwear was the Auter Collarless Cashmere Long Sleeve Belted Coat. Auter is the luxury label founded by celebrity stylist Rob Zangardi, and WWD lists the coat at US$1,250. It is cut from double-faced cashmere wool and has a tie belt that cinches the waist, which is what lets it pass as a dress.

Anderson kept the rest of the outfit restrained so the colour did the talking. She added black leather gloves and sheer tights, according to WWD. The shoes were the only other statement piece.

## The Giuseppe Zanotti pumps

She chose the Giuseppe Zanotti Guepiere Pump 105. WWD describes them as black mesh and leather with a 105mm stiletto heel, or about four inches, that tapers toward the base. The toe is elongated with a square-cut vamp and grosgrain-trimmed edges, and a cap toe gives the sheer fabric some structure.

The pumps retail for US$995 and also come in a light brown shade, WWD says. For context, the outlet notes this is not her first outing in the brand this year: she wore Giuseppe Zanotti shoes most recently at the Academy Museum Gala in October.

WWD also points out that the star of "The Last Showgirl" does not stick to one designer. She has moved between Khaite, Tory Burch and Carolina Herrera, as well as Giuseppe Zanotti, to complete her looks. That variety is part of why her shoes get as much attention as her dresses.

## The new bob gets its own spotlight

The same outing drew attention to her hair. Harper's BAZAAR UK called her latest bob voluminous, glossy and versatile, and said she wore it with a deep side parting. The sweep creates lift at the roots and the look of thicker, fuller hair, according to the magazine.

Hairstylist Elliot Bute of Hershesons told the magazine that short hair does not need length to feel full. He credited soft layers, texture and a little lift at the root for the movement and shape. Harper's BAZAAR presents the style as inspiration for anyone refreshing a short cut before the festive season.

The magazine's recreation tips are practical. One stylist, Sarah McKenna, recommends overnight satin heatless curlers with a light oil, then a hairspray or texturising spray in the morning. For a quicker result, the piece suggests blow-drying with a large round brush at the roots and finishing with a lightweight texturising spray or volumising mousse.

Another stylist quoted, Zoë Irwin of John Frieda, advises keeping the cut to one length rather than adding many small layers, and avoiding thinning scissors.
