**Quick summary**

- DC Studios co-head [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) says Harley Quinn is "almost certainly" coming to the DC Universe (DCU).
- He is not sure [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) wants to play her again: "She may be over that."
- If Robbie is keen, Gunn says he will "bend over backwards" to explain it in the story. If not, he may recast.
- The comments came on a one-off podcast, *The Suicide Squad: Is It Canon?*, which was due out on 30 October but reached Spotify early.

Margot Robbie's future as Harley Quinn is now an open question, and the man running DC Studios is the one asking it. On a special podcast episode, James Gunn said Harley will almost certainly appear in the DCU, but that he does not know whether Robbie, who has played her three times, wants to go again.

## What Gunn said on the podcast

According to [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources), the episode was meant to be released on 30 October but was uploaded to Spotify early, and clips quickly spread on X. Gunn appeared with *Peacemaker* stars Jennifer Holland and Steve Agee to work out whether his 2021 film *The Suicide Squad* counts as DCU canon, as reported by The A.V. Club.

Gunn called Robbie "one of my favorite actors to work with" and said he loves her as Harley. Then came the line that headlined most coverage: "I don't know if she wants to play Harley again. She may be over that."

He went on: "I don't know where Margot's head is with all of this. Harley is a character I'm almost certainly going to use in the DCU, and if Margot wants to play her, then I'll bend over backwards to explain that in the world. But if she doesn't, then I might go with somebody else, where I don't have to be attached to what happens to Harley in this movie."

Deadline reports that Holland pointed out Robbie has become an even bigger star since the film. CBR quotes Gunn describing her as "kind of the biggest female star in the world."

## Why Harley is a canon problem

Gunn's hesitation is not only about casting. He said Harley is "one of the more difficult things" to justify as canon, because her story is tied to the Joker, and the Joker has not been established in the DCU. Deadline notes that, so far, only Batman has appeared, in a cameo on the animated series *Creature Commandos*.

Other characters from the film have carried over, among them Peacemaker and Weasel, according to ComicBook.com. Harley is the notable absentee. ComicBook.com adds that Gunn described the film as essentially canon apart from "one small asterisk": what exactly is the future of Harley Quinn.

## Robbie's three turns as Harley

Robbie first played the character in David Ayer's *Suicide Squad* in 2016, returned in 2020's *Birds of Prey*, and appeared again in Gunn's 2021 film, her latest outing so far. CBR says all three can be streamed on HBO Max.

ComicBook.com argues that none of the three were clear box-office or fan-favourite wins, while Robbie's career has since moved on. It points to the billion-dollar *Barbie* and her work as a producer through LuckyChap Entertainment, the company behind *Promising Young Woman*, *Saltburn*, *Wuthering Heights* and *I, Tonya*. The same outlet reports she is set to reunite with Warner Bros. for a Tim Burton remake of *Attack of the 50 Foot Woman*. Those are that outlet's assessments, not Gunn's. For more on her recent work, see our [Margot Robbie archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/margot-robbie/).

## Batman casting also came up

The same episode produced a Batman tease. Just Jared quotes Gunn saying "it won't be too long before we're casting Batman" and that no one has been cast yet, calling online speculation nonsense. He said he wants the best actor for the role rather than a movie star, and that Batman and Superman are roughly the same age, which points to actors close to *Superman* star David Corenswet.

CBR reports no major progress yet on *The Brave and the Bold*, the DCU's first solo Batman film, which remains in early development. That means any Robbie return would not come soon. Task Force X is also defunct in the DCU, so a new *Suicide Squad* film is an unlikely route back.

## What happens next

Nothing is confirmed. Neither Robbie nor Warner Bros. has responded in the coverage we reviewed, and Gunn himself says the decision is hers. The podcast officially launches on 30 October on all platforms. On the film side, ComicBook.com lists *Man of Tomorrow* as the next DCU release, on 9 July 2027. ## Frequently asked questions

### Will Margot Robbie play Harley Quinn in the DCU?
Not confirmed. James Gunn says he does not know whether she wants to return, and will recast if she does not.

### Will Harley Quinn appear in the DCU at all?
Gunn says he is "almost certainly" going to use the character, though no film or date has been announced.

### Is The Suicide Squad (2021) DCU canon?
Gunn told listeners the film is largely canon, with Harley's future as the one open "asterisk", according to ComicBook.com.

### Has the DCU Batman been cast?
No. Gunn said casting is not far away but that no one has been cast yet.
