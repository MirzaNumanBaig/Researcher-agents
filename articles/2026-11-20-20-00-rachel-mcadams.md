**Quick summary**

- [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams) auditioned for the 2002 TV movie Nancy Drew and did not get the part, according to Far Out Magazine.
- While in Los Angeles for that audition, she was given a chance to read for The Hot Chick and won a role in it.
- The Hot Chick, a [Rob Schneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_Schneider) comedy, grossed about US$54 million, per the same report.
- She then played Regina George in [Mean Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_Girls) the following year.

A missed audition in 2002 turned out to be the best thing that happened to Rachel McAdams. The Canadian actor failed to win the lead in a [Nancy Drew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Drew) TV movie, but the trip led to a part in a studio comedy and, a year later, to her best-known role. [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/the-failed-2002-audition-that-led-rachel-mcadams-to-mean-girls/) published the account on 20 November.

## An actor who thought Hollywood was out of reach

McAdams studied theatre at university after acting in amateur productions as a child. Her first roles were in Canadian projects. She told Variety she only travelled to Los Angeles when someone asked her to audition. She admitted that sounds arrogant, and put it down to self-preservation, because Hollywood "felt so far away and impossible", as quoted by Far Out.

That attitude shaped how she approached the trip in 2002. She wanted to be a star, but she did not expect much from it.

## The Nancy Drew miss

The audition that brought her to Los Angeles was for the lead in a TV-movie version of Nancy Drew. [Maggie Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maggie_Lawson) won the part of the teenage sleuth. Far Out says the film made little impact on release in 2002, and that Lawson's later career has been mostly in other TV movies.

McAdams went home from that audition without the role. Far Out's argument is that the rejection mattered more than a win would have.

## The Hot Chick and a lucky relaxed reading

While she was in town, McAdams was offered an audition for The Hot Chick, a comedy starring Rob Schneider. She said she felt very relaxed because she did not think she had any chance of getting it.

She got it. McAdams played Jessica Spencer, a rude high-schooler who swaps bodies with Schneider's criminal character, Clive. Far Out notes the film arrived a year before the Disney body-swap comedy Freaky Friday, which it describes as the better film. Critics largely dismissed The Hot Chick, but it still made around US$54 million at the box office, according to the same outlet.

McAdams described the experience as both petrifying and exhilarating.

## From Jessica Spencer to Regina George

The next year she appeared as [Regina George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_George), the queen bee of the clique known as The Plastics, in Mean Girls opposite [Lindsay Lohan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindsay_Lohan). Far Out calls it the role she needed to establish herself in Hollywood, and says it may not have happened without the Schneider comedy before it.

She did not stay in one lane afterwards. In the same year as Mean Girls she was in the romantic drama The Notebook. Later credits listed by Far Out include the thriller Red Eye, the science-fiction romance The Time Traveler's Wife, the queer romance Disobedience, the drama Spotlight and a Marvel appearance in [Doctor Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Strange).

## What happens next

This is a look back, not an announcement, and the source does not mention any new project. For now, the story is a reminder of how much of a career can turn on one unplanned audition.
