**Quick summary**

- [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) says he would like [Andrew Garfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Garfield) to play a villain on [*Tulsa King*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa_King).
- He made the comment in a November 2026 interview on the podcast "The Rest Is Entertainment", according to TVLine.
- Stallone pictured a "thoughtful" threat rather than a "big, lumbering" one, possibly AI-driven.
- No casting for Garfield has been announced.

Sylvester Stallone has named the actor he would like to see take on his character in *Tulsa King*: Andrew Garfield. In a November 2026 interview on "The Rest Is Entertainment" podcast, Stallone said he would love Garfield as a villain, according to [TVLine](https://www.tvline.com/2318659/sylvester-stallone-wants-andrew-garfield-tulsa-king-villain/). It is a wish, not a casting announcement.

## What Stallone said about Garfield

Stallone told the podcast he had been with Garfield the night before. He said he never expected the two to get along, and that they did. That gave him the idea. "I'd love to have him as a villain in 'Tulsa King' because it would create a whole different dynamic," he said, as quoted by TVLine.

The appeal, as Stallone described it, is the contrast with the usual type. Instead of a "big, lumbering" bad guy, he imagined someone thoughtful. He added that the threat might be AI-driven, or something his character has "no idea how to stop".

TVLine notes that Garfield is not known for menacing roles, which is part of what makes the suggestion unexpected. The outlet points to his 2022 crime drama *Under the Banner of Heaven* as a television credit worth watching, and recommends it to fans of [*True Detective*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective).

## How much say Stallone has

*Tulsa King* was created by [Taylor Sheridan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_Sheridan), the writer behind *Yellowstone*, so casting is not Stallone's decision alone. He did tell the podcast that he has more creative control than most actors do.

Stallone recalled that his character, Dwight "The General" Manfredi, was originally very dark and violent. He asked Sheridan whether it could be toned down. By his account, Sheridan told him he was on his own and to enjoy it. "I write my character, whatever scenes I'm in," Stallone said.

That control is why the idea carries some weight, though TVLine is clear on the limits. As of its report, no official plans have been announced for Garfield to join the series, and Stallone has only made his interest known.

## What happens next

Nothing has been confirmed. A casting announcement would normally come from the network or the show's producers, and none has been made. For now this is a stated wish from the show's lead.
