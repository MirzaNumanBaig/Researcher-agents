**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams), 25, spoke to The Hollywood Reporter for a digital cover story published this week.
- He said his close team shrugs off online hate by leaning on each other and laughing at made-up rumours.
- He admitted to being nervous about returning to the set of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) for season two.
- Filming is under way, and Williams says the set still feels small and intimate despite new cast members.

[Hudson Williams](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hudson-williams/) has said the internet can be cruel to anyone in the spotlight, and that the people around him are what keep it bearable. In a new interview with The Hollywood Reporter, reported by [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/18/hudson-williams-reveals-how-he-his-team-handle-online-hate-why-he-was-nervous-to-return-for-heated-rivalry-season-2/) on 18 November, the actor also opened up about his nerves before going back to work on Heated Rivalry season two.

## Who is in the "quad squad"?

Williams, 25, has had a fast year. Just Jared notes that Heated Rivalry, the Crave series that premiered nearly a year ago, set off a run of public appearances for him, including fashion events, awards shows and the Met Gala.

He is usually joined at those events by three people: his stylist Anastasia Walker, groomer Aika Flores and branding agent Leilani Houston. The group calls itself the "quad squad". Our earlier piece on [Williams and Aika Flores](https://moduscarry.com/hudson-williams-aika-flores/) looks at that friendship in more detail.

## How the team deals with online hate

In the Hollywood Reporter cover story, Williams and Flores were asked about the commentary their group attracts online. Williams put it down to the nature of social media. "A lot of times social media is just inherently mean, and so everyone gets shot at online," he said, adding that the group always has each other.

Flores described much of the commentary as speculation or invention. She said the team laughs when it comes across the more outlandish claims, because they find them so silly. Williams agreed that having each other is the comforting part.

None of the claims was specified in the report, so there is nothing to confirm or deny. What the quotes show is a team that treats the noise as a joke rather than something to answer.

## Why he was nervous about season two

The second season is the part fans have been waiting for. According to Just Jared, it will largely follow Rachel Reid's novel "The Long Game", the second book about Shane and Ilya's love story, and a third season has been reported. Williams has spent the past few months filming.

He told The Hollywood Reporter that he grew anxious in the lead-up, worried about how the set would feel after the show's success. Those fears eased once he was back. It is the same crew, he said, and director Jacob Tierney still makes the production feel small and intimate.

The new cast members also settled in quickly. "They immediately picked up on the vibe and were so sweet," Williams said, describing a group that supports one another. In his words, it is "just a bigger family now."

He was candid that the material is heavy. Filming is "going good, but it's sad," he said. Just Jared explains that the book has Shane and Ilya hiding their relationship because Shane fears for his career and reputation, while Ilya struggles with depression and goes to therapy.

## What happens next

Season two of Heated Rivalry is still in production, and no premiere date appears in the sources we reviewed. Just Jared reports that several characters from the wider Game Changers universe are joining the cast.

Williams will also appear in Netflix's The Altruists, which the same report describes as coming soon. For more of our coverage, see everything we publish on [TV & Dramas](https://moduscarry.com/category/tv-dramas/).
