**Quick summary**

- Marvel Animation has confirmed that Deadpool will appear in Season 3 of the Disney+ series X-Men '97, according to The Direct.
- The reveal came in the season's first trailer at New York Comic-Con, which showed Deadpool as a guest at Rogue and Gambit's wedding.
- Ryan Reynolds has not been confirmed for Avengers: Doomsday; his appearance there remains a widely repeated rumour.
- Mid-Day reports that Reynolds is also expected at the Los Angeles premiere of Ramayana, though we could not read the full report.

[Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) has a confirmed Deadpool appearance on the way, but not the one fans have been guessing about. Marvel Animation used a first trailer at [New York Comic-Con](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Comic_Con) to say that [Wade Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deadpool) will turn up in the third season of X-Men '97, according to [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/marvel-studios-deadpool-avengers-doomsday-cameo).

## What Marvel has actually confirmed

The trailer places Deadpool as a guest at the long-awaited wedding of Rogue and Gambit. The Direct notes that this is the character's first true appearance in the animated series. In Season 2 a Deadpool was seen on screen, but that was Morph impersonating him.

The outlet adds a caution that is easy to miss. The version in Season 3, due next summer, is a Variant and is likely to be a non-speaking guest, so fans should not expect a full Reynolds performance. The Direct also points out that Marvel Studios once described Wade Wilson as "off-limits" to the show's original showrunner, Beau DeMayo, during its early seasons. Putting him in the wedding scene is a change of course.

## The Avengers: Doomsday rumours

The confirmed cameo landed just as talk about [Avengers: Doomsday](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avengers%3A_Doomsday) reached what The Direct calls a new high. Reynolds has played Deadpool across four outings, starting with the Variant in X-Men Origins: Wolverine, then two solo films under Fox, and the 2024 team-up Deadpool & Wolverine with Hugh Jackman.

None of that has been officially tied to Doomsday. The Direct says Marvel has begun featuring Deadpool in official Doomsday merchandise, which it reads as a strong hint. It also reports, as rumour, that Deadpool and Wolverine appear in the film's opening sequence, alongside the X-Men, facing [Tobey Maguire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobey_Maguire)'s Spider-Man during a collision between two worlds. Treat all of that as unconfirmed until Marvel says otherwise.

Reynolds appeared at the Marvel Studios panel at San Diego Comic-Con in July with a stunt related to Jean Grey, the outlet notes, despite not being announced for any upcoming Marvel project.

## A tricky fit for the next Marvel era

The Direct raises a practical question about where an R-rated Deadpool sits in Marvel's plans. Reynolds is 50, a decade older than Christopher Abbott, the new actor playing [Charles Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X), and the rebooted mutants around him are mostly in their 20s. A PG-13 Doomsday scene would also be Deadpool's first outside X-Men Origins, so the adults-only humour would be off the table.

There are reports, which the outlet frames as unconfirmed, that Reynolds wants to bring back Wolverine, Cable and Storm in an untitled Deadpool & Wolverine sequel. The Direct notes that Marvel already has a new, 23-year-old Storm in Maya Boyd, which complicates that idea. Reynolds himself said in April that Deadpool might step back into a supporting role after the success of Deadpool & Wolverine.

## Ramayana premiere

Separately, Mid-Day reports in a short item that Reynolds will join [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) and Yash at the Ramayana premiere in Los Angeles. The page was not readable for us, so we have only the headline and no further detail on the event or his role.

## What happens next

X-Men '97 Season 3 is due next summer, and Doomsday's release will show whether the Deadpool rumours hold. The Direct says Marvel appears to be keeping his role a surprise for release day. Until then, only the animated cameo is confirmed. ## Frequently asked questions

### Is Ryan Reynolds in Avengers: Doomsday?
Marvel has not confirmed it. The Direct describes his appearance as heavily rumoured, and it cites Deadpool's use in official merchandise as a hint only.

### Is Deadpool in X-Men '97 Season 3?
Yes. Marvel Animation confirmed it in the season's first trailer. The character appears at Rogue and Gambit's wedding, and The Direct says he is a Variant and probably non-speaking.

### When does X-Men '97 Season 3 arrive?
The Direct says the Deadpool appearance is set for next summer. We could not find a more precise date in the sources.
