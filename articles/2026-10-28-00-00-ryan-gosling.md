**Quick summary**

- Dead Man's Bones, the band of [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) and Zach Shields, has released "Lost in the Night", its first new song since 2009.
- A black-and-white video stars [Noah Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Jupe) and [Rain Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_Spencer).
- The single was produced with longtime collaborator [Tim Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Anderson_(musician)) and is available on 12-inch vinyl through the band's website, according to Bloody Disgusting.
- No album has been announced, but the band's Instagram post promised "More coming soon", Rolling Stone reports.

Seventeen years after their only album, Ryan Gosling and Zach Shields have brought Dead Man's Bones back to life. The duo released "Lost in the Night" on 27 October 2026, with a music video that follows a young couple out of a cemetery and into a city after dark.

It lands just before Halloween, which suits a band whose debut was built around spooky organ tunes and a children's choir.

## What "Lost in the Night" sounds like

Rolling Stone describes a track that opens with an eerie guitar clang before picking up speed, landing between raw post-punk production and arena-sized emotion. Billboard calls it spectral, with deadpan vocals from Gosling and a spare, driving beat that recalls 1970s synth-punk pioneers Suicide.

The band's own description, quoted by Bloody Disgusting, goes further. It says the single mixes Suicide's drum-machine rawness with Bruce Springsteen's anthemic sweep, new wave synths and post-punk guitars. The chorus repeats the line "Lost in the night with you", per the lyrics Billboard printed.

That is a long way from 2009. Bloody Disgusting notes the shift towards electronic music, though it argues the song still fits the group's eerie legacy.

## How it was made

Gosling and Shields first tried to record a follow-up in a couple of Los Angeles studios and couldn't find the sound they wanted, according to Rolling Stone and Billboard. They took two guitars, two drum machines and two synths to a barn or farm outside the city instead. Billboard, citing the release, says the song was recorded in a day.

Anderson, who also produced the 2009 debut, handled production again. Billboard adds that the song was written by Gosling and Shields and co-produced by the pair with Anderson.

## The video and the teaser

The clip stars Jupe (*Hamnet*) and Spencer (*The Summer I Turned Pretty*). Billboard says they dance out of a cemetery and through the night, laughing and hugging. UNN, citing Deadline, says the video opens with Spencer walking through the cemetery before cutting to Jupe lying on a bench.

The first hint came on Instagram on 22 October, UNN reports. Actress Eva Mendes reposted the teaser, and Billboard quotes her caption: "He came through on this promise to me."

## Where the band left off

Dead Man's Bones released one self-titled album in 2009, recorded with the Silverlake Conservatory Children's Choir. Bloody Disgusting says the band used local children's choirs on its 2009 and 2010 tours. Billboard notes Gosling played piano, guitar, synthesizer, percussion, cello and bass on it, as well as singing lead.

Rolling Stone says the album drew positive reviews and buzz but faded quickly. Bloody Disgusting adds that James Wan later used the track "In the Room Where You Sleep" in *The Conjuring*.

Meanwhile, both men have been busy. Gosling's recent credits include *Barbie* and *Project Hail Mary*, and Bloody Disgusting lists *Star Wars: Starfighter* and a *Ghost Rider* film as upcoming. Shields has written and produced for Hollywood, including *Krampus*, and Rolling Stone says he is working on the *Weapons* prequel *Gladys*.

## What happens next

The band hasn't said whether a record is coming. The Instagram promise of "More coming soon" is the only guidance so far. For now, the single is out, the video is online, and the vinyl is on sale through the band's official site.
