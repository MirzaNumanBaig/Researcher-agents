**Quick summary**

- The Walt Disney Studios announced on 20 October that Christopher Nolan's *The Prestige* will return to cinemas on 29 January 2027.
- The 20th-anniversary run includes the film's first IMAX presentations, plus 70mm, 35mm and digital 2D screenings.
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and [Christian Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bale) play the rival magicians at the centre of the story.
- Ticket details and participating cinemas are expected closer to the release. No Australian dates have been published in the reports we read.

Hugh Jackman's turn as a ruthless Victorian showman is heading back to the big screen. The Walt Disney Studios announced on 20 October that [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan)'s *The Prestige* will be re-released in cinemas on 29 January 2027, marking 20 years since it opened.

## What Disney has announced

According to [FlickDirect](https://flickdirect.com/news/8850/the-prestige-returns-for-20th-anniversary-with-first-ever-imax-release/article.ashx), the announcement landed exactly 20 years after the film first opened in US theatres. The anniversary engagement will include IMAX screenings for the first time, along with 70mm, 35mm and digital 2D showings. Disney has also released a new poster for the occasion.

The IMAX billing carries a twist. FlickDirect notes that *The Prestige* was not shot with IMAX cameras, so this will be the first time the film has been presented in the format at all. Nolan is closely tied to large-format filmmaking, which makes the choice a natural fit for the anniversary.

Nolan said in a press statement, quoted by [Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/entertainment/articles/christopher_nolans_the_prestige_gets_theatrical_re_release/s1_17316_44452574) from Deadline, that he was "very excited to bring The Prestige back to the big screen". He added that he was particularly thrilled to offer audiences the chance to see it on 70mm film, and on IMAX for the first time.

## Jackman's role in the story

Jackman plays Robert Angier, a charismatic showman. Bale plays Alfred Borden, a gifted illusionist. The two begin as friends and partners in turn-of-the-century London, until a stage illusion goes tragically wrong. What follows, as FlickDirect describes it, is a rivalry of deception and sabotage as each man becomes consumed with building the ultimate trick.

The cast also includes [Michael Caine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Caine) and [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson). Yardbarker, which syndicated a Nerdist piece, also points to [David Bowie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Bowie) as Nikola Tesla.

The timing in Jackman's career is worth noting. Yardbarker observes that he had already been playing Wolverine for six years when the film came out, and that Bale's first turn as Batman in [Batman Begins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Begins) had arrived shortly before. Two superhero names were facing off as stage rivals.

## Behind the camera

Nolan and [Jonathan Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Nolan) adapted the screenplay from Christopher Priest's novel of the same name, according to FlickDirect. The film was produced by [Emma Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Thomas), [Aaron Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Ryder) and Nolan.

On its original release it received two Academy Award nominations, for Best Cinematography and Best Art Direction. FlickDirect says its intricate storytelling and performances have kept a following over the years. The film's structure also mirrors the three stages of a magic trick, using misdirection and carefully revealed information to keep viewers questioning what they have seen.

## What happens next

The re-release is set for 29 January 2027. FlickDirect reports that ticket information and participating locations are expected to be announced closer to the date. The reports we read describe the release as nationwide in the US and do not say when, or whether, the same formats will screen in Australia, so local fans will need to wait for cinema listings.
