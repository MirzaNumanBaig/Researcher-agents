**Quick summary**

- A Yahoo round-up of 22 outfits traces how Alexander Skarsgård's red carpet style changed over roughly nine months of press.
- It dates the first real shift to the Murderbot series premiere on 28 April 2025, when he wore a long black trench coat and sunglasses.
- His most talked-about look, according to Yahoo, was a black leather and white halter outfit at the BFI London Film Festival on 18 October 2025.
- He told GQ that long press tours risk monotony, so he prefers to have fun with what he wears.

[Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) used to be a black-suit-and-grey-suit regular. A [Yahoo round-up of 22 of his recent outfits](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/22-times-alexander-skarsg-rd-161601603.html) argues that this changed during the press runs for *Murderbot* and *Pillion*, and that the gap between his old and new wardrobe is now hard to miss.

## Why he says he dresses this way

Yahoo quotes a GQ interview in which the actor explained his thinking. A long press tour can mean 40 stops in two months, he said, and that can get monotonous, so he asks why not enjoy it instead of rotating between a black suit and a grey one. It is a practical answer rather than a statement about fashion.

## The Murderbot start

According to the round-up, the experiment began quietly. At the *Murderbot* series premiere on 28 April 2025 he wore a long black trench coat and sunglasses, which Yahoo calls a gentle first step. On 13 May 2025 he appeared on *The Kelly Clarkson Show* in a salmon-pink top and a floral cardigan, and on the same day on *The Late Show With Stephen Colbert* in a jacket the writer describes as pure 1980s.

## Pillion pushes it further

Yahoo ties the louder outfits to the promotion of *Pillion*, a film it describes as a BDSM story. At the 78th Cannes Film Festival he stood with co-star [Harry Melling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Melling) in leather trousers, boots and a graphic tee. Other Cannes looks listed include a pinstripe jacket with an oversized pink bow tie and dark blue sequinned trousers, and a double-breasted black suit finished with thigh-high leather boots.

After a quiet few months, the list picks up again in October 2025:

- Zurich Film Festival, early October: a cream shirt with a graphic print that nods to the themes of *Pillion*.
- New York Film Festival: a green-and-red plaid shirt with an oversized leather jacket and cuffed jeans.
- BFI London Film Festival, 18 October 2025: black leather trousers that tie at the front, a white leather halter top and a black leather tie, worn to the *Pillion* headline gala. Yahoo calls it arguably his most iconic look of the run.
- Valladolid International Film Festival in Spain, late October: a vintage-style graphic tee, green-laced sneakers and orange aviator sunglasses.

## Awards season and Sundance

The pace continued after the festival circuit. On 15 November 2025 he attended a special *Murderbot* screening in a rope-stripe suit. At the 28th British Independent Film Awards on 30 November he chose a black double-breasted suit with a bright blue top and a bow tie. The 35th Gotham Film Awards on 1 December saw a pink suit jacket over a black shirt and cream tie.

In late January, Yahoo notes, he wore a white collared shirt with high-waisted, double-buckled trousers to a Sundance party, and a heavily layered outfit with black flip-flops at the festival. The visible source text ends partway through the list, so the remaining entries are not covered here.

## What happens next

Nothing in the round-up announces a new tour, but his recent fashion has tracked his film calendar closely. More outfits are likely whenever his next project reaches a premiere or festival.

### When did Alexander Skarsgård's style start to change?
