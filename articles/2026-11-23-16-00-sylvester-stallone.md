**Quick summary**

- *I Play Rocky*, the biopic about [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone)'s early career, earned about US$9.3 million over the 20 to 22 November weekend, according to MovieWeb.
- It finished second, behind the much larger opening of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- Audiences gave it an A+ CinemaScore and a 98% verified audience score on Rotten Tomatoes.
- It played in 2,477 theatres after expanding from seven.

*I Play Rocky* finished second at the US box office this weekend with an estimated US$9.3 million, according to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/i-play-rocky-box-office-update/). That is a strong result for a mid-budget biopic that only reached a wide audience days ago.

## How the weekend played out

Amazon MGM added 2,448 theatres to the seven that carried the film earlier in November, bringing the total to 2,477. MovieWeb reports that the first wide Friday took US$3.8 million, nearly 1,959% above the previous week's figure.

The film was up against a franchise launch playing in 4,018 theatres, so second place is a realistic position. For comparison, *I Play Rocky* used roughly 60% as many screens as the winner.

## The audience scores

Directed by Peter Farrelly, the film stars [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) as the young Stallone. Audiences gave it an A+ CinemaScore, a mark MovieWeb says only 136 films have reportedly reached. Rocky III is on that list too.

On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the verified audience score sits at 98%. According to MovieWeb, that is higher than any of the nine *Rocky* and *Creed* films, where *Creed III* held the franchise high at 95%. Critics are warm as well, with the film [Certified Fresh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes) at 88 to 89%.

Farrelly has been here before. MovieWeb notes that *Green Book* gave him his first A+ CinemaScore in 2018, on its way to winning [Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_common_film_awards_categories).

## Where the demand came from

The limited run hinted at this. The seven-theatre opening averaged US$48,209 per location, which MovieWeb counts among the strongest limited openings of the year. The film had also drawn attention on the awards circuit after its Toronto premiere.

The wide release landed alongside celebrations for the 50th anniversary of the original *Rocky*. The story the film tells is a fitting match: in 1976 Stallone turned down big money because [United Artists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Artists) wanted an established star in the lead, and he insisted on playing it himself. Ippolito, a relative newcomer, has drawn most of the praise for his portrayal.

MovieWeb adds that Stallone's own blessing has hovered over the project without becoming a full endorsement. For more on that thread, see our report on [Farrelly's account of the mix-up](https://moduscarry.com/sylvester-stallone-i-play-rocky-mix-up/).

The coming Thanksgiving period is the next test, and MovieWeb suggests the underdog angle may keep helping the film.
