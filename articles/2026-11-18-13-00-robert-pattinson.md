**Quick summary**

- On the Robert Pattinson Bond casting, Amazon founder Jeff Bezos says he was "heavily involved" in picking the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- He told Semafor's Silicon Valley & The World conference that a large group was cut to five finalists, who each did three scenes in costume on real sets.
- The final decision was made on Friday; Amazon MGM announced the casting on Monday.
- Bezos admitted the team was initially "slightly biased" against Pattinson because he is already so famous.

The Robert Pattinson Bond casting was settled by a full screen-test process, according to Jeff Bezos. Speaking at a Semafor conference on Tuesday, the Amazon chairman said he took an active part in choosing the next 007 (now [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)) and that the shortlist was tested the old-fashioned way, [as reported by Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/jeff-bezos-chose-robert-pattinson-james-bond-1237227282/).

## How the Robert Pattinson Bond casting was decided

"We ran a really tight process, and we made the final decision on Friday," Bezos said, as quoted by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/jeff-bezos-involved-casting-robert-pattinson-james-bond-1236950970/). He described a big group of candidates that was narrowed to five finalists.

Each of the five then did three different scenes in full costume, on real sets, opposite other actors. The group met on Friday for a couple of hours to go through the footage.

The Hollywood Reporter [says](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/jeff-bezos-chose-robert-pattinson-as-james-bond-amazon-mgm-1236757909/) Bezos worked alongside the Amazon MGM leadership team, producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman, and director Denis Villeneuve. The outlet names the finalists as Callum Turner, Josh O'Connor, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult and Pattinson. Variety is more careful: it notes Bezos never named them, and that industry analysts had pointed to the same four names before the weekend.

## "Slightly biased" against fame

The most telling remark was about Pattinson's profile. Bezos said the group started "slightly biased" against him because he is already so famous, and had thought about choosing someone more unknown.

The screen tests changed that. "It was so obvious: He is James Bond," Bezos said. He added that all five actors were extraordinarily talented, but that the response to Pattinson was "immediately un-debated" and that Villeneuve and the others gravitated to him.

## The man holding the keys

The Hollywood Reporter calls the casting the biggest move yet for Amazon since it acquired MGM. Variety adds that the deal for MGM closed in 2022, but Amazon only gained creative control of the franchise after buying out longtime Bond producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli for US$20 million. Villeneuve and Pattinson's film will be the first released since that deal.

The Hollywood Reporter also points out the irony that Bond's villains have often been tech tycoons and media figures, such as Hugo Drax in Moonraker and Elliot Carver in Tomorrow Never Dies.

## Hollywood reacts

The announcement drew quick responses. In a separate [Hollywood Reporter roundup](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/hollywood-celebs-react-robert-pattinson-james-bond-casting-1236757856/), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) said Pattinson had been the "no. 1 vote" in her household for a long time. [Brad Pitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Pitt) told ExtraTV he loved the idea and called Pattinson "the real deal".

[Rami Malek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rami_Malek), who worked with him on the last Twilight film, said he "could not be more proud". Elizabeth Debicki, his Tenet co-star, called him "perfect"; we covered her comments in [Elizabeth Debicki Calls Robert Pattinson 'Perfect' for Bond](https://moduscarry.com/elizabeth-debicki-james-bond/). Ralph Fiennes, who has played M, said he is still attached to Daniel Craig but is sure Pattinson "will be great".

In his own statement, Pattinson called Bond "the honor of a lifetime" and thanked Villeneuve, Pascal, Heyman, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios.

## What happens next

No release date has been given in the sources. Pattinson's recent work includes Primetime, which Wikipedia lists with a US$14.5 million budget and about US$62 million at the box office. His next scheduled film is The Take, due on 15 January 2027, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primetime_(film)). Casting for the rest of the Bond film has not been announced; for earlier coverage, see [Robert Pattinson Is the New James Bond: What We Know](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-casting/) and [our full Robert Pattinson archive](https://moduscarry.com/tag/robert-pattinson/).
