**Quick summary**

- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) says he is writing a film that includes an ex-hockey player, and the overlap with his role in [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) has the team "getting tripped up".
- He made the comments to [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly) while promoting the series Yaga, which premiered on 23 October.
- The script has no announced title, studio or release window, and Williams did not describe the plot.
- Heated Rivalry Season 2 began production in August and is set to premiere in spring 2027.

Hudson Williams has revealed that he is writing a film, and that one of its characters, a retired hockey player, is causing an unexpected headache. Speaking to Entertainment Weekly, and reported by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/hudson-williams-new-film-heated-rivalry-parallel-1236930742/) and [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/10/hudson-williams-writing-film-ex-hockey-player-character-1237172776/) this week, the actor said the echo of his breakout hockey series is making the team reconsider.

## What Williams said about the script

The remarks came during press for Yaga, the Canadian witchy detective-thriller in which Williams stars. According to Variety, he was responding to co-star [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss), who had a hang-up about referencing her real-life friend [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) and his John Wick films in the show.

"I'm writing a movie right now, but one character is an ex-hockey player," Williams said, as quoted by both Variety and Deadline. He explained that the team worried about pairing a hockey player with an actor who has "done a hockey show", and that they are "considering other sports". Still, he said, the character made far more sense with a hockey past.

He described the problem as needing to "bust through the prison of your own mind". Variety noted that he did not elaborate on the project.

## Why the hockey link matters

Williams plays Shane Hollander, a Montreal Metros player, in Heated Rivalry, the Crave Original series adapted by Jacob Tierney from Rachel Reid's Game Changers novels, according to Deadline. The show follows two rival hockey players whose relationship becomes hard to separate from their careers, per The Times of India.

That success is the very reason the new script is awkward. A former player on screen invites comparison with Shane, and Williams and his collaborators clearly want to avoid it.

Williams is not new to writing. Culture.org reports that before Heated Rivalry he wrote and directed short films including Soakers, Buttons and Inside Augustine Mendoza's Quotidian Apartment. The same outlet, citing PinkNews, put his age at 25.

## A busy slate on and off the ice

Yaga arrived on 23 October, and Culture.org, citing PinkNews, says Williams plays two roles in the AMC+ series. Production on Season 2 of Heated Rivalry began in August, Variety reports.

Two further films are lined up, according to Variety. Williams appears opposite Dylan O'Brien in the dark comedic thriller Apparatus. He is also part of the ensemble culinary thriller Tyrant, alongside [Charlize Theron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlize_Theron), [Julia Garner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Garner), [Demi Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demi_Moore) and [Omar Apollo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Apollo).

## Heated Rivalry Season 2 and its growing cast

Bell Media has confirmed that Season 2 will premiere in spring 2027, as The Times of India reports. It draws mainly on The Long Game, the sixth book in Reid's series, and Williams returns alongside Connor Storrie as Ilya Rozanov.

Deadline lists a long supporting cast: Justice Smith, Charlie Gillespie, [Daniel Diemer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Diemer), Shaheen Jafargholi, Emily Hampshire, Edvin Ryding, Robert Naylor, Sabrina Jalees, Priyanka, Brendan Morgan, David Tomlinson, Gerry Dee, Michelle Mylett, Lauren Chan and Nick Stahl.

The Times of India adds that Smith and Gillespie play Harris Drover and Troy Barrett, characters from the fifth book, Role Model. Diemer and Jafargholi, announced on 25 August, play Ryan Price and Fabian Salah, who come from Tough Guy.
