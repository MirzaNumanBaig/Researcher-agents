**Quick summary**

- Waitrose has released *The Perfect Gift 2*, a six-minute Christmas romcom sequel starring [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) as herself, according to B&T.
- [Joe Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Wilkinson) returns as Phil, and Alex Hassell joins as a new love rival.
- Knightley told Heart she has seen *Love Actually* only once, at its premiere when she was 18.
- Season 2 of *Black Doves*, which she leads alongside [Ben Whishaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Whishaw), is now streaming on Netflix.

The Keira Knightley Waitrose ad is back for Christmas. The British supermarket has unveiled *The Perfect Gift 2*, a sequel to last year's festive short film, and Knightley again plays a version of herself, this time caught on her wedding day between the wrong groom and a beef Wellington, [B&T reports](https://www.bandt.com.au/in-between-two-men-a-beef-wellington-keira-knightley-returns-for-sequel-to-waitroses-the-perfect-gift/).

It lands in the same week as her candid remarks about a very different Christmas favourite, and a new season of her Netflix spy series.

## What happens in The Perfect Gift 2

According to B&T, Knightley is due to marry at three o'clock, but not to Phil. Joe Wilkinson's lovelorn character won her heart in last year's ad over the Waitrose cheese counter. This time the groom is Hugo Bunty-Griffin, played by Alex Hassell and described as a suave rival who treats his own wedding as a photoshoot.

Phil, meanwhile, is in full heartbreak mode. His friends turn up with Waitrose bags and a plan to bake what the release calls the beef Wellington of his life.

Knightley said she was not expecting to find herself "caught between two men and a beef Wellington", adding that the shoot was great fun.

## A six-minute ad, built like a film

Waitrose chief customer officer Nathan Ansell said customers asked what happened next after last year's film. The retailer cut trailers to draw viewers in and put the full film online, as a studio would.

Ansell said the mini-film has been extended by two minutes to six, which Waitrose describes as the longest advert produced by a UK supermarket. B&T's credits list Biscuit Filmworks as the production company. That is a company claim, not an independent ranking.

## Knightley on Love Actually

Separately, Knightley has been talking about *[Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually)*. Speaking to Heart in a video shared on 9 November, she said she had not watched it since the premiere, when she was 18. "I think I'm the person that knows it the least of anybody," she said, as quoted by [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437987/keira-knightley-reveals-shes-only-seen-love-actually-once).

She said she can tell when people are quoting the film at her, but that "it means nothing to me". Her time on set was brief: "a week when I was 17", so she does not remember it much. She still likes that the film has "such a big life out there".

[The A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/keira-knightley-love-actually-references-dont-compute) notes she was absent from a 2022 reunion for the film. It also recalls her 2024 interview with the Los Angeles Times, in which she said she remembers the "slightly stalkerish" aspect of the film's cue-card scene.

## Black Doves returns for season 2

Knightley also headlines the second season of *[Black Doves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Doves)*, now on Netflix. She plays Helen, a spy who is also the wife of the prime minister, opposite Whishaw as the assassin Sam.

Reviews are warm but mixed. [Fangirlish](https://fangirlish.com/2026/11/10/black-doves-season-2-review-netflix-keira-knightley/) praised the writing and the friendship at the show's centre, while flagging plot questions, such as how Helen moves around without security. [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/black-doves-season-2-review-keira-knightley-ben-whishaw-spy-thriller-netflix-swings-between-gore-grit-and-giggles/article71685412.ece) found the season better plotted than the first but unsure whether it wants to be gritty or cartoonish.

Waitrose has made the full six-minute film available online alongside shorter trailers. *Black Doves* season 2 is streaming now, so viewers can judge the reviews themselves.
