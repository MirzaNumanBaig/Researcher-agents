**Quick summary**

- Four masked men, two reportedly armed with knives, entered the Broken Head home of Chris Hemsworth and Elsa Pataky early on Wednesday, 11 November, and took three vehicles.
- No one was hurt, two vehicles have been found, and New South Wales Police say their investigation continues with no arrests reported.
- On 12 November, Hemsworth announced he is co-founder of KINSYN, a personalised wellness platform.
- His 2015 film In the Heart of the Sea is back in Netflix's global top ten after arriving on 5 November.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) had a news-heavy 48 hours: police are investigating a break-in at his family home near Byron Bay, he has put his name to a new health company, and one of his older films has found a second life on streaming.

## What police say about the Broken Head break-in

The incident happened in the early hours of Wednesday, 11 November, at the couple's property in Broken Head, south of Byron Bay. [MARCA](https://www.marca.com/en/lifestyle/celebrities/2026/11/12/elsa-pataky-and-chris-hemsworth-s-mansion-in-australia-is-raided.html) reports that New South Wales Police confirmed four people entered the grounds and took three vehicles from the garage, and that no injuries were reported.

The story was first reported by Australia's 7News, according to MARCA, citing sources close to the couple. Security cameras reportedly captured four men with their faces covered, two of them apparently carrying knives. They are said to have got past the perimeter fencing and into a garage where the keys were left in the vehicles.

[Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky) was at home with the couple's three children. She reportedly woke around 2am when the dogs barked, then went back to sleep, and only saw the intruders later while reviewing camera footage, [El Mundo America](https://www.mundoamerica.com/celebrity/2026/11/12/6af5cf63fdddff3a7f8b45a8.html) says. Police were alerted at about 7:30am.

The stolen vehicles were reportedly a BYD, a Ram truck and a [Ford Raptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Raptor). Two have since been recovered, one at Coraki, roughly an hour away, and one at Tabulam, more than two hours away. The third is still missing. Police have asked the public for information. We covered the first reports in our [earlier post on the break-in](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-home-break-in/).

El Mundo America adds that sources close to the family described the episode as distressing but said the area remains a quiet one.

## The Chris Hemsworth KINSYN launch

Away from the headlines at home, Hemsworth's business news arrived on 12 November. According to a [PR Newswire release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chris-hemsworth-launches-kinsyn-as-co-founder-bringing-personalized-wellness-medical-care-and-health-data-together-in-one-platform-302937319.html) issued from Los Angeles, KINSYN is a personalised wellness platform that combines physician-guided care, lab testing, supplements and health data in one service.

The company says it is designed to connect records from participating US healthcare systems with biomarker and wearable data, using AI to help providers shape individual recommendations. Medical care is delivered through affiliated practices run by licensed professionals, and a scientific advisory board of physicians and researchers guides the platform. These are the company's own claims, and the release comes from KINSYN itself.

"Longevity is not just about living longer," Hemsworth said in the release, describing it as a question of how people experience their lives along the way. KINSYN chief executive Matt Kawadler said the aim is for healthcare to be as unique as the individual.

The launch is described in US terms, so it is not clear from the release whether it will be available to Australian customers.

## In the Heart of the Sea returns to Netflix

The film side of the week is lighter. [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard)'s sea-disaster drama In the Heart of the Sea, released in December 2015, was added to Netflix on 5 November. [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/in-the-heart-of-the-sea-stream-netflix-november-2026/) says it was the eighth most-watched film on the service globally at the time of writing, while [The Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/tv/tv-news/masterpiece-period-movie-starring-chris-37864514) reported it had reached the top of the most-watched list. The two outlets measured at different moments, so the position has clearly moved.

The comparison with its original run is striking. MovieWeb puts the budget at about $100 million and the box office at roughly $90 million, with a 42% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes from more than 200 reviews and a 54% audience score. It opened against Star Wars: The Force Awakens, which MovieWeb says grossed about $2 billion.

Hemsworth plays Owen Chase, a whaler on the Essex, a ship sunk in 1820 by a giant whale. The Mirror notes the story is based on Nathaniel Philbrick's 2000 book and was a real-life inspiration for Herman Melville's Moby-Dick. Cillian Murphy and Tom Holland co-star.

## Frequently asked questions

### Was anyone hurt in the Chris Hemsworth break-in?

No injuries were reported. The family was at home, according to Australian media reports cited by MARCA and El Mundo America.

### Have the stolen vehicles been found?

Two of three were recovered, at Coraki and Tabulam. The third had not been found at the time of the reports.

### What is KINSYN?

KINSYN is a personalised wellness platform co-founded by Hemsworth. It says it combines medical care, testing, supplements and health data, starting with participating US healthcare systems.

### Why is In the Heart of the Sea trending?

The 2015 film joined Netflix on 5 November and quickly climbed the global viewing charts, according to MovieWeb and The Mirror.
