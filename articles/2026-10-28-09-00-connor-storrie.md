**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is reportedly in talks to join Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider*, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- He would play a villain opposite [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) as Johnny Blaze, but the character has not been named.
- The film is set for release on 28 July 2028, with filming planned for New York in 2027.
- Nothing is final: the reports describe negotiations, not a signed deal.

Connor Storrie, the breakout star of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), is in talks to join the cast of Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider*, The Hollywood Reporter has reported. Outlets that followed the story on 27 October say he would play an antagonist to Gosling's Johnny Blaze, in a film that Marvel has dated for 28 July 2028.

## What has been reported

[Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/10/27/is-connor-storrie-joining-the-mcu-heated-rivalry-star-in-talks-to-join-ryan-goslings-ghost-rider/) says the 26-year-old actor is in talks, citing The Hollywood Reporter, and that the trade describes the part as a villain without saying which one. [Collider](https://collider.com/ghost-rider-connor-storrie-villain-cast-marvel/) also describes him as negotiating to appear, and says the identity of his character is still under wraps.

That caution matters. Talks can collapse, and neither Marvel nor Storrie has confirmed anything in the material we reviewed. Treat this as a report of a possible casting until the studio says otherwise.

## Who could he play?

Johnny Blaze, Gosling's character, has no single signature enemy, according to Just Jared. The comics offer plenty of candidates. Just Jared lists Blackheart, Zarathos, Lucifer, Blackout, Deathwatch, Centurious, Zadkiel and Nightmare among the villains tied to Ghost Rider.

Mephisto is the name most fans will reach for. [Bloody Disgusting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/movie/3973576/ryan-gosling-ghost-rider-movie-may-have-found-villain-in-connor-storrie/) notes that Mephisto tricked Blaze into selling his soul in the first place, and that the earlier Nicolas Cage films featured both Mephisto and his son Blackheart. In the Marvel Cinematic Universe, Sacha Baron Cohen played Mephisto in the series *Ironheart*. Collider is explicit that there is currently no indication Storrie would play either Mephisto or Blackheart.

## The film around him

Gosling was announced as the lead earlier this year at San Diego Comic-Con, after years of showing interest in the character, according to Just Jared. Shawn Levy, who directed Gosling on the upcoming *Star Wars: Starfighter*, is directing. Bloody Disgusting reports that Jonathan Tropper is writing, and that Linus Sandgren, known for *Dune: Part 3*, has been hired as director of photography, with the production planning to shoot on film.

Kevin Feige produces alongside Louis D'Esposito, Stephen Broussard, Levy and Gosling, per the same report. The July 2028 date was confirmed in August, and filming is expected in New York next year.

It would not be Storrie's first brush with comic book material. Just Jared notes he had a small part in *Joker: Folie à Deux*.

## A year that changed everything

*Heated Rivalry* premiered over the US Thanksgiving weekend in 2025. It follows rival ice hockey players [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander) and [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov), played by [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) and Storrie. [People](https://people.com/hudson-williams-connor-storrie-sexy-year-in-photos-12183062) reports that the pair then spent early 2026 on a global run of appearances, including presenting at the Golden Globes on 11 January and Storrie hosting *Saturday Night Live* in February.

People also says Storrie made his Met Gala debut on 4 May in Saint Laurent, for which he is now a global ambassador. Collider adds that the show's success brought him an Emmy nomination and major brand deals.

Season two is now filming. Just Jared says Storrie is also attached to the films *Peaked*, *Turpentine*, *Please* and *The Bell Jar*, and has an indie called *Transaction Planet* that he wrote and directed. People says *Heated Rivalry* season two is expected in 2027, so his screen calendar is already full.
