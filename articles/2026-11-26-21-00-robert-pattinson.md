**Quick summary**

- [Charlie Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Cox) says he asked to audition for the next James Bond and was told he was "too old", even though he is about the same age as the man who got the job.
- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), 40, was announced as 007 on 16 November by Amazon MGM Studios.
- Radio Times has listed eight actors rumoured for the Bond girl role, stressing these are rumours only.
- Filming is expected to start in 2027, and no release date has been announced.

The Robert Pattinson Bond casting, which makes [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), is still rolling in more than a week after the announcement, and the latest voice is a candid one. Daredevil star Charlie Cox told a UK radio audience this week that he was turned away from auditioning because of his age, according to [Soap Central](https://www.soapcentral.com/entertainment/news-how-old-robert-pattinson-daredevil-star-charlie-cox-says-rejected-auditioning-denis-villeneuve-s-james-bond-due-age).

## What Charlie Cox said about the Bond audition

Cox appeared on Virgin Radio UK's The Chris Evans Breakfast Show on 23 November, as reported by Soap Central. He said he had asked his agents to look into an audition and was told he was too old for the part.

He did not hide his surprise. Cox, who turns 44 next month, pointed out that Pattinson is the same age as him. "How is 44 too old?" he asked on air. Soap Central puts Pattinson at 40 and Cox at 43, so the gap is closer to three years, but the point of the joke was clear.

There was no sourness in it. Cox praised the career Pattinson has built, noting that the actor worked with directors early in their careers who later became major names. He also said he had expected the role to go to Jack Lowden or Callum Turner, two of the names widely reported as contenders.

## Robert Pattinson Bond casting: how he won the role

Pattinson became the seventh actor to officially play Bond in the film series, following Sean Connery, Roger Moore, George Lazenby, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), according to Yahoo Lifestyle Australia. It reported that the shortlist reportedly included Turner, Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Nicholas Hoult.

Soap Central says Amazon founder Jeff Bezos has stated that five finalists took part in screen tests before the decision. Pattinson called the job "the honor of a lifetime" and said it was "a dream come true".

The film will be directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) and written by [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight). Villeneuve, who has already directed Pattinson in Dune: Part Three, said the actor "has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new".

## Bond girl rumours begin

With the lead settled, attention has moved to the supporting cast. Radio Times ran a list of eight actors who could play the Bond girl, with the caveat that everything on it is speculation. It points to Zendaya, Florence Pugh and Anya Taylor-Joy, all of whom have worked with Villeneuve on the Dune films, and notes that Zendaya also shares screen time with Pattinson in several 2026 releases.

The list also mentions Sydney Sweeney, citing an earlier report in The Sun that she was being eyed for the role. Radio Times is careful to say nothing confirms that. No actor has been announced for any role beyond Pattinson, and parts such as the villain, Q and M remain open.

## Jokes and well wishes

Others have responded with humour. [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), the breakout star of the horror hit Obsession, joined the running gag about Pattinson's busy year in a video shared by [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features) on Instagram. "I'm deeply afraid," she said, claiming she was the second choice for Bond and that Pattinson only got it because she was busy. The clip had passed 50,000 likes at the time of Yahoo's report.

[Dame Judi Dench](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judi_Dench), who played M in seven Bond films, told the UK programme Lorraine she "can't wait" to see Pattinson in the role. She added that her macular degeneration means she can only vaguely see him, but that "he looks terrific to me".

## What happens next

The film is expected to enter production in 2027, according to Soap Central, and Amazon MGM Studios has not given a release date. Further casting news is the next thing to watch. Until then, expect the speculation to keep coming, most of it unconfirmed.
