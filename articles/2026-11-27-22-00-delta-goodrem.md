**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) landed in the dance-off last weekend and beat Jaime Winstone and Carlos Gu to stay in the competition.
- This weekend is week 10 of Strictly. The pair dance a Couples' Choice routine to an AC/DC medley, and every couple takes on the Instant Dance.
- A body language expert, speaking for Paddy Power, has offered her reading of Goodrem's behaviour. It is one person's opinion, not a confirmed account of how Goodrem feels.
- Strictly continues on Saturday 28 November at 6.30pm on BBC One and BBC iPlayer.

Delta Goodrem and her professional partner Kai Widdrington head into week 10 of [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) with one dance-off already behind them. The Australian singer survived it last weekend, during Blackpool Week, according to [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/real-delta-goodrem-exposed-masks-34973762) and the [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/tv/strictly-delta-goodrems-true-colours-37921454).

## How the dance-off played out

Goodrem and Widdrington went head to head with [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) and [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu). Winstone and Gu were the couple sent home from Blackpool Week.

The Daily Star adds that Widdrington had been ill the previous week, which meant Goodrem danced with fellow professional Aljaž Škorjanec. The outlet doesn't say more about the illness, and neither do we.

You can follow everything we publish on her under the [Delta Goodrem](https://moduscarry.com/tag/delta-goodrem/) tag.

## What the couple face in week 10

Goodrem and Widdrington dance their Couples' Choice to a medley of Thunderstruck and Back in Black by AC/DC. Every couple also takes part in the Instant Dance, which both outlets say made its debut last year to a warm reception from viewers.

The format is simple and a little frantic. Each couple draws a dance style at random, then dashes to pick and change into their costumes. A ten-second countdown follows, and then the music starts.

## A body language expert's view

Body language expert Judi James, speaking on behalf of Paddy Power, told the outlets that Goodrem tends to look stoic on the show. In James's reading, a small habit during the wait for the judges' verdicts gave something away: one thumb kept rubbing the other hand as the pair stood with hands clasped.

James took that as a sign of self-soothing and of how much the competition matters to Goodrem. She also said Goodrem's signals are subtle and easy to miss.

James's advice for the week was a dance with "fiery, passionate, explosive" emotional content, rather than another tearful video package. That is commentary from a sponsor's spokesperson, and it comes from watching television, not from Goodrem herself. We have treated it that way.

## The noises during the judges' comments

Last weekend's Blackpool special also drew attention for another reason. Apparent "sex noises" were heard during the judges' critiques of Goodrem and Widdrington, and internet prankster Daniel Jarvis claimed responsibility, according to both outlets.

Head judge [Shirley Ballas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Ballas) told [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) this week that she shuts everything out when she is working and focuses on the couple in front of her. She said fellow judge Motsi Mabuse seemed a little taken aback, though Ballas didn't know what had caught her attention.
