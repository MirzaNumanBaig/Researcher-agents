**Quick summary**

- A report says Marvel has met [Jeremy Allen White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Allen_White) about a part in its upcoming "X-Men" reboot, according to [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2306781/marvel-jeremy-allen-white-hugh-jackman-wolverine-replacement/).
- White would reportedly start in a small role that grows over time. The report does not say he has been offered Wolverine.
- Marvel has not commented on White or on who will play Wolverine next.
- The film is set for 5 May 2028, and [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) last played the character in "Deadpool & Wolverine" in 2024.

Marvel is reportedly in talks with the Emmy-winning actor Jeremy Allen White about its "X-Men" reboot, and the chatter has quickly turned to whether he could one day take over from Hugh Jackman as Wolverine. The claim was reported by BGR on 4 November, and nothing has been confirmed by the studio.

## What the report says

White won his Emmy for playing [Carmy Berzatto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carmy_Berzatto) in "The Bear". According to the report BGR relayed, he has met Marvel about a role in the reboot. That part would be "a small role" at first, one that "would lead to more screen time down the line."

That wording matters. A small role that grows is not the same as a lead part, and BGR notes the report does not say whether White has been offered Wolverine at all. Marvel has said nothing publicly about him, or about who its next Wolverine will be.

The report does point to one reason he might suit the character. White is 5'7", which BGR says is not far from the comics version of Wolverine, listed at 5'3". Jackman, by contrast, has played the character since the first "X-Men" film in 2000.

## Where Jackman stands

Jackman's last outing in the claws was 2024's "Deadpool & Wolverine", which brought him back seven years after "Logan" was meant to be his farewell. BGR points out that Wolverine is one of several Marvel characters who could be recast once "Avengers: Doomsday" and "Secret Wars" are done.

Jackman may not be finished yet. BGR says [Kevin Feige](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kevin_Feige) has said the story of Deadpool and Wolverine is not over, and that Marvel keeps looking for places to fit the pair in. ## The new X-Men cast so far

Marvel revealed a first group of its "X-Men" cast at D23 in August, and no Wolverine was in it. According to BGR, the lineup includes:

- [Sadie Sink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadie_Sink) as Jean Grey
- [Kit Connor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kit_Connor) as Cyclops
- [Christopher Abbott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Abbott) as [Professor Xavier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_X)
- [Samara Weaving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samara_Weaving) as [Emma Frost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Frost)
- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) as Rogue
- [Maya Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Boyd) as Storm
- [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) as the villain [Mister Sinister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mister_Sinister)

Feige has said the cast is not complete. [Jake Schreier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jake_Schreier), who made "Thunderbolts*", is directing, and he was announced for the film in May 2025. In April he said the script was being rewritten by [Lee Sung Jin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Sung_Jin) and [Joanna Calo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanna_Calo). Calo also wrote "Thunderbolts*" and co-ran "The Bear" with [Christopher Storer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Storer), so she has worked with White for years. BGR says there is no word on whether she has been involved in any casting talks.

## White's history with Marvel

This would not be White's first meeting with the studio. In 2023 he said he went into a Marvel meeting with "an attitude" and even asked the executives why he should do their movie. He later said he "played it all wrong". He returned to the story in October 2025 on the "Happy Sad Confused" podcast, hosted by [Josh Horowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Horowitz), saying he had been disrespectful and that his issue was never with the genre itself, but with not knowing what he could add to it.

On the same podcast, he was asked about fans fancasting him as Wolverine. He did not say whether he wanted the part, but he called Jackman's performance great.

## What happens next

"X-Men" is scheduled for 5 May 2028, after "Avengers: Secret Wars" closes out the [Multiverse Saga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Marvel_Cinematic_Universe_films). Until Marvel says otherwise, treat the White story as an unconfirmed report.
