**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) earned the first 10 of the series for a Rumba on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) on Saturday, her first dance back after a rib injury.
- Judge Anton Du Beke called it his favourite dance of the series "by a million miles", while fellow judge Craig gave a seven.
- Body language expert Sean Johns told publisher Reach the post-dance hug with partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) showed how much the week had cost her.
- Goodrem and Widdrington stay in the competition and will dance a Viennese Waltz to [Unchained Melody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unchained_Melody) in Halloween week.

Delta Goodrem has answered the question hanging over her Strictly Come Dancing campaign. The Australian singer returned to the ballroom on Saturday night after a rib injury and walked away with the first perfect score of the series, according to the [Daily Star](https://www.dailystar.co.uk/tv/strictly-favourite-delta-goodrem-danger-37817964) and [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/lifestyle/tv/strictly-fans-rush-support-delta-34827756). Now commentators are reading every gesture that followed.

## A Rumba chosen to protect the rib

The routine was a Rumba with Widdrington, set to a song by [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion), according to a viewer quoted by Wales Online. The sources report that the dance was picked with the injury in mind. Sean Johns said Widdrington "chose the rumba to rest the rib".

The slower style did not stop it landing. Anton Du Beke said he is old-fashioned about the Rumba and wants love rather than unpleasantness from it. "This is my favourite dance of the series, by a million miles," he said, as quoted by both outlets. He gave it a 10. Craig, by Johns's account, gave a seven.

That three-point gap is the detail Johns kept returning to. In his words, it sits "between the emotion she showed and the risk she couldn't take".

## What the hug may have said

Johns spoke exclusively to Reach, the publisher of the Daily Star and Wales Online. His reading focuses on the first seconds after the music stopped. Goodrem did not turn to the judges or the audience, he said. She went straight to Widdrington, face buried in his shoulder, and the embrace lasted longer than a typical stage hug.

"Hiding the face in a partner is what the body does when it has been holding something for a week and can finally put it down," Johns said. He also noted that her hand went to her cheek before she looked at the crowd, which he described as the gesture used to catch a tear not yet decided on. Off the floor, he said, her arm stayed hooked around Widdrington's neck during the walk.

This is one expert's interpretation of broadcast footage. Neither Goodrem nor Widdrington has confirmed how she felt, and Johns himself said she wants the injury "filed as a blip, not a story".

## A warning about playing it safe

Johns added a caution. If Goodrem keeps dancing carefully, he argued, it will show, and that could cost her in a close contest. "Careful reads as careful," he said. The Daily Star frames this as a risk to her chances of winning. It is an opinion, not a judging verdict.

## Fans split on the marks

Reaction on X was mixed. One viewer wrote that she "knocked it out of the park" and deserved the 10. Another asked, "What injury?" A third called her a mesmerising dancer.

Others thought the marks were too generous. One poster said Du Beke had "handed out sympathy votes", and another wrote that Goodrem was "hugely overmarked". Both outlets quote these posts, and they represent individual viewers rather than the wider audience.

Goodrem addressed the night herself on Instagram, as quoted by Wales Online. "Couldn't have asked for a better first night back in the ballroom," she wrote. She thanked supporters and said she and Widdrington were grateful to be dancing another week.

## What happens next

Goodrem and Widdrington made it into Halloween week. They will dance a Viennese Waltz to Unchained Melody by The Righteous Brothers, according to both reports. It is a faster, more physically demanding style than the Rumba, so it will be the first real test of how the rib is holding up. Strictly returns to BBC One on Saturday at 6.30pm UK time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who scored the first 10 on Strictly this series?
Delta Goodrem did, for her Rumba with Kai Widdrington. Anton Du Beke awarded the 10, according to the Daily Star and Wales Online.

### What injury did Delta Goodrem have?
Both outlets describe it as a rib injury. The sources do not give further medical detail, and none has been confirmed here beyond that.

### What will Delta Goodrem dance in Halloween week?
She and Widdrington will perform a Viennese Waltz to Unchained Melody by The Righteous Brothers.

### Is the body language analysis official?
No. It is the view of Sean Johns, a body language expert speaking to Reach, and not a statement from Goodrem, her partner or the BBC.
