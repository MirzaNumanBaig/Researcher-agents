**Quick summary**

- Israeli outlet Ynetnews published a column on 18 November 2026 arguing that [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsgård) has turned red-carpet dressing into a playful show.
- The column ties the look to the press tour for *Wicker*, a satirical comedy starring [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman).
- It lists a skirt, a tuxedo without trousers, a leather halter top and an all-gold outfit.
- This is an opinion piece, not a report of new casting or film news.

Alexander Skarsgård's Wicker red carpet looks have drawn a full column of commentary. In a piece published on 18 November 2026, Ynetnews argues that the Swedish actor is treating the carpet as a stage, and that he is enjoying it far more openly than most of his peers.

## What the column says about the Wicker red carpet looks

According to [Ynetnews](https://www.ynetnews.com/culture/article/bjbdxhsafe), Skarsgård has spent recent months wearing outfits that push well past standard formalwear. The column singles out the promotional run for *Wicker*, a satirical comedy in which Colman plays a social outcast whose ideal husband is woven from wicker and comes to life. For that tour, the writer says, he arrived in a skirt and a shirt printed with Colman's face.

Other looks named in the piece include a tuxedo worn without trousers but with a tight thong and knee-high socks, a leather halter top with towering boots, and an entirely gold ensemble with transparent shoes. The column also mentions a shimmering purple sequined top. We have only the column's description of these outfits, and the source text does not say which events each one was worn to.

For more on his recent appearances, see our earlier coverage of his [pink boxers and Governors Awards looks](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-pink-boxers/) and his [Tom Ford outfit at the Governors Awards](https://moduscarry.com/alexander-skarsgard-tom-ford/).

## How the writer compares him with other stars

The Ynetnews writer contrasts Skarsgård with [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) and [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), along with Timothée Chalamet. In the column's view, those actors use fashion to support a carefully built public image or to make a point about gender norms. Skarsgård, it says, shows no interest in fashion-press approval or a Vogue cover.

The argument is that his appeal comes from being comfortable with looking ridiculous. The column describes his approach as openly enjoying being admired, rather than delivering a serious artistic message. That reading is the writer's own, and Skarsgård has not been quoted on it.

## The roles behind the image

The column places the style shift against a varied career. It recalls his breakout as Viking vampire [Eric Northman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Northman) in [True Blood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Blood), then his role as Nicole Kidman's abusive husband in *Big Little Lies*. It also names an engineered cyborg in Apple's *Murderbot* and a gay biker in the comedy-drama *Pillion*.
