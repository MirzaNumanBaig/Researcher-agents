**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) and [Lewis Capaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lewis_Capaldi) are set to headline the opening night of the Yasalam After Race Concerts at the 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix.
- The show is at Etihad Park on Yas Island, according to Goal.com's Australian edition.
- Tickets are listed through Ticketmaster and the Yas Marina Circuit website, with Golden Circle access starting at around 695 AED.
- Entry is generally available to holders of eligible Grand Prix tickets.

Swedish pop star Zara Larsson and Scottish singer-songwriter Lewis Capaldi will share the stage on the opening night of the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix weekend, with fans now looking for tickets. The pairing was set out in a ticket guide published by [Goal.com Australia](https://www.goal.com/en-au/news/zara-larsson-tickets/blt6c8d467e99c5a78b) on 16 November 2026.

## What the Abu Dhabi concert is

The Yasalam After Race Concerts return for the 2026 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix weekend, according to Goal.com. The series is a regular fixture of the Formula 1 entertainment calendar, with live music staged after each day's racing on Yas Island.

Larsson and Capaldi are billed as the headline acts for the opening night. Goal.com describes the evening as a mix of dance-pop hits and lyrical ballads, which fits the two acts: Larsson is known for upbeat pop, while Capaldi is known for emotional, big-voiced songs.

The guide does not give the exact date or set times, so check the official listings before you plan travel.

## Where to buy tickets

Goal.com says tickets are sold officially through Ticketmaster and the Yas Marina Circuit website, as part of the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix packages. Both early-bird and general admission passes are mentioned.

The same guide points to the resale marketplace GrintaHub for fans who find official listings sold out. Goal.com does not give resale prices, saying only that they depend on demand. Buyers on any resale site should check the seller's terms and the ticket's validity before paying.

## How much tickets cost

Official prices for the Grand Prix weekend start at around 695 AED for Golden Circle access, according to Goal.com. General admission is described as the cheapest way in. For Australian readers, that is a figure in United Arab Emirates dirhams, so the converted price will depend on the day's exchange rate and any booking fees.

The guide advises booking early to get the lowest prices on the official platforms.

## Where it takes place

Etihad Park is described as the main outdoor entertainment venue on Yas Island. Under the Yasalam model, admission to the concerts is generally open to holders of eligible Grand Prix tickets, so the music is bundled with race access rather than sold as a stand-alone show. Goal.com does not say whether separate concert-only tickets exist.

Fans who want to go should start with the official Ticketmaster and Yas Marina Circuit pages, where availability and any further line-up details will be confirmed. Neither artist's own channels were part of the reporting used here, so any change to the bill would come from the organisers.
