**Quick summary**

- [Dafne Keen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dafne_Keen), now 21, says [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) looked out for her on the set of *Logan* (2017).
- She told the "Inside of You" podcast he made sure no one abused her working hours, according to Variety.
- Keen also said she was offered cocaine at Hollywood parties as a young teenager, and she still avoids drugs and alcohol.
- Separately, an unconfirmed report links Jeremy Allen White to the *X-Men* reboot, which is due on 5 May 2028.

Dafne Keen has described Hugh Jackman as the "best on-screen dad" she could have asked for, and says he took on a protective role when she made *Logan* at the age of 11. The comments came in a recent episode of the "Inside of You" podcast, as reported by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/logan-dafne-keen-cocaine-11-years-old-hollywood-1236939718/), which cited [Entertainment Weekly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entertainment_Weekly).

## What Keen said about Jackman

Keen played the young mutant Laura, also known as X-23, opposite Jackman's ageing Wolverine. She shot the film at 11, and it reached cinemas a couple of months after her 12th birthday, Variety notes.

Looking back, she said Jackman gave her "a lot of really positive reinforcement" and good advice, and that he felt like a parental figure on set. In her words, he made sure that no one was abusing her hours, that she was having fun, and that she was shielded from any tensions behind the scenes. She called him "so incredibly protective and amazing".

That detail matters because child performers on big productions work under tight rules, and Keen's account suggests the lead actor helped keep them in view. Variety does not report any complaint about the production itself.

## The comments about Hollywood parties

The same interview covered a darker topic. Keen said she was offered cocaine at Hollywood parties when she was a young teenager, according to Variety's summary of the podcast. She did not, as reported, accuse any named person.

Keen said she still stays away from drugs and alcohol as an adult. She explained that she can stay out until 6am "but sober", because she likes the music, the dancing and the people. As a teenager, she said, she sometimes claimed she had already taken something so that she would not be pressured to join in.

## Where Keen's career has gone since

Logan was her breakthrough. She went on to lead HBO and BBC One's *His Dark Materials* for three seasons, then appeared in the *Star Wars* series *The Acolyte*. Variety reports that she plays Artemis in the third season of *Percy Jackson and the Olympians* and that she returned as X-23 in *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

Her path shows how far the *Logan* role carried her, from a first major film at 11 to long-running television leads before she turned 22.

## A separate rumour: who plays Wolverine next?

Jackman's own Wolverine future keeps drawing attention. A report from iXBT.games, translated by AI from a Russian original, says insider Jeff Sneider claims *The Bear* star Jeremy Allen White met Marvel Studios representatives about the *X-Men* reboot.

Treat this as unconfirmed. Per the same report, Sneider described it as a smaller part that could grow in later films, and speculation that White would be the new Wolverine came from fans and commentators, not from Marvel. The studio has not named a new actor for Logan. We covered the earlier version of this story in [Jeremy Allen White Reportedly Eyed to Replace Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackman-wolverine-replacement/).

Other details in the report are more concrete: Jake Schreier, who directed *Thunderbolts\**, is set to direct the reboot, filming is expected in the UK in early 2027, and the premiere is scheduled for 5 May 2028. Ben Wang is reportedly in talks to play Iceman.
