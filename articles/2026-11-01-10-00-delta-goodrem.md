**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) scored 33 out of 40 for a Viennese Waltz on Halloween Week of [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing).
- The routine was set to [Unchained Melody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unchained_Melody) and themed on The Addams Family, with Goodrem as Morticia.
- Their score put them in equal third place, two points behind the runners-up.
- Three couples will face elimination in this week's dance-off, and Goodrem and Widdrington are due back next Saturday.

Delta Goodrem has kept her place near the top of the Strictly leaderboard. The Australian singer and Kai Widdrington danced a Viennese Waltz on Saturday night's Halloween Week show and earned 33 out of 40, according to [Aussievision](https://www.aussievision.net/post/that-was-sensational-delta-goodrem-impresses-with-halloween-week-viennese-waltz-on-strictly-come), a site that covers Eurovision news.

## An Addams Family waltz

Goodrem danced as Morticia Addams, and the pair performed to the Righteous Brothers' Unchained Melody. [inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/delta-goodrem-impresses-judges-again-with-sensational-halloween-week-routine) reports that Widdrington dressed as Gomez Addams. Aussievision adds that the couple re-enacted the pottery scene from the film Ghost, a fitting nod given the song's place in that movie.

It follows last week's Rumba, which earned Goodrem the first score of 10 of the series, as Aussievision notes. Our earlier coverage looked at [that Celine Dion Rumba](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrems-celine-dion-rumba-wins-strictlys-first-10/).

## What the judges said

The judges gave 8, 8, 8 and 9, a total of 33. [Anton Du Beke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Du_Beke) called the dance "incredible", according to Aussievision. [Motsi Mabuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motsi_Mabuse) praised Goodrem's characterisation of Morticia and said she loved the fluidity of her movement. "I actually love the way you make something so difficult look so easy 'cause it was not easy!" she said.

## Where she sits on the leaderboard

Eleven couples danced on the night. Aussievision lists the top of the table this way:

- [Lacey Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacey_Turner) and [Vito Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Coppola) led with 36 for a Charleston.
- John Nellis and [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones) were second, two points ahead of Goodrem.
- Goodrem and Widdrington tied on 33 with [Cach Mercer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cach_Mercer) and [Maddie Ingoldsby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddie_Ingoldsby).
- [Will Best](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Best) and [Dianne Buswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dianne_Buswell) tied with [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) and [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin) on 32.

At the other end, Graeme Hall with [Lauren Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Oakley) and Tabby Stoecker with [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita) shared last place on 25. Aussievision says Goodrem has finished in the podium positions every week she has danced.

## What happens next

Viewers vote after the show, and their votes are combined with the judges' scores. Aussievision says this week has a Halloween twist: three couples, not two, will land in the dance-off, and the judges will vote to eliminate one. The results show airs on Sunday evening in the UK. Goodrem and Widdrington are due back on the dance floor next Saturday.
