[Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) has spent 2026 going from a newcomer to one of television's most photographed names, and People marked the year on 27 October with a month-by-month photo look back. The feature follows Williams and his co-star [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) from the premiere of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) in late 2025 to the start of filming on its second season.

It is a long way from the show's early days. People's own summary says the pair went "from waiters to the hottest names in town", and the timeline backs that up with a run of dates.

## How did Heated Rivalry make Hudson Williams a star?

Heated Rivalry premiered over the US Thanksgiving weekend in 2025, according to [People](https://people.com/hudson-williams-connor-storrie-sexy-year-in-photos-12183062). The series is based on Rachel Reid's sports romance novel of the same name and follows two ice hockey prodigies, [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander), played by Williams, and [Ilya Rozanov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilya_Rozanov), played by Storrie. The story is told as a rivals-to-lovers drama.

People says viewers grew more attached to both actors with each weekly episode. By January, the attention had moved well beyond the show itself.

## A January that went global

Williams made his late-night debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on 7 January. People reports that crowds of fans waited outside Rockefeller Center to see him arrive.

Four days later, on 11 January, the Golden Globes invited Williams and Storrie to present an award. Williams wore Giorgio Armani and Storrie wore Saint Laurent. They handed Best Supporting Actress in a TV Series to Erin Doherty of Adolescence, after a routine joking that everyone in the room had already seen them undress on screen.

The travel kept coming. Williams attended the Giorgio Armani show at Milan Fashion Week on 19 January, in a tailored grey suit. Ahead of the 2026 Winter Olympics, both actors took part in the torch relay, and the Olympics Instagram account shared photos of them carrying the flame on 25 January. Storrie appeared at the Saint Laurent show in Paris two days later.

There was one more twist to come before the end of winter.

## Saturday Night Live, the Oscars party and a Peloton ad

In February, Storrie hosted Saturday Night Live, only two months after the series took off. Williams made a cameo to support him, as fans had hoped, per People. Storrie had already made his own late-night debut with [Seth Meyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Meyers) the day after the Golden Globes.

On 15 March the pair turned up at the Vanity Fair Oscars afterparty in coordinated sheer tops. A month later, on 14 April, Peloton released an advert starring Williams in a workout video set to David Bowie's "Fame".

## The Met Gala and a Canadian Screen Awards win

The Met Gala on 4 May was the first for both actors. Storrie wore Saint Laurent, the label he now fronts as a global ambassador, according to People. Williams chose custom Balenciaga: a powder-blue bolero jacket with an open face and multicoloured eyeshadow. For the afterparties he changed into an ERL outfit with a beret, knee-high socks and a cummerbund. People says the two spent the night together, beginning at GQ's afterparty.

The most formal recognition came on 31 May. Williams won Best Lead Performer, Drama Series at the Canadian Screen Awards for his role. Heated Rivalry also took Best Dramatic Series and 14 more trophies, People reports.

## What happens next

After what People describes as a few months out of the public eye, Williams and Storrie returned to Canada in August to film season 2. People says the second season is expected in 2027. No release date was given in the coverage we read.
