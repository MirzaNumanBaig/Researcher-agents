**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) plays Hester Pearl in the crime thriller Paper Tiger, a role first intended for Anne Hathaway.
- Director [James Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gray_(director)) told IndieWire that Hathaway's commitment to Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey forced the change.
- The film opened in six US theatres with a $215,009 weekend and a record $35,834 per-screen average for Gray, according to ScreenRant.
- A wide release follows on 20 November.

Scarlett Johansson stepped into Paper Tiger because the part she now plays was written with Anne Hathaway in mind, and the director refused to wait for Hathaway's schedule to clear. James Gray explained the switch in an interview with IndieWire, reported by [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/paper-tiger-scarlett-johansson-replace-anne-hathaway-james-gray-explained/) on 19 November, a day before the crime thriller opens widely.

## Scarlett Johansson Paper Tiger: how the casting changed

Paper Tiger began life as a follow-up of sorts to Gray's 2022 film [Armageddon Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_Time), which draws heavily on his own parents and childhood. [Anne Hathaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_Hathaway) was meant to return as Esther Graff, the character based on Gray's mother.

Then Hathaway signed on to Nolan's The Odyssey. "You can't wait a year and a half," Gray told IndieWire, according to ScreenRant. He began to see the delay as "a blessing in disguise" and let the film take a new shape. Like any movie, he said, it was "a wild horse that's going to get away from you."

That shift also removed [Jeremy Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Strong) from the picture. Strong had played the patriarch in Armageddon Time, based on Gray's father, and would not have returned as Irving Graff. The husband is now Gary Pearl, played by [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver).

## Why Gray went to Johansson

Gray says he had met Johansson years earlier on a project that never happened and liked her. He calls her "a total New Yorker through and through", and a Jewish woman from New York, which mattered to him given the autobiographical roots of the material.

He didn't expect a yes. His note with the script read, in his words, "There's probably no chance in hell you're going to do this, but I love you, can you read this?" Johansson texted back "very, very quickly", he said, with "Let's go."

Gray also thinks she suits the new material better than Hathaway suited the old. The Paper Tiger character is "a more operatic idea" than Hathaway's Esther, with "much more" high emotion, because the story is more tragic. "She embraced it completely," he told IndieWire.

## What Paper Tiger is about

Johansson plays Hester Pearl. Her husband Gary and his brother Irwin, played by [Miles Teller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miles_Teller), get tangled up with the Russian mafia. Gray wrote, directed and produced the film.

## Early numbers and reviews

The opening figures are small in absolute terms but unusual for an arthouse release. Paper Tiger took $215,009 over three days in six theatres, a per-screen average of $35,834. ScreenRant says that beats Gray's previous best debut average of $27,453, set by The Lost City of Z in 2016.

Reviews are mostly positive. The film holds a "Certified Fresh" 86% Tomatometer score from 125 reviews. ScreenRant's own reviewer, Patrice Witherspoon, praised Driver and Teller but called Johansson "criminally underused" and gave the film five out of 10.

The comparison with the film Hathaway chose is stark. ScreenRant reports that The Odyssey has grossed $1.8 billion worldwide, becoming the highest-grossing R-rated and IMAX film ever, and that Hathaway is expected to earn an Oscar nomination for her role as Penelope. ScreenRant also notes that both actresses have played comic-book characters: Johansson as Marvel's Black Widow, Hathaway as Catwoman in [The Dark Knight Rises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dark_Knight_Rises).
