**Quick summary**

- Mckenna Grace Hunger Games news: [Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), 20, says she brought lists of book details to the set of *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* every day, according to an exclusive interview with People.
- She plays twins Maysilee Donner and Merrilee Donner, and pushed for items such as Mockingjay pins, a hummingbird and her character's braid.
- Co-star [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)) recalls Grace arriving with a binder of requests.
- Grace also quizzed [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson) about filming the original movies. The prequel is in cinemas now.

Mckenna Grace Hunger Games fans now know how far she went to make *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* feel true to the books. In an exclusive interview with [People](https://people.com/movies-mckenna-grace-had-a-binder-of-easter-eggs-from-the-books-she-wanted-to-include-in-sunrise-on-the-reaping-adaptation-exclusive-12264995), published on 26 November, the actress said she turned up to set with a running list of ideas for director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence). The conversation took place at the film's press junket last month.

## A binder of book details

Grace, who plays both Maysilee Donner and her twin sister Merrilee Donner, told People that she arrived each day with a list of thoughts and questions "to bother" the director with. Several of those requests made it into the final film. By her account, the hummingbird, the Mockingjay pins and Maysilee's braid all landed on screen.

Her co-star Whitney Peak, who plays Lenore Dove Baird, backed up the story. Peak remembered Grace walking in with a binder and listing what she needed. Peak's summary of the process was that Grace was effectively "in that writers room", according to People.

She told the magazine she could not name anything she had not fought for.

## Why Maysilee's eyeshadow mattered

Some of the details were small. Grace said she lobbied for a different coloured eyeshadow with each of Maysilee's outfits. Her reasoning was that anyone in the Capitol who found a way to hold on to a personal style would be Maysilee. She wanted something that set the character apart from whatever the Capitol handed her to wear.

It is a small choice. For more on the character, see our piece on [Maysilee's quiet rebellion](https://moduscarry.com/sunrise-on-the-reaping-mckenna-grace-maysilee/).

## A fan before she was a cast member

Grace says she was already a devoted fan of [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) before the casting. As a child actress she went to school in on-set classrooms. While filming one of the Annabelle movies, she read all of the books, and the classroom walls were covered in Mockingjay poster boards. The group then started watching the films, and she said she fell for the whole franchise.

She described herself as a fangirl who now had some credibility.

## Advice from the original cast

Joining the prequel also gave Grace a chance to talk to a veteran of the series. She had worked with Hutcherson on *Five Nights at Freddy's 2*, and she used that connection to ask how he handled the emotionally and physically hard days on the original films.

Grace said she wanted to hear what it was like for Hutcherson and [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) to make those movies at around her age, including whether it was as brutally physical as her own shoot. She told People that theirs was.

## How the film is doing

*Sunrise on the Reaping* opened on 28 October and is still in cinemas. According to [Wikipedia's film entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games:_Sunrise_on_the_Reaping), it has taken about US$237.2 million against a reported budget of US$150 million. The same page lists a 68 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and 55 out of 100 on Metacritic, so reviews have been mixed to positive. For the commercial side, see our report on the [opening-week haul](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office/).
