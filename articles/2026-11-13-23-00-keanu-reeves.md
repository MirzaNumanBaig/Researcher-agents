**Quick summary**

- Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) says a script for a Constantine sequel exists and the team still wants to make it.
- He wants a "hard-R" film on a budget well under $100 million, according to The Hype Magazine.
- Lawrence says key creatives, including [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves), would accept lower upfront pay.
- Nothing is official: the studio has not approved the sequel and no release date is set.

Constantine 2 now has a rough shape, even if it has no green light. Speaking to Collider while promoting *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, Lawrence said the sequel he has chased for years would be a hard-R horror thriller made lean, with Reeves back as John Constantine, according to [The Hype Magazine](https://www.thehypemagazine.com/2026/11/13/constantine-director-hopeful-sequel-dc-studios-shakeup/).

## Why Francis Lawrence is more hopeful

The comments follow a leadership change at DC Studios. [JubileeCast](https://www.jubileecast.com/articles/41038/20261112/constantine-2-director-francis-lawrence-more-hopeful-keanu-reeves-sequel.htm) reports it was announced on 9 November that [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) and [Peter Safran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Safran) will step down as co-CEOs on 1 December. Former Marvel Studios executive [Jeremy Latcham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Latcham) is set to take over.

Lawrence told Collider the team has had an idea and a script for a very long time. He said the people running DC had been an obstacle to making it, and that the news made him "more hopeful". He also named those still attached: himself, Reeves, writer [Akiva Goldsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akiva_Goldsman) and producer J.J. Abrams. We covered the first wave of reaction in our earlier [Constantine 2 update](https://moduscarry.com/constantine-2-update-keanu-reeves/).

## A hard-R film on a lean budget

The new detail is how Lawrence wants to make it. The Hype Magazine says he was frustrated that the 2005 original was shaped for a PG-13 rating, only for the [Motion Picture Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association) to give it an R anyway. This time the plan is to commit to R from the first day of development.

The budget would be "well under $100 million", according to the same report. For context, JubileeCast puts the original at about $230 million worldwide on a reported $100 million budget, and notes that mixed reviews did not stop it building a loyal cult following. Lawrence also said Reeves and other key creatives are willing to take less money upfront so the film can be made "scrappily" without heavy corporate oversight, as The Hype Magazine relays it.

That is Lawrence's account of the plan, not a studio commitment, and neither outlet quotes Reeves directly on the pay question.

## Where the sequel sits in the DC plan

Lawrence argued the film need not clash with whatever shared universe DC builds next. "If they just let us do the sequel to our version, they can coexist," he said, pointing to audience appetite for standalone stories outside strict continuity.

There is a complication. JubileeCast, citing ComicBook.com, says DC Studios reportedly hired [Francis Galluppi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Galluppi) last week to write a separate, standalone Constantine movie, and that this project has not been cancelled. How the two would relate has not been explained in the sources we read.

## What happens next

The sequel needs approval from the incoming leadership, and neither report gives a date for that decision. Latcham takes over on 1 December, so any formal conversation is likely to follow after that. Until then the project remains a script, a director and a star who want to do it. For more on the actor, see [everything we publish about Keanu Reeves](https://moduscarry.com/tag/keanu-reeves/) and our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) coverage.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Constantine 2 officially happening?

No. Both reports say the studio has not approved the sequel and no release date has been set.

### Will Keanu Reeves return as John Constantine?

Lawrence says Reeves is still involved and backing the project, according to The Hype Magazine. Reeves has not commented in the reports we read.

### How much would Constantine 2 cost?

Lawrence wants a budget well under $100 million, which is below the reported $100 million spent on the 2005 film.

### Is the new Constantine movie the same project?

No. A separate standalone film is reportedly being written by Francis Galluppi, according to JubileeCast citing ComicBook.com.
