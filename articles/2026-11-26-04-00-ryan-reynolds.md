**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) has signed with publicist Kelly Bush Novak, founder and CEO of the firm ID, according to Page Six.
- The report was published on 25 November 2026 and describes it as an exclusive.
- His wife, [Blake Lively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blake_Lively), signed with Bush Novak about two months earlier, after leaving her longtime representative.
- The couple previously shared the same PR firm, Vision PR.

## Ryan Reynolds' new publicist is Kelly Bush Novak

Ryan Reynolds has hired a new Ryan Reynolds publicist. [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/25/hollywood/ryan-reynolds-signs-with-power-publicist-two-months-after-blake-lively-did-the-same-after-baldoni-saga/) reported on 25 November that the "Deadpool" star has signed with Kelly Bush Novak, who founded and runs ID. The outlet says it learned of the move exclusively. Neither Reynolds nor Bush Novak is quoted in the report, so the details rest on Page Six's own reporting.

The timing follows a pattern. According to Page Six, Lively joined Bush Novak less than two months ago, and Reynolds is now following her to the same firm.

## From a shared firm to a shared publicist

Until now, the couple used the same company. Page Six says Lively was represented by Leslie Sloane, the founder of Vision PR, while Reynolds worked with Jami Kandel at the same firm. That makes the switch to a single new firm a logical step rather than a surprise, in the outlet's telling.

Lively's change, which Page Six first reported in September, came after she parted ways with Sloane. The outlet links that shift to her widely covered legal dispute with her "It Ends With Us" director and co-star [Justin Baldoni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Baldoni). Page Six does not say that the dispute is the reason Reynolds has moved as well, and it is not clear from the report whether it played any part in his decision.

## Who else is on the ID client list

Reynolds joins a well-known group of clients. Page Six names the Oscar-winning director [Christopher Nolan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Nolan), [Seth Rogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seth_Rogen), [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem) and [Lady Gaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Gaga) as people Bush Novak represents.

## A busy producer as well as a star

Page Six describes Reynolds as one of the biggest movie stars working today and also a busy producer. It points to his FX docuseries "Welcome to Wrexham", which earned four Emmy nominations this year. They were for Outstanding Unstructured Reality Program, Cinematography, Picture Editing and Sound Mixing. The show won one of those, Outstanding Sound Mixing for a Reality Program, for the episode "Do a Wrexham".

## What happens next

No announcement from either side has been cited in the report, and there is no word yet on whether Reynolds or ID will comment publicly.
