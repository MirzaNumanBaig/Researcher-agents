**Quick summary**

- [Alexander Skarsgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Skarsg%C3%A5rd) says playing a kind, morally upright husband in *Wicker* was the frightening part of the job.
- He spoke at the Contenders Film: Los Angeles panel on 14 November, and earlier at the Stockholm Film Festival.
- Prosthetics glued to his face left him unable to eat, drink or sweat, and he needed a week off to recover after two weeks of shooting.
- His next series release is *Murderbot* season 2, which Screen Rant reports is expected in summer 2027.

Alexander Skarsgård has said the most unnerving part of making *Wicker* was not the heavy makeup or the awards-season spotlight. It was playing someone wholly good. Speaking at the Contenders Film: Los Angeles panel on 14 November, the Swedish actor described his reed-woven husband opposite [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman) as a role well outside his comfort zone, according to a [Creepy Catalog](https://creepycatalog.com/for-alexander-skarsgard-the-scary-part-of-playing-olivia-colmans-husband-in-wicker-was-being-good-and-sweet/) report.

## Why a sweet role felt risky for Skarsgård

In *Wicker*, Colman plays a fisherwoman who answers pressure to marry by ordering a husband made of reeds. Skarsgård plays that husband. He told the film's Sundance audience in January that he usually gravitates toward conflicted characters with plenty of inner turmoil, so a good-natured, sweet and morally righteous man was a worry.

What won him over, he said, was the script and Colman. At the Los Angeles panel he praised its blend of humour, romance and commentary on patriarchy, marriage, jealousy and social hierarchies, and said it never felt too preachy.

The same thinking shapes how he picks work more broadly. He has said he does not see his career as a ladder in which every film must be the next rung, and that he would rather check in with himself about what he wants. In a December 2025 conversation with his father, Stellan, about *Pillion*, he said a phenomenal script and a director he trusts take the fear out of a role.

## Performing through a prosthetic mask

The practical side was harder than the emotional one. According to the report, the wicker pieces were glued to his face, including his eyelids and lips. His eyes dried out, and he could not eat, drink or sweat while wearing them. After two weeks of filming he needed a week off so his skin could recover.

The mask also swallowed small expressions, so he had to play bigger. "I had to be my own puppeteer," he said. At the Stockholm Film Festival this month he recalled feeling like a clown next to the subtle work of Colman, [Elizabeth Debicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Debicki) and [Peter Dinklage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Dinklage). Directors Alex Huston Fischer and Eleanor Wilson told him his performance was not as broad as it felt.

There was one upside. When he and Colman made each other laugh mid-scene, the mask hid it. "You have no idea what's happening underneath," he said at the panel.

## The Los Angeles premiere look

His fondness for his co-star was on show at the film's Los Angeles premiere in October. He wore a black shirt printed with Colman's face and a skirt made of multicoloured carabiners. "I wanted to wear something special for this lovely lady over here, Olivia, whom I adore," he told Variety, as quoted in the report.

He works with stylist Harry Lambert and says he never buys clothes himself, enjoying the creativity of it. On how the film will land, he was relaxed: some people might hate it, he said. ## Where this sits in his wider career

Skarsgård is best known for television such as *True Blood*, *Big Little Lies* and *Succession*, but Screen Rant notes his filmography reaches well beyond it, including *The Northman*, *Wicker* and *The Little Drummer Girl*.

Collider's retrospective on *The Northman* calls his performance career-defining. It says the 2022 Viking revenge film was made for between US$70 million and US$90 million and took about US$75 million at the box office, so it struggled to recoup its cost despite strong reviews. Collider argues its reputation has grown since.

On screen, he also plays the title role in Apple TV's *Murderbot*. Screen Rant says season 1 premiered in May 2025 and that, according to co-star Steve Buscemi, season 2 has finished filming.

## What happens next

Fans of the android series have a wait ahead. Screen Rant reports that *Murderbot* season 2, adapting *Artificial Condition*, is expected in summer 2027, though Apple TV has not announced an exact date. *Wicker* remains in the awards conversation, with Skarsgård still on the panel circuit.
