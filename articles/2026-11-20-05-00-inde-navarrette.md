**Quick summary**

- The Gotham Film & Media Institute will give [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) its Spotlight Tribute for her role as Nikki Freeman in *Obsession*.
- The 36th Gotham Film Awards take place on 30 November 2026 at Cipriani Wall Street in New York.
- *Obsession* cost US$750,000 to make and has grossed more than US$520 million worldwide, according to trade reports.
- Navarrette is next due to play Rogue in Marvel Studios' upcoming *X-Men* film.

Inde Navarrette has been named the recipient of the Spotlight Tribute at this year's Gotham Film Awards, an honour for her lead performance in Curry Barker's horror-romance *Obsession*. The Gotham Film & Media Institute announced the tribute on 19 November, with the ceremony set for Monday 30 November in New York, according to [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/obsession-inde-navarrette-spotlight-tribute-gotham-awards-1236759136/), which broke the news.

## What the Spotlight Tribute recognises

The institute created the Spotlight Tribute for artists whose work in the year's most successful films left a strong mark on audiences. Navarrette is being honoured for playing Nikki Freeman, a role the institute says shows real range as the character changes across the film.

Jeffrey Sharp, the institute's executive director, said her performance moves between horror and romance with "remarkable depth, vulnerability and nuance". He added that her commitment helped pull one of the year's most talked-about horror films into a wider cultural conversation.

The award has a short but notable history. [Tessa Thompson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tessa_Thompson) received it in 2025 for *Hedda*, and Zendaya won it in 2024 for *Challengers*, as [Next Best Picture](https://nextbestpicture.com/the-gotham-awards-to-present-inde-navarrette-with-the-spotlight-tribute-award/) also noted.

## The film behind the honour

*Obsession* follows Bear, played by [Michael Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnston_(actor)), a young man who breaks a mysterious One Wish Willow in the hope that his friend Nikki will love him more than anyone. Things go badly wrong. Barker wrote, directed and edited the film, and it is his first theatrical feature after he built an audience on YouTube.

The numbers are unusual for an independent horror release. After premiering at the Toronto International Film Festival in September 2025, the film was picked up by [Focus Features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Focus_Features) and opened in cinemas on 15 May 2026. The Hollywood Reporter puts its budget at US$750,000 and its worldwide gross above US$524 million. Next Best Picture, writing slightly earlier, cites more than US$521 million. Either way, the film is Focus Features' highest-grossing release of all time. Next Best Picture also reports a 93 per cent score on Rotten Tomatoes.

That return on a budget under US$1 million is the clearest sign of how far the film travelled. Our earlier coverage of [Obsession's arrival on Netflix](https://moduscarry.com/obsession-netflix-inde-navarrette/) tracks the same rise.

## Awards season picture

The tribute is not the film's only Gotham connection. *Obsession* is nominated for the [Audience Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audience_award), where it competes with *Backrooms*, *La Bola Negra*, *The Drama*, *The Invite*, *Michael*, *Project Hail Mary* and *You Can See Everything*.

The Hollywood Reporter describes the film as an Oscar contender and says Navarrette is widely predicted to earn a best actress nomination. That is a forecast, not an announcement, and nominations are not yet out.

Other tributes this year go to the cast of *Being Heumann* (Ensemble), *Elsinore* (Legacy), *Michael* (Musical), *La Bola Negra* (Vanguard), Danny Boyle as director for *Ink*, Ellen Burstyn (Performer) and LAIKA (Anniversary).

## Where Navarrette's career goes from here

Before *Obsession*, Navarrette appeared as Estela de la Cruz in Netflix's *13 Reasons Why* and as Sarah Cushing in *Superman & Lois* on The CW. Her upcoming projects include Rogue in Jake Schreier's *X-Men* for Marvel Studios, the horror-thriller *Invertigo*, and the sci-fi thriller *The Waffle House Index* for Apple Original Films.

For more on her year, see everything we publish about [Inde Navarrette](https://moduscarry.com/tag/inde-navarrette/), including her thoughts on [wanting to be a role model](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-role-model/) and her place in the [BAFTA Breakthrough US class of 2026](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-bafta-breakthrough/).

## What happens next

Winners of the 36th Gotham Film Awards will be revealed at the ceremony at Cipriani Wall Street on 30 November 2026. Navarrette will collect her tribute that night, and the Audience Award result for *Obsession* will be announced at the same event.
