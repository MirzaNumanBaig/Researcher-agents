**Quick summary**

- Delta Goodrem returned to Strictly Come Dancing on Saturday 24 October after missing the previous week's Icons episode with a fractured rib.
- She and partner Kai Widdrington scored 34 for a rumba, placing second on the week-five leaderboard.
- Judge Anton Du Beke gave the series' first 10.
- Tabby Stoecker and Mark Karmalita topped the board with 37.
- The pair are due back for Halloween week on Saturday 31 October.

[Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) came back to the [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) ballroom on Saturday 24 October and earned the first 10 of the 2026 series for a rumba, a week after a fractured rib kept her off the floor. The 41-year-old Australian singer and her professional partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) finished the night on 34 points. Only one couple beat them.

## How did Delta Goodrem's rumba score?

The couple danced to [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion)'s "It's All Coming Back To Me Now". According to the [Central Fife Times](https://www.centralfifetimes.com/leisure/national/26667666.delta-goodrem-scores-series-first-10-strictly-return-injury/), the score put them second, and the 10 came from [Anton Du Beke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Du_Beke), who called it his favourite dance of the series "by a million miles". He then joked that she should keep the rib injury.

[Motsi Mabuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motsi_Mabuse) told her "I missed you" and described it as one of the best rumbas she had seen on the show. Aussievision reported that [Shirley Ballas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shirley_Ballas) said she loved it and [Craig Revel Horwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Craig_Revel_Horwood) called it "totally and absolutely gorgeous". The same report said the routine included a wind machine, a nod to her Eurovision appearance this year.

Viewers were just as warm. Digital Spy quoted fans on X who called her an "incredible dancer" and celebrated "the first 10 of the series".

## Who finished ahead of her?

Winter Olympic champion Tabby Stoecker and [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita) scored 37, the highest total of the series so far, with two 10s for a couple's choice dance to "[Stupid Song](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stupid_Song)" by [Olivia Rodrigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Rodrigo). The Liverpool Echo reported that the dance was dedicated to Stoecker's late father.

That left Goodrem three points behind, according to Aussievision, with 12 couples dancing in the fifth live show. John Nellis and [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones) were third on 32.

At the other end, [Shaun Wright-Phillips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaun_Wright-Phillips) and [Alexis Warr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexis_Warr) scored 17 for a jive to "Faith", watched by his father [Ian Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Wright). [Lawrence Robb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Robb) and [Jowita Przystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jowita_Przysta%C5%82) scored 19.

## What did Goodrem say about the injury?

Before the show Goodrem told Metro and other press that she had noticed some pain after the couple's salsa, which had put them top of the leaderboard. "At the end of the night, the rib decided it had had enough," she said. She could not pinpoint a cause and put it down to "a combination" of lifts and tricks.

She called it a "new injury" she had never encountered before. A visit to hospital made it clear she would not be dancing that week. Aussievision reported that she later posted a video confirming the injury was a fractured rib and showing her progress behind the scenes. After Saturday's dance she said it had been a tough week, "waves" of emotion, and that having a time frame helped her keep going step by step.

## What happens next

The pair are due back for Halloween week on Saturday 31 October, according to Aussievision. Widdrington told Metro he is being careful about the choreography. "We're not gonna go out there and do some crazy lifts and backbends," he said, adding that they are taking it step by step. He denied that the pair had asked producers to change their dance schedule, and said the Charleston they had planned is probably a few weeks away if they stay in the competition. There is still a chance of reviving the dance prepared for Icons week.

The results show airs on Sunday in the UK, with the two lowest-ranked couples facing a dance-off. Goodrem has finished in the top two on the leaderboard every week she has danced, Aussievision noted.
