**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) was photographed in New York City in November 2026 wearing a full-length red wrap coat.
- The coat is by Auter and has wide sleeves, a belted waist and a collarless neckline, according to Yahoo.
- Yahoo lists the cashmere coat at US$1,250 and suggests cheaper lookalikes from Wallis, ASOS and Zara.
- She paired it with black gloves, sheer black tights, black stilettos and a curled platinum bob.

[Pamela Anderson](https://www.pamelaandersonfoundation.org/) has given winter dressing a jolt of colour. This month the actor was photographed walking through New York City in a full-length red wrap coat, and [Yahoo's style team](https://shopping.yahoo.com/style/clothing/articles/forget-black-puffers-pamela-anderson-010000441.html) has called it the alternative to the usual black puffer.

## What Pamela Anderson wore in New York

According to Yahoo, the coat is from the label Auter. It has wide sleeves, a belted waist and a collarless neckline, and it falls to full length. Yahoo describes the fabric as cashmere and puts the price at US$1,250.

The rest of the outfit was kept deliberately plain. Anderson added black gloves, sheer black tights and black stilettos, so the coat did the talking. Her hair was styled in a curled platinum bob that Yahoo compares to Marilyn Monroe, which gave the look an Old Hollywood feel.

The outlet's reading is simple: with understated make-up and dark accessories, the red coat becomes the single focal point that holds the whole outfit together.

## Why the collarless red coat is being pushed this winter

Yahoo frames the coat as a break from the black puffers and tan trenches that dominate the cold months. It also ties the outfit to Anderson's wider style shift, noting that she has stepped away from her old make-up approach and leans on individuality. The piece refers to her as the star of *The Last Showgirl* and as a *Baywatch* and *Barb Wire* alum.

This is not the first time her wardrobe has been in the news this week. Her deep red manicure at the CFDA Fashion Awards was covered in [Pamela Anderson's Winter Cherry Nails Set the Holiday Trend](https://moduscarry.com/pamela-anderson-winter-cherry-nails-2/), and red is clearly a running theme.

## How to get the look for less

Yahoo's article is a shopping guide as well as a style note, so the prices come from its own picks rather than from Anderson. For readers who do not want to spend US$1,250, it suggests three alternatives:

- The Wallis Collarless Belted Wrap Coat, which copies the cinched waist and bold red.
- The Collarless Formal Jacket from ASOS, with toggle fastenings and bell-shaped sleeves.
- The Short Wool-Blend Coat from Zara, which keeps the collarless neckline and wide sleeves in a shorter, oversized shape.

Yahoo says it chose these by matching the look of the original coat and keeping to modest price points. Prices and availability in Australia may differ, so check local stockists.

## What happens next

Nothing official has been announced about the coat or about Anderson's next public appearance, and Yahoo does not say where she was heading in New York.
