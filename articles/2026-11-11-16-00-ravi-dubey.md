Days after Ramayana reached cinemas, the attention around Ravi Dubey's Lakshman has moved from the screen to two unexpected places: a village workshop in Assam and the social media debate about the sequel. Nitesh Tiwari's mythological epic opened on 5 November with [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) as Lord Rama and Dubey as his devoted younger brother.

## The Assam artisans who built the armour

According to [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/entertainment/bollywood/assamese-craftsmen-who-created-ranbir-kapoor-ravie-dubey-armour-never-knew-it-was-for-ramayana-worked-3-years-on-cane-rubber-armour-101794274724147.html), master artisan Jaynal Abdin of Jania Banglipara village in Barpeta district led a 15-member team that handcrafted 30 to 35 pieces of the film's cane-and-rubber armour. The work stretched over roughly three and a half years.

Abdin told ANI the job reached him through a local organisation, Gram Unnayan, rather than the production house. A file of drawings and designs came first, followed by visitors from the film's team who checked measurements and fittings.

The first prototype had fit problems around the neck and shoulders. Then the main actor tried it on, and Abdin said it felt hard and tight, as pure cane naturally is. The team was sent black and green rubber, but weaving with rubber alone did not work either. Abdin suggested a half-cane, half-rubber hybrid to the costume designer, who asked for a sample. That design was approved.

The pace was uneven. Abdin said months could pass with no word, then a call would ask for eight or ten pieces at short notice. Finished jackets went from Barpeta to Guwahati Airport and on to the shoot in Mumbai.

The most striking detail is the secrecy. The artisans were told only that the pieces were for a film shoot and learned the project was Ramayana after release. "I feel immense pride," Abdin said, describing the recognition as belonging to his team, his village and the whole district.

## Why fans are already looking at Part 2

Ramayana ends on a cliffhanger, and [MensXP](https://www.mensxp.com/entertainment/bollywood/186734-ravi-dubey-lakshman-raghav-juyal-meghnad-battle-ramayana-part-2.html) reports that Raghav Juyal's Meghnad has become a major talking point online. The character first appears through Jatayu's narration, in a sequence showing him doing penance as snakes rise from a fire. MensXP reads that image as a hint of the Naag Paash weapon he uses against Rama and Lakshman in the second film.

In the epic, Meghnad, also called Indrajit, strikes Lakshman down with the Shakti weapon. Hanuman then travels to the Himalayas for the Sanjeevani herb and brings back the whole mountain to save him. One fan post quoted by MensXP imagines the impact of that scene, and others call the clash an "epic semifinal" before the Rama and Ravana finale.

Anyone hoping to see it will wait. Part 2 arrives on Diwali 2028, MensXP says.
