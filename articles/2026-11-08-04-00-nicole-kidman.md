**Quick summary**

- Babygirl, the 2024 erotic thriller starring [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), is now on Hulu.
- Hulu named the film in its full November 2026 lineup of movies and TV shows, as reported by [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources).
- The film was previously available on HBO Max, where it reached the platform's Top 10 in 2025.
- Kidman's next project is the horror film The Young People, which has no release date yet.

Nicole Kidman's erotic thriller Babygirl has found a new streaming home. Hulu has added the 2024 film to its November 2026 lineup, according to [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/babygirl-thriller-coming-to-hulu-november-2026/), after the movie's earlier run on HBO Max. The listing gives viewers another chance to catch one of the most talked-about performances of Kidman's recent career.

## Nicole Kidman Babygirl Hulu: what is changing

Hulu recently published its full November schedule, and Babygirl sits among the new additions. The film runs 115 minutes. Comic Book Resources reports that it was previously on HBO Max, where it spent time in the Top 10 during 2025. The report does not say how long the film will stay on Hulu, and it does not cover availability in Australia, so local viewers should check their own streaming services.

## What Babygirl is about

Kidman plays Romy Mathis, a high-powered chief executive who is unhappy in her marriage and personal life. After meeting a younger man named Samuel, played by [Harris Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_Dickinson), she learns he is an intern at her company. The two begin an affair, and Romy keeps seeing him despite her doubts as their encounters become more intense and she explores desires she has long suppressed.

Samuel then starts pushing the limits of the arrangement. Romy has to keep the relationship hidden while facing how much she wants to continue it.

The supporting cast includes [Sophie Wilde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Wilde), [Antonio Banderas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antonio_Banderas), [Esther-Rose McGregor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_McGregor), Vaughan Reilly, [Victor Slezak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Slezak), [Leslie Silva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Silva) and [Gaite Jansen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaite_Jansen). [Halina Reijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halina_Reijn), the filmmaker behind [Bodies Bodies Bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bodies_Bodies_Bodies), wrote and directed it.

## How the film performed

Babygirl was a moderate box office success, according to Comic Book Resources. It took in more than US$64 million against a reported budget of US$20 million, roughly three times what it cost to make.

Critics were largely positive. The film holds a 76 per cent score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), though the audience score is much lower at 48 per cent. The National Board of Review named it one of the 10 best films of 2024 and gave Kidman its [Best Actress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Actress) prize for the role.

## Where Kidman goes from here

Kidman has stayed busy since. She voiced a character in the 2024 animated musical fantasy Spellbound and starred in Mimi Cave's 2025 psychological thriller Holland. In 2026 she reunited with [Sandra Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandra_Bullock) for Practical Magic 2, the sequel to their 1990s cult favourite [Practical Magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Practical_Magic). The sequel received mixed reviews, per the same report.

Next up is The Young People, a horror film from Longlegs writer and director [Osgood Perkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osgood_Perkins). Lola Tung, Nico Parker, Brendan Hines, Tatiana Maslany, Johnny Knoxville and Heather Graham are also in the cast. It marks another return to horror for Kidman, who earlier starred in the supernatural thriller The Others.
