**Quick summary**

- [Zara Larsson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zara_Larsson) appears in season 57 of Sesame Street, which premiered on Monday, 23 November 2026, on Netflix and PBS Kids.
- In a clip from the episode, she sings the Swedish alphabet after Elmo asks her to teach it to him.
- She also features in the season trailer, singing an original song with Elmo and the puppet cast.
- The cameo arrives just after her second Grammy nomination, for best dance pop recording with "Blue Moon".

Zara Larsson has swapped the concert stage for Sesame Street. The Swedish pop singer turns up in the show's 57th season, which began on Monday, 23 November, and spends part of her episode teaching Elmo the Swedish alphabet, according to [People](https://people.com/zara-larsson-teaches-elmo-swedish-abcs-in-sweet-sesame-street-clip-12275541).

## What happens in the Sesame Street clip

The released clip is short and gentle. Elmo tells Larsson he has heard that the alphabet is different in English and in Swedish, then asks whether she will teach it to him. She agrees, and People reports that she sang the letters while Elmo danced along. At the end Elmo asks for a hug and thanks her for the lesson.

Yahoo, which republished a Billboard report, adds a small detail. Asked whether she can do it, Larsson answers "a little bit" before singing the sounds of the Swedish letters. The same piece says she also performs a song about dancing with the Sesame Street gang in the episode.

Larsson is a Stockholm native, so the lesson comes from her own language. Wisevoter, summarising Billboard's coverage, notes that the segment introduces young viewers to a non-English alphabet through a familiar pop voice.

## A trailer a month earlier

Her involvement was not a surprise. According to Yahoo, Larsson appeared in the season 57 trailer about a month before the premiere. In it she sings an original song with Elmo and dances alongside the furry cast, with lines such as "Wag your tails, let's put on a show".

## Part of a long line of musical guests

Sesame Street has a habit of booking music stars. Yahoo lists guests from the past year or so as [Post Malone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post_Malone), SZA, Reneé Rapp, [Noah Kahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Kahan) and [Chris Stapleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Stapleton). People adds that Larsson is one of several stars scheduled for the new season.

The season is available on both Netflix and PBS Kids, so American families can watch through streaming or public television. Viewership figures for the premiere have not been reported.

## Why the timing suits her

The appearance follows a busy stretch. Earlier in November, nominations for the 2027 Grammy Awards were announced, and Larsson earned her second career nomination, in the best dance pop recording category, for "Blue Moon", according to People and Billboard.

"Blue Moon" is on her album Midnight Sun. Billboard says the record reached No. 36 on the Billboard 200 and No. 2 on the Top Dance Albums chart in May. People notes the album first came out in June 2025 and that a deluxe remix edition, titled Girls Trip, followed in May. She has since finished the world tour for the album, and People quotes her Instagram post from the final show in Bangkok, where she said she was "very happy" and "very much filled with love" and planned to eat mango sticky rice.

## What happens next

Larsson's next live date is in Abu Dhabi on 3 December, according to People. She is then scheduled to play Coachella in April 2027. The Grammy Awards ceremony itself is still ahead, with the nominations already announced.

Sesame Street fans can expect more guests across the season, though the sources do not list which other stars appear or when.
