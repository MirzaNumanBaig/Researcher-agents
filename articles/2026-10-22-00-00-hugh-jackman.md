Two of Hugh Jackman's films are back in the spotlight this week. Christopher Nolan's *The Prestige* is returning to the big screen for its 20th anniversary, and Jackman's recent thriller *The Death of Robin Hood* has reached the top of HBO Max's US movie chart.

## The Prestige returns on 70mm and IMAX

According to [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/film/christopher-nolan-the-prestige-imax-cinema-date-anniversary-3973710), the 2006 mystery will play in select cinemas on 70mm film and IMAX from 29 January 2027. The date was announced by Walt Disney Studios on 20 October. NME reports that Disney holds the North American distribution rights, while Warner Bros. owns the international rights and has yet to announce release plans outside the US. No Australian dates have been confirmed.

The film follows two rival magicians in Victorian England, played by Jackman (as Robert Angier) and Christian Bale (as Alfred Borden), whose partnership collapses into an obsessive feud. Scarlett Johansson, Michael Caine and David Bowie, as Nikola Tesla, also star. It is based on Christopher Priest's 1995 novel.

NME notes the film holds a 77 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and took US$109 million against a US$40 million budget. In a statement quoted by the outlet, Nolan said he was "particularly thrilled" to offer audiences the chance to see it on 70mm and, for the first time, on IMAX.

## The Death of Robin Hood climbs the streaming charts

Meanwhile, [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2210087-hugh-jackman-the-death-of-robin-hood-beats-sydney-sweeney-hbo-max) reports that *The Death of Robin Hood* reached No. 1 on HBO Max's US Top 10 movies list within days of arriving on the platform on 16 October. The report cites FlixPatrol data for 20 October and says the film also sits at No. 5 on HBO Max's overall chart from Amazon Channels. It edged out *The Housemaid*, the Sydney Sweeney and Amanda Seyfried thriller, which sits at No. 2.

Written and directed by Michael Sarnoski, the A24 film reimagines the Robin Hood legend as a dark thriller. Jackman plays an aged Robin Hood haunted by his violent past, who takes refuge in a priory after a serious wound. Jodie Comer, Bill Skarsgard, Murray Bartlett and Noah Jupe co-star.

The film premiered at the Sydney Film Festival on 12 June 2026 and opened in US cinemas a week later. ComingSoon.net says it earned US$9.5 million on a US$20 million budget, and that reviews were mixed: a 69 per cent Rotten Tomatoes rating from 189 critics, a Metacritic score of 61 and a C+ from CinemaScore audiences.

## What happens next

For local fans, the key question is whether Warner Bros. brings the anniversary screenings of *The Prestige* to Australian IMAX cinemas. At this stage that has not been announced. The streaming chart figures for *The Death of Robin Hood* are a snapshot taken a few days after launch, so its position may shift.
