**Quick summary**

- Vogue has named [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) its Best Dressed of 2026 and put him on a digital cover, according to Parade and AOL.
- The cover shoot was photographed by Collier Schorr and styled by Spencer Singer, with looks from Gucci, Loewe and Prada.
- In a Vogue podcast interview this week, Storrie said he currently lives with co-star [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) while season 2 of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) is filmed.
- Season 2 is reported to be due in April 2027, though details remain under wraps.

Connor Storrie has been crowned Vogue's Best Dressed of 2026, fronting a trio of digital covers and sitting down for an hour-long podcast with the magazine. The Heated Rivalry actor, 26, used the conversation to talk about fashion, unwanted attention from fans and the unusual living arrangement he shares with his co-star while the next season is in production.

## What Vogue's Best Dressed covers look like

According to [AOL's report](https://www.aol.com/articles/connor-storrie-crowned-vogue-2026-205915000.html), which was first published by theFashionSpot, the shoot was photographed by Collier Schorr and styled by Spencer Singer. Storrie wears Gucci, Loewe and Prada across the three cover images. The same report notes that Vogue under editor [Chloe Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloe_Malle) has recently given covers to names including Jenna Ortega, Mikey Madison, Kaia Gerber and Doja Cat.

Reaction was not uniformly warm. AOL's piece quotes several members of theFashionSpot's forum who criticised the photography and the styling, a reminder that a cover can split opinion even when the headline is flattering.

## What Storrie said about fashion

Per [Yahoo News UK's syndication of Parade's story](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/connor-storrie-named-best-dressed-000452797.html), Storrie spoke with Malle in a podcast recorded at Vogue's offices. He said he has always liked to express himself, but that red carpets, events and fashion shows changed how he thinks about clothes. He credited his stylist, James Yardley, with showing him how much thought and effort goes into getting dressed for these occasions.

Asked whether he and Williams coordinate their outfits, he said they do not discuss it. He described a "natural cohesion" between them and said they are "cut from the same cloth". He also said Williams has a street-style, edgier look that he envies.

## Living with Hudson Williams during filming

The interview also produced a detail that other outlets picked up quickly. [Queerty reported](https://www.queerty.com/wait-connor-storrie-hudson-williams-are-living-together-20261123/) that Storrie said he and Williams are sharing a home while the show films. Storrie explained that they had been staying in a hotel, which he called brilliant, but that he needed a washing machine and a kitchen.

Queerty adds that the series is shooting its second and third seasons back to back, which means the two actors spend long stretches together on set and off it. We covered the same revelation in our earlier post, [Connor Storrie Is Living With Hudson Williams During Filming](https://moduscarry.com/connor-storrie-hudson-williams/).

## Fans, photos and staying offline

In a separate part of the same Run-Through with Vogue conversation, reported by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/connor-storrie-shares-surprising-fan-232747637.html), Storrie said he has been photographed regularly since Heated Rivalry premiered in November 2025. He said people are "not subtle" about it and that he almost always notices a camera pointed his way.

He described being unsettled when someone photographed his new haircut at a restaurant without him knowing, and when two people appeared to follow him down a street. He was clear that most encounters are positive, saying many people simply want to tell him what the show means to them.

Queerty says he stays off social media entirely. By his account, he only learned of the online nickname "Buzznor", a joke about his buzzed hair, when fans told him in person. His response, as quoted, was that he did not care about it.

## What happens next

Heated Rivalry follows hockey players Shane Hollander, played by Williams, and Ilya Rozanov, played by Storrie. It is based on Rachel Reid's book The Long Game. Parade describes an April 2027 release for season 2 as a rumour, while Queerty says the season is slated for that month. No official date is cited in the sources we read, so treat April as unconfirmed until the network says otherwise.
