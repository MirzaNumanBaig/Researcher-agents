**Quick summary**

- [Justine Lupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justine_Lupe), 37, has announced on Instagram that she is pregnant with her second child.
- Her husband, artist Tyson Mason, is the father. The couple's first child, a daughter named Ellis, was born in August 2024.
- The news comes weeks after season three of Netflix's *Nobody Wants This* premiered on 22 October.
- Grazia reported the announcement on 26 November 2026.

## Justine Lupe confirms she is pregnant again

Justine Lupe, who plays Morgan in Netflix's *Nobody Wants This*, has told fans she is expecting her second child with husband Tyson Mason. According to [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/us/articles/nobody-wants-this-star-justine-lupe-37-announces-she-is-pregnant-with-her-second-child-grateful-for-all-things-cookin-and-fully-baked-today/), the actress shared the news on Instagram, where she described being grateful for "all things cookin' and fully baked today".

The post gives no due date in the material Grazia reported, so the timing of the birth is not yet known.

## A family story that began with a dog portrait

Lupe and Mason, a professional artist, married in an intimate courthouse ceremony in February 2025, according to Grazia. Their daughter Ellis and the couple's dog were there. Ellis was born in August 2024, so the new baby will give her a sibling roughly two years or so after her own arrival, going by the reported dates.

The couple's story began when Lupe commissioned Mason to draw a portrait of her dog. The relationship moved quickly, and Grazia reports she became pregnant with their first child shortly after they began dating. In October 2026 she spoke about that unplanned path to parenthood on Jesse Tyler Ferguson's *Dinner's On Me* podcast.

## Motherhood and a busy acting career

Lupe has been open about working while pregnant. Grazia reports she was more than five months pregnant while filming the first season of *Nobody Wants This*. She praised her co-stars, [Kristen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Bell) in particular, and the production team for making the set supportive for expectant mothers.

She has also pushed back on the idea that becoming a mother means giving up a successful acting career, and has called for attitudes inside the entertainment industry to change.

The actress is also known for her role in *Succession*. In *Nobody Wants This* she appears alongside Bell and [Adam Brody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Brody). Season three arrived on Netflix on 22 October, so the pregnancy announcement lands while the show is still fresh with viewers.
