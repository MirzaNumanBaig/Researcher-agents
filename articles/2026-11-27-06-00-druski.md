**Quick summary**

- Comedian [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) appeared on the First We Feast interview show Hot Ones, with host Sean Evans, on 26 November 2026.
- He talked about his career while eating increasingly hot wings, and his glasses fogged up along the way.
- The episode comes shortly after he hosted [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live).
- His Netflix series *Collect & Praise* launches on 1 December, according to First We Feast.

Druski, the comedian whose real name is Drew Desbordes, took on the Hot Ones wing challenge this week, and the episode landed on 26 November. Between bites he spoke about his rise online, his comedy heroes and a busy few weeks that now includes a Netflix launch.

## What happened on Hot Ones

Hot Ones works on a simple formula: host Sean Evans asks questions while the guest works through a line of wings with ever hotter sauces. [Mashable](https://mashable.com/life/druski-hot-ones) reports that Druski's glasses fogged up as the heat built, and that the pair covered his career from his early content-creation days to his upcoming Netflix series.

Mashable also quoted him mid-challenge, after a large bite of a high-tier wing. He said he was trying hard because he loves the show, and admitted he disliked being such a fan while suffering through the sauce. The outlet said he called time after the sauce known as Da Bomb, and judged that he "did great".

First We Feast, which makes the show, framed the episode around his comedy heroes and his rise. It also noted he became the first digital creator to host Saturday Night Live, a claim made by the publisher itself.

## Why the timing matters

The wings arrived at a busy moment. [First We Feast](https://firstwefeast.com/blogs/lead-gallery/druski-hits-a-wall-while-eating-spicy-wings) describes him as fresh off hosting SNL, with characters that have collected billions of views, from the frat bro Kyle Rogger to a megachurch pastor in Louboutins.

That pastor is the centre of his next project. Druski stars as Pastor Dru in *Collect & Praise*, a semi-scripted comedy series launching on Netflix on 1 December. Mashable's write-up also mentions that he worked with [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone) and hosted SNL, both topics of the conversation with Evans.

The sources do not give viewing figures for the Hot Ones episode. [Wisevoter](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/2026/11/26/druski-hot-ones-spicy-wing-appearance), which says its report draws on Mashable, states the same: no viewership numbers were reported.

## A wider pattern for online comedians

Wisevoter points out that Hot Ones has become a regular stop for comedians and performers who want to reach audiences beyond traditional talk shows. For a creator who built his name on short online sketches, an interview-and-challenge format is a natural fit, and this one came right before a streaming launch.
