**Quick summary**

- *Wild Horse Nine*, the new spy comedy starring [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) and John Malkovich, has lost 1,000 US cinemas heading into its fourth weekend, according to Collider.
- Collider, citing The Numbers, puts its domestic total at US$18.31 million.
- Last weekend it took US$2.46 million, down 46% on the weekend before.
- It is up against a crowded Thanksgiving weekend, which makes screens harder to hold.

Sam Rockwell's latest film is shrinking fast at the US box office. *Wild Horse Nine*, directed by [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh), will play in 1,000 fewer cinemas this weekend, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/sam-rockwell-wild-horse-nine-removed-from-1000-theaters/), which cites theatre-count data from The Numbers. That is the start of its fourth weekend in release.

## What the box office numbers show

Collider reports that the film opened in 1,100 US cinemas on 6 November. It grew to 2,175 locations in its second weekend, then started to contract. Losing 1,000 sites leaves it in roughly half the venues it had at its peak, and Collider says it has shed more than 54% of its locations since that second weekend.

The takings tell the same story. The film has made US$18.31 million domestically so far, so it is close to the US$20 million mark but not there yet. Of that, US$2.46 million came last weekend, a fall of 46%.

Per-cinema figures are thin. Collider says it averaged US$1,231 per location over the weekend, then only US$192 on Monday. Those are the sort of figures that lead exhibitors to hand screens to newer releases, especially over Thanksgiving, one of the most competitive weekends of the American cinema year.

These are US figures only. The Collider report does not cover international takings, so there is no worldwide total to quote here.

## The film and its cast

*Wild Horse Nine* is set in Chile in 1973. Two CIA agents are sent to Easter Island shortly before the military coup. Malkovich plays Chris Carlisle, who is increasingly preoccupied with his own mortality. Rockwell plays Lee Chesterfield, and the two have a long shared history to reckon with. Things get harder when Chris befriends a pair of students on the island, per Collider's description.

McDonagh wrote and directed. The cast also includes Steve Buscemi, Mariana di Girolamo, Ailín Salas, Tom Waits and Parker Posey, according to the same report. You can read more background on the film on its [Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine).

## A familiar partnership

Rockwell and McDonagh have worked together before. Rockwell won an Academy Award for *Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri*, and he was also in McDonagh's [*Seven Psychopaths*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Psychopaths), as Collider notes. McDonagh's last film, *The Banshees of Inisherin*, reunited [Colin Farrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Farrell) and [Brendan Gleeson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Gleeson) and drew nine Oscar nominations.

That history sets a high bar, which is part of why the current numbers stand out. Collider frames the cinema cut as a sign that the film will be harder to find on Thanksgiving weekend.

## What happens next

The film stays in release, but with far fewer screens. The next test is how it holds up this weekend with half its footprint gone. If Monday's US$192 per-cinema average is a guide, the cut may not slow the slide, though one weekend is not a verdict. No streaming or home release date has been confirmed in the sources used for this article.
