**Quick summary**

- [Troye Sivan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troye_Sivan) released his fourth album, *She's the Best*, on 9 October 2026.
- In interviews with W Magazine and Dazed, the 31-year-old Australian says it grew out of a feeling of isolation during the Sweat tour era.
- The title-track video features a slow dance with [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), who plays a burlesque club owner.
- W Magazine also named Sivan in its Originals 2026 line-up, alongside Karl-Anthony Towns and [Jordan Firstman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Firstman).

Troye Sivan has spent the weeks since *She's the Best* came out explaining why a pop star known for the dance floor made a record about what happens when the music stops. In fresh interviews published on 26 October, he told [W Magazine](https://www.wmagazine.com/culture/troye-sivan-interview-shes-the-best) and [Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/music/article/71229/1/troye-sivan-shes-the-best-yearning-love-after-the-party-interview-2026) that the album is about wanting both connection and something more lasting.

## A record about the morning after

Sivan's last album, *Something to Give Each Other*, made him the face of sweaty, euphoric dance-pop. Dazed describes it as his most critically acclaimed release to date. *She's the Best* asks a different question: who do you go home to when the night is over?

"At the core of the album is this love and appreciation for partying and feeling connected to everyone," he told Dazed, "and then also admitting that I'm actually looking for something that feels more true to this very pure version of myself." He added that he is looking for love and a long relationship, and that the two desires can feel opposed. "I do want both," he said.

The sound shifts too. Sivan said he had spent his career chasing "perfect pop", and after pushing hard for that on the previous record he wanted something looser and "almost manic". Dazed reports that he wrote much of the album at home with his brother, and that he drew on the music of Cornelius and [Arthur Russell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Russell). He also pointed to Annie Ernaux's novel *A Simple Passion* about a woman consumed by an affair.

## What went wrong after the Sweat tour

The turning point, according to Sivan, came during the Sweat tour with Charli xcx. He told W Magazine the tour produced "some of the best weeks of my entire life". Over time, though, he said an isolation set in, and he felt exposed in a way he had not before.

He recalled telling a friend that his dating life was dry, and the friend replying with a reference to his hit "Rush". That, Sivan said, made him realise he was no longer telling the whole story. Dazed reports he said much the same from the stage at the album's first show at the Apple Music Hall in Battersea, where he briefly broke down and spoke of a disconnect between the person people assumed he was and his actual life.

W Magazine used the same passage to open its Originals 2026 feature, which also lists Karl-Anthony Towns and Jordan Firstman among its subjects.

## The title track and the Nicole Kidman video

The title track was written with [George Daniel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Daniel), [Jack Antonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Antonoff), [Styalz Fuego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Styalz_Fuego) and Leland. Sivan told W Magazine he had known Daniel through Charli xcx but had never written with him. He said he knew straight away the song was unusual and that it was "not 'Rush'". He also admitted that release days can feel deflating, though a stranger showing him the song on their phone in New York the next morning helped.

In the video, he plays a new performer at a burlesque nightclub. Kidman is the owner who trains him up and puts him in drag for the first time. He said she worked to disarm people around her and was fully committed, to the point that texts about her character began weeks before filming.

He has described the album as an ode to glamorous women in his life, though he said he did not want to try to sum up womanhood. Instead he drew on specific people he knows, and some lyrics are about himself.

## From Perth to the pop charts

Dazed notes that Sivan began posting videos on YouTube at 12, growing up in the suburbs of Perth, and released his debut album [*Blue Neighbourhood*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Neighbourhood) in 2015. He already had more than three million YouTube subscribers then. W Magazine puts the new record as his fourth.

## What happens next

Sivan's first show for the album took place in London days after release, and Dazed notes he made a surprise appearance during Charli xcx's set at ACL. ## Frequently asked questions

### When was Troye Sivan's *She's the Best* released?
The album came out on 9 October 2026, according to W Magazine. It is his fourth studio album.

### Who is in the *She's the Best* music video?
Nicole Kidman appears opposite Sivan as the owner of a burlesque nightclub, and the pair share a slow dance. Sivan told W Magazine he was "completely dazzled" by her.

### What is the album about?
Sivan told Dazed it explores the tension between loving the party and wanting a deeper, lasting connection. W Magazine reports it is also an ode to the women in his life.
