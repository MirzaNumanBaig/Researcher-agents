**Quick summary**

- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) arrived at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney with a sleek, chin-length blonde bob.
- The show was held on 5 November at The Cutaway in Barangaroo, according to the Hindustan Times.
- She wore a sheer white gown with beadwork, black heels and a black handbag, HELLO! and the Hindustan Times reported.
- In earlier interviews she has said her natural hair is badly damaged and that stylists often add hair pieces for shoots.

Margot Robbie's new blonde bob was the talking point at Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney this week, where the 36-year-old walked in with a cropped, side-parted cut that fell to just below her chin, according to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/933461/margot-robbie-36-looks-incredible-in-completely-sheer-white-dress-as-she-unveils-striking-hair-transformation/).

## The look: a sheer white gown and a sharper cut

HELLO! described the dress as a sheer white gown with mesh detailing, a tiered skirt and a large feathery embellishment on the front. She added a black handbag, black heels and eye-catching earrings. Her makeup stayed minimal, with a pink lip and defined brows.

The [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/lifestyle/fashion/margot-robbies-sheer-white-dress-to-nicole-kidmans-chic-pantsuit-celebrities-bring-glamour-to-chanel-cruise-2026-27-show-in-sydney-101793959271630.html) read the outfit as part of the lingerie-inspired dressing trend. It called it a sheer white slip dress with delicate textures and beadwork, and said the simple styling let the detail do the work.

The cut itself, per HELLO!, had a slight swoop at the ends. It is a clear change from the longer golden lengths she has often worn on red carpets.

## Who else was in the room

The Chanel presentation drew a strong Australian crowd. The Hindustan Times lists [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman), [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), filmmaker [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann), actor [Joel Edgerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_Edgerton) and Olympic champion [Cathy Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cathy_Freeman) among the guests.

Kidman wore a black suit with a white waistcoat layered over a matching top. Minogue chose an all-white cardigan and pencil skirt with layered chain belts. Freeman wore a white shirt made with the French shirtmaker Charvet, with jeans.

BeachGrit also reported that surfer Jack Robinson sat in the front row near Robbie and Kidman. Its piece leans on a body-language reading of the row, which is speculation rather than reporting, and nothing in it suggests anything beyond guests seated side by side.

## Why the hair matters to her

Robbie has been unusually frank about her hair. HELLO! quotes her telling Harper's Bazaar that her hair is hard to style well and that "my hair is snapped off", adding that stylists arrive with "suitcases full of fake hair" to make it look full for shoots.

She blamed years of dyeing and hot roller sets, rushed photoshoot styling and the hard water in London. A shorter, blunt cut is a practical answer to that history, though neither HELLO! nor the Hindustan Times reports her own reason for the change.

She has also spoken about dressing for her friends. HELLO! quotes her telling Entertainment Tonight that she is "doing it for my girlfriends" and hopes they get a kick out of each look.

## A collection with a coastal story

The Sydney show brought Chanel's Cruise 2026/27 collection to Australia after its debut in Biarritz, France, in April, according to the Hindustan Times. It was designed by creative director Matthieu Blazy and draws on the seaside town where Gabrielle Chanel opened her first couture house.

The runway mixed tweed suits, fishnet dresses, striped sailor-style tops and Mediterranean prints, with woven bags and bold footwear. ## What happens next

Neither Robbie nor Chanel has commented on the new cut beyond what the reports describe. HELLO! notes that her method dressing began with the 2023 Barbie press tour, when she worked with stylist Andrew Mukamal, and returned for the Wuthering Heights press tour. Her next public appearance will show whether the bob stays.
