**Quick summary**

- Director Paul Schrader says his erotic thriller *Non Compos Mentis*, starring Liam Hemsworth, has been pushed back.
- The reason, according to Schrader: Hemsworth received a competing offer of US$6 million.
- Schrader had earlier told Deadline he planned to shoot in New York from October through November.
- Sam Worthington and Cara Delevingne are also listed in the cast.

[Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) has become the reason a Paul Schrader film missed its start date. According to [The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/paul-schrader-domhnall-brendan-gleeson-liam-hemsworth-film-delay-20261121/), Schrader said on "The Moynihan Report" that *Non Compos Mentis* was meant to be shooting now, but the schedule moved after the actor was offered far more money to do something else.

## What Schrader said about the delay

Schrader, who wrote *Taxi Driver* and directed *First Reformed*, was talking through several projects he has in development at once. *Non Compos Mentis* came first.

His account, as reported by The Playlist, is simple. He was paying Hemsworth a modest sum, in the region of US$100,000 by his own description. Hemsworth then received an offer of US$6 million and asked whether the film could be delayed. Schrader agreed and the production was pushed back.

That is a sixty-fold gap between the two figures, which helps explain why the request was hard to refuse. It is worth noting that the numbers come from Schrader's own recollection in a podcast interview. Neither Hemsworth nor a studio has confirmed them, and the report does not say what the competing project is.

## What the film is about

According to The Playlist, *Non Compos Mentis* is an erotic thriller. It follows two wealthy brothers whose strained relationship worsens when an affair with a younger woman sets off a dangerous love triangle and a fight over the family fortune.

Alongside Hemsworth, the cast includes Sam Worthington and Cara Delevingne. The report does not say which roles each actor plays.

## The original schedule

Schrader had previously told Deadline that he planned to shoot the film in New York from October through November, as The Playlist notes. That window has now passed without cameras rolling, going by his latest comments.

The same interview covered other Schrader projects, including casting news for his ayahuasca drama *The Doors of Perception*, where Domhnall Gleeson has reportedly been cast. The article's excerpt available to us was cut short, so we have not reported any new start date for *Non Compos Mentis*. If one has been set, it was not in the text we could read.
