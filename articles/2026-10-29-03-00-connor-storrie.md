**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is reportedly in talks to join Marvel's *Ghost Rider* as a villain, according to The Hollywood Reporter.
- The film stars [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling), is directed by Shawn Levy and is set for release on 28 July 2028.
- Marvel has not commented on the casting, and the character Storrie would play has not been revealed.
- Fans online are guessing at Blackheart, though that is speculation only.

[Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie), the breakout star of [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), is reportedly in talks to play a villain in Marvel's *Ghost Rider*. The Hollywood Reporter broke the news this week, and the casting would put the 26-year-old opposite Gosling in one of the studio's biggest upcoming releases.

## What has been reported

As [Tempo.co](https://en.tempo.co/read/2122556/connor-storrie-circling-villain-role-in-marvel-ghost-rider-who-is-he) notes, Marvel has not responded to the report, and that outlet cites Variety in saying there is no official word on the casting. Storrie's role is unknown, and so is which version of Ghost Rider the film will follow.

What is known is the framework around it. Tempo.co reports that Shawn Levy, who directed *Deadpool & Wolverine*, is behind the camera, with a script by Jonathan Tropper. Marvel announced the 28 July 2028 release date at this year's San Diego Comic-Con, and production is due to begin in 2027.

The Hollywood Reporter also said the film will be shot on film, with Linus Sandgren, who worked on *Dune Part 3*, as cinematographer. Tempo.co adds that Marvel boss Kevin Feige has said Gosling brought him a "specific vision" of the character, based on particular comics.

## Who could he play?

Nobody outside the studio can say yet. [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) reports that Reddit users have floated several names, and one keeps coming up: Blackheart, the demon son of Mephisto. In the comics he began as a tall, black, tendril-covered creature and has more recently appeared in human form. [Wes Bentley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Bentley) played him in the 2007 *Ghost Rider* film with Nicolas Cage.

Other fans suggested Danny Ketch, a second Ghost Rider, or Daniel Helstrom, the son of Satan from the series *Helstrom*. These are fan theories, not anything Marvel has confirmed.

Mephisto himself is unlikely to be the answer. Tempo.co points out that the character has already been introduced in the Disney+ series *Ironheart*, played by [Sacha Baron Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacha_Baron_Cohen). LaineyGossip makes a related point: Ghost Rider has no signature villain, so Storrie could be playing almost anyone.

## A fast rise

Tempo.co describes Storrie as a Texas native who was waiting tables in Culver City before *Heated Rivalry* creator Jacob Tierney found him. The series, which co-stars Hudson Williams, made him a household name. He hosted *Saturday Night Live* in February and earned an Emmy nomination for guest actor in a comedy series, though the award went to Rob Reiner for *The Bear*.

He was not entirely new to screen work. Tempo.co notes that he played the "young inmate" in 2024's *Joker: Folie à Deux*, and made his film debut in *Riley* in 2023. He also makes horror shorts for free on YouTube and, according to GQ as cited by Tempo.co, is working on a self-financed feature shot on iPhone.

His slate is already crowded. LaineyGossip lists Molly Gordon's comedy *Peaked*, the thriller *Turpentine* with Melissa McCarthy and Paul Walter Hauser, a new film from director Halina Reijn, and Sarah Polley's adaptation of *The Bell Jar*. *Heated Rivalry* season two is filming, with a premiere slated for next spring, per Tempo.co.

## One film or a long contract?

LaineyGossip argues that a villain role could make this a one-off. Its writer compares it to Tony Leung's single appearance in *Shang-Chi*, while noting that Marvel sometimes calls actors back years later, as it did with James Spader. The same column suggests Storrie, who is still building his name and favouring filmmaker-led projects, may prefer one film to a multi-picture deal. That is the outlet's opinion, not reporting.

## What happens next

Marvel has yet to confirm Storrie's involvement, so the first sign will be an official announcement or a studio statement. Filming is due in 2027, and the film opens on 28 July 2028. Until then, the character remains the main open question.
