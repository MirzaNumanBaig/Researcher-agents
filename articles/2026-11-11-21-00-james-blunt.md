**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) is promoting his new album, Airplane Mode, and has been unusually active on X.
- On 7 November 2026, [Jeremy Clarkson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Clarkson) said he would buy the record once Blunt bought Hawkstone beer for his pub.
- Blunt replied the next day, telling Clarkson to stop stalling and buy the album.
- Clarkson said he would buy it for an upcoming clay pigeon shoot; Blunt offered him vinyl.

James Blunt and Jeremy Clarkson spent the weekend of 7 and 8 November 2026 trading barbs on X, with the singer's new album Airplane Mode as the prize. According to [The Poke](https://www.thepoke.com/2026/11/09/james-blunt-clarkson-album-beer-comeback/), which collected the posts, the exchange began with Clarkson and ended with a deal of sorts.

## How the exchange started

Blunt has been promoting Airplane Mode with a steady run of posts. Earlier that week, on 5 November, he shared a mash-up aimed at Leonardo DiCaprio, which The Poke had already picked up.

On 7 November, Clarkson wrote that his "dear friend" Blunt had asked him to buy the new record. He would, he said, as soon as Blunt bought some Hawkstone for his pub. The Poke notes that both men own a pub, though it says Blunt took the idea more literally than the former Top Gear presenter might have expected.

## Blunt's reply

Blunt did not take long. On 8 November he answered with a blunt message of his own, telling Clarkson to stop dithering and buy the album. The post carried an attached image, but The Poke's write-up does not describe what it showed.

Clarkson came back the same day. He accepted the point with a "fair dos" and said he had a clay pigeon shoot coming up, for which he would buy the record. Blunt closed the thread by saying Clarkson would get the vinyl, while everyone else would get the CD.

## What readers made of it

The Poke gathered a handful of reactions. One user called it peak Twitter banter, another praised two people being rude to each other as proof of friendship, and a third boiled the whole thing down to a short summary of the beer, the album and the pigeons. One reader joked that it could become the next Nescafe Gold Blend saga, a reference to the long-running coffee adverts.

None of this is a formal promotion, and neither man has said whether the beer or the record has actually changed hands. The exchange is best read as friendly teasing between two public figures who clearly enjoy needling each other.

## What happens next

Nothing official is scheduled. The open questions are whether Clarkson buys the vinyl and whether Blunt returns the favour with a round of Hawkstone.
