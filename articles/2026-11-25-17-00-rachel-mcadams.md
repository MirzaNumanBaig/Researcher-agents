**Quick summary**

- Marvel Studios reportedly sent the *Iron Man* script only to [Rachel McAdams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_McAdams) in December 2006, hoping she would play Pepper Potts.
- She declined, and the role went to Gwyneth Paltrow.
- McAdams told Bustle in 2023 that she turned down several blockbusters because fame was not suiting her and she needed to stay grounded.
- She later joined the Marvel universe in *Doctor Strange* and now stars in Sam Raimi's *Send Help*.

Rachel McAdams was Marvel's first choice to play [Pepper Potts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pepper_Potts) in *Iron Man*, and she said no. According to a recent [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2325241/why-rachel-mcadams-turned-down-peppers-potts-marvel-iron-man/) retelling, the studio sent the script to her alone in late 2006, before the film that launched the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe) was a sure thing.

## How Marvel came to Rachel McAdams first

SlashFilm points to a December 2006 report by Umberto Gonzalez of Latino Review, which said the *Iron Man* script went exclusively to McAdams. The timing made sense. She had broken out in 2004 as [Regina George](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regina_George) in [Mean Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_Girls) and as Allie Hamilton in *The Notebook*, then starred in Wes Craven's thriller *Red Eye* the following year.

Back then, a film about a second-tier superhero was far from a guaranteed hit. Gwyneth Paltrow got the part instead, according to SlashFilm, and went on to become one of the longest-serving actors in the franchise, last appearing in *Avengers: Endgame* in 2019.

## Why she walked away

McAdams explained her thinking to Bustle in 2023, as quoted by SlashFilm. *Iron Man* was one of several big projects she passed on in that period, along with *Casino Royale*, *Mission: Impossible III* and *The Devil Wears Prada*. She had been lifted into stardom quickly and wanted time to adjust.

"I knew I was in such a lucky spot, but I also knew it wasn't quite jiving with my personality and what I needed to stay sane," she told Bustle.

The record backs that up. SlashFilm notes she made only two films between 2006 and 2008, *Married Life* in 2007 and *The Lucky Ones* in 2008.

## A Marvel link that came anyway

The break did not stall her career. She earned a Best Supporting Actress Oscar nomination for *Spotlight*, and her first big studio film after the pause was the 2009 *[Sherlock Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sherlock_Holmes)*, directed by [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie). She played thief [Irene Adler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irene_Adler) opposite [Robert Downey Jr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Downey_Jr.), who had just become a bigger leading man thanks to *Iron Man*.

Marvel had not forgotten her either. She later played Dr. Christine Palmer, the ex of Stephen Strange, in *[Doctor Strange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Strange)* alongside [Benedict Cumberbatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedict_Cumberbatch), and returned for *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*. That sequel put her in the orbit of director [Sam Raimi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Raimi).

## From Marvel to Send Help

SlashFilm reports that Raimi felt the Marvel film had not made the most of McAdams and resolved to work with her again. The result is [*Send Help*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Send_Help), a survival thriller comedy in which she plays an overlooked office worker stranded on a desert island with her boss, played by Dylan O'Brien. SlashFilm calls it her most deliciously twisted performance yet.

It is a neat circle. The role she skipped in 2006 helped shape a career built on choices made on her own terms rather than the studio's schedule. ## What happens next

The sources do not name any new Marvel role for McAdams. Her most recent work to note is *Send Help*, and SlashFilm's piece suggests that film is where the attention now sits. We will update this story if she or Marvel comments further.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who was originally meant to play Pepper Potts in Iron Man?

According to SlashFilm, citing a December 2006 Latino Review report, Marvel sent the script exclusively to Rachel McAdams. Gwyneth Paltrow was cast after McAdams declined.

### Why did Rachel McAdams turn down Iron Man?

She told Bustle in 2023 that fame was not fitting her personality and that she needed time to stay grounded. She also passed on *Casino Royale*, *Mission: Impossible III* and *The Devil Wears Prada* around the same time.

### Has Rachel McAdams appeared in a Marvel film?

Yes. She played Dr. Christine Palmer in *Doctor Strange* and its sequel, *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*.
