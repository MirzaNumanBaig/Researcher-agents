**Quick summary**

- Lucasfilm has unveiled a new logo for *Star Wars: Starfighter*, the film starring [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling).
- It turns the Rebellion emblem upside down so it reads as a skull, with two crossed blasters beneath it.
- It was shown at New York Comic-Con, according to The Direct, to promote three books tied to the film.
- *Starfighter* opens in cinemas on 28 May 2027.

The new Star Wars Starfighter logo is a skull, and it belongs to Ryan Gosling's new [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) film. Lucasfilm showed the design for *Star Wars: Starfighter* at New York Comic-Con, according to [The Direct](https://thedirect.com/article/star-wars-starfighter-ryan-gosling-film), which says the film reaches theatres on 28 May 2027.

## What the new Star Wars Starfighter logo looks like

The design takes the familiar Rebellion symbol from earlier Star Wars films and flips it upside down so the shape suggests a skull. Below it, two blasters cross to form an "X", which The Direct says echoes the skull and crossbones on real-world pirate flags.

It is not the only look Lucasfilm has tried. An earlier version, first shown at the 2026 D23 Expo, used an angled "Star Wars" in the style of the opening crawl with "Starfighter" set on top. Another used black lettering for the franchise name and a slightly slanted gold title. A third sat inside a red-bordered triangular patch, with orange and yellow lettering ringed by stars.

Actor Aaron Pierre also showed a version on a baseball cap. The Direct describes it as similar to the triangle patch, though the picture was blurry and in black and white.

## Why the logo matters for Lucasfilm

The new artwork was used to promote three books that arrive after the film: an Art Of volume by Phil Szostak, a Visual Dictionary by Pablo Hidalgo, and an expanded novelisation by Delilah Dawson.

The stakes are real. According to The Direct, *Starfighter* will be Lucasfilm's second cinema release since 2019's *The Rise of Skywalker*. The outlet describes this year's *The Mandalorian & Grogu* as arguably the biggest flop in Star Wars history on the big screen. A distinct look is one way to signal that this film is a fresh start.

## Behind-the-scenes pictures and a gritty tone

Promotion so far has leaned on set photos. They include Gosling with newcomer Flynn Gray beside a large body of water, and a black-and-white shot of the pair in front of a new land-speeder. Gosling also shared a candid picture on a boat with director [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy), and another photo shows Levy watching a scene unfold on set.

The Direct reads the pirate imagery as a sign of a grittier film. It notes that Gosling's character, Kade Auberon, is described as a Han Solo-style scoundrel who keeps people at a distance, with a cynical streak and a mysterious past. The outlet also points out that the cast is largely non-Jedi, which it says suggests a more down-to-earth story. Plot details remain under wraps, and that reading is the outlet's interpretation, not a studio statement.

## Gosling's next role: Ghost Rider

Gosling and Levy will work together again on Marvel Studios' *Ghost Rider*, scheduled for 28 July 2028, according to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/ryan-gosling-facial-features-suddenly-131155000.html), republishing a Bored Panda report. Levy, best known for directing [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things), is set to direct, with [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper) attached as screenwriter. Gosling takes over a role [Nicolas Cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Cage) played in two earlier live-action films.

The same report says cinematographer Linus Sandgren told Variety on 14 November that he sees the film as "grounded and emotional". Some fans online pushed back, wanting more of the character's fantastical side.
