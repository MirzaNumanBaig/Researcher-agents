**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) shared Instagram photos of her Strictly partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) being taken away in an ambulance, after he pulled out of Saturday's live show.
- [Aljaž Škorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in and danced an Argentine Tango with her to "Missing" by Everything But The Girl.
- Now To Love reports the pair scored 35 out of 40, her best mark on the show so far.
- An unnamed insider told Woman's Day that Goodrem and her husband could consider spending longer periods in London. This is not confirmed.

Delta Goodrem has called it "a week full of curveballs" after her *Strictly Come Dancing* partner Kai Widdrington was forced out of Saturday's live show at the last minute and a stand-in partner took his place. The Australian singer, 42, posted pictures on Instagram of Widdrington in an ambulance, alongside photos of her dancing with Aljaž Škorjanec, according to the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16296377/Delta-Goodrem-photo-Strictly-Kai-Widdrington-ambulance.html).

## What happened before the Saturday show

The Daily Mail reports that Widdrington, 31, could not compete because of an illness and that his fellow professional Škorjanec took over for the Argentine Tango. The paper describes the illness as a mystery, and no cause has been given in the sources we read.

Goodrem addressed it directly in her post. "It takes a few to tango! A week full of curveballs but I am so proud of our performance and what we managed to pull off," she wrote, adding that the nerves "were definitely high". She also thanked Widdrington, calling him a "trooper" who taught her everything and stayed with the preparation all week.

Now To Love adds that Widdrington was hospitalised, which left Goodrem with less than 24 hours to learn the dance with a new partner. That is a tight turnaround on a show where couples usually spend the week drilling a single routine. For more on how that night played out, see our earlier coverage of the [Strictly tango with Aljaž after Kai's exit](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-strictly-tango/).

## The score that followed

Despite the disruption, the judges rewarded the performance. Now To Love says Goodrem and Škorjanec earned 35 out of 40, which it describes as her highest score on the series and close to the top of the leaderboard. We covered the marks in more detail in [Delta Goodrem scores her best Strictly marks with Aljaž](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-strictly-score/).

Goodrem has also had a physical hurdle this season. Now To Love notes she recently recovered from a rib injury, with her husband, the musician Matthew Copley, helping her back to health. Copley plays in her band, and the two married in Malta in June 2025, according to the same report.

## Is a London move on the cards?

Now To Love's piece, which cites a source speaking to Woman's Day, suggests the *Strictly* run could be the start of a bigger British chapter. The insider said Goodrem "has always had international ambitions" and that London "could be an exciting base" for her and Copley if more opportunities arrive.

This is an anonymous claim, not an announcement, and neither Goodrem nor her team has confirmed any plan to relocate. The same source says friends would not be surprised if the couple spent longer periods in London, and stressed that it would not necessarily mean leaving Australia for good.

The report points to a busy year in Europe as context. It says an estimated 1.3 million viewers watched her perform "Eclipse" at Eurovision in May, where it describes her as taking third place, and that she followed it with appearances on *The Voice UK* and *The Voice of Germany*. Goodrem herself is quoted describing a life of touring the world with a partner she loves.

## What happens next

Goodrem's *Strictly* run continues, and the main open question is whether Widdrington is well enough to return to the studio for the coming live show. Neither source says. ## Frequently asked questions

### Why did Delta Goodrem have a different partner on Strictly?

Her usual partner, Kai Widdrington, pulled out of Saturday's show at the last minute because of illness. Aljaž Škorjanec danced the Argentine Tango with her instead.

### What did Delta Goodrem score for the tango?

Now To Love reports 35 out of 40, which it calls her highest score on the show so far.

### Is Delta Goodrem moving to London?

Not officially. A source told Woman's Day that she and her husband could spend longer periods in London, but this is unconfirmed and no move has been announced.
