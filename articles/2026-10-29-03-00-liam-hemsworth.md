**Quick summary**

- [Jennifer Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Lawrence) addressed an old claim by [Liam Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liam_Hemsworth) on the Call Her Daddy podcast, released on 28 October 2026.
- Hemsworth said in 2014 that she ate tuna or garlic before their kissing scenes in The Hunger Games.
- Lawrence told host Alex Cooper she did not do it on purpose and that long shoots explain the rest.
- She also shared a kissing-scene anecdote about [Bradley Cooper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bradley_Cooper) from Silver Linings Playbook.

Jennifer Lawrence has answered a claim that has followed her for more than a decade. On Call Her Daddy this week, the actress said she never deliberately ate tuna or garlic to put off Liam Hemsworth before their kissing scenes in [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games), according to [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/celebrity/jennifer-lawrence-hunger-games-kiss-scenes-liam-hemsworth-bad-breath-427733-20261028) and [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/10/28/jennifer-lawrence-responds-to-liam-hemsworths-tuna-sandwich-dig-reveals-cringey-remark-she-made-to-bradley-cooper/).

## What Hemsworth said in 2014

The story starts with an appearance by the Australian actor on Jimmy Fallon's chat show in 2014. Hemsworth said kissing Lawrence on set was "pretty uncomfortable", while stressing that she is "one of my best friends".

As quoted by LADbible, he said she would "make a point of eating garlic or tuna fish, or something that was disgusting" and then announce it just before the scene. His joking reply, in his telling, was that he couldn't wait to "taste it".

Lawrence, now 36, played [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) in the franchise. Hemsworth played Gale Hawthorne. LADbible reports the pair have said they kissed on screen only "one time" and that their relationship has always been platonic.

## Lawrence's response on Call Her Daddy

Host Alex Cooper put the question to her directly, asking why she would eat tuna and garlic before kissing him. Lawrence's answer, as quoted by both outlets, was a flat "I didn't!"

She then spoke to Hemsworth directly: "I don't even know what to say, Liam." Her explanation was practical. Filming runs for many months and takes a lot of time, she said, and he made it sound as if she had simply decided to eat tuna on purpose.

This is not her first attempt to clear it up. LADbible notes she addressed it in 2023 on the YouTube series Hot Ones, where she said the breath was "not intentional" and that it was "just what I was eating". Her line then was that he "should just get over it".

## A different rule for American Hustle

LADbible also points back to a 2016 interview on The Graham Norton Show. There Lawrence said she did not care what she had eaten before kissing her Hunger Games co-stars, including [Josh Hutcherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Hutcherson).

The mood changed on [American Hustle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Hustle) in 2013. Working opposite [Christian Bale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bale), she said, she was "really clean" and ate "lots and lots of mints". It is a light-hearted account, and it fits her argument that the tuna was a lunch choice rather than a tactic.

## The Bradley Cooper kiss

Just Jared reports the same interview covered Cooper, her co-star in Silver Linings Playbook, Serena, Joy and American Hustle. Silver Linings Playbook ends with a long kiss between their characters.

Lawrence recalled asking him, "Aren't you so sick of kissing?" She said he did not like the remark, although she thinks he laughed. In her words, it was one of those moments where you say "Right?" and the other person answers "Oh?". Just Jared adds that Cooper has previously described another line she delivered after kissing him, which he also didn't enjoy.

## What happens next

Neither outlet reports any reply from Hemsworth to the latest comments. Just Jared lists a separate item that Lawrence and Hutcherson are officially returning to The Hunger Games as Katniss and Peeta, but gives no further detail in the text we could read, so treat it as a headline only for now.
