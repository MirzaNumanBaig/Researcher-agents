**Quick summary**

- Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, 18, is on the cover of the November issue of Vogue Germany, her first solo Vogue cover.
- In the accompanying interview she said she is "very aware" of the opportunities her family name has given her, according to Page Six and Pedestrian TV.
- She is the daughter of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban), and says Kidman would not let her model until she turned 16.
- She shared the cover on Instagram on Tuesday, calling it "a dream come true".

Sunday Rose, the 18-year-old daughter of Nicole Kidman and Keith Urban, has landed the cover of Vogue Germany's November issue and used the accompanying interview to address the "nepo baby" label that has followed her into fashion. She told the magazine she knows she has been given chances not everyone can get, and that she tries to earn them by turning up prepared and treating people well.

## What Sunday Rose said about the "nepo baby" label

Page Six reports that Sunday Rose made her cover debut in a dressed-down Louis Vuitton look. Her most-quoted line was direct: "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have, and I'm truly grateful to be able to take advantage of them."

She went on to describe how she approaches a job. According to Page Six, she said she always gives her best, never arrives with a negative attitude "no matter how tired or jet-lagged" she may be, and treats everyone with respect. Pedestrian TV described it as the first time she has publicly acknowledged her privilege in those terms.

She also spoke about growing up in Nashville, Tennessee. Most people know who her parents are, she said, but she hasn't "intentionally told anyone". She put that down to the area being small, so most classmates simply knew when she started school.

## How she got into modelling

Her parents were not keen on an early start. Sunday Rose told Vogue Germany that Kidman, who is 59 and stars in *Practical Magic 2*, wouldn't let her model until she was 16, according to Page Six. Her first job was opening the Miu Miu Spring/Summer 2025 show. Since then she has worked with Dior and Calvin Klein.

Pedestrian TV adds that she has wanted a place in fashion since she was 14 ("I kept nagging my mum about it") and that she made her runway debut at Paris Fashion Week in 2024.

Kidman's influence is a recurring theme. In March, Sunday Rose told Elle Australia that her mother is "my biggest inspiration in life" and "a key part of everything I do". Page Six says the most useful industry advice Kidman gave her was about punctuality: an early call time is never fun, but being on time shows you are prepared and grateful to be there.

## From the Met Gala to the cover

The cover follows a busy year. Sunday Rose made her Met Gala debut at this year's event in New York, walking the steps with Kidman, who was a co-chair alongside Beyoncé, Venus Williams and Anna Wintour. ABC11 reported that Kidman wore a red sequined Chanel gown with feathers, styled by [Jason Bolden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bolden), while her daughter wore pink Christian Dior Haute Couture with floral appliques, according to Vogue Australia.

"I wanted to symbolize something blooming. It's my first Met," Sunday Rose said that night. Kidman told the outlet her daughter had been "so calm" beforehand, and that she still had school the next morning.

On Instagram, Sunday Rose thanked the Vogue team and her agency. "To see those words above my head means everything to me like seriously I am in shock," she wrote, per Pedestrian TV. Kidman marked her daughter's 18th birthday in July with a post that read, "Happy 18th my angel, you couldn't be more loved." [Reese Witherspoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reese_Witherspoon) was among the stars who sent wishes.

The cover lands while Kidman is having a busy stretch of her own. Pedestrian TV points to the success of *Practical Magic 2*, her appearance in Troye Sivan's music video for "She's The Best", and the Adéla song named after her reaching number six on the Billboard Hot 100 this week.
