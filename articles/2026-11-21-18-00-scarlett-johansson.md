**Quick summary**

- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) plays Hester Pearl in *Paper Tiger*, a family crime drama written and directed by James Gray.
- [Adam Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Driver) and Miles Teller play brothers who become entangled with the Russian mob in 1980s New York.
- According to TheWrap, Neon was due to release the film on 13 November 2026, after a Cannes premiere in the Palme d'Or competition.
- Johansson told the Press Association that her ease with Driver helped on set, now that they play in-laws rather than the divorcing couple of *Marriage Story*.

Scarlett Johansson and Adam Driver are back on screen together in *Paper Tiger*, a New York crime drama from director James Gray. Johansson plays the wife of Miles Teller's character, which makes her Driver's sister-in-law, according to the Press Association. The film follows a suburban family pulled into the orbit of the Russian mob.

## A reunion that is not a repeat

The pairing will be familiar to anyone who saw *Marriage Story* in 2019. Johansson and Driver played a divorcing couple in that film, and each earned an individual Oscar nomination, the [Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/entertainment/scarlett-johansson-says-comfort-with-adam-driver-helped-new-film-paper-tiger-4EIVKTA65RLJJHVSQEPXMQKYDE/) reported.

This time the relationship is very different. "Our characters have a different relationship in this film of course because we are in-laws," Johansson said. She called Driver "one of my favourite actors" and said the comfort between them helped the work. She also described Teller as "such a huge part of the dynamic", and said the three actors' friendly way of working adds to the intimacy audiences feel on screen.

That detail matters because the story is built on family friction. The film is not only a crime picture. TheWrap's reviewer described it as a tense thriller that doubles as a warm family drama.

## What Paper Tiger is about

Gray wrote and directed the film. According to [The Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/film/review/2026/05/21/cannes-first-look-review-paper-tiger-gangsters-and-fixers-in-grimy-1980s-new-york/), Teller plays Irwin Pearl, a cautious reservoir engineer who lives with his wife Hester and their two sons. Driver plays Gary, Irwin's older brother and a former NYPD officer who presents himself as a well-connected fixer.

Gary persuades Irwin to join a lucrative canal-redevelopment project linked to Russian mob figures. He promises financial security with no risk. In the Irish Times account, the payoff is largely imagined, and the deal puts Irwin's family in danger. Hester begins on the margins of the brothers' conflict, and the review says her failing eyesight becomes a significant secret.

The film is set in the 1980s and, according to the review, recreates a grimy New York with detailed period design. The Irish Times singled out a home-invasion scene, with Irwin hovering on the stairs while gangsters whisper below, and a shoot-out in a cornfield.

## Cannes, Neon and the critics

*Paper Tiger* premiered at the Cannes Film Festival, where it competed for the Palme d'Or, TheWrap noted. It is Gray's first feature since *Armageddon Time* in 2022. The film also screened at the BFI London Film Festival in October, per the Press Association.

The Irish Times review, written from Cannes, ran to a runtime of one hour and 55 minutes. Its critic called the set pieces flawless and the period recreation exceptional. She also argued that Gray's critique of masculine bravado and the illusions of the American dream ranks among the strongest threads in US cinema. That is one critic's view, not a consensus.

TheWrap added that the part of Hester was first intended for Anne Hathaway, before Johansson took it on. The first trailer arrived on 10 August 2026.

## What happens next

TheWrap reported a Neon release date of 13 November 2026, so the film should already be in cinemas in the United States. Check local listings for Australian dates, as none appears in the sources we read. With festival screenings behind it, the conversation now moves to audiences and awards voters.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who stars in Paper Tiger?
Adam Driver, Miles Teller and Scarlett Johansson lead the cast. Driver and Teller play brothers, and Johansson plays Teller's wife, Hester.

### Who directed Paper Tiger?
James Gray wrote and directed it. It is his first feature since *Armageddon Time* (2022), according to TheWrap.

### Is Paper Tiger a sequel to Marriage Story?
No. It is a separate story. Johansson and Driver worked together on *Marriage Story* in 2019, but here they play in-laws.

### What is Paper Tiger about?
A suburban American family in 1980s New York is drawn into dealings with the Russian mob after one brother pulls the other into a risky property scheme.
