**Quick summary**

- A week after *Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* opened on 5 November 2026, members of the Nitesh Tiwari Ramayana cast are answering questions about the film.
- Lara Dutta told NDTV that viewers should "just enjoy the cinematic experience" and defended Sai Pallavi's warrior version of Sita, according to The Times of India.
- Addinath Kothare, who plays Bharat, says he went to school with Ranbir Kapoor and played cricket with him.
- Sacnilk puts the film at Rs. 934.55 crore worldwide gross after seven days.

Seven days after [Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari)'s *Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* reached cinemas, the Nitesh Tiwari Ramayana cast is fielding questions on two fronts: a debate over how the film portrays Sita, and some unexpected off-screen history between its stars. Both stories were reported by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/) on 12 November.

## Lara Dutta backs Sai Pallavi's Sita

[Lara Dutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Dutta) plays Kaikeyi, the third wife of King Dasharatha and mother of Bharata. In the story, it is Kaikeyi who claims the two boons that send Rama into a 14-year exile. Speaking to NDTV, as reported by The Times of India, Dutta responded to fresh criticism of [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi)'s Sita, which now centres on one sequence in which she appears as a fighter.

Dutta noted that there are many versions of the Ramayana. She argued that royal children of that era, boys and girls alike, were trained in the arts and in weapons, so a Sita skilled in combat is not far-fetched. A woman who chooses a forest exile, she said, "is not going to be a helpless woman" and would eventually pick up something to defend herself. Her closing advice to critics was to stop being "so small-minded".

The newspaper notes that objections to Pallavi's casting predate the release. Early complaints targeted her looks, hair texture and accent. Since opening, the focus has moved to the warrior scenes.

## What the film shows of Sita

According to The Times of India, this adaptation presents Sita as a trained warrior princess. She helps with farming and teaches local children and young people sword fighting. During the exile she stands beside Ram and Lakshman in the forest, armed with twin swords and a bow, and faces demons directly. That version departs from the more passive image some viewers expected, which explains why this one sequence has drawn so much attention.

## The Nitesh Tiwari Ramayana cast and an old school connection

A lighter story comes from [Addinath Kothare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adinath_Kothare), who plays Bharat, Lord Ram's younger brother. He told an interviewer that [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor), who plays Rama, was his senior at school. They lived in the same building and played cricket together, he said.

Kothare also pointed to a family coincidence. His father, a child actor in his younger days, played Neetu Singh's brother in the television show *Ghar Ghar Ki Kahani*. Now the son plays Ranbir's brother. "What a coincidence," he said.

Kothare first appeared on screen as a child in his father's film [Majha Chakula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majha_Chakula), and made his adult debut in *Ved Lavi Jeeva*. This year he was seen in [Yami Gautam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yami_Gautam)'s *Nayi Navelli*.

## The film by the numbers

*Ramayana: Rise of a Legend* is the first instalment of a two-part adaptation. It follows Rama from childhood to his 14-year exile. Kapoor plays Rama, Pallavi is Sita and Yash plays Ravana. [Ravi Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey) is Lakshman and [Sunny Deol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunny_Deol) plays Hanuman. The cast also includes [Anupam Kher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anupam_Kher), [Kajal Aggarwal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kajal_Aggarwal), [Rakul Preet Singh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rakul_Preet_Singh), [Vivek Oberoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivek_Oberoi), [Arun Govil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arun_Govil) and [Indira Krishnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indira_Krishnan). [Namit Malhotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namit_Malhotra) and Yash back the project.

Trade tracker Sacnilk, quoted by The Times of India, reports Rs. 511.10 crore India net and Rs. 611.05 crore India gross by day seven. Overseas gross stands at Rs. 323.50 crore, for a worldwide gross of Rs. 934.55 crore.
