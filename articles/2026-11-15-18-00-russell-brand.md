**Quick summary**

- The UK government says it will consider calls to change the age of consent, after Mia Latter waived her right to anonymity to speak about her allegations against [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand).
- Latter, 37, told The Sunday Times that Brand raped her when she was 16 and he was 30. Brand denied her allegations when they were first reported in 2023.
- Brand is serving 10 years and six months for one count of rape and three of sexual assault involving other women. Latter was not a complainant in that trial.
- A Netflix documentary released on 13 November has added further claims from his former partners.

The UK government has said it will look at changing the age of consent, a day after a woman who alleges Russell Brand raped her as a teenager gave up her anonymity. According to [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/law/2026/nov/15/uk-government-to-consider-changing-age-of-consent-after-allegations-against-russell-brand), deputy prime minister Louise Haigh called a change "a massive step" that "deserves proper consideration and scrutiny". The reports were published on 15 November 2026.

## Who is Mia Latter and what does she allege?

Mia Latter, now 37, was known as "Alice" in the 2023 joint investigation by The Times, The Sunday Times and Channel 4. She has now identified herself in an interview with The Sunday Times, as [reported by the ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-15/russell-brand-alleged-victim-speaks-out/107387312).

Latter says she met Brand in central London when she was 16 and he was 30. She alleges the relationship lasted about three months and that he raped her towards its end. She also alleges he groomed her and was controlling. Brand denied the allegations in 2023.

In April, Brand told [Megyn Kelly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megyn_Kelly) that he had slept with a 16-year-old when he was 30, saying he was "a very different person" then, the ABC reports. Latter told The Sunday Times that police removed her from the trial after that interview. The Guardian reports the Crown Prosecution Service decided not to include her case.

Latter was not a complainant at Southwark Crown Court. The ABC reports she has also stepped back from a possible second trial. The Metropolitan Police told The Sunday Times that an investigation into a separate rape allegation, opened in January, is ongoing.

## What is the government considering?

Latter is campaigning for a staggered age of consent. Under current UK law the age is 16, and adults of any age can legally have sex with 16 and 17-year-olds, The Guardian notes. She wants relationships between adults and under-18s to be criminal.

Haigh said the government needs to "listen to Mia and other victims like her". Women and equalities minister Bridget Phillipson praised Latter's courage. Jess Brown-Fuller, the Liberal Democrat justice spokesperson, backed a review, and The Guardian reports that MPs outside Labour may support a bill.

Haigh also raised non-disclosure agreements. Earlier this week, the Metropolitan Police confirmed it is investigating "serious" allegations against comedian David Walliams, after a Guardian and ITV News investigation. Haigh said there was a "very strong case" for banning NDAs in harassment and discrimination cases. Walliams's accusers are not part of the Brand case.

## Where does Brand's sentence stand?

Brand was sentenced on Monday 9 November to 10 years and six months, Us Weekly reports. The Guardian says he was convicted of attacks against four of the six women who gave evidence. Yahoo reports he was cleared of two further rape charges and one count of attempted indecent assault. He is held at HMP Wandsworth in south London.

Brand has always maintained his innocence, according to [Us Weekly](https://www.usmagazine.com/celebrity-news/news/russell-brands-ex-gf-claims-star-married-katy-perry-for-fame/). The Met told that outlet that files on two other complainants have gone to the Crown Prosecution Service.

## What does the Netflix documentary add?

The documentary *Russell Brand: Open Secret*, directed by Olivia Isaacs, arrived on Netflix on 13 November. [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/russell-brands-former-girlfriend-speaks-222553765.html) says Brand was invited to respond through his lawyers and did not reply.

His former girlfriend Jordan Martin, who dated him in 2007, says in the film that he always wanted to marry someone famous. She says he asked her to join him in Hawaii during the filming of [Forgetting Sarah Marshall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgetting_Sarah_Marshall). [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/russell-brand-ex-jordan-martin-makes-bombshell-claims-katy-perry-marriage-documentary-russell-brand-open-secret-20261115-p61e7d.html) reports that Brand married [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) in India in 2010. He filed for divorce 14 months later, and it was finalised in July 2012. Us Weekly says it contacted representatives for Brand and Perry for comment.

Yahoo adds that Martin's allegations are separate from the charges Brand was convicted of. Nine.com.au reports that on 12 November The Times and The Sunday Times published allegations from six more women.

## What happens next

No bill has been tabled. The government has promised consideration, not a decision, and Haigh has described any change as a big one. The Met's open investigation and the files before the Crown Prosecution Service will also decide whether more charges follow. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the age of consent in the UK?

It is 16 for everyone. The Guardian notes adults of any age can legally have sex with 16 and 17-year-olds, though the ABC says there are added protections for under-18s.

### Is the UK changing the age of consent?

Not yet. The government says it will consider the idea and that it needs "proper consideration and scrutiny". No legislation has been announced.

### Why was Mia Latter not part of Russell Brand's trial?

She told The Sunday Times that police removed her after Brand's April interview. The Guardian reports the Crown Prosecution Service decided not to include her case.

### What was Russell Brand convicted of?

One count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009, per Yahoo. He denies non-consensual sex.
