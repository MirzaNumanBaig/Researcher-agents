**Quick summary**

- [Martin McDonagh's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh) spy comedy-drama [Wild Horse Nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) has lost 1,000 US theatres going into its fourth weekend, according to Collider, citing The Numbers.
- That cuts its domestic footprint by exactly half, to about 1,000 screens.
- The film has taken US$18.31 million in the US so far, including US$2.46 million last weekend.
- Thanksgiving weekend is crowded with new releases, which makes screens harder to hold.

The film's run is shrinking at an awkward moment. According to [Collider](https://collider.com/sam-rockwell-wild-horse-nine-removed-from-1000-theaters/), which cited theatre counts from The Numbers, Wild Horse Nine will play in half as many US cinemas this weekend as it did a week earlier. That is the headline for a film many viewers were hoping to catch over the holiday.

## What the theatre numbers show

Wild Horse Nine opened on 6 November in 1,100 theatres. It widened to 2,175 locations in its second weekend, then began to pull back. Losing 1,000 theatres leaves it in roughly 1,000, which is about 100 fewer than on opening day.

Collider puts the loss at more than 54% of its locations since that second weekend peak. For anyone planning a Thanksgiving trip to see it, the practical result is fewer showtimes and, in some towns, none.

## How much the film has earned

The US total stands at US$18.31 million, per Collider, which notes the film is nearing the US$20 million mark. Last weekend brought in US$2.46 million, a drop of 46% from the weekend before.

The per-theatre figures show the slowdown. The film averaged US$1,231 per theatre over the weekend, then only US$192 on Monday. Those numbers are domestic only. The sources do not give a worldwide total or a production budget, so there is no way to say here whether the film will cover its costs.

## The story and the cast

McDonagh wrote and directed the film, which is set in Chile in 1973. Two CIA agents are sent to Easter Island shortly before the military coup. John Malkovich plays Chris Carlisle, a man increasingly preoccupied with his own mortality, and [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) plays Lee Chesterfield, whose shared history with Chris has to be faced. Things grow more tangled when Chris befriends two students on the island.

The supporting cast, as listed by Collider, includes Steve Buscemi, Mariana di Girolamo, Ailín Salas, Tom Waits and Parker Posey.

## A familiar partnership

Rockwell won an Academy Award for his role in McDonagh's Three Billboards Outside Ebbing, Missouri, and had earlier appeared in [Seven Psychopaths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_Psychopaths). Four years ago McDonagh reunited [Colin Farrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Farrell) and [Brendan Gleeson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Gleeson) for The Banshees of Inisherin, which drew nine Oscar nominations, Collider notes. Wild Horse Nine again turns his dark comedy on a pair of men and the history between them.
