**Quick summary**

- Comedian [Dane Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dane_Cook), 54, and his wife Kelsi Taylor, 28, announced on Tuesday (US time) that they are expecting their first child.
- The news came in a joint Instagram post of three black-and-white maternity photos, including a shot of the couple holding ultrasound images.
- The caption read "We've been bursting with excitement to share this news!" and cited the Bible verse 1 Samuel 1:27.
- The couple married in Hawaii in September 2023. No due date has been shared.

Dane Cook and his wife, Kelsi Taylor, are going to be parents. The couple posted the news on Instagram on 24 November, and outlets including [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/dane-cook-wife-kelsi-taylor-pregnant-first-baby-20261125-p61h07.html) and [WBAL](https://www.wbal.com/dane-cook-54-is-expecting-first-baby-with-wife-kelsi-taylor) reported it within hours. It is the first child for both.

## How Dane Cook and Kelsi Taylor made the announcement

The post was a three-photo carousel from a maternity shoot, according to [The Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/dane-cook-wife-pregnant-baby-37911693). In one frame the couple smile at each other while holding up ultrasound pictures. In another, both rest their hands on her bump in a close-up.

The caption said: "We've been bursting with excitement to share this news!" It also pointed to 1 Samuel 1:27, which reads: "I prayed for this child, and the Lord has granted me what I asked of him."

Taylor reshared the post to her Instagram Story with a note that she is "so grateful for this beautiful chapter of our lives". Cook did the same and added only a red heart emoji, as reported by [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/dane-cook-becoming-dad-first-200948697.html).

Fans were quick to respond. The Mirror quoted several well-wishers in the comments, one of whom said they "had a feeling".

## A relationship that began in 2017

Cook and Taylor started dating in 2017 after meeting at a game night he hosted at his home, according to Yahoo. The pair were friends first. In a 2018 Instagram Q&A quoted by The Mirror, Cook said they "upgraded to love" soon after.

Their 26-year age difference has drawn online criticism over the years, and Cook has typically answered with humour. He told People after the engagement that the couple expected to be a target, adding: "if we make the joke first and we're laughing at it, you can't really hurt us with that."

They got engaged in July 2022, with Nine.com.au reporting that Cook proposed at a lighthouse in Maine. They married on 23 September 2023 at a private estate on Oahu, Hawaii, in front of about 20 guests, per The Mirror. [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/celebrity/dane-cook-announces-going-dad-54-1454183.html) reported that the celebrations ran for a full week, with the ceremony in the middle.

The announcement arrived roughly two months after their third wedding anniversary.

## What each of them has said about starting a family

Both have spoken about wanting children before now. In November 2022, Cook told People: "I can't wait, to be very honest with you." He said he loved seeing Taylor with her sister's new baby and added: "When the time is right, we'll check it out."

Taylor told the same magazine around the engagement that family "has always been such an important part of my life", according to The Mirror.

## Who Kelsi Taylor is

Taylor is a singer and fitness instructor. Yahoo reports she is a certified Pilates and TRX instructor and nutritionist, and has sung background vocals for artists including Demi Lovato and Little Big Town. She released an EP, Fear of Yourself, in 2018.

Cook is best known for stand-up comedy and for films including [Good Luck Chuck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Luck_Chuck), according to WBAL. Taylor also appeared in his recent documentary, Funny Money: The Dane Cook Story, which The Mirror says reached Netflix this month.

## What happens next

Neither Cook nor Taylor has said when the baby is due, and neither has shared the child's sex. For now, the Instagram post and the two Story reshares are the only public statements. Any further news will most likely come from the couple's own accounts.
