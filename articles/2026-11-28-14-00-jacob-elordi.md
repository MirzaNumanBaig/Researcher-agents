Jacob Elordi's take on a literary classic is now an easy night in. Mashable's [streaming guide](https://mashable.com/article/how-to-watch-wuthering-heights) says Emerald Fennell's reimagining of Emily Brontë's [*Wuthering Heights*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wuthering_Heights) can be bought, rented or streamed, nearly eight months after the debate over its casting began.

## Where to watch Wuthering Heights at home

Mashable reports that the film arrived on digital-on-demand services on 31 March 2026. In the United States it costs US$19.99 to buy or US$9.99 to rent on Apple TV, Fandango at Home, Prime Video and YouTube. A rental stays available for 30 days, but you have only 48 hours to finish once you press play.

The subscription option came a month later. The film began streaming on HBO Max on 1 May 2026. Mashable notes that Warner Bros. releases go to HBO Max before any other streaming service, a pattern it says the studio also followed with *One Battle After Another*, *Weapons* and *The Conjuring: Last Rites*.

Mashable's prices are in US dollars and written for American viewers. Where the film sits in Australia was not covered in the guide, so check your local services before subscribing.

## What the film is about

Fennell, the Oscar-winning writer-director of *Promising Young Woman* and *Saltburn*, kept the bones of Brontë's 1847 novel. According to Mashable, Robbie plays Catherine Earnshaw, a motherless rich girl raised by an alcoholic father. Elordi plays Heathcliff, the poor boy brought up beside her.

The pair run wild on the Yorkshire moors and grow into an all-consuming romance. Catherine then chooses luxury and agrees to marry the wealthy Edgar Linton, played by Shazad Latif. A betrayed Heathcliff leaves, only to return five years later as a rich man.

Fennell put quotation marks around the title on purpose, Mashable says, to signal a modern adaptation that departs sharply from the book.

## Why it divided audiences

The casting drew complaints before the film opened. Mashable says some fans thought Robbie, who starred in *Barbie*, was too old for Catherine, and that Elordi did not fit the way Heathcliff is described in the novel. Audiences still went to cinemas anyway, the outlet adds.

The scores are split in the same way. Mashable cites 57 per cent from critics and 76 per cent from audiences on Rotten Tomatoes. Its own film critic, Kristy Puchko, called the film "a passionate but incoherent collage of teenage lust and rebellion". Mashable's advice is to adjust your expectations before pressing play.

## Elordi's other big-screen year

*Wuthering Heights* is not Elordi's only major film of 2026. Ridley Scott's post-apocalyptic drama *The Dog Stars*, which also stars Margaret Qualley, Josh Brolin, Guy Pearce and Benedict Wong, was released on 26 August 2026.

Figures compiled on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dog_Stars_(film)) put its budget at US$70 million and its box office at about US$39 million. That is roughly 56 per cent of what it cost, which makes it a weak result for a big-studio release. The same page lists a 41 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic average of 49 out of 100 from 46 critics.
