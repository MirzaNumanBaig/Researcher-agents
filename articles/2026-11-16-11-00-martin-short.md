**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) says he was offered the role of Harry in *Dumb and Dumber* and could not get through the script, according to Far Out Magazine (15 November 2026).
- He described the film as "grosser than anything I had ever done" and said the studio offered "lots of money".
- Jim Carrey and Jeff Daniels were cast as Lloyd and Harry in late 1993.
- Short and [Selena Gomez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selena_Gomez) are listed for *Jimmy Kimmel Live* on Thursday 19 November, according to What's On Disney Plus.

Martin Short has revealed why one of the biggest comedies of the 1990s never had him in it. Far Out Magazine reported on 15 November that Short was asked to play Harry Dunne in *Dumb and Dumber*, read the script and decided it was not for him.

## Why Martin Short said no to Dumb and Dumber

According to [Far Out Magazine](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/steve-martin-martin-short-refused-iconic-comedy-grosser-than-anything/), Short and his long-time collaborator [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) were both offered lead roles in the film, which the Farrelly brothers directed. Short confirmed he had been approached for Harry.

His explanation was blunt. He said the studio "were really offering lots of money, but I couldn't get through the script", adding that the material was "grosser than anything I had ever done" and "wasn't my cup of tea". Far Out says the money did not change his mind.

The magazine's writer guesses that Short may have stopped at a scene involving explosive diarrhoea. That is the writer's speculation, not something Short has said.

## A partnership built on a different kind of comedy

Far Out notes that Short and Martin met on *Three Amigos!* in 1986 and have worked together ever since, in films, on tour and, more recently, in *Only Murders in the Building*. In 1998 they both voiced characters in *The Prince of Egypt*, Hotep and Huy, and chose to record their lines side by side.

The article argues that gross-out humour was never part of their shared style, which helps explain the refusal. It also credits the Farrellys with finding a strong pairing elsewhere.

## Who played Harry instead

Carrey and Daniels were cast as Lloyd Christmas and Harry Dunne in late 1993, Far Out reports. Daniels's agents reportedly tried to talk him out of taking the part. The magazine adds that Nicolas Cage, Woody Harrelson and Gary Oldman also turned the film down, and that Cage was reportedly sounded out for the part Carrey eventually played. Those claims come from a single outlet, so treat them as reported rather than confirmed.

## Short heads to Jimmy Kimmel Live

The revelation lands in a busy week for Short. What's On Disney Plus [lists him and Gomez](https://whatsondisneyplus.com/martin-short-selena-gomez-chris-pratt-to-appear-on-jimmy-kimmel-live/) for the Thursday 19 November episode of *Jimmy Kimmel Live*. The site connects them to *Only Murders in the Building*, though it does not state outright that they are promoting it.

The show airs weeknights at 11:35 p.m. ET on ABC and streams the next day on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+. Chris Pratt is announced for Wednesday 18 November, alongside Gordon Ramsay. Other names that week include Chris Rock, John Mulaney and Billie Jean King.
