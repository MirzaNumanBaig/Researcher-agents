**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) turned 50 on 23 October, according to WalesOnline and OneFootball.
- His net worth is estimated at about $400m (£295m), and Forbes ranked him the second-highest-paid star of 2024 on around £79m.
- Wrexham, which he co-owns with [Rob McElhenney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney), visits Sheffield United on Friday night while sitting in the bottom half of the Championship.
- His Apple TV spy comedy Mayday, co-starring Kenneth Branagh, has drawn strong reviews, The Mac Observer reports.

Ryan Reynolds has reached 50 at a moment when his football club, his film career and his business record are all in the headlines at once. The Canadian actor marked the birthday on 23 October, and UK outlets used the occasion to take stock of a career that now stretches well beyond Hollywood.

## A milestone birthday and a resurfaced school photo

WalesOnline reports that a black and white photo from Reynolds' junior year at Kitsilano Secondary School in Vancouver, taken in 1993 when he was about 16, has been circulating online. Both WalesOnline and OneFootball framed it as a "glow-up" story, contrasting the teenager with the bearded 50-year-old.

The background is well documented in those reports. Reynolds began acting at 13, graduated in 1994, enrolled at Kwantlen Polytechnic University and then moved to Los Angeles. WalesOnline adds that he still keeps in touch with his old school and gave a graduation speech for its class of 2020.

His credits span rom-coms such as The Proposal and [Just Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Just_Friends), the Deadpool films, DC's Green Lantern and voice roles in The Croods, Turbo and Detective Pikachu. The outlets list two Primetime Emmys, a Critics' Choice Award and a People's Icon Award among his honours, plus a place on Time's 100 list in 2017.

## The money behind the name

Net worth figures are estimates, and both outlets treat them that way. They put Reynolds at about $400m (£295m). According to WalesOnline, Forbes ranked him second in its 2024 list of highest-paid stars at around £79m, behind only Dwayne Johnson.

Much of that year's income came from Deadpool and Wolverine, the family film IF and the Emmy-winning documentary Welcome to Wrexham, WalesOnline says.

The larger sums came from business. WalesOnline reports that Reynolds held a 20% stake in Aviation Gin when it sold in a $600m (£443m) deal in 2020. In 2023 he sold a major share of Mint Mobile to T-Mobile in a $1.35bn (£997m) agreement, reportedly earning more than $300m (£220m).

## Wrexham: from £2m purchase to a Championship club

Reynolds and McElhenney bought Wrexham in 2021 for £2m. Since then the club has won three successive promotions, taking it from the National League into the Championship, according to both WalesOnline and OneFootball.

Roundtable.io adds the detail. The owners attended their first match in October 2021, a 3-2 away defeat at Maidenhead United, and their first game at the Racecourse ended in a draw with Torquay United. Wrexham won the National League title in 2022/23, went up from League Two the following season and reached the Championship in 2024/25. Their first season back in the second tier ended in seventh place, the club's highest league finish, two points short of the play-offs after a 2-2 draw with Middlesbrough.

The reports say the owners have put more than £20m into the club, and that it is now valued at around £350m. That figure is helped by close to £50m from Apollo Sports Capital after the Allyn family bought about 15% in 2024. WalesOnline also recalls a 2023 episode of Welcome to Wrexham in which the pair revealed losses of around $12m (£8.9m).

This season is harder. Phil Parkinson's side are in the bottom half of the table. Our earlier report covers the club being [fined £75,000 by the EFL](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-reynolds-wrexham-fined-75000-by-the-efl/).

## Mayday lands on Apple TV

On screen, The Mac Observer reports that Reynolds stars in Mayday, a Cold War buddy comedy directed by John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein. He plays a Navy pilot shot down over Soviet territory who wakes in the cabin of an ex-KGB agent played by Kenneth Branagh, who turns out to love American pop culture.

The site says the critical response is "overwhelmingly positive". It cites Empire for slick fights and witty quips, The Hollywood Reporter for the mismatched chemistry of the leads, and Collider for calling it a love letter to Cold War action hits. IGN is described as appreciating the action set pieces. Branagh draws particular praise for unexpected martial arts skills.
