**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios announced on Monday that Robert Pattinson will play James Bond, the seventh actor in the role.
- Denis Villeneuve directs and Steven Knight is writing the script, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing.
- No title, release date or further cast has been announced.
- Reaction is mixed: some writers call him a safe, strong pick, others doubt he is the Bond for this moment.

The [Villeneuve Bond film](https://moduscarry.com/tag/denis-villeneuve/) has its 007. Amazon MGM Studios confirmed on Monday that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) will play [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) in the reboot, which [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) will direct, according to [Forbes Australia](https://www.forbes.com.au/life/entertainment/robert-pattinson-new-james-bond/).

## What Amazon MGM has confirmed

Pattinson becomes the seventh actor to hold the role, following Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig). [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight) is writing the script, and Amy Pascal and David Heyman are producing, Forbes Australia reports.

The studio has not released a title, a release date or any other casting. That leaves the announcement as a statement of intent rather than a schedule.

Villeneuve said there "can only be one 007" and that Pattinson has "the timeless soul" of the character along with a spirit that is "completely new", as quoted by [The Autopian](https://www.theautopian.com/a-good-james-bond-has-to-be-a-little-funny-can-robert-pattinson-be-a-little-funny/). Pattinson called the part "the honour of a lifetime". For more on the director's thinking, see our earlier piece, [Denis Villeneuve Explains Why Pattinson Is His Bond](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-robert-pattinson-007/).

## How the choice was made

Forbes Australia says the reported shortlist included Callum Turner, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden), Josh O'Connor and Nicholas Hoult. Hoult was reportedly the last name still in contention alongside Pattinson, and both are said to have auditioned for Villeneuve.

Puck News reported last week that Pattinson had become the favourite. Per that report, his deal covers three films, while other candidates were asked to commit to four, and his was the most expensive offer. These are reported details, not confirmed by the studio.

Deadline's Baz Bamigboye wrote that Villeneuve asked to see what Pattinson could do with the *From Russia with Love* screen test that every Bond contender performs. That is the columnist's account, and the studio has not commented on it.

## The critics are not agreed

Bamigboye was cautious. He expects Pattinson to be good, but argues that casting a 40-year-old movie star is hardly fresh, and that his turn as Batman may colour how audiences see him as 007. Writers Robert Wade and Neil Purvis, who worked on several Bond films, told Deadline he was "a terrific choice" and praised the sly wit he showed in *Tenet*.

The Autopian's columnist called Pattinson a safe choice, given his fame and range, but wondered whether a darker Bond suits the present mood. Den of Geek took a lighter tack, using the news to suggest Villeneuve's past collaborators for supporting roles, from Charlotte Rampling to Daniel Kaluuya. Those are the outlet's wish list, not casting news.

## The numbers behind the casting

Forbes Australia cites Box Office Watch, which puts Pattinson's films at about US$7.6 billion worldwide. By that measure he arrives far better known than most of his predecessors were when cast. This is also the first Bond casting since Amazon took creative control of the franchise in a 2025 deal with Barbara Broccoli and Michael G. Wilson. The last film, *No Time to Die*, took about US$774 million in 2021.

Pattinson is still shooting *The Batman Part II*, and he is also in Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* and *Primetime*. He and Villeneuve have already worked together on *Dune: Part Three*, which Forbes Australia says is due later this year.

## What happens next

Pre-production on the Bond film is under way, according to Deadline. Expect further casting news to follow, since the supporting roles remain open. Until the studio gives a title and a date, the best guide is the work Villeneuve and Pattinson have already done together.
