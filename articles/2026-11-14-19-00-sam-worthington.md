**Quick summary**

- Netflix has released a teaser trailer for *I Will Find You*, an eight-episode limited series, according to [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/i-will-find-you-teaser/).
- [Sam Worthington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Worthington) plays David Burroughs, a father serving a life sentence for the murder of his son.
- The series is based on Harlan Coben's 2023 novel and is the first project under his exclusive deal with Netflix.
- JoBlo reports that it streams on Netflix on June 18.

Netflix has put out the first teaser for *I Will Find You*, and it puts Sam Worthington at the centre of a prison-break thriller. The Australian actor plays a man convicted of killing his own child, who learns behind bars that the boy may still be alive. The footage was reported by JoBlo on 13 November.

## What the teaser sets up

Worthington's character is David Burroughs. JoBlo quotes Netflix's description of him as an innocent father serving a life sentence for his son's murder, "a crime he didn't commit, yet a tragedy he punishes himself for every day". When evidence arrives that the child may be alive, David has to break out of prison to learn the truth.

That gives the series two clocks running at once: one to clear his name and one to find his son. It is a familiar shape for the genre, and one that suits the author behind it.

## The Harlan Coben connection

*I Will Find You* is adapted from Harlan Coben's novel, published in 2023. It runs for eight episodes and is the first of Coben's projects made under his exclusive deal with the streamer, JoBlo reports.

Netflix is no newcomer to his work. The outlet lists earlier adaptations including *Fool Me Once*, *Stay Close*, *The Stranger*, *The Innocent*, *Gone for Good*, *Hold Tight*, *The Woods* and, most recently, *Missing You*. That is nine titles before this one, which shows how much the platform has leaned on his plots.

Coben is an executive producer through his company, Final Twist Productions. [Robert Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Hull) co-created the show and serves as executive producer and showrunner. Netflix's Tudum site quoted Hull calling the cast "nothing short of phenomenal" and saying that drawing them all in was a testament to the power of Coben's storytelling.

## The cast around Worthington

Worthington is joined by [Britt Lower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britt_Lower), [Milo Ventimiglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milo_Ventimiglia), [Logan Browning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logan_Browning), [Erin Richards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erin_Richards), [Chi McBride](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chi_McBride) and [Jonathan Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tucker).

The guest list is long. It includes [Peter Outerbridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Outerbridge), [Eric Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Johnson_(actor)), [Greg Bryk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Bryk), [Kate Vernon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Vernon), [Aaron Ashmore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Ashmore), [Nicola Correia-Damude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicola_Correia-Damude), [Rachel Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Wilson), [Billy MacLellan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_MacLellan), [Clancy Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clancy_Brown) and [Madeleine Stowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madeleine_Stowe), along with several other names JoBlo lists.

## Behind the camera

According to JoBlo, the writers are Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt, Steven Lilien and [Heather Mitchell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heather_Mitchell). The episodes are directed by [Brad Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Anderson_(director)), Maja Vrvilo, Adam Davidson and Maggie Kelly. Wynbrandt, Lilien and John Weber are executive producers, and Desmond Paes and Zoe Cheng are producers.

## What happens next

The teaser is the first look, and JoBlo says the series streams on Netflix on June 18. The report does not give a full trailer date or say which episodes arrive at launch, so those details remain to be confirmed.
