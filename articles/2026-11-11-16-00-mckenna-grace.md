**Quick summary**

- Mckenna Grace, 20, plays Maysilee Donner in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which opens in theatres on 20 November.
- At a Los Angeles fan screening this week she wore a custom Elisabetta Franchi gown based on Katniss Everdeen's Capitol party dress from Catching Fire, according to WWD and Hindustan Times.
- She told Bustle her lavender Vera Wang gown at the Sydney premiere was a nod to Maysilee.
- Her stylist on the tour is Molly Dickson.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) has spent the Sunrise on the Reaping press tour dressing as the franchise she grew up loving, and her latest Mckenna Grace Hunger Games look, a black gown at a Los Angeles fan screening, is her most literal yet. With 10 days to go before release, the references are getting harder to miss.

Grace plays Maysilee Donner, a District 12 tribute in the 50th Hunger Games. Her red carpet choices have become a talking point in their own right.

## What did Mckenna Grace wear in Los Angeles?

Grace wore a custom black and red Elisabetta Franchi gown to the fan event at AMC Century City, according to [WWD](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-aldo-heels-elisabetta-franchi-1239409032/). It nods to the dress [Katniss Everdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katniss_Everdeen) wore to the Capitol party in [Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire). The gown has sequins, lace, a flame-like neckline and red feathered shoulders.

[Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/lifestyle/fashion/mckenna-grace-channels-another-katniss-everdeen-look-from-catching-fire-for-the-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-press-tour-101794366211326.html) calls it one of her most faithful recreations. It adds a few touches of her own: lace embroidery on the skirt, a thigh-high slit and black beaded tassels. Her hair was in a slicked-back bun.

The outlets disagree on the date. WWD and Yahoo place the event on Monday 9 November, while Hindustan Times says 10 November. On Instagram, Grace thanked fans for dressing up and called it "our first US fan screening".

The shoes were a surprise. WWD reports she paired the gown with Aldo's Thalie platform sandals, which cost $140 and have a 5.5-inch stiletto heel.

## Which other Katniss looks has she copied?

Los Angeles is only one stop. Happy Mag counts the tour as a deliberate game of spot-the-reference, put together with stylist Molly Dickson.

In Berlin she wore a custom Cong Tri gown in reds, oranges and yellows that echoed Katniss's flaming chariot outfit. In Paris she recreated the wedding dress Tex Saverio designed for Catching Fire. In London, archival Alexander McQueen butterflies pointed to Effie Trinket, and a cream-and-grey outfit referenced the promotional looks of Jena Malone, who played Johanna Mason.

Not every nod stays inside Panem. At the Academy Museum Gala she wore a red Mônot gown inspired by the Calvin Klein dress Jennifer Lawrence wore to the 2011 Oscars, as Happy Mag tells it. WWD adds a blue dress referencing Rue, the character Amandla Stenberg played in the first film.

That is a lot of references. But the one fans missed was the one closest to the film itself.

## What was the Sydney Easter egg?

For the Sydney premiere in October, Grace wore a lavender Vera Wang HAUTE mermaid gown in macramé lace, with oversized silk organza flowers on one strap, Bustle reports. Fans online compared her to Rapunzel or guessed an Effie reference.

Grace told Bustle the real inspiration was Maysilee. "I wore this purple dress in Australia that was a nod to Maysilee," she said. In the trailers, Maysilee is reaped in a lavender minidress, wears floral necklaces and pins a chamomile flower to her lapel.

The flowers in Grace's hair were real. "In the morning, I had all these wilted flowers on my floor," she told the outlet. She also conceded the Effie theory was not entirely wrong, saying the look "did remind me of Effie a bit".

## Why is she dressing this way?

According to Happy Mag, this is not a stunt. Grace has been a self-confessed Hunger Games superfan for years, and the outlet reads the looks as a tribute to the characters, the actors and the earlier films. Bustle places her alongside Zendaya and Jenna Ortega as an actor known for method dressing.

The film is based on Suzanne Collins's 2025 book. Hindustan Times says it follows 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, played by Joseph Zada, on the morning of the reaping for the Second Quarter Quell.
