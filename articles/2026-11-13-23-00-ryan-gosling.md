**Quick summary**

- Director Shawn Levy marked [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s birthday by sharing a set photo of him as Kade Auberon in *Star Wars: Starfighter*, according to Zoom TV.
- The photo was taken at Pinewood Studios in London during production last year.
- Gosling's birthday was on 12 November, and Anne Hathaway, who shares the date, posted an "I'm Just Ken" tribute, Geo News reports.
- *Blade Runner 2049*, Gosling's 2017 sci-fi film, is streaming free on Pluto TV, and the *Blade Runner 2099* series reaches Prime Video on 25 November, per BGR.

Ryan Gosling's birthday week has turned into a small showcase of his sci-fi career. Filmmaker Shawn Levy shared a behind-the-scenes picture of the actor on the set of *Star Wars: Starfighter*, and the film that first put Gosling in a replicant's world is now free to watch. Here is what was reported, and what it tells us about where his career sits.

## Shawn Levy's birthday post from the Starfighter set

According to [Zoom TV](https://www.zoomtventertainment.com/hollywood/shawn-levy-celebrates-ryan-gosling-birthday-star-wars-starfighter-arrival-anniversary-article-156503004), Levy posted a candid photograph of Gosling in character as Kade Auberon, calling him his "buddy". The image was taken at Pinewood Studios in London last year, during production.

*Starfighter* is a new entry in the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) universe. Levy directs from a script by Jonathan Tropper, and the story is set five years after *The Rise of Skywalker*. That places it later in the timeline than the sequel trilogy, which gives the film room to introduce new characters rather than lean on old ones.

The reported cast around Gosling is large: Flynn Gray, Matt Smith, Mia Goth, Aaron Pierre, Simon Bird, Jamael Westman, Daniel Ings and Amy Adams. Zoom TV did not report a release date, and none appears in the sources reviewed for this article.

## Levy also looked back at Arrival

Levy used the same moment to mark ten years since *Arrival*. The 2016 film was directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), written by Eric Heisserer and based on Ted Chiang's 1998 novella *Story of Your Life*. It starred Amy Adams as linguist Louise Banks, with Jeremy Renner, Forest Whitaker and Michael Stuhlbarg.

Levy's production company, 21 Laps, was involved, and he described the film as a turning point for the company. It was also the team's first look at Villeneuve's approach to science fiction, Zoom TV reports. That link matters here: Villeneuve later directed Gosling in *Blade Runner 2049*.

## Anne Hathaway's "I'm Just Ken" tribute

Gosling's birthday was on 12 November. [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/690885-anne-hathaway-wishes-birthday-twin-ryan-gosling-with-im-just-ken-tribute) reports that Anne Hathaway, who shares the date, posted a clip on Instagram of his performance at the 96th Academy Awards in 2024. In it he rises from his seat behind [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) and takes the stage in a hot-pink sequined suit to sing the *Barbie* anthem.

Her caption was brief: "Once again, happy birthday to Ryan Gosling". Geo News says the two have often posted for each other on the day. For a longer look at his career, see our piece on [Ryan Gosling turning 46](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-turns-46/).

## Blade Runner 2049 is streaming free

The other Gosling headline comes from BGR, which reports that *Blade Runner 2049* is streaming for free on Pluto TV. The timing is tied to Amazon's *Blade Runner 2099*, due on Prime Video on 25 November. [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2315458/ryan-gosling-harrison-ford-sci-fi-epic-blade-runner-2049-streaming-free/) says that series is set 50 years after *2049* and stars [Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh) as Olwen, a replicant nearing the end of her life.

In *2049*, Gosling takes the lead from [Harrison Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrison_Ford), who returns as [Rick Deckard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_Deckard) from [Ridley Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridley_Scott)'s original [Blade Runner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner). The film's box-office record is mixed. BGR puts its worldwide take at about $259 million, against an estimated break-even of around $400 million.

Critics were kinder. The film holds an 88% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), and it won two Oscars at the 2018 ceremony, for visual effects and for Roger Deakins' cinematography. It was Deakins' first win after 14 nominations.

## What happens next

Fans have two dates to watch. *Blade Runner 2099* arrives on 25 November, and *Star Wars: Starfighter* will keep releasing set material as it moves toward release. No date was confirmed in the reports above.
