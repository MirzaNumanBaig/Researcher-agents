**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) released the music video for his Christmas single "Another Year" on Friday 27 November 2026.
- The video was filmed in a London pub and features a long list of television and showbusiness names.
- All UK streaming revenue and CD proceeds go to the charity Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders), according to All Music Magazine UK.
- The release follows his album *Airplane Mode*, which entered the UK charts at number one.

James Blunt has put a pub full of famous faces on screen for "Another Year", his new Christmas single, with the video arriving on Friday 27 November. The song is a wistful piano ballad, and the money it raises is going to charity.

## What is in the "Another Year" video

According to [All Music Magazine UK](https://www.allmusicmagazine.co.uk/post/james-blunt-releases-christmas-single-another-year-with-star-studded-music-video), the clip was shot in a cosy London pub. It features friends Blunt made on [The Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors), including [Jerry Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerry_Hall), [Richard E. Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_E._Grant) and King Kenny.

The rest of the line-up reads like a television guide: [Spencer Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spencer_Matthews), [Denise Welch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denise_Welch), [Fearne Cotton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fearne_Cotton), [Cat Deeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat_Deeley), [Amanda Holden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Holden), [Prue Leith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prue_Leith), [Davina McCall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davina_McCall), [Kate Garraway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kate_Garraway), [Clare Balding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_Balding) and [Bimini Bon Boulash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bimini_Bon-Boulash). That is a dozen-plus guests on one set, which is a lot of diaries to line up in the busiest month of the year.

The Independent describes the story the video tells. The stars start out having drinks in the pub, then drift towards a piano and sing along while Blunt plays the song.

## Singing was not part of the plan for everyone

Garraway, a presenter on [Good Morning Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Good_Morning_Britain), told Smooth Radio that she had not realised the guests would actually have to sing on the track, [The Independent reported](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/james-blunt-celebrities-another-year-celebrities-b3092771.html). She joked that auto-tune and real drinks on set helped everyone relax into their vocals.

It is a light-hearted detail, and it fits the tone of a video built around a pub singalong rather than a polished studio performance.

## Where the money goes

The single was co-written and co-produced by Blunt. It is out digitally and as a limited edition CD, and it is released on Atlantic Records.

All Music Magazine UK says all UK streaming revenue and proceeds from CD sales will be donated to the global charity Médecins Sans Frontières, also known as Doctors Without Borders. The Independent reports that all profits from the release are going to the organisation. Neither report gives a fundraising target or a total so far.

## The album behind the single

The song arrives soon after *Airplane Mode*, Blunt's latest album, which All Music Magazine UK says went straight in at number one on the UK charts. The magazine calls the record acclaimed, though the source gives no chart figures beyond the number-one placing.

## What happens next

The video is now out, and the single is available to buy or stream. The sources do not name a Christmas chart campaign or any live performance dates for the song, so those details remain unconfirmed. Readers in Australia should check their usual streaming service, as the sources only describe the UK arrangement for streaming revenue going to charity.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was James Blunt's "Another Year" video released?

The video was released on Friday 27 November 2026, alongside the single, according to The Independent and All Music Magazine UK.

### Who appears in the "Another Year" video?

Jerry Hall, Richard E. Grant, King Kenny, Spencer Matthews, Denise Welch, Fearne Cotton, Cat Deeley, Amanda Holden, Prue Leith, Davina McCall, Kate Garraway, Clare Balding and Bimini Bon Boulash are named by the outlets.

### Where does the money from "Another Year" go?

According to All Music Magazine UK, UK streaming revenue and CD proceeds go to Médecins Sans Frontières (Doctors Without Borders). The Independent says all profits from the release are donated to the same organisation.
