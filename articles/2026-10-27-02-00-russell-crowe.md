**Quick summary**

- Scottish wrestler [Drew McIntyre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_McIntyre) calls [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) "a mentor for sure", according to The Economic Times.
- The two met on the Highlander reboot, and Crowe later invited McIntyre to join The Last Druid.
- McIntyre says he spent about 12 weeks with Crowe and will return to WWE eventually, with no date given.
- His comments came in an interview with Fox News, as reported on 26 October 2026.

Drew McIntyre has been off WWE television since WrestleMania 42, and he says the reason is a film career that Russell Crowe has helped shape. Speaking to Fox News, the former WWE champion credited the Oscar-winning Australian actor as a mentor, as [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/why-is-drew-mcintyre-still-away-from-wwe-how-hollywood-and-russell-crowe-are-shaping-his-next-chapter/articleshow/135213521.cms?from=mdr) reported on Monday.

## How Crowe and McIntyre met

The pair first crossed paths on Highlander, the upcoming reboot starring [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill). McIntyre plays Angus in the film, which also features Crowe and [Dave Bautista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Bautista) and is expected in 2027, according to the report.

McIntyre told Fox News he watched a scene between Cavill and Crowe and had so many questions that he stayed about four hours past his own call to watch "two of the best in action". He then spent time talking with Crowe on set.

## The phone call he hung up on

The story gets better from there. McIntyre says that after he finished Highlander, his agents rang to say Crowe wanted him in a movie. His first reaction, by his own account, was "I'm sure he did", and he hung up. They called back to say they were serious.

McIntyre says he wanted confirmation from Crowe himself before raising it with WWE. He says [Nick Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Khan), the company's president, and Triple H, head of creative, saw it as a big opportunity and approved the trip. The film is The Last Druid, shot in Barcelona. No release date has been confirmed.

"He's a mentor for sure," McIntyre said of Crowe, describing about 12 weeks with him. He said the lessons apply to acting, to the WWE product and to life more broadly, adding that Crowe "has really seen a lot and done a lot".

## McIntyre's busy screen schedule

The Economic Times lists four projects keeping him away from the ring:

- **Highlander:** the Cavill-led reboot, expected in 2027.
- **The Last Druid:** the Crowe-linked film shot in Barcelona, with no release date yet.
- **Agent of Shadows:** an action-drama made through a WWE and ReelShort collaboration, which premiered on 23 October 2026.
- **Hexed:** a Disney animated film in which he voices a character, due in cinemas on 25 November.

## Is he coming back to WWE?

McIntyre says yes, but not yet. He told Fox News he is "always ready to go" and is 285 pounds, about 10 pounds heavier than during Agent of Shadows. He says a video of him running the ropes drew more than 8 million views across his social platforms.

"Right now, I'm focused on Hollywood Drew," he said. He also said WWE "is my home" and that his eventual return "is going to be a big deal". The report notes he left WrestleMania 42 after an unsanctioned match against Jacob Fatu.

Agent of Shadows is already out, Hexed opens on 25 November, and Highlander is expected in 2027. No date has been given for The Last Druid or for McIntyre's return to WWE. More on Crowe's films is on our [Movies](https://moduscarry.com/category/movies/) page.
