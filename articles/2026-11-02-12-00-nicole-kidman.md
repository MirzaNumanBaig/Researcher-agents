**Quick summary**

- A song called "Nicole Kidman" by the singer Adéla, released in September, is circulating widely on TikTok, according to marketing outlet B&T.
- A Chanel video using the track has Kristen Wiig, Pedro Pascal and Dua Lipa lip-syncing before the camera reveals Kidman herself.
- Kidman's 18-year-old daughter Sunday Rose dressed as Adéla for Halloween, HELLO! reports.
- Sunday captioned her Instagram post "babygirl in control" and set it to the song.

[Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) has spent most of her career being written about by other people. This month she is the subject of a pop song, and the whole family appears to be in on the joke.

Adéla's track "Nicole Kidman" came out in September and is still travelling around TikTok at the start of November. Over the Halloween weekend, the actress's teenage daughter turned it into a costume.

## A song named after the actress

B&T's monthly TikTok round-up, written by Fabulate product and marketing director Lucy Ronald, singles out the track as one of the month's talking points. In its [TikTok trends column](https://www.bandt.com.au/tiktok-trends-dancing-boyfriends-outraged-mums-a-very-good-month-to-be-nicole-kidman/), the outlet says "Nicole Kidman" has been circulating across the feed and calls it a viral song named after the star.

The column puts it in busy company. Other trends it covers this month include partner "soft launch" videos set to Taylor Swift's "New Romantics", Victoria's Secret Fashion Show recreations, and a Halloween costume reveal format. B&T's headline, though, calls it "a very good month to be Nicole Kidman".

## The Chanel clip

The standout video, according to B&T, came from Chanel. [Kristen Wiig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Wiig), [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal) and [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa) take turns lip-syncing to the song. The camera then pans to reveal Kidman for the payoff.

The column describes it as a "fairly unbeatable casting decision" and notes that joining the trend herself is a strong follow-up to having a song named after her. B&T does not say who made the video or how it was produced, and we have not seen further confirmation from Chanel beyond its description.

That is worth keeping in mind. A single trade column is the source for the clip, so details such as where it first appeared or how long it runs are not established.

## Sunday Rose dresses as the singer

The family angle comes from HELLO!, which [reported](https://www.hellomagazine.com/us/932521/nicole-kidman-daughter-sunday-shocks-bright-pink-hair-transformation-new-photos/) that Kidman's daughter Sunday Rose, 18, posted a series of photos over the Halloween weekend. They show her with long pink hair and a black choker.

The look was her Halloween costume. According to HELLO!, she was paying tribute to Adéla, who is known for her pink hair and who released "Nicole Kidman" in September. Sunday also shared a preview of the Instagram post on her Stories with the song playing, and wrote the caption "babygirl in control".

Fans responded warmly. HELLO! quotes comments including "Umm yes!", "Was waiting for this post to drop" and "Heck yeah."

For regular readers, Sunday is not new to this column. She recently appeared on a Vogue Germany cover, which we covered in [Nicole Kidman's Daughter Sunday Rose Fronts Vogue Germany](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidmans-daughter-sunday-rose-fronts-vogue-germany/).

## What Sunday has said about her career

HELLO! also draws on that Vogue Germany interview. Sunday said she is aware she has been given opportunities not everyone can have, and that she tries to give her best at work, stay positive however tired or jet-lagged she is, and treat everyone with respect.

She also said filmmaking, especially directing, has always fascinated her. As a child she was the one watching the preview monitor on set. Looking ahead, she said she has ideas about being a filmmaker in Paris, though she added "you never know". Her biggest worry, she said, is what happens to her friendships once school ends.

HELLO! notes that she shares her younger sister Faith, 15, with her father, Keith Urban. Kidman also has two older children, Isabella and Connor, from her first marriage.

## What happens next

Neither source says whether Kidman has commented publicly on the song, and neither mentions a response from Adéla about Sunday's costume. Trends like this usually fade within weeks, so the Chanel clip and the Halloween photos may be the peak.
