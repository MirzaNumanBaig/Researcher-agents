**Quick summary**

- Newly unsealed testimony from a 2025 New York deposition shows lawyers asked Russell Brand about his marriage to [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry).
- The questions came in a civil lawsuit brought by a woman who worked as an extra on the 2011 film Arthur. Brand denies her allegations.
- Perry is not accused of wrongdoing in the case, according to Yahoo News UK.
- Brand was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in a separate British criminal case.

The Katy Perry Russell Brand deposition made headlines after Brand was asked under oath whether he was already married to Katy Perry when he filmed Arthur, according to deposition testimony made public this week. Reports published on 20 and 21 November say he could not give a clear answer on the timeline. Perry is not a party to the lawsuit, and none of the coverage suggests she did anything wrong.

## What Brand said about Perry

The deposition was taken in Manhattan in 2025 as part of a civil case, and transcripts were obtained by the Daily Mail. [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/russell-brand-makes-wild-katy-183000671.html) reports that when lawyers asked whether he was married during filming, Brand said it would not be hard to find out because it would be online. He said he did know he was in a relationship with "obviously a very high-profile woman".

He then added that "even when I was married to her, I was sort of drowning in love". [JubileeCast](https://www.jubileecast.com/articles/41230/20261120/russell-brand-questioned-about-katy-perry-marriage-in-newly-unsealed-sexual-assault-deposition.htm), citing Page Six, says he recalled being with Perry at the time.

## The lawsuit behind the questions

The plaintiff is identified in court records as Jane Doe and filed in 2023. According to Yahoo News UK, she alleges Brand exposed himself to her in front of cast and crew during filming at a Manhattan restaurant in July 2010, and later sexually assaulted her in a bathroom. She brought the case under New York's Adult Survivors Act and also named Warner Bros. and other production companies.

Brand has said his sexual encounters were consensual. Asked whether he had ever exposed himself without consent, he said he could not rule it out and did not remember doing so, per Yahoo News UK. That outlet notes his general comments are not an admission of what the plaintiff alleges. The court decision it cites concerns a dispute over expert testimony, not whether the alleged assault happened.

## How the dates line up

The alleged incident dates to July 2010. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/katy-perry-poses-with-justin-trudeau-and-emmanuel-macron-in-paris-as-unsealed-court-documents-bring-russell-brand-marriage-back-into-spotlight/articleshow/136042704.cms) reports that Perry and Brand met in 2009, when she made a cameo in Get Him to the Greek. They married in October 2010 in India, about three months after the date in the lawsuit. The divorce was finalised in 2012.

That means the events alleged in the case predate the wedding. The same outlet adds that Brand's legal team kept the marriage out of his recent UK criminal trial, arguing it was irrelevant to the charges.

## The separate UK case

The New York civil case is separate from Brand's British prosecution. A jury convicted him of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women between 2004 and 2009, and a judge sentenced him on 9 November, according to JubileeCast. He maintained his innocence at trial. He was acquitted of other charges, the Times of India says.

## Perry's week in Europe

The attention landed while Perry was travelling with former Canadian Prime Minister [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau). A selfie of the singer with Trudeau and French President [Emmanuel Macron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Macron) in Paris has been circulating online, the Times of India reports, after stops in Barcelona and Milan. We covered the trip in [Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau Spotted in Paris After Week in Europe](https://moduscarry.com/katy-perry-justin-trudeau-paris/). The deposition and the photo are unrelated events that happened to surface together.
