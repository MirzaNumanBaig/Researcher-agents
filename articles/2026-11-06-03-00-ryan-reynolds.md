**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) posted on Instagram on Wednesday that he took an "intensive class" with the watchmakers Aubert & Ramel in Switzerland.
- Robb Report says he appears to own one of the brand's first six platinum Ouréa watches, but the brand has not confirmed it.
- The titanium version of the Ouréa is priced at 72,000 francs, according to the same report.
- Separately, the Daily Star reports that Reynolds and Rob McElhenney are not expected to sack Wrexham manager Phil Parkinson yet.

The latest Ryan Reynolds Aubert Ramel story is a trip to La Chaux-de-Fonds, Switzerland, where the actor spent a day learning watchmaking from two of the industry's youngest independent names. He shared the visit on Instagram on Wednesday, and [Robb Report](https://robbreport.com/style/watch-collector/ryan-reynolds-rare-aubert-ramel-ourea-watch-1238817483/) noticed what looked like a very rare watch on his wrist.

## What Ryan Reynolds said about the class

Reynolds described it as an "intensive class" with "independent watchmaking geniuses", adding that the pair "did not sign up to be teachers" but he signed up to be their student. He called it "one of the best days of my life", according to Robb Report's account of the post.

The magazine also says he seems to have left with a separate keepsake movement engraved with "The Deadpool". That detail comes from the photos, not from a statement by Reynolds or the brand.

## Who are Aubert & Ramel?

The brand is young. Robb Report says it was founded by Thomas Aubert, winner of the 2024 F.P. Journe Young Talent Competition, and Alexis Ramel-Sartori. Both are twentysomething Franco-Swiss watchmakers who trained at the Lycée Edgar Faure in Morteau, France. They opened their own atelier in La Chaux-de-Fonds in 2024.

After a year making and decorating parts for other brands, they released their first watch, the Ouréa. It is a limited run: the first six pieces were sold by subscription in platinum, followed by 14 in titanium at 72,000 francs.

## The Ouréa and the Reynolds watch

According to Robb Report, the platinum Ouréa has a 40.4 mm case and a silver dial with a hand-applied "Silver Frost" finish, which makes each dial different. The hand-wound movement shows hours and minutes only. It beats at 18,000 vibrations per hour and holds a 72-hour power reserve. The magazine puts the design and development work at more than 800 hours, with each watch taking two years to complete.

Robb Report judged from Reynolds's post that he holds one of the six subscription pieces. It asked the watchmaker to confirm and had not received a reply when it published, so treat the ownership as unconfirmed.

If it is true, it continues a pattern. The magazine says Reynolds used to alternate between a gold Omega Speedmaster and an Apple Watch. It has since spotted him in an Audemars Piguet Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar, an A. Lange & Söhne Datograph Up/Down Lumen, an F.P. Journe Chronomètre à Résonance and a vintage Rolex Daytona "Paul Newman".

## Meanwhile at Wrexham

Football fans have a different Reynolds story to follow. The Daily Star reports that Reynolds and [Rob McElhenney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney) are unlikely to dismiss manager [Phil Parkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Parkinson) in a panic, even with pressure around the club. Wrexham beat Middlesbrough on Wednesday night but still sit 16th in the Championship, the paper says.

It also notes that rumours before that game suggested Parkinson could leave after the next match, away to Wolves, who are second in the table. Those rumours are unconfirmed. The piece argues the owners have a record of patience, but says Apollo Capital, now on board after [Humphrey Ker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humphrey_Ker) moved out of the picture, may be less patient. That is the paper's opinion, not an announcement from the club.

## What happens next

Aubert & Ramel has not yet responded publicly on whether Reynolds bought an Ouréa. Wrexham's trip to Wolves is the next test for Parkinson, with the international break after it.
