**Quick summary**
- Blake Lively posted an Instagram video for [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds)' 50th birthday, calling him her "manny", according to E! News.
- The clip shows Reynolds holding one of the couple's puppies in a baby carrier while "Rock-a-bye Baby" plays.
- Peacock adds The Hitman's Bodyguard and its sequel on 1 November, per AOL's weekly roundup.
- Far Out Magazine revisits Buried (2010), which Reynolds has described as a role that changed him.

Blake Lively marked her husband's 50th birthday this week with a short, teasing video, and the internet did the rest. The post, shared on Instagram, showed [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) cradling a fluffy puppy in a black-and-white baby carrier, with a lullaby playing over the top.

## What Lively posted

According to [E! News](https://www.eonline.com/news/1437446/blake-lively-sends-birthday-love-to-her-manny-ryan-reynolds), the caption read "Happy Birthday to my manny, @vancityreynolds." The outlet describes the video as the latest example of the couple's trademark habit of poking fun at each other.

In the footage, Lively rests a hand on Reynolds' shoulder. She is smiling, makeup-free and dressed in a matching blue and green striped set, while Reynolds chats to someone off camera. E! News says the post played on his reputation as a devoted dog dad.

The two sources disagree slightly on timing. E! News dates the post to 23 October, while [Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/forum/topic/blake-lively-shares-cheeky-birthday-tribute-to-her-manny-ryan-reynolds-on-his-50th-birthday/?part=1) gives 24 October, which may reflect time zones. Both agree on the wording and the content.

## A couple who tease in public

Lively and Reynolds have been married 14 years and have four children: James, 11, Inez, 9, Betty, 6, and Olin, 3, per E! News. The outlet points back to Reynolds' own tribute for Lively's 39th birthday in August, when he wrote that "August 25th is this family's Super Bowl" and called her "unreasonably cute".

Their children rarely appear clearly online. In that August carousel, Reynolds either cropped the kids out or covered their faces with an emoji. Lively explained the thinking to Marie Claire UK in 2016: "in order to give her as much normality as possible, we want her to have a childhood like we had."

## Two Reynolds films head to Peacock

Birthday week also brings a practical viewing option in the US. AOL's roundup of the week's [Peacock](https://www.aol.com/articles/salma-hayek-ryan-reynolds-r-075211000.html) releases says The Hitman's Bodyguard (2017) arrives on 1 November. Reynolds plays Michael Bryce, a once top-tier protection agent whose career collapses after a client is assassinated. The cast includes [Samuel L. Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_L._Jackson), [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman) and [Salma Hayek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salma_Hayek).

Its 2021 sequel, The Hitman's Wife's Bodyguard, lands the same day. Bryce is back with Jackson's hitman Darius Kincaid and Hayek's Sonia Kincaid, and the trio is blackmailed by Interpol into stopping a Greek terrorist who plans to cripple Europe's power grid. The AOL piece, originally published by Mandatory, doesn't say whether either film will be available in Australia.

## The role that changed his approach

Far Out Magazine marked the occasion differently, looking back at Buried (2010), in which Reynolds plays an American truck driver in Iraq who wakes up in a coffin. The film was shot mostly in the dark with the camera locked on his face.

"I definitely felt like I emerged from the box as a different person than the one that went in," Reynolds said, as quoted by the magazine. The writer argues the experience steered him towards the blockbuster work that followed, starting with Green Lantern and then Deadpool. That reading is the magazine's interpretation, not something Reynolds is quoted as saying.
