- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) plays twin sisters Maysilee and Merrilee Donner in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which opens in cinemas on November 20.
- Early projections cited by ScreenRant, via TheWrap, put the US opening weekend between US$80 million and about US$105 million.
- Her animated film *PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie* has been the most popular film on Paramount+ in the United States since November 13, according to FlixPatrol.
- Her press tour dresses have paid tribute to Jennifer Lawrence's looks from the original films, according to her stylist.

The Mckenna Grace Hunger Games box office story starts this week. The 20-year-old actress stars in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, the sixth film in the franchise, which arrives in cinemas on Friday, November 20. Another of her 2026 films is already topping a streaming chart in the United States.

## Mckenna Grace Hunger Games box office: what the projections say

According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office-domestic-debut-projections/), which cites TheWrap, the film is expected to take between US$80 million and about US$105 million over its first three days in North America. The top of that range would beat the US$102.7 million opening of *Mockingjay – Part 2* in 2015. It would also make this Lionsgate's first US$100 million opening since that film.

The lower end still clears the previous entry, *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes*, which opened with US$44.6 million in the same pre-Thanksgiving slot in 2023.

Critics are also kinder this time. ScreenRant reports a 76% Rotten Tomatoes score for the new film, against 64% for *Songbirds and Snakes* and 70% for both *Mockingjay* films. *Catching Fire* remains the best reviewed at 90%.

The film reportedly cost about US$200 million, which ScreenRant says could put break-even near US$500 million. It adds that Lionsgate often lowers its costs through international pre-sales, so the real figure is likely lower.

## Where her character fits

The film follows the 50th Hunger Games, 24 years before Katniss Everdeen's story, and a young Haymitch Abernathy, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada). It is based on the prequel novel by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins), part of the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series. The cast also includes [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes), [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close), [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons), [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning), [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin), [Ben Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Wang), [Maya Hawke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maya_Hawke) and [Billy Porter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Porter).

Grace also contributes the song "Half Alive" to the companion album, released November 13, according to Yahoo's interview report, which cites Universal Music Canada. In a SiriusXM conversation on November 16, she and Zada said they had matching ladybug tattoos. They explained that the design refers to a scene in which their characters wash blood off one another.

## A press tour built on Jennifer Lawrence's wardrobe

The tour has drawn as much attention for fashion as for the film. Stylist Molly Dickson told [Marie Claire](https://www.marieclaire.com/fashion/celebrity-style/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-press-tour-stylist-molly-dickson/) that Grace, a lifelong fan of the series, wanted "method dressing" that honoured Jennifer Lawrence.

At the Musée Rodin installation in Paris, she wore a tulle Monique Lhuillier gown inspired by the wedding dress from *Catching Fire*. A silver piece on the bodice, 3D-printed and set with more than 6,000 Swarovski crystals, was built in under 24 hours. At the Academy Museum Gala she wore a red Mônot column dress inspired by Lawrence's Calvin Klein gown from the 2011 Oscars.

For the Berlin premiere on November 2, the plan was to recreate Katniss's flaming entrance dress. Dickson called it one of the hardest looks to source. More of this story is on our [Hunger Games press tour looks explainer](https://moduscarry.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-looks-3/).

## PAW Patrol is topping Paramount+

Away from Panem, *PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie* reached Paramount+ on November 12. By November 13 it was the platform's number one film in the US, according to FlixPatrol data cited by [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-paw-patrol-the-dino-movie-paramount-plus-streaming-success-november-2026/). Grace voices Skye. The film took US$163 million worldwide on a reported US$40 million budget after opening on August 14.

Skye will also front her own Nickelodeon series, *Skye's Pup Squad*, from March 2027. Whether Grace voices her there has not been confirmed.

Separately, Grace told Andy Cohen that playing Britney Spears would be "a dream", but only with the singer's approval. Universal holds the rights to Spears' memoir, and director Jon M. Chu told Variety in September that casting was not yet on his mind.
