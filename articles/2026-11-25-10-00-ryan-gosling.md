**Quick summary**

- *The Nice Guys*, the 2016 comedy with Ryan Gosling and Russell Crowe, is part of Apple TV's free movie rotation for November, according to Pocket-lint.
- The offer is confirmed for the United States only; other regions depend on local availability.
- Pocket-lint reports a 91 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score, but only $63 million at the box office against a $50 million budget.
- Separately, ScreenRant argues that *Star Wars: Starfighter*, set five years after *The Rise of Skywalker*, could pull the franchise away from the Skywalker family.

[Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) has two films in the conversation this week. One is a cult comedy anyone with an Apple TV account in the United States can watch for nothing until the month ends. The other is a Star Wars adventure still to come. Here is what the reports say about each, and why the older film is worth a look first.

## Where to watch Ryan Gosling in The Nice Guys

[Pocket-lint](https://www.pocket-lint.com/last-chance-to-watch-the-nice-guys-apple-tv-free/) reports that Apple TV is offering 20 extra movies free to subscribers for November, and *[The Nice Guys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Nice_Guys)* is among them. The outlet says Apple has not confirmed what replaces the titles in December, though a new rotation seems likely. That makes the end of the month the practical deadline.

The film runs about one hour and 56 minutes. Pocket-lint says it is available in the United States, with regional availability depending on where you live, so Australian readers should check their own Apple TV listings before planning a night in.

The numbers are mixed. Pocket-lint gives the film a Certified Fresh score of 91 per cent on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), which is high for a comedy. Commercially it was a different story: the outlet, citing Box Office Mojo, says it grossed $63 million worldwide on a $50 million budget and was a relative flop. A cult following has grown since.

## The plot and the cast

Gosling plays Holland March, a private detective, opposite [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) as Jackson Healy, a hired enforcer. The two end up as partners in Los Angeles in 1977, searching for a missing woman named Amelia, played by [Margaret Qualley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Qualley). Their search also touches on the apparent suicide of an adult-film star, and Pocket-lint says the story turns increasingly strange and comic as it goes.

[Shane Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Black) directed and Joel Silver produced. Pocket-lint credits the film's reputation mostly to the chemistry between its two leads.

## What ScreenRant says about Star Wars: Starfighter

On the other side of Gosling's schedule, [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/ryan-gosling-star-wars-starfighter-fix-sequel-trilogy-problem/) says *[Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars): Starfighter* could fix a problem that has followed the franchise for seven years. Gosling plays Kade Auberon, a pilot from the New Jedi Order era who travels across the galaxy with a child, played by [Flynn Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flynn_Gray). ScreenRant says the plot is being kept under wraps and that the film is a standalone story.

The supporting cast includes [Matt Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Smith), [Mia Goth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Goth), [Aaron Pierre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Pierre_(actor)), [Simon Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Bird), [Jamael Westman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamael_Westman), [Daniel Ings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Ings) and [Amy Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Adams). [Shawn Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shawn_Levy) directs from a script by [Jonathan Tropper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Tropper), and ScreenRant notes that neither has worked on the franchise before.

## The Skywalker problem

ScreenRant's argument is that Star Wars has struggled to leave the Skywalker Saga. The sequel trilogy introduced Rey, Finn and Poe but kept returning to Luke, Han and Leia, and the Disney+ shows have mostly stayed inside the same timeline, with Anakin and Luke both reappearing.

*Starfighter* is set five years after *The Rise of Skywalker*, the furthest point forward the films have gone, according to the outlet. That distance means it cannot overlap with the original Skywalker stories. This is ScreenRant's opinion and not a studio statement, but it gives a clear idea of what the film is expected to try.

## What happens next

The Apple TV offer is the only deadline in these reports: the free rotation is tied to November, and Pocket-lint expects a new line-up in December. Neither source gives a release date for *Starfighter*, and ScreenRant says the story details remain undisclosed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is The Nice Guys free on Apple TV?

Yes, in the United States for November, according to Pocket-lint. Apple has not confirmed what happens in December, and availability elsewhere varies by region.

### Who stars in The Nice Guys?

Ryan Gosling plays Holland March and Russell Crowe plays Jackson Healy. Margaret Qualley plays the missing woman, Amelia. Shane Black directed.

### How did The Nice Guys do at the box office?

Pocket-lint, citing Box Office Mojo, says it earned $63 million on a $50 million budget, which it calls a relative flop despite strong reviews.

### Who plays opposite Ryan Gosling in Star Wars: Starfighter?

ScreenRant lists Flynn Gray, Matt Smith, Mia Goth, Aaron Pierre, Simon Bird, Jamael Westman, Daniel Ings and Amy Adams.
