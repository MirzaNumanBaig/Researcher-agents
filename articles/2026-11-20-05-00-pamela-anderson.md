Pamela Anderson has given the holiday manicure a new direction. At the CFDA Fashion Awards in New York on 2 November, the 59-year-old wore a rich "winter cherry" shade, and [Grazia](https://graziamagazine.com/us/articles/goodbye-bright-red-pamela-anderson-59-is-wearing-the-deeper-winter-cherry-manicure-for-the-holidays-25497/) has since named it the colour to book as party season begins. Everything we have published on her is gathered on our [Pamela Anderson tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/pamela-anderson/).

## What is the winter cherry manicure?

According to Grazia, winter cherry is a deeper, more nuanced version of classic cherry red. It carries extra depth and a high-gloss finish, sitting somewhere between a fire-engine red and a burgundy. The magazine compares it to a glass of red wine rather than a fire truck.

Anderson wore it with a Carolina Herrera outfit and a playful slingback heel. Grazia says the colour gave the whole look a final touch of warmth without competing with the clothes.

## Why it works for winter

Grazia's argument is that classic bright red, while festive, can feel loud in winter light and next to dark clothing. A deeper cherry reads as warmer and a little more grown-up, yet still looks celebratory enough for holiday parties.

The magazine also points to practical advantages:

- It pairs well with winter fabrics such as black velvet, camel wool and cream knits.
- Darker shades tend to hide small imperfections.
- The colour looks polished on short nails, so it is easy to wear every day.

## How to get the look at home

Grazia suggests shades in the cherry-to-wine range, naming Essie Nail Color in Bordeaux, Londontown Lakur Nail Polish in Vendetta and CND Vinylux Longwear Nail Polish in Books & Beaujolais. Its advice on undertones is simple: warm skin suits a cherry with a hint of brown, and cool skin suits one with a touch of blue or berry. It also recommends testing the colour on a single nail in daylight first.

Preparation matters most with a dark polish. Susan Carroll, founder of the London beauty destination Young LDN, told Grazia to start with neatly shaped nails and well-prepped cuticles, then make sure the nail plate is completely clean before the base coat. The magazine's routine is:

1. Apply a base coat.
2. Add two thin coats of colour, letting each dry.
3. Finish with a glossy top coat for the high-shine effect.
4. Clean the edges with a small brush dipped in remover.
5. Reapply top coat every two or three days to keep the shine going.
