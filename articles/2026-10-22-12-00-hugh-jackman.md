Hugh Jackman's dark take on the Robin Hood legend, *The Death of Robin Hood*, is reportedly doing far better on streaming than it did in cinemas. Entertainment site [Collider](https://collider.com/hugh-jackman-the-death-of-robin-hood-streaming-success-hbo-max-october-2026/) said this week that the R-rated film is ruling the streaming charts, describing it as a bullseye for the Australian star.

## A quiet cinema run

According to Collider, the film struggled to find an audience on the big screen. It reportedly earned $9.5 million worldwide against a budget of $20 million. The site put that down in part to the film's sombre tone and an uneven second half, which it said divided viewers.

Reviews were more generous than audience scores. Collider cites a Rotten Tomatoes rating of 69 per cent from critics, compared with 56 per cent from audiences. The article's web address points to HBO Max as the platform where the film is now finding its audience, although the portion of the piece we could read did not spell out the chart figures.

## What the film is about

Directed by Michael Sarnoski, the film is set in 1247 and strips away the heroic image of the outlaw. Jackman plays an ageing, battered Robin Hood living in self-imposed exile, still haunted by a lifetime of violence and the enemies it created.

As Collider describes it, his old companion Little John, played by Bill Skarsgård, tracks him down for help with a personal score. The confrontation goes badly wrong and Robin is gravely wounded. He wakes on a remote island, where Sister Brigid, played by Jodie Comer, cares for him. The story then turns to the cycle of revenge, morality and redemption.

## Jackman's grittier roles

Collider places the film alongside other mature roles in Jackman's career, including *Logan*, *Prisoners* and the HBO film *Bad Education*. It also points to *Deadpool & Wolverine*, which the site says became the highest-grossing R-rated film until *The Odyssey* took the title this year.

The site calls his turn as Robin Hood one of his more underrated performances, a view that reflects Collider's opinion rather than a settled verdict. Its own figures show audiences were split.

## What happens next

Neither Jackman nor the film's producers have commented in the material we reviewed. Streaming figures are usually released by the platforms themselves, so it is worth watching for official numbers that show how large the film's second life really is. For Australian viewers, availability will depend on local streaming rights, so check your usual services.
