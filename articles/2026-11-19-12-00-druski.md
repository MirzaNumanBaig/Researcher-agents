**Quick summary**

- Netflix released the first trailer for *Collect & Praise* on Wednesday 18 November (US time), according to Complex.
- Druski plays a money-hungry megachurch pastor and Mike Epps plays his unpredictable deacon.
- The semi-scripted series has six episodes and premieres on Netflix on 1 December 2026, per ComingSoon.net.
- It is the first project in Druski's multi-year Netflix deal, announced in November.

[Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) is taking his viral church sketch to Netflix. The streamer has released the first trailer for *Collect & Praise*, a six-episode comedy in which the comedian plays a greedy megachurch pastor alongside [Mike Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps), and the series arrives on 1 December 2026.

For readers wondering about the Druski *Collect & Praise* release, the date is firm: ComingSoon.net and Complex both report 1 December. The show lands only days after its star's biggest television week yet.

## What the trailer shows

Netflix's official synopsis, quoted by The Playlist, reads: "Faith comes second to fortune as a money-hungry pastor and his unpredictable deacon scheme their way through scandal, chaos, and controversy."

According to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/trailers/2226612-druski-mike-epps-turn-church-into-chaos-netflix-comedy-trailer), the trailer opens with Druski's character, Pastor Dru, claiming he was "one of the biggest pastors in the world". It then cuts to him feeding stacks of cash into a money-counting machine, with a wine bottle on the table. Epps appears as his partner in the money-making schemes, and the pair are seen riding in a white Rolls-Royce.

The mood turns quickly. The same outlet says people with guns chase and threaten the duo, and the footage includes Druski behind bars and in courtrooms. Complex adds that the clips include a heated Epps firing a gun inside the church and Druski hanging in mid-air again, a nod to the original sketch.

## Where the idea came from

*Collect & Praise* grows out of a sketch Druski posted in January called "Mega Church Pastors LOVE Money". [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/jaelaniturnerwilliams/druski-collect-praise-trailer-netflix) describes it as him playing a hustling pastor suspended above a congregation while Kirk Franklin's "Revolution" plays. The outlet calls the Netflix series his first long-form project based on one of his viral skits.

The sketch also drew a response from Franklin, who defended it and criticised wealthy pastors, according to a Complex headline linked from its coverage. At this year's BET Awards, Druski recreated the sketch and turned it into a celebrity roast.

The Playlist compares the premise to *The Righteous Gemstones*, with a strong focus on satirising Black church culture. ComingSoon.net calls the show semi-scripted.

## Cast and crew

Beyond the two leads, ComingSoon.net lists Navv Greene, Roy Woods Jr., Rob Moran and Michael Blackson as guest stars. David Janoff, who has worked with Druski on his satirical projects, directs. The show is produced by 4Lifers Entertainment, Ask Media and Janoff Films.

## A big year for Druski

The series follows a run of milestones. Complex notes that he hosted the 7 November episode of *Saturday Night Live*, which drew 5.6 million viewers and the show's biggest ratings in two years. It also lists him as the youngest person to host the BET Awards and a cover star of TIME's TIME100 Next issue.

The Playlist reports that his Instagram following stands at 13.7 million, and describes the Netflix deal as his step into traditional media. Complex says the deal also lets Netflix license *Coulda Been Love*, *Coulda Been Daddy*, *Coulda Been Records Auditions* and his comedy skits.

He also has a role in *The Catch*, a rom-com that stars Emma Stone and Chris Pine, per Complex.

## What happens next

All six episodes of *Collect & Praise* are due on Netflix on 1 December 2026. Complex says it kicks off the multi-year partnership, so more Druski projects for the platform are expected, though no other titles or dates were named in the sources.
