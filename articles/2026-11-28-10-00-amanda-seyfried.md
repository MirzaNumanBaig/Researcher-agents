**Quick summary**

- BAFTA's draft Entered Films List for the 2027 EE BAFTA Film Awards has 126 best film contenders and 728 performance entries, according to Variety.
- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) has no entry, because BAFTA rules make the performances in "You Can See Everything" ineligible.
- Variety reports she is also ineligible at the Golden Globes and the Actor Awards, with Critics Choice the only televised precursor open to her.
- Amendments are due by noon GMT on Monday, 30 November, and the official list lands on Friday, 4 December.

Amanda Seyfried will not be in the running for a BAFTA performance award next year, at least on the evidence of the draft entries list published this week. Variety reported on 27 November that her return as Elizabeth Holmes in Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's A24 documentary does not appear among the 728 performance entries, because the awards body's rules deem the film's performances ineligible.

## Why the list leaves her out

The draft Entered Films List names 126 best film contenders across the 2027 EE BAFTA Film Awards, according to [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/awards/bafta-2027-entries-full-list-amanda-seyfried-1236959765/). The documentary itself is on it. It is entered for best film, director, documentary and seven of the nine craft categories, including costume design and makeup and hair.

What it lacks is any performance entry. Variety says that is down to BAFTA's own eligibility rules, not a choice by the filmmakers.

Seyfried won an Emmy for playing Holmes in Hulu's "The Dropout". In the A24 film she returns to the role in dramatised reenactments. For more on how the part came together, see our earlier story on [how Amanda Seyfried landed in You Can See Everything](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-you-can-see-everything-how-she-joined/).

## An awards season with one open door

BAFTA is not the only gap. Variety writes that the Golden Globes and the Actor Awards, formerly the SAG Awards, also treat Seyfried as ineligible for this performance. Critics Choice stands alone in accepting her, in the supporting actress category, which makes it the only televised stop on the precursor circuit where she can be nominated. Nomination voting for those awards is under way.

The stakes are not small. Variety currently projects her performance to win the Oscar for supporting actress. That is the trade paper's forecast, not a result, and nominations are a long way off. Still, a contender who is shut out of three major precursors has fewer chances to build momentum before Academy voters make up their minds.

## What else is on the list

The list shows how crowded the field is. Variety counts films entered in every category, including Denis Villeneuve's "Dune: Part Three", Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey", Steven Spielberg's "Disclosure Day", Robert Eggers' "Werwulf", "Project Hail Mary" and "Spider-Man: Brand New Day".

Six performers are entered four times each. Zendaya appears in lead for "Dune: Part Three", "The Drama" and "Spider-Man: Brand New Day", and in supporting for "The Odyssey". Her "Drama" co-star Robert Pattinson matches her total. Anne Hathaway has three lead entries, for "The Devil Wears Prada 2", "Mother Mary" and "Verity", plus a supporting entry in "The Odyssey".

Against that, a single supporting role in a documentary would have been one entry among 728. Its absence says more about the rulebook than about the reception of her work.

## New voting rules for acting

BAFTA has also changed how the acting races work. Casting chapter members now vote alongside the acting chapter in Rounds One and Two. The chapter vote fills the first five of 10 longlist places in each category, and a jury picks the other five from performers ranked sixth to 15th. Each category still ends with six nominees.

## What happens next

Distributors and producers have until noon GMT on Monday, 30 November, to request changes to the draft. Variety expects a few small corrections. The official Entered Films List follows on Friday, 4 December, the day Round One voting opens.

Nothing in Variety's report suggests the BAFTA position will change, so the next real test for Seyfried is Critics Choice.
