**Quick summary**

- DC Studios co-chief [James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) says he will "bend over backwards" to explain Harley Quinn in the DCU if [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) wants to play her again.
- If Robbie does not, Gunn says he might cast someone else in the role.
- Gunn made the comments on a one-off podcast episode released on Thursday, as reported by the Los Angeles Times.
- He also said the DCU Batman is not cast yet, but that it "isn't very far away".

[James Gunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Gunn) has put the decision about Harley Quinn's future in the DC Universe in [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie)'s hands. Speaking on a podcast episode released on Thursday, the DC Studios co-chief said he would go out of his way to make her version of the character fit, if she wants to return, according to the [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/movies/story/2026-10-30/james-gunn-dcu-batman-harley-quinn-margot-robbie).

## What Gunn said about Robbie and Harley Quinn

The remarks came in an episode of "The Suicide Squad: Is It Canon?", where Gunn spoke with "Peacemaker" stars [Jennifer Holland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jennifer_Holland) and [Steve Agee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Agee). The episode looked at whether his 2021 film "The Suicide Squad" belongs in the DCU's official continuity.

Gunn called Harley "one of the more difficult things" to justify as canon. The reason, he explained, is her backstory. Her path to villainy is tied to her romance with the Joker, and the DCU has not yet shown whether the Joker exists.

Still, he was warm about Robbie. He described her as one of his favourite actors to work with and said she was very good in his film. "I love her as Harley," he said, as quoted by the Los Angeles Times.

## Why the choice sits with Robbie

Gunn said he is "almost certainly" going to use Harley in the DCU. The open question, the paper reported, is whether Robbie wants to return to the role, given how far her career has risen since she first played it.

"If Margot wants to play her, then I'll bend over backwards to explain that in the world," Gunn said. If she does not, he said he might go with another actor, so that he is not tied to what happened to Harley in "The Suicide Squad".

Robbie has not commented in the sources we reviewed. Our earlier report on [Gunn's view that Robbie may be 'over' Harley Quinn](https://moduscarry.com/james-gunn-says-margot-robbie-may-be-over-harley-quinn/) covers the previous round of this discussion.

## Batman casting is closer, but nobody is in the running

Gunn also spoke about the DCU's Batman, a character first confirmed in the animated series [Creature Commandos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creature_Commandos) last year. He said the role has not been cast and dismissed rumours that a particular actor will appear in this project or that one as "all b—".

He said an actor with a small part in "The Suicide Squad" would not be ruled out. He also said he wants the best actor for the part, rather than a movie star, noting that Supermen have always been chosen that way while Batmen usually come from the ranks of stars.

Gunn hinted that the new Batman will be about the same age as Superman, played by 33-year-old [David Corenswet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Corenswet). On Friday he replied to a fan on Threads about reported front-runners: "No one is in the running," he wrote. "We haven't started casting yet."

The DCU Batman is separate from [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson)'s version in [Matt Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Reeves)' "The Batman" films, which sit outside the shared continuity of "Superman" (2025), "Supergirl" (2026) and "Lanterns". The Los Angeles Times also noted that [Steven Soderbergh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Soderbergh) will help finish shooting "The Batman Part II" while Reeves deals with a family matter, and that Gunn says Reeves remains the director.

## What happens next

No casting has been announced for either role. Gunn said Batman "won't be too long" away, but he gave no date. For Harley, the next step appears to be a conversation with Robbie, and neither side has said whether one has happened.
