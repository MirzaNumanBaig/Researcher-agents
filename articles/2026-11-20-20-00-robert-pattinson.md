**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios announced on 16 November that [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) will play James Bond, according to AOL.
- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried), 40, commented "Am I too old?" under a Variety list of possible Bond girls that left her out.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) is set to direct, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay.
- Watch site Time+Tide says Pattinson's role as a Jaeger-LeCoultre ambassador raises questions about Bond's watch.

Robert Pattinson's casting as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) has done what every Bond announcement does: it has set off a hunt for his co-star. This week the loudest voice in that conversation belongs to an actress who was not on the list.

## Seyfried joins the conversation

Variety published a list of actresses who could play opposite the new 007. According to [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/amanda-seyfried-responds-bond-girl-175927000.html), the names included Zendaya, [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), Lupita Nyong'o, Sydney Sweeney, Anya Taylor-Joy, Gemma Chan and Sophie Turner.

Seyfried was not among them. The 40-year-old commented on the post, "Am I too old?", and fans quickly took up the question. One commenter pointed out that she is the same age as Pattinson and asked why a 40-year-old can play Bond while a 40-year-old Bond girl is treated as unthinkable. Another praised her "sweetness, the sensuality, the mystery, and the class".

None of this is casting news. AOL is clear that the speculation remains unconfirmed, and nobody has named a leading lady. It is a social media exchange, though a telling one about how age is discussed for men and women in Hollywood.

## What has been confirmed

On 16 November, Amazon MGM Studios shared the casting announcement on social media with an image of Pattinson as the spy. In the statement, he called Bond "one of the most legendary characters in cinema" and said being trusted with 007 was "the honour of a lifetime".

Villeneuve is attached as director. Time+Tide notes that he and Pattinson worked together on the final Dune instalment, so the pairing is less surprising than it first looks. AOL says the film is reportedly slated for 2028.

Pattinson's schedule is full until then. He is currently filming Matt Reeves' The Batman Part II, due in cinemas on 18 February 2028, according to AOL. That leaves him with both Bruce Wayne and 007 on his résumé, as Time+Tide put it.

## The watch question

Bond and watches go together, and the horology press has noticed. Time+Tide describes the announcement as the biggest story in the watch world this week. [Aaron Taylor-Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Taylor-Johnson), an Omega ambassador, had long been seen as the frontrunner, while [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) were also rumoured.

The site's writer argues that brand ties had been used to rule out some candidates. [Henry Cavill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Cavill), for example, is a Longines ambassador. Pattinson is a Jaeger-LeCoultre ambassador, so fans are curious how the watch side will be handled. "It remains to be seen," the writer says. No watch has been announced.

## What happens next

The film is reportedly aimed at 2028, so a long wait lies ahead. Cast announcements for the supporting roles are the next thing to watch, and any confirmed leading lady would come from the studio, not from a fan list.
