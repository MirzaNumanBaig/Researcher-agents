**Quick summary**

- Sunday Rose, 18, is on the cover of Vogue Germany's November 2026 issue, her first Vogue cover.
- She is the eldest daughter of [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) and [Keith Urban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Urban).
- In the accompanying interview she said she is "very aware" of the opportunities she has been given.
- Nine.com.au reports Kidman set a rule that her daughter could not model until she turned 16.
- Woman's Day, via Now To Love, cites an unnamed insider on the surname printed on the cover. That is unconfirmed.

Sunday Rose has landed her first Vogue cover, fronting the November 2026 edition of Vogue Germany at 18. The daughter of [Nicole Kidman](https://nicolekidmanofficial.com/) and Keith Urban used the accompanying interview to address the "nepo baby" label directly, according to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/lifestyle/fashion-beauty/sunday-rose-nicole-kidman-keith-urban-vogue-cover-nepo-baby-interview-20261021-p617cx.html).

## The cover and the shoot

Nine.com.au says Sunday Rose appears in Louis Vuitton, photographed by Sebastián Faena. HOLA describes a pink shirt with orange details and an orange and brown vest. She shared the cover on Instagram, writing that she was "in shock" and calling the shoot "a dream come true", as quoted by [Now To Love](https://www.nowtolove.com.au/celebrity/sunday-rose-secret-sign-huge-step/).

She thanked the crew for taking her to Maine, where she lay in the grass on a beach between setups. "To see those words above my head means everything to me," she wrote.

It's a quick rise. Nine.com.au traces her runway career to the Miu Miu Spring/Summer 2025 show in 2024, which she remembers as "truly insane". By her own account she thought she knew a lot about modelling and "knew nothing at all". Since then she has been to the Met Gala and walked in shows in New York, Milan and Paris.

## "Very aware" of the privilege

The line that has travelled furthest is her answer on the nepo baby tag. "I'm very aware that I'm given opportunities that not everyone can have, and I'm truly grateful to be able to take advantage of them," she told Vogue, as reported by Nine.com.au and [HOLA](https://www.hola.com/us/celebrities/20261021930496/nicole-kidman-daughter-sunday-rose-nepo-baby-magazine-cover/).

She said that at work she tries to give her best, avoids a negative attitude however tired or jet-lagged she is, and treats everyone with respect.

HOLA also quotes her on growing up in Nashville, Tennessee. Few people live in the area, she said, so most classmates already knew who her parents were by the time she started school.

## A 16-year rule and a school formal

Nine.com.au reports that Kidman's one firm condition was that Sunday Rose could not start modelling until she was 16. HOLA says the two agreed on that age, even though she had wanted to model from a young age. According to Nine.com.au, it took her only a short time after reaching 16 to build a career.

She is also still at school. Nine.com.au says that means homework, exams and a school formal, where she wore an Oscar de la Renta dress the outlet prices at $17,900.

Kidman's influence comes up in earlier interviews too. HOLA quotes Sunday Rose telling Elle Australia that her mother is "my biggest inspiration in life". The one piece of advice from Kidman it cites is about punctuality: an early call time is easier to face when you remember that arriving on time shows you're prepared and grateful.

## The surname detail

Now To Love, drawing on a Woman's Day report, notes that the cover credits her as Sunday Rose Kidman-Urban. It says she dropped "Kidman-Urban" from her Instagram name before her parents' split in September 2025, after 19 years of marriage. An unnamed insider told Woman's Day the cover wording could signal openness to repairing ties with her father.

None of that has been confirmed by Sunday Rose, Kidman or Urban. The insider's reading is one interpretation of a name on a magazine cover, and it should be treated that way.

## What happens next

Vogue Germany's November 2026 issue carries the cover. Now To Love notes she also had what its source called an "extraordinary week" at Paris Fashion Week earlier in October. Neither Kidman nor Urban has publicly commented on the cover in the sources reviewed.
