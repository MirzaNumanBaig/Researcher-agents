**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) is Tiffany & Co.'s newest ambassador and the star of its 2026 holiday campaign, "Love Is a Gift", according to Galerie Magazine.
- She placed an ornament on the Blue Box tree this week to light the Bird on a Rock installation at the brand's Madison Avenue flagship, The Landmark.
- The campaign includes a short film, You & Me, that moves between the present day and the early 1990s.
- Separately, the 40-year-old told The Hollywood Reporter she keeps her awards-season expectations low for the documentary You Can See Everything.

## Amanda Seyfried lights up Tiffany's Landmark

Amanda Seyfried switched on the Tiffany & Co. holiday season in New York this week. [Galerie Magazine](https://galeriemagazine.com/tiffany-co-and-amanda-seyfried-kick-off-an-enchanting-holiday-season-at-the-landmark/) reports that the actress, introduced as the house's newest ambassador, hung a Tiffany ornament on the Blue Box tree, which lit the return of the brand's Bird on a Rock installation at The Landmark on Madison Avenue.

The evening included festive cocktails and a venue dressed as a winter wonderland. Guests included fellow house ambassador [Mikey Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikey_Madison), [Louisa Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisa_Jacobson), [Jordyn Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordyn_Woods) and [Danny Ramirez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Ramirez). The window displays and holiday decor stay on view through the season.

## What is in the "Love Is a Gift" film

Seyfried also stars in the house's 2026 holiday campaign, "Love Is a Gift". Its centrepiece is a short film, You & Me, which Galerie describes as travelling between the present day and the early 1990s. The idea is that meaningful objects carry memories as they are handed down through generations.

In the early scenes, Seyfried wears Tiffany & Co. HardWare jewellery as she dashes through the city with her young daughter to see the shop windows. The film then cuts to today. Seyfried hugs a young relative while her now-grown daughter holds a blue Tiffany box that, in the magazine's words, holds more than a single token.

For readers who follow red-carpet and brand news, it is a notable role for a star better known for screen work. You can find more of our coverage on [Fashion](https://moduscarry.com/category/fashion/).

## Low expectations for awards season

The ambassador news arrives as Seyfried talks up a very different project. According to a [Yahoo Style Canada](https://ca.style.yahoo.com/amanda-seyfried-low-expectations-why-040000410.html) report, drawing on an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, she stars in You Can See Everything, a documentary film that follows Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes shortly before she began a prison sentence for fraud.

Seyfried said she usually goes into awards season with low expectations so she is not disappointed, and that she was not sure the film would ever be released. She admitted that campaigning is time-consuming and drains energy, but said she is willing to do it. She praised distributor A24 as excited and said she is proud of the film and loved working with its small crew.

## A recent hit role

The same report recalls her turn in the 2025 mystery-thriller The Housemaid, opposite [Brandon Sklenar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandon_Sklenar) and Sydney Sweeney. Seyfried told W Magazine she felt "so liberated" playing an unhinged housewife. She compared the film to Black Swan or [Single White Female](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single_White_Female), "but funny", and said director [Paul Feig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Feig) gave her unusual directions, such as asking her to make her co-star think a kiss was coming.

## What happens next

Tiffany's displays and decor will remain at The Landmark through the holidays, so more campaign moments are likely over the coming weeks. On the film side, no awards decisions have been announced in the sources we read. Seyfried's own comments suggest she will take part in any campaign A24 runs.
