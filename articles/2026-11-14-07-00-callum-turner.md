**Quick summary**

- Puck reports that all five finalists for Bond 26 signed preliminary deals, according to Just Jared's summary of the piece.
- [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor reportedly agreed to four films; [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) reportedly held out for three and a bigger salary.
- The terms for [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) were not disclosed.
- Nothing is official. TMZ says an announcement is expected before Christmas.

New reporting on the hunt for the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) has put the Callum Turner Bond audition back in the spotlight, though not because of anything the actor did. A Puck piece by [Matthew Belloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Belloni), published on 13 November 2026, describes how the finalists were signed up and why Pattinson's deal looks different. Amazon MGM Studios has not confirmed a choice.

## What Puck reports about the finalist deals

According to [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/13/robert-pattinsons-james-bond-deal-is-different-from-the-other-finalists-heres-why-what-that-suggests/), which summarised the Puck article, every one of the five finalists had to sign a preliminary deal before the process went further. That is a notable detail on its own: it suggests the studio wanted terms locked in before any final decision.

The reported numbers differ by actor. Lowden and O'Connor are said to have agreed to four Bond films if chosen. Pattinson is said to have agreed to three, and to have negotiated a larger salary than the others.

The outlet adds that Pattinson's deal was reportedly hard to close. Per the Puck quote it relays, some people involved wondered why the filmmakers would bother if they did not really want him. Just Jared says it is still waiting for word from Amazon MGM Studios.

## Where Callum Turner fits in

Turner is named among the original contenders. [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/13/robert-pattinson-james-bond-casting-shakeup/), citing Puck, lists Lowden, O'Connor and Turner as the first group to audition. It says Pattinson and Hoult screen-tested later.

TMZ's reading is that director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) and the producers were not sold on the first group, so they brought in bigger names. The headline on that story says Lowden and Turner "failed to impress" executives. That is a theory "making the rounds", as TMZ puts it, rather than a confirmed account. Neither outlet quotes anyone at the studio.

Just Jared is explicit about the gap in the reporting: the specific contract terms for Turner and Hoult were not disclosed. So there is no public information on how many films Turner would have signed for, or on his pay.

## Why Pattinson is the name in focus

Just Jared says the Puck argument for Pattinson rests on his global recognition from Twilight and The Batman, his record of leading major franchises, and his dedicated fan bases. He also has a working relationship with Villeneuve from Dune.

TMZ describes that link as the upcoming Dune: Part Three and says the filmmaker reportedly always wanted to see Pattinson audition. Whether this makes Pattinson the frontrunner is a question Just Jared poses but cannot answer.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who are the reported finalists for Bond 26?

The reports name Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner, Robert Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult. Just Jared says five finalists signed preliminary deals.

### Has Callum Turner been cast as James Bond?

No. Neither report says a decision has been made, and Amazon MGM Studios has not announced one. TMZ says an announcement is expected before Christmas.

### How is Robert Pattinson's reported deal different?

Puck reportedly says he agreed to three films rather than four and negotiated a higher salary than the others. His deal was also said to be difficult to close.

### Were Callum Turner's contract terms reported?

No. Just Jared notes that the specific terms for Turner and Hoult were not disclosed in the Puck article.
