- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) and [Steve Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_Martin) have reflected on attending Selena Gomez's wedding.
- Martin called the wedding and the couple "delightful", according to AOL's report of his remarks to Capital on 23 November 2026.
- Short said he and Martin "adore" Gomez and joked that he cut the wedding cake.
- The three star together in *Only Murders in the Building*.

Martin Short and Steve Martin have shared how they remember Selena Gomez's wedding, with both praising the bride and the day itself. The remarks, reported by [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/steve-martin-martin-short-reflect-222432000.html), came at an *Only Murders in London* event in London this week.

## What the pair said about the wedding

Gomez married record producer Benny Blanco in Santa Barbara, California, on 27 September 2025, according to AOL. Short and Martin were among the guests.

Speaking to Capital on 23 November, Martin said the two comedians had been invited and described both the wedding and the couple as "delightful". AOL also notes that he told *Entertainment Tonight* in November 2025 that the best part was simply being there.

Short was more playful. He said he and Martin "adore" Gomez, and called her "a delight" with "no weirdness", despite being a major pop star. He also joked that he had cut the wedding cake, and that it was "delicious".

## A friendship built on set

The warmth is not new. According to AOL, Short had already spoken about Gomez's marriage on the *Good Hang with Amy Poehler* podcast in September 2025, when he said he was "thrilled for her happiness".

The three have worked side by side for years on *Only Murders in the Building*, the comedy-mystery series that has become one of the more durable hits of streaming television. Their on-screen chemistry has often been matched by affectionate comments off screen. Our earlier piece on [Short and Martin's real friendship ritual on screen](https://moduscarry.com/martin-short-only-murders-easter-egg/) shows how that bond carries into the show.
