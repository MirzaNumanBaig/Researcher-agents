Olivia Wilde says the end of her relationship with Jason Sudeikis felt like a personal failure, yet she believes it was the right call for their children. She made the remarks in *The Interview*, published by The New York Times on 14 November 2026, and they have been widely picked up since.

## What Wilde said about the split

According to [Yahoo Entertainment](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/olivia-wilde-says-splitting-jason-170024822.html), Wilde described the decision to separate as the hardest and most painful of her life. She said she is proud of the co-parenting arrangement the two have built and that they have found a "healthy equilibrium".

Her argument for the split was simple. Children notice when adults hide their feelings, she said, and she would rather her kids have honest parents than performing ones.

She also pushed back on a popular idea. Exes who separate amicably with little effort, she suggested, make it look easier than it is. Fashion Times UK reported her calling that notion "BS" and saying co-parenting remains hard.

## The timeline on record

The reported background is consistent across both outlets. Wilde and Sudeikis dated from 2011 and became engaged in 2013. They separated in 2020 and never married. They share a son, now 12, and a daughter, now 10.

The split became public in a awkward way in April 2022, when Wilde was handed custody papers on stage at CinemaCon in Las Vegas while presenting *Don't Worry Darling*, according to Yahoo. In September 2023 the pair reportedly reached a joint custody settlement with the children alternating weeks between homes.

## The child support figure

Fashion Times UK put the monthly child support at US$27,500 (about £20,816), citing court documents reported in 2023. It broke that down as US$10,300 for their son and US$17,200 for their daughter, plus 25% of Wilde's childcare costs. The outlet notes these are the 2023 terms and does not confirm whether they have changed. Yahoo's report gives no financial figures at all, so treat the number as dated rather than current.

## Sudeikis has spoken too

Wilde's comments follow a run of public remarks from both sides. On the 17 June episode of the *Call Her Daddy* podcast, she said the alternating-week arrangement lets her concentrate on acting and directing in her weeks without the children.

Sudeikis addressed the breakup on 18 October on *Talk Easy with Sam Fragoso*, according to Yahoo. He said "I could have communicated better", and objected to accounts that cast complicated relationships as one person being right and the other wrong.

Read together, the two sets of comments show little dispute over the basics. Both describe a hard separation and both stress the children.
