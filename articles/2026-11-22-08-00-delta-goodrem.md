**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) and [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) opened Blackpool Week on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) on Saturday with a quickstep.
- The judges gave 9, 9, 9 and 9, a total of 36 out of 40 and her highest mark of the series.
- That put the pair second on the leaderboard, three points behind a three-way tie on 39.
- Kai returned after missing the previous show through illness.
- Only four weeks of the series remain.

Delta Goodrem had the perfect reason to smile on Saturday night. She and Kai Widdrington opened Strictly Come Dancing's Blackpool special with a quickstep to Eternal's "I Wanna Be the Only One", and the judges rewarded it with 36 out of 40, the best score of her run so far.

## A quickstep worth 36 points

According to [Aussievision](https://www.aussievision.net/post/oh-what-a-star-delta-goodrem-lights-up-blackpool-s-tower-ballroom-with-quickstep-on-strictly-co), each of the four judges scored the dance a 9. That left the Australian singer in second place, behind three couples who each scored 39: [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita) and Tabby Stoecker for a Charleston, [Katya Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katya_Jones) and John Nellis for a Viennese waltz, and [Cach Mercer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cach_Mercer) and [Maddie Ingoldsby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddie_Ingoldsby) for a jive.

Behind Goodrem, two pairs shared third on 35: [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) with [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin), and [Lacey Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lacey_Turner) with [Vito Coppola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Coppola). The same outlet notes that Goodrem has landed in the top places of the leaderboard every week she has danced.

The routine used Tunnel of Love sets and props, a nod to Blackpool's fairground rides. Goodrem wore a pink sequinned and tulle dress. The Tower Ballroom floor measures 37 metres by 31 metres, about nine times the size of the dancefloor at Elstree Studios, where the show normally films.

## What the judges said

The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16311839/Strictly-Kai-Widdrington-reunites-Delta-Goodrem-Blackpool-highest-score-mystery-illness-hospitalised.html) reported that Anton Du Beke opened with "What a start!", while Motsi Mabuse said the dance "felt like a celebration". Craig Revel Horwood took issue with Goodrem's left shoulder, then praised the "fantastic musicality" and called the routine "absolutely brilliant". Head judge Shirley Ballas said the late Len Goodman would be "so so proud".

## The dress that nearly got in the way

It was not entirely smooth. The News & Star reported that the sleeves of Goodrem's dress crossed over and tangled mid-dance, and that Widdrington quickly freed them. Du Beke remarked that the "frock went a bit funny" but said it did not spoil the dance, according to [that report](https://www.newsandstar.co.uk/news/national/uk-today/26751146.delta-goodrem-avoids-dress-malfunction-strictly-blackpool/). That article gives the pair's score as 39. The leaderboard figures in the other reports, 36 for Goodrem and 39 for the three leaders, point to 36 as the correct total.

## Kai's return after illness

The result carried extra weight because Widdrington was back at all. The 31-year-old missed the previous week after being taken ill, and Aljaz Skorjanec danced with Goodrem in his place. Speaking to The Mirror, as quoted by [Female First](https://www.femalefirst.co.uk/tv/news/kai-widdrington-called-delta-goodrem-ambulance-after-collapse-1454041.html), Widdrington said a bad bug had left him depleted and that doctors told him to rest and take on fluids. He added that he told Goodrem from the ambulance he would be back the next day, though he knew that was not realistic.

Goodrem told the same outlet she was thrilled to have him back and grateful to Skorjanec. She is also dealing with a rib injury that has bothered her for five weeks. When host Emma Willis welcomed Widdrington back on Saturday, he replied: "It's good to be back!"

## What happens next

The results show airs on Sunday in the UK. Jaime Winstone and [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu), and Graeme Hall and [Lauren Oakley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Oakley), each scored 32 and sit at the bottom, although the public vote is combined with the judges' marks before two couples face the dance-off.

Goodrem and Widdrington hope to dance again next Saturday. Aussievision notes that every couple left in the contest has topped the leaderboard at least once, a first for Blackpool Week, so the Glitterball race looks wide open. ## Frequently asked questions

### What score did Delta Goodrem get in Blackpool Week?
She and Kai Widdrington scored 36 out of 40 for their quickstep, with a 9 from each judge. It was her highest score of the series so far.

### Who is leading the Strictly leaderboard?
Three couples tied on 39 points: Tabby Stoecker and Mark Karmalita, John Nellis and Katya Jones, and Cach Mercer and Maddie Ingoldsby.

### Why was Kai Widdrington absent last week?
He was taken ill with what he described as a bad bug and was depleted. Aljaz Skorjanec stepped in to dance with Delta Goodrem.
