**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) will host [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) for the first time on December 5, with [Don Toliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Toliver) as musical guest.
- The announcement was made live on the November 14 episode, hosted by John Mulaney.
- Billy Evans, partner of Elizabeth Holmes, told CBS News he was "glad Nathan was there to help her" when asked about Seyfried's performance.
- Seyfried's animated Cinderella film *Steps* arrives on Netflix on November 20.

Amanda Seyfried is set to make her Saturday Night Live hosting debut on December 5, and the timing is hard to miss. The announcement came within days of a public back-and-forth with Billy Evans, the partner of Theranos founder [Elizabeth Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holmes_(writer)), over the documentary *You Can See Everything*.

## Amanda Seyfried's SNL hosting debut

According to [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/culture/tv-film/snl-amanda-seyfried-don-toliver-december-2026-1236382445/), the December 5 episode will be a first for two people: Seyfried as host and Toliver as musical guest. The show announced it live during its November 14 episode, which John Mulaney hosted with [Phoebe Bridgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoebe_Bridgers) as musical guest.

The series is now taking a three-week break. [TheWrap](https://www.thewrap.com/creative-content/tv-shows/snl-amanda-seyfried-host-don-toliver-musical-guest/) reports that Seyfried will be the fourth first-time host of Season 52, after Jalen Brunson, [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) and Druski. Hosts and musical guests for the December 12 and December 19 shows have not been announced.

Season 52 began on September 26. Billboard lists [Dakota Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dakota_Johnson) and [Shane Gillis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Gillis) among the hosts so far this season.

## A busy run for Seyfried

The SNL date caps a crowded few weeks. The A.V. Club notes that Seyfried voices Cinderella in the animated film *Steps*, which streams on Netflix from November 20. She also appears in *You Can See Everything*, the documentary from [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and Lance Oppenheim, which opened in cinemas on October 16.

In the film, Seyfried plays Holmes again in re-enacted scenes, a role she first took in the series *The Dropout*, which earned her an Emmy, according to Variety. She also appears as herself. Variety reports that distributor A24 plans to campaign her in the supporting actress category at the 99th Academy Awards.

## What Billy Evans said

Evans gave his first broadcast network interview to CBS News' Matt Gutman, which aired on Sunday. [Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/billy-evans-you-can-see-everything-tv-interview-amanda-seyfried/) summarised the highlights.

Evans said he was "really glad" Seyfried was included because it shows the gap between the real Holmes and "how eager she was to play a character". Asked whether she played Holmes well, he answered: "I'm glad Nathan was there to help her."

Gutman then put it to him that he did not seem to hold a high opinion of her acting or of her as a person. Evans declined to go further. He said he would try not to speak poorly of others right now, because "a lot of people" had done that about him recently and he knew how it felt.

Evans also discussed a shower scene in the film. He said he knew he was being filmed but did not expect his whole body to be shown, and that he had asked the filmmakers to pixelate it. Gold Derby reports he said they refused. The filmmakers' side of that account was not in the sources reviewed here.

## Seyfried's own account

Variety reports that Seyfried recently told Rolling Stone she had not told her agents about the project and had "no approval" over filming. She also said her opinion of Evans "only got worse" during production and that she felt he "disrespected" her during the tense dinner conversation in the film.

TheWrap describes that argument as the centre of one of the film's most discussed scenes. It adds that SNL has already touched on the documentary: the November 7 episode, hosted by Druski, ran a pre-taped sketch imagining Holmes and Evans asking him to make their film instead.

## What happens next

Seyfried takes the stage at Studio 8H on December 5, after the show's three-week break. Whether the documentary makes it into her monologue or sketches is unconfirmed. Before that, *Steps* lands on Netflix on November 20, and awards campaigning for *You Can See Everything* continues.
