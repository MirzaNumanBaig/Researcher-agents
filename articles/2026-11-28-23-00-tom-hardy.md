**Quick summary**

- ScreenRant reports MobLand is the #2 most-watched series on Paramount+ worldwide heading into the holiday weekend.
- Season 2 ended on Friday, 20 November, and the show has already been renewed for a third season, according to ScreenRant.
- Good Housekeeping is pointing viewers to Locke, the 2014 one-car drama that stars [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy).
- Comic Book Resources says the 2018 film Venom will stream free on Tubi from 29 December 2026.

[Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) has three very different projects drawing viewers this weekend. His gangster series MobLand is climbing Paramount+ charts after its season 2 finale, an older psychological drama is being rediscovered, and his first Venom film is about to become free to watch.

## Why Tom Hardy MobLand is the weekend binge

According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-2-gangster-thriller-perfect-weekend-binge/), MobLand ranks second among the most-watched series on Paramount+ worldwide and has stayed popular across the whole of autumn 2026. The outlet counts 20 episodes across two seasons, which makes a full catch-up realistic over a long weekend.

Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, the fixer and enforcer for the Harrigans, a feared London crime family. Pierce Brosnan plays Conrad Harrigan, Helen Mirren plays his wife Maeve, and Paddy Considine plays eldest son Kevin. ScreenRant says Harry spends the series trying to keep order inside the family while carrying out its orders.

Season 2 added Johnny Flynn as Frankie Cooper, a rival to the Harrigans. ScreenRant says the clashes between Harry and Frankie drive much of the action. It gave the season 9 out of 10 and described it as an improvement on the first run.

The same piece says the show has been renewed for a third season. Our earlier coverage looked at [Tom Hardy back on the MobLand set for season 3 filming](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-2/).

## Locke: the quiet drama that is getting new attention

Good Housekeeping UK has called [Locke](https://www.goodhousekeeping.com/uk/lifestyle/a74549195/locke-tom-hardy/) as good as MobLand, even though the two could hardly be more different. The film was written by [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), who also wrote Peaky Blinders, the series that gave Hardy the role of [Alfie Solomons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfie_Solomons). It reached cinemas in 2014.

Hardy plays Ivan Locke, a Welsh construction worker driving south on the M1 at night. He is the only person on screen for the entire 82 minutes, and he never leaves his car. The rest of the cast is heard only through his hands-free phone calls.

Those calls carry the plot. Ivan is rushing to be at the birth of a child he conceived in a one-night stand with Bethan, voiced by [Olivia Colman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Colman). He has also walked away from a major concrete pour, leaving his deputy, voiced by [Andrew Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Scott_(actor)), to cope. His wife, played by [Ruth Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Wilson), and his two sons are waiting for him at home.

Good Housekeeping also suggests two other Hardy dramas: Capone, which it says is on Netflix, and Taboo, which it lists on BBC iPlayer and Netflix. We covered the first in [Tom Hardy's Capone on Netflix while MobLand season 3 films](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-capone-netflix/).

## Venom heads to free streaming

The third item is a date for the diary. Comic Book Resources reports that [Venom](https://www.cbr.com/venom-marvel-superhero-streaming-tubi-december-2026/), the 2018 film in which Hardy plays journalist Eddie Brock, will stream on Tubi from 29 December 2026. Tubi is free and needs no subscription or sign-in, though films carry ad breaks.

The outlet says the film earned upwards of US$800 million worldwide and led to two sequels, Venom: Let There Be Carnage and Venom: The Last Dance. It is currently on Disney+ and Hulu, alongside the other two films.

CBR also notes that Sony is working on an animated Venom film with Hardy as a producer, but it is unclear whether that connects to the earlier movies. The same article describes a return in Avengers: Secret Wars in 2027 as a possibility. That is speculation, not a confirmed casting.

## What happens next

Streaming dates are the firmest news here. Venom reaches Tubi on 29 December, and MobLand's first two seasons are available on Paramount+ now. A third season is on the way, though ScreenRant gives no release date.
