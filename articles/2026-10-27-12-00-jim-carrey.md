Jim Carrey has quietly married Min Ah. The 64-year-old actor and his longtime girlfriend wed in a small, private ceremony in Los Angeles, and a representative for Carrey confirmed the news, [LEADERSHIP Newspapers reports](https://leadership.ng/hollywood-star-jim-carrey-marries-longtime-girlfriend-min-ah-after-vowing-never-to-wed-again/). The wedding was first reported by TMZ on 30 September 2026, according to a [syndicated US news report](https://abcnews4.com/news/entertainment/jim-carrey-marries-girlfriend-min-ah-in-private-ceremony-report). Neither report gives the exact wedding date.

## A relationship kept out of view

Carrey and Min Ah were seen together as far back as 2022, but they said little in public about the relationship. That changed in February 2026, when Carrey received an honorary award at the César Awards in Paris. LEADERSHIP Newspapers reports that he acknowledged Min Ah during his acceptance speech, and the US report says he called her his "sublime companion" and told her he loved her.

That appearance was effectively the couple's public debut, roughly seven months before the wedding was reported.

## Why the news surprised fans

The marriage reverses a position Carrey took more than once. In a 2008 interview, while he was dating [Jenny McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jenny_McCarthy), he said that after two marriages "you really should stop doing it". Six years later, on Howard Stern's radio show, he said he did not see marriage as necessary "at this point". He also admitted he could not know how he would feel if he met someone who "knocks my head off".

Both comments are quoted in the LEADERSHIP Newspapers report. It describes the wedding as a notable change of heart, and the US report likewise calls the remarriage surprising given what he had said.

## His earlier marriages

Carrey was married to Melissa Womer from 1987 to 1995, and the two have a daughter, Jane. In 1996 he married his *Dumb and Dumber* co-star [Lauren Holly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Holly). They divorced the following year. The wedding to Min Ah is his third marriage.
