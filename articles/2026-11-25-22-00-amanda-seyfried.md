**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) appeared on *The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon* on 25 November 2026 to promote *You Can See Everything*.
- She described a dinner at the home of Elizabeth Holmes's partner, Billy Evans, as feeling like "The Twilight Zone", according to HuffPost UK.
- The film, made with Nathan Fielder, opened in UK cinemas over the past weekend, about a month after its US debut.
- Seyfried won an Emmy for playing Holmes in *The Dropout* and says she had not planned to return to the role.

Amanda Seyfried has opened up about the most unsettling day of making *You Can See Everything*, the Nathan Fielder film that has become one of the year's most talked-about releases. Speaking to Jimmy Fallon on 25 November, she said a dinner at Billy Evans's home left her wondering whether she had gone too far, [HuffPost UK reports](https://www.huffingtonpost.co.uk/entry/amanda-seyfried-filming-you-can-see-everything_uk_6b06a195e4b03d22836102d8).

## What Seyfried said about the dinner

The scene is one of the standout sequences in a film that runs for roughly three hours. Seyfried told Fallon the experience felt like "The Twilight Zone". She pictured herself in Evans's house with Holmes's children, Fielder, a crew member named Lance and the cameras, and asked herself whether she had stepped over a line.

She explained that Fielder's team already had a rapport with Evans, because they had lived with the family two years earlier. Seyfried said she went into the evening hoping to learn something or make a connection. According to HuffPost UK, Holmes then phoned from prison during the evening. Seyfried said she has still not fully processed what happened and called the whole thing "meta".

The same report says the dinner turns into a heated exchange between Seyfried and Evans. The topics include *The Dropout*, how he managed Holmes's public image while she was in prison, and US politics.

## Why she came back to Elizabeth Holmes

Seyfried won an Emmy for playing Holmes in the miniseries *The Dropout*. On Fallon's show she said she never intended to play the Theranos founder again. That changed when Fielder, the comedian and filmmaker, approached her about a documentary featuring Holmes.

She agreed to appear in the film and to play Holmes in recreated scenes. Those recreations have already attracted Oscar buzz ahead of the awards season, HuffPost UK says. Christopher Abbott, her *The Testaments of Ann Lee* co-star, plays Evans in other recreated scenes.
