**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) danced a cha cha to "Let's Get Loud" on Saturday 7 November and scored 31 out of 40.
- She sits third on the leaderboard, and many viewers say the marks were too low given her fractured rib.
- She missed a live show last month because of the injury and returned two weeks ago with a rumba.
- On Sunday she shared a "home" video with her husband, Matthew Copley, ahead of her next performance.

Delta Goodrem has kept her place near the top of this year's [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) leaderboard, scoring 31 for a cha cha on Saturday 7 November while still recovering from a fractured rib. Viewers quickly argued online that the judges had undermarked her, according to the [Manchester Evening News](https://www.manchestereveningnews.co.uk/news/tv/strictly-come-dancing-fans-rush-34880296.amp).

## What happened in week seven

The Australian singer and her professional partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) danced the cha cha to Jennifer Lopez's "Let's Get Loud". The couple took 31 points from a possible 40, and judge [Motsi Mabuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motsi_Mabuse) told her that her "heart is on the dancefloor", adding that she loved the "spice" Goodrem brings to the contest.

Week seven was played with nine of the expected 10 couples. Actress [Jaime Winstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaime_Winstone) and her partner [Carlos Gu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_Gu) sat out the show after Winstone tore an abdominal muscle. The Manchester Evening News reports they receive a bye to next week, when she is expected to return.

Elsewhere on the night, presenter [Will Best](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Best) and [Dianne Buswell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dianne_Buswell) opened the show with a quickstep to Maroon 5 and earned 29.

## Where Goodrem stands

Goodrem's 31 puts her third. Ahead of her are [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) with [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin), and Olympian Tabby Stoecker with [Mark Karmalita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Karmalita). A third-place result is solid in a field this size, but the reaction on social media suggests viewers expected more.

Several posts quoted by the Manchester Evening News pointed to her injury. One viewer called the routine "diabolically undermarked", another said she looked to be in "considerable pain" afterwards, and a third praised the speed and energy of the dance given that she is still recovering. These are viewer opinions, not a verdict from the judges, and the scores stand as given.

## The injury behind the headlines

Nine.com.au reports that the 41-year-old fractured a rib last month while competing on the BBC One series. In October she shared previously unseen footage from hospital visits and MRI appointments taken just after the injury. In one clip she said she felt "very, very gutted" and hoped to be back the following week, according to [Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/strictly-come-dancing-delta-goodrem-at-home-with-husband-matthew-copley-after-rib-injury-update-20261108-p61cdv.html).

She missed that week's live show and, per Nine, made her return two weeks ago with a rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back to Me Now". During the recovery she took her dance lessons with Widdrington over FaceTime, while her husband stood in during practice until she could rejoin the studio.

Widdrington has spoken about her effort. "This girl puts her heart on that dance floor and she works so, so hard," he said, as quoted by Nine.

## A quiet night at home

On Sunday Goodrem posted a short video to her [Instagram stories](https://www.instagram.com/deltagoodrem/), captioned simply "home". It shows her with Matthew Copley over a home-cooked steak dinner. "Here, with my beautiful hubby," she says in the clip. The couple are living in the UK while she competes.

The post gives the clearest sign yet that she is back to normal routine, though neither report says she is fully healed. Nine describes her as appearing to have made a full recovery, and the dance floor evidence from Saturday points the same way.

## What happens next

Goodrem and Widdrington are due back on the dance floor next week, and Winstone is hoped to return then too. Whether the judges' marks catch up with the audience's view of her recovery is the question to watch.
