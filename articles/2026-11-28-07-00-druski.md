**Quick summary**

- *Collect & Praise*, [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski)'s first Netflix series, launches on December 1, according to PCMag's weekly Netflix guide.
- It is based on his YouTube character, a charismatic megachurch pastor, and co-stars [Mike Epps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Epps) as his deacon.
- The show follows his recent hosting turn on Saturday Night Live.
- Mashable also picked his Hot Ones appearance as one of the week's best internet interviews.

Druski's first Netflix series is now days away, and this week's streaming guides have put it near the top of the list. PCMag's roundup of what is new on Netflix for the week of November 27 highlights *Collect & Praise*, which arrives on December 1 and comes straight off his hosting job on Saturday Night Live.

## What Collect & Praise is about

According to [PCMag](https://www.pcmag.com/news/whats-new-to-watch-on-netflix-streaming-this-week-nov-27-2026), the comedy grows out of the megachurch pastor character Druski built in his YouTube sketches. Mike Epps plays the pastor's deacon, and the two spend the season trying to keep a lid on a string of scandals while the money keeps rolling in.

The cast list in the same guide names Drew "Druski" Desbordes, Hasani Vibez Comer and Epps. The series is rated TV-MA and is classed as a comedy.

PCMag's pitch is blunt: it calls the show a likely winner and tells fans of *The Righteous Gemstones* to take a look. That is one outlet's view, not a verdict from viewers, and the series has not yet streamed.

## Why the timing matters

Orlando Weekly's streaming column also flags "Druski's new show" in its headline, alongside the Prime Video series *Blade Runner 2099* and a Tupac interview podcast. A new streaming title rarely gets that billing without a built-in audience, and Druski has one. His sketches already draw large numbers online, and the SNL hosting gig put him in front of a broader television crowd just before launch.

The pastor character is the same one that powers the series, so the Netflix version is not a new invention but a longer run of something fans already know.

## The Hot Ones link

[Mashable](https://mashable.com/entertainment/best-podcasts-druski-hot-ones-las-culturistas-ariana-grande-moments-nov) listed Druski's *Hot Ones* episode among its five favourite internet interview series of the week, next to an [Ariana Grande](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariana_Grande) episode of *Las Culturistas*. In it, Mashable says, he sat down with host Sean Evans for the hot wing challenge and talked about his career, from early content creation to the Netflix series and working with [Emma Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Stone). We covered that interview in more detail in [Druski Takes On Hot Ones Days Before Netflix Debut](https://moduscarry.com/druski-hot-ones/).

Mashable describes Druski as an alumnus of its Mashable 101 list of influential creators, which gives a sense of how far his online profile had already travelled before Netflix came calling.

## What happens next

The series lands on December 1, when all of season one is listed in Netflix's new-series schedule for that day. Netflix has not, in the sources reviewed here, said how many episodes there are.
