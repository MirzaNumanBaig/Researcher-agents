**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) space adventure Project Hail Mary finished its original run with about $685 million worldwide, according to figures cited by Koimoi.
- That leaves the film roughly $15 million short of the $700 million mark.
- It returns to cinemas on 11 December in all formats, including IMAX and 70mm.
- The film cost an estimated $200 million to make and opened to more than $80.5 million in North America.

Project Hail Mary has one last chance to reach a major box office milestone. Citing Box Office Mojo, [Koimoi reports](https://www.koimoi.com/box-office/project-hail-mary-box-office-how-much-does-ryan-goslings-film-need-from-its-december-re-release-to-cross-700m-worldwide/) that the Ryan Gosling film closed its first run at about $685 million worldwide, and a re-release on 11 December gives it a realistic path to $700 million.

## Where the Project Hail Mary box office stands

The numbers are split almost evenly between home and abroad. Koimoi lists the domestic total at $344 million and the international total at $341 million, which together make about $685 million worldwide.

Against a reported production budget of $200 million, that is a gross of more than three times the cost. The opening weekend was one of the biggest of the year in North America, with more than $80.5 million, according to the same report.

The outlet says only about $15 million separates the film from $700 million. For a title that has already finished its main theatrical run, that is a small gap.

## Why the December re-release matters

The film returns to screens on 11 December across all formats, including IMAX and 70mm. Premium large-format screens tend to draw fans who want to see a spectacle-heavy film again, and Koimoi reads the release as giving the film a solid chance of passing the line.

That is the outlet's assessment, not a confirmed forecast. Re-release totals are hard to predict, and nothing in the report suggests how many screens will carry the film. We have covered the plans in more detail in our posts on the [IMAX 70mm return](https://moduscarry.com/project-hail-mary-imax-return/) and the [11 December re-release](https://moduscarry.com/project-hail-mary-rerelease/).

## What the film is about and how it was received

Project Hail Mary is based on Andy Weir's [2021 novel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary). Gosling plays a science teacher who wakes alone on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there. As his memories return, he learns he is on a mission to stop a mysterious substance from killing Earth's sun, and an unexpected friendship may hold the key to saving humanity.

Koimoi says the film was directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller. It also reports strong scores from critics and audiences on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). Critics called it a visually dazzling space odyssey carried by Gosling at his most winning, while audience reviews described it as "lightning in a spaceship", according to the report.

The film is also available to stream on Amazon Prime Video, so the re-release is aimed at viewers who want the big-screen version.

The next date to watch is 11 December. Weekend returns from the re-release will show quickly whether the extra $15 million is within reach.
