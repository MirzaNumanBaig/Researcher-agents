**Quick summary**

- [Isla Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isla_Fisher), [Bob Odenkirk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Odenkirk) and [Zosia Mamet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zosia_Mamet) have been added to the Tribeca Festival Lisboa 2026 line-up.
- The festival runs from 9 to 13 December, with screenings at Cinema São Jorge from 10 to 13 December.
- Six more titles join the Tribeca Film by Toyota programme.
- Single tickets cost €12 until 23 November, according to Señal News.

Isla Fisher is one of three international names added to the guest list for Tribeca Festival Lisboa 2026, which takes place in the Portuguese capital from 9 to 13 December. Señal News reported the additions on 18 November, alongside six new films for the festival's programme.

## Who is joining the guest list

Fisher arrives with Odenkirk, known for [Breaking Bad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad) and [Better Call Saul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul), and Mamet. They join guests announced earlier: [Alec Baldwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alec_Baldwin), [Edward Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Burns), [Zach Woods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zach_Woods) and [W. Kamau Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._Kamau_Bell).

The report does not say what Fisher will do at the festival, such as a screening, talk or panel. Only Odenkirk's appearance is described in detail. Those details are likely to come as the schedule fills out.

## Odenkirk's screening

Odenkirk will attend a special screening of "Bob and David Climb Machu Picchu", directed by Michael LaHaie. It features his longtime comedy partner [David Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Cross). A conversation with the audience follows the film, according to Señal News.

## The six new films

The festival has added six titles to its Tribeca Film by Toyota programme:

- "In Memoriam", a dramatic comedy from Rob Burnett starring [Marc Maron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Maron), [Michael McKean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_McKean) and [Lily Gladstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_Gladstone).
- "Solos", by Sebastião Salgado.
- "Dias Livres", directed by Lucila Mariani.
- "The Last Day", Rachel Rose's feature debut, starring [Alicia Vikander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alicia_Vikander), [Victoria Pedretti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_Pedretti) and [Wagner Moura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner_Moura).
- "Born Blind", a Portuguese short film by Ângelo Rodrigues.

"The Last Day" is described as a contemporary reimagining of Virginia Woolf's "Mrs Dalloway". "Born Blind" looks at a community in the Mauritanian desert where many children are born blind. The festival says the mix of international and Portuguese work reflects its interest in different perspectives and emerging stories.

## Tickets and organisers

Individual screening tickets are €12 until 23 November. Cinema Packs give access to three films for €40 or six for €60, sold through BOL. The festival is co-organised by Impresa, [Tribeca Enterprises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tribeca_Enterprises) and Lisboa Cultura, with support from the [Lisbon City Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisbon_Municipal_Chamber), MEO, Toyota, Turismo de Lisboa and Turismo de Portugal.
