Alexander Skarsgård has lent his voice to a new erotic audio story, with the app Quinn announcing on Tuesday that he narrates *Felled*, a fan-fiction piece inspired by his film *Wicker*. The release follows a month of heavy attention on the film, and gives its audience a very different way to stay in that world.

## What is Felled?

According to [Mashable](https://mashable.com/life/wicker-alexander-skarsgard-audio-erotica-fan-fiction), *Felled* is an erotic romance written by Emma Parkinson. It was picked as the winner of a fan-fiction contest that Quinn launched ahead of *Wicker*'s October release, asking for original erotic stories set in the film's world.

The story follows a willow tree that forms a bond with a farmer's daughter across the seasons. Mashable says that when an axe falls, their connection looks severed for good, until the pair are reunited years later. Parkinson's tale takes its starting point from "The Wicker Husband" by Ursula Wills Jones, the short story the film itself is based on.

Queerty's weekly Hot Sheet describes the project in similar terms, calling it an original fan-fiction story about a young woman who falls for a tree, and notes that Skarsgård is the latest star to work with the app.

## Who else has recorded for Quinn?

Quinn Originals launched in 2022, and Mashable lists a long run of actors who have read stories for it. They include Andrew Scott, Hudson Williams and Connor Storrie of *Heated Rivalry*, Manny Jacinto, Victoria Pedretti, Matt Smith, Lili Reinhart, Jesse Williams and Jamie Campbell Bower.

That puts Skarsgård in a catalogue built around recognisable voices. For the app, a lead actor from a current film is a natural fit for a contest tied to that film's release.

## What Skarsgård said

In a statement quoted by Mashable, Skarsgård praised the contest idea. "It was quite refreshing to have something sensual and erotic that wasn't androcentric for a change!" he said. The comment points to the app's push for stories that move away from male-centred fantasies, though the sources do not say more about how he approached the recording.

Neither outlet reports a price, a running time or a release window beyond the announcement, so listeners will need to check the Quinn app for those details.
