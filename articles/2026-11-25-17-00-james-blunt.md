**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) says he was "very touched" after Romesh Ranganathan donated to his chosen charity.
- The charity is the Blue Marine Foundation, according to Blunt's appearance on the Dish from Waitrose podcast.
- Romesh and Sebastian "Bash" Croft won *Celebrity Traitors* and split an £83,000 prize.
- Blunt joked that he texted Romesh "I know where you live" after the final.

[James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) has a gentle update on his fallout with [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan). The singer says the comedian made a donation to his chosen charity after voting him out of [*Celebrity Traitors*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors), and that it left him "very touched". Blunt shared the story on the Dish from Waitrose podcast this week, according to [Geo News](https://www.geo.tv/latest/692546-james-blunt) and the [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16315429/James-Blunt-Celebrity-Traitors-winner-Romesh-Ranganathan-charity.html).

## What James Blunt said about the donation

Blunt was talking to hosts [Nick Grimshaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Grimshaw) and [Angela Hartnett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angela_Hartnett) when he explained that Romesh gave money to the [Blue Marine Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Marine_Foundation), the marine conservation organisation he had named as his charity of choice.

The singer played down any talk of bad feeling. He said he had to get Romesh's number first, because the comedian "wasn't giving it" to him. Then, by his own account, he sent a text saying: "I know where you live."

He also told Romesh he could not buy his way out of it. "But he did, yeah, which is a very, very touching, touching moment," Blunt said, according to Geo News.

The Daily Mail describes the donation as an apology for the banishment. Blunt's own quoted words, as reported by both outlets, do not use that word, so it is best read as the paper's framing.

## How the Celebrity Traitors finale played out

Blunt came close to sharing in the prize money. Both outlets report that he was undone when Romesh questioned his Faithful status and he was eliminated.

Romesh and actor and musician Sebastian "Bash" Croft were the ones who came out on top. They were crowned champions of the BBC show and divided the £83,000 prize pot between them. For scale, that is £41,500 each if the pot was split evenly, though the sources do not give the exact split.

The donation gives the story a tidy ending. A show built on betrayal produced a gift to a marine charity rather than a lasting grudge, at least as Blunt tells it.
