**Quick summary**

- India Today reviewed the eight-episode series Yaga on 13 November 2026 and called it a seductive, unsettling mystery.
- [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) plays two roles: missing heir Henry Park and his lookalike roommate Logan.
- Logan was created for the television adaptation, and the reviewer says Williams separates the two through prosthetics, physicality and voice.
- [Carrie-Anne Moss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie-Anne_Moss) co-stars as a university professor at the centre of town gossip.

Hudson Williams is being praised for a double act in Yaga, a new mystery series in which he plays both a missing heir and the awkward lookalike who lives with him. In a review published on 13 November, [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/yaga-review-carrie-anne-moss-hudson-williams-seductive-mystery-3038075-2026-11-13) says the performance shows his range and sits far from the warm image fans know from [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).

## What Yaga is about

The series is set in the coastal town of Whittock. According to India Today, private investigator Charlie Rapp, played by [Noah Reid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Reid), is hired to find Henry Park, heir to a wealthy fishing family. He teams up with local detective Carson, played by [Clark Backo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_Backo).

What looks like a simple missing-person case then opens into secrets, sexual politics and hints of the supernatural. The reviewer describes the town itself as almost a character, with residents who all seem to know something they will not share.

## Hudson Williams in two roles

The review singles out Williams for playing Henry and Logan, the roommate who looks like him. Logan is a character written specifically for the screen version, so the actor had room to build him from scratch.

India Today describes Henry as confident and used to getting his way, while Logan is awkward, guarded and hard to read. Williams reportedly marks the difference with prosthetics for Logan and a distinct voice for Henry, and the reviewer says the contrast helps sustain the mystery around the two men.

That is a notable change of pace for an actor whose recent profile has been built on a more likeable lead role. You can follow more of his work on our [Hudson Williams page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hudson-williams/).

## Carrie-Anne Moss and the Baba Yaga angle

The trail leads to Katherine Hargrove, a university professor whose interest in younger men has made her a subject of local talk. The review says Moss gives her an effortless allure and keeps her difficult to read.

The series also draws on the Slavic folklore figure Baba Yaga. India Today says it uses the legend to ask why women who refuse to conform are so often labelled dangerous, and leaves open whether the magic is real or a way for characters to explain their fears and desires.

## Format and pacing

Episodes run about 30 minutes each. The reviewer says that keeps the pace brisk, with each instalment adding a clue or complicating a relationship. One criticism: the show sometimes seems more interested in atmosphere than in tying off every thread, and some character shifts feel convenient.
