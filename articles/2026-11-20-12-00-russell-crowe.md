**Quick summary**

- A Russell Crowe Unabomber documentary tie-in is coming: National Geographic has greenlit *My Brother The Unabomber*, according to Deadline as reported by ComingSoon.net.
- It will stream on Disney+, Hulu and National Geographic, with no release date announced yet.
- It follows Netflix's drama *Unabomber*, which stars [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe), and tells the case through the eyes of Ted Kaczynski's brother, David.
- Daniel Vernon will direct for David Abraham's Wonderhood Studios.

After the Russell Crowe Unabomber drama on Netflix comes a documentary on the same case, told from the family's side. National Geographic has ordered *My Brother The Unabomber*, a documentary that follows the Netflix drama starring Crowe, according to a [ComingSoon.net report](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2227946-new-unabomber-movie-announced-disney-russell-crowe-netflix-hit) that cites Deadline.

## Who is making the documentary

The film comes from Wonderhood Studios, the company run by David Abraham. Daniel Vernon is set to direct. Jessie Versluys, Samantha Anstiss and Carolyn Payne are named as executive producers.

It is headed for Disney+, Hulu and National Geographic. ComingSoon.net says no premiere date has been announced.

## A different view of the case

Where the Netflix film follows Ted Kaczynski himself, the documentary centres on his younger brother, David Kaczynski. During the last year of the manhunt, David and his wife, Linda, had to decide whether to tell investigators about their suspicions of a family member.

According to the report, the film will look at how the couple reached that decision after reading the manifesto when it appeared in a newspaper. Their concerns led them to contact the FBI, which helped investigators identify the person behind the mail bombings.

The project reportedly has access to archival material that has not been seen before, including letters exchanged between the brothers. David has told his story before. In 2016 he wrote a piece titled "My Brother, The Unabomber" for Psychology Today, covering his wife's first suspicions, his brother's early life and the family's decision to go to the FBI.

## How it sits beside the Netflix drama

The Netflix film, simply titled *Unabomber*, was directed by [Janus Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janus_Metz_Pedersen) and stars Crowe, Jacob Tremblay and [Shailene Woodley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shailene_Woodley). ComingSoon.net describes it as following Kaczynski's path from a young Harvard prodigy to the man behind a series of deadly mail bombings. It is a different kind of project from the documentary: a scripted drama about the bomber against a factual account of the family's choice.

ComingSoon.net calls the drama a success, but the critical record is more mixed. According to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unabomber_(film)), which lists its release as 25 September 2026, it holds a 25 per cent rating on Rotten Tomatoes and a Metacritic score of 46 out of 100 from 18 critics. No budget or box office figures have been published. Reviewers praised the acting more than the film's argument, and The Hollywood Reporter's Richard Lawson was critical of the lead performances.

## What happens next

No release date has been set for *My Brother The Unabomber*. Until National Geographic confirms one, the main thing to watch is how the documentary uses the brothers' letters and whether it adds to what David Kaczynski has already said in public.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is My Brother The Unabomber?

It is a National Geographic documentary about David Kaczynski and his wife Linda, and their decision to tell the FBI about their suspicions of his brother Ted.

### Does Russell Crowe appear in the documentary?

The report does not say so. Crowe stars in the separate Netflix drama *Unabomber*, which the documentary follows.

### Where will the documentary stream?

It will premiere on Disney+, Hulu and National Geographic. A release date has not been announced.

### Who is directing it?

Daniel Vernon is directing for Wonderhood Studios.
