**Quick summary**

- [Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried) will host Saturday Night Live for the first time, according to CinemaBlend.
- The booking follows her role in the Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder documentary You Can See Everything.
- Gold Derby lists her as a Best Supporting Actress contender for that film.
- Her Netflix animated film Steps has a 71% Rotten Tomatoes score and 53 on Metacritic.
- The Cut says she wore a black gown and coat to Tiffany and Co.'s holiday event.

Amanda Seyfried SNL plans are official: she is set to host Saturday Night Live for the first time, a milestone that arrives after more than 20 years on screen. CinemaBlend reported on 20 November that the booking came over the weekend, and that Seyfried marked it with a post asking fans and friends to "send cake".

## Why Seyfried is hosting now

CinemaBlend's Jessica Rawden points to You Can See Everything as the reason. The documentary, from Lance Oppenheim and Nathan Fielder, includes a twist that involves Seyfried, and Rawden calls it her favourite film of the year so far.

The timing is notable. Seyfried has been in Mean Girls, Mamma Mia!, Les Misérables, Big Love and The Dropout, and CinemaBlend notes that none of those led to a hosting slot. A documentary did.

She is also known for her love of cake. CinemaBlend says she turned 40 during the promotional tour for The Housemaid, which produced several viral cake moments, so the request fits.

## The Oscar conversation

The hosting news lands in the middle of an awards push. Gold Derby calls Seyfried a Best Supporting Actress Oscar contender for You Can See Everything. In a separate column on Robert Pattinson's casting as the next James Bond, the site said Seyfried had "earned her license to campaign" for the documentary.

That column was mostly about Amazon MGM's announcement that Pattinson will lead Denis Villeneuve's Bond 26, due in 2028. Gold Derby argued the news changed the awards conversation for Pattinson's other films. Seyfried's own link to the story was lighter. We covered her take on the franchise in [Amanda Seyfried Jokes She Is 'Too Old' to Be a Bond Girl](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-bond-girl/).

## Steps gets mixed reviews

While one project builds momentum, another is getting a cooler reception. Steps, which began streaming on Netflix on Friday, casts Seyfried as the voice of Cinderella. Ali Wong voices the stepsister Lilith, Stephanie Hsu voices Margot and Bette Midler is the Fairy Godmother, according to Gold Derby.

The film holds 71% on Rotten Tomatoes and 53 on Metacritic. Gold Derby says critics praised the voice work but called the story "ham-handed" and "underdeveloped". The New York Times' Maya Phillips wrote that Seyfried and Wong "convey real feeling", while The Hollywood Reporter's David Rooney found its handling of cross-cultural themes "too tentative".

Netflix is campaigning for the film, and its song "Someone to Hold", performed by Jennie, was nominated at the Hollywood Music in Media Awards this week. For more on her singing, see our earlier report on [Seyfried singing Cinderella in Steps](https://moduscarry.com/amanda-seyfried-steps-netflix-songs/).

## A busy week on the carpet

Seyfried was also seen in New York. The Cut included her in its roundup of the month's best looks, saying she brought "old Hollywood glamour" to Tiffany and Co.'s holiday event in a black gown and coat. The same roundup featured [Anok Yai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anok_Yai) and [Halle Bailey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_Bailey).
