**Quick summary**

- Director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) has explained to GQ why he chose [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond).
- His first test was whether he could believe the actor as a killer, with "anger deep in the eye".
- He also wants charisma, dark humour and physical strength in 007.
- Pattinson succeeds [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig), and filming is reportedly due to start in 2027.

Denis Villeneuve has given his clearest account yet of why Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Speaking to GQ, the Dune director said he cast an actor he could believe as an assassin first and a spy second, according to a report by [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/entertainment/english/hollywood/news/dune-director-denis-villeneuve-reveals-why-robert-pattinson-beat-callum-turner-jack-lowden-to-bag-james-bond-role/articleshow/136043801.cms).

## What Villeneuve says he was looking for

The director's starting point was blunt. "The first quality is that I need to believe that this man is a killer, because more than a spy, Bond is an assassin," he told GQ. He added that he needs "that kind of anger deep in the eye", and that "it's not everybody who has that."

He then described the rest of the profile. Villeneuve wants someone with "unapologetic panache", a person around whom a room starts to gravitate. He spoke of sensuality, dark humour and a man who is "not afraid to put his hand in the mud". Physically, he said, Bond must be strong and muscular, with a "deep intelligence in the eyes", someone who always seems a step ahead of everyone else.

It is a more physical and darker brief than the polished spy of earlier eras, and it fits the way Villeneuve has framed the character since the announcement.

## A divisive choice, and he knows it

Villeneuve did not pretend the decision would please everyone. "No matter who I choose, some people will be excited and others will be disappointed," he said.

The Times of India says Pattinson beat several frontrunners to the part. Its headline names [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) as rivals, but the text of the report does not detail how the shortlist was decided, and we have not confirmed that from a second source.

At the time of the announcement, Villeneuve said he was "overjoyed" to welcome Pattinson, adding that the actor "has the timeless soul of 007" and a spirit that is "completely new". The same outlet reports that Pattinson succeeds Daniel Craig and will reportedly begin filming in 2027.

## What Pattinson has on his plate

Bond is not his only job. Brief data for this batch lists *The Take*, directed by Fernando Meirelles, with a release date of 15 January 2027, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Take_(2027_film)).
