**Quick summary**

- [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) won The Icon Award at the ZYN Rolling Stone UK Awards 2026, the final prize of the night.
- Asked about Glastonbury 2027, the 52-year-old said "we'll see", pointing to his summer tour.
- He has played the Pyramid Stage twice, in 2005 and 2008, and called Glastonbury his favourite festival.
- The award follows his UK number one album, Airplane Mode.

James Blunt left the Rolling Stone UK Awards in London on Thursday with The Icon Award, and with a typically dry answer about whether he will return to Glastonbury next year. The singer, 52, told reporters that his summer touring schedule means "we'll see".

## Blunt takes the night's final award

According to [Rolling Stone UK](https://www.rollingstone.co.uk/music/james-blunt-the-icon-award-zyn-rolling-stone-uk-awards-2026-winner-66195/), The Icon Award, supported by Visit West Hollywood, was the last prize handed out at the ceremony. The outlet described Blunt as a Celebrity Traitors star and "national treasure", and said he received the trophy from his fellow Faithful Bash Croft.

His acceptance speech leaned hard on self-mockery. Blunt joked that he was probably the only person in the room to have committed "crimes against humanity" after leaving the military, and said he knew he was not an icon but "just an internet meme at this stage". He then ran through his career in a few lines: the dream of being a rock star, the one-hit-wonder label, a stretch as what he called Queen of Twitter, and a second-place finish on Traitors.

He also had a message for younger musicians. Stick around long enough, he said, and someone will eventually hand you a prize.

## What he said about Glastonbury 2027

Speaking to The Press Association at the ceremony, as reported by the [Lancashire Telegraph](https://www.lancashiretelegraph.co.uk/leisure/national/26746792.james-blunt-playing-glastonbury-im-tour-summer-see/), Blunt was asked whether he might play Worthy Farm in 2027. He kept the door open without promising anything.

"It's been my favourite festival ever to play, and I've deeply loved it," he said. He counted twice on the Pyramid Stage and once on the New Band stage, adding: "I am on tour in the summer, so you know we'll see."

The record backs up the affection. Blunt played the Pyramid Stage in 2005 and 2008, and appeared on the New Stage in 2004, before his breakthrough album [Back To Bedlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_Bedlam) came out.

The 2027 line-up is still taking shape. This week [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles), [Billie Eilish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Eilish), [Sam Fender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Fender) and Cher were named as the main headliners. Other confirmed acts include [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue), [Lily Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_Allen), [Sir Rod Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rod_Stewart) and Sombr. More names are still to be announced.

## The rest of the Rolling Stone UK Awards

Danny Dyer hosted the ceremony at London's Camden Roundhouse. Among the other winners, according to the two outlets:

- Giggs received The Trailblazer Award, presented by The Streets' Mike Skinner.
- Rachel Chinouriri won The Artist Award.
- Wolf Alice took The Group Award.
- Holly Humberstone won song of the year for To Love Somebody.
- Blossoms were named live act, and Madra Salach won the breakthrough award.
- Saturday Night Live UK won the television award, and its second season returns to Sky on Saturday 16 January.

Jacob Lusk gave a headline performance in tribute to Freddie Mercury, who would have turned 80 this year, and George Michael, who died 10 years ago in December. Lusk sang Who Wants To Live Forever, performed I Knew You Were Waiting For Me with Marisha Wallace, and closed with his cover of Natural Blues. ## What happens next

Blunt has not confirmed any Glastonbury booking, and the festival has not named him. His own words point to a summer tour as the priority, so any appearance would have to fit around it. Glastonbury says more acts are still to come, so fans will have to wait for the next announcements.
