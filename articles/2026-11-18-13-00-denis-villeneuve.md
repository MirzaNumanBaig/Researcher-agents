**Quick summary**

- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) says *Dune: Part Three* achieves things the first two films did not, according to Deadline.
- The film opens in cinemas on 18 December and is based on Frank Herbert's novel *Dune Messiah*.
- Separately, a Puck report relayed by DMARGE claims Josh O'Connor was the director's early favourite for James Bond. Nothing here is confirmed.
- Villeneuve is now turning to Bond, with Robert Pattinson cast as 007.

Denis Villeneuve has called *Dune: Part Three* his strongest work in the series, telling an interview that the final chapter does what the earlier two could not. The Canadian filmmaker made the remarks on the *One More Question* podcast, part of a GQ interview, as reported by [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/denis-villeneuve-dune-part-three-something-even-better-1237227247/). The film arrives in cinemas on 18 December.

## What Villeneuve said about Dune: Part Three

Villeneuve framed the trilogy as a progression. He said the second film was much better than the first because he had learned along the way and the storytelling had improved. The first, he explained, was more contemplative, and that was deliberate.

For the third, he wanted something else. "I wanted to drift into something a bit more muscular," he said, adding that he believes he delivered "something even better."

He went further, speaking as an artist rather than a marketer. He said he managed things he could not achieve in the first two films, pointing to tension and a strong emotional journey. He was candid about the unknown: he does not know how audiences will receive it, but he is satisfied there was a clear progression.

It is a notable claim from a director who is usually measured. Directors often praise their own films, so the real verdict will come from critics and ticket buyers in December.

## The film and its cast

According to Deadline, Villeneuve co-wrote the script with Brian K. Vaughan. It adapts Frank Herbert's *Dune Messiah*, the second novel in the series.

The cast includes Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, Jason Momoa, Florence Pugh, Rebecca Ferguson, Isaach De Bankolé, Charlotte Rampling, Anya Taylor-Joy, Robert Pattinson and Javier Bardem. Newcomers Nakoa-Wolf Momoa and Ida Brooke also appear.

Deadline also reports that the film broke a BFI Imax record, with 33,000 admissions sold in the first 24 hours. That figure comes from a Deadline headline and has not been checked against the cinema's own numbers.

## The Bond question

The same GQ profile has fuelled a second storyline. Villeneuve is directing *James Bond 26*, with Steven Knight writing the screenplay, as [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/james-bond-26-robert-pattinson-casting-biggest-inspiration-sean-connery-denis-villeneuve/) notes. Robert Pattinson has been unveiled as the new 007.

Villeneuve said he adores Sean Connery's era of Bond. He told GQ he needed to believe the actor was a killer, because "more than a spy, Bond is an assassin," as quoted by [Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/article/denis-villeneuve-pattinson-bond-killer.html). He also listed charisma, wit, dark humour and a deep intelligence in the eyes. The interview was filmed before Pattinson's casting was confirmed, Vulture says.

We have also covered this in [Villeneuve's James Bond vision](https://moduscarry.com/villeneuve-james-bond-vision/) and [why Pattinson is his Bond](https://moduscarry.com/denis-villeneuve-bond-robert-pattinson-007/).

## An unconfirmed report on Josh O'Connor

A new claim has added a twist. DMARGE, citing Puck's Matt Belloni, says Josh O'Connor was Villeneuve's early favourite for Bond, to the point that insiders believed the role was his to lose. The report says O'Connor then withdrew, having had doubts about the baggage that comes with the part and about the deal with Amazon MGM.

Treat this as a report, not a fact. Neither Villeneuve, O'Connor nor the studio is quoted confirming it in the sources we read. DMARGE also notes that Jeff Bezos told a conference that Pattinson was "immediately undebated" once screen tests were in. The two accounts are not necessarily in conflict, but they differ in tone.
