## What Mckenna Grace said about nude scenes

Mckenna Grace has said she is not willing to do nude scenes or very hypersexual roles at this stage of her career. The comments came in an interview with Cosmopolitan, reported by Fox News Digital on 4 November 2026.

"At this point, I definitely don't do nudity or very hypersexual things," Grace said, according to Fox News. She went on: "I respect anybody who can, but it's terrifying to me. I don't know if that'll ever change."

The remark is a statement about her own limits, not a judgement of other actors. She framed it as respect for colleagues who work differently, and she left the door open on whether her view might shift with time.

## The Disney princess comment

Grace also said her dream is to play a Disney princess someday. Fox News reports that she added she does not intend to restrict herself to wholesome material, and pointed to her character's death in *Scream* as an example of the range she wants.

Some of the coverage that followed linked the two ideas, suggesting the Disney ambition was her reason for avoiding nudity. Grace pushed back on that reading. In a later Instagram post, described by Fox News as appearing on a Tuesday, she said her comfort level with these roles is a personal matter and has nothing to do with the Disney remarks.
