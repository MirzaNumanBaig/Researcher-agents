**Quick summary**

- *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*, which stars [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), opened in the US on 20 November.
- It took US$119.3 million over its opening weekend, according to ScreenRant.
- It then earned US$12.9 million on its first Monday, a franchise record, and US$15.98 million on Tuesday, another one.
- The Hollywood Reporter projects a No. 1 finish over the Thanksgiving frame, with a US$60 million to US$80 million three-day weekend.

[Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) is part of the biggest film in American cinemas this week. *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* has broken two weekday records for the franchise since opening on 20 November, and forecasters expect it to win the Thanksgiving box office as well.

## A record Monday, then a record Tuesday

According to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mckenna-grace-hunger-games-sunrise-on-the-reaping-box-office-opening-monday-record/), the film earned US$12.9 million on its opening Monday. That is the best first Monday in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series. It edged out the US$12.3 million that *[Catching Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catching_Fire)* made on the same day. *Catching Fire* is still the highest-grossing film in the series.

Tuesday went better again. ScreenRant added an update noting a US$15.98 million opening Tuesday, the best for the franchise and the best for any Lionsgate release.

The weekend before was strong too. The film debuted with US$119.3 million, the fourth-best opening in the series and the fifth-best of any 2026 release so far. The next film on the chart, *I Play Rocky*, took US$9.3 million.

## What the film is and who is in it

*Sunrise on the Reaping* is the sixth film in the series and the second prequel. It adapts a book by [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins), the author of the original novels.

Australian actor [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) plays the young Haymitch. Grace appears opposite him alongside [Whitney Peak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_Peak_(actress)), [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) and [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close). ScreenRant's image from the film shows Grace as Maysilee Donner. Wikipedia lists Francis Lawrence as director.

## The Thanksgiving race

Every Hunger Games film after the first has opened in the week before Thanksgiving and then taken No. 1 on the holiday, according to ScreenRant. This year the competition is tougher. The film faces two big releases at once: Disney's animated *Hexed* and the comedy sequel *Focker-in-Law*.

The Hollywood Reporter, as cited by ScreenRant, projects a US$60 million to US$80 million three-day gross for the traditional weekend, with a chance of passing US$80 million. ScreenRant notes that the expected second-weekend drop of between 32.9 and 49.7 per cent is narrow for a film of this size.

## Audiences are warmer than critics

Reviews have been solid rather than glowing. The film holds a 68 per cent Fresh score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes). Audiences have been kinder: ScreenRant reports a Popcornmeter score of 94 per cent, the best in the franchise, and a CinemaScore of A-. Word of mouth like that usually helps a film hold up in later weeks.

Wikipedia's summary of the reviews is more mixed. It quotes one critic who called the film bloated and limited, while the critics' consensus it cites says the prequel retreads familiar ground sturdily enough to keep the odds in the franchise's favour.

## What happens next

If the film wins Thanksgiving, the next test is its third weekend. Neither *Catching Fire* nor the 2023 prequel *The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes* topped the chart that weekend. ScreenRant estimates a third weekend of US$30 million to US$54 million for *Sunrise on the Reaping*. The only major new opener that weekend, *Violent Night 2*, is tipped to take US$8 million to US$12 million. The original [*Violent Night*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violent_Night), starring [David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour), opened with US$13.4 million in 2022.
