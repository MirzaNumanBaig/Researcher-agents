**Quick summary**

- *The Take*, a heist thriller, is set for release on 15 January 2027 in select cinemas and on Netflix.
- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) plays a retired Army captain recruited to rob a New York bank, opposite Denzel Washington as a master thief.
- Fernando Meirelles directs from a script by Simon Kinberg, according to Netflix's Tudum site.
- The film was retitled *The Take* in October 2026, having begun life as *Here Comes the Flood*.

*The Take* now has a firm date. [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/robert-pattinson-movies-series) lists the heist thriller for 15 January, in select cinemas and on the streamer, with Pattinson and Washington leading the cast. Netflix is using the release to point viewers towards Pattinson's earlier work on the service.

## What The Take is about

Pattinson plays Noah, a recently retired Army captain, according to Netflix's own guide. Washington plays Christian Carter, a master thief who recruits him to rob what is described as the richest bank in New York City.

[Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Take_(2027_film)) gives a slightly wider picture: a bank guard, a teller and a master thief are drawn into a lethal chain of schemes and betrayals. Daisy Edgar-Jones plays Rose, Noah's girlfriend and a bank teller. Danai Gurira, Sean Harris, Moisés Arias and Justin Kirk are also in the cast.

## Who is behind the camera

Fernando Meirelles, who made *The Two Popes*, directs. Simon Kinberg, whose credits include *Mr. & Mrs. Smith*, wrote the screenplay. Wikipedia lists Kinberg as a producer with Audrey Chon and Meirelles, and names César Charlone as cinematographer, with Genre Films as the production company.

The project has a long history. According to Wikipedia, Netflix bought Kinberg's script in May 2020 in a seven-figure deal, and Jason Bateman was reported to be in talks to direct that year. Washington, Pattinson and Edgar-Jones were announced in May 2025, with the rest of the cast following in November 2025.

## Filming and the new title

Principal photography began on 3 November 2025 across Jersey City, Hoboken, Newark, Atlanta and New York City, and wrapped on 22 January 2026, per Wikipedia. The film was originally called *Here Comes the Flood* and took its new title in October 2026, roughly three months before release.

No budget, box office figures or review scores are available yet, so there is nothing to say about how it will perform. Those numbers will only appear after the film opens.

## A big year for Pattinson

Netflix describes 2026 as a busy year for its star. He appeared opposite Zendaya in *The Drama*, in Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey*, and in Lance Oppenheim's *Primetime*, where he plays Chris Hansen. He has also been cast as the next James Bond, succeeding Daniel Craig. Our earlier report covers [the Bond announcement](https://moduscarry.com/robert-pattinson-james-bond-2/).

Netflix's guide also lists Pattinson films already on the service, among them *The Devil All the Time* (2020), *The King* (2019), *Twilight* (2008) and *Waiting for the Barbarians* (2019). Deadline ran a gallery of his biggest roles, from *Twilight* to *Tenet*, but the page could not be read in full for this report.
