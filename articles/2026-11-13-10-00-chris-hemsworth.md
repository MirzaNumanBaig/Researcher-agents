**Quick summary**

- Four people broke into [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth)'s Broken Head home near Byron Bay on Wednesday 11 November and stole three vehicles, according to NSW Police.
- Police were called about 7.30am. No injuries were reported.
- Two vehicles were found soon after, and a third was recovered at Lennox Head on Thursday, police said.
- All three cars have been seized for forensic examination. There have been no arrests and no charges.

Police have recovered all three vehicles stolen from the Byron Bay-area home of Chris Hemsworth and [Elsa Pataky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsa_Pataky), but nobody has been arrested. The break-in happened in the early hours of Wednesday 11 November, and The Sydney Morning Herald reports that officers were called to the Broken Head property at about 7.30am.

## What police say happened

According to a statement from NSW Police quoted by [The Sydney Morning Herald](https://www.smh.com.au/culture/celebrity/masked-thieves-raid-chris-hemsworth-and-elsa-pataky-s-byron-bay-mansion-20261113-p61dvf.html), officers from the Tweed-Byron Police District were told four unknown people had entered the property and taken three vehicles. Police set up a crime scene and began investigating.

Two vehicles were found in the first stage of the inquiry. On Thursday, police said, a third was recovered at Lennox Head. All three have been seized for forensic examination, and police are asking anyone with information to come forward.

## The CCTV account

Much of the detail about the night comes from a 7NEWS report, repeated by several outlets and not independently confirmed by police. It says Pataky woke at around 2am to the family's dogs barking and went back to sleep. When she checked the CCTV footage later that morning, she reportedly saw four men in balaclavas, two of them holding knives.

The same report says the men climbed the front fence and went into a garage where three vehicles were parked with the keys inside. [Power 100](https://www.power100.com.au/trending/stories/chris-hemsworths-shopping-centre-sized-home-robbed/) reports the family, including their three children, was home at the time. The vehicles are reported to be a BYD, a Ram and a [Ford Raptor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Raptor) ute.

Reports put the recoveries well away from the property: one near Coraki, about an hour's drive from Broken Head, and another near Tabulam, more than two hours away.

## A home chosen for its distance

The couple moved back to Australia from Los Angeles in 2014. On the SmartLess podcast in February, Hemsworth said the move came around the time his sons were born and that he had not enjoyed living in LA. He called coming home "the greatest decision", according to the Herald, and described life on a farm with horses, motorbikes and surf.

The property's value is reported inconsistently. The Herald says around $50 million, while Power 100 estimates about $75 million. Page Six, citing earlier reporting, says the couple bought it in 2014 for $7 million and has since added a dirt bike track, a pool and tennis courts. None of those figures has been confirmed by the family.

Page Six said it had asked police and a representative for Hemsworth for comment and had not received a reply when it published. Our earlier coverage of the first reports is in [Chris Hemsworth Home Break-In: Three Cars Stolen at Broken Head](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-home-break-in/).

## What happens next

Police inquiries are continuing, and the forensic results from the three vehicles are the next step to watch. Anyone with information is asked to contact police.

On the work side, Hemsworth, 43, returns as Thor in Avengers: Doomsday in December, the Herald reports.
