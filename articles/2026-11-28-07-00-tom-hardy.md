**Quick summary**

- [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)'s 2020 crime drama Capone is streaming on Netflix now, according to ELLE.
- MobLand season three is filming, with Hardy confirmed to return as Harry Da Souza.
- ELLE reports that [Stanley Tucci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Tucci) is reportedly joining the MobLand cast; this is not confirmed.
- Cosmopolitan points viewers to his 2012 comedy This Means War, which is on Disney+ in the UK and Ireland.

With new MobLand episodes still some way off, Tom Hardy fans have two very different films to stream this weekend. Capone, his 2020 portrait of the gangster Al Capone, is on Netflix now, while the romcom This Means War sits on Disney+ in the UK and Ireland, according to ELLE and Cosmopolitan.

The recommendations arrive as [MobLand](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-2/) season three is filming. ELLE says Hardy is confirmed to return as Harry Da Souza, and that Tucci is reportedly joining the cast. We have covered the production before, including the news that [Gregg Sulkin joins the show for season three](https://moduscarry.com/mobland-season-3-cast-gregg-sulkin/).

## What Tom Hardy's Capone is about

Directed by [Josh Trank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Trank), Capone is a biographical crime drama set in the last year of Al Capone's life. ELLE describes a film that skips the rise through Chicago's underworld. Instead it finds him at his Florida estate, years after prison, with his health failing and his memory unreliable.

The central hook is money. Capone tells an old associate he buried $10 million somewhere on the property, but he can no longer remember where. The FBI still wants to know, and federal agent Crawford, played by [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) of [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses), looks for answers. Capone's wife Mae, played by Linda Cardellini, tries to care for him as memories and hallucinations intrude on the present.

## A physical transformation

ELLE highlights how much the role asked of Hardy's body. His appearance changes through the film with make-up and specially designed contact lenses, and the prosthetics alone took about five hours to apply. As Capone declines, the performance becomes more physical, from the altered voice to the way he moves.

The supporting cast includes [Matt Dillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Dillon), [Al Sapienza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Sapienza), [Katherine Narducci](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathrine_Narducci), [Noel Fisher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noel_Fisher) and [Kyle MacLachlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyle_MacLachlan), best known for [Twin Peaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twin_Peaks).

## How it compares with his other gangster roles

Hardy has plenty of criminal credits. ELLE points to Camden gang leader [Alfie Solomons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfie_Solomons) in Peaky Blinders and both Kray twins in Legend. The magazine argues that Capone is less about a mobster at the height of his power and more about what is left when mind and body begin to fail. It admits the shifting perspective can be disorienting, but says that unease is the point.

For MobLand viewers, ELLE calls it a more surreal and intimate version of familiar territory.

## A lighter option: This Means War

If you want something less brooding, Cosmopolitan recommends This Means War, released in 2012. Hardy plays Tuck, a CIA agent who is best friends with fellow agent FDR, played by Chris Pine. Their friendship is tested when they discover they are dating the same woman, Lauren, played by Reese Witherspoon, and use their CIA resources to sabotage each other's dates.

Cosmopolitan concedes it is not the most critically acclaimed film on his CV, but calls it an entertaining break from his usual roles. In the UK and Ireland it streams on Disney+, and it can be rented on Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV and Rakuten TV. Availability in Australia was not covered in the sources, so check your local services.
