The [Brave New Love film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brave_New_Love) is a Scandinavian relationship drama from director Maria Bäck, and it is now listed for release on 10 December 2026. The story starts with an affair and turns into a wider question: can traditional ideas of commitment survive when people want different things from love?

## What Brave New Love is about

According to [InsightTrendsWorld](https://www.insighttrendsworld.com/post/brave-new-love-2026-by-maria-b%C3%A4ck), the film follows Kate, played by Kathrine Thorborg Johansen. Her romantic life becomes tangled when her lover leaves for Greece, and she decides to go after him.

The trip becomes more than a pursuit. The outlet describes Kate as forced to reconsider the social and emotional rules around love, family and commitment, including what monogamy asks of people. The outlet's own summary of the film's arc runs from the affair to the lover's departure, then the journey, then uncertainty about what a partnership should be.

That framing sets the film apart from a standard infidelity story. InsightTrendsWorld says it treats the affair as a starting point for examining relationships, not only as a moral failing or a source of conflict. This is the outlet's reading of the film, written ahead of wide release, so it is worth treating as a guide to the premise and not a review.

## Who is in the cast

Johansen leads. The cast also includes [Anders Danielsen Lie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anders_Danielsen_Lie), who InsightTrendsWorld notes is known for *The Worst Person in the World* and *Oslo, August 31st*, and [Simon Sears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Sears), the Danish actor.

Wikipedia's listing for the film also names Angeliki Papoulia and Noemi Carbone Mannov among the cast. That list comes from the encyclopedia and Wikidata, not from a news report, so roles have not been confirmed here.

Bäck is described by the same outlet as the director of *Psychosis in Stockholm*. The film is a Danish, Swedish and Greek production, which fits a story that moves from Scandinavia to a Greek setting.
