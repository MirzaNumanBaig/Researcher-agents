Jim Carrey has married again, and almost nobody noticed. According to [The Telegraph](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/films/2026/10/26/hollywood-extrovert-jim-carrey-fade/), the 64-year-old actor wed Min Ah, 42, at a small ceremony in Los Angeles last month. The paper describes her as reportedly an actress and artist.

It is a muted turn for a man who was once the loudest presence in Hollywood.

## From record pay cheque to near silence

In 1996 Carrey was paid a then-record $20 million for The Cable Guy, which The Telegraph says marked him out as Hollywood royalty. Thirty years later, the paper writes, he has essentially retired and surfaces mainly for the occasional Sonic the Hedgehog film.

His public appearances are now rare. At the César Awards in Paris in February, he called Min Ah his "sublime companion", which The Telegraph says is about all the public knows of her.

This is Carrey's third marriage. He was married to Melissa Womer from 1987 to 1995, and they have a daughter, Jane. He was then married to his Dumb and Dumber co-star [Lauren Holly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lauren_Holly) for less than a year in 1996 and 1997.

## Did he step away, or did Hollywood?

The Telegraph asks whether the retreat was his choice. An unnamed comedy screenwriter told the paper Carrey was "always a bit of a loose cannon" but never someone people were warned off. Pam Lyddon, founder of PR agency Bright Star Digital, who once worked with him at a film event, said the word she kept hearing from industry contacts was "difficult". She said studios lost patience with disruptive stars in the early 2000s because delays and set fights were costing too much. These are opinions reported by the paper, not established facts.

The timing overlaps with Carrey's attempt to move beyond the blockbuster comedies that made him famous, among them [Ace Ventura: Pet Detective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ace_Ventura%3A_Pet_Detective), The Mask and Dumb and Dumber. Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004) worked. The Majestic (2001), a period romance, lost around $35 million, according to The Telegraph.

## The method-acting legend

Carrey's most talked-about project may be Man on the Moon (1999), in which he played [Andy Kaufman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Kaufman) and Kaufman's lounge-singer alter ego [Tony Clifton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Clifton), staying in character throughout. Co-star [Paul Giamatti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Giamatti) told BuzzFeed in 2013 it was "one of the weirdest experiences" of his filmmaking life and said Carrey kept Limburger cheese in his pockets. The 2017 Netflix documentary Jim & Andy: The Great Beyond showed behind-the-scenes footage of the approach.

On [Batman Forever](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batman_Forever) (1995), Carrey said in a 2017 TV interview that co-star [Tommy Lee Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee_Jones) resented his success, recalling Jones telling him: "I cannot sanction your buffoonery."

## Old roles back in the conversation

Carrey's older work is getting fresh attention. Foro3D revisited the eight-hour makeup sessions on How the Grinch Stole Christmas (2000). It cites a 2013 CBS News interview in which [Ron Howard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ron_Howard) called Carrey a genius, and says Carrey considered quitting after the first day. Rick Baker and Gail Ryan won the Oscar for the film's makeup.

ScreenRant, meanwhile, looked back at the 2007 psychological thriller The Number 23, in which Carrey plays a man consumed by the number. The site says its dark tone divided audiences on release and looks stranger now that younger viewers know him through the family-friendly Sonic films. It also points to the 2016 drama Dark Crimes as another departure from his comedy image.

## What happens next

Neither Carrey nor Min Ah appears to have commented publicly on the wedding, and The Telegraph's account rests on its own reporting. There is no announced acting project in the sources we could read. An IMDb headline this week mentioned a film called Pierre Pierre with Larry Charles directing and Carrey starring, but the page could not be read, so the detail is unconfirmed.
