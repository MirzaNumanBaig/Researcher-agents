**Quick summary**

- Two Brendan Fraser Mummy props from the 1999 film will be sold at Propstore's Winter Entertainment Memorabilia Live Auction.
- The auction runs from 4 to 6 December and includes the Hamunaptra Key and the Book of the Dead.
- Propstore's estimate for the two items is US$26,400 to US$52,800, according to The Hollywood Reporter as relayed by ComingSoon.net.
- [Brendan Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Fraser) and [Rachel Weisz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Weisz) are set to return for a fourth film, *The Mummy: Book of the Dead*.

Two Brendan Fraser Mummy props, the most recognisable objects from the 1999 film, are heading to auction just as the actor prepares to step back into the role that made him a franchise star. Propstore will sell the Hamunaptra Key and the Book of the Dead from 4 to 6 December, [ComingSoon.net reported](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2228852-the-mummy-artefacts-props-auction), citing The Hollywood Reporter.

## What is going under the hammer

The Brendan Fraser Mummy props come from the original 1999 adventure. The first is the Hamunaptra Key, the intricate puzzle box that guards the map to the City of the Dead. The second is the Book of the Dead, the object used to bring the priest Imhotep back to life.

Both are mechanical pieces with detailed Egyptian designs, as they appear on screen. ComingSoon.net, citing The Hollywood Reporter, puts the combined estimate at US$26,400 to US$52,800. The sale is part of Propstore's Winter Entertainment Memorabilia Live Auction.

For fans, the timing is hard to miss. Props that carried the plot of a film now more than 25 years old reach the market while the franchise is waking up again.

## The sequel behind the sale

Fraser played Rick O'Connell across three films, and Weisz played Evelyn in the first two. Both have signed on for the new instalment, which ComingSoon.net says is titled *The Mummy: Book of the Dead*.

The film will be co-directed by [Matt Bettinelli-Olpin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Bettinelli-Olpin) and [Tyler Gillett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyler_Gillett), with David Coggeshall credited as writer. According to ComingSoon.net, it will follow on directly from *The Mummy* and *The Mummy Returns* and ignore the events of *The Mummy: Tomb of the Dragon Emperor*.

## Who else is returning

Several faces from the original films are listed to come back. John Hannah returns as Jonathan Carnahan, Evelyn's bumbling older brother. [Arnold Vosloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Vosloo) is back as Imhotep, and [Oded Fehr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oded_Fehr) returns as Ardeth Bay.

New to the project are [Michael Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Johnston_(actor)), [Numan Acar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numan_Acar) and [Tara Emad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tara_Emad). ComingSoon.net does not say which roles they will play.

## A release date that has not settled

ComingSoon.net says Universal Pictures has the film scheduled for 15 October 2027. That does not match an earlier report from [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/global/the-mummy-4-release-date-may-2028-brendan-fraser-rachel-weisz-32961/), which put the wide release at 19 May 2028. Until Universal clarifies, treat the date as unconfirmed.

## What happens next

The auction opens on 4 December, so the final prices will be known within days of it closing on 6 December. On the film itself, the open questions are the confirmed release date, the start of production and a first look at the story. None of these has been announced in the reports we read. ## Frequently asked questions

### Which props from The Mummy are being auctioned?

Propstore is selling the Hamunaptra Key, the puzzle box that protects the map to the City of the Dead, and the Book of the Dead, which is used to resurrect Imhotep. Both are from the 1999 film.

### How much are the Mummy props expected to sell for?

The estimate for the two items is US$26,400 to US$52,800, according to The Hollywood Reporter as cited by ComingSoon.net. Final prices will depend on bidding.

### Is Brendan Fraser in the new Mummy film?

Yes. Fraser is set to return as Rick O'Connell, with Rachel Weisz back as Evelyn, according to ComingSoon.net.

### When is The Mummy 4 out?

Reports differ. ComingSoon.net lists 15 October 2027, while Variety Australia earlier reported 19 May 2028. Neither date has been settled by an official update we could find.
