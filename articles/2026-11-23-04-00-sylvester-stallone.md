**Quick summary**

- *I Play Rocky*, the biopic about [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone)'s rise, has expanded nationwide in the United States.
- Amazon MGM put it in more than 2,400 theatres, up from about 30 the week before, according to [Collider](https://collider.com/i-play-rocky-box-office-expansion-2448-theaters/).
- Audiences gave it an A+ on CinemaScore and a 98% score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes); critics sit at 89%.
- The film opened with nearly US$4 million on its first wide Friday and is projected to pass US$10 million for the weekend.

*I Play Rocky*, the film telling how Sylvester Stallone went from struggling New York actor to Hollywood name, has moved from a limited run to a nationwide release in the United States. Amazon MGM Studios opened it in more than 2,400 theatres this weekend, and early numbers suggest audiences are turning up in strength, Collider reports.

## How the wide release is going

The expansion follows a slow build. The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival, where it won the People's Choice Award, then debuted in seven theatres in early November. The following week it grew to nearly 30 locations and took US$1 million, with strong per-theatre averages, according to Collider.

This week's jump to around 2,448 theatres is a different scale altogether. Peter Farrelly directs, and the film grossed nearly US$4 million on its first Friday in wide release. Collider projects more than US$10 million for the weekend.

Collider notes that the audience skews older, so it does not expect the sharp opening-weekend drop a younger-skewing release would face. Its view is that word of mouth will carry the film over the coming days and weeks. That is the outlet's forecast rather than a result, and the next few weekends will show whether it holds.

## What audiences and critics say

The most striking number is the grade. *I Play Rocky* earned an A+ on CinemaScore, the exit-poll rating of opening-night audiences. Collider says that puts it among roughly 140 films in history to receive that mark. Its audience score on Rotten Tomatoes is 98%.

Critics are warmer than lukewarm but less rapturous. The film holds a "Certified Fresh" 89% on the same site. The site's consensus describes it as a feel-good tribute to the underdog who created one of cinema's best-known underdogs, while allowing that it may not match the power of the original film.

## The story behind the film

The biopic puts the film first and the man second. It follows Stallone's rags-to-riches path before and after *Rocky*, the 1976 film that made him a household name almost overnight. Newcomer [Anthony Ippolito](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Ippolito) plays Stallone.

Collider also calls the film an awards contender, though that is the writer's expectation, not a nomination or a ruling from any awards body. For more on the film and its star, see our earlier coverage of [how I Play Rocky traces Stallone's rise](https://moduscarry.com/i-play-rocky-sylvester-stallone-story/), [Anthony Ippolito's transformation into Stallone](https://moduscarry.com/anthony-ippolito-stallone-transformation/) and [the film's Oscars odds](https://moduscarry.com/i-play-rocky-oscars-odds/).

## What happens next

The first full weekend of the nationwide run is the real test. If the projection of more than US$10 million holds, the film will have a solid base to build on in the weeks that follow, and Collider expects steady business rather than a rapid fade.
