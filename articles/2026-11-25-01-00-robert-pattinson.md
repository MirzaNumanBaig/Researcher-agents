**Quick summary**

- The Robert Pattinson Bond watch is undecided: [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has been confirmed as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), and the luxury industry is already positioning itself around him.
- His casting announcement did not show a watch, which leaves Bond's wrist open for the first time in three decades, according to The National.
- Pattinson became a Jaeger-LeCoultre ambassador in September, while Omega has been Bond's official watch since 1995.
- Jaeger-LeCoultre has since released a new Reverso that Esquire says suits 007, but Esquire notes there is no suggestion Omega is losing its place.
- The film is directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) for Amazon MGM Studios and is expected in 2028.

The Robert Pattinson Bond watch question has become the first big commercial puzzle of the new 007 era. Days after Amazon MGM Studios confirmed the actor in the role, watchmakers, tailors and car brands are working out who gets to stand next to him, according to [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/magazine/2026/11/24/could-robert-pattinsons-james-bond-spell-the-end-of-omega-and-tom-fords-007-era/).

## Why Bond's wrist matters

Bond has been a marketing platform for more than 60 years, since [Sean Connery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sean_Connery) appeared in Dr No. The National says his ties to watchmakers, tailors and carmakers are so deeply embedded that a place in a film means a share of the character's mystique.

Omega has held the best-known of those deals since [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) wore a Seamaster in GoldenEye in 1995. Before that, the character was linked with Rolex, on screen and in Ian Fleming's novels, The National reports.

The announcement of Pattinson's casting left out a watch altogether. The National calls that a small detail that has probably set phones ringing across the product-placement business.

## Jaeger-LeCoultre complicates things

Pattinson became a global ambassador for Jaeger-LeCoultre in September. The Swiss maker is owned by Richemont. The National notes that an actor's personal endorsements and a character's on-screen partnerships are separate arrangements, and that the final call is expected to rest with Villeneuve and the producers.

A week after the casting news, the brand unveiled a new Reverso Classic Monoface Small Seconds. Esquire describes it as a 7.56mm-thick watch in an 18-carat pink-gold case with a black alligator strap, priced at US$18,400. That fits Pattinson's own taste. "I definitely like really thin watches," he said in a recent interview quoted by Esquire.

## Tom Ford against Dior

The wardrobe is just as open. Tom Ford has dressed Bond since 2008's Quantum of Solace, covering four Daniel Craig films through No Time to Die in 2021. Before him, Brosnan wore Brioni.

Pattinson has appeared in Dior campaigns since 2013. The National says Dior was quick to congratulate him and that it is "very pleased to continue" the relationship, though it declined to comment on any Bond partnership. Tom Ford has not addressed the question publicly. The National speculates that creative director Jonathan Anderson could take 007's tailoring somewhere new.

Aston Martin, in around a dozen Bond films, has also declined to comment.

The National adds that Pattinson and Villeneuve favour considered, artistic choices, so a bigger cheque may not be enough.

## What else is known about the casting

According to [Forbes India](https://www.forbesindia.com/life/entertainment/in-seventh-heaven-robert-pattinsons-role-of-a-lifetime-article/3000970), the 40-year-old is the seventh actor to play the spy, after Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Brosnan and Craig. It is also the first Bond film since Dr No in 1962 not produced by Eon Productions. Pattinson called the role "the honour of a lifetime".

The Guardian says there is no release date yet, though autumn 2028 looks likely given the gaps between earlier Brosnan and Craig films.

One unconfirmed report is also circulating. Plataforma Media, citing The Sun, says Amazon's Bond executives blocked Pattinson from agreeing to a third Batman film, so his run as Bruce Wayne would end after The Batman Part II. Nothing has been confirmed, so treat it as a rumour.

## What happens next

Nothing is decided on the watch, the suits or the car, and with a 2028 release expected the deals may stay quiet for a while.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will Robert Pattinson wear an Omega watch as James Bond?
Not confirmed. Omega has been Bond's official watch since 1995 and Esquire says nothing suggests that is changing, but Pattinson is a Jaeger-LeCoultre ambassador and the decision is expected to rest with Villeneuve and the producers.

### Who made Bond's suits before Pattinson?
Tom Ford dressed Daniel Craig in four films from Quantum of Solace to No Time to Die. Brioni dressed Pierce Brosnan, and Anthony Sinclair made Sean Connery's suits in Dr No.

### When is the new Bond film out?
There is no official date. The National expects 2028, and The Guardian suggests autumn that year.
