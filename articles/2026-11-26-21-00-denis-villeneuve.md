**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was officially unveiled as the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) on 16 November, according to Soap Central.
- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) is directing and [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight) is writing, Radio Times reports.
- Other roles, including the villain, Q, M and the main female lead, have not been announced.
- Production is expected to begin in 2027 and no release date has been set.

The next James Bond film now has its star, its director and its writer, and the guessing has moved on to who will stand beside Robert Pattinson. Two reports this week show how the conversation is developing: Radio Times has looked at who might play opposite him, while actor Charlie Cox has joked about being told he was too old to audition.

## What is confirmed about the Bond film

According to [Soap Central](https://www.soapcentral.com/entertainment/news-how-old-robert-pattinson-daredevil-star-charlie-cox-says-rejected-auditioning-denis-villeneuve-s-james-bond-due-age), Pattinson was announced as the new 007 on 16 November. The film will be the 26th in the series and is produced by Amazon MGM Studios. Pattinson, who turned 40 in May 2026, called the role "the honor of a lifetime" and said it was a dream come true.

[Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/movies/james-bond-girl-cast-denis-villeneuve-comment/) notes that Villeneuve and Knight had been attached to the project for some time, which means the star, director and writer are now all known. Pattinson succeeds [Daniel Craig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Craig) in the part.

Soap Central adds that Amazon founder Jeff Bezos has said five finalists took part in screen tests before Pattinson was chosen. The film is expected to start production in 2027. No release date has been given.

## Who could play opposite Pattinson?

Radio Times is clear that its list of eight possible co-stars is built on rumour, and that nothing has been confirmed. Several of the names are actors Villeneuve has already worked with on the Dune films.

Zendaya is one of them. The outlet points out that she appears alongside Pattinson in several 2026 releases, including the upcoming Dune: Part Three, and that she has a working relationship with the director.

[Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh) is another, and is returning as Princess Irulan in the third Dune film. Radio Times says Villeneuve has spoken warmly of her, though it also notes that Pugh has said she would be interested in playing a villain.

[Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy) had a brief cameo in the second Dune film and is said to have a bigger part in the third.

Among actors who have not worked with Villeneuve, Radio Times mentions Sydney Sweeney. It attributes the claim to a report in The Sun, which said she was "the top name on the casting sheet". Radio Times stresses that there is nothing to confirm this. Treat it as unverified.

The outlet also recalls how the franchise has moved towards more fully drawn female characters, citing Eva Green's Vesper Lynd, Léa Seydoux's Madeleine Swann and [Ana de Armas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Armas) in No Time to Die.

## Charlie Cox says he was told he was too old

The age question came up on 23 November, when [Charlie Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlie_Cox) appeared on Virgin Radio UK's The Chris Evans Breakfast Show. Soap Central quotes him saying he asked to audition and was told he was "too old", even though Pattinson is the same age as him. Cox, who is 43 and turns 44 in December, said he had expected [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) or [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) to get the part.

He was generous about the winner, praising Pattinson's career and his history of working with directors before they became major names. Soap Central, citing Variety, says Lowden, Turner, Nicholas Hoult and Josh O'Connor were all reported to be under consideration.

## What happens next

The remaining casting is the next thing to watch. The villain, Q, M and the lead female role are all open, and the rumours above may or may not prove right. Villeneuve is still tied to the Dune franchise, with Dune: Part Three on the way, so his two projects are likely to keep overlapping in the news.
