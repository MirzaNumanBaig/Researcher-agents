**Quick summary**

- A24's documentary *You Can See Everything*, made by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and Lance Oppenheim, has arrived on digital platforms.
- It went straight to number one on the US iTunes chart, according to Collider, after six weeks in cinemas.
- Collider reports it earned about US$65 million in its first three weeks of release, an unusual figure for a documentary.
- Wikipedia lists a budget of US$7 million and a worldwide gross of US$105 million.

*You Can See Everything*, the three-hour documentary from Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, has moved from cinemas to digital and gone straight to the top of the US iTunes chart, [Collider reported](https://collider.com/you-can-see-everything-digital-debut-box-office-removed-from-1400-theaters/) this week. The A24 release is the latest sign that a film built on secrecy and word of mouth has found a much larger audience than most documentaries ever reach.

## How a secret documentary became a hit

According to Collider, the film's existence was kept quiet while it was being made. It was revealed at this year's Telluride Film Festival and then opened in just eight locations across the United States.

Buzz did the rest. Collider credits strong reviews, an intriguing marketing campaign and plain supply and demand. In its second week the film expanded to more than 2,650 cinemas, and in week three to more than 3,250.

Over those first three weeks it took roughly US$65 million, which Collider calls almost unheard of for a documentary feature. The cinema count has since fallen, as is normal, and the film is now in about 1,200 locations in its sixth week.

## Where it sits among A24's recent hits

Collider places the documentary in a run of strong results for the studio. *The Drama*, a psychological dark comedy starring Zendaya and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson), has grossed more than US$130 million worldwide. Over the summer, the horror film *Backrooms* became A24's biggest hit to date with US$400 million globally.

Against those, the outlet describes Fielder's film as the least likely success of the three. It is a long, genre-bending documentary rather than a straightforward thriller or horror.

## What the film is, and what critics say

The film follows Elizabeth Holmes, the Silicon Valley founder and convicted felon, in the month before she was sent to prison. According to the film's synopsis quoted by The Hindu, she invites Fielder and a film crew into her home to document her movements. The Hindu lists the running time as 174 minutes.

The Hindu's reviewer gave it a glowing, if bewildered, write-up. They described a documentary that asks whether you can record deception without becoming part of it, and noted Fielder's long record of meta-documentary work from *Nathan for You* to *The Rehearsal* and *The Curse*. The same page links to a report that the film became Letterboxd's highest-rated feature of all time, with a 4.8 rating.

The critical response has been strong elsewhere too. Wikipedia records a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score and a Metacritic score of 97 from 52 critics. It also quotes Manohla Dargis of The New York Times, who wrote that few movies speak so uncannily to the confusions of the present moment.

## The numbers in context

Wikipedia puts the budget at US$7 million and the worldwide box office at US$105 million, around 15 times the cost. Those figures come from a crowd-edited source and have not been confirmed by A24 in the pages we read, so treat them as a guide rather than an audited result.

## What happens next

The film is now available digitally in the United States, and its top spot on iTunes suggests a second wave of viewers who skipped the cinema because of the length. Collider's piece does not give an Australian release or streaming date, so local viewers should check their usual platforms for listings.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is You Can See Everything?

It is a three-hour documentary by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, released by A24. It centres on Elizabeth Holmes in the month before she began a prison sentence.

### Where can I watch You Can See Everything?

In the United States it has just debuted on digital and topped the iTunes chart, according to Collider. Collider does not report an Australian date.

### How much has it made?

Collider says about US$65 million in its first three weeks. Wikipedia lists US$105 million worldwide on a US$7 million budget.

### Who directed it?

Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim share the directing credit, according to The Hindu and Wikipedia.
