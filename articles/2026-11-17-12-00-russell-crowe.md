**Quick summary**

- Michael Chiklis has joined the cast of the sci-fi action thriller Bluefly, according to a Deadline report picked up by AOL and MovieWeb on 16 November 2026.
- He plays John Broyles, who leads a covert special-operations team in the Congo.
- [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) plays Benny, a former pilot with a troubled past. Priyanka Chopra Jonas and Daniel MacPherson are also in the cast.
- Nimród Antal directs. Filming is under way on Australia's Gold Coast, and no release date has been announced.

[Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe)'s next science-fiction film has added a recognisable face from the superhero genre. Deadline reported on 16 November that [Michael Chiklis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chiklis) has signed on to Bluefly, the Highland Film Group thriller now in production on the Gold Coast. [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/russell-crowe-priyanka-chopra-jonas-200644000.html) and [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/bluefly-russell-crowe-michael-chiklis/) both relayed the news.

## Who Michael Chiklis plays in Bluefly

Chiklis will play Broyles, the man who leads a covert special-operations unit sent into the Congo. Their task is to recover a crashed military aircraft that, officially, does not exist. Crowe's character, Benny, is described as an enigmatic former pilot who becomes part of that recovery effort.

Chiklis is best known to comic-book fans as [Ben Grimm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thing_(Marvel_Comics)), the rock-skinned Thing, in 2005's Fantastic Four and its 2007 sequel. MovieWeb also points to his Emmy-winning turn as Vic Mackey in The Shield and his role as Red Auerbach in Winning Time: The Rise of the Lakers Dynasty. AOL notes that he recently spoke to ComingSoon about the original Fantastic Four films, calling them "familial and kitschy and cheeky and fun" and thanking fans who keep finding them.

MovieWeb adds that some fans are already asking whether he might return to the Thing in 2027's Avengers: Secret Wars. Nothing in the reporting on Bluefly suggests that, so treat it as speculation.

## What Bluefly is about

According to the AOL report, the story follows Myla Reese, a grieving United Nations translator working in the Congo. She is drawn into a covert mission to find the missing aircraft and travels with a special-operations team through areas held by armed militias. As the group gets closer to the wreck, a mysterious threat appears, and the team starts to question what the assignment is really for.

Priyanka Chopra Jonas plays Myla. [Daniel MacPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_MacPherson), the Australian actor, is also attached, though MovieWeb says his role has not been disclosed.

## The team behind the camera

Nimród Antal directs. MovieWeb singles out his 2010 film Predators as his best-known work, and quotes him describing Bluefly as an "intense character-driven thriller wrapped inside a bold science fiction premise". The same outlet says the film is meant to feel more grounded in the real world than its Predator-style comparisons suggest.

The screenplay is credited to [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio), [William Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Eubank) and [Carlyle Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlyle_Eubank). MovieWeb notes the trio previously worked together on 2014's The Signal, which William Eubank also directed. Frigerio is producing with a long list of colleagues, including RuthAnne Frigerio, Michael Jefferson, Deborah Glover, Arianne Fraser, Delphine Perrier, Vanessa Yao Guo and Petr Jákl, according to AOL. Highland Film Group is handling worldwide sales.

## Crowe's recent run

MovieWeb describes the past decade as a mixed one for Crowe's releases. It cites the Marvel titles Thor: Love and Thunder (2022) and Kraven the Hunter (2024) as films that did not land with fans, against critical favourites such as The Nice Guys (2016) and Boy Erased (2018).

His most recent release, Unabomber, opened on 25 September 2026. It currently sits at 25 per cent on Rotten Tomatoes and 46 out of 100 from 18 critics on Metacritic, according to the figures listed on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unabomber_(film)). Bluefly gives him a chance to reset in a genre he has not worked in for a while.

Away from film, Crowe also made headlines this week for a social media post. He gave a shout-out on X to Edmonton Oilers goalie Devon Levi, who credited Crowe's Gladiator for a ritual on the ice, as [Oil On Whyte](https://oilonwhyte.com/russell-crowe-is-a-surprising-supporter-for-devon-levi-and-the-edmonton-oilers-01m7gjf1aawk) reported. We covered that separately in [Russell Crowe Salutes Oilers Goalie's Gladiator Ritual](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-gladiator-oilers-devon-levi/).
