**Quick summary**

- Paramount's *Ebenezer* took $7.1 million in the US on Friday, previews included, and is tracking for $20 million or more over the weekend, according to Deadline.
- That is close to the openings of Johnny Depp's *Black Mass* ($22.6 million) and *Public Enemies* ($25.2 million).
- Overseas, the film is expected to add about $20 million from 63 markets, Australia among them.
- In a CBS Mornings interview aired Friday, Depp said becoming a father gave his career its purpose, as reported by Page Six.

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp)'s Christmas horror *Ebenezer* is on course for a $20 million-plus opening in the United States, a respectable start for a star-led film that is not part of a franchise. Deadline reported on Friday that the Ti West-directed film made $7.1 million on its first day, including previews, from 3,535 theatres.

## How Ebenezer is tracking at the box office

Deadline's Friday afternoon update put the weekend at $20 million or more. The trade outlet pointed to Depp's own history for comparison. *Black Mass* opened to $22.6 million in 2015 and *Public Enemies* to $25.2 million in 2009. Both were R-rated crime dramas, while *Ebenezer* is PG-13, so the comparison is loose, but the numbers sit in the same range.

The film took $1.5 million in previews that began at 2pm on Friday. Deadline noted that this is below the $3.7 million previews of *Red One*, the action comedy starring [Dwayne Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwayne_Johnson) and Chris Evans, and below the $2.2 million made by Illumination's *The Grinch* in 2018. Deadline cautioned that comparisons are hard, because few Christmas family films have been released since the pandemic and *Ebenezer* leans spooky rather than action-heavy.

The report called it the first big greenlight of Paramount's new administration since last year's Skydance merger. Paramount also backed the launch at San Diego Comic-Con, where Depp made a surprise appearance in character.

## Critics are cooler than audiences

Reviews are mixed. Deadline gave the film a 53 per cent Rotten Tomatoes score and argued that critics are rarely generous to Christmas films: *The Polar Express* sits at 55 per cent, the live-action *How the Grinch Stole Christmas* with Jim Carrey at 50 per cent, and *Red One* at 29 per cent.

Audiences were warmer on opening night. Exit polling from PostTrak gave *Ebenezer* 4.5 stars, with 63 per cent saying they would definitely recommend it. For more on the critical response, see our coverage of [how critics split on his Scrooge](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-reviews/) and [why the film is a low-cost bet on a comeback](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-comeback/).

## A crowded weekend

*Ebenezer* is expected to take first place. Deadline has Warner Bros Animation's *The Cat in the Hat* second with about $12 million in its second weekend, and the Japanese hit [Godzilla Minus Zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godzilla_Minus_Zero) third at around $8 million, a steeper drop than the outlet's sources had predicted. A24's *You Can See Everything*, co-directed by Lance Oppenheimer and [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder), is among the films fighting for fifth place.

## What Depp told CBS about fatherhood

Away from the numbers, Depp has been talking about why he works. Speaking to CBS Mornings in an interview that aired Friday, he said the birth of his first child lifted "a great veil", according to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/13/celebrity-news/johnny-depp-shares-moment-that-changed-his-career/). His daughter Lily-Rose was born in 1999. He shares her and son Jack with Vanessa Paradis.

Depp said he had spent years asking himself why he was making movies, and that the answer arrived the moment he became a parent. Asked whether he still holds on to that purpose now his children are grown, he answered, "Oh, for sure."

Page Six also recalled his June 2025 Sunday Times interview, in which he described himself as an empty-nester and spoke fondly of being "Papa" while the family lived in the south of France. He is 63.

Rotten Tomatoes marked the release this week with a [ranking of his films by Tomatometer](https://editorial.rottentomatoes.com/guide/johnny-depp-movies-ranked/), running from *A Nightmare on Elm Street* and *Edward Scissorhands* to *Ebenezer*.

## What happens next

Final weekend figures are due on Sunday and will show whether *Ebenezer* clears $20 million. Deadline also expects roughly $20 million from international markets, including Australia, the United Kingdom, Japan and Germany.
