**Quick summary**

- [Alisha Boe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alisha_Boe) is the new global ambassador for YSL Beauty, according to The Cold Magazine.
- She fronts the Pink Glaze campaign for Black Opium, a fragrance that first launched in 2014.
- Pink Glaze adds strawberry to the scent's coffee, jasmine and vanilla notes.
- The campaign video was directed by C Prinz and is soundtracked by [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears).

Alisha Boe has moved from screen to scent. The Norwegian-Somali actor, known for *13 Reasons Why* and *The Buccaneers*, has been named the newest global ambassador for YSL Beauty and the face of its Black Opium Pink Glaze campaign, [The Cold Magazine](https://thecoldmagazine.co.uk/fashion/alisha-boe-the-new-face-of-ysls-black-opium/) reported on 30 October 2026.

## What is Black Opium Pink Glaze?

Black Opium first launched in 2014. Pink Glaze is its newest interpretation and brings strawberry into the familiar mix of coffee, jasmine and vanilla. Boe described it to the magazine as sweet with a little bite. "Strawberry takes me back to that bright, sweet world of childhood," she said, adding that the coffee note feels darker, warmer and more grown-up.

She linked the scent to getting ready for a night out: something sweet to start, then a last coffee before the evening begins. Boe also said she had been a fan of Black Opium for a while, which made the partnership feel natural.

## The campaign: nostalgia and friendship

The campaign video was directed by C Prinz. According to The Cold Magazine, it plays with nostalgia and drops viewers into a pink-tinted, Miami Vice-style world of vintage cars and diners. The soundtrack is by Britney Spears.

The magazine also says the campaign takes inspiration from a post-pandemic generation interested in human connection. Rather than treating a night out as a solitary pursuit, it puts friendship and shared experiences at the centre. Boe said female friendships have shaped her life. "They are my chosen family," she said.

## Boe on the perfect night out

The interview is built around nightlife, and Boe has clear views. Her ideal evening starts with a long dinner, usually involving seafood, then another cosy bar, some dancing and a karaoke room to finish.

Having grown up in Los Angeles and spent a lot of time in London, she compared the two cities. "LA knows how to make a night feel spontaneous; London knows how to make a night feel like it might go on forever," she said. In her view, both get it wrong when they try too hard to be cool.

The Cold Magazine puts that in context. It cites the Night Time Industries Association, which found Britons increasingly prefer to go out earlier, with bars earning much of their income between 5pm and 7pm. It also cites the London Nightlife Taskforce Report, which says nightlife venues contribute £21 billion a year to London's economy.

## From Oslo to Hollywood

Boe was born in Oslo and moved to Los Angeles at age seven. She said growing up between cultures gives her a perspective she carries into her work and personal life.

She gained international recognition at 19 as Jessica Davis in Netflix's *13 Reasons Why*, based on the novel by [Jay Asher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Asher). More recently she has played Conchita in Apple TV+'s *The Buccaneers*, a period drama about young American women facing the strict expectations of 1870s British society. After three seasons, she says Conchita is not fearless because nothing can hurt her. She is brave because she knows what is at stake.
