**Quick summary**

- Director [Tommy Wirkola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Wirkola) told the News-Herald that the first Violent Night was written partly to settle the "is Die Hard a Christmas movie?" debate.
- Violent Night 2 is set to reach theatres on 4 December, according to the News-Herald.
- A first sequel script, closer to Die Hard With a Vengeance, was dropped because it was too expensive and did not feel right.
- Almost all of the new film takes place in a fictional shopping centre, the Silver Bell Mall.

Tommy Wirkola says the Violent Night films exist because someone wanted to end an argument. In an interview published on 27 November by the [News-Herald](https://www.news-herald.com/2026/11/27/violent-night-2-director-tommy-wirkola-talks-franchises-die-hard-roots/), the director of the Christmas action comedy and its sequel, due in theatres on 4 December, said the original script was written as a deliberate "Die Hard" Christmas movie.

## Why Violent Night 2 leans on Die Hard

The question of whether the 1988 classic [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard), starring [Bruce Willis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Willis), counts as a holiday film has run for decades. Wirkola told the News-Herald he believes writers [Pat Casey and Josh Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Miller_and_Patrick_Casey) had exactly that in mind: end the debate by making a Die Hard story that is unmistakably about Christmas.

The 2022 film, [Violent Night](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violent_Night), put a worn-out Santa Claus inside a family compound under attack by mercenaries. Wirkola said the script was "so up my alley it was scary". The Norwegian filmmaker, whose earlier work includes the horror film [Dead Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Snow) and the 2013 fantasy action film Hansel & Gretel: Witch Hunters with [Jeremy Renner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Renner), said he knew he could handle the action, the humour and the gore. What drew him in was mixing those with a Hallmark-style Christmas feeling.

## The balance between gore and goodwill

Tone was his main worry. Wirkola said too much comedy drains the impact, while too much gore and action turns the film into something else, so he kept asking how far to push each scene.

He also wants audiences to leave in the right mood. Even with characters being cooked in ovens, he said, the aim in the edit is for people to feel ready for Christmas when they walk out of the cinema.

## David Harbour was the first choice

[David Harbour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harbour), known for [Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things) and Thunderbolts*, plays Santa. Wirkola said he was the first actor the team approached, after his name came up while going through agency lists. The director described a moment when everyone looked at each other and agreed he was perfect.

Harbour, he said, asked to do the physical work himself, including the fights and the training. Wirkola added that Harbour treats the character seriously, which matters because the audience has to believe in Santa and feel for him despite the chaos around him.

## A sequel that took time to crack

The sequel was not straightforward. Wirkola said the team knew they wanted [Kristen Bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kristen_Bell) involved as Mrs Claus, and a whole different script was developed first. That version was closer to the third Die Hard film, Die Hard With a Vengeance, with Santa pulled into a series of Christmas riddles across an entire city. It proved too expensive and too big, he said, and it did not feel completely right.

Casey and Miller then settled on a story in which Santa has learned the wrong lessons from the first film. The result is set almost entirely at the fictional Silver Bell Mall. The supporting cast includes [Jared Harris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jared_Harris), [Joe Pantoliano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Pantoliano), [Maxwell Friedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Jacob_Friedman), [Daniela Melchior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniela_Melchior) and [Andrew Bachelor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Bach).

Wirkola, who released the Netflix shark film Thrash in April, gave the interview from a mall in Manhattan Beach, California, which the studio had decorated for a press event.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does Violent Night 2 come out?
The News-Herald says it is set to reach theatres on 4 December. It did not give an Australian date.

### Who directs Violent Night 2?
Tommy Wirkola directs, as he did the 2022 original.

### Is Violent Night 2 a Die Hard-style film?
Yes. Wirkola says the franchise began as a deliberate Die Hard Christmas movie, and the sequel stays in that mould inside a shopping mall.

### Who plays Santa and Mrs Claus?
David Harbour plays Santa Claus and Kristen Bell plays Mrs Claus.
