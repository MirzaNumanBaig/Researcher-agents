**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios has acquired rights to the sci-fi thriller Bluefly in the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, France, Italy, Spain and other markets, according to Screen Daily.
- The film stars Russell Crowe and Priyanka Chopra Jonas, with Michael Chiklis newly joined, and is directed by Nimród Antal.
- Production has begun in Queensland and is expected to put $7.3 million into the state's economy, per Newsreel.
- Two of Crowe's older films, Master and Commander and A Good Year, are also back in the conversation this week.

[Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) has a new sci-fi thriller moving quickly towards audiences. Amazon MGM Studios has bought all rights to Bluefly in a long list of major territories, [Screen Daily reported](https://www.screendaily.com/news/amazon-mgm-studios-buys-major-territories-on-bluefly-with-russell-crowe-priyanka-chopra-jonas/5222267.article) on 11 November, just as cameras roll on the Gold Coast.

## Russell Crowe's Bluefly: where Amazon MGM has bought

According to Screen Daily, Amazon has picked up the UK, Australia and New Zealand, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Scandinavia, Benelux, Switzerland and Canada. Highland Film Group, which sells the film worldwide, has also closed deals elsewhere. Those include Latin America with California Filmes, Poland with Monolith Films, former Yugoslavia with Blitz Film, Romania with Karpat Media and Hungary with Media Squad. Picture Works has taken India and airline rights.

Selling this many markets while a film is only starting production is a useful signal. It means buyers are committing on the strength of the cast and the pitch, before anyone has seen a frame.

[Priyanka Chopra Jonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra) co-stars, and Screen Daily says Michael Chiklis has now joined the cast too. The story comes from David Frigerio, William Eubank and Carlyle Eubank. Nimród Antal directs.

## What Bluefly is about

The film follows a covert operation to recover a missing military aircraft in the Congo. [Newsreel describes](https://newsreel.com.au/article/arts/priyanka-chopra-and-russell-crow-film-on-the-gold-coast/) a US special operations unit sent to retrieve a missing high-tech aircraft. Crowe plays a former pilot named Benny, Chopra Jonas is a UN interpreter named Myla Reese, and Chiklis is a government operative named John Broyles.

As the team travels upriver into militia-held territory, it runs into a mysterious force hidden deep in the jungle. That is the sci-fi turn, and neither outlet says more about it.

## Filming on the Gold Coast

Filming is under way at Village Roadshow Studios and at locations across the Gold Coast. Newsreel reports that the production is expected to inject $7.3 million into the Queensland economy and directly employ around 190 crew.

It is Crowe's third Queensland-made film in recent years, after Land of Bad and his 2026 release The Get Out. Chopra Jonas is also back in the state after filming The Bluff in 2025. Our earlier coverage covers [the start of filming](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-bluefly-filming/) and [how The Get Out performed on Paramount+](https://moduscarry.com/russell-crowe-get-out-paramount-top-10/).

Queensland Arts Minister John-Paul Langbroek said the shoot would create work for local businesses and film professionals. Screen Queensland's Julie Eckersley pointed to the state's crews and their skill with stunts, weapons handling and water-based shoots. The production is backed by Australian government screen incentives, Screen Queensland's Production Attraction Strategy and Gold Coast screen programs.

## Two older Crowe films get a second look

Crowe's back catalogue is also drawing attention. Men's Journal, via [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/2003-film-ranked-among-russell-141302000.html), revisited Master and Commander, the 2003 film written and directed by [Peter Weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Weir) with [Paul Bettany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bettany) and [Billy Boyd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billy_Boyd). The piece notes that it opened against [Will Ferrell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Will_Ferrell)'s Elf, which dominated the box office that season. Reviews were generally positive, but [Slant Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slant_Magazine) called it "dull". The Guardian still ranks it among Crowe's best.

A second Men's Journal piece, [also on AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/gladiator-duo-ridley-scott-russell-191300000.html), marked 20 years since A Good Year opened in America on 10 November 2006. It reunited Crowe with director Ridley Scott after Gladiator. Los Angeles Times critic Kenneth Turan thought the story too thin, while Wine Folly includes the film in a list of 23 notable wine movies. Crowe said he spent two months in Provence and loved waking up there.
