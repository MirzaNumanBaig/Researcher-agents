**Quick summary**

- [Miranda Kerr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_Kerr), 43, says she was invited to walk the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show and declined.
- She explained her reasons on the "Not Skinny But Not Fat" podcast, released on Tuesday, 3 November, according to People.
- Kerr said she "seriously considered" it, but felt a knot in her stomach that only eased once she decided against it.
- She last walked the show in 2012 and made a surprise return to the runway for Stella McCartney in September.

The Miranda Kerr Victoria's Secret Fashion Show comeback will not happen this year. The Australian model, 43, told the "Not Skinny But Not Fat" podcast that she was asked back for the 2026 show, which fell on the 20th anniversary of her debut, and chose to say no. She had thought about it carefully first.

## The invitation and the knot in her stomach

Kerr told host Amanda Hirsch her first reaction was excitement. "When Victoria's Secret asked me, I was like, 'Ooh, that could be fun,'" she said, as quoted by [Yahoo News UK](https://uk.news.yahoo.com/miranda-kerr-explains-why-she-175635052.html), which carried Entertainment Weekly's report. She remembered how playful the shows had been and the idea of giving the brand "a wink" on the runway.

Then the nerves arrived. According to [People](https://people.com/why-miranda-kerr-declined-victorias-secret-fashion-show-return-12213968), she said she began meditating on how it would actually feel to stand there in her underwear. "Everyone's in their underwear," she said. "I'm 43. I have four boys."

She described a physical reaction that meditation did not fix. "I got this knot in my stomach," she said, and the only way to settle it was to tell herself she was not doing the show. Once she did, she said the knot "released". [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/03/style/miranda-kerr-was-seriously-considering-walking-in-the-vs-fashion-show-2026/) reports she felt at peace straight away, which is how she knew the decision was right.

## "That was then and this is now"

Kerr said plenty of people around her pushed the other way. Entertainment Weekly's report mentions "a lot of noise" and "feedback" from people trying to change her mind, and Page Six says many on her team wanted her to do it. She held her position anyway.

"It also feels like that was then and this is now, and I'm at a different stage in my life," she said. She kept the tone warm towards the brand. She spoke of "beautiful memories" and called the invitation an honour, adding that the brand "have so many other choices of all these beautiful young women."

One detail she gave Entertainment Weekly: the prospect of catching up with old friends such as Rosie Huntington-Whiteley was appealing, but it was not enough to change the answer.

## A Stella McCartney return a month earlier

The decision is more striking given what Kerr did a month before the lingerie show. In September she made a surprise return to the runway for [Stella McCartney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stella_McCartney), according to Page Six, nearly a decade after she last walked in a fashion show. On the podcast she said the nerves felt different before that appearance.

Her Victoria's Secret history is long. Kerr debuted in 2006, became an official Angel in 2007 and, per People, walked in 2007, 2008, 2009, 2011 and 2012 as well, six shows in all. In 2011 she wore the $2.5 million Fantasy Treasure Bra, set with nearly 3,400 gems including 142 carats of diamonds. This year's show, People notes, centred on a $50 million Fantasy Bra worn in a surprise appearance by [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner).

## What Kerr has said publicly, and what she is doing now

Kerr first addressed the invitation on Sunday, 18 October, in an Instagram post marking 20 years since her first show. People quotes her saying it "meant so much to be invited to return this year", but that she "ultimately felt in my heart it was right to cherish this chapter as it was." She sent her love to everyone in this year's show.

These days her focus is her skincare label, [Kora Organics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KORA_Organics), and her four sons, according to Page Six. She shares her eldest with ex-husband [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) and her three younger boys with husband Evan Spiegel, whom she married in 2017.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. Kerr has not announced another runway appearance, and the 2026 show has gone ahead without her.
