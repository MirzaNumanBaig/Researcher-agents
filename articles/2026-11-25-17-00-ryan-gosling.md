**Quick summary**

- *Star Wars: Starfighter* opens in cinemas on Friday 28 May 2027, according to SFFGazette.com's round-up of what is known.
- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) plays Kade Auberon, a cynical rogue whose past catches up with him.
- Shawn Levy directs from a script by Jonathan Tropper, and the story is set about five years after *The Rise of Skywalker*.
- The film was shot for IMAX, with IMAX 70mm prints planned for select locations.

[Ryan Gosling](https://moduscarry.com/tag/ryan-gosling/) has a release date, a character name and a long supporting cast for his first trip into the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) universe. *Star Wars: Starfighter* arrives in cinemas on 28 May 2027, according to a guide published by SFFGazette.com on 24 November 2026. Gosling plays Kade Auberon, the only role Lucasfilm has named so far.

## What is Kade Auberon?

Per SFFGazette.com, Kade is a cynical rogue whose past catches up with him. That is the extent of the official description. Lucasfilm has not said who he is running from or what the title's starfighter has to do with it, so any further talk about his arc is speculation.

Gosling is also an executive producer on the film, alongside Mary McLaglen, Josh McLaglen, Dave Filoni and Dan Levine, the report says. Shawn Levy produces with Kathleen Kennedy.

## Cast and crew

Gosling leads a cast that, according to the same guide, includes Flynn Gray, Matt Smith, Mia Goth, Aaron Pierre, Jamael Westman, Daniel Ings and Amy Adams. We have already covered how [Gosling helped Aaron Pierre land the Starfighter role](https://moduscarry.com/ryan-gosling-starfighter-aaron-pierre/).

Levy directs and Jonathan Tropper wrote the screenplay. Production began in the United Kingdom around the time the cast was announced in August 2025, SFFGazette.com reports.

## A new corner of the timeline

The film is a standalone, original story set about five years after *Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker*, in a period not previously shown on screen. That gives the writers room to move without leaning on existing characters, though Lucasfilm has not said how much it will connect to earlier films.

The release date is also symbolic. 28 May 2027 falls on the 50th-anniversary weekend of the original *Star Wars*. The 1977 film itself returns to cinemas in IMAX on 19 February 2027, according to the guide.

## The wider rollout

Lucasfilm is building a merchandise and publishing push around the film. SFFGazette.com lists an Abrams art book, an expanded novelisation by Delilah Dawson, a DK visual dictionary, a Marvel prequel mini-series called *The Rock Pirate of Kaneen*, Little Golden Books, junior novels and a colouring book.

## What happens next

The next date to watch is 19 February 2027, when the original film returns in IMAX. Starfighter follows on 28 May 2027. A trailer date has not been confirmed in the source material, and this article will be updated if Lucasfilm announces one. This report rests on a single outlet's summary, so details may change.
