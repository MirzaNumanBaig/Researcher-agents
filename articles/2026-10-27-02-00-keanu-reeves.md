**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves), 62, is reportedly attached to star in Netflix's "Maelstrom", according to Page Six.
- [Alan Ritchson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Ritchson) had been in talks for the lead but has dropped out.
- [Jaume Collet-Serra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaume_Collet-Serra), who made Netflix hit "Carry-On", is set to direct.
- The script was first sold to Warner Bros. 33 years ago, in the wake of [Die Hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Hard).
- Representatives for Reeves and Netflix did not comment on the report.

Keanu Reeves is reportedly the new lead of "Maelstrom", a Netflix action thriller built on a script that first went on sale in 1993. Page Six Hollywood [reported on 26 October](https://pagesix.com/2026/10/26/hollywood/keanu-reeves-to-star-in-netflix-revival-of-die-hard-inspired-action-movie-first-pitched-in-1993/) that the "John Wick" star has replaced Ritchson, the "Reacher" actor who was recently in talks. Neither Reeves' nor Netflix's representatives commented, and Ritchson's did not respond.

## What Maelstrom is about

According to Page Six, the story follows a federal marshal assigned to guard the fiancée of an arms dealer. She is seeking witness protection and preparing to testify against him. Everything happens over one night as a hurricane arrives and knocks out communications, leaving the pair to fight off the dealer's henchmen and the weather.

That one-location, one-night setup is the Die Hard template. Page Six cites a 1993 Variety piece saying screenwriter Greg Fitzpatrick's script was part of a studio hunt for new action films in the mould of the 1988 original, for stars such as [Steven Seagal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Seagal), [Sylvester Stallone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Stallone) and [Clint Eastwood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clint_Eastwood). Warners bought it as a spec script. It then sat for 33 years.

## Why Netflix dusted it off

Collet-Serra has done this before with the streamer. Page Six says "Carry-On" had been dormant for 11 years before Netflix revived it with [Taron Egerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taron_Egerton) and [Jason Bateman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Bateman), and that it became the third most viewed film in Netflix history.

Netflix executive Ori Marmur, who revived that film, reportedly asked top producers to bring their best unmade, high-concept projects to the platform. "Maelstrom" is the result. Former Warner Bros. film boss Jeff Robinov is producing and was involved in the original 1993 sale, when he was an agent at ICM. The report says it is the first big title in a Netflix deal he signed.

Mark Bianculli has freshened up the script. He also wrote the upcoming remake of Stallone's 1993 film "Cliffhanger", which stars [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Lily James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lily_James) and is also being directed by Collet-Serra.

## Reeves and the John Wick franchise

The casting news landed as another Reeves title returned to the spotlight. [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/keanu-reeves-john-wick-action-thriller-returns-prime-video/) reports that "John Wick: Chapter 4" is back in Prime Video's top-10 most-watched list, citing FlixPatrol. The 2023 film holds 94 per cent from critics and 93 per cent from audiences on Rotten Tomatoes.

It is also the franchise's biggest earner. CBR lists $447.3 million at the box office against a $100 million budget, ahead of "Chapter 3 - Parabellum" (2019) at $326.7 million and "Chapter 2" (2017) at $174.3 million. Reeves returned as Wick in the 2025 spinoff "From the World of John Wick: Ballerina".

A fifth film is not locked in. Lionsgate motion picture group president Joe Drake told The Hollywood Reporter there is "a will and an openness", but "no guarantee", adding that Reeves and director Chad Stahelski are protective of the audience. CBR also notes a second spinoff, centred on Donnie Yen's character Caine, is due in 2027.

## What happens next

Nothing about "Maelstrom" has been officially confirmed, and no release date, shooting schedule or supporting cast has been reported. Page Six says Netflix and Reeves' team declined to comment, so the casting remains a trade report until either side announces it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Keanu Reeves confirmed for Maelstrom?
Not officially. Page Six reports he is attached to star, but representatives for Reeves and Netflix did not comment.

### Who was meant to star in Maelstrom before Reeves?
Alan Ritchson, known for "Reacher", was recently in talks for the lead role but has dropped out, according to Page Six.

Jaume Collet-Serra, who also directed Netflix's "Carry-On" and is directing the "Cliffhanger" remake.

Lionsgate says it is exploring ideas but has given no guarantee, per Joe Drake's comments to The Hollywood Reporter, as quoted by CBR.
