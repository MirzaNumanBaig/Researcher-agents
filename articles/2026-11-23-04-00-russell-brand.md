**Quick summary**

- Tabloid reports say [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) is weighing a memoir and a streaming series about her marriage to [Russell Brand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Brand). This is unconfirmed.
- The claim traces to an unnamed source quoted by the Daily Mail, as relayed by the Irish Star. Perry has not commented publicly.
- Brand was sentenced on 9 November to 10 years and six months in prison for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
- Perry was photographed with [Justin Trudeau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Trudeau) in Venice on Friday, as old court testimony from Brand resurfaced.

Katy Perry is reportedly in "active discussions" about telling her side of her marriage to Russell Brand in a book and a streaming series, according to a source quoted by the Daily Mail and repeated by the [Irish Star](https://www.irishstar.com/culture/entertainment/katy-perry-russell-brand-book-37890225). The report appeared on 21 November. Neither Perry nor her representatives have confirmed it, so it should be read as an unverified claim.

## What the reports actually say

The Irish Star says an unnamed source told the Daily Mail that Perry has received offers over the years but would not consider any until after Brand's trial. The same source described the marriage as "a very dark period" in her life. The outlet adds that insiders claim her team has spoken with streaming platforms, a detail it attributes to the Mirror US.

There is no named person, no platform statement and no publisher confirmation in any of this. The figures in circulation come from a PR commentator, Chad Teixeira, who told the Mirror US that a book could fetch roughly US$3 million to US$5 million and a series US$3 million to US$7 million. Those are one adviser's estimates, not reported offers.

## The legal backdrop

Brand, 51, was sentenced on 9 November after being convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault against four women between 2004 and 2009, as reported by the [Mirror US](https://www.themirror.com/entertainment/celebrity-news/russell-brand-deposition-katy-perry-2117679). The Mirror reports he is now held at HMP Littlehey in Cambridgeshire after a transfer from HMP Wandsworth. [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/entertainment/celebrity/russell-brand-katy-perry-celebrity-comments-crime-838350-20261121) notes that any mention of Perry during his trial was forbidden.

Perry and Brand met while working on *Get Him to the Greek* and married in Rajasthan, India, on 23 October 2010. Brand filed for divorce on 30 December 2011, according to the Mirror, and LADbible puts the divorce as finalised in 2012.

## Why the marriage is back in the news

Much of the renewed attention comes from a 2025 deposition filed in a separate New York civil case. A woman identified as Jane Doe alleges Brand assaulted her during the filming of *Arthur*. Brand has denied the allegations, and [TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/11/20/russell-brand-forgets-katy-perry-wedding-date/) reports that a New York judge recently refused to dismiss negligence claims against Warner Bros. and other production companies in the case.

Asked in the deposition whether he was married when *Arthur* was shot, Brand said it would not be hard to find out because it would be "on the internet somewhere". He also described himself as "drowning in love" during the marriage. Several outlets, including the Daily Mail first, picked up the exchange. It shows how his own words about Perry have become a fresh talking point, but it says nothing about any book.

## Perry in Venice

[IndulgExpress](https://www.indulgexpress.com/entertainment/celebs/2026/Nov/22/katy-perry-and-justin-trudeau-hold-hands-in-venice-after-russell-brands-weird-marriage-confession) reports that Perry and Trudeau, the former Canadian prime minister, were seen holding hands on arrival in Venice on Friday. They have been linked since July 2025, and the outlet says they were also seen in Paris, Milan and Spain over the past week. Before that, Perry was in a relationship with [Orlando Bloom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orlando_Bloom) from January 2016 to June 2025.
