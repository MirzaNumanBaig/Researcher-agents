**Quick summary**

- Amazon founder Jeff Bezos says the five James Bond finalists, including Callum Turner, each filmed three scenes in full costume on real sets.
- Robert Pattinson was chosen over Turner, Jack Lowden, Nicholas Hoult and Josh O'Connor, according to ThePrint and People.
- Esquire India reports the screen tests took place in London in late October.
- The next Bond film is expected in cinemas in 2028, with production in 2027, though those dates are not official.

[Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) did not win the role of [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), but a new account from Amazon's Jeff Bezos shows how seriously the studio took his audition. Bezos says Turner and four other finalists each filmed three scenes in costume before [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was named the next 007 this month.

## How the Callum Turner Bond audition worked

Bezos described the process as "real old-fashioned screen tests", according to [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/entertainment/jeff-bezos-says-he-was-biased-against-robert-pattinson-playing-james-bond/3101723/). He said they used full costume, real sets and other actors, and that each finalist played three different scenes.

The five names reported are Turner, Pattinson, Lowden, Hoult and O'Connor. [Esquire India](https://www.esquireindia.co.in/entertainment/at-the-movies/james-bond-actors-list-robert-pattinson-jack-lowden-josh-oconnor-nicholas-hoult-007-screen-test) says the tests ran in London in late October and covered stunts, dialogue and camera presence. It adds that director Denis Villeneuve and casting director Nina Gold oversaw them.

ThePrint notes that one classic test scene comes from *From Russia with Love* (1963), where Bond leaves the bathroom and finds a Soviet agent in his bed. It does not say which scenes this year's finalists played.

## Why Pattinson won

Bezos said he was at first "biased against" Pattinson because he is already so famous. The role has traditionally gone to less established actors. The screen test changed his mind. "There was something special about Rob and his performance, and it was kind of immediate," he said.

Bezos also said the selection team gravitated towards Villeneuve, who directed Pattinson in *Dune: Part Three*. ThePrint adds that the Broccoli family, which produced the films for decades, no longer holds creative control after Amazon MGM's takeover, and that several reports say it was not closely involved in the choice.

Pattinson, 40, said in a statement carried by [People](https://people.com/robert-pattinson-looking-like-james-bond-suit-red-carpet-photos-12260481) that being trusted with 007 is "the honor of a lifetime". The Guardian, in a light profile, notes that the news is "bad news" for Turner, [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Josh O'Connor.

## Where this leaves Turner

Nothing in the sources suggests Turner's audition went badly. Bezos said the team was impressed with all five actors. Our earlier report looked at [why Turner lost the Bond race to Pattinson](https://moduscarry.com/callum-turner-bond-screen-test-2/), and you can follow everything else on the [Callum Turner tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/callum-turner/).

His most recent big-screen release was *One Night Only*, directed by Will Gluck and released on 7 August. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Night_Only_(2026_film)), it earned about US$25.2 million against a US$25 million budget, and holds a 44% Rotten Tomatoes score. A Bond shortlist place keeps him in the conversation for bigger roles.

## What happens next

Esquire India reports that the new Bond film is due in 2028 and goes into production in 2027, but says those dates are not official. Pattinson's casting is the first major step of the Amazon MGM era.
