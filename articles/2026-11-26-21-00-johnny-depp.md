**Quick summary**

- Johnny Depp Pirates 6 talk grew after Puck's Matthew Belloni said on 24 November that *Ebenezer* has grossed about US$81 million worldwide.
- The film reportedly cost less than US$40 million, and Belloni says Disney is now keener to bring Johnny Depp back for a sixth *Pirates of the Caribbean* film.
-  Belloni reportedly expects a formal greenlight for development soon.
- Depp's next film, the Lionsgate thriller *Day Drinker*, has been rated R and opens on 26 March 2027.

[Johnny Depp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Depp) has had a good fortnight at the box office. According to [Toy People](https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=117027), which summarised a podcast appearance by Puck co-founder [Matthew Belloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Belloni) on 24 November, Depp's Christmas fantasy *Ebenezer* has reached US$81 million worldwide and Disney is warming to a sixth *Pirates of the Caribbean* film with him in it.

## Ebenezer's numbers in context

*Ebenezer* opened on 12 November. Belloni's US$81 million sits close to the US$79.6 million in the film's [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebenezer_(2026_film)).

The budget is the more telling number. Toy People reports the production cost under US$40 million, and Wikipedia lists US$38 million. On those figures the film has grossed roughly twice its cost, although cinemas keep a share of ticket sales, so that ratio is not the same as profit. 

Critics have been split. Wikipedia records a Rotten Tomatoes score of 55 per cent and a Metacritic score of 46 out of 100 from 38 critics. Peter Bradshaw of The Guardian gave it three stars and called it a serviceable, faithful adaptation in which Depp is pretty good. The New York Times called it bland.

Reviews from smaller outlets lean warmer. Writing for the [Lehigh Valley Press](https://www.lvpnews.com/20261125/at-the-movies-ebenezer-an-in-depp-th-performance/), one critic called Depp a great Scrooge under heavy prosthetics and said he deserves an Oscar nomination. Ti West directs, adapting the [Charles Dickens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dickens) novella.

## What the Johnny Depp Pirates 6 report says

Earlier this month Depp said he would be willing to return to the franchise and hoped to give it the most satisfying ending possible, according to Toy People. That is a notable change from the position he took when the studio parted ways with him years ago.

Belloni's argument, as relayed by Toy People, is that *Ebenezer*'s result shows audiences are ready to see Depp again, and that this has made Disney more determined to move on a sixth film. The rest of the cast and the story are not settled. Belloni said he expects the studio to formally greenlight development in the near future.

Treat that as an industry read, not an announcement. We have not seen a statement from Disney, and a greenlight for development is a long way from a start date.

## Day Drinker gets its rating

Depp's next release has a firm date. *Day Drinker* has received an R rating from the Motion Picture Association for strong bloody violence, language and brief drug use, according to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/movies/news/2230534-johnny-depp-comeback-movie-abenezer-surprising-mpa-rating). Lionsgate opens it on 26 March 2027.

Marc Webb directs from a script by Zach Dean. Depp plays a mysterious guest named Kelly on a private yacht, where he crosses paths with a bartender played by Madelyn Cline, grieving the loss of her lover. Penélope Cruz plays a criminal figure drawn into their story. ComingSoon describes it as a supernatural revenge thriller with themes of revenge and the afterlife.

[iHorror](https://ihorror.com/day-drinker-rated-r-johnny-depp-lionsgate/) notes that the rating description does not reveal what Depp's character actually is, and that the marketing has leaned on a full moon and a growling soundtrack. Lionsgate has sold it as an action thriller. See also our earlier coverage of [Day Drinker's rating](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-day-drinker-rated-r/) and [Ebenezer's box office run](https://moduscarry.com/johnny-depp-ebenezer-box-office-3/).

## What happens next

Watch for two things. The first is whether Disney confirms a development greenlight for the next Pirates film, and who else is attached. The second is how *Ebenezer* holds through the rest of its holiday run, which will show whether the US$81 million is a strong start or close to a ceiling.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Johnny Depp returning for Pirates of the Caribbean 6?

Nothing is official. Depp said earlier this month he would be willing to return, and Puck's Matthew Belloni says Disney is more interested after *Ebenezer*'s results. Disney has not confirmed it.

### How much has Ebenezer made?

Belloni cited about US$81 million worldwide, while Wikipedia lists US$79.6 million. The reported budget is under US$40 million.

### When is Day Drinker released?

Lionsgate has set 26 March 2027. The film is rated R for strong bloody violence, language and brief drug use.
