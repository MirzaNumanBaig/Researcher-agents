**Quick summary**

- MobLand, the Paramount+ crime thriller starring [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy), is No. 1 on the streamer's global rankings, according to MovieWeb.
- The series has now spent more than 260 days on the charts.
- Season 2 premiered in September 2026 and holds a 90% critics' score on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), against 77% for Season 1.
- Season 3 has been confirmed, and MovieWeb reports that it has already entered production. There is no release date yet.

Tom Hardy's gangster drama MobLand has reached the top of Paramount+'s worldwide chart straight after its Season 2 finale, according to [MovieWeb](https://movieweb.com/mobland-tom-hardy-stream-paramount-november-2026/). The latest chart update also shows the show has now been charting for more than 260 days, a long run for a series that only arrived in March 2025.

## What MobLand is about

Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, a loyal fixer working for the Harrigan family as they try to take over London's criminal underworld. [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) and [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) play the family's heads, Conrad and Maeve. [Joanne Froggatt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Froggatt) and [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) also appear in the second season.

The series is produced by [Guy Ritchie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Ritchie), who directed some episodes in both seasons. MovieWeb notes that Hardy and Ritchie had worked together only once before, on the 2008 film RocknRolla, where Hardy played Handsome Bob. The writer describes the pairing as a natural one that has simply not happened often.

## How the show has performed

According to MovieWeb, Season 1 broke launch records for the streamer and came close to matching the success of Taylor Sheridan's shows on the platform. Critics were more measured: Season 1 sits at 77% on Rotten Tomatoes, with reviews singling out Hardy's imposing screen presence.

Season 2 did better with reviewers. It carries a 90% score, and critics quoted by MovieWeb called the return "bigger in every way possible". That is a 13-point jump on the first season's rating.

It is worth reading the headline claim carefully. MovieWeb's own figures describe a No. 1 position on Paramount+'s global rankings, which is a statement about one service, not every streamer.

## The behind-the-scenes noise

Before Season 2 aired, rumours circulated that Hardy might leave because of creative differences with showrunner [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth). Those reports were never confirmed in the source we reviewed, and MovieWeb says the reported production issues were resolved. Hardy has since suggested the show could run for "years".

Hardy is also known for films such as [Mad Max: Fury Road](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mad_Max%3A_Fury_Road), Inception and The Dark Knight Rises, as well as television work including Peaky Blinders and Taboo. ## What happens next

Season 3 is official. Brosnan and Mirren are set to return, and Hardy will come back as Harry. MovieWeb reports that the new season has already entered production, though Paramount+ has not announced a premiere date.

The Season 2 finale ended on a cliffhanger that MovieWeb says will change how Harry operates. Whether Ritchie directs more episodes has not been confirmed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is MobLand really the biggest show in the world?
Not by the evidence available. MovieWeb reports it is No. 1 on Paramount+'s global rankings. That does not cover other streaming services.

### Is there a MobLand Season 3?
Yes. The series has been renewed, and MovieWeb says production has begun. No release date has been announced.

### Who stars in MobLand?
Tom Hardy plays fixer Harry Da Souza, alongside Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren as Conrad and Maeve Harrigan.
