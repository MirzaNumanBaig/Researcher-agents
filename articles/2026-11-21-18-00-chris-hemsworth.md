**Quick summary**

- [Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) posted a conditioning workout to social media on 19 November.
- The session ran from an air bike sprint to a SkiErg and then a rowing machine, according to Kulturistika.
- He has said the training is part of his preparation for *Extraction 3*.
- The film has no official release date. Kulturistika reports it could arrive in 2027, but that is not confirmed.

Chris Hemsworth has shared a new training clip as he prepares for *Extraction 3*. The 43-year-old Australian posted the video on 19 November, and it shows a three-machine conditioning circuit rather than a heavy lifting session, according to [Kulturistika](https://www.kulturistika.com/en/magazine/fitness-espresso/at-43-chris-hemsworth-is-in-great-shape).

## What Hemsworth did in the Extraction 3 workout

The clip moves through three stations in turn. He starts with a hard sprint on an air bike, shifts to a SkiErg, which mimics the pull of cross-country skiing, and finishes on a rowing machine.

Each machine works as a stand-alone cardio tool. Chained together, they load the legs, back, shoulders and heart in different ways without a break in between. Kulturistika notes that Hemsworth did not share the intervals, rest periods or number of rounds, so the clip is a glimpse of his routine rather than a programme anyone can copy.

That fits what the *Extraction* films ask of their lead. The series is known for long, physical action sequences, and Kulturistika argues that endurance, recovery and coordination matter as much as muscle size for scenes like that.

## Why endurance matters more now

The clip lines up with how Hemsworth has described his training in his forties. According to [Vietnam.vn](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/co-bap-luc-luong-cua-than-sam-chris-hemsworth), he now mixes weights with functional work: bench presses, pull-ups and kettlebell squats, alongside running, endurance sessions and boxing.

The same report says he trains six or seven days a week, though he has admitted his body aches more than it used to. His current schedule is built in cycles, with a weights day followed by rest or cardio. Strength days are split into pushing, pulling and legs, and each session lasts about 45 minutes, adjusted to how he feels.

It is a change from his bulkiest period. Vietnam.vn reports that for *Thor: Love and Thunder* he trained for a year and ate about ten meals a day, each of roughly 450 calories, to add size. He reportedly does not want to repeat that, partly because the extra weight got in the way of surfing, one of his favourite pastimes.

## Recovery is part of the plan

The same source says he pays close attention to recovery. His routine reportedly includes sauna and cold-water sessions, meditation and breathing exercises, and about an hour away from screens before bed, with some reading in the evening. His trainer, Luke Zocchi, is said to encourage him to wind down after heavy lifting days.

For his return as Thor in *Avengers: Doomsday*, the report adds, he is no longer chasing maximum size. He spends extra time on shoulders and arms because the costume shows off the upper body, and he eats four to five meals a day instead of ten. Not much is confirmed. Kulturistika says the third film has no official release date, and that "according to available information" it could land in 2027. It stresses that the date is not definitive, and we have not seen a studio announcement to confirm it. Treat the timing as unconfirmed until Netflix says otherwise.
