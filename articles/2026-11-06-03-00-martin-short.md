**Quick summary**

- [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) is a guest on "Merry Christmas", the final track of Alanis Morissette's first holiday album.
- The album, *Good Weather for Immaculate Conception*, is released on 6 November 2026 through Epiphany Music and Virgin Music.
- Short's longtime collaborator Steve Martin appears on a different song, the lead single "Beautiful Star of Bethlehem".
- The record has 14 tracks, including two new originals.

## Martin Short on Alanis Morissette's Christmas album

Martin Short features on "Merry Christmas", the closing song of Alanis Morissette's debut holiday record, which arrives on 6 November 2026. According to [Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2514139/alanis-morissette-announces-new-christmas-album-feat-brandi-carlile-steve-martin-martin-short/music), the album was announced on 9 October, and Short's turn on the last track is his only appearance on it.

[SPIN](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2026/10/alanis-morissette-holiday-album/) reports that the album is called *Good Weather for Immaculate Conception* and runs to 14 songs. The Canadian singer-songwriter has released holiday material over several years, and this record collects those songs alongside new ones.

## Where "Merry Christmas" sits in the tracklist

Both outlets list "Merry Christmas (feat. Martin Short)" at number 14, so it is the final song on the record. Neither report says what the track sounds like, whether it is a duet or a spoken feature, or who wrote it. Those details will become clear once the album is released.

The tracklist leans on well-known seasonal material. According to SPIN, it includes covers of John Lennon and Yoko Ono's "Happy Xmas (War Is Over)", Joni Mitchell's "River" and Wham!'s "Last Christmas", alongside traditional pieces such as "O Holy Night", "What Child Is This?" and "Silent Night".

## Steve Martin and the other guests

Short's friend and *Only Murders in the Building* co-star Steve Martin appears elsewhere. He joins Brandi Carlile on "Beautiful Star of Bethlehem", a reworking of a gospel hymn most famously recorded by the Judds, according to Stereogum. That song is the lead single and was shared on the day of the announcement.

The rest of the guest list, as both outlets set it out:

- Amythyst Kiah on "Momma Mary"
- Trans Voices, described by SPIN as the UK's first professional trans choir, on "O Tannenbaum"
- Joy Oladokun on "Silent Night"

Two songs are newly written: "To an Empath at Christmas Time" and "Christmas on the Dancefloor".

## A busy week for Morissette

The release lands in the middle of a live run. SPIN notes that her *Butterfly With a Machete* residency returns to Los Angeles, with the latest stint starting on 5 November at YouTube Theater, one day before the album comes out. She was also inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in June, according to the same report.

## What happens next

The album is out on 6 November 2026, and Morissette's Los Angeles shows begin the day before. Listeners will then be able to hear exactly what Short contributes to the closing track. "Beautiful Star of Bethlehem" can already be streamed.
