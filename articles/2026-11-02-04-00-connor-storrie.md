**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) earned an Emmy nomination for hosting [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) on 28 February 2026, not for [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).
- According to Martin Cid Magazine, the series was ineligible for the Primetime Emmys because it was financed in Canada, with HBO Max only distributing.
- Storrie did not win at the Creative Arts ceremony in September.
- His upcoming work includes A24's Peaked and Please, the thriller Turpentine and Sarah Polley's The Bell Jar.

The role that made Connor Storrie famous never had a chance at an Emmy. [Martin Cid Magazine](https://www.martincid.com/people/connor-storrie/) reports that his turn as Russian hockey star Ilya Rozanov in Heated Rivalry fell outside the Primetime Emmy rules, so his only nomination came from a single night hosting Saturday Night Live. He was nominated for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series and did not win.

## Why Heated Rivalry missed the Emmys

Heated Rivalry premiered on Crave in Canada and on HBO Max in the United States in November 2025. It runs for six episodes and follows a secret relationship between two rival hockey captains.

According to Martin Cid Magazine, the Canadian financing is the problem. HBO Max joined only as a distributor, and the Primetime Emmys require an American co-production in place before cameras roll. The series was then submitted to the International Emmys and missed there as well.

It did win elsewhere. The cast took the ACTRA Toronto award for series ensemble, and the [Television Critics Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_Critics_Association) nominated Storrie for Individual Achievement in Drama.

## From waiting tables to Ilya

Storrie arrived at the part with few of the character's skills. Variety, as cited by Martin Cid Magazine, reported that he could not skate, did not speak Russian and was waiting tables at the Laurel Grill in Culver City when the offer came. He learned enough Russian to build the accent.

In the chemistry reads, he backed [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) for the role of [Shane Hollander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Hollander). Williams was the third actor Storrie read with, and Jacob Tierney's adaptation of Rachel Reid's Game Changers novels came to rest on that pairing.

Storrie was born in Aurora, Colorado, on 22 February 2000 and grew up in Odessa, Texas, where a performing arts school and competitive tumbling gave him his first stage. He later attended Westlake High School in Southern California, spent a junior year in France, and took improv classes at The Groundlings in Los Angeles.

## The SNL nomination

Storrie hosted Saturday Night Live on 28 February 2026. According to the same profile, he was the only host of the season to receive a nomination. He attended the September Creative Arts ceremony with his mother but did not take the trophy.

The profile also notes a second challenge. Much of the coverage treats him as half of a pair, with his name rarely printed without Williams' beside it. The two have also voiced rival fae princes in the audio series Ember & Ice for the platform Quinn.

## What he has lined up

Before the Heated Rivalry press tour, Storrie wrapped [Transaction Planet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transaction_Planet), a micro-budget feature he wrote, directed and shot entirely on an iPhone. It has no release date.

The offers since then pull in several directions:

- Peaked, Molly Gordon's comedy for A24, where he joined the ensemble.
- Turpentine, Craig Zobel's thriller, shot around Atlanta in May and June opposite [Melissa McCarthy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melissa_McCarthy) and produced by [Rian Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rian_Johnson)'s company T-Street.
- Please, a period film for A24 from [Halina Reijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halina_Reijn), led by Gracie Abrams.
- Criminal Minds, where a planned single episode grew into a four-episode arc as Lance Kingston in season nineteen.
- The Bell Jar, Sarah Polley's adaptation for Focus Features, in which he plays Marco, the blind date of Billie Eilish's Esther Greenwood. Production wrapped in September.

Earlier, he played a young Arkham inmate in the final scene of Joker: Folie à Deux. His first feature was the queer coming-of-age sports drama Riley.

## What happens next

The profile says Ilya is not finished with him, pointing to a second season of Heated Rivalry, though the source text available to us cuts off before the details. Transaction Planet, Turpentine, Please and The Bell Jar all still lack confirmed release dates in the reporting we read.

## Frequently asked questions

### Was Connor Storrie nominated for an Emmy for Heated Rivalry?

No. He was nominated for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Comedy Series for hosting Saturday Night Live. Heated Rivalry did not qualify for the Primetime Emmys because it was financed in Canada.

### Did Connor Storrie win the Emmy?

No. He did not win at the Creative Arts ceremony in September, according to Martin Cid Magazine.

### What is Connor Storrie doing next?

He has roles in Peaked, Turpentine, Please and The Bell Jar, plus a four-episode Criminal Minds arc. A second season of Heated Rivalry is also mentioned in the source profile.
