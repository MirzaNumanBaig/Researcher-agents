**Quick summary**

- A report published on 22 October says Australian actor Jacob Elordi is back in contention to play James Bond.
- According to The Express Tribune, citing HuffPost UK, journalist Jeff Sneider claims screen testing is complete and Elordi is expected to get the role.
- The claim is unconfirmed. Neither Elordi nor Amazon MGM has publicly commented on the casting.
- Denis Villeneuve is set to direct the next film, with Steven Knight attached to write it.

[Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) has re-emerged as a leading name in the race to succeed Daniel Craig as [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to a report from [The Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2636300/jacob-elordi-emerges-as-james-bond-favourite-amid-fresh-007-casting-rumours) on 22 October. The Australian actor is said to be the expected pick. But the claim rests on a single newsletter, and nobody involved has confirmed it.

## Where does the Jacob Elordi Bond rumour come from?

The Express Tribune says entertainment journalist [Jeff Sneider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Sneider) made the claim in his newsletter, The InSneider. It reports that, according to HuffPost UK, Sneider says the screen testing process is finished and that Elordi is now expected to land the part. He also suggested an announcement could come soon.

That is a long way from a casting decision. The outlet is explicit that these reports remain unconfirmed, and that neither Elordi nor Amazon MGM has said anything publicly.

It is also not the first time the name has surfaced. Our earlier piece, [Jacob Elordi Reportedly Not in the Final James Bond Screen Tests](https://moduscarry.com/jacob-elordi-reportedly-not-in-the-final-james-bond-screen-tests/), covered a report pointing the other way. The two accounts sit awkwardly together, which says plenty about how unsettled the story is.

## Who else has been linked to the role?

Speculation has intensified in recent weeks, per The Express Tribune, with British actors [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) and Jack Barton also named in connection with the part.

Earlier reports had put [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) and [Paul Mescal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Mescal) in the frame as well, alongside [Harris Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harris_Dickinson). That is at least six names in circulation, and the outlet says the shifting list suggests the search for Craig's successor is far from settled.

Whether any of them has actually been tested is not something the source can say. It only relays what the newsletter claims about Elordi.

## What has Jacob Elordi done so far?

The Express Tribune traces Elordi's rise to The Kissing Booth film series, followed by wider recognition for playing Nate Jacobs in Euphoria. His later film work, it notes, includes Saltburn and Frankenstein.

His growing profile, the outlet says, has made him a recurring name in talk about major Hollywood roles. For an actor who started in teen romance films, a Bond casting would be a sizeable jump in scale, though that is context rather than anything the sources confirm will happen. You can follow everything we publish on him in our [Jacob Elordi coverage](https://moduscarry.com/tag/jacob-elordi/).

## Who is making the next Bond film?

The next instalment in the official series will be directed by [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), known for Dune and Blade Runner 2049. [Steven Knight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Knight), the creator of Peaky Blinders, is attached to write the screenplay.

Producer Amy Pascal has previously said the creative team wants a fresh direction for the character. The Express Tribune quotes her describing the aim as finding someone "stirring and exciting and different".

That brief is broad enough to fit almost any of the names above, which is part of why the rumours keep shifting.

## What happens next

No date has been given for an announcement. Sneider's suggestion that one could arrive soon is the only timing on offer, and it is unverified. Until Amazon MGM or the filmmakers speak, the identity of the next James Bond remains unknown.
