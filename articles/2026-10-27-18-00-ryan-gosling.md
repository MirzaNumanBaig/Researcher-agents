**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s band Dead Man's Bones has released a new single, "Lost in the Night", with a music video.
- It is the duo's first new song in 17 years, since their only album in 2009, according to Deadline.
- The black-and-white video stars Rain Spencer and Noah Jupe and arrives just ahead of Halloween.
- No album has been announced, though Showbiz411 speculates one is likely.

Ryan Gosling has brought his side project back from the grave. Dead Man's Bones, the band he formed with screenwriter Zach Shields, released "Lost in the Night" and a Halloween-themed video on 27 October, ending a 17-year gap in new music, [Deadline reports](https://deadline.com/2026/10/ryan-gosling-dead-mans-bones-new-song-music-video-1237194091/).

## What Dead Man's Bones has released

The duo put out one self-titled album in 2009, recorded with the Silverlake Conservatory Children's Choir. Deadline says "Lost in the Night" is the first song they have shared since. Showbiz411 notes the release is on the independent label Anti Records and calls the track moody.

The band's Instagram first teased new music on 22 October, according to Deadline, and Gosling's partner, actress Eva Mendes, collaborated on the post that promoted the video.

## The video

The clip stars [Rain Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_Spencer), known for *The Summer I Turned Pretty*, and [Noah Jupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Jupe), whose credits include *Hamnet*. Deadline describes an opening in black and white, with Spencer walking through a graveyard before the video cuts to Jupe lying on a bench.

Shields has worked with both actors before. Deadline reports that he and Spencer collaborated on the play *Dad's Leg*, which ran at Los Angeles' Hudson Theatre from 20 February to 2 March. Spencer and Jupe also teamed up on a theatrical workshop of another play written by Shields.

## A song with an archive trail

The track is not entirely new to fans who followed the band early. South African outlet 2oceansvibe points to an LA Weekly review of a 26 June 2010 show at Eagle Rock's Center for the Arts, which listed "Lost in the Night" as the opening song. Gosling and Shields appeared with a children's choir again that night.

That fits the band's original idea. According to 2oceansvibe, which cites a Rolling Stone interview from the time of the debut, Shields said the children were meant to do all the singing, and the two men only became vocalists while working out the parts. The pair also played almost all the instruments themselves. Their Los Angeles shows at the Bob Baker Marionette Theater featured dancing neon skeletons and glowing ghosts, and the North American tour was planned around a different local choir in each city.

## Where Gosling's career stands

Showbiz411 reminds readers that Gosling is better known to many for *La La Land*, *Barbie* and *Project Hail Mary*. He performed "I'm Just Ken" at the 2024 Oscars, and Deadline notes he has *Star Wars: Starfighter* due next year and a Marvel *Ghost Rider* film slated for 2028.

Shields, meanwhile, is writing the script for the *Gladys* prequel to *Weapons* (2025) with Zach Cregger, according to Deadline, and previously produced *Godzilla vs. Kong: The New Empire*.

## What happens next

Neither the band nor the outlets we read have confirmed an album or live dates. Showbiz411 says an album is "probably" coming, but that is the outlet's guess, not an announcement. For now, the video is the place to start, and the 2009 album is the place to go next.
