**Quick summary**

- BAFTA North America announced its 2026 Breakthrough U.S. cohort on 17 November, naming 14 rising creatives across film, television and games.
- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) is listed as a performer in the TV series [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry).
- Members get a year-long programme with mentoring, full BAFTA voting membership and access to events and screenings.
- The programme is supported by Apple TV and is in its seventh year, according to The Hollywood Reporter.

Connor Storrie, the actor known for *Heated Rivalry*, has been chosen for BAFTA's 2026 Breakthrough U.S. cohort. BAFTA North America announced the 14 names on 17 November, as reported by [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/news/general-news/bafta-us-breakouts-2026-connor-storrie-inde-navarette-1236756614/), [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/biz/news/connor-storrie-inde-navarrette-parsons-bafta-breakthrough-1236950441/) and [Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/bafta-breakthrough-cohort-connor-storrie-inde-navarrette/).

## Who else is in the Connor Storrie BAFTA Breakthrough class

Storrie is the TV performer in a group that mixes actors, directors, writers and game developers. Variety lists him under television as a performer in *Heated Rivalry*.

Other names include [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette), listed as a performer in the film *Obsession*, and [Kane Parsons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kane_Parsons), director and composer of *Backrooms*. Gold Derby describes Navarrette as an *Obsession* breakout.

The full list, as printed by Variety, also covers:

- Adam Meeks, writer-director of *Union County*
- Celeste Hughey, creator of *The 'Burbs*
- [Gabriela Osio Vanden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriela_Osio_Vanden), director of the documentary *Nuisance Bear*
- Gaby Acosta, costume designer of *1923* and [*Frisco King*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frisco_King)
- Georgia Bernstein, writer-director of *Night Nurse*
- Jennifer Svedberg-Yen, lead writer and voice and localisation producer on *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*
- Julián Cordero, lead developer of *Despelote*
- Matt Hixon, editor of the documentary [*Barbara Forever*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbara_Forever)
- Stephanie Ahn, writer-director of *Bedford Park*
- Zaire Lanier, writer of *South of Midnight*

The Hollywood Reporter also names composer Marcus Norris among the 14. Its list and Variety's differ slightly in format, but both put the total at 14.

## What the programme gives its members

According to The Hollywood Reporter, BAFTA Breakthrough is the arts charity's flagship talent initiative, built for creatives at a pivotal point in their careers. The cohort takes part in a year-long programme.

That includes one-on-one meetings and career guidance, full BAFTA voting membership, access to BAFTA events and screenings, and networking opportunities in the U.S. and internationally. Voting membership is a notable perk, because BAFTA members choose the winners of the British Academy Film Awards.

## What BAFTA said

BAFTA North America executive director Courtney LaBarge Bell said the cohort reflects "imagination, ambition and distinct perspectives that continue to push our industries forward". She added that emerging creatives face growing challenges in finding opportunity and mentorship, and that BAFTA wants to give talent a place to be recognised and connected.

Each member, she said, brings "a unique point of view and a commitment to their craft".

The Hollywood Reporter says a jury picked the group. Film and TV jurors included Adolpho Veloso, Alysa Nahmias, Julia Kim and Rolfe Kent, while a separate panel judged the games category.

## What happens next

The cohort now begins its year with BAFTA. The sources do not give a start date for the first events, and none of the outlets reports a comment from Storrie himself.

The Hollywood Reporter also links to a separate report that a film called *Wet*, starring Storrie, Rebecca Ferguson and Odessa A'zion, has been acquired by Lionsgate. We have not independently confirmed the details of that deal, so check the original report for them.

### What is the BAFTA Breakthrough U.S. programme?
It is BAFTA's talent initiative for rising creatives, supported by Apple TV and now in its seventh year. Members receive a year of mentoring, networking and BAFTA membership benefits.

### Why was Connor Storrie chosen?
The outlets list him as a performer in *Heated Rivalry*, the project named as his breakout. None of the reports gives a separate reason for his selection beyond that credit.

### How many people are in the 2026 cohort?
There are 14 creatives from film, television and games.
