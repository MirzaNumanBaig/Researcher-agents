**Quick summary**

- Actor Brian Cox told The Times that Margot Robbie was the wrong choice to play Cathy in Emerald Fennell's Wuthering Heights.
- He said she is "far too beautiful" for the role, then added that it may still be a brilliant film.
- Cox said he is too old to be careful, noting he will be 80 this year.
- JoBlo reported the comments on 14 November 2026. Robbie has not been quoted in response in the source.

[Brian Cox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_Cox_(actor)) has questioned the casting of [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie) as Cathy in Emerald Fennell's Wuthering Heights, telling The Times she is "far too beautiful" for the part. The remarks, reported by [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/brian-cox-margot-robbie-wuthering-heights/), came in an interview in which the Scottish actor said he no longer sees a reason to hold back.

## What Brian Cox said about Margot Robbie

According to JoBlo's account of the interview, Cox first performed a mock Australian accent, voicing an imagined greeting between Cathy and Keith Cliff. He then said Robbie was too beautiful for the role and that he thinks there should be "something more of the Gypsy" about the character.

He did not leave it there. Cox admitted it was wrong of him to judge and said the film "may be a brilliant film". The comments are an opinion about casting, not a review, and nothing in the report suggests he has seen the finished film.

## Why Cox says he speaks his mind

The Times asked Cox whether his candour had cost him work. He said he does not yet know, and that his wife, Nicole Ansari-Cox, keeps telling him to be careful. His answer, as quoted by JoBlo, was blunt: he will be 80 this year and intends to say what he wants to say.

JoBlo describes Cox as an actor with a long record of outspoken remarks about Hollywood and his peers. That context matters when weighing this latest comment: it fits a pattern of public opinions rather than a new dispute with Robbie.

## The Jeremy Strong comments that came up again

The same interview revisited Cox's earlier criticism of his Succession co-star [Jeremy Strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeremy_Strong), whose method acting Cox once called "annoying" in stronger language. According to JoBlo, that prompted [Daniel Day-Lewis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Day-Lewis) to offer a conversation about immersing yourself in a role.

Cox told The Times he has not taken up the offer, saying it has nothing to do with Day-Lewis, whom he described as discreet. He said Strong has asked him to stop talking about him, and called Strong a wonderful actor. His objection, he said, is to "all the bollocks that goes with it".

## What happens next

Neither JoBlo nor The Times interview, as relayed, includes a reply from Robbie or Fennell. Casting remarks of this kind often fade quickly, and Cox himself ended on a gracious note about the film. For now the story rests on one interview and one outlet's report, so treat it as one actor's view.
