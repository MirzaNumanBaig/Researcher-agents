**Quick summary**

- [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2302361/hugh-jackman-sci-fi-flop-district-9-director-chappie/) published a retrospective on *Chappie* on 1 November 2026.
- The 2015 film starred [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) as Vincent, a soldier turned engineer and the main antagonist.
- It was directed by [Neill Blomkamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neill_Blomkamp), who made *District 9*.
- SlashFilm says critics were unimpressed and the film under-performed; Blomkamp has been frank about its failure.

Hugh Jackman's turn in *Chappie*, a 2015 robot film that most people have quietly forgotten, is back in focus after [SlashFilm](https://www.slashfilm.com/2302361/hugh-jackman-sci-fi-flop-district-9-director-chappie/) revisited it this week. The site describes it as a strange, flawed sci-fi curio, and notes that Jackman's hairstyle in the film may be as memorable as his performance.

This is a retrospective rather than breaking news, but it is a useful reminder of a very different role from the one fans associate with the actor.

## From District 9 to a harder second act

SlashFilm sets the scene with Blomkamp's debut. *District 9* arrived in 2009 on a budget of about $30 million. It told a political story about alien refugees confined to a Johannesburg shantytown, and it took more than $210 million at the box office. It earned four Oscar nominations, including [Best Picture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_common_film_awards_categories).

Blomkamp became a sought-after name almost overnight. According to SlashFilm, his next two sci-fi films were high-profile but received less warmly. *Elysium*, with [Matt Damon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Damon), came out in 2013, and *Chappie* followed in 2015.

## What Chappie is about

The film is set in a near-future Johannesburg where crime has surged. The government has bought squads of armed robot "scouts" to police the streets. [Dev Patel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dev_Patel) plays Deon, a scientist who creates software that gives robots free will and emotions. His boss, played by [Sigourney Weaver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigourney_Weaver), forbids him to test it on a police robot.

Deon takes a damaged robot anyway and is kidnapped by Ninja and Yolandi, members of the South African rap duo [Die Antwoord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Antwoord) playing versions of themselves. They make him reprogram the machine. It wakes up with a childlike personality, and they raise it as one of their own. [Sharlto Copley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharlto_Copley), who led *District 9*, plays the title robot through motion capture.

## Jackman's role as the villain

Jackman plays Vincent, who SlashFilm describes as a soldier turned engineer. He is developing a large, tank-like enforcer robot called MOOSE, which the site says closely resembles the ED-209 machines from *RoboCop*. Reviewing the film at the time, /Film called Vincent a volatile psychopath willing to sacrifice hundreds of people for his own professional gain.

It is a sharp contrast with the heroic image Jackman built through the *X-Men* films. Readers who want more of that side of his career can see our recent coverage of [*The Wolverine* arriving on HBO Max](https://moduscarry.com/hugh-jackmans-the-wolverine-lands-on-hbo-max-on-1-november/) and everything we publish on [Hugh Jackman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/hugh-jackman/).

## Why it flopped

SlashFilm points to a mix of familiar borrowings and an unusual centrepiece. It says the story echoes *RoboCop* and *Short Circuit*, and that the film is built as much around Die Antwoord as around its robots. With a budget of $49 million, it says, audiences unfamiliar with the duo were likely baffled.

Critics were not kind. /Film called the plot full of holes and the politics about class unsubtle. Peter Sobczynski of RogerEbert.com gave it one star out of four and predicted that Blomkamp would spend part of his future apologising for it. SlashFilm adds that Blomkamp has since been open about the film's failure.

## Frequently asked questions

### What role does Hugh Jackman play in Chappie?
He plays Vincent, a soldier turned engineer who is the film's central villain and the creator of a large enforcer robot called MOOSE, according to SlashFilm.

### Who directed Chappie?
Neill Blomkamp, the South African-born filmmaker behind *District 9* and *Elysium*.

### Did Chappie do well?
SlashFilm says it under-performed across the board and drew poor reviews. Blomkamp has said openly that it failed.
