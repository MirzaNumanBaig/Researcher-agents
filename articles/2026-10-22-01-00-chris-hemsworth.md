Australian actor Chris Hemsworth has welcomed Muay Thai star Rodtang "The Iron Man" Jitmuangnon to the Thailand set of his action sequel *Extraction 3*, according to a report from [myKhel](https://www.mykhel.com/more-sports/one-championship-s-rodtang-meets-hollywood-star-chris-hemsworth-on-extraction-3-set-in-thailand-456933.html).

## A visit to the set

The former ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Champion travelled to Ratchaburi, Thailand, where Hemsworth and his team are currently filming, myKhel reports. The outlet says the Australian star is in the country to shoot the third instalment of the action franchise.

Hemsworth shared the meeting on Instagram. As quoted by myKhel, he wrote that he was honoured to have the "Muay Thai legend" visit the set, calling Rodtang "all class, absolute champion". He also thanked the fighter for a pair of personalised gloves, which he said were headed straight to his trophy room.

Rodtang posted his own video on Instagram showing the two men hugging and laughing together. According to myKhel, his message thanked Hemsworth for the chance to meet and said he would "always love and respect" the actor.

## Who is Rodtang?

myKhel describes the 29-year-old as one of the biggest names in Muay Thai, fighting out of Jitmuangnon Gym. The report lists his career record as 274 wins and 44 losses, including a 17-3 record in ONE Championship, and says he remains a global fan favourite.

Hemsworth, best known for playing Thor in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, is described by the outlet as one of the highest-paid actors in the world.

## What happens next

Rodtang's attention will soon turn back to the ring. myKhel reports he is preparing for a quarterfinal in the 2026-2027 ONE Flyweight Muay Thai World Grand Prix against fellow Thai great Nong-O Hama, the former Bantamweight Muay Thai World Champion. The bout is set for The Inner Circle 38 on Friday, 13 November.

The two were due to meet last November, but the fight was cancelled after Rodtang fell ill, according to the report. The winner will earn a place in the Grand Prix semifinals.

No release date or plot details for *Extraction 3* were included in the report, so fans will need to wait for official word from the production. For now, the exchange of gloves and hugs suggests a warm day on set.
