**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) drove the top three finishers of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix to their post-race engagements.
- The ride was a red-and-black [Lincoln Town Car](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Town_Car) stretch limousine fitted with an F1 number plate, according to AUTOJOSH.
- His passengers were race winner Max Verstappen, Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri, heading to the Bellagio Hotel.
- Reeves, 62, made his professional racing debut in 2024, so the wheel is familiar territory.

Keanu Reeves swapped the red carpet for the driver's seat at the 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix, taking the podium finishers to the Bellagio Hotel in a stretch limousine after the race. The moment quickly became one of the weekend's talking points, according to the motoring site [AUTOJOSH](https://autojosh.com/keanu-reeves-chauffeurs-top-3-f1-winners-las-vegas-grand-prix/).

## What happened after the chequered flag

AUTOJOSH reports that Reeves took charge of a "cooldown limo" to carry Verstappen, Norris and Piastri from the circuit to the Bellagio, where post-race interviews and celebrations were waiting. The car was a Lincoln Town Car stretch, finished in red and black and carrying an F1 number plate.

Video footage described by the outlet shows Reeves at the wheel with the three drivers seated behind him. In a second clip, the trio stand up through the large open roof of the moving limousine and wave to fans along the route.

It is a long way from the usual post-race routine, where drivers are normally moved around by team staff and event organisers. Having one of Hollywood's best-known action stars behind the wheel gave the evening an unusual twist.

## The race behind the ride

The limousine run followed a 50-lap race. According to AUTOJOSH, Verstappen won for Red Bull Racing, his third Las Vegas Grand Prix victory in four years. Norris finished 0.639 seconds behind the Dutchman, and Piastri took third place. Norris and Piastri both drive for McLaren.

A margin of 0.639 seconds is under a second over 50 laps, which suggests the finish stayed tight to the end. AUTOJOSH describes the race as dramatic, though its report does not go into lap-by-lap detail.

## Not a first behind the wheel

The report notes that Reeves is no stranger to motorsport, even though he is known for keeping a relatively low-key lifestyle. The 62-year-old made his professional racing debut in the Toyota GR Cup at Indianapolis Motor Speedway in 2024.
