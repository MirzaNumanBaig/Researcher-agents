**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) has filmed her annual TV event *Christmas with Delta* in London, according to That Eurovision Site.
- It is the first edition shot in the UK, and was recorded over two days at the start of November.
- The special will air on Channel 9 in Australia later this year.
- Goodrem is currently competing on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) in Britain.

Delta Goodrem has taken her festive television tradition abroad. The Australian singer has filmed this year's Delta Goodrem Christmas special in London, a two-day shoot at the start of November, according to [That Eurovision Site](https://thateurovisionsite.com/2026/11/02/delta-goodrem-films-christmas-special-in-london/). Channel 9 will broadcast it in Australia later in the year.

## A first for the series in the UK

*Christmas with Delta* has been filmed every year since it debuted in 2020, but never on British soil until now. The report says fans could enter a ballot for a place in the studio audience.

The location is the second move away from home. The 2024 edition was filmed in Hollywood, which was the first time the show left Australia. London now becomes the second overseas base for what began as a local festive special.

The reason for the trip is simple. Goodrem is in the UK for the 24th series of *Strictly Come Dancing*, so the production came to her rather than the other way round. The site says she was never going to skip the festive season, even with a weekly dance schedule.

## How the series began

The first special in 2020 arrived alongside her Christmas album [*Only Santa Knows*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Only_Santa_Knows). It has since become a yearly fixture.

Last year's show carried guest spots from 5 Seconds of Summer, [Cody Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cody_Simpson), David Campbell and [Sophie Monk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophie_Monk), among others. That edition was also made available on Amazon's Prime Video in some territories for the first time. This report does not name the 2026 guests, so who joins her in London remains unconfirmed.

## A busy year after Eurovision

The special lands at the end of a crowded 2026. Goodrem represented Australia at Eurovision in Vienna with "Eclipse", a song she wrote with [Ferras Alqaisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferras), [Jonas Myrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonas_Myrin) and Michael Fatkin. She placed third in her semi-final, which sent Australia to the Grand Final for the first time since 2023.

In the final she finished fourth, which That Eurovision Site describes as Australia's second-best result at the contest. Australia collected 287 points: 165 from the juries and 122 from the public televote.

Since then she has recorded covers of every [Eurovision Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurovision_Song_Contest_Asia) 2026 entry and released new music ahead of her album *Pure*. For more on her dance-floor run, see our coverage of her [Celine Dion rumba and first 10](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrems-celine-dion-rumba-wins-strictlys-first-10/) and her [33-point Addams Family waltz](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-scores-33-for-addams-family-waltz-on-strictly/).

## Her background

The report describes Goodrem as a 41-year-old singer-songwriter born in Sydney. Her 2003 debut album drew critical praise and remains among the biggest-selling albums by an Australian artist. She has written for [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion) and [Brian McFadden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_McFadden), and has supported [Shania Twain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shania_Twain). She also coached eight seasons of *The Voice Australia* between 2012 and 2020, with a break in 2014 when she coached on *The Voice Kids*.
