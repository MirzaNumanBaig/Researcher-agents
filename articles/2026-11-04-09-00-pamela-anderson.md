**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson) attended the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards at the American Museum of Natural History in New York on Monday.
- She wore a black halter dress with a feathered, tasselled neckline from Carolina Herrera's spring 2027 collection, according to WWD.
- Her shoes were fringed slingback pumps made to mark the label's 45th anniversary.
- Harper's Bazaar described her pin-curled bob and cherry-red nails as the other talking points.

Pamela Anderson arrived at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards in an all-black Carolina Herrera outfit, finishing it with a pair of fringed slingbacks that drew as much attention as the dress. The ceremony took place on Monday at the American Museum of Natural History in New York, and the actress, known for *The Last Showgirl*, kept the rest of the look deliberately classic.

## The Carolina Herrera dress and clutch

According to [WWD](https://wwd.com/footwear-news/shoe-trends/pamela-anderson-carolina-herrera-slingback-cfda-awards-1239398815/), the dress came from Carolina Herrera's spring 2027 collection. It is a mid-length black halter with a neckline trimmed in tassels and feathers. She added matching black pumps and a polka-dot clutch, a combination WWD said channelled old-Hollywood elegance.

Harper's Bazaar described the clutch as satin and black and white, and noted that the tassel detail at the neckline set the tone for the whole outfit.

## The slingbacks that stole the detail

The shoes were the Carolina Herrera Tassel Slingback Pumps, also from the spring 2027 ready-to-wear line. WWD reports they were designed under creative director [Wes Gordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Gordon) to mark the brand's 45th anniversary.

They have a pointed toe, a spool stiletto heel and a slim slingback strap. The feature is a burst of black fringe gathered at the toe box. WWD says it echoes the texture at her neckline, so the top and the bottom of the outfit read as a pair. Inside, the insole is red leather, which matched her red nails.

## A pin-curl bob and a red manicure

Harper's Bazaar [focused on her hair and nails](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/beauty/hair/a74323141/pamela-anderson-old-hollywood-curls-red-nails/). The magazine described a coiffed bob with old-Hollywood pin curls that fell away from her face, paired with a berry lip and her usual minimal makeup. WWD, by contrast, described a retro French twist with soft, swept-back waves and no hard parting. The two outlets describe the hairstyle differently, and we have not confirmed which is more precise.

Harper's pointed out that Anderson said in 2023 she would wear less makeup on red carpets, and that she has kept hair as the centrepiece since. The magazine also noted a bright, cherry-red almond manicure, which it called a classic alternative to newer nail trends. It added that she wore a head of volume-heavy curls with subtle bangs at the 2026 Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles a few weeks earlier.

In an interview with the magazine last year, Anderson said people link her with big blond hair but she has always changed her look. "Your hair is another way to express yourself, and I've never been shy," she told Bazaar.

## The night itself

The 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards, presented by Amazon Fashion, were hosted by [Keke Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keke_Palmer). WWD lists nominees including Michael Kors, Tory Burch, Ralph Lauren, Willy Chavarria and Ashley and [Mary-Kate Olsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary-Kate_Olsen) of The Row. Anthony Vaccarello received the International Designer of the Year Award, Tory Burch the Positive Change Award and Peter Marino the Fashion Icon Award.
