**Quick summary**

- [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) told GQ that his [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) must be a believable killer, "more than a spy".
- The interview was recorded before [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was announced as 007 on Monday 16 November.
- Florence Pugh joked at an ELLE event that she wants a villain role opposite Pattinson.
- A Puck News report claims Josh O'Connor was an early favourite but stepped back.
- The 26th Bond film is currently untitled and set for 2028.

The **Villeneuve James Bond vision** is now on the record. In a GQ interview published a day after Pattinson was named the next 007, the *Dune* director said his Bond has to be, above all, someone the audience believes could kill.

## Villeneuve James Bond vision: the qualities he wanted

Speaking on GQ's "One More Question" podcast, Villeneuve said the first requirement was menace. "More than a spy, Bond is an assassin," he said, adding that he wanted "that kind of anger deep in the eye", according to [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/denis-villeneuve-james-bond-robert-pattinson/).

He then listed what else he was after: unapologetic panache, a presence that pulls a room towards him, sensuality, wit and dark humour. He also wanted someone "not afraid to put his hands in the mud", physically strong and visibly intelligent. [The A.V. Club](https://www.avclub.com/denis-villeneuve-james-bond-qualities) noted that the list reads like a profile Pattinson can plausibly fill.

Villeneuve said the process gave him real influence. He described his input as "massive", though the choice still needed sign-off from many people. He joked that the list included his mother and his brother. He also expects mixed reactions whoever is cast, pointing out that Daniel Craig was criticised at first for being too short and blond.

## Why the casting took so long

The interview was conducted while the search was still under way. Villeneuve said he had told the studio that anyone wanting to work with him would have to wait, because he was giving his full attention to *Dune: Part Three*. That, he said, was why casting took longer than fans wanted.

## The Josh O'Connor report

Casting details are still emerging, and some of them are unconfirmed. Puck News writer Matthew Belloni said on his podcast that Josh O'Connor was an early favourite but "had reservations about even testing", as relayed by [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/denis-villeneuve-james-bond-robert-pattinson/). Belloni added that Amazon was not sold on O'Connor and that the final pick was Villeneuve's, with producers Amy Pascal and David Heyman backing it.

[The Playlist](https://theplaylist.net/denis-villeneuve-josh-oconnor-james-bond-bond-26-20261117/) reports that O'Connor withdrew after talks did not go as planned. This is one journalist's account, not a statement from the studio or the actor.

## Florence Pugh makes her pitch

The reaction was not limited to the director. At ELLE's Women in Hollywood event in Beverly Hills on 16 November, Villeneuve introduced Florence Pugh before her acceptance speech. She said she had messaged him that morning to congratulate him on "a new Bond", then asked whether he had any villains "with the size of 5'4"", according to [People](https://people.com/florence-pugh-pitched-herself-as-a-bond-villain-to-denis-villeneuve-12260887).

Brad Pitt, asked at the Los Angeles premiere of *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth*, said he would be up for a villain role too, citing Javier Bardem's turn in *Skyfall*. Neither remark amounts to a casting.

## What happens next

Pattinson, Pugh and Villeneuve will first reunite in *Dune: Part Three*, which opens in cinemas on 18 December. Pattinson plays the antagonist Scytale and Pugh returns as Princess Irulan, People reports. The Bond film has no title or release date beyond a 2028 target, and no other cast has been announced.
