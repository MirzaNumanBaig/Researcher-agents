## Mckenna Grace matching tattoos: what they revealed

Mckenna Grace and Australian actor Joseph Zada have turned a film friendship into permanent ink. During a 16 November appearance on SiriusXM's *Front Row with [Andy Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Cohen)*, the two co-stars said they had matching ladybug tattoos, done only a day earlier, [InkedMag reports](https://inkedmag.com/culture/mckenna-grace-and-joseph-zada-reveal-matching-tattoos).

Zada showed off his new tattoo on his ankle. Grace said she chose a different spot for hers, though the report does not say where. The timing is hard to miss: the pair are in the final days of promotion for the latest film in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) series.

## Why a ladybug?

The design is tied to the story they tell on screen. In *Sunrise on the Reaping*, Zada plays a young Haymitch Abernathy and Grace plays fellow District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner. According to InkedMag, their characters form a family-like bond after a pivotal ladybug sequence, and the tattoos nod to that moment.

The prequel returns to the 50th Hunger Games and follows Haymitch as he enters the arena. It reaches cinemas on 20 November.

## A joke about the rest of the cast

The co-stars also joked on air about getting director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) and castmate [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) to join in. Nothing suggests either has agreed, and it was presented as a joke rather than a plan.

InkedMag notes that actors often mark a project with a tattoo, particularly when a shoot brings people close. Here the ladybug stands for both the characters and the time the two spent making the film.
