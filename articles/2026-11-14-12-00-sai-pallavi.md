**Quick summary**

- [Dipika Chikhlia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipika_Chikhlia), who played Sita in Ramanand Sagar's TV series Ramayan, says she predicted that the new film would be a hit.
- She told photographers the film is well made and urged them to watch it in cinemas, according to India Weekly.
- Nitesh Tiwari's Ramayana, released on 5 November, had earned about US$115.6 million worldwide by Thursday 12 November, per Sacnilk.
- In July, Chikhlia questioned how Sai Pallavi would look as Sita, while calling her a "phenomenal actor".

[Dipika Chikhlia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dipika_Chikhlia), the actress long identified with Sita on Indian television, has welcomed the box-office run of the new Ramayana, in which [Sai Pallavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sai_Pallavi) plays the same role. Asked about the film's success this week, she said she had always expected it to do well, [India Weekly reported](https://www.indiaweekly.biz/sai-pallavi-sita-ramayana-dipika-chikhlia-reacts-115-million-box-office/) on 13 November.

## What Dipika Chikhlia said about Sai Pallavi's film

According to India Weekly, photographers told Chikhlia that Ramayana had crossed the Rs 1,000 crore mark worldwide. She answered that she had already said the film was well made and bound to succeed. "See how it crossed 900-1000 crore in just 4-5 days. Superb," she said.

She also praised the look of the production, pointing to its big-name cast and visual effects. She encouraged the photographers to see it on the big screen, calling it a good cinematic experience.

The comment lands because of what she said earlier. The reaction has put her pre-release remarks about Sai Pallavi back in focus, and those remarks were more mixed.

## Her earlier doubts about Sai Pallavi as Sita

In a July interview with Variety India, Chikhlia called Sai Pallavi "a phenomenal actor" but said she could not yet picture her as Sita and would wait to see the result. She also questioned the idea of a Sita with curly hair, saying it is not mentioned in the Ramayan.

She added that the new film would have to live with comparisons to the Ramanand Sagar series. That show was watched by a new generation when Doordarshan aired it again during the pandemic, she said, so it would be very hard to erase.

Those comments were about expectations, not a verdict on the finished film. This week's remarks suggest she is now happy with the result, at least as a piece of cinema. She did not, in the report, comment further on Sai Pallavi's performance itself.

## No role for the original Sita

India Weekly also reports that Chikhlia was never approached to appear in the film. She said she would not have accepted a part if she had been, because having played Sita she could not imagine playing any other character in the epic.

She said the same about her former co-star [Arun Govil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arun_Govil), who played Ram in the television series and plays King Dasharath in the new film. "If you played Ram, then you are Ram," she said, admitting she finds it hard to see him in a different role.

## The numbers behind the "I told you"

Directed by [Nitesh Tiwari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nitesh_Tiwari) and produced by [Namit Malhotra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namit_Malhotra), Ramayana opened in cinemas on 5 November. India Weekly, citing Sacnilk, says it had earned roughly US$115.6 million worldwide by the end of Thursday 12 November, which makes it the third-fastest Indian film to enter the Rs 1,000 crore club.

The same report breaks that total down:

- India net collection: about US$63.2 million.
- India gross: about US$75.5 million.
- Overseas markets: about US$40.1 million.

The two-part adaptation stars [Ranbir Kapoor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranbir_Kapoor) as Ram, Sai Pallavi as Sita, Yash as Ravana and [Ravie Dubey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_Dubey) as Lakshman, with [Sunny Deol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunny_Deol) as Hanuman. Box-office figures come from a tracking site and are estimates, so they may shift as the studio reports its own numbers.
