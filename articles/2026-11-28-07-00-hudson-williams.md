**Quick summary**

- Connor Storrie says he is living with co-star Hudson Williams while the second season of Heated Rivalry is filmed.
- Storrie made the comment in an interview with Vogue, as reported by Rolling Stone Canada on 27 November 2026.
- Filming is under way in Ontario and Quebec, and season 2 is expected in spring 2027.
- Williams also appears in the finale of the AMC+ thriller Yaga, which has been renewed for a second season.

[Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) and [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) are sharing a home while they shoot the next season of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry). Storrie confirmed the arrangement in a Vogue interview, according to [Rolling Stone Canada](https://ca.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/connor-storrie-hudson-williams-roommates-heated-rivalry-season-2/), which ran the story on 27 November. For fans of the Crave and HBO Max series, it is a small but telling detail about the pair behind its biggest hit.

## Why the Heated Rivalry co-stars became housemates

Asked by Vogue how often he and Williams are in touch, Storrie said they are in the same house for the length of the shoot. "Right now we're filming, so I literally live with him, which is amazing," he said, as quoted by Rolling Stone Canada.

The two had first stayed in a hotel. Storrie said it was a good one, but a long production made him miss everyday basics. "I needed a washing machine. I needed a kitchen," he told the magazine.

The interview accompanied Vogue's Best Dressed 2026 digital cover. In it, Storrie also said the pair do not plan their red-carpet outfits together, even though fans have noticed matching looks. He called it natural synchronicity and said he admires Williams' street-style instincts.

Williams plays Shane Hollander and Storrie plays Ilya Rozanov, the two hockey players at the centre of the series. Their on-screen rivalry has turned the show into a global hit since its debut.

## Where Heated Rivalry season 2 is up to

Rolling Stone Canada reports that season 2 is in production in Ontario and Quebec. The outlet says it is expected to arrive in spring 2027. No exact release date was given in the reporting we read, so treat the season as a window rather than a firm schedule.

The living arrangement suggests a long shoot, which is what a lengthy production usually means for a series with this much attention. Neither actor has described how many weeks of filming remain.

## Yaga finale gives Williams another talking point

Williams is also in the news for a very different project. In the two-part finale of the Canadian thriller Yaga, streaming on AMC+, he plays Logan and Henry, a pair of men the show's three generations of witches target in their plan to continue their bloodline. Gold Derby describes them as the first of three male sacrifices.

Showrunner Kat Sandler told [Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/tv/2026/yaga-season-1-finale-cliffhanger-explained-hudson-williams-showrunner-interview/) that the finale ends on a problem rather than a solution. A baby is born, and it is a boy rather than the girl the witches expected. Gold Derby says the series has drawn one of the biggest audiences in AMC+ history and that a second season has just been announced.

Sandler said Yaga was meant to be a limited series, and that the ending was her pitch for continuing it. Only Carrie-Anne Moss is confirmed for season 2 so far, according to Gold Derby. Whether Williams returns has not been stated.

The Gold Derby headline frames his role as the show's [Laura Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Palmer), a reference to the murdered character whose death drives Twin Peaks. We only have the headline and the opening of that interview, so we cannot say more about how Sandler explains the comparison.

## A beauty ambassador role

Separately, SheKnows notes in a sponsored shopping guide that the Korean skincare brand SKIN1004 counts Williams as its global brand ambassador. The piece is a Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals roundup, and SheKnows says SKIN1004 is a sponsor. It is a commercial mention rather than news about the actor, but it shows how far his profile has grown in a short time.
