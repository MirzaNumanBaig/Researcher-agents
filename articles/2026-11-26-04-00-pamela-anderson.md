**Quick summary**

- [Pamela Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pamela_Anderson), 59, shared a makeup-free photo of herself in dark skinny jeans and Wellington boots on Monday.
- The image appeared on the page of her skincare brand, Sonsie, and was taken for the brand's Christmas campaign.
- It was shot in the garden of her childhood home in Ladysmith, British Columbia, according to HELLO!.
- Days earlier she wore a ruby-red coat to a dinner in New York City.

Pamela Anderson has put skinny jeans back in the spotlight with a relaxed, makeup-free photo taken in her own garden. According to [HELLO!](https://www.hellomagazine.com/fashion/celebrity-style/937134/pamela-anderson-59-wears-skinny-jeans-make-up-free/), the 59-year-old posted the snapshot on Monday, sitting in a wheelbarrow in dark denim and Wellington boots.

## The photo behind the skinny jeans story

The picture was shared on the page of Sonsie, Anderson's skincare brand, rather than on a red-carpet account. HELLO! reports that it comes from the shoot for Sonsie's Christmas campaign. Anderson wears no visible makeup, and her hair is styled in a large, voluminous bouffant.

The outlet describes the outfit as a pair of close-fitting dark skinny jeans with sleek rubber boots. It does not say who took the photo or when the shoot happened, so those details remain unconfirmed.

## Why skinny jeans are a talking point

Skinny jeans are widely seen as a Y2K staple that has been written off as dated in recent years. HELLO! notes that barrel-leg and turn-up styles are currently seen as more fashionable, with wider cuts leading the denim conversation.

The magazine's view is that the skinny cut still flatters most people, and that Anderson's choice shows her own sense of style rather than a rejection of trends. That is the publication's opinion, not a measured shift in the market, so treat the "skinny jeans are back" idea as a style-desk take rather than a fact.

## A garden she has been restoring since 2020

The setting matters to the story. HELLO! says the star of *The Last Showgirl* moved from Malibu to her childhood home in Ladysmith, British Columbia, known as Arcady. She has been renovating the property since 2020, and it includes a seven-acre garden.

The report also describes her as the former wife of [Tommy Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tommy_Lee) and the mother of two sons, Brandon and Dylan Lee. Garden shots have become a regular backdrop for the way she presents her style.

## From garden to Upper East Side

The jeans photo follows a very different look from last week. HELLO! says Anderson attended an intimate dinner with Pandora on New York City's Upper East Side. She wore a ruby-red cashmere coat by Auter with a waist-cinching shape, black leather gloves, sheer tights and Giuseppe Zanotti 'Guêpière Pump 105' heels.

Her icy-blonde hair was set in voluminous curls in her signature bob, and her makeup was natural with a glossy lip. According to the magazine, several people compared the look to [Marilyn Monroe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marilyn_Monroe), who was known for a similar hairstyle and bold red outfits.
