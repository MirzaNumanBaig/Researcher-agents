**Quick summary**

- [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves) is set to star in and produce an adaptation of the spy thriller Kill Current, according to ScreenRant.
- The novel by Oliver Lowe is due in May 2027 and is the first in a planned series about former CIA operative Jon Hendricks.
- The project was announced in September 2026 and has already moved into development.
- It is reportedly the first major project from Reeves' production company, Two Dragonflies.

Keanu Reeves has picked his next big-screen thriller, and it comes with a built-in series. [ScreenRant reports](https://screenrant.com/kill-current-oliver-lowe-keanu-reeves-next-adapation-thriller/) that the actor will star in and produce Kill Current, based on a novel by Oliver Lowe that is due to be published in May 2027. The adaptation was announced in September 2026 and is already in development.

## What is Kill Current about?

The story centres on Jon Hendricks, described as a cynical former CIA operative. Hendricks has left active duty, but he returns to the field when his former trainee, Rivers Elliot, disappears. ScreenRant's summary says the search quickly spirals once a piece of technology that was not expected to be usable for at least another decade becomes part of the case.

The plot also involves international travel and a wider geopolitical conflict. According to the same report, Hendricks reaches conclusions along the way that he could never have predicted at the start.

Kill Current is the first of a planned series, which matters for any studio weighing a film. A single adaptation can be a one-off, but a book with sequels already planned gives a producer something closer to a franchise to build on.

## Why the Reacher and John Wick comparisons?

ScreenRant says the novel is most often compared with the Jack Reacher books by Lee Child, and has also been likened to the work of John le Carré. Hendricks is not a copy of Reacher, the outlet notes, but he shares many of the same qualities as the wandering hero. The technology angle gives the series a different flavour.

For Reeves, the comparison is a natural one. He fronted the John Wick films, and ScreenRant frames Kill Current as a project that blends the appeal of that franchise with the book-led momentum behind Reacher. That framing is the outlet's reading, not an official description from the production.

## A producer's role for Reeves

The detail worth watching is the producing credit. ScreenRant describes Kill Current as the first major project from Reeves' company, Two Dragonflies, and says this shows his involvement goes beyond lending his face to the film. The outlet argues that being on the development side lets him bring his earlier experience to the project.

Getting involved before the book is even on shelves is also unusual. Readers who want to follow a series from the start will have the chance, since the adaptation is moving ahead alongside the novel's release.

## Meanwhile, fans debate Reeves' unfinished franchises

In a separate piece, ScreenRant [ranks ten Keanu Reeves films](https://screenrant.com/10-keanu-reeves-movies-that-deserved-sequels-starring-him-ranked/) it believes could have become franchises. Its list includes Chain Reaction, The Replacements, [Johnny Mnemonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Mnemonic), Street Kings and [Point Break](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_Break).

The writer makes a case for each. Of Chain Reaction, which also starred [Rachel Weisz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_Weisz) and [Morgan Freeman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Freeman), the piece says the ending leaves room for a sequel but a modest box office and mixed-to-negative reviews held it back in the 1990s.

On Street Kings, the 2008 thriller co-starring [Chris Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Evans_(actor)), [Forest Whitaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forest_Whitaker) and [Hugh Laurie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Laurie), ScreenRant notes a 2011 direct-to-DVD follow-up, Street Kings 2: Motor City. Only [Clifton Powell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_Powell) returned, in a different role, and the piece says critics and fans panned it. The writer argues the original cast deserves a proper sequel.

These are one outlet's opinions, not announcements. No studio has confirmed a sequel to any film on the list based on the sources we read.

## What happens next

No release date, director or cast has been confirmed for Kill Current in the reports we read.
