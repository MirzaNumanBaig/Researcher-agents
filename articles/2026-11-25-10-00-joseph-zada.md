**Quick summary**

- Fans are speculating that Australian actor [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) could join the planned *Red Rising* film.
- The talk started when author [Pierce Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brown) was noticed following Zada on Instagram, according to CinemaBlend.
- There is no official word that Zada is in talks for any role.
- Separately, Irish outlet Stellar reported on 24 November that Zada has been seen in Dublin after the global press tour for *Sunrise on the Reaping*.

The **Joseph Zada Red Rising** rumour is a fan theory for now, not a casting announcement. According to [CinemaBlend](https://www.cinemablend.com/movies/red-rising-fans-want-joseph-zada-movie-cast-character-golds), readers of [Red Rising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Rising) noticed that Brown, who wrote the 2014 novel, follows the *Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* star on Instagram, and the speculation took off from there.

## Why fans think Zada could be in Red Rising

CinemaBlend says a *Red Rising* movie is now in the works, with [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) set to direct. Lawrence also directed Zada's latest film, the newest chapter in the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) franchise. That overlap is the main reason the name is circulating: a director often returns to actors he has already worked with.

The same article is careful to point out the other reading. Brown and Lawrence have known each other for years, according to Lawrence, so Brown may simply have met Zada through the director and followed him as a fan would. In the writer's words, the follow could mean nothing or it could mean something.

## Which role fans have in mind

The character most often named in fan comments is Cassius, one of the Gold caste. CinemaBlend notes that the character is described as strikingly handsome, with blond curls and a tall frame, and that IMDb lists Zada at 6'2". The outlet also acknowledges that the film will have to find a way to show the size gap between the book's colour castes, whether through forced perspective, effects or other techniques.

CinemaBlend's own pick is different. Its writer would rather picture Zada as Darrow, the young Red at the centre of the first book, partly because of his turn as a District 12 teenager in *Sunrise on the Reaping*. The writer ruled out Pax, imagining a much bulkier actor, and found Roque an interesting but imperfect fit. The Jackal was floated as a darker option, given that Zada has just played the hero in a Hunger Games film.

None of this is sourced to the production. It is a columnist and fans reading a social media follow, and it should be treated that way.

## Zada spotted in Ireland

Away from the speculation, Zada has been busy with a more ordinary kind of attention. Stellar, the Irish entertainment site, reported on 24 November that he has been spending time in Dublin after the film's press tour. *Sunrise on the Reaping* is a prequel that follows Haymitch Abernathy, the character originally played by [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson), during his own Hunger Games.

According to Stellar, Zada met up with Irish co-star Molly McCann, who plays Louella McCoy in the film, and the pair went to [Noah Kahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noah_Kahan)'s final show at Dublin's 3Arena on Sunday night. He was also seen at Blanchardstown Shopping Centre. McCann's brother Aidan posted photos from a lunch at a local Nando's, captioned with a line saying he had enjoyed lunch with Zada.

His run of recent headlines has been steady. Our earlier coverage of [Joseph Zada's record Australian opening](https://moduscarry.com/joseph-zada-box-office-record/) shows how quickly the film's release has put him in the spotlight, and you can find everything else on the [Joseph Zada tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/joseph-zada/).

## What happens next

Nothing about *Red Rising* casting has been announced, so the next real signal will be an official statement from the studio, Lawrence or Brown. Cassius is the most common suggestion in comments, according to CinemaBlend, though the writer prefers Darrow.
