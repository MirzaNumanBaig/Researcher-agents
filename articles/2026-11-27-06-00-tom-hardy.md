**Quick summary**

- [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) was photographed on the London set of *MobLand* season 3 on Tuesday, according to Page Six.
- It is the first time he has been pictured filming since reports in May that he had been fired from the show.
- Paramount+ confirmed in September that Hardy would return for the third season.
- Season 3 has no official release date yet, while season 2 is streaming on Paramount+.

Tom Hardy is back at work on *MobLand*. Photos obtained by Page Six show the actor walking around the show's London set on Tuesday, his first day of filming on season 3, and laughing with crew members. The pictures arrive roughly six months after the rumours that he had been dropped from the Paramount+ crime drama.

## What the set photos show

According to [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/26/entertainment/tom-hardy-puts-mobland-drama-to-rest-as-hes-spotted-on-set-for-first-time-since-firing-rumors/), Hardy was seen in high spirits as he joked with the crew. He plays Harry Da Souza, the calm and fiercely loyal fixer working for the Harrigan crime family. The report describes the 49-year-old as the "Venom" star.

[ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/mobland-season-3-tom-hardy-set-photos/) says TMZ also published images of Hardy on set, and notes that the pictures follow Paramount+ confirming that the series has officially entered production. The series is Guy Ritchie's London-set crime drama.

## The firing rumours, and what was actually confirmed

The photos land against a noisy backdrop. Page Six says Variety reported in the spring that Hardy had been let go after "on-set issues" with executive producer [Jez Butterworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jez_Butterworth), 101 Studios and others. Puck News reportedly claimed he was regularly late during season 2, offered frequent script notes and was unhappy about the ensemble cast.

Other outlets went further. The Daily Mail quoted an unnamed source who said [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) was frustrated with his attitude, and the Hollywood Reporter cited another anonymous insider who alleged he kept castmates waiting in his trailer. Those are unconfirmed claims from unnamed sources, and none of them has been verified on the record.

What is on the record points the other way. Mirren, 81, posted a photo of Hardy on Instagram in May with the caption "Love you now and always," according to Page Six. In September, Paramount+ confirmed he would be back for season 3.

## Where the show stands

*MobLand* has had a strong run. ScreenRant reports that season 2 reached its highest Rotten Tomatoes score, 90% from critics across 21 reviews. Its penultimate episode, "Nervous Breakdown," drew a record IMDb rating of 8.7 for the series, one of its best-rated hours.

Mirren and [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) are due to return as Maeve and Conrad Harrigan. The wider ensemble includes [Anson Boon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anson_Boon), [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn), Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt and Toby Jones. Newcomers are also joining; we covered [Gregg Sulkin's arrival in season 3](https://moduscarry.com/mobland-season-3-cast-gregg-sulkin/), and the show's earlier run at the top of the charts in [MobLand hitting No. 1 on Paramount+ worldwide](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-paramount-number-one/).

## Hardy's other crime roles back in the spotlight

The return has also sent viewers back to his earlier gangster work. Cosmopolitan UK points to the 2014 thriller *The Drop*, in which Hardy plays a quiet Brooklyn bartender, as a gritty alternative for viewers missing *MobLand*. It gives the film a 9/10 and says Hardy shows a more vulnerable side than in his usual tough-guy parts.

MovieWeb, meanwhile, recommends Ritchie's 2008 crime comedy *RocknRolla*, now on Prime Video. Hardy plays Handsome Rob alongside Gerard Butler and Idris Elba. MovieWeb says the film grossed about US$25.7 million worldwide against an US$18 million budget, and sits at 61% with critics on Rotten Tomatoes. ## What happens next

Filming on season 3 has only just begun, and no release date has been announced, according to ScreenRant. Paramount+ confirmed Hardy's return, but the claims about what happened behind the scenes in season 2 remain unverified. Expect casting and release news to follow as production moves ahead.
