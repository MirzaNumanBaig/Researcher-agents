**Quick summary**

- Netflix released a new trailer on Thursday 12 November for *Ray Gunn*, an animated sci-fi noir directed by Brad Bird.
- [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell) voices the title character, a private detective in a world shared by humans and aliens.
- [Scarlett Johansson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scarlett_Johansson) voices the pop star at the centre of the case.
- The film opens in select cinemas on 4 December and arrives on Netflix on 18 December.

Netflix has unveiled a new trailer for [*Ray Gunn*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ray_Gunn), the animated noir starring the voices of Sam Rockwell and Scarlett Johansson, according to [UPI via Yahoo News Canada](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/watch-sam-rockwell-scarlett-johansson-160624713.html). The film opens in select cinemas on 4 December, then streams on Netflix from 18 December.

## What the Ray Gunn trailer shows

Rockwell plays Ray Gunn, a private detective in a retro-futuristic setting where humans and aliens live side by side. UPI reports the story is set in an alternate 1939. Ray and his alien partner Eye-Ra, voiced by [Tom Waits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Waits), take on a case involving Venus Nova, a multimedia star and icy femme fatale voiced by Johansson.

[Punch Drunk Critics](https://punchdrunkcritics.com/2026/11/ray-gunn-trailer-sam-rockwell-and-scarlett-johansson-voice-brad-birds-long-awaited-sci-fi-noir/) places the film in a city called Metropia and describes Gunn as the last human private detective, investigating a murder that involves aliens and Venus Nova.

Director [Brad Bird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brad_Bird) has described the pitch as *The Maltese Falcon* meets Buck Rogers, a quote he gave to Netflix's Tudum and which UPI repeats.

## The cast around Rockwell

The wider voice cast is long. Punch Drunk Critics lists [Alan Tudyk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Tudyk), [Matt Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Berry), [Bobby Cannavale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bobby_Cannavale), [Michael Chernus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Chernus), [Kether Donohue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kether_Donohue), [Michelle Rodriguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Rodriguez), [Aasif Mandvi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aasif_Mandvi), [Matt Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Jones_(actor)), [Kathryn Hunter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathryn_Hunter), John Ratzenberger, [Justin Peck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Peck) and [Patton Oswalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patton_Oswalt) alongside the leads.

Blogcritics, reviewing the film after its Texas premiere at the Austin Film Festival, singles out Johansson's singing as Venus Nova, which the reviewer said came as a surprise.

## A project nearly three decades in the making

*Ray Gunn* is Bird's first film since *Incredibles 2* in 2018, according to Punch Drunk Critics. His earlier credits include *The Incredibles*, *Ratatouille*, *The Iron Giant*, *Tomorrowland* and *Mission: Impossible - Ghost Protocol*.

At the Austin screening, Bird told the audience the idea had been with him far longer than a decade. [Blogcritics](https://blogcritics.org/austin-film-festival-movie-review-ray-gunn-brings-sam-rockwell-and-scarlett-johansson-into-sci-fi-noir/) reports he tried to get it made before *The Iron Giant* in 1999, but the producer involved passed. The same report says he stepped away from directing a third *Incredibles* film to concentrate on this one.

Bird also explained his approach to the setting. Most people now imagine the future as dystopian, he said, so he preferred a utopian city where noir characters still lie, steal and kill. He named *The Big Sleep*, *The Big Lebowski*, *L.A. Confidential* and *Chinatown* among his influences.

## Early reaction

Both critics who have seen the film are positive. The Blogcritics reviewer called it the most amazing animation they had seen, and noted it is rated PG but carries adult themes and well-staged violence. Punch Drunk Critics says it saw the film last month and was blown away. These are individual opinions, not a consensus, and the film has not yet opened widely.

*Ray Gunn* opens in select cinemas on 4 December before its Netflix premiere on 18 December. Netflix has not, in the sources reviewed here, announced Australian cinema dates, so local viewers should expect the streaming release to be the main way to watch.
