- [Johnny Flynn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnny_Flynn) told Collider his episode 9 pub scene with [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) was among his favourites to film on MobLand.
- Flynn plays Frankie Cooper, who is sent by Kat McAllister to kill the Harrigans in the season 2 finale.
- The finale reveals Harry's long game and puts the family back on top.
- Flynn also shares a short scene with [Helen Mirren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Mirren) at the end of the episode.

*Spoiler warning: this article discusses the MobLand season 2 finale.*

Johnny Flynn has named his episode 9 pub scene with Tom Hardy as one of his favourite days on MobLand. Speaking to Collider after the season 2 finale, the actor said the chess-driven face-off between their characters was thrilling to play, and that Hardy himself is "a wonderful person, very strange, and talented".

## Why the pub scene worked

In the scene, Flynn's Frankie Cooper sits down with Hardy's Harry for a long, tense conversation built around chess. According to Flynn, the two characters are both master players with very different styles. He told Collider they each recognise something of their own power and darker nature in the other, and described them as "yin and yang" versions of the same ruthless attitude. That, he suggested, is why both men sit at the top of their respective organisations.

Flynn also pointed to a detail that changes how the scene reads on a second watch. Frankie appears to give things away rather easily, which seems out of character for such a sharp operator. Flynn said the slips are deliberate. Frankie needs Gina, played by [Teddie Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddie_Allen), out of the way because he is carrying out a larger plan with Kat, and he is not distracted by Michael's death.

## A gangster who prefers to unsettle

Asked about his separate scene with Allen in the panic room, Flynn said Frankie is not an action hero. He gets some time in shootouts, but his real strength is unnerving people. Flynn described the character as almost vampiric, someone who draws information and power out of others before he ever pulls a trigger. He said that is why the scene with Gina feels so dark, because the viewer cannot tell what Frankie wants from her.

## How the finale lands

According to Collider, the finale finds the Harrigans on the run and branded terrorists by police, holed up in Harry's remote safe house while the family turns on itself. Harry appears to sell them out to Kat McAllister, played by [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer). She sends Frankie to kill them, but he walks into an ambush. The twist shows Harry was playing a long game, and the family ends the season back on top.

Flynn said he was glad that Frankie survives. His closing scene is opposite Helen Mirren as Maeve. The two had previously worked together on the Christmas film Goodbye June, so MobLand gave them a chance to try a different dynamic.
