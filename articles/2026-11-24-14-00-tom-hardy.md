**Quick summary**

- MobLand season 2 ended on 20 November, and critics are steering fans towards Tom Hardy's 2012 film Lawless while they wait for season 3.
- ELLE and SlashFilm both recommend the Prohibition-era drama, in which Hardy plays bootlegger Forrest Bondurant.
- Gary Oldman appears briefly as gangster Floyd Banner, though he and Hardy share no scenes.
- According to SlashFilm, Lawless made $54.6 million on a $26 million budget and holds 67% on Rotten Tomatoes.
- ELLE says the film can be rented or bought on Amazon Video, Apple TV+ and Sky Store in the UK.

With the second season of MobLand now over, [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy) fans are being pointed back to a gangster film he made more than a decade ago. Both ELLE and SlashFilm published pieces this week recommending Lawless, the 2012 bootlegging drama in which Hardy stars alongside [Gary Oldman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Oldman).

## Why critics are pushing Lawless now

ELLE calls the film the "perfect watch" for MobLand viewers who are still waiting on a third season. The logic is simple. In MobLand, Hardy plays Harry Da Souza, a fixer for a London crime family. In Lawless, he plays the quiet, dependable middle brother of a family running an illegal moonshine business in Franklin County, Virginia, in 1931.

SlashFilm describes the performance as quietly intense and says Hardy's trademark mix of aggression and insouciance, which has been on show in MobLand, is on display in the earlier film too. It also notes that the film's accent work will feel familiar to anyone who remembers his turn as Bane that same year.

## What the film is about

Lawless follows the three Bondurant brothers: Forrest (Hardy), Howard (Jason Clarke) and Jack (Shia LaBeouf). When a corrupt lawman, Charley Rakes, played by [Guy Pearce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guy_Pearce), arrives and tries to shut them down, the family is pulled into a wider criminal world that includes the Chicago gangster Floyd Banner.

According to ELLE, the story is inspired by the real Bondurant family and adapted from Matt Bondurant's novel The Wettest County in the World. The magazine calls it a dramatised account rather than a scene-for-scene retelling. Jessica Chastain and Mia Wasikowska round out the cast.

Director John Hillcoat made the film with a script by [Nick Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cave), who also wrote Hillcoat's 2005 western The Proposition, SlashFilm notes. Cave supplied the score as well. The site describes the result as a less brutal [Southern Gothic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Gothic) outing than that earlier film.

## The Oldman connection

Oldman's role is small, but SlashFilm says it includes one of the film's best scenes, in which he fires a car full of bullets while chewing a cigar. ELLE links the casting to [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses), where Oldman plays the scruffy Jackson Lamb, and says his Banner is far more openly menacing.

The pair have worked together on four projects, according to SlashFilm: Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy in 2011, then Lawless and The Dark Knight Rises in 2012, then Child 44 in 2015. Only the last gave them real screen time together. Asked about Oldman by [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy), Hardy called him "my hero" and said he has "stolen everything" from him.

## How the film performed

Lawless premiered at the 2012 Cannes Film Festival and reached cinemas in August that year. SlashFilm reports a worldwide gross of $54.6 million against a $26 million budget, so it roughly doubled its cost. Reviews were solid rather than glowing, and its 67% Rotten Tomatoes score reflects some critics being baffled by the accent. ## Where MobLand stands

Collider reports that the season 2 finale, which aired on 20 November, ended with a car bomb meant for Harry that instead killed his wife, Jan, played by Joanne Froggatt. Spoilers follow for anyone who has not watched: according to Collider, Eddie, Maeve's grandson, planted the device and set it off by accident. The outlet says Harry's loyalty to the Harrigan family has now cost him dearly.

Esquire, meanwhile, has used the show's popularity for a budget style guide, naming Zara as the source of the closest match to Harry's leather-look jacket at £69.99. For more on the show, see our post on [MobLand passing 321 million minutes watched](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-streaming-milestone/), or browse [everything we publish about Tom Hardy](https://moduscarry.com/tag/tom-hardy/).

## What happens next

None of the sources give a season 3 date or confirm story details, so viewers have a wait ahead. In the meantime, Lawless is the film critics are suggesting to fill the gap. ELLE lists it as available to rent or buy through Amazon Video, Apple TV+ and Sky Store, though availability in Australia may differ.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Tom Hardy in Lawless with Gary Oldman?

Yes, both are in the 2012 film, but SlashFilm says they share no scenes. Oldman plays gangster Floyd Banner in a brief role.

### What is Lawless about?

It follows three bootlegging brothers in Prohibition-era Virginia who come under pressure from a corrupt lawman. It is based on Matt Bondurant's novel The Wettest County in the World.

### How did Lawless do at the box office?

SlashFilm reports $54.6 million on a $26 million budget, with a 67% Rotten Tomatoes score.

### Has MobLand been renewed for season 3?

The sources do not confirm a release date. ELLE refers to fans awaiting season 3, and Collider's coverage points to a new direction for Harry.
