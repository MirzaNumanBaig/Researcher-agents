**Quick summary**

- James Blunt Ryan Bel Air: [James Blunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Blunt) apparently changed his name by deed poll after his album Airplane Mode reached number one in the UK.
- The Official Charts Company says it is his first UK number one album in 19 years.
- He took the Icon Award at the Rolling Stone UK Awards in London on Thursday 19 November 2026.
- The Queen greeted him with "Hello Ryan" after the Royal Variety Performance on Friday.
- He did not rule out a return to Glastonbury in 2027.

A bet made half in jest has turned into a very good week for James Blunt. The 52-year-old singer, who promised to take the name Ryan Bel Air if Airplane Mode topped the charts, shared a screenshot of a deed poll submission after it did, and the James Blunt Ryan Bel Air story has since followed him from an awards stage to the Royal Albert Hall.

## The name change that started it

Blunt had vowed to change his name to Ryan Air if the record reached number one, according to the [London Evening Standard](https://www.standard.co.uk/news/uk/james-blunt-queen-royal-albert-hall-jason-manford-camilla-b1305801.html). After Airplane Mode hit the top spot, he posted a screenshot of a deed poll submission that changed James Hillier Blount to Ryan Bel Air.

Asked whether he really had done it, Blunt winced and said his wife was not pleased, adding that she might return to her maiden name.

Two years ago he promised to become Blunty McBluntface if the 20th anniversary re-release of [Back To Bedlam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Back_to_Bedlam) reached number one. It peaked at number seven, so he was spared.

## Why this number one matters

Airplane Mode is Blunt's first UK number one album in 19 years, according to the Official Charts Company. His previous chart-topper was All The Lost Souls in 2007, and Back To Bedlam from 2005 spent 10 weeks at the summit. "It's number one! My mother's bought a lot of copies," he told reporters at the Royal Albert Hall.

For a career that began with the global hit [You're Beautiful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You're_Beautiful), a gap of almost two decades makes this result more than a novelty. ## "Hello Ryan" at the Royal Variety Performance

Blunt played his new song Circles at the Royal Variety Charity's 99th show on Friday night. Host [Jason Manford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Manford) introduced him as both James Blunt and Ryan Air. [Sam Ryder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Ryder) and [Idina Menzel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idina_Menzel) were also on the bill, according to the Standard.

After the show, the Queen greeted him with "Hello Ryan". She also asked about his time on [The Celebrity Traitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Celebrity_Traitors), wondering whether he had been thrilled to get out. Blunt reached the final before winners Sebastian "Bash" Croft and [Romesh Ranganathan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romesh_Ranganathan) banished him. He has said he joined the show to promote his new music.

## An Icon Award and a meme

The night before, Blunt collected the Icon Award at the ZYN Rolling Stone UK Awards at London's Roundhouse, [Music-News.com reported](https://www.music-news.com/news/UK/192380/James-Blunt-jokes-he-s-just-an-internet-meme-after-collecting-Icon-Award-at-Rolling-Stone-UK-Awards). He used the speech to mock the title. Pointing to host [Danny Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danny_Dyer), he said Dyer was the icon and that he himself was "just an internet meme at this stage".

He ran through his own story: aspiring rock star, one-hit wonder, "Queen of Twitter", then a sore second place on Traitors. He compared himself to [Bill Nighy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bill_Nighy) in [Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually) and recalled NME naming his album the worst of 2006.

Giggs received the Trailblazer Award and Rachel Chinouriri was Artist of the Year. Our earlier report, [James Blunt Wins Icon Award, Keeps Glastonbury 2027 Open](https://moduscarry.com/james-blunt-icon-award-glastonbury/), covers the awards night in more detail.

## What happens next

At the awards, Blunt answered "we'll see" when asked about Glastonbury in 2027, [The Independent reported](https://www.independent.co.uk/bulletin/culture/james-blunt-2027-glastonbury-festival-b3088545.html). He last played the Pyramid Stage in 2008 and has confirmed he will be touring in the summer of 2027. Harry Styles, Billie Eilish, Sam Fender and Cher were announced as the main headliners this week.
