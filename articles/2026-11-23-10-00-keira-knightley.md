**Quick summary**

- [Keira Knightley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keira_Knightley) plays a version of herself again in The Perfect Gift 2, a six-minute Waitrose Christmas advert.
- She told the Press Association she is "quite worried" about her on-screen self and what has happened to her life.
- [Alex Hassell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alex_Hassell) joins as her self-obsessed fiance, Hugo, opposite returning comedian [Joe Wilkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Wilkinson).
- Alison Hammond and Dermot O'Leary make cameo appearances, according to the Express and Star.

Keira Knightley has joked that she is "quite worried" about the version of herself she plays in the new Waitrose Christmas advert, The Perfect Gift 2, a romcom-style sequel running to six minutes. The actress spoke to the Press Association, as reported by the [Express and Star](https://www.expressandstar.com/whats-on/showbiz/keira-knightley-worried-about-version-of-herself-she-plays-in-christmas-ad-9366798).

## What Keira Knightley said about playing herself

Asked about returning to a fictionalised version of herself, Knightley said it was "very tough" to get into character. "I'm just wondering what happened to my life," she told the news agency. "I'm quite worried about me."

She also explained why the format appealed. Knightley is known for romcoms such as [Love Actually](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Actually) and Begin Again, but she said she had not made many for years. "Now is the time," was her view.

## The plot of The Perfect Gift 2

The sequel follows last year's The Perfect Gift, in which Knightley and Wilkinson, who plays "Phil Phil", fell in love after meeting at the British supermarket.

This time the film opens with Knightley preparing to marry Hugo. Phil makes one last attempt to win her over and tries to stop the wedding with a Beef Wellington. The gesture works. After Phil is kicked out and heads home, he finds Knightley waiting in his kitchen, where she proposes with a cheese board.

## The cast around her

Hassell, known for Rivals, joined the cast as Hugo. He said the shoot was "really fun" and that everyone had a good time despite the heat, because it was filmed at the height of summer. He joked that the first advert was "such a lovely romantic thing" and that he "wanted to come and mess that up".

Wilkinson's role as Phil was his first as a romantic lead. Asked whether more leading parts could follow, he said he could not see it "not happening", according to the Express and Star. The video also features Alison Hammond and Dermot O'Leary of This Morning.

## What happens next

The advert is part of Waitrose's Christmas campaign, and the sources do not mention a third instalment.
