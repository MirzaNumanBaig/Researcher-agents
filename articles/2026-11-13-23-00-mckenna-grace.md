**Quick summary**

- [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace) wore a sheer black lace minidress to Cultured magazine's Artists on Artists dinner in Los Angeles on 10 November, according to Fashion Times UK.
- At the Mexico City premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping she wore an ALmée Couture gown, reports theFashionSpot via Yahoo.
- Her stylist, Molly Dickson, tied a woodgrain Celine top to District 7, the lumber district, says Reality Tea.
- The film opens in cinemas on 20 November.

Mckenna Grace's Hunger Games looks have become a talking point this week, with the 20-year-old moving from gothic lace in Los Angeles to couture in Mexico City, and a stylist's hint at the franchise's lumber district. The prequel, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, reaches cinemas on 20 November, so the red carpets are doing plenty of promotional work.

## Black lace at the Artists on Artists dinner

Grace attended Cultured magazine's Artists on Artists dinner in Los Angeles on 10 November, [Fashion Times UK reports](https://www.fashiontimes.co.uk/mckenna-grace-britney-spears-lace-dress-1767678). She wore the Spiritus dress from Annie's Ibiza, priced at £1,295, a sheer black lace minidress with floral embellishments, long fitted sleeves, a scalloped hem and a square neckline. A matching lace choker and Christian Louboutin pumps finished it.

The outlet compared the look with the sheer black Dolce & Gabbana lace dress [Britney Spears](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britney_Spears) wore to the 2001 MTV Video Music Awards. That comparison is the publication's, not Grace's. Fashion Times UK also pointed to Spears' planned biopic, which Universal Pictures is developing from her memoir The Woman in Me, with [Jon M. Chu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_M._Chu) directing and a screenplay by [Liz Meriwether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Meriwether). No lead has been announced, and Grace has not been named as a candidate.

She was joined at the dinner by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), her co-star in Sunrise on the Reaping, and [Maxwell Jenkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_Jenkins), who plays Fred Jones to her Daphne Blake in Netflix's live-action series Scooby-Doo: Origins, due in 2027.

## A couture gown in Mexico City

For the film's premiere in Mexico City, Grace chose a champagne-toned gown from ALmée Couture's The Art of Remembering collection, according to [theFashionSpot on Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/fashion/articles/mckenna-grace-art-plunging-almee-080743352.html). The dress has a plunging neckline, crystal embellishment, a thigh-high slit and a lace-up back. Sculpted bird ornaments sit on both shoulders, inspired by the mythical Cambodian Keno bird, and a King Protea bloom in blush pink and iridescent blue sits at the hips before a champagne train.

It is Look 23, "Aetherial Majesty", from the label's Paris Fashion Week show. Grace paired it with metallic gold platform heels, flower-shaped diamond studs and a low bun with a deep side part. Styling was again by Molly Dickson.

## A District 7 clue in a Celine top

The most talked-about outfit may be the simplest. [Reality Tea](https://www.realitytea.com/2026/11/13/mckenna-grace-woody-top-micro-minidress-celine-press/) says Grace wore an archival Celine top with a woodgrain print as a micro minidress in Mexico City, with knee-high chocolate-brown boots. Her hair was in glossy waves with one braid decorated with small green leaf-like accents.

Dickson explained the idea in an Instagram post, quoted by the outlet: "McKenna x Céline by Phoebe Philo District 7. Lumber. Trees". District 7 is the lumber district in the Hunger Games books, which makes the print a quiet nod to the story rather than a costume.

## What happens next

Sunrise on the Reaping opens on 20 November. Grace plays Maysilee Donner, with Zada as a young Haymitch Abernathy, according to Fashion Times UK. The Hunger Games series is based on [Suzanne Collins' novels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games). Scooby-Doo: Origins follows in 2027.
