**Quick summary**

- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) was announced as the next James Bond on Monday 16 November 2026.
- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) had been among the favourites, but reports say he did not do enough at the screen-test stage.
- [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) is said to have missed out for the same reason.
- The film is expected to start shooting in 2027 and reach cinemas in 2028, with no exact dates confirmed.

After his Callum Turner Bond screen test, the actor has lost the race to play 007. Robert Pattinson was named on Monday 16 November, and British outlets say Turner and Jack Lowden failed to impress at the final screen tests.

## How Callum Turner lost out

According to [The Mirror](https://www.mirror.co.uk/3am/celebrity-news/robert-pattinson-james-bond-007-37884434), the last candidates were seen on different days and put through their paces in front of cameras and several directors. The paper reports that the decision then came quickly, and that Turner and Lowden "just didn't do enough" at that stage. The studio has not commented on why any actor was passed over.

The [Daily Mail](https://www.dailymail.com/tvshowbiz/article-16296889/How-Robert-Pattinson-finally-got-Bond-role-Callum-Turner-Jack-Lowden-failed-impress-bosses-Batman-star-won-talents-friendship-director-ability-deal-scrutiny.html) tells a similar story. It says Pattinson's name only entered the conversation last week, while Turner, Lowden and a "mystery actor" had been described as the frontrunners. In its account, something was still missing until the screen tests of the later contenders.

## The case for the late entrants

Journalist [Matthew Belloni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matthew_Belloni) offered one reading of the shift. As quoted by both The Mirror and the Daily Mail, he wrote that the studio "wouldn't have gone through the trouble of bringing in established stars like Pattinson and Hoult" if it had been happy with the lesser-known candidates.

He also pointed to Pattinson's experience of scrutiny. Belloni noted that the actor has been a star since 2007 and survived the backlash to his Twilight casting. Pattinson is 40, according to the Daily Mail, and had already worked with director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve) on the upcoming Dune 3. The Mirror, citing The Hollywood Reporter, says it was Villeneuve who told him he had the part.

## Turner's run as a favourite

For a long stretch, Turner looked like the safe bet. India TV News reports that he was considered one of the strongest favourites for a considerable period, and that former Bond actor Pierce Brosnan and actor George Clooney publicly backed him.

He was not alone. India TV says five actors reportedly reached the final screen-test stage, with Nicholas Hoult, Josh O'Connor and Lowden competing alongside Turner and Pattinson. The Mirror adds that O'Connor had been the bookmakers' favourite, while Pattinson was once available at 50/1 with some of them. [Slow Horses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slow_Horses) star Lowden reportedly had public support from his co-star Gary Oldman.

## The team behind the new Bond

This is the first Bond film since long-time producers Barbara Broccoli and Michael G Wilson stepped back and Amazon MGM Studios took creative control, according to The Mirror. Villeneuve will direct, [Amy Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Pascal) and [David Heyman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Heyman) will produce, and Steven Knight is writing the screenplay, India TV reports.

Pascal and Heyman said Pattinson's "fearlessness as an actor" and "self-deprecating wit" made him the right choice. Pattinson called the role a dream come true, according to The Mirror. He is the seventh actor to play [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) on the big screen.

## What happens next

Production on the film, currently known as Bond 26, is expected to begin in 2027 ahead of a 2028 release, according to The Mirror. Nothing more specific has been announced. Turner has not publicly responded to the reports in the sources we reviewed.

### Did Callum Turner audition for Bond?
Reports say he was one of the final candidates to take part in screen tests, alongside Pattinson, Nicholas Hoult, Josh O'Connor and Jack Lowden. The studio has not confirmed the list.

The Mirror and the Daily Mail report that he did not do enough at the screen-test stage. That is a media report, not an official explanation.
