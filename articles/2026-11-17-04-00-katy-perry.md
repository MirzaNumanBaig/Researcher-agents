**Quick summary**

- [Katy Perry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katy_Perry) has not publicly commented on ex-husband Russell Brand's sentence of 10 years and six months, handed down on 9 November, according to SheKnows.
- The Mail on Sunday reports her team has spoken to Netflix and Hulu about a possible docuseries. Nothing is confirmed, and no release date exists.
- An old Vogue interview, in which she spoke of "the real truth" about the split, has drawn fresh attention.
- Perry attended the Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday with her partner, former Canadian prime minister Justin Trudeau.

Katy Perry has said nothing in public about Russell Brand's prison sentence, but the story keeps circling back to her. Brand, her husband for about 14 months, was sentenced on 9 November, and in the days since, reports have surfaced about a possible docuseries and a decade-old interview that now reads differently.

## What Perry has and has not said

According to [SheKnows](https://www.sheknows.com/entertainment/articles/1235093327/katy-perry-russell-brand-prison-response/), Perry has not commented on the sentence. The outlet points to a remark she reportedly made at a concert in Oxfordshire in July 2026, citing Fashion Times UK: "I hope my ex isn't here. He should be in jail." She did not name Brand, and the line is second-hand, so it should be read with care.

Her earlier, on-the-record words are clearer. In a 2013 Vogue interview, she called Brand "very controlling" and said she first blamed herself for the end of the marriage. Then she learned something she described as "the real truth", which she kept "locked in my safe for a rainy day". She has never said what it was.

## Docuseries talks are reported, not confirmed

[Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/interviews/articles/katy-perry-reportedly-docuseries-talks-233000946.html) reports that a source told The Mail on Sunday that Perry had received offers to tell her story over the years and waited until Brand's trial ended. Her team has reportedly been in touch with Netflix and Hulu, and an autobiography is said to be under consideration.

The same report is clear about the limits: the project is at the talks stage, with no streamer, format or date confirmed. The Crown Prosecution Service also told the paper that Perry was never approached for a witness statement. A separate source told PEOPLE that she has come to see the divorce as ultimately for the best.

## The case and the new documentary

A jury at Southwark Crown Court convicted Brand on 5 November of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault involving four women, with offences between 2004 and 2009. He was acquitted on three other charges. Those dates fall before he married Perry in India in October 2010. Brand pleaded not guilty, and SheKnows reports that his wife, Laura Gallacher, read a statement on their YouTube show on Sunday in which he said he is not guilty of the offences. A spokesman for the prime minister told the BBC that the claim is being looked into, noting that prisoners are barred from issuing statements that distress victims.

The Netflix documentary *Russell Brand: Open Secret*, directed by Olivia Isaacs, arrived on 13 November and runs 84 minutes. According to [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/russell-brands-ex-says-told-180103678.html), former girlfriend Jordan Martin says Brand told her in 2007 he should date "somebody more famous". She links that remark to his later marriage to Perry. Martin's account is her own claim, and the film's producers say Brand did not respond to their invitation to reply through his lawyers. The judge said he disregarded allegations in the film when sentencing. Away from the courtroom coverage, Perry appeared at the Baby2Baby Gala in West Hollywood on Saturday with Trudeau, per [Page Six](https://pagesix.com/2026/11/16/hollywood/orlando-bloom-awkwardly-seated-near-exes-miranda-kerr-katy-perry-and-their-new-partners-at-hollywood-gala/). "It didn't seem to bother anyone," the source said. The 15th edition of the event raised more than US$20 million for children's essentials. ## What happens next

Perry may keep her silence, or the reported talks may produce something public. Neither outcome is confirmed. On the legal side, SheKnows reports that six more women have made allegations against Brand, published on 13 November by The Times and Sunday Times once his criminal case ended. Yahoo reports he is reportedly under investigation over further allegations. Those are allegations only, and he has not been convicted of them.
