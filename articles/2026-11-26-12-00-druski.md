**Quick summary**

- Chris Rock Druski praise: [Chris Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Rock) called [Druski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druski) "the most confident, well-adjusted young comedian I've ever seen" on The A24 Podcast, released on 25 November.
- Rock said the only comedian he had seen with that much confidence was [Eddie Murphy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eddie_Murphy).
- He was reflecting on Druski's debut as host of [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live) on 7 November, which drew 5.6 million viewers, according to Wisevoter.
- Druski's Netflix series Collect & Praise premieres on 1 December.

Chris Rock Druski praise is the talk of comedy this week, and it is some of the highest in stand-up. Speaking with [Ben Stiller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_Stiller) on The A24 Podcast, released on 25 November, Rock said Druski's confidence when hosting Saturday Night Live was unlike anything he had seen in a young comic, apart from Eddie Murphy. The remarks land days before Druski's Netflix series arrives.

## What Chris Rock said about Druski

Rock told Stiller he had watched the SNL episode in person. According to [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/tracewilliamcowen/chris-rock-praises-druski-confidence-eddie-murphy), he first singled out Druski's "funny sketch stuff", including a parody of Joe Jackson, before turning to the hosting debut.

"It was the most confident, well-adjusted, young comedian I've ever seen. It was so healthy," Rock said. He added that Druski "was sure of himself" and that the show was a great one.

Then came the comparison that spread online. "The only guy that I've ever seen that confident was Eddie Murphy," Rock said, per [The Quintessential Gentleman](https://www.theqgentleman.com/post/2026-11-chris-rock-gives-druski-his-flowers-and-reflects-on-the-next-generation-of-comedy/).

## A joke at his own generation's expense

Rock turned the praise on his own peers. Comedians of his era, he said, had no confidence at all and spent their time asking audiences to love them. [Hip Hop Vibe](https://hip-hopvibe.com/news/chris-rock-druski-confidence-eddie-murphy-snl/) reports that he also keeps watching new stand-up specials and sometimes checks how many each performer has made, calling the confidence of newer comics "sickening sometimes", with amazement rather than bitterness.

A clip of the exchange was shared online as Rock "calling out" young comedians. Hip Hop Vibe notes that many viewers pushed back and read it as a shout-out instead. The full quotes support that reading.

Stiller suggested that social media lets comedians build their voices and audiences before they reach traditional entertainment, according to The Quintessential Gentleman.

## The SNL night in numbers

Druski hosted Season 52 of Saturday Night Live on 7 November. Wisevoter reports the episode drew [5.6 million viewers](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/2026/11/25/druski-hosts-snl-5-6-million-viewers) across NBC and Peacock, the show's biggest audience in two years. It describes him as the first digital content creator to host. Hip Hop Vibe words it more carefully, calling him the first host known mainly for online work.

The Strokes were the musical guest, and Emma Stone, his co-star in the upcoming film The Catch, joined his monologue, per Hip Hop Vibe. At the end of the show, Rock and Dave Chappelle walked out on stage together.

Rock's praise has a history. Hip Hop Vibe says he texted Druski after the 2026 BET Awards, calling him the "best host ever".

## Collect & Praise is next

The timing matters. Netflix has announced a multi-year partnership with Druski. According to [Wisevoter](https://wisevoter.com/world/us/2026/11/25/druski-netflix-partnership-collect-and-praise), the deal includes the semi-scripted comedy Collect & Praise, premiering on 1 December, and a licensing agreement for his YouTube back catalogue. Mike Epps plays a deacon opposite Druski's megachurch pastor. Financial terms have not been disclosed.

The series grows out of a sketch that went viral in January. [ChurchLeaders](https://churchleaders.com/news/2226134-druski-megachurch-satire-collect-praise-trailer.html) reports the official trailer takes a darker turn, with the pastor and his deacon running into trouble with the law. Wisevoter puts his social following at nearly 40 million and his YouTube views above one billion.

## What happens next

Collect & Praise streams on Netflix from 1 December, so the first real test of the Rock comparison comes within days. Druski also has the film The Catch with Stone on the way, though the sources give no release date.
