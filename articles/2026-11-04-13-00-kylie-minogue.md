**Quick summary**

- [Kylie Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Minogue) announced on 3 November 2026 that her song "Light Up" has been nominated at the Hollywood Music in Media Awards.
- The track is up in the Song – Documentary Film category, according to ComingSoon.net.
- She wrote it for her three-part Netflix documentary, "Kylie", which premiered on 20 May 2026.
- The 17th annual ceremony is scheduled for 18 November, when the winners will be revealed.

Kylie Minogue says she is "lost for the words" after "Light Up", the song she wrote for her Netflix documentary, was nominated at the 2026 Hollywood Music in Media Awards. The singer shared the news on [X](https://x.com/kylieminogue) on 3 November, and fans will not have long to wait for the result.

## Kylie Minogue's Light Up nomination

According to [ComingSoon.net](https://www.comingsoon.net/tv/news/2217515-kylie-minogue-netflix-tv-show-song-hollywood-award-nomination), the song is nominated in the Song – Documentary Film category. The 17th annual Hollywood Music in Media Awards ceremony is set for 18 November, which is 15 days after her announcement.

In her post, Minogue described the track as "a really dedicated endeavour and experience". That wording comes from her own message, which ComingSoon.net quoted in full. The outlet described her reaction as emotional, and the song clearly carries personal weight for her.

## What the song is about

Minogue explained the idea behind the track in the same post. She wrote that it is about how people who truly love you can see your potential even when you cannot, and how "beautiful and profound" that is, whether it is spoken or unspoken.

She expanded on this at a Q&A at Spotify's Listening Lounge in London, as reported by ComingSoon.net. There she said the song is about the love a parent, or someone who cares for you, can offer when you are lost, confused, lonely or distressed. Someone who loves you, she said, can look into your eyes and see a world of potential.

It is a gentle theme for a documentary built around looking back, and it explains why the song sits where it does. "Light Up" plays in the final episode of the series.

## The documentary behind the song

"Kylie" is a three-part Netflix series directed by Michael Harte. It premiered on 20 May 2026 and follows Minogue as she reflects on her life and career through personal photographs, home videos and interviews.

The series also features contributions from her sister [Dannii Minogue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dannii_Minogue), her former Neighbours co-star [Jason Donovan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Donovan), musician [Nick Cave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Cave) and producer [Pete Waterman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Waterman), according to ComingSoon.net. ## A separate Kylie mention in the beauty news

Minogue also turned up in lifestyle coverage this week. The Daily Mail [reported](https://www.dailymail.com/lifestyle/beauty/article-16263433/bloom-cosmetics-lip-gloss-kylie-minogue-queen-mary-comeback.html) that Bloom Cosmetics, the Melbourne brand founded in 1993 by Natalie Bloom, is making a comeback with the help of her daughters Chloe and Amber.

The paper says Minogue reportedly wore the brand's tangerine lip gloss on tour, a detail it did not tie to any named source. It also lists [Elle Macpherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Macpherson), [Naomi Campbell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naomi_Campbell) and the late [Olivia Newton-John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Newton-John) among the brand's admirers. Treat the tour claim as unconfirmed.
