**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) returns to [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) on Saturday 24 October after missing week four.
- She sat out the previous show with a fractured rib, according to The Irish News.
- The BBC says she has been given the all clear to dance again.
- She and partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) will dance a rumba to a [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion) ballad.

Australian singer Delta Goodrem says she is "grateful" to be back on the Strictly Come Dancing floor this weekend, after a fractured rib forced her to miss last week's show. The 41-year-old and her professional partner Kai Widdrington are due to dance in week five on Saturday night, with the show airing at 6.30pm on BBC One and iPlayer.

## What Goodrem said about the injury

In an Instagram post, Goodrem shared clips of her time in hospital, which she filmed over the past two weeks. According to [The Irish News](https://www.irishnews.com/entertainment/delta-goodrem-grateful-ahead-of-strictly-come-dancing-return-from-injury-QJL3GQVEAZJ7LKZPBZYXGAQWNE/), she said she filmed small moments to remind herself that progress would come even on the frustrating days.

"I was gutted when the injury happened as I've been truly loving this experience so much," she wrote. She added that it is hard to be patient when you want to be at full strength, but that she had been listening to advice, taking things carefully and learning to appreciate small steps.

In the clips she becomes tearful as she says she hopes to return, and she thanks fans for their support. She closed her post with a message to "all my warriors out there in your own battles", adding, "we are fighters."

## Cleared by the show

The [Harwich and Manningtree Standard](https://www.harwichandmanningtreestandard.co.uk/news/national/uk-today/26667029.delta-goodrem-strictly-come-dancing-tonight-injury/) reports that Goodrem was given a bye last weekend, meaning she did not dance and was not at risk of a public vote. A Strictly spokesperson said the production was "delighted to confirm" she had been given the all clear to dance again.

Goodrem also spoke on the programme's Training Room Diaries this week. "I'm very, very grateful to be back," she said, describing her approach as "step-by-stepping it very gently."

The break comes after a strong start. The Standard says she and Widdrington topped the judges' leaderboard in weeks one and three, so the injury arrived at a point when the pair were among the front runners.

## The week five routine

Goodrem and Widdrington will dance a rumba to Celine Dion's "It's All Coming Back to Me Now". The rumba is the slowest of the Latin dances and leans on control and emotion rather than speed, which suits a careful return, although neither source comments on how the choreography has been adjusted.

All 12 remaining couples are back in the ballroom this week. [Dani Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dani_Dyer) and [Nikita Kuzmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Kuzmin) will dance a salsa to Rosalia's "Despecha". Actress [Bethany Antonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethany_Antonia), who narrowly avoided elimination last week after landing in the bottom two, will dance an Argentine tango with [Nancy Xu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Xu).

The judging panel is Shirley Ballas, Motsi Mabuse, Anton Du Beke and Craig Revel Horwood. Josh Widdicombe, Emma Willis and Johannes Radebe present.

## What happens next

The show continues on Sunday at 7.10pm on BBC One and iPlayer. How Goodrem's rib holds up over the coming weeks will depend on what she and the production team judge safe, and neither source gives a timetable for a full recovery.
