**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) attended the Chanel Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on Thursday with her youngest daughter.
- The event was Chanel's first runway show in Australia, held at The Cutaway in Barangaroo, according to The West Australian.
- [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), also a Chanel ambassador, attended, as did Kylie Minogue, Cathy Freeman and Baz Luhrmann.
- The shows coincided with the opening of a new Chanel flagship store in Sydney. A renovated Melbourne store is due later this month.

[Nicole Kidman](https://nicolekidmanofficial.com/) chose a quieter plus-one for the biggest night of Australian fashion this year. At the Chanel Cruise 2026/27 show in Sydney on Thursday, the Oscar winner sat front row with her youngest daughter, who is 15, rather than her eldest biological daughter, who usually joins her at fashion events.

## What Nicole Kidman wore to the Chanel Cruise show

According to Harper's BAZAAR, Kidman went for a classic monochrome look: a cropped black jacket with coattails at the back, matching straight-leg trousers, and white layers underneath. She left the jacket open over a thin white vest and a white tank top, and finished the outfit with black satin pumps.

Her daughter chose something softer. Harper's BAZAAR describes a cream ribbed zip-up jacket with two tiers of gold-studded pockets, a matching mini skirt, a camel suede handbag and strappy silver mules.

The choice of companion stood out. The magazine notes that Kidman's older daughter, 18, accompanied her to the Met Gala in May, to a Dior couture show earlier in the year and to the 2024 Paris Olympics. This time it was the younger sister's turn.

## A growing run of public appearances

Harper's BAZAAR says the teenager has appeared with her mother more often lately. The two were seen at the US Open in September, and last month she sat front row beside Kidman at the Chanel Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear show. The outlet adds that Kidman once seemed more inclined to keep her youngest out of the spotlight, which makes the recent run of outings a change.

## Why this Chanel runway mattered for Australia

The Sydney show was a first for the brand. The West Australian reports that Chanel staged its first ever runway shows in Australia across a two-show day at The Cutaway in Barangaroo, with Kidman and Robbie leading a guest list of local stars.

Kidman, a Chanel ambassador, wore a black blazer and trousers, the paper says. Robbie, also an official brand representative, wore a sheer white dress with floral embellishment. Beside them sat Western Australian surfer Jack Robinson, who partnered with Chanel earlier this year to promote its J12 watch.

Others named in the crowd included Olympic champion Cathy Freeman, pop star Kylie Minogue, filmmaker Baz Luhrmann and DJ Dom Dolla. Our earlier coverage looked at [Kidman landing in Sydney for Chanel's Australian debut](https://moduscarry.com/nicole-kidman-chanel-sydney/) and at [Robbie joining her at the show](https://moduscarry.com/margot-robbie-chanel-sydney/).

Bruno Pavlovsky, the long-time president of fashion at Chanel, told The Australian the country was ready. He said the brand had wanted to do something "very unique and special" in Australia and had now reached the point where it could. For a house that normally stages its cruise collections in far-flung locations, that is a notable endorsement of the local market.

## What happens next

The runway shows coincided with the opening of a new Chanel flagship store in Sydney, according to The West Australian. A renovated Melbourne store is set to be unveiled later this month, which gives the brand a second local moment before the year ends. Neither report says whether Kidman will attend further events on this trip.
