**Quick summary**

- Director Francis Lawrence confirmed that a Maysilee Donner scene, played by Mckenna Grace, was filmed for *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping* and later cut.
- The scene has Maysilee and a District 4 tribute turning on Gamemakers in the arena, followed by a fight with Haymitch.
- Lawrence says it did not fit the film's themes. He believes it will appear among the deleted scenes on the home release.
- The first assembly cut ran four hours and 12 minutes, according to ScreenRant's account of a BAFTA interview.

The Mckenna Grace cut scene many book readers wanted to see was shot, then dropped. [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace), who plays Maysilee in the new prequel, and director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) have both explained why, in interviews reported this weekend by Cinemablend and ScreenRant. Minor spoilers for the film follow.

## What was cut from Sunrise on the Reaping

In the [Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) novel, Maysilee joins a District 4 career tribute named Maritte in killing a Gamemaker inside the arena. Cinemablend says that sequence, and the argument with Haymitch that follows it, did not make the final film.

Lawrence told Cinemablend the scene was filmed during principal photography. He called it probably the most debated sequence of the whole development process. ScreenRant, citing an interview with Bustle, reports that he said "there was a lot of discussion around it" before the team decided it was not doing enough for character or theme.

ScreenRant adds that readers have reacted loudly online, and the Gamemaker sequence frustrated them most because, in the book, it sets off Maysilee's death.

## Why the director says it was dropped

The reasoning centres on a theme from [Suzanne Collins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzanne_Collins)'s book. In the novel, the fight between Maysilee and Haymitch is about what the author calls implicit submission to the Capitol. Lawrence told Cinemablend that by this point in the film, Haymitch, played by [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada), is no longer submitting, so the argument "didn't feel like it thematically fit".

In the BAFTA interview quoted by ScreenRant, he described the argument shifting towards what he called war theory, and said the sequence was shortened repeatedly before being removed entirely. He added that Collins was fine with the cut.

ScreenRant says the Gamemaker scene ran roughly five minutes. Producer Nina Jacobson also spoke about the trade-off, saying the filmmakers feel pressure over how long an audience can be asked to wait before the arena. Sometimes, she said, lifting a scene is painful.

## What Mckenna Grace said about it

Grace told Cinemablend the argument with Zada was one of her favourite things to film. She described the pair screaming at each other in the woods.

She also agreed with the decision. As she explained it, the film does not have the time to show the characters repairing a rift the way the book does, including the shared-sardines moment. Splitting them at that point made little sense when their bond had just become so strong.

Cinemablend notes that the result keeps the two District 12 tributes united until Maysilee is killed by bird mutts, which gives her death and Haymitch's reaction more weight. The outlet also says reviews of the film were mixed, though its writer called the adaptation a success.

## The four-hour first cut

Lawrence told BAFTA the first assembly came to four hours and 12 minutes, according to ScreenRant. He said he does not want anyone to see it, and that most of what was cut is simple trimming for pace. A few full scenes were removed.

## What happens next

The film is in cinemas now, according to Cinemablend. Lawrence believes the Maysilee sequence will be one of the deleted scenes when the film reaches home release, though neither outlet gives a release date or a confirmed list of extras. ## Frequently asked questions

### Was Mckenna Grace's Maysilee scene actually filmed?

Yes. Francis Lawrence confirmed to Cinemablend that the Gamemaker sequence was shot during principal photography before being cut in the edit.

### Why was the Maysilee Gamemaker scene cut from Sunrise on the Reaping?

Lawrence says it did not fit thematically. He argues Haymitch is no longer submitting to the Capitol at that point in the film, so the argument that follows in the book did not track.

### Will the deleted scene be released?

Lawrence believes it will be among the deleted scenes on the home release. No release date or extras list has been confirmed in the reports we read.
