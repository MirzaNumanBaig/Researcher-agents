**Quick summary**

- The Sun reports that Stanley Tucci has joined MobLand season 3 as a powerful villain, according to Radio Times.
- The same report says former Manchester United striker Zlatan Ibrahimović will play his henchman.
- Radio Times has asked Paramount+ for comment, and the casting is unconfirmed.
- Filming on the third season is under way, with Tom Hardy returning as Harry Da Souza.

Stanley Tucci is reportedly the newest face in [Tom Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hardy)'s crime drama MobLand, with a tabloid report on 27 November saying he will play the show's most dangerous antagonist yet. The claim comes from The Sun and was relayed by [Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times), which says it has approached Paramount+ for a response. Until the streamer replies, treat it as a report rather than a fact.

## What is being reported about Stanley Tucci

According to The Sun, as summarised by Radio Times, Tucci will play an "evil villain" described as a rich and powerful watch horologist. The paper's unnamed insider said production bosses are delighted to have him and that his character will challenge the Harrigan family business by targeting its weaknesses.

The same report adds a surprise: Swedish former striker Zlatan Ibrahimović is said to be playing the villain's henchman. The source reportedly described the pair as an unlikely on-screen duo that the team is excited to see together. Neither casting has been confirmed by Paramount+, and the insider is not named.

## Where this fits in the season 3 cast

Radio Times notes these are the latest castings reported for the new season. [Gregg Sulkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregg_Sulkin) is also joining, according to Deadline, a move we covered in [Gregg Sulkin Joins Tom Hardy's MobLand for Season 3](https://moduscarry.com/mobland-season-3-cast-gregg-sulkin/).

On the returning side, Radio Times says only two stars are officially confirmed: Hardy and Helen Mirren, along with [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) as Conrad Harrigan. Several others are expected back but not yet confirmed: [Paddy Considine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paddy_Considine), [Lara Pulver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lara_Pulver), [Anson Boon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anson_Boon), [Mandeep Dhillon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandeep_Dhillon), [Jasmine Jobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jasmine_Jobson), [Teddie Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teddie_Allen), Emmett J Scanlan, [Ophelia Lovibond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ophelia_Lovibond) and [Toby Jones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toby_Jones).

## Why a new villain makes sense

Radio Times points out that a fresh threat to the Harrigans would fit where the story was left. In the season 2 finale, Hardy's Harry Da Souza took down [Janet McTeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janet_McTeer)'s Kat McAllister, while Johnny Flynn's Frankie was left at the mercy of Mirren's Maeve. With those storylines closed or cliffhanging, an outside power is a natural next step. The publication's piece contains spoilers for that finale.

Filming on season 3 is now officially under way, as we reported in [Tom Hardy Back on MobLand Set for Season 3 Filming](https://moduscarry.com/tom-hardy-mobland-season-3-2/). You can find everything we have published on the actor on our [Tom Hardy tag page](https://moduscarry.com/tag/tom-hardy/), and more in [TV & Dramas](https://moduscarry.com/category/tv-dramas/).

## What happens next

Radio Times is waiting on a reply from Paramount+. Official confirmation, or a denial, would settle whether Tucci and Ibrahimović are on the call sheet. Radio Times only says season 3 is "coming soon" on Paramount+, with no release date given.
