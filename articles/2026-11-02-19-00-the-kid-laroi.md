**Quick summary**

- [The Kid LAROI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kid_Laroi) opened the UK leg of his A Perfect World Tour at O2 Academy Birmingham, according to Happy Mag.
- He played a 26-song set that leans on his latest album, *Before I Forget*, and keeps room for older favourites.
- Live Nation lists the Birmingham show on 27 October, with the European run ending in Prague on 17 November.
- Remaining stops include London, Paris and Berlin.

The Sydney-raised singer and rapper has started the European half of his world tour, and the **Kid LAROI tour setlist** from the first night is now public. [Happy Mag](https://happymag.tv/setlist-the-kid-laroi-a-perfect-world-tour/) reports that he performed 26 songs at O2 Academy Birmingham to open the UK leg of A Perfect World Tour.

## What the Kid LAROI tour setlist looks like

Happy Mag says much of the night is given to *Before I Forget*, his newest project. [Live Nation's tour announcement](https://news.livenationentertainment.com/news/the-kid-laroi-announces-global-a-perfect-world-tour-kicking-off-april-27) describes it as his third full-length album, a 15-track record released on 9 January 2026 through Columbia Records.

The new material sits alongside the songs that built his audience. Happy Mag names "Without You", "Girls" and "Nights Like This" among the fan favourites, along with "Stay", the chart-topping collaboration with [Justin Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justin_Bieber). With 26 songs on the list, the show gives the recent album a large share without pushing the older hits aside.

Happy Mag's report does not break down the order of the songs in the text we could read, so check the original setlist post if you want the running order.

## How the album has performed

The tour is built around a record that had a solid start. Live Nation says *Before I Forget* debuted at No. 6 on the Billboard 200 with 41,000 equivalent album units in its first week. That was his second top-10 entry on the chart. His 2023 album *The First Time* had opened lower, at No. 26.

The same release describes the new album as defined by vulnerability and emotional clarity. A setlist weighted towards it suggests he is happy to put those songs at the centre of the show.

## The route from Birmingham to Prague

A Perfect World Tour began on 27 April in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and Live Nation lists 35 dates in all across North America and Europe. The North American run finished on 11 June. The European leg runs from 27 October to 17 November and covers 10 cities.

Birmingham, Manchester and London are the UK stops. After those, the schedule moves on to Brussels, Amsterdam, Paris, Milan, Berlin, Copenhagen and finally the O2 Arena in Prague on 17 November, as Happy Mag also notes. Tommy Richman supported on the North American dates, while Live Nation had not announced European support when the tour was revealed.

## A homecoming earlier in the year

Happy Mag points out that Laroi, a proud Kamilaroi man from the southern suburbs of Sydney, played a small homecoming earlier this year as part of the Australian Open Festival lineup. The outlet says he has never been quiet about his roots, and that he is now selling out arenas across Europe. Live Nation notes he is 22 and received a Best New Artist nomination at the 64th Grammy Awards.

## What happens next

The tour has several dates to go. After the UK shows, Laroi heads through continental Europe before the final night in Prague on 17 November. Fans with tickets for the later shows can expect a similar mix of new songs and older hits, though the exact set can change from city to city. Tickets for remaining dates are sold through the artist's site, [tkl.world](https://www.tkl.world/).
