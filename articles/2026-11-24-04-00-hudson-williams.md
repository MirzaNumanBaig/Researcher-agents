**Quick summary**

- [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) told Vogue on 23 November that he is living with co-star [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) while they film season 2 of Heated Rivalry.
- The show is expected back in spring 2027, though no premiere date has been announced.
- Separately, Crave has ordered a second season of the mystery series Yaga, in which Williams stars.
- Yaga's two-part season 1 finale airs on 27 November.

Two of the busiest young actors on television are now sharing a roof. [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie) says he is living with [Hudson Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hudson_Williams) while the pair shoot the second season of [Heated Rivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry), a detail he shared in a Vogue interview published on Monday 23 November. On the same day, Williams' other new show, Yaga, picked up a second-season order.

## Why Connor Storrie moved in with Hudson Williams

Storrie, 26, appears on Vogue's Best Dressed 2026 digital cover, in an interview by [Chloe Malle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chloe_Malle) with photography by [Collier Schorr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collier_Schorr). Asked how often he sees his co-star, he answered plainly: "Right now we're filming, so I literally live with him, which is amazing," as [Just Jared](https://www.justjared.com/2026/11/23/connor-storrie-living-with-hudson-williams-heated-rivalry-season-2-vogue-interview/) reported.

The reason is practical. According to [Attitude](https://www.attitude.co.uk/culture/connor-storrie-living-with-heated-rivalry-hudson-williams-540415/), the two had been staying in a hotel. Storrie called it a brilliant hotel but said he needed a washing machine and a kitchen. The Express Tribune notes that [production on season 2 began in Toronto in August 2026](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2641896/connor-storrie-reveals-surprising-details-about-his-life-with-hudson-williams).

## Style, friendship and boundaries

The interview was mostly about fashion. Storrie said the two do not coordinate their red carpet looks, yet "we are very cut from the same cloth," and he admitted he envies Williams' street-style edge. He also credited stylist James Yardley for his growing interest in dressing for events, and said he had no idea about the "Buzznor" nickname fans gave his shorter haircut until people mentioned it in person, because he keeps social media off his phone.

[PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/11/23/connor-storrie-hudson-williams-heated-rivalry-season-2/) recalled that Williams has spoken about keeping some distance in public. In an earlier Wonderland interview he said their friendship "doesn't need to be public" and that appearing together constantly "starts to feel like branding." Both actors made their Met Gala debuts in May 2026, but did not pose together on the red carpet, the outlet noted.

## What Heated Rivalry season 2 looks like

Storrie plays Russian hockey star Ilya Rozanov and Williams plays Canadian player Shane Hollander. Attitude reports that season 2 will draw mainly on Rachel Reid's novel The Long Game and that creator Jacob Tierney told CBS News in October he expected a "late spring" release.

Attitude also says earlier reports claimed seasons 2 and 3 are being filmed back to back in Toronto and elsewhere in Canada. A third season has not been officially confirmed, though both leads reportedly signed a three-season deal. Those points are reports, not announcements, so treat them with care.

## Yaga gets a second season

Williams' other project is moving fast. [The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-news/crave-second-season-yaga-1236761048/) says Crave has greenlit season 2 of Yaga, Kat Sandler's reworking of the Baba Yaga myth about a missing college student. The series premiered on 23 October on Crave. Carrie-Anne Moss returns, and Justin Stockman of Bell Media said the response "has blown us away."

In season 1 Williams plays the spoiled son of a business magnate who disappears. [Comic Book Resources](https://www.cbr.com/hudson-williams-yaga-season-2-renewal/) says he has a dual role, as missing heir Henry Park and his roommate Logan Casey, and that Moss is so far the only cast member confirmed for season 2. It also reports that the show holds a 75 percent Certified Fresh score from 32 critics.

Australian viewers can find season 1 on BINGE and Foxtel, according to The Hollywood Reporter.

## What happens next

The two-part Yaga finale airs on 27 November. Heated Rivalry season 2 is expected in spring 2027, with no date yet. No official word has been given on whether Williams returns for Yaga season 2.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Hudson Williams living with Connor Storrie?
Storrie told Vogue he is, saying he lives with Williams while they film Heated Rivalry season 2. Williams has not commented in the sources reviewed.

### When does Heated Rivalry season 2 come out?
No date is set. Reports point to spring 2027, and Tierney has spoken of a late spring release.

### Is Yaga coming back for season 2?
Yes. Crave has ordered a second season, with Carrie-Anne Moss set to return.

### Where can Australians watch Yaga?
According to The Hollywood Reporter, season 1 streamed on BINGE and Foxtel in Australia.
