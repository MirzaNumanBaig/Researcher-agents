**Quick summary**

- Billy Evans, Elizabeth Holmes's partner, says he never saw anything romantic between Holmes and director Nathan Fielder during filming of *You Can See Everything*, according to Variety.
- Evans says Fielder "cares a lot" about Holmes, but that he did not get that impression "for one minute" as romance.
- He also confirmed he and Fielder have not spoken since the film came out.
- The film opened on 6 September 2026 and holds a 96% Rotten Tomatoes score, according to Wikipedia.

Billy Evans has answered the question many viewers took home from *You Can See Everything*: was there more than friendship between director [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and Elizabeth Holmes? Speaking in an interview reported by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/nathan-fielder-elizabeth-holmes-billy-evans-1236948256/) on 16 November, Evans said no.

## What Billy Evans said about Fielder and Holmes

Variety reports that some audiences left screenings wondering about the nature of the relationship between Fielder and the convicted Theranos founder. Evans, who is Holmes's partner, told the interviewer that "a lot of people are infatuated by Elizabeth Holmes". He added that Fielder cares about her, but that he "didn't get the impression for one minute that it was romantic".

Evans also addressed a much-discussed shot of the two "frolicking in the water", the phrase used by the interviewer, Gutman. He said he was about five feet away, in the water himself, when it was taken. That detail matters because the image has fed much of the speculation.

These are Evans's own accounts. Neither Fielder nor Holmes is quoted in the Variety report, so this is one person's view of what happened on set.

## No contact since the release

Evans confirmed he and Fielder have not spoken since the documentary was released. Asked why, he said he thinks "they're probably worried I'm going to sue him". He then said he has "no intention to", adding that "maybe I'm a different person than most people think I am".

Variety does not report any legal action or threat. The comment was Evans's guess about how the filmmakers feel, and nothing in the source suggests a dispute beyond the silence he describes.

## His view of Amanda Seyfried's role

The interview also covered Amanda Seyfried's portrayal of Holmes inside the film. Evans said he was glad she was included, because it shows the gap between "the real thing" and how eager Holmes was to play a character. He said he would try not to speak poorly of others, because "a lot of people" had done that about him recently.

## The film behind the conversation

*You Can See Everything* is co-directed by Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, with Holmes, Fielder and Christopher Abbott in the cast, according to [Wikipedia's entry on the film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything). It was released on 6 September 2026. The same entry lists a budget of about US$7 million and a box office of about US$86.5 million, roughly twelve times what it cost.

Critics have been warm. Wikipedia records a 96% score on Rotten Tomatoes from 226 reviews and 97 out of 100 on Metacritic from 42 critics.
