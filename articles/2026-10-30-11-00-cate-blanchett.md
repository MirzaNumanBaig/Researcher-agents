**Quick summary**

- [Cate Blanchett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cate_Blanchett) will host the New York premiere of five Displacement Film Fund shorts at Film Forum on 11 November.
- Directors Maryna Er Gorbach and Mo Harawe will attend in person, according to Film Forum's listing.
- The fund, co-founded and led by Blanchett, backs filmmakers who are displaced or who tell stories about displacement.
- Separately, Foro3D reports that Todd Haynes will adapt Jean Genet's play Les Bonnes with Blanchett attached.

Cate Blanchett is taking a project close to her heart to New York. The actor and producer will host the city's premiere of the first films backed by the Displacement Film Fund on 11 November at Film Forum, the New York cinema said in its [event listing](https://filmforum.org/events/event/displacement-film-fund-november-11). Elsewhere, a report says her next stage-to-screen role is already taking shape.

## What the Displacement Film Fund is

Blanchett co-founded the Displacement Film Fund (DFF) and leads it. According to Film Forum, its purpose is to champion and finance filmmakers who are themselves displaced, or who have a proven record of telling authentic stories about displaced people.

The scale of the issue explains the focus. Film Forum cites a figure of one in every 70 people on earth forcibly displaced by conflict, war or persecution, which it describes as an unprecedented crisis. The idea for the fund was first conceived at the 2023 Global Refugee Forum, hosted by UNHCR, and it launched at the International Film Festival Rotterdam (IFFR) in 2025.

The International Film Festival Rotterdam's Hubert Bals Fund is the management partner. Film Forum lists the Amahoro Coalition, MASTERMIND, the Tamer Family Foundation, UNIQLO and Droom en Daad as founding partners, with the SP Lohia Foundation joining as main partner in 2026.

## The five films

The slate was directed by five filmmakers from five countries. After premiering at IFFR in 2026, the collection was invited to the London, Sao Paulo (Mostra) and Tokyo film festivals, according to Film Forum.

- **Rotation** (12 minutes), by [Maryna Er Gorbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryna_Er_Gorbach) of Ukraine, follows a young woman who has moved from civilian life into military service and undergoes a therapeutic hypnosis ritual. Gorbach's earlier film Klondike won a directing award at Sundance in 2022.
- **Super Afghan Gym** (14 minutes), by [Shahrbanoo Sadat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shahrbanoo_Sadat), is set in a Kabul gym during the one hour a day reserved for women. Her latest feature, No Good Men, opened the 2026 Berlin Film Festival.
- **Allies in Exile** (40 minutes), by Hasan Kattan, documents two Syrian filmmakers living in a UK asylum hotel. Kattan was cinematographer on the Oscar-winning The White Helmets.
- **Whispers of a Burning Scent** (27 minutes), by [Mo Harawe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mo_Harawe), centres on a wedding musician whose private life is exposed on the day of a court hearing. His debut feature, The Village Next to Paradise, was the first Somali film selected for Cannes, premiering there in 2024.
- **Sense of Water** (39 minutes), by [Mohammad Rasoulof](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammad_Rasoulof), follows an Iranian writer in exile who must find emotion again in a foreign language. Rasoulof directed The Seed of the Sacred Fig, which won the Special Jury Prize at Cannes in 2024.

Gorbach and Harawe will appear in person at each program, Film Forum says.

## A Genet adaptation with Todd Haynes

In a separate report, the site Foro3D says [Todd Haynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todd_Haynes) will bring Jean Genet's 1947 play [Les Bonnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Maids) to the screen with Blanchett. The play follows two servant sisters who act out a ritual of domination and submission while their mistress is away. Foro3D attributes the confirmation to an interview Haynes gave Le Point, and we have not seen that interview ourselves, so treat the casting details as reported rather than officially announced.

Blanchett knows the material. Foro3D notes she played Claire in the Sydney Theatre Company's stage production alongside [Isabelle Huppert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isabelle_Huppert). The report adds that filming will follow Haynes's current project, De Noche, with [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal), and that Genet already shaped Haynes's 1991 debut feature, Poison.

The main challenge, Foro3D observes, is translating a play built on a closed room and swapping roles into cinema without losing its symbolic weight.

## What happens next

The Film Forum screenings take place on 11 November, with Gorbach and Harawe answering to audiences in person. No release date, studio or start of filming has been reported for Les Bonnes.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Displacement Film Fund?

It is a fund co-founded and led by Cate Blanchett to support displaced filmmakers, and filmmakers who tell authentic stories about displacement. IFFR's Hubert Bals Fund manages it.

### When and where is the New York premiere?

It takes place on 11 November at Film Forum in New York, where Blanchett will host the screenings of the five shorts.

### Is Cate Blanchett confirmed for Todd Haynes's Les Bonnes?

Foro3D reports that Haynes confirmed the project to Le Point and that Blanchett is attached. We could not verify this with a primary source, so it should be treated as a report for now.
