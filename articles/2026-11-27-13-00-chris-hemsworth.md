**Quick summary**

- Netflix is expanding the Extraction films into a shared universe, according to [ScreenRant](https://screenrant.com/netflix-extraction-franchise-renewed-more-movies-tv-show/).
- Extraction 3 has finished production and is expected in 2027, with no release date yet.
- A South Korean spin-off film, Tygo, and a TV series, Mercenary: An Extraction Series, are also planned.
- The first two films each drew around 135 million views in their first 91 days on Netflix.

[Chris Hemsworth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Hemsworth) is about to see his Netflix action franchise grow well beyond its two films. Extraction 3 has wrapped production, and Netflix has lined up a Korean spin-off and a TV series, turning the thriller into a shared universe, ScreenRant reported on 26 November.

## What Netflix has announced for Extraction 3 and beyond

The third film brings Hemsworth back as Tyler Rake. It has no release date, though ScreenRant says it is expected in 2027. Two other projects sit alongside it.

The first is Tygo, a South Korean spin-off. It stars [Don Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_Dong-seok), Lisa and [Lee Jin-wook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Jin-wook), who is known for his work on [Squid Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid_Game). The story follows a former child soldier on a revenge mission against Korea's underworld, so it steps away from the rescue-job format of the main films.

The second is Mercenary: An Extraction Series, the franchise's first TV show. [Omar Sy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omar_Sy), [Natalie Dormer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natalie_Dormer) and [Boyd Holbrook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyd_Holbrook) lead the cast. A mercenary is hired to rescue hostages in Libya, which sits closer to the original formula.

## Why Netflix is building on the franchise

The numbers explain the push. According to Netflix data cited by ScreenRant, the first Extraction film collected 135.7 million views in its first 91 days on the service. Extraction 2 was close behind with 134.9 million views over the same period.

Critics were kinder to the sequel. On [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), the first film holds a 67% critics' score and a 72% audience score. Extraction 2 sits at 79% from critics and 84% from audiences. That is a clear step up, and it is the pattern ScreenRant says the spin-offs will need to repeat.

## Hemsworth's busy December

The timing is notable. Hemsworth's other big franchise, the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marvel_Cinematic_Universe), returns to cinemas with Avengers: Doomsday on 18 December, according to ScreenRant. Thor is one of the film's main players. Hemsworth has played the character in four solo films, a record shared with Tom Holland's Spider-Man. We also covered [how Hemsworth felt like an elder on the Avengers: Doomsday set](https://moduscarry.com/chris-hemsworth-avengers-doomsday-elder/).

ScreenRant's view is that Extraction will take over streaming conversation once Doomsday has had its run in theatres. That is the outlet's opinion, not a Netflix announcement.

## The John Wick comparison

ScreenRant frames the expansion as a race with John Wick, the R-rated franchise led by [Keanu Reeves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keanu_Reeves). Its two spin-offs so far have not matched the main films, the outlet notes. The Continental holds a 65% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, and the [Ana de Armas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ana_de_Armas) film Ballerina holds 75%. The lowest-rated John Wick film, the original, sits at 87%.

Ballerina also underperformed at the box office, according to ScreenRant. It took US$140.2 million on a reported US$90 million budget, which the outlet says fell short of the US$180 million to US$225 million it needed to break even. If the Extraction spin-offs land better, the franchise could pull ahead on that front.

Netflix has not confirmed a date for Extraction 3, Tygo or Mercenary. The first firm date on Hemsworth's calendar is Avengers: Doomsday on 18 December. Watch for a release window for the third film in the new year.
