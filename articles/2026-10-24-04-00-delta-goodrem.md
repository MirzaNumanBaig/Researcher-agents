**Quick summary**

- [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) told Digital Spy she would "highly support" a Eurovision week on *Strictly Come Dancing*.
- She finished fourth at this year's Eurovision in Vienna with the song "Eclipse".
- She missed last weekend's Icons week with a rib injury and returns this weekend with partner Kai Widdrington.
- A UK tour is planned, including a concert at the London Palladium next February.

Delta Goodrem has a request for the *Strictly Come Dancing* producers: give the show a Eurovision week. The Australian singer, who came fourth at this year's contest in Vienna, made the pitch in an exclusive interview with [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy), published on 23 October, as she prepares to get back on the dancefloor after a rib injury.

## A Eurovision veteran on a dance show

Goodrem finished fourth at this year's Eurovision Song Contest with "Eclipse", according to Digital Spy. That result gives her a rare perspective in the [*Strictly Come Dancing*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing) ballroom, where she is now competing.

"I would highly support a Eurovision week," she said. "I am now a dedicated Eurovision fan myself, it was one of the most amazing experiences of my life."

She even has a staging condition. "As long as I get to fly out of a piano," she joked.

## The songs she would dance to

Goodrem named two of her idols as possible inspirations. [Olivia Newton-John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivia_Newton-John) and [Celine Dion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celine_Dion) both took part in Eurovision, and she said she would be happy to perform as either of them.

She had a more recent option too. She told Digital Spy she would learn the routine for "Bangaranga" by this year's winner, Dara. Digital Spy did not report that the show has any plans for a Eurovision week, so for now it remains a contestant's wish.

## Back after the rib injury

The comments follow a difficult week. Goodrem was forced to sit out *Strictly*'s Icons week last weekend because of a rib injury, as Digital Spy reports. We covered her return plans in [Delta Goodrem returns to Strictly after rib injury](https://moduscarry.com/delta-goodrem-returns-to-strictly-after-rib-injury/).

This weekend she and professional partner [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) are due to perform a Rumba to Celine Dion's 1996 hit "It's All Coming Back To Me Now". The song choice nods to one of the idols she named in the interview.

"I feel so grateful for returning and I'm excited to be able to perform," she said. "Everyone has been giving me a lot of gentle hugs and it's very welcoming."

## Singing plans beyond the ballroom

Dancing has her full attention for now, but music is not far behind. Digital Spy reports that she is set to return to singing with a UK tour, including a planned concert at the London Palladium next February.

Her record gives that tour some weight. Her 2003 debut album *Innocent Eyes*, which produced the singles "[Born To Try](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Born_to_Try)" and "Lost Without You", has sold more than four million copies worldwide and went 15 times Platinum in Australia, according to the same report.

Asked whether Widdrington might join her on the road, she suggested a duet of sorts. "We need to do a dance where we're both singing on stage together," she said. "We'll get him singing soon."

## What happens next

Goodrem and Widdrington take to the floor this weekend with their Rumba. *Strictly Come Dancing* airs on BBC One and streams on BBC iPlayer. The Palladium concert is planned for next February, and no Eurovision week has been announced.
