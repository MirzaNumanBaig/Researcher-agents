**Quick summary**

- Warner Bros. Pictures and Legendary Pictures have released a new trailer for Dune: Part Three, the final film in Denis Villeneuve's trilogy.
- The film adapts Frank Herbert's 1969 novel Dune Messiah and is set nearly two decades after Paul Atreides took control of the Imperium.
- It opens in cinemas and IMAX internationally from 16 December 2026, and in North America from 18 December 2026.
- Villeneuve wrote the screenplay with Brian K. Vaughan.

Warner Bros. Pictures and Legendary Pictures have released a fresh trailer for Dune: Part Three, the closing chapter of [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve)'s science-fiction trilogy, with the film due in cinemas just over three weeks from now. According to [Flickering Myth](https://www.flickeringmyth.com/its-a-family-affair-in-the-latest-trailer-for-denis-villeneuves-dune-part-three/), the latest teaser leans on the family drama at the story's core.

## What the Dune: Part Three trailer sets up

The studio's synopsis, as reported by Flickering Myth, places the story nearly two decades after Paul Atreides seized control of the Imperium. Paul is now a ruthless Emperor and has to face the consequences of his rule. Old allies return, new threats appear and betrayal is never far away.

Paul is haunted by visions of the Imperium collapsing, and by the return of his long-lost love. That pulls him into a wide conspiracy, with Chani at the heart of the mystery. The synopsis says he must face the true cost of power and what it means for the people he loves most.

For readers who have followed the first two films, that is a sharp turn. The earlier story was about a young man's rise. This one, going by the studio's own description, is about what he does with the power once he has it.

## Who is in the cast

Flickering Myth lists a large ensemble: Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, [Jason Momoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Momoa), [Florence Pugh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence_Pugh), [Rebecca Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebecca_Ferguson), Isaach de Bankolé and [Charlotte Rampling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlotte_Rampling). [Anya Taylor-Joy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anya_Taylor-Joy), [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) and [Javier Bardem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javier_Bardem) are credited as well.

The report does not say which actor plays which part in the new footage, so this article does not guess at the roles beyond Paul and Chani, who are named in the synopsis.

## From the novel to the screen

The film is based on [Dune Messiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dune_Messiah), the 1969 novel by [Frank Herbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Herbert). Villeneuve directs from a screenplay he wrote with [Brian K. Vaughan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_K._Vaughan). Flickering Myth describes the director as an Academy Award-nominated filmmaker and the film as the finale of his science-fiction opus.

The same report points to licensed merchandise already on the way. It links to a separate story about McFarlane Toys unveiling Dune: Part Three figures of Paul Atreides and Chani. That is a sign of how much weight the studio expects the film to carry.

## What happens next

The release dates are the firm part. Dune: Part Three arrives in cinemas and IMAX internationally from 16 December 2026, and across North America from 18 December 2026, according to Flickering Myth. The report does not give a separate Australian date, so check local cinema listings closer to the time.

Expect more footage and promotion between now and then, since the studio has already released more than one teaser. Nothing in the source says when the next one will land.
