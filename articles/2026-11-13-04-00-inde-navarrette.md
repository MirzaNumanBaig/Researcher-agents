**Quick summary**

- [Inde Navarrette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inde_Navarrette) hosted the Halloween episode of season 52 of [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live), with [Gracie Abrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gracie_Abrams) as musical guest.
- The Michigan Daily's review says she was funny and fully committed, but held back by weak sketches and limited screen time.
- Her fame follows the horror film "Obsession", which the review says passed $500 million worldwide on a $750,000 budget.
- The same review says she has been cast as Rogue in Marvel's X-Men reboot and topped a Complex list of the best actors in their 20s.

Inde Navarrette has gone from relative unknown to Saturday Night Live host within a year. She fronted the show's Halloween episode in season 52, and a review in The Michigan Daily judged her debut a success despite uneven material.

## A breakout year behind the hosting job

The review, written by Daily Arts Writer Julianna Laban, places the hosting slot in the context of Navarrette's sudden rise. According to [The Michigan Daily](https://www.michigandaily.com/arts/tv/saturday-night-rewind-inde-navarrette-is-deservingly-a-rising-star/), the 25-year-old had previously appeared in "13 Reasons Why" and "Superman & Lois" before leading Curry Barker's psychological horror film "Obsession", released in May.

The numbers explain the attention. The review says "Obsession" earned more than $500 million worldwide on a budget of just $750,000, a return of roughly 650 times its cost. It describes her performance as emotionally intense and physically exhausting, and credits it with placing her among Hollywood's "scream queens".

Her profile has kept climbing. The review reports that she has been cast as Rogue in Marvel's planned X-Men reboot. It also says Complex placed her first on its "26 Best Actors in their 20s" list, ahead of Oscar nominees such as [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and Elle Fanning, and Best Actress winner Mikey Madison. For more on her recent run, see our earlier piece, [Inde Navarrette on Obsession Fame, Oscar Buzz and Rogue](https://moduscarry.com/inde-navarrette-obsession/).

## What worked on the night

The Halloween episode opened with the return of [Tom Hanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Hanks)' character [David S. Pumpkins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_S._Pumpkins), first seen in 2016, and Bobby Moynihan's Drunk Uncle. Weekend Update included surprise appearances from three members of the World Series-winning Milwaukee Brewers, according to the review.

Abrams played her second SNL visit with a set styled after Baz Luhrmann's 1996 film "Romeo + Juliet". The reviewer preferred her season 50 appearance but called the live performances of "Hit the Wall" and "Here You Come, There You Go" well done.

Navarrette's own contribution drew the warmest words. The reviewer called her effortlessly funny and said her charisma showed from the opening monologue, which included a topical jab at earlier host [Shane Gillis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shane_Gillis). She played a disruptive passenger in the pretaped "New Airline Class" and a bachelor contestant in "Uninterested Woman Game Show".

The standout, in this reviewer's view, was "Telenovela: El Corazón Que No Siente", where she was paired with cast member Marcello Hernández in a sketch built on exaggerated telenovela tropes and an absurd product placement plot.

## Where the writing let her down

The review is not uncritical of the show. It says the live sketch "Plates" began in the vein of [Pedro Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedro_Pascal) and Ego Nwodim's "Lisa from Temecula" but turned into a drawn-out fart joke centred mostly on [Ashley Padilla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashley_Padilla). "Queen Mary Ghosts" started well, the writer says, but leaned on repeated interruptions and ended without a real finish.

The verdict is that Navarrette was limited by the material and her screen time, not by her ability. The reviewer wrote that she leaned into the show's absurdity and showed clear chemistry with the cast. On the Complex ranking, the writer remains unconvinced she is number one but sees her SNL turn as proof she is a genuine rising star.

This is one student reviewer's opinion, and the piece is the only source for these details. Viewers' own ratings of the episode may differ.
