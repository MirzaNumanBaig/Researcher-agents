**Quick summary**

- [Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling) is reportedly reviving his band Dead Man's Bones with musician Zach Shields after 17 years.
- New music is expected on 27 October, according to the Times of India as relayed by Ukrainian outlet UNN.
- The duo's only album so far came out in 2009.
- Separately, Gosling was announced as Marvel's Ghost Rider at San Diego Comic-Con, with the film due in 2028.

Ryan Gosling is going back to music. Reports published on 25 October say the actor and Zach Shields are reviving Dead Man's Bones, with the first new release in 17 years expected on 27 October. It is a quiet return for a project many fans assumed was finished, and it arrives while Gosling's film slate is busier than ever.

## What is known about the Dead Man's Bones return

According to [UNN](https://unn.ua/en/news/ryan-gosling-returns-to-music-after-a-17-year-hiatus), which cites the Times of India, Gosling and Shields plan to release new material under the Dead Man's Bones name. The duo's debut album arrived in 2009 and mixed indie rock, eerie melodies and a theatrical, spooky atmosphere. Nothing has been released since, as Gosling put his energy into acting.

That gap is the headline number: 17 years between records. For context, it is longer than the entire run of many film franchises, and it spans Gosling's move from indie drama to some of the biggest releases of the past decade, including *La La Land* and *Barbie*, both of which UNN names.

The reports leave key questions open. The musicians have not said whether the new songs will keep the band's gothic, homemade sound or head somewhere different, and the UNN report carries no direct quotes from either of them. We could not read the Times of India original, so the 27 October date rests on that second-hand account. Treat it as reported rather than confirmed by the band.

## The Ghost Rider film is still the bigger project

Music is not slowing the screen work down. [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/GMA/Culture/ryan-gosling-joins-marvel-ghost-rider-new-film/story?id=135104561) reported that Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige announced Gosling as Ghost Rider during the studio's Hall H panel at San Diego Comic-Con in July. The film is set for theatres in 2028.

Gosling reacted on stage by asking, "Wow, is this really happening?", and said Ghost Rider is a character he has wanted to play for a long time. He then introduced director Shawn Levy, who made *Deadpool & Wolverine*.

Levy told the crowd the pair began swapping Ghost Rider ideas while working together on [*Star Wars: Starfighter*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars), which opens on 28 May 2027. After Gosling described his vision, Levy said he suggested they go for it with the line, "Bro, let's ride."

## How this Ghost Rider differs from earlier ones

The character has been on screen before. Sony released two films with Nicolas Cage as Johnny Blaze: *Ghost Rider* in 2007 and *Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance* in 2012. Gabriel Luna also played a different version, Robbie Reyes, in the Marvel and ABC series *Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.*

ABC News describes the comic-book hero as the Spirit of Vengeance, known for a flaming skull and a motorcycle. Which version Gosling will play has not been spelled out in the source we read.

Other outlets have run stories this week on the budget and box-office targets of *Star Wars: Starfighter*, but the pages were not readable to us, so we have left those figures out.
