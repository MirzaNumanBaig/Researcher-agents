**Quick summary**

- Australian actor [Joseph Zada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Zada) plays a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy in *The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping*.
- He told Rolling Stone Australia he never aimed to imitate [Woody Harrelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woody_Harrelson), who played the older Haymitch.
- Harrelson has reportedly praised Zada, according to Vanity Fair as cited by Rolling Stone.
- The film opens in Australian and New Zealand cinemas on 19 November.

Joseph Zada has described how he approached one of the franchise's best-known roles, telling Rolling Stone Australia that stepping into Haymitch Abernathy was not a matter of copying the actor who came before him. The Australian actor leads [The Hunger Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hunger_Games) prequel *Sunrise on the Reaping*, which reaches local cinemas on 19 November.

## Why Zada did not find the role daunting

Taking over a character made famous by Woody Harrelson in the 2012 original might sound intimidating. Zada said it was not, because imitation was never the goal. "I don't think it was daunting because I wasn't… it wasn't my goal at all," he told [Rolling Stone Australia](https://au.rollingstone.com/movies/movie-news/joseph-zara-interview-hunger-games-woody-harrelson-102849/).

The real challenge, he explained, was working out how to keep some of Harrelson's energy and life in the character while still building his own performance. That balance, rather than the weight of the franchise, is what he says occupied him.

## What the director wanted

Director [Francis Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Lawrence) shares that view. According to Rolling Stone, which cited an interview with [Radio Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio_Times), Zada tried out elements of Harrelson's performance during auditions. Lawrence said the aim was for audiences to sense a young version of Harrelson in the character without it feeling like an impersonation.

Lawrence applied the same thinking across the cast, Rolling Stone reports. [Ralph Fiennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Fiennes) plays a younger [President Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coriolanus_Snow) and [Elle Fanning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elle_Fanning) plays a younger Effie Trinket, roles earlier played by the late [Donald Sutherland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Sutherland) and Elizabeth Banks.

## Harrelson's reported verdict

The most striking endorsement comes from Harrelson himself. Rolling Stone, citing Vanity Fair, quotes him as saying Zada is "about to be a big star" and has "his head on his shoulders". Harrelson added that Zada would be tough enough to handle what comes with stardom. We have not independently confirmed the Vanity Fair remarks, which are reported second-hand here.

## The story of the film

*Sunrise on the Reaping* is set 24 years before the original films. It follows Haymitch at 16, selected to compete in the 50th Hunger Games, also known as the Second Quarter Quell. In this edition the Capitol doubles the usual number of tributes, sending 48 young people into the arena, according to [Variety Australia](https://au.variety.com/2026/film/news/mckenna-grace-interview-hunger-games-femininity-42581/).

Haymitch forms an alliance with fellow District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner, played by [Mckenna Grace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mckenna_Grace). Both outlets describe that partnership as the start of the experiences that shape the man audiences meet in the first film.

## Mckenna Grace on her co-star's world

Grace told Variety Australia that Maysilee's love of dresses and ribbons is a form of resistance. In a system built to strip young people of their identity, she said, holding on to those details is a way of keeping her humanity. Grace also said she spent much of her childhood rejecting traditionally feminine interests before learning to enjoy them, and that "a lot of people can mistake that for weakness".

Beyond Zada and Grace, the cast includes Fiennes, Fanning, [Jesse Plemons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Plemons), [Glenn Close](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glenn_Close) and [Kieran Culkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kieran_Culkin), with Lawrence directing. Lawrence is a veteran of the franchise.

## What happens next

The film opens in Australian and New Zealand cinemas on 19 November, so audiences will soon judge for themselves whether Zada's Haymitch carries enough of Harrelson's spirit without echoing it. The press interviews suggest the cast and director are comfortable with that line.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who does Joseph Zada play in Sunrise on the Reaping?

He plays Haymitch Abernathy at age 16, the character Woody Harrelson played in the original 2012 film. The story follows Haymitch's time in the 50th Hunger Games.

### Did Joseph Zada try to copy Woody Harrelson?

No. He told Rolling Stone Australia that copying Harrelson was never his goal. He wanted to keep some of Harrelson's energy while building his own take.

### When does Sunrise on the Reaping open in Australia?

The film arrives in Australian and New Zealand cinemas on 19 November, according to Rolling Stone Australia and Variety Australia.

### Who else stars in the film?

Mckenna Grace plays Maysilee Donner. Ralph Fiennes, Elle Fanning, Jesse Plemons, Glenn Close and Kieran Culkin also appear, and Francis Lawrence directs.
