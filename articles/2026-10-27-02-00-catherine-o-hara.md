**Quick summary**

- Netflix begins streaming the documentary "SCTV: The Greatest Show You've Never Heard Of" on 30 October, according to Newsday.
- It marks 50 years since the sketch show premiered in Canada in 1976.
- The cast featured includes Catherine O'Hara, Eugene Levy, Martin Short, Andrea Martin, Dave Thomas, Joe Flaherty, Harold Ramis and John Candy.
- O'Hara's best-known later roles are in "Home Alone" and "Schitt's Creek", the latter earning her a Primetime Emmy Award.

[Catherine O'Hara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catherine_O'Hara) is one of the faces of a new Netflix documentary on "SCTV", the Canadian sketch series that turns 50 this year. "SCTV: The Greatest Show You've Never Heard Of" begins streaming on 30 October, [Newsday reports](https://www.newsday.com/entertainment/tv/sctv-top-moments-glhefz4d), and it traces the history of a show that quietly shaped a generation of comedy stars.

## What was SCTV?

The series premiered in Canada in 1976 and reached New York viewers on WOR/9 the following year, according to Newsday. Its idea was simple and clever: each episode was framed as a full day of programming from a bargain-basement television station in the fictional town of Melonville.

Newsday compares it with [Saturday Night Live](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturday_Night_Live), which had launched in 1975 and made staying home on a Saturday night appealing. "SCTV", the paper says, did the same for Friday nights, and was often funnier. The half-hour format and the fake-station setting gave the cast room to parody everything from game shows to soap operas.

## Who is in the documentary?

Newsday says the stars of the original run are all featured. That list includes O'Hara, [Eugene Levy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Levy), [Joe Flaherty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Flaherty), John Candy, [Dave Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Thomas_(actor)), [Harold Ramis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Ramis) and [Andrea Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Martin). [Martin Short](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Short) is also named among the show's alumni.

Newsday's round-up of signature characters shows the range of the ensemble:

- O'Hara played Vegas-style singer Lola Heatherton, lounge singer Dusty Towne and soap character Violet McKay. The paper singles out her impression of [Katharine Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katharine_Hepburn) selling tea as a must-see moment.
- Levy played smarmy comic Bobby Bittman and news anchor Earl Camembert.
- Short created the neurotic Ed Grimley and lounge singer Jackie Rogers Jr.
- Martin played the brassy station manager Edith Prickley.
- Candy played TV host Johnny LaRue and the mad scientist Dr. Tongue. He died in 1994, aged 43, less than a year after "Cool Runnings" opened.

## Where the cast went next

Newsday describes O'Hara as a superb character actor. She played the mother who leaves her son behind in the 1990 film [Home Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_Alone), then the wig-loving matriarch Moira Rose in [Schitt's Creek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schitt's_Creek), for which she won a Primetime Emmy Award.

Levy's film work includes "Splash", the "American Pie" films and the [Christopher Guest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Guest) mockumentaries "Best in Show" and "A Mighty Wind", made with O'Hara. He also won an Emmy for playing Johnny Rose in "Schitt's Creek". Short appeared in "Mars Attacks!" and "Father of the Bride", and has found fresh success alongside Steve Martin in the Hulu series "Only Murders in the Building". Andrea Martin earned laughs in "My Big Fat Greek Wedding" in 2002 and won a Tony Award for the 2013 revival of "Pippin".
