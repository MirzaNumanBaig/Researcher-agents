**Quick summary**

- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) is the 5/4 favourite with William Hill to be named the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond), according to OLBG.
- [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) is next at 7/4, and [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) has entered the market at 3/1.
- Pattinson and [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) have reportedly auditioned for director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve).
- Nothing has been confirmed by Amazon MGM, and reports of contract talks remain unverified.

Callum Turner has moved to the front of the betting for the role of 007. On 12 November, betting site guide OLBG reported that William Hill makes the Londoner the 5/4 favourite to succeed Daniel Craig, as Robert Pattinson joins the race at 3/1.

## Where the Callum Turner James Bond odds stand

OLBG lists the William Hill market this way: Turner at 5/4, Jack Lowden at 7/4, Robert Pattinson at 3/1 and Josh O'Connor at 6/1. In plain terms, a 5/4 price implies roughly a 44 per cent chance, while 6/1 implies about 14 per cent. Bookmaker odds measure betting interest, not inside knowledge, so they are a guide to the mood of the market rather than a prediction.

OLBG's betting writer describes Turner as the man to beat. The outlet says his profile rose with Masters of the Air and The Boys in the Boat, and that he has the height and presence many fans associate with the part.

## The reported shortlist

According to OLBG, reports suggest the shortlist has been cut to three names: Turner, Lowden and Josh O'Connor. Turner is described as the presumptive frontrunner, and some reports claim he is already negotiating terms with the studio. OLBG is clear that none of this has been confirmed.

Lowden, the Scottish actor known for Slow Horses, is the nearest challenger. OLBG says he would suit the spy genre as an MI5 agent on screen and has strong support among Bond fans.

## Pattinson and Hoult enter late

The twist came last week. OLBG reports that Pattinson and Hoult were both said to have auditioned for Villeneuve, and Pattinson went straight into the market at 3/1, placing him among the top three. The outlet calls him one of the most respected leading men of his generation, citing The Batman and his arthouse work, but notes that his commitment to the next Batman film could complicate any deal.

These are reported auditions, not announcements. Neither actor, nor the studio, has confirmed them in the material OLBG cites.

## Villeneuve feels the pressure

Amazon MGM took full creative control of the franchise in early 2025 and turned to Villeneuve, the filmmaker behind Arrival, Blade Runner 2049 and the Dune films. Asked about progress by GQ for its Men of the Year issue, he said there was huge pressure to get Bond 26 right. He joked that he has "the target of the sniper" on his chest and told the magazine: "We cannot f*** it up. We do not have the choice."

OLBG adds that whoever wins the role is likely to play Bond across several films. That is why producers are expected to favour an actor in his late twenties or thirties who can commit for the long haul.

## What happens next

OLBG says the hunt for Craig's successor is entering its final stages, but no announcement date has been given. Until Amazon MGM speaks, the shortlist and the odds remain reports and market prices.
