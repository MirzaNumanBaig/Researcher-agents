**Quick summary**

- [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart) died on Wednesday 28 October at his Los Angeles home, aged 86, according to a statement shared on his social media.
- His family said he died after "a brief and poignant journey" with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia.
- [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman), his co-star in seven X-Men films, posted a tribute on Instagram calling him "a tremendous friend and man".
- Stewart's final appearance as Professor X is due in *Avengers: Doomsday*, which opens this winter.

Hugh Jackman was among the first to pay tribute after Sir Patrick Stewart's death was announced on Thursday, writing on Instagram that Hollywood had lost "one of the greats of stage and screen". The Australian actor, 58, shared two photos of himself with the man who played Professor X opposite his Wolverine for more than two decades.

## What Hugh Jackman wrote

Jackman's caption was short. "We lost one of the greats of stage and screen today. And, in my book, a tremendous friend and man," he wrote, according to [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/entertainment/movies/2026/10/29/patrick-stewart-death-celebrity-reactions-tributes/92502731007/). "I learned so much from him. I will miss you, Patrick Stewart, all of my days."

[PerthNow](https://www.perthnow.com.au/entertainment/celebrity-gossip/hugh-jackman-shares-sweet-photos-of-star-trek-actor-patrick-stewart-before-his-death-in-heartbreaking-tribute-c-23091860) described the pictures: one shows the pair at the side of a stage with Stewart's hand on Jackman's shoulder, the other shows them laughing as Jackman hugs him. A second outlet, [Redshirts Always Die](https://redshirtsalwaysdie.com/star-trek-patrick-stewart-death-hugh-jackman-reaction), said one image appeared to come from the set of *Logan* (2017).

Yahoo's report, first published by Closer Weekly, said the post passed one million likes and 5,500 comments. Spanish-British actor Dafne Keen, who starred alongside Jackman in *Logan*, wrote in the comments: "A giant amongst men."

## A friendship that began on the *X-Men* set

The two first worked together on *X-Men* in 2000, and [Wide Open Country](https://www.wideopencountry.com/hugh-jackman-pays-tribute-to-late-x-men-icon/) notes they stayed together across six more films. Yahoo's report says Jackman only landed Wolverine after Dougray Scott, the actor first cast, had scheduling conflicts, a detail it attributes to /Film.

Stewart wrote about their first meeting in his 2023 memoir *Making It So*, according to the same report. Jackman arrived on the second day of filming to screen test for director Bryan Singer. "We were all rooting for him; he had that intangible star quality," Stewart wrote. Jackman left convinced he had missed out, then learned the part was his.

That mentor-and-student dynamic carried onto the screen. In the films, Stewart's Charles Xavier acts as a father figure to Jackman's Wolverine.

## Who else paid tribute

Jackman was not alone. [William Shatner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shatner), 95, who played Captain Kirk opposite Stewart's Captain Picard in the 1994 film *Star Trek Generations*, called him "a magnificent actor" and said "Captain Picard stood for the best of us, and so did the man who played him."

[Nine.com.au](https://www.nine.com.au/entertainment/celebrity/patrick-stewart-death-hollywood-tributes-hugh-jackman-william-shatner-more-20261030-p619x2.html) reported that [Ian McKellen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_McKellen), who played Magneto opposite Stewart and had known him for about 50 years, wrote of his friend's Yorkshire roots and his "stern honesty". [Jonathan Frakes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Frakes) told USA Today that he had acted with and directed no one more often than Stewart.

The loss comes only days after another *Star Trek* actor, Walter Koenig, died aged 90, according to PerthNow.

## A career of more than six decades

The Australian Women's Weekly notes that Stewart made his first stage appearance in 1959 and won awards for *Hamlet*, *Antony and Cleopatra* and *Who's Afraid of Virginia Woolf?*. PerthNow says he worked on more than 150 film and television productions.

He is survived by his wife, musician Sunny Ozell, and two children, Daniel and Sophie, USA Today reported.

## What happens next

Stewart's last performance as Professor X will be seen in *Avengers: Doomsday*, due this winter, according to Wide Open Country. Nine.com.au says McKellen's tribute also reminded fans that the performance is still to come.

Jackman has not said whether he will share any further remembrance. ## Frequently asked questions

### What did Hugh Jackman say about Patrick Stewart?

He called Stewart "one of the greats of stage and screen" and "a tremendous friend and man", adding that he would miss him "all of my days".

### How did Patrick Stewart die?

His family said he died at home in Los Angeles on 28 October after a brief illness with Alzheimer's disease and Lewy body dementia. He was 86.

### How many X-Men films did Jackman and Stewart make together?

Seven, beginning with *X-Men* in 2000 and including *Logan* in 2017, according to USA Today.
