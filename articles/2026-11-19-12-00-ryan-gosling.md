**Quick summary**

- Amazon MGM Studios will bring *Project Hail Mary* back to IMAX 70mm screens on 11 December, according to Comic Book Resources.
- The film has earned $685 million worldwide, making it one of the biggest releases of 2026.
- It holds a 95% score from both critics and audiences on Rotten Tomatoes.
- It is also streaming on Prime Video.

[Ryan Gosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Gosling)'s space adventure *Project Hail Mary* is heading back to the biggest screens in cinemas. Amazon MGM Studios will re-release the film in IMAX 70mm on 11 December, about a month from now, [Comic Book Resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comic_Book_Resources) reported on 18 November. The return follows a worldwide haul of $685 million, and it lands as awards season gets going.

## What is coming back, and when

The IMAX 70mm run begins on 11 December, according to the [CBR report](https://www.cbr.com/project-hail-mary-theatrical-rerelease-theaters-tickets/). The outlet describes the film as one of the year's biggest sci-fi releases and says the timing suits Hollywood's awards conversation.

The re-release is not happening in isolation. CBR notes that Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* also returns to IMAX 70mm on 11 December. *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* gets a similar return a week earlier, on 4 December, with a new title and new footage. The outlet suggests these studios are trying to get ahead of a crowded 18 December, when *Dune: Part Three* and *Avengers: Doomsday* are due to open.

For anyone who missed the film on a large screen, or who wants a second look, the film remains available on Prime Video.

## The story behind the film

Gosling plays Ryland Grace, a science teacher who wakes aboard a spaceship light-years from home with no memory of who he is. As his memory returns, he learns his mission: work out what is making the sun fade, and stop the extinction of life on Earth. An unexpected friendship along the way means he may not be working alone, CBR says.

The film is based on [Andy Weir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Hail_Mary)'s 2021 novel of the same name. [Phil Lord and Christopher Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Lord_and_Christopher_Miller) directed, working from a screenplay by [Drew Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drew_Goddard). Gosling leads a cast that includes Sandra Hüller, [James Ortiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Ortiz), [Lionel Boyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lionel_Boyce), [Ken Leung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Leung), [Milana Vayntrub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milana_Vayntrub) and [Priya Kansara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priya_Kansara).

## How it has been received

Beyond the takings, the response has been strong. According to CBR, the film carries a 95% score from critics on [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes), earning the "Certified Fresh" label, and a matching 95% from audiences, which the site tags "Certified Hot". The critics' summary quoted by the outlet calls it a "visually dazzling space odyssey" carried by Gosling at his most winning.

CBR adds that some viewers rank it among the smartest sci-fi films of the decade, that it appears among Letterboxd's ten best-reviewed sci-fi titles, and that some fans think it improves on the book. Those are opinions the outlet reports rather than measured results, so treat them as a sign of the mood around the film.

## A busy run in science fiction for Gosling

The film sits in a growing run of genre work for the actor. CBR points to his lead role in *Blade Runner 2049*, the sequel to Ridley Scott's [*Blade Runner*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blade_Runner). It also says he will lead *Star Wars: Starfighter* next year, his entry into the [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) franchise.

## What happens next

The IMAX 70mm screenings start on 11 December. CBR's report did not list venues or ticket details in the text we reviewed, so check your local IMAX cinema for sessions. *Star Wars: Starfighter* is the next Gosling project the outlet names, due next year.
