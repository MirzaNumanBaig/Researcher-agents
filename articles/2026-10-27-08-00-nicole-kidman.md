**Quick summary**

- [Nicole Kidman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicole_Kidman) is the November digital cover star of Vogue Australia, which published online on Friday 23 October 2026.
- The shoot was photographed by Lachlan Bailey and styled by editor-in-chief Christine Centenera, with every look from Chanel.
- In the interview, Kidman told [Baz Luhrmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baz_Luhrmann) that her height was once an obstacle, then became the thing that "gave me my fire".
- The cover arrives as she promotes *Practical Magic 2* this season.

Nicole Kidman has been told she was too tall for most of her life, and she now says it is part of what drove her. In a new Vogue Australia cover story, the 59-year-old Oscar winner reflects on that early obstacle while fronting a Chanel-only shoot that went online on 23 October.

## The Chanel shoot

According to [Sports Illustrated Lifestyle](https://lifestyle.si.com/fashion-beauty/nicole-kidman-vogue-australia-cover-chic-designer-moodboard), Kidman posed for three digital cover images, all in Chanel, a label for which she is a global brand ambassador. One shows her in a sparkling dress, sitting in clear water in black and white. Another has her in a textured orange cropped jacket and matching skirt. In the third she sits on the floor in a tasselled skirt and a striped polo.

Yahoo's report, which was first published by Parade on 26 October, describes further looks from the November issue. These include a black Chanel bra with the double-C logo worn with sparkling jeans, and swimwear-inspired outfits. The shoot has a relaxed, beach-style mood, with Kidman's blonde hair styled wet. Hair was by Shay Ashual and makeup by Mark Carrasquillo, the SI report says, with warm, golden tones.

The choice of Chanel is not a surprise. Parade notes that Kidman also attended the label's Spring/Summer 2027 show in Paris earlier this month with her 15-year-old daughter, Faith Margaret. Our earlier coverage of the campaign is collected under the [Nicole Kidman](https://moduscarry.com/tag/nicole-kidman/) tag.

## "Too tall" becomes a strength

The interview is built partly around questions from people who know her. Sports Illustrated Lifestyle says the list includes [Margot Robbie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margot_Robbie), [Hugh Jackman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_Jackman) and Zoe Saldaña. Luhrmann, who directed her in *Moulin Rouge!* and *Australia*, asked her to name something that was an obstacle when she was young but is a strength now.

Her answer was her height. At 5ft 11in, she said she was the tallest in her class, taller than all the boys. "My height was an obstacle," she told the magazine. "And then, suddenly, when you are cast first in something, you're not too tall; everyone has to adjust around you." She called that moment "kind of amazing", and said that after a lifetime of hearing she would be too tall, "it gave me my fire".

Yahoo adds that the comment sits within a wider conversation about her early ambitions in ballet and acting, where her height once presented challenges.
