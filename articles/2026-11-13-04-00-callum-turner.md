**Quick summary**

- [Callum Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Callum_Turner) leads the Kalshi prediction market for the next [James Bond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bond) at 34%, according to Kalshi News.
- [Robert Pattinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Pattinson) sits second at 32% after a Deadline report that he screen tested for the role.
- [Jack Lowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Lowden) is third at 30%, with [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) on 8% and Josh O'Connor on 5%.
- No casting has been announced. Bond producer Amy Pascal said earlier this year that an answer by the end of the year was a "good bet", per JoBlo.

Callum Turner has retaken the top spot in the race to play 007, but only just. A Kalshi News report published on 12 November puts the English actor at 34% in its James Bond casting market, two points clear of Robert Pattinson, with the whole contest still unannounced and unconfirmed.

## Where the Callum Turner James Bond odds stand

Prediction-market numbers are a mood gauge, not a casting call, and Kalshi News itself labels its report as informational only. Still, the movement is telling. Turner's odds hit a high of 72% after [Pierce Brosnan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierce_Brosnan) backed him, then fell when news broke that Pattinson had been in the running too.

Lowden has had the longest run at the top. Kalshi News says he reached 64% in October 2026 before slipping to third. [Nicholas Hoult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_Hoult) and Josh O'Connor were both named as finalists by trade outlets reporting on the screen tests, yet neither has gained ground in the market.

Kalshi also notes a Variety article from 6 November claiming [Harry Styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Styles) "has had discussions about the part" and that [Theo James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theo_James) hasn't been ruled out. Theo James trades at 3%.

## Why Pattinson changed the race

Pattinson's rise followed a Deadline report, cited by Kalshi News, that he had screen tested. Opinion pieces in Esquire, Vogue and The Guardian then cheered the idea, with The Guardian's Sian Cain calling the long-running casting saga suddenly "exciting for the first time".

There are doubts. Kalshi News says some fans worry his commitments to The Batman will count against him, and that precedent tends to favour a lesser-known actor. His working relationship with director [Denis Villeneuve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denis_Villeneuve), who directed him in the forthcoming Dune: Part Three, is another factor nobody can yet weigh.

## The tabloid case for Turner

The case for Turner rests largely on British tabloid reports, so treat it as rumour. [JoBlo](https://www.joblo.com/callum-turner-james-bond-rumour/) reports that he has resigned as a director of Twentytwo Films Ltd, the production company he founded with his wife, [Dua Lipa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dua_Lipa). The site says this is being read as clearing his schedule for Bond.

It also reports he was at Bray Studios in Berkshire for a screen test. Kalshi News adds that NME reported many Bond experts believed he had already won the part, and quotes an unnamed source telling The Sun that producers see the role as all-consuming.

None of this is confirmed by the Bond producers. Lipa has also been floated as a possible singer of the Bond theme, which is speculation. Lowden has a famous partner of his own: his wife, [Saoirse Ronan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saoirse_Ronan), has said she would like to play a Bond villain, according to Kalshi News.

## Turner's other news: Green Room is streaming

Away from the Bond chatter, Turner's 2015 thriller Green Room has landed on Amazon MGM+, [Digital Spy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Spy) reports. He plays a member of a punk band alongside [Anton Yelchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_Yelchin) and [Alia Shawkat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alia_Shawkat), trapped in a remote club after witnessing a murder.

The film holds a 90% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes, per Digital Spy. It also featured the late [Patrick Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Stewart), who died recently aged 86. Director Jeremy Saulnier said Stewart's interest rescued the production when it was close to shutting down.
