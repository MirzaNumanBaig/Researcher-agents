**Quick summary**

- [Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) says he was taken to hospital by ambulance after a bug left him unable to perform on [Strictly Come Dancing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_Come_Dancing).
- He told the Mirror he phoned partner [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) from the ambulance, insisting he would be back the next day.
- [Aljaz Skorjanec](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alja%C5%BE_%C5%A0korjanec) stepped in for the Argentine Tango.
- Widdrington is now recovered, and the pair dance a quickstep at the Tower Ballroom in Blackpool this week.

[Kai Widdrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kai_Widdrington) has explained how a sudden illness put him in an ambulance and out of last week's live show, and how he kept promising his partner [Delta Goodrem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_Goodrem) he would return. The Australian singer and her dance partner now head to Blackpool, according to a report by the [Liverpool Echo](https://www.liverpoolecho.co.uk/news/tv/kai-widdrington-hospital-rush-ambulance-34946593) drawing on a Mirror interview.

## What Widdrington says happened

The professional dancer described "a really, really bad bug" that began earlier in the week. He lost his appetite and stopped eating, and by the end he felt depleted. Doctors told him he needed fluids and rest before he could regain his strength.

He credited the paramedics and hospital staff, saying they had him back on his feet faster than he expected. The Echo reports that he was replaced for the Argentine Tango by fellow professional Aljaz Skorjanec.

## The phone call from the ambulance

The detail that stands out is the call. Widdrington said he sat in the ambulance telling Goodrem he would be there the next day. He admitted he was never going to make it, and he thinks she knew that too. In his words, he had to check in with himself and accept the situation.

Goodrem was more blunt about how it felt from her side. "We nearly lost him last week," she said, adding that he had tried all week to push through before a "very wild Friday". She also thanked Skorjanec for stepping up, and says the routine earned her highest score yet for the Argentine Tango.

## A second health scare for the pair

The Echo notes that Widdrington's illness came five weeks after Goodrem was forced to withdraw in week four. She had fractured a rib while training for Icons Week. It means both halves of the partnership have now missed a live show through injury or illness.

## What happens next

This week the pair perform a quickstep to Eternal's "I Wanna Be The Only One" in the Tower Ballroom at Blackpool. Widdrington says he walked in on Monday unsure of his strength, but the routine has left them both as fit as they have been on the show, thanks to the cardio involved. Goodrem calls it "really quick" and a joyful, traditional ballroom dance.

Widdrington has also taken her on a tour of the seaside town. She says they saw the illuminations, visited an arcade, ate fish and chips, fed the seagulls and tried fried chocolate bars.
