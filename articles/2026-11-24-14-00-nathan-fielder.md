**Quick summary**

- A "Dumb" Nathan Fielder Rave will be held at The Rave/Eagles Club in Milwaukee on Friday, 5 February, according to Milwaukee Record.
- The night starts at 10 p.m. for ages 18 and over, with a costume contest, games and a staring contest with a cash prize.
- The venue and promoter Cool Is Dead both state that Fielder will not be there.
- The event follows the success of his documentary *You Can See Everything*, which has a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score.

A night-long tribute to [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) is heading to Wisconsin. On Friday, 5 February, The Rave/Eagles Club in Milwaukee will host a "Dumb" Nathan Fielder Rave, a celebration of the comedian's work, [Milwaukee Record reported](https://milwaukeerecord.com/film/theres-going-to-be-a-dumb-nathan-fielder-rave-in-milwaukee/) on 23 November.

## What the Nathan Fielder rave involves

The event is presented by a company called Cool Is Dead and starts at 10 p.m. Entry is limited to people aged 18 and over.

Milwaukee Record says the ticketing page promises "surprises, a guest costume contest, games, and more", and mentions a staring contest with a cash prize. The music side is built from remixes of clips taken across Fielder's television and film work, set to house, techno and dubstep.

The title appears to nod to "Dumb Starbucks", the pop-up cafe stunt from the second season of [Nathan For You](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_for_You), according to the outlet. The original episode is covered on Wikipedia's page for [Dumb Starbucks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dumb_Starbucks).

## Will Nathan Fielder be there?

No, at least officially. Both the venue and Cool Is Dead stress that Fielder will not be present. Milwaukee Record jokes that this could itself be part of an elaborate bit, then concludes he almost certainly will not turn up. Treat any appearance as a joke, not a plan.

## Why Fielder is everywhere right now

The rave arrives while Fielder is enjoying one of the busiest stretches of his career. Milwaukee Record notes that it comes a year after the latest season of *The Rehearsal*, and that his documentary *You Can See Everything* is drawing strong reviews and some Oscar talk.

The film was co-directed with [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim) and centres on [Elizabeth Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_Holmes_(writer)), the Theranos founder who was convicted of fraud and sentenced to 11 years in prison. According to [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/uk/you-can-see-everything-key-scene-nathan-fielder), Fielder and his crew entered the private life of Holmes and her husband, Billy Evans, during her final 34 days of freedom.

The numbers are unusual for a documentary. Wikipedia lists a budget of about US$7 million against a box office of roughly US$93 million, around 13 times the cost. It holds a 97% Rotten Tomatoes score from 229 reviews and 97/100 on Metacritic from 43 critics. For the UK side of that run, see our report on how the [film broke a UK documentary box office record](https://moduscarry.com/nathan-fielder-you-can-see-everything-box-office/).

## A film that keeps people talking

JustWatch's writer singles out one scene as the key to the film: a late shot of Fielder watching two swimmers from the beach house, which is later revealed to be his own memory of swimming with Holmes. The writer reads it as proof that Fielder is willing to expose his own lapse in objectivity. That is one critic's interpretation, not a statement from the filmmakers.

Fielder himself told a New York Film Festival audience, as quoted by JustWatch, that he kept reminding himself Holmes was known for deception even while she seemed genuine. Moments like that explain why clips from the film and his earlier shows are now raw material for a dance floor.

## What happens next

The rave is set for 5 February at The Rave/Eagles Club. Tickets are on sale through the event's ticketing page, according to Milwaukee Record. Fielder has not been announced for the night, and the organisers say he will not attend.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the Nathan Fielder rave in Milwaukee?

It takes place on Friday, 5 February at The Rave/Eagles Club, starting at 10 p.m. The event is for ages 18 and over.

### Is Nathan Fielder attending his own rave?

No. The venue and organiser Cool Is Dead both say he will not be present.

Organisers list a costume contest, games, surprises and a staring contest with a cash prize. DJs will play house, techno and dubstep remixes of clips from Fielder's shows and films.
