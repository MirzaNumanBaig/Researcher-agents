**Quick summary**

- Wrexham AFC announced on Friday 6 November that chief executive Michael Williamson has left the club with immediate effect.
- Club director Shaun Harvey is now acting CEO, with no end date given for the interim role.
- Co-owners [Ryan Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Reynolds) and [Rob Mac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rob_McElhenney) issued the statement on behalf of the board; no reason for the exit was given.
- Wrexham sit 16th in the Championship and host Wolverhampton Wanderers on Saturday.

The Ryan Reynolds Wrexham CEO story broke on Friday afternoon, when the club confirmed that Michael Williamson was leaving after about two years in the job. Reynolds and Rob Mac, who co-own the Welsh club, thanked him in a joint statement and named Shaun Harvey as acting chief executive. The club did not say why Williamson left.

## What the owners said

According to [Wales Online](https://www.walesonline.co.uk/sport/football/football-news/ryan-reynolds-announces-major-wrexham-34875690), the statement was issued on behalf of the board. It thanked Williamson "for his work taking the club from League 1 to the Championship" and said the owners remain focused on making the Wrexham community proud.

The statement also looked ahead. It described [Shaun Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaun_Harvey) as "an integral part of this journey from the beginning" and said he would back head coach [Phil Parkinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Parkinson) and the players. [OneFootball](https://onefootball.com/en/news/ryan-reynolds-confirms-wrexham-ceo-michael-williamsons-exit-as-shaun-harvey-steps-in-43737204) noted that no timeframe was set for Harvey's interim spell.

## Who is Shaun Harvey?

Harvey is no newcomer. The [North Wales Pioneer](https://www.northwalespioneer.co.uk/news/26707634.ryan-reynolds-rob-mac-statement-wrexham-afc-ceo-exit/) reports that he was chief executive of the English Football League between 2013 and 2019, and earlier held the top job at Leeds United and senior roles at Bradford City.

He joined Wrexham as an adviser in February 2021, according to Wales Online, and was appointed to the board in December 2023. That means he has been part of the project since the early days of the Hollywood ownership.

## Williamson's two years at Wrexham

Williamson arrived in May 2024, replacing Fleur Robinson, as the club prepared for its first League One season after back-to-back promotions. Wales Online says he came with experience at Inter Milan, DC United and Miami FC.

Under Parkinson, Wrexham then won a third successive promotion, reaching the Championship. [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/soccer/ryan-reynolds-rob-mac-shock-wrexham-boardroom-shakeup) adds that the club finished seventh last season, its highest league finish. Williamson was named Football Industry Leader of the Year at the inaugural Global Football Industry Awards in November 2025, and the North Wales Pioneer links his tenure to a new academy facility at Darland High School and growth in the women's and girls' department.

## A difficult run off the pitch

Sports Illustrated also lists friction during his time in charge. It reports that a £10 fan zone surcharge was scrapped after supporter backlash, that some merchandise was withdrawn, and that he took control of summer transfer business from Harvey. Wrexham spent about $48 million on seven signings, according to that report, and Sheffield United manager Chris Wilder criticised the club's approach late in the window.

[Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/ryan-reynolds-rob-mac-address-180936847.html) points to the results: four wins from 14 league games, a 4-5-5 record, and a first-round EFL Cup exit against Middlesbrough. Late in October the EFL also fined the club for two player-eligibility breaches, involving Issa Kaboré and Callum O'Hare. Sports Illustrated puts the fine at £75,000.

The departure follows that of board member [Humphrey Ker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humphrey_Ker), who stepped down in late September. None of the outlets say these events are linked to Williamson's exit, and the club has offered no explanation.

## What happens next

Wrexham host Wolverhampton Wanderers, second in the table, on Saturday. Harvey will run the club in the meantime, and the owners have not said whether a permanent chief executive will be appointed.
