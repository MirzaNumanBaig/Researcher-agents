**Quick summary**

- *April X*, the psychological sci-fi thriller starring [Connor Storrie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_Storrie), starts streaming on HBO Max on Friday, 18 December (US time).
- It airs on HBO's linear channel the next day, Saturday, 19 December, at 8 p.m. ET.
- Deadline describes it as Storrie's first film vehicle to be released since *Heated Rivalry* made him a global name.
- The film opened in UK cinemas on 18 September and in limited US release on 2 October.
- Separately, Saint Laurent has named Storrie in its campaign for the 60th anniversary of Le Smoking.

Connor Storrie's first film since his breakout on [*Heated Rivalry*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heated_Rivalry) has a streaming date. *April X*, a near-future thriller about a man searching for his missing twin, arrives on HBO Max on 18 December, according to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/connor-storrie-april-x-hbo-max-streaming-release-date-1237229307/). The announcement came on 19 November, the same week the actor appeared in a major fashion campaign.

## When and where to watch Connor Storrie in April X

The film streams on HBO Max on Friday, 18 December, then premieres on HBO's linear channel at 8 p.m. ET on Saturday, 19 December. [BroadwayWorld](https://www.broadwayworld.com/bwwtv/article/APRIL-X-to-Stream-Exclusively-on-HBO-Max-Connor-Storrie-Stars-in-Near-Future-Thriller-20261119) says Vertigo Releasing's title will debut exclusively on HBO Max in the US.

The release follows a short theatrical run. According to Deadline, *April X* reached UK cinemas on 18 September and opened in limited release in the US on 2 October. That is a gap of about 11 weeks between the US limited release and the streaming debut.

The sources do not say when, or whether, the film will reach Australian viewers. We will update this page if a local date is confirmed.

## What April X is about

The story follows twins Bax, played by Storrie, and April, played by Lily Krug. When April disappears, Bax combs the dark corners of a post-Soviet cityscape to find her. Deadline's synopsis says the search ends with him descending into madness. [Mezha.net](https://mezha.net/eng/news/mezha-7ba6820bf2cced97f80c808d_connor_storrie-s_-april/) places the story in the near future.

BroadwayWorld calls it a "high octane" thriller. Deadline identifies Krug by her earlier credit in *Plane*.

## Who is behind the film

[Michel K. Parandi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_K._Parandi) directed *April X* and wrote the screenplay with Jack Coulton. Lavinia Postolache and Parandi produced it, and Postolache's US banner Lavi Star is credited as the producing company.

Alongside Storrie and Krug, the cast includes Tudor Chirila, [Serban Pavlu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C8%98erban_Pavlu), Tourialay Akbari and Lulian Postelnicu. The executive producers listed by Deadline are Melissa Schon, Lavinia Postolache, Alfred DeCarolis, Andrew Robl, Eddie Ting, Andrew Sasson, Berite Labelle, Tracy Paul and Ben Barenholtz.

## Why it matters for Storrie's career

*Heated Rivalry* turned Storrie into one of the most talked-about young actors of the year, and Deadline and Mezha.net both frame *April X* as the first film to reach audiences since that rise. A dark, twin-driven thriller shot in a post-Soviet setting is a clear step away from romance, and a home-streaming release gives it a far wider audience than its limited cinema run.

## Storrie joins Saint Laurent's Le Smoking campaign

In fashion news, Saint Laurent named Storrie among the faces of its Winter 2026 campaign marking 60 years of Le Smoking, the women's tuxedo Yves Saint Laurent introduced in 1966, according to [DSCENE](https://www.designscene.net/2026/11/saint-laurent-le-smoking-campaign.html). Mark Kean photographed the campaign.

The cast also includes Kate Moss, ROSÉ, Zoë Kravitz, Charli XCX, Charlotte Gainsbourg, Anok Yai, Anja Rubik, Rauw Alejandro and Kento Yamazaki, among others. DSCENE adds that the house plans to bring Le Smoking production back to France in 2027, to its own Atelier de Confection Saint Laurent. ## What happens next

*April X* is on HBO Max from 18 December, with the HBO broadcast the following evening. No sequel, further release dates or other upcoming Storrie films were confirmed in the sources we read. Local availability in Australia remains unannounced.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does April X stream on HBO Max?

It starts streaming on Friday, 18 December, according to Deadline and BroadwayWorld. The HBO linear premiere follows on Saturday, 19 December, at 8 p.m. ET.

### Who stars in April X?

Connor Storrie plays Bax and Lily Krug plays his twin, April. Tudor Chirila, Serban Pavlu, Tourialay Akbari and Lulian Postelnicu are also in the cast.

### What is April X about?

It is a near-future psychological thriller. When April goes missing, her twin brother Bax searches a post-Soviet city for her and slides into madness.

### Is April X Connor Storrie's first film since Heated Rivalry?

Deadline calls it his first film vehicle to be released since the series made him a global star. *Heated Rivalry* itself is a television series.
