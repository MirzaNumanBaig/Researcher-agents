**Quick summary**

- Casting director [Sarah Finn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Halley_Finn) says John Malkovich was the only choice to play Chris in [Martin McDonagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_McDonagh)'s *Wild Horse Nine*.
- She spoke to The Credits, the [Motion Picture Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_Picture_Association)'s publication, in an interview published on 23 November 2026.
- Malkovich was not attached when Finn began work, so the part started as an open search.
- The dark comedy has drawn strong reviews out of Venice, according to the same interview.

Casting director Sarah Finn has described why John Malkovich was, in her words, the only actor who could play Chris in Martin McDonagh's *Wild Horse Nine*. Finn gave the interview to The Credits, the [Motion Picture Association](https://www.motionpictures.org/2026/11/national-treasure-casting-director-sarah-finn-on-why-john-malkovich-was-the-only-choice-for-martin-mcdonaghs-wild-horse-nine/)'s awards-season series, published on 23 November 2026.

## A dying spy on Easter Island

[*Wild Horse Nine*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Horse_Nine) is set in Chile in the months before Pinochet's military coup in 1973. Malkovich plays Chris, a veteran CIA agent with a terminal illness who wants to spend his last days on Rapa Nui, also known as Easter Island. He travels there with fellow agent Lee, played by [Sam Rockwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Rockwell), after a recent mission.

The Credits describes the film as a dark comedy with rave reviews from Venice. It moves between sharp bickering from its odd-couple leads and quieter reflections on mortality and regret. Chris is also fond of moths and wants to protect them from harm, a detail the interview singles out.

## What Finn looked for

Finn's credits include *Spider-Man: Brand New Day* and *Deadpool & Wolverine*. She said Malkovich had not been attached to *Wild Horse Nine* when she joined, so the first conversations were about finding the right actor for Chris.

She says McDonagh's writing has a musicality and a very specific sense of humour. That meant every actor needed the dark comic touch built into the dialogue, plus the skill to turn a scene on a dime, so a viewer is offended one moment and laughing the next. Finn also said she read the script repeatedly until she could hear the characters, and that she researched the political history of Chile and the region.

## Why Malkovich

According to Finn, McDonagh is a big fan of Malkovich, but it was still her job to be thorough. She and the director spent hours imagining what other actors might bring to the role before settling on him.

She points to his stage pedigree as a founding member of the Steppenwolf Theatre Company. Finn and McDonagh both come from theatre, and she calls him one of the national treasures of American theatre.

The surprise, she said, was the warmth. Finn described Malkovich as capable of being dangerous and mercurial, which suits Chris's violent past, but also vulnerable and at times almost childlike. She said audiences had not necessarily seen those qualities in his earlier work, so she had to make the case for him. The film, she said, needs viewers to care about Chris by the end, and she credits Malkovich's performance, McDonagh's direction and writing, and the ensemble for making that happen.

Finn also talked about the Oscars creating a casting category, now in only its second year. She called the recognition moving and overdue, and named pioneers such as [Marion Dougherty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Dougherty), [Mary Selway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_Selway) and [Lynn Stalmaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynn_Stalmaster).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who plays Chris in Wild Horse Nine?

John Malkovich plays Chris, a veteran CIA agent who is dying of a terminal illness. Sam Rockwell plays his fellow agent Lee.

### Where and when is Wild Horse Nine set?

The film is set in Chile, including Rapa Nui (Easter Island), in the months before Pinochet's 1973 military coup.

### Who cast Wild Horse Nine?

Sarah Finn is the casting director. According to the interview, the producer called her about the project, and Malkovich was not yet attached when she started.
