**Quick summary**

- [Daniel MacPherson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_MacPherson) has joined the cast of sci-fi action thriller Bluefly, according to Variety as relayed by Cinema Express and ARY News.
- He plays Dugan, a Special Forces soldier, alongside [Russell Crowe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Crowe) and [Priyanka Chopra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priyanka_Chopra).
- Production is expected to start soon on Australia's Gold Coast, which stands in for the Congo.
- Separately, Collider reports that Crowe's 2016 film The Nice Guys has reached No. 1 on Apple TV.

Daniel MacPherson has been added to the cast of Bluefly, the sci-fi action thriller starring Russell Crowe and Priyanka Chopra, with filming due to begin shortly in Queensland. The news comes from Variety, as reported by [Cinema Express](https://www.cinemaexpress.com/english/news/2026/Nov/06/daniel-macpherson-joins-priyanka-chopra-and-russell-crowe-in-bluefly) and [ARY News](https://arynews.tv/daniel-macpherson-joins-russell-crowe-priyanka-chopra-for-film-bluefly) on 6 November 2026.

## Who Daniel MacPherson plays in Bluefly

According to ARY News, the Australian actor will play Dugan, a Special Forces soldier hardened by years of combat. He joins the two leads, and the role puts him back alongside Crowe after both appeared in the mixed martial arts drama Beast.

The reunion runs deeper than the cast list. Cinema Express notes that screenwriter [David Frigerio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Frigerio) also worked on Beast, so Bluefly brings the same trio together for a second time.

MacPherson's earlier credits, listed by ARY News, include Land of Bad, Poker Face, A Wrinkle in Time, The Osiris Child and Infini. On television he has appeared in Foundation, Strike Back, APB and The Shannara Chronicles.

## What the Russell Crowe Bluefly story is about

Priyanka Chopra plays a United Nations translator who is still grieving a personal loss when she is pulled into a secret mission in the Congo. The goal is to recover a mysterious aircraft that has crashed. Cinema Express says a Special Ops team pushes upriver through a militia-held zone, and the mission becomes a descent as the team learns a hard truth about the aircraft.

ARY News says Crowe's character has not been described publicly. Cinema Express adds that the film is the first time Crowe and Chopra have worked together.

[Nimród Antal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nimr%C3%B3d_Antal), known for Predators, is directing. The screenplay is credited to Frigerio, [Carlyle Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlyle_Eubank) and [William Eubank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Eubank). Michael Jefferson of Polite Society produces with Frigerio's Broken Open Pictures, Highland Film Group's Delphine Perrier and Arianne Fraser, Deborah Glover of G2 Dispatch and Vanessa Yao Guo.

## Gold Coast stands in for the Congo

Both outlets say production will start soon on the Gold Coast. The region will double for the Congo in the story. For local crews and suppliers, that makes Bluefly another sizeable shoot on the Queensland coast, although neither report gives a start date or a release window.

Cinema Express lists Crowe's other upcoming films as The Last Druid, Billion Dollar Spy and Highlander. Chopra is attached to S.S. Rajamouli's Varanasi, Amri with [Mira Nair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mira_Nair) and Judgment Day.

## The Nice Guys tops Apple TV ten years on

Crowe is also in the streaming charts this week. Collider reports that The Nice Guys, the 1970s Los Angeles detective comedy from director Shane Black, has climbed to No. 1 on Apple TV. Crowe plays enforcer Jackson Healy opposite Ryan Gosling's private eye Holland March.

The path there was slow. Collider says the film earned only about $71 million against a $50 million budget on release, even though reviews were positive. A decade of word of mouth has since built a loyal following. Collider credits the sharp dialogue, inventive action and the pairing of Crowe's deadpan "straight man" with Gosling's physical comedy.
