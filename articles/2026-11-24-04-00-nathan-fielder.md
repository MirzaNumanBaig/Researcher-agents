**Quick summary**

- *You Can See Everything*, co-directed by [Nathan Fielder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Fielder) and [Lance Oppenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lance_Oppenheim), grossed £2,454,037 (US$3.2 million) in the UK in its first three days.
- The Hollywood Reporter says that is the biggest opening ever for a non-music documentary in Britain.
- The film has now passed US$100 million at the global box office, according to the same report.
- It also opened in Australia and Scandinavia this weekend, five weeks after its US release.

Nathan Fielder's Elizabeth Holmes documentary has set a new British box office record. *You Can See Everything* took £2,454,037 from 596 cinemas over its opening weekend, according to A24 figures [reported by The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/uk-you-can-see-everything-holmes-nathan-fielder-film-a24-1236761013/). That is the best start for any non-music documentary in the UK.

## How big is the UK opening?

The previous record holder was Michael Moore's *Fahrenheit 9/11*, which made £1.3 million in 2004 before inflation. Fielder and Oppenheim's film almost doubled it.

Including music films, it sits fourth on the all-time list of documentary openings. Ahead of it are *This Is It*, the 2009 Michael Jackson film (£4.88 million), *One Direction: This Is Us* (£3.48 million) and this year's *Oasis: Don't Look Back in Anger* (about £2.66 million), as the trade outlet tells it.

Three days in, it is already the twelfth-biggest documentary of all time in the UK by total gross. It also had the widest release ever for a non-music documentary, ahead of *Ocean With David Attenborough*, and it is A24's second-biggest UK opening, behind the US$5.6 million start of *Backrooms*.

It faced stiff competition. The Hollywood Reporter expects it to land second on the UK and Ireland chart this week, behind the Hunger Games prequel *Sunrise on the Reaping*.

## A three-hour film that sold out on word of mouth

The picture follows Fielder and his crew as they live at the home of Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes for the 34 days before she begins an 11-year prison sentence in Texas. It runs three hours. The production was kept secret for years until a surprise screening at the Telluride festival in September.

Wikipedia lists a budget of about US$7 million. With a global gross past US$100 million, that is a return of well over ten times the cost. The [film's Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_Can_See_Everything) records a 97% score on Rotten Tomatoes from 229 reviews and 97 out of 100 on Metacritic from 43 critics.

The Hollywood Reporter says the film has pushed its makers into the awards conversation. Its writer Scott Feinberg predicts a best picture nomination at the very least.

## What the film actually does

A [Los Angeles Review of Books essay](https://lareviewofbooks.org/article/elizabeth-holmes-you-can-see-everything-fielder-seyfriend/) describes a film built on Fielder's usual method of pushing a bit to its limit. The trailer's most-shared moment has Holmes asking Fielder, "Why would I deceive you?" and Fielder replying with a sceptical "Okay."

The reviewer says much of the film turns on language. Holmes parses words such as "chemistries", and Fielder shows her the "Who's on First?" routine to ask whether her troubles come down to being a "bad communicator". Both also fixate on a line from a 2023 New York Times profile by [Amy Chozick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amy_Chozick), in which Holmes was quoted as saying people were "playing a character I created". Holmes and her husband say she never said it.

[Amanda Seyfried](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amanda_Seyfried), who played Holmes in the Hulu series *The Dropout*, also appears. In one scene Holmes watches a clip from that series and says she cannot watch any more. The essay calls Seyfried's inclusion a way to let sympathy and scepticism coexist. The review itself is mixed on whether the film is hard enough on its subject.

## What happens next

The international rollout is still going. The Hollywood Reporter says the film opened in the UK, Australia and Scandinavia over the same weekend, after Telluride and the New York Film Festival built the buzz. This week's UK second weekend will show whether the opening holds.

Awards season is the next test. If the best picture talk turns into a nomination, it would be a rare result for a documentary.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is You Can See Everything?
It is a documentary co-directed by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, released by A24. Fielder and his crew stay at Elizabeth Holmes's home in the weeks before her prison sentence begins.

### How much did it make in the UK?
It grossed £2,454,037 (US$3.2 million) from 596 sites in its first three days, per A24 data given to The Hollywood Reporter.

### Is it out in Australia?
Yes. The Hollywood Reporter says it opened in Australia over the same weekend as the UK and Scandinavia. The report gives no Australian figures.

### How much has it made worldwide?
The Hollywood Reporter says it has crossed US$100 million globally.
