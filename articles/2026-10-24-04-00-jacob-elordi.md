**Quick summary**

- An unnamed source told Page Six on 21 October that [Kylie Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kylie_Jenner) is frustrated that [Jacob Elordi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Elordi) and [Kendall Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kendall_Jenner) could marry before she and Timothée Chalamet do.
- The claim is unconfirmed. Neither couple has announced an engagement.
- Representatives for all four did not respond to Page Six, according to Yahoo's account of the report.
- Elordi and Kendall Jenner appeared together at the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on 17 October.

Talk of a possible engagement for Australian actor Jacob Elordi and Kendall Jenner has turned into a story about her sister. According to a Yahoo report on an October 21 Page Six article, an unnamed source says Kylie Jenner is jealous that the pair could get engaged first. It is a single, anonymous account, and nobody involved has confirmed it.

## What the source claims

Kylie, 29, has dated Chalamet since 2023, while Kendall, 30, and Elordi were publicly linked this spring, [Yahoo reports](https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/kylie-jenner-reportedly-jealous-kendall-233059268.html). The insider says Kylie has told Chalamet she wants marriage in their future, though she has not issued an ultimatum or asked for a ring.

The same source describes Chalamet as in love and invested, but not ready to propose. Elordi is portrayed differently: the "Euphoria" actor is said to believe he has found "the one", with the relationship moving quickly. These are the source's characterisations, not statements from either man.

The report landed three days after Chalamet watched Kylie make her Victoria's Secret Fashion Show debut. Her mother, [Kris Jenner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Jenner), was also there, according to PEOPLE as cited by Yahoo.

## A big night out at the Academy Museum Gala

Elordi and Kendall attended the Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on 17 October and posed individually for photographers. Yahoo says Elordi took candid photos of Kendall while her dress was adjusted. She waited with [Kim Kardashian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kim_Kardashian) and [Hailey Bieber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hailey_Bieber) while he posed.

She wore a black-and-white polka-dot Valentino dress with Cartier jewellery. He wore a custom Bottega Veneta tuxedo with a white shirt and sunglasses. The two were also photographed sitting together at dinner inside the event.

## The double date in May

The four were reportedly out together in Los Angeles on 16 May. Photographs published by DeuxMoi showed Kendall and Elordi in the front of a car, with Kylie and Chalamet in the back, and some of the group covered their faces from photographers.

In a 18 May report, a separate source told PEOPLE that Kylie liked Elordi and was excited about Kendall's relationship, and that the four got along. That account added that Chalamet and Elordi already knew each other through Hollywood awards-season circles.

## What Chalamet has said in public

Chalamet has spoken about his relationship only briefly. Accepting the best actor award for [Marty Supreme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marty_Supreme) at the Critics Choice Awards on 4 January, he thanked Kylie, saying "I love you, I couldn't do this without you", as InStyle reported.

At a 1 February Q&A for the film in London, filmmaker [Richard Curtis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Curtis) asked if he had a girlfriend. He said yes. Asked whether he would ever marry, he called the question "so personal", then answered "Yes. Yes" when pressed, according to PEOPLE's account. That is a general answer, not a comment on timing.

## What happens next

Nothing is scheduled. No engagement has been announced, and the only evidence for the jealousy claim is one unnamed insider. Readers should treat it as unverified gossip until either couple speaks for themselves.
