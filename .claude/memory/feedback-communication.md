The owner repeatedly asks for short, to-the-point answers ("just say yes or no", "I don't want long outputs", "in these very simple words") and wants links when they ask for one. They approve work in plain words ("run it", "yes build these") and expect results they can look at (pictures, dashboard) rather than descriptions.

**Why:** They follow the work from the Claude app, often on a phone, and decide quickly.

**How to apply:** Lead with the answer or the result, keep it to a few lines or one small table, say plainly what could not be tested, give the one decision needed, and give the GitHub / Cloudflare link when asked. Report outcomes honestly (failed runs, flagged images) without padding.
